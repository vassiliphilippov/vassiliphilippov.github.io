challenge

noun
demand for password or identification (by guard)
Pronunciation: / ˈtʃælɪndʒ / (AmE), / ˈtʃælɪndʒ / (BrE)
a demand for identification or proof, especially as a security measure (including computer security)

Examples

a security challenge
At the checkpoint, the guard issued a challenge to every approaching vehicle.
Failure to respond to the challenge resulted in the patrol raising the alarm.
The sentry's challenge was answered with the correct password, allowing entry to the base.
