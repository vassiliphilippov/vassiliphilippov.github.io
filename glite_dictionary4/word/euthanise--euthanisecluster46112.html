to euthanise

verb
cause painless death (often to relieve suffering)
Pronunciation: / ˈjuːθəˌnaɪz / (AmE), / ˈjuːθənaɪz / (BrE)
to intentionally cause the painless death of a person or animal, usually to relieve suffering

Examples

to euthanise a suffering animal
New laws regulate how and when doctors may euthanise terminally ill patients.
Some families must make the difficult choice to euthanise a beloved pet.
The veterinarian decided to euthanise the horse to end its suffering.
