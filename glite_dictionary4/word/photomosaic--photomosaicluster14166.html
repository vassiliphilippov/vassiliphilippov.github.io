photomosaic

noun
composite picture from multiple photographs
Pronunciation: / ˌfoʊtoʊmoʊˈzæɪk / (AmE), / ˌfəʊtəʊməʊˈzeɪɪk / (BrE)
a large image made by arranging many smaller photographs so that they form a single, continuous picture

Examples

photomosaic of faces
