repository

noun
facility for storage or safekeeping
Pronunciation: / rɪˈpɑzəˌtɔri /; / rɪˈpɑːzɪˌtɔri / (AmE), / rɪˈpɒzɪtəri / (BrE)
a place (physical or digital) where things are stored or kept for safekeeping
