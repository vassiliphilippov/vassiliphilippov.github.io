region

noun
large area of land or territory
Pronunciation: / ˈriːdʒən / (AmE), / ˈriːdʒən / (BrE)
a large area that is distinct from others because of particular characteristics, boundaries, or conditions (can refer to land, space, or virtual/cloud areas)

Examples

mountain region
Many ancient civilizations developed in the fertile region surrounding the Nile River.
The tropical region is home to a wide variety of plant and animal species.
Wildfires often occur in the western region of the country during dry summers.
Astronomers study different regions of the universe to understand its origins.
The Amazon basin is an important region for global biodiversity.
The company plans to expand into the Asian region next year.
