convertible

noun
sofa that becomes a bed
Pronunciation: / kənˈvɝː.tə.bəl / (AmE), / kənˈvɜː.tɪ.bəl / (BrE)
an object that can change its form or function, such as a car with a removable roof, a sofa that becomes a bed, or a computer that can be used as a tablet
