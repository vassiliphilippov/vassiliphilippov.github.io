to legitimize

verb
make legal or lawful
Pronunciation: / ləˈdʒɪtəˌmaɪz / (AmE), / lɪˈdʒɪtɪmaɪz / (BrE)

Examples

to legitimize same-sex marriage

Recent reforms aim to legitimize previously prohibited business practices.
The government decided to legitimize the use of medical cannabis.
