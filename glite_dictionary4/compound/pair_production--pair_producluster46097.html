pair production

noun
creation of an electron-positron pair by a photon
Pronunciation: / ˈper prəˌdʌkʃən / (AmE), / ˈpeə prəˌdʌkʃən / (BrE)
the process in physics where a high-energy photon is converted into an electron and a positron

Examples
