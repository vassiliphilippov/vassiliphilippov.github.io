contact lens

noun
lens worn on eye to correct vision
Pronunciation: / ˈkɑntækt lɛnz / (AmE), / ˈkɒntækt lɛnz / (BrE)
a thin lens worn directly on the surface of the eye to correct vision, deliver medication, or provide electronic functions such as health monitoring or information display

Examples

wear a contact lens
I find that wearing a contact lens is more comfortable than glasses.
She lost her contact lens while swimming in the pool.
The doctor prescribed a new type of contact lens for my astigmatism.
