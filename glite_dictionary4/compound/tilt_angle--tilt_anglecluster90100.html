tilt angle

noun
angle between rocket and vertical
Pronunciation: / tɪlt ˈæŋɡəl / (AmE), / tɪlt ˈæŋɡəl / (BrE)
the angle at which something (such as a rocket, surface, or device) is tilted relative to a reference direction (vertical or horizontal), often to optimize performance
