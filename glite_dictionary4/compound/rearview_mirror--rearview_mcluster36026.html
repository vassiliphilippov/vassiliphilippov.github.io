rearview mirror

noun
mirror for seeing behind in a car
Pronunciation: / ˈrɪr.vju ˌmɪr.ɚ / (AmE), / ˈrɪə.vju ˌmɪr.ər / (BrE)
a mirror or screen in a vehicle that lets the driver see what is behind the car, either using reflective glass or a camera feed
