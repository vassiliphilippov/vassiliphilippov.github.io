titanium dioxide

(in materials science and consumer products) a white inorganic compound (TiO2) that acts as a photocatalyst under light, decomposing organic pollutants and microbes and so used in self-cleaning coatings and air or water purification.

Examples

titanium dioxide cleans surfaces

  • titanium dioxide – singular (base)
  • titanium dioxides – plural
