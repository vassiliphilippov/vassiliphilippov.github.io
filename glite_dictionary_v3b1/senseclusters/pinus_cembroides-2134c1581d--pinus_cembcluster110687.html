Etymology

Pinus cembroides is the scientific name for a small pine that makes edible 'pinyon nuts'. Imagine low pines on dry hills in Mexico and southern Texas with groups of two or three short needles and many brown nuts that animals and people gather. That picture helps you remember the name means a small pine with tasty seeds.
