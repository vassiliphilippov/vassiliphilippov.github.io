Etymology

Scrub plane comes from the idea of 'scrubbing' rough wood away with a hand tool. The name links scrub (like scrubbing dirt) and plane (a tool for shaving wood), because you use it to quickly take off a lot of wood, so it means 'a tool to remove a lot of wood quickly'.
