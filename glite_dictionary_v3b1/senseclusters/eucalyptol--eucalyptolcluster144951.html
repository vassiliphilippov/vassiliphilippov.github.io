Etymology

eucalyptol comes from the tree name eucalyptus, because this chemical is the main part of eucalyptus oil. The ending -ol, seen in the word alcohol, tells you it is an 'alcohol', so eucalyptol is the 'alcohol' from eucalyptus that gives its minty, camphor-like smell.
