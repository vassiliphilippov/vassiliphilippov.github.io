Etymology

Dacrydium laxifolium may come from old plant naming, because the second part, laxifolium, joins laxi which means 'loose' and folium which means 'leaf'. This gives an easy picture of a low, trailing shrub with loose, spreading leaves, so the name fits the plant today.
