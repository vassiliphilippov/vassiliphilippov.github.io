Etymology

The name black whale was used by old whalers to mean large whales hunted for their valuable 'oil' and 'ambergris' because the animals and their oil looked dark. That's why black whale came to mean a big whale, like the sperm whale, with a space in its head that held this useful oil and perfume material.
