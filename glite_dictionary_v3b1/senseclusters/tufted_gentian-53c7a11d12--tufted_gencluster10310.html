Etymology

tufted gentian is a name that shows what the plant looks like: tufted means it grows in small 'tufts' or clumps, and gentian is the old name for a bright blue mountain flower, said to be named long ago after King Gentius. So the name helps you picture a small blue gentian growing in little clumps on the mountain.
