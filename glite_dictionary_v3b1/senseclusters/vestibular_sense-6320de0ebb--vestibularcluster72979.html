Etymology

The term vestibular sense comes from small parts in the inner ear that tell your brain about balance and movement. Tiny stones and fluid move and bend little hairs when you turn or stop, so that feeling tells you 'which way is up' and explains why vestibular sense means your body's sense of 'balance' and 'movement'.
