Etymology

creatio ex nihilo is a Latin phrase made of creatio meaning 'creation', ex meaning 'out of', and nihilo meaning 'nothing'. It comes from the religious belief that God made the world when there was nothing before. So, creatio ex nihilo means 'creation out of nothing' and is used to name that belief.
