Etymology

indefinite pronoun comes from the parts indefinite, meaning 'not fixed', and pronoun, meaning 'a word that stands for a noun'. Picture a word that points at 'someone' or 'something' without naming them, and you will see why it means a pronoun that does not point to a named person, thing, or amount.
