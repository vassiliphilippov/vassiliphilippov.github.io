Etymology

polemarch comes from Greek parts polemos 'war' and archos 'leader'. In some city-states like Athens, a polemarch was an elected official who handled public ceremonies, legal cases about foreigners, and funerals for soldiers, so the name fits someone with both military and civic authority.
