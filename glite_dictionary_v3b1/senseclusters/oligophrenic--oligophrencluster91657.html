Etymology

oligophrenic comes from Greek parts oligo- 'few' or 'small' and phren 'mind', so it literally meant 'small mind'. A helpful way to remember this is that the origin makes the word feel insulting, which is why oligophrenic is now an outdated and offensive noun and people use respectful words like 'person with an intellectual disability' instead.
