Etymology

Tent flap is a simple, literal name: tent + flap describes the small piece of cloth that covers the tent entrance. Imagine lifting the flap to get inside, or the wind making it flap, and you can see why it means 'a piece of material that can be opened or closed to allow entrance to a tent'.
