Etymology

The word Yenisei-Samoyed comes from Yenisei, the name of a big Siberian river, and Samoyed, an old Russian name for some Uralic 'people'. So the name simply means the Uralic language of the people by the Yenisei River, and today scholars often use other, more specific names.
