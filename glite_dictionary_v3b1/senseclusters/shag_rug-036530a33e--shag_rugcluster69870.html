Etymology

shag rug probably comes from the old word shag, meaning long, rough hair or fur, and the word rug for a floor covering; these carpets were made with long, fluffy fibres that look like shaggy hair. So, shag rug means a rug with a deep, thick, 'shaggy' pile.
