Etymology

hypophysectomy breaks into hypo- 'under', hypophysis 'pituitary gland', and -ectomy 'cutting out', like in appendectomy. It means surgery to remove the pituitary, the small gland under the brain, so the word literally says 'cutting out the gland under the brain'.
