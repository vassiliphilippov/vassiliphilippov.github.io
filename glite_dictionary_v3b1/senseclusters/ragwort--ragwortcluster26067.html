Etymology

The word ragwort comes from rag ('torn cloth') and wort ('plant'), like in St John's wort, because the plant's leaves look ragged and torn. So ragwort is a 'rag-like plant' with yellow, daisy-like flowers and is often seen as a weed that farmers remove because it can be toxic to animals.
