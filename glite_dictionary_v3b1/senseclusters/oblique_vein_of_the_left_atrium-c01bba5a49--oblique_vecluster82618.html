Etymology

oblique vein of the left atrium is named for how it looks and where it sits: the small obliquevein runs slanted across the back wall of the left atrium and pours into a larger vein called the coronary sinus, and it is sometimes called 'Marshall's vein' after the doctor who described it. So the name reminds you that it is a slanted vein on the left upper heart chamber that drains into the coronary sinus.
