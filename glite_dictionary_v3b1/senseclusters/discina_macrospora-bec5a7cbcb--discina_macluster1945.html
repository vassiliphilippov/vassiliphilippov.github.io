Etymology

Discina macrospora comes from old words that describe how the fungus looks: Discina is like the word 'disk', meaning a flat plate, and macro means 'big' while spora means 'spores'. So the name gives a clear picture of a brown, saucer-shaped fungus with large spores, which helps you remember what it looks like and that it is not for eating.
