Etymology

hopped-up as slang can be remembered from hop ('to jump' or 'be excited') plus up ('high'), a bit like saying someone is 'pumped up'. A helpful way to remember it is that hopped-up means someone is made excited or 'high' by drugs, which is why it describes being on drugs.
