Etymology

The word hyperpiesis comes from Greek parts hyper- meaning 'over' and -piesis meaning 'pressure'. Think of hyper- like in hyperactive and the everyday word 'pressure', so hyperpiesis means 'too much pressure' and is used for very high blood pressure with no clear cause.
