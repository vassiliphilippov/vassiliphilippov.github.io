Etymology

harmonic progression comes from music: when a string gives higher notes, the reciprocals, which are the numbers you get by dividing 1 by each value, fall into even steps. So a harmonic progression is a list of numbers whose reciprocals make an 'arithmetic progression' or equal steps, and that is why the name connects the math idea to musical harmonics.
