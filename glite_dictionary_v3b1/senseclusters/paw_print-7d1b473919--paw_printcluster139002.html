Etymology

paw print shows the simple picture of a paw (an animal's foot) pressing into soft ground and leaving a print. Imagine a cat or dog making that small stamp in mud or on the floor; that clear image is why paw print means 'a mark left by an animal's foot' and helps people tell which animal was there.
