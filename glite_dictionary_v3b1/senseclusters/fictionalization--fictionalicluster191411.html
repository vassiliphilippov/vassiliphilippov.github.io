Etymology

fictionalization starts with fiction, which means a 'made-up' story, and ends with -ization, a common ending that shows a process or result. The root idea of fiction is 'to make' or 'shape', similar to fabricate which also means to 'make', so a fictionalization is the process of 'making up' a version of real events.
