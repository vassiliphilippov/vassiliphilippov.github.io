Etymology

Ulysses Grant was actually born Hiram Ulysses Grant, but one popular story says a congressman accidentally wrote his name as 'Ulysses S. Grant' when he sent it to West Point, and the new name stuck. He later led the Union armies to victory in the Civil War and became the 18th President, so that name is remembered for military skill and national leadership.
