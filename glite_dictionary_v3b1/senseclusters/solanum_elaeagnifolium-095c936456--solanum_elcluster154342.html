Etymology

Solanum elaeagnifolium is a scientific plant name that describes how the plant looks: Solanum shows it is a 'nightshade', and elaeagnifolium combines elaeagnus, a word for 'olive-like', with folium, the Latin for 'leaf'. That's why the name points to the plant's silvery, olive-like leaves and helps people identify it.
