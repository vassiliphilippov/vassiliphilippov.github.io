Etymology

Sarcobatus vermiculatus probably comes from Greek and Latin words that describe the plant: Sarcobatus from Greek 'sarco' meaning 'flesh' for its thick, fleshy stems, and vermiculatus from Latin 'vermiculatus' meaning 'worm-like' or 'full of small wrinkles' for its wrinkled fruits and branches. So the name gives a simple picture of the low, hardy greasewood shrub found in salty desert soils.
