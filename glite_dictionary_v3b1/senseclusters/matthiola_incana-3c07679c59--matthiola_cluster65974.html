Etymology

Matthiola incana may come from a person's name and a simple Latin word that describes the leaves. The first part, Matthiola, honors Pietro Andrea Mattioli, a doctor who wrote about plants, and the second part, incana, means 'gray' because the leaves have tiny hairs that make them look a little gray, so the name helps you remember this sweet-scented garden flower.
