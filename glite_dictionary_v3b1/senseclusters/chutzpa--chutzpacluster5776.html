Etymology

chutzpa comes from Yiddish and Hebrew chutzpah, which means 'shameless audacity' or 'insolence'. Think of it like the English word 'nerve' — the bold, rude courage to do something surprising, and that is why chutzpa means daring, impudent behaviour.
