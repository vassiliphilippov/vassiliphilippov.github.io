Etymology

a little slice of Mayberry comes from the old TV show 'The Andy Griffith Show', where the made-up town of Mayberry was calm, old-fashioned, and full of friendly people. So when someone says it, they mean 'a small, peaceful, friendly town feeling', a simple and charming life like in the show.
