Etymology

benevolence comes from Latin parts bene meaning 'well' and volence meaning 'wishing' or 'willing', so it literally means 'wishing well' and shows a wish to do good. A helpful link is volunteer with the same vol- root; that's why benevolence means kindness or an act of kindness.
