Etymology

Jinnah is the family name of Muhammad Ali Jinnah, the founder of Pakistan, so it usually refers to that famous leader. A helpful way to remember it is that Jinnah sounds like the Arabic word jannah meaning 'paradise', which helps explain why many people say his name with deep respect.
