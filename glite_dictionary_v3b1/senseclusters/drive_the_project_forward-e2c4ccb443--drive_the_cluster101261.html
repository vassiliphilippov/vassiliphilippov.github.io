Etymology

The phrase drive the project forward comes from the image of someone driving a car or leading a team so the work moves ahead: to drive means to push or steer and forward means ahead. So, imagining a person at the wheel moving the work past obstacles helps explain why it means 'to actively promote or make progress with a project'.
