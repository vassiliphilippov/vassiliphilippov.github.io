Etymology

The noun yellow watercress names a water plant: watercress is a peppery green that grows in wet places, and yellow points to its small yellow flowers or yellow leaves. Picture those low green plants with yellow flowers spreading in ditches, and you can see why people add them to a 'salad' for flavor but also call them a 'weed' when they take over fields.
