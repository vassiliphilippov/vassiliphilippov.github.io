Etymology

pine tar comes from heating pine wood until a dark, sticky liquid called tar came out. People put that sticky substance on wooden ships and ropes to 'waterproof' them, rubbed it on bats for a better grip, or used it in ointments to 'soothe' skin, so today pine tar means that dark, sticky protective substance.
