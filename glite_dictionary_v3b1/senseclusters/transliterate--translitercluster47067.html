Etymology

transliterate comes from Latin parts trans- 'across' and littera 'letter', which gives a clear image of moving letters from one place to another. So, to transliterate today is to write the sounds or letters of one alphabet using the letters of another alphabet, for example writing Arabic or Japanese with Roman letters.
