Etymology

The exclamation Bah humbug comes from Charles Dickens' story 'A Christmas Carol', where the grumpy man Scrooge shouts Bah! and calls everything humbug to mean 'nonsense'. That's why today people say Bah humbug to show contempt or to reject festive cheer.
