Etymology

Carduelis cucullata may come from old Latin words: Carduelis was used for small finches like the 'goldfinch', and cucullata means 'hooded' because the bird has a dark, hood-like head. That's why the name points to a small, bright finch with a dark 'hood', the red siskin.
