Etymology

The card game gin rummy may get its name by joining rummy, the older family of card games, with gin, the strong drink. One popular story says players called this version like a quick 'shot' of gin because it was faster and sharper than older rummy games, so the name came to mean the fast two-player game where you try to make sets and finish early.
