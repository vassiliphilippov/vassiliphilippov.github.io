Etymology

blindman's bluff comes from the simple scene where one player is blindfolded, the blind man, while the others try to sneak past or trick him. The word bluff here means 'a trick', so the name shows a blind player trying to catch people who are fooling him, which is why it means the children's game with a blindfolded seeker.
