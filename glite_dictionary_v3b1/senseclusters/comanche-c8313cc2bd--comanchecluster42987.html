Etymology

Comanche comes from a Ute word komantsi meaning 'enemy' or 'anyone who wants to fight', which the Spanish used as the name for the tribe. Because the Comanche were famous as strong horse riders and fighters on the plains, this name stuck and is still used for the people today.
