Etymology

os triquetrum comes from Latin words: os meaning 'bone' and triquetrum meaning 'three-cornered'. The name fits the small triangular wrist bone because it is shaped like a three-cornered piece and touches three nearby bones, so the word helps you remember its shape and place.
