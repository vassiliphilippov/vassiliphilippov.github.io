Etymology

The noun brazil-nut tree names a very tall Amazon tree that makes the hard seeds called 'Brazil nuts'. The name joins brazil, nut, and tree to show it is literally the tree that produces those nuts, so brazil-nut tree means 'the tree that produces Brazil nuts'.
