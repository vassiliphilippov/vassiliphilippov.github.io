Etymology

jack bean may get its name from jack, a word for something ordinary and useful, plus bean. Farmers used this strong bean for many jobs, as animal feed and to make the soil better, so people called it a 'jack-of-all-trades' plant. That's why the name suggests a tough, 'all-purpose' crop.
