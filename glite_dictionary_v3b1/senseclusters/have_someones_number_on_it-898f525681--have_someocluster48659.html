Etymology

have someone's number on it may come from the simple image of writing a person's number on an object to mark who it is for, like a ticket or a label that shows who is chosen. So if a rock or a rule 'has your number on it', the picture is that you are marked, and it is 'meant to affect or cause trouble to you'.
