Etymology

The phrase ecumenical council comes from an old church idea: ecumenical means 'the whole world' and council means 'a meeting of leaders'. So the image is leaders from everywhere meeting together, which is why the phrase now means a gathering of bishops from across the Christian world to decide important church teaching.
