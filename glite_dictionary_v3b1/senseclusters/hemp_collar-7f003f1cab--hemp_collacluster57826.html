Etymology

Hemp collar comes from the simple idea of a collar made from hemp, a strong natural plant fiber people used for ropes and clothes. Because hemp is durable and gentle on skin, the term today means a pet or fashion collar that is 'eco-friendly' and 'gentle on skin'.
