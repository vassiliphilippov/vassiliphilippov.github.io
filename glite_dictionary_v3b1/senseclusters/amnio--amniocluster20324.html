Etymology

amnio is a short form of amniocentesis, the medical test that takes fluid from the baby's sac in the womb. It comes from Greek amnion, from amnos meaning 'lamb'—a helpful way to remember is to think of the sac like a little lambskin, so amnio names the procedure on that sac.
