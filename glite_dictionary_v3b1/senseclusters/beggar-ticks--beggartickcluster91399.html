Etymology

beggar-ticks comes from the image of the plant's small, barbed fruits as ticks that cling, and the beggar part suggests they 'beg' a ride on passing people or animals. So the name helps you remember these yellow-flowered plants have prickly fruits that stick to fur and clothing.
