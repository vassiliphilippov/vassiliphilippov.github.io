Etymology

The word afibrinogenemia breaks into three parts: a- meaning 'not', fibrinogen the protein that helps blood form clots, and -emia meaning 'blood condition' like in anemia. So afibrinogenemia means the blood has no fibrinogen, which is why people with it can bleed for a long time.
