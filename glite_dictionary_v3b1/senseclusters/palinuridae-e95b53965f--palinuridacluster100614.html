Etymology

Palinuridae comes from the genus Palinurus. A helpful way to remember it is to see palin- like the start of palindrome, meaning 'again', and to think of a 'tail', so picture a spiny lobster flicking its tail, which fits because Palinuridae are the family of spiny lobsters.
