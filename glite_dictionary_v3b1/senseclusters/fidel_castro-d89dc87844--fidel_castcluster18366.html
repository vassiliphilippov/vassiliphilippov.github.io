Etymology

Fidel Castro is a Cuban leader whose name comes from Spanish roots: Fidel means 'faithful' and Castro comes from a word for a 'fort' or 'castle'. He led the 1959 revolution and then ruled Cuba for decades, so today the name stands for that man and his government.
