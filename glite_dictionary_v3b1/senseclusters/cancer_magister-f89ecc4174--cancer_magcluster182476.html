Etymology

Cancer magister comes from Latin words: Cancer meant 'crab' and magister meant 'master', so the name gives the image of a 'master crab', a large, important crab on the Pacific coast. That's why the Latin name refers to the edible animal we now call the 'Dungeness crab'.
