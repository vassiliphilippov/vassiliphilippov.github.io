Etymology

Corvidae comes from the Latin corvus, meaning 'raven' or 'crow', plus the scientific ending -idae that means animal 'family'. So, Corvidae is the 'family' of crow-like birds such as crows, ravens, magpies and jays, which helps you remember it as the group of clever, adaptable birds.
