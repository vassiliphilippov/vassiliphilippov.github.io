Etymology

bittersweet nightshade comes from two parts: bittersweet because the bright red berries look sweet but taste bitter and can be poisonous, and nightshade because the plant often grows in dark, shady places and was grouped with other 'nightshade' plants known for danger. So the name warns that the plant is 'poisonous'.
