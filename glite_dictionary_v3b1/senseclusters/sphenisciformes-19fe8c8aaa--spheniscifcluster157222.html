Etymology

The word Sphenisciformes comes from the Greek name Spheniscus, where sphen- means 'wedge' and the ending -iformes is a scientific ending for a 'group of animals'. A helpful image is a penguin's short, wedge-shaped body and beak, and that's why Sphenisciformes names the order of flightless water birds we call penguins.
