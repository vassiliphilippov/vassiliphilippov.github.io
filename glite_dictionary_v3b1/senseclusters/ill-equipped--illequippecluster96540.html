Etymology

ill-equipped breaks into ill- meaning 'badly' or 'not' and equip meaning 'to give tools or prepare', as in equipment. So ill-equipped literally means 'badly prepared' or 'not having the right tools', which explains why we use it for people or places that cannot handle a task or situation.
