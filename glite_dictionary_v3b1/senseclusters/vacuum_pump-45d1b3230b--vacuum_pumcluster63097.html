Etymology

vacuum pump comes from a clear image: vacuum means 'empty' and pump is a machine that moves or pushes, so it is a machine that pumps air out to make a space empty. That's why vacuum pump means a device that 'removes the air or gas' from a closed space to create a vacuum.
