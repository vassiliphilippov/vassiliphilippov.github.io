Etymology

Commonwealth of Nations comes from the old idea of a common good and wealth meaning shared resources or well‑being. It began when Britain and many places it once ruled chose to stay friends and share benefits after becoming 'independent', so today the name means a group of countries that 'cooperate' and help each other.
