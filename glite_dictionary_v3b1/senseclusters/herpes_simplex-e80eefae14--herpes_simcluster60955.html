Etymology

herpes simplex takes its name from herpes, an old word meaning 'to creep', because the painful blisters can appear to crawl or spread along the skin. The second part, simplex, means 'simple' or 'single' to mark it as the usual, not-complicated form, which is why the name now describes the viral infection with small blisters.
