Etymology

what, me worry comes from the American comic magazine Mad, where the silly, gap-toothed cartoon Alfred E. Neuman always wore a grin and used the line as a joke. Because the character looked happy and relaxed when he said what, me worry, the phrase now means 'not worried' or 'carefree'.
