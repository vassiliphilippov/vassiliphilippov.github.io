Etymology

antineoplastic antibiotic comes from joining anti (meaning 'against') and neoplastic (meaning 'new growth', a tumor) with antibiotic (a drug first found in bacteria). Many cancer drugs were discovered in bacteria and found to stop the growth of fast-dividing tumor cells, so today antineoplastic antibiotics are drugs used in chemotherapy to fight cancer.
