Etymology

siree is a friendly, old-fashioned way to say 'yes' strongly and probably comes from the spoken word sure with a playful ending -ee added for extra force. So saying siree is like saying a very firm 'sure' or 'certainly', which is why people use it to show a definite 'yes'.
