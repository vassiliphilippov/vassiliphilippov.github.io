Etymology

prosit comes from the Latin word prodesse, with pro- 'for' (like the English pro- meaning 'for') and esse 'to be', so it originally meant 'may it be for your good'. That's why people shout prosit when they raise a glass — they are wishing good health or good fortune to the group.
