Etymology

Sphenopsida comes from Greek parts sphen- meaning 'wedge' and -opsida meaning 'appearance'. A helpful way to remember it is to picture horsetails and their fossil relatives with jointed, stacked stems that look like a row of wedge-like parts, so the name fits this group of plants.
