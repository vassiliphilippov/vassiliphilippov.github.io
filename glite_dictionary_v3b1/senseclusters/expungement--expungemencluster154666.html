Etymology

expungement comes from the parts ex- 'out' and pungere 'to prick', so it once meant to 'prick out' or mark something off a page, like crossing out a line; you can see the same root in puncture 'to make a small hole'. So expungement now means completely erasing or removing something from a document, record, or memory.
