Etymology

Saint Elmo's light may come from sailors' stories: when a pointed thing like a ship's mast or a plane's wing glows blue or purple in a storm, sailors thought Saint Elmo was giving them 'protection'. So today Saint Elmo's light means that strange blue glow you sometimes see on the tips of ships or aircraft in bad weather.
