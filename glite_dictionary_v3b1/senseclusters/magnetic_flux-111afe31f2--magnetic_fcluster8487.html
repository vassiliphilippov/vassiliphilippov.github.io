Etymology

magnetic flux comes from a simple image: magnetic is about magnets and flux means 'flow', so it suggests magnetism moving through a surface. Imagine magnetic field lines like water flowing through a loop; the more 'flow' that goes through, the greater the magnetic flux, so the term means the total magnetic field passing through an area.
