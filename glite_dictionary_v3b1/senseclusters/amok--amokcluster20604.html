Etymology

amok comes from the Malay/Indonesian word amuk, which meant 'to attack violently' or rush at people in a wild way. Think of the English phrase 'run amok' — people running wildly and out of control — and that's why amok means behaving in a frenzied, uncontrolled, or violent way.
