Etymology

hemming-stitch comes from hem meaning 'edge' and stitch meaning 'to sew', so it is a small stitch used to hold a folded edge of cloth in place. Think of tiny, neat stitches along the bottom of a skirt or dress — that is a hemming-stitch and it keeps the hem tidy.
