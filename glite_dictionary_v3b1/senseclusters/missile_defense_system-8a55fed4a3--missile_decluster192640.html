Etymology

missile defense system comes from its parts: missile meaning 'a flying weapon', defense meaning 'protecting', and system meaning 'a group of parts'. Picture radars and small rockets working like a shield that find and stop incoming missiles, so the phrase means 'a set of tools to protect against missile attacks'.
