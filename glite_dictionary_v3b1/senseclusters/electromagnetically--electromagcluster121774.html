Etymology

electromagnetically comes from joining electro- ('electricity') and magnet ('a thing that pulls metal'), with -ly to make an adverb. Think of an electromagnet — a piece of metal that becomes magnetic when electricity flows — so to do something electromagnetically is to do it 'by using electricity and magnets'.
