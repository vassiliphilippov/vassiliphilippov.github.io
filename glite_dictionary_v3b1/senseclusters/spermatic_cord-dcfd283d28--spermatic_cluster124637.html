Etymology

spermatic cord is a name for the rope-like bundle that hangs the testis and carries the tiny tubes and blood vessels for the testis. The word spermatic points to 'sperm' and cord means 'rope', so the phrase gives a clear image of a 'rope' that holds and supplies the testis.
