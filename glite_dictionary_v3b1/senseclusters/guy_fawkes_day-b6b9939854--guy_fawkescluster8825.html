Etymology

Guy Fawkes Day is named after Guy Fawkes, a man who helped plan to blow up the English Parliament in 1605. When the plan failed, people lit bonfires, set off fireworks and burned a model of Guy Fawkes, and today the day is still marked by bonfires and fireworks to 'remember' that event.
