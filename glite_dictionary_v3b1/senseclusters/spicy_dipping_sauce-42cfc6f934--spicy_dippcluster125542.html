Etymology

spicy dipping sauce gives a simple picture: spicy means hot, dipping shows you put food into it, and sauce is the liquid you eat with food. So, the name may come from the image of dipping a fry or a piece of food into a small bowl of hot sauce, and that's why it means 'a sauce with a spicy flavor served for dipping'.
