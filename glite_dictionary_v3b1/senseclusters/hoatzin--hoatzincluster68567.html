Etymology

hoatzin comes from a native South American name, often written huacín or hoazin, that reached English through Spanish or Portuguese; it was the local name for this strange Amazon bird. A helpful way to remember hoatzin is to think of the 'stinkbird' of the forest — its strong smell and the clawed fingers on its chicks make the name easy to link to the animal.
