Etymology

Pollyannaish comes from the name Pollyanna, a girl in a story who always looked for the good in everything, plus the ending -ish, which means 'like'. So, to be Pollyannaish is to be 'like Pollyanna' — very hopeful and sometimes unrealistically positive.
