Etymology

boeuf Bourguignonne is a French name: boeuf means 'beef' and Bourguignonne means 'from Burgundy', a region famous for red wine, so cooks would stew beef in wine with onions and mushrooms. That's why today it means a dish of beef cooked in red wine and vegetables.
