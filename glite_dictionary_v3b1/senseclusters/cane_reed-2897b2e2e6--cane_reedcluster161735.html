Etymology

cane reed comes from the two words cane and reed, where cane is a strong, hollow stem like a walking stick and reed is a grass that grows by water. So cane reed describes a tall, strong wetland grass people used for baskets and mats, and today it means 'a tall grass that grows in wet places'.
