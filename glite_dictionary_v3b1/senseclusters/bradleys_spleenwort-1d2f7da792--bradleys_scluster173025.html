Etymology

Bradley's spleenwort is named for a botanist called Bradley and the old name spleenwort, a word for ferns people once thought could help the 'spleen'. So the name simply means the particular 'spleenwort' that Bradley found, which is why it refers to this species of fern that grows in rocky places.
