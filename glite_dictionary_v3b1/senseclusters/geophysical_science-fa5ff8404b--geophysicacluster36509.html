Etymology

geophysical science combines geo- meaning 'Earth' and physical meaning 'using physics or physical tools'. Imagine scientists sending waves into the ground or using magnets to 'see' inside the planet, so the phrase means the part of science that uses physical methods 'to study' the 'Earth'.
