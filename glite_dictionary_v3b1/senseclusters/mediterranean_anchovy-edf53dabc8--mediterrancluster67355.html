Etymology

The name Mediterranean anchovy comes from a simple picture: Mediterranean shows the sea region it comes from and anchovy is a very small, salty fish. These tiny fish were often salted and kept to give strong 'flavor' to sauces and dishes, so the name now means that preserved, strong-tasting fish used to add depth to food.
