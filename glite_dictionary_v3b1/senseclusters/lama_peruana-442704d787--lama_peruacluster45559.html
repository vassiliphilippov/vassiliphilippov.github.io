Etymology

Lama peruana comes from the Spanish words Lama and peruana meaning 'Peruvian', so it literally names a Peruvian llama. Picture a strong, woolly animal carrying loads up mountain paths — that image explains why Lama peruana refers to the tame Andean animal people use for carrying goods and for its wool.
