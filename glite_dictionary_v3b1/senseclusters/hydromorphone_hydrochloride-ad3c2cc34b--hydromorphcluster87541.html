Etymology

hydromorphone hydrochloride mixes word parts to show what it is: hydro- means 'water', morphine is a strong pain drug named after Morpheus, the Greek god of 'sleep', and hydrochloride shows it is a salt form that helps the medicine work in the body. So the name points to a powerful painkiller used for serious pain.
