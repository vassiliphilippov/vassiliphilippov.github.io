Etymology

Richard III was a 'king of England' from 1483 to 1485 who took the crown from his young nephew and died in the battle that ended his rule. His story of the missing princes, Shakespeare's play, and the later discovery of his bones under a parking lot is why people still remember him as that troubled king today.
