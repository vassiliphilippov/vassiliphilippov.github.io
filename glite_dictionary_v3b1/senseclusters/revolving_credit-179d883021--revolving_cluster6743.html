Etymology

revolving credit may come from the image of a turning door or wheel, where revolving means 'turning back' and credit means borrowing. So, like a door that keeps opening, revolving credit lets you borrow up to a limit, pay some back, and borrow again, which is why it means 'a credit line you can use again and again'.
