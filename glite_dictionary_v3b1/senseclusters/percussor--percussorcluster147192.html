Etymology

percussor comes from the Latin word percutere meaning 'to strike' or 'to hit', and you can see the same idea in percussion instruments like drums that are hit. So, a percussor is a small hitting tool, a rubber-headed hammer doctors use to tap your knee and check reflexes.
