Etymology

hypodermic needle The word hypodermic needle comes from Greek parts: hypo meaning 'under' and dermic meaning 'skin'. It pictures a needle that goes just under the skin to put in medicine or take out blood, so it now means 'a needle used to inject or remove fluids through the skin'.
