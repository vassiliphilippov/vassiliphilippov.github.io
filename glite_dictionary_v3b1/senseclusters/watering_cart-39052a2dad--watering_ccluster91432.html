Etymology

The name watering cart is very literal: it is a cart that does watering, with a tank and a sprinkler that drives along streets to 'spray' water, make the dust fall, and clean the road. That's why it means 'a vehicle with a tank and sprinkler used to spray roads or clean surfaces'.
