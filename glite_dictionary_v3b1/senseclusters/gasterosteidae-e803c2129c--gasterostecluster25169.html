Etymology

The word Gasterosteidae comes from the Greek roots gaster- 'stomach' and oste- 'bone', giving the image of a 'bony belly'. This is a helpful image because sticklebacks have hard bony plates and spines along their belly and sides, so the name fits this family of small spiny fish.
