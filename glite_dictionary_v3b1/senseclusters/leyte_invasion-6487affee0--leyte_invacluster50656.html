Etymology

Leyte invasion names the Allied landing on Leyte island in October 1944, when General MacArthur came back and said 'I have returned'. The strong picture of ships and soldiers returning, and the first use of Japanese 'kamikaze' attacks there, is why the name marks the big return of US forces and a turning point in the war.
