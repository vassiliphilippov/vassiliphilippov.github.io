Etymology

kitten-tails comes from people noticing that the plant's fluffy flower spikes look like a baby cat's tail, with the parts kitten ('small cat') and tails ('tail') showing that comparison. That's why kitten-tails is the name for a North American plant called Besseya with soft, tail-shaped spikes of tiny flowers.
