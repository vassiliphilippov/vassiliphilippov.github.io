Etymology

Metrazol shock treatment is named for the drug Metrazol and the violent shock the drug caused in the body. Doctors injected Metrazol to bring on strong 'fits' and hoped that sudden 'shocks' would help people with severe 'mental illness', but the method was dangerous and is no longer used.
