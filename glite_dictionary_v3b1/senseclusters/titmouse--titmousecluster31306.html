Etymology

titmouse comes from old English parts: tit meaning 'small' and an old word mase meaning 'small bird', but people later changed mase to mouse, probably because the bird is tiny and quick like a little mouse. So titmouse simply means a 'small bird', which fits the tiny songbird you see moving through the branches.
