Etymology

Tomistoma schlegeli may come from the words tomis meaning 'cut' and stoma meaning 'mouth', which gives the image of a long, thin snout like a 'cut' mouth. The second name, schlegeli, comes from the scientist Schlegel, so the full name helps you remember this long-snouted crocodile often called the 'false gharial'.
