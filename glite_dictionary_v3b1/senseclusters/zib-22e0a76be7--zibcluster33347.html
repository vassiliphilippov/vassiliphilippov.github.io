Etymology

ZiB is short for zebibyte, made from zebi- and -byte, and it follows names like gigabyte and megabyte so the pattern is familiar. The zebi- part includes bi, which means 'two' or 'binary', so a ZiB is a very large binary block of bytes equal to 2 to the power of 70 and is used to measure huge amounts of digital data.
