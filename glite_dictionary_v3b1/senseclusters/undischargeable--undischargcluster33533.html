Etymology

undischargeable is made of un- 'not', dis- 'away' and charge 'load' or 'debt'. Think of discharge as 'take away a debt or obligation', so an undischargeable debt is one that cannot be wiped out or released, which is why it means 'not able to be forgiven or removed'.
