Etymology

disingenuousness is built from the beginning dis- 'not' plus ingenuous 'open' or 'honest', so it means the quality of being not honest or pretending to be less involved than you really are. A helpful way to remember it is to think of genuine 'real'—disingenuousness is when someone is not 'real' or is pretending.
