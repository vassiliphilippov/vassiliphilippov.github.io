Etymology

exquisiteness comes from Latin parts ex- 'out' and quisit 'seek', so it originally meant something 'sought out' or carefully chosen. So, to call something exquisite is to say it was made or chosen with great care, and exquisiteness means 'extreme beauty' and 'delicacy'.
