Etymology

order Loricata comes from the Latin word lorica meaning 'armor', so Loricata literally means 'armored ones' and gives the image of crocodiles' bony plates. Early scientists used this Latin name for the group, but today the animals are usually called 'Crocodylia', so the phrase once named the 'armored' order of crocodiles and their relatives.
