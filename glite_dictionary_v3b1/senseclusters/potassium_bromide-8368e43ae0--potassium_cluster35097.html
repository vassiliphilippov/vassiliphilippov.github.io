Etymology

potassium bromide simply names the substance: it is a salt made from potassium and bromide, so the words tell you what is inside. In the 1800s this salt was used as a 'sedative' to calm people and also in early photography to help make images, so the name brings to mind both a medicine and a photographic chemical.
