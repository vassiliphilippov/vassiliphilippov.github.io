Etymology

The plant black nightshade may get its name from its dark berries and old stories that it could make people sleepy or sick. The word black points to the dark berries and nightshade comes from beliefs that the plant caused 'sleep' or was 'poisonous', so the name warns that some parts can be dangerous.
