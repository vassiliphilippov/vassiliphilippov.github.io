Etymology

The noun conic section comes from a simple picture: imagine cutting a cone with a flat slice and the edge of the slice makes different curves like an ellipse, a parabola, or a hyperbola. So conic, meaning the cone, and section, meaning a cut, together explain why the phrase means 'a curve made by cutting a cone'.
