Etymology

eucalyptus oil comes from the leaves of tall Australian eucalyptus trees; people crush or boil the leaves to make a strong-smelling oil. One popular story says Aboriginal people used the leaves as 'medicine' and early visitors learned to use the oil for chest rubs and cleaning, so today eucalyptus oil means the strong, healing-smelling oil used in remedies and products.
