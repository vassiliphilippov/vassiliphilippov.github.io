Etymology

calming atmosphere paints a picture of a room with soft light, quiet music and plants that make the space feel gentle and quiet. The word calming shows the effect and atmosphere means the surrounding space, so it refers to an environment that makes people feel 'relaxed' and 'peaceful'.
