Etymology

The term anxiolytic drug may come from two word parts: anxio- meaning 'anxiety' and -lytic meaning 'to loosen or break'. So the idea of a medicine that 'loosens' or 'breaks' anxiety explains why it means 'a drug that reduces anxiety and calms tension'.
