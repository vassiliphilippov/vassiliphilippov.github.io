Etymology

nicotinamide adenine dinucleotide is a name that tells you what the molecule is made of: nicotinamide from vitamin B3, the base adenine, and dinucleotide, which means two small blocks joined. As a tiny 'helper molecule' it carries electrons between reactions to help cells make energy.
