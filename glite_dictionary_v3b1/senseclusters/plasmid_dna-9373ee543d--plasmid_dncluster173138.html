Etymology

plasmid DNA comes from the word plasmid, based on 'plasma' meaning 'something formed', and DNA for the cell's genetic material, so it gives the image of a small, formed piece of DNA. That's why plasmid DNA means a small circular DNA molecule that sits apart from the cell's main DNA and can copy itself.
