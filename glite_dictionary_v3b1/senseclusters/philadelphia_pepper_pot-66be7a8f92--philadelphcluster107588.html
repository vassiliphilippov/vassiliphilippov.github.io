Etymology

Philadelphia pepper pot comes from a simple picture: market cooks in old Philadelphia stirred a big pot full of a hot, peppery stew, often made with tripe (cow stomach). So the name literally described that 'spicy soup' from Philadelphia and today means that same hearty local dish.
