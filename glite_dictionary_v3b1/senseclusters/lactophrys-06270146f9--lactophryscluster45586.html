Etymology

Lactophrys looks like it joins lacto- 'milk' (as in lactose) and -phrys, an old Greek part that can mean 'brow' or 'face', so a helpful way to remember it is to imagine a box-shaped fish with a pale, 'milky' face. That's why Lactophrys is a fitting name for a group of boxfishes found in warm seas.
