Etymology

cool jazz probably comes from the word cool meaning 'calm' or 'not excited'. Musicians in the 1940s and 1950s chose a soft, relaxed sound instead of the loud, fast style called 'hot' jazz, so cool jazz now means jazz that sounds smooth and relaxed.
