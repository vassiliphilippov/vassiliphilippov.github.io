Etymology

The word undersuit comes from two simple parts: under meaning 'below' and suit meaning 'a set of clothes', just like the familiar word undershirt. So an undersuit is a tight piece of clothing worn below a main suit, for example a spacesuit, to help keep the person warm and protected.
