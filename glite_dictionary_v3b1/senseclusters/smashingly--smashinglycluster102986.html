Etymology

smashingly comes from the simple verb smash, which means 'to hit or break', plus the ending -ly that makes it a word that describes how something is done. People began to call a very successful show or song a 'smash' ('a hit'), so smashingly means 'in a very impressive, excellent, or successful way'.
