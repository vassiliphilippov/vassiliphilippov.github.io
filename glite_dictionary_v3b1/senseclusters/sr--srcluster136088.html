Etymology

sr stands for the unit steradian, which comes from Greek stere- meaning 'solid' and from radian (from radius) meaning 'ray'. A helpful image is a cone of rays from the centre of a ball: a steradian measures how wide that cone is in three dimensions, so sr is the unit for a 'solid' angle.
