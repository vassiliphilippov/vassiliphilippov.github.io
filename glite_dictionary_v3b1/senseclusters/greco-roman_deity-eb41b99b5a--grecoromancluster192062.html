Etymology

Greco-Roman deity: The term Greco-Roman deity comes from joining Greco (Greek) and Roman with deity meaning 'a god'. Long ago the Romans borrowed many Greek gods and gave them new names, so the phrase means 'a god from ancient Greece and Rome'.
