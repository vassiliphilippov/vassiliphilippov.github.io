Etymology

Pieris floribunda comes from Latin: the genus Pieris and the word floribunda, which means 'many flowers'. The name paints the image of a small evergreen bush covered in clusters of white, bell-shaped blooms, so it tells you the plant is a shrub with lots of flowers.
