Etymology

The name acetyl group comes from acetyl, from the word 'acetic' meaning the acid in vinegar, and group, meaning a small part that attaches to a molecule. Imagine a tiny piece like vinegar sticking to a larger molecule and changing how it behaves, and that's why adding an acetyl group often changes a compound's properties.
