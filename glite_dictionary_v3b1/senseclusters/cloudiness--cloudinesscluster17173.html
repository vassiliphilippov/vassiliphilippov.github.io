Etymology

cloudiness comes from cloud plus the ending -ness meaning 'state', and cloud is also used to mean 'make unclear' (as in the phrase 'cloud someone's judgment'). So, cloudiness can mean the 'state of being unclear', for example when a liquid or view is not clear.
