Etymology

alcoholic abuse comes from joining alcoholic (about alcohol) and abuse ('to use wrongly' or 'to harm'). Imagine someone drinking so much that the alcohol 'harms' their body, family, or life, and that picture explains why the phrase means very harmful or excessive drinking today.
