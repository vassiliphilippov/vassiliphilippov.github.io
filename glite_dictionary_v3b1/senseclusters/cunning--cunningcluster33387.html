Etymology

Cunning as a noun also comes from the old root cunnan meaning 'to know' and first meant 'skill' or 'knowledge'. It later became the name for the particular skill of outsmarting or deceiving people. So when you talk about someone's cunning, you mean their clever, sometimes dishonest, ability.
