Etymology

Lithocarpus densiflorus comes from old Greek and Latin words: litho means 'stone', carpus means 'fruit', and densiflorus means 'many flowers'. So the name paints a picture of a tree with hard, 'stone-like' nuts and lots of flowers, which fits the tanoak today.
