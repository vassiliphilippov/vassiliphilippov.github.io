Etymology

Pope Alexander VI was a leader of the Catholic Church from the Borgia family who used his power to promote his children, Cesare and Lucrezia. Because he openly favoured his family and was tied to secret deals and bribery, his name is remembered as a symbol of a 'corrupt' and 'powerful' pope.
