Etymology

caramel bun gets its name from how it is made: dough is rolled with sugar and nuts, put in a tin with sugar or honey, and in the oven the sugar melts into sticky caramel under the bun. So the name gives the image of a small 'sweet, sticky roll', which is why it means 'a sweet pastry'.
