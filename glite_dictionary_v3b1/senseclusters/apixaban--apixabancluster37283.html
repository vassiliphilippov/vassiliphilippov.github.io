Etymology

apixaban is a modern drug name you can remember by its parts: the Xa points to 'Factor Xa', a blood protein that helps make clots, and the ending ban sounds like 'to stop'. So apixaban is a medicine that 'stops' Factor Xa to prevent blood clots and lower the risk of stroke.
