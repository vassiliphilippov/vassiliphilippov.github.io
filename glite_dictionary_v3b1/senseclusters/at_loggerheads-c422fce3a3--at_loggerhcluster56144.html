Etymology

at loggerheads may come from the old word loggerhead, a heavy iron tool or wooden block that was used like a club; imagine two people facing each other with such heavy 'loggerheads' and fighting, and you get a strong image of conflict, which is why the phrase means 'in strong disagreement'.
