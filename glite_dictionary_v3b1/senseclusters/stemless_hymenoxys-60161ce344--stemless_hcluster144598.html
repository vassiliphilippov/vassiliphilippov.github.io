Etymology

stemless hymenoxys gets its name from how the plant looks: stemless says the flower has 'no long stem' and the bright yellow head sits on a short, leafless stalk above a small tuft of leaves. That's why the name means a low-growing plant with a single yellow flower close to the ground.
