Etymology

intoxicated comes from old Latin parts in- and toxicum, where toxicum means 'poison' and you can see the same idea in words like toxic and toxin. So to be intoxicated is literally to be 'poisoned' by alcohol or drugs, and by extension it can mean being very excited or strongly affected by a feeling.
