Etymology

The word unreasonableness comes from three parts: un- meaning 'not', reason meaning 'clear thinking' and the same root you see in reasonable, and -ness meaning 'a state'. So unreasonableness is the state of 'not having reason', which is why it means being unfair, irrational, or stubborn.
