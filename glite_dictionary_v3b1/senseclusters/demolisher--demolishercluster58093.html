Etymology

The word demolisher comes from the same idea in demolish of bringing something 'down' (from Latin de- 'down' + moliri 'to build'), so by a simple step it came to mean someone who 'completely defeats' or overwhelms an opponent. That's why a top player or a powerful argument can be called a demolisher.
