Etymology

niftiness comes from nifty ('clever' or 'handy') plus the ending -ness that makes a noun for a quality. A helpful way to remember is to think of nifty as like 'neat' or 'skillful', so niftiness is the 'quality of being clever, attractive, or well-designed'.
