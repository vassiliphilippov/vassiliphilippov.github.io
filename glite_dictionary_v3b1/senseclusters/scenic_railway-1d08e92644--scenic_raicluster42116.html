Etymology

scenic railway: The noun scenic railway comes from small trains in parks and seaside resorts that carried people slowly along tracks so they could enjoy the scenic ('beautiful') views on a railway ('track'). So, the name now means 'a short amusement or tourist train that gives a pleasant ride'.
