Etymology

alkalemia comes from alkali, a word originally meaning 'ashes' that gave English alkaline meaning 'not acidic', plus the ending -emia, which means 'blood' as in anemia. So alkalemia literally means 'blood that is more alkaline', which is why it describes blood with a higher pH.
