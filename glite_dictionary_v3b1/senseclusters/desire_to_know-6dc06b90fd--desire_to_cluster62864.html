Etymology

desire to know paints a clear picture: imagine someone lifting a curtain or peeking through a keyhole because they must see what is hidden. The image of desire (wanting) reaching for know (what is inside) shows the strong 'curiosity' that pushes people 'to learn', so the phrase means a powerful wish to find out or study something.
