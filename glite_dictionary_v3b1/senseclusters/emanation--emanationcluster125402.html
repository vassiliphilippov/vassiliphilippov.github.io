Etymology

The word emanation comes from the Latin parts e- 'out' and manare 'to flow', giving the image of something 'flowing out' from a source. That's why theologians use emanation for the Holy Spirit, because it describes the Spirit 'flowing out' from the Father and the Son at Pentecost.
