Etymology

diesel-hydraulic combines diesel, named after the inventor Rudolf Diesel for the diesel engine, and hydraulic, from hydro meaning 'water' or 'fluid'. So a diesel-hydraulic is a train engine that uses a diesel motor and fluid power to move its wheels.
