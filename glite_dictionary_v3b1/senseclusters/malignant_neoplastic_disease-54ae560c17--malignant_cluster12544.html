Etymology

malignant neoplastic disease comes from older words: malignant meaning 'bad', neoplastic meaning 'new growth', and disease meaning 'illness'. So the phrase gives the image of a 'bad new growth' that is a tumor and can spread to other body parts, which is why it means a serious, often cancerous illness.
