Etymology

symbolizer comes from the word symbol ('sign' or 'picture that stands for something') plus the endings -ize ('make') and -er ('person or thing that does it'). So, a symbolizer is someone who makes or explains symbols, which fits the idea of a person skilled in interpreting or representing symbols.
