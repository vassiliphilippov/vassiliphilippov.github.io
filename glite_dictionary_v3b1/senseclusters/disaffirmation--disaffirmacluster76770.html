Etymology

The word disaffirmation comes from the prefix dis-, meaning 'not', and affirm, meaning 'to say yes' or 'agree', as in affirmation. So a disaffirmation is the act of saying 'not yes', refusing to accept or confirm something, which is why it means formally rejecting a statement or contract.
