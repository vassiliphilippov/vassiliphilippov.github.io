Etymology

sexual abstention comes from the parts sexual, meaning about sex, and abstention, meaning holding back from something. So picture someone stepping back from a kiss and deciding not to take part, and that simple image shows why the term means 'choosing not to have sex' for religious, moral, or personal reasons.
