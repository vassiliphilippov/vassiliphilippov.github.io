Etymology

Cornus stolonifera is a plant name that tells you about the bush: Cornus is the 'dogwood' family and stolonifera means it sends out long runners along the ground that root and grow into new bushes. That's why the name helps you remember that this red-twig shrub 'spreads' by making new shoots.
