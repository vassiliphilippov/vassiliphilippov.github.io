Etymology

imago comes from Latin and means 'image' or 'picture', and it shares the same root as the English word image. In psychoanalysis it refers to the ideal inner 'image' of a person formed in childhood and kept into adulthood, so it helps explain how that mental picture shapes a person's relationships.
