Etymology

burr-headed comes from burr, a small, rough seed or knot that sticks to clothes, and -headed, meaning 'having a head'. So burr-headed is easy to remember: it describes hair cut very short that looks or feels rough, like a head covered in burrs, which is why it means 'having a head of very short, rough hair'.
