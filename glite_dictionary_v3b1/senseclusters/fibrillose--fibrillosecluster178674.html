Etymology

The word fibrillose comes from the root fibr- meaning 'fiber', as in the common word fiber, and the ending -ose meaning 'full of'. So, fibrillose describes a surface 'full of' very small fibers or threads, which is why a fibrillose stem looks like it has a fine hairy coating.
