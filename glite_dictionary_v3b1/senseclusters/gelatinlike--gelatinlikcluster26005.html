Etymology

gelatinlike comes from gelatin, which traces back to the old Latin root gelare meaning 'to freeze', and it is like the common word gel or 'jelly' — a soft, wobbly, semi-solid. The ending -like means 'similar to', so gelatinlike describes something that has a 'jelly'-like, semi-solid texture.
