Etymology

Shaking palsy comes from the clear picture of a person whose body trembles: shaking describes the movement and palsy was an old word for weakness. A doctor named James Parkinson used this name in 1817 to describe the illness we now call 'Parkinson's disease', so the phrase came to mean that illness.
