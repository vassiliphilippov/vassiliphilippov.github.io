Etymology

The noun somatosensory system comes from parts: somato means 'body' and sensory means 'sensing'. Think of it as a network of sensors on your skin, muscles and joints that send messages to the brain about touch, temperature and pain, so the name means the system that helps the brain understand body sensations.
