Etymology

postage franking comes from the simple image of a visible mark on a letter: postage is the cost to send mail and franking is the stamp or official mark on the envelope. That stamp shows the letter is 'paid' and will be 'accepted' by the postal service, so today postage franking means the stamps or markings that show payment and acceptance.
