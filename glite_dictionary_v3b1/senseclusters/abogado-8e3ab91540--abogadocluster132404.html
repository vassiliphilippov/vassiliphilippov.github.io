Etymology

Abogado is a Spanish family name that comes from the common Spanish word abogado, meaning 'lawyer' or 'advocate' and similar to the English word advocate. A helpful way to remember it is that the name probably began with an ancestor who worked as a lawyer, so the family kept that job as their name.
