Etymology

self-mortification breaks into self- meaning 'your own' and the root mort meaning 'death', which you can see in the familiar word immortal. A helpful way to remember it is that it originally meant 'to make dead', so self-mortification is about trying to 'kill' or control parts of yourself by causing pain or denying comforts, often for religious reasons or to show regret.
