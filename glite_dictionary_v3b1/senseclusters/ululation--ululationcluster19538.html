Etymology

ululation comes from the Latin verb ululare, which means 'to howl'. Think of a long, high cry like a wolf's howl or a loud wail at a funeral or a joyful shout at a wedding, and you can see why ululation means a long, loud, high-pitched cry that shows strong feeling.
