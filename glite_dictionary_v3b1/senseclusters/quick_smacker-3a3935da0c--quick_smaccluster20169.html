Etymology

The expression quick smacker likely comes from the old word smack, which meant a quick, sharp hit or a noisy kiss. That's why a quick smacker gives the image of a very brief 'kiss' or light 'slap', so it means a sudden little 'kiss' or quick light 'hit'.
