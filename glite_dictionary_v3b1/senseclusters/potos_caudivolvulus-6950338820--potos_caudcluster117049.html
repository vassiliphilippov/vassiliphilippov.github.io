Etymology

Potos caudivolvulus may come from old words meaning a 'rolling tail', with Potos as the animal name and caudivolvulus meaning a tail that curls. Think of a small tree animal using its long curling tail like a hand to hold branches and fruit, and that's why the name fits the 'kinkajou', a fruit-eating mammal that lives in trees.
