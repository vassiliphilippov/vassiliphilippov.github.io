Etymology

jook-sing comes from Cantonese and likely combines juk 'bamboo' and sang 'born'. A helpful way to remember it is to think 'born of bamboo', meaning someone who looks Chinese on the outside but was raised outside Chinese culture. That's why the term is often used as an offensive label for people seen as not knowing Chinese language or traditions.
