Etymology

Republic of Kazakhstan brings together two clear ideas: Republic, which means 'a country with elected leaders', and Kazakhstan, the name of the Kazakh people plus -stan, a word that means 'land', so it literally means 'the land of the Kazakhs'. That's why the official name tells you this country is the home of the Kazakh people and is a republic today.
