Etymology

put one's name in the hat may come from a simple old practice: when a group needed to choose someone, people wrote a name on a piece of paper and put it into a hat to be drawn. So, to put your name in the hat came to mean 'to offer yourself as a candidate' or 'to try to be chosen'.
