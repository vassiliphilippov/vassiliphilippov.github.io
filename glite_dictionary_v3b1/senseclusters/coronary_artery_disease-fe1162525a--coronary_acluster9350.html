Etymology

coronary artery disease comes from how the heart's vessels look and what goes wrong. Coronary comes from a word for 'crown' because the arteries circle the heart like a crown, and 'disease' means they can become narrowed or 'blocked' by fatty deposits, so the name describes those vessels not carrying enough blood to the heart.
