Etymology

Forficula auricularia comes from old Latin words: Forficula means 'little scissors' and auricularia means 'ear', because people long ago thought these insects crawled into ears. So the name gives a clear picture: a small garden insect with pincer tails like 'little scissors' and the old 'ear' story helps explain the common name 'earwig'.
