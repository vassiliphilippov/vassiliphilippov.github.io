Etymology

vertebral artery comes from the words vertebral and artery, where vertebral points to the 'backbone' bones and artery means a 'blood vessel'. Picture that blood vessel running up through the vertebrae beside the spine to reach the brain, and that image explains why the term means the arteries that pass through the vertebrae to supply the brain and spinal cord.
