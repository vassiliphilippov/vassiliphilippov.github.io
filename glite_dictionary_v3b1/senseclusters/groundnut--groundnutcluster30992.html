Etymology

The word groundnut comes from the English parts ground meaning 'ground' and nut meaning 'nut'. It is used for the peanut because its pod develops at or under the soil, so calling it a 'groundnut' reminds you the 'nut' comes from the ground, and in British English people also call peanuts 'monkey nuts'.
