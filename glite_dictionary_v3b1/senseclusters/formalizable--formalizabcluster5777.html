Etymology

formalizable comes from the common word form, which means 'shape' or 'structure', plus the parts -ize ('make') and -able ('can'). So, if something is formalizable it can be 'made into a clear form' or 'expressed with rules', which is why we use it for things that can be put into a formal system.
