Etymology

The word pathologist comes from the Greek root path- meaning 'disease' or 'suffering' and the ending -logist, from -logy, meaning 'study', as in biology. So a pathologist is a doctor who 'studies' disease by looking at tissues, cells, and bodily fluids.
