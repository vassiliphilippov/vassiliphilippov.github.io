Etymology

The plant name Polemonium van-bruntiae comes from two parts: the first part Polemonium was long linked to the idea of 'Jacob's ladder' because the many leaflets of the plant look like the steps of a ladder, and the second part van-bruntiae is a name given to honour a person called Van Brunt who found or studied the plant. So the name points to the plant's ladder-like leaves and the person connected with it.
