Etymology

flowering crab: The name flowering crab comes from small crabapple trees that are grown for their bright flowers. The part crab refers to the 'crabapple', a small sour apple tree, and flowering shows the tree is chosen for its blooms, so the name means a 'small tree grown for pretty flowers'.
