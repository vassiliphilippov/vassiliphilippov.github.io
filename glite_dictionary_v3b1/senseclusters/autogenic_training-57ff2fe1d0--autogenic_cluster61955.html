Etymology

autogenic training gets its name from the idea of making calm from within: auto means 'self' and genic means 'to produce'. In this method you quietly tell your body to feel warm and heavy and focus on those feelings, so you 'produce' relaxation yourself, which is why it means a way to relax by your own suggestion.
