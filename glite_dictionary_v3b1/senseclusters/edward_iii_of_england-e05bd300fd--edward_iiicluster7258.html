Etymology

Edward III of England brings to mind a crowned king pointing at France on a map — his claim there began the long 'Hundred Years' War. He also lived through the 'Black Death' and watched the 'House of Commons' grow in importance, so his name often stands for a time of war, disease, and big political change.
