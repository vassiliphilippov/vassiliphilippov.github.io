Etymology

Portuguese Guinea comes from when Portugal ruled that part of West Africa: Portuguese (the rulers) was put before Guinea (the region), like a label on a map. So the name meant 'the Guinea ruled by Portugal', and it changed to Guinea-Bissau after 'independence' in 1974.
