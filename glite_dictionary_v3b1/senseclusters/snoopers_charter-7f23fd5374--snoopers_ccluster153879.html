Etymology

Snoopers' Charter comes from a simple image: snoopers are people who secretly look or listen, and a charter is like an official rule book or law. So the name suggests a law that lets the government 'snoop' or 'spy' on people's online messages and activity, which is why it means a law that gives authorities wide powers to watch people online.
