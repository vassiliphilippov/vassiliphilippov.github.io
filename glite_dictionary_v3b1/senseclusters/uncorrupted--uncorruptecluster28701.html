Etymology

uncorrupted starts with un- meaning 'not' and the rest comes from corrupt, which goes back to an old root rupt meaning 'to break', the same root in rupture and interrupt. So an uncorrupted person is 'not broken' by bribes or bad influence and stays morally pure.
