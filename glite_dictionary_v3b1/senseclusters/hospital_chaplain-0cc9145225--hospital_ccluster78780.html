Etymology

hospital chaplain comes from the idea of a chaplain, a religious person who worked in a small chapel, visiting a hospital to help the sick. Picture that person quietly offering prayer, a calm talk, or a hand to hold, so the term now means someone who gives 'spiritual care' and 'comfort' to patients, families, and staff.
