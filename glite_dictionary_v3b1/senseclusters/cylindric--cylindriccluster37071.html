Etymology

cylindric comes from the Greek word kylindros, which means 'roller' or 'rolled thing', and it is the same root as the everyday word cylinder. So, imagine a rolling log or drum: something 'cylindric' has a long, round, tube-like shape, which is why we use the word for objects that look like a cylinder.
