Etymology

Saint Thomas refers to Thomas Aquinas, a thinker from the 1200s who used clear, logical arguments to explain religious ideas and to argue for God's existence. Because he tried to bring 'faith' and 'reason' together, people use Saint Thomas when they mean religious ideas explained by logic.
