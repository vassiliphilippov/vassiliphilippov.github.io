Etymology

The word psychiatrically comes from psychiatry, which joins psyche meaning 'mind' and iatros meaning 'healer', so a psychiatrist is like a 'mind doctor'. Adding -ally makes the word describe how something is done, so to act psychiatrically is to act 'in a way that relates to the care or study of the mind'.
