Etymology

Curative treatment comes from an old word meaning 'to care' or 'to cure'; curative means 'to cure' and treatment means care, so imagine a doctor removing the illness rather than only easing pain. That's why curative treatment means medical care that aims 'to cure' the disease, not just 'to relieve' symptoms.
