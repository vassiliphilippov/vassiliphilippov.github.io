Etymology

The word shape-up names an old dock practice where workers gathered on the spot to be chosen for the day's jobs. A helpful way to remember it is to think of shape as 'form' and up as 'together' — people would 'form up' or line up to be picked, which is why shape-up means this rough, on-the-spot hiring at the docks.
