Etymology

abruptness comes from old Latin parts ab- 'away' and rupt from rumpere 'to break', the same root seen in interrupt. A helpful way to remember it is to think of someone 'breaking off' their words suddenly, which is why abruptness can mean a sudden, often rude manner.
