Etymology

The word unthrifty is made from un- 'not' and thrifty, which comes from the old word thrift meaning 'prosperity' or 'to thrive', the same idea you see in the word thrive. So, unthrifty means 'not careful with money or resources' because it literally says 'not letting money thrive'.
