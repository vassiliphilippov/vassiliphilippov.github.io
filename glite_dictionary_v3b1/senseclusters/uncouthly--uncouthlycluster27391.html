Etymology

uncouthly comes from un- 'not' and an old English root couth 'known', a root that is like the word 'know', so it originally meant 'not known' or 'unfamiliar'. So to behave uncouthly is to act in a way that seems strange or not polite, which is why it means 'rude or awkward'.
