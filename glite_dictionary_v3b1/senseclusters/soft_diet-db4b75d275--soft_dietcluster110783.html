Etymology

soft diet comes from the clear image of a diet made of soft foods like mashed potatoes or porridge that need little chewing. Doctors often ask people to follow a soft diet after dental work or when they have trouble swallowing, so it means 'a plan of food that is easy to chew and swallow'.
