Etymology

Yucca whipplei is the plant name Yucca plus the species name whipplei, which was named for an explorer called Whipple. Imagine an explorer finding a spiky yucca with a tall, creamy flower spike, and that picture helps you remember that Yucca whipplei is the yucca with dramatic tall flowers.
