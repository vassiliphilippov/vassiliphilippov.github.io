Etymology

mouse-ear chickweed probably gets its name from the plant's tiny, fuzzy leaves: the small rounded leaves look like a mouse-ear, and the plant itself is a low wild plant called chickweed. So the name reminds you it is 'a small, low-growing wild plant with tiny white flowers and hairy leaves'.
