Etymology

photo-offset printing gets its name from how it works: photo shows the image starts on a photo plate, and offset means the image is first moved off the plate onto a rubber blanket and then onto the paper. That's why it means 'a printing method' where the picture is transferred from plate to blanket to the printing surface.
