Etymology

cutesy-wootsy comes from the simple word cute 'small and pretty' plus a playful second part wootsy that makes a singsong, childlike phrase like itsy-bitsy. So, cutesy-wootsy means overly cute or sentimental, in a childish or exaggerated way.
