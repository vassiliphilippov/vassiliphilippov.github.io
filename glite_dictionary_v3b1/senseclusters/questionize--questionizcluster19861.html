Etymology

questionize comes from question ('to ask') plus the ending -ize ('to make'), so you can think of it as 'make into a question'. Like organize means 'make into order', this word means turning something into a question or challenge. So, to questionize is to look closely at or challenge an idea by asking it hard questions.
