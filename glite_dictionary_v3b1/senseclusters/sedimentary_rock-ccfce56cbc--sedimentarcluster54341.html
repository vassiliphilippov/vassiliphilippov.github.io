Etymology

sedimentary rock gives a simple picture: tiny pieces like sand, mud and shells slowly fall and pile up in layers, and the word sedimentary points to these pieces. Over time the layers press together and turn into hard rock, so the phrase means 'rock made from layers of small pieces'.
