Etymology

Penstemon deustus comes from its parts: Penstemon is built from old words meaning 'five' and 'stamen' (the flower's five inner parts), and deustus is Latin for 'burnt' because the flower often has a dark, scorched-looking throat. So the name paints a picture of a 'beardtongue' flower with a burnt-looking centre.
