Etymology

dining-room attendant comes from a simple, literal image: the dining-room is the room where people eat and an attendant is someone who looks after and helps guests. Picture a person moving between tables, serving food and clearing plates, and that's why the phrase means 'a person employed to provide service in a dining room'.
