Etymology

Ostrobogulous is a playful made-up word; a helpful way to remember it is to break it into ostro-, bogus, and -ulous, where bogus means 'fake' or 'odd' and -ulous sounds like the end of ridiculous. So, ostrobogulous feels like something strangely 'absurd' or 'eccentric', which is why it means 'bizarre' or 'unusual'.
