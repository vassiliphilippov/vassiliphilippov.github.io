Etymology

shroud-waving coverage comes from the picture of someone waving a shroud, the cloth used to cover a dead body, to show grief in a loud way. That strong picture of showing sorrow explains why the phrase is used for news that focuses on tragedy to 'shock' people or 'get attention'.
