Etymology

The term Fauci ouchie grew during the COVID vaccine rollout when Fauci, the doctor many people saw on TV, was the face of the shots and ouchie meant the small sting and the tiny bandage or sticker people wore after the shot. One popular story says people showed their stickers in selfies, so Fauci ouchie came to mean 'the COVID-19 vaccine' or 'getting vaccinated'.
