Etymology

rent-a-gob comes from the phrase rent-a-, as in rent-a-car, meaning 'to hire', and the British slang gob, meaning 'mouth'. So a rent-a-gob is like a 'hired mouth', a person who talks loudly or too much in public, often to get attention.
