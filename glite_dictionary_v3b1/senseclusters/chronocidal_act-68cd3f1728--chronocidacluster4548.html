Etymology

chronocidal act may come from the parts chrono, meaning 'time', and -cidal, meaning 'to kill'. Picture someone smashing a clock or tearing a calendar so time seems to stop; that image shows why the phrase means an act that breaks or destroys the normal flow of time.
