Etymology

Bermuda lily may come from sailors or gardeners who found the tall, white trumpet flowers on the islands of Bermuda and began calling that striking lily by the place name. That's why today Bermuda lily means a tall, fragrant white flower grown to 'decorate' gardens and events.
