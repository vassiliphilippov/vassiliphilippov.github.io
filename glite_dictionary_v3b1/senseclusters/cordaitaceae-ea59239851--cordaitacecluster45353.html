Etymology

Cordaitaceae comes from the plant genus Cordaites plus the botanical ending -aceae, which means 'family'. The genus Cordaites was named for the 19th-century naturalist Corda, so the name points to 'Corda's plants'. That is why Cordaitaceae simply means the plant family of ancient Cordaites, the extinct plants we know from fossils.
