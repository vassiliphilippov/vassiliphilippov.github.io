Etymology

genus Haemoproteus may come from Greek parts: haemo- meaning 'blood' and proteus the name of a god who could 'change' shape. So the name explains its use today: it is the 'genus' name for tiny parasites that live in the 'blood' of birds and reptiles and 'change' form during their life.
