Etymology

interleave comes from the prefix inter- 'between' and the idea of a leaf 'page', so it originally meant putting a sheet between other sheets. That's why to interleave now is to insert or alternate pages, sheets, or data 'between' others to keep them safe, in order, or to work faster.
