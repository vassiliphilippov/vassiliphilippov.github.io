Etymology

Pinus strobus comes from Pinus, the old Latin word for 'pine', and strobus, a word that may come from Greek for 'cone'. Early plant scientists used 'strobus' to point to the tree's long cones and soft needles, so the name helps you remember this eastern white pine.
