Etymology

The phrase draw inspiration from comes from two simple images: to draw is to pull water up from a well, and inspiration is like a fresh breath entering you. So, to draw inspiration from something is like pulling fresh water or a fresh breath from that source, meaning 'to get ideas' from it.
