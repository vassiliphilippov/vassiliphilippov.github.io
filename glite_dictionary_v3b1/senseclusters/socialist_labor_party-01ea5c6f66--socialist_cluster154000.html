Etymology

Socialist Labor Party comes from a simple idea: a 'party' of Socialist people who stood for Labor, meaning 'workers'. Socialist showed they wanted 'socialism', or more sharing of business, and Labor showed they spoke for 'workers', so the name tells their goal to improve working people's lives peacefully.
