Etymology

Octans comes from Latin meaning 'eighth' and was named after the sailor's measuring tool, the octant, which helped navigators measure angles at sea. It has the same root as words like octagon and octopus, which remind you of 'eight', so the faint constellation Octans was chosen as a navigation marker near the south celestial pole.
