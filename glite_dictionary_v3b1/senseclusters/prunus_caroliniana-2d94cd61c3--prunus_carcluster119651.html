Etymology

Prunus caroliniana comes from two parts: Prunus is the Latin word for 'plum' and caroliniana means 'from Carolina'. That's why the name gives the image of a small evergreen tree with plum-like fruit, glossy leaves, and white flowers, and is used for that southern tree.
