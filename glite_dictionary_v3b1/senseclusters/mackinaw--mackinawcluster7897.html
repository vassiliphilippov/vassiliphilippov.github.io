Etymology

mackinaw for a flat-bottomed boat comes from the place name Mackinac, said 'Mackinaw', in the Great Lakes area and from the Ojibwe people. Boats of this kind were used around that place in shallow water, so mackinaw now names a flat-bottomed Great Lakes boat.
