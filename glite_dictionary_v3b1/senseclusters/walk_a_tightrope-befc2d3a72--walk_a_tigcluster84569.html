Etymology

The idiom walk a tightrope comes from the circus image of a performer carefully moving along a very thin, high tightrope, where one small mistake can make them fall. So, we say someone is walking a tightrope when they are in a 'risky situation' and must act very carefully to avoid failure or trouble.
