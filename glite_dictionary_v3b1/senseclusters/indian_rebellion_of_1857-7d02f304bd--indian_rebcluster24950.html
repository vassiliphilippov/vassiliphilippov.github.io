Etymology

Indian Rebellion of 1857 began when Indian soldiers refused to use new gun cartridges said to be greased with cow and pig fat, a story that deeply offended both Hindu and Muslim beliefs. The refusal turned into a wide revolt against British rule, so the name points to a major 'uprising' that some people call the 'First War of Indian Independence'.
