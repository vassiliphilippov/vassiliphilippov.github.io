Etymology

bearing rein comes from old horse gear: a rein (a strap) was used to change a horse's bearing (its head and posture) by pulling the head up so the horse looked proud. So, the term means 'a rein used to keep a horse's head in a particular position' and the picture of a strap forcing the head up explains the phrase and why people often call it cruel.
