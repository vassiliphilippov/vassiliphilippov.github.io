Etymology

Scorpion comes from the Latin scorpio and the Greek skorpios, both meaning 'scorpion', the animal with a poisonous sting. So the zodiac Scorpion is named after that creature, which helps explain why people say the sign is passionate, secretive, and can have a powerful 'sting' in its character.
