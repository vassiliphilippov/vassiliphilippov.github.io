Etymology

weisenheimer is a slang word from German or Yiddish that looks like wise ('smart') plus the German ending -heimer (seen in some family or place names), so it feels like calling someone a 'wise guy'. That's why a weisenheimer means a cheeky, rude person — a smart-aleck.
