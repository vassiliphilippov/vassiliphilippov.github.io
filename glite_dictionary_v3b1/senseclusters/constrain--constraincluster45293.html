Etymology

constrain comes from Latin parts con- meaning 'together' and stringere meaning 'to bind' or 'to draw tight', and it shares this idea with the everyday word string. So to constrain someone is like 'tying' or 'tightening' them, which explains why it means to limit or force someone by reducing their options.
