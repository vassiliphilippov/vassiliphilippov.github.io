Etymology

deplore comes from Latin parts de- 'down' and plorare 'to cry out', so it once meant to 'cry down' or shout against something. That's why today to deplore is to strongly disapprove of or show deep regret about something, and you can see the same 'crying out' root in implore, which means to 'beg'.
