Etymology

girl math comes from a social media joke where the words girl and math suggest using playful number tricks to make shopping feel smarter, for example saying a sale, a return, or reward points make an item 'free'. That's why girl math means a humorous way to explain or excuse personal spending that does not follow normal money rules.
