Etymology

The word Charophyceae comes from the name Chara, the name for a group of green water plants, and the ending -phyceae, from an old Greek word meaning 'alga' or 'seaweed'. So Charophyceae is a name for green freshwater algae that are important for studying how plants moved from water to land.
