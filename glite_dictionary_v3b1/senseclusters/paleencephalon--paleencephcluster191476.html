Etymology

The word paleencephalon comes from the parts paleo- meaning 'old' and -encephalon meaning 'brain', the same paleo- you see in paleontology. So, it literally means 'old brain', and it refers to the oldest, basic parts of the brain that control survival and instinctive behavior.
