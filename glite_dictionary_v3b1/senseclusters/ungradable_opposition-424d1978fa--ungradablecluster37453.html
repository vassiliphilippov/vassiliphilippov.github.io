Etymology

Ungradable opposition paints a simple image: ungradable means there is no middle, and opposition means two opposite sides, like a light switch that is either 'on' or 'off'. So, like that switch, one side or the other is true and there is no in-between, which is why the term describes pairs like 'alive'/'dead' or 'true'/'false'.
