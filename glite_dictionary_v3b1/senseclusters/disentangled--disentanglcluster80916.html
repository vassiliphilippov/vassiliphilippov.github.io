Etymology

disentangled comes from the prefix dis- meaning 'not' or 'remove' and tangle meaning a 'mess of twisted threads', so it literally means 'made untangled'. So something disentangled is freed from knots or confusion, like a necklace or a set of wires that is now easy to use.
