Etymology

Centaurea nigra gets its name from a Greek story: Centaurea points to the 'centaur' Chiron, who was said to use plants to heal, so the flower was named after that healing image. The Latin word nigra means 'black' and refers to the dark centre of the purple flower, which is why the plant is also called 'black knapweed' today.
