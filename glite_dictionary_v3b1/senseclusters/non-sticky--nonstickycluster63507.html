Etymology

non-sticky is built from non- meaning 'not' and sticky, from the common verb stick meaning 'to stay on a surface'. So non-sticky simply means 'not likely to stick', which is why we call pans, notes, or glues 'non-sticky' when they do not leave marks or stay attached.
