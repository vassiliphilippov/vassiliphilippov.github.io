Etymology

The game Chinese checkers got its name as a way to sell it. One popular story says makers called the star-shaped game Chinese to sound foreign and added checkers because people knew that word, even though it is 'not from China' and is not the game 'checkers'. That's why today Chinese checkers means the star-shaped marble board game.
