Etymology

Elizabeth Peabody was an American teacher who in the 1800s opened the first 'kindergarten' in the United States, a small classroom where young children learned by playing with toys and singing. That clear picture of a friendly room of children learning through play is why her name is remembered as a pioneer of early childhood education.
