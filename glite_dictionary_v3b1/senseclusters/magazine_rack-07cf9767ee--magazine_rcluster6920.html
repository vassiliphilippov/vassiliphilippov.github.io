Etymology

Magazine rack is a simple, literal name: a rack is a frame or shelf and a magazine is a small book or paper you read. The word magazine once meant a 'store' for goods, so the phrase simply names a stand for keeping and showing printed 'magazines', which is why it means 'a frame or stand used for holding and displaying magazines'.
