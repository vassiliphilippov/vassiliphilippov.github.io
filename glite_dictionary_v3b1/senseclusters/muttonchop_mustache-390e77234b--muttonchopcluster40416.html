Etymology

muttonchop mustache comes from the image of thick side hair that looks like a cut of meat, with mutton meaning sheep meat and chop meaning a piece of meat. In the 1800s men often wore heavy hair down the sides of the face, so today the phrase means 'a style of mustache' that looks like those meat 'chops'.
