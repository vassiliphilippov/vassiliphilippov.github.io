Etymology

Prunus amygdalus is the scientific name for the almond tree: Prunus links it to plums and cherries and amygdalus comes from an old Greek word for the 'almond'. The name gives a clear picture of a small tree with pink blossoms and hard, edible nuts, so it refers to the tree grown for 'almond' nuts and flowers.
