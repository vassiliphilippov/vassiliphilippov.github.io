Etymology

Cerambycidae comes from the old Greek word kerambyx, which meant 'a horned beetle', and the ending -idae means 'family' in scientific names. So Cerambycidae literally names the 'family of horned beetles', a helpful way to remember why these insects have very long, horn-like antennae.
