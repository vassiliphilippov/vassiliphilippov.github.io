Etymology

clayiness comes from the simple word clay, meaning 'soft, sticky earth', plus the endings -y meaning 'full of' and -ness meaning 'state of'. So clayiness is the 'state of being like clay', which is why it describes heavy soil that holds water and drains slowly.
