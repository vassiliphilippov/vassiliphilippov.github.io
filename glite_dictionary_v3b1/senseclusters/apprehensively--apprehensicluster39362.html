Etymology

apprehensively comes from the Latin root prehend meaning 'to grasp', and it is related to the common word apprehend which means 'to catch' or 'to understand'. A helpful way to remember it is to picture someone 'grasping' a bad thought or being 'caught' by worry, which is why apprehensively means showing worry that something bad may happen.
