Etymology

The phrase bits per second comes from early computing as a simple way to say how fast data moves. It literally counts how many bits, the smallest pieces of information that are 0 or 1, pass in each second, like saying 'miles per hour' for tiny pieces of data, so it means the 'data speed' of a connection.
