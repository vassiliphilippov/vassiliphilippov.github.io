Etymology

antianxiety agent is a medical name made from three parts: anti meaning 'against', anxiety meaning 'worry', and agent meaning 'a substance that works in the body'. Picture a small shield that pushes worries away, so the term means a medicine that reduces stress while keeping the mind clear.
