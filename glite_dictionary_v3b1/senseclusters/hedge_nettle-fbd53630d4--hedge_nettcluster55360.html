Etymology

hedge nettle may come from a simple picture: the word hedge shows it often grows in hedges or at the edge of woods, and nettle points to its nettle-like leaves and the strong smell when they are crushed. So the name helps you remember it is a common 'plant' that spreads along hedges and roadsides.
