Etymology

Anemone quinquefolia comes from older Greek and Latin words: anemone means 'wind' and quinquefolia means 'five leaves'. Because the little white flower often nods in the breeze and the plant usually has five leaves, the name gives a simple picture of this small woodland wildflower.
