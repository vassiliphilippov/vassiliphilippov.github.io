Etymology

The phrase put in a hundred and ten percent may come from the image of someone giving more than a full amount—a hundred and ten percent—like filling a cup until it overflows or an athlete pushing past their usual limit. That's why it means 'to give your maximum possible effort' or 'to do more than expected'.
