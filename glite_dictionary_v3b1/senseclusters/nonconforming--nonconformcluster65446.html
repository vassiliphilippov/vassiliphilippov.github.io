Etymology

nonconforming begins with non- meaning 'not' and contains conform, from form meaning 'shape' or 'rule', so conform is 'to follow the usual shape or rules'. So a nonconforming person or idea does not 'fit the form' and refuses to follow the usual customs or the official church.
