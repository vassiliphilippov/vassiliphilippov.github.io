Etymology

anaplastic comes from Greek parts ana- meaning 'again' and plastic meaning 'to shape', as in the everyday word plastic, which can be shaped. In medicine it describes cells that have lost their normal shape and role and seem to be shaped again into an earlier, less special form, which is why anaplastic tumors often grow and spread faster.
