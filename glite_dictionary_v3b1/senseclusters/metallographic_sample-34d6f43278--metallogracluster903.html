Etymology

Metallographic sample comes from the parts metal, meaning the material, and graphic, from a word meaning 'to draw' or 'picture', so it literally means a metal piece made to show a picture of its inside. That's why a metallographic sample is a carefully prepared small piece of metal you look at under a microscope to study its inner structure.
