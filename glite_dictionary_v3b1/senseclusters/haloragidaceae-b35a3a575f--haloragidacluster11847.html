Etymology

Haloragidaceae comes from the plant genus Haloragis, which begins with the Greek part halo- meaning 'sea' or 'salt', a helpful clue that many of these plants live in water. The ending -aceae marks a plant 'family', as in Rosaceae the 'rose family', so Haloragidaceae simply names the family of water milfoil and other aquatic plants.
