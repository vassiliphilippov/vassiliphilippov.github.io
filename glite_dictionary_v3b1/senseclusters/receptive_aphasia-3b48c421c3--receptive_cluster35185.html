Etymology

receptive aphasia comes from the idea that receptive means 'to receive' and aphasia means a problem with language, so imagine a radio that can play out words but cannot tune in to others. That's why someone with receptive aphasia may speak in fluent sentences yet cannot 'understand' what people say or read.
