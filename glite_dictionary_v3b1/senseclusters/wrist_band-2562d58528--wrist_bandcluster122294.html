Etymology

wrist band comes from the simple image of a wrist plus a band: a strip of cloth, paper, or plastic wrapped around the wrist, like the bands athletes use to wipe sweat or the paper bands hospitals use to show a patient's name. That's why a wrist band means 'a band you wear on the wrist for an accessory or identification'.
