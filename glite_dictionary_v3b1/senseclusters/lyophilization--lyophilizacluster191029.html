Etymology

lyophilization comes from scientific Greek parts lyo- and phil-, where lyo- is used for processes involving freezing and phil- means 'loving' or 'having an affinity for'. A helpful way to remember it is to think of lyophilization as 'freeze-drying' — a method that 'removes water by freezing' to preserve food, medicines, or samples.

Description: A process of freeze-drying in which material (such as food, blood plasma, pharmaceuticals, or biological samples) is frozen and then dried under reduced pressure so that frozen water sublimes directly from solid to gas; used to preserve products and maintain their structure.
