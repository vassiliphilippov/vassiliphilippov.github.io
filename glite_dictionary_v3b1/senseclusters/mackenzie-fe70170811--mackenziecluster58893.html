Etymology

Mackenzie comes from a Scottish family name, Gaelic MacCoinnich, where Mac- means 'son of' and Coinneach means 'handsome' (the same root as the name Kenneth). The river was named for the explorer Alexander Mackenzie, so the name of the river is simply his family name.
