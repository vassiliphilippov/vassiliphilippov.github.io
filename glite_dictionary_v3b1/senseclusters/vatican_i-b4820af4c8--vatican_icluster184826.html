Etymology

Vatican I gets its name from the place and the number: it was the first big meeting of Catholic leaders held in the Vatican, and the Roman numeral I means 'one'. So Vatican I now refers to that 1869–1870 meeting and to its decision that the pope 'cannot be wrong' when he speaks officially.
