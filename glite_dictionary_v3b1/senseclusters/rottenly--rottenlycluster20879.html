Etymology

rottenly comes from the word rot meaning 'to decay' and the ending -ly which makes it 'in this way'. Think of a rotten apple—spoiled, unpleasant, or harmful—so to do something rottenly is to do it in a very bad, unpleasant, or morally wrong way.
