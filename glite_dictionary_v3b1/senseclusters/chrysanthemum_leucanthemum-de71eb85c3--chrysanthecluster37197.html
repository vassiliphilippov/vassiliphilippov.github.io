Etymology

Chrysanthemum leucanthemum may come from old Greek parts: chrys- meaning 'gold', leuc- meaning 'white', and -anthemum meaning 'flower', which paints the simple image of a white flower with a golden center. That's why the name fits the tall, daisy-like plant often called the 'oxeye daisy'.
