Etymology

The name Byelorussian language comes from the older country name Byelorussia, which joined a word meaning 'white' with 'Rus', an old name for the people and lands there. So, Byelorussian language simply meant the language of 'White Rus' and is an older form of the modern name 'Belarusian'.
