Etymology

jimjams likely comes from a playful repeating sound, like jim-jam, used to name a small, jumpy feeling of 'nervousness'. A helpful way to remember its sleep meaning is that it sounds like jammies, a common word for 'pyjamas', so jimjams can mean either 'the jitters' or 'pyjamas'.
