Etymology

Old World hop hornbeam is a name that paints a simple picture: Old World means it comes from Europe and Asia, hop refers to its hanging seed clusters that look like the hops used in beer, and hornbeam points to its very hard wood, with 'horn' meaning 'hard' and 'beam' meaning 'tree'. So the name helps you remember both what the tree looks like and where it is from.
