Etymology

mole-eyed scrutiny may come from the picture of a mole with tiny eyes that noses close to the ground to find hidden things, so mole-eyed suggests looking very closely. That's why mole-eyed scrutiny means a sharp, careful examination that finds small errors others do not see.
