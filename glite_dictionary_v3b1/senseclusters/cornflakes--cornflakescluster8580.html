Etymology

The word cornflakes is made from the two simple words corn, meaning 'grain', and flakes, meaning 'thin, flat pieces'. It was used for the crisp, toasted breakfast cereal made from corn, so the name simply means 'thin pieces of corn' and explains why we call that cereal cornflakes.
