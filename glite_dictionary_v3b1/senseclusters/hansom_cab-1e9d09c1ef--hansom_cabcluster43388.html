Etymology

hansom cab is named after the inventor Joseph Hansom and the word cab, which meant a small 'carriage'; he made a low, fast two-wheeled vehicle with a covered place for passengers and the driver sitting high behind them. That clear picture of a small covered box pulled by a horse helps explain why the name means that kind of city horse-drawn 'taxi'.
