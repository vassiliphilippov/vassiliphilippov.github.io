Etymology

Vasco Núñez de Balboa was a Spanish explorer who crossed the narrow land between two seas and climbed a hill in 1513 to look out and see the huge Pacific Ocean. Because he was the first European to reach and see that ocean from the American side, he is remembered as 'the first European to see the Pacific'.
