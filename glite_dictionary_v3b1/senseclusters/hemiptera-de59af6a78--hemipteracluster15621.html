Etymology

Hemiptera comes from Greek parts hemi- 'half' and ptera 'wings', and the hemi- part is the same as in hemisphere. The insects were named for their front wings that are partly hard and partly soft, so their name means 'half-wings' and now refers to the group of bugs that have those wings and piercing and sucking mouthparts.
