Etymology

Hopey McChange comes from a playful nickname that joins Hopey (someone full of hope) and McChange as a joking last name. One popular idea is that the 'Mc' makes it sound like a character name used to tease someone, so it is used to call a person 'very optimistic' or a bit 'naive' about big change.
