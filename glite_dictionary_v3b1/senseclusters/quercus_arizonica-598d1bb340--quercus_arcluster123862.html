Etymology

Quercus arizonica is a scientific name made from Latin and a place name: Quercus was the old Latin word for 'oak', and arizonica means 'from Arizona'. So the name gives the simple image of an 'Arizona oak', which explains why it is used for the oak tree native to that region.
