Etymology

cardiac pacemaker combines cardiac (about the heart) and pacemaker (someone or something that 'sets the pace'). Think of a person clapping at the front of a group to keep everyone in step; the heart's pacemaker sends small electrical signals that keep the heart's 'beat' regular, so the phrase means 'the part that controls the heartbeat'.
