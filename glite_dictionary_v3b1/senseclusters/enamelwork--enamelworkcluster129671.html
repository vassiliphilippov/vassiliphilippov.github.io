Etymology

enamelwork comes from enamel, which comes from the old French esmail, meaning a 'glassy coat' used to cover metal. Think of the hard, shiny 'enamel' on a tooth — enamelwork is simply objects made by fusing that glassy coat onto metal to make bright, smooth decoration.
