Etymology

tympanoplasty comes from Greek parts: tympano- meaning 'drum' and -plasty meaning 'to shape' or 'repair'. So tympanoplasty is literally 'drum shaping' and refers to the surgery that fixes the eardrum and the small bones in the middle ear.
