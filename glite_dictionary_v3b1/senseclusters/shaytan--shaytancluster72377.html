Etymology

shaytan comes from Arabic shayṭān, meaning 'devil' or 'adversary', and is related to the English name Satan. Think of a sly spirit that 'whispers' bad ideas to people — that's why shaytan means an evil spirit or demon who tries to lead people astray.
