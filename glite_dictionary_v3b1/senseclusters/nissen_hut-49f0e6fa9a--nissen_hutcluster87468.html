Etymology

Nissen hut comes from the name of its 'inventor', Peter Nissen, who made a simple curved metal shelter for soldiers in World War I. The roof is shaped like a half circle and made of wavy metal so people could put the hut up quickly, and that's why Nissen hut now means a quick, simple metal shelter used for soldiers or storage.
