Etymology

Eudromias morinellus may come from old Greek and Latin parts: eu- + dromias meaning 'good runner' and morinellus meaning 'of the moor'. So the name gives a simple picture of a small bird that runs across mountain areas and moors, which fits the Eurasian dotterel today.
