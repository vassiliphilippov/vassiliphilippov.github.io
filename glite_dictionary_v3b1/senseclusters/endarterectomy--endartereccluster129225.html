Etymology

endarterectomy breaks into three simple parts: en- meaning 'in', arter meaning 'artery', and -ectomy meaning 'cutting out', the same idea you see in appendectomy. So, an endarterectomy is a surgery that 'cuts out' the inner part or lining of an artery to remove a blockage and let blood flow again.
