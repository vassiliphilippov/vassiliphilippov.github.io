Etymology

Lagostomus maximus may come from old words meaning 'hare' and 'mouth', and maximus means 'largest'. So imagine a large, rabbit-faced rodent with a big mouth living in noisy, shared burrows on the open plains; that image explains why the name describes the plains viscacha.
