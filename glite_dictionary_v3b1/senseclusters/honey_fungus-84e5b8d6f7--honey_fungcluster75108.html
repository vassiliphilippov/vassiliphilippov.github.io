Etymology

honey fungus: The name honey fungus comes from the mushrooms' honey-colored caps and sometimes sweet smell, so people thought of 'honey' when they named it. That's why honey fungus refers to an edible mushroom with honey-colored caps that usually grows at the base of trees and should be cooked before eating.
