Etymology

heat capacity ratio compares two kinds of heat capacity: one when the pressure stays the same (constant pressure) and one when the volume stays the same (constant volume). So, it tells 'how much one is of the other', which is why engineers and scientists use it to compare how a gas takes in heat under different conditions.
