Etymology

Ark of the Covenant was a special wooden chest, an ark, covered in gold that held the two stone tablets of the Ten Commandments and was carried on poles. Because it held God's laws and was seen as the place where God was close to the people, the Ark of the Covenant became a strong symbol of God's 'promise' to them.
