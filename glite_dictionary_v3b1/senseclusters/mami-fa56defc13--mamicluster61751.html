Etymology

Mami is the name of an ancient Mesopotamian mother goddess and comes from an old Sumerian form built from ma, a simple word for 'mother' often repeated like 'mama' to show warmth. So Mami was seen as a giver of life and childbirth, which is why her name feels like a caring 'mother' figure.
