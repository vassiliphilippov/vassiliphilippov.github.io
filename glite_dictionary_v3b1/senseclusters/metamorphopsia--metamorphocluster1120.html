Etymology

metamorphopsia comes from Greek parts: meta- 'change', morph 'shape', and -opsia 'vision', and meta- is the same start in metamorphosis, the change of a caterpillar into a butterfly, while -opsia is about seeing like in optic. So, metamorphopsia means that shapes in your sight look changed or distorted, which is why straight lines can seem wavy.
