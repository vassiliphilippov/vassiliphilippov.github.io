Etymology

chiromancer comes from two Greek parts: chiro- meaning 'hand' and -mancer meaning 'one who predicts', and you can see chiro- in chiropractor and -mancer in necromancer. So a chiromancer is a 'hand' fortune-teller who reads the lines on a palm to tell the future.
