Etymology

The plant name alpine azalea combines alpine, meaning 'of the mountains', and azalea, meaning 'a small, low-growing flowering shrub'. Imagine a carpet of pink flowers on a rocky slope, so the name means a mountain shrub that grows close to the ground.
