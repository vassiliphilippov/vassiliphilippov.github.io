Etymology

commitment to wokeness combines commitment (a promise to keep doing something) and wokeness (from 'woke', meaning 'awake' to social wrongs). Picture someone who stays 'awake' to spot unfairness and keeps working to change it, so the phrase means a strong dedication to noticing and acting on social injustice.
