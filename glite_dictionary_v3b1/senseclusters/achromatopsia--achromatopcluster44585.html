Etymology

achromatopsia comes from Greek parts: a- meaning 'not', chroma meaning 'color', and opsis meaning 'sight'. Think of monochrome ('one color') — achromatopsia is the opposite, 'no color sight', which is why people with it can only see shades of gray.
