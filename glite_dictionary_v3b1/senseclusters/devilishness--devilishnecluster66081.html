Etymology

devilishness comes from the familiar word devil plus the endings -ish (meaning 'like') and -ness (meaning 'state' or 'quality'). So devilishness literally means the 'quality of being like a devil', which helps explain its modern sense of playful mischief or wicked behaviour.
