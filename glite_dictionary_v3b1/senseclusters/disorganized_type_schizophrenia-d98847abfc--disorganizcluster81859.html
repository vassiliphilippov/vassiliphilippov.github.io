Etymology

disorganized type schizophrenia comes from doctors using disorganized to describe very messy speech and behavior and schizophrenia, a word meaning 'split mind'. Imagine a person's words and actions like a room in chaos; that image helps remember why it means a form of schizophrenia with confused thinking and strange, unpredictable behavior.
