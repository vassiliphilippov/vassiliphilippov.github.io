Etymology

actinic dermatitis comes from the parts actinic (meaning 'caused by light') and dermatitis (meaning 'skin inflammation'). It paints the image of strong sunlight or rays hitting the skin and making it red and sore, so it means 'skin inflammation caused by sunlight'.
