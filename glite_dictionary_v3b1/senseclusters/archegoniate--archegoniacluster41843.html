Etymology

archegoniate comes from Greek parts arche- meaning 'beginning' and gonia (from gonos) meaning 'birth' or 'offspring', the same idea you see in generation. So, archegoniate describes things related to the female organ in some plants that is the 'beginning' place where new plants start.
