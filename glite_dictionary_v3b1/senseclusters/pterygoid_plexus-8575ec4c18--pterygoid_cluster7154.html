Etymology

pterygoid plexus comes from the words pterygoid meaning 'wing' and plexus meaning 'braid' or 'network'. Imagine a small braided net of veins near the jaw like a little wing, and that image explains why the term means the network of veins that collects blood from the jaw and mouth.
