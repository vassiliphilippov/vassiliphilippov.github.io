Etymology

parahaoma comes from old Persian parts: haoma is the sacred plant and drink used in Zoroastrian ceremonies, and the prefix para- means something made 'for' or 'with' that plant. So parahaoma literally names the special haoma drink made for the ceremony, which is why it means that ritual drink today.
