Etymology

Theodore Roosevelt Memorial National Park is named for President Theodore Roosevelt, who had a ranch in the North Dakota badlands and loved the wild country there. The park was made as a 'memorial' — a place 'to remember' him and his work to protect the land, so the name shows it honors his life.
