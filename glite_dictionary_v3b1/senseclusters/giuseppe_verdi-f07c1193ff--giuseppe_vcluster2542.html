Etymology

Giuseppe Verdi was a 19th-century Italian composer famous for his operas. His last name Verdi means 'greens' in Italian, and one popular story says people used his name as a secret shout for 'Long live Victor Emmanuel' during Italy's fight to become one country, so his name came to stand for both powerful music and Italian pride.
