Etymology

The word unsweetly starts with un- meaning 'not', has sweet meaning 'pleasant', and the ending -ly which makes it tell how something is done. So, unsweetly means 'not pleasantly', like replying in a harsh or unfriendly way, the opposite of sweetly.
