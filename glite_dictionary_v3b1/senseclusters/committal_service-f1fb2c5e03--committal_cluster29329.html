Etymology

committal service comes from the image of a short ceremony where people 'commit' a body to its 'final resting place', such as being lowered into a grave or taken to a cremation site. The word committal is the act of placing the body and service is the ceremony, so the phrase means the final ceremony when the body is put to rest.
