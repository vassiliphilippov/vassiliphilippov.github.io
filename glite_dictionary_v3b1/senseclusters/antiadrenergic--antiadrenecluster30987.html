Etymology

antiadrenergic begins with anti- meaning 'against' and ends with adrenergic, which comes from adrenaline, the body chemical for 'fight or flight'. So an antiadrenergic drug works 'against adrenaline' and reduces its effects, for example slowing the heart or lowering blood pressure.
