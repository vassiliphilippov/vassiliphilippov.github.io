Etymology

t'ai chi chuan comes from Chinese words t'ai chi meaning 'supreme ultimate' and chuan meaning 'fist'. It paints the picture of slow, round movements that show a balance between two opposite forces, so the practice is a gentle fighting art that helps the body and mind stay calm and balanced.
