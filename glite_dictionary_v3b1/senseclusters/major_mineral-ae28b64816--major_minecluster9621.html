Etymology

major mineral comes from nutrition: major means 'needed in larger amounts' and mineral means a natural nutrient the body uses. Think of a big jar of salt as a major mineral and a tiny spice jar for smaller needs, so major mineral means a mineral the body needs in relatively large quantities.
