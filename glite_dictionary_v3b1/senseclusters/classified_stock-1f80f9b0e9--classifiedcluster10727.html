Etymology

Classified stock comes from the idea of shares that are classified, meaning 'sorted' into groups. Imagine piles of share cards labelled Class A and Class B: the cards are still stock, but each class has 'different rights', like more 'voting power'. That's why classified stock means shares divided into classes with different rights.
