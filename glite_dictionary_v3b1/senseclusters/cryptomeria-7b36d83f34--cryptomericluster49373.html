Etymology

The word Cryptomeria comes from Greek roots krypto- meaning 'hidden' and -meria meaning 'part'. So the name suggests 'hidden parts', a helpful image to remember this tall Japanese cedar whose small cones and inner wood are not easily seen and whose peeling bark can hide the trunk.
