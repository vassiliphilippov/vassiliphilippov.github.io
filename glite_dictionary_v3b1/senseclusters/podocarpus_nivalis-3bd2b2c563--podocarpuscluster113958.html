Etymology

Podocarpus nivalis combines two images: Podocarpus points to the plant's small fruit that sit on short stalks like tiny 'feet', and nivalis means 'of the snow' because it grows in snowy mountains. So, the name helps you remember this low, wide shrub with stalked fruit that lives on cold mountain slopes.
