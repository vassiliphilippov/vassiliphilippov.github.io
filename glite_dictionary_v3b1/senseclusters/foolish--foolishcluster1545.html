Etymology

The word foolish comes from fool plus the ending -ish. Long ago fool came from the Latin follis, meaning 'bellows' or 'windbag' as a picture of someone 'empty-headed', and -ish means 'having the quality of', so foolish means 'having the quality of a fool', that is 'not wise'.
