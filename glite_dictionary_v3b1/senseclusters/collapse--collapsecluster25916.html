Etymology

collapse comes from the Latin collabi, which means 'to fall together' and is made of col- 'together' and labi 'to slip'. The same idea appears in the word lapse, which means a 'slip'. So to collapse is to suddenly fall down or fail, like a person, building, or business dropping in on itself.
