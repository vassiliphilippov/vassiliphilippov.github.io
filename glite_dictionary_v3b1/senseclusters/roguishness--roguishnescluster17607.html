Etymology

roguishness comes from the simple word rogue, meaning a person who is a little dishonest or playful, plus -ish and -ness, which make it a quality. So, roguishness is the quality of being 'mischievous' or 'playfully dishonest', like someone with a cheeky grin who likes small pranks.
