Etymology

pyorrhea comes from Greek parts pyo- meaning 'pus' and -rrhea meaning 'flow', like in diarrhea which also uses -rrhea for 'flow'. So pyorrhea literally means 'pus flow' from the gums, which explains the pus discharge and loose teeth.
