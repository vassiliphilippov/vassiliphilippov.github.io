Etymology

The name semiconductor device comes from materials that are partly a conductor and partly an insulator, because semi means 'partly'. Imagine a part that lets electricity through sometimes and blocks it other times. That 'in-between' ability to control electricity is why the phrase means an electronic component used in circuits.
