Etymology

liquidate comes from the Latin word liquidus meaning 'fluid' or 'to melt', and later it came to mean 'clear away' or 'settle' a problem. A helpful way to remember the violent sense is to think of 'clearing away' a dangerous person, which is why it can mean to 'get rid of' someone, often by killing.
