Etymology

Pachyrhizus tuberosus comes from Greek and Latin parts: pachy- meaning 'thick', rhizus meaning 'root', and tuberosus meaning 'having tubers'. This name creates a picture of a climbing plant with large, edible roots, so it fits the plant we use and study today.
