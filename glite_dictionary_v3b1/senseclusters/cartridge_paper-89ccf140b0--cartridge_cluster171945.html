Etymology

cartridge paper comes from an old use: people made cartridges, small paper tubes for holding gunpowder, so the paper had to be thick and strong. So, because that paper was strong and smooth, artists began to use it for drawing and printing, which is why cartridge paper today means 'thick, high-quality paper' for art.
