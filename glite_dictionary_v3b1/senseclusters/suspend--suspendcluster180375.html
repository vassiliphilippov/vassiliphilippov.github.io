Etymology

suspend comes from the Latin parts sub- 'under' and pendere 'to hang', the same root you see in words like 'pendulum' and 'depend'. Think of 'hanging up' a task: to suspend something is to 'hang it up' for a time, so it is officially stopped or paused.
