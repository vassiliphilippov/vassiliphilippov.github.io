Etymology

Doctor of Sacred Theology comes from old university and church words: Doctor meant 'teacher', Sacred meant 'holy', and Theology meant the 'study of God'. So the name literally says 'teacher of the holy study of God', which is why the Church gives it as its 'highest degree' in theology.
