Etymology

abrasiveness comes from the Latin parts ab- 'away' and radere 'to scrape', and originally described the power to wear something away by scraping. Think of sandpaper or a rough sponge that 'scrapes' a surface — that is why abrasiveness means a material's rough, wearing quality.
