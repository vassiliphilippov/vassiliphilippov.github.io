Etymology

The noun Tudor arch comes from the Tudor time in England, where builders often used a low, slightly pointed arch over doors and windows. The image of that low, flattened point, between a round arch and a tall pointed arch, helps you remember, so Tudor arch now means 'a low, slightly pointed arch'.
