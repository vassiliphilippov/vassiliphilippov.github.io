Etymology

The phrase myth and legend joins two kinds of old story: myth (stories about gods and how the world began) and legend (stories about heroes or past events that are often made more exciting). Together they paint a picture of a culture's 'traditional stories' that mix fact and wonder, so the phrase means tales people tell that are often not fully true.
