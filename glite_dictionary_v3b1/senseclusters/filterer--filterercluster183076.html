Etymology

filterer comes from the basic word filter, which is built on the idea of passing something through a cloth or small holes to keep some parts and remove others, and the ending -er means 'one who'. So a filterer is 'one who' removes unwanted things by letting the wanted parts pass through, like a water filter or a kitchen sifter.
