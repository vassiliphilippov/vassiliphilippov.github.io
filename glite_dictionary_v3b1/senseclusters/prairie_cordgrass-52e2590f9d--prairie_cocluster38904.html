Etymology

The name prairie cordgrass comes from two words: prairie, meaning 'open grassland', and cordgrass, a name for grasses with long, strong stems that look like thin cords. So the name paints a simple picture of a tall, tough grass that grows in prairies and wetlands, and that is why it is used to protect riverbanks and to give shelter to birds and small animals.
