Etymology

Gentiana pneumonanthe mixes a name and a picture: Gentiana honors King Gentius, who was said to use gentian plants as medicine, and pneumonanthe comes from Greek pneumon 'lung' and anthe 'flower', so it literally means 'lung-flower'. So the name helps you picture this blue marsh gentian and remember that people once linked it to lung or chest remedies, which makes the plant easier to recall.
