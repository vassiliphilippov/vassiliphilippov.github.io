Etymology

swelteringly comes from the old English verb sweltan, which meant 'to be overcome', and the simple idea in swelter is being overwhelmed. A helpful way to remember the figurative use is to think of something so strong it overwhelms you, so swelteringly can mean 'to an extremely intense or excessive degree'.
