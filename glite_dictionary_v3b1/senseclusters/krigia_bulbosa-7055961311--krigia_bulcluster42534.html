Etymology

The plant name Krigia bulbosa comes from the way plant scientists name species: the first part, Krigia, is the group name and the second part, bulbosa, means 'bulb-like' because the plant has a small rounded base. So, the name helps you remember this small yellow flower that looks like a dandelion.
