Etymology

Tetrao urogallus is the scientific name for the large forest grouse called the capercaillie. The second part, urogallus, may come from old words meaning 'tail' and 'rooster', so imagine a huge rooster with a fan-shaped tail in the woods and you can see why the name points to this bird's big tail and rooster-like look.
