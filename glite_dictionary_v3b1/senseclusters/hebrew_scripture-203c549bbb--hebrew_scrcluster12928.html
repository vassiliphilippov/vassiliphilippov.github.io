Etymology

Hebrew Scripture literally means sacred writings in the Hebrew language and the word Scripture means 'writing'. Think of old scrolls with Hebrew letters being read in the community, like the 'Torah', the 'Prophets', and the 'Writings' together called the 'Tanakh', so the phrase refers to the Jewish sacred books today.
