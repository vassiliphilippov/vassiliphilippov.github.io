Etymology

green foxtail gets its name from the plant's look: the long, bushy, green flower heads look like a small fox's tail. Because those bristly 'tails' appear on many stems and the grass spreads fast in fields, people call this common 'weed' green foxtail, which is why the name means that particular grassy plant today.
