Etymology

The word shearwater comes from the parts shear, meaning 'to cut' or 'to skim', and water, meaning 'water', because this sea bird flies so low it looks like it is cutting or skimming the waves. So a shearwater is a long-winged ocean bird known for flying just above the water.
