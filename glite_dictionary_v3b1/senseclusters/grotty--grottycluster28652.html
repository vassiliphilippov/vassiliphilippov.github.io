Etymology

grotty probably comes from the short English slang grot, meaning 'rubbish', or from grotesque, meaning 'ugly'. A helpful way to remember it is to think of something that looks like rubbish or is ugly and dirty, which is why grotty means unpleasant, dirty, or shabby.
