Etymology

macaroni and cheese is named for its two main parts: macaroni pasta and melted cheese. One popular story says cooks in Italy mixed pasta and cheese, and when the idea reached England and America it became a cheap, warming 'comfort' food, which is why the name simply describes the dish.
