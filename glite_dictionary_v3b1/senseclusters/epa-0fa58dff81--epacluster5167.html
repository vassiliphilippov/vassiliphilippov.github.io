Etymology

E.P.A. is made from the first letters of Environmental, Protection, and Agency. That name tells its job: Environmental means about the natural world, Protection means 'keeping safe', and Agency means 'an organization', so the E.P.A. is the United States government organization that works to keep the environment safe.
