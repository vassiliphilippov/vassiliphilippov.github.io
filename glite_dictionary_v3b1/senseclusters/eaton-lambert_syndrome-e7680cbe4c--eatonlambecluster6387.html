Etymology

Eaton-Lambert syndrome is named after the doctors Eaton and Lambert, who first described patients with unusual muscle weakness. So the name now refers to a rare condition where the body's defence system attacks the connection between nerves and muscles, causing 'weakness' and tired hips and legs, and it is often linked to cancer.
