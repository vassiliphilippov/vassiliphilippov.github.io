Etymology

The word emaciate comes from Latin parts e- 'out' and macies 'thinness'. So to emaciate someone is to make them very thin or weak, as if their strength has been taken out. You can also see the same root in meager, which means 'small' or 'not enough', and that helps explain the idea of lack and thinness.
