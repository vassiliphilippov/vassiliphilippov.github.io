Etymology

The noun vacuum gauge comes from early glass or metal instruments where a scale or pointer showed how much air had been taken out; vacuum means 'empty space with no air' and gauge means 'a tool with a scale'. So, vacuum gauge now means 'a device that shows how strong the vacuum is'.
