Etymology

racily comes from racy, which in turn comes from race meaning 'speed' or 'excitement', and the ending -ly makes it an adverb. A helpful way to remember it is to think of a fast race that feels lively and a little spicy, so racily means 'in a lively, entertaining, and slightly risqué or suggestive way'.
