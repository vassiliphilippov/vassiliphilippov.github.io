Etymology

Serum globulin comes from serum, the clear part of the blood, and globulin, a word for small, round 'globe' proteins. Picture tiny round fighters floating in that clear blood called 'antibodies', so serum globulin means the round proteins in blood serum that include most 'antibodies'.
