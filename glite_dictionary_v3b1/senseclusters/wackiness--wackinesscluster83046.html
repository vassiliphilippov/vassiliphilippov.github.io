Etymology

The word wackiness comes from the slang adjective wacky, which means 'silly' or 'odd', plus the ending -ness that makes it 'the state of' something. A helpful way to remember it is to think of wacky like whack or wack — a sudden, strange action — so wackiness is the 'state of being amusingly odd or unpredictable'.
