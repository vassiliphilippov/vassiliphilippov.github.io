Etymology

Milwaukee goiter may come from the funny image of someone at a big local feast so full that their throat looks swollen; goiter is a word for a swollen neck and Milwaukee hints at hearty local meals. That's why people use Milwaukee goiter to joke about 'feeling overly full around the neck' after a large meal.
