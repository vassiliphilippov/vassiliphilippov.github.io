Etymology

Convexo-convex comes from the Latin root convex meaning 'arched' or 'curved outward', and it is like saying convex twice to show both sides are bulging out. Think of a lens shaped like a lentil with both sides curving outward; that is why a convexo-convex lens makes light come together.
