Etymology

The noun hypothetical imperative comes from two words: hypothetical, meaning 'if', and imperative, meaning 'a command'. Picture a sign that says 'If you want coffee, press this button' — it tells you what to do to get the result. So the term means a rule that tells you to act only when you have a specific goal.
