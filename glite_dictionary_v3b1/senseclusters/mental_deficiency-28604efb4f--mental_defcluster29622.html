Etymology

mental deficiency puts together mental (the mind) and deficiency (a lack), so it creates the image of a 'lack in the mind'. It was used to mean someone with 'below average' thinking ability, but today people prefer 'intellectual disability' because mental deficiency sounds old and can be hurtful.
