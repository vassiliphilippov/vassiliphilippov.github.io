Etymology

lowbush cranberry: The name lowbush cranberry comes from the plant's look: it grows on low shrubs, which gives the low and bush parts of the name, and it makes small red berries like a cranberry. So, the name tells you it is a 'small' and 'tart' berry that grows close to the ground.
