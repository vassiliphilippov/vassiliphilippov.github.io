Etymology

optic chiasm gets its name from its parts: optic means 'eye' and chiasm comes from the word 'chi', the name of a letter that looks like an 'X'. The name gives a clear picture: the nerves from each eye meet and cross in an X shape there, so the term means the X-shaped place where the optic nerves cross to send sight signals to the brain.
