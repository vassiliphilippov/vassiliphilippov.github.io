Etymology

Trombiculidae comes from the genus name Trombicula, which has the root tromb- meaning 'lump' and the small ending -cula meaning 'little'. So, it paints the picture of 'little lumps', a useful image because these tiny mites make small, itchy red bumps on the skin.
