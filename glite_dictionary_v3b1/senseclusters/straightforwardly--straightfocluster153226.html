Etymology

The word straightforwardly comes from the simple words straight ('not turning' or 'direct') and forward ('toward the front' and by extension 'honest'), with the adverb -ly. So to speak straightforwardly is to speak 'directly' and 'without hiding', like going straight ahead without turning.
