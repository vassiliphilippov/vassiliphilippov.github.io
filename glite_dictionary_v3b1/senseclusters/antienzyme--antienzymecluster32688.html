Etymology

antienzyme comes from the parts anti- 'against' and enzyme, a Greek-based word that refers to the 'leaven' or working part of yeast that makes dough rise. So, as a noun, an antienzyme is a substance that acts 'against' an enzyme and can block or slow its work.
