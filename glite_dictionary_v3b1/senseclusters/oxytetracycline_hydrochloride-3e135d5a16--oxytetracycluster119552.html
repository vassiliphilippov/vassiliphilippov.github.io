Etymology

oxytetracycline hydrochloride is a name that tells a tiny story: tetracycline uses 'tetra' for 'four' because the drug has four linked parts, oxy marks a small oxygen change, and hydrochloride shows the form that easily dissolves in water. So, the name helps you remember it is a medicine made from soil bacteria and is an 'antibiotic' used to treat infections.
