Etymology

myrtle flag comes from two words: myrtle, a fragrant plant, and flag, an old name for a 'marsh plant' with long, sword-shaped leaves. So the name points to a wetland plant that smells like myrtle and has sword-shaped leaves, which is why we use it for this plant today.
