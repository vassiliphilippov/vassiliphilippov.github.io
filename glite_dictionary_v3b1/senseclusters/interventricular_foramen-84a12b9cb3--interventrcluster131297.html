Etymology

interventricular foramen literally names a small foramen, a 'opening' between the brain's ventricles, the spaces that hold brain fluid. Imagine a tiny doorway that lets brain fluid move from the side rooms into the middle room, so the term means the small holes that connect the side and middle ventricles.
