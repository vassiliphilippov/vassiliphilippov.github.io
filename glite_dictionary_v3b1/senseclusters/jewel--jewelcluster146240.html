Etymology

jewel comes from an old French word and the Latin jocale, which meant 'plaything' or 'delight' and is related to jocus 'play', the same root as the English word joke. People treasured these little shining items so they became signs of value. So a jewel today is a small, carefully cut and polished stone kept because it is beautiful and precious.
