Etymology

The noun radiation pattern comes from the picture of an antenna like a lamp that sends out radiation more in some directions and less in others, and when you draw this on a graph you see a clear pattern of strong and weak spots, so the phrase means 'how the signal is stronger or weaker in each direction'.
