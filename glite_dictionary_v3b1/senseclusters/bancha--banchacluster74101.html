Etymology

bancha comes from the Japanese parts ban and cha, where ban means 'late' or 'evening' and cha means 'tea'. It was made from leaves picked later in the season, so the drink is milder and cheaper, which is why bancha today refers to a simple, easy-drinking green tea.
