Etymology

The word bulrush can be remembered as bull + rush: the rush part is a common name for 'wetland plants' that grow in water, and thinking of a 'bull' gives a picture of a big, strong plant with thick stems that people used to make mats and baskets. So the name fits its meaning as a tall, sturdy plant from marshes and riverbanks.
