Etymology

hypercholesterolemia breaks into three parts: hyper- meaning 'too much', as in hyperactive, cholesterol meaning the fatty substance in the blood, and -emia meaning 'blood', as in anemia. So it simply means 'too much cholesterol in the blood', which explains the medical condition.
