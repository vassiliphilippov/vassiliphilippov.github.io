Etymology

The term lubricating system comes from the simple image of oil being moved to parts that touch: lubricating means putting oil on parts and system means the connected parts that do the job. In an engine a pump pushes oil to bearings so moving parts do not rub, so the phrase means 'a system that sends oil to engine parts to stop rubbing and wear'.
