Etymology

concelebrate comes from Latin parts con- meaning 'with' and celebrare meaning 'to celebrate', and it shares the same root as the everyday word celebrate. So, imagine two or more priests 'celebrating with' each other — to concelebrate is to lead a Mass or religious ceremony together.
