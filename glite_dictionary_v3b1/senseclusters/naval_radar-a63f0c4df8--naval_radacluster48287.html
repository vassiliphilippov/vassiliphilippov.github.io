Etymology

naval radar combines naval, meaning 'ships', and radar, a machine that sends invisible radio pulses and listens for an 'echo'. Imagine a ship sending a pulse and waiting for the echo to find other boats or land, so naval radar means a radar system on ships used to detect objects and help them find their way at sea.
