Etymology

Number Ten comes from the house number 10 Downing Street, the official home and office of the 'Prime Minister' in Britain. Because people often use the address to mean the person who works there, Number Ten came to mean the Prime Minister or their office. In sport the shirt 'number 10' was worn by the player who leads the team's attacks, so Number Ten also means a key creative player.
