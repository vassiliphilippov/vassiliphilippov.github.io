Etymology

Obtuseness comes from the Latin obtusus, meaning 'dull' or 'blunt'. A helpful way to remember it is the 'obtuse' angle from school, which is wide and not sharp, so obtuseness can also mean a mind that is not 'sharp' and is slow to understand.
