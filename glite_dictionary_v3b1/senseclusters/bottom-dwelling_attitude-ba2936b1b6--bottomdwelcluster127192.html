Etymology

The phrase bottom-dwelling attitude gives a picture of animals that live on the bottom of the sea and keep dwelling there in the dark, always looking down. One popular idea says this image became a way to describe people who focus on the worst parts of life, so it now means being 'negative' or 'pessimistic'.
