Etymology

Zoomastigote comes from Greek parts zoo- meaning 'animal' and -mastigote meaning 'whip', referring to the whip-like tail these cells use to swim. So a zoomastigote is a single-celled, animal-like organism that moves with a whip-like tail and does not act like a plant.
