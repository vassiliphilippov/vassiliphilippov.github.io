Etymology

blitzed comes from the German word blitz, which means 'lightning'. In English blitz was used for a sudden, powerful attack—like being 'bombed'—and people later used that idea as slang for being overwhelmed by drink or by tiredness, so blitzed means very drunk or completely wiped out.
