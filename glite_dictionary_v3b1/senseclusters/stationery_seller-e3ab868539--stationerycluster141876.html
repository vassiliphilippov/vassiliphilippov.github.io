Etymology

stationery seller comes from the simple image of a person at a fixed stall selling paper and pens. Imagine a small shop full of notebooks, envelopes and pens: stationery is the paper and writing things, and seller is the person who sells them. So the phrase means 'a person or business that sells writing materials and office supplies'.
