Etymology

bleary-eyed comes from the old word bleary, which meant 'dim' or 'blurred', plus eyed meaning 'having eyes'. Think of someone's eyes like a fogged window after no sleep, which is why bleary-eyed describes tired, unfocused eyes from lack of sleep or exhaustion.
