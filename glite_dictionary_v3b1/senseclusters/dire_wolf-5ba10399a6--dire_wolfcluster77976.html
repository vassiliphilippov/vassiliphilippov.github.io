Etymology

In stories, a dire wolf is a very large or supernatural wolf because writers used the real Ice Age animal's name to imagine an even bigger, scarier creature. The words dire and wolf give the image of a dreadful giant wolf, so the phrase means a 'magical' or 'huge' wolf in tales.
