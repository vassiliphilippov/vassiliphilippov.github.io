Etymology

Linker histone is the name for a small protein; linker shows its job of 'linking' and histone is the protein that DNA wraps around. Think of DNA as beads on a string and the linker histone as a tiny clip between the beads that helps 'pack' the string tightly inside the cell, so the name describes its job.
