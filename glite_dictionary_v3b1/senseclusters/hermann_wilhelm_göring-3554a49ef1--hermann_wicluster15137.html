Etymology

Hermann Wilhelm Göring was a top German Nazi leader in the 1930s and 1940s. He helped set up the secret police called the 'Gestapo', played a leading role in Germany's war effort, and after World War II he was tried for 'war crimes', so his name is remembered as that of a leading Nazi and a criminal.
