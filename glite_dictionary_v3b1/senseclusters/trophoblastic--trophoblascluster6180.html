Etymology

trophoblastic comes from Greek parts troph- meaning 'nourishment' and -blast meaning 'young cell'. So trophoblastic describes the early cells that help feed the embryo and build the placenta. A helpful way to remember troph- is to think of atrophy, which shows what happens when something has no 'nourishment' and becomes weaker.
