Etymology

The word sweet-breathed joins sweet, meaning 'pleasant' or 'nice-smelling', with breathed, from breathe, meaning 'to send out air'. So it describes someone or something that 'breathes' a pleasant scent, which is why it means having pleasantly fragrant breath.
