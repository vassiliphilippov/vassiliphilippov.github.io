Etymology

sleazy later moved from meaning 'shabby in look' to meaning 'morally low' or 'dishonest', because a dirty, cheap place was thought to hide bad behavior. The same root gives us the noun sleaze, so think of sleazy as a warning about someone or somewhere that seems 'shady' and untrustworthy.
