Etymology

meadow spittlebug is named for what it does and where it lives: the insect lives in a meadow and its young cover themselves in a foamy, spit-like mass called spittle on grass and plants. That clear image — little bugs hiding in 'spittle' foam in a meadow — is why the name refers to this insect today.
