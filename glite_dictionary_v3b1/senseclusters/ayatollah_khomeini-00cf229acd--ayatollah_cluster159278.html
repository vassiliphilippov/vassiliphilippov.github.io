Etymology

Ayatollah Khomeini has a title and a place name: Ayatollah is a high religious title meaning 'sign of God', and Khomeini shows he came from the town of Khomein. So, the name tells you he was a senior religious leader from Khomein who became Iran's 'Supreme Leader' after the 1979 revolution.
