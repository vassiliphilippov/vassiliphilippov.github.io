Etymology

yellow lupine gets its name from its bright yellow flowers and from lupine, a word that may come from the Latin 'lupus' meaning 'wolf' because people long ago thought the plant 'ate' the soil. That's why yellow lupine became a name for a yellow flower that farmers grow as 'animal feed' and to improve the soil.
