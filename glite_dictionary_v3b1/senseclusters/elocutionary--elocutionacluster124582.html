Etymology

elocutionary also came to describe a very formal or showy way of speaking. It comes from the Latin parts ex- 'out' and loqui 'to speak', and because taught public speech often sounded careful and stiff, elocutionary can mean speech that is 'overly formal' or 'ornate'.
