Etymology

electroplating joins electro- (from 'electric') and plate (a 'thin metal layer' or 'metal plate'), so it literally means putting a metal plate onto something. So electroplating is using electricity to lay a thin metal layer on an object, which is why it makes things shiny or stops rust.
