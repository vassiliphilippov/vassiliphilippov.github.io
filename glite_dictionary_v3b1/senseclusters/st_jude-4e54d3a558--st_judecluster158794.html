Etymology

St. Jude was one of Jesus' twelve followers and became known for giving hope to people in very hard, hopeless situations. One popular story says that when a problem seemed impossible, people began to pray to him, and that is why St. Jude is now the saint people ask for help with 'lost or desperate causes'.
