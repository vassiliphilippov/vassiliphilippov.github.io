Etymology

dideoxycytosine breaks into di- 'two', deoxy 'without oxygen', and cytosine 'a DNA base'. So the name means a cytosine missing two oxygens, and as a drug it looks like a normal DNA piece that fools the virus's copying machine and stops the virus from making more copies.
