Etymology

sweet-birch oil is oil taken from the bark and twigs of the sweetbirch tree and has a strong minty smell. People rubbed this oil on sore muscles and joints and felt a warm, soothing feeling, so the name now means the oil used 'to relieve' minor muscle and joint pain.
