Etymology

isoflurane is made of parts: iso- meaning 'same', fluor- from the element you also see in fluoride (toothpaste), and -ane which marks a simple chemical. So isoflurane is a fluorine-containing chemical used as a gas or liquid to make patients sleep during surgery.
