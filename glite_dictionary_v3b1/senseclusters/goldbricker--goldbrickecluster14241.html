Etymology

goldbricker comes from the slang gold brick, a 'fake' or 'worthless' bar of gold once sold by scammers. Because a gold brick looked valuable but did no real work, people began to call someone who pretends to be busy or sick a goldbricker, so it means a person who avoids work.
