Etymology

The phrase make a blunder may come from an old word that meant 'to close the eyes', so imagine someone moving blindly and bumping into things. That picture of acting blindly explains why blunder means 'a serious or embarrassing mistake', so to make a blunder is to make a big, obvious error.
