Etymology

The word smotherer comes from the verb smother, which began with the idea of 'covering' someone so they 'cannot breathe', and the ending -er makes it a person who does the action. So a smotherer is someone who 'stops' or 'holds back' another person, which is why we call overprotective people or harsh critics 'smotherers'.
