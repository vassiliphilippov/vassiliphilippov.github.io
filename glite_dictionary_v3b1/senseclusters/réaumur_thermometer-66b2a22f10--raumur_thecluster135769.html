Etymology

Réaumur thermometer is named after the French scientist Réaumur, who in the 1700s made an alcohol thermometer and set a scale called degrees Réaumur with 0 for ice and 80 for boiling water. So a Réaumur thermometer is a thermometer that shows temperature in his scale.
