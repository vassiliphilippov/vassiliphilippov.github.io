Etymology

The plant name Languas speciosa may come from Latin and gives a simple picture: Languas probably refers to a long, tongue-like leaf or flower lip and speciosa means 'showy' or 'beautiful'. So the name points to the plant's shiny leaves and bright, shell-like flowers you see today.
