Etymology

energy-absorbing is made of energy and absorbing, and the root absorb means 'to take in' — think of a sponge that soaks up water. So an energy-absorbing material or process 'takes in' energy instead of giving it out, which is why it can protect in a crash or needs heat in a reaction.
