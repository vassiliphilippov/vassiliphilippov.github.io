Etymology

The name subfamily Papilionoideae comes from the Latin root 'papilio' meaning 'butterfly' because many pea-like flowers have petals that look like a small butterfly, and the word subfamily shows it is a group inside a larger plant family. That's why the name fits this group of plants such as beans and peas.
