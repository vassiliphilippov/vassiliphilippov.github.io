Etymology

attaboy probably comes from the quick spoken phrase 'that's a boy', with atta as a short, casual form of 'that's a' and boy meaning a male. A helpful way to remember it is to imagine someone shouting 'atta boy!' like 'well done', which is why we use it to cheer or praise someone.
