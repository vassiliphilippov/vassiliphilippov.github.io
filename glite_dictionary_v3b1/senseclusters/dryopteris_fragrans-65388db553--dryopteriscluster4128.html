Etymology

Dryopteris fragrans comes from old Greek and Latin words. The first part, Dryopteris, comes from words for 'oak' and 'fern', and fragrans means 'fragrant', so the name gives the image of a sweet-smelling fern and explains why it refers to a fern with scented fronds.
