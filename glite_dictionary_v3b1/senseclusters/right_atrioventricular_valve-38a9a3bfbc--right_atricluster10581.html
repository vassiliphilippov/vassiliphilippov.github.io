Etymology

Right atrioventricular valve names a one-way flap on the right side of the heart: atrioventricular means between the 'atrium' and the 'ventricle', and valve is like a door that opens and closes. So this valve lets blood move from the right 'atrium' to the right 'ventricle' and stops it from flowing back.
