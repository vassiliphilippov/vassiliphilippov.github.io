Etymology

The name pocket flask comes from the words pocket, the small space in your clothes, and flask, a flat small bottle for alcohol. Picture someone slipping a flat bottle into their jacket pocket to take a quick sip later. That image explains why pocket flask means 'a small, flat container carried in a pocket for alcohol'.
