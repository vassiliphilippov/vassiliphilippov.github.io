Etymology

The name Virginia fence may come from early Virginia farms where people stacked long wooden rails in a zigzag pattern. These zigzag rails could stand 'without posts' and made a strong border, so today Virginia fence means that kind of wooden fence used to 'mark a boundary' or for decoration.
