Etymology

Erigeron annuus gets its name from old words: Erigeron suggests an 'old man' because the plant's white, fluffy seed heads look like a tiny beard, and annuus means 'one-year' because the plant completes its life in a single year. That's why the name helps you picture a common, daisy-like wildflower that appears and finishes its life within one season.
