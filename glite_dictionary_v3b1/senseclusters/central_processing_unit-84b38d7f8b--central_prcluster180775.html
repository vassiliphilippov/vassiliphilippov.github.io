Etymology

central processing unit is a name that gives a clear picture: central means 'main', processing means 'working on data', and unit means 'one device'. Think of it as the computer's 'brain' in the middle that does most of the thinking and tells the other parts what to do, so today the term means the main part that controls and processes data.
