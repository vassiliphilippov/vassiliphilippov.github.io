Etymology

Polypodium glycyrrhiza may come from old Greek words: Polypodium meaning 'many little feet' and glycyrrhiza meaning 'sweet root'. The name fits the picture of a fern with many small leaf parts and a sweet root people chewed, so it helps you remember this sweet-tasting plant.
