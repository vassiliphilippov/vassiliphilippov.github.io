Etymology

The noun pyramidal tract comes from pyramid-shaped groups of nerve fibers near the base of the brain; pyramidal points to the pyramid shape and tract means a bundle or 'road' of nerves. So the pyramidal tract is the main nerve 'road' that carries brain signals for 'voluntary movement' down to the spinal cord.
