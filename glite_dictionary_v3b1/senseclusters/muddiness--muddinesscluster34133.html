Etymology

muddiness comes from the simple word mud 'wet earth' or 'dirt', plus the endings -y 'full of' and -ness 'state of'. Picture a river after a storm, full of brown earth so you cannot see the bottom, so muddiness means the state of being covered in or full of mud.
