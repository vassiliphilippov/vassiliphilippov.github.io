Etymology

Uniformitarian comes from the root uniform ('same' or 'one form') plus a word ending that describes a belief, so it paints a picture of nature using the 'same' slow processes over time. That's why a uniformitarian idea means changes happen gradually and consistently rather than by sudden events.
