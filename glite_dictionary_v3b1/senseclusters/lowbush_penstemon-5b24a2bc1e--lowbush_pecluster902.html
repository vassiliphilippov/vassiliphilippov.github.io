Etymology

The plant name lowbush penstemon combines lowbush (a plant that grows close to the ground) and penstemon, a flower often called 'beardtongue' because one inside part of the flower looks like a small hairy tongue. So the name simply tells you it's a penstemon that grows low and bushy, which matches the plant's shape and bright flowers.
