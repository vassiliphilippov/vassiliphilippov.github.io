Etymology

mechanical power comes from two simple ideas: mechanical means 'about machines' and power means the 'ability' to do work. Imagine a motor lifting a weight—the phrase gives the picture of how fast a machine does work, so today mechanical power means the 'rate' at which a machine does work, often measured in watts.
