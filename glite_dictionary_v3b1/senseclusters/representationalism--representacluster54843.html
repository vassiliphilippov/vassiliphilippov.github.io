Etymology

representationalism comes from represent meaning 'to show' (as in present) and -ism meaning 'a theory or belief'. So, in philosophy, representationalism is the idea that our minds make 'inner pictures' that 'show' the world to us, and that our knowledge comes from these pictures rather than from direct experience.
