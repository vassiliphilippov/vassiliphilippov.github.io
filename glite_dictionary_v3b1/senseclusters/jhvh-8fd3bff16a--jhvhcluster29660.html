Etymology

JHVH is a written form of the four Hebrew letters YHWH, the special name for God in the Old Testament. Hebrew writing often leaves out vowels, and later European readers used J for the first sound, so JHVH is a way to show the divine name on the page without giving its exact pronunciation, and you may also see the related forms Yahweh or Jehovah.
