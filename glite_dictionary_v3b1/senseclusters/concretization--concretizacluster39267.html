Etymology

concretization shares roots con- ('together') and crescere ('to grow') with the word concrete, which suggests something 'solid' or 'real', and a helpful way to remember it is to think of an idea 'growing together' into a clear thing. So concretization means making an abstract idea more specific, real, or concrete.
