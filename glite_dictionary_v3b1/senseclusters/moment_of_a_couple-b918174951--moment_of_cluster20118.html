Etymology

moment of a couple uses couple to mean a pair of equal pushes in opposite directions and moment to mean the amount of turning they cause. Imagine two people pushing on opposite sides of a wheel; the farther apart they push, the more the wheel will turn, so the 'moment' tells how strong that turn is.
