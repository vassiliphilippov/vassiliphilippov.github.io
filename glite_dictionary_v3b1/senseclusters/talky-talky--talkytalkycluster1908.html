Etymology

talky-talky starts with talk plus the friendly ending -y, and the whole word is repeated like in 'chit-chat' or 'wishy-washy' to make it feel extra or playful. So talky-talky means a style with 'too much talking' or lots of chat, often in a way that feels long or slow.
