Etymology

The term mass culture comes from the words mass (a large group of people) and culture (the music, stories and ideas people share). When newspapers, radio and TV sent the same songs, shows and ideas to lots of people, those things became common across the public, so mass culture now means culture 'shared by many people' through television, radio and the internet.
