Etymology

portacaval shunt comes from its parts: porta, the vein from the gut, caval, the large vein to the heart, and shunt, a new passage. Imagine joining two rivers with a small canal so water can 'bypass' a lake; a portacaval shunt lets blood 'bypass the liver', which is why the name describes this surgical passage.
