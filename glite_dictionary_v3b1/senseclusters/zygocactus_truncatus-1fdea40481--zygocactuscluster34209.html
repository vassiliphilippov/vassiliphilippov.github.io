Etymology

Zygocactus truncatus may come from Greek and Latin words that describe the plant: zygo means 'joined' and refers to its flat stem pieces that fit together, and truncatus means 'cut off' and points to the blunt ends of those pieces. So the name helps you remember this houseplant with joined stem segments and bright winter flowers.
