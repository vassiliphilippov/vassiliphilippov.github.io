Etymology

zauschneria is a plant name formed from a person's name Zauschner plus the common plant ending -ia, so it means 'Zauschner's plant'. A helpful way to remember zauschneria is to think of this 'Zauschner's plant' as the bright red California fuchsia, a small group of flowering plants that attract hummingbirds.
