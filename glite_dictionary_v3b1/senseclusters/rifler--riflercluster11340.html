Etymology

rifler comes from rifle + -er, meaning 'someone who uses a rifle'. The word rifle comes from an old word meaning 'to cut grooves', because rifles have grooves inside the barrel that make a bullet spin and fly straighter, so a rifler is a shooter or soldier who uses that kind of gun.
