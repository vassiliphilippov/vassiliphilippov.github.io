Etymology

transfer RNA is named for what it does: transfer means to carry and RNA is a small helper molecule, so picture a tiny helper carrying one 'amino acid' to the cell's protein-making machine. That's why transfer RNA means the molecule that brings specific 'amino acids' so the cell can build a 'protein'.
