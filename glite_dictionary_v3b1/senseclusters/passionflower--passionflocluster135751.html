Etymology

passionflower takes its name from the familiar word passion, which here means Christ's 'suffering'. Christian missionaries in South America saw parts of the showy flower as signs of that story and called the plant a 'passion' flower, so the name stuck to the climbing plant with bright blooms and edible fruit.
