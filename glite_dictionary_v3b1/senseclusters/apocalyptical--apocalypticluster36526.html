Etymology

apocalyptical comes from the Greek apokalypsis, made of apo- 'away' and kalyptō 'to cover', so it originally meant an 'uncovering' or 'reveal'. So apocalyptical is used for visions or warnings that 'uncover' terrible events, which is why it now describes things that predict great destruction or the end of the world.
