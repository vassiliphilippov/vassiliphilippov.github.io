Etymology

Euphorbia cyparissias may be named after an old doctor and for how the plant looks. The word Euphorbia comes from a man called Euphorbus who used the plant's milky sap, and cyparissias means 'like a cypress' because its thin, pointy leaves look like a small cypress tree, so the name helps you remember the plant's shape.
