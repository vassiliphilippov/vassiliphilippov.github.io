Etymology

Woodwardia virginica may be named after an early botanist called Woodward, and the second part, virginica, means 'from Virginia' because the fern grows there. Its long, arching leaves with chain-like rows of small dots made people call it the 'Virginia chain fern', so the name gives a clear picture of the plant today.
