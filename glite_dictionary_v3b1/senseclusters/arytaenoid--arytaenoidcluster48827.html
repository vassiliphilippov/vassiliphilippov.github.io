Etymology

arytaenoid comes from a Greek part arytaen-, meaning 'ladle', plus the ending -oid, meaning 'like' and seen in asteroid. A helpful way to remember it is that the two small, 'ladle-like' cartilages at the back of the voice box have that cup shape, which explains the name and how they help move the vocal cords.
