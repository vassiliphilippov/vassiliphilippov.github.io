Etymology

Amanita phalloides comes from the mushroom group name Amanita and the word phalloides, which means 'penis-shaped' because the white stalk and rounded cap make that image clear. It is commonly called the 'death cap' because, though it looks harmless like edible mushrooms, eating it can be deadly, so the name reminds you it is dangerous.
