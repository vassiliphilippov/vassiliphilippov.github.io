Etymology

Paraguayan is the name for a person from Paraguay, formed from the country name Paraguay plus the ending -an meaning 'from' or 'belonging to'. Paraguay comes from a local Guaraní word for the long river in the area, so a Paraguayan is simply 'a native or inhabitant of Paraguay'.
