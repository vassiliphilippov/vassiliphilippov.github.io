Etymology

lysogenization comes from parts that mean 'to break' and 'gene', with lyso- from a Greek word for 'to break or loosen' and -gen like in gene or genetic meaning 'origin' or 'birth'. A helpful way to remember lysogenization is that it is the process when a virus's genes become part of a bacterium and can later cause the cell to 'break', so the old roots explain the modern meaning.
