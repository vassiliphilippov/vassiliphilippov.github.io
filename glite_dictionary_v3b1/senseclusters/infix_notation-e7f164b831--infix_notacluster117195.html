Etymology

Infix notation simply names the 'in-the-middle' way of writing math: the operator, for example the word 'plus' or 'times', sits between its two operands, the numbers it combines. So when we write 3 plus 4 and use parentheses to show 'which to do first', we are using Infix notation.
