Etymology

Oncorhynchus nerka comes from old Greek and a Russian name: Oncorhynchus may mean 'hooked snout' because male fish grow a hooked jaw when they return to rivers to lay eggs, and nerka is the Russian name people used for this red salmon. So the name points to the fish's hooked mouth and its life as a red salmon that returns to rivers to lay eggs and then dies.
