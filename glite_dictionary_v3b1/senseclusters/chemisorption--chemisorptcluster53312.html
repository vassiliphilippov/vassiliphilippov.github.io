Etymology

chemisorption joins chemi- as in chemical or chemistry and sorption like 'absorb' or 'adsorb', with 'sorption' meaning 'take up' or 'stick to'. So, chemisorption is when a substance 'sticks' to a surface by forming 'chemical bonds', and this sticking is often hard to reverse.
