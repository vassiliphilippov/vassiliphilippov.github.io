Etymology

The word nonexhaustive starts with non- meaning 'not' and the rest is exhaustive, which comes from exhaust meaning 'to use up' or 'draw out'. So exhaustive is like 'using up everything' or 'complete', and nonexhaustive means 'not complete' or 'only partial'.
