Etymology

The phrase antisocial tendency comes from its parts: anti ('against'), social ('people' or 'group'), and tendency ('habit' or 'leaning'). Picture someone who steps back from others or breaks group rules; that's why the phrase means a way of behaving that avoids or goes against social interaction.
