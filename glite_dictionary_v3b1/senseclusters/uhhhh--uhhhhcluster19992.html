Etymology

The word uhhhh is simply a stretched hesitation sound, like saying uh or um for longer when you are thinking. A helpful way to remember it is to imagine someone dragging the sound out while they search for the right words, so it shows 'hesitation' or 'uncertainty' and fills a 'pause' in conversation.
