Etymology

The plant honey mesquite is a thorny shrub or small tree whose name paints a picture: it makes long, sweet seed pods that taste like honey, and mesquite is the shrub itself. Farmers and grazing animals ate those sweet pods as 'feed', and the plant's deep roots and thorny clumps explain why the name refers to a dense, hard-to-clear bush.
