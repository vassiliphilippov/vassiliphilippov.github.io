Etymology

wally is British slang for a silly or clumsy person. Its exact origin is unclear, but it probably came from using the common name Wally, a short form of Wallace, as a joking insult in Britain from the 1960s, much like calling someone 'silly Billy'. So, think of wally as a name turned into a word for 'a foolish person'.
