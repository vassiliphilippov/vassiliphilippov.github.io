Etymology

The phrase overly sugary comes from the simple image of food that is very sugary: imagine a cake with so much sugar that it tastes 'too sweet' and almost fake. So, when words, films, or stories feel 'too sweet' or 'too emotional', we call them overly sugary because they give that same fake, too-much feeling.
