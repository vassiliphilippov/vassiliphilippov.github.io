Etymology

absurdist As a noun, absurdist joins absurd ('not making sense') with the person-ending -ist, like in artist or dentist, so a helpful way to remember it is 'a person who follows the absurd'. That's why an absurdist (a person) is someone who believes life can be confusing and without clear purpose.
