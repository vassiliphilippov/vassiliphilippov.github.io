Etymology

Marshalling yard comes from the idea of a 'marshal' who puts people or things in order, like soldiers standing in lines, and yard meaning a work area. So a marshalling yard is the place where railway cars are lined up and attached to make trains, which is why the name now means 'a yard for assembling and sorting trains'.
