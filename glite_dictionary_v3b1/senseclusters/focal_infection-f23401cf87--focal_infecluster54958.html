Etymology

The medical term focal infection comes from the image of one bad spot in the body: focal means a single spot and infection means germs that cause harm. Like a small fire that sends smoke into other rooms, a focal infection is one local infection that causes problems in other parts of the body, so it means 'an infection in one place that affects other places'.
