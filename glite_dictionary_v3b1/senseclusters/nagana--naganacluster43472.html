Etymology

nagana comes from a Southern African language, probably Zulu, where the name meant 'to be weak' or 'sick' in animals and was used by local people for that illness. So, nagana today is the name for the disease that makes cattle weak because of tiny parasites, and thinking of it as the local word for 'animal weakness' helps you remember its meaning.
