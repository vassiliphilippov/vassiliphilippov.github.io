Etymology

aluminum bronze comes from a simple idea: it is a 'alloy', a metal mix where aluminum is added to bronze, a metal made mainly of 'copper', so the name literally describes its parts. That's why the term means a strong metal that does not corrode easily and is used for things like ship propellers and valves.
