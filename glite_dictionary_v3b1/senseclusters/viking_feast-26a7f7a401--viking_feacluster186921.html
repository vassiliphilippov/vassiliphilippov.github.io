Etymology

Viking feast may come from the image of Vikings sitting at a long wooden table, sharing roasted meat and strong drink in a loud, happy celebration, so the words Viking and feast make a clear picture of a hearty, communal meal. That's why today Viking feast means a 'big, special meal' inspired by those old gatherings.
