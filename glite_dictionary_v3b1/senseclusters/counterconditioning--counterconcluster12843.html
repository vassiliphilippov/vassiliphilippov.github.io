Synonyms

  • reconditioning
    0.02/m6.8
    Changes or relearns conditioned responses through training or therapy
    vscounterconditioning: is broader meaning relearning responses generally rather than specifically replacing an unwanted one
  • counter conditioning
    0.00/m7.7
    Teaches a new incompatible response to a stimulus through therapy
    vscounterconditioning: has identical meaning but is written as a single word
  • reciprocal inhibition
    0.00/m8.4
    Deliberately elicits one response to inhibit an incompatible response like relaxation for anxiety
    vscounterconditioning: instead teaches a new desired response to replace the unwanted reaction
  • reciprocal-inhibition therapy
    0.00/m8.8
    Uses an incompatible response to suppress another as a therapeutic technique
    vscounterconditioning: focuses on replacing an unwanted reaction with a desired one rather than mere suppression

Etymology

counterconditioning comes from the prefix counter- meaning 'against' and conditioning meaning 'training a response'. So, counterconditioning is a therapy method that 'trains' a new, positive response to replace an unwanted one.
