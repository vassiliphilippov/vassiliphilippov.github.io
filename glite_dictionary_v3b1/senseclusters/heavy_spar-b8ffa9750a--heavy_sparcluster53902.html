Etymology

The name heavy spar paints a simple picture: spar was an old word for a shiny mineral, and this kind of spar is very heavy because it contains the metal 'barium', so people called it heavy spar. That's why the term now refers to the white, heavy mineral 'barite' that is a main source of 'barium'.
