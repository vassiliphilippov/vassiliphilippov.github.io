Etymology

The word self-pleasing comes from self- ('one's own') and pleasing ('to make happy' or 'to satisfy'), so picture someone who tries to make themselves happy first. That's why self-pleasing describes a person who cares mainly about satisfying their own desires, often without thinking of others.
