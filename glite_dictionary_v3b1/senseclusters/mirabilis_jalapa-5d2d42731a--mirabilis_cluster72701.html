Etymology

Mirabilis jalapa has a name that gives a simple picture: mirabilis means 'wonderful' and its flowers open each late afternoon like a small surprise, so people call it 'four o'clock'. So today the name refers to a garden plant with fragrant, colorful blooms that open in the late afternoon.
