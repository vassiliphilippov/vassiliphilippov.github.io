Etymology

Stalinoid approach comes from the name of leader Joseph Stalin, where Stalinoid means 'like Stalin' and approach means a way of doing things. Stalin ruled with strict orders and no room for disagreement, so the phrase now means a way of managing that uses 'strict control' and leaves little space for other ideas.
