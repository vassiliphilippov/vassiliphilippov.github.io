Etymology

sponge morel is named for how the mushroom looks: its rounded cap is full of pits and ridges like a soft kitchen sponge, and it is a kind of morel mushroom. That's why the name tells you it is an 'edible' mushroom with a sponge-like, pitted 'cap'.
