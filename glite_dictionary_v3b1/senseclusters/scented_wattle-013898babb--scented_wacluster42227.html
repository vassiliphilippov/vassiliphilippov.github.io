Etymology

scented wattle gets its name from its sweet-smelling flowers and the old word wattle, which meant a fence made of woven sticks. Because the plant has thin, stick-like branches and very fragrant yellow blooms used in perfume, the name points to a fragrant shrub used in perfumery.
