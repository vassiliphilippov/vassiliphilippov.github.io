Etymology

Caulophyllum thalictroides may come from Greek words: caulo meaning 'stem' and phyllum meaning 'leaf', because the plant's large leaves seem to grow right from the stem. The ending thalictroides means 'like Thalictrum', a meadow plant, so the full name is simply a description of how the plant looks today.
