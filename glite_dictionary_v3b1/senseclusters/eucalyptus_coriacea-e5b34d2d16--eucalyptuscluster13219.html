Etymology

Eucalyptus coriacea comes from two old words: Eucalyptus means 'well-covered' because the flower buds wear a little cap, and coriacea means 'leathery', referring to the tough leaves. So the name gives a simple picture — a capped-bud, leathery-leaf Australian tree, which helps you remember this smooth, peeling-bark species.
