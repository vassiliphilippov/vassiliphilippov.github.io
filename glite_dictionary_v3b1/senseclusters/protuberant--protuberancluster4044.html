Etymology

The word protuberant comes from pro- meaning 'out' and tuber meaning 'lump' or 'swelling', and a 'tuber' is the kind of lump you find in a potato. So something protuberant is like a lump pushing out from a surface, which is why it means 'sticking out' or bulging.
