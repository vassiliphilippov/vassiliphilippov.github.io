Etymology

Junto comes from the Spanish word junto, which means 'together' and is from the same idea as the English word join. Benjamin Franklin named his small discussion club the 'Junto', and thinking of people 'joined' together helps explain why junto means a small group that works together, often in secret, to gain power.
