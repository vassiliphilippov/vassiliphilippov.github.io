Etymology

Capsicum frutescens baccatum comes from Latin words that describe the plant: Capsicum means 'pepper', frutescens means 'small bush', and baccatum means 'small fruit'. This image — a small bush covered in many tiny, very hot red peppers — helps you remember that the name refers to a wild ancestor of today’s peppers.
