Etymology

The phrase healthy mind and body paints a picture of two connected parts, the mind, your thoughts, and the body, your physical self. If both parts are cared for with good food, exercise, rest and calm thinking, the person is 'healthy' in both ways, so the phrase means being well in both mental and physical health.
