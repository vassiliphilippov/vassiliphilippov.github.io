Etymology

The word lovebird is simply love ('affection') + bird ('a flying animal'), and it was given to these small parrots because they form very close pairs and often sit together and clean each other's feathers like a loving couple. That's why a lovebird today means a small, brightly colored parrot known for being very affectionate.
