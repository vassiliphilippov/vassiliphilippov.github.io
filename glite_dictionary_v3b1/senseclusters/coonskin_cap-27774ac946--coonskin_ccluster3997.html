Etymology

The noun coonskin cap comes from a simple, literal image: people made the hat from the skin of a 'raccoon', so the words coon and skin name what it is and the long tail often hung down the back. That's why coonskin cap today means 'a hat made from raccoon skin' or a playful, old-fashioned pioneer hat.
