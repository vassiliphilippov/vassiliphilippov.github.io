Etymology

The term blind staggers may come from farmers seeing animals that looked like they could not see and kept swaying or falling, a literal mix of blind and staggers, where staggers means to walk unsteadily. So it became the name for a 'disease' that makes horses and cattle have an 'unsteady walk'.
