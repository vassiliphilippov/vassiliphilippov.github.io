Etymology

bottle bill comes from a simple idea: when you buy a drink you pay a small 'deposit' on the bottle, and you get the money back when you return it. This made people bring bottles back so they could be reused or recycled. That's why today a bottle bill means 'a law' that makes people pay a deposit and return drink containers.
