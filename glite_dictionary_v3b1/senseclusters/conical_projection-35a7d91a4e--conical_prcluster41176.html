Etymology

conical projection comes from a simple picture: a cone is placed over the globe, so conical means cone-shaped and projection is the globe being 'projected' onto that cone. When the cone is opened into a 'flat' map, places near the cone's band keep their shapes better, so mapmakers use this projection for regions that stretch east to west.
