Etymology

Wonderfulness comes from the everyday word wonder, meaning 'a feeling of surprise and admiration', plus the parts -ful ('full of') and -ness ('the state or quality of'). So, wonderfulness is literally 'the state of being full of wonder', which is why it describes something extremely good, delightful, or admirable.
