Etymology

Pope Urban II was the pope who, at a big meeting in Clermont, France in 1095, called on Christians to go to the Holy Land and fight, which started the 'First Crusade'. That's why his name is remembered for starting the 'First Crusade' and for bringing many European Christians together.
