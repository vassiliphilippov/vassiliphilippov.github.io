Etymology

Strine culture comes from a playful way of writing how people say 'Australian' in a very broad accent. One popular story says the word Strine was made by writers who wrote speech as it sounds, turning 'Australian' into 'Strine', so the phrase now names the sound, words and culture of 'Australian English'.
