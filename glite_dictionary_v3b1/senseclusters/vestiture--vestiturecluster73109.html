Etymology

Vestiture comes from the Latin vestire meaning 'to clothe' and is related to the simple English word vest, which is a piece of clothing. So vestiture, now old-fashioned, originally meant 'clothing' or the set of clothes someone wore, and that is why it refers to garments.
