Etymology

tortoise plant: The noun tortoise plant comes from the plant's big, hard root that looks like a tortoise's 'shell' and from its slow growth, so people compared the plant to a tortoise. That's why the name now refers to a vine with a large, sculpted base that helps it survive dry weather.
