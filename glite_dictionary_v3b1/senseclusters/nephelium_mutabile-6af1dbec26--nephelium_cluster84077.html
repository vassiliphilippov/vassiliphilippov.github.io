Etymology

Nephelium mutabile may come from a word meaning 'cloud' because the tree's round fruits often grow in soft clusters that look like little clouds. The part mutabile suggests 'changeable', so the name helps you remember a sweet fruit tree related to the rambutan whose fruits can vary in size or taste.
