Etymology

conversionism comes from the word convert, which comes from an old word meaning 'to turn', plus the ending -ism, meaning a 'practice' or 'belief'. So conversionism points to the practice of trying to 'turn' others to a particular religion or belief, which is why it often means active outreach or trying to convert people.
