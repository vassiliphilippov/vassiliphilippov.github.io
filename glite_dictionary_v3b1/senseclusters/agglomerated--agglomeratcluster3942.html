Etymology

agglomerated comes from Latin parts ad- meaning 'to' and glomus meaning 'ball' or 'lump'. So something agglomerated is 'formed into a lump' or gathered into a cluster or mass, which is why we use it for dust, seeds, or cells stuck together.
