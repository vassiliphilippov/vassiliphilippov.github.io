Etymology

spiegeleisen comes from the German words Spiegel ('mirror') and Eisen ('iron'), because the metal often makes shiny, mirror-like surfaces. Today spiegeleisen means a type of iron with a lot of manganese that steelmakers add to make steel stronger and to help control its mix.
