Etymology

poinciana comes from a person's name plus the Latin ending -ana, which means 'belonging to' or 'connected to'. Like other plant names such as gardenia or begonia, it remembers the person who first described the tree, and today poinciana simply names the bright tropical tree with orange or red flowers.
