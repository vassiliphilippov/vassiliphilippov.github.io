Etymology

The word Euronithopoda combines Euro- meaning 'Europe' and ornithopoda meaning 'bird-foot', with ornitho- meaning 'bird' and -poda meaning 'foot'. It names a group of plant-eating dinosaurs, like duck-billed hadrosaurs, so thinking 'European bird-footed dinosaurs' helps you remember what the word means.
