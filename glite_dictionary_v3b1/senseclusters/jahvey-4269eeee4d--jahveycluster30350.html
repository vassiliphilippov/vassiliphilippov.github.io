Etymology

The word Jahvey comes from the old Hebrew name for God written YHVH, a four-letter form that points to the idea 'to be', for example 'I am'. It is like the more familiar English forms Yahweh and Jehovah, so Jahvey is a way to write and say that ancient 'name of God'.
