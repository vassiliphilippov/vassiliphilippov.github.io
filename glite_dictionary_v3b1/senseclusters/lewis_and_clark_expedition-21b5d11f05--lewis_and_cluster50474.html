Etymology

Lewis and Clark Expedition is named for the two leaders, Lewis and Clark, whom President Jefferson sent to 'explore' the new western lands. They traveled from St. Louis to the Pacific, making maps and studying plants and people, so the name now refers to that famous early American journey.
