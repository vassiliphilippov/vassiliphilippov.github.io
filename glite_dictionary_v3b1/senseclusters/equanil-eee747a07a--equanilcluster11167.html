Etymology

Equanil is a brand name for a calming drug called meprobamate that was used for anxiety and muscle tension. A helpful way to remember the name is to see equa like 'equal' or 'even' and nil like 'nothing'. That's why the name suggests making feelings 'even' or quiet, which fits its calming effect.
