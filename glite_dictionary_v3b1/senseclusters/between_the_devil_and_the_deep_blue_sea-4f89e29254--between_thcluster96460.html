Etymology

between the devil and the deep blue sea may come from old sailors. One popular story says sailors had to work on a narrow board with the dangerous devil, a name for a hard part of the ship, on one side and the deep blue sea on the other, so they were stuck between two dangers, which is why the phrase now means 'having to choose between two bad options'.
