Etymology

Sciaena aquila may come from two old words: Sciaena, a name for fish called 'drums' because they can make a low sound, and aquila, the Latin word for 'eagle', maybe because the fish's head or a dark mark looked like an eagle. So the name gives a clear picture of a large sea 'drum' with an 'eagle' look, helping you remember it is a big marine fish.
