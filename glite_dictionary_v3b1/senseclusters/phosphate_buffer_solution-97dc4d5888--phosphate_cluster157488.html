Etymology

phosphate buffer solution gets its name from the parts phosphate (the chemical used) and buffer (something that cushions change). Think of it like a sponge: the phosphate soaks up extra acid or base so the liquid keeps a 'stable pH', which is why scientists use it to keep conditions steady.
