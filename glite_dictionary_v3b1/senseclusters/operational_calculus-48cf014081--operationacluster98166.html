Etymology

operational calculus comes from a simple trick: mathematicians treated actions like 'take a derivative' as ordinary algebra tools called operations that could be moved and combined like numbers. Imagine turning a steep hill into a flat road with one tool, so the phrase means a way to change hard differential equations into easier algebra problems.
