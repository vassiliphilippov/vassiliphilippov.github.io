Etymology

Anna Mary Robertson Moses started painting in her seventies on her farm, making bright, simple pictures of country life that people loved so much she became known as 'Grandma Moses'. That warm, late-blooming story is why her name now stands for the beloved American folk painter of charming rural scenes.
