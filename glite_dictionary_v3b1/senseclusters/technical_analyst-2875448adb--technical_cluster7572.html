Etymology

technical analyst paints a simple picture: technical points to the use of 'charts' and tools, and analyst is a person who studies the repeating 'patterns' in those charts to guess where prices will go. So a technical analyst is someone who uses past price 'charts' rather than company facts to predict market moves.
