Etymology

Reverse split is like a normal split done the other way: imagine putting ten small pieces together to make one larger piece, so there are fewer pieces but the total size stays the same. That's why a reverse split means a company combines many shares into fewer shares so each share is worth more while the 'total value' for owners stays the same.
