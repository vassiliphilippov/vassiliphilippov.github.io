Etymology

The word snazzy probably comes from mixing the English words snappy ('lively' or 'smart') and jazzy ('bright' or 'flashy'). A helpful way to remember it is to think of something that is both 'smart' and 'flashy', which is why snazzy means stylish and noticeable.
