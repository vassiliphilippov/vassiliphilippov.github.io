Etymology

The term sabbatical year comes from the word sabbatical, which goes back to the old idea of the 'sabbath', a time of rest, and from the practice of taking a whole year of rest every seven years. So today a sabbatical year means a year away from work for 'rest', 'research', or 'travel'.
