Etymology

rankly comes from rank, which once meant 'rotten' or 'having a strong, bad smell', the same idea you see in rancid. So, to say something is rankly done is to say it is done in a strong, obvious, often unpleasant way, and the word can also suggest too much or wild growth, as when plants grow rankly.
