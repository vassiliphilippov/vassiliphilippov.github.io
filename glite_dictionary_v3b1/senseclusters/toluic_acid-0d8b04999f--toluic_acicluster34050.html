Etymology

toluic acid gets its name from 'toluene', a liquid first made from a sticky plant resin brought from the town of 'Tolu' in Colombia. The part toluic means 'from toluene' and acid shows it is an acid, so toluic acid means an acid related to toluene and used in dyes and medicines.
