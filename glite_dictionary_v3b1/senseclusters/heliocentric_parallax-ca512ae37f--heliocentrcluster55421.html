Etymology

heliocentric parallax: The noun heliocentric parallax names a simple sky trick: heliocentric means 'sun-centred' and parallax is when something seems to 'shift' if you look at it from two different places. Astronomers watch a nearby star from opposite points as Earth moves around the sun, see that small 'shift', and use the measured parallax to find the star's 'distance'.
