Etymology

lesser bullrush comes from a simple picture: marsh plants called rushes had very strong stems, so people compared them to a bull and called them 'bull rushes'. The word lesser means the 'smaller' kind, so the name helps you picture a smaller, tough reed that grows in wetlands with long leaves and brown flower spikes.
