Etymology

stockinette stitch may come from its old use for stockings, because knitters used this smooth, even fabric to make close-fitting socks. One side of the stitch looks smooth while the other is bumpy, so that smooth look became the main idea and today stockinette stitch means this smooth knit used in many garments.
