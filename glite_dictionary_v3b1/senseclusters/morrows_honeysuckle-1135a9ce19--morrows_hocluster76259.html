Etymology

Morrow's honeysuckle is the name of a shrub that is simply a honeysuckle linked to a person called Morrow who first introduced it to gardeners. A honeysuckle has paired white flowers with sweet nectar that people and insects could 'suck' and the blooms turn yellow as they age, so the name points to both the flower type and the person behind it.
