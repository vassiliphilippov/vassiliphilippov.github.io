Etymology

tuba comes from the old Latin word tuba, which meant a 'trumpet' or 'horn' often used in battle, and when instrument makers in the 1800s created a much larger brass instrument they kept that name. So the modern tuba is like a very big, deep trumpet that gives the low bass sound.
