Etymology

snake's head fritillary gets its name from the flower's nodding, bell-shaped head and its checkered petals that look a little like a snake's head or skin. The word fritillary comes from an old word for a 'dice box' because of the checkered pattern, so the whole name points to this patterned spring flower.
