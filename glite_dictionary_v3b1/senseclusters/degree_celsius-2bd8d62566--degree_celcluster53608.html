Etymology

degree Celsius is named after the Swedish scientist Anders Celsius, who made a temperature scale using water's freezing and boiling points. He divided the gap between these two points into 100 equal steps, so a degree is one of those steps. That's why degree Celsius means a unit of 'temperature' tied to water's freezing and boiling points.
