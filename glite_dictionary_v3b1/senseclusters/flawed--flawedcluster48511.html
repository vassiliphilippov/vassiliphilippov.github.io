Etymology

flawed comes from the older noun flaw, from an old Norse word flaga meaning 'a slice' or 'a flake', and it is the same root as the English word flake. So something flawed has a small 'break' or bit missing, a defect, which is why we call things that are not perfect 'flawed'.
