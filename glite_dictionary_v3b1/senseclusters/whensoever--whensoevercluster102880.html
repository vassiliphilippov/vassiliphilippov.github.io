Synonyms

  • anytime
    30/m1.0

    vswhensoever: is unconditional and does not limit use to convenience
  • anywhen
    0.02/m9.7

    vswhensoever: is the same meaning but uses a different written form
  • when
    0.01/m1.0
    Refers to the specific time or occasion in which something happens or is planned
    vswhensoever: is general, meaning any time rather than one specific occasion
  • whenever
    0.00/m1.0

Etymology

whensoever is formed from when + ever, so it means 'at any time that' and is used like 'whenever' to join two parts of a sentence.
