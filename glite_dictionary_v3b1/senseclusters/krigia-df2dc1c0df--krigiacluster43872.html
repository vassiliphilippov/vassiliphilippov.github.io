Etymology

Krigia is the scientific name for a group of small, yellow, dandelion-like flowers and was given to honor a person, which is a common way to name plants. So to remember Krigia, think of a 'dwarf dandelion' and that will help you recall it is a little yellow flower carrying someone's name.
