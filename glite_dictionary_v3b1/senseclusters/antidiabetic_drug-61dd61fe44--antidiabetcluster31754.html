Etymology

The name antidiabetic drug comes from anti meaning 'against' and diabetic meaning 'diabetes'. Imagine the medicine as tiny soldiers fighting extra sugar in the blood, so that's why an antidiabetic drug helps 'prevent or manage diabetes'.
