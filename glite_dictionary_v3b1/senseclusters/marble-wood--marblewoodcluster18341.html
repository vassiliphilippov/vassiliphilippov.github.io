Etymology

The word marble-wood comes from the two parts marble ('a stone with streaks or patterns') and wood, because the tree's timber shows strong, marble-like stripes in its grain. The tree grows in Asia and its species name malabarica refers to the Malabar area of India, so the name helps you remember a wood that looks like marble and is used for fine furniture.
