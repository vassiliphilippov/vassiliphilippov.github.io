Etymology

The word tetchiness comes from the word tetchy, meaning someone who gets annoyed easily, and the ending -ness turns that quality into a noun. A helpful way to remember it is to think of tetchy like the common word touchy — someone whose feelings are easily 'touched' or 'upset', so tetchiness is the state of being easily irritated.
