Etymology

mesquite gum comes from the sticky sap inside the pods of the mesquite tree, and the word gum shows it is a sticky material. People collected this natural gum to use as a 'sweetener' or to make 'glue' and 'ink', so today the term means the sticky substance from mesquite pods used in food and everyday items.
