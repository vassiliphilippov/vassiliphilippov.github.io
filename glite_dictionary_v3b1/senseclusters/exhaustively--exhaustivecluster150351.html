Etymology

exhaustively comes from the parts ex- meaning 'out' and -haust meaning 'to draw out', the same root as exhaust and exhaustion, which once meant 'to empty' or 'use up'. So, to do something exhaustively is like emptying a box to check every single thing—you look at everything and leave nothing out.
