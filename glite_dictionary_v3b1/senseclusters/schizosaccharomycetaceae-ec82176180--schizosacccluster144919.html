Etymology

Schizosaccharomycetaceae breaks into parts you can read: schizo- 'split', saccharo- 'sugar', mycet- 'fungus', and -aceae meaning a 'family' in biology. So the name points to a 'family of split fungi' — yeast-like organisms that divide by splitting and often live on sugars, which explains the modern scientific meaning.
