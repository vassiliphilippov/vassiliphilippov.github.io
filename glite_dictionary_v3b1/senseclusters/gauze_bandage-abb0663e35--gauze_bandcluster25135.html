Etymology

gauze bandage may come from a thin cloth once made near the city of Gaza, so gauze came to mean a light cloth with a loose weave. Because bandage is a cloth for a 'wound', people used this light cloth to cover injuries, and that is why gauze bandage means a thin cloth used to dress wounds.
