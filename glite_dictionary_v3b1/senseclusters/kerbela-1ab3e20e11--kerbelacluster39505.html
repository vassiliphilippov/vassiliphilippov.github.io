Etymology

Kerbela comes from the Arabic name Karbala, the place of a famous battle in 680 where Husayn ibn Ali was killed. Because of that event, Kerbela is remembered as a place of 'mourning' and 'pilgrimage', which is why it is a holy city for Shiite Muslims.
