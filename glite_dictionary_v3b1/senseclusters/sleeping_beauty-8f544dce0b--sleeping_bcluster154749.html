Etymology

Sleeping Beauty comes from the fairy tale image of a sleeping princess who stays a beauty while she waits to be woken by a prince. Because of that picture, people call someone who sleeps very deeply or a quiet place waiting to be fixed a 'Sleeping Beauty'.
