Etymology

Lebanese Hizballah comes from Arabic: Hizballah literally means 'Party of God' and Lebanese shows the group is based in Lebanon. It began in the 1980s with support from Iran and grew into both a political party and an armed group, so the name helps you remember it is a Lebanese movement that mixes religion, politics and fighting.
