Etymology

vena musculophrenica comes from Latin parts vena, meaning 'vein', plus musculo for 'muscle' and phrenica for 'diaphragm'. Picture a thin vein running along the diaphragm muscle under the lower ribs and carrying blood from the lower ribs and upper belly, so the name tells you it is the vein that drains that area.
