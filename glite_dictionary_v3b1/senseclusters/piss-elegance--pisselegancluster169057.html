Etymology

The word piss-elegance is a modern slang mix of piss, a crude word meaning 'rude' or 'cheap', and elegance, meaning 'refined style'. Think of someone in a fancy suit who wears dirty sneakers, where the rude detail clashes with the high style. So piss-elegance means a showy, affected look that mixes elegance with obvious casualness or rudeness.
