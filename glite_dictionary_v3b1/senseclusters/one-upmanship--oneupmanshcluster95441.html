Etymology

one-upmanship comes from the phrase one up, which means 'to get the advantage', plus the ending -manship, used in words like sportsmanship and showmanship to mean a kind of skill or habit. So one-upmanship is the habit or 'skill' of always trying to 'be one better' than someone else.
