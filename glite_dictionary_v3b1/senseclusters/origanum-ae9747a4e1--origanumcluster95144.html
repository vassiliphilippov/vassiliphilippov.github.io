Etymology

Origanum comes from the Greek word origanon, made from oros 'mountain' and a part meaning 'joy', so it originally meant 'mountain joy' for a sweet-smelling wild herb. The common herb oregano is in this group, and these fragrant plants often grow on hills, which helps explain the name.
