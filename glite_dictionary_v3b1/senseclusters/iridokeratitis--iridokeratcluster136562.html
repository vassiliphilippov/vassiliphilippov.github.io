Etymology

iridokeratitis comes from three parts: irido- meaning 'iris', kerat- meaning 'horn' which here refers to the cornea, and -itis meaning 'inflammation'. So iridokeratitis means inflammation of both the iris and the cornea, which is why it causes eye pain and blurred vision.
