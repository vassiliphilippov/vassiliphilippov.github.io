Etymology

The word ferricyanide comes from two parts: ferri- from Latin ferrum meaning 'iron' and cyanide from Greek kyanos meaning 'dark blue', like the color 'cyan'. So ferricyanide names an 'iron' chemical joined to a cyanide part, which helps you remember it is an iron-containing compound often used in lab tests that show blue color changes.
