Etymology

Rhapis excelsa may come from old Greek and Latin words: Rhapis possibly from a Greek word meaning 'needle' or 'rod' for its thin, reedlike stems, and excelsa from Latin meaning 'tall'. So the name paints a simple picture — a 'tall' thin palm — which fits the plant's slim stems and upright growth.
