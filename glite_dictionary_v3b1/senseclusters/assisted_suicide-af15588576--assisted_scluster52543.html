Etymology

assisted suicide comes from the words assisted, meaning 'help', and suicide, meaning 'to kill oneself'. Imagine someone too weak or in great pain and another person giving help to end their life, and that image is why the phrase means 'ending a person's life with help', often to stop suffering.
