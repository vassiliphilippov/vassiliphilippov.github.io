Etymology

The word breakstone comes from the simple parts break + stone, meaning 'stone-breaker', and is like the Latin name saxifraga which also means 'stone-breaker'. These small plants grow in cracks and between rocks and seem to push the stone apart, so the name explains why we call them breakstone.
