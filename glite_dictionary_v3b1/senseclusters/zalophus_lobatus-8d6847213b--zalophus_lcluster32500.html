Etymology

Zalophus lobatus is the scientific name for a sea lion found in Australia. The name has two parts: Zalophus (the group name) and lobatus, which may come from a Latin word meaning 'lobed' and probably points to a rounded or split shape on the animal, so the name helps people picture and remember this particular sea lion.
