Etymology

saturation bombing uses saturation, the idea of soaking something completely, and bombing, attacking with bombs. Imagine pouring so much water on a cloth that every part is wet; in the same way, many bombs are dropped until the whole area is hit. So the phrase means 'to destroy an entire area' by bombing.
