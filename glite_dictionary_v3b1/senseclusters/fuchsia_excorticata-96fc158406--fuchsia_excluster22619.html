Etymology

Fuchsia excorticata gets its name from what the plant looks like: the first part, Fuchsia, is the group with dark red, bell-like flowers, and the second part, excorticata, points to the tree's rough, peeling bark. So the name helps you picture a small tree with maroon flowers and peeling bark, the 'tree fuchsia'.
