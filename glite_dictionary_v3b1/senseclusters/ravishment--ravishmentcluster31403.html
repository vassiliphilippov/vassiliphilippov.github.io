Etymology

ravishment also means a state of great joy because it comes from the same root as Latin rapere 'to seize', and the idea shifted to being 'carried away' by strong feeling, just like the word rapture. So to be in ravishment is to be so delighted that you are almost 'carried away' by the feeling.
