Etymology

piping crow-shrike is named for its sound and its look. People called it piping because its clear, high song sounds like a small 'pipe', and crow-shrike because its black-and-white colors and shape remind people of a little 'crow' and a 'shrike', so the name points to a bird known for its pleasant song.
