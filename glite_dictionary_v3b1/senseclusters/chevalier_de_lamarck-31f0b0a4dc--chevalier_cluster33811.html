Etymology

The name Chevalier de Lamarck belonged to a French naturalist whose title 'chevalier' means 'knight'. He suggested that animals could 'pass on traits' they gained in life; for example, he pictured giraffes stretching their necks for food and their young being born with longer necks. So his name now reminds us of that early idea about how species can change.
