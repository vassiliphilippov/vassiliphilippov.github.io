Etymology

bull dyke probably comes from putting together bull and dyke. bull means a strong male animal and dyke is an offensive word for 'a lesbian', so the image of a tough, bull-like woman explains why it came to mean 'a very masculine, assertive lesbian' and why the whole term is seen as 'offensive'.
