Etymology

phosphoresce comes from old Greek parts phos meaning 'light' and phoros meaning 'to carry'. A helpful way to remember it is to think 'light-carrier', like the photo in photograph, which also means 'light'. So, to phosphoresce is to give off a soft light without heat, often after the object has been in light.
