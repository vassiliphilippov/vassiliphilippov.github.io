Etymology

tailwort breaks into tail and wort; wort is an old word for 'plant', seen in names like St. John's wort, and tail points to a tail-like stem or root on the flower. So tailwort means 'a plant with a tail', a simple image that helps remember this small herb used in salads and medicine.
