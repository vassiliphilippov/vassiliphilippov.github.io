Etymology

Jacquemier's sign is named after a doctor who first noticed a purplish patch on the vaginal lining in early pregnancy. That purple patch was an easy 'sign' that pregnancy had begun, so today Jacquemier's sign refers to 'an early sign of pregnancy'.
