Etymology

barbarize comes from the ancient Greek word barbaros, which meant 'foreign' and even copies the sound 'bar-bar' people thought strange speech made. You can see the same idea in barbarian and barbaric, so to barbarize is to make language or behavior rough, crude, or uncivilized.
