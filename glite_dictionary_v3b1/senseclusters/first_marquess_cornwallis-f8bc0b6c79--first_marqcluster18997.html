Etymology

First Marquess Cornwallis is the title of Charles Cornwallis, a British general who is best known for his 'surrender' at Yorktown in the American War of Independence and for later service in India. So, when you see First Marquess Cornwallis, think of the British commander whose surrender helped end the main fighting in the American Revolution.
