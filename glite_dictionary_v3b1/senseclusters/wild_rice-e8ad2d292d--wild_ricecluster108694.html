Etymology

The noun wild rice comes from a clear picture: long, dark seeds that grow in lakes and rivers instead of on farms. People in canoes would knock the seeds into the boat when they harvested them, so it was called wild, because it grew in the wild, and rice, because the seeds look like rice, and today wild rice means that water-grown grain used as 'food'.
