Etymology

Dalbergia retusa is the name of a tropical tree whose dark, richly patterned wood shines when polished and is very hard. Because this wood is both beautiful and strong, people have long used it for fine furniture, pens and musical instruments, so the name is now linked to 'hard, dense wood' prized in luxury work.
