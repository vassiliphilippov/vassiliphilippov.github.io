Etymology

shock-headed comes from the old use of shock, which meant a big 'bundle' or 'tuft' (as in a 'shock of wheat'), and -headed meaning 'having on the head'. So shock-headed literally describes someone with a thick, rough bunch of hair, which is why we call a person with untidy, bushy hair 'shock-headed'.
