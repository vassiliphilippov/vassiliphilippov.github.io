Etymology

The plant name painted leaf comes from its bright, colorful leaves that look as if someone had painted them, so the words painted and leaf create a clear picture. So, painted leaf means the tropical 'poinsettia' plant known for its bright leaves and a milky, 'poisonous' sap.
