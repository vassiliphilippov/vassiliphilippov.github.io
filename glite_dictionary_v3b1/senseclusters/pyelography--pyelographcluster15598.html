Etymology

pyelography comes from pyelo-, meaning the 'pelvis' or basin-like part of the kidney, and -graphy, meaning 'recording', as in photography. So pyelography is a way to 'record' images of the kidney pelvis, which is why it means an X-ray exam of the kidneys using dye.
