Etymology

Lecythidaceae comes from the plant name Lecythis plus the family ending -aceae, and Lecythis comes from the Greek lekythos meaning 'oil jar'. Think of the trees' hard, jar-like fruits that protect the seeds — that image helps explain why the Brazil nut and similar trees belong to the Lecythidaceae family.
