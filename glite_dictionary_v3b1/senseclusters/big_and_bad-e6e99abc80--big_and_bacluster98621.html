Etymology

The phrase big and bad brings to mind the old children's story about the 'Big Bad Wolf', a large, scary creature that blew down houses. The words big and bad give a clear picture of something powerful and dangerous, so calling something big and bad means it is 'extremely powerful or intimidating'.
