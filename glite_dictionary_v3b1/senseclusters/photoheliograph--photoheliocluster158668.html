Etymology

photoheliograph comes from three parts: photo- meaning 'light', helio- meaning 'sun', and -graph meaning 'to write' or 'record', the same photo- you see in photograph. So a photoheliograph literally means a machine that 'records the sun with light', in other words a camera for taking pictures of the Sun.
