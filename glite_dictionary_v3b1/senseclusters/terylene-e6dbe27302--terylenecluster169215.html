Etymology

Terylene is a brand name made from parts of the chemical words terephthalate and ethylene, so it sounds like a chemistry name. A helpful way to remember it is to think of Terylene as a 'man-made' polyester fabric used for clothes and textiles because it comes from those chemicals.
