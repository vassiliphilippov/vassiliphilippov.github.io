Etymology

Hypericum pyramidatum may come from an old name for 'St. John's wort' and a word meaning 'pyramid-shaped'. The part Hypericum links the plant to 'St. John's wort' and pyramidatum describes how its many yellow, star-shaped flowers grow in a narrow, pyramid-like group, so the name helps you imagine the shrub and its blooms.
