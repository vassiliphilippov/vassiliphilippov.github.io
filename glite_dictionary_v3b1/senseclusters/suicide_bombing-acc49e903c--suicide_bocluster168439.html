Etymology

The noun suicide bombing names a violent act that comes from a plain image: the parts suicide ('to kill yourself') and bombing ('using a bomb') show a person who carries or wears a bomb and sets it off, causing their own death. So, the phrase means an attack where the attacker deliberately explodes a bomb and dies.
