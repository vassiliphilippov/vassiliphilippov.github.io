Etymology

Heuchera americana was named to honor a doctor who studied plants: Heuchera is his name and americana shows the plant comes from America. The name points to the American 'alumroot', a small long-lived plant with patterned leaves and loose clusters of flowers that Native Americans once used as medicine.
