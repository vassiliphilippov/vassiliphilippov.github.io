Etymology

ectomorphy comes from Greek parts ecto- meaning 'outside' and -morph meaning 'shape', like the common word 'form'. So ectomorphy points to the body's outer shape — a slim, lightly muscled outline, which is why it means a slender, lightly muscled body type.
