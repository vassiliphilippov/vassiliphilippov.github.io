Etymology

push through adversity comes from the image of forcing something heavy through a narrow gap or a storm: to push is to move forward and adversity means hard problems. So, it shows keeping effort and moving on despite trouble, which is why it means 'to continue making effort and succeed despite facing difficulties'.
