Etymology

The word superfan joins super and fan. Super is the same 'super' you see in superhero or supermarket, meaning 'very', and fan is short for fanatic, which means 'an enthusiastic person'. So a superfan is someone who is very enthusiastic and loyal to a person, team, or hobby.
