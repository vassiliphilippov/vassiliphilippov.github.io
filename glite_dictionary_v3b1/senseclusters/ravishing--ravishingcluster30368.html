Etymology

ravishing comes from the old verb ravish, which goes back to Latin rapere meaning 'to seize' or 'carry away'. Think of something so beautiful it 'carries you away' with pleasure—that's why ravishing means extremely attractive or stunning, a bit like being in rapture.
