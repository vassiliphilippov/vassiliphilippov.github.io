Etymology

rubbernecker comes from rubber ('flexible') and neck ('the part of the body you turn to look'). It gives the image of someone who can 'turn their neck' like it is made of rubber to stare at accidents or unusual events, so a rubbernecker is a person who looks with too much curiosity.
