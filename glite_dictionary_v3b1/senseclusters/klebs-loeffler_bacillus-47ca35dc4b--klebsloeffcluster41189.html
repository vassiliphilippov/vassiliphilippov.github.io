Etymology

Klebs-Loeffler bacillus The name Klebs-Loeffler bacillus comes from two doctors, Klebs and Loeffler, who discovered the tiny germ in the throats of sick people long ago. That's why the name now means the bacillus that causes 'diphtheria'.
