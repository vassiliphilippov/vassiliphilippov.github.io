Etymology

The noun coefficient of self induction puts together coefficient (a 'number') and self induction (when a coil makes a voltage because its own current changes). Picture a coil that 'pushes back' when the current tries to change; the coefficient is the number that tells how strong that push is, so it measures 'how much voltage the coil makes for a given change of current'.
