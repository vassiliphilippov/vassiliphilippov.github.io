Etymology

common facial vein is a name that tells you what it is: facial means from the face, vein is a blood vessel, and common shows where two veins meet. Picture two small rivers from the face joining into one stream that 'drains' into the main neck vein, so the name means the vein formed when the facial and retromandibular veins join.
