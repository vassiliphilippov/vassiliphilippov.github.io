Etymology

drip mold is a small ledge on a building that sticks out above a window or door. The name shows the picture of drip, water falling off an edge, and mold, a shaped strip of stone or wood. So rain drops off the ledge and away from the wall, which is why it means 'to keep rainwater away from the area below'.
