Etymology

apocryphalness comes from the Greek apokryphos, meaning 'hidden', and was first used for books kept away from the Bible. Because those hidden books were seen as doubtful, apocryphalness now means the quality of being not genuine or probably not true. A helpful memory hook is the idea of 'hidden', and you can also see this in the word cryptic, which comes from Greek for 'hidden'.
