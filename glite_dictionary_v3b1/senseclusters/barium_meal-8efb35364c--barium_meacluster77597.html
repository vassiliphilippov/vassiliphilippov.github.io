Etymology

barium meal comes from the simple image of drinking a special white liquid: barium is a chalky substance that shows up clearly on an X-ray and meal is what the patient swallows. The barium coats the inside of the stomach like white paint so the X-ray can 'see' the shape and find problems, so barium meal means 'a medical test where you swallow barium so doctors can see your stomach on an X-ray'.
