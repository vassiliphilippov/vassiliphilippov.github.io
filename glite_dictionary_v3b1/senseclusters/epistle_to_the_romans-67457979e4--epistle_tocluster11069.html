Etymology

Epistle to the Romans comes from the word epistle, an old word for 'letter', and Romans, meaning 'Christians in Rome'. It is the long letter Saint Paul wrote to that church to explain Christian beliefs, so today it is the New Testament book where Paul explains his ideas about faith and grace.
