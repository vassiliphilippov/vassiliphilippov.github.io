Etymology

Tricholoma pardinum may come from old Greek and Latin words: the first part, Tricholoma, points to 'hair' or a small 'fringe' because the cap has fine grey hairs, and pardinum comes from a word for 'leopard' because the pale cap shows dark, spot-like marks. So the name gives a picture of this 'poisonous' species — a pale, hairy, spotted mushroom you should avoid.
