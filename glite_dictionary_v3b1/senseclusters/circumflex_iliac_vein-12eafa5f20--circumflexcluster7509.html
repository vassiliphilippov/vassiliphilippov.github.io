Etymology

The term circumflex iliac vein comes from old Latin words: circumflex means 'around' or 'bending', iliac means 'hip', and vein is a blood vessel. So the name gives the picture of a vein that bends around the hip and runs beside the circumflex iliac artery, which is why it refers to that specific vein.
