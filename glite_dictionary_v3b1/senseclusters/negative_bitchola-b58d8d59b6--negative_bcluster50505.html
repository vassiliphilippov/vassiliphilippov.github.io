Etymology

negative bitchola mixes negative (always saying no), bitch (a rude word for someone who complains), and the playful ending -ola, so imagine a noisy nickname for a person who always sees the bad side. That's why it is a rude name for someone who is always 'negative' or 'pessimistic'.
