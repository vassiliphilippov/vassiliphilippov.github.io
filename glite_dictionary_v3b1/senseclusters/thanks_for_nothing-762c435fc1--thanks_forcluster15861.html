Etymology

The phrase thanks for nothing comes from the simple image of someone handing you an empty box or promising to help and then doing nothing, so you say thanks for nothing when you really receive nothing useful. That's why it is said 'sarcastically' to show 'disappointment' or 'annoyance'.
