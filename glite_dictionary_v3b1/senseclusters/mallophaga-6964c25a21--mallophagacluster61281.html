Etymology

Mallophaga comes from the Greek parts mallos 'wool' (by extension 'hair' or 'feather') and phagein 'to eat'. So Mallophaga literally means 'wool-eaters', a fitting name for the biting lice that feed on birds' feathers or mammals' hair.
