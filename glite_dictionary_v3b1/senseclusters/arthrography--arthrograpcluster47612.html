Etymology

arthrography comes from Greek parts arthro- meaning 'joint' and -graphy meaning 'writing' or 'drawing', the same root you see in photography. So an arthrography is like 'drawing' a picture of a joint and means a medical test that makes a clear image of the joint's inside.
