Vangueria madagascariensis is a scientific name that tells a simple story: the first word, Vangueria, shows the plant group and the second word, madagascariensis, is a Latin way to say 'from Madagascar'. So, the name points to a shrubby tree native to Madagascar that produces apple-shaped, edible fruit.
