Etymology

Pisonia aculeata comes from how the tree looks: aculeata means 'spiny', and the plant has small sharp points and very sticky fruits. Imagine seeds that 'cling' to a bird's feathers like glue, and you see why the name fits a spiny tree whose sticky fruit travels on animals.
