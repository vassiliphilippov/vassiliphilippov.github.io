Etymology

propellant explosive combines propellant, meaning something that 'pushes' forward, and explosive, a material that burns or explodes very quickly. Imagine a small blast behind a bullet or rocket that gives it a strong push; that picture explains why the term means an explosive used to 'launch' or push projectiles and rockets.
