Etymology

sal soda may come from old words sal and soda, meaning 'salt' and 'ashes', because people long ago made a white, salty powder by burning plants and using the ashes. That's why the name came to mean the substance we now call 'sodium carbonate', a white powder used to clean clothes and to make glass and paper.
