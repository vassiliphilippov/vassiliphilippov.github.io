Etymology

universal veil comes from the idea of a thin skin that covers the whole young mushroom. The thin skin, called a veil, wraps the mushroom and then breaks, leaving bits at the base or on the cap, so the name links universal meaning 'whole' and veil meaning 'covers' to the mushroom bits we see today.
