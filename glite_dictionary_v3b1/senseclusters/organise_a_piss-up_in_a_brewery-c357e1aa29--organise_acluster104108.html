Etymology

organise a piss-up in a brewery gives a simple picture: trying to plan a drinking party, a piss-up, inside a place already full of beer, a brewery. The joke is that even this very easy job would fail, so saying someone 'couldn't organise a piss-up in a brewery' means they are 'extremely incompetent'.
