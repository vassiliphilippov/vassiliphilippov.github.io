Etymology

kerfuffle probably comes from an old Scottish or Irish word curfuffle that meant a 'confused mess' or 'disorder'. The noisy sound of ker and fuffle feels like things being mixed up or people fussing, so kerfuffle now means a small noisy argument or 'fuss'.
