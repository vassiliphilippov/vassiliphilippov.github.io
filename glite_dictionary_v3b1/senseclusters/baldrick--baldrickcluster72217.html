Etymology

Baldrick comes from an old French word baudrier, which meant a 'shoulder belt' and probably from an older Germanic word for a belt or strap. So a baldrick is simply a special belt worn over one shoulder and across the chest to 'carry' a sword or bugle, which explains its meaning today.
