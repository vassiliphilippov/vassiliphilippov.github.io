Etymology

wild potato vine uses the words wild, potato, and vine to paint a simple picture: a climbing or trailing plant with a big, starchy root that looks like a potato. People in tropical America ate or studied that potato-like root, and because the plant is an early relative of the sweet potato, the name explains why we call it a wild potato vine.
