Etymology

Bryonia dioica gets its name from old Greek and Latin words that picture the plant: Bryonia comes from a Greek word meaning 'to swell', because of its thick, fleshy root, and dioica means 'two houses' since 'male' and 'female' flowers grow on separate plants. So the name helps you remember the swollen root and that there are separate 'male' and 'female' plants.
