Etymology

lambda hyperon may come from the Greek letter lambda, which was used as its symbol, and the word hyperon, a name for particles thought to be 'above' normal ones because they contain a 'strange' quark. So the name hints that this neutral particle is a heavier 'strange' cousin of the proton and neutron.
