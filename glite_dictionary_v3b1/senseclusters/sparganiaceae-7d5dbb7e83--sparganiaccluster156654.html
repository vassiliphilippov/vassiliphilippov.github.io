Etymology

The word Sparganiaceae comes from the plant name Sparganium, with the ending -aceae that means 'family' in plant names. Sparganium comes from a Greek word for 'little band' or 'swaddling cloth' because the plant's fruit looks wrapped, so Sparganiaceae simply named the group of bur-reeds that botanists once treated as a family and that are now usually included in the Typhaceae family.
