Etymology

The word jauntiness comes from jaunt ('short trip') and the adjective jaunty ('lively' or 'cheerful'). A helpful way to remember it is to picture someone coming back from a quick, fun walk, moving with a bouncy, confident step — that bright, carefree look is jauntiness.
