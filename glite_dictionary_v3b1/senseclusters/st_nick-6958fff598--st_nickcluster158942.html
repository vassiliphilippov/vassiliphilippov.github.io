Etymology

St. Nick comes from the real man Saint Nicholas, a kind church leader who used to leave coins and small gifts in people's shoes. One popular story says this gave rise to the jolly figure called 'Santa Claus', so St. Nick now means the man who 'brings presents' at Christmas.
