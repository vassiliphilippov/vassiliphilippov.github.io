Etymology

Palestinian National Authority was the name made in 1994 for a temporary government set up after the Oslo Accords to 'run' parts of the West Bank and Gaza. The three words show its job: Palestinian means for the Palestinian people, National points to a nation's affairs, and Authority means an official body with power, so the name now means the official body that represents and manages those areas.
