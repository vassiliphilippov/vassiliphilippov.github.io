Etymology

tea tortrix comes from the tiny caterpillars that roll or twist young tea leaves into little shelters, and tortrix is from a word meaning 'to twist'. So, the name gives a picture of a small moth that lives in rolled leaves and is a 'pest' that damages tea and coffee plants.
