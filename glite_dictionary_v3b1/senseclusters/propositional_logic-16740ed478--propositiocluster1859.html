Etymology

The term propositional logic literally joins proposition and logic: a proposition is a simple sentence that is either 'true' or 'false', and logic is the rules for joining these sentences with words like 'and', 'or', and 'not'. So, propositional logic is the part of logic that looks at whole statements and how combining them with 'and', 'or', 'not' changes whether they are true.
