Etymology

shea butter comes from the nuts of the shea tree, where people crush and heat the nuts to make a soft, creamy butter. Imagine that white, oily 'fat' made from nuts used to smooth the skin or for cooking, and you can see why the name means a natural skin-care and food 'fat' today.
