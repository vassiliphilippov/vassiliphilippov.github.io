Etymology

The word biffy is a casual North American name for a toilet or small outdoor 'outhouse'. Its exact origin is uncertain, but it probably began as a playful, shortened word in everyday speech, so a helpful way to remember biffy is to think of a small, simple toilet, which is why biffy means 'toilet'.
