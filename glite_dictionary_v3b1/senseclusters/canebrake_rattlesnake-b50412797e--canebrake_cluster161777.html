Etymology

canebrake rattlesnake gets its name from where the snake lives: a canebrake is a thick patch of tall wild cane, and a rattlesnake is a snake with a noisy rattle on its tail. The picture of a rattling snake hiding in the cane explains why the name means a large venomous snake that lives in cane thickets in the southeastern United States.
