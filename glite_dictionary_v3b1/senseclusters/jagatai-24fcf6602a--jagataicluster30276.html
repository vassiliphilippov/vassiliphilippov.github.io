Etymology

The word Jagatai comes from the name of the Mongol prince Chagatai, who was a 'son of Genghis Khan'. Because writers and courts used that language in medieval Central Asia, Jagatai became the name for a Turkic literary language and explains why it helped shape several modern Turkic languages.
