Etymology

percussive maintenance comes from the simple picture of someone hitting a stuck machine, like tapping a radio or printer, to make it work. Percussive means 'to hit' and maintenance means 'care to keep something working', so the phrase means trying to 'fix' a device by hitting or tapping it, often as a joke or last attempt.
