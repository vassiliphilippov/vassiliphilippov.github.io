Etymology

reprehension comes from Latin parts re- 'back' and prehendere 'to seize', the same root you see in apprehend and comprehend. Imagine 'grabbing back' someone who did wrong — that image became 'blaming' or 'expressing strong criticism', which is why reprehension means 'the act of expressing strong criticism or disapproval'.
