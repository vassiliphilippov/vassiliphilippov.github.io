Etymology

Guinea grains gets its name from tiny, peppery seeds from West Africa called 'grains of paradise'. Europeans named them after the region called 'Guinea', so Guinea shows where they came from and grains means the small seeds, which is why Guinea grains now means that West African 'spice'.
