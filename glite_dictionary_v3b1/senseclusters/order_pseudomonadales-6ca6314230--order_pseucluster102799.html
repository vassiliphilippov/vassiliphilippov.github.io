Etymology

The name order Pseudomonadales comes from the genus Pseudomonas plus the group ending -ales. The word Pseudomonas is made of Greek parts pseudo meaning 'false' and monas meaning 'single', a name early scientists used for tiny one-celled bacteria. The ending -ales shows it is a larger group, so the whole name means a group of related bacteria.
