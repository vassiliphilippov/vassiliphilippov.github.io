Etymology

The word theurgy comes from Greek parts theos ('god') and ergon ('work'), so it originally meant 'work of the gods'. The theo- part is the same as in theology, which helps you remember the 'god' part. That's why today theurgy means rituals or practices to call on gods or their power.
