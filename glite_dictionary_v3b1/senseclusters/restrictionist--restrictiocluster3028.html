Etymology

restrictionist comes from the word restrict, which originally meant 'to draw tight', like pulling a rope, and so came to mean 'to limit'. The ending -ist, seen in words like artist, marks an approach or belief, so a restrictionist approach is one that favors limits or stricter rules.
