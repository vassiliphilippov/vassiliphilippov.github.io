Etymology

machilid comes from the scientific family name Machilidae, built from the group name Machilis that scientists gave to these small, wingless insects. A helpful way to remember machilid is to picture a tiny creature that 'leaps' in dark, damp places, because machilids are wingless bristletails known for jumping when disturbed.
