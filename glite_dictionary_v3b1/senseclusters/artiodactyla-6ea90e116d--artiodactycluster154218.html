Etymology

The word Artiodactyla comes from Greek parts artios meaning 'even' and dactylos meaning 'finger' or 'toe', the same dactyl you see in pterodactyl. So Artiodactyla means 'even-toed' and is used for hoofed animals with an even number of toes like deer, cows, and pigs.
