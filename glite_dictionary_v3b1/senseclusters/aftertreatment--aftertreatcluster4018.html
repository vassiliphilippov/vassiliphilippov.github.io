Etymology

The word aftertreatment is made of after ('after') and treatment ('care' or 'process'). Think of it as extra care or a second process that comes later—like medicine after surgery or a filter put after a car's exhaust—so aftertreatment means a later step that improves results or reduces harmful effects.
