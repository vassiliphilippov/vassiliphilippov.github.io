Etymology

Asian don't raisin is a joking, nonstandard saying that uses the picture of a raisin, a small dried grape that looks wrinkled. One popular story says people used that picture to mean someone 'stays looking young' or 'doesn't get wrinkled', so the phrase now jokingly means a person ages slowly.
