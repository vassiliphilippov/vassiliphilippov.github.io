Etymology

cellular telephone comes from the idea of radio 'cells' that cover an area. Picture a map split into many small boxes, each with a tower that connects your telephone, so as you move your signal jumps from box to box. That's why cellular telephone means a portable 'mobile phone' that works while you travel.
