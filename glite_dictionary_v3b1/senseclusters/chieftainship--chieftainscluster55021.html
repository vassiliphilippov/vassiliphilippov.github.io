Etymology

chieftainship starts with chief, which comes from an old word for 'head' and is the same idea you see in captain, so a chieftain is literally the 'head' of a group. The ending -ship means 'the state or position of', like in friendship or leadership, so chieftainship is the 'head' position or power of a tribe or clan.
