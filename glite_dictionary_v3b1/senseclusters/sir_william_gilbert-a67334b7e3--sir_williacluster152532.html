Etymology

Sir William Gilbert was an English writer who wrote the words, the 'librettos', for many funny musical plays with a composer named Arthur Sullivan. Picture him at a small desk inventing clever rhymes and silly characters, and that image is why his name now reminds people of 'witty comic operettas'.
