Etymology

orthotic insole comes from two words: orthotic (a word for special foot support) and insole (the soft pad inside a shoe). Imagine a shaped pad you slip into your shoe to hold your foot in the right place, so it means a removable insert that 'supports' and 'aligns' the foot.
