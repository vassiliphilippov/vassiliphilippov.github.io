indefinite numeral

0.00/m7.0
A word that shows an amount that is not exact, like many or few
noun US / ɪnˈdɛfɪnət ˈnuːmərəl /

Examples

An indefinite numeral word
The teacher used an indefinite numeral when she said, "Several students have completed the assignment."
Unlike exact numbers, an indefinite numeral gives a general sense of quantity without specifying the count.
Words like most and many are considered indefinite numerals because they do not state a precise amount.
I have too many tasks to complete today!
I have many things to do this weekend.
The teacher has too many assignments to grade.

Etymology

indefinite numeral comes from the words indefinite, meaning 'not exact', and numeral, a 'number word'. Imagine holding a handful of stones and saying 'several' instead of counting them, so indefinite numeral means a word that gives a general amount rather than an exact number.
