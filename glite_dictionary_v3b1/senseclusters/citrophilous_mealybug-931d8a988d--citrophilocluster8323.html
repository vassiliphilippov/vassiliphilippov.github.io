Etymology

citrophilous mealybug comes from citro- (meaning 'citrus') and -philous (meaning 'loving'), while mealybug describes a small, white, powdery insect. So it literally names a white bug that 'loves' citrus trees and feeds on their juices, which explains why it harms orange and lemon crops.
