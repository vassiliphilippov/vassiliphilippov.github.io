Etymology

Carduelinae comes from the old Latin name Carduelis, which itself comes from carduus meaning 'thistle', because these finches eat thistle and other seeds. So the name points to a group of seed-eating finches like the goldfinch, explaining why Carduelinae is the name for this small family of birds.
