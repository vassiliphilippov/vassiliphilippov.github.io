Etymology

broom snakeroot may come from the plant's many thin branches that look like a broom, and from an old belief that a snakeroot could help with 'snakebite'. So the name gives a simple picture of a low, bushy plant with thin branches and roots linked to snakes, which is why we call this yellow-flowered shrub broom snakeroot.
