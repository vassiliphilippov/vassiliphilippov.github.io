Etymology

Prunus demissa comes from Latin: Prunus means 'plum' or 'cherry' and demissa means 'low' or 'drooping'. That's why the name paints the picture of a low, drooping wild chokecherry shrub with small, tart fruit that people can use for jam and animals often eat.
