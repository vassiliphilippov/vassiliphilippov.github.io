Etymology

slushy can also mean overly sentimental; a helpful way to remember this is to imagine feelings that are like slush — soft, messy, and mushy. The word is built from slush ('soft, wet mass') and -y ('full of'), so calling something slushy says it is too soft or emotionally overdone.
