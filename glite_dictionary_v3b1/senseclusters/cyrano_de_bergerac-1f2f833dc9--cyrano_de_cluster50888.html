Etymology

Cyrano de Bergerac comes from a real French soldier and writer, but most people know him from a famous play where he is brave, clever, and has a very large nose. In the play he secretly writes love letters for another man so the woman he loves falls for the words, not his face, so today his name often means 'a romantic, selfless lover'.
