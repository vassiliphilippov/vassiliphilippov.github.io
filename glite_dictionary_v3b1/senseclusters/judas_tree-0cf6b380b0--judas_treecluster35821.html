Etymology

Judas tree comes from a popular legend: after betraying Jesus, Judas is said to have hanged himself on a tree, and people imagined the tree's flowers were stained by his blood. That's why the name links the tree to 'betrayal', even though today the Judas tree is mostly known for its bright spring flowers.
