Etymology

The name Norfolk wherry comes from the county Norfolk and the old word wherry for a flat-bottomed river boat. These boats used one tall mast and a wide hull to carry heavy goods across shallow rivers and lakes, so the name came to mean 'a large cargo sailing barge' from that area.
