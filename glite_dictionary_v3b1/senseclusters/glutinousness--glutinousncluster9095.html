Etymology

The word glutinousness comes from the Latin root gluten, which means 'glue' and is like the English word glue and the cooking word gluten that makes dough sticky. So glutinousness is the quality of being 'sticky' or clinging, like rice that holds together or a sauce that coats noodles.
