Etymology

Eumetopias jubatus comes from old Greek and Latin words that describe the animal's head and mane: Eumetopias roughly means 'good forehead' and jubatus means 'maned' or 'with a mane'. That's why the name points to the big sea lion whose males have thick fur around the neck, and it refers to the Steller sea lion today.
