Etymology

A helpful way to remember the verb loaf, meaning 'to be idle', is to imagine someone sitting with a loaf of bread doing nothing; the verb likely grew from casual speech that linked the noun to slow, lazy behaviour. So, to loaf is to spend time lazily or do nothing productive.
