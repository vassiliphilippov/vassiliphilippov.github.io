Etymology

The compound savory stew comes from two simple ideas: savory meaning 'tasty' and full of spices, and stew meaning food cooked slowly in a pot. The picture of meat and vegetables simmering together until the smells are rich explains why it means 'a hot, flavorful dish made by slow cooking'.
