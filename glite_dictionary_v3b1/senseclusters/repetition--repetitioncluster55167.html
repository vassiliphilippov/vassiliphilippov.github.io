Etymology

The word repetition comes from Latin parts re- 'again' and petere 'to seek', and it has the same root as the familiar verb repeat. A helpful way to remember the use in speech or writing is to picture a speaker or poet saying a phrase 'again and again' on purpose to make it stronger or more musical, which is why repetition is used for emphasis and rhythm.
