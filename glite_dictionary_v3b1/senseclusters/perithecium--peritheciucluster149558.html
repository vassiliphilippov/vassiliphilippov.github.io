Etymology

perithecium comes from Greek parts peri- meaning 'around' and theke meaning 'case' or 'box', the same peri- you see in perimeter. So it means a 'case around' the spores, which is why a perithecium is the flask-shaped body that surrounds and protects the spore sacs.
