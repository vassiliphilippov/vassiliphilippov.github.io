Etymology

Solanum rostratum is a scientific name from Latin: Solanum was the old word for nightshade plants and rostratum means 'beaked' because the plant's spiky fruit has a small pointed tip like a bird's beak. So the name gives a simple picture of the prickly buffalobur and helps you remember what the plant looks like.
