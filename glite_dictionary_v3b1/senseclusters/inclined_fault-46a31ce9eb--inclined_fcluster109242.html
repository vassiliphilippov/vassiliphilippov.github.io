Etymology

inclined fault comes from a simple picture: a fault is a 'break' in the rock and inclined means it 'tilts' or 'slopes', so one side can 'move up' over the other. That's why an inclined fault means a tilted fracture in the ground where rock has shifted along a slope, often causing earthquakes or lifting mountains.
