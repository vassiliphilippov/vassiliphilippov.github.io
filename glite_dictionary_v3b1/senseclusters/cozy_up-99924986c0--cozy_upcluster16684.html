Etymology

cozy up: The phrasal verb cozy up comes from the picture of moving close to someone to feel warm and comfortable, from cozy (comfortable) and up (moving closer). So, to cozy up to someone today means to act friendly and close, often 'to get a favor' or help, sometimes not sincere.
