Etymology

remoulade comes from the same French name but became a local Danish version that is yellow and made with chopped pickles, mustard, and oil. A helpful way to remember the Danish remoulade is to think of it as a 'mixed' relish, which explains why it is a tangy condiment for hot dogs and sandwiches.
