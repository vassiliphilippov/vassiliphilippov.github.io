Etymology

The phrase darnedest effort comes from darnedest, a softer form of the older word 'damnedest', which long ago meant very extreme or the most possible. So, saying someone gave their darnedest effort makes the image of them trying as hard as they can, which is why it means 'the greatest effort'.
