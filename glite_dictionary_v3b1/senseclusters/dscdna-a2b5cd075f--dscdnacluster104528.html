Etymology

dscDNA is made of dsc + DNA, where dsc means 'double-stranded' — think 'double' as 'two' and 'strand' like a 'thread' or hair. So dscDNA is a two-strand copy of DNA often made from an mRNA template, which is why it is used as a stable template in lab work.
