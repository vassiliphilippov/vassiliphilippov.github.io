Etymology

ferrocene comes from ferro- meaning 'iron' and -cene, a name for a group of 'sandwich' compounds where a metal sits between two ring shapes, so it literally names the structure. That's why ferrocene is easy to remember as an 'iron sandwich' with an iron atom between two rings.
