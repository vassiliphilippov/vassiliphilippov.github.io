Etymology

Cumbersome comes from the old word cumber, meaning 'to burden' or 'to hinder', plus the ending -some meaning 'causing'. Imagine a heavy suitcase that 'causes a burden' and gets in the way. That's why cumbersome describes things that are large, heavy, and hard to carry.
