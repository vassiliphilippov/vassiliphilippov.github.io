Etymology

Oligocene comes from Greek parts oligo- meaning 'few' (seen in oligarchy, 'rule by a few') and -cene from kainos meaning 'new'. A helpful way to remember it is 'few new' because it names a period when many modern mammals were just beginning to appear, so the name marks an early 'new' stage in Earth's history.
