Etymology

Typewriter paper comes from sheets made to work inside a mechanical typewriter: they were thinner, stronger, and very smooth so the keys could hit clearly and the paper could move through without getting stuck. That's why 'typewriter paper' means paper made specifically 'to be used in a typewriter'.
