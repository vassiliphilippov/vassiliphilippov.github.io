Etymology

The word fluorite comes from the Latin root fluor-, from fluere 'to flow', because people used it to help metal flow when it was melted. You can see the same root in fluid ('flow') and fluorescent ('glow'), so the name links its old use in metal work and the way some crystals 'glow' under ultraviolet light.
