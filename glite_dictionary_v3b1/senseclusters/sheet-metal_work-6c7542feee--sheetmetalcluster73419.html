Etymology

sheet-metal work comes from the simple picture of a worker shaping thin sheet pieces of metal, cutting, bending and joining them to make things like roofs or air ducts. So, the phrase means 'the craft of shaping and putting together thin metal pieces', which helps you remember it is about making parts for buildings and machines.
