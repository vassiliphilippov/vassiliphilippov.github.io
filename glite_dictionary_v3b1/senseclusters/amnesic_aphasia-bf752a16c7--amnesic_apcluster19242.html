Etymology

amnesic aphasia comes from the words amnesic (meaning 'memory loss') and aphasia (meaning 'loss of words'). Imagine seeing a chair but your mind cannot find its name; that's why amnesic aphasia means trouble 'naming' objects or 'recognizing' their names.
