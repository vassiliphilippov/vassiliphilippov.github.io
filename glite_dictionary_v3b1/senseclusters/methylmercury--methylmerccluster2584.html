Etymology

methylmercury is made of two parts: methyl, a 'small carbon-and-hydrogen piece' used in chemistry, and mercury, the 'liquid metal'. So methylmercury is literally a form of mercury with that small part attached, which is why it can build up in fish and be very poisonous.
