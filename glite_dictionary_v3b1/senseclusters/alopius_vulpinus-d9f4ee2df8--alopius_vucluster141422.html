Etymology

Alopius vulpinus may come from old words meaning 'fox-like', with the second part vulpinus pointing to a fox. The thresher shark has a very long, whip-like tail that it uses to swat and herd small fish when it 'hunts', so the name helps you remember its long tail and clever hunting.
