Etymology

-opsy comes from the ancient Greek word opsis, meaning 'sight' or 'view'. You find it at the end of medical words like biopsy, with bio- = 'life', and autopsy, with auto- = 'self', where it points to 'looking at' or 'examining' tissue or a body. That's why -opsy means 'to view' or 'inspect' in medical words.
