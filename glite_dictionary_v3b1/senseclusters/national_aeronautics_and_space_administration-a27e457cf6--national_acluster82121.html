Etymology

National Aeronautics and Space Administration literally names the agency's job: National means for the whole country, Aeronautics is about making and flying 'planes', Space is work beyond the Earth, and Administration means a 'government agency' that runs programs. So, the name refers to the United States government body that runs the country's civilian space and aircraft programs.
