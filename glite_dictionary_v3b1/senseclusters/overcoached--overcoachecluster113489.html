Etymology

overcoached starts with over- meaning 'too much' and ends with coach meaning 'trainer' or 'helper'. The word coach originally comes from the Hungarian word kocsi for a 'carriage' that 'carried' people to a place, so a coach is someone who 'carries' you to a goal, and overcoached means you have had so much instruction that your natural style is lost.
