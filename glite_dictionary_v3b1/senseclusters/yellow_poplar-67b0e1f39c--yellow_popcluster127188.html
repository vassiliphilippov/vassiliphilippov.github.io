Etymology

yellow poplar may come from a simple picture: the tall tree makes pale, tulip-shaped flowers and has light-coloured wood, so people called it yellow poplar because of its 'yellow' flowers and soft wood like 'poplar'. That's why yellow poplar refers to the tree itself, even though it is really a 'tulip tree'.
