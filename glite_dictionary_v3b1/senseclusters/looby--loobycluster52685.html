Etymology

looby is an old, friendly insult for a clumsy or foolish person; a helpful way to remember it is to think of the older English word lubber or the word lout, both meaning a big, 'awkward' or 'clumsy' person. So calling someone a looby paints them as clumsy or silly, which is why we use it for someone who trips or makes silly mistakes.
