Etymology

Difflugia comes from Latin parts dif- meaning 'apart' and flu- meaning 'to flow', the same 'flu' in fluid and flux. A helpful way to remember it is to picture a small amoeba that 'flows' in water and gathers tiny bits to build a shell, which is why the name fits these shelled freshwater creatures.
