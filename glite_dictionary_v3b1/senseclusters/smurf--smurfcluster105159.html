Etymology

smurf comes from the comic name Schtroumpf, the small blue characters created by Belgian cartoonist Peyo. People call splitting large sums into many tiny deposits 'smurfing' because it is like using lots of little 'smurfs' to hide the money, so the term means making many small transactions to avoid notice.
