Etymology

barium dioxide gets its name from the metal barium and the parts di- and oxide. Barium comes from a Greek word meaning 'heavy', and di- plus oxide literally say 'two' 'oxygen', so the name tells you it is a heavy element joined to two oxygen atoms, a clear image that helps you remember this oxygen-rich, toxic white powder.
