Etymology

Hydrangeaceae comes from the plant name Hydrangea, which itself comes from Greek words hydor 'water' and angeion 'vessel' because the plant's seed pods look like little cups. The ending -aceae shows it is a plant family, so Hydrangeaceae simply means the 'hydrangea' family of flowering shrubs and related plants.
