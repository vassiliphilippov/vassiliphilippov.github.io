Etymology

Flacourtia indica is named after Étienne de Flacourt, a French governor who wrote about the plants of Madagascar, and the genus name Flacourtia honors him. The second word, indica, means 'from India' or 'from the Indies', so the name links the man who studied the plant and the tropical region where this thorny hedge tree with small red fruits is found.
