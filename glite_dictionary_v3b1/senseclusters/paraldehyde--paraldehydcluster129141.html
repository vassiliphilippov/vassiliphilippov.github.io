Etymology

paraldehyde comes from the parts para- meaning 'beside' or 'related to', as in parallel, and aldehyde, a chemical name. So, paraldehyde is a 'related' form of aldehyde made by joining three aldehyde molecules, which is why it is used as a special solvent and as a sedative.
