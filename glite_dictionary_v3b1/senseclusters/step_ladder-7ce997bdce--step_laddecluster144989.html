Etymology

step ladder is a name for a small ladder that makes a clear picture: it has flat steps to stand on and two sides that open so it can stand by itself. So the name links the flat steps and the idea of a 'stand-alone' ladder, which is why step ladder means a short, foldable ladder you can carry to reach high places.
