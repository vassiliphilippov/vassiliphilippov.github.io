Etymology

stand pat comes from old card games. One popular story says a player with a good hand would stand and keep a pat hand, a 'pat' meaning 'complete' and needing no new cards, so they would not change their choice, and that's why stand pat means 'to refuse to change' or 'to remain firm'.
