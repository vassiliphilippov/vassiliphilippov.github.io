Etymology

methyl ethyl ketone gets its name from the chemical parts that make it: a small methyl part and a slightly larger ethyl part joined to a ketone core, so the name is like a short picture of the molecule. That's why the name helps you remember it is a simple 'solvent' used to dissolve paints and cleaners.
