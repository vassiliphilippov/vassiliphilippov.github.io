Etymology

openside plane gets its name from a clear picture: one side of the tool is open, so wood shavings can fall away instead of getting stuck. That open side lets the plane cut wide 'rabbets', a kind of edge groove, without the tool blocking the cut, so the name tells you what the tool does.
