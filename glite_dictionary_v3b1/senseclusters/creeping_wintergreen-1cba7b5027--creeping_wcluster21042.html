Etymology

creeping wintergreen gives a clear picture: creeping shows it grows low and spreads along the ground, and wintergreen means its leaves 'stay green in winter' and have a minty smell. So the name helps you remember it is a low, spreading shrub with shiny, minty leaves and red berries.
