Etymology

suborder Lacertilia comes from Latin: lacertilia is from the word 'lacerta' meaning 'lizard', and suborder shows it is a smaller group inside a larger group. So the name literally marks the group of 'true lizards', which is why we use it for chameleons, geckos and other lizards.
