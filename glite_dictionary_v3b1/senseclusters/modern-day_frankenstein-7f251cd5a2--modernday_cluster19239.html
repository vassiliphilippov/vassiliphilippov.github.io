Etymology

modern-day Frankenstein comes from Mary Shelley's story about Victor Frankenstein, a man who joined dead body parts to make life and then lost control of his creation. So, calling someone a modern-day Frankenstein means they are seen as 'playing God' by creating life or doing bold, risky experiments.
