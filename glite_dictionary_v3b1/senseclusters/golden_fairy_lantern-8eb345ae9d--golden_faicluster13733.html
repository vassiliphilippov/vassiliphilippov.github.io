Etymology

golden fairy lantern gets its name from how the flower looks: small, clear yellow, egg-shaped blooms that hang like little lanterns, look golden, and seem delicate like a fairy. So the name helps you picture this 'globe lily' and remember it as a small yellow flower shaped like a tiny lantern.
