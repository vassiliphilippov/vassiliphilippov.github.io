Etymology

epicondylitis breaks down into parts: epi- meaning 'on', condyl meaning 'bony bump' near a joint, and -itis meaning 'inflammation', like in arthritis. So epicondylitis is 'inflammation on the bony bump', which helps explain the pain around the elbow from repeated use.
