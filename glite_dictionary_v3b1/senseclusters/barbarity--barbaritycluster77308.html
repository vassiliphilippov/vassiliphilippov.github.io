Etymology

barbarity comes from an old Greek word barbaros, used for people whose speech sounded like 'bar-bar' and so seemed 'foreign' or 'strange'. Over time barbarian and barbaric came to mean 'uncivilized' or 'cruel', so barbarity now means great cruelty or brutal behaviour.
