Etymology

Atharva-Veda comes from Sanskrit: Atharva was the name of a family of priests and Veda means 'knowledge' or 'sacred text'. So the Atharva-Veda is the 'knowledge' or collection of hymns, spells, and healing words connected to that priestly tradition, which fits its use in rituals for protection and healing.
