Etymology

invalidness breaks into in- meaning 'not', valid meaning 'strong' or 'acceptable', and -ness meaning 'the quality or state of'. So invalidness is the 'state of not being valid', which helps you remember it as something that does not hold up, like a weak argument or a contract without a proper signature.
