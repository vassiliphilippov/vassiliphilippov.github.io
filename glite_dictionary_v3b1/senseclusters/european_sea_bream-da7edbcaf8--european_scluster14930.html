Etymology

The name European sea bream simply describes a kind of fish: the word bream was used for a round, silvery fish, sea shows it lives in salt water, and European shows where it is usually found. So the name means a 'bream' from the sea around Europe, which is why it refers today to the edible coastal fish used in cooking.
