Etymology

radio-frequency spectroscopy gets its name from the image of tuning a radio: scientists choose a radiofrequency like selecting a station to hear a different signal, and spectroscopy is the act of 'looking' at those signals to learn about tiny parts. So the phrase means studying small energy changes in atoms or molecules by using radio waves.
