Etymology

gruelingly comes from gruel, a 'thin porridge', and a helpful way to remember it is to imagine a long, slow punishment of eating plain gruel — boring, unpleasant and wearing you out. So, doing something gruelingly means doing it in a very tiring, demanding and exhausting way.
