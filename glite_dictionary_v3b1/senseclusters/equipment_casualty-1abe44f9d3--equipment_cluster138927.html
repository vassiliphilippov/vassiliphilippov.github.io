Etymology

The term equipment casualty puts equipment (machines and tools) together with casualty, a war word for someone who is 'killed or injured'. Imagine a damaged tank or a broken radio that can no longer be used — it becomes a casualty of battle, so the phrase means the 'loss or destruction' of military gear.
