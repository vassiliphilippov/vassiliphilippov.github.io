Etymology

The word Tartar comes from the name Tatar, which named a real group of peoples from central Asia. Europeans later added an extra 'r' and compared them to Tartarus, a Greek word for 'hell', because they feared the group's fierce attacks, so Tartar came to mean those Mongolian peoples who invaded parts of Russia and Eastern Europe.
