Etymology

The name Avalokiteshvara comes from an old Indian language and joins avalokita ('to look down') with ishvara ('lord'). So, it literally means 'the lord who looks down', a helpful image for remembering that this Bodhisattva watches the world with compassion and helps those who suffer.
