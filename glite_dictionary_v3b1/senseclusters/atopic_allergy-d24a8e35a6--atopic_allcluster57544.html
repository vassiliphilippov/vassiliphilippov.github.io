Etymology

atopic allergy comes from atopic, an old word that means 'out of place' and was used for reactions that seem to come from nowhere and appear quickly on the skin, nose, or eyes. So, atopic allergy means a fast allergic reaction in people who are more likely to have eczema or hay fever.
