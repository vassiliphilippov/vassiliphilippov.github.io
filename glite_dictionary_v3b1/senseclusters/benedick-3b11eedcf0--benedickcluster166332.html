Etymology

Benedick comes from the name Benedict, which has the parts bene 'well' and dict 'speak', and the name came to mean 'blessed'. A famous Shakespeare character called Benedick was a proud bachelor who later married, and that is why Benedick now means a man who has recently married, especially one who used to be single.
