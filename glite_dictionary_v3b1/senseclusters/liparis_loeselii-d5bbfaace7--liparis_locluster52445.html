Etymology

Liparis loeselii may come from Greek and a person's name: Liparis comes from a Greek word meaning 'shiny' or 'oily', which fits the plant's smooth, glossy leaves, and loeselii is 'named after' the botanist Loesel who first studied it. So the name helps you picture a small orchid with shiny leaves and remember the person who found it.
