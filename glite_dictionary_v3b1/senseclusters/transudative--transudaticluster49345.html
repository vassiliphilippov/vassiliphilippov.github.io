Etymology

transudative comes from the Latin parts trans- 'across', as in transfer, and sudare 'to sweat', so it literally means a fluid that 'sweats across' a membrane. That's why a transudative fluid is a clear fluid low in protein that passes through membranes because of problems in the whole body, not by a local injury or infection.
