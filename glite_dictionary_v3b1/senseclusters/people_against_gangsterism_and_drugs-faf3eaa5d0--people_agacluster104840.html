Etymology

People against Gangsterism and Drugs began in South Africa in 1996 when local people joined to fight gangsters and drug dealers. The name is a plain, literal description: People 'against' Gangsterism and Drugs. The group later was accused of taking the law into their own hands and of links to extremist groups, so its actions became controversial.
