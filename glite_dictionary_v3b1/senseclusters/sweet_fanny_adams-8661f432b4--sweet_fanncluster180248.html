Etymology

sweet Fanny Adams may come from a sad story long ago in England about a girl named Fanny Adams, and sailors later used her name to mock poor tinned food as being worthless. Over time the name was shortened to sweet FA and came to mean 'nothing at all', which is why we use it today.
