Etymology

genus Plesianthropus comes from Greek parts: plesi- meaning 'near' and -anthropus meaning 'human'. Scientists used this name for early fossils that looked close to humans, so it literally meant 'near human', though today those fossils are usually placed in 'Australopithecus'.
