Etymology

Scabiosa atropurpurea gets its name from old Latin words: Scabiosa may come from a word for 'itch' because related plants were once used to treat skin problems, and atropurpurea means 'deep purple'. So the name helps you picture a deep, purple, pincushion-like flower and remember this species.
