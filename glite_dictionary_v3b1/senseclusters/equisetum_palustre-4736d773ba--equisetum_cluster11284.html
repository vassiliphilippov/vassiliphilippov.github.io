Etymology

Equisetum palustre comes from Latin: Equisetum means 'horse's tail' because the plant's long, jointed stems look like a horse's tail, and palustre means 'of the marsh' because it grows in wet, boggy places. So the name gives a simple picture—a horsetail plant that lives in marshes.
