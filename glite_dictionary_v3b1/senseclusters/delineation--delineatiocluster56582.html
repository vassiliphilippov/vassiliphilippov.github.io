Etymology

delineation comes from Latin parts de- and linea, where linea means 'line' and is the same root as the English word line. A helpful way to remember the meaning 'a detailed description' is to imagine 'drawing' a clear line with words, so a delineation is a careful description that makes a clear picture for the reader.
