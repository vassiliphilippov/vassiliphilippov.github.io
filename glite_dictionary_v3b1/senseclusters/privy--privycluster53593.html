Etymology

privy as a noun comes from the old phrase 'privy house', meaning a 'private' building, based on the root priv- meaning 'private' or 'set apart' and like private. A helpful way to remember it is that the old outdoor toilet was a 'private' place for private business, which is why privy now means an outside toilet.
