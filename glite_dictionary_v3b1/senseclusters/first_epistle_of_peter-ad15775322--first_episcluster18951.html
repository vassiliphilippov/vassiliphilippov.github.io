Etymology

The First Epistle of Peter comes from church tradition that Peter wrote a letter to early Christian groups. The word epistle is an old word for 'letter', and 'First' shows it is the earlier of two letters thought to be written by Peter, so the title today means the first New Testament letter meant to give advice and encouragement to early believers.
