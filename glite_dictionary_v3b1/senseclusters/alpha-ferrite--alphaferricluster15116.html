Etymology

The word alpha-ferrite combines alpha, the Greek letter meaning 'first', and ferrite, from Latin ferrum meaning 'iron'. So it names the 'first' or main form of iron that appears in steel at low temperatures, which is why alpha-ferrite is the stable iron form below 906°C.
