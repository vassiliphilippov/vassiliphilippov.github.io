Etymology

jauntily comes from the adjective jaunty, which means 'lively' and 'stylish'. Think of someone walking with a bouncy, confident step or as if they had just enjoyed a short happy trip called a jaunt — that's why jauntily means 'in a lively, cheerful, and confident way'.
