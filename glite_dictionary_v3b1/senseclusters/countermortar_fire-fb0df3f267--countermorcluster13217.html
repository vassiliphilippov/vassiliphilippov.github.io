Etymology

countermortar fire comes from a clear military image: mortar is a short gun that fires explosive bombs, fire means 'shooting', and counter means 'against'. So countermortar fire is shooting back at enemy mortars to destroy or stop them, which is why it means 'attacking enemy mortar positions'.
