Etymology

eastern chipmunk gets its name from two parts: eastern shows where it lives, and chipmunk probably comes from a Native American word that sounded like 'chipmunk' and named the little striped animal that fills its cheek pouches with food. So eastern chipmunk means the 'small striped rodent' found in the eastern United States and Canada.
