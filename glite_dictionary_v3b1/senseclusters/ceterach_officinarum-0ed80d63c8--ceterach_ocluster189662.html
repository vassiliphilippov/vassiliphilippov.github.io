Etymology

Ceterach officinarum comes from Latin and old folk names: officinarum means 'from the medicine shop' because people once used the fern in simple remedies, and Ceterach links to the brown, 'rusty' hairs found under its leathery leaves. So the name is a good memory hook for the small, rocky fern people once used in old medicine and still call the 'rusty‑back' fern today.
