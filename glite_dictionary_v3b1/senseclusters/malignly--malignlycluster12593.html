Etymology

malignly comes from the root mal- meaning 'bad', the same root you see in malicious and malfunction; think of 'mal-' as a mark for harm. A helpful image is someone quietly saying nasty things to hurt another person—so to act malignly is to behave or speak in a hurtful, 'bad' way.
