Etymology

The phrase inevitable accident puts together inevitable ('cannot be stopped') and accident ('an unexpected event'). Imagine a strong storm that knocks down a tree despite all careful action. Because no one could stop it, people call it an inevitable accident, meaning 'an unavoidable event' for which no one is to blame.
