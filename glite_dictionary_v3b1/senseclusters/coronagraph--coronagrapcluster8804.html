Etymology

The word coronagraph comes from corona meaning 'crown' and the ending -graph meaning 'to record', the same ending as in photograph. A coronagraph is a tool that 'records' the star's 'crown', the faint ring of light around it, by blocking the bright center so we can see faint objects nearby.
