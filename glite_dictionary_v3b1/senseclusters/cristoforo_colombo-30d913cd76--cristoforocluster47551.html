Etymology

Cristoforo Colombo is the Italian name of the sailor Christopher Columbus, who in 1492 sailed west across the sea and found new lands, a clear picture that links the name to long trips and finding new places. That's why in Italy Cristoforo Colombo means 'the famous explorer' and you see his name on airports, statues, and streets.
