Etymology

muscular contraction may come from old Latin words: muscular from 'musculus' meaning 'little mouse' because muscles under the skin looked like small mice, and contraction from a word meaning 'to get shorter'. So the phrase paints the picture of a muscle getting shorter or tight, which is why it means a muscle tightening or shortening.
