Etymology

slatted comes from the simple word slat, meaning a 'thin strip' of wood or metal, with the -ed ending that turns it into an adjective. Think of light shining through the slats of a blind or fence; slatted describes something made from thin, flat strips placed side by side with small gaps so light or air can pass through.
