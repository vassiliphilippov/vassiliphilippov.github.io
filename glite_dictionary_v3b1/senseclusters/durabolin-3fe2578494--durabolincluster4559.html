Etymology

Durabolin is a brand name made to sound meaningful: the part dura- is like 'durable' meaning 'lasting', and the ending -bolin sounds like anabolic, which is about 'building' (especially muscle). A helpful way to remember Durabolin is that it is a medicine meant to 'build' and 'keep' muscle, which fits its use in treating muscle loss and hormone problems.
