Etymology

Al-Mukalla comes from Arabic: Al- means 'the' and Mukalla is the town's name, so the full name is 'the Mukalla'. Think of it as 'the' port city on Yemen's Gulf of Aden, which helps you remember it is a coastal gateway for goods and travel.
