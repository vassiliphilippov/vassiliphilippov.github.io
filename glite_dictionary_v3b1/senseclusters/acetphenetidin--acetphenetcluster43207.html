Etymology

Acetphenetidin is an older chemical name made of two parts: acet- meaning 'vinegar' or 'acid' and phenetidin which names the kind of chemical it comes from. So the name shows a chemical made by joining an 'acet' part to a 'phenetidin' part, and that is why it was used as a medicine to reduce pain and fever.
