Etymology

Leon Trotsky was a Russian leader who helped lead the 1917 revolution and built the Red Army, but he was later 'exiled' by his rivals and was killed in Mexico. So his name reminds people of a strong leader who fought the government, had to leave his country, and kept speaking out from abroad.
