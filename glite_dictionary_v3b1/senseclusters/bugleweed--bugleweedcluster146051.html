Etymology

bugleweed joins bugle and weed, and one easy memory is to think of its small, tube-shaped flowers like a tiny 'bugle' and 'weed' meaning a wild plant. That's why bugleweed is the name for a mild herbal plant sometimes used in teas to help calm people.
