Etymology

reach Canossa comes from a famous story: Emperor Henry IV walked to the castle of Canossa and stood outside in the cold, begging Pope Gregory VII to forgive him. So, reach Canossa means 'to make peace after a hard time' or 'to admit you were wrong and ask for forgiveness'.
