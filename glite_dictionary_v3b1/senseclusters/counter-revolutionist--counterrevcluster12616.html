Etymology

counter-revolutionist comes from parts: counter- meaning 'against', revolution meaning 'a turn' like in revolve, and -ist meaning 'a person'. So a counter-revolutionist is literally 'a person against a revolution', which explains someone who tries to reverse a previous revolution.
