Etymology

Ptolemy I belongs to a Greek general who was a close friend of Alexander the Great and who took control of Egypt after Alexander died. He became 'king of Egypt', started a long-ruling family and made Alexandria a famous place of learning, so his name is remembered as the founder of that royal line.
