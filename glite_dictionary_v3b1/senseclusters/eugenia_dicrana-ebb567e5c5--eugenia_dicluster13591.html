Etymology

Eugenia dicrana follows old plant-naming habits: Eugenia comes from the name 'Eugene' and the Greek idea of 'well-born', while dicrana is a descriptive word that hints at a visible feature of the tree, like the way its flowers or seeds are joined. So the full name both honors a name and gives a simple clue to what makes this species different.
