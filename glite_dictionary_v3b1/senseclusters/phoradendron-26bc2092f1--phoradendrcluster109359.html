Etymology

Phoradendron comes from Greek parts phor- 'to carry' and dendron 'tree', the second part you can also see in rhododendron. Think of it as a 'tree-carrier' — a plant that lives on and clings to trees and takes water and food from them, which is why the name fits this parasitic 'false mistletoe'.
