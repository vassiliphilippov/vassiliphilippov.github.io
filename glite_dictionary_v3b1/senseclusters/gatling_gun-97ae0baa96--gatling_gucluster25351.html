Etymology

Gatling gun is named after its inventor Richard Gatling and refers to an early 'machine gun' that used several barrels in a circle turned by a hand crank. Picture a wheel of barrels that turn and fire one after another, and that image explains why the name means a fast, continuous firing gun today.
