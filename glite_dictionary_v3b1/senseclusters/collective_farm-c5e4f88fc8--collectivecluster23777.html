Etymology

collective farm combines collective, meaning shared by a group, and farm, meaning land worked to grow food. In the Soviet Union the government joined many small farms into one large farm where people worked together and shared tools and animals. So, collective farm means a farm 'run together by many people' or 'controlled by the government'.
