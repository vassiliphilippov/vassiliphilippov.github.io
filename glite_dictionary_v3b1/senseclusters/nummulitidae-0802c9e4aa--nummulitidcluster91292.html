Etymology

Nummulitidae comes from the fossil name Nummulites, from Latin nummulus 'little coin' and nummus 'coin', plus the family ending -idae. So these single-celled fossils were named because their shells look like small coins, which helps you remember they are round, coin-shaped shells used by geologists to study old rocks.
