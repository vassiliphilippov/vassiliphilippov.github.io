Etymology

Convallariaceae comes from the plant name Convallaria, which itself comes from the Latin parts con- 'with' and vallis 'valley'. The ending -aceae marks a plant 'family', so Convallariaceae is the family of 'valley' plants like lily-of-the-valley, named because they often grow in valleys.
