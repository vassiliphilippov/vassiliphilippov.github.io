Etymology

Transorbital lobotomy comes from its literal method: trans means 'through', orbital refers to the eye socket, and lobotomy means cutting part of the brain. Doctors once pushed a tool under the eyelid and through the eye socket to cut connections in the front of the brain. That's why the phrase means 'a surgical method using the eye socket to cut brain connections' and why the practice is now rejected.
