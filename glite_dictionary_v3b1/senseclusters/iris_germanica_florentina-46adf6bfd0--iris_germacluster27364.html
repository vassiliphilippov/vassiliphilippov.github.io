Etymology

Iris germanica florentina comes from three name parts: Iris (the flower named after the goddess of the 'rainbow' because many irises are colorful), germanica ('from Germany'), and florentina ('from Florence'). So the name points to a German type of iris tied to Florence and helps you remember it is a special iris known for its large flowers and lovely scent.
