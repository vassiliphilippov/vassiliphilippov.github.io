Etymology

circumflex scapular artery comes from Latin words that describe a blood vessel that bends around the 'shoulder blade'. The word circumflex means a bend, scapular points to the 'shoulder blade', and artery is a blood vessel, so the name helps you remember that this vessel curves around and 'supplies blood' to the muscles near the shoulder.
