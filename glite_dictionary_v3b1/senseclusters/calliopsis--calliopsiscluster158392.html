Etymology

calliopsis comes from Greek parts calli- meaning 'beauty' and -opsis meaning 'appearance' or 'look', the same calli- you find in calligraphy ('beautiful writing'). So, calliopsis is a plant named for its 'beautiful appearance', which fits its bright, showy flowers.
