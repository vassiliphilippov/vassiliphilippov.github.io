Etymology

contestable comes from contest, which originally used Latin parts meaning 'together' (con-) and a root meaning 'witness' — the same root you see in testify and testimony. Because a 'contest' came to mean 'argue' or 'challenge', contestable means 'able to be argued or challenged'.
