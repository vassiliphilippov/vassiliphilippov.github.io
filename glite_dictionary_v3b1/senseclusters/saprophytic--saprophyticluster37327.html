Etymology

The word saprophytic comes from Greek parts sapro- meaning 'rotten' and -phytic meaning 'plant' or 'to grow'. So a saprophytic organism is like a 'plant' that lives on dead or decaying matter and gets its food from rot, which is why mushrooms and many soil bacteria break down leaves and wood.
