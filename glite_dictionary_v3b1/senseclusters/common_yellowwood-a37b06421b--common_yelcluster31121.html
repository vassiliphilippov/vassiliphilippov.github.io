Etymology

The noun common yellowwood comes from a simple picture: its wood is pale yellow, so yellowwood joins yellow and wood, while 'common' shows it is the usual kind. That's why the name now means a medium-sized evergreen tree from South Africa, often used for furniture and gardens.
