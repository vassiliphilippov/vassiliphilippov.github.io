Etymology

capitalist roadism gives a simple picture: imagine someone taking a road marked capitalist, meaning they choose markets and profit instead of shared social goals. So the term is used to say a policy or person 'favors capitalist principles' or 'moves a country toward capitalism' rather than protecting social equality.
