Etymology

Bhagavad-Gita comes from Sanskrit and breaks into bhagavad meaning 'Lord' or 'divine' and gita meaning 'song', so it can be remembered as 'the Lord's song'. The title fits because the text is a spiritual talk where divine advice is given about duty and the meaning of life.
