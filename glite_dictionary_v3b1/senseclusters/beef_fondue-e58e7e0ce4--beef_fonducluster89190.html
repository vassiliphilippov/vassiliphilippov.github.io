Etymology

beef fondue gets its name from a Swiss table idea where people share a hot pot and dip food. The word fondue comes from French meaning 'melted', and cooks began using the same shared pot of hot oil to cook cubes of beef, so today beef fondue means 'beef cooked at the table in a shared pot'.
