Etymology

barococo design comes from mixing two old styles, Baroque and Rococo. Picture a room with big, gold shapes and heavy detail from Baroque mixed with small, curly, playful decorations from Rococo. That picture shows why the term means a decorative look that is both 'grand' and 'playful'.
