Etymology

The word tetanilla comes from the same root as tetanus and the English word tense, where tetan- meant 'to stretch' or 'to tighten'. The ending -illa is a Spanish small form meaning 'small', so tetanilla is like a 'small' or mild state of muscle tightening, which explains the cramps and twitching.
