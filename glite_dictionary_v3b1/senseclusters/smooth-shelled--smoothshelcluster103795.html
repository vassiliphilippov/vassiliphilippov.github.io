Etymology

The word smooth-shelled comes from two common parts: smooth, meaning 'even' or 'without bumps', and shelled, from shell, meaning 'hard outer covering'. So smooth-shelled literally means 'having a shell that is smooth', which is why we use it for eggs, snails, or turtles with shells that are even and without bumps.
