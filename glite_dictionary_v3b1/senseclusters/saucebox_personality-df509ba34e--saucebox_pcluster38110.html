Etymology

The expression saucebox personality may come from a simple picture: one popular story says people called a cheeky mouth a saucebox — a box that gives out sauce, meaning bold or cheeky talk. So a person with a saucebox personality speaks in a lively, cheeky way, which is why it means 'lively and talkative' today.
