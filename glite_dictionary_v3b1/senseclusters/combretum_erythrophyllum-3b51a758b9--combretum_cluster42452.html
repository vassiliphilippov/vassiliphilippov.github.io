Etymology

The name Combretum erythrophyllum is a scientific name for a tree. It joins Combretum, the group name for some African river trees, and erythrophyllum, made of erythro- ('red') and -phyllum ('leaf') because the leaves sometimes look red. So the name points to a small South African tree that grows by streams and has creamy yellow, fragrant flowers.
