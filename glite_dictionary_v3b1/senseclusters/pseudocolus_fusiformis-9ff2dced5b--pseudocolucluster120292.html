Etymology

Pseudocolus fusiformis comes from the name parts Pseudocolus, with pseudo- meaning 'false', and fusiformis, meaning 'spindle-shaped'. The fungus starts as a small puffball and then splits open into a narrowing stalk with arching arms that meet at a point, so the name helps you remember its odd, pointed shape.
