Etymology

smartish outfit comes from smart meaning neat or dressy, the little ending -ish meaning 'a bit', and outfit meaning clothes. Imagine someone wearing a nice jacket with jeans, not a suit, and that image shows why it means 'somewhat stylish but not fully formal'.
