Etymology

Painter's colic comes from the old fact that painters often swallowed or breathed lead from their paint while they worked, and the lead made them very sick. The parts painter's (the worker) and colic (very bad belly pain) show why the phrase came to mean 'a kind of lead poisoning that causes strong belly pain and constipation'.
