Etymology

Asparagus asparagoides comes from the plant word asparagus and the ending -oides, which means 'like'. Early plant experts thought its thin green stems and shiny leaves looked like small asparagus shoots, so the name says the plant is 'asparagus-like' and explains why florists use it for green decoration.
