Etymology

grammar mistake is a simple combination of grammar, meaning the rules of a language, and mistake, meaning something wrong. Imagine writing a sentence like building with bricks: put a wrong brick and the wall looks wrong, so a grammar mistake is an 'error' in how you write or speak.
