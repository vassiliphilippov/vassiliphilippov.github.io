Etymology

Veronica comes from the name of Saint Veronica, and a helpful way to remember that name is to think of the Latin parts vera 'true' and icon 'image', from a famous cloth said to show a 'true image' of a face. That's why the name veronica is used for a plant with small, showy blue or white flowers, often called 'speedwell'.
