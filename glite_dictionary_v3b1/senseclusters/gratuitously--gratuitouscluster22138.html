Etymology

The word gratuitously comes from the Latin gratuitus, which meant 'given for free', and is related to English words like gratuity 'a tip' and gratitude 'thankfulness'. Because the root meant 'given without payment or cause', gratuitously now often means 'done without good reason' or 'unnecessary', so think 'given for no reason'.
