Etymology

radionics comes from the root rad meaning 'ray' or 'radiation', which you can see in radio and radiation, and the ending -ics that makes it sound like a system. That's why radionics names a set of devices and methods that claim to use invisible 'energy rays' to find and treat illness, even though science does not support those claims.
