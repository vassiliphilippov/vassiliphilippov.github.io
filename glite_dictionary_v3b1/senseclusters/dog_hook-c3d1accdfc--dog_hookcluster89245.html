Etymology

dog hook comes from the image of a sharp hook that grips wood like a dog's bite. One popular story says the dog part means 'to hold', because the tool digs into a log so a worker can 'move' and 'roll' heavy timber. That's why dog hook means a strong lever used to grab and move logs.
