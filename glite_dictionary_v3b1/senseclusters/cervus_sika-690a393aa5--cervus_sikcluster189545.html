Etymology

Cervus sika comes from Latin and Japanese: Cervus is Latin for 'deer' and sika comes from the Japanese word 'shika', also meaning 'deer', so the name literally says 'deer deer'. That's why Cervus sika refers to the small, spotted deer of Japan.
