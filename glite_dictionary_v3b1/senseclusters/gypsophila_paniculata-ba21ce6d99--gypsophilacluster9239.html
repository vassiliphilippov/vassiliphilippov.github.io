Etymology

Gypsophila paniculata comes from old Greek: gypsophila means 'gypsum-loving' because the plant often grows in chalky ground, and paniculata means 'with many small flower clusters' because its flowers form airy groups. So the name describes a plant that likes chalky soil and has delicate, cloud-like sprigs used in bouquets.
