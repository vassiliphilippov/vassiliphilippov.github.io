Etymology

dolor comes from the Latin verb dolere, which means 'to feel pain', and the Latin noun dolor meant 'pain' or 'sorrow'. Think of English words like condolence and doleful, which show sadness, so dolor today means deep, often poetic, sorrow or grief.
