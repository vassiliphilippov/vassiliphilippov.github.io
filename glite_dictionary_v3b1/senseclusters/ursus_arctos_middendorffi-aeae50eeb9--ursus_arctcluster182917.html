Etymology

Ursus arctos middendorffi comes from older words and a person's name: Ursus and arctos both mean 'bear', and middendorffi is the surname of the scientist who studied them. So the full name literally means 'Middendorff's brown bear', which is why it refers to the large Kodiak brown bear.
