Etymology

The word grosgrain comes from French words gros 'big' and grain 'grain' or 'texture', giving the picture of a 'big grain' or coarse, ribbed surface. So, grosgrain today means a strong, ribbed ribbon or fabric used for hatbands, trims and gift boxes.
