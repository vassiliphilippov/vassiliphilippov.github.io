Etymology

jack-o-lantern fungus gets its name from its bright orange caps and the strange greenish light that sometimes shines from its gills at night, a sight that looks like a tiny lantern or a carved pumpkin. So the name helps you remember that this mushroom has orange tops and can glow in the dark.
