Etymology

Greco-Roman wrestling gets its name from people who wanted to copy the way wrestlers look in old Greek and Roman pictures and statues, so they called the style Greco-Roman to suggest fighting with the arms and upper body only. That's why the sport means a form of wrestling where you may not hold below the waist and must use 'upper body' moves only.
