Etymology

scorched-earth policy comes from armies burning crops and buildings as they moved back, literally leaving the earthscorched so the enemy could not find food or shelter. That's why it now means 'destroying your own resources on purpose so an enemy or opponent cannot use them', even if it hurts you too.
