Etymology

The noun organ pipe comes from the simple picture of long vertical pipes on a church organ that air is blown through to make music. Each pipe is a different length, so when air moves through it it makes a different 'note'. That's why organ pipe today means a 'tube' in an organ that produces musical 'sound'.
