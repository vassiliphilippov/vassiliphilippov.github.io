Etymology

Vaccinium uliginosum alpinum comes from Latin words that describe the plant: Vaccinium is a word for a kind of berry, uliginosum means 'swampy' or 'growing in wet ground', and alpinum means 'from the Alps' or 'high mountains'. So the name creates the image of a bog bilberry that lives in wet, high places, which is why it refers to this alpine shrub today.
