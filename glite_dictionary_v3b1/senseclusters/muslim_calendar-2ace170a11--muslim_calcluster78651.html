Etymology

Muslim calendar names the system Muslims use to count months and years and it begins from the Prophet Muhammad's move in 622 AD, called the 'Hegira'. Because this calendar follows the moon ('lunar' cycle), its months are shorter than the regular year, so festivals like Ramadan move through the seasons, which explains the calendar's meaning and use today.
