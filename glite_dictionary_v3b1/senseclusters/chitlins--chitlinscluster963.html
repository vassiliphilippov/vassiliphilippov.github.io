Etymology

chitlins comes from an older English word chitterlings, which meant 'small intestines'. A helpful way to remember it is to think of 'chitter' (as in 'chitter-chatter') suggesting something small and wriggly, like the twisty parts of a pig that are cooked in the American South. That's why chitlins today means the cooked small intestines of pigs.
