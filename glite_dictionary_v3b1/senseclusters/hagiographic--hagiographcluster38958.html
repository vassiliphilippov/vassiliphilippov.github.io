Etymology

hagiographic comes from the Greek parts hagio- meaning 'holy' and -graphy meaning 'writing', the same 'writing' you see in biography ('life writing'). So, a hagiographic account treats a person like a saint or only shows their good side, which is why it feels too perfect and not balanced.
