Etymology

The word bloodthirstiness is made from blood and thirst with the ending -ness, so it literally means a 'thirst for blood'. This is an easy image: someone who has a 'thirst' like for a drink but for blood instead, which is why the word means a strong wish to hurt or kill and to enjoy violence.
