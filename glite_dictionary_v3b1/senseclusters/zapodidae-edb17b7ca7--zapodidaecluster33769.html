Etymology

Zapodidae is the scientific name for the family of small rodents called jumping mice. A helpful way to remember it is to think of zap as a quick, sudden move and the ending -idae which means 'family' in animal names, so Zapodidae is the family of quick, jumpy, 'zap'-like mice.
