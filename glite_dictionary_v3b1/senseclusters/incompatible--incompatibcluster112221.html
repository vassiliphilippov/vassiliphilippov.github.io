incompatible

0.06/m5.1

noun US / ˌɪnkəmˈpætəbəl /

Examples

Some medicines are incompatibles and should never be taken together.

Etymology

incompatible as a noun names a person or thing that is 'not' (in-) a 'match' (compatible), so it means someone or something that cannot be used or joined together with another.
