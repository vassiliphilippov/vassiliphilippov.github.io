Etymology

pouf paste may come from the image of a soft dough that puffs up in the oven: pouf suggests a puff and paste means a soft dough. When baked, the batter puffs into hollow, crisp shells you can fill, so the name explains why it means a 'light batter' for hollow pastry cases.
