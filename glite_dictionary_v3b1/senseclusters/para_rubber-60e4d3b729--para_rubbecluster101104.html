Etymology

The name Para rubber may come from the Brazilian state called 'Pará', where workers collected the sticky sap that makes natural rubber. The word Para named the place and rubber named the material, so sellers often wrote that label on exported bundles. Because of that label, Para rubber came to mean the natural rubber from those trees and today it refers to that kind of rubber used for things like tires and waterproof clothing.
