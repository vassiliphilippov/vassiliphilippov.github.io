Etymology

encyclopedic comes from the Greek phrase enkyklios paideia, where enkyklios means 'in a circle' and paideia means 'education', so it originally meant a 'circle of learning'. That's why something encyclopedic covers a wide range of knowledge, like an encyclopedia.
