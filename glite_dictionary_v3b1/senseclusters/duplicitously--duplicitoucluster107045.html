Etymology

The word duplicitously comes from the Latin root duplex meaning 'double', and you can see the same idea in duplicate ('to make a copy' or 'double'). It is like someone with 'two faces' who shows one side but hides the other, so to act duplicitously is to be dishonest and two-faced.
