Etymology

Pilgrim's Progress is a famous book by John Bunyan about a pilgrim (a traveller) who makes progress on a hard walk from a town full of sin to 'heaven', facing dangers and learning as he goes. Because the story gives a clear picture of a person changing on a long journey, people use Pilgrim's Progress to talk about a long 'spiritual' or 'moral' journey.
