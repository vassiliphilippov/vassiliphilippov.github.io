Etymology

Jacobinic comes from Jacobin, the name of a radical group in the French Revolution, and that name comes from Jacques, the French form of 'James' or 'Jacob', because the group met near a place named for Jacques. So, Jacobinic means 'like the Jacobins' and describes extreme or very political ideas and actions.
