Etymology

uncuttable comes from un- 'not' + cut 'to divide' + -able 'can be done', so it literally means 'not able to be cut'. By the same idea, we also use uncuttable to describe a person or thing that cannot be removed or dropped, like a player who cannot be cut from a team.
