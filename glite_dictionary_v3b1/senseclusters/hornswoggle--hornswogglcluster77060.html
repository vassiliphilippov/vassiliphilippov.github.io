Etymology

hornswoggle is a playful American slang word that means 'to cheat' or 'to trick' someone. A helpful way to remember it is to imagine someone putting horns on you to make you look foolish and an ending that sounds like the word swindle, so hornswoggle means being fooled or cheated.
