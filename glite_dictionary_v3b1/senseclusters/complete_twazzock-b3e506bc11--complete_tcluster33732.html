Etymology

complete twazzock pairs the word complete, meaning 'total', with the old British insult twazzock, a silly-sounding rude word used for someone foolish. Because the sound feels childish and strong, the image is of a person who is a total fool, so the phrase means 'an extremely foolish or irritating person'.
