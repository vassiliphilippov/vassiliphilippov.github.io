Etymology

The word psychosynthesis comes from the Greek parts psyche meaning 'mind' or 'soul' and synthesis meaning 'putting together', as in psychology and the verb 'synthesize'. So, psychosynthesis is like 'putting the mind together', which explains why the therapy helps different parts of a person's personality join into one balanced self.
