Etymology

St. Thomas Aquinas shows his title and where he came from: St. means he is a saint, Thomas is his name, and Aquinas originally means 'from Aquino', a small town in Italy. He was a thinker and teacher from the 1200s who used clear logic to explain Christian belief, so remember him as the saint who joined 'faith' and 'reason'.
