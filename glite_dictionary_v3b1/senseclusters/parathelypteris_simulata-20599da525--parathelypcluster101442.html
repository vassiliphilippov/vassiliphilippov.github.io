Etymology

Parathelypteris simulata comes from how this fern looks. The parts para- (meaning 'near'), Thelypteris (the name of a similar fern), and simulata (meaning 'similar' or 'copied') together say 'a fern that looks like Thelypteris', which explains the species name.
