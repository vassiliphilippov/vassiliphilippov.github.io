Etymology

Jacques-Louis David is remembered for clear, strong paintings that used the style of ancient Rome and Greece, so imagine him painting a proud Roman hero in simple shapes and bright light. That's why his name now means a leading 'painter in the classical style' and a 'supporter of the French Revolution'.
