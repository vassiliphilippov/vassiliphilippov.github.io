Etymology

Sault Sainte Marie comes from early French explorers. The word Sault is an old French form meaning 'jump' and points to the fast rapids where the river seems to jump, and Sainte Marie means 'Saint Mary', so the name pictures the rapids by Saint Mary and fits the city on the St. Marys River.
