Etymology

Mary Pickford was a Canadian-born actress who became famous in the early days of movies for playing lively little girls on screen and for her curly hair and bright smile in 'silent films'. She also helped start movie companies, so today her name means one of the first big movie stars and someone who helped build early Hollywood.
