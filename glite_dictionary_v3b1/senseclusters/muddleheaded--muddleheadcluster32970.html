Etymology

The word muddleheaded comes from muddle ('mix up' or 'make messy') and -headed ('having a head') and gives the picture of a messy, confused head. A helpful way to remember it is to think of the opposite clear-headed, which shows why muddleheaded means unable to think clearly.
