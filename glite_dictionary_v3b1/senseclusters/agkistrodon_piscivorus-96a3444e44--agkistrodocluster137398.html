Etymology

Agkistrodon piscivorus comes from old Greek and Latin words: Agkistrodon means 'hook-tooth' (for its curved fangs) and piscivorus means 'fish-eating', because it lives by water and often eats fish. So the name is a simple picture of a water snake with hooked fangs that eats fish.
