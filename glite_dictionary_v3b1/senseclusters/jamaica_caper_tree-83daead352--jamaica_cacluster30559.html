Etymology

Jamaica caper tree gets its name from where and how it looks: Jamaica points to the islands where it grows, caper shows it looks like the caper plant, and tree tells you it is a woody plant. So the name simply means a 'caper-like' shrub or 'small tree' from Jamaica, which fits its glossy leaves and fragrant flowers.
