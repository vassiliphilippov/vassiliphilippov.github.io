Etymology

The expression nudge nudge wink wink comes from the real actions of a small push (nudge) and a quick eye signal (wink) that people use to hint at something without saying it. When someone gives a nudge and a wink together, they are joking or suggesting secretly, so the phrase means 'to imply something indirectly'.
