Etymology

The word televangelist joins tele-, seen in television and telephone, meaning 'far', and evangelist meaning 'someone who brings good news about their faith'. So a televangelist is a religious messenger who reaches people 'from far away' by television, which is why it means someone who spreads religious messages through TV.
