Etymology

caffeine intoxication joins caffeine (the stimulant in coffee and energy drinks) and intoxication (an old word for being made very sick by a substance). Imagine someone drinking so much coffee they become jittery, can't sleep, and their heart races— that's why the phrase means 'being badly affected by too much caffeine'.
