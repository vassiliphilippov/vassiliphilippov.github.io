Etymology

zooflagellate comes from zoo- ('animal') and flagellum ('whip'), the little tail many tiny cells use to swim. So a zooflagellate is an animal-like single cell that moves with a whip-like tail and does not make food from light, which helps you remember it is not plant-like.
