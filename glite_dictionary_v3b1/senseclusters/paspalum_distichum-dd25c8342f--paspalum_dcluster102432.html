Etymology

Paspalum distichum comes from old plant words: paspalum was a name for a kind of grass and distichum means 'two rows'. That last word points to the plant's little flower heads that grow in two lines along the stem, so the name helps you picture this low 'knotgrass' or 'weed' that likes wet places.
