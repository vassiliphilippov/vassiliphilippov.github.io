Etymology

The phrase sell your birthright comes from a well-known Bible story: Esau sold his birthright, the special right of the firstborn, to his brother Jacob for a simple meal when he was very hungry. That image of trading a very important right for something small is why the phrase means 'to give up something very valuable for something of little worth'.
