Etymology

turfgrass is simply two words joined: turf, which means 'a piece of earth with grass' or 'sod', and grass. Think of rolling out a green patch to make a lawn or a sports field; that's why turfgrass is grass grown to make a thick, strong surface for lawns and playing fields.
