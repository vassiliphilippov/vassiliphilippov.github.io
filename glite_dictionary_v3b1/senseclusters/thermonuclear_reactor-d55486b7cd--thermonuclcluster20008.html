Etymology

The name thermonuclear reactor comes from the parts thermo meaning heat and nuclear meaning the atom's centre, so it pictures using very high heat to make tiny atoms join. That joining, called 'fusion', gives a lot of 'energy', so the phrase means a reactor that makes power by 'fusion'.
