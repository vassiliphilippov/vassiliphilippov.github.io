Etymology

Lythrum hyssopifolia paints a simple picture: Lythrum may come from an old word for 'blood-red' because of its pink flowers, and hyssopifolia means 'hyssop-like leaves'. So the name tells you it is a Lythrum with small pink flowers and leaves like hyssop, which helps you remember the plant.
