Etymology

The noun holy oil comes from a simple religious practice: priests used oil that they had made holy or 'blessed', and they would touch people's foreheads or hands with it during ceremonies. That image of putting special oil on someone is why holy oil today means 'oil set apart for religious ceremonies'.
