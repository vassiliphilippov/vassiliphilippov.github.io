Etymology

Knife blade is a simple name that joins knife and blade. Long ago people used the word 'blade' for a thin, flat thing like a leaf, so blade came to mean the thin, sharp, cutting part of a knife, which is why knife blade means the flat, sharp edge used 'to cut'.
