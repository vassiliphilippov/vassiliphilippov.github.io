Etymology

The idiom give someone chills comes from the simple body reaction to cold or fear: a sudden cold or scary moment makes you shiver and get goosebumps, so something that gives you chills produces that same feeling. So it now means 'to cause someone to feel a sudden shiver from fear, excitement, or awe'.
