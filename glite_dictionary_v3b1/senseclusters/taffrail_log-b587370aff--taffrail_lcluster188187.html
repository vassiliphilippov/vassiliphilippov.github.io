Etymology

taffrail log comes from sailors measuring a ship's speed by towing a small 'log' from the ship's taffrail, the rail at the back; the little device had a spinning wheel or fins that turned in the water. That's why taffrail log means 'a speed-measuring device towed behind a ship'.
