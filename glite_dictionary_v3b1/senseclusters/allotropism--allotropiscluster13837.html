Etymology

allotropism comes from the Greek parts allo- meaning 'other' and -tropism meaning 'turn' or 'form', as in phototropism when a plant turns toward light. So, allotropism is an element's ability to take 'other forms', which is why carbon can be both diamond and graphite.
