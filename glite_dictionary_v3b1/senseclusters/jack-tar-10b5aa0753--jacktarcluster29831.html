Etymology

Jack-tar comes from the common name Jack, used to mean 'a man', and from tar, the sticky waterproof material sailors used on ropes and clothes, so picture a man smeared with tar on a ship. So a Jack-tar was a man who worked on ships, which is why the word means 'sailor' (especially in the British navy).
