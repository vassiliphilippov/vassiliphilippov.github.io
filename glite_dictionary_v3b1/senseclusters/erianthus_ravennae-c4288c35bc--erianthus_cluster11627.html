Etymology

Erianthus ravennae may come from a Greek word and a place name: Erianthus means 'wool flower', a picture of the plant's soft, feathery plumes, and ravennae means 'from Ravenna', so the name helps you remember this tall ornamental grass with white, woolly plumes.
