Etymology

Abronia umbellata gets its name from old languages: Abronia may come from a Greek word meaning 'delicate', and umbellata comes from Latin for 'little umbrella', a picture of its umbrella-shaped flower clusters. That's why the name helps you imagine a low, delicate plant with round, umbrella-like blooms on the sandy shore.
