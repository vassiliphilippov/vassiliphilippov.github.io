Etymology

magnetic head: The name magnetic head comes from old tape recorders, where a small metal piece called the head touched the moving tape and used a magnet to 'write' sounds on the tape or 'read' them back. So today magnetic head means the part of a player or recorder that reads or writes information on magnetic tape.
