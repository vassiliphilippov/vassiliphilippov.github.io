Etymology

Steller's sea eagle is named after Georg Steller, an explorer and scientist who first described this big coastal bird. Imagine Steller pointing at a huge bird by the sea—so Steller's + sea eagle means the sea eagle linked to Steller, and today it names the large eagle found on the coasts of northeastern Asia.
