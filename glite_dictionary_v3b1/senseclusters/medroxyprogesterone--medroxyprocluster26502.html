Etymology

medroxyprogesterone names a man-made form of the natural hormone progesterone, and the pro- part means 'for' and points to 'pregnancy' (as in gestation). The medroxy- part shows small chemical changes, so medroxyprogesterone is literally a modified progesterone used in medicines for menstrual problems and hormone therapy.
