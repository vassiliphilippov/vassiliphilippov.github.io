Etymology

Seneca the Younger was a Roman thinker and writer known as the Younger to show he was the younger man in a famous family and who served as an adviser to Emperor Nero. Imagine him writing calm letters of advice and fierce stage plays while living in a dangerous court; that's why his name is linked to 'Stoic' ideas of calm self control and to tragic drama.
