Etymology

Subularia aquatica comes from Latin: Subularia from 'subula' meaning 'awl', a small pointed tool, and aquatica meaning 'of the water'. So the name creates an image of tiny, stiff, awl-shaped leaves growing in water, which explains why it is the name for this little aquatic plant.
