Etymology

rakija comes from the Arabic word araq, which means 'sweat' and was used for the liquid that 'sweats' out when a drink is distilled, then became Turkish raki and later the Balkan rakija. So, rakija today is a strong distilled drink, often made from plums or grapes, and the name remembers the liquid that comes out during distilling.
