Etymology

hamadryad is also used for the king cobra and comes from the same Greek parts hama 'together' and dryas 'tree'. A helpful way to remember this is that the snake was often seen in trees or woods, so people thought of it as 'living with' the tree, which is why the name can mean a very large forest snake.
