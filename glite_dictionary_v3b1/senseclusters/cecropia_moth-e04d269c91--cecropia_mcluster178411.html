Etymology

cecropia moth may come from an ancient Greek name 'Cecropia' that early scientists used for things that seemed large or noble. They gave this name because the moth is very big and has wide wings with eye-like spots, which made it look impressive. That's why today cecropia moth means a very large, showy North American moth.
