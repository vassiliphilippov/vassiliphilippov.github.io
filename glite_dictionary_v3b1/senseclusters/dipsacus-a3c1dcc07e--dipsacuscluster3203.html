Etymology

Dipsacus comes from an old Greek root dipsa meaning 'thirst'; a helpful way to remember it is to think of the teasel, a tall, prickly plant with dry, spiny seed heads. So the name links to these hardy, dry-looking herbs and their sharp flower heads.
