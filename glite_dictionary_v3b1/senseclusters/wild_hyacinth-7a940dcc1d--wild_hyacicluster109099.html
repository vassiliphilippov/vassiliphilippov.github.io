Etymology

wild hyacinth mixes hyacinth, the familiar garden flower with blue or purple blooms, and wild, meaning 'in nature'. So the name paints a simple picture: a plant that looks like a hyacinth but grows in meadows or woods, and that is why it means 'a blue or purple flowering plant found in the wild'.
