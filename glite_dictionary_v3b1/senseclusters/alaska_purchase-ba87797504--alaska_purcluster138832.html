Etymology

Alaska Purchase names the 1867 deal when the United States paid Russia to buy the large, icy land of Alaska. People at the time called the deal 'Seward's Folly' because it seemed like paying for a cold, empty place, so the picture of buying a frozen land helps you remember it means 'the United States bought Alaska in 1867'.
