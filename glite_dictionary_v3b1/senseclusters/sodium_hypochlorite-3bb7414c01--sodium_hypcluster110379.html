Etymology

sodium hypochlorite comes from the names of its parts: sodium, the salt metal, and the parts hypo and chlorite, words that point to a milder form of chlorine. So the name gives a clear picture of a salt joined to a chlorine-type chemical, which is why it is used as a 'bleach' and 'disinfectant'.
