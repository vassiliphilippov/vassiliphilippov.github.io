Etymology

one-handedness is made from the simple parts one, hand, and -ness, where one means 'single', hand means 'the body part you use to hold things', and -ness means 'a state or quality'. So, one-handedness is the state of using one hand, just like in left-handed or right-handed, which is why it means using one hand for most actions.
