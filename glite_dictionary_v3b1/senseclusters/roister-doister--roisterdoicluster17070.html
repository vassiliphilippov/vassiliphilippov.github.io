Etymology

The word roister-doister is a playful rhyming pair made from roister, an old word for 'to party loudly', plus the matching rhyme doister added to make the phrase sound fun. A helpful way to remember it is that the pair sounds like boisterous, so it brings to mind a loud, merry person, which is why it means someone who acts loudly and wildly at parties.
