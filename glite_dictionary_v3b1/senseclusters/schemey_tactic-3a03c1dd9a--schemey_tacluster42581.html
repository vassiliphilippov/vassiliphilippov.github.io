Etymology

The phrase schemey tactic comes from the idea of a hidden scheme, a secret plan, and a tactic, a way of acting. Imagine someone quietly making a plan to trick others and then using it, and that's why the phrase means 'a tricky or dishonest strategy to get an unfair advantage'.
