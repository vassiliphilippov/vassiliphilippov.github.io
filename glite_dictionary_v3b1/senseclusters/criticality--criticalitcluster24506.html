Etymology

The word criticality comes from an old Greek root crit- that means 'to judge', the same root you see in critic and critical. Today criticality often means a 'tipping point', the moment when something becomes crucial, unstable, or able to keep going on its own, so the origin helps you remember it is a decision or turning point.
