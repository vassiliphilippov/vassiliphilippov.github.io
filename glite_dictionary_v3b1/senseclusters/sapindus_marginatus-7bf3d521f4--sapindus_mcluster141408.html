Etymology

Sapindus marginatus comes from words that point to the tree's soap-like fruit and its leaf edges. Sapindus means 'soap' because the fruit makes a soapy foam, and marginatus means 'with an edge' or 'border', so the name helps you remember it is a soap-producing tree with noticeable leaf margins.
