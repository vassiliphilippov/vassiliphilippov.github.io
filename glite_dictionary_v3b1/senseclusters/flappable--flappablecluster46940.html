Etymology

flappable comes from the verb flap, meaning 'to move wings or arms quickly', plus the ending -able meaning 'can be'. Think of a small bird suddenly flapping when it is scared; a flappable person reacts in the same quick, nervous way, so flappable means easily upset or made nervous.
