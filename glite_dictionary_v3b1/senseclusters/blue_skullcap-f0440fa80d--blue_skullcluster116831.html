Etymology

blue skullcap gets its name from the plant's small blue flowers that sit like tiny caps on the stems, a shape people thought looked like a small skullcap. Because of this look, early herbalists used blue skullcap as a remedy to 'calm' the nerves and ease 'muscle spasms', so the name links the plant's shape with its calming use.
