Etymology

draw a straight furrow comes from farming, where to draw a straightfurrow means to plow a neat, straight line in the field. Making straight furrows showed steady care and a clear direction, so the phrase now means 'to follow a steady course' or 'to do a task carefully and without being distracted'.
