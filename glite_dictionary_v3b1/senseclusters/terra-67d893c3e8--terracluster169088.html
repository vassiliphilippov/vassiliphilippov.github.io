Etymology

Terra comes from the Latin word terra, meaning 'earth' or 'land'. As a Roman goddess she was like 'Mother Earth', who gives life to crops and people, so farmers prayed to her for fertile soil and help with marriage and family. You can see the same root in words like terrain and territory, which also talk about land.
