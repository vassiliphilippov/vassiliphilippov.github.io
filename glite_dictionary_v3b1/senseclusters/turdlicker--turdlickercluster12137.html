Etymology

turdlicker is a rude modern insult made from turd, a crude word for 'poo', and licker, meaning 'one who licks'. It creates a shocking image of someone who enjoys filth, so it is used to call a person nasty, untrustworthy, or insincere and is therefore very offensive and best avoided.
