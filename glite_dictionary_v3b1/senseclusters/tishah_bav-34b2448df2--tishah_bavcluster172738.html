Etymology

Tishah b'Av literally means the 'ninth of Av' in Hebrew, the calendar day when long ago both ancient temples in Jerusalem were destroyed. People mark that day with 'fasting', not eating, and 'mourning', showing deep sadness, so the name now means the yearly Jewish day of sorrow and remembrance.
