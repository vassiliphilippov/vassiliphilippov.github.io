Etymology

unapproachability comes from the parts un- ('not'), approach ('come near' or 'come to speak to'), and -ability ('the quality of being'), much like the word approachable with 'un-' added. So unapproachability means the state of being hard to come near or talk to, often because someone seems unfriendly or distant.
