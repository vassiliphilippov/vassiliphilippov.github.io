Etymology

hierarchization comes from hierarchy, which has Greek parts hieros 'sacred' and arch 'rule', the same arch you see in monarch. A helpful image is making a ladder of ranks, putting people or things into higher and lower levels, so the word means arranging or creating an order of ranks.
