Etymology

The term multiple voting comes from the clear image of one person putting more than one paper into the ballot box or returning to the polling place to vote again. So, multiple, meaning many, plus voting, meaning choosing by ballot, explains why it means 'casting more than one vote', which is 'illegal' in many elections.
