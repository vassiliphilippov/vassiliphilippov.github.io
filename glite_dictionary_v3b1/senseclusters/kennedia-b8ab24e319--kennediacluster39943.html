Etymology

Kennedia comes from a person's surname, like Kennedy, because plant names are often formed from the names of people who studied or collected them; a helpful way to remember it is to think of 'Kennedy' with an extra 'a'. So Kennedia is the name of a 'genus' — a group of Australian vine plants in the 'pea family' — and remembering it as a person's name helps you recall it refers to these plants.
