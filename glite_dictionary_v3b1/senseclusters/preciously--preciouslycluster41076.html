Etymology

The word preciously comes from precious, which is related to the common word price and originally meant 'valuable' or 'worth a lot'. People began using precious to show something was so rare or small it seemed 'valuable', so phrases like 'precious little' mean 'very little'. That's why preciously can be used to emphasize a very small amount.
