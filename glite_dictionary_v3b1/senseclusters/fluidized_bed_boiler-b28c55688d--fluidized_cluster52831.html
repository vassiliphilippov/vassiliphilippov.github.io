Etymology

fluidized bed boiler gets its name from the image of a bed of small hot particles that is made fluidized, so it moves and bubbles and the fuel is 'burned while floating' among the grains. That bubbling, floating mix helps the fuel burn more fully, so the boiler is more efficient and makes fewer harmful gases.
