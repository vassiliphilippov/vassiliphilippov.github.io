Etymology

text-matching joins text from Latin texere 'to weave' and match 'to fit together'. A helpful image is looking for parts of the cloth that have the same color or pattern even if the threads are not exactly the same, so in computing this sense it means finding texts that are similar in meaning, not just identical words.
