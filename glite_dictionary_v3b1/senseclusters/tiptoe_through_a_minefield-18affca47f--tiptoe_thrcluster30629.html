Etymology

tiptoe through a minefield creates the image of a person walking on tiptoe across a field of hidden bombs called a minefield, taking tiny, careful steps to avoid an explosion. So the phrase means 'to go very carefully through a risky or sensitive situation' to avoid mistakes or causing offense.
