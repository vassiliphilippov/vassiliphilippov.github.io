Etymology

capitulate comes from the Latin word capitulum, which meant 'little head' and later 'chapter', a short section that often listed rules or terms. Because people once agreed to conditions written in these 'chapters', capitulate came to mean 'agree to terms' or give up resisting, which is why today it means to surrender.
