Etymology

potty seat comes from the small child word potty, meaning a little pot or low chair used for toilet needs, and the word seat for something to sit on. Imagine a tiny seat or a ring that fits on a toilet for a toddler, so it means 'a small seat used to help young children learn to use the toilet'.
