Etymology

continuous creation theory comes from the clear image of new matter being made all the time as the universe grows, like filling gaps as space stretches. Scientists used that image to say the universe keeps the same 'average amount of matter', so the name refers to the idea of 'continuous creation', making matter to fill the empty space.
