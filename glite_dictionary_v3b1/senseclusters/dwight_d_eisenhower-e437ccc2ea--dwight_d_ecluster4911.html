Etymology

Dwight D. Eisenhower was a five-star U.S. general and the 34th president who led the Allied landings at Normandy. His last name comes from the old German name Eisenhauer, meaning 'iron' and 'cutter', and the image of an 'iron cutter' helps you remember him as a strong military leader.
