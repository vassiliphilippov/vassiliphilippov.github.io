Etymology

allelomorphic comes from allel-, meaning 'of one another' and seen in the word allele, and -morphic, meaning 'shape', as in metamorphosis. So allelomorphic describes the different 'shapes' or forms of a gene, which is why it means something relating to genetic variants.
