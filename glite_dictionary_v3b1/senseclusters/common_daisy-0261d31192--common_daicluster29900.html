Etymology

common daisy gets its name from daisy, which may come from the old phrase 'day's eye' because the small white flower opens in the morning like an eye and closes at night. 'Common' simply means it is found in many lawns and fields today, so the name helps you remember both its daily opening and that it is a familiar little flower.
