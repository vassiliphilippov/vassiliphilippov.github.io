Etymology

Scalper comes from scalp, which means 'the skin of the head', and long ago 'to scalp' also meant to take something by force. A helpful way to remember scalper is to think of someone who 'takes' tickets to sell them for extra money, which is why a scalper resells tickets at a much higher price.
