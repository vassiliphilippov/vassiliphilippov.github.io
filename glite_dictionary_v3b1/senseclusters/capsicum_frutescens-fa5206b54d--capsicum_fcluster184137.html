Etymology

Capsicum frutescens comes from Latin words that describe the pepper: capsicum comes from 'capsa' meaning 'box' because the seeds sit in a small pod like a little box, and frutescens means 'becoming a shrub', showing the plant grows as a small bush. So the name helps you remember it is a hot, medium-sized red pepper plant often used for sauces.
