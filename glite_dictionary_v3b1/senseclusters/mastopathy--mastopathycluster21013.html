Etymology

mastopathy comes from Greek parts mast- meaning 'breast' and -pathy meaning 'disease' or 'suffering'. So mastopathy simply means a disease or problem of the breast. You can also see mast- in mastectomy, a word for the operation to remove a breast, which helps remember the 'breast' part.
