Etymology

Acanthocereus comes from Greek acantha meaning 'thorn' and Latin cereus meaning 'candle', a name long used for certain cacti. So imagine a 'thorny candle' to remember this genus of spiny, often trailing cacti that open large white flowers at night in tropical America and the Caribbean.
