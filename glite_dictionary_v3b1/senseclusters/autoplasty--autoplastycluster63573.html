Etymology

The word autoplasty comes from the Greek parts auto- meaning 'self' and -plasty meaning 'to form' or 'to shape'. So autoplasty is a surgical repair where surgeons 'shape' tissue taken from the patient's own body, which is why the patient's own tissue is used.
