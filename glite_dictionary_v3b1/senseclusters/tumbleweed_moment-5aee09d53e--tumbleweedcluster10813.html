Etymology

The phrase tumbleweed moment comes from the image of a dry plant, the tumbleweed, rolling across an empty street in old cowboy movies. So when a joke or comment makes everyone go quiet, people picture that lonely street and call it a tumbleweed moment, meaning 'an awkward silence'.
