Etymology

Rhus dermatitis comes from the plant name Rhus and the word dermatitis, which means 'skin inflammation'. It refers to the itchy skin reaction you get after touching plants like poison ivy, poison oak, or sumac, so the name literally points to 'skin inflammation' caused by those plants.
