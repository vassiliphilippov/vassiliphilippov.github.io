bad taste

1.0/m4.1
An unpleasant feeling that stays after something rude or upsetting
noun US / bæd teɪst /

Her insensitive remarks gave everyone a bad taste about the whole meeting.

The decor in the restaurant left a bad taste for many visitors.

I can't stand the bad taste of that dish!
After the incident, he felt a lingering bad taste about the event.

Etymology

bad taste comes from the simple image of something leaving a badtaste in your mouth. If food leaves a bad taste that stays, we say it left a 'bad taste', and by the same image a rude action or upsetting event can leave a 'bad taste' meaning a lasting 'bad feeling'.
