Etymology

The word monocyte comes from two Greek parts: mono- meaning 'single' and -cyte meaning 'cell'. So a monocyte is literally a 'single cell', which explains why it is a large white blood cell with one nucleus that helps defend the body by eating bacteria and other invaders.
