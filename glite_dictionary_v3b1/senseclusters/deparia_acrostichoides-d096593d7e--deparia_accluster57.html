Etymology

Deparia acrostichoides comes from old plant-naming words: the part acrostichoides means 'like Acrostichum' and is made of acro 'top' and stichos 'row'. This image of rows at the top of a leaf, with silvery outgrowths that enclose the developing spores, explains why the name fits this fern.
