Etymology

Malaysian Mujahidin Group joins the country word Malaysian with mujahidin, an Arabic word that means 'people who struggle for their faith'. Because groups use mujahidin to show they are 'religious fighters', this name signals an armed group from Malaysia, so it is used for a militant organization active in the region.
