Etymology

toggle bolt comes from a metal fastener made of a bolt and a moving toggle that folds to fit through a hole and then opens behind a hollow wall to hold things from the back. So, because the toggle opens and catches the inside of the wall, a toggle bolt 'secures' heavy items to drywall or hollow walls.
