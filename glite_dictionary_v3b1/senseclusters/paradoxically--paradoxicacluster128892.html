Etymology

paradoxically comes from Greek parts para- 'beside' and doxa 'opinion', and you can see doxa in the familiar word orthodox meaning 'correct belief'. So, to do something paradoxically is to act 'beside' or against common opinion, which is why it seems to contradict what we expect.
