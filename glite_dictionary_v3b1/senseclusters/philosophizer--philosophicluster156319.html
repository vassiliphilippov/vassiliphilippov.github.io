Etymology

The word philosophizer comes from Greek parts philo- meaning 'love' and sophia meaning 'wisdom', so it pictures someone who 'loves wisdom'. The ending -izer, like in organizer, means 'one who does', so a philosophizer is a person who thinks deeply about big questions.
