Etymology

hairy darling pea likely gets its name from how it looks: the stems and the underside of its leaves are soft and fuzzy—so hairy—and it has small pea-type flowers gardeners thought were very pretty and called a 'darling'. That's why the name today means a bushy native plant with fuzzy parts and bright pink pea flowers.
