Etymology

The noun May Day comes from old May 1st spring festivals, when people danced around a decorated 'maypole' and put up flowers to welcome summer. Later, workers chose the same date for strikes and protests, so May Day also became a 'workers' holiday', and today it can mean both a spring celebration and a day for workers' rights.
