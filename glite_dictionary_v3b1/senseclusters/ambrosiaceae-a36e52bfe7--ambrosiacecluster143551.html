Etymology

The word Ambrosiaceae comes from the plant name Ambrosia, from Greek ambrosia meaning 'food of the gods' or 'immortal', plus the botanical ending -aceae that marks a plant family. So Ambrosiaceae is the family of flowering plants named after Ambrosia (the ragweeds), and a helpful way to remember it is to imagine a 'divine' name given to these common plants that often cause allergies.
