Etymology

wedgy comes from the word wedge, which means 'a piece that is wide at one end and narrow at the other', plus the ending -y that means 'having' or 'full of'. So something wedgy feels tight or has a wedge-like shape, which explains why shoes or cushions can feel 'wedgy'.
