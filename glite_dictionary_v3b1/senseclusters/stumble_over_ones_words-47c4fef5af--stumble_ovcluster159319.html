Etymology

Stumble over one's words comes from the image of tripping while walking: your mouth seems to stumble or 'trip' on the words, stopping your flow like a foot on a stone. So the phrase now means 'to make mistakes or hesitate while speaking', often because of 'nervousness' or 'uncertainty'.
