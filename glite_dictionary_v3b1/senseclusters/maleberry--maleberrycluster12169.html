Etymology

A helpful way to remember the word maleberry is to see it as male + berry, where berry means 'small fruit' and hints that it is the name of a plant. Gardeners use this name for a many-branched shrub with dense clusters of small, bell-shaped white flowers that later make tiny fruits, so thinking 'berry' links the name to the plant's look.
