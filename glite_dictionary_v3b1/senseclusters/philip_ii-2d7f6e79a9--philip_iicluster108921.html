Etymology

Philip II of France is often pictured as a king who gathered lands like puzzle pieces, winning the big Battle of Bouvines in 1214 and taking back Normandy, Anjou and most of Poitou. That image helps explain why he is remembered for expanding royal power and starting the modern French state.
