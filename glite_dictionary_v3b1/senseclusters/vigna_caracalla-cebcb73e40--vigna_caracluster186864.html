Etymology

Vigna caracalla is a tropical vine often called the 'corkscrew vine' because its yellow and purple flowers have tight, spiral shapes like little snail shells. Gardeners grow it for its scent and those twisted, showy blooms, so the name helps you remember its shell-like flowers.
