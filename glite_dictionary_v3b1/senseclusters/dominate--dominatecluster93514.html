to dominate

2/m3.7

verb US / ˈdɑː.mə.neɪt /

Etymology

The word dominate comes from the Latin dominus, which means 'lord' or 'master'. Picture a lord on a hill looking down over his land — that helps explain why dominate can mean 'to stand over' or 'overlook' something from above.
