Etymology

Drug Enforcement Administration is a name that simply says what the agency does: drug for illegal medicines, enforcement for enforcing the law, and administration for a government office. It was set up in the 1970s to join smaller teams fighting drug crime, so the name now means the U.S. government agency that enforces drug laws.
