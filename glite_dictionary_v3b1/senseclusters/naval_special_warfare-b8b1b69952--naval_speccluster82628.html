Etymology

Naval Special Warfare comes from its parts: naval ('at sea'), special ('small, trained teams'), and warfare ('fighting'). Picture small teams in boats or on shore doing secret attacks and missions at night; that image explains why the name means the navy units that do unusual, often secret, fighting against small enemy groups.
