Etymology

abrasiveness comes from the Latin parts ab- 'away' and radere 'to scrape', which gives the image of something that 'scrapes away' friendliness. Think of a harsh tone as 'scraping' at people's feelings, so abrasiveness means a rough, rude way of acting that can hurt others.
