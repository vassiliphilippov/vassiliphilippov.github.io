Etymology

aplacophoran breaks into the parts a- 'without', placo- 'plate' and -phoran 'carrying', so the name literally means 'without plates'. That's why an aplacophoran is a soft, worm-like sea mollusk with no hard plates or shell, only a skin covered in tiny, spiky bits.
