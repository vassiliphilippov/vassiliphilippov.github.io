Etymology

mullein pink probably comes from the two words mullein and pink. mullein refers to the plant's 'soft, woolly leaves' and pink was an old word for a 'small bright flower'. So the name describes a fuzzy-leaved plant with bright pink or red blooms, which is why it is used for the garden plant with silvery leaves and crimson flowers.
