Etymology

treated comes from an old Latin word that meant 'to handle' or 'to work on', and it shares the root tract (think tractor) meaning 'to pull' or 'move'. So when water, wood, or fabric is treated, it has been 'worked on' by a process or chemicals to change or improve it.
