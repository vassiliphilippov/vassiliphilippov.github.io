Etymology

Euskadi ta Askatasuna comes from three Basque words: Euskadi meaning 'Basque Country', ta meaning 'and', and Askatasuna meaning 'freedom'. The phrase literally says 'Basque Country and Freedom', so the group used it to show its aim for the Basque region to be free.
