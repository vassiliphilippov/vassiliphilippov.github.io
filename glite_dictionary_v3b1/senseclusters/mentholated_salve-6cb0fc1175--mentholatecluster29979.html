Etymology

mentholated salve comes from menthol, the minty ingredient that makes skin feel cool, and salve, a soft ointment you rub on the skin. People rub this minty ointment on sore muscles or chests to 'soothe' or 'relieve' irritation, so the name means an ointment that gives a cooling, soothing effect.
