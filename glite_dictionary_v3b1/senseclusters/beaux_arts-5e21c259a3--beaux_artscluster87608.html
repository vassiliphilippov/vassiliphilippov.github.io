Etymology

beaux arts comes from French and literally joins beaux 'beautiful' and arts 'works'. It was the name of a famous art school in Paris where artists learned large, classical designs and rich decoration, so beaux arts now means both the study of 'fine art' and a grand, highly decorated style.
