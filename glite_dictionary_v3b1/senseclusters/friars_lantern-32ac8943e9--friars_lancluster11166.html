Etymology

friar's lantern may come from people long ago seeing a pale, flickering light on misty marshes and thinking it was a wandering monk's lamp, a friar carrying a lantern. So, the name stuck and today friar's lantern means that strange, ghost-like light seen over wet ground that people thought could lead travelers 'astray'.
