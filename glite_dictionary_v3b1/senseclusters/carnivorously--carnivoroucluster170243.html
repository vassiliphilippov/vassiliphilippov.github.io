Etymology

carnivorously comes from Latin parts carni- 'meat' and -vorous 'eating', the same parts you see in carnivore and voracious. A helpful way to remember the second sense is to think of a predator trying to 'eat up' rivals, so it means acting fiercely to destroy or take over competitors.
