Etymology

meshugana comes from Yiddish meshugene, from Hebrew shagúa, meaning 'mad' or 'crazy', and as a noun it names a person who acts wildly or foolishly. That's why calling someone a meshugana means they are being a playful or odd 'crazy' person.
