Etymology

The word thermocouple comes from thermo- meaning 'heat' and couple meaning 'a pair' or 'to join'. So a thermocouple is literally a joined pair of different metal wires that makes a small electric voltage when their joined ends are at different temperatures, and that voltage lets you measure temperature.
