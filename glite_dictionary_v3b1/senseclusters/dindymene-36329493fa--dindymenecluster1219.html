Etymology

Dindymene gets her name from a sacred mountain called Dindymon in ancient Phrygia in what is now Turkey. Because people linked her to that mountain, she was seen as a great 'mother' of the land and life, which is why she is often identified with the Greek Rhea and the Roman Ops.
