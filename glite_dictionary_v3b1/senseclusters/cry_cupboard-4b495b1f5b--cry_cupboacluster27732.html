Etymology

cry cupboard may come from the image of a small, private cupboard where someone hides personal things. With cry meaning 'to let out feelings' and cupboard as a secret, small space, it paints a picture of a private spot for emotions, so today it means a quiet place to feel or keep strong feelings.
