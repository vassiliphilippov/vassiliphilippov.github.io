Etymology

Nobel disease mixes Nobel (the famous prize) and disease (an illness) to create a simple picture: after a big win the praise can feel like a 'sickness' that makes someone more sure of themselves and sometimes believe 'strange ideas'. That's why Nobel disease is used for the tendency of very successful people, especially Nobel winners, to become 'overconfident' or adopt odd views.
