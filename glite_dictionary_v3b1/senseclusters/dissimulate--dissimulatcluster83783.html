Etymology

The word dissimulate comes from the parts dis- (meaning 'not' or 'away') and simulare (meaning 'to pretend'), the same root as simulate. So to dissimulate is to 'pretend' in a way that hides your true feelings or plans, like putting on a mask to cover what you really think.
