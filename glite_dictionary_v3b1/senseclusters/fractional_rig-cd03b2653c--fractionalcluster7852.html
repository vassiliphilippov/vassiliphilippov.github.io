Etymology

fractional rig comes from the fact that the front sail, the foresail, takes only a part of the mast and is fixed partway up instead of at the very top. That smaller, lower foresail makes the sails easier to shape and control, so the name now means a rig where the foresail 'does not reach the top of the mast' and is 'easier to control'.
