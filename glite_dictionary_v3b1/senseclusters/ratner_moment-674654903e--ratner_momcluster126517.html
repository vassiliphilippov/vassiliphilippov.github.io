Etymology

Ratner moment comes from a real event in Britain when Gerald Ratner, the owner of a jewellery company, joked in public that some of his own goods were low quality and people were shocked, which badly hurt the business. So, a 'Ratner moment' now means an embarrassing public mistake or a 'careless remark' that damages a person's reputation.
