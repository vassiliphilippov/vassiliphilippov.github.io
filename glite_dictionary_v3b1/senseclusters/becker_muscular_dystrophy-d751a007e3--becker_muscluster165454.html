Etymology

Becker muscular dystrophy is named after Dr. Becker, who first described this milder form of the disease. The words muscular and dystrophy give a simple picture: 'muscles' that slowly 'get weak and smaller', so the name tells us it is a muscle condition that gets worse slowly.
