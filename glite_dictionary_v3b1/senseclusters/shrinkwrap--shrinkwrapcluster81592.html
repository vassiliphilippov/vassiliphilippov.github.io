Etymology

shrinkwrap comes from the simple words shrink ('to become smaller') and wrap ('to cover something'). It refers to a plastic film that 'shrinks' when heated and 'wraps' tightly around an object, so to shrinkwrap something is to cover it with a tight, secure plastic covering.
