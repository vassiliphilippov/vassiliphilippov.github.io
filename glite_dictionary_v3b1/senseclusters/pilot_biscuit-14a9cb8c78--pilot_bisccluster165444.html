Etymology

pilot biscuit comes from life at sea: sailors kept very hard, dry biscuit that lasted for months on long trips. One popular idea is that the name pilot showed these biscuits 'guided' or helped the crew survive the journey, so today pilot biscuit means 'a very hard, without-salt biscuit once eaten by sailors'.
