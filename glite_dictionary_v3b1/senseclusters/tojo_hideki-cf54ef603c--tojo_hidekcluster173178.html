Etymology

Tojo Hideki is the name of a Japanese army officer who became Prime Minister during World War II and is often remembered in his uniform as the country's wartime leader. He ordered the attack on Pearl Harbor and was later tried and executed for 'war crimes', so his name is linked to wartime leadership and responsibility for Japan's actions.
