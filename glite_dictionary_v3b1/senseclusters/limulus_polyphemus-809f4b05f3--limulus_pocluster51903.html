Etymology

Limulus polyphemus is a scientific name given by early naturalists. Limulus is like an old Latin word for a 'mud creature' and polyphemus refers to the giant 'Polyphemus' from Greek stories because the crab's large, round shell reminded people of a giant's eye, so the name points to the animal's big, unusual shape.
