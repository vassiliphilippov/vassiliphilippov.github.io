Etymology

hypersensitization starts with hyper- meaning 'too much', as in hyperactive, includes sensitive meaning 'feeling', and ends with -ization meaning 'the process of becoming'. So hypersensitization is the process of becoming 'too sensitive', which fits how bodies or cells react strongly after repeated exposure.
