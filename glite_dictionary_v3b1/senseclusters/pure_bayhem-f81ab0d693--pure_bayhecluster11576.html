Etymology

pure Bayhem comes from mixing movie director Michael Bay's name with the word 'mayhem', because his films are full of big explosions and nonstop action, so Bayhem creates a picture of loud, chaotic excitement. So, pure Bayhem means 'extreme excitement or chaotic action'.
