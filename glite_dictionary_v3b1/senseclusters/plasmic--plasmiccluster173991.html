Etymology

plasmic comes from the Greek plasma, meaning 'something formed', from plassein 'to form', and is related to plastic, which also means 'able to be shaped'. So, in biology plasmic describes the formed parts or the fluid that surrounds a cell's contents, helping you remember it as the material that holds and shapes what is inside a cell.
