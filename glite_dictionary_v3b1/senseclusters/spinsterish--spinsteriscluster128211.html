Etymology

spinsterish comes from spinster, which began as the name for a woman who spun thread or yarn from the verb spin meaning 'to spin' and later came to mean an unmarried older woman. The ending -ish means 'like', so spinsterish means 'like a spinster' — someone who is very formal or old-fashioned.
