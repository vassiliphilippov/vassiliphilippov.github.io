Etymology

ravel comes from an old Dutch word that meant 'to tangle' or 'to fray', but because of the related English word unravel people also started using ravel to mean 'to untangle' or separate threads. So a helpful way to remember this sense is to think of pulling threads apart to 'undo' a knot, like the action in unravel.
