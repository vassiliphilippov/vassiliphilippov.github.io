Etymology

sheep polypore may come from old shepherds who found this pale, round fungus where their sheep grazed, and the second part, polypore, simply means a mushroom with 'many pores' on its bottom. So the name gives a clear picture of an edible mushroom with lots of tiny holes often found under pine trees, which helps you remember what it is.
