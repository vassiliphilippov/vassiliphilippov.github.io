Etymology

modern font comes from a style of printed letters first made in the late 1700s by designers such as Bodoni. Those letters had very thin small lines at the ends of letters and strong thick vertical strokes, so they looked clean and elegant, and that's why a modern font today is used to give a design a 'stylish' or 'high-end' look.
