Etymology

abruptness comes from old Latin parts ab- 'away' and rupt from rumpere 'to break', and you can see the same root in interrupt and rupture. Imagine a road that suddenly 'breaks away' into a steep drop like a cliff; that's why abruptness describes a very steep or sudden slope.
