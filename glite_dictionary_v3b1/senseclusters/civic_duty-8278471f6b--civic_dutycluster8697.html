Etymology

civic duty connects civic (from the old word for 'city' or 'town') and duty ('a job you must do'). Imagine neighbors meeting to vote, paying taxes, or helping when the town has a problem; doing these things is a civic duty, which is why the phrase means the responsibilities of a citizen.
