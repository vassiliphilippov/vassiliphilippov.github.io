Etymology

millenniumism comes from millennium ('a thousand years') and the ending -ism ('belief' or 'doctrine'). It literally means the belief in a 'thousand-year' rule, which is why it refers to the Christian idea of Christ's predicted thousand-year reign described in the Bible.
