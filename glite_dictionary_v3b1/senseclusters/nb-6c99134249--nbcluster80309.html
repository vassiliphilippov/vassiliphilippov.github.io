Etymology

NB comes from the Latin phrase nota bene, where nota means 'note' or 'mark', like the English word note, and bene means 'well'. A helpful way to remember it is to imagine a teacher writing 'note well' in the margin to tell you to 'pay attention', which is why NB marks something important.
