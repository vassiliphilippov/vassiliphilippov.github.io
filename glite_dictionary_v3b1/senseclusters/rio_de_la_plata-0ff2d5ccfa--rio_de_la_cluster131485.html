Etymology

Rio de la Plata means 'river of silver' in Spanish; rio means 'river' and plata means 'silver'. Early Spanish explorers called the wide, shining river this after hearing stories of silver inland and because the bright water often looked like silver, so the name stuck for the large river mouth between Argentina and Uruguay.
