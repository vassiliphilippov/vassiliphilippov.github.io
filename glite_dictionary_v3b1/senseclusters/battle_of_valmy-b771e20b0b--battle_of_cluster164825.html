Etymology

Battle of Valmy was fought in 1792 when a mostly new French army held its ground and the loud fire of French cannons made the Prussian and Austrian forces stop and fall back. Because this fight stopped the foreign advance and kept the Revolution alive, it is remembered as the moment that 'saved the French Revolution' and became a 'turning point'.
