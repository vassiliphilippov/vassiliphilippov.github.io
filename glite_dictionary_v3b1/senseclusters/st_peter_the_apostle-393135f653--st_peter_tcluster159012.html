Etymology

St. Peter the Apostle comes from the Bible story where Jesus gave the fisherman Simon the name Peter, from a word meaning 'rock', to show he would be the strong base of the new church. That's why people remember him as the first leader and give him the 'keys' as a sign that he is in charge.
