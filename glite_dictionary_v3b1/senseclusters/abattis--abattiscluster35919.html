Etymology

abattis comes from the old French word abattre, which means 'to beat down' or 'to fell' and is related to the English word beat. So an abattis is literally trees beaten or cut down with their sharp branches facing out to form a defence, which is why it means a barrier of felled trees and sharpened branches.
