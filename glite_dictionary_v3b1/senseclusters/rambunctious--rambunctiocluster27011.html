Etymology

The word rambunctious probably grew from the older playful word rumbustious, which meant 'noisy' and wild, and it also sounds like ram ('to push') and bouncy ('energetic'), so a helpful way to remember it is someone who pushes and bounces around. That's why rambunctious means noisy, energetic, and hard to control.
