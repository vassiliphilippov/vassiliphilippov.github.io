Etymology

Luxembourg City may come from an old name, Lucilinburhuc, which meant 'little castle' or 'small fortress' on a rock. The town grew around that tiny strong place on high cliffs, so the name reminds us of the old fortress and explains why the city sits on steep cliffs today.
