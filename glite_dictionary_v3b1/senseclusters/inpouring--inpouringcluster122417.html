Etymology

inpouring comes from the parts in- ('into') and pour ('to flow'), just like pouring water into a cup. Think of a strong flow coming 'into' a place — an inpouring is a sudden arrival or influx of people, feelings, or help, so the origin shows why it means a flow 'into' something.
