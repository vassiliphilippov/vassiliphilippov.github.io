Etymology

lateen-rigged comes from lateen, a word that originally meant 'Latin' because this triangular sail was used by Latin-speaking sailors around the Mediterranean. So a lateen-rigged boat is fitted with that long, angled pole and triangular sail, which helps it turn well and even sail toward the wind.
