Etymology

Boletus mirabilis gets its Latin name from two parts: Boletus, an old word for a type of mushroom, and mirabilis, which means 'wonderful' or 'remarkable'. People named it that because the mushroom looks special and tastes good, so the name helps you remember it as a 'remarkable mushroom' often eaten when fresh.
