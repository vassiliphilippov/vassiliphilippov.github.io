Etymology

cataplasm comes from Greek parts kata- 'down' and plassein 'to form', so it originally meant something 'formed and spread down' on the skin. Think of it as a warm 'paste' or plaster you put on a sore place to ease pain and swelling, which is why a cataplasm is a soft, moist poultice used to soothe the body.
