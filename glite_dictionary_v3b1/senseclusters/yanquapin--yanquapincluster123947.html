Etymology

yanquapin sounds like the better-known word chinquapin, which names small, edible nuts and likely comes from a Native American name for plants with 'nutlike' seeds. So a yanquapin is easy to remember as a water lily known for its pale yellow flowers and its edible, 'nutlike' seeds.
