Etymology

east African cedar is the name of a real tree and comes from its parts: east (where it grows), African (the continent) and cedar (a type of timber tree) with strong, fragrant wood. Because people used this wood for furniture and chests that kept clothes smelling good and lasting long, the name now means 'durable, aromatic timber'.
