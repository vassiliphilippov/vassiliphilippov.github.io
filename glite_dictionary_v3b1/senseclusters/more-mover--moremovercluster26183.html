Etymology

more-mover comes from the plain parts more ('a larger number') and mover (from move, 'to move' or 'a move'). In chess, a more-mover is a puzzle where White must checkmate Black in a set, larger number of moves, so the name reminds you it needs 'more' moves than the usual short puzzles.
