Etymology

Canachites canadensis is a scientific name with two parts: Canachites (the group name) and canadensis, which means 'from Canada'. Because this bird lives in spruce forests and eats spruce needles, it is often called the 'spruce grouse', so the name helps you remember it is a North American forest bird.
