Etymology

flip-flop on issues comes from the image of a simple sandal called a 'flip-flop' that makes a quick flip and flop movement, changing direction easily. So when a person flip-flops on an issue, they move back and forth and 'change their opinion', which can make others feel confused or think they are 'not reliable'.
