Etymology

The phrase fuckwitted response comes from joining the rude word fuck to wit, meaning 'brain' or 'sense', and creates the image of someone whose thinking seems broken or wrong. So a fuckwitted response is a very foolish reply that shows little sense and often makes people angry.
