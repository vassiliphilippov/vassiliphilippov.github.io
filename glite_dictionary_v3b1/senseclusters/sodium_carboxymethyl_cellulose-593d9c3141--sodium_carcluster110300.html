Etymology

sodium carboxymethyl cellulose is named for what it is: plant fiber (cellulose) with small carboxymethyl pieces added and then turned into a sodium salt so it soaks up water and becomes a white, gummy powder. That's why sodium carboxymethyl cellulose is used as a 'thickener' and 'binder' in foods and medicines.
