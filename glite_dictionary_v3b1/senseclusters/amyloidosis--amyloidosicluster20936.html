Etymology

amyloidosis comes from amyloid (from a Greek word meaning 'starch') and the medical ending -osis (meaning 'condition'). Early doctors called the protein deposits 'starch-like' because they looked like starch in tests, so amyloidosis means a condition where 'starch-like' proteins build up in organs.
