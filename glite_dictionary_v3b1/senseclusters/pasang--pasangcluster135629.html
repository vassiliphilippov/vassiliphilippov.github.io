Etymology

pasang is a local name used in the mountain regions of Iran for a wild goat with large curved horns and probably comes from nearby local words meaning 'horn' or 'mountain'. So, thinking of a pasang as a strong mountain goat with impressive horns makes the meaning easy to remember.
