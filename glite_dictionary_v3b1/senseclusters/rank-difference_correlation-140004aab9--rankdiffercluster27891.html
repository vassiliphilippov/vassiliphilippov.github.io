Etymology

Rank-difference correlation comes from the image of two ordered lists: you compare each person's rank in one list with their rank in the other and look at the difference in positions. You then use those differences to judge how the lists relate. So, if many differences are small, the 'correlation' is strong and the phrase means 'how much two rankings match'.
