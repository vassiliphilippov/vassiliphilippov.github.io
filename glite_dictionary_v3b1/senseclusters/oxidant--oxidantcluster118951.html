Etymology

oxidant comes from the root oxid-, from oxygen, which originally comes from a Greek word meaning 'acid' or 'sharp' because early chemists thought oxygen made acids. An oxidant is something that 'accepts' electrons or combines with oxygen, so the root helps explain why oxidants make other substances change by taking electrons or joining with oxygen.
