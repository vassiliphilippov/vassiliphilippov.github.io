Etymology

The word WiFi was created as a catchy brand name that copies the sound of Hi-Fi ('high fidelity'), the old phrase for very good sound. Many people think WiFi stands for 'Wireless Fidelity', but a helpful way to remember it is to think of wireless as 'wire-free' internet, so WiFi means connecting devices to the internet without wires.
