Etymology

pyromaniacal comes from the parts pyro- meaning 'fire' and mania meaning 'madness', as in the word maniac; a helpful way to remember the second meaning is to think of someone who 'lights fires' in a group by saying or doing things that cause trouble. So it is used for words or actions that deliberately cause conflict or chaos.
