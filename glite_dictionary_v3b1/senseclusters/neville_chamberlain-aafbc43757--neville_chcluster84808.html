Etymology

Neville Chamberlain is the name of the British leader who, in 1938, agreed to give up part of Czechoslovakia to Germany and came home saying 'peace for our time'. Because he tried to avoid war by giving in to strong demands, his name today often stands for leaders who make big concessions to keep the peace.
