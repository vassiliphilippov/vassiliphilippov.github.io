Etymology

for crying out loud likely began as a mild way to avoid a stronger religious swear; one popular story says people replaced 'for Christ's sake' with the gentler image of someone 'crying out loud' to show strong feeling. That's why for crying out loud is used today to show 'annoyance' or impatience.
