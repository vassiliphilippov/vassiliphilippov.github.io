Etymology

balance of payments comes from the simple image of an account book where all a country's money payments to and from other countries are listed, and the balance is the final number showing if more money goes out than comes in. So, it means the 'record of transactions' that shows whether a country has a 'surplus' (more comes in) or a 'deficit' (more goes out).
