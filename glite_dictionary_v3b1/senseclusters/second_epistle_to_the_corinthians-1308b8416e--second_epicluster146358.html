Etymology

The title Second Epistle to the Corinthians simply names Paul's second 'letter' to the people of Corinth: epistle is an old word for 'letter' and Second shows it came after an earlier one. That's why the name refers to the New Testament book that contains that second letter with advice and news for the Corinthian church.
