Etymology

astatic galvanometer gets its name from astatic and galvanometer: imagine two small magnets or wire loops placed to pull in opposite directions so the Earth's steady magnetic pull is cancelled. Because 'astatic' means 'not fixed' in this setup, the instrument is 'not affected by' the Earth's magnetic field and so can measure very small electric currents accurately.
