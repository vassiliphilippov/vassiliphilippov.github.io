Etymology

family Holocentridae may come from two old Greek words, holos and kentron, meaning 'whole' and 'spine', because these fish have a long line of strong spines along their backs. Their common name squirrelfish comes from their bright red colour and quick, nervous movements that remind people of a squirrel, so today the name refers to this family of colourful reef fish.
