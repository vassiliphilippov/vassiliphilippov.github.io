king

5/m1.0
A man who is or was married to a queen and does not rule the country
noun US / kɪŋ /

Examples

Queen's king
Prince Philip was not crowned as king despite being the husband of Queen Elizabeth II.
Traditionally, a king consort holds a different status than a ruling monarch.
When the reigning queen died, her king continued to play an important ceremonial role.

Etymology

king comes from an old word kin meaning 'family' or 'people', so the word was used for important men in the royal family. That's why the husband or widower of a queen can be called a king, even when he does not always hold the ruling power.
