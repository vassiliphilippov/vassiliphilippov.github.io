Etymology

The name shammy leather comes from the French word 'chamois', the soft skin of a mountain goat-like animal that people long ago used for cleaning and polishing. Because that skin was very soft and soaked up water, the name now means a soft leather often made from 'sheepskin' and used to dry and polish things.
