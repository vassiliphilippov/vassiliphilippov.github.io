Etymology

terrestrial time comes from the idea of time tied to the Earth; 'terrestrial' means 'of the Earth', and the phrase grew from using the Earth's movement to mark time. Today scientists keep that Earth-based time with very accurate atomic clocks, so terrestrial time means the official time scale 'measured by the Earth's movement' that astronomers use for precise work.
