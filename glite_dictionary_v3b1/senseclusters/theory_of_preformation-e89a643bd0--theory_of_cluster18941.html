Etymology

The noun theory of preformation comes from an old idea that a tiny, fully formed person lived inside the sperm or egg. People believed this little person simply 'got bigger' as the baby grew, so the phrase means the idea that development is just the 'enlargement' of a preformation and was later shown to be 'wrong'.
