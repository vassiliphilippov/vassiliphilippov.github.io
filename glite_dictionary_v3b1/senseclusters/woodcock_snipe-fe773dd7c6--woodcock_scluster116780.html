Etymology

woodcock snipe may come from people saying the bird looked like both a woodcock and a snipe — two shy, long-billed birds that live in wet woods and marshes. Saying both names made a clear picture for hunters and birdwatchers, so the name came to mean this small, long-billed bird often hunted as a 'game' bird.
