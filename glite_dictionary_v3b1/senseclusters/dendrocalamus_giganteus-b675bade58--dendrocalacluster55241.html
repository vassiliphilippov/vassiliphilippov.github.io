Etymology

Dendrocalamus giganteus comes from old Greek and Latin words: dendro meaning 'tree', calamus meaning 'reed', and giganteus meaning 'giant'. So the name paints a picture of a 'giant reed-tree', which fits because this bamboo grows very tall with thick, tree-like stems used for building.
