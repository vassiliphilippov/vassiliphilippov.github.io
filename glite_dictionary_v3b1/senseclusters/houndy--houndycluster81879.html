Etymology

houndy comes from hound ('dog' known for chasing) plus the ending -y meaning 'like' or 'full of', and it is like the common verb to hound ('to chase' or 'to bother'). So a houndy person or action keeps pursuing someone and never gives up, like a hunting dog.
