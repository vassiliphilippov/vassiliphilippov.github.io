Etymology

Dissimilation comes from the parts dis- meaning 'not' and similis meaning 'like', so it literally means 'not alike'. In biology, dissimilation is the breaking of complex substances into simpler parts to release energy, so the root shows it is about making things less alike by breaking them down.
