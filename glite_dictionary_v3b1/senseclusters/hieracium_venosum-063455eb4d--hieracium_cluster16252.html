Etymology

Hieracium venosum comes from old Greek and Latin words: Hieracium may come from a word for 'hawk' because people once thought hawks ate the plant, and venosum means 'veined', which points to the purple veins on the leaves, so the name describes both the hawk idea and the plant's veined leaves.
