Etymology

certifiably comes from certify, meaning 'to make certain', and is connected to certain and certificate. A helpful way to remember the second, more informal use is to think 'officially so' — people use certifiably to mean 'completely' or 'extremely', which is why someone can be called certifiably exhausted.
