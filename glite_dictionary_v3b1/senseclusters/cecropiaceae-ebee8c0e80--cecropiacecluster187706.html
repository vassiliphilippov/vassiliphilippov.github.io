Etymology

Cecropiaceae comes from the tropical tree name Cecropia plus the family ending -aceae, which means 'plant family'. Think of Rosaceae, the 'rose family'—it uses the same ending. So Cecropiaceae simply names the plant family that includes Cecropia, which is why botanists used it for that group.
