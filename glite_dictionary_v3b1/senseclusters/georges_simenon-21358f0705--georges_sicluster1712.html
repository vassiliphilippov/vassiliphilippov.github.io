Etymology

Georges Simenon was a Belgian-born writer who created the calm, pipe-smoking detective 'Inspector Maigret' and wrote hundreds of detective books. His stories focus on people's feelings and rainy city streets, so his name is remembered as a master of thoughtful detective fiction.
