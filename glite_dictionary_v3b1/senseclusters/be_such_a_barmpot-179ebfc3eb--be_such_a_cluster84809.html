Etymology

be such a barmpot comes from the old word barmpot for a silly person and from barm, the foam on beer that helped make the word 'barmy' for mad. So calling someone a barmpot gives the picture of a head full of foam and messy ideas, which is why it means 'to behave foolishly'.
