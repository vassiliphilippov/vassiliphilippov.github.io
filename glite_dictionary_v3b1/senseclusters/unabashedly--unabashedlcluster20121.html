Etymology

unabashedly breaks down into un- ('not'), abashed (from the idea of being 'ashamed' or 'embarrassed', and related to the common word bashful) and -ly (which makes it an adverb). So, to act unabashedly is to do something 'not embarrassed' or without shame.
