Tridentine

0.00/m9.9

Etymology

Tridentine comes from the Council of Trent, and the town's Latin name is like trident, which means 'three-toothed'. So a Tridentine is someone 'of Trent' — a person who supports or attends the traditional Latin Mass that the council established.
