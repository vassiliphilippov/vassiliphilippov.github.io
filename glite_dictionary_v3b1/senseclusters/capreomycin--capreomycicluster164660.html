Etymology

capreomycin comes from two parts: capreo-, from the name of the microbe that makes the drug, and -mycin, a common ending for antibiotics that originally meant 'from a fungus' or 'produced by microbes'. So capreomycin is literally the name of an antibiotic made by a microbe, which fits its use as a medicine for tough, drug-resistant tuberculosis.
