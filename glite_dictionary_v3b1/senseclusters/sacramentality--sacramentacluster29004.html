Etymology

sacramentality comes from sacrament, a word for a religious act that is 'sacred', and from the root sacr- meaning 'holy', the same root as in sacred and sacrifice. So sacramentality means the quality of being like a sacrament, that is being 'holy' or 'sacred'.
