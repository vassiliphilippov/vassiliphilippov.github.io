Etymology

Bolognese pasta sauce comes from the Italian city of Bologna. One popular story says cooks there slow-cooked a meat and tomato pasta sauce, so Bolognese came to mean 'from Bologna' and the name now means a rich meat-and-tomato sauce used with pasta.
