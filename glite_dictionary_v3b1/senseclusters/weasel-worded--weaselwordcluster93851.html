Etymology

The word weasel-worded comes from the small animal weasel, which people think of as sly and good at slipping away. A helpful way to remember it is to imagine someone using vague words to 'slip away' from a question or 'avoid' making a clear statement, so weasel-worded means language that hides a real meaning.
