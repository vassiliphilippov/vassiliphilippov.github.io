Etymology

The word coagulant comes from the Latin coagulare, which means 'to curdle' or 'to form a clot', and the prefix co- means 'together'. So a coagulant is a substance that makes a liquid parts come 'together' and become thick or form clots, like making cheese or stopping bleeding.
