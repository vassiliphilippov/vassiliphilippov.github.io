Etymology

Cystophora cristata comes from old Greek and Latin words that describe the seal: cysto- means a 'bladder' or inflatable sac and cristata means 'crested' or ridged. That's why the name fits the hooded seal, known for its big inflatable nose sac and crest-like head.
