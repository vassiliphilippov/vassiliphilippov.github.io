Etymology

The noun superficial middle cerebral vein is a name that describes where it is and what it does. Superficial means near the brain's surface, middle cerebral points to the middle side area of the brain, and vein is a blood channel that 'drains blood', so the name shows it runs along a side groove and takes blood away from that area.
