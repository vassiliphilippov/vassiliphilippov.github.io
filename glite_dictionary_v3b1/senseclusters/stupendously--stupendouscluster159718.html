Etymology

The word stupendously comes from stupendous, which comes from an old Latin root stupere meaning 'to be stunned' or 'amazed'. It is also related to the common word stupid, which long ago meant 'stunned'. So to do something stupendously is to do it in a way that leaves people very impressed or astonished.
