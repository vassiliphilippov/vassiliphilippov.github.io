Etymology

chaff as a verb meaning to tease probably comes from the idea of chaff as 'light' or 'unimportant' material, so to chaff someone is to treat them lightly with friendly jokes. A helpful way to remember it is to think of playful teasing as throwing 'chaff' at a friend.
