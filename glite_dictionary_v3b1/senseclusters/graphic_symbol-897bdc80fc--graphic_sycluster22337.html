Etymology

graphic symbol comes from the words graphic and symbol, where graphic means 'drawn or written' and symbol means 'a sign that stands for something'. So, imagine a small mark on paper that stands for a sound or unit in a language, and that image explains why it means 'a written symbol for speech sounds'.
