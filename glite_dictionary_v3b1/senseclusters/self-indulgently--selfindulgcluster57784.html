Etymology

self-indulgently is made from self ('your own') and indulge ('to give in to a desire' or 'treat yourself'), and the ending -ly turns it into a word that describes how something is done. So to act self-indulgently is to 'treat' yourself or let yourself have what you want, often when it is not good for you.
