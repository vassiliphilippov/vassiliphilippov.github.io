Etymology

Sesbania grandiflora may come from a local Asian name plus a Latin description: the first word, Sesbania, reflects the tree's local name, and the second word, grandiflora, is Latin for 'large-flowered', so the name literally points to a tree with big, showy blooms often used for food and decoration.
