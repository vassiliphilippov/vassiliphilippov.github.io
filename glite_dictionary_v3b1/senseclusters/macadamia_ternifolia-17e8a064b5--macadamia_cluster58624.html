Etymology

Macadamia ternifolia is named for a person and a leaf pattern: Macadamia honors John Macadam, a scientist from the 1800s, and the Latin word ternifolia means 'three leaves' because the tree often has its leaves in groups of three. So the name helps you remember a small Australian tree with pink flowers that is grown for sweet macadamia nuts.
