Etymology

Aspalathus linearis may come from old Greek and Latin words: Aspalathus was a name for a small, thorny shrub and linearis means 'long, thin'. That's why the scientific name fits the South African shrub whose narrow leaves are dried to make the herbal tea 'rooibos'.
