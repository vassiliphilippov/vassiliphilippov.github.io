Etymology

The phrase oily mess comes from the simple image of oil spreading and making things dirty. People saw the oily liquid stick and look shiny, leaving a mess that is slippery and hard to clean, so the phrase now means 'a dirty or greasy substance that is messy or difficult to clean up'.
