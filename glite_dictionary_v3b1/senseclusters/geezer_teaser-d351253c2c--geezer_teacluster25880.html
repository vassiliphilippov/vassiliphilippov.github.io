Etymology

geezer teaser may come from two simple images: geezer is a slang word for an older man and a teaser is a short clip or thing that tries to attract you. One popular idea is that films and shows began using short, familiar scenes and aging stars to 'tease' and draw in 'older people', so the phrase now means entertainment made to appeal to older viewers.
