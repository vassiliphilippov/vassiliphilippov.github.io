Etymology

Pedioecetes phasianellus may come from words meaning 'plain-dweller' (Pedioecetes) and 'little pheasant' (phasianellus). This gives a clear picture of a pointy-tailed bird living on open grassland, which is why the name refers to the sharp-tailed grouse today.
