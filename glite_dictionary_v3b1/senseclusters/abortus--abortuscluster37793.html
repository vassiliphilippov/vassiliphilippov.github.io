abortus

0.01/m9.9

Etymology

abortus comes from Latin parts ab- 'away' and oriri 'to rise' and is related to the English verb abort. It originally meant 'born away' or coming out too early, so today abortus refers to a very small fetus that is expelled before it fully develops.
