Etymology

illuminatingly comes from the Latin root lumen 'light' and the verb illuminate 'to light up'. In the Middle Ages artists used gold and bright pictures to make pages seem to shine, so to do something illuminatingly in this sense is to decorate a text so it looks bright and beautiful.
