Etymology

Treponemataceae comes from the bacterial name Treponema plus the family ending -aceae. Treponema breaks into Greek parts trepo- meaning 'to turn' and -nema meaning 'thread', because these bacteria look like a long twisting thread. So Treponemataceae is the family of those spiral, thread-like bacteria, some of which can cause disease.
