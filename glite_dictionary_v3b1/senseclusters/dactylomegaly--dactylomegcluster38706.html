Etymology

The word dactylomegaly breaks into Greek parts: dactyl meaning 'finger' or 'toe', seen in pterodactyl, and -megaly meaning 'large', like 'mega' in megaphone. So, dactylomegaly is a condition of 'unusually large' fingers or toes.
