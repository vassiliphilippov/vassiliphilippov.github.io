Etymology

The word tarnation is an old-fashioned exclamation that grew as a softer form of damnation, and a helpful way to remember it is to imagine people saying 'eternal damnation' so quickly that it sounded like tarnation. That's why it was used to show surprise, anger, or strong feeling.
