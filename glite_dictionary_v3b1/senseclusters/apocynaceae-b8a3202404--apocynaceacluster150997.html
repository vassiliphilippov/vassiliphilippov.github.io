Etymology

Apocynaceae comes from Greek parts apo- 'away' and kyon 'dog'. It recalls that many plants in this family have milky sap and can be poisonous, so they were used to drive dogs away or as dog poison, which is why the group includes plants called 'dogbane' and other toxic ornamentals.
