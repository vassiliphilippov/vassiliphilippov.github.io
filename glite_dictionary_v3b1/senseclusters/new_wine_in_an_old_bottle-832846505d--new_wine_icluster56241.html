Etymology

new wine in an old bottle comes from the image of pouring fresh new wine into an old, hard bottle that cannot stretch. One popular story says the new wine and the old bottle do not fit, so the wine loses its strength, and that's why the phrase means putting 'new ideas' into 'old ways' without real change.
