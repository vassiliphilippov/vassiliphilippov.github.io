Etymology

The term military vehicle comes from the simple idea of a military (soldiers, the 'armed forces') using a vehicle (a machine for carrying people or things). Armies have long used special wagons, trucks and tanks made 'for war', so the phrase means a vehicle built or used by the armed forces.
