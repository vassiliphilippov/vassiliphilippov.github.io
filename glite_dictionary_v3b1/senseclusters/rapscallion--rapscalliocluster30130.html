Etymology

rapscallion looks like a playful mix of the familiar word rascal and the word scallion, a green onion, and it may come from Italian rascalone meaning 'big rascal'. A helpful way to remember it is to picture a cheeky person who plays small tricks, which is why a rapscallion means a mischievous, playful troublemaker.
