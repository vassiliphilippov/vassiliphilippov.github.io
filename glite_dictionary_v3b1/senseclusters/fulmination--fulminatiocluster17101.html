Etymology

fulmination comes from the Latin fulmen meaning 'lightning' and the verb fulminare meaning 'to strike with lightning'. So imagine a sudden lightning strike or loud thunder: a fulmination is a sudden, angry, and forceful verbal attack, which explains its sharp, explosive sense.
