Etymology

Gulf Coast of the United States paints a simple picture: the Gulf is the large sea area called the Gulf of Mexico and the Coast is the land next to the water. So the name means the US states that lie along that warm gulf shore, and that's why we use it to talk about their beaches, weather, and towns.
