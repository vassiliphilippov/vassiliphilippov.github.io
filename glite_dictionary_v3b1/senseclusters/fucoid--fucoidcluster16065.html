Etymology

fucoid comes from the Latin fucus, meaning 'seaweed', and the ending -oid, meaning 'like', as in humanoid or asteroid. So a fucoid fossil is a mark or cast 'like' seaweed left in rock, which is why geologists call seaweed impressions by this name.
