Etymology

carpenter's plane comes from the simple tool a carpenter uses: a flat block with a sharp blade called a plane that is pushed along wood to shave off thin pieces. Those small shavings make the surface smooth or change the wood's shape, so the name means 'a hand tool for smoothing or shaping wood'.
