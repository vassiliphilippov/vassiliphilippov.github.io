Etymology

The name cathode-ray tube comes from a simple picture: inside a long tube a hot metal part called the cathode sends out tiny particles that travel like a ray to hit a glowing screen. That's why the old 'TVs and computer monitors' that made pictures this way are called cathode-ray tubes.
