Etymology

dysphonic comes from the Greek parts dys- meaning 'bad' or 'difficult' and phon- meaning 'sound' or 'voice', as in telephone. So something described as dysphonic makes a 'bad' or harsh sound, like a rough alarm or a broken engine.
