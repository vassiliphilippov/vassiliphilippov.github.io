Etymology

Capriciously comes from the word caprice, which means a 'sudden change' of feeling, and one popular idea links it to an old Latin word capra meaning 'goat'. So, think of a goat's sudden jumps: to act capriciously is to behave in an unpredictable, quickly changing way.
