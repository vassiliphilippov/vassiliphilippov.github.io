Etymology

carob tree gets its name from the small seeds inside its long, sweet pods; one popular story says people used these seeds as a small weight to measure gold and gems, and that weight name later became 'carat'. So the word points to the tree that makes those edible pods, which are still eaten and used as a 'chocolate substitute' today.
