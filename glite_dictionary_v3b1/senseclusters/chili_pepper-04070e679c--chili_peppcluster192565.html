Etymology

chili pepper joins two ideas: chili, a word from the people of Mexico for hot red or green pods, and pepper, the familiar spice Europeans knew. Because Europeans compared the sharp heat to black pepper, the combined name stuck, and today chili pepper means 'a small spicy pod used to add heat and flavor to food'.
