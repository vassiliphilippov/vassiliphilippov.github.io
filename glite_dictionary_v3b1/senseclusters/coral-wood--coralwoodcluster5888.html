Etymology

The word coral-wood breaks into the parts coral and wood and was named because the tree's bright red seeds and sometimes its timber look like pieces of 'coral'. So, the name helps you remember this East Indian ornamental tree is valued for its pretty flowers and for making small decorative objects.
