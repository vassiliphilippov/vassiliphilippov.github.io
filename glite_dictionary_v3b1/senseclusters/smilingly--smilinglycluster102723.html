Etymology

smilingly comes from the simple word smile ('show happiness with your mouth') plus -ing (making it an action) and -ly (making it describe how something is done), and so the parts put together make a word about the act of smiling; that's why smilingly means 'in a smiling way' or 'with a smile'.
