Etymology

Dis Pater comes from Latin words Dis, from a word meaning 'rich', and Pater, which means 'father', so the name gives the image of a 'rich father'. People thought the world under the earth held both the dead and hidden treasure, so Dis Pater became the 'rich father' who rules the world below and the souls of the dead.
