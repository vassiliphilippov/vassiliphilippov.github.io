Etymology

Nemean lion comes from an old Greek story about a huge lion from Nemea whose thick fur could not be cut by swords or arrows. Hercules killed it with his bare hands and wore its skin as a coat, so the name now means a 'very strong, almost impossible to beat' lion in stories.
