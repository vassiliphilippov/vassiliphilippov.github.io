Etymology

Ethanoyl radical comes from ethanoyl, formed from the word 'ethane' meaning 'two carbons' plus the ending 'oyl' for a part taken from an acid, and from radical, an old word for a 'small active piece' of a molecule. So it means the small CH3CO- part that comes from acetic acid, like vinegar, and can attach to other molecules.
