Etymology

checkroom starts with check, which here means 'to leave something with someone for safekeeping', and ends with room. A helpful way to remember it is to picture handing your coat to staff and getting a small ticket or 'claim check' to get it back. So a checkroom is simply a room where staff hold your coats or bags while you visit.
