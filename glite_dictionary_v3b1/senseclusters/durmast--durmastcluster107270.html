Etymology

The word durmast can be thought of as dur- (like in durable, meaning 'long-lasting' or 'hard') plus -mast (meaning 'acorns' or 'forest nuts'). So it pictures an oak with hard wood and heavy acorns, which is why durmast refers to a tough oak valued for strong, flexible wood.
