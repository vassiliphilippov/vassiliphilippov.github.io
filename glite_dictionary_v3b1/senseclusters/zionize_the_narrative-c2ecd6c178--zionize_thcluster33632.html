Etymology

Zionize the narrative comes from 'Zion', a name for Jerusalem and the idea of a Jewish homeland, and from the verb Zionize and the word narrative, which together mean to shape a story around that idea. So when someone Zionizes the narrative, they are telling a story with a filter that favors Zionist views, which is why it means 'presenting information to support Zionist ideas'.
