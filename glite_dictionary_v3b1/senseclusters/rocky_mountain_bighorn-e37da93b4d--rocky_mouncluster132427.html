Etymology

The name Rocky Mountain bighorn tells you where the animal lives and what it looks like. It comes from sheep living in the Rocky Mountains and from the word bighorn, because these sheep have very large, curled horns, so the name means 'a wild sheep with big curled horns that lives in rocky mountains'.
