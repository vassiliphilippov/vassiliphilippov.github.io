Etymology

nalidixic acid gets its name from the way chemists name new chemicals: the first part nalidixic comes from old chemical name parts and the second part acid shows it is a chemical. It was one of the first medicines of its kind, so the name now refers to a drug 'used to kill bacteria', especially for 'urinary tract' infections.
