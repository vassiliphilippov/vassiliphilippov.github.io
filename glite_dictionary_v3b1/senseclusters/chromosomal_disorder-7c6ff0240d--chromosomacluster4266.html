Etymology

chromosomal disorder gets its name from chromosome, a word born when scientists looked at cells and saw dark, colored 'bodies' inside them. A chromosomal disorder happens when those tiny 'bodies' are the wrong number or shape, so they cause problems in a person's growth or health.
