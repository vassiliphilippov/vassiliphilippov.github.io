Etymology

schwa comes from the Hebrew word shva, which means 'empty' or 'rest'. A helpful way to remember it is to think of the schwa as an 'empty' or very weak vowel, the quiet neutral sound you hear in unstressed parts of words such as the first and last vowels of 'banana', and that is why it sounds soft and unstressed.
