Etymology

coureur de bois comes from the French words coureur ('runner') and bois ('woods') and may come from the image of a person moving quickly through the forest to trap animals and trade furs. That's why it came to mean a French-Canadian woodsman or fur trapper who travelled and traded in the wild.
