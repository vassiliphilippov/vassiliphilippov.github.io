Etymology

Saratoga chip comes from Saratoga Springs, New York. One popular story says that in the 1850s a cook there thinly sliced potatoes and fried them until very crisp, and people began to call the thin fried slices Saratoga chips. So the name joins Saratoga (the town) and chip (a thin slice) and explains why it means a thin, fried 'potato chip'.
