Etymology

flushy cheek comes from the idea of a cheek suddenly filling with colour as blood rushes to the face when you are hot or shy. So, a flushy cheek means a cheek that goes 'red' or 'rosy' from 'embarrassment', 'exertion', or strong feeling.
