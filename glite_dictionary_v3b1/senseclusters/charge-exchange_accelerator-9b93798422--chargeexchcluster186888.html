Etymology

charge-exchange accelerator describes the machine's steps: a 'charged particle' swaps an electric charge in a charge-exchange, and then the accelerator pushes it to high speed so it can escape the hot gas. So, the name means a machine that makes charged particles move faster after they swap charge.
