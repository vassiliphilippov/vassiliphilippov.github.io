Etymology

Chinese fried rice comes from a simple kitchen idea: cooks in China used 'leftover' boiled rice and quickly stir-fried it in a very hot pan with eggs, scallions and small pieces of meat or shrimp. The name uses the three words Chinese, fried, rice, so it simply tells you what the dish is today: a quick stir-fried rice meal.
