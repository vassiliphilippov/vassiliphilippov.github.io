Etymology

scrawniness comes from the adjective scrawny plus the ending -ness, where scrawny comes from old dialect words meaning 'thin' and -ness means 'state of'. A helpful image is a 'scrawny' person like a thin twig, so scrawniness means being very thin or lacking flesh.
