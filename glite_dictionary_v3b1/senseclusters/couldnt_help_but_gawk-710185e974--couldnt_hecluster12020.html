Etymology

couldn't help but gawk paints a clear picture: you see something so surprising that you are frozen and cannot look away. Couldn't help shows you are 'unable to stop' and gawk means to stare openly in a rude or amazed way, so the whole phrase means 'unable to prevent yourself from staring'.
