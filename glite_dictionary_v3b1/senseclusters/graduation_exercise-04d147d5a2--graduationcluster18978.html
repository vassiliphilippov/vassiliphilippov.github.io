Etymology

graduation exercise may come from an older use of the word exercise for a public school event where students showed their work or skills. Imagine students standing before an audience to perform or read — that public 'exercise' became the formal ceremony when diplomas are given, so the phrase means the official 'ceremony' for graduates.
