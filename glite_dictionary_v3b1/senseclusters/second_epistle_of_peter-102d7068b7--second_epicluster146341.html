Etymology

The book Second Epistle of Peter gets its name from the words: epistle was an old word for a 'letter', and Second shows it was thought to be the second such letter linked to Peter. So the title simply means 'the second letter of Peter' and explains why the book is a short letter giving advice to early Christian groups.
