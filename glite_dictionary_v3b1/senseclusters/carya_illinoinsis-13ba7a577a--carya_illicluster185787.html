Etymology

Carya illinoinsis is the formal name for the pecan tree: Carya comes from an old Greek word for a nut and illinoinsis means 'from Illinois', where early writers noted the tree. So the name gives a simple picture—a nut tree 'from Illinois'—which explains why this tree is known for producing 'pecans'.
