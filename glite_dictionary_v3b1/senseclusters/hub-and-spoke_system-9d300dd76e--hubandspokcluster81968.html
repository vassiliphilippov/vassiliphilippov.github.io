Etymology

hub-and-spoke system comes from the picture of a wheel: the hub is the center and the spokes are the lines that go out from it. So in transport, small routes are like the spokes that 'connect' towns to one main airport, the hub, which is why the phrase means a system where many local routes feed a central point.
