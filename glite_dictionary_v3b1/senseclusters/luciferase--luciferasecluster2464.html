Etymology

The word luciferase comes from the Latin lucifer meaning 'light-bringer' and the enzyme ending -ase, like in lipase. So luciferase is an enzyme that 'brings light', which is why it makes fireflies glow and why scientists use it to make cells glow in experiments.
