sincerely yours

0.1/m2.1
A polite way to end a formal letter when you know the person's name
adverb US / sɪnˈsɪrli jʊrz /

Etymology

The closing sincerely yours comes from old letter traditions when writers used long phrases like 'truly yours' to show honest feeling. Over time people shortened those phrases to sincerely and yours, so today the closing politely shows the writer is being 'honest and respectful'.
