Etymology

The word utopian comes from utopia, the title used by the writer Thomas More for an imagined perfect island; the Greek parts ou- and topos mean 'no' and 'place', so utopia literally means 'no place'. That's why calling an idea utopian says it is a beautiful, perfect plan that probably does not exist in real life.
