Etymology

PhD comes from the Latin phrase Philosophiae Doctor, which means 'doctor of philosophy' and where Doctor originally meant 'teacher' and Philosophiae means 'love of wisdom'. That's why a PhD is a high university degree that shows someone is an expert after long study and research.
