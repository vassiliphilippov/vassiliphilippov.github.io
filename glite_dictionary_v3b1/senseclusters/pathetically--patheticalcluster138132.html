Etymology

pathetically comes from the Greek root pathos meaning 'feeling' or 'suffering', and the word pathetic once meant 'full of feeling'. A helpful way to remember the first sense is this: if something is so weak or sad that people feel scorn or pity, we call it 'pathetically', which is why the word can mean 'in a way that arouses scornful pity or contempt'.
