Etymology

Voltaic battery comes from the scientist Alessandro Volta, who made a simple stack of metal discs and wet cloth that produced 'electricity'. People called such devices voltaic, meaning 'of Volta', and battery, meaning many cells joined, so today voltaic battery means a group of cells that make 'electricity'.
