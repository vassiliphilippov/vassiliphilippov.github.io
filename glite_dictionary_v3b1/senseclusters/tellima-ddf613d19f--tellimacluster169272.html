Etymology

Tellima is the scientific name for a small woodland flower often called fringecup because its petals have 'fringed' edges. A helpful way to remember Tellima is to picture its delicate, nodding flowers in shady gardens, so the name brings to mind this hardy, fringe-edged plant.
