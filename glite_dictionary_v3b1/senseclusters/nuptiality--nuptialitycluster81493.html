Etymology

Nuptiality comes from the Latin word nuptia, meaning 'marriage', and it shares the same root as the English words nuptial and nuptials. The Latin root comes from nubere, a verb meaning 'to cover', like a bride's veil, so the word stayed tied to marriage — that is why nuptiality means how often people marry or the state of being married.
