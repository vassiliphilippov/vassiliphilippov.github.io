Etymology

snaffle bit may come from an old word meaning 'to snatch', creating the image of a small piece that 'takes hold' of the horse's mouth. Because a snaffle is a simple jointed bit with no curb, that picture helps explain why the name is used for a mild mouthpiece that gives the rider light control.
