Etymology

Bromoil process gets its name from the two main parts of the method: the original print used silver 'bromide', which gives the bromo part, and the final picture was made with oil-based 'ink', which gives the oil part. So the name refers to a printing method where the silver image is removed and an oil ink image is added, making a photograph that looks like a painting.
