Etymology

barrage balloon comes from wartime use: large balloons, held by cables and set in a line like a floating 'barrage' or barrier, made a clear picture of obstacles in the air. That's why a barrage balloon means a big balloon held by cables to block or scare away low-flying enemy planes.
