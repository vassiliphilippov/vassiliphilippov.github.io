Etymology

Bolivar comes from a Basque place name made of bolu ('mill') and ibar ('valley'), so it originally meant 'mill valley'. So Bolivar began as a family or place name and later became the famous name of Simón Bolívar, who helped free several South American countries and gave his name to many cities and monuments.
