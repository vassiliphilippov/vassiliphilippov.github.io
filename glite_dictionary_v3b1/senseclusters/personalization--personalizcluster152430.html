Etymology

The word personalization comes from personal, which comes from person meaning 'a human', plus the endings -ize and -ation meaning 'make' and 'the act of'. So personalization is literally 'making something for a person', which is why it means changing or choosing things to fit one person's likes, like adding your name or showing ads you prefer.
