Etymology

choc is a short, informal form of chocolate. The word chocolate comes from a Native American word xocolātl that means 'bitter water' because people first drank cocoa as a bitter drink. So calling something a choc simply names a small piece or sweet made from cocoa, keeping the idea of chocolate in a short word.
