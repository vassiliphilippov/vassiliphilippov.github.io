Etymology

proconvertin comes from the parts pro- ('before') and convert ('change' or 'turn into'), so it names something that is 'before' it is changed into an active form. That's why proconvertin is a protein made in the liver with the help of vitamin K that is changed into an active clotting factor to help blood stop bleeding.
