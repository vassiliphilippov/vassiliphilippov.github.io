Etymology

Nagari comes from the Sanskrit nagarī, from nagara meaning 'city'. So Nagari meant 'from the city' and became the name for the script used to write Sanskrit and Hindi. You can also see it in Devanagari, where deva means 'god' and nagari means 'city', the main script for Hindi.
