Etymology

The noun funeral march comes from two simple images: a march is music with a steady beat for people to walk to, and at a funeral people walked slowly in a 'funeral procession' behind the coffin. Because the music matched those slow, heavy steps, funeral march means 'a slow, solemn piece of music played for a funeral'.
