Etymology

snarkiness comes from snarky, which comes from snark, a made-up creature in a Lewis Carroll poem that felt small and nasty. That image helps: being 'snarky' is like making 'biting' or mocking remarks, so snarkiness is the quality of being sarcastic or sharply critical.
