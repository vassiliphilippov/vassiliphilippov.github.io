Etymology

Fomorian is a name from Irish myth; one popular idea is that it comes from old Irish parts fo- 'under' and muir 'sea'. That image of 'under-sea' giants makes it easy to remember why Fomorians are shown as big sea monsters and dangerous forces of nature.
