Etymology

Tweedy comes from the fabric tweed, a rough, warm wool cloth linked with Scotland's River Tweed, and one story says a seller misread the Scots word tweel, which means 'twill'. So tweedy means 'like tweed' and describes something rough, woolly, and warm.
