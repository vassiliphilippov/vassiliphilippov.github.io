Etymology

occupational hazard paints a simple picture: occupational means 'about a job' and hazard means 'danger', so imagine a worker surrounded by dangers that are just part of the work, like a chef getting burns or a miner breathing dust. That's why it means a 'risk' that is a normal part of a job.
