Etymology

Lilium michiganense comes from Latin for the plant and the place: Lilium is the Latin word for 'lily' and michiganense literally means 'from Michigan'. So the name points to a lily that grows in Michigan and nearby parts of North America and helps you remember its bright orange-red, spotted flowers.
