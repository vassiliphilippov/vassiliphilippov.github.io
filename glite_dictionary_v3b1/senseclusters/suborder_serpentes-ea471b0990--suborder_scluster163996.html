Etymology

suborder Serpentes gets its name from Latin: Serpentes comes from the word 'serpens', meaning 'a snake' or 'to crawl', and suborder means a smaller 'group' inside a larger one. So the name literally points to the group of animals that are snakes, which is why it means 'the group of all snakes'.
