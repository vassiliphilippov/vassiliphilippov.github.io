Etymology

The plant name Quamoclit pennata may come from an old native word for the vine and the Latin word pennata, meaning 'feathered'. The name points to the vine's very fine, feather-like leaves and the way it climbs on trellises, so you can remember it as the 'feathered' climbing plant with bright red flowers.
