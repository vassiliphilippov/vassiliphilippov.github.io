Etymology

pot-girl is made of the simple parts pot ('cooking pot') and girl ('young woman' or 'female servant'), so picture a woman washing and carrying pots in an inn. That's why pot-girl means a female worker in a pub or inn who serves customers and does chores like cleaning pots.
