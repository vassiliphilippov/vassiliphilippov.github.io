Etymology

space biology joins space and biology, so picture scientists in a spacecraft watching plants, tiny organisms and astronauts float and grow differently without normal gravity. Because it is literally biology done in space, it means 'the study of how living things are affected by outer space and the search for life beyond Earth'.
