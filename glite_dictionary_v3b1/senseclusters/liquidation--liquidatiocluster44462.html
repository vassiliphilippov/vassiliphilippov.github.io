Etymology

Liquidation comes from the Latin word liquidare, which meant 'to make liquid' and then 'to settle' or 'to pay' a debt. From the idea of 'settling' or 'clearing something away', people began using it for 'getting rid of' someone, so it can mean killing or eliminating people.
