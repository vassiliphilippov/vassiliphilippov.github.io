Etymology

Pseudococcus comstocki paints a picture: pseudo means 'false' and coccus means 'berry', so the name suggests a tiny, round 'false berry' on plants, and 'comstocki' honors the scientist Comstock who first found it, which is why the name refers to this small insect that can damage citrus and apple trees.
