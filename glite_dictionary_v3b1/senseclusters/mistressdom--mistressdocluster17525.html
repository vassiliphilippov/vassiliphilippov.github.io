Etymology

The word mistressdom comes from mistress (the female form of master) plus the ending -dom, which means 'state' or 'domain', as in kingdom. So, mistressdom is the 'state' or 'rule' of a mistress, which explains its modern meaning of the power or area a woman is in charge of.
