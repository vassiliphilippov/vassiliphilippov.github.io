Etymology

antiberiberi factor comes from early nutrition work when scientists found a substance that stopped the disease beriberi in people who ate mostly white rice. The name shows its job: anti means 'against' and beriberi is the disease, so the phrase names the vitamin that 'prevents' beriberi, and today we call that vitamin thiamine or vitamin B1.
