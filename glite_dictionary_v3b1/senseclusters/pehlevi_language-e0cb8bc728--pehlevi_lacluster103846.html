Etymology

Pehlevi language gets its name from an old group of people called the Parthians, and the word Pehlevi was used for their speech and writing. Because priests kept using that speech in Zoroastrian books for centuries, 'Pehlevi' came to mean the 'Middle Persian' language of those sacred texts.
