Etymology

bronchogenic breaks into broncho- meaning 'main air passage' or 'windpipe' and -genic meaning 'produced by' or 'coming from', like in generate. So bronchogenic describes something that 'comes from' the bronchi, which is why doctors use it for growths or cysts that begin in those lung air passages.
