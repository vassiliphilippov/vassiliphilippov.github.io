Etymology

Rh-negative blood gets its name from tests on a kind of monkey called the rhesus monkey, where doctors found a small marker on red blood cells. Rh is that marker and negative means it is not there, so the term means 'blood that does not have the Rh marker' and this matters for pregnancy and blood transfusions.
