Etymology

Kazakh language comes from the name of the people, Kazakh, a word that may come from an old word meaning 'free man' or 'wanderer', and language meaning their speech. Imagine nomads on the wide grassland talking to one another — that picture explains why Kazakh language means 'the language spoken by the Kazakh people'.
