Etymology

Chrysophrys auratus may come from old words where chryso- means 'gold' and auratus means 'golden', because the fish often has a golden band near its eye and a shiny, pinkish body with blue spots. So the scientific name points to the fish's golden look and helps you remember it is the common Australian snapper.
