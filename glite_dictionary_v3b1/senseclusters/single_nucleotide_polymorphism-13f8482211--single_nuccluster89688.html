Etymology

single nucleotide polymorphism is often explained with a simple picture: think of DNA as a long sentence and a nucleotide as a single letter, so a single letter change makes a different 'form', which is what polymorphism means. So, the term refers to 'a one-letter change in DNA' that makes a small genetic difference between people.
