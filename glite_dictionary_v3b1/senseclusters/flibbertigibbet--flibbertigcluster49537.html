Etymology

The word flibbertigibbet began as a playful name for a trouble-making spirit in old stories, and its jumpy, repeating sound makes you think of 'quick talk'. That's why today a flibbertigibbet means a flighty, overly talkative person, since the word itself echoes 'silly speech' like gibberish.
