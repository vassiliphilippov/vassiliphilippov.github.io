Etymology

Catch-22 comes from Joseph Heller's novel, where a military rule called Catch-22 trapped pilots: if you asked to be excused from dangerous missions you were seen as sane, so you could not be excused. So Catch-22 now means a 'no-win' situation or a problem you cannot escape because different rules stop you.
