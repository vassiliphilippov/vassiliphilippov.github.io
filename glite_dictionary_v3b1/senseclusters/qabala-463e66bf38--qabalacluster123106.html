Etymology

The word Qabala is an alternate spelling of Kabbalah from a Hebrew word meaning 'to receive' or 'tradition'. It refers to secret or hidden spiritual teachings that are thought of as 'received' wisdom. A helpful way to remember it is the English word cabal, a common word for a secret group, which comes from the same idea of hidden knowledge.
