Etymology

superfamily Tyrannidae is a scientific name made of two parts: superfamily means a very large group of related animals, and Tyrannidae comes from a word meaning 'tyrant' because these flycatcher birds often behave boldly and bossily. So, the name points to a very large group of related 'tyrant' flycatchers, many bold little birds mostly in Central and South America.
