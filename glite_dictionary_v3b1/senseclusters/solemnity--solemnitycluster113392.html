Etymology

The word solemnity comes from the old Latin word sollemnis, a word for formal religious ceremonies and the source of English solemn. A helpful way to remember it is that sollemnis may be linked to sol 'sun', because many old ceremonies followed the sun's yearly cycle, so solemnity means a serious, formal, and dignified feeling.
