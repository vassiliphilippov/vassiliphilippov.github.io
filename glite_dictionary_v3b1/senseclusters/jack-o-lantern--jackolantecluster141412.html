Etymology

The word jack-o-lantern comes from an old phrase made of jack, o' and lantern, where o' means 'of' and the phrase once meant 'a man with a lantern'. Because this mushroom glows at night and has an orange cap, people thought it looked like a small lantern, so they called it a jack-o-lantern.
