Etymology

The word self-terminate is made from self- meaning 'by oneself' and terminate meaning 'to end', the same part you see in terminal, a place at the 'end' of a journey. So, self-terminate means to end something yourself or for a machine to stop itself, and it is sometimes used as a polite way to say ending one's own life.
