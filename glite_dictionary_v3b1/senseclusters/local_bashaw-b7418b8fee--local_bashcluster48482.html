Etymology

Local bashaw comes from the Turkish title bashaw, related to the word 'pasha' for a powerful leader, and was used by foreign rulers to name officials put in charge of a town. So a local bashaw gives the picture of a local boss with strong, often strict, power and means 'a local ruler'.
