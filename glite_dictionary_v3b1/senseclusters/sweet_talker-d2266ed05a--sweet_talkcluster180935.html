Etymology

The noun sweet talker comes from a simple image: a person uses sweet words like candy—the talker makes their speech taste pleasant to others. One popular idea is that this 'sweet' speech hides real aims, so the phrase means 'someone who flatters or tries to persuade with not honest talk'.
