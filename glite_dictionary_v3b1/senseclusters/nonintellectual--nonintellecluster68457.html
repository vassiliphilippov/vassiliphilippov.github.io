Etymology

nonintellectual starts with non- meaning 'not' and the rest comes from intellect, the same root you see in intelligent, which means 'understanding' or 'thinking'. So nonintellectual means 'not about thinking or ideas', used for things that focus more on action or feeling than on deep thought.
