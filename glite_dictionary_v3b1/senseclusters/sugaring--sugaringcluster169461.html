Etymology

sugaring comes from the word sugar, from an old Indian word sharkara meaning 'sand' because sugar was first known as tiny crystals. In places that make maple syrup, sugaring is the seasonal process of collecting tree sap and turning it into sugar or syrup, so the name points to making sugar from the sap.
