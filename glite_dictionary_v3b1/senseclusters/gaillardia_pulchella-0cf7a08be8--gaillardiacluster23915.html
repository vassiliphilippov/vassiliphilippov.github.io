Etymology

Gaillardia pulchella has two parts: Gaillardia honors a French plant scientist, and pulchella comes from Latin meaning 'pretty little'. The bright red-and-yellow flower heads look like a small colorful blanket or wheel, so the name helps you remember it is a 'pretty little' flower often called an 'Indian blanket' or 'firewheel'.
