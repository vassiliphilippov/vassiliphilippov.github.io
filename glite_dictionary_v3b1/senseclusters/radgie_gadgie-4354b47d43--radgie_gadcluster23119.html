Etymology

The noun radgie gadgie may come from two older words: radgie, a word for someone full of 'rage' or quick anger, and gadgie, a word for a 'man' in northern speech. Together the picture is of a man who is angry and causes trouble, so radgie gadgie now means 'an aggressive or rowdy person'.
