Etymology

The noun arteria circumflexa ilium comes from Latin and literally combines arteria 'artery', circumflexa 'bends around', and ilium 'hip bone'. Because this artery bends around the hip bone, the name fits the blood vessel that gives blood to the lower belly and nearby muscle.
