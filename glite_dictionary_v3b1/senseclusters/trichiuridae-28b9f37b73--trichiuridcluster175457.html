Etymology

Trichiuridae comes from Greek parts trich- 'hair' and -oura 'tail', so it says 'hair-tail'. That image fits the cutlassfishes, which have very long, ribbon-like bodies, and the ending -idae 'family' shows this name is for a group of similar fish.
