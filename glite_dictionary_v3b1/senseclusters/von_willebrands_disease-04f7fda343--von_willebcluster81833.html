Etymology

von Willebrand's disease is named after a Finnish doctor, Erik von Willebrand, who in the early 1900s described a family on a small island where many people bled easily. Because he showed this was a particular bleeding problem, the name now means a 'bleeding disorder' that causes easy bruising and nosebleeds.
