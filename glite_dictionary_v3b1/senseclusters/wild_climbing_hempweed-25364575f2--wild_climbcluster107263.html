Etymology

The noun wild climbing hempweed names a wild plant that really climbs up tree trunks like a 'vine' and has small flowers that smell of 'vanilla'. So the parts wild, climbing, and hempweed help you picture a wild, climbing herb with a sweet, vanilla scent.
