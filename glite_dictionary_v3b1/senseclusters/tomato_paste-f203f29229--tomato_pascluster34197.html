Etymology

Tomato paste comes from the simple idea of cooking ripe tomato until it becomes a thick paste. Cooks cook and strain tomatoes to make a sticky, rich paste, so today tomato paste means a 'concentrated' tomato product used to add strong flavor to dishes.
