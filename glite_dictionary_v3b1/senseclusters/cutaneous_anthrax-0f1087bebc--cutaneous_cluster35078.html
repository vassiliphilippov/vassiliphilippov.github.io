Etymology

The medical name cutaneous anthrax comes from two words: cutaneous, meaning 'skin', and anthrax, meaning 'charcoal', because the infection often makes a dark, charcoal-like sore on the skin. So, the name refers to the form of anthrax that affects the skin.
