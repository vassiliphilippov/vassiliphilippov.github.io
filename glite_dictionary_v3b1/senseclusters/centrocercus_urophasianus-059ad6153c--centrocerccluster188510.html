Etymology

The name Centrocercus urophasianus comes from old Greek words that describe its tail: centrocercus points to a spurred or fan-like tail and urophasianus links 'tail' with 'pheasant'. So the name creates a clear picture of the bird's big, showy tail and helps you remember that it is the greater sage-grouse.
