Etymology

impenetrable reasoning compares an argument to a very thick wall: impenetrable means you cannot get through it and reasoning means the ideas behind an argument. So, if you cannot get through the wall, you cannot reach the ideas, and the phrase means 'reasoning that is very hard or impossible to understand'.
