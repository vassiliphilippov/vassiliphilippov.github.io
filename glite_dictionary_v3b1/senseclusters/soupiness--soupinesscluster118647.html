Etymology

soupiness in the emotional sense comes from the same image of things that are soft and wet like soup, and a helpful way to remember it is to think of a story that feels 'soppy' or 'soupy' and so seems overly soft. So, when critics say a book or film has soupiness, they mean it has too much, not very sincere feeling.
