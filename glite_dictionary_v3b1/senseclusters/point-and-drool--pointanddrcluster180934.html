Etymology

point-and-drool comes from two simple actions, point ('to show with your finger') and drool ('to let saliva fall when you really want something'), creating the image of someone pointing at an object while clearly wanting it. So, to point-and-drool is an informal way to say you show excessive admiration or desire for something.
