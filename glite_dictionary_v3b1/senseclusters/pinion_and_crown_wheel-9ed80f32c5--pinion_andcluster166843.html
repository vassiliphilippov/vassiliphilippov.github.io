Etymology

Pinion and crown wheel are two gears that work together: the small gear, the pinion, fits its teeth into the edge of the larger ring gear, the crown wheel, whose teeth look a little like a crown. When the pinion turns and pushes the crown wheel, it makes the motion 'change direction', so the name refers to the gear pair that turns movement at right angles in things like a car's differential.
