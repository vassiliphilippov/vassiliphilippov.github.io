Etymology

anti-inflammatory is made of anti- meaning 'against' and inflammation, which comes from a root meaning 'to set on fire', so it pictures fighting a small 'fire' in the body. That's why an anti-inflammatory is a medicine that reduces swelling, heat, and pain.
