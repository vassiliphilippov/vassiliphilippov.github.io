Etymology

Plicatoperipatus jamaicensis is a scientific name for a kind of velvet worm. The first part, Plicatoperipatus, names the group of similar worms and the second part, jamaicensis, means 'from Jamaica', so the name tells you it is a velvet worm found in Jamaica.
