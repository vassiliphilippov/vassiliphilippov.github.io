Etymology

bullsheet response comes from the idea of bull (from 'bullshit', a rude word for 'nonsense') and sheet as a cover, so picture someone hiding facts under a sheet of nonsense. That's why a bullsheet response means an answer that is 'nonsense' or meant to 'hide the truth'.
