Etymology

hexanoic acid comes from the part hex meaning 'six' because the molecule has six carbon atoms, and from acid, which tells you it is an acid. It was also called 'caproic acid' from a word for 'goat' because it smells like goats, so the name links both its six-carbon structure and its strong, goat-like odor.
