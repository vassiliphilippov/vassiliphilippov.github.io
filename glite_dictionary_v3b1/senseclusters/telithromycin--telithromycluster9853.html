Etymology

The word telithromycin is built like many antibiotic names: the ending -mycin is the same as in common medicines such as erythromycin and azithromycin, so it shows this drug is an antibiotic related to that family. The first part, telithro-, is a lab-made element to show a changed or 'new version' of those drugs, so a helpful way to remember the name is to think of it as a modified antibiotic used to treat certain infections.
