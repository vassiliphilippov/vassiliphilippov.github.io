Etymology

Rhode Island bent may come from one simple idea: the place Rhode Island plus the old word bent, which meant a kind of 'grass'. Imagine a fine, thin grass from Rhode Island that bends easily under your foot, and that image explains why the name now means this lawn and golf-course grass.
