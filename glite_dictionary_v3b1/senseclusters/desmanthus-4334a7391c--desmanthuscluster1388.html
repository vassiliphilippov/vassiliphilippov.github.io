Etymology

The word Desmanthus comes from Greek parts desma and anthos, where desma means 'bundle' and anthos means 'flower'. You can see the same anthos in flower names like chrysanthemum, and that helps: Desmanthus is a plant name that points to its small, grouped flowers.
