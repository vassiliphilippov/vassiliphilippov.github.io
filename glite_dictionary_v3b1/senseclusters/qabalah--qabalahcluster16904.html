Etymology

qabalah comes from the Hebrew word qabbalah, which means 'reception' or 'what is received' and comes from a root meaning 'to receive', like the English word 'receive'. It names a set of mystical teachings that were said to be 'received' from earlier teachers and kept secret, so qabalah now means a secret or mystical tradition.
