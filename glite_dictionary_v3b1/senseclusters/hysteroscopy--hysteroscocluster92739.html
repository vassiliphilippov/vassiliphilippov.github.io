Etymology

The word hysteroscopy comes from two Greek parts: hystero- meaning 'uterus' and -scopy meaning 'to look', just like in microscope or telescope. So a hysteroscopy is 'looking into the uterus' with a small scope, which explains why it is a medical exam of the inside of the uterus.
