Etymology

decumary is a botanical name likely from Latin, and a helpful way to remember it is to link the start decu- to decem, which means 'ten', so you can imagine a small group of white flowers gathered like a ten-point cluster. That's why decumary fits a climbing plant known for its many white blooms in tight clusters.
