Etymology

hagiographical comes from the Greek parts hagio- meaning 'holy' or 'saint' and -graphical meaning 'writing', the same idea as biography ('life writing'). Originally it meant 'writing about saints', so calling a modern story hagiographical often means it praises someone like a saint and ignores their faults.
