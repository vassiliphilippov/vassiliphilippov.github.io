Etymology

sacrilegiousness comes from Latin parts sacer and legere, where sacer means 'sacred' and legere can be remembered as 'to take' or 'to steal'. So sacrilegiousness is the quality of treating things people call 'sacred' with no respect, as if you could take or damage them.
