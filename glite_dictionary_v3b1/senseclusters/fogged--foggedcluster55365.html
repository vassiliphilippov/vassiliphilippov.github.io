Etymology

fogged comes from the word fog, which means 'a mist or low cloud' and you can also see the same base in foggy. Think of your breath on a mirror or a cold window: tiny drops make the glass cloudy and 'fogged', so you cannot see through it. So, fogged means 'made difficult to see through'.
