Etymology

Calluna vulgaris may come from a Greek word meaning 'to beautify' and the Latin word vulgaris means 'common', so the name creates the picture of a 'common beautifier' that covers hills with pink or purple flowers and explains why it refers to the low, widely found shrub called heather.
