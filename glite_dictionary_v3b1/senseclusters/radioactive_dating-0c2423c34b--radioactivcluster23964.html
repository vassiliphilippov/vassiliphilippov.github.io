Etymology

radioactive dating comes from using a material's natural radioactivity as a tiny, steady 'clock' inside objects. Scientists measure how much of a radioactive atom like carbon-14 has broken down to work out the 'age' of bones or rocks, so the phrase means 'finding how old something is'.
