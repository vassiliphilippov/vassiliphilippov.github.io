Etymology

The title resident commissioner comes from old government practice: a resident is an official who lives in a territory, and a commissioner is someone sent to 'represent' that territory in the central government. This image of someone who lives at home but is sent to speak for their place in the capital helps explain why the role represents Puerto Rico in Congress but is 'non-voting'.
