Etymology

Stellaria holostea may come from old words that describe the plant: Stellaria means 'star' because of its small white star-shaped flowers, and holostea may mean 'whole' or 'unbroken', perhaps pointing to its long, thin stems. So the name helps you picture the plant as a low-growing plant with white star flowers and slender stems.
