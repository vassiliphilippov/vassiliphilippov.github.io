Etymology

nuclear warhead literally names the part of a missile or bomb that holds a nuclear explosive. The warhead is the 'head' or front piece that carries the bomb, so imagine the front of a missile carrying a very small but hugely powerful bomb that will explode — that image explains why the phrase means a weapon piece meant to cause a nuclear explosion.
