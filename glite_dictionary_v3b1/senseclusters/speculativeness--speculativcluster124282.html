Etymology

speculativeness comes from the Latin root specere meaning 'to look', and long ago it grew to mean watching for chances. A helpful way to remember the financial sense is to imagine people 'looking for' opportunities to make money, so speculativeness describes risky or uncertain investments based on such guesses.
