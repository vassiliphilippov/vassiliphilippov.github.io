Etymology

Dracaena draco gets its name from words for 'dragon' — both Dracaena and draco point to dragons because the tree gives a dark red sap called 'dragon's blood' and its thick, spreading branches look dramatic like a dragon. So the name explains why people call it the 'dragon tree' and why its red sap was long valued.
