Etymology

carbon-14 dating comes from a method that uses a rare kind of carbon called carbon-14 which slowly disappears from a plant or animal after it dies. Scientists measure how much carbon-14 is left to estimate the object's 'age', so the name links the carbon type with the idea of 'dating' something to find how old it is.
