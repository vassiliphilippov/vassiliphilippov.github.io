Etymology

President Johnson refers to Andrew Johnson, the vice president who suddenly became president after Abraham Lincoln was killed. Imagine a vice president stepping up to the top job and then facing a trial in Congress—he was 'impeached' but kept the office by 'one vote', so the name is remembered for that dramatic change and trial.
