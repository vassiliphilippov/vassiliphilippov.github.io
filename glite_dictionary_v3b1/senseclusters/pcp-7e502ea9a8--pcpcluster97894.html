Etymology

PCP is short for phencyclidine, the full chemical name for a powerful lab-made drug. The name is built from parts like phen- ('ring') and cycl- ('circle'), which is a helpful image to remember that it is a man-made chemical with ring shapes, and that's why PCP refers to a strong anesthetic that can cause severe hallucinations when misused.
