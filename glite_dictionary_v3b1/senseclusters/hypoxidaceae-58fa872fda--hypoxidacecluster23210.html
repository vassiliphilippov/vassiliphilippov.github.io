Etymology

Hypoxidaceae comes from the plant name Hypoxis plus the family ending -aceae; the parts hypo- and oxis come from Greek meaning 'under' and often thought to mean 'sharp', so a helpful image is the small, pointed, star-like flowers of these plants. So, Hypoxidaceae simply means the plant 'family' made of Hypoxis-like species.
