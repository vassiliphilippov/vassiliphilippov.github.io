Etymology

The noun egg foo yong comes from a Chinese name 'fu yung' that means 'hibiscus' or 'lotus' and points to the egg's fluffy, flower-like look. So the name came to mean the Chinese-style omelette with eggs, vegetables and meat, often served with a brown sauce.
