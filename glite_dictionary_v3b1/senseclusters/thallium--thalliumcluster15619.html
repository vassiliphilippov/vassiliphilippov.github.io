Etymology

The word thallium comes from the Greek root thallos, meaning 'green shoot', because when scientists looked at the light from this element they saw a bright green line, so the metal was named for that green mark. So, remember thallium as a soft, grey but very toxic metal that is used in some electronics and was once used in poisons.
