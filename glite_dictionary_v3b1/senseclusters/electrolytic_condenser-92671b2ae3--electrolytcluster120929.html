Etymology

electrolytic condenser is named for how it is made: electrolytic means there is a liquid between metal plates, and condenser is an old word for a device that 'stores' electric charge. So, the name points to a fixed circuit part that uses a liquid to help hold electricity and must be put with the correct positive and negative sides.
