Etymology

The plant name Yucca filamentosa points to how the plant looks: Yucca is the plant group and filamentosa comes from a word meaning 'many threads', because its long, stiff leaves have thin white threads along their edges. That's why it is also called 'Adam's needle' and why the name reminds you of the plant's needle-like leaves and thread-like edges.
