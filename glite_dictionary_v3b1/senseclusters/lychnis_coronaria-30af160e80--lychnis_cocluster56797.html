Etymology

Lychnis coronaria comes from old Greek and Latin words: Lychnis from a Greek word for a 'lamp' and coronaria from Latin for a 'crown' or garland. One popular story says people used the bright flowers to make garlands or to decorate lamps, so the name gives a clear image of its bright, crown-like blooms and helps you remember the plant.
