Etymology

Polygonum aubertii gets its name from old Greek parts: poly- meaning 'many' and -gonum meaning 'knee', because the plant's stems have many jointed spots that look like knees. The second word, aubertii, is the name of a person called Aubert, so the full name points to a 'many-jointed' climbing plant linked to that collector.
