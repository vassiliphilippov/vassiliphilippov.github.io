Etymology

Zoysia matrella comes from the way scientists give plants two-part Latin names: the first part, Zoysia, likely honors a person named Zois, and the second part, matrella, is a Latin-style word that marks this particular grass. So, when people say Zoysia matrella, they mean 'a specific warm-season lawn grass' used in hot climates.
