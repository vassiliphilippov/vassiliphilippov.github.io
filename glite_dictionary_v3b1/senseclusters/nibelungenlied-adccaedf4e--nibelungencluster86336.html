Etymology

Nibelungenlied means 'song of the Nibelungs'. The name Nibelung comes from Nebel meaning 'mist' and -lied means 'song', so you can think of it as the 'song of the people of the mist', which helps you remember it is an old epic poem about mysterious clans, treasure and tragic events.
