Etymology

at Her Majesty's pleasure comes from a time when the queen or king could decide how long someone stayed in a job or in prison, and being Her Majesty'spleasure meant you stayed as long as the ruler wanted, not for a fixed time. So today it means being kept 'for an indefinite time' or 'until the authorities decide'.
