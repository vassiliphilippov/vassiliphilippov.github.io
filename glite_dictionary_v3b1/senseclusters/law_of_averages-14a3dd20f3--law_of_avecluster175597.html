Etymology

law of averages may come from old gamblers and people who counted many results and saw that, after many tries, the numbers tended to move toward the usual amount. People treated that idea like a rule (a law) about the averages, so law of averages means believing that, over many tries, things will 'even out' or 'match the average'.
