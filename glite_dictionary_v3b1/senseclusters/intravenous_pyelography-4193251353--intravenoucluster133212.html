Etymology

intravenous pyelography breaks into parts: intravenous means 'into a vein', pyelo refers to the kidney's collecting area, and -graphy means 'taking pictures'. In the test, a dye is put into a 'vein' and goes to the 'kidneys', so X-rays take pictures of the 'urinary tract', which is why the name means 'an X-ray test of the urinary system'.
