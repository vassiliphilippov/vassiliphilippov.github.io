Etymology

billy-ho may come from a loud shout or call, like someone saying 'Billy, ho!' to drive animals or give a big push, so it carries the idea of great force or amount. So when you say someone did something 'like billy-ho' you mean they did it with a lot of energy or 'to a great degree'.
