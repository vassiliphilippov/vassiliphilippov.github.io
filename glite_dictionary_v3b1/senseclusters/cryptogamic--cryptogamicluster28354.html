Etymology

cryptogamic comes from Greek parts crypto- meaning 'hidden' and -gamic from a word for 'marriage', as in monogamy. It helps you remember that cryptogamic plants like ferns, mosses, algae and fungi have a 'hidden' way of reproducing by spores, without obvious flowers or seeds.
