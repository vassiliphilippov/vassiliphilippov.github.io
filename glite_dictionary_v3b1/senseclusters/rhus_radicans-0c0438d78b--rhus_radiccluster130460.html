Etymology

Rhus radicans comes from old Latin words: Rhus names the sumac group and radicans means 'rooting' or 'climbing', because the plant often spreads and climbs on trees. Because this climbing plant has an oily sap that makes skin 'itchy' and causes a 'rash', the name refers to the species known today for causing skin irritation.
