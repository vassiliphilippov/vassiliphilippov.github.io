Etymology

soulfully comes from the word soul meaning 'inner self' or 'deep feeling', plus the ending -ful meaning 'full of' and -ly which makes it describe how something is done. So to do something soulfully is to do it 'full of deep feeling', like singing or looking with real emotion.
