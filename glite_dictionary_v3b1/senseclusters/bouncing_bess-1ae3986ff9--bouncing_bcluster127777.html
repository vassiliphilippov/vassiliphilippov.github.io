Etymology

bouncing Bess may come from the bright flowers that seem to bounce in the wind and from Bess, a common short name for Elizabeth given to plants long ago. One popular story says people used this lively plant as a natural 'soap' to wash delicate clothes, so the name stuck to the soap-producing herb we now call soapwort.
