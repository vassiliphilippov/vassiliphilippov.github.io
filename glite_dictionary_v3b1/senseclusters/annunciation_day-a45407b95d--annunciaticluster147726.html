Etymology

Annunciation Day comes from the Bible story when the angel Gabriel told Mary she would become pregnant and bear Jesus on March 25, so people held a special church festival for that 'announcement'. Because the date was also used in old England and Ireland as a day to pay rents and settle accounts, the name now refers to the church celebration of that message.
