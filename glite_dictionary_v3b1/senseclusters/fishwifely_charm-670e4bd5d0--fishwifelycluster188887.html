Etymology

fishwifely charm may come from market fishwives, women who sold fish and spoke in a loud, plain way while also being warm and friendly to customers. So the picture of a rough but caring seller helps explain why the phrase means a 'warm, down-to-earth' and 'welcoming' manner.
