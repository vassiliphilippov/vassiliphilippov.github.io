Etymology

cardiac glucoside comes from its two parts: cardiac means 'heart' and glucoside names a plant compound that contains sugar. People found these sugar-containing plant chemicals in foxglove could make the heart beat stronger, so the name means a plant chemical used to help the 'heart'.
