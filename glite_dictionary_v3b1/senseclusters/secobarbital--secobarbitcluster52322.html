Etymology

secobarbital comes from the parts seco- and barbital. The barbital part names the family of 'barbiturate' drugs used as 'sedatives', and seco- marks it as a special 'secondary' kind. So, secobarbital is a particular barbiturate used to calm people and to treat convulsions.
