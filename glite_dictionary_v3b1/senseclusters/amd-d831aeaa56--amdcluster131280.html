Etymology

AMD stands for age-related macular degeneration, and the name tells you what it is: age-related means it happens as people get older, macular and degeneration point to the part and the change. Macular comes from a word meaning 'spot' and degeneration means 'getting worse', so AMD is when the important 'spot' in the centre of the eye breaks down with age, causing loss of central vision.
