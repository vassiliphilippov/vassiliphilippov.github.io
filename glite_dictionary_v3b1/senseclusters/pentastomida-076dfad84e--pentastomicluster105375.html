Etymology

Pentastomida comes from Greek parts penta ('five') and stoma ('mouth'). It refers to these parasites, often called 'tongue worms', which have five mouth-like openings, so a helpful way to remember is to picture small worm-like animals with 'five mouths' living in the lungs and breathing tubes of animals.
