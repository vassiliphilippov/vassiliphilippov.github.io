Etymology

Double saucepan comes from the simple image of double meaning two and a saucepan sitting inside another: the inner pan holds the food while the outer pan holds hot water. This set lets you 'heat gently' so things like chocolate or sauce do not burn, which is why the name means a tool for gentle cooking.
