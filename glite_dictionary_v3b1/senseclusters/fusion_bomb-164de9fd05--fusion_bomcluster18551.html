Etymology

fusion bomb names what the weapon does: fusion means 'joining' and a bomb makes a 'big explosion'. When tiny hydrogen atoms are forced to 'join', that joining releases a huge amount of energy, so the phrase means a 'very powerful' nuclear weapon.
