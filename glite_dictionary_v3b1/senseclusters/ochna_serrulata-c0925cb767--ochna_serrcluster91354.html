Etymology

Ochna serrulata is often called the 'Mickey Mouse plant' because after its yellow flowers it makes bright red sepals that hold shiny black seeds, and the red-and-black look makes a tiny 'face'. That's why the name helps you remember the shrub's bright flowers and red-and-black fruit.
