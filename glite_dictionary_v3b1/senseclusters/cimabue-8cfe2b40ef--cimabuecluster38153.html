Etymology

Cimabue is a nickname that probably comes from the Italian parts cima ('top') and bue ('ox' or 'bull'), so you can imagine a 'bull's head' as a strong, leading figure. That's a helpful image to remember him as an early painter who pushed art away from stiff, formal pictures toward more natural ones.
