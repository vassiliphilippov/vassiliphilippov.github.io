Etymology

ecumenism comes from the Greek word oikoumenē, which meant 'the inhabited world', and is linked to the Greek root oikos for 'house', a root you see in words like ecology and economy. So ecumenism is about trying to bring churches or religious groups together across the whole 'world', promoting unity and cooperation.
