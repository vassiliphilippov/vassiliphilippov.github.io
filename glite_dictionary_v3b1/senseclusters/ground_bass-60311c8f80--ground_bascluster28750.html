Etymology

ground bass comes from the picture of a low, repeating tune that works like the ground under a house. The word bass points to the low voice and ground means 'foundation', so the repeating low line supports the changing melodies above it and that's why it means 'a short phrase or melody in the bass that repeats while the upper parts vary'.
