Etymology

heterometaboly comes from Greek parts hetero- meaning 'different' and metaboly meaning 'change', and the metaboly part is like the familiar word metamorphosis ('big change'). So, heterometaboly describes a 'different change' in insect growth where the young look like small adults and there is no pupal stage.
