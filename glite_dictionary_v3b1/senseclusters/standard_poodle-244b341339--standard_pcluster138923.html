Etymology

The name standard poodle comes from two simple ideas: poodle may come from an old German word meaning 'to splash', because these dogs often worked in water, and standard was used for the 'full-size' or original working dog. So, standard poodle means the larger, original poodle breed, known today for being smart and elegant.
