Etymology

gauze-like starts with gauze, a very thin cloth often used for bandages, and the ending -like means 'similar'. Gauze likely comes from the city of Gaza or an old Arabic word for 'silk', so gauze-like means 'like that thin, nearly see-through cloth'.
