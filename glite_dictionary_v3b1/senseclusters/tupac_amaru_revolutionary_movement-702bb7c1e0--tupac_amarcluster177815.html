Etymology

Tupac Amaru Revolutionary Movement was named after a native leader long ago, Tupac Amaru, who led a brave fight against Spanish rulers. By using his name and the words Revolutionary Movement, the group showed they wanted to continue that fight, so the name tells you they were a 'rebel' or 'revolutionary' group.
