Etymology

The word vestrywoman combines vestry, meaning the church room for priests' clothes, and woman. Vestry comes from the old Latin vestis, meaning 'garment', the same root as English vest, and because the church council met in that room, a vestrywoman is a woman who is a member of that church council.
