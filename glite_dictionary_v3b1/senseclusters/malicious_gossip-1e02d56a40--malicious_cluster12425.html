Etymology

malicious gossip combines two simple ideas: gossip means 'talk' about other people's private life, and malicious means 'meant to hurt'. Imagine a secret whispered that spreads like a stain and harms someone's 'reputation', so malicious gossip means talk that tries to damage a person's good name.
