Etymology

The noun deposit account comes from the simple idea of putting money into the bank and keeping it safe as a record. To deposit means 'to put in' and an account is the bank's 'record' of your money. So a deposit account is an account where you put money and leave it for a time, often to earn interest or where you must give notice to take it out.
