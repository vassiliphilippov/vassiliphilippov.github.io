Etymology

September 11 comes from the date of the terrorist attacks in the United States on 11 September 2001, when planes taken by terrorists hit the World Trade Center towers in New York and damaged the Pentagon. Because that day was so terrible and changed many lives, September 11 now means that event and the yearly time people spend remembering it, often called '9/11'.
