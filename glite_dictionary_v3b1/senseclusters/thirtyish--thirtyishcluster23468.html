Etymology

Thirtyish joins thirty and -ish, with -ish meaning 'around' or 'about'. A helpful way to remember it is to picture the minute hand near the 6 on a clock, so 'thirtyish' means 'around thirty minutes past the hour' or 'in about thirty minutes'.
