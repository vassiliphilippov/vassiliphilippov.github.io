Etymology

designate comes from the Latin designare, from de- and signare, from signum meaning 'mark' or 'sign', the same root as sign and signal. Imagine putting a mark on a map or a sign on a door to show a place. So, to designate is to point out or mark a place, direction, person, or thing.
