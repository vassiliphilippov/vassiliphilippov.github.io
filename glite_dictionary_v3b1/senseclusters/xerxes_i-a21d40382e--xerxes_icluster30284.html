Etymology

Xerxes I is the name of a Persian king who led a large 'invasion' of Greece in the early fifth century BC. Picture his huge army at the narrow pass of Thermopylae and the great sea battle at Salamis; that image helps you remember him as the powerful Persian ruler who tried to conquer Greece but was finally defeated.
