Etymology

Potassium dichromate gets its name from older words: potassium comes from 'potash', the ashes people collected in pots, and di- means 'two' while chromate comes from a word for 'colour' because the crystals are bright orange. That's why the name helps you remember it is an orange-red salt often used in labs and in making dyes and photos.
