Etymology

The name resting spore comes from the simple image of a tiny spore that is resting, like a little seed that 'sleeps'. In nature, fungi and algae make these 'sleeping' spores to survive bad times and 'wake up' later and grow, so the name shows they stay inactive for a long time but can start living again.
