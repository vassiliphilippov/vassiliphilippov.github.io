Etymology

fatigue duty comes from an old use of fatigue meaning 'tiring work'. Soldiers were often given hard jobs like cleaning, digging or carrying heavy loads as a duty, so fatigue duty now means those manual jobs given to soldiers, sometimes as a punishment.
