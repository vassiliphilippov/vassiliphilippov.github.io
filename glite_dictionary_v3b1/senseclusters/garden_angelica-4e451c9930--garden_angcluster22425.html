Etymology

garden angelica gets its name from old stories that an angel showed people the plant because it could help with sickness, so angelica became linked to 'healing'. People grew it in gardens for food and medicine, and because its stems were often candied and its roots used to help digestion, the name came to mean the edible, healing plant we call garden angelica today.
