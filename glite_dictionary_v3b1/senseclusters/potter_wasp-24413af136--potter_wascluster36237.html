Etymology

potter wasp: The name potter wasp comes from the tiny mud 'pots' these wasps make; like a human potter shaping clay, the wasp builds a small vase of mud to hold its eggs. That's why the name means a wasp that makes vase-shaped mud nests for its eggs.
