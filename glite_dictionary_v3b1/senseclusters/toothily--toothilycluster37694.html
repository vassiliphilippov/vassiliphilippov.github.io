Etymology

toothily comes from the adjective toothy, from tooth 'tooth', with -ly added to mean 'in a ... way'. Since teeth look like small points or the teeth of a saw, toothily can mean 'with tooth-like or serrated action', as when a blade cuts in a rough, jagged way.
