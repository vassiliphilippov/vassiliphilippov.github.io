Etymology

stubbiness comes from the little word stub, which meant a short, broken piece or 'stump', plus -ness, which makes a noun for a quality. Think of a tree stump or a short pencil: that 'stubby' shape shows 'stubbiness', the quality of being 'short and thick'.
