Etymology

roseate outlook may come from the picture of looking at the world through 'rose-coloured' glass, where everything seems warmer and nicer than it really is. So, roseate means 'rose-coloured' and outlook means 'view', which gives a clear image for being 'too hopeful' or unrealistically positive.
