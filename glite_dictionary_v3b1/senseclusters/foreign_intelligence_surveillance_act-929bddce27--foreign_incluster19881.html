Etymology

Foreign Intelligence Surveillance Act name says its job: foreign means from other countries, intelligence means information about threats, surveillance means watching, and act means a law. That's why the name refers to the U.S. law that sets the rules and the special 'court' that must give permission for government 'watching' of foreign targets.
