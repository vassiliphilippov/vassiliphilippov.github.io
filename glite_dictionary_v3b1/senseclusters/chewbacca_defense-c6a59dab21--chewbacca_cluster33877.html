Etymology

Chewbacca defense comes from a funny TV episode where a lawyer started talking about Chewbacca, a big, hairy space creature, to distract the jury. One popular story says the lawyer used that silly talk as a defense, so today the term means using nonsense to 'confuse' or 'distract' from the real issue.
