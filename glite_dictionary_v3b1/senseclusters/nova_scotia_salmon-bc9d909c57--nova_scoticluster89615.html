Etymology

Nova Scotia salmon comes from the Canadian coastal region of Nova Scotia, where fishers gently cured salmon with salt and sometimes a little sugar so the fish kept a mild, delicate taste. That's why the name now means a softly cured fish that is 'less salty' and has a 'mild and delicate' flavor.
