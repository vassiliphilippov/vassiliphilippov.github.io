Etymology

The plant name white potato vine comes from a simple image: a climbing vine that makes potato-like tubers under the soil and has pale or white flowers or skin, so people called it a potatovine. That's why the name today means a vine with edible starchy 'tubers' but poisonous above-ground vines.
