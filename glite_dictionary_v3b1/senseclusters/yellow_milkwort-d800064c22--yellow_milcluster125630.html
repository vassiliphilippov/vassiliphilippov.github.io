Etymology

yellow milkwort may come from an old farming belief. People thought milkwort joined the words 'milk' and 'wort', an old word for 'plant', because the plant was said to help cows give more 'milk', and yellow describes its bright flowers. So the name refers to a yellow plant once thought to help cows give more 'milk'.
