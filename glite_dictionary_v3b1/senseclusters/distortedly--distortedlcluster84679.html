Etymology

distortedly comes from parts dis- meaning 'apart' and -tort meaning 'twist', so it literally means 'twisted apart'. Think of a picture seen through cracked glass or a voice bent out of shape; that's why distortedly describes something changed, twisted, or told in the wrong way.
