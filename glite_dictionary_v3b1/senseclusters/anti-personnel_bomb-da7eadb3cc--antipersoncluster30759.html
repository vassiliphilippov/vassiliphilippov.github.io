Etymology

anti-personnel bomb: The name anti-personnel bomb comes from a simple idea: anti means 'against', personnel means 'people' or 'soldiers', and bomb is an explosive, so the name means a bomb made to hurt people. That's why today it means a weapon meant to injure or kill people.
