Etymology

spectroscopically comes from the parts spectro- meaning 'appearance' or 'to look' and -scopy meaning 'to look' or 'examine', the same idea you see in spectator and telescope. So, to study something spectroscopically is to 'look' at its light pattern or spectrum to find out what it is.
