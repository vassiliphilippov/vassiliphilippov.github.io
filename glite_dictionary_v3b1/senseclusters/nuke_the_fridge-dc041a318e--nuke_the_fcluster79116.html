Etymology

nuke the fridge comes from a famous movie scene where Indiana Jones hides inside a metal fridge during a nuclear blast and the blast sends the whole fridge flying. Because viewers saw this as a wild, extreme way to fix a story, people now say nuke the fridge when they 'abandon' something or 'start over' completely.
