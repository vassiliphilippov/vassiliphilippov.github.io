Etymology

The phrase hanger and flogger probably comes from old punishments: a hanger (one who hangs) and a flogger (one who whips) would punish people, creating a strong image of ropes and whips. So the phrase now means someone who 'criticizes' or 'punishes' others too much, hurting people's confidence.
