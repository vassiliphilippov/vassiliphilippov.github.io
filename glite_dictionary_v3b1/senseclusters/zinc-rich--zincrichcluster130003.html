Etymology

zinc-rich joins zinc, the metal, and rich, which means 'full of'. The name zinc comes from an old German word for a 'point' because zinc often forms sharp points, so zinc-rich simply means 'full of zinc', used for foods or materials that have a lot of this mineral.
