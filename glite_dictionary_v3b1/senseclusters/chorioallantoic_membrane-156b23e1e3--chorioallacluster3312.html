Etymology

chorioallantoic membrane is named because two parts, the chorion and the allantois, join together to make a thin, blood-rich layer around the egg. That layer helps the growing embryo 'breathe' and grow blood vessels, so the name refers to the fused membrane in bird and reptile eggs that supports gas exchange and development.
