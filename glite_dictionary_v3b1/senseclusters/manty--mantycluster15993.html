Etymology

manty comes from the Turkic word mantı, the name for small meat-filled dumplings across Central Asia, and it may also be linked to the Chinese word mantou, which means 'steamed bun'. So, thinking of manty as a Central Asian 'steamed dumpling' helps explain its meaning today.
