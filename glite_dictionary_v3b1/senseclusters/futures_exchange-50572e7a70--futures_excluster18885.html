Etymology

Futures exchange comes from the simple idea of a market where people trade futures, meaning 'promises to buy or sell later', and the exchange, the place where buyers and sellers meet to trade. Imagine a farmer and a buyer agreeing now on the price of next year's crop; that image shows why the phrase means 'a market for contracts about the future' and is used to manage price risk.
