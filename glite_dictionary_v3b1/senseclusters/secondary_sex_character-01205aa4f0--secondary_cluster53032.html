Etymology

secondary sex character comes from the idea that some features are secondary ('second' or not the main), they show sex (being male or female), and a character is a visible feature. These are not the body parts used for 'making babies' but signs like facial hair or a deeper voice, so the name explains why they show sex without being for 'reproduction'.
