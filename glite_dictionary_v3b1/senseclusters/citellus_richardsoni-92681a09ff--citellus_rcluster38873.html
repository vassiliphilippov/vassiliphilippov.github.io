Etymology

Citellus richardsoni comes from the animal name and the person who described it: Citellus is an old word for a small ground squirrel and richardsoni shows it was named after the scientist John Richardson. So the name simply means 'Richardson's ground squirrel', which helps you remember it is that particular ground squirrel species.
