Etymology

Amorphophallus paeonifolius gets its name from old Greek and Latin parts: amorpho- ('odd-shaped') + phallus (a long central stalk) and paeoni- + -folius ('peony-like leaves'). So the name paints an easy picture — a plant with a strange, smelly flower stalk and leaves like a peony, which helps you remember this smelly plant with an edible root.
