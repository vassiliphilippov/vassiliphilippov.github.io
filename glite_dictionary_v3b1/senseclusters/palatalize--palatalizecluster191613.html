Etymology

The word palatalize comes from the Latin root palatum, which means 'palate' or 'roof of the mouth', and you can see the same idea in the English word palate. So to palatalize a sound is to say it by moving the tongue toward the roof of the mouth, which changes how the sound feels and sounds.
