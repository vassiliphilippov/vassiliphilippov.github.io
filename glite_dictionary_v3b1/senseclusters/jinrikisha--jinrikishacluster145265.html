Etymology

jinrikisha comes from Japanese parts jin 'person', riki 'power', and sha 'vehicle', so it literally means 'person-powered vehicle'. That's why a jinrikisha, also known in English as a rickshaw, is a small two-wheeled cart pulled by a person.
