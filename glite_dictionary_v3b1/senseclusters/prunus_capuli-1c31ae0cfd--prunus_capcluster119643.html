Etymology

Prunus capuli is a scientific name that mixes Latin and a local word: Prunus means 'plum' or 'cherry' in Latin, and capuli may come from the Spanish and Nahuatl word 'capulín' for a small dark cherry. So the name shows that this tree is a cherry-like plant with edible dark fruit.
