Etymology

The noun decaffeinated coffee comes from the word decaffeinated, meaning 'without caffeine', plus coffee. People discovered ways to take the caffeine out of the beans so the drink kept its taste but not the wake-up effect. So, the term means 'coffee with most or all caffeine removed'.
