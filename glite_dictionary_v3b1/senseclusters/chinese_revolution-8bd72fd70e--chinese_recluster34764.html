Etymology

Chinese Revolution refers to the 1911 event in China when many people and soldiers rose up to remove the ruling Qing family and start a republic. The word revolution shows the picture of turning the old system upside down, so Chinese Revolution means the historical change that 'ended' imperial rule and began the Republic of China.
