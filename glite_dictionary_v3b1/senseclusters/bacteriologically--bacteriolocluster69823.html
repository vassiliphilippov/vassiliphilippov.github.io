Etymology

bacteriologically comes from bacteria plus the ending -ly; bacteria comes from the old Greek bakterion meaning 'small rod' or 'staff'. So bacteriologically means 'in the way of bacteria', and it is used when something is checked or treated with bacteria in mind.
