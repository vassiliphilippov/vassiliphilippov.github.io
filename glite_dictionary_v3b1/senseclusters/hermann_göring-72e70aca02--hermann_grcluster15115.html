Etymology

Hermann Göring was a top leader in 'Nazi Germany' who helped create the 'secret police' called the Gestapo and played a large role in starting the war, and after the war he was tried at Nuremberg for 'war crimes'. That's why his name is remembered as a powerful Nazi leader and a convicted war criminal.
