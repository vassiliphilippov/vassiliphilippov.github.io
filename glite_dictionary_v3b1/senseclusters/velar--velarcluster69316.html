Etymology

velar comes from the Latin word velum, which means 'veil' or 'cover'. A helpful image is the soft palate as a small 'veil' at the back of the mouth, and a velar consonant is made when the back of the tongue touches that 'veil', as in the 'k' of 'cat'.
