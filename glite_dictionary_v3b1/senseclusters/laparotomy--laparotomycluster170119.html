Etymology

laparotomy comes from Greek parts laparo- meaning 'flank' or 'abdomen' and -tomy meaning 'cutting'. So a laparotomy is a surgical 'cut' into the belly to look at or treat the organs, and you can see the same -tomy in appendectomy.
