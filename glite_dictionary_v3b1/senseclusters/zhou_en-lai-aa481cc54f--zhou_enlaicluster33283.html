Etymology

Zhou En-lai was a Chinese leader who helped start the People's Republic of China and served as its first 'Premier' from 1949 to 1976. Imagine him calmly sitting at a table, quietly solving problems and talking with other leaders. That steady image helps explain why people remember him as a skilled negotiator and a steady leader.
