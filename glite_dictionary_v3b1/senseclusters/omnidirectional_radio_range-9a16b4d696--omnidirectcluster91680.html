Etymology

The term omnidirectional radio range comes from how the system works: a ground station sends radio signals in every direction, so omnidirectional means 'in every direction' and radio range is the radio area pilots use to find their position and direction. That's why the name refers to a radio system that gives pilots exact navigation information.
