Etymology

Sagittarius serpentarius comes from Latin words: Sagittarius meaning 'archer' and serpentarius meaning 'of snakes'. One popular idea is that the bird's long legs and the way it hunts and kills snakes made people think of an 'archer' fighting snakes, so the name points to the bird that hunts snakes.
