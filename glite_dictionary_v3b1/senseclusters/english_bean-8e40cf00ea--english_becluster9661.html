Etymology

English bean — one popular story says the name comes from the long use of this large, flat bean in English gardens and cooking, so people abroad began to call this kind the 'English bean' instead of the 'broad bean' or 'fava bean'. That's why today English bean means the garden plant Vicia faba, the big, flat bean used in many dishes.
