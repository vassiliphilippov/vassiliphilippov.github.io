Etymology

lincomycin comes from the place name Lincoln, where the bacteria that made the drug were first found, plus the common drug ending -mycin. The ending -mycin appears in many antibiotic names like streptomycin, so lincomycin can be remembered as 'the antibiotic from Lincoln', which explains why the medicine has that name.
