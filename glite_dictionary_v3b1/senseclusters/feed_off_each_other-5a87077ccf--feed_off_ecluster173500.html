Etymology

feed off each other comes from the image of two living things taking energy from the same source: imagine two flames that grow when they touch, where feed means to take energy and each other shows both give and take. So, it means people or groups 'gain energy, ideas, or strong feelings from one another', making each other's actions stronger.
