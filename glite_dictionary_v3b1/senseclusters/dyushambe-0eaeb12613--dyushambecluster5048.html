Etymology

Dyushambe comes from the Tajik/Persian parts du and shanbe, which mean 'two' and 'day' and together mean 'Monday'. The city was named for a busy 'Monday' market that grew into a town, so the capital's name still reminds us of that market, even though it was later called Stalinabad.
