Etymology

absolute viscosity combines absolute (meaning fixed or real) and viscosity (the stickiness of a liquid). Think of honey: because it is sticky and moves slowly, absolute viscosity is the measured 'resistance to flow' that tells how hard a liquid is to move, so the name points to the liquid's real resistance to flow.
