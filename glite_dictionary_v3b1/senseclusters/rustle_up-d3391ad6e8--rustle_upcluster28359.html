Etymology

The phrasal verb rustle up may come from the quick rustle sound you hear when someone moves things fast, like leaves or paper. Imagine someone hurriedly opening cupboards and bags, making that noise as they put a meal together, so rustle up means 'to make or find something quickly', often food.
