Etymology

electrophorus comes from Greek parts electron meaning 'amber', an old material that makes static electricity when rubbed, and -phorus meaning 'bearer' or 'carrier', as in phosphorus 'light-bearer'. So an electrophorus is literally an 'electricity-bearer', which fits a simple device that carries and gives a static electric charge.
