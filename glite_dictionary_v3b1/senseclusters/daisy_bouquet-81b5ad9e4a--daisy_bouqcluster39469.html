Etymology

The noun daisy bouquet comes from the flower daisy and the word bouquet, which is a bunch of flowers tied together. Picture many cheerful daisies gathered and tied into a bright bunch — that's why a daisy bouquet means a neat group of daisies used as a 'gift' or 'decoration'.
