Etymology

Venus's flower basket gets its name from how the sponge looks: its thin, glass threads make a tiny woven basket that seems to hold a delicate flower, and the name Venus adds the idea of beauty. So the name refers to this deep-water 'sea sponge' with a glass-like skeleton that people admire as a natural piece of art.
