Etymology

Verpa conica is a scientific name from Latin: Verpa is the group name for morel-like mushrooms and conica means 'cone-shaped'. So the name gives a simple image — a smooth, cone-shaped cap over a fragile stem, which helps you remember what the mushroom looks like.
