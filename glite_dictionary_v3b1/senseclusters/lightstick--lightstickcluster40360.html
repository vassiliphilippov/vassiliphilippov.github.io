Etymology

lightstick is simply light ('brightness' or 'not dark') plus stick ('a small rod'), so imagine a little rod that gives light like a tiny hand-held flashlight. That's why a lightstick is a small, hand-held stick that glows and is used at concerts, parties, or in an emergency.
