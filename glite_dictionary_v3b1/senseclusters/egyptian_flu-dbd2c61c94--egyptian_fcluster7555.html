Etymology

Egyptian flu comes from travelers joking that the Egyptian part points to the trip and the flu part to a small illness they caught from new food, dust or long travel. So, people use it to mean 'mild flu-like symptoms' or 'travel tiredness' after visiting Egypt or similar places.
