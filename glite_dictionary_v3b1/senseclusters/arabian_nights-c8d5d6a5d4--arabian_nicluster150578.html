Etymology

Arabian Nights gets its name from a famous old book of stories where a clever woman, Scheherazade, told a new tale each night to the king so he would not kill her. These nightly tales, called 'One Thousand and One Nights', are full of magic and adventure, and that is why Arabian Nights means the well-known collection of magical Middle Eastern stories.
