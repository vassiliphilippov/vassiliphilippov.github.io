Etymology

The word chautauqua is named after Chautauqua Lake in New York, where summer gatherings of lectures and music began in the 1800s. The lake's name comes from a Native American word that some people say meant 'jumping fish', and the place gave its name to any community festival of learning and culture, which is why a chautauqua today is a public event with talks, music, and shows.
