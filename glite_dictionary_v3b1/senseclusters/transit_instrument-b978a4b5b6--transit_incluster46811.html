Etymology

Transit instrument is named for what it watches: a transit is when a star or planet crosses an imaginary north-south line in the sky, and the instrument is a telescope fixed on an east-west axis to watch that crossing. So the name means a tool used to record the exact time a star or planet 'transits' that line.
