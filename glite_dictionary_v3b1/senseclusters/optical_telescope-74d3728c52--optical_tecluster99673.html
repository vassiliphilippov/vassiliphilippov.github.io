Etymology

optical telescope comes from the parts optical (about light and seeing) and telescope (a word that means 'to look far'). It literally names a device that uses lenses or mirrors to gather and focus light so faraway objects look closer, which is why it means 'an instrument that gathers and focuses visible light to observe distant objects'.
