Etymology

early coral root is a name that creates a clear picture: early because the orchid flowers near the start of the season, and coral and root because its pale, branching underground parts look like small pieces of coral instead of green leaves. So the name helps you remember this almost leafless orchid that appears 'early' with coral-like roots.
