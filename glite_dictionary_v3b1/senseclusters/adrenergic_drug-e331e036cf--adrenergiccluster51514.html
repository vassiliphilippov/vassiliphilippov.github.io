Etymology

adrenergic drug comes from adrenal (from 'adrenaline', the body's 'alarm' chemical) and the word part -ergic, which means 'acting on'. These drugs work like adrenaline, making the heart beat faster and opening the airways, so they are used for 'asthma' or to raise a low heart rate.
