Etymology

Stigmatization comes from the Greek word stigma, meaning 'mark' or 'brand' once used to show shame. So to stigmatize someone is to give them a bad 'mark' in the eyes of others, which is why stigmatization means treating a person as disgraceful or socially unacceptable.
