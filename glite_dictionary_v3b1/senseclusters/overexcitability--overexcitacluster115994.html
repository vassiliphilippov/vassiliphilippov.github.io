Etymology

overexcitability breaks into over- meaning 'too much', excite meaning 'to stir up', and -ability meaning 'the quality of being'. So think of someone whose feelings are easily stirred or 'too excited' by sights, sounds, or emotions, which is why the word means being unusually sensitive to experiences and feelings.
