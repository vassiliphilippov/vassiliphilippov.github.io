Etymology

stratigraphy comes from parts that mean 'layer' and 'writing': stratum (a 'layer') and -graphy ('writing' or 'record'), the same -graphy in geography. So, stratigraphy is like 'reading' the pages made by rock layers to learn Earth's history.
