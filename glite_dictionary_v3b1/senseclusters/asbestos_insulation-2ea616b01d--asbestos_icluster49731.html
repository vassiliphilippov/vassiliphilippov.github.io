Etymology

asbestos insulation gets its name from the mineral asbestos, a soft rock made of many thin fibers that does not burn and was used as insulation to keep buildings safe from heat and fire. So it came to mean the 'heat-resistant' material used in walls and pipes, but today it is known to be 'dangerous to health'.
