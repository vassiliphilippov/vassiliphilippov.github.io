Etymology

provocateur comes from French and from Latin parts pro- meaning 'forward' and vocare meaning 'to call'. Like the English verb provoke, a provocateur 'calls people forward' to react, often to cause trouble, which is why the modern meaning is a person who deliberately provokes others.
