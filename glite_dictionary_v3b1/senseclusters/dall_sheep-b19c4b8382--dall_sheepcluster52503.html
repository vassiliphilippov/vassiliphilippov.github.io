Etymology

Dall sheep may come from William Dall, an American scientist who studied animals in Alaska, so the sheep were named after him. He wrote about these white mountain sheep, and people started calling them 'Dall's sheep', which is why today Dall sheep means the large, white wild sheep with curved horns.
