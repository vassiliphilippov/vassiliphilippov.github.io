Etymology

esthesiology comes from the Greek root aesthesis meaning 'sensation' and the ending -logy meaning 'study'. So esthesiology literally means the 'study of sensations', which is why the field looks at sense organs and how we feel things, and you can see the same root in aesthetic (about feeling or taste).
