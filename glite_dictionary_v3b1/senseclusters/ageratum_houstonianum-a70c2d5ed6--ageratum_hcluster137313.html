Etymology

Ageratum houstonianum gets its name from two ideas: the first part Ageratum may come from an old word meaning 'not aging' because the small, fluffy flowers keep their color a long time, and the second part houstonianum honors a plant collector named Houston. So the name fits this little herb with fluffy, long-lasting blue flowers.
