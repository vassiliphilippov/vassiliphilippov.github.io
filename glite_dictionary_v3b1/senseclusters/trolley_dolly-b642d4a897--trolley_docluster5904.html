Etymology

trolley dolly may come from the image of a woman pushing a drinks cart on a plane: trolley is the cart and dolly is like a small doll, a word people used for a neat, friendly woman. So the phrase became a casual name for a 'flight attendant' who 'serves food and drinks'.
