Etymology

person from Porlock comes from a famous story about the poet Samuel Taylor Coleridge: he said a person from the village of Porlock arrived and stopped him while he was writing a poem, so he lost his 'inspiration'. So today person from Porlock means 'a person who interrupts at the wrong moment'.
