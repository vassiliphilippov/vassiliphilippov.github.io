Etymology

catatonic type schizophrenia gets its name from the word catatonic, which makes you imagine someone frozen like a statue and not moving for a long time. That's why this 'type' of schizophrenia means long periods of 'immobility' or 'stupor', sometimes broken by short moments of 'agitation'.
