Etymology

Cydippea comes from the ancient Greek name Cydippe, a 'woman's name' from myth. A helpful way to remember it is to picture the jelly's two long, feathery tentacles as the woman's long hair, so Cydippea is the name scientists give to comb jellies with two long, feather-like tentacles.
