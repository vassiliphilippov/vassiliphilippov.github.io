Etymology

dixie for a large camp pot probably comes from the American nickname Dixie for 'the South'; one popular story says pots made or sold there were marked 'Dixie', so soldiers began calling the big cooking pot a dixie. So thinking of Dixie helps you remember a dixie is the large metal pot used for cooking in camps.
