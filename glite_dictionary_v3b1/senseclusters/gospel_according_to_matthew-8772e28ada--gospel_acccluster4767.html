Etymology

Gospel According to Matthew gets its name because gospel means 'good news' and the words 'according to Matthew' mean 'as told by Matthew'. Matthew was one of Jesus's followers, so this book tells Jesus's life and teaching from his point of view, which is why we call it the Gospel According to Matthew.
