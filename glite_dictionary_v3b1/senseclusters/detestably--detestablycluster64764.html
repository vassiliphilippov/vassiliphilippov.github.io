Etymology

The word detestably comes from detest, which means 'hate strongly', plus the ending -ably meaning 'in a way', so it means 'in a hateful or offensive way'. Long ago detest came from Latin parts de- and testari and was used like a strong curse, which helps explain its sense of great dislike.
