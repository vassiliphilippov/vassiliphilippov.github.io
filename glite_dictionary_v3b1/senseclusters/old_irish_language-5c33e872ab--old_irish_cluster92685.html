Etymology

Old Irish language gets its name from the words Old and Irish: it refers to the 'earlier' form of Irish that people in Ireland spoke and often wrote long ago, for example in old books by monks. So, Old Irish language means the version of Irish used in Ireland up to about 1100 AD.
