Etymology

Vena bronchialis comes from Latin words vena meaning 'vein' and bronchialis meaning 'of the bronchi', the small air tubes in the lungs. So the name gives a clear image: it is the vein that carries blood away from the bronchi into a larger vein called the 'azygos', which is why it means 'a vein that drains blood from the bronchi and empties into the azygos vein'.
