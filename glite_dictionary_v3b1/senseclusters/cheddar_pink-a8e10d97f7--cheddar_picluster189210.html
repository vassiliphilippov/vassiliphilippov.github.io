Etymology

cheddar pink may be named after the Cheddar area in England where the plant grew wild, while pink points to the flower's soft pink color and its jagged petal edges once called 'pinked' when cloth was cut with little points. So the name simply means a pink, fragrant flower from Cheddar, which makes the word easy to remember.
