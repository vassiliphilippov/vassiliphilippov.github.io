Etymology

clarino as the fabric name was created as a brand and uses the same root clarus, meaning 'clear' or 'bright', like English clear. A helpful way to remember it is to think of clarino as a smooth, leather-like material that gives a neat, bright look and works as an easy-to-care substitute for real leather.
