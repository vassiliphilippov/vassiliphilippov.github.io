Etymology

The phrasal verb blunder out creates a clear picture: blunder is a clumsy mistake and out shows words coming from your mouth. Imagine someone who stumbles with their words and suddenly says something without thinking — that's why blunder out means 'to say something impulsively or clumsily'.
