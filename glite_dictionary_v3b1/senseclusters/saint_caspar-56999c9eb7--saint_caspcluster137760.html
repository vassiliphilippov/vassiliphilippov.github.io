Etymology

Saint Caspar is one of the three wise men in the Christmas story who travelled from far away to bring gifts to the baby Jesus. Imagine him as a learned man from India carrying shining 'gold', so this clear picture shows why Saint Caspar is remembered as the wise gift-bringer and linked with 'gold'.
