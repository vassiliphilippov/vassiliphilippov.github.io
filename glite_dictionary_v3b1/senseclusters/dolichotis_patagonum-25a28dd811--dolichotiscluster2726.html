Etymology

Dolichotis patagonum comes from old Greek words: Dolichotis meaning 'long ear' and patagonum meaning 'Patagonia', the region of Argentina where it lives. That picture of a long-eared, rabbit-like animal in Patagonia is why the name fits the Patagonian mara today.
