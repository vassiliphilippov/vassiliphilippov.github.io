Etymology

Kingdom of the Netherlands comes from the idea of one king or queen ruling over several lands. Think of a single ruler whose rule includes the European country Netherlands and the Caribbean islands Aruba, Curaçao and Sint Maarten, so the term means 'one state made of different countries'.
