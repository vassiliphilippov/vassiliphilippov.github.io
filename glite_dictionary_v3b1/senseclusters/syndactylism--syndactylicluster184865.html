Etymology

syndactylism comes from the Greek parts syn- 'together' and dactyl 'finger', and you can see the same idea in the common word digit which also means 'finger' or 'toe'. So syndactylism literally means 'fingers together', which explains the condition where two or more fingers or toes are joined.
