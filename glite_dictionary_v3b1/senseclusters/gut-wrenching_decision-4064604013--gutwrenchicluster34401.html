Etymology

Gut-wrenching decision comes from the strong image of your gut being wrenched or twisted, like the tight, sick feeling in your stomach when you are deeply upset. That's why a gut-wrenching decision means a choice that is 'very painful' or 'emotionally distressing' to make.
