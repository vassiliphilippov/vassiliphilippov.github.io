Etymology

The phrase pot of gold comes from old Irish stories that imagined a shiny pot full of gold at the end of a rainbow. One popular story says a leprechaun guards that treasure and, because the rainbow's end always moves, the pot of gold became a picture of a hoped-for 'reward' that is hard to reach.
