Etymology

sign on in computing borrows that same image of writing your name to show you belong. In computers you enter your name and password like putting your name on a list, so sign on means 'to log in' or 'to gain access to a system'.

Related Terms

  • synonym: sign in - to enter credentials to access an account or service (often used interchangeably with 'sign on').
  • antonym: sign off - to log out of a system or end a session; often the opposite action to signing on.
