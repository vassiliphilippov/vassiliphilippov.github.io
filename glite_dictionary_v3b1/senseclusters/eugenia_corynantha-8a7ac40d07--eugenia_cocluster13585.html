Etymology

Eugenia corynantha is the scientific name for a small myrtle tree that bears sour red fruit. The first part, Eugenia, groups it with other myrtle trees and the second part, corynantha, brings to mind tight flower clusters, so you can remember it as a myrtle tree with small clustered flowers and sour red fruit.
