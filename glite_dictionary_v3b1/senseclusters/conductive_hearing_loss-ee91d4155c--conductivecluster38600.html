Etymology

Conductive hearing loss gets its name from the idea of 'carrying' sound: conductive means 'carrying' and hearing loss means 'losing' hearing. When the tiny bones in the middle ear cannot carry sound to the inner ear, you 'lose' some hearing, so the phrase means a hearing problem caused by that.
