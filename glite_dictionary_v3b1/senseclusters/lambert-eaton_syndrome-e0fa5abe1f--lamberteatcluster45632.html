Etymology

Lambert-Eaton syndrome is named after the two doctors, Dr. Lambert and Dr. Eaton, who first reported the illness. Their names stuck to a rare condition where nerve signals do not make muscles work well, so the name helps you remember it means 'muscle weakness and tiredness' often linked to cancer.
