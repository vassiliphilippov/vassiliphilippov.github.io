Etymology

The word egg-and-dart is a simple name for a classic carved border: it shows repeating egg shapes next to pointed dart shapes, like eggs and small arrows in a row, and this pattern was used on ancient Greek and Roman buildings. That's why an egg-and-dart molding means the decorative carved pattern you see along the top of columns and cornices.
