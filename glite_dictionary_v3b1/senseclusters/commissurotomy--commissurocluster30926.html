Etymology

commissurotomy comes from the parts commissure meaning 'joining' and -otomy meaning 'cutting', as in lobotomy. So it means 'cutting a join', and a commissurotomy can be used to separate joined parts of the heart, for example to open a narrowed valve and help the heart work better.
