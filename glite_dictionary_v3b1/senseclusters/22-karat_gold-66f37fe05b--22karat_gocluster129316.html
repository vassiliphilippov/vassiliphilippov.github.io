Etymology

22-karat gold may come from an old way of measuring gold: a karat was a tiny weight named after carob seeds, so 22 of the 24 parts were gold and the rest were other metals. That's why 22-karat gold means jewellery that is mostly gold but mixed with other metals to be 'stronger'.
