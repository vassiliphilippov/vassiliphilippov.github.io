Etymology

The term azo group comes from the old word 'azote' for nitrogen, whose Greek roots meant 'no life' because nitrogen gas does not help you breathe. An azo group is the small piece –N=N– (two nitrogen atoms joined), so the name points to that nitrogen link and helps you remember why many dyes with an azo part are brightly colored.
