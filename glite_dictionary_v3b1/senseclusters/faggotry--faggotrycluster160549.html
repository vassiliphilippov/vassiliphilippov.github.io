Etymology

faggotry comes from the older word faggot, which originally meant a 'bundle of sticks' used as firewood. Over time faggot became an insult for someone seen as useless or to be 'thrown away', and later it became an extremely hurtful slur against gay men, so the word is offensive and should not be used.
