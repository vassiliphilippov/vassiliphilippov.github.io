Etymology

abo is a shortened, offensive form of Aborigine or Aboriginal, words that come from a phrase meaning 'from the origin' and share the same root as origin and original; this explains why they once meant the first peoples of a place. It is now a hurtful slur for Aboriginal Australians and should not be used.
