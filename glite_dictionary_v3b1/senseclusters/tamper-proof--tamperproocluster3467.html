Etymology

Tamper-proof as a verb means to make something hard to 'tamper' with; it comes from tamper ('to touch or change something secretly') and proof (as in bulletproof meaning 'resistant'). That's why people tamper-proof packages or machines by adding seals or special screws so others cannot open or change them without leaving a sign.
