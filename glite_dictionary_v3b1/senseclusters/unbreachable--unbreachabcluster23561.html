Etymology

unbreachable comes from three parts: un- ('not'), breach ('a break or opening') and -able ('able'), so you can remember it as 'not able to be broken through or entered'. Think of a castle wall with no gap you can open — that picture shows why we call a very strong wall, a secure vault, or a solid friendship unbreachable.
