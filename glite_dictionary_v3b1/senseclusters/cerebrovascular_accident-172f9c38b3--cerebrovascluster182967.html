Etymology

cerebrovascular accident is a medical name made of parts: cerebro meaning 'brain', vascular meaning 'blood vessels', and accident meaning 'an unexpected event'. So it shows a sudden problem with blood flow to the brain, which is why the term means 'stroke'.
