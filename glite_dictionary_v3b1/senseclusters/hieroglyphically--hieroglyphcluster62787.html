Etymology

The word hieroglyphically comes from Greek parts hieros 'sacred' and glyphein 'to carve', and is related to hieroglyph, the picture signs carved by ancient Egyptians. Think of carved pictures on a temple wall: to do something hieroglyphically is to write or arrange it in picture-like symbols, so it means 'in a manner relating to or like hieroglyphs'.
