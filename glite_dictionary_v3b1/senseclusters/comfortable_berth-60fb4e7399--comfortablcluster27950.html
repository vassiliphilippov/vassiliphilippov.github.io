Etymology

The noun comfortable berth comes from ships, where a berth was a small bed or space to lie down in a cabin and sailors wanted a cozy berth after hard work. So today comfortable berth gives the image of a safe, cozy sleeping spot, often on a train or ship, meaning 'a cozy place to sleep'.
