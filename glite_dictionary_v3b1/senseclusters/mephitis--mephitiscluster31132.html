Etymology

mephitis comes from Latin mephitis, a word for a 'poisonous vapor' that was said to rise from marshes or volcanoes, like a dangerous, bad-smelling gas from the ground. That's why mephitis can mean a poisonous or foul-smelling gas emitted from the earth.
