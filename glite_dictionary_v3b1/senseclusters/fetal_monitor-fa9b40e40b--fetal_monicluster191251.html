Etymology

fetal monitor comes from two plain words: fetal meaning 'about the unborn baby' and monitor meaning 'something that watches or warns'. So a fetal monitor is simply a device that 'watches' the baby's heartbeat and the mother's contractions during birth, which explains the name.
