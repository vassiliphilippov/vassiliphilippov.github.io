Etymology

The noun diaphragmatic pleurisy comes from a simple image: the thin lining called the pleura that covers the diaphragm becomes sore and swollen, so each breath can rub it and cause sharp pain. So, it means 'sudden, severe chest pain' when that lining is inflamed.
