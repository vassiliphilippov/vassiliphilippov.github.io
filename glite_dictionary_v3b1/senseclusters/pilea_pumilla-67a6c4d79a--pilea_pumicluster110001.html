Pilea pumilla may come from old Latin words that describe a tiny plant: Pilea suggests a small, cushion-like plant and pumilla means 'very small' or 'dwarf'. So the name paints a picture of a low, delicate 'clearweed', which helps you remember it is a small, low-growing plant.
