Etymology

warm-up routine comes from the simple idea of warm-up, making muscles warmer, and routine, a set of repeated actions. Think of light exercises that slowly wake your muscles and get them ready, and that's why a warm-up routine means 'a series of light exercises before harder activity' to 'prepare' the body and 'reduce the risk of injury'.
