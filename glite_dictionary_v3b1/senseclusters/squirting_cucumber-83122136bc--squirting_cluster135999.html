Etymology

squirting cucumber is named for a real plant whose long, cucumber-like fruit suddenly shoots out seeds and juice when it is ripe or touched. So the words squirting (shooting out liquid) and cucumber (the shape of the fruit) describe the plant that 'ejects seeds and liquid' in a quick spray.
