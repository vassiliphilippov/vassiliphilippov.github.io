Etymology

air sickness comes from a simple image: when a plane moves or hits turbulence, your body feels the motion and your stomach can turn. The parts air, meaning being on a plane, and sickness, meaning 'feeling sick', explain why it now means 'feeling sick' or nausea during a flight.
