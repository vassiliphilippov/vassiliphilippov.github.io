Etymology

Prunus cerasus austera gets its name from old Latin words: Prunus means 'plum or cherry', cerasus was an old name for cherry, and austera means 'severe' or 'bitter', pointing to the fruit's sour taste and dark colour. That's why the name fits a cultivated sour cherry tree with dark skin and dark juice.
