Etymology

arc tangent comes from a simple picture of a circle: a tangent is a straight line that just touches the circle, and an arc is the curved part of the circle that shows an angle. One easy way to remember it is this — if you know how steep the line is, the arc tangent 'finds the angle' on the circle that matches that steepness.
