Etymology

Centaurea americana gets its name from the group name Centaurea, which comes from the old story of the 'centaur', and the Latin word americana, meaning 'from America'. One popular story says a centaur named Chiron used plants in this group to heal wounds, so the name reminds us this is a Centaurea plant with purple flowers that comes from America.
