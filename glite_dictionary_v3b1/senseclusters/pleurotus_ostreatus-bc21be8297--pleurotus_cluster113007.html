Etymology

Pleurotus ostreatus comes from old Greek and Latin words and paints a simple picture: Pleurotus means 'side' and ostreatus means 'oyster', because the mushrooms look like small oyster shells growing on the sides of logs. So, the name helps you remember this edible 'oyster' mushroom.
