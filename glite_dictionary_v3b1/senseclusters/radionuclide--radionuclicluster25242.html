Etymology

radionuclide combines radio- (from a Latin word meaning 'ray', the same idea as in radiation) and nucleus (a Latin word meaning 'core', like the 'nucleus' of a cell). So a radionuclide is an atom whose 'core' gives off 'rays', which is why the word means a radioactive atom used in medicine and science.
