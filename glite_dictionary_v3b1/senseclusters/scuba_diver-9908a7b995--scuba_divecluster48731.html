Etymology

scuba diver comes from scuba, a short form of 'self-contained underwater breathing apparatus', and diver, a person who goes underwater. The picture is of someone carrying their own 'air tank' while swimming under the sea, so the term means 'a person who swims underwater using an air tank'.
