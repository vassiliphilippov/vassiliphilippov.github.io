Etymology

unattackable is made of three parts: un- meaning 'not', the verb attack meaning 'to try to harm', and the ending -able meaning 'can be'. So, unattackable means 'not able to be attacked', like unbreakable, and is used for things that seem impossible to harm or weaken.
