Etymology

Egretta thula comes from words for a small heron and a local name used where the bird lives. Egretta is like 'egret', meaning 'small white heron', and thula may come from a native word from the Americas. So the name paints a picture of the small white bird with black legs and bright yellow feet that we now call the 'snowy egret'.
