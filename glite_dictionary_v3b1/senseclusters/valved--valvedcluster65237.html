Etymology

valved comes from the Latin word valva, meaning 'leaf' or 'folding door', an image of a small door that opens and closes. So, a valved brass instrument uses tiny 'doors' to change the air path and therefore change the pitch, which is why a valved trumpet can play more notes than a natural trumpet.
