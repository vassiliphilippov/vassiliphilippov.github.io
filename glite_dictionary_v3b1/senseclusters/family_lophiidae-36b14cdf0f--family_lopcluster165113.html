Etymology

family Lophiidae is the name for a group of strange fish called 'anglers' that have very large heads and a fishing-like 'rod' on their head. One simple image is an angler waving a glowing 'bait' to attract and catch small fish, so the family name points to how they hunt and to their odd look.
