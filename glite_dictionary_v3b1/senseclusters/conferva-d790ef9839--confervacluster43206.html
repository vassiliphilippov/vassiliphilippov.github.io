Etymology

Conferva comes from a Latin term meaning 'a tangled mass' and uses the prefix con- meaning 'together'. So early botanists used Conferva for many thin, threadlike green algae that grow in mats or tangles, which is why the name fit even though it is now an obsolete group name.
