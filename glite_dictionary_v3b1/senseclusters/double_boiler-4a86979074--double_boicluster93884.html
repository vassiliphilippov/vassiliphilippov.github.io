Etymology

double boiler is a pair of pans used together: the word double points to the two parts and boiler refers to the hot water below, with a smaller pan sitting inside a larger one so the heat is gentle. This slow heat melts chocolate or cooks custard without burning, so it means 'a tool for gentle heating'.
