Etymology

cycloheximide starts with cyclo- meaning 'circle', like the familiar word cycle, and contains hex- meaning 'six', like hexagon, which together suggest a ring-shaped chemical. So, think of a tiny circular compound that blocks cells from making proteins and can kill fungi, which is what cycloheximide does.
