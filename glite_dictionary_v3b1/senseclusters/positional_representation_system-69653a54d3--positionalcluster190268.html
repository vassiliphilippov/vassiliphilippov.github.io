Etymology

The term positional representation system comes from the simple idea of writing digits into different positions, like putting numbers into boxes labeled 'ones', 'tens' and 'hundreds'. The same digit, for example '5', means 5 in the 'ones' box but 50 in the 'tens' box, so the system gives each digit its value by its position.
