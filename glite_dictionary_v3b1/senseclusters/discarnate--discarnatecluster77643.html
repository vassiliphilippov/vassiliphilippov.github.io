Etymology

discarnate comes from the parts dis- meaning 'not' and carn- meaning 'flesh', so it originally meant 'not flesh'. Think of the same carn- in carnivore ('flesh eater') to help you remember that discarnate means 'without a physical body' or a being that exists beyond the flesh.
