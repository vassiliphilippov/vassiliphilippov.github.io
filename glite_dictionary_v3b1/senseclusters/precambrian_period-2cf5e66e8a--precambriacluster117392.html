Etymology

Precambrian period gets its name from the parts pre (meaning 'before') and Cambrian, which comes from Cambria, an old name for 'Wales' where scientists first studied rocks full of fossils. So the Precambrian period means the very long time 'before' the Cambrian, when Earth had mostly simple life and few large fossils.
