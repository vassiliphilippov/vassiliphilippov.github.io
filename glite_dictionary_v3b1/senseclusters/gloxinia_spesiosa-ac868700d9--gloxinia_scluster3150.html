Etymology

Gloxinia spesiosa is named for a person and a Latin word: the genus Gloxinia honors the German plant scientist Benjamin Gloxin, and the word spesiosa comes from Latin meaning 'showy'. So the name gives a simple picture of a 'showy' Gloxinia with large, soft leaves and bright bell-shaped flowers, which is why it is prized as a houseplant.
