Etymology

confarreation comes from the Latin parts con- 'with' and farreum 'spelt bread', where spelt is an old kind of wheat, so the image is people 'together with bread'. So a confarreation was a marriage ceremony in ancient Rome where the couple shared bread, which is why the word means that old wedding ritual.
