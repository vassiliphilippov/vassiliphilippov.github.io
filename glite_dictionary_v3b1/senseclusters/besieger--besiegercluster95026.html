Etymology

besieger comes from besiege, which once meant to 'sit around' a place — be- with the idea 'around' and siege from an old word for 'seat' or 'sit'. So a besieger is literally someone who 'sits around' a town to block its supplies and capture it, which is why the word means a person who surrounds and attacks a place.
