Etymology

trigeminal neuralgia: The medical name trigeminal neuralgia comes from the face nerve that has three branches: trigeminal points to 'three' and neuralgia means 'nerve pain'. Imagine one thin nerve with three branches suddenly sending sharp, electric shocks to parts of the face, so the name describes the sudden, severe pain along that nerve.
