Etymology

pedagog comes from the Greek paidagogos, made from pais 'child' and agogos 'leader'. A paidagogos was the person who led or looked after a child on the way to school, so a pedagog today is a teacher or guide for young learners. You can see the same root in pediatrician 'child doctor'.
