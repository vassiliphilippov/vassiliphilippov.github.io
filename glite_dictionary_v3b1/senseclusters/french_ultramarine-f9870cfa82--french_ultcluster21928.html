Etymology

French ultramarine is a bright blue pigment made in France to copy the precious natural blue called ultramarine, whose name literally means 'beyond the sea' because ultra meant 'beyond' and marine meant 'sea'. One popular story says French scientists learned to make the same deep blue in a lab so artists could use the color more cheaply, and today the name shows that this made-in-France pigment matches the famous imported blue.
