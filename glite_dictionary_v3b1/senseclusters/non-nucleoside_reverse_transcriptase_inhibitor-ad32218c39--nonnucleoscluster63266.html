Etymology

non-nucleoside reverse transcriptase inhibitor gets its name from what it does: non-nucleoside means it is not a small 'nucleoside' building block, reverse transcriptase is the virus's copy machine that turns RNA into DNA, and inhibitor means it 'stops' that machine. So the name shows a simple image—a non-nucleoside medicine that jams the copy machine, which is why it helps stop HIV from making new DNA.
