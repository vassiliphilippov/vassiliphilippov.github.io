Etymology

Qaddafi is an Arabic 'family or tribal name', and you will also see it written as Gaddafi or Qadhafi because English letters try to show the Arabic sound in different ways. A helpful way to remember it is to think of it like a last name such as 'Smith' or 'Garcia', which is why Qaddafi refers to the Libyan leader.
