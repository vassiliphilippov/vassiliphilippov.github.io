Etymology

charlatanry comes from the Italian ciarlatano, from ciarlare meaning 'to chatter' or talk a lot. A helpful way to remember it is to think of English chatter, because charlatanry is the dishonest talk or show people use to trick others when they pretend to have knowledge or skill.
