Etymology

whimsically comes from the simple words whim and whimsy, which mean 'a sudden, playful idea' or 'a fancy'. Think of a quick, fun choice like wearing mismatched socks just for laughs, and you will remember that whimsically means doing something in a playful, fanciful way.
