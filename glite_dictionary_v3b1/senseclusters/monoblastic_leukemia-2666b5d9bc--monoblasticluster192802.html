Etymology

Monoblastic leukemia gets its name from three parts: mono meaning 'one', blastic meaning 'young or not fully grown cells', and leukemia meaning 'blood cancer'. So the name helps you remember the meaning: it describes a blood cancer with too many young cells of one type.
