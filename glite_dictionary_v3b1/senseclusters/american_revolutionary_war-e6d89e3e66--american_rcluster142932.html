Etymology

American Revolutionary War comes from the American colonies rising up to make a big change, which is why the word Revolutionary is used, and from the fact they fought a war against Britain to win 'independence'. So the phrase refers to the fighting from 1775 to 1783 when the colonies tried to become free.
