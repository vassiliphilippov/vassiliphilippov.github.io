Etymology

Tragelaphus strepsiceros may come from old Greek words: Tragelaphus joins 'goat' and 'deer' because the animal looks a bit like both, and strepsiceros means 'twisted horn' to describe its spiral horns. So the name fits the greater kudu, a large antelope known for its long, spiralled horns.
