Etymology

The word chuckhole likely comes from the simple word chuck, which can mean a quick 'push' or 'bump', so a chuckhole is a hole in the road that gives your car a sudden jolt. A helpful image is your car being 'chucked' up when it hits one, which explains why the word means a dangerous hole in the road.
