Etymology

mud-beplastered is built from mud plus the old prefix be- and plaster, where plaster means 'to cover' and be- gives the idea 'covered with'. So it paints the picture of someone smeared with mud like a layer of plaster, which is why it means 'covered with or appearing as if covered with mud'.
