Etymology

The place name Little Bighorn River comes from a simple image: the 'Little' part shows it is the smaller branch of the larger Bighorn River, and Bighorn probably refers to the big horned sheep once seen on the hills. That's why the name fits the river today and is also remembered as the site of the famous 'Battle of the Little Bighorn' or 'Custer's Last Stand'.
