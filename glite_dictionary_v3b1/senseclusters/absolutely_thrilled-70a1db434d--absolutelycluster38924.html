Etymology

Absolutely thrilled comes from one popular story that sees thrill as a sharp, sudden feeling, like a quick shiver when something exciting happens, and absolutely means 'completely'. So, to be absolutely thrilled is to feel that strong, happy shiver all over, meaning 'very pleased'.
