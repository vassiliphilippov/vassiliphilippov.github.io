Etymology

turbulency comes from the old Latin root turba, meaning 'crowd' or 'confusion', and is also the base of words like disturb and turbulent. Imagine a crowd pushing and moving in every direction — that image shows why air or water can break into wild, mixed movement. So turbulency means chaotic, unpredictable motion in a fluid like air or water.
