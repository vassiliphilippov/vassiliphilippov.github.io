Etymology

Oil of cloves comes from the simple idea of getting oil from cloves, the small dried flower buds with a very strong smell. People pressed or soaked cloves to make this strong oil and used it to 'numb' tooth pain and to 'flavor' medicines, so today oil of cloves means the clove oil used for flavoring and for soothing.
