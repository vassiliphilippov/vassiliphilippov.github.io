Etymology

European shrike is named for the bird's odd habit: it catches insects and small animals and sticks them on thorns or barbed wire. People compared this to a butcher hanging meat, so it got the nickname 'butcherbird', and that's why the name means 'a small predatory songbird that impales its prey'.
