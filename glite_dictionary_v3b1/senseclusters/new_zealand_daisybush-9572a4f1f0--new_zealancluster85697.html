Etymology

New Zealand daisybush gets its name from a simple picture: New Zealand shows where the plant comes from and daisy + bush shows it is a bush covered in daisy-like flowers. So the name means 'a bush with daisy-like blooms from New Zealand', which fits the fragrant white flower heads gardeners plant.
