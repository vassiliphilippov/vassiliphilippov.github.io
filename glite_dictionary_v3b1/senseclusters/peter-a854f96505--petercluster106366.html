Etymology

Peter comes from the Greek Petros 'rock' and the Aramaic Cephas 'rock'. Jesus gave this name to one apostle to show he would be the 'rock' or base of the church, which is why Peter is seen as the leader of the apostles and, in Catholic tradition, the first pope.
