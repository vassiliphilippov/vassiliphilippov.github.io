Etymology

Borago officinalis may come from an old Latin word Borago that meant something like 'wool' or 'hair', because the plant has very hairy leaves. The second word, officinalis, was used for plants sold in old medicine shops, so together the name points to a hairy herb people used as a remedy and also ate in salads.
