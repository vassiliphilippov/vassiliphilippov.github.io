Etymology

narrow-leaved white-topped aster is a simple plant description: narrow-leaved means it has thin leaves and white-topped means it has white flowers at the top. The word aster comes from a Greek word for 'star' because the flowers look like small stars, so the whole name tells you it is a kind of aster with narrow leaves and white tops.
