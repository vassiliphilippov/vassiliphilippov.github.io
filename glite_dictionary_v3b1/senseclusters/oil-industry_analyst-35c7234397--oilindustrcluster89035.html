Etymology

oil-industry analyst comes from a simple picture: oil (the product), the industry (the companies and oil fields), and an analyst (someone who studies numbers and news about them). So an oil-industry analyst 'explains' and 'reports' on oil market changes and gives 'advice' to people and companies making decisions.
