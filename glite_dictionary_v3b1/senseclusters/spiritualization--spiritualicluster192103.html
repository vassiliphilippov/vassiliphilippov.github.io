Etymology

The word spiritualization comes from spirit, a word that originally meant 'breath' in Latin, as in inspire which means 'to breathe into'. The parts -ize ('to make') and -ation ('the act of') show that spiritualization is the act of making something more 'spiritual' or giving it 'spirit'.
