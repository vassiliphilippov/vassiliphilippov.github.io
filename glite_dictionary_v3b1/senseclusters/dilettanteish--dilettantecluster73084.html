Etymology

dilettanteish comes from dilettante, which comes from an Italian verb meaning 'to delight', and the English word delight shows the same idea. The ending -ish means 'somewhat like', so dilettanteish describes a shallow or not-serious interest — someone who enjoys something for fun but does not study it deeply.
