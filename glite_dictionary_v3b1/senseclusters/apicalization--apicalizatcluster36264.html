Etymology

apicalization comes from apical, which comes from the Latin apex meaning 'tip' or 'top', and the ending -ization meaning 'the act of'. So apicalization is the act of using the 'tip' of the tongue, called the apex, to touch the roof of the mouth and make a consonant sound.
