Etymology

family Lauraceae comes from the old Latin word laurus, the laurel tree whose leaves were used for victory crowns, and the ending -aceae shows it is a plant family. So, family Lauraceae means the group of related trees and shrubs like laurel, cinnamon, and avocado.
