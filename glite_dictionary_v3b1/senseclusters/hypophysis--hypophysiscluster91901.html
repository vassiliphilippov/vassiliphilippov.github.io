Etymology

The word hypophysis comes from Greek parts hypo- meaning 'under' and physis meaning 'growth' or 'nature', as in hypodermic meaning 'under the skin'. That's why the hypophysis is the pituitary gland, a small organ under the brain that controls hormones.
