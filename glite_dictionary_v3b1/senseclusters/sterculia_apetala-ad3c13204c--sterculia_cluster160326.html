Etymology

Sterculia apetala may come from an old Latin word for 'dung' because some related trees have a strong bad smell, and it is also called the 'Panama tree' for its very large leafy crown and bare trunk. So the name helps you remember a big, shade-giving tree that is common in Panama and may even have helped give the country its name.
