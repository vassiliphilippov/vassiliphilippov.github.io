Etymology

Vladimir Ilich Lenin was the public name he used: he was born Vladimir Ulyanov, Ilich means 'son of Ilya', and he chose Lenin—probably after the River Lena—as a secret name. He used Vladimir Ilich Lenin when he led the Bolsheviks, so today the name stands for 'the Russian revolutionary leader and first head of the Soviet Union'.
