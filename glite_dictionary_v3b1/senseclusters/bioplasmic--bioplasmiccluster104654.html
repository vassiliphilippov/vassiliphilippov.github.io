Etymology

bioplasmic joins bio- meaning 'life', as in biology, and plasm meaning 'formed substance', as in plasma the liquid part of blood. So a bioplasmic field is imagined as a kind of 'life substance' or energy around living things, which is why the word is used for supposed life energy.
