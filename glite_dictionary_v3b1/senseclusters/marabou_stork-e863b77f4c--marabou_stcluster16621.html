Etymology

marabou stork may come from a word for a holy man called a 'marabout' because early visitors thought the tall, bare-necked bird looked like a man in loose robes. The bird's soft feathers were later used to trim hats and dresses, so 'marabou' also came to mean fluffy fashion trim.
