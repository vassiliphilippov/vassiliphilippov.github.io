Etymology

phenylhydrazine combines two parts: phenyl, a common chemical name for a 'ring' group found in many molecules, and hydrazine, the name for a small, very 'reactive' nitrogen compound. So the name tells chemists it is a hydrazine with a phenyl ring attached, which helps explain why it reacts with sugars in lab tests and must be handled carefully.
