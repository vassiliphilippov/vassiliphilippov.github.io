Etymology

schnorkel comes from the German word Schnorchel, the same root as the English snorkel, and is linked to the verb schnorcheln meaning 'to snore' or make breathing sounds. Think of a long tube that lets a submarine 'breathe' while hiding under the water, which is why a schnorkel lets the submarine draw air from above the surface.
