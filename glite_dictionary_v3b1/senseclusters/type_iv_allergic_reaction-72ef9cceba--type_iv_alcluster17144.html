Etymology

type IV allergic reaction comes from a medical system that numbers different kinds of allergy; the label type IV, meaning 'four', is a reminder that this is the slow, late kind. It means the body's special immune cells make a 'delayed' reaction that appears hours or days after contact, not the quick antibody response.
