Etymology

courtesan comes from the simple word court, meaning 'royal court' where kings, nobles and their helpers lived. Over time the word came to mean a woman who lived at or near the court and used her ties to rich or powerful men to gain money or influence, so today a courtesan is a woman linked to wealthy or influential men.
