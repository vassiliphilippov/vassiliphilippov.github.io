Etymology

The noun cant dog comes from old logging words: cant was a partly cut or shaped log and dog was a metal spike used to hold wood. A cant dog is a pointed lever that grips a log so workers can roll or lift it, so the name reminds you it is a tool 'to move or handle logs'.
