Etymology

The word scramsax comes from old German words meaning 'cut' or 'wound' (scram-) and 'knife' (sax, the same root as the Old English seax and the name Saxon). So a scramsax is a 'cutting knife', which fits its use by Anglo-Saxon fighters and hunters.
