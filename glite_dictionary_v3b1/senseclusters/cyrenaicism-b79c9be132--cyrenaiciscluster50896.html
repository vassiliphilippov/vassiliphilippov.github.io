Etymology

Cyrenaicism comes from the ancient Greek town Cyrene, because the philosophers who began the idea lived there and were called Cyrenaics, who taught that life is about 'immediate pleasure'. So, Cyrenaicism is the name for the teaching that seeking pleasure now is the highest good, not planning for the future.
