Etymology

Tadeusz Andrzej Bonawentura Kosciuszko was a Polish military leader who fought for American independence and later led a national uprising in Poland. Because he fought for 'freedom' in both places, his name is remembered as a symbol of 'patriotism' and 'liberty'.
