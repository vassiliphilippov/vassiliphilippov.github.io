Etymology

Gilgamesh comes from ancient Sumer in Mesopotamia and likely combines old Sumerian parts gil and gamesh, which are thought to mean 'mighty' and 'man' or 'hero'. So, the name Gilgamesh fits the story: he is a 'mighty man' and the legendary king who stars in one of the earliest epics.
