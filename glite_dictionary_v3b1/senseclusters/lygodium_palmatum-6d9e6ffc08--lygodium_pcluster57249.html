Etymology

Lygodium palmatum may come from old Greek words meaning 'flexible vine' (Lygodium) and 'hand-shaped' (palmatum). That fits the plant: it climbs with twisting stems and has fronds that spread like a hand, so the name describes this delicate climbing fern.
