Etymology

curly pondweed gets its name from how the plant looks and where it grows: its leaves are wavy, so curly, and it lives in a pond, while weed shows it is a spreading water plant often called a 'weed' because it can be hard to remove. So the name describes a water plant with wavy leaves.
