Etymology

Blighia sapida is named after Captain William Bligh; one popular story says sailors like him helped bring the tree's fruit from West Africa to the Caribbean, so the name reminds us of its travel and history. The second word sapida comes from a word meaning 'tasty', which fits the bright, edible fruit called 'ackee'.
