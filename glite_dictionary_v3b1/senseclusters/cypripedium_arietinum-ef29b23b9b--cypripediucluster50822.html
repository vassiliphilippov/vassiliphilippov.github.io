Etymology

Cypripedium arietinum has two parts: Cypripedium may come from an old word meaning a 'slipper' because the flower has a pouch like a small shoe, and arietinum comes from Latin for a 'ram' because the flower's lip can look like a ram's head. So the name gives a simple picture—a slipper-shaped orchid with a ram-like part—which explains why the plant has this name today.
