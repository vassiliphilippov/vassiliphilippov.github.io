Etymology

flying drainpipe The noun flying drainpipe comes from World War II when people saw a long, metal tube that flew and looked like a house's drainpipe. Because the V-1 and similar weapons used a simple 'jet engine' that made a loud buzzing sound, people began calling each such tube a flying drainpipe, and the name now means a simple, tube-shaped 'jet engine'.
