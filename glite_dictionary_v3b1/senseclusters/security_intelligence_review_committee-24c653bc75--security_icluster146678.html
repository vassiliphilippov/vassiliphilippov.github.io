Etymology

Security Intelligence Review Committee is a name that tells its job: Security ('safety'), Intelligence ('secret information'), Review ('to check'), and Committee ('a small group'). So, it means a group that works on its own to check Canada's secret service and make sure the law and people's rights are respected.
