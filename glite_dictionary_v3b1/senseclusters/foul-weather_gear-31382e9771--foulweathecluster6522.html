Etymology

The compound foul-weather gear comes from a simple picture: people putting on special gear when foul, an old word for 'bad', weather arrives. Sailors and outdoor workers wore heavy coats, hats and boots to stay dry and warm, so the phrase now means 'clothes and equipment' that keep you dry and warm in bad weather.
