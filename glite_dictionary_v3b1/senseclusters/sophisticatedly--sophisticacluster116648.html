Etymology

The word sophisticatedly comes from the root soph- meaning 'wise', like the soph- in philosophy, and the ending -ly means 'in a ... way'. That's why someone who acts sophisticatedly shows worldly taste or polished manners, doing things 'in a cultured or refined way'.
