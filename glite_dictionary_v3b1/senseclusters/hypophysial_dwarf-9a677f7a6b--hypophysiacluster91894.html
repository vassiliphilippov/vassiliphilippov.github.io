Etymology

hypophysial dwarf The term hypophysial comes from an old name for the small gland at the base of the brain that makes 'growth hormone', and dwarf means a very short person. So a hypophysial dwarf is someone very short because that gland does not make enough 'growth hormone', not because of 'genes'.
