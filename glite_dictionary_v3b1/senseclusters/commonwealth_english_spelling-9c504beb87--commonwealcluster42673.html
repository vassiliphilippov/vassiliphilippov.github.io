Etymology

Commonwealth English spelling comes from the fact that many countries in the Commonwealth kept the same rules for Englishspelling after they stayed linked to the UK. So it now means the usual spellings used in the UK and most Commonwealth countries, for example 'colour' instead of 'color'.
