Etymology

imatinib is a modern drug name made by scientists, and a helpful way to remember it is the ending -nib, which appears in many cancer medicines that 'block' certain proteins in cells. That's why imatinib is a medicine that 'blocks' cell signals and is used to treat some types of leukemia.
