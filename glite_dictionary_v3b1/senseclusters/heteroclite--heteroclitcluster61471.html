Etymology

heteroclite comes from old Greek parts hetero- ('other', as in heterogeneous) and -clite (from a root meaning 'lean' or 'bend', like 'incline'), so it paints a picture of 'leaning away from the usual' and therefore means 'irregular' or 'unusual'.
