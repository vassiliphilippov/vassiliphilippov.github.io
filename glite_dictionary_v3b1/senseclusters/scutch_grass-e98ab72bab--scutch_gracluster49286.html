Etymology

scutch grass may come from the old word scutch meaning 'to beat' or 'to scrape', a word used when people cleaned flax. One popular story says the grass was called that because it forms a tough, flattened mat that looks as if it has been beaten down, so the name stuck for this strong, fast-spreading lawn grass.
