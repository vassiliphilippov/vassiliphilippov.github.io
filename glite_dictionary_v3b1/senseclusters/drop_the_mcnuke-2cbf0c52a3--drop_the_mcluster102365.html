Etymology

drop the McNuke may come from combining the image of 'drop the bomb' with a huge 'nuke': imagine the verb drop and a big, loud McNuke falling into a quiet room and the calm suddenly explodes. That's why it means to suddenly bring up a shocking or controversial topic that changes the whole conversation.
