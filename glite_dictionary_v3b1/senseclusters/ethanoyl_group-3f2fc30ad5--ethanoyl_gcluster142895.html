Etymology

ethanoyl group: The name ethanoyl group comes from ethanoic acid, the main acid in vinegar often called 'acetic acid'. It is the small piece CH3CO– left when that acid loses a hydrogen and sticks to other molecules, so the name helps you remember it is the vinegar-made part that attaches to a molecule and changes how it acts.
