Etymology

The word leptospirosis comes from the bacteria name Leptospira, made from Greek leptos 'thin' and Greek speira 'coil' because the bacteria are very thin and spiral-shaped, and the ending -osis means 'disease'. So leptospirosis is the disease caused by those thin, coiled bacteria.
