Etymology

foregather comes from the simple parts fore- and gather, where fore- means 'before' or 'in front' and gather means 'collect' or 'come together'. A helpful way to remember it is to imagine people 'gathering in front' of a place, which is why to foregather means to meet or to come together in one place.
