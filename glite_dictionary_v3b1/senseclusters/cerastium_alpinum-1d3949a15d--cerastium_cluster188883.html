Etymology

Cerastium alpinum may come from old Greek and Latin words. Cerastium comes from a Greek word meaning 'horn' because its small seed pod is pointy like a tiny horn, and alpinum means 'from the Alps' or 'mountain', so the name describes a small, horn-seeded plant that lives on mountains.
