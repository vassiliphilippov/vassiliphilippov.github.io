Etymology

propenonitrile breaks into two parts: propeno-, from propene, which means a 'three-carbon' molecule with a 'double bond', and -nitrile, from nitrogen, meaning it has a nitrogen-containing group. So, the name tells you it is a 'three-carbon' nitrile made from propene, which explains why it is a reactive liquid used in chemical synthesis.
