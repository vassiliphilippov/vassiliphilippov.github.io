Etymology

Habenaria nivea comes from Latin words that describe the plant: Habenaria may come from a Latin word meaning 'strap' because the flower has long narrow parts, and nivea means 'snowy' for its white blooms. So the name helps you remember this slender orchid with delicate white flowers.
