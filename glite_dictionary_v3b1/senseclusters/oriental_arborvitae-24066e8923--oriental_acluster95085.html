Etymology

Oriental arborvitae comes from old Latin words arbor and vitae, meaning 'tree of life', and Oriental shows it is 'from the East', meaning Asia. The picture of a small evergreen 'tree of life' explains why this name fits the tough Asian shrub often used in gardens and hedges.
