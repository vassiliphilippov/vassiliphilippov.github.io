Etymology

The word Cryptogrammataceae comes from Greek parts crypto- 'hidden', gramma 'letter', and the plant-family ending -aceae 'family'. Think of cryptic or cryptogram to remember 'hidden writing': the name points to the small hidden patterns on the leaves where the spores sit, so it names this family of ferns.
