Etymology

The term balance sheet analysis comes from the idea of a paper sheet that lists two sides: 'what a company owns' and 'what it owes', like a simple balance or scale. To do an analysis is to check that list carefully, so the phrase means studying the sheet to judge a company's 'financial health'.
