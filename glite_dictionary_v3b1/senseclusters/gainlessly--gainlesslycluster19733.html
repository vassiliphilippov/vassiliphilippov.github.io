Etymology

gainlessly comes from the simple parts gain + -less + -ly, where gain means 'profit' or 'benefit', -less means 'without', and -ly makes it 'in a way'. So, to do something gainlessly is to do it 'without profit' or 'in a way that brings no benefit', which explains its current meaning.
