Etymology

Feodor Dostoevski has a first name Feodor that comes from a Greek name meaning 'gift of God' and a last name Dostoevski that shows his family came from a place called Dostoevo because the ending '-ski' means 'from'. He was a Russian writer, and these name parts help us remember the famous novelist who wrote about deep human suffering and hard moral questions.
