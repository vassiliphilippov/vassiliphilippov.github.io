Etymology

Conuropsis carolinensis comes from two parts: Conuropsis meaning a 'small parrot' and carolinensis meaning 'from Carolina'. So the name tells us it was a little parrot from the Carolina area, the bird we now know as the extinct Carolina parakeet.
