Etymology

The phrase paradise on earth may come from old stories about the perfect Garden of Eden where everything was beautiful and happy. The word paradise meant that perfect garden, and on earth shows it is here in our world. So, calling a place a paradise on earth means it feels like that 'place of perfect beauty and happiness'.
