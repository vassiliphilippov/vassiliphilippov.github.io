Etymology

mossy saxifrage combines mossy (it grows like a soft green mat) and saxifrage, a word from old languages that means 'rock-breaker' because the plant often grows in cracks in rocks. So, the name gives a clear picture of a low, moss-like plant that likes rocks and helps you remember how it looks and where it grows.
