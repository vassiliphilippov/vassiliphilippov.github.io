Etymology

Adventitiously comes from the Latin advenire 'to come' and is related to the familiar word advent ('arrival'). A helpful way to remember it is to think of something that 'arrives' by chance or from outside, so adventitiously means 'happening by chance' or 'coming from an unexpected source'.
