Etymology

toxemia of pregnancy comes from the old idea that toxemia means 'poison in the blood', so people imagined something harmful in a mother's blood during pregnancy. That's why the phrase refers to the serious condition we now call 'preeclampsia', with high blood pressure and swelling.
