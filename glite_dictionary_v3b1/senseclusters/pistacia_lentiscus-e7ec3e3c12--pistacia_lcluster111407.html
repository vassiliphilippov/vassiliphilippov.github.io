Etymology

Pistacia lentiscus. The first part, Pistacia, comes from words for the pistachio family, and the second part, lentiscus, may come from old words for the 'mastic' resin the plant makes. So the name points to a small Mediterranean evergreen that people grow and collect for its sticky, aromatic resin.
