Etymology

Thousand Island dressing gets its name from the Thousand Islands, a group of small islands on the river between the US and Canada, where one popular story says a local cook mixed mayonnaise, ketchup and chopped pickles to make a creamy sauce for fishermen and visitors. So, because people liked that sauce it was called 'Thousand Island' and today the phrase means the creamy salad dressing you order or buy in restaurants.
