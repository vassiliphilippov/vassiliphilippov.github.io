Etymology

Dürer comes from the old German word Tür, which means 'door' and is the same idea as the English word 'door'. It probably began as a family name for someone who made or fixed doors, so a helpful way to remember Dürer is to imagine a 'door' and think of the artist as 'Mr Door'.
