Etymology

Pillage comes from an old French verb piller, and before that from Latin pilare 'to pluck', from pilus 'hair'. Imagine people 'plucking' things away like pulling hair, and you get the image of the violent act of stripping a place of its goods, which is why pillage means such stealing and destruction.
