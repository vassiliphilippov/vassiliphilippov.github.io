Etymology

external oblique muscle: The name external oblique muscle gives a clear picture: external means 'outside', oblique means 'slanted', and muscle is a soft band. This flat, slanted band on the side of the belly helps you 'twist' your upper body and 'pull in' the belly, so the name shows both where it is and what it does.
