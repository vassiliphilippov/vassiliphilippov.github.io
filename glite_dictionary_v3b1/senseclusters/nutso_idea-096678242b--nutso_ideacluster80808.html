Etymology

nutso idea comes from calling someone a nut, an informal word for a 'crazy' person, with '-so' added to make it stronger, so you picture an idea so wild only a 'nut' would have. That's why nutso idea means 'a crazy, eccentric, or wildly unconventional idea'.
