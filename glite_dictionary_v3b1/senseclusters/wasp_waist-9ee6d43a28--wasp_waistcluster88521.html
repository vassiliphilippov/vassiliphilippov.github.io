Etymology

The phrase wasp waist comes from the picture of a wasp with a very thin middle and a human waist made very small by tight clothes like a corset. So, people compared a tiny, squeezed waist to a wasp's body, and the phrase came to mean 'an extremely slender or narrow waist' and to suggest 'fashionable'.
