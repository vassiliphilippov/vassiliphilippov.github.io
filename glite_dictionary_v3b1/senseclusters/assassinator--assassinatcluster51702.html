Etymology

assassinator begins with assassin, a word that likely comes from Arabic hashishin, meaning 'hashish users', and one popular story says secret killers were given hashish, which gives a strong image of hidden, dangerous men. The ending -ator, as in creator, makes a word mean 'a person who does something', so assassinator is 'a person who kills secretly or suddenly'.
