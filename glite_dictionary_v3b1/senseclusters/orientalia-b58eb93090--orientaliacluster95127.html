Etymology

The word Orientalia comes from the Latin oriens, meaning 'rising' and used for the 'east' where the sun rises, and you can see the same root in the English word orient. The ending -alia means 'things about', so Orientalia is a fitting title for journals that collect articles about the languages, history, and cultures of the East.
