Etymology

capitalist economy comes from capital, meaning money or goods used to start or grow a business, and from capitalist, a person who owns that money. So, picture private people using their own money to run businesses and try to make 'profit', and you can see why the phrase means an economy where 'private' owners control trade and business rather than the state.
