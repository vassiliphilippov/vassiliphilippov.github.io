Etymology

so long, and thanks for all the fish comes from Douglas Adams' funny book The Hitchhiker's Guide to the Galaxy, where the dolphins leave Earth and send a polite note that says 'so long, and thanks for all the fish'. So today people use it as a joking way to say 'goodbye' and 'thanks' when they are leaving.
