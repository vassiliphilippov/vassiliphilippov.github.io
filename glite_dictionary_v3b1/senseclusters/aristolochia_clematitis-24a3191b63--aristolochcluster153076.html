Etymology

Aristolochia clematitis may come from old Greek words because people once used this plant to help women in childbirth. The odd curved yellow-green flower looks a bit like a small womb or baby, so folk healers called it a 'birth herb' and that image explains why the name is linked to 'help with birth'.
