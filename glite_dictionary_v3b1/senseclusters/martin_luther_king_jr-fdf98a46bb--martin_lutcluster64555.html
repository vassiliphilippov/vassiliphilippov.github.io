Etymology

Martin Luther King Jr. was born Michael King Jr., but his father later renamed him after the famous church leader Martin Luther, who started big religious change long ago. That's why Martin Luther King Jr. is remembered as a 'civil rights' leader who used 'peaceful protest' to fight for equality.
