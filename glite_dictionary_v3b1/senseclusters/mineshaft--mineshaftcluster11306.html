Etymology

mineshaft comes from two simple words: mine means a place where people 'dig for minerals' and shaft means a 'long, narrow pole' or 'narrow passage'. So a mineshaft is literally a long, narrow way down into a mine, which is why it means the deep vertical passage miners use to reach the mine or to bring fresh air underground.
