Etymology

Cassia fistula is often called the 'golden shower tree' because its long, hanging yellow flowers look like a bright shower of gold. Its long seed pods and sweet pulp were used in traditional medicine, so today the name makes you think of a tropical tree with showy yellow blooms and pods used as medicine.
