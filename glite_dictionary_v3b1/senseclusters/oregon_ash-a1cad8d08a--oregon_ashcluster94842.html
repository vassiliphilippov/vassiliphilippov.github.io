Etymology

Oregon ash names a kind of tree: Oregon shows the place where it grows, and ash means it is an ash tree, a 'type of tree' with hard, light wood. So the name simply means 'an ash tree from Oregon', which fits why people use its wood for furniture and sports equipment.
