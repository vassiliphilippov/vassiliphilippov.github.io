Etymology

Vaccinium corymbosum comes from old Latin and Greek words: Vaccinium was a Latin word for a 'berry' and corymbosum comes from a Greek word for a 'cluster' or 'bunch' of flowers. So the name simply shows a 'berry plant with flower clusters', which fits this shrub that produces many blueberries.
