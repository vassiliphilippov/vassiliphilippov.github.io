Etymology

hydronephrosis breaks into parts: hydro- 'water', nephro- 'kidney', and -osis 'condition', and hydro- is the same start you see in hydrate. So hydronephrosis literally means a 'water' condition of the kidney, which helps you remember that urine builds up and makes the kidney swell.
