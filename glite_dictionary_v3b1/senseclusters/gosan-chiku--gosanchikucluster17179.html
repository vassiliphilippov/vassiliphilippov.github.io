Etymology

gosan-chiku comes from Japanese and Chinese naming, and the second part chiku means 'bamboo', so the name is like saying 'gosan bamboo'. So remembering chiku as 'bamboo' helps you recall that gosan-chiku is a small, slender bamboo often used in gardens.
