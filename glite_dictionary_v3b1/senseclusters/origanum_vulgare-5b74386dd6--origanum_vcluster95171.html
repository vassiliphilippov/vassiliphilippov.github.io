Etymology

Origanum vulgare may come from old Greek and Latin words: Origanum was like 'mountain joy' because the herb grew on hills and smelled lovely, and vulgare means 'common' in Latin. So the name points to the everyday wild herb we call 'oregano' and helps explain why it is used to flavor food.
