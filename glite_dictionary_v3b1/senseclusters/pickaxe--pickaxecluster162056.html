Etymology

The word pickaxe comes from joining pick, meaning 'a sharp point', and axe, meaning 'a cutting tool', so it names a tool that has a pointed end for breaking and an axe-like side for cutting. That's why a pickaxe is a heavy tool used to break hard ground and rock.
