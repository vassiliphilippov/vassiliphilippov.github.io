Etymology

Mirounga is the scientific name for elephant seals and is thought to come from an old local or sailor name for these large sea mammals of the southern oceans. A helpful way to remember Mirounga is to link it to the seals' huge size and 'elephant-like' nose, because the name marks this special group of very large seals.
