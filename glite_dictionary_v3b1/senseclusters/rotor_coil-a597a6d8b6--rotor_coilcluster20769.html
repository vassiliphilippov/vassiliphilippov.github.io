Etymology

rotor coil combines rotor (the part that 'turns') and coil (a 'loop of wire'). Imagine a loop of wire wrapped on a wheel that spins inside a motor — that spinning wire is the rotor coil, and because it carries electricity while turning it helps the machine make motion or produce electricity.
