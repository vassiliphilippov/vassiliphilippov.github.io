Etymology

Pure eyewash comes from the image of a gentle liquid used to rinse the eye — a wash that only cleans the surface and does not fix the real problem. One popular story says people began to call any show or promise that only looks good but does nothing 'eyewash', so pure eyewash means 'something meant to deceive or make things seem better than they are'.
