Etymology

Thymelaeaceae comes from the plant name Thymelaea plus the scientific ending -aceae, which means 'family' in plant names. Thymelaea is thought to come from Greek words for 'thyme' and 'olive' because these plants often smell a little like thyme and have tough bark like olive wood, so the family name fits these shrubs and trees.
