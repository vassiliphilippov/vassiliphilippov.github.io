Etymology

Rubus hispidus comes from Latin words that describe the plant: Rubus means 'bramble' or 'blackberry' and hispidus means 'bristly' or 'hairy'. So the name shows a low shrub with rough stems and small dark berries, which is why it is called the bristly dewberry.
