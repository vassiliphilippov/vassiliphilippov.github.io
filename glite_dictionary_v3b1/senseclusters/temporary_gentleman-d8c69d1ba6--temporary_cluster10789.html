Etymology

temporary gentleman comes from World War I, when men from ordinary jobs were given short officer jobs and so were called temporarygentlemen. So the image of someone who is a 'gentleman' only for a short time explains why the phrase means a man who behaves politely or charmingly but does not stay that way.
