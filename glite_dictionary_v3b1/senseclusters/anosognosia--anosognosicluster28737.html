Etymology

anosognosia comes from Greek parts: a- 'not', nosos 'disease', and gnosis 'knowledge', and you can see gnosis in the word diagnosis, which means finding out a disease. So, anosognosia literally means 'not knowing a disease', which explains why it describes someone who is unaware of their own illness or disability.
