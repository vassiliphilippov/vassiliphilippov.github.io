Etymology

The word ionized comes from the root ion, a word for a 'charged particle', and the ending -ize, meaning 'to make'. So to be ionized is to be 'made' into charged particles, as when atoms gain or lose tiny parts called electrons, which is why ionized substances have an electric charge.
