Etymology

Origanum dictamnus may get its name from Mount Dikti in Crete, where the small herb with pink flowers grows, and the second word dictamnus links it to that mountain and the old name 'dittany'. People long ago used the plant to help heal wounds and other 'healing' needs, so the name became tied to its reputation and the strong, soothing smell it still has today.
