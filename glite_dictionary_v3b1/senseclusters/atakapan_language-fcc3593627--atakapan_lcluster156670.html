Etymology

Atakapan language comes from the name of the Atakapa people who once lived along the Gulf Coast of Louisiana and Texas, so the phrase simply means the language of that group. The language is now 'extinct' because no native speakers remain, which helps you remember that Atakapan language refers to a once-spoken local language that is no longer used.
