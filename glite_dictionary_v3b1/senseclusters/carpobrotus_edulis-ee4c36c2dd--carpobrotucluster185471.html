Etymology

Carpobrotus edulis gets its name from old Greek and Latin words meaning 'edible fruit'. The parts Carpobrotus, meaning 'fruit', and edulis, meaning 'edible', refer to the juicy fruit people once ate and made into jam, so the name helps you remember the plant's fruit is 'edible' today.
