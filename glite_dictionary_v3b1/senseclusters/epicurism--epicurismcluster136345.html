Etymology

epicurism comes from the name of the Greek thinker Epicurus, who said 'pleasure' is an important part of life, and it is related to the word epicure, which means a lover of fine food. So epicurism means living for 'pleasure', especially 'good food and drink' and enjoying fine things.
