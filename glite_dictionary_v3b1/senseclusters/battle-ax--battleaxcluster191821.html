Etymology

battle-ax comes from the real weapon named from battle ('fight') and ax ('a tool with a blade'), and people started using it as a strong name for a person—often a woman—who is very forceful and speaks sharply. So calling someone a battle-ax suggests they 'cut' others with words and are hard to argue with.
