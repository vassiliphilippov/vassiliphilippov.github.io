Etymology

The noun taxi dancer comes from old dance halls where customers paid for each dance. People compared these paid dances to hiring a taxi for a short ride, so the paid dancers were called 'taxi dancers'. That's why taxi dancer means 'a woman employed to dance with patrons for a fee per dance'.
