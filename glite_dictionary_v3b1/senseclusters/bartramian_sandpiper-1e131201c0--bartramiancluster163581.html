Etymology

Bartramian sandpiper gets its name from William Bartram, a naturalist long ago whose name was given to the bird, and sandpiper tells you it is a bird that walks on soft ground. So the name means 'the sandpiper named after Bartram' and is used for this large, plover-like bird seen in fields and uplands.
