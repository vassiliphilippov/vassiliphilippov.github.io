Etymology

pteroylmonoglutamic acid is the scientific name for the vitamin we call 'folic acid'. The long name may come from its parts: ptero meaning 'wing', mono meaning 'one' and glutamic acid, so you can picture a single 'wing' on a glutamate to help remember this particular form of vitamin B that helps cells grow and divide.
