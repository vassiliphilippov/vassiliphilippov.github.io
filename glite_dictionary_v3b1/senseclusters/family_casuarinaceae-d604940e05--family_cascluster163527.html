Etymology

family Casuarinaceae gets its name from the genus Casuarina, which a plant scientist named after the 'cassowary' bird because the trees' thin, drooping branchlets looked like the bird's feathers. So the name points to this group of trees and shrubs with fine, feather-like branches.
