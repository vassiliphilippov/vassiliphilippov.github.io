Etymology

reincarnate comes from Latin parts re- 'again' and carne 'flesh', the same 'carne' you see in carnivore which means 'meat-eater', so it literally means 'become flesh again'. A helpful way to remember the verb use for ideas or products is to imagine giving them a new 'body' or new life.
