Etymology

The word unbearableness breaks into parts: un- meaning 'not', bear meaning 'to carry' or 'to put up with', -able meaning 'can be', and -ness meaning 'the state'. So, imagine a very heavy load you cannot carry; that quality of being impossible to endure or tolerate is the 'unbearableness'.
