Etymology

Liposcelis is the scientific name for tiny insects known as booklice. A helpful way to remember it is to think of the parts lipo- meaning 'fat' and -scelis meaning 'leg', so imagine a small, rounded bug with short legs, which connects the name to these little booklice.
