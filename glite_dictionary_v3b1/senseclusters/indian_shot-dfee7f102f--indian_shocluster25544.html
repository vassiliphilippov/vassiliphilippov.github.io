Etymology

Indian shot may come from the plant's hard, round seeds that looked like the small balls people used in old guns, so the word shot was used and the plant was linked to the 'Indies' where it was first seen. Because the plant has a thick, edible root used for starch, the name Indian shot came to mean this edible canna plant.
