Etymology

genus Sepia comes from an old word 'sepia' that meant 'cuttlefish' and the dark brown 'ink' they make. Because artists and sailors used that ink as a brown dye, scientists later used the same word as the name for this group of cuttlefish, so genus Sepia now refers to that type of cuttlefish.
