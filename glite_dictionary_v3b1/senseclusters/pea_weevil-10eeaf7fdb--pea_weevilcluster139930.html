Etymology

Pea weevil comes from the simple image of tiny beetles that get inside pea seeds and whose young live there and eat the peas. Farmers often opened damaged peas and found the insects inside, so the name links pea (the seed) and weevil (a beetle) and now means 'a small beetle that damages pea crops'.
