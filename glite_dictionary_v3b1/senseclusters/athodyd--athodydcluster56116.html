Etymology

The word athodyd is a modern technical short form built from parts that mean 'air' (aero-, as in aeroplane), 'heat' (thermo-), 'movement' (dynamic) and 'pipe' (duct, as in an air duct). So, an athodyd is like an 'air-heat pipe' engine with no moving parts, which is why it must be started or launched at high speed to work.
