Etymology

Atlantic herring was named simply for the fish: it is the herring that lives in the Atlantic Ocean and moves in huge, shiny schools, so fishermen caught it for food. That's why today Atlantic herring means the species of herring from the Atlantic that people use as a 'food fish'.
