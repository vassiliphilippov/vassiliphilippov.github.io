Etymology

The meaning of rub as a problem comes from the idea of rubbing that causes 'friction' or a stop in movement, and one famous line in Shakespeare used 'the rub' to mean 'the difficulty'. So today 'the rub' is used for the unexpected snag that makes a plan more complicated.
