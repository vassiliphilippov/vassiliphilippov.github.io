Etymology

Erysimum allionii may come from two name parts: the first part Erysimum comes from an old Greek word for this group of flowering plants, and the second part allionii is 'named after' the Italian botanist Carlo Allioni. So the full name is the scientific name people use for this bright, orange-yellow garden flower.
