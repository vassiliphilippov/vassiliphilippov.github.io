Etymology

Lake Tana may come from a local word meaning 'source' or 'spring', because this large Ethiopian lake is where the Blue Nile begins. Picture a wide lake with many islands and old churches giving water to the river, and that image helps you remember why the name is linked to being a river 'source'.
