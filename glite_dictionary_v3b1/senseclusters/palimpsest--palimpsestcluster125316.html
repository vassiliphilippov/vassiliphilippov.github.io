Etymology

palimpsest comes from the Greek term palimpsestos, made of palin 'again' (the same palin you see in palindrome) and psēstos 'to scrape', so it literally meant 'scraped again'. Originally it named a piece of writing that was scraped so new text could be put on it, and today it describes anything that shows older layers still visible under newer ones, like a book page, a city, or a digital file.
