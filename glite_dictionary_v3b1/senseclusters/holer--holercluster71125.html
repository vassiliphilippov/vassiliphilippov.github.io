Etymology

holer comes from the simple word hole, meaning 'a hollow', plus the ending -er, meaning 'one who does' or 'a tool that does', as in baker or a 'drill'. So a holer can mean a tool that makes holes, which explains its use for a device used to make holes in wood, soil, or other materials.
