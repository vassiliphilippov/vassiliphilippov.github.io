Etymology

gimcrack names a showy but worthless trinket, and its exact origin is unclear. A helpful way to remember it is to think of gim sounding like gem and crack meaning 'broken', so a 'broken gem' looks nice but has little value, which is why gimcrack means a cheap ornament.
