Etymology

pharyngoplasty comes from two parts from Greek: pharyng- meaning 'throat' and -plasty meaning 'to shape' or 'repair'. It shares the same root as plastic ('made or shaped'), so pharyngoplasty means 'shaping or repairing the throat', which is why it refers to throat surgery.
