Etymology

Zoomastigina comes from Greek parts zoo- 'animal' and mastig- 'whip', and you can remember zoo- because it is the same as the English word zoo. It was used for single-celled, animal-like organisms that move with whip-like tails called flagella, so the name helps you remember their 'animal' nature and how they swim, though this group name is no longer used in modern biology.
