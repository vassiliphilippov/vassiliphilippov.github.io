Etymology

Oregon pine comes from the big evergreen trees that grew in Oregon and were sent as wood for building; workers called them pine because the wood looked and worked like pine even though they are a different type of tree. So today Oregon pine means 'a large evergreen tree with strong timber', often the Douglas fir used for construction.
