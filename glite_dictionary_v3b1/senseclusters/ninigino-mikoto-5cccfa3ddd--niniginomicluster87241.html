Etymology

The word Ninigino-Mikoto is a Japanese name that shows he is a divine ancestor: Ninigi is his name, no means 'of', and Mikoto is an old title meaning 'honored' or 'divine'. So the name tells us he is a respected, godlike founder and the grandson of the sun goddess Amaterasu.
