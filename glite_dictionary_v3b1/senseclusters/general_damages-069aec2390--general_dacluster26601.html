Etymology

general damages comes from two words: general ('not specific') and damages ('money for harm'). Imagine two piles after an accident — one with exact bills you can add up, and one that is a general payment for pain, suffering or loss you cannot easily count in money. So general damages means the money a court gives for harm that is hard to put a price on.
