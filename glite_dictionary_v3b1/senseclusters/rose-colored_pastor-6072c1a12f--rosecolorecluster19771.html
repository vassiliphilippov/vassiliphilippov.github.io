Etymology

The name rose-colored pastor comes from two simple images: rose-colored points to the bird's pink back, and pastor is an old word meaning 'shepherd' used in bird names. So the name paints a clear picture and simply describes a glossy black bird with a pink back, also called the 'rosy starling'.
