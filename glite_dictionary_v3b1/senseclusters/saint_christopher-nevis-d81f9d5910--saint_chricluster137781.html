Etymology

Saint Christopher-Nevis comes from the two islands: Saint Christopher and Nevis. Saint Christopher was named for the saint who protects travellers, and Nevis comes from the Spanish phrase 'Our Lady of the Snows' because the island's mountain often wears a white cloud, so the country name simply joins the two island names.
