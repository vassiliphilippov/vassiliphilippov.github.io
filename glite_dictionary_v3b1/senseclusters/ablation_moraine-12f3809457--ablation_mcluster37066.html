Etymology

ablation moraine shows a simple picture: ablation means the ice is 'melting' or 'wearing away', and a moraine is a pile or layer of rocks on the ice, so as the glacier melts the loose rocks are left behind. That's why the term means a layer of rock debris sitting on the surface of a glacier.
