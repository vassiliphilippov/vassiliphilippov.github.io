Etymology

bristlelike comes from bristle, the stiff hairs on a brush or toothbrush, and the ending -like, which means 'similar to'. So something bristlelike is 'similar to a bristle' — thin, stiff and hairlike, like the tiny hairs on a toothbrush or the spines of a cactus.
