Etymology

nervus trigeminus comes from Latin: nervus means 'nerve' and trigeminus comes from parts meaning 'three' and 'twin', so it pictures a nerve that splits into three main branches. That's why the name fits the large face nerve that gives feeling to the face and helps with chewing.
