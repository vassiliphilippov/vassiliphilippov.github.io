Etymology

melodramatize comes from the familiar words melody meaning 'song' and drama meaning 'play', plus the verb ending -ize meaning 'make'. So to melodramatize something is to 'make' it like a loud, very dramatic stage play, which explains why it means to treat something in an exaggerated, overly dramatic way.
