Etymology

Hyperglyphe perciformis comes from older scientific words that describe the fish's look: Hyperglyphe may mean 'big carving' or 'deep groove' and perciformis means 'perch-like'. So the name paints a simple picture of a dark, deep-bodied fish that looks like a large perch, which helps explain why this species is called the barrelfish.
