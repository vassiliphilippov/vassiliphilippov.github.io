Etymology

crepe Suzette may come from a famous kitchen story about a young woman named Suzette: a chef poured butter, orange juice and a little brandy over a thin crepe and the sauce accidentally caught fire. That's why the name now means a thin pancake with a bright orange sauce that is often 'set on fire' at the table.
