Etymology

The color name vinegarish green comes from vinegarish meaning 'like vinegar' and green for the color. Imagine pickles or olives soaked in vinegar — they get a sharp, slightly yellowish-green look, so the name means a green that looks 'sour' or a bit yellow, not a clear bright green.
