Etymology

misocainea comes from Greek parts miso- meaning 'hate' and kainos meaning 'new', so it literally means 'hate of the new'. Think of miso- like in misogyny for 'hate' and kainos like the 'neo-' in neon for 'new', which is why misocainea means a strong dislike of new ideas or change.
