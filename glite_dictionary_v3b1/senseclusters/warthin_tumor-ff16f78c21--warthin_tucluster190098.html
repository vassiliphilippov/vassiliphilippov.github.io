Etymology

Warthin tumor is named after the American doctor Aldred Warthin, who first described this painless lump in the salivary gland in the early 1900s. So when doctors say Warthin tumor they mean 'a usually non-cancerous salivary gland lump', and the name is simply the doctor's label for that specific type of lump.
