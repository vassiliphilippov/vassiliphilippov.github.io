Etymology

Ninurta is the name of an ancient Mesopotamian god of war, hunting and farming who was also seen as a 'sun' god. A helpful way to remember him is to picture a strong hero who fights chaos and teaches people to grow crops, so Ninurta is both a warrior and a bringer of agriculture and is sometimes linked to the biblical Nimrod.
