Etymology

Dutch-processed cocoa comes from a method made long ago in the Netherlands where a maker used a mild chemical to change cocoa so it was less sour and darker. So Dutch-processed cocoa literally means cocoa that was Dutch 'processed', that is, 'treated' in that special way, and that is why it tastes milder and looks richer.
