Etymology

The word cyanamid comes from two parts: cyano- (from cyan, meaning 'blue') and -amid (from 'ammonia' or 'amide'). So cyanamid is a name for a nitrogen chemical related to ammonia, which helps explain why it is an acid used in chemistry.
