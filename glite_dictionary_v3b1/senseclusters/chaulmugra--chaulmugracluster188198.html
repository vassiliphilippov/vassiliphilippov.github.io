Etymology

chaulmugra comes from a local Indian name for a tree whose seeds produce a 'healing oil' long used in traditional medicine in India and Burma. English took that local name for both the plant and the oil, so chaulmugra now means the tree (Hydnocarpus wightiana) and the medicinal oil once used to treat leprosy.
