Etymology

The word dermoskeleton comes from dermo- meaning 'skin' and skeleton meaning 'support' or 'frame', like the bones that hold a body up. So a dermoskeleton is a hard outer 'skin-frame', such as plates, scales, or shells, that protects and supports an animal.
