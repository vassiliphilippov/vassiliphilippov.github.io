Etymology

common matrimony vine may come from an old idea that its twisting branches and bright berries were used in wedding gifts or charms for 'marriage' and 'having children'. The word matrimony points to 'marriage' and vine shows it is a climbing shrub, so the name helps us remember this plant with spiny branches and orange-red berries.
