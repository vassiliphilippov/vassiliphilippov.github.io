Etymology

inadequately starts with in- meaning 'not' and contains adequate, which comes from a root meaning 'equal' or 'good enough', like the familiar word equal. The ending -ly turns it into a way of doing something, so inadequately means doing something 'not well enough' or 'not enough'.
