Etymology

Zea mays indurata comes from Latin words: Zea mays means corn and indurata comes from a word meaning 'hardened'. So the name gives the image of corn with a hard outer shell on each kernel, which is why it refers to 'flint corn', a corn with very hard kernels used for food and decoration.
