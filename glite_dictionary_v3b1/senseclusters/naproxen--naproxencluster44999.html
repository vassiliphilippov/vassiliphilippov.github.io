Etymology

The word naproxen comes from parts of its chemical name: naphth- from the chemical 'naphthalene', which you may know from 'mothballs', plus -prox- from 'propionic' acid and -en as a common ending. So the name points to the drug's chemical make-up, which is why naproxen is the name of this pain and inflammation medicine.
