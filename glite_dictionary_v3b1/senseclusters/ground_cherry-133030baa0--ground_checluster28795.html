Etymology

ground cherry comes from the plant's small, cherry-like fruit that grows close to the ground inside a thin paper husk. The words ground (it grows low) and cherry (the fruit looks like a tiny cherry) explain the name, so it simply means this small 'fruit' that grows near the earth.
