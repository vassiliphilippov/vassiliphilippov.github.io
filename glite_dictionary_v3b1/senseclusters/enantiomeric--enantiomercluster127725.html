Etymology

enantiomeric comes from parts meaning 'opposite', called enanti-, and 'part', called -mer, a part you also see in polymer. So an enantiomeric molecule is a mirror image of another — you cannot put one on top of the other and have them match — and that is why mirror-image molecules can behave very differently, for example in drugs.
