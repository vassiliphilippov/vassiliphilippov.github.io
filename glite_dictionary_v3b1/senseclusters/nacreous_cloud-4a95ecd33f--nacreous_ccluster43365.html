Etymology

nacreous cloud The name nacreous cloud comes from nacreous, which comes from the word 'nacre' for the shiny inside of a pearl. These clouds are high in the sky and, when the sun is just below the horizon, they catch the light and look pearly, rainbow colours, so the name now means a bright, pearl-like cloud.
