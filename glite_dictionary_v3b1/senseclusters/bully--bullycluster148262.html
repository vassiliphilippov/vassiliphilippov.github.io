Etymology

The adjective bully once meant 'excellent' or 'first-rate' and came from the old Dutch word boel meaning 'friend' or 'good fellow', so it was a way to praise something or someone. That's why calling an idea or event bully used to mean it was very good, though this use is now old-fashioned.
