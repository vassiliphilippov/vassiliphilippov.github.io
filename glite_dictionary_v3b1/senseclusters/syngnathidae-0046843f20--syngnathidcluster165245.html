Etymology

Syngnathidae comes from old Greek parts: syn- 'together' and gnathos 'jaw', and the ending -idae shows it is a family of animals. That's why these fishes have a name that means 'joined jaws', because many have long, tube-like mouths with the jaws joined together into a snout.
