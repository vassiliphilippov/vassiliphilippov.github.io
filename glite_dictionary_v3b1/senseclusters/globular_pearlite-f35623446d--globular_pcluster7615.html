Etymology

Globular pearlite is a name for a steel pattern where globular means 'round' and pearlite is the mix of soft and hard parts that usually makes thin 'layers'. When steel cools very slowly, the hard parts form tiny round 'globules' like little 'pearls' instead of 'layers'. So globular pearlite describes this round, slow-cooled structure and shows the metal cooled slowly and will behave differently.
