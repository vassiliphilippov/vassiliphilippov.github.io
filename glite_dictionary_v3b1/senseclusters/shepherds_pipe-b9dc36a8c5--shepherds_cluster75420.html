Etymology

Shepherd's pipe comes from the simple picture of a shepherd playing a small pipe or flute to call, calm, or entertain his sheep, and people began to call the instrument the shepherd's pipe. That's why it means 'a simple traditional wind instrument' often played by shepherds.
