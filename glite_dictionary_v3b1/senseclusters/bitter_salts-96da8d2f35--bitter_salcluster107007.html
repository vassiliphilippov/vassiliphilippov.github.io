Etymology

bitter salts gets its name from old Epsom salt, a white crystal people used as a home medicine; the crystals tasted bitter and like salts, so they were called 'bitter salts'. That's why the name now refers to the medicinal salt used 'to relieve constipation' and sometimes in hospitals 'to prevent seizures'.
