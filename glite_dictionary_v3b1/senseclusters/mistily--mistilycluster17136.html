Etymology

mistily comes from the simple word mist, which means 'fog', plus the adverb ending -ly, so it literally means 'in a fog-like way'. Think of trying to explain something while looking through mist—details are hard to see, which is why mistily means 'vaguely' or 'unclearly'.
