Etymology

areca nut comes from local names in South and Southeast Asia for the small seed of the betel palm, the hard nut people wrap in betel leaf with lime to chew. Because it has long been chewed as a mild 'stimulant' and in social ceremonies, the name now simply means that seed used for chewing.
