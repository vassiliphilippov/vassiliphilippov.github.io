Etymology

The composer Erik Satie often wrote very short, simple piano pieces and added odd performance notes and titles that surprised listeners. Because he chose plain, calm sounds instead of big, dramatic music, people remember him as an early maker of 'minimalism' and a rebel against the older 'Romantic' style.
