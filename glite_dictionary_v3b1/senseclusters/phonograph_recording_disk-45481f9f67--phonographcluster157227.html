Etymology

phonograph recording disk combines phonograph, recording, and disk: the phonograph is the old machine that plays sound, recording means saving sound, and disk is a flat round plate. Early sound was 'written' as tiny grooves on the disk so a machine could play it again, so the name means a flat disk used 'to record and play back sound'.
