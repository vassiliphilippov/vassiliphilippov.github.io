Etymology

Ecce Mono got its name after a badly failed restoration of a painting of Jesus that made the face look like a monkey. People used the joke because mono means 'monkey' in Spanish and the original title Ecce Homo means 'behold the man'. That's why Ecce Mono now names the funny picture and is used for jokes about bad restorations.
