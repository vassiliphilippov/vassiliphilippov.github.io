Etymology

The phrasal verb give off comes from the simple image of an object sending something away: the verb give plus the small word off shows the thing moves out, as when a flower gives off a smell or a fire gives off heat. So, give off means 'to produce or send out' smells, light, heat, gas, or feelings.
