Etymology

fille de chambre comes from French and literally means fille ('girl') of the chambre ('room'). In old hotels and large houses a young woman with that name cleaned and looked after the bedrooms for guests, so the phrase came to mean 'a maid who cleans bedrooms' and is now often used in historical contexts.
