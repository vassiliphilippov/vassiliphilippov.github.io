Etymology

ondansetron is a modern drug name, and a helpful way to remember it is the ending -setron, which connects to 'serotonin', the brain chemical that can cause feelings of nausea. Many medicines that stop vomiting share -setron, so ondansetron is a medicine that blocks that signal and helps prevent nausea and vomiting.
