Etymology

The word scoldingly comes from the verb scold, which goes back to old words meaning 'to tell someone they are wrong' or 'to shame' them. The ending -ly, like in quickly, turns a word into an adverb, so scoldingly means doing something 'in a scolding way' or 'showing disapproval'.
