Etymology

Kalmia latifolia is a plant name made from a person's name and a Latin description: Kalmia honors the Swedish plant collector Pehr Kalm, and latifolia means 'broad leaves'. So the name gives a simple picture — the Kalmia with 'broad leaves', which helps you remember it is the 'mountain laurel' shrub with shiny, wide leaves.
