Etymology

hidden clue gives a simple picture: imagine a small note tucked under a rug or a tiny object hidden inside a book, so it is there but hard to see. The word hidden means covered and clue means a small hint. So, because you must look carefully or search to find it, a hidden clue means 'a clue that is not obvious'.
