Etymology

Inocor mixes two simple parts that show what the drug does: ino- comes from an old Greek word meaning 'muscle' and cor is Latin for 'heart'. So Inocor is a name that suggests 'helping the heart muscle', which fits a medicine that makes the heart pump stronger.
