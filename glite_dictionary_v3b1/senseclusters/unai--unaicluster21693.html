Etymology

unai comes from a Native South American language and was borrowed into Portuguese and Spanish as the 'local name for the two-toed sloth'. A helpful way to remember it is to imagine people in the rainforest using that simple name for the animal they saw, so unai today means that small, two-toed sloth.
