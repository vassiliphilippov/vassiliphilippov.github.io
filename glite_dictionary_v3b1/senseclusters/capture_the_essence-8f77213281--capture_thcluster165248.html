Etymology

The phrase capture the essence uses the literal ideas capture, meaning to catch, and essence, meaning the basic smell or heart of something. Imagine catching a flower's scent and putting it in a bottle to keep its true smell. That's why capture the essence means 'to show the most important qualities' of something.
