Etymology

John Dos Passos can look like the phrase 'two steps' because dos and passos resemble the words for 'two' and 'steps' in Spanish/Portuguese, so imagine him walking across the country to see many lives. He was an American novelist best known for the 'U.S.A.' trilogy that shows life across the United States, and that walking picture helps you remember his books about American life.
