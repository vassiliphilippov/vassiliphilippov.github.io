Etymology

milk of magnesia comes from its white, milky look and from magnesia, an old name for the magnesium mineral found near a place called Magnesia. Because the medicine is a milky liquid made from that mineral, milk of magnesia now means the common 'antacid' and 'laxative' people use for heartburn or constipation.
