Etymology

camanchaca comes from a local Indigenous name in northern Chile for the thick coastal 'fog' that moves in from the Pacific Ocean. Because people there used the word to talk about the sea 'mist' that gives water to desert plants, camanchaca now means that special dense coastal fog.
