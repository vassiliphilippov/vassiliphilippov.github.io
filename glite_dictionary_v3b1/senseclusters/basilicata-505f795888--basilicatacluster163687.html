Etymology

Basilicata likely comes from basilica, a word that goes back to Greek basileus meaning 'king' and later came to mean a 'large church'; one helpful way to remember it is to imagine the region as land of 'royal' churches or imperial power. That's why the name Basilicata fits a historic region in southern Italy.
