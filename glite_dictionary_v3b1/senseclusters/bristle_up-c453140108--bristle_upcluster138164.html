Etymology

The phrase bristle up comes from the image of short, stiff hairs called bristles standing up on an angry or scared animal. When a person suddenly acts like those raised bristles, looking hard, prickly and defensive, they 'show anger' or 'take offence', so bristle up means 'to show anger or take offence'.
