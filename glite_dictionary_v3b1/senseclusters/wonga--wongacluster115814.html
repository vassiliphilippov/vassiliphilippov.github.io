Etymology

wonga as slang for money appeared in British English in the 1900s and people are not sure exactly where it started; one idea is that it came from Romani or other immigrant words, but a helpful way to remember it is to treat wonga like 'cash' or 'dough'. That's why wonga is used today to mean 'money'.
