Etymology

Duns Scotus is the name of the scholar John Duns: Duns is his family or place name and Scotus is Latin for 'the Scot', so the name literally means 'Duns the Scot' and is used for this famous Scottish thinker. He was nicknamed the 'Subtle Doctor' because people thought his ideas were very careful and clever, which helps you remember him.
