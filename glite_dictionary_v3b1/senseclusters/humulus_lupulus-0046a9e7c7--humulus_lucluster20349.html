Etymology

The plant Humulus lupulus gets its name from old Latin words: humulus ('small ground plant') and lupulus ('little wolf'). One popular story says hops climbed and seemed to 'bite' other plants like a little wolf, and because its flowers are used to flavor beer the name now refers to the hop plant used in brewing.
