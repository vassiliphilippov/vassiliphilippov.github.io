Etymology

gouty arthritis may come from an old idea about gout and arthritis: people long ago thought small 'drops' of bad fluid fell into a 'joint' and caused sudden pain, so the word gout comes from a word for 'drop'. That's why gouty arthritis today means painful 'inflammation' in a joint, often from tiny crystals.
