Etymology

The saying fair suck of the sauce stick comes from the simple image of people passing a sauce bottle and taking a quick taste. To ask for a fair suck of the sauce stick was to ask for a fair turn, which is why the phrase now means 'an equal opportunity' or 'fair treatment'.
