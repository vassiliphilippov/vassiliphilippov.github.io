Etymology

Duralumin starts with dura, like the English word 'durable', meaning 'hard' or 'lasting', and ends with aluminum, the light metal. So, Duralumin is a metal mix that keeps aluminum's lightness but adds strength, which is why it is used for aircraft parts.
