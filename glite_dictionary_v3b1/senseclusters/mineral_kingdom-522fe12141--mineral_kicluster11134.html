Etymology

mineral kingdom comes from old ideas that divided nature into three 'kingdoms': animals, plants, and minerals. Imagining minerals as their own kingdom, a realm of rocks, gems and other 'non-living' things, makes it clear why the phrase means 'the group of all non-living natural substances'.
