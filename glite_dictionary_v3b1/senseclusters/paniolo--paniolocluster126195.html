Etymology

paniolo comes from the Hawaiian way of saying the Spanish word Español, which means 'Spanish'. When Spanish and Mexican cowboys came to Hawaii and taught the locals how to herd cattle, that name stuck, so paniolo now means a Hawaiian cowboy.
