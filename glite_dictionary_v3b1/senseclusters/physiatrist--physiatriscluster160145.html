Etymology

The word physiatrist comes from the parts physio- meaning 'body' or 'physical' and -iatrist meaning 'doctor' or 'healer', the same ending you see in psychiatrist. So a physiatrist is a doctor who treats the body's physical problems and helps people regain movement and strength.
