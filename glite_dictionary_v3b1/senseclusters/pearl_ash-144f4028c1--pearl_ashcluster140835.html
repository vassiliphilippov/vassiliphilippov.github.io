Etymology

The noun pearl ash comes from old ways of making a white salt from burned wood: when people washed and boiled the ash, it left small white lumps like pearls. These 'pearls' were actually a not-pure kind of salt used to make soap and 'help bread rise', so the name pearl ash came to mean that material used in baking and soap-making.
