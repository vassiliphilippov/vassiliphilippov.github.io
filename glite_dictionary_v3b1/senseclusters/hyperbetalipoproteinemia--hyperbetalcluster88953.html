Etymology

The word hyperbetalipoproteinemia breaks into parts: hyper- 'too much', beta 'a label for a particle that carries fat in the blood', lipo- 'fat', protein, and -emia 'in the blood'. So it means 'too much beta fat-protein in the blood', which is why it describes a condition with very high cholesterol.
