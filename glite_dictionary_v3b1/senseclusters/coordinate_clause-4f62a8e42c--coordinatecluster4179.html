coordinate clause

0.00/m5.6
A part of a sentence that has same importance as another and is joined by words like and or but
noun US / koʊˈɔːrdɪnət klɔːz /

Etymology

coordinate clause creates a picture of two small sentence parts standing side by side. coordinate means 'equal' and clause means a small sentence, so a coordinate clause is one of two or more clauses of equal importance joined by words like 'and' or 'but'.
