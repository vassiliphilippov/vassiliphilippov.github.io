Etymology

white lupine combines white (its flower colour) and lupine, which one popular story says comes from an old word for 'wolf' because people thought the plant 'took' food from the soil. Today white lupine is a white-flowered plant farmers grow to stop erosion and to improve the land, so the name helps you remember both its colour and that old 'wolf' idea.
