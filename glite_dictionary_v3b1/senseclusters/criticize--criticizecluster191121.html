Etymology

criticize comes from an old Greek word kritikos meaning 'able to judge' and from krinein meaning 'to decide'. You can see the same root in critic and critical, so to criticize is to 'judge' by pointing out faults, which explains its modern meaning of saying what is wrong with someone or something.
