Etymology

Acorus calamus comes from old words: Acorus may come from a word for 'cure' because people used the plant as a medicine, and calamus means 'reed' because its long, pointed leaves and roots look like reeds. So the name helps you remember it is a fragrant, reed-like plant used as a remedy.
