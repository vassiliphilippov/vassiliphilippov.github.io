Etymology

able-bodied seawoman comes from old sailors' talk: able-bodied described someone strong and able to do heavy work on deck, and seawoman meant a woman sailor. So the phrase means a woman sailor who is fully trained and 'qualified' to handle the hard jobs aboard a ship.
