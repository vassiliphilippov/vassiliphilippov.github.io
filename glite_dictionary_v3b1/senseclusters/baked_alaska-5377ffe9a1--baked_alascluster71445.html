Etymology

baked Alaska is a dessert with ice cream on cake covered in meringue that is quickly browned, so the outside is 'warm' while the inside stays 'frozen'. One popular story says chefs named it baked and Alaska to show this 'hot' outside and 'cold' inside contrast, so the name helps you remember the dessert.
