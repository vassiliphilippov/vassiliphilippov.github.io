Etymology

Lithophragma affinis comes from old Greek words: litho- meaning 'stone' and phragma meaning 'fence', giving the image of a small plant growing among rocks with flower parts that look like tiny divided fences. The second word affinis means 'related', so the name tells us this plant is close to other similar species.
