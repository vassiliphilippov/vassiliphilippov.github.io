Etymology

The word unblinkingly breaks into three simple parts: un- meaning 'not', blink meaning 'to close your eyes quickly', and the ending -ly which makes it describe how something is done. So to act unblinkingly is to do it 'without blinking', and that gives the clear image of doing something without surprise or emotion.
