Etymology

The phrase backroom negotiation comes from the image of people meeting in a quiet back room away from the public to talk and make a deal, and negotiation means a talk to reach an agreement. That's why it now means a 'secret' or 'behind-the-scenes' discussion to 'make decisions'.
