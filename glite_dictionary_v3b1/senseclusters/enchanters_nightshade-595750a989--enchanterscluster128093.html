Etymology

enchanter's nightshade may come from the idea that its small white flowers look almost 'magical' when they appear on the dark forest floor. The word enchanter's suggests a charm and nightshade points to plants that grow in shade, so the name helps you remember it is a delicate woodland herb that seems to 'enchant' shady places.
