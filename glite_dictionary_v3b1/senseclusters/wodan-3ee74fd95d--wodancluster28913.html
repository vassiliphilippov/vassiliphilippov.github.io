Etymology

The name Wodan comes from an old Germanic word meaning 'inspired fury' or 'wild spirit', and it is the same figure known as Odin or Woden in other northern stories. That's why he was seen as the chief god who could give fierce strength and sudden inspiration, and you can still see his name today in the English weekday Wednesday ('Woden's day').
