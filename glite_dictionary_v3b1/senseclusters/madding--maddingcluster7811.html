Etymology

madding comes from the simple word mad, which means 'crazy' or very upset, and the old -ing form turned it into an adjective for things that act wildly. Think of the phrase Far from the madding crowd — it means away from noisy, wild, and out-of-control life, so madding describes that frantic, crowded feeling.
