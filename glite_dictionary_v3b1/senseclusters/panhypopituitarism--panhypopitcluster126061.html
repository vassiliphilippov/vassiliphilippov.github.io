Etymology

panhypopituitarism breaks into pan- 'all', hypo- 'low', pituitary 'gland that makes hormones', and -ism 'condition'. So it means a condition where the pituitary gland makes very little or none of its hormones, which is why it affects growth, metabolism, and other vital functions.
