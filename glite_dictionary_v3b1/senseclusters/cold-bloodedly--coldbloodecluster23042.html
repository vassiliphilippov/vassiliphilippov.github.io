Etymology

cold-bloodedly comes from cold-blooded, made of cold + blood; people first used 'cold-blooded' for animals with cold blood, and later to mean acting 'without feeling' or pity. The -ly part turns it into a manner, so to do something cold-bloodedly is to do it calmly and cruelly, without feeling.
