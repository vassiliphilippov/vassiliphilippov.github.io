Etymology

gopherwood appears in the Bible and the first part, gopher, is an old Hebrew word we do not know for sure. A helpful way to remember it is to think of gopherwood as 'special tree wood', perhaps a strong, worked wood like cypress, which explains why it is named as the wood used to build Noah's Ark.
