Etymology

third cranial nerve names one of the nerves in the head: third because it is number three, cranial because it is in the head, and a nerve is like a thin cable that carries messages. Think of it as the number-three cable that 'moves the eye' and 'controls the pupil', which is why the term refers to the nerve that moves the eyeball and changes pupil size.
