Etymology

The noun galvanic pile comes from old experiments where metal made dead frogs' legs twitch; people called that effect galvanic after the scientist Galvani, and Alessandro Volta stacked metal discs into a pile to make a steady flow of electricity. So galvanic pile means an early 'battery' that made a steady 'electric current'.
