Etymology

extremes of wokeness combines extremes (the furthest limits) with wokeness, a word from the phrase 'stay woke' meaning to be 'awake' to social injustice; the picture is of people so alert to issues that they go to the far edge. That's why the phrase is used for very radical or 'excessive' forms of social awareness today.
