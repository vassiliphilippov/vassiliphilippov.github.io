Etymology

speech communication joins speech, meaning 'spoken words', and communication, meaning 'sharing ideas'. Imagine a person saying an idea and passing it to others like handing them a note—this picture helps you remember how talking shares information. That's why it means 'the process of conveying information or ideas through spoken language'.
