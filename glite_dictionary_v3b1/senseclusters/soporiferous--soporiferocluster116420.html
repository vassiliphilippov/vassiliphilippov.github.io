Etymology

soporiferous comes from two Latin parts: sopor meaning 'deep sleep' and -ferous meaning 'bearing' or 'carrying', the same 'fer' you see in transfer which means 'to carry'. So, soporiferous literally means 'carrying sleep' and is used for something that makes you sleepy.
