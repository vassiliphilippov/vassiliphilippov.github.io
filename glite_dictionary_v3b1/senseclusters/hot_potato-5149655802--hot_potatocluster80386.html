Etymology

The phrase hot potato may come from a children's game where a hotpotato was passed quickly from hand to hand because no one wanted to hold it. So, when people call an issue a hot potato, they mean everyone is trying to 'pass on' that difficult or awkward topic and 'avoid dealing' with it.
