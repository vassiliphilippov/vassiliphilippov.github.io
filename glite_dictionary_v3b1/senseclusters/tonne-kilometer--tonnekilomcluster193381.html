Etymology

tonne-kilometer joins tonne ('a large weight') and kilometer ('one thousand meters' of distance), so a helpful way to remember it is to imagine taking one heavy box one kilometer — that is one tonne-kilometer, a simple measure that combines weight and distance to show how much freight has been moved.
