Etymology

Ogcocephalidae comes from Greek parts ogco- meaning 'swollen' and -cephal- meaning 'head', and -idae which shows it is a 'family' name. These fish have a wide, flat head and a strange, bat-like body that often walks on the sea floor, so the name points to their big head and odd shape.
