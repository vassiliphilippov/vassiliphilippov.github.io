Etymology

Nitroglycerin comes from two parts: nitro- and glycerin. The nitro- part links to 'nitric' chemicals used in explosives, and glycerin means a 'sweet oil' found in soaps and medicines, so the name hints that nitroglycerin is an oily substance that can be very explosive but is also used carefully as medicine.
