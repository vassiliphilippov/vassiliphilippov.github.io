Etymology

ring-shaped is made of two easy parts: ring, meaning a circle or a jewelry ring, and -shaped, meaning 'in the form of'. So ring-shaped simply means 'in the form of a ring' or 'like a circle', which is why we use it for things that look like a loop or circle.
