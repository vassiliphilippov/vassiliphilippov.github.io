Etymology

Regional ileitis comes from doctors who saw patches of inflamed tissue in the ileum, the last part of the small intestine. The ending -itis means 'inflammation' and regional shows it affects certain areas, so the name explains why it means a long-term inflammation of that gut area now often called Crohn's disease.
