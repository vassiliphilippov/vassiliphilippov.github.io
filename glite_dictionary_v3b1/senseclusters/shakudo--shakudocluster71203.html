Etymology

shakudo as an adjective comes from the same Japanese parts aka 'red' and dō 'copper', so it means 'made of or like shakudo'. It describes a deep, dark finish made from a copper-and-gold mix used in Japanese metalwork, which is why we call objects 'shakudo' when they have that rich, dark look.
