Etymology

The name bergamot mint comes from its smell: bergamot is the name of a citrus fruit and mint is the herb, so the name describes a mint that smells like a citrus. That's why bergamot mint now means a mint plant with a fresh, orange-like scent often used in teas and potpourri.
