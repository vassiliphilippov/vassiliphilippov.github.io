Etymology

orale comes from Mexican Spanish órale, probably from the short call ora plus the little ending -le, and is related in feeling to ahora meaning 'now'. A helpful way to remember it is to think of a quick 'now?' or 'really?', which is why people use orale to show disbelief or ask for confirmation.
