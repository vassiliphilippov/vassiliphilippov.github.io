Etymology

promiscuousness comes from the Latin parts pro- ('before') and miscere ('to mix'), and it originally meant 'mixed together'. Think of the simple word 'mix' or miscellaneous: when people are mixed without careful choice, that is why promiscuousness means casual, indiscriminate sexual relations.
