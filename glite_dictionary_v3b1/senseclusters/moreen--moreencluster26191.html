Etymology

moreen names a heavy, ribbed cloth used for curtains and upholstery, and a helpful way to remember it is to think of the start more as 'more' — that is, 'thicker' or 'heavier' than ordinary fabric. So, moreen means a thick, durable material used for drapes and furniture.
