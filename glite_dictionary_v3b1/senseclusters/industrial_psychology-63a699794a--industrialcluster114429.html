Etymology

industrial psychology comes from the words industrial, meaning workplaces and factories, and psychology, meaning the study of people's minds. Imagine someone watching workers on a factory floor to find better ways to do the job and to help people feel and work better, so the phrase means 'the study of people's behavior in workplaces'.
