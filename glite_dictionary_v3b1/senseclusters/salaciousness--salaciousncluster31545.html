Etymology

The word salaciousness comes from the adjective salacious, from Latin salax meaning 'lustful', plus the ending -ness, which means 'state' or 'quality'. So salaciousness means the 'quality of being lustful' or of causing sexual interest, which is why we use it for obscene or sexually exciting behavior.
