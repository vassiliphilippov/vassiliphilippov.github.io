Etymology

startle reflex comes from the clear picture of a surprised baby: a sudden loud sound makes the baby fling the arms out and pull the legs in. The word startle (to be surprised) and reflex (a quick, automatic movement) explain why it means a 'fast, automatic reaction' to a sudden noise or surprise.
