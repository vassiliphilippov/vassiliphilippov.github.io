Etymology

Selective amnesia combines selective (choosing) and amnesia ('forgetting'). Imagine someone with a remote control who presses a button to erase only the bad memories, and that's why the phrase means 'forgetting' only certain events, often 'on purpose' or for a 'convenient' reason.
