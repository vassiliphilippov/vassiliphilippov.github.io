Etymology

Adam-and-Eve comes from the Bible's first people, Adam and Eve, and one popular idea says people gave this name because the plant's leaves or flowers often look like a little 'pair' or couple on the forest floor. So gardeners call this small, one-leaf orchid Adam-and-Eve because its look reminds people of a simple 'pair'.
