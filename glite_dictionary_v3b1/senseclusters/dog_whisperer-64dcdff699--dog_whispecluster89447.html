Etymology

The phrase dog whisperer may come from the older idea of a whisperer who calms animals by speaking softly, like the earlier term horse whisperer. The picture is of someone quietly talking and using gentle touch so the dog relaxes, so the phrase means 'a person who can calm and train dogs easily'.
