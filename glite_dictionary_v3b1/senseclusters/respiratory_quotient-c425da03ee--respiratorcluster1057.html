Etymology

respiratory quotient links respiratory meaning about breathing and quotient meaning a number you get when you divide one thing by another. So it is a simple 'ratio' that compares how much carbon dioxide the body gives off to how much oxygen it takes in, and that number helps show which fuel the body is using.
