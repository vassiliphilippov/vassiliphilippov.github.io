Etymology

pyroxylin comes from Greek parts pyro- 'fire' and xylon 'wood', so it paints the image of 'fire-wood' and helps you picture something that burns easily. That's why pyroxylin is the name for a very flammable treated plant material made from cellulose and known as nitrocellulose, used in early plastics, films, and lacquers.
