Etymology

Acrocomia vinifera may come from old Greek and Latin words: Acrocomia points to the palm's high 'crown' of leaves and vinifera means 'vine-bearing' or 'wine-bearing' because its fruit or sap was used to make drinks. So the name helps you remember a palm with a showy top that also gives fruit useful for food and drink.
