Etymology

Allium scorodoprasum may come from old Latin and Greek words that mean 'garlic' and 'leek': Allium points to the onion family and scorodoprasum mixes the words for 'garlic' and 'leek'. So the name is an easy memory hook — it is a wild leek with a mild, garlicky taste and is used like regular leeks today.
