Etymology

The word base wallah comes from South Asian English. Here wallah, from Hindi/Urdu, means 'a person who does a particular job' and base is the place where people work, so base wallah literally names a person who works at a base. That's why today base wallah is used for someone who looks after supplies and daily tasks at a base.
