Etymology

Betula cordifolia comes from Latin words that describe the tree: Betula means 'birch' and cordifolia means 'heart-shaped leaves'. The name shows its white, peeling bark and heart-shaped leaves, so it helps you remember this small birch often used for baskets and toy canoes.
