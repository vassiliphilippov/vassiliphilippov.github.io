Etymology

The word hoo-ha probably comes from the short exclamations 'hoo' and 'ha', like people making loud, surprised noises, so it feels like an imitation of noise. That's why a hoo-ha means a noisy fuss or unnecessary commotion, much like a hubbub or hullabaloo.
