Etymology

titanium oxide comes from what it is made of: titanium, a metal, plus oxide, a word for a compound with oxygen, so the name literally describes the chemical. That mix makes a very fine, bright 'white powder' that covers surfaces well and does not fade, so the name now points to the ingredient used in 'paint' and 'sunscreen'.
