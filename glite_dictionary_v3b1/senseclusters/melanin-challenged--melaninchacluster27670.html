Etymology

melanin-challenged is a funny modern phrase for someone with little melanin, the dark colour in skin; melanin comes from a Greek word meaning 'black', and -challenged is a joking way to say 'having little' or 'not enough'. So, melanin-challenged means having very light or pale skin.
