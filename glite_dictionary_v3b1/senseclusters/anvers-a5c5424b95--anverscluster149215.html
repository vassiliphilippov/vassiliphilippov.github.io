Etymology

The word Anvers is the French name for the city called Antwerpen in Dutch, which English speakers know as Antwerp. A helpful way to remember it is the old parts hand ('hand') and werpen ('to throw'), because a popular story says a giant threw a hand into the river, and either way the name points to the city's river and busy port.
