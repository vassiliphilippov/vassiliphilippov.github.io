Etymology

ditzy probably comes from the short word ditz, which is linked to dizzy meaning 'giddy' or 'light-headed'. So, if your head feels a little 'dizzy' you may act 'silly' or forgetful, and that is why ditzy means 'silly' or 'scatterbrained'.
