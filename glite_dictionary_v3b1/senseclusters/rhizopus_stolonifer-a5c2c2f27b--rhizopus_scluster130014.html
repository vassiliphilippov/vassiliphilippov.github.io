Etymology

Rhizopus stolonifer is the Latin name for the black, fuzzy mold that often spoils bread and fruit. The second part, stolonifer, comes from a word for 'runner' and gives the picture of thread-like runners that 'spread' across food, so the name helps you remember it causes stored produce to 'spoil'.
