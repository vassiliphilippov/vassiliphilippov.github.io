Etymology

The name estradiol patch combines the hormone name estradiol and patch, a small sticky band you put on the skin. The picture is simple: the patch slowly sends the hormone into the body through the skin, so the term means a stick-on medicine that gives 'estrogen' over time to treat low levels.
