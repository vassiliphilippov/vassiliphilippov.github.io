Etymology

choriocarcinoma is made of two parts: chorio-, from a Greek word for the 'membrane around a baby in the womb' called the placenta, and -carcinoma, from a Greek root that gave us the word 'cancer' meaning a 'malignant tumor'. That's why choriocarcinoma is the name for a fast-growing cancer that starts in reproductive tissues like the uterus, ovaries, or testes.
