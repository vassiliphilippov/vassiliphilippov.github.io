Etymology

inherent aptitude creates a clear picture: inherent means something already 'in' you and aptitude is a natural skill, like a seed inside a baby bird that helps it know how to peck and build a nest. That's why inherent aptitude means 'a natural ability' someone has without learning or that is present 'from birth'.
