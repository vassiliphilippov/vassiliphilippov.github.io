Etymology

monoamine neurotransmitter: The name comes from its parts: mono means 'one' and amine is a small chemical piece with nitrogen, so the term literally names a brain 'messenger' that has one amine group. That's why it refers to chemicals like serotonin or dopamine that help nerve cells send signals.
