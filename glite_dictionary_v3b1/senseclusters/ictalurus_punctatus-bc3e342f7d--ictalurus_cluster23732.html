Etymology

Ictalurus punctatus may come from old words: the part Ictalurus suggests 'fish' and 'cat' because the fish has whisker-like feelers, and the Latin word punctatus means 'spotted' for the small spots on young fish. So the name describes the channel catfish you find in rivers and often eat as food.
