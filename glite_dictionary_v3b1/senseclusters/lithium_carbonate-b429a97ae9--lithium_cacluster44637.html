Etymology

lithium carbonate is named from its two parts: lithium (the metal) and carbonate (a type of salt related to carbon). Together they form a stable white powder that is used in making glass and ceramics and, in carefully measured doses, as a medicine to 'stabilize mood', so the name tells you it is the salt form of lithium used for those purposes.
