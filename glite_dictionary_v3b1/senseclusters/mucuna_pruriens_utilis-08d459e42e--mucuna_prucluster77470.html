Etymology

Mucuna pruriens utilis gets its name from the plant itself: Mucuna pruriens has very hairy pods that can make your skin 'itch', and the added word utilis means 'useful' because farmers grow this variety to improve soil and feed animals. So the name links the itchy pods to the plant's use as a helpful cover crop and grazing plant.
