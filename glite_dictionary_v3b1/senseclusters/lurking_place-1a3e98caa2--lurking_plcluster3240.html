Etymology

lurking place comes from the image of someone lurking, quietly hiding behind bushes or in a dark corner, and a place where they can wait unseen. You can imagine a person staying out of sight to watch or surprise someone, which is why the phrase means a spot used 'to hide' and 'to wait', often for a secret or bad purpose.
