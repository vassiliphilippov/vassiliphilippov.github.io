Etymology

tornadolike comes from tornado, which shares its root with the English word turn meaning 'turn', plus the English ending -like meaning 'similar to'. So something tornadolike is 'similar to' a tornado — it moves or hits in a strong, turning way, which is why we use it for powerful, swirling forces or sudden outbursts.
