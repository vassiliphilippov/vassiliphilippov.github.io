Etymology

The word pipistrelle comes from the Italian pipistrello, the everyday word for a 'bat'. A helpful way to remember it is to think of the tiny 'pip' sound and the bat's small, quick flight, so a pipistrelle is a small, fast bat often seen at dusk.
