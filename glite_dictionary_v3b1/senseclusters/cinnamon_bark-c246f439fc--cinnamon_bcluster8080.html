Etymology

cinnamon bark is the fragrant skin people peel from cinnamon trees, and when the thin inner bark dries it curls into the familiar sticks used in cooking. That's why cinnamon bark means the tree 'bark' used as a 'spice' to add a warm 'flavor' to food and drinks.
