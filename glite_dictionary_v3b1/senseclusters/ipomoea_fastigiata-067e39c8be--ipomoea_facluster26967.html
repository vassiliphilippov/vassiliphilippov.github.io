Etymology

Ipomoea fastigiata may come from older words: Ipomoea meaning 'like a worm' because its stems twist and climb, and fastigiata meaning 'pointed' or 'upright' for the plant's shape. So the name gives a picture of a climbing plant with big, starchy roots that people grow for food.
