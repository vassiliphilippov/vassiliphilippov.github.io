Etymology

The phrase old boy network comes from school life: an old boy was a man who had once been a pupil at the same private school, and a network was the group of former classmates who stayed in touch. These men often used their ties to 'help each other' get jobs or better positions and to leave 'outsiders' out, so today the phrase means an informal system where people with the same background use personal connections to advance each other.
