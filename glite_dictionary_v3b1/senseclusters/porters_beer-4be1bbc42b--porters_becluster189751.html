Etymology

porter's beer may come from the workers called porters in old London, who carried heavy loads and often drank a dark, strong beer to stay warm and full. Because these porters liked that roasty, heavy drink, the name came to mean the dark, rich beer we now call 'a very dark, sweet ale'.
