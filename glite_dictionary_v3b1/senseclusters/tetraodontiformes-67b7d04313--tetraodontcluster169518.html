Etymology

Tetraodontiformes comes from three parts: tetra- 'four', odont 'tooth', and -formes 'shape'. It literally means 'four-toothed shapes', a useful picture because many puffers and their relatives have four strong teeth that form a beak and help explain their odd bodies and spiny skins.
