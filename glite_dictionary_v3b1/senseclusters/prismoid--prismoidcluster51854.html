Etymology

The word prismoid comes from prism, a solid with two equal, parallel flat ends, and the ending -oid, which means 'like' or 'shape'. So a prismoid is a solid 'like' a prism: it has two parallel many-sided flat ends and other faces that are four-sided shapes.
