Etymology

mercury-contaminated combines mercury, the name of a shiny liquid metal once called 'quicksilver' that is poisonous, and contaminated, which means 'made dirty or unsafe'. So mercury-contaminated describes something that has dangerous mercury in it and is not safe to eat, drink, or use.
