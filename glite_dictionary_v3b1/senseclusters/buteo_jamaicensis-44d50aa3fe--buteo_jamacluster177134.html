Etymology

Buteo jamaicensis may come from old Latin naming for animals. Buteo is a Latin word for a kind of 'hawk' and jamaicensis means 'from Jamaica', so the name paints the simple picture of a hawk from Jamaica and is now used for the red-tailed hawk.
