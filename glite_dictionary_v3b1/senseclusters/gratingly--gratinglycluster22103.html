Etymology

gratingly comes from the word grate, which meant 'to scrape' and brings to mind the sharp scraping sound of a kitchen grater or a chair rubbing across the floor. So when something is done gratingly, it 'scrapes' against your ears or feelings and feels harsh and unpleasant.
