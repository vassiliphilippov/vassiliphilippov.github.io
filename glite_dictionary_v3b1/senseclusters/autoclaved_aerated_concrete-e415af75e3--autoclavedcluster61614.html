Etymology

Autoclaved aerated concrete comes from how the blocks are made: aerated means the mix has tiny air bubbles, autoclaved means they are treated with hot steam in a strong closed oven, and concrete is the building material. Those air bubbles make the blocks light and help them keep heat out, so the name describes a 'light' material that helps 'keep heat out'.
