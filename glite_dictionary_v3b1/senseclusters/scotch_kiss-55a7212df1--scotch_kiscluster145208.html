Etymology

The candy Scotch kiss gets its name from Scotch (short for 'butterscotch') and kiss (a small sweet shaped like a kiss). That image of a little drop of butterscotch, sometimes with a soft marshmallow center, explains why the name now means this kind of small sweet treat.
