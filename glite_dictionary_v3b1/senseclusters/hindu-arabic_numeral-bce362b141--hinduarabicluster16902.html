Etymology

The term Hindu-Arabic numeral comes from the history of the digits: the ten symbol shapes were first used in India, so Hindu, and later brought to Europe by Arab teachers, so Arabic. Because these ten symbols, including the important '0', made counting and calculation much easier, they became the standard 'numbers' we use today.
