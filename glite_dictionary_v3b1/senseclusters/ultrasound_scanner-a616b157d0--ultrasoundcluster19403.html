Etymology

The noun ultrasound scanner comes from the idea of using very high sound we cannot hear to make pictures. Like a bat that sends sounds and listens for echoes, the scanner turns those echoes into a picture to 'see inside' the body, so the name means a machine that makes images of the inside of the body.
