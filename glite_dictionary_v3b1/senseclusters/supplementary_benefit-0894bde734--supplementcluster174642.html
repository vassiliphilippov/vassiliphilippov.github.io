Etymology

The noun supplementary benefit comes from the idea of adding extra money: supplementary means 'added on' and benefit means a payment. Imagine the government putting extra coins on top of a small wage until it reaches the legal 'minimum', so the phrase means a payment that 'tops up' someone's income.
