Etymology

superfluousness comes from the Latin parts super- 'over' and fluere 'to flow', the same root you see in fluid and fluent, so it gives the idea of 'overflowing'. Think of a cup that keeps overflowing — superfluousness is the extra, unnecessary things that flow beyond what you need.
