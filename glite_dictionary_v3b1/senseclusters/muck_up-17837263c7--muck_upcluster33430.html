Etymology

muck up also grew a figurative meaning from that same image of mess: if you cover a plan or a job in muck it is ruined or unusable, so to muck up came to mean to spoil or make a mess of something. That's why we now use muck up to say someone 'spoiled' a task by making mistakes.
