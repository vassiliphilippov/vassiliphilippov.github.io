Etymology

The noun butea kino comes from the red sap of the butea, the dhak tree, and people scraped the sap and let it dry into hard, reddish lumps called kino. Those lumps were used in medicine and for making leather because they help 'tighten and heal' skin, so the name means a dried, red juice used as an 'astringent'.
