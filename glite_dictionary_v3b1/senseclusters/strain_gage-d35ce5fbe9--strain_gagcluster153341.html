Etymology

The term strain gage comes from the words strain and gage, where 'strain' means 'a stretch or bend' and 'gage' is an old spelling of 'gauge', a measuring tool. A strain gage is a small device glued to a surface to show how much it stretches or bends, so the name literally means 'a tool to measure strain'.
