Etymology

mischief-making comes from mischief, which began as an old French idea meaning 'bad luck', and it uses the same mis- you see in mistake to show something wrong. So mischief-making is literally 'making trouble', which explains why it means behaving to cause trouble, annoyance, or harm to others.
