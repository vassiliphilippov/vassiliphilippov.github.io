Etymology

Calamintha nepeta glantulosa is a Latin plant name that paints a simple picture: Calamintha connects it to 'calamint', a mint-like herb, nepeta links it to 'catnip', and glantulosa may come from a word for 'many little drops' on the leaves that hold the plant's strong smell. So the name helps you remember it is a low, strongly scented herb often grown for its pleasant smell and for cooking.
