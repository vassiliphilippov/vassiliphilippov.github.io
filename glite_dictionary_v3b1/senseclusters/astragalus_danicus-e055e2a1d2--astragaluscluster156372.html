Etymology

Astragalus danicus comes from older words: Astragalus comes from a Greek word for an 'ankle bone' because the seed pods can look like small bones, and danicus means 'from Denmark'. So the name points to this purple-flowered plant and helps you remember its bone-like seed pods and that it comes from Denmark.
