Etymology

The phrase stunning landscapes uses stunning, from the word 'stun' meaning to be shocked or stopped, and landscapes, meaning wide natural views. Imagine a scene so beautiful it stops you in your tracks or takes your breath away, so we call very beautiful views 'stunning landscapes'.
