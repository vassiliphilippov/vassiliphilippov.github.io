Etymology

fluent aphasia combines fluent, meaning 'smooth, flowing speech', and aphasia, meaning 'loss of language'. Imagine a river of words that flows easily but carries the wrong things, so the person speaks in long, easy sentences that 'make little sense' and has trouble understanding others.
