Etymology

The compound traveler's letter of credit comes from the image of a traveler carrying a letter from their bank that promises credit, or money, abroad. The paper shows the bank will pay, so the traveler can use it to get cash. That's why it means 'a bank-issued guarantee that lets a person obtain funds while traveling'.
