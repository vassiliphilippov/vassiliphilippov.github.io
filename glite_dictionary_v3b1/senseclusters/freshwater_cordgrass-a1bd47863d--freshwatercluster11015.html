Etymology

freshwater cordgrass gives an easy picture: freshwater means it grows in 'not salty' water, and cordgrass suggests long, strong stems and roots that hold mud together like a 'cord'. So the name means a cord-like grass that lives in fresh water, which helps you remember its habitat and why it helps hold riverbanks.
