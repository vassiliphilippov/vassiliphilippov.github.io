Etymology

Scotch pancake: The name Scotch pancake comes from Scotch, meaning 'from Scotland', and from the way they are made. Cooks would drop a spoonful of batter onto a hot pan to make small, thick pancakes, so the name now means 'a small, thick pancake'.
