Etymology

meticulosity comes from meticulous, which in turn comes from the old Latin root metus meaning 'fear'. A helpful way to remember it is that a person who is a bit 'fearful' of making mistakes becomes very 'careful' about small details, so meticulosity means paying very close attention to every tiny part.
