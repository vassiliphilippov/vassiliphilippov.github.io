Etymology

The word chlordiazepoxide breaks into parts that tell its chemistry and use: chloro- shows it has a chlorine part (think of chlorine), diazep- puts it in the family of calming drugs you may know from diazepam, and the ending -oxide means it is 'with oxygen' as a specific chemical form. So the name explains why chlordiazepoxide is a particular tranquilizer used for anxiety and withdrawal.
