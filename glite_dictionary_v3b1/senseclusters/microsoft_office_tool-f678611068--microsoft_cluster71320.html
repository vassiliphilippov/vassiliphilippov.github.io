Etymology

The name Microsoft Office tool comes from the picture of computer programs as tools people use in an office. Microsoft grouped programs like Word, Excel and PowerPoint into an Office set, so a tool here means a 'program' you use to do office work, which is why the phrase means a software program for office tasks.
