Etymology

Crucible of adversity uses a strong image: a crucible is a very hot pot used to melt metal, and adversity means hard times. So being in a crucible of adversity is like being put under great heat to be tested and grow stronger, which is why the phrase means 'a hard test that makes people or relationships stronger'.
