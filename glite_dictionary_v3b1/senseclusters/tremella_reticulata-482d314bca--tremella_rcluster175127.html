Etymology

Tremella reticulata may come from old Latin words: Tremella meaning 'to tremble' and reticulata meaning 'net-like', because the fungus has a soft, jelly body that can look like a trembling coral or a little net. That's why the name fits this whitish, upright jelly fungus.
