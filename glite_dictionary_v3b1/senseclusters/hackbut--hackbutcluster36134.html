Etymology

hackbut comes from old Dutch and French words: hak meaning 'hook' and busse meaning 'barrel', so you can imagine a 'hooked barrel' or a long tube with a hook to steady it. That's why hackbut names an old long-barrel firearm used from the 15th to the 17th centuries.
