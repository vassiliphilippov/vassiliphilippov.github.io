Etymology

The noun fragrant bedstraw comes from the old use of putting the sweet-smelling plant into beds as a kind of bedstraw to scent mattresses and keep clothes fresh. That's why the name points to a pleasant-smelling plant used to perfume linens and as a ground cover.
