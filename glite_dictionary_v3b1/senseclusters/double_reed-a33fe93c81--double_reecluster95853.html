Etymology

The noun double reed comes from the simple picture of two thin pieces of reed joined together, because the word double means two. When air is blown between the two reeds, they move quickly against each other to make a strong 'sound', so instruments like the 'oboe' and 'bassoon' that use a double reed have that special tone.
