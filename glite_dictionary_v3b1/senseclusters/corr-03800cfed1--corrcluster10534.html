Etymology

corr. can mean 'correspondent' or 'correction'. 'Correspondent' comes from cor- 'together' and respond 'answer', so a correspondent is a reporter who sends news back, and 'correction' comes from correct 'make right', so a 'corr.' note in editing asks to fix a mistake.
