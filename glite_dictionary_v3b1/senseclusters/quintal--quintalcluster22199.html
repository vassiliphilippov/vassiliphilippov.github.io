Etymology

quintal comes from old trade words that meant a 'hundred' or a 'hundredweight', a idea you can see in cent and century which mean 'hundred'. So in older or local U.S. use a quintal meant a unit of '100 pounds', which is why you may find prices or weights given per quintal.
