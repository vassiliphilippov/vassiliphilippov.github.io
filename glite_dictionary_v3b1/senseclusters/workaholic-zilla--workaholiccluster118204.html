Etymology

workaholic-zilla mixes two ideas: work plus -aholic, which comes from alcoholic and means 'addicted', and the ending -zilla from Godzilla meaning 'monster' or 'huge'. So a workaholic-zilla is someone who is 'addicted to work' in an extreme, monster-like way.
