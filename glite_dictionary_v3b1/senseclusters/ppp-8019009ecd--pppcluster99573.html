Etymology

PPP is short for palatopharyngoplasty, which breaks into palato- 'palate', pharyngo- 'throat', and -plasty 'to shape'. So PPP is a surgery that reshapes the palate and throat to open the airway and help with snoring and sleep breathing problems.
