Etymology

Vicodin is the brand name for the medicine that combines hydrocodone and acetaminophen; it is a made-up commercial name for a prescription drug. So, Vicodin means a doctor-prescribed medicine used to ease 'pain' and it must be used carefully because it can cause 'addiction'.
