Etymology

eat humble pie may come from an old dish called umble pie, made from the less nice parts of a deer and often eaten by servants after a hunt. Because eating that cheap, shameful food was embarrassing, eat humble pie came to mean 'to admit that you were wrong' or feel humiliated.
