Etymology

Sturm und Drang was the name used by a group of German writers in the late 1700s who wanted wild feeling in art, with Sturm ('storm') and Drang ('stress' or 'urge') giving the image of strong emotion. So the term now names that literary movement and its habit of dramatic, intense feeling.
