Etymology

total fuck-knuckle likely comes from joining the rude word fuck (used to make an insult stronger) with knuckle, as in 'knucklehead' for a thick or clumsy person, creating a blunt image of someone very foolish, so it means 'a thoroughly incompetent or foolish person'.
