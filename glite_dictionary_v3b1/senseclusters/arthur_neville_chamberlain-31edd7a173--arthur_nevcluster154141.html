Etymology

Arthur Neville Chamberlain was the British prime minister who, in 1938, signed the Munich Agreement and returned home saying 'peace for our time'. Because he tried to avoid war by giving in to Hitler, his name is now linked to the idea of 'appeasement' and is remembered for that choice.
