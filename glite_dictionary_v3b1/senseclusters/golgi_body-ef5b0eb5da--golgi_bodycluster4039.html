Etymology

Golgi body is named after the Italian scientist Camillo Golgi, who first saw this cell part with a microscope by using a special stain. He saw a stack of flat sacs that 'package' and send proteins, so the name stuck and Golgi body now means the cell's little 'post office' that processes and ships substances.
