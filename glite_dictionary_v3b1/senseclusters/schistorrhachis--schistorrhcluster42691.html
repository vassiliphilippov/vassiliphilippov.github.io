Etymology

schistorrhachis comes from Greek parts schisto- meaning 'split' (as in the word schism) and -rhachis meaning 'spine' or 'back'. So schistorrhachis literally means 'split spine', which helps you remember it is a birth condition where part of the spine bones are not formed correctly.
