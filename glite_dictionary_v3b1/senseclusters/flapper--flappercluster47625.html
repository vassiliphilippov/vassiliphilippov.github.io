Etymology

The word flapper comes from the simple word flap, meaning 'to move up and down' or 'to move quickly', plus the ending -er. In the 1920s young women wore loose, short dresses and danced with quick, lively movements that looked like 'flapping', so flapper came to mean a young woman who broke old rules and lived more freely.
