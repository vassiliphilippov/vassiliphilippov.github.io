Etymology

contrite comes from the Latin word contritus, built from con- 'together' and terere 'to rub', so it originally meant 'worn down' or 'crushed'. Think of someone who feels emotionally 'crushed' by guilt — that's why contrite means deeply sorry and full of regret.
