Etymology

The term fertilization age comes from the simple idea of starting the clock when the fertilization — the meeting of egg and sperm — happens. That picture of counting the baby’s age 'from fertilization' rather than 'from the mother's last menstrual period' explains why the phrase means the embryo or fetus' age measured from that moment.
