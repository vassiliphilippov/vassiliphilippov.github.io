Etymology

DNA fingerprint compares a person's unique DNA pattern to a human fingerprint, giving a clear picture of identity that is different for each person. So, scientists read the DNA pattern like a print to 'identify' someone, and the phrase means 'a genetic ID' used to match a person to evidence or to check family links.
