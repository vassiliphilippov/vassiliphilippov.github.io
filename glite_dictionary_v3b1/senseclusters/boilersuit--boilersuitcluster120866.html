Etymology

boilersuit comes from the words boiler, a big hot 'machine' that makes steam, and suit, meaning a 'set of clothes'. Workers who fixed boilers wore this one-piece outfit to keep their clothes clean, so today boilersuit means a loose, protective all-in-one garment.
