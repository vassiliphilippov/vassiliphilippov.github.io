Etymology

The word strabotomy comes from the root strab- meaning 'to look sideways' or 'squint', and the ending -otomy meaning 'cutting', like in lobotomy. So a strabotomy is an operation where a surgeon cuts an eye muscle to straighten misaligned eyes.
