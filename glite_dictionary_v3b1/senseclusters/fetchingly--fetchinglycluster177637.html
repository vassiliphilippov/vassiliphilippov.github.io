Etymology

Fetchingly comes from the simple verb fetch, which means 'to go and bring' and later came to mean 'to attract' or 'catch attention'. Think of a dog that fetches a ball — something fetching 'fetches' people's eyes, so fetchingly means 'in an attractive or charming way'.
