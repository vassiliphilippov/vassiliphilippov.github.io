Etymology

tendosynovitis breaks into three parts: tendo ('tendon'), synov ('the thin cover and fluid around a tendon'), and -itis ('inflammation'), the same ending you see in tonsillitis. So, tendosynovitis is when a tendon and its cover become painful and swollen, which is why movement can be hard.
