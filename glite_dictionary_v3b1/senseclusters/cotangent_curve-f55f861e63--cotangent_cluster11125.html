Etymology

cotangent curve names the wavy graph of the function called cotangent. The word cotangent comes from 'co-' meaning 'partner' and 'tangent', because it was seen as the partner of the tangent in a right-angle pair. So, the cotangent curve is the curve that shows that partner's values and makes a repeating wavy pattern with jumps where values go very high or very low.
