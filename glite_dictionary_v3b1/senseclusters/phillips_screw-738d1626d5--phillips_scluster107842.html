Etymology

The noun Phillips screw is named after inventor Henry F. Phillips, who made a screw with a small cross-shaped slot on the head. The cross lets a matching Phillips screwdriver fit into the screw and turn it more easily, so today the term means 'a screw with a cross-shaped head'.
