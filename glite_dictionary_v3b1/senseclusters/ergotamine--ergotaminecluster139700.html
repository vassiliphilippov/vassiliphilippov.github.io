Etymology

The word ergotamine comes from ergot, the fungus that grows on grain and whose name once meant 'spur' because it looks like a small spur, and from -amine, a common ending meaning 'a chemical with nitrogen'. So ergotamine is the nitrogen chemical taken from ergot, which is why it is used to narrow blood vessels and treat migraines.
