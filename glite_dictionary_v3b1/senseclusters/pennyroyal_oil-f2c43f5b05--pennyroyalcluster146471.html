Etymology

pennyroyal oil: The name pennyroyal oil comes from a small mint plant — people long ago crushed the leaves of pennyroyal to make a strong-smelling oil. So the phrase simply means 'an oil made from the pennyroyal plant', a scent and home remedy once used in soaps and to repel insects, though it can be poisonous in large amounts.
