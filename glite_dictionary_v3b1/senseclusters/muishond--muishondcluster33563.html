Etymology

muishond comes from the Afrikaans words muis 'mouse' and hond 'dog', literally 'mouse-dog'. It was used for a small, dog-like southern African animal that hunts mice and uses strong-smelling scent glands to defend itself, so the name helps you remember what kind of creature it is.
