Etymology

damask violet may come from the name for the city of Damascus: one popular story says the sweet-smelling flowers or their scent were brought from 'Damascus', so people began to call the plant 'damask'. That's why the name joins damask, the place, and violet, the flower, and now refers to a fragrant garden plant.
