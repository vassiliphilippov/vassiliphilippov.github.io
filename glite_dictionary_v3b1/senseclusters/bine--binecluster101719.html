Etymology

The word bine is an old English word for a climbing plant that winds its stem around a support. A helpful way to remember bine is to think of the word bind, which means 'to tie', because the plant seems to 'bind' itself to a pole by twisting its stem, so a bine climbs by winding rather than using tendrils.
