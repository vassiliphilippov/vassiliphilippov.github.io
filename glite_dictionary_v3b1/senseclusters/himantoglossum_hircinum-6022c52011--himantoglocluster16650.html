Etymology

Himantoglossum hircinum comes from old words that describe the plant's flower and smell. Himantoglossum means 'strap-tongue' and gives the image of the long, twisted flower part, and hircinum means 'goat-like', which explains the strong, goat-like scent, so the name points to the orchid's shape and smell.
