Etymology

pyridoxine 5'-phosphate gets its name from the vitamin pyridoxine, which is vitamin B6, with a small phosphate 'tag' added at the 5' position. This 'tag' turns the vitamin into the 'active form' the body uses to work with proteins and starch, so the name shows it is the usable form in many body reactions.
