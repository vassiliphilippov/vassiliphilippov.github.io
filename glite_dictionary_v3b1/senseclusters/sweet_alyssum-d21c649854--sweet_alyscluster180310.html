Etymology

sweet alyssum comes from two ideas: sweet for its pleasant smell and alyssum from an old word because people once thought the plant could help with 'madness' or 'rabies'. That story about healing, plus the flower's 'sweet' scent, is why the name now means the small, fragrant garden flower.
