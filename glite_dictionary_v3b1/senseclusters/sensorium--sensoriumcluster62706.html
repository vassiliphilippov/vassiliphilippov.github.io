Etymology

sensorium comes from the Latin root sens- meaning 'feel' or 'sense' and the ending -orium meaning 'place'. Think of it as the brain's 'place for sensing' — the same root is in English sense and sensor, so sensorium means the parts of the brain that let you consciously feel sights, sounds and touch.
