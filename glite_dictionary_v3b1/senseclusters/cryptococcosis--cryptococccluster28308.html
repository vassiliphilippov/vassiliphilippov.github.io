Etymology

cryptococcosis is made of three parts: crypto- meaning 'hidden', as in cryptic, coccus meaning 'small round thing' like a 'berry', and -osis meaning 'condition' or 'disease'. So, cryptococcosis is a disease caused by a hidden round fungus, which helps explain why it often starts in the lungs and can reach the brain.
