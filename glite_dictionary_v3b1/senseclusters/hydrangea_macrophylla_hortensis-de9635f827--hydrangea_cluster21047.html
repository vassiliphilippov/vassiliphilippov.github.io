Etymology

Hydrangea macrophylla hortensis comes from words meaning 'water' and 'jar' because hydrangea refers to the seed pods that look like small jars, macrophylla means 'big leaves', and hortensis means 'from the garden'. So the name reminds us it is a garden shrub with large leaves and round flower clusters that like moist soil and can turn pink or blue.
