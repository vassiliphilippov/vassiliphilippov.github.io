Etymology

Issus is the old name of a town on the coast of what is now southern Turkey, and the fight there in 333 BC became famous when Alexander the Great defeated Darius III. So Issus usually refers to that battle and the place where it happened, which makes it easy to remember as the site of an important historical victory.
