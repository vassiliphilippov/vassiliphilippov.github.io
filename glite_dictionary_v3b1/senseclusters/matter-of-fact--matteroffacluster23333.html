Etymology

matter-of-fact comes from putting together matter ('thing') and fact ('something true'), so it literally means 'about true things'. A helpful way to remember it is to imagine someone who talks only about facts, with no feelings, which is why matter-of-fact describes a calm, straightforward way of speaking.
