Etymology

Atlantic halibut gets its name from where the fish lives and an old word for a flat fish. One popular story says halibut comes from the old words holy and butt, because butt meant a flat fish and holy refers to church days when people often ate it, so the name came to mean the large flat fish found in the Atlantic.
