Etymology

probiotic bacterium comes from old Greek words: pro- meaning 'for', biotic meaning 'life', and bacterium meaning a 'small rod' or tiny living cell. So it literally says a tiny living thing 'for' life, which is why the term means a small, helpful microbe that lives in the gut and helps digestion.
