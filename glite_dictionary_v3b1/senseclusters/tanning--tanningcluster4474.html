Etymology

tanning (a beating) comes from the same root as tan in leather work; people once spoke of treating a hide roughly, and a helpful way to remember it is to think of a harsh punishment as treating someone's 'skin' like a hide to be worked on. That's why 'getting a tanning' means a severe, old-fashioned beating.
