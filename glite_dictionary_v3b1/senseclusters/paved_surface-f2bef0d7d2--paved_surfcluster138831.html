Etymology

The compound noun paved surface comes from the simple image of a path or area covered with hard material like stones or concrete so it is smooth and firm. It joins paved, meaning covered with paving, and surface, meaning a flat area to walk or drive on, so paved surface means 'a flat area covered with paving material'.
