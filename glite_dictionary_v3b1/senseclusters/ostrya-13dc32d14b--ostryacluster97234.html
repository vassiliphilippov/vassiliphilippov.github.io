Etymology

Ostrya names a group of trees often called the hop-hornbeam, because their hanging seed clusters look like 'hops' used in beer; the name points to that hop-like shape. So, thinking of Ostrya as the tree with 'hop-like' fruit helps you remember this hardy, strong-wooded tree.
