Etymology

state of bonkersdom comes from a playful image: bonkers means 'crazy' and the ending -dom makes it like a small kingdom or place. Imagine being inside a tiny kingdom where everything is silly and mixed up; that's why the phrase means 'a funny, over-the-top state of being crazy or confused'.
