Etymology

sloe gin comes from a simple country recipe: people put the dark berry called a sloe into a bottle of gin, add sugar, and leave it for months so the gin takes the berry's flavour and colour. That's why sloe gin means a 'sweet drink' made by infusing gin with sloes.
