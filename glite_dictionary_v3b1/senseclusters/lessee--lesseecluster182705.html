Etymology

lessee comes from lease, a word for a rent agreement, and the ending -ee, which marks the person who 'receives' an action, as in employee. The root lease is like the verb let, which means 'to allow someone to use'. So a lessee is the person who receives the lease — the tenant who rents and uses the property.
