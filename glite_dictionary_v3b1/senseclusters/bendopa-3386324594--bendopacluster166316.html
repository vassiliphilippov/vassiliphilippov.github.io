Etymology

Bendopa is a brand name made of ben- and -dopa. The -dopa part comes from DOPA, the medicine that helps the brain make the movement chemical dopamine, and a helpful way to remember the ben- part is to think of Latin 'bene' meaning 'well', so Bendopa suggests helping people move 'well'.
