Etymology

propagandistic comes from propaganda, which in turn comes from the Latin propagare meaning 'to spread' and is the same root as the English verb propagate. Think of ideas being 'spread' like seeds or posters to grow support, so propagandistic describes things that try to push a one-sided or biased view.
