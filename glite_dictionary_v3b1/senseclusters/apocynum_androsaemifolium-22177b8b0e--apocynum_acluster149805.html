Etymology

Apocynum androsaemifolium is a Latin plant name that gives a clear picture: the part Apocynum may mean 'harm to dogs' because plants in this group can be poisonous, and androsaemifolium points to the plant's rose-like leaves and pink flowers. That's why the name fits a plant people used in folk medicine for 'joint pain' but also treated carefully because it can be 'poisonous'.
