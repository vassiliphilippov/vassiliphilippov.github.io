Etymology

truncus pulmonalis comes from Latin: truncus means 'trunk' and pulmonalis means 'of the lungs'. Think of a single 'trunk' leaving the heart that then splits into two branches to reach the lungs, so the name fits the large artery that carries blood from the heart to both lungs.
