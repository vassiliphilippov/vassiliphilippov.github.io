Etymology

The word scratchily comes from the simple word scratch, which means 'to make small cuts or a rough scraping sound', plus the endings -y and -ly that turn it into a word showing how something is done. So, to do something scratchily is to do it in a rough, harsh, or scraping way, like writing with a nearly empty pen or a radio with damaged speakers.
