Etymology

lawn cart combines two clear images: a cart, a small wheeled vehicle, and a lawn, the grassy area of a garden. Imagine pushing a little cart over the grass to carry soil, clippings, or tools, and you see why it means 'a small cart for carrying garden materials'.
