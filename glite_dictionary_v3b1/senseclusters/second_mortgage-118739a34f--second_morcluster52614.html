Etymology

The phrase second mortgage comes from a simple idea: when a house already has a 'first' mortgage, the owner can take a secondmortgage using the same house as a promise to the bank. Because this loan comes after the 'first' one, the second lender stands 'behind' the first for being paid, so the phrase means another loan on a property that already has a loan.
