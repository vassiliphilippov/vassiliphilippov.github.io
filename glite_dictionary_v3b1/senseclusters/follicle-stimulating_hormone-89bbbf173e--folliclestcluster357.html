Etymology

follicle-stimulating hormone is a chemical message from a small gland in the brain; its name shows what it does: follicle means the tiny 'egg sacs' in the ovary, stimulating means 'making them grow', and hormone is a chemical message. So, the hormone 'tells' those follicles to grow and, in men, helps the body make sperm.
