Etymology

Nitrostat joins nitro, which points to nitroglycerin, a chemical used both as a heart medicine and in explosives, and stat, the medical word meaning 'immediately'. That's why the name fits a quick-acting drug for chest pain and also reminds us the same chemical can be used in explosives.
