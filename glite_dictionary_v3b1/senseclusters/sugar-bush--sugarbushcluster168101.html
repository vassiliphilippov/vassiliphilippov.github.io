Etymology

sugar-bush comes from the plain English parts sugar and bush, so it paints a picture of a bush that is 'sweet'. A helpful way to remember the name is that people noticed the plant's flowers or sap are sweet or attract bees, so sugar-bush refers to a shrub known for its sweet nectar or sap.
