Etymology

uncongeniality breaks into un- 'not' and congenial, which comes from genial meaning 'friendly' or 'pleasant'. So uncongeniality is the quality of being 'not friendly, not pleasant, or not suitable', which is why it makes people or places feel uncomfortable.
