Etymology

The term corneal graft combines cornea, the clear front 'window' of the eye, and graft, a gardening word for joining a healthy piece to a damaged one. One clear image is doctors placing healthy tissue from another person onto the damaged cornea, so the phrase means 'to replace a damaged cornea with healthy tissue'.
