Etymology

Quiet oasis comes from the image of a green, water-filled spot in a dry desert: an oasis is a calm, safe place in the middle of harsh land, and quiet adds the idea of peace. So calling a place a 'quiet oasis' means it is a 'peaceful place' in the middle of noise or stress.
