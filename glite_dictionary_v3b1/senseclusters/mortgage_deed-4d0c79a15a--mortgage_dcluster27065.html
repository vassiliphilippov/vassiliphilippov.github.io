Etymology

The term mortgage deed may come from old words: mortgage from a 'dead promise' and deed meaning a written paper. Long ago 'dead' in this phrase meant the promise would end when the loan was paid or the borrower died. So the image is a paper that holds a house or land as a promise to a lender until the loan ends, and that's why it now means the legal document giving a lender a right to the property.
