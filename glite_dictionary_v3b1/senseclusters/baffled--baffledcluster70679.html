Etymology

The word baffled comes from the older verb baffle, which once meant 'to beat' or 'to surprise' and later 'to confuse'. A helpful way to remember it is to imagine your plans hitting a wall and stopping; you feel 'blocked' and puzzled, which is why baffled means 'confused'.
