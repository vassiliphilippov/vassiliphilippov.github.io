Etymology

The noun order Alcyonaria may come from an old name for soft corals that have eight branch-like tentacles. Picture a flower-like sea animal with eight little arms; that clear 'eight-armed' image is why order Alcyonaria means 'a group of corals and anemones with eight-branched tentacles'.
