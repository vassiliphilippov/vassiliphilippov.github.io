Etymology

Third Battle of Panipat was a huge fight near the town of Panipat in 1761 where the Afghan leader Ahmad Shah Abdali met the Maratha army on a wide plain and many soldiers died. Because the Marathas lost and their power fell, this battle is remembered as a 'turning point' that changed who ruled much of India.
