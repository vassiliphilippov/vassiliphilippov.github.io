Etymology

The adjective radially symmetrical creates a simple picture: imagine a starfish or a flower whose arms or petals spread out from the 'center' like spokes on a wheel. Because the parts go radially, or out from the 'center', and are symmetrical, or 'the same' around that point, radially symmetrical means the shape looks the same from any direction around the center.
