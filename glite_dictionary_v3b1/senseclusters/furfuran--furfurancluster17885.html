Etymology

furfuran comes from the Latin furfur, meaning 'bran', because the first chemicals of this type were made from grain waste, and from furan, the name for a small 'ring' with oxygen in it. So, furfuran is a plant-derived chemical that serves as an important building block in industry, for example in making nylon.
