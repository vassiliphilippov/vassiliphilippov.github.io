Etymology

burnination of the foliage uses the plain word burn with a silly '-ination' ending to make the act sound huge and dramatic, and foliage means leaves and plants. So it paints a funny picture of eagerly setting fire to plants and is used today as a joking way to say 'to burn' a lot of vegetation.
