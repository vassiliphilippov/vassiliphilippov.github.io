Etymology

Bissau comes from a local name used by the people of the area before the Portuguese arrived, so it has long been the name of the town by the sea. A helpful way to remember it is that Bissau is also the part of the country name 'Guinea-Bissau', so the city's name points to that place on the West African coast.
