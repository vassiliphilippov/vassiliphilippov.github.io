Etymology

tetrodotoxin comes from the pufferfish name Tetraodon, which breaks into the parts tetra 'four' and odon 'tooth', plus toxin 'poison'. So it means the 'poison of the four-toothed fish', and that fits because it is a powerful poison that blocks nerve signals.
