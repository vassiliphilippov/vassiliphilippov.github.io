Etymology

Pithecanthropus comes from Greek parts pithec- 'ape' and -anthropus 'man', so it literally means 'ape-man'. It was the old name for certain early human remains (the famous 'Java man') and that name shows why people thought these bones were between apes and humans, though scientists now call them Homo erectus.
