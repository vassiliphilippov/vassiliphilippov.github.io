Etymology

Sequoia Wellingtonia may come from two honor names: the first part, Sequoia, may refer to Sequoyah, a man who created a Cherokee alphabet, and the second part, Wellingtonia, was used to honor the Duke of Wellington. Because people gave this very tall, ancient tree a grand name, the name helps you remember it is a huge, famous tree.
