Etymology

skip-bomb comes from the two parts skip and bomb, where skip is like 'to bounce' a stone across water and bomb is the explosive, so picture a bomb skipping along the sea to reach a ship. So, to skip-bomb means to drop a bomb low so it 'skips' over the surface before exploding at the target.
