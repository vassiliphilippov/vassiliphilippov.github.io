Etymology

The noun chestnut canker joins chestnut (the tree) and canker, an old word for a 'sore' or 'wound' on plants or animals. The image is of a dark, spreading sore on the tree's bark that eats the wood, so the name now means a disease caused by a fungus that can kill chestnut trees.
