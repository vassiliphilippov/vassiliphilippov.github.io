Etymology

The word orthodoxly comes from Greek parts ortho- meaning 'straight' or 'right' and doxa meaning 'belief' or 'opinion'. Think of orthodontist, who makes teeth straight, to remember ortho-, and so to act orthodoxly is to do things in the 'right' or accepted way, following tradition.
