Etymology

The word hoser is Canadian slang that became popular on 1980s TV when two comedy characters used it for a foolish or unreliable person. A helpful way to remember it is to think of hose, which means 'to spray with water' and brings to mind a simple, messy job at the hockey rink, so hoser came to mean someone a bit useless or a fool.
