Etymology

neutralizer comes from neutral, a root that says 'neither' or 'not taking sides', plus -ize meaning 'to make' and -er meaning 'one who does something'. A neutralizer is therefore a person or tactic that 'takes away' an opponent's power or makes them not a threat.
