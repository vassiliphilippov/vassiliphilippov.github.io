Etymology

Ursus horribilis comes from Latin: Ursus means 'bear' and horribilis means 'terrible' or 'dreadful'. Early explorers used that scary name because the animal looked large and fierce, so the Latin name stuck and now refers to the powerful 'grizzly bear'.
