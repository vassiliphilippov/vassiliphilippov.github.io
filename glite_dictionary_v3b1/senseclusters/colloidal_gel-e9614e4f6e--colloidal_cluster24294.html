Etymology

colloidal gel comes from two simple images: colloidal meant 'glue-like' because tiny particles stick together, and gel meant 'jelly' or a soft, semi-solid. Picture tiny bits stuck in a web that holds liquid, and you can see why it now means a semi-solid material with particles trapped inside.
