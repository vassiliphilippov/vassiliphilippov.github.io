Etymology

Physalis pruinosa comes from old Greek and Latin words that describe how the plant looks. Physalis means a small 'bladder' or lantern because the fruit sits in a paper husk, and pruinosa means 'frosty' or covered in a dusty coating, so the name describes its little paper-covered, frosty fruit.
