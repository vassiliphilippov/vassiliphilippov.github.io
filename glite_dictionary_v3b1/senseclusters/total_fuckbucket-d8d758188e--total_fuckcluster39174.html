Etymology

total fuckbucket may come from joining the strong swear fuck with the image of a bucket full of trash, making a picture of someone 'full of bad things' and unpleasant. The word total just makes the insult stronger, so the phrase is used to call someone 'worthless' or 'disgusting'.
