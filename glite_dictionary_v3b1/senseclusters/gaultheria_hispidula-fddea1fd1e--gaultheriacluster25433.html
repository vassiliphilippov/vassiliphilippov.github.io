Etymology

Gaultheria hispidula comes from a person’s name and a Latin word. The genus Gaultheria honors the botanist Jean-François Gaultier, and hispidula means 'a little hairy', a neat image for its tiny hairy white berries and low, creeping way of growing, so the name points to what the plant looks like today.
