Etymology

theory of inheritance comes from early scientists like Gregor Mendel, who grew peas and watched how traits like seed colour moved from parent plants to new plants. The word inheritance was used like things passed down in a family, so the phrase means an idea that explains how physical traits are 'passed on' from parents to children.
