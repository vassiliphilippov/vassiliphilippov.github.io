Etymology

Taraktogenos kurzii is the Latin name for an East Indian tree whose oily seeds are pressed to make 'chaulmoogra oil', a traditional medicine once used to treat 'leprosy'. Picture small seeds being squeezed for healing oil, and that image helps you remember that this name refers to an oil-producing medicinal tree.
