Etymology

Holocanthus tricolor is a scientific name that paints a simple picture of the fish: Holocanthus points to its spiny, angelfish shape and tricolor means 'three-coloured'. So the name helps you remember this bright, rock-beauty angelfish by its strong shape and its three main colours.
