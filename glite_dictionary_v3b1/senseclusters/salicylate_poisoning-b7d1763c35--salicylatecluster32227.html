Etymology

salicylate poisoning uses salicylate, a substance first taken from willow bark and used in medicines like aspirin, and poisoning, meaning 'becoming very sick from too much of a substance'. So, the phrase means 'serious illness from taking too much aspirin or similar drugs'.
