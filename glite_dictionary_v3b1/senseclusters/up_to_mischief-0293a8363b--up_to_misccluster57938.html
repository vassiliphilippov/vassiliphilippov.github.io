Etymology

up to mischief comes from everyday talk where up to means 'busy with' and mischief means playful trouble. Picture a quiet child sneaking around doing small naughty things; they are literally 'up to' trouble, so the phrase means 'engaged in playful or mildly troublesome behavior'.
