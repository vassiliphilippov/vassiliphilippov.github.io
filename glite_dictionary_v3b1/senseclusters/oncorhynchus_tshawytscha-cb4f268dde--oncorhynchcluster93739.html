Etymology

Oncorhynchus tshawytscha comes from older words that give a simple picture. The first part, Oncorhynchus, joins words for hook and nose, like the hooked jaw male salmon grow when they lay eggs, and the second part, tshawytscha, comes from a local native name for the 'Chinook' or 'king salmon', so the full name points to the large, famous salmon we know today.
