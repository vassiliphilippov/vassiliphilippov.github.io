Etymology

Martin Luther King Day began after the civil rights leader Martin Luther King Jr. was killed, when many people wanted a special day to remember his fight for equal rights. That's why the United States sets aside the third Monday in January as a public holiday with parades and volunteer work to 'remember' his life and work.
