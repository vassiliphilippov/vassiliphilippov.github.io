Etymology

Annona reticulata comes from a native word and a Latin word: Annona from a Caribbean word 'anón' for the sweet fruit, and reticulata meaning 'net-like' because the fruit's skin often has a rough, net pattern. So the name points to the custard apple tree and its netted, heart-shaped fruit.
