Etymology

The word coffee-and-chat is a simple combination of coffee and chat: coffee came into English from an old Arabic/Turkish word for the 'drink', and chat means 'friendly talk'. So a coffee-and-chat is an informal meeting to drink coffee and talk in a relaxed way.
