Etymology

Neoceratodus forsteri comes from parts that mean 'new' and 'horn-tooth': neo ('new') and ceratodus ('horn-tooth'), so imagine a fish with strong, horn-like teeth. It is called forsteri after a scientist, and that name helps you remember this air-breathing 'lungfish' that looks like a 'living fossil'.
