Etymology

Silver-leaved combines silver 'the shiny grey metal' and leaved 'having leaves', so it describes plants with pale, silvery leaves. A helpful image is leaves that shine like silver and reflect sunlight, which helps explain why these plants often save water in dry, sunny places.
