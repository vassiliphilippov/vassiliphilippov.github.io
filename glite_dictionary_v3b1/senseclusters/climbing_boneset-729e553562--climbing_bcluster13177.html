Etymology

climbing boneset is named because of how it grows: climbing shows it climbs up trees, and boneset was a common name for plants people used to help bone pain and fever. Imagine its sweet, vanilla-scented flowers climbing a tree; that image helps you remember it means 'a boneset plant that climbs'.
