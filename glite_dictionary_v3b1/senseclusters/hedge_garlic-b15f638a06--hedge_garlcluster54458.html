Etymology

hedge garlic is named for where it grows and how it smells: the plant often grows by hedges and its crushed leaves smell like garlic, so people put together hedge and garlic. The plant is also called 'garlic mustard', and this explains why the name means a herb with a garlic scent that grows near hedges.
