Etymology

Huainaputina comes from a local indigenous language of Peru and is the name of a high Andean volcano best known for its huge eruption in 1600. A helpful way to remember it is to notice it looks like Huayna in Huayna Picchu, where Huayna means 'young' in Quechua, so you can link Huainaputina to the Andes and to that famous volcanic event.
