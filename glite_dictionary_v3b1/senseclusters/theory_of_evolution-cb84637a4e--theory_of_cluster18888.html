Etymology

theory of evolution comes from the words theory (a careful 'explanation') and evolution (a slow 'change over time' or 'unfolding' of living things), giving the image of a big idea that explains how life unfolds. So, it means the scientific explanation for how living things have slowly changed and become different over many generations.
