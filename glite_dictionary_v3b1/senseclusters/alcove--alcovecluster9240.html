Etymology

The word alcove comes from Arabic al- 'the' plus a word for 'vault' or 'dome', so it originally named a covered, set-back space. Think of a 'cove', a small sheltered bay, and you have an image of an alcove: a little tucked-away space inside a room for a bed or shelves.
