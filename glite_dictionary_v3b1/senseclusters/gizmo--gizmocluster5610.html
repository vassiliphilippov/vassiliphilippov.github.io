Etymology

The word gizmo is a playful American word from the early 1900s used like gadget or 'thingamajig' for a small device when you don't know or forget its name. No one is sure where it first came from, so a helpful way to remember gizmo is to think of any small, clever object whose real name you can't recall. That's why we use gizmo as a quick name for a little tool.
