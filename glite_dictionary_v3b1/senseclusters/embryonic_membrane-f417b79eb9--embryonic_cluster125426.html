Etymology

embryonic membrane uses the words embryonic and membrane to name the thin inner 'skin' that surrounds the embryo before birth. Because this inner covering can sometimes stay on a baby's head when they are born, the term is used for the inner layer 'covering the embryo' or the newborn's head.
