Etymology

prescriptivism comes from the verb prescribe, which has the parts pre- 'before' and script 'write', so it originally meant to 'write before' or to give an order. So, in language, prescriptivism 'prescribes' rules like a doctor who gives a prescription — it tells people how they should use words.
