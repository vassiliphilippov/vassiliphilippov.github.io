Etymology

dock-walloper combines dock and wallop, where wallop means 'to hit' or 'to swing hard'. A helpful way to remember the word is to picture a strong dock worker who lifts and slams heavy cargo, which is why dock-walloper refers to a longshoreman who loads and unloads ships.
