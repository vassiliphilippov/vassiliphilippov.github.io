Etymology

law-maker is a simple compound: law, meaning 'a rule' from an old word for 'something laid down', plus maker, like in baker or builder, meaning 'one who makes'. So a law-maker is someone who 'makes' or 'sets' rules, which explains their job of creating, changing, or removing laws.
