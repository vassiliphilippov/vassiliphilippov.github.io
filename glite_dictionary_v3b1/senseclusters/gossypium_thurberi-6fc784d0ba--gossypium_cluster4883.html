Etymology

Gossypium thurberi gets its name from two parts: Gossypium, an old word for 'cotton', and thurberi, which honors a botanist named Thurber who studied plants in the American West. So the name tells us this is a kind of cotton shrub and that it was named after the scientist who worked with it.
