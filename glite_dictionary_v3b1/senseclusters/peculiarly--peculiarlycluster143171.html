Etymology

The word peculiarly comes from peculiar, which first meant 'particular to' someone because of the Latin peculium 'private property', and so it also took on the idea of being 'special' or 'not like the rest'. That's why peculiarly can mean 'to a greater degree than usual' or 'especially'.
