Etymology

The word metamorphism comes from the Greek parts meta- meaning 'change' and morph meaning 'form' or 'shape', the same root you see in metamorphosis. So metamorphism is the 'change of form' of rocks — how heat, pressure, or new chemicals change their shape and make-up.
