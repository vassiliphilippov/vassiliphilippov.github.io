Etymology

Melanocyte-stimulating hormone is a scientific name that literally says what it does: it is a hormone that stimulatesmelanocytes, the skin cells that make pigment. So the name reminds you that this chemical tells those cells 'to make more pigment', which is why it changes skin color.
