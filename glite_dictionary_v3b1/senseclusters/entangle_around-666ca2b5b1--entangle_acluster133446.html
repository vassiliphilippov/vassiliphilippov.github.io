Etymology

The verb entangle around comes from a clear picture: imagine vines, roots, or long hair twisting and wrapping themselves tightly around a post, stone, or necklace. Because of that image, entangle, meaning 'to twist', plus around, meaning 'to wrap', now means 'to wrap tightly around something' or 'to become stuck by wrapping'.
