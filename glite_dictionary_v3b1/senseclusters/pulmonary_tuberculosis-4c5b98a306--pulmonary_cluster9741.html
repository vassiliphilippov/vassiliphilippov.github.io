Etymology

The term pulmonary tuberculosis uses two parts that describe the illness: pulmonary means 'of the lungs' and tuberculosis comes from an old word for a small lump called a 'tubercle' that doctors saw in sick lungs. So the name literally says a 'lung' disease with small lumps, which helps you remember it is a serious infection of the lungs.
