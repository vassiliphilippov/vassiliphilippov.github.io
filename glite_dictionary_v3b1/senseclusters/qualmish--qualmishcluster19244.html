Etymology

qualmish comes from the simple noun qualm, which once meant a sudden bad feeling in the stomach and was linked long ago to an old word for 'sudden illness' or 'death'. A helpful way to remember it is to imagine a quick wave in your stomach that makes you feel 'sick' or disgusted, which is why qualmish describes feeling faint or easily upset by unpleasant sights.
