Etymology

Cadra cautella is the name scientists use for a small moth whose larvae eat stored grains, nuts and dried fruit and leave silk webbing. Picture tiny caterpillars weaving threads over jars in a pantry; that image helps you remember it is a pest that 'infests' stored food.
