Etymology

The word unscrupulously comes from the prefix un- meaning 'not' and scruple, which originally meant a 'small stone' that made people feel uneasy and hesitate about doing something wrong. So to act unscrupulously is to act without that 'moral' hesitation and without concern for 'right or wrong'.
