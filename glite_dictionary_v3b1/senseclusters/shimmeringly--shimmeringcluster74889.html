Etymology

shimmeringly comes from the verb shimmer plus the endings -ing and -ly, where shimmer means 'to shine with a soft, moving light' and -ly makes it an adverb. Think of light moving gently on water or a dress that seems to flicker, so something done shimmeringly shines in that soft, flickering way.
