Etymology

Croton tiglium takes its name from the first part Croton, a Greek word for 'tick' because the seeds look like little ticks, and the second part tiglium probably comes from a local name for the shrub. So, the name points to the plant's small seeds and the strong 'croton oil' made from them, which was used as a medicine.
