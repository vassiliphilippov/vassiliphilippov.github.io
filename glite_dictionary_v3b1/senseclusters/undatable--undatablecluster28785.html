Etymology

undatable in informal speech also breaks into un- 'not', date 'go out with someone', and -able 'able to'. A helpful way to remember this slang use is that it means someone is seen as 'not able to be dated'—that is, judged unlikely to be chosen as a romantic partner, and that label can be hurtful.
