Etymology

unimpeachably puts un- ('not') in front of impeach ('to accuse' or 'to blame'), so it literally means 'not able to be accused'. A helpful way to remember it is to think of someone acting so honestly or perfectly that there is no room for criticism, so they behave 'unimpeachably'.
