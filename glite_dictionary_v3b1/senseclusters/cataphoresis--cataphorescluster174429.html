Etymology

Cataphoresis comes from Greek parts cata- meaning 'down' or 'toward' and phorein meaning 'to carry', and the phor part is the same idea you see in metaphor. So cataphoresis literally means 'carrying toward', which helps remember it is the movement of charged particles toward an electrode called the cathode.
