Etymology

plausibleness comes from plausible, which originally came from a Latin word meaning 'to clap' or 'applaud'. Imagine people clapping for a story that sounds believable — that 'clap' idea changed to mean 'seems true or reasonable'. So plausibleness is the quality of seeming believable.
