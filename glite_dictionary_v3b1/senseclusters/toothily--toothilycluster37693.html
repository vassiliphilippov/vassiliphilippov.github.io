Etymology

toothily comes from the adjective toothy, which is built on tooth 'tooth' with the ending -ly that makes it 'in a ... way'. So, because teeth are for biting, toothily can mean 'in a biting or sharp way', which fits speech or behaviour that is harsh or cutting.
