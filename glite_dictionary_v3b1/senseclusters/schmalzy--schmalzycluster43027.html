Etymology

The word schmalzy comes from the Yiddish and German word schmaltz, which means 'chicken fat' and later came to mean 'too much feeling'. Think of something covered in greasy fat or syrup, too sticky and over the top. That's why schmalzy describes songs, movies, or letters that feel overly sweet or emotional.
