Etymology

X-linked gene gives a simple picture: the word linked means 'tied' to the X chromosome, like a small tag attached to the letter X. So it means a gene that sits on the 'X chromosome', and its effect often shows more in males because they have only one X and cannot 'hide' a bad copy.
