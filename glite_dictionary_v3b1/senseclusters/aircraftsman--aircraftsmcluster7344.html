Etymology

The word aircraftsman comes from aircraft ('a vehicle that flies') and craftsman ('a worker who makes or fixes things'). So an aircraftsman is literally a person who works on aircraft, which fits the junior Royal Air Force rank for people who maintain and help repair planes.
