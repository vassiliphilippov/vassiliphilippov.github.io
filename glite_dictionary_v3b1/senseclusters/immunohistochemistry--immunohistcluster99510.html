Etymology

immunohistochemistry comes from three parts: immuno- meaning 'immune' or 'defense', histo- meaning 'tissue', and chemistry meaning 'chemical methods'. So, immunohistochemistry is a method that uses the body's immune tools and chemical tags to find and show specific things in tissue samples, which is why doctors and scientists use it to spot disease.
