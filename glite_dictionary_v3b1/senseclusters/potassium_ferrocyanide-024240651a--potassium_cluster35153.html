Etymology

potassium ferrocyanide: The chemical name potassium ferrocyanide shows what it is: potassium comes from 'potash', a salt made from wood ashes, ferro means 'iron', and cyanide comes from a word for 'dark blue' because related iron chemicals were used to make a deep blue dye. So the name tells us it is a potassium salt that contains iron joined to cyanide groups.
