Etymology

in Oz comes from the book and film 'The Wizard of Oz', where Oz is a strange, magical land far from normal life. Saying you are in Oz creates the image of being in a place where the usual rules do not apply and 'anything is possible', so it means 'in a surreal or magical situation'.
