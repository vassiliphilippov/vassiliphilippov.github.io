Etymology

The word offhandedly comes from the phrase off-hand, where off means 'away' and hand means 'hand'; think of answering without reaching for notes. A helpful way to remember it is that an off-hand reply is quick and 'not planned', so to speak offhandedly is to say something casually and without careful thought.
