Etymology

creepo vibe comes from two words: creepo, a slang form of the word 'creepy', and vibe, a short way to say a feeling or the energy someone or some place gives. Imagine a quiet room or a person's look that makes you nervous; that bad feeling is the picture behind the phrase, so creepo vibe means 'an uneasy or suspicious feeling'.
