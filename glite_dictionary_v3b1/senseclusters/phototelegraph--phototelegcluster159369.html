Etymology

phototelegraph joins photo- ('light' or 'picture') with tele- ('far') and -graph ('write'), the same parts you see in photograph and telegraph. So a phototelegraph is a machine for 'sending pictures over a distance', which explains why it means a device that transmits photographs by telegraph.
