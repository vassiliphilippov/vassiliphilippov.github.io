Etymology

hysteric comes from the Greek root hystera meaning 'womb', because early doctors blamed the womb for sudden strong feelings. That old idea made the word mean 'out of control with feeling', so hysteric is used to describe behavior or reactions that are very extreme and not under control. Today we use it for strong, uncontrolled emotion, but the original idea about the womb is now considered incorrect.
