Etymology

Schizomycetes comes from Greek parts schizo- meaning 'split' and -mycetes meaning 'fungus', so it originally meant 'split fungi'. Scientists once used the name for bacteria that reproduce by splitting, which helps you remember the old name even though it is no longer used.
