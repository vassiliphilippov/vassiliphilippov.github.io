Etymology

Retail merchant comes from the word retail, which comes from an old French word meaning 'to cut' or 'to sell in small pieces', because sellers would divide goods to sell little by little, and merchant simply means a seller. So a retail merchant is a seller who sells small amounts directly to ordinary people rather than selling large amounts for resale.
