Etymology

gallowglass comes from Irish words gall 'foreign' and óglach 'young warrior', so it originally meant a 'foreign young soldier'. These were strong, often Scottish fighters hired in medieval Ireland, so gallowglass now means a heavily armed mercenary or 'a soldier who was paid to fight'.
