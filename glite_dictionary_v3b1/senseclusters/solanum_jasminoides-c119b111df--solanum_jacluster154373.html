Etymology

Solanum jasminoides gets its name from two parts: Solanum tells us it is in the 'nightshade' group, and jasminoides means 'like jasmine' because its small white flowers look like jasmine. That's why the name helps gardeners remember it as a jasmine-like climbing plant.
