Etymology

The name Gloria Steinem belongs to a well-known American feminist and journalist who helped lead the women's movement in the 1960s and 1970s. Because she spoke at meetings, wrote powerful articles, and helped organize groups, her name now often stands for 'feminist' activism and the fight for women's rights.
