Etymology

Pezizaceae comes from the fungus group name Peziza, known for small, cup-shaped mushrooms, plus the Latin ending -aceae that means 'family' in plant and fungus names. So Pezizaceae means the 'family' of cup fungi that includes the Peziza types.
