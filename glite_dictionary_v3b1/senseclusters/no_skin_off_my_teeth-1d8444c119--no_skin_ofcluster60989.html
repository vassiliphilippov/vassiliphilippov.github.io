Etymology

The idiom no skin off my teeth may come from the older phrase no skin off my nose, which used the picture of losing 'skin' from a body part to show real harm. The odd image of skin coming off your teeth makes it clear nothing is hurt, so the phrase means 'it doesn't bother me'.
