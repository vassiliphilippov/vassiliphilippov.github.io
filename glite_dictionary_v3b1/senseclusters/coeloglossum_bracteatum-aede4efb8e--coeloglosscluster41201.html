Etymology

Coeloglossum bracteatum comes from old scientific words: Coeloglossum means a 'hollow tongue' and bracteatum means 'with leaf-like parts' near the flowers. So the name paints a simple picture — a hollow, tongue-shaped part and extra leaves by the blooms — which helps people recognise this orchid today.
