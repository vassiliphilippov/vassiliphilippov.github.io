Etymology

Western Wall gets its name from being the wall on the 'western' side of the ancient Temple in Jerusalem; it is the last wall left from that Temple. People come to pray, leave notes in its cracks and remember and mourn the past, so the name points to a real, very old place of prayer and sorrow.
