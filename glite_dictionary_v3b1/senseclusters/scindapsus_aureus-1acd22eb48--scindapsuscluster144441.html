Etymology

Scindapsus aureus is a name that describes the plant: aureus means 'gold', because its leaves often have golden patches, and Scindapsus comes from an old word for a climbing plant, showing how it climbs or hangs on stems. That's why the name fits this common houseplant often called 'pothos' or 'devil's ivy'.
