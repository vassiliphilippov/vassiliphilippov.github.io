Etymology

Centrarchidae comes from Greek parts like kentron 'sharp point' and archos a word used in fish names, and it even shares the same root as the English word centre. So, the name points to the family's sharp, spiny fins seen in sunfish, bass, and bluegill, which helps you remember these fish by their fin 'spines'.
