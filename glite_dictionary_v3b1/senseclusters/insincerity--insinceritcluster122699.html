Etymology

insincerity comes from in- 'not' and sincere 'pure' or 'honest'. One popular way to remember it is the story that sincere once meant 'without wax' — imagine a potter hiding cracks with wax to make a pot look perfect — so insincerity is when someone does not show true, honest feelings.
