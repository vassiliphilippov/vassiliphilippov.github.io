Etymology

deglaze comes from the parts de- ('remove') and glaze (a 'shiny coating', like the sweet glaze on a doughnut). So in cooking, to deglaze a pan is to 'remove' or loosen the browned 'glaze' by adding liquid so those bits become a tasty sauce.
