Etymology

Quirk bead gets its name from two small parts: a rounded bead and a tiny groove called a quirk next to it. Craftspeople cut the little groove to separate the bead from the flat surface so the edge casts a small shadow and 'stands out' as decoration, so today quirk bead means this small decorative edge.
