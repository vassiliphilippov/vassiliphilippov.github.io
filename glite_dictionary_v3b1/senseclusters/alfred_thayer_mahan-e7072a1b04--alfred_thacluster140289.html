Etymology

Alfred Thayer Mahan was a United States naval officer and writer who made a simple, powerful idea famous: that control of the sea helps a country become strong. His books showed how ships and ports give a nation strength, so the phrase 'sea power' became a key idea in how countries planned their navies.
