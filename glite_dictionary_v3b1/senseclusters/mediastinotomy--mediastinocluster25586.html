Etymology

The word mediastinotomy comes from mediastinum, the part of the chest that sits 'in the middle' between the lungs, and the suffix -otomy, which means 'cutting into', as in tracheotomy. So, a mediastinotomy is a surgery that makes a cut into the middle of the chest to reach organs there.
