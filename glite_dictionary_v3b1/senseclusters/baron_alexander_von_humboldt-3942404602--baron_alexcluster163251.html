Etymology

Baron Alexander von Humboldt is the name of a German explorer and scientist who travelled mountains, rivers and jungles and carefully recorded plants, animals and maps. Because he made many important discoveries, people began to use his name for towns, mountains and species, so today Baron Alexander von Humboldt often names a 'place' or a 'species' that honours him.
