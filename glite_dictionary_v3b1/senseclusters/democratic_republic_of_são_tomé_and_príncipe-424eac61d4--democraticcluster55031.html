Etymology

Democratic Republic of São Tomé and Príncipe combines the official title Democratic Republic with the names of the two islands, São Tomé and Príncipe. Portuguese sailors named São Tomé after Saint Thomas because they arrived on his day, and they called Príncipe 'the prince' to honour a Portuguese prince, and after 'independence' in 1975 the country kept this full name.
