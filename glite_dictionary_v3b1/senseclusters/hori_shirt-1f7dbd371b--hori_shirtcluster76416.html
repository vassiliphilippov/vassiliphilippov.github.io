Etymology

hori shirt is a traditional Japanese shirt often seen at festivals; one popular story says the name comes from a short, loose shirt that festival helpers wore, and that short shirt was called hori. Those helpers carried heavy floats and worked in the streets, so the image of their simple shirts became linked to festival dress, which is why hori shirt now means a traditional, 'casual' shirt for 'festivals' and everyday wear.
