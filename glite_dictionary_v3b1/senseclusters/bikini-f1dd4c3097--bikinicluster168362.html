Etymology

Bikini is the name of an atoll in the Marshall Islands, and the name comes from the local language. The atoll became famous when the United States used it for nuclear tests after World War II. Because of those powerful tests, the place's name is often linked with the explosions, and it later gave its name to the small swimsuit called the 'bikini'.
