Etymology

dihydric alcohol breaks into parts: di- means 'two' and alcohol refers to a chemical with -OH groups. So a dihydric alcohol is a molecule with two -OH parts, also called a 'glycol', and imagining it as a tiny object with two sticky hands helps explain why it reacts more in chemical reactions.
