Etymology

Ciudad de Mexico comes from the old Aztec capital Tenochtitlan, a city built on an island in a lake and the home of the Mexica people. When the Spanish gave it the name Ciudad de Mexico—literally Ciudad ('city') de ('of') Mexico—the name stayed, so today it simply means the 'city of Mexico' and refers to the country's capital.
