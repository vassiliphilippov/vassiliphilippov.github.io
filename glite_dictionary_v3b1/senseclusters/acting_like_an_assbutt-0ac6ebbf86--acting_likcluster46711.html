Etymology

acting like an assbutt probably comes from joining the rude words ass and butt to make a funny picture of someone who is stubborn or behaves in a silly, rude way. The image is of a person acting like a noisy, stubborn donkey or letting their 'backside' seem to be in charge, which feels annoying and foolish. That's why the phrase means 'behaving in a foolish, irritating, or rude way'.
