Etymology

The noun naiad family comes from naiad, the name for the water spirits in old Greek myths called 'water nymphs', and family, meaning a group of related plants. Because these plants live in lakes and rivers and are delicate, the name points to 'water plants', so today naiad family means a group of small plants that grow in fresh water.
