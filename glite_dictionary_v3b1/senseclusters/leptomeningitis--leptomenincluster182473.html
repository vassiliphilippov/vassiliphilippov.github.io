Etymology

leptomeningitis comes from Greek parts: lepto- meaning 'thin', mening- meaning 'membrane', and -itis meaning 'inflammation', with -itis seen in words like arthritis. So, leptomeningitis literally means 'inflammation of the thin membranes' that cover the brain and spinal cord.
