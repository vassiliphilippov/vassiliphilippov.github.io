Etymology

The term efferent fiber comes from old words: efferent meaning 'to carry away' and fiber meaning 'thread'. Imagine a tiny thread or road carrying messages out from the brain to the muscles or glands, so a efferent fiber is a nerve that sends signals away from the body's command center to make muscles move or glands work.
