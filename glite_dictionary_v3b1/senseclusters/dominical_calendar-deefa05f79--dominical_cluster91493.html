Etymology

dominical calendar The noun dominical calendar comes from a church word dominical, meaning 'of the Lord' and used for 'Sunday', and the word calendar, meaning 'a list of dates'. Churches long ago used these calendars to mark which days were Sundays, so today dominical calendar means a calendar that shows the dates of Sundays.
