Etymology

pedipulator design may come from two parts: pedi meaning 'foot' and manipulator meaning 'tool that handles things'. Imagine a machine leg or hand that steps and grips in small, careful moves; that's why pedipulator design describes a robot design that gives very precise, arm- or leg-like movement control.
