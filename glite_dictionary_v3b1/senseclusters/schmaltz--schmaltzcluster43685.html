Etymology

The word schmaltz comes from the Yiddish and German word schmalz, which means 'fat' or 'chicken fat'. Just as fat makes food very rich and heavy, emotional schmaltz makes music or films feel too rich and sentimental, which is why the word means 'excessive sentiment'.
