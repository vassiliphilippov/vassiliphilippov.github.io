Etymology

inhalation anesthesia comes from the simple image of inhalation ('to breathe in') and anesthesia ('not feeling pain'). Doctors give a medicine as a gas that the patient breathes in so they 'sleep' and do not feel pain during surgery, so the phrase means using gas breathed in to produce general anesthesia.
