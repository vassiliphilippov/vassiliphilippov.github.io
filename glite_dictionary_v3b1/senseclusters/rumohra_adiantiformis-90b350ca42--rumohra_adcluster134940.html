Etymology

Rumohra adiantiformis may come from the old group name Rumohra and the Latin word adiantiformis, which means 'shaped like Adiantum', the maidenhair fern. So the name gives a clear image: a fern with maidenhair-like fronds, which explains why florists use it in arrangements.
