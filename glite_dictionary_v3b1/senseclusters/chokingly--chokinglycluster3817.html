Etymology

Chokingly comes from the common verb choke, which means 'to have your breath stopped' or to feel your throat closing, plus the endings -ing and -ly that turn it into a word that tells how something happens. So, when something happens chokingly, it is so strong or tight that it makes it hard to breathe or speak.
