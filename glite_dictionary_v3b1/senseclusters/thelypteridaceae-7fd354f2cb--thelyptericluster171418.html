Etymology

The word Thelypteridaceae comes from the fern group name Thelypteris, which has Greek parts thely- meaning 'female' and pteris meaning 'fern'. The ending -aceae shows it is a plant family, as in Rosaceae for roses, so the whole name means the 'family of Thelypteris ferns' and helps you remember it is a group of ferns.
