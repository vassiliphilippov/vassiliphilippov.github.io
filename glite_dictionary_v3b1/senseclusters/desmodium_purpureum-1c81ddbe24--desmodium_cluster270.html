Etymology

Desmodium purpureum may come from two old words. The first part, Desmodium, comes from the Greek word 'desmos' meaning 'a chain' because its seed pods break into linked pieces, and purpureum is Latin for 'purple', so the name pictures a plant with purple flowers and chain-like pods.
