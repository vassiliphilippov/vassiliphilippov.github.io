Etymology

paralyzing as a verb comes from the Greek parts para- 'beside' and lyein 'to loosen', so it literally carries the idea of making something 'loose' beside normal control. A helpful way to remember it is that to paralyze someone is to stop their normal movement or action, so they cannot work or behave as before.
