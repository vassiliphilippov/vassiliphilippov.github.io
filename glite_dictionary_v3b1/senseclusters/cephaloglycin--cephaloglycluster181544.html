Etymology

cephaloglycin combines cephalo- and -glycin. cephalo- comes from a Greek word meaning 'head' and links the drug to the cephalosporin family which was named after the mold Cephalosporium, and -glycin comes from the simple amino acid glycine, so the name shows it is a chemical antibiotic related to cephalosporins.
