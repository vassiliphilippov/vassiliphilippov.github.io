Etymology

Dies Irae comes from the Latin words Dies meaning 'day' and Irae meaning 'wrath', and it is the opening line of an old Latin hymn about the 'Last Judgment'. Because the hymn pictures the end of the world, its short, sad tune was often used by composers to show death or doom, so today Dies Irae can mean the hymn or that musical theme.
