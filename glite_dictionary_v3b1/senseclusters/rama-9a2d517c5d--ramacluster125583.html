Etymology

Rama comes from the Sanskrit root ram meaning 'to delight' or 'be pleased', so his name suggests someone who is beloved or brings joy. In Hindu stories Rama is an incarnation of the god Vishnu and the hero of the Ramayana, which is why he is remembered as the ideal, good king.
