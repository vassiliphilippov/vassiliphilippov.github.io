Etymology

The verb winnow can also mean 'to blow gently through', and this sense comes from the link to wind ('moving air'); a helpful way to remember it is to imagine a soft breeze that moves and lifts things like grass or hair. That's why we say the wind 'winnows' the field when it blows through it.
