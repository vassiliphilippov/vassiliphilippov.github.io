Etymology

radio dispatch comes from two parts: radio (sending sound without wires) and dispatch (a short, urgent message sent quickly). When police, ambulances, or news teams used radio to send quick reports to their central office, people began to call those reports radio dispatches, so today radio dispatch means 'a message sent by radio'.
