Etymology

Brahmanism comes from the ancient Indian word Brahman, which meant 'ultimate spirit', and from Brahmin, the name for the priest class who kept the Vedas. So Brahmanism originally named the old religious system led by those priests and is the ancestor of modern Hinduism.
