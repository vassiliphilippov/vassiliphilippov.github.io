Etymology

Rye whiskey comes from the simple idea of making 'whiskey' with rye grain. When makers used mostly rye instead of corn or barley, the drink often tasted spicier and drier, so the name tells you what kind of whiskey it is. So today rye whiskey means 'a whiskey made from rye grain'.
