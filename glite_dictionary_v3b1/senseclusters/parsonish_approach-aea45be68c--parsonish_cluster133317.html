Etymology

The phrase parsonish approach comes from the image of a parson, a church priest, standing at the front and giving a sermon about how people should live, and the bold way of speaking becomes the approach. So calling something a parsonish approach means it feels 'moralistic' or 'preachy' rather than relaxed.
