Etymology

Conodonta comes from Greek parts konos 'cone' and odont- 'tooth', because these ancient animals are known mainly from tiny, cone-shaped tooth fossils. So the name helps you remember that their 'teeth' are the small fossils scientists use to study and date old rocks.
