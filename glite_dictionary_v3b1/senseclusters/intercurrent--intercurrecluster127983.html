Etymology

intercurrent comes from the prefix inter- meaning 'between', as in international, and current from a root meaning 'to run' or 'flow', like a water 'current'. So an intercurrent illness or event is one that 'runs' in the middle of another one — it happens during and can interrupt the original event.
