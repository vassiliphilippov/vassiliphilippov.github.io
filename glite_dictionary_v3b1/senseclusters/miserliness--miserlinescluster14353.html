Etymology

The word miserliness comes from the old word miser, which meant 'wretched' or 'unhappy', and you can see the same root in miserable and misery. A helpful way to remember it is that a miser clings to money and often makes life 'wretched' for themselves and others, so miserliness means being very unwilling to spend money.
