Etymology

Senna marilandica is a plant name made of Senna, a word for plants long used as a medicine, and marilandica, a Latin form meaning 'from Maryland'. So the name gives a simple image of a wild senna herb from Maryland with yellow flowers whose leaves were used for remedies.
