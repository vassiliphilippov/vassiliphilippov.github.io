Etymology

thermal resistor is a simple name: thermal means 'heat' and resistor is 'something that resists' the flow of electricity. Imagine a small part that resists electric current more when it gets hot and less when it is cool; that's why it 'changes resistance with temperature' and is used to protect or adjust a circuit when the temperature changes.
