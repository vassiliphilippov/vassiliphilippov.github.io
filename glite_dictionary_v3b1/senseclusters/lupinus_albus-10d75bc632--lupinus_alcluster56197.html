Etymology

Lupinus albus comes from Latin: lupinus linked to the word 'wolf' because people thought the plant took nutrients from the soil like a wolf, and albus means 'white' for its pale flowers. So the name gives a clear picture of a 'white wolf' plant and its old link to soil, even though today Lupinus albus is grown for animal feed and to protect and improve soil.
