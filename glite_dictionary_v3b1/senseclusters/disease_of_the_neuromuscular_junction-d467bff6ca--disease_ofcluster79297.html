Etymology

disease of the neuromuscular junction comes from the idea of a tiny meeting place in the body where a nerve meets a muscle, the junction. If that meeting place is not working, 'messages' cannot pass and the muscle becomes weak. That's why the term means a sickness that stops the 'communication' between nerves and muscles.
