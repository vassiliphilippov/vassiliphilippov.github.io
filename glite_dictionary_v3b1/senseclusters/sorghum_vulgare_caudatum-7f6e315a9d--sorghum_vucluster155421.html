Etymology

Sorghum vulgare caudatum comes from words that describe how the plant looks: Sorghum is the grain, vulgare means 'common', and caudatum means 'with a tail' because the plant's seed head often has a long, tail-like part. So the name points to this Sudan variety known for its very large, soft, white grains.
