Etymology

Natrix maura may come from old Latin words: Natrix meaning 'water snake' and maura meaning 'dark' or 'from Mauritania'. So, the name creates a clear image of a dark, water-loving snake, which helps explain why it refers to the small, non-poisonous, viper-like snake often seen by rivers in Europe and North Africa.
