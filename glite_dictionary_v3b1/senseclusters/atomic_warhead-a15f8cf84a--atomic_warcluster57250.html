Etymology

Atomic warhead brings together two images: atomic means energy from tiny 'atoms' that can be split to make a huge blast, and warhead is the 'head' or front part of a missile that holds the weapon. So, an atomic warhead is the explosive front of a bomb or missile that uses atomic energy, which is why it means 'the explosive part of a missile or bomb that carries a nuclear weapon'.
