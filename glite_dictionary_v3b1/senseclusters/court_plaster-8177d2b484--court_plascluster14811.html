Etymology

court plaster got its name from small, neat sticky patches used at royal courts long ago. The name links court, meaning royal or fancy, and plaster, meaning a sticky patch, and these were made from fish glue on silk to hide and heal small cuts. That's why court plaster now means a small sticky patch for minor wounds.
