Etymology

Dardic comes from the name of the Dards, an old people who lived in the mountain valleys of Kashmir, eastern Afghanistan and northern Pakistan. So Dardic simply means 'of the Dards', which is why it is used for the group of languages such as Kashmiri and Shina.
