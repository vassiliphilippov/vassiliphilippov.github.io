Etymology

Bibliopole comes from Greek parts biblio- meaning 'book' and -pole meaning 'seller', so it literally means 'book seller'. You can see biblio- in bibliography, which also has to do with books. Today a bibliopole is a dealer who buys and sells rare or secondhand books, which explains its modern meaning.
