Etymology

Thelypteris dryopteris is a scientific name that comes from older plant naming: Thelypteris groups certain ferns and dryopteris was used for ferns that often grow by oak trees. So the name gives a picture of a fern living in shady, damp woods, which matches how the plant grows today.
