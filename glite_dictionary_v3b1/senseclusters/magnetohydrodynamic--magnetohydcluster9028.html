Etymology

magnetohydrodynamic combines magneto- ('magnet'), hydro- ('water' or 'fluid') and dynamic ('movement'). So a magnetohydrodynamic device makes electricity by moving a fluid that carries electric charge through a magnetic field, using the magnetic force on the moving fluid to produce current.
