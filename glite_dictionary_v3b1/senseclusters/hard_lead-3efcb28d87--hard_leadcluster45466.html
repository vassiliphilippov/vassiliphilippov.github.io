Etymology

hard lead comes from the simple idea of making soft lead stronger by adding a harder metal. Workers mixed in a metal like antimony so the soft, bendy lead became hard and could be used in pipes, bullets, and other parts, so hard lead means 'lead made harder for industrial use'.
