Etymology

The word eclogue comes from the Greek word eklogē, meaning 'a choosing' or 'selection' and related to the English word 'select'. Poets used eclogue for a short, chosen poem, often a little conversation about country life, so the origin helps explain why an eclogue is a short poem about rural life.
