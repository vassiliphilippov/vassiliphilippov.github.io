Etymology

uncircumcision begins with un- 'not' and the word circumcision, which comes from parts meaning circum- 'around' and a root 'to cut' (the same circum- you see in circumference). So uncircumcision literally means 'not cut around', which explains its modern use for someone who has not had the foreskin removed.
