Etymology

workers' rights grew from the image of many workers' standing together with signs and asking for rights like 'fair pay' and 'safe working conditions'. So today workers' rights means the 'legal and social protections' people have at work, including 'fair pay', 'safe conditions' and the right 'to organize'.
