Etymology

Crataegus pedicellata may come from old Greek and Latin words. The first part, Crataegus, was an old name for 'hawthorn' and points to 'strong wood', and pedicellata means 'with little stalks', so the name describes a hawthorn with small stalks that hold its clusters of flowers and red fruit.
