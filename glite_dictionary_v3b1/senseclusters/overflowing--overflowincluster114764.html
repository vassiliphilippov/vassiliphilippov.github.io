Etymology

The word overflowing breaks into over meaning 'too much' or 'above', flow meaning 'to move in a stream', and -ing showing an action or state. Picture water rising and going 'over' the edge and 'flow' out, so overflowing means so full that the contents, especially liquid, spill out.
