Etymology

Wild sarsaparilla is named for a forest plant whose aromatic roots were used like sarsaparilla, the sweet drink, so people used the wild plant as a substitute. That's why people called it 'wild sarsaparilla' and why the name means 'a wild herb used instead of sarsaparilla'.
