Etymology

mind-blower comes from the two simple words mind and blow, like the phrase 'to blow your mind'. Imagine a sudden strong wind that surprises you and carries things away; a mind-blower is something so surprising or impressive it feels like your mind is blown, which is why it means a very amazing or shocking thing.
