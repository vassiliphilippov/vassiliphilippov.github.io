Etymology

The character Minnie Mouse was created as a female, small partner for Mickey Mouse, so picture a tiny mouse in a dress and bow. Because the name Minnie sounds like 'mini', which means 'small', Minnie Mouse became the famous cartoon mouse who is Mickey's 'partner'.
