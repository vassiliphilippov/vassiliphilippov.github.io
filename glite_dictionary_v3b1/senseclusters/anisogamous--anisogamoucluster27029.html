Etymology

anisogamous comes from Greek parts an- 'not', iso- 'equal', and -gamous 'marriage' or 'union', the same ending you see in monogamous. So it literally means 'not equal union', which helps you remember it describes reproductive cells called gametes that are different in size or form.
