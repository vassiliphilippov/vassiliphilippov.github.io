Etymology

The word deathblow is a simple compound: death, which means 'the end of life', and blow, which means 'a strong hit'. It originally meant a hit that 'kills', so today deathblow can also mean a final act or 'final defeat' that ends something, like a battle, a person's suffering, or a company.
