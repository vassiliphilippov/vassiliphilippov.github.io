Etymology

ecosphere combines eco- meaning 'home' and -sphere meaning 'ball' or 'area', like the -sphere in atmosphere. Think of a small 'home in a glass ball' where plants and animals keep each other alive, which is why an ecosphere is a sealed, self-sustaining environment.
