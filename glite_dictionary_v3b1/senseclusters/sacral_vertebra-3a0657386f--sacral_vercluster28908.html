Etymology

sacral vertebra comes from an old idea: sacral comes from Latin for 'sacred' and vertebra means a bone of the spine. Long ago people called the bones at the base of the spine the 'sacred bone' or 'sacrum', so the name now refers to the lower vertebrae that fuse together to form that strong base.
