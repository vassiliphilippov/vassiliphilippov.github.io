Etymology

Pseudopleuronectes breaks into pseudo- ('false'), pleuro- ('side') and -nectes ('swimmer'), so it literally means 'false side-swimmer'. So the name points to a group of flatfish called flounders that lie on one side and are like, but not the same as, other 'side-swimmer' fish.
