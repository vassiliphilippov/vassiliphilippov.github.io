Etymology

burweed marsh elder comes from two images: burweed means the plant makes clingy burrs on its seeds, and marsh elder shows it grows in wet, marshy fields and looks a bit like elder plants. Those clingy burrs and the heavy pollen explain why the plant can 'irritate skin' and cause 'hay fever'.
