Etymology

chavtastically fun mixes chav and 'fantastically' and one popular story says it comes from British youth style known for bright clothes, tracksuits, big jewellery, and loud pop music. Imagine that loud, glittering party—this showy, high-energy picture is why the phrase means 'extremely enjoyable in a flashy, chav style'.
