Etymology

The word autarkical comes from Greek parts auto- meaning 'self' and arkēs meaning 'enough', and you see auto- in words like automatic and autonomy. So an autarkical policy is one that tries to be 'enough by itself' — a country aiming to make what it needs and not depend on imports.
