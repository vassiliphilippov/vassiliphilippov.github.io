Etymology

Douglas hemlock probably got its name from a Scottish plant collector named Douglas who first brought this tall fir to Europe. People called it hemlock because its short needles and small egg-shaped cones looked like other hemlock trees, so the name helps you remember 'a tall fir tree with short needles and egg-shaped cones'.
