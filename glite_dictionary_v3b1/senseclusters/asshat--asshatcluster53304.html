Etymology

asshat is made from the two words ass and hat, where ass is a rude word for 'donkey' or 'fool' and the image of someone 'wearing' that idea (a donkey hat) makes a sharp, funny insult. So, calling someone an asshat means they are acting in a foolish, annoying, or rude way.
