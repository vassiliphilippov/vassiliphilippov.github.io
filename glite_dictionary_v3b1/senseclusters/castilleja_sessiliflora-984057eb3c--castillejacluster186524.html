Etymology

Castilleja sessiliflora is a plant name made from a Spanish plant scientist's name and a Latin description. The second part, sessiliflora, means 'flowers without stalks' and so the name paints a picture of flowers sitting right on the stem, which helps you remember what this prairie plant looks like.
