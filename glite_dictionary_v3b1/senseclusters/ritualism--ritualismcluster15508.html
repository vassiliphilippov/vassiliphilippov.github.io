Etymology

ritualism comes from ritual, which goes back to Latin ritus meaning 'rite' or 'ceremony', and the ending -ism meaning 'a practice' or 'belief'. So ritualism can mean the study or use of set ceremonies and rites, often as a way of learning about or taking part in them.
