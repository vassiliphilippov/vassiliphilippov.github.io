Etymology

The botanical name Matricaria tchihatchewii starts as a simple label: Matricaria names the daisy-like plant group and tchihatchewii likely comes from a person's name. Scientists often add '-ii' to show a plant is 'named after' someone, so the full name means 'Tchihatchew's Matricaria' and helps people know what kind of plant it is and who first found or studied it.
