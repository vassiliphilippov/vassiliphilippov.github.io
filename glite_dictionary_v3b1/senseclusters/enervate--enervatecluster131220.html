Etymology

enervate comes from Latin parts e- meaning 'out' and nervus meaning 'sinew' or 'nerve', so it once meant to 'take the sinew out' of someone. So, think of taking away someone's 'nerve' or strength—today to enervate is to drain someone's energy or make them weak.
