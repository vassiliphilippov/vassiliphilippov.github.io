Etymology

atopognosis comes from Greek parts a- meaning 'without', topo- meaning 'place', and -gnosis meaning 'knowledge', and you can see topo- in topography (maps of places) and -gnosis in diagnosis (knowing). So, atopognosis means 'without knowledge of place', which explains why it is the inability to tell where on the body someone has been touched.
