Etymology

imparipinnate comes from Latin parts: im- meaning 'not', par meaning 'equal', and pinnate from a Latin word for 'feather'. So an imparipinnate leaf is a feather-like leaf that is 'not equal' at the tip, with a single extra leaflet at the end, giving an odd number of leaflets.
