Etymology

encumber comes from the prefix en- and the old word cumber; en- means 'to make' and cumber meant 'to weigh down' or 'block the way', which you also see in cumbersome ('heavy and awkward'). So, to encumber someone is to 'burden' them or make movement or action difficult.
