Etymology

Caliphate State comes from the two parts caliphate and state; a caliphate is an old word for a Muslim ruler's country and state means 'country'. The name was chosen by a violent group from Turkey to show it wanted to create a religious 'state', so the words explain the group's aim and why it is described as a terrorist group.
