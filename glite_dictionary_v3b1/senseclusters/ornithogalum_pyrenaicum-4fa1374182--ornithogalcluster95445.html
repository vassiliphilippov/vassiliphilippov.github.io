Etymology

Ornithogalum pyrenaicum may come from old Greek parts ornitho meaning 'bird' and galum meaning 'milk', a picture like 'bird's milk' that points to its bright white, star-shaped flowers. The second part pyrenaicum says it is 'from the Pyrenees', so the name helps you remember a white-flowered plant from that region whose young shoots are edible.
