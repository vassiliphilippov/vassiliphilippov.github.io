Etymology

Eighteenth Amendment was the eighteenth change to the U.S. Constitution that made the making and selling of alcohol illegal and started the era called 'Prohibition' with secret bars and bootleggers. So, the phrase refers to the law that 'banned alcohol' in the United States from 1920 until it ended in 1933.
