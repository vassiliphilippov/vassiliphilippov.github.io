Etymology

Gaultheria procumbens is named after a French-Canadian doctor called Gaultier, and the species name procumbens means 'lying down' in Latin, which gives the picture of the plant's low, creeping habit. So the name both honors the person and describes a small, ground-hugging shrub whose leaves are used to make wintergreen oil.
