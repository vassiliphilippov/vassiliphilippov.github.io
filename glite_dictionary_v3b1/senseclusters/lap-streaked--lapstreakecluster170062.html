Etymology

The word lap-streaked comes from lap- meaning 'to overlap' and the part streak meaning 'a long strip' of wood, so picture roof tiles or fish scales lying over each other. So a lap-streaked boat is made with wooden planks that overlap along the side of the boat, which makes it stronger and keeps out water.
