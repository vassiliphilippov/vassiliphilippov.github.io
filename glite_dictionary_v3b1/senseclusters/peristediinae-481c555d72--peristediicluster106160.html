Etymology

Peristediinae comes from the scientific genus Peristedion, built from Greek parts peri- 'around' and stethos 'chest', which gives a picture of bony plates around the fish's body. That's why the name fits these 'armored' searobins — their bodies are covered with armor-like plates.
