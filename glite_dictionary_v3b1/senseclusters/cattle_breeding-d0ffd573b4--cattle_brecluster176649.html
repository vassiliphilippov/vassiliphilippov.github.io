Etymology

cattle breeding comes from the everyday act of choosing which farm animals should mate. Farmers picked the best cattle, strong cows and bulls, to breed so their calves would give more milk or stay healthy, so cattle breeding means 'careful mating and raising of cattle' to improve the herd.
