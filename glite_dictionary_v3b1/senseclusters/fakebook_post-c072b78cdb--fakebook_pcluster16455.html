Etymology

Fakebook post comes from the idea of making a 'fake' Facebook update; imagine a post that looks real on a made-up site called Fakebook but is changed to look silly to 'mock' real posts. That's why the phrase means a social media post that 'mocks' or makes fun of typical Facebook content.
