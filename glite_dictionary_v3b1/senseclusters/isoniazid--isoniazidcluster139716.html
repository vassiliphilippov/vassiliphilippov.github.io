Etymology

isoniazid comes from the chemical name isonicotinic acid hydrazide, the long name for the chemical when it was first used as a medicine. Scientists shortened that chemical name to isoniazid, so a helpful way to remember it is that the word is the short name for the drug used to treat 'tuberculosis'.
