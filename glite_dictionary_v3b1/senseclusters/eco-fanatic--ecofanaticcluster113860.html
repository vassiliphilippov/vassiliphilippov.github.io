Etymology

eco-fanatic combines eco- (from a Greek root meaning 'home' or 'environment') and fanatic, which is like a very strong 'fan' with extreme passion. So an eco-fanatic is someone with intense enthusiasm for environmental causes, sometimes to the point of being too extreme.
