Etymology

oxytetracycline breaks into oxy- 'oxygen', tetra- 'four', and -cycline 'ring', like the common word 'cycle'. It names a medicine in the tetracycline family of antibiotics that has a four-ring chemical shape with oxygen in it, so the name helps you remember it is a specific antibiotic.
