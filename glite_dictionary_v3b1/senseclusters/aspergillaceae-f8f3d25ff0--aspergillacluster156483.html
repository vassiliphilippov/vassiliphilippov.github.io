Etymology

Aspergillaceae comes from the genus name Aspergillus, which in turn comes from the Latin aspergillum meaning 'sprinkler' because the fungus' spores look like a tiny brush that sprinkles. The ending -aceae shows it is a 'family', so Aspergillaceae means the family of Aspergillus molds, many of which are important in industry and medicine.
