Etymology

staghorn sumac gets its name from the plant's young branches: the word staghorn points to the soft, fuzzy twigs that stick out like a 'stag's' antlers. So the picture of antler-like, hairy branches helps you remember this shrub with bright red berries and red leaves in autumn.
