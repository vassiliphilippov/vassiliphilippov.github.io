Etymology

repetitiousness comes from the familiar verb repeat, with the prefix re- meaning 'again' and the ending -ness showing a 'quality'. So repetitiousness is the quality of doing or saying the same thing again and again, which is why it often feels boring or makes speech hard to follow.
