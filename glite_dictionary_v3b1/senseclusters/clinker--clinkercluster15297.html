Etymology

Clinker for a hard paving brick comes from an old Dutch and German word klinker for a very hard tile that used to 'to ring' when struck. Think of the English word clink ('to ring'); because these bricks are so hard they could make a ringing sound, the name stuck and now a clinker means a very hard brick used for paving.
