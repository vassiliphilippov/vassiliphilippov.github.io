Etymology

Neroli comes from Italy and the place name Nerola, and it is also the name given to a perfume oil made from bitter orange flowers ('orange blossom') that a noblewoman long ago liked to wear. So, as a girl's name, Neroli suggests the idea of a sweet floral scent and elegant beauty.
