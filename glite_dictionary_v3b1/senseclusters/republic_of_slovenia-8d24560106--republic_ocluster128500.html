Etymology

Republic of Slovenia is the country's official name and it simply means the state of the Slovene people. The word Republic shows it is a state and Slovenia comes from the word 'Slovene', the name for the local people, so the full name means 'the country of the Slovenes' and has been used since independence in 1991.
