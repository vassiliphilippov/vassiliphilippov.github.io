Etymology

Threskiornithidae comes from Greek parts threskio- meaning 'religious' or 'sacred' and -ornith- meaning 'bird', with -idae showing a family. So it literally means a 'sacred bird family', which fits because ibises were seen as sacred in ancient times and today the name is used for the family of ibises and spoonbills.
