Etymology

epizoology comes from Greek parts epi- 'on', zoo- 'animal' and -logy 'study', with zoo- being the same root as the everyday word zoo. So epizoology is the 'study of disease on animals', which explains why it means the scientific study of disease outbreaks in groups of animals.
