Etymology

Entoloma lividum comes from old Greek and Latin words that describe how the mushroom looks. The part Entoloma may mean 'inner edge' because the cap can curl in, and lividum means 'lead-coloured', so the name points to its dull, grey-yellow cap and helps people spot and avoid this poisonous mushroom.
