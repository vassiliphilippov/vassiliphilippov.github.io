Etymology

sophisto type comes from a short, informal word 'sophisticate', often shortened to sophisto, for someone with fancy tastes in art and culture. Picture a stylish person at a gallery talking about modern art, and that's why the phrase means 'a person who likes fine art and fashionable things'.
