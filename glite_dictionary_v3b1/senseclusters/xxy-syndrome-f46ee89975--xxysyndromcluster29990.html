Etymology

XXY-syndrome: The name XXY-syndrome comes from the way scientists write the sex chromosomes: X and Y, and XXY means 'two Xs and one Y'. So the name tells you the cause — a man has an 'extra X chromosome', which helps explain the effects on growth and the ability to have children.
