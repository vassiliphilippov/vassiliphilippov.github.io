Etymology

fricative consonant comes from the image of air rubbing through a small gap. To make a fricative, you push air through a very small gap so it 'hisses' or 'rubs', and a consonant is a sound made by blocking the breath a little. So, the name tells you it means a sound made by air rubbing through a narrow space.
