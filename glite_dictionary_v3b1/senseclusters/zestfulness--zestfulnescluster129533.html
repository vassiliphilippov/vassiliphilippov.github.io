Etymology

zestfulness comes from zest, which first meant a little citrus 'peel' or the bright 'flavour' that peel gives to food, and the ending -ful means 'full of' while -ness shows a quality. So zestfulness is being full of that bright, lively 'flavour'—in other words, full of energy and eager enjoyment.
