Etymology

Copper pyrites gets its name from the parts copper and pyrites. One popular story says pyrites comes from an old word for 'fire' because the yellow, hard stone makes sparks when hit, and copper shows the rock has copper inside. So the name tells us this yellow, metallic rock is a 'source of copper'.
