Etymology

CalArts style comes from the California Institute of the Arts, a school where many animation students learned to draw with simple, rounded shapes and very expressive, often quirky faces. Because so many cartoons used that school look, people now use the phrase to describe a cartoon with a 'simple, playful' design.
