Etymology

soaked comes from the verb soak, which means 'to make very wet', as when clothes are 'soaked to the skin' in the rain. A helpful way to remember the slang meaning 'extremely drunk' is to imagine someone 'drenched' in drink—so full of alcohol they can hardly stand, which is why soaked can mean very drunk.
