Etymology

monosemy starts with mono- 'one', the same 'mono' you see in monarch or monologue, and ends with -semy from a Greek root meaning 'sign' or 'meaning'. So monosemy literally means 'one meaning', which is why it describes a word or phrase that has only a single meaning.
