Etymology

The noun aeronautical engineer comes from two parts: aero meaning 'air' and nautical meaning 'sail', so picture someone who 'sails the air' and works on flying machines. That's why the phrase means 'a person who designs, builds, and looks after airplanes and other flying machines'.
