Etymology

Landing deck comes from the literal words landing (what a plane does when it comes down) and deck (a flat floor on a ship). It paints the picture of a flat 'runway' on a ship where aircraft 'land' and 'take off', so it means the top deck of an aircraft carrier used as a 'runway'.
