Etymology

sweet coltsfoot may be named the same way: its broad, round leaves look like a young horse's hoof, a colt's foot, and it gives off a pleasant, sweet scent. So the name points to a sweet-smelling plant with round leaves and bright yellow flowers often found near creeks.
