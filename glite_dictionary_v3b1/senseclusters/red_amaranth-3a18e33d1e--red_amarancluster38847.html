Etymology

Red amaranth gets its name from the plant's bright colour and an old word for a flower that 'does not fade'. One popular story says amaranth comes from a Greek word meaning 'unfading' because the flowers keep their colour, and the red part simply marks the red variety, so the name tells you it is a long-lasting red plant often used for food or decoration.
