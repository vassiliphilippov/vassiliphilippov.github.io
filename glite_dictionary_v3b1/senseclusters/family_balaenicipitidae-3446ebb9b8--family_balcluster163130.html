Etymology

family Balaenicipitidae comes from the scientific name of the shoebill, Balaeniceps, plus the family ending -idae. The name Balaeniceps joins Latin parts balaena and -ceps, meaning 'whale' and 'head', because the bird's very large bill and head looked a bit whale-like to early scientists, so the family name points to that single big-billed African bird.
