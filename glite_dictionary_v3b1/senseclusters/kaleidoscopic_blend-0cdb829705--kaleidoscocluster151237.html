Etymology

The phrase kaleidoscopic blend comes from the image of a kaleidoscope, a tube with small colored pieces that make changing bright patterns when you turn it. Because of that, blend here means a 'mix' of many different, often colorful parts, so the phrase means a lively, varied mixture.
