Etymology

Act like a dorkus probably comes from the word dork plus a playful made-up ending that makes the person sound like a silly, awkward creature. So, picturing someone as that silly creature helps explain why the phrase means 'to behave in an awkward, silly, or foolish way', often in a way that brings mild teasing.
