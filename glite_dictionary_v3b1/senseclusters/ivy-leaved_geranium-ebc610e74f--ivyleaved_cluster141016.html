Etymology

ivy-leaved geranium: The name ivy-leaved geranium comes from the plant's look: its leaves look like ivy and the plant often 'trails down'. That's why gardeners call this geraniumivy-leaved — it means a geranium with 'ivy-like' leaves that is good for hanging baskets.
