Etymology

Korsakoff's psychosis comes from a Russian doctor, Korsakoff, who first wrote about people who could not make new memories after long heavy drinking. That's why the term means 'a serious memory disorder' often with loss of 'short-term memory' in people with long-term alcohol problems.
