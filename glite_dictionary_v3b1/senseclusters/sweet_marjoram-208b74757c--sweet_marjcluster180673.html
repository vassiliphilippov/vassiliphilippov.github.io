Etymology

sweet marjoram gets its name from the plant's mild taste and nice smell. People used the small 'herb' in cooking and simple medicine, so they added sweet to marjoram to show its gentle flavor, and today sweet marjoram means the fragrant 'herb' used to season food and soothe minor ailments.
