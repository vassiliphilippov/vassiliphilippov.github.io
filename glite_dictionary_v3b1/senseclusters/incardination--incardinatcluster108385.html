Etymology

The word incardination comes from the Latin parts in- 'into' and cardo 'hinge'. You can see the same root in cardinal, which originally meant a main 'hinge' or important point. So incardination means being formally 'attached into' a church or diocese, like a priest becoming officially part of that local church.
