Etymology

buckbean family may come from a marsh plant called the buckbean (often called 'bogbean') whose broad, bean-like leaves grow in wet places. Because people used the plant name for similar kinds, the word family came to mean a group of those marsh plants, so today it names that group of wetland plants.
