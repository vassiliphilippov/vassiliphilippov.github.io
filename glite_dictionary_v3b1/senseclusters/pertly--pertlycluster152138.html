Etymology

pertly comes from the short adjective pert meaning 'bold' or 'lively', with the ending -ly meaning 'in a ... way'. So to speak or act pertly is to be 'bold' or 'cheeky' in how you do something, a bit like the word perky, which also suggests lively confidence.
