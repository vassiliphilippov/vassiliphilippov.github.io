Etymology

bed in words like riverbed or road bed comes from the idea of a 'place where something lies' or the 'bottom' of something. So a river bed or road bed is the base layer that holds the water or the road.

Related Terms

  • synonym: riverbed - the bottom of a river
  • synonym: seam - layer of mineral, especially coal, that can be mined
