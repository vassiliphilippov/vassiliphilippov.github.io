Etymology

Trebucket is easy to remember if you imagine a 'throwing bucket' — a big arm that hurls heavy things. It comes from the old word trebuchet, which meant 'to overturn' or 'to throw', so a trebucket is a medieval siege machine that throws stones over walls.
