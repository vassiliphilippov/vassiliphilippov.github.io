Etymology

Arundinaria tecta may come from old Latin words arundo ('reed') and tecta ('covered' or 'sheltered'), giving the picture of a reed that hides and covers wet ground. That's why the name fits a small cane grass that grows in dense, sheltered thickets along rivers.
