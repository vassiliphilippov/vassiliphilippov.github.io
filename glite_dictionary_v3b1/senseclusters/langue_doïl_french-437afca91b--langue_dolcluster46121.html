Etymology

Langue d'oïl French comes from the old northern word oïl for 'yes'. Long ago people in the south said oc for 'yes', so calling the northern speech Langue d'oïl, which means 'language of oïl', gave a clear name to that group of dialects and explains why modern French 'oui' comes from oïl.
