Etymology

The phrase mathematical statement joins mathematical, meaning about numbers and shapes, with statement, meaning a clear sentence that says something. Imagine a teacher writing 'two plus two equals four' on the board; that clear sentence is a mathematical statement, so the phrase means 'a clear and exact sentence about numbers or shapes'.
