Etymology

modern-day Johnny Appleseed comes from the real man John Chapman, called Johnny Appleseed, who walked across the country long ago and planted apple seeds for people. So calling someone a modern-day Johnny Appleseed means they 'plant trees' or encourage others to do so and help the environment today.
