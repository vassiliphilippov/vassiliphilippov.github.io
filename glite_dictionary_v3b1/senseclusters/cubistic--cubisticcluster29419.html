Etymology

The word cubistic comes from the familiar word cube, a simple three-dimensional shape, and the ending -istic meaning 'relating to'. A helpful image is artists like Picasso breaking objects into flat parts and showing many sides at once, like looking at a cube from different angles, so cubistic means 'in the style of cubism'.
