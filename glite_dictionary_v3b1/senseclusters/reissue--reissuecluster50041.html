Synonyms

  • reprint
    0.2/m3.7
    Refers to a printed work published again without any changes or editing
    vsreissue: is used for releases across different media, not only printed works
  • republication
    0.05/m6.2
    Refers to publishing again often as a new edition that may change content
    vsreissue: is limited to releases that keep original content unchanged
  • rerelease
    0.05/m3.6
    Describes issuing a previously released work again, often with changes or additions
    vsreissue: keeps the same original content when released again rather than adding changes
  • reprinting
    0.03/m3.2

Etymology

reissue is made from re- 'again' and issue 'a published release', so a reissue is a book, album, or card that is 'released again' after its first appearance.
