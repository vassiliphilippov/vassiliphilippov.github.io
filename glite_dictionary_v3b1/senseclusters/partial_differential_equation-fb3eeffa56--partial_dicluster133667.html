Etymology

The term partial differential equation comes from three simple ideas: partial means 'only part or one direction', differential means 'a very small change', and equation means 'a rule that connects things'. So it is a rule that connects small changes in different directions, which is why people use it to describe heat, waves and other changes over space and time.
