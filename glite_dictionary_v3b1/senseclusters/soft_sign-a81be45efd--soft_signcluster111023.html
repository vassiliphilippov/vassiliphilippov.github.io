Etymology

Soft sign comes from the idea that this small letter is a sign that makes the consonant before it soft: it has no sound of its own but adds a light 'y' sound to the consonant. So, in Russian and other languages the soft sign shows that the previous consonant should be pronounced 'soft', which is why the name matches its job today.
