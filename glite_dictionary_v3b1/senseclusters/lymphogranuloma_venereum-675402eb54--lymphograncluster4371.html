Etymology

lymphogranuloma venereum comes from old word parts: lympho- meaning 'lymph', the body fluid and the glands, granuloma meaning 'small lump', and venereum linked to Venus, so to 'sex'. So the name describes a sexually spread infection that makes swollen lumps in the groin.
