Etymology

Primula polyantha comes from old Latin and Greek words: Primula means 'first' because these small flowers appear early in spring, and polyantha means 'many flowers' because the plants form large clusters of blooms. So, the name fits this group of hybrid primroses valued for their bright, many-colored flowers.
