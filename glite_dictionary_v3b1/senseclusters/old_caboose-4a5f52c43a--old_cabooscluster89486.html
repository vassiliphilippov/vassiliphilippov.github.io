Etymology

The term old caboose comes from the small cabin at the back of a freight train called a caboose, where the crew slept or worked. Because that 'last car' was often left outside and became worn, many were later turned into 'reading nooks' or 'museum pieces', so an old caboose today is the worn last train car often reused or kept as an interesting object.
