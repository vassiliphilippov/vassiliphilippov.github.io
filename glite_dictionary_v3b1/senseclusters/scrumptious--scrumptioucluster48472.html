Etymology

scrumptious probably grew from a fun form of sumptuous, which means 'rich' or 'luxurious', so it came to mean food that is very tasty and delicious. Some people also connect it to scrump, which meant 'to steal' fruit, a helpful picture for food you want to sneak another bite of.
