Etymology

NSWC stands for Naval Surface Warfare Center, where Naval means 'related to the navy', Surface means 'ships', Warfare means 'fighting', and Center means a 'place' for work. So, NSWC is the Navy's place for research and technical support for ship systems, which is why you see it linked to new ship weapons and technology.
