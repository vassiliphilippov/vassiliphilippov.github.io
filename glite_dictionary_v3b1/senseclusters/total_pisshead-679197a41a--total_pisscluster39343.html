Etymology

total pisshead may come from a simple image: piss is a rude word tied to drinking, and head often means a person, so a pisshead is someone known for drinking a lot. One popular story says the name was used for people who drank so much they often behaved badly, so today total pisshead means 'a person who drinks too much'.
