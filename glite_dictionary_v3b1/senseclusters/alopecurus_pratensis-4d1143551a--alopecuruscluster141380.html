Etymology

Alopecurus pratensis comes from old words: Alopecurus means 'fox tail' because the grass's flower spike looks like a fox's tail, and pratensis means 'meadow'. So the name is like 'fox-tail of the meadow', which helps you remember it is a meadow grass with soft, tail-like flower spikes used for grazing and hay.
