Etymology

Morelia spilotes variegatus is the scientific name for a type of Australian python, and the last word variegatus means 'varied' or 'many-coloured', pointing to the snake's mixed, carpet-like pattern on its back. So the name helps you remember it is the 'carpet python' with a patterned back.
