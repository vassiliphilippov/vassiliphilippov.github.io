Etymology

sphenoid fontanel names the 'soft spot' on each side of a baby's head where several skull bones meet; sphenoid is one of those bones and fontanel refers to a 'soft spot'. As the bones grow together this gap closes, so the term points to that temporary meeting place on the baby's skull.
