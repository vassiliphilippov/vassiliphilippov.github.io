Etymology

hypoesthesia comes from Greek parts hypo- meaning 'under' or 'less' and -esthesia meaning 'feeling' or 'sensation', the same root as in anesthesia. So, hypoesthesia means 'less feeling', which is why it describes reduced sensitivity to touch or other senses.
