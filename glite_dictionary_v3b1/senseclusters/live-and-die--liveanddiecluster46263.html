Etymology

The word live-and-die comes from the simple parts live and die, which mean 'to be alive' and 'to die'. A helpful way to remember this shrub is that its sensitive leaflets fold and droop when touched, so they look as if they 'die' for a moment, which is why the name fits the plant.
