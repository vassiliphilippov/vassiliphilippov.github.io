Etymology

The word ima comes from how people say I'm gonna in fast, casual speech; the phrase 'I'm going to' can sound like 'ima' when spoken quickly. So, ima is an informal way to say 'I'm going to' or sometimes 'I'm a', and you hear it in casual talk and messages.
