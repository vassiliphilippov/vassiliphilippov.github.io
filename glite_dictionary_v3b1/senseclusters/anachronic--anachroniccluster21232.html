Etymology

The word anachronic comes from the Greek parts an- meaning 'not' and chronos meaning 'time', and it shares the same root as chronological, which is about order in 'time'. So anachronic describes something that is 'out of time' or in the wrong time period, which is why a rotary phone feels anachronic in a modern office.
