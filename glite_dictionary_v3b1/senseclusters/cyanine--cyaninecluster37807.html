Etymology

cyanine comes from the word cyan, a common English word for a bright blue color that goes back to a Greek word meaning 'dark blue', plus the chemical ending -ine. So, cyanine is basically a 'blue' dye, which explains why scientists use it as a bright or fluorescent stain in imaging.
