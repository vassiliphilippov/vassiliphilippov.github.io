Etymology

class Lycopodineae gets its name from old Greek parts lyco- meaning 'wolf' and -pod meaning 'foot', because the small, branched clubmoss plants looked a bit like tiny feet. That's why class Lycopodineae is the name for this group of clubmosses and their relatives.
