Etymology

sodium hydride simply tells you what it is: the metal sodium joined with hydride, a form of hydrogen. Because that form of hydrogen is very reactive, sodium hydride can burn or react with water, and chemists use it as a very strong 'base' to pull hydrogen from other molecules.
