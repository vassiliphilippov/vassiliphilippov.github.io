Etymology

lamb chop is a very literal name: lamb means 'young sheep' and chop means 'a small cut of meat', so imagine a small, bone-in piece cut from a lamb. That's why lamb chop now means 'a small piece of lamb meat, usually with a bone, that is cooked and eaten'.
