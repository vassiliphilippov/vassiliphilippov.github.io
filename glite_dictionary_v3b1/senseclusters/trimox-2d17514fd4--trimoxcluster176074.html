Etymology

Trimox is a brand name formed from the medicine name amoxicillin, with -mox taken from 'amox' in amoxicillin and Tri- suggesting 'three' as a small memory hook. So, a helpful way to remember Trimox is that it is a brand of the antibiotic amoxicillin, a medicine you take to fight bacterial infections.
