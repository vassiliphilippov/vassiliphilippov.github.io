Etymology

mesdemoiselles is the French plural of mademoiselle and comes from mes 'my' plus demoiselle 'young lady', so it literally means 'my young ladies'. A helpful way to remember it is to think of saying 'my misses' or 'my young ladies', which is why people use it to address several young women.
