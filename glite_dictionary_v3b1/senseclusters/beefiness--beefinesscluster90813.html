Etymology

beefiness comes from the word beef ('meat from cattle') plus the endings -y ('full of') and -ness ('the state of'), so it paints a picture of being 'full of beef' — thick and strong. That's why beefiness can describe a muscular, powerful body or a heavy, solid engine.
