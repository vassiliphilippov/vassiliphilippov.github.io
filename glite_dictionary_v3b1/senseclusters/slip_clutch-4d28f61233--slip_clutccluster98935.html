Etymology

slip clutch comes from the literal parts of the name: a clutch is a part that joins two moving pieces and to slip means to slide instead of hold. One clear picture is that the clutch will slide when there is too much force so the machine does not break, so a slip clutch is a clutch that 'gives' to 'protect' the machine.
