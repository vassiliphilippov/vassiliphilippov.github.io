Etymology

Forbidden City comes from when the emperor's palace in Beijing was closed to almost everyone. The word forbidden means 'not allowed' and city shows it was a very large, separate place for the royal family. Today it is a museum open to visitors, but the old name still reminds us that it used to be closed.
