Etymology

swollen-headed paints a clear picture: swollen means 'bigger' and head is 'the mind' or the self, so it is like someone's head has 'got bigger' with pride. It is like the common word big-headed, which is why swollen-headed means 'full of oneself' or proud and arrogant.
