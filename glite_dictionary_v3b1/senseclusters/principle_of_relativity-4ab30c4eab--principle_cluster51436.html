Etymology

principle of relativity comes from a simple image: a principle is a rule and relativity is the idea that what you see can depend on the person looking. Imagine doing the same test inside a smoothly moving train and on the platform and getting the same result, so the phrase means the laws of physics are 'the same' for people 'moving at a steady speed'.
