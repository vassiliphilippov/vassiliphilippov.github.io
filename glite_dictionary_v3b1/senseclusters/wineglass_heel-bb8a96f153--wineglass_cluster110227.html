Etymology

wineglass heel comes from a simple picture: the heel looks like an upside-down wineglass, thin at the bottom, wider in the middle and narrow at the top. One popular idea is that shoemakers used that image to name this 'type of heel', so today wineglass heel means a curved women's shoe heel with that shape.
