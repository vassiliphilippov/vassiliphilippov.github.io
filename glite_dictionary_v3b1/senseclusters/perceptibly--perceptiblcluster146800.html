Etymology

perceptibly comes from the verb perceive, which comes from the Latin parts per- meaning 'thoroughly' and capere meaning 'to take', the same root as capture. So, to perceive is like 'taking in' something with your senses, and perceptibly means 'in a way you can notice'.
