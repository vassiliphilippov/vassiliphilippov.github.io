Etymology

9/11 comes from the United States way of writing dates as 'month/day', so '9/11' stands for 'September 11, 2001'. Because of the terrorist attacks on that day, the numbers 9/11 became a short, memorable name for those events, so today 9/11 refers to the attacks and their anniversary.
