Etymology

The word dysthymic comes from Greek parts dys- meaning 'bad' or 'difficult' and thym- meaning 'mood' or 'spirit', and the same dys- appears in dysfunctional. So, dysthymic describes a long-term low mood, a mild but lasting form of depression.
