Etymology

The term nonlinear distortion puts together nonlinear (literally 'not in a straight line') and distortion ('a change that makes a sound or signal wrong'). Think of an old speaker: if you push it too hard the sound gets strange and new tones appear, so nonlinear distortion means 'when what comes out does not follow what goes in and creates unwanted changes in the sound or signal'.
