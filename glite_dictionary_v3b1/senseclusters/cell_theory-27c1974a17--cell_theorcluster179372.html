Etymology

cell theory comes from the word cell, which a scientist named Robert Hooke used after he saw tiny, box-like shapes in cork that looked like 'small rooms'. Scientists later found that these cells are the 'basic parts' of all living things, so cell theory says life is built from these simple units.
