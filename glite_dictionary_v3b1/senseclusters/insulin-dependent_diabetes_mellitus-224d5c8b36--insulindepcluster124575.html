Etymology

insulin-dependent diabetes mellitus is a medical name that tells the problem: insulin-dependent means the body 'depends on' insulin because it cannot make enough, and diabetes mellitus was an old name for the illness where extra sugar appears in the urine and 'mellitus' meant 'sweet'. So the whole phrase means a form of diabetes that needs regular insulin injections to control blood sugar.
