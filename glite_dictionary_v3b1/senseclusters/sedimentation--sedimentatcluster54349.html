Etymology

The word sedimentation comes from the Latin root sed-, from sedere, meaning 'to sit'. Imagine tiny bits 'sitting' on the bottom of a river or a glass of water, and that 'sitting' is what sedimentation is: solids settling and collecting at the bottom.
