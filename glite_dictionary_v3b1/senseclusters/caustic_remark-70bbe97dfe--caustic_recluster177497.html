Etymology

The phrase caustic remark comes from the word caustic, once used for strong chemicals that 'burn' or 'eat away' the skin. A caustic remark is like those chemicals but with words: it 'burns' or hurts someone, so it means a very sharp, mocking, or hurtful comment.
