Etymology

The word proscribable comes from the Latin parts pro- and scribere, where scribere means 'to write' and the same root appears in scribe and script. Long ago authorities would 'write' names on public lists to mark people or things to be outlawed, so proscribable means 'able to be banned' by law or authority.
