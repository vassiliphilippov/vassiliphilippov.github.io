Etymology

walla for background crowd noise gets its name from a simple film trick: extras would murmur nonsense like 'walla walla' so the scene sounded like many people without real words. A helpful way to remember it is that walla sounds like the soft, unclear talk you hear in the background, which is why it means 'indistinct crowd noise'.
