Etymology

Good-King-Henry comes from old names that mean 'good Henry', and the plant's Latin name is bonus-henricus. A helpful story says it was named for a useful gardener or a kind saint called Henry, so people remembered it as a 'good' edible herb, which is why the name highlights its value as a potherb.
