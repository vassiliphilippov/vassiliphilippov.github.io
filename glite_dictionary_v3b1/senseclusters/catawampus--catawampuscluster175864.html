Etymology

The word catawampus comes from playful American speech, like the similar word cattywampus, and one popular idea is that it paints the picture of a cat sitting at an angle. A helpful way to remember it is to think of something 'askew' or 'diagonal', like a rug laid 'kitty-corner' across a room, so catawampus means 'not straight' or 'off to one side'.
