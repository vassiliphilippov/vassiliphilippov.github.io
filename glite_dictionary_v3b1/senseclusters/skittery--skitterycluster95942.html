Etymology

The word skittery comes from the verb skitter, a word that copies the quick, light sound of something sliding or 'darting' across a surface. It is closely related to the word skittish, which also means nervous and jumpy. So, skittery describes someone or something that is easily frightened and moves quickly in a nervous way, like a small animal that suddenly runs away.
