Etymology

mistrustful comes from the prefix mis- (as in mistake) meaning 'not' or 'wrong' and the word trust meaning 'to believe or rely on'. With the ending -ful meaning 'full of', mistrustful is 'full of not trusting', so it describes someone who is suspicious and does not want to rely on or confide in others.
