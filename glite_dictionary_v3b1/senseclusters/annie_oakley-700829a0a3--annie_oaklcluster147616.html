Etymology

Annie Oakley was the stage name of Phoebe Ann Moses, a small but amazingly accurate shooter who became a star in Buffalo Bill's Wild West Show. People remember the clear image of a tiny woman who could hit any target, so Annie Oakley now stands for a very skilled female shooter and a symbol of women who broke rules in the Old West.
