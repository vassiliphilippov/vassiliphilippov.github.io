Etymology

Guttiferae comes from the Latin parts gutta 'drop' and -fer 'bearing' or 'carrying'. So the name means 'drop-bearing', which helps you remember this family of tropical trees and shrubs because many of them make drops of resin or oil used for medicine or wood.
