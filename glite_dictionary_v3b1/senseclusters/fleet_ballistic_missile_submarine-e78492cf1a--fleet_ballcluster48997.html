Etymology

The noun fleet ballistic missile submarine names a type of warship: a fleet is a 'group of ships', a ballistic missile is a 'long-range rocket that flies up then falls on its target', and a submarine is an 'undersea ship'. Put together, the phrase means an undersea ship that carries and can launch these long-range rockets, so it describes a submarine used to help a country stop or warn off enemies.
