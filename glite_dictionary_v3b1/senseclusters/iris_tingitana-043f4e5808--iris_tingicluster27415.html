Etymology

Iris tingitana gets its name from two parts: Iris, a word for 'rainbow' used for the bright, colorful flower, and tingitana, which may come from 'Tingis', the old name for Tangier in Morocco. So the name tells you it is a colorful iris 'from Tangier', which fits a blue-flowered iris that grows in North Africa.
