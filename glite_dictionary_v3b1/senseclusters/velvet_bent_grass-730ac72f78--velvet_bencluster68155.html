Etymology

velvet bent grass gets its name from how it looks and feels: the grass's very fine leaves feel like velvet, and bent was an old name for fine, low-growing grass that bends into a smooth surface. So the name helps you remember it is a soft, dense grass often used on lawns and golf greens.
