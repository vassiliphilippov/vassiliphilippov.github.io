Etymology

Aphyllanthaceae comes from Greek parts: a- meaning 'without', phyll- meaning 'leaf' and anth- meaning 'flower', with -aceae used for a plant 'family'. So it literally suggests a 'family of leafless flowers', a simple image to help you remember this small plant family that includes the genus Aphyllanthes.
