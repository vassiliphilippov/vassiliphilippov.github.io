Etymology

hyperesthesia comes from hyper- meaning 'over' or 'too much' and -esthesia meaning 'feeling' or 'sensation', so it is like 'too much feeling'. That's why hyperesthesia describes a condition where someone feels touch and other sensations much more strongly than usual.
