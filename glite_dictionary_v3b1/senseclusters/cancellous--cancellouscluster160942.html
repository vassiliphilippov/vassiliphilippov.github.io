Etymology

cancellous comes from the Latin cancellus, meaning 'lattice' or 'grid', which gives the image of a small net or sponge. It even shares the same root as cancel, from the old practice of crossing lines, so cancellous means 'lattice-like' and full of little spaces, which explains why cancellous bone is light and porous.
