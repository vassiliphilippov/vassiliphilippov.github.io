Etymology

Ali Baba comes from a famous story in the Arabian Nights about a poor woodcutter who finds a secret cave full of the thieves' treasure and opens it with the magic words 'open sesame'. That's why Ali Baba often suggests someone who discovers hidden treasure or a secret fortune.
