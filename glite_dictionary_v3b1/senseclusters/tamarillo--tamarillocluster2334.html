Etymology

tamarillo comes from Spanish and is like the English tomato with the Spanish ending -illo, which means 'little'. A helpful way to remember it is to think of a 'little tomato' from South America, also called the 'tree tomato', which explains its small, egg-shaped fruit and tomato-like taste.
