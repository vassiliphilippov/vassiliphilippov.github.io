Etymology

The noun goatskin leather comes from a simple, literal image: goatskin is the skin of a goat and leather is treated animal skin. Because goat skin is naturally soft and strong, people long used it for fine items like book covers and gloves, so the phrase now means 'leather made from a goat' used for quality goods.
