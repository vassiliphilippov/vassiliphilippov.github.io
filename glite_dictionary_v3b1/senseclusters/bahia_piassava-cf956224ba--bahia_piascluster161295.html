Etymology

Bahia piassava is named after the state of Bahia in Brazil and the word piassava, a local word for the strong, stiff 'fibers' of a palm tree. Those long, tough 'fibers' were used to make ropes, mats and brooms, so the name now refers to the palm tree whose fibers are used for those items.
