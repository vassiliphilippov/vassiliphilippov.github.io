Etymology

The name whole meal flour comes from the idea of grinding the whole wheat rather than only the soft inner part, and meal was a word for 'ground grain', so it is flour made from the entire grain including the bran. So, it means flour that keeps all parts of the wheat and has more fiber and a richer taste.
