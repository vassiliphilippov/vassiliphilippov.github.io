Etymology

bombardier beetle gets its name from the insect's strong defense: when scared it shoots a hot, smelly 'spray' with a loud pop, like a small explosion. Because bombardier means a soldier who drops 'bombs' and beetle is the insect, the two words make a picture of a beetle that 'bombs' attackers, so the name helps you remember its defensive spray.
