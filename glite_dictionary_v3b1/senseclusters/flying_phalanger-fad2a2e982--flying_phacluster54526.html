Etymology

The noun flying phalanger names a small Australian animal with a pouch that can 'glide' between trees by stretching skin between its legs like a little parachute. The word flying gives the picture of moving through the air and phalanger is the name of this pouch animal, so the name helps you remember it 'glides' rather than truly flies.
