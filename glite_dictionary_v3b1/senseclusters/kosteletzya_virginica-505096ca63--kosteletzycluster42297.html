Etymology

The plant name Kosteletzya virginica may come from a plant scientist named Kosteletzky, with Kosteletzya from his name and virginica meaning 'from Virginia' on the U.S. east coast. So the name gives the image of 'Kosteletzky's plant from the Virginia coast', and that is why it refers to the coastal mallow or 'seashore mallow' found in salt marshes today.
