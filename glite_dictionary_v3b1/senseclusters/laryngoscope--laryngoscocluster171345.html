Etymology

Laryngoscope comes from laryngo- meaning 'voice box' and -scope meaning 'to look'. Like microscope and telescope, a laryngoscope is a tool doctors use to look into the throat and see the vocal cords, so its name literally means 'a tool for viewing the voice box'.
