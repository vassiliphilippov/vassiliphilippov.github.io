Etymology

Fifth Amendment is the fifth change to the U.S. Constitution's list of rights and it names important legal protections. One famous part lets a person refuse to answer questions that would blame them, so people say 'plead the Fifth' or 'invoke the Fifth Amendment' to 'avoid testifying'. It also protects against being tried twice and says you must get fair legal steps and payment if the government takes your property.
