Etymology

The word aggravatingly comes from aggravate plus -ly. Aggravate comes from an old Latin root grav- meaning 'heavy', so it originally meant 'to make heavier' or 'to make worse'. Think of adding weight to a problem until it becomes more annoying, and that is why aggravatingly means 'in a way that causes annoyance'.
