Etymology

Royal coronation comes from old public ceremonies when a king or queen had a crown placed on their head and was given special items like a scepter while church leaders blessed them. This public, formal act made them the ruler in the eyes of the people, so the phrase now means 'the formal ceremony to make a monarch officially crowned'.
