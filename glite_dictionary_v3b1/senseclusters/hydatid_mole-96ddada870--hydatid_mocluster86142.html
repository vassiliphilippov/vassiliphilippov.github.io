Etymology

hydatid mole comes from older words: hydatid means a 'watery sac' and mole means a 'lump' or mass. Imagine a cluster of small, clear sacs growing in the womb instead of a normal baby; that's why the term refers to an abnormal pregnancy where fluid-filled sacs replace the baby.
