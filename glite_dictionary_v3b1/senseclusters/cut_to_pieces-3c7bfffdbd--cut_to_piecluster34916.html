Etymology

cut to pieces comes from a simple, violent image of cut and pieces: imagine a knife slicing meat or a storm tearing a barn into many small parts. That's why it now means 'to break something into small parts' and, by that strong picture, 'to hurt someone deeply' with words or actions.
