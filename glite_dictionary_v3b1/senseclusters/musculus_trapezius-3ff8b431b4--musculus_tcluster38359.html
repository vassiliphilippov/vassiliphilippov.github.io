Etymology

musculus trapezius comes from old Latin and Greek words that create a clear picture: musculus means 'little mouse' because a moving muscle looked like a mouse under the skin, and trapezius describes its four-sided shape on the upper back and shoulder. So the name tells you it is a 'small, four-sided muscle' that helps move the shoulders and neck.
