Etymology

bassine likely comes from local South Asian names for the palm and its leaves and was used for the rough fibres those leaves make; it refers to the tough leaf fibre from the palmyra palm used for brushes and brooms. So, to remember bassine, picture a stiff broom made of palm leaves, because the word means a coarse plant fibre used for bristles.
