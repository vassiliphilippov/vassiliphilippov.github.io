Etymology

hitting the punching bag gives a clear picture: you are hitting a heavy, padded punching bag to practice punches or to let out strong feelings. Because people can imagine their problems on the bag and feel calmer after, the phrase often means 'stress relief' or part of 'exercise' and 'training'.
