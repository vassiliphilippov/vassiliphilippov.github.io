Etymology

Ipomoea orizabensis may come from a mix of old words and a place name: Ipomoea may mean 'worm-like' because the plant's long climbing stems look like worms, and orizabensis means 'from Orizaba', a place in Mexico where it was first found. So, the name helps you remember both the plant's worm-like climbing shape and where it came from.
