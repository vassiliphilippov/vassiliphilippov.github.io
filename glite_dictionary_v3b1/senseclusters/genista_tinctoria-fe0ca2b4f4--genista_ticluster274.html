Etymology

Genista tinctoria gets its name from old words that link the plant to dye. Genista names the small broom-like shrub and tinctoria means 'used for dyeing'. People used its bright yellow flowers to make yellow dye, so the name helps you remember it as a yellow-flowering shrub used to dye cloth.
