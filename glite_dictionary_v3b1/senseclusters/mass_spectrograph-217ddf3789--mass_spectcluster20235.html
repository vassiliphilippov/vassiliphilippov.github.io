Etymology

The name mass spectrograph comes from the parts mass, spectro, and graph: mass is the weight of tiny particles, spectro points to a 'spectrum' (a range), and graph means a 'drawing' or line that shows data. The machine sorts charged particles by their weight and makes that 'spectrum' into a picture, so the name means an 'instrument' that shows which masses are present.
