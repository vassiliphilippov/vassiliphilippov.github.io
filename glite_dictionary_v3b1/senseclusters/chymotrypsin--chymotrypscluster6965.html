Etymology

The word chymotrypsin comes from the parts chymo- meaning 'juice', like chyme which is partly digested food, and -trypsin from a Greek root meaning 'to rub' or 'to wear down'. So, chymotrypsin is an enzyme that works in the digestive 'juice' to 'rub apart' proteins and help digest them in the small intestine.
