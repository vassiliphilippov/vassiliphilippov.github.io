Etymology

Insanely comes from Latin parts in- 'not' and sanus 'healthy', the same root as English sane and sanity. It once meant 'not mentally well', but a helpful way to remember it is that today it often means 'very' or 'extremely', as if something is so strong it feels 'not sane'.
