Etymology

Quercus muehlenbergii comes from Quercus (Latin for 'oak') and muehlenbergii, which is 'named after' the plant scientist Muehlenberg; so the name says 'Muehlenberg's oak' and helps you remember it is a type of oak with strong, long-lasting wood.
