Etymology

Charlie Parker is the name of a famous American saxophone player who was often called 'Bird'. One popular story says the nickname came from the word 'yardbird', a farm chicken, and that lively bird image helps you remember his fast, new jazz style called 'bebop' that changed modern jazz.
