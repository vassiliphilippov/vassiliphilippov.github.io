Etymology

The word Pandanaceae comes from the Malay word pandan, a fragrant leaf used in Southeast Asian cooking, and the Latin ending -aceae meaning 'family of plants'. So Pandanaceae literally means 'pandan family', a group of tropical woody plants such as the screw pine.
