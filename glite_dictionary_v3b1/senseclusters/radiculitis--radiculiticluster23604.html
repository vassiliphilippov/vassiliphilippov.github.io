Etymology

The word radiculitis comes from the parts radicul- meaning 'root' and -itis meaning 'inflammation', and the radicul- part is the same idea you find in radish, a root vegetable. So radiculitis is an 'inflammation' of a nerve 'root', which is why it causes pain that runs along the nerve.
