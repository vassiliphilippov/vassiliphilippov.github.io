Etymology

scalp lock comes from the literal image of a single long tuft of hair left on a shaved head, with scalp meaning the top of the head and lock meaning a bunch of hair. Many Native American warriors wore a scalp lock as a sign of 'identity' or 'bravery', so today scalp lock means that long tuft of hair.
