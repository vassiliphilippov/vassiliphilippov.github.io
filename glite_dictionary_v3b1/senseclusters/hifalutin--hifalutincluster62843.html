Etymology

hifalutin comes from a playful American phrase high-falutin, made of high ('fancy' or 'important') and a showy-sounding 'falutin' that you can remember like flaunt ('to show off'). So, hifalutin describes speech or behaviour that is overly fancy and meant to impress.
