Etymology

spruce beer comes from an old recipe where people added parts of the spruce tree, like needles or sap, to a sweet brew. Sailors and settlers drank this brewed mix to stay healthy and warm, so today spruce beer means a beer or fermented 'drink' flavored with spruce.
