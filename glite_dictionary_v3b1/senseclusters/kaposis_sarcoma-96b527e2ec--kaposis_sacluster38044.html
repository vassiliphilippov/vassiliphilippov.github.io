Etymology

Kaposi's sarcoma is named after Dr Moritz Kaposi, who first described the unusual purple skin spots in patients in the 1800s. Because his name was used for that illness, Kaposi's sarcoma today means a rare 'cancer' that makes purplish or brownish skin patches and can spread inside the body.
