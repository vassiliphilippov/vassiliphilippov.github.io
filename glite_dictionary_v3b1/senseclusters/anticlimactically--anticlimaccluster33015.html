Etymology

anticlimactically comes from anti- meaning 'against' and climax meaning 'high point', a idea you can remember from the simple word climb which goes up to the top. So, to say something happened anticlimactically is to say it ended as a 'letdown' instead of a big, exciting finish after a long build-up.
