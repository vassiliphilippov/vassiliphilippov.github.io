Etymology

The word rive comes from the old English rīfan meaning 'to tear', and you can see the same root in the word riven, which means 'torn apart', and in rift meaning 'split'. So, rive can mean both a physical splitting and a strong emotional tearing, as when a heart is 'riven' with grief.
