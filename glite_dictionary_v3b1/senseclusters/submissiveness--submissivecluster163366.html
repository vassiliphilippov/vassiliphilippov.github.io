Etymology

submissiveness comes from the Latin parts sub- ('under') and mittere ('to send'), and you can see the same mit root in English words like submit and mission. So it originally meant 'sent under' or 'put below', which explains why submissiveness means being ready to 'give in' or accept another person's authority.
