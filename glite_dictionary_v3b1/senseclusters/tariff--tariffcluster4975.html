Etymology

tariff comes from the Italian tariffa and French tarif, and before that from the Arabic ta'rīf meaning 'to make known' or 'a notice'. It first meant a list of prices or charges—like a posted 'price list'—so today a tariff is a government 'tax' or 'duty' on imported goods.
