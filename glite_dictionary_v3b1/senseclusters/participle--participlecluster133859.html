Etymology

participle comes from Latin words meaning 'to take part' and has the root part- meaning 'part'. A helpful way to remember it is that a participle is a verb form that 'takes part' as both a verb and an adjective, which is why forms like 'walking' can both describe something and help make verb tenses.
