Etymology

bummed comes from American slang like bum and bummer, words people used for a 'disappointment' or when something 'makes someone sad'. A helpful way to remember is to think of a 'bummer' — a bad thing that 'bums you out' — so if you are bummed, you feel sad or disappointed.
