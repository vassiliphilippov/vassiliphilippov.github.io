Etymology

crackpotism starts from the familiar word crackpot, meaning a person with odd or 'crazy' ideas; think of crack as 'broken' and pot as a 'container', like a 'cracked pot' holding strange thoughts. The ending -ism turns that idea into a condition or 'way of doing things', so crackpotism means a system or situation run by eccentric, impractical ideas or people.
