Etymology

The word psychopharmacological breaks into three parts: psycho- meaning 'mind', the same part you see in psychology, pharmaco- meaning 'drug' or 'medicine', like in pharmacy, and -logical meaning 'study' or 'science'. So, psychopharmacological is about the study or use of medicines that affect the mind.
