Etymology

glycerol tripalmitate literally names the parts: glycerol is the three-armed backbone, tri means 'three', and palmitate comes from palmitic acid, a common fat first linked to palm oil. So the name helps you pictu re one glycerol holding three palmitic acid chains, which is why it means 'a fat molecule with three palmitic acids'.
