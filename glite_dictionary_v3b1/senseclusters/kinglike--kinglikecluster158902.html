Etymology

kinglike comes from the simple noun king and the ending -like, where king is the same 'ruler' you see in kingdom and -like means 'similar to'. So, to be kinglike is to have the calm, strong behavior of a king, which is why the word means 'regal' or 'like a ruler'.
