Etymology

je ne sais quoi literally breaks down as je 'I', ne 'not', sais 'know', and quoi 'what' in French, and people used it when they could not 'name' or 'describe' a special, attractive quality. That's why je ne sais quoi now means 'a quality that cannot be easily described' or a charming feeling you can't explain.
