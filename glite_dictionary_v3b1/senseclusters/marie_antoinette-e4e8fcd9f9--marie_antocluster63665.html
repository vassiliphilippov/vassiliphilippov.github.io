Etymology

Marie Antoinette was the queen of France in the late 1700s who became famous for a very rich and wasteful life. One popular story says she said 'let them eat cake' when told people had no bread, and that image of a queen ignoring the poor made her a symbol of royal luxury and the people's anger, which helped lead to her downfall.
