Etymology

redeemability comes from the verb redeem, which comes from the Latin parts re- 'back' and emere 'to buy', so to redeem originally meant to 'buy back' and by extension to 'save' or restore a person. So redeemability can mean the ability to be forgiven or brought back to good standing.
