Etymology

The compound drug trafficker comes from the words drug for the goods and trafficker, from an old use of 'traffic' meaning 'to trade or move goods', so a trafficker is someone who moves or sells them. Because the goods are illegal drugs, a drug trafficker is 'a person who illegally buys, sells, or moves drugs'.
