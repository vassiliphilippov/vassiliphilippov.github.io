Etymology

lubriciously comes from the Latin root lubric- meaning 'slippery', as in lubricate. A helpful way to remember the sexual sense is to think that 'slippery' was also used for someone morally loose, so lubriciously can mean 'in a sexually suggestive or lewd way'.
