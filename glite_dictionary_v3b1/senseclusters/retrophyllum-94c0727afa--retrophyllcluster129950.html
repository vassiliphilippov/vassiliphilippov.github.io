Retrophyllum comes from the parts retro- meaning 'back' and -phyllum meaning 'leaf'. It is the name for a group of tropical evergreen trees and shrubs, and the parts help you remember that these plants are known for leaves that often point or grow backward, so the name matches the plant's leaf arrangement.
