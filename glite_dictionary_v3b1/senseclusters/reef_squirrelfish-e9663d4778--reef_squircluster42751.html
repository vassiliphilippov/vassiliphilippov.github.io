Etymology

reef squirrelfish may come from where and how the fish lives: reef shows it lives among coral, and squirrelfish suggests it darts and hides among the coral branches like a squirrel. So the name helps you remember a bright, fast fish that hides in reef holes by day and comes out at night to feed.
