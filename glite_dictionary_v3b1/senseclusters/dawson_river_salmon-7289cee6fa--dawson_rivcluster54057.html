Etymology

Dawson River salmon may come from how the fish looked and where people found it: locals saw a big fish in the Dawson River with a pink spot on each scale and it reminded them of a salmon, so they called it that. That's why today Dawson River salmon refers to the large native river fish known for its pink spots.
