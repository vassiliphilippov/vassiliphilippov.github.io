Etymology

The word Agaricaceae comes from agaric, an old word for 'mushroom', and the ending -aceae, which means 'family' in plant and fungus names. So, Agaricaceae is the 'mushroom family', which helps explain why it includes familiar gilled mushrooms like the button mushroom.
