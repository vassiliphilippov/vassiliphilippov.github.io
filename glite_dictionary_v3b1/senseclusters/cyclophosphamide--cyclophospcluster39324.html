Etymology

The word cyclophosphamide comes from parts that describe its chemistry: cyclo- means 'circle' (like cycle), phosph- refers to 'phosphorus', and -amide is a chemical ending related to 'ammonia'. So, the name tells you it is a ring-shaped chemical with phosphorus used as a medicine to slow or stop cancer cells, which is why doctors use it in chemotherapy.
