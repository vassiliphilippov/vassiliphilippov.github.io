Etymology

mountain fetterbush may come from the old word fetter, which means a chain or a thing that ties, and bush for a shrub. People thought its strong roots and low shape helped 'hold' soil on hills, so the name gives the image of a shrub that holds the mountain and helps you remember this hardy evergreen with small white bell-shaped flowers.
