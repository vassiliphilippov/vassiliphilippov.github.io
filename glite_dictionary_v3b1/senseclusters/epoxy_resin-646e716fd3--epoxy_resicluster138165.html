Etymology

epoxy resin combines epoxy (a man-made 'sticky' liquid) and resin (the natural 'sap' or sticky material from trees). When the two are mixed and left to set, they become very 'hard' and shiny, so the name helps us remember it is a strong glue and glossy coating.
