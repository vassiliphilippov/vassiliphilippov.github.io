Etymology

Gymnosporangium juniperi-virginianae may get its name from how it looks and where it lives: Gymnosporangium points to 'naked spore' because it makes orange, jelly-like horns that show its spores, and juniperi-virginianae names its common tree, the 'Virginia juniper'. So the name helps you remember that this rust fungus makes bright, exposed spore growths on junipers and causes orange-brown spots on apples and pears.
