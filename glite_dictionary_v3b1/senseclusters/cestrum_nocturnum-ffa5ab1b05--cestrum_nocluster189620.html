Etymology

Cestrum nocturnum shows its habit in its name: the second word nocturnum means 'night', and its pale, funnel-shaped flowers open and give a strong scent after dark to attract moths. That's why people call it 'night-blooming jasmine' and plant it near patios to enjoy the evening smell.
