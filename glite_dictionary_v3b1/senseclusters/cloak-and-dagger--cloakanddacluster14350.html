Etymology

cloak-and-dagger comes from the old image of a person wearing a cloak ('cover') and hiding a dagger ('small knife') to act in secret, like the shadowy characters in old plays. So a cloak-and-dagger story or action is one full of secrets, spies, and hidden danger.
