Etymology

imperialization comes from imperial, which is related to emperor and empire and means 'rule' or 'power', plus the ending -ization meaning 'the process of making'. A helpful way to remember this sense is to think of local customs or ceremonies slowly taking on the 'look' and authority of an empire. So, imperialization here means the process of making something act or feel like an empire even without taking land.
