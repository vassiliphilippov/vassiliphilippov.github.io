Etymology

Rubiales comes from the plant name Rubia, the madder plant whose root was used to make a strong 'red' dye, and the plant family Rubiaceae gave the order its name. Think of ruby, which has the same root meaning 'red', to remember that Rubiales is a group name for these plants.
