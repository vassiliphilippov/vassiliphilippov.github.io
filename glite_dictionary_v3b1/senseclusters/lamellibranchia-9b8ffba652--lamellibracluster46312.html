Etymology

Lamellibranchia comes from parts that mean 'small plate' and 'gills'. The lamella part (like in laminate) means 'small plate' and -branchia means 'gills', so the name points to animals whose gills are made of many thin plates, which is why it named clams, oysters and mussels.
