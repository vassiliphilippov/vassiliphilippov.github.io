Etymology

The noun magneto-dynamic microphone comes from a simple picture: magneto means 'magnet' and dynamic means 'movement'. Inside the microphone a small metal part moves near a magnet when sound hits it and this movement makes an electric signal, so the name refers to turning 'sound into an electrical signal'.
