Etymology

Litocranius walleri may come from old word parts lito meaning 'slender' and cranius meaning 'head', plus the surname Waller who first described the animal. The animal is a thin-necked antelope that often stands up to reach high leaves, so the name fits its 'long neck' and slim shape.
