Etymology

The word amygdalotomy comes from amygdala, a Greek word meaning 'almond' because that part of the brain looks like a small almond, and the ending -otomy means 'to cut', as in lobotomy. So an amygdalotomy is literally 'cutting the almond' part of the brain, which is why the word refers to a surgical cut in the amygdala to try to reduce extreme violent behavior.
