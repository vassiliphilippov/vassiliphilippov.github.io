Etymology

figure loom may come from the loom's job: the word figure means a 'small picture' or 'pattern' and the loom is the tool that weaves threads. This kind of loom lifts selected threads to 'draw' shapes in the cloth, so the name explains why it means a loom for 'patterned fabric'.
