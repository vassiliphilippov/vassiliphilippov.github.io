Etymology

hemangioma comes from old Greek parts: hem- meaning 'blood', angio- meaning 'vessel', and -oma meaning 'tumor'. So, a hemangioma is literally a 'blood-vessel tumor' and often shows as a red birthmark. You can remember hem- from hemoglobin and -oma from melanoma.
