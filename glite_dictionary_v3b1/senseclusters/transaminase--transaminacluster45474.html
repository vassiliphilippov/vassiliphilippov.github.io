Etymology

transaminase breaks into trans- meaning 'across', amine meaning 'an amino group', and the enzyme ending -ase, the same ending as in lactase. So a transaminase is an enzyme that moves an amino group 'across' from one molecule to another, which is why it helps change amino acids.
