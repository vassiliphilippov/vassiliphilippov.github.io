Etymology

bicapsular breaks into bi- meaning 'two', as in bicycle, and capsule meaning 'a small box or container', like a medicine capsule. So bicapsular describes something made of two connected 'small boxes' or parts, which is why scientists use it for organs or fruits split into two sections.
