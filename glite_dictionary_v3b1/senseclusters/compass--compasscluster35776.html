Synonyms

  • magnetic compass
    0.02/m3.1
    Specifies an instrument that uses a needle aligned with Earth's north to show direction
    vscompass: is the general name used for that instrument rather than the specific label
  • navigational instrument
    0.02/m4.1
    Refers to any device used to find position, direction, or course when navigating
    vscompass: is a specific kind of device that usually uses a needle to show north
  • gyrocompass
    0.01/m8.8
    Uses a spinning mechanism to find true north instead of relying on magnetism
    vscompass: is the instrument that typically uses a needle that points to magnetic north
  • mariner's compass
    0.00/m6.3
    Used aboard ships with a circular card that aligns its north point for navigation
    vscompass: is the general instrument used broadly, not only the one used aboard ships
  • radio compass
    0.00/m6.5
    Determines direction by detecting the bearing of incoming signals for navigation
    vscompass: finds direction by using a needle that points toward north not by detecting signals

Etymology

compass comes from old parts com- 'together' and passus 'step', so it originally meant 'going around' or a circle. The navigation instrument took this name because it shows directions on a circular card, helping you find your way.
