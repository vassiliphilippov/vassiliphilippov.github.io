Etymology

The word particularism comes from particular, which in turn comes from part meaning 'a piece' or 'a part', plus the ending -ism meaning 'a belief or system'. So, particularism is the idea of focusing on single people, cases, or groups — the separate 'parts' — rather than on the whole.
