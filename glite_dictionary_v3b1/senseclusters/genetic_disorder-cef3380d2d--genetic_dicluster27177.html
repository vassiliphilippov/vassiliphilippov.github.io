Etymology

Genetic disorder links two simple ideas: genetic points to the body's tiny 'genes' that act like an instruction book, and disorder means something is not working right. So a genetic disorder is a health problem caused by a mistake in those 'genes', often 'inherited' from a parent, which is why the phrase means a disease passed down in a family.
