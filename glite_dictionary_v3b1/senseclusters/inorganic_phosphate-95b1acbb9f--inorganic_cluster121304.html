Etymology

inorganic phosphate comes from two simple ideas: phosphate is the phosphorus part and inorganic means 'not made of carbon'. So inorganic phosphate is a phosphate that is not attached to carbon, which is why the term is used for the free phosphate found in blood, soil, and fertilizers.
