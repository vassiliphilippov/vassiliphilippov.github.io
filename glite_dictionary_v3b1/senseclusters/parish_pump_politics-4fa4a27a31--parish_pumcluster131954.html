Etymology

The term parish pump politics comes from the old image of people meeting at the village water pump to talk about everyday problems. Because parish means the area around a church, the picture is of neighbors discussing 'local' or 'small' issues at the pump, so the phrase now means politics about local matters rather than big national ones.
