Etymology

The noun armamentary stockpile probably comes from joining armamentary, meaning 'weapons', and stockpile, meaning 'a stored pile'. Imagine a guarded place full of guns, ammo and other supplies kept for an army. That picture shows why the phrase means 'a collection of weapons or military supplies'.
