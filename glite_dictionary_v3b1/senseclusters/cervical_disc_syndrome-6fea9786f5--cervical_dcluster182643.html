Etymology

cervical disc syndrome comes from the words cervical (the neck) and disc (the soft cushion between the neck bones). When one of these cushions presses on the nerves in the neck, it causes pain, weakness, or strange feelings in the arms, so the name now means a 'group of symptoms' from this pressure.
