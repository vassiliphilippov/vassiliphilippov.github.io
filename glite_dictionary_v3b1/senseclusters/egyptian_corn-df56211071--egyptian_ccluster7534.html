Etymology

Egyptian corn gets its name from old trade and simple naming. When Europeans saw a grain called sorghum that looked a bit like common corn and often came from Egypt or nearby lands, they called it Egyptiancorn because 'corn' once meant any 'grain', and that name stayed for sorghum that can grow in dry places.
