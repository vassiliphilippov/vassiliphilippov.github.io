Etymology

vertebrate paleontology links the bold words vertebrate ('animals with backbones') and paleontology ('the study of fossils'). Imagine a scientist digging up a backbone or big bones and studying them to learn how the animal lived, and that is why the phrase means 'the study of fossils of animals with backbones'.
