Etymology

Deoxycytidine breaks into parts you can remember: de- 'without', oxy 'oxygen', and cytidine from cyto- 'cell', as in cytosine. So deoxycytidine is a cytidine that lacks an oxygen in its sugar, which is why it is one of the building blocks of DNA.
