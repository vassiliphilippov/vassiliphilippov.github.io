Etymology

poikilothermous comes from the Greek parts poikilo- meaning 'varied' and thermo- meaning 'heat', the same 'therm' you see in thermometer and thermal. So a poikilothermous animal has a body temperature that changes with its surroundings, which is why frogs and snakes move more slowly when it is cool.
