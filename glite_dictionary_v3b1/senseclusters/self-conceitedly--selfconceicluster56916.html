Etymology

self-conceitedly combines self meaning 'you' and conceit, which is related to familiar words like conceive and concept and comes from a root meaning 'to form or hold an idea'. So, to act self-conceitedly is to behave as if you 'hold' a very high idea of yourself, which makes you seem overly proud.
