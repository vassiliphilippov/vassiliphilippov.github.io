Etymology

sea eryngium gets its name from sea, because it grows by the coast, and eryngium, an old name for a spiky plant. One popular story says people long ago used its strong roots as a medicine and even as an 'aphrodisiac', so today the name brings to mind a tough, spiky coastal plant once used for such remedies.
