Etymology

The phrase pachydermatous response comes from the word pachyderm, used for animals with very thick skin like elephants and rhinos. The picture of pachydermatous as 'thick skin' plus a response makes us think of an answer that does not feel hurt, so it means 'showing little or no concern for criticism or feelings'.
