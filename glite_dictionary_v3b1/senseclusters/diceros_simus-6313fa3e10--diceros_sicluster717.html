Etymology

Diceros simus comes from old Greek and Latin words that describe the animal: di 'two' + ceros 'horn' and simus 'flat nose'. So the name creates a clear image of a large African rhino with two big horns and a wide mouth, which helps you remember it is the two-horned rhino now endangered.
