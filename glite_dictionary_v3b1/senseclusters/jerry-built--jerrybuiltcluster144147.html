Etymology

jerry-built likely comes from an old slang name jerry for something cheap or poorly made, and one popular story says it was used from calling Germans 'Jerry' in wartime, though no one is sure. So jerry-built means 'built cheaply' or 'hastily', which is why we use it for weak or unsafe buildings.
