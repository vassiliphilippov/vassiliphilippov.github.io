Etymology

photocell comes from photo meaning 'light', as in photograph, and cell meaning 'a small unit' or a small 'battery'. So a photocell is like a little 'light battery' that catches light and turns it into an electrical signal, which is why it is used to detect light for doors, streetlights, and alarms.
