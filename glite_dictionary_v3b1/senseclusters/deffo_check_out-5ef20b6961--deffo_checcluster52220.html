Etymology

The phrase deffo check out puts slang deffo, short for 'definitely', before the two-word verb check out, which means 'to look at or visit'. Imagine a friend excitedly pointing and saying 'Deffo, check out this place!' That eager push explains why it means 'definitely look at or visit'.
