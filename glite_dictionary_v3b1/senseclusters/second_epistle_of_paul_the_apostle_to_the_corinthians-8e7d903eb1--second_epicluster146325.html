Etymology

The Second Epistle of Paul the Apostle to the Corinthians is simply the second epistle Paul sent to the Christian church in Corinth, and the word 'epistle' means 'letter'. Think of a stack of letters marked '1' and '2'; this is number two, so people often call it '2 Corinthians' today.
