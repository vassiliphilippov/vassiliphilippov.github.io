Etymology

wire matrix printer gets its name from how it prints: many thin wire pins hit an ink ribbon in a grid called a matrix, and each hit makes a tiny 'dot' that together form letters. So, the name tells us it is a printer that forms characters from 'dots' when wires hit an ink ribbon.
