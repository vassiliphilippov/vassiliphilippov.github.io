Etymology

Brachycome Iberidifolia comes from Greek roots: Brachycome means 'short hair' and Iberidifolia means 'Iberis-like leaves'. So the name points to the plant's short, fluffy seed hairs and leaves that look like the small white-flowered candytuft, which helps you picture the flower.
