Etymology

Indian cress gets its name from early European visitors who found the plant in the Americas, which they often called the 'Indies', and noticed its leaves tasted like garden 'cress'. So Indian (from the New World) and cress (a peppery salad herb) create a clear image and explain why people eat its spicy leaves and use its seeds like capers today.
