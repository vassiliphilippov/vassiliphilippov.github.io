Etymology

automatic face recognition comes from the idea of a machine that works by itself (automatic) and that can 'recognize' a face (recognition). Imagine a camera that looks at your face and compares it with a list to tell who you are, so it means a computer system that 'identifies' or 'verifies' people by their faces.
