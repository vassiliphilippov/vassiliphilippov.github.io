Etymology

The enzyme glutathione peroxidase is named for what it does: glutathione is the small helper molecule it uses, and peroxidase means 'a protein that breaks down peroxides'. So the name tells you it protects cells by using glutathione to break down harmful peroxides like hydrogen peroxide.
