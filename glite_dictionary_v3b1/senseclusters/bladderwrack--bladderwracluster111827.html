Etymology

bladderwrack comes from two parts: bladder, meaning 'a small bag', and wrack, meaning 'seaweed' or 'what the tide leaves on the shore'. So the name describes a brown seaweed with little bubbles that help it float and often wash up, which is why people used it as fertilizer and kelp.
