Etymology

Garcinia gummi-gutta is the scientific name of a tree; Garcinia is named after a French plant scientist Garcin, and gummi-gutta literally means 'gum drop' because the tree makes drops of sticky resin used as a yellow 'pigment'. That's why the name points to the tree known for its gum resin.
