Etymology

The name Bertholletia excelsa is named for French scientist Claude-Louis Berthollet, and the part excelsa is Latin for 'tall' or 'lofty'. So the name gives a clear picture of a very tall Amazon tree named for a person, which helps you remember it is the big South American tree that makes Brazil nuts.
