Etymology

aleph-nought joins the Hebrew letter aleph and the English word nought, which means 'zero' or 'nothing', so you can imagine a 'first nothing' as a picture for the first level of infinity. That's why aleph-nought means the smallest infinite size, the number of counting numbers 1, 2, 3 and so on.
