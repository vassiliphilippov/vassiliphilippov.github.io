Etymology

van der Waals forces comes from the scientist van der Waals, who noticed tiny pulls between neutral molecules when they get very close. Imagine tiny particles giving each other a quick, weak handshake when they pass; these small, temporary forces are why we use the term today for the 'weak' attraction between atoms and molecules.
