Etymology

decompression sickness comes from a simple physical image: decompression is a fast fall in pressure and sickness is feeling ill. Imagine a diver coming up too fast and tiny gas bubbles forming inside the body like a shaken soda; this causes pain, so the term means 'illness caused by a rapid drop in pressure'.
