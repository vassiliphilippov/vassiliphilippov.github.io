Etymology

homologous comes from Greek parts homo- meaning 'same' and -logos meaning 'relation' or 'reason', the same homo- you see in homophone or homogeneous. So in biology homologous describes things (like a bat wing and a human arm) that have the 'same' origin or structure even if they do different jobs.
