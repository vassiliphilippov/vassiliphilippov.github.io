Etymology

tergiversate comes from Latin parts tergum meaning 'back' and versare meaning 'to turn', so it literally gives the image of someone 'turning their back'. A helpful way to remember it is to think of a person who leaves old loyalties or beliefs, which is why tergiversate means to abandon or give up previous commitments.
