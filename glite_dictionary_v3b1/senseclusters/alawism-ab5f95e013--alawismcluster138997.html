Etymology

Alawism comes from the Arabic name Alawi, which means 'of Ali', and from the ending -ism, which means 'a system of belief'. So Alawism is the belief system of people who follow teachings linked to Ali, and it refers to a branch of Shia Islam mainly found in northwestern Syria and nearby parts of Turkey and Lebanon.
