Etymology

The word gorgeousness comes from gorgeous, which came from an old French word meaning 'showy' or 'splendid'. A helpful way to remember this is the old link to gorge meaning 'throat', because people used fine clothes and necklaces there to look splendid. So, gorgeousness means the quality of being very beautiful and impressive.
