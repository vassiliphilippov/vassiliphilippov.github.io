Etymology

Bed linen is a simple name that joins bed and linen, where bed is the place you sleep and linen is a strong cloth once used for 'sheets' and pillowcases. The image is a bed covered in that cloth, so the phrase means the sheets, pillowcases and other fabric used on a bed.
