Etymology

Semiconductor diode comes from its parts: a semiconductor is a material that only partly lets electricity through, and a diode is a device that lets electricity go in one direction. So, imagine a small one-way gate for electric current made from that special material, and that is why a semiconductor diode means 'a device that allows current to flow in one direction only'.
