Etymology

Sterculiaceae comes from the Latin word stercus, meaning 'dung', and the Roman name Sterculius, because some plants in this group have a strong, bad smell; this gives an easy image to remember the name. So Sterculiaceae was the name for a group of flowering trees and shrubs (including the cacao tree), though many of these plants are now placed in the Malvaceae family.
