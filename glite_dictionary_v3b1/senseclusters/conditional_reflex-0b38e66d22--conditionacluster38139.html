Etymology

The term conditional reflex comes from Ivan Pavlov's dog experiments, where a bell was rung before food and the dog soon began to drool at the bell alone. The word conditional shows the response depends on a condition (the bell) and reflex shows it becomes an automatic action, so it means a 'learned automatic response' to a specific signal.
