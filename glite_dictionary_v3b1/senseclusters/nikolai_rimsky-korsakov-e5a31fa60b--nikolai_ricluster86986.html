Etymology

Nikolai Rimsky-Korsakov has a double family name made of Rimsky and Korsakov, so picture two family lines joined together. He was a Russian 'composer' famous for bright orchestra music that often used 'folk' tunes, so the two-name image helps you remember his music as a blend of small folk ideas and big orchestral sound.
