Etymology

astronomical telescope is named from astronomical meaning 'about the stars and space' and telescope meaning 'a device for seeing far away'. Long ago people who study the stars used long tubes like this to make distant planets and stars look closer, so the phrase means 'a telescope used to look at space'.
