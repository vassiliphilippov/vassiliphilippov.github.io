Etymology

indistinctness comes from the parts in- meaning 'not' and distinct meaning 'separate' or 'standing apart', the same root you see in distinguish. So indistinctness is literally 'not separate' — a lack of clear edges or features, which is why it means being unclear or hard to see or hear.
