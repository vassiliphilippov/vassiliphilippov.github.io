Etymology

ionic beam paints a simple picture: ionic means made of charged particles called 'ions' and beam means a narrow, focused 'stream'. Imagine a thin, aimed stream of tiny charged particles moving together to hit a material, so the term means a focused flow of 'ions' used in labs and industry.
