Etymology

Saint Boniface was an Anglo-Saxon monk who went to what is now Germany to spread Christianity in the 700s. One famous story says he chopped down a huge sacred oak that people worshipped to show their old gods had no power, and he was later killed while preaching. That's why he is remembered as a 'saint' and a 'martyr' who helped bring Christianity to that region.
