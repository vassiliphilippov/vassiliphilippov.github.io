Etymology

immigrant class is a phrase that joins immigrant, meaning 'people who moved to a country', and class, meaning a 'social group' by income or job. It likely comes from the image of recent arrivals living and working in the same poor neighborhoods, so immigrant class refers to a group of new immigrants seen as a lower social group.
