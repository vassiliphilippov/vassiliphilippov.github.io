Etymology

The noun private detective comes from the idea of someone who is private, meaning they work for one person and not the police, and a detective, meaning someone who finds clues. They often work quietly, follow people, and find hidden facts, so today private detective means 'a person hired to investigate or find information, not a police officer'.
