Etymology

sing-kwa comes from Chinese parts sing 'silk' and kwa 'gourd', so it literally means 'silk gourd'. The plant has soft, silk-like fibers inside and is eaten as a vegetable or dried to make a natural sponge, which is why the name fits its use.
