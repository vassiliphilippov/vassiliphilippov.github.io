Etymology

Straight-fluted drill is named for the long, straight grooves called flutes cut along the drill's shaft instead of a twisted spiral. Those straight channels push out dust and help keep the bit steady in hard rock or brick, so the name shows why this drill is used for making clean, steady holes in stone and masonry.
