Etymology

The word Periophthalmus comes from the parts peri- 'around' and ophthalm- 'eye', the same 'around' in perimeter and the 'eye' in ophthalmology. These mudskipper fish have eyes set high so they can look 'around' while on mud and water, which is why the name fits.
