Etymology

coarse-grained comes from the parts coarse meaning 'rough' and grain meaning 'small seed or piece'. A helpful image is a map made of big blocks where you see broad shapes but not small detail, so a coarse-grained plan or dataset uses large, general parts instead of fine, detailed ones.
