Etymology

The compound digital scanner mixes digital, meaning made of numbers, with scanner, a machine that 'scans' a page like your eyes moving across it. So it paints a picture of a device that 'reads' a document line by line and makes a 'digital copy', which is why it means an electronic device that creates a digital version of a picture or paper.
