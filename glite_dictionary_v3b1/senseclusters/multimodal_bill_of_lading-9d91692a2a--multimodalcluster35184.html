Etymology

multimodal bill of lading comes from the idea of one paper that travels with goods as they move by different kinds of transport. Multimodal means 'many ways', and bill + lading is an old phrase for a note about loaded goods. So the paper acts like a single travel ticket for cargo, which is why it covers a shipment moved by two or more modes under one 'contract'.
