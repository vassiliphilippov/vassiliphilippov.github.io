Etymology

insinuate comes from Latin parts in- 'into' and sinus 'fold' or 'curve', giving the picture of slipping into a small space. Think of someone quietly sliding into a group or the leader's inner circle without a clear invitation. So to insinuate yourself is to introduce or insert yourself into a situation in a subtle, often secret, way.
