Etymology

alleviator comes from the verb alleviate, which comes from the Latin levis meaning 'light', so it carries the idea of 'making lighter'. As a noun for a substance or treatment, an alleviator is something that makes pain or discomfort less strong, giving relief without always fixing the main cause.
