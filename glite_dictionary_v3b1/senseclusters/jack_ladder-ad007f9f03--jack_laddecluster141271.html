Etymology

Jack ladder comes from life on ships, where sailors used a light rope or chain ladder with wooden steps to climb between the ship and a small boat, and one popular idea is that jack showed it was small and easy to carry. So Jack ladder today means 'a rope or chain ladder used on a ship for boarding or moving between decks'.
