Etymology

red-shafted flicker gets its name from the bird's bright red shafts, the long middle parts of its wing and tail feathers, and from flicker, a name for this kind of woodpecker that often flicks its tail. So the name really points to a woodpecker you can spot by the red flash of its wings and tail.
