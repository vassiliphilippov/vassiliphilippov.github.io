Etymology

bighead attitude may come from the picture of someone's head growing bigger with pride, as if their ideas of themselves fill their head. Calling someone a bighead suggests they think too highly of themselves, and the word attitude shows it is about the way they act. That's why the phrase means 'too proud' or 'arrogant'.
