Etymology

yuppity neighborhood may come from the word yuppie, a name for young city professionals who like coffee shops, boutiques, and new companies, and neighborhood, meaning an area where people live and work. So a yuppity neighborhood is an area full of those young workers and trendy places, which is why it means 'fashionable' or 'upscale' and popular with young professionals.
