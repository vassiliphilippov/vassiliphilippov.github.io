Etymology

phylum Chordata: The name phylum Chordata comes from the Latin word chorda, meaning 'cord' or 'string', because these animals have a simple 'back cord' called the 'notochord' at some stage of life. So the name groups all animals with that 'back cord', like fish, birds, amphibians, reptiles and mammals.
