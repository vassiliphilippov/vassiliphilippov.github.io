Etymology

Duplicity comes from a Latin idea of 'double' and is related to familiar words like duplicate and duo, which mean a copy or 'two'. So duplicity is like being 'two-faced', acting one way to someone and another way to others, which is why it means dishonest, double-dealing behavior.
