Etymology

Katsuwonus pelamis is the scientific, Latin-style name for the fish English speakers call 'skipjack' or 'bonito'. The first part, Katsuwonus, may come from the Japanese word 'katsuo' for the fish used to make dried flakes, and pelamis comes from an old word for 'tuna', so the name reminds us it is a tuna used in Japanese cooking.
