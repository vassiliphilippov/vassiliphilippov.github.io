Etymology

The word earthwork comes from the two simple words earth ('ground' or 'soil') and work ('something made by people'), so it literally means 'work made of earth'. Think of a big sandcastle or a raised bank of soil — an earthwork is earth shaped into a wall, mound, or bank to defend a place, help construction, or control water.
