Etymology

buffoon comes from the Italian buffone, from buffare meaning 'to puff' or 'to blow', because early jesters and clowns often puffed their cheeks and made loud, silly faces to get laughs. So a buffoon is someone who behaves in a silly, clownish way to amuse others.
