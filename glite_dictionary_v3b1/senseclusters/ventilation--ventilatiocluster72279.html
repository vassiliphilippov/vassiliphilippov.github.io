Etymology

ventilation comes from the Latin root vent- meaning 'wind' or 'blow', which also gave the verb ventilate; a helpful way to remember the discussion sense is to think of 'airing' ideas. So, to ventilate a topic is to let ideas be 'aired' and talked about openly.
