Etymology

Safety bike was named in the 1800s for a new kind of bike with two same-size wheels and a chain to the back wheel instead of a very large front wheel. People called it safety because this design was much 'safer' and more 'stable' than the old high-wheel bicycles, so the name came to mean that safer kind of bicycle.
