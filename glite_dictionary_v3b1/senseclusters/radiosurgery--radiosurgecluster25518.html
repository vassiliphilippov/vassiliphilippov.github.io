Etymology

radiosurgery joins radio-, a root meaning 'ray' or 'radiation' and seen in radiation, with surgery ('operation'). So radiosurgery is like doing an 'operation' with focused rays or beams instead of cutting the body, which is why it can treat tumors or bad tissue without cuts.
