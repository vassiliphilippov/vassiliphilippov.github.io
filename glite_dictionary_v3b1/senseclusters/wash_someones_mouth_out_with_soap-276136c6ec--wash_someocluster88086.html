Etymology

wash someone's mouth out with soap comes from a real punishment parents used: they would put soap in a child's mouth or wash the mouth after the child said bad words. The bitter taste and cleaning were meant to teach a lesson, so the phrase now means 'to punish someone for using bad language'.
