Etymology

Gymnosporangium comes from the parts gymno- meaning 'naked' (the same root as in gymnasium) and sporangium meaning 'spore case'. So the name helps us remember that this fungus makes visible 'naked' spore cases on trees, which spread rust disease to apples, pears, and other plants.
