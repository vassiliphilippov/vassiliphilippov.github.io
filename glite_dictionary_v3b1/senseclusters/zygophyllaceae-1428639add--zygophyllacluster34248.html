Etymology

Zygophyllaceae comes from Greek parts zygon 'yoke' or 'pair' and phyllon 'leaf', plus the plant family ending -aceae. So the name means 'plants with paired leaves', which helps you remember this family of shrubs and herbs often found in dry places.
