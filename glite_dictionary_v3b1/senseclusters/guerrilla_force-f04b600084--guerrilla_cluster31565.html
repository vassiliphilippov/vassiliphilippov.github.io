Etymology

Guerrilla force comes from the Spanish word guerrilla, which means 'small war' and brings to mind fighters in small bands who hide in the countryside and make quick surprise attacks. That's why a guerrilla force means 'a group' of irregular fighters who use sabotage and harassment against a larger army.
