Etymology

Gnetopsida comes from the plant name Gnetum and the class ending -opsida, meaning 'a class of plants'. So Gnetopsida is the name for the group of seed plants that includes Gnetum and related plants, and that is why scientists use it for this group seen as a link between conifers and flowering plants.
