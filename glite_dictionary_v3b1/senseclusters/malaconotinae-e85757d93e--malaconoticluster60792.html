Etymology

Malaconotinae comes from the bird name Malaconotus, which uses Greek parts malako- meaning 'soft' and -notus meaning 'back'. The ending -inae means 'a group', so the whole name is like 'soft-backed group' and is used for this group of African bush shrikes.
