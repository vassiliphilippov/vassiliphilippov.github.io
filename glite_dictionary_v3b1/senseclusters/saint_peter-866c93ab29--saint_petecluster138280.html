Etymology

Saint Peter comes from the Bible: Jesus gave his friend Peter a name that means 'rock' to show he would be a strong leader. That's why Saint Peter is remembered as the first leader of the Church and is often shown with keys, a picture that suggests he is 'guarding' the gates of heaven.
