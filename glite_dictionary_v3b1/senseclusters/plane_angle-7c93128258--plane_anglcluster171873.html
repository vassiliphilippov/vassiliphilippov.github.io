Etymology

plane angle comes from two simple words: plane meaning a 'flat surface' and angle meaning the 'space between two lines'. Imagine two straight lines drawn on the same piece of paper that meet at a point; the space between them is the plane angle, so the term means an angle formed by lines on the same flat surface.
