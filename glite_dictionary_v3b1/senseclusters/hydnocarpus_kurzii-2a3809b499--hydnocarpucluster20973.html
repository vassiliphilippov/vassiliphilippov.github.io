Etymology

Hydnocarpus kurzii has a name that helps you remember it: Hydnocarpus may come from words meaning 'oil' and 'fruit', and kurzii honors Kurz, a scientist who studied plants in India. Because its seeds are oily, people once made an oil from them to treat 'leprosy'.
