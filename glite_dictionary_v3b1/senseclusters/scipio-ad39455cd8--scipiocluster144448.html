Etymology

Scipio comes from an old Latin word scipio meaning 'staff' or 'stick', so you can picture a leader holding a staff as a sign of command. His famous title Africanus means 'of Africa' because he won a great battle there, and that is why Scipio is remembered as the Roman general who beat Hannibal.
