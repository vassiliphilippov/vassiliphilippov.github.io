Etymology

Pyralididae comes from the scientific name Pyralis plus the family ending -idae; Pyralis is linked to the Greek root pyr- meaning 'fire', and a helpful way to remember this is that these moths often come to lamps or flames. So, Pyralididae are a family of moths—many of them pests that damage crops and stored food—and the name reminds you they are moths attracted to light.
