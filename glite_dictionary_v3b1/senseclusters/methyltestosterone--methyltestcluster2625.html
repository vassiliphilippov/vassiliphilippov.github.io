Etymology

methyltestosterone is made from two parts, methyl and testosterone. Methyl is a 'small chemical group' you also see in methane, and testosterone is the male hormone named from testis, which means 'testicle'. So methyltestosterone is a lab-made, slightly changed form of the male hormone used as medicine to add or replace testosterone.
