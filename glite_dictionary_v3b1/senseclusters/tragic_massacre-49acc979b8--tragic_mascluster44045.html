Etymology

tragic massacre comes from joining tragic (meaning 'very sad') and massacre (meaning 'the killing of many people'). Picture a place where many people are suddenly killed and everyone is left in deep sadness and shock; that strong image is why the phrase means 'an extremely violent event where many people are killed and that causes great sorrow and shock'.
