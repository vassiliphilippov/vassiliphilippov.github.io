Etymology

Lysiloma bahamensis may come from old Greek words: Lysiloma possibly means 'loose fringe', a vivid image of the tree's soft flower clusters or hanging pods, and bahamensis means 'from the Bahamas'. So the name helps you picture a tropical tree with fringe-like flowers and long flat pods that grows in the Bahamas and nearby coasts.
