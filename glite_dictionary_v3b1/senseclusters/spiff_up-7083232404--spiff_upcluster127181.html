Etymology

The phrasal verb spiff up comes from the old word spiff, which meant to make someone look smart by polishing clothes or shoes. One popular story says shopkeepers would 'spiff' a customer into a shiny, neat shape, so today spiff up means 'to make something look neater or more attractive'.
