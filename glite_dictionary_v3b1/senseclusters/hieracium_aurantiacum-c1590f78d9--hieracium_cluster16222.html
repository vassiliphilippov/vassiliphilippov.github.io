Etymology

Hieracium aurantiacum comes from old words that mean 'hawk' and 'orange'. One popular story says people believed hawks ate this plant to sharpen their sight, so Hieracium links to 'hawk' and aurantiacum describes the bright orange flowers, which is why the name points to this orange hawkweed today.
