Etymology

arcsecant comes from two parts: arc and secant. arc means a curved 'part of a circle' (think of the 'arc' of a rainbow), and secant comes from a root that means 'to cut', like in the word section, because a secant is a line that 'cuts' a circle. So, arcsecant names the angle (an arc) that has a given secant value, in other words the angle whose secant is that number.
