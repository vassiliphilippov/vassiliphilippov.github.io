Etymology

The word trunkful is made from trunk, meaning a large box or the back part of a car, and -ful, meaning 'full of'. Think of an old travel trunk filled with clothes or a car trunk filled with bags; a trunkful is simply the amount that 'fills a trunk', like a cupful fills a cup.
