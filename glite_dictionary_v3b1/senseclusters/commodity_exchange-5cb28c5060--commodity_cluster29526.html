Etymology

The noun commodity exchange comes from old markets where people met to trade raw goods. Here commodity means 'a raw good' and exchange means 'a place to swap money and goods'. So today commodity exchange means an organized market where traders buy and sell these goods, often agreeing to deliver them later.
