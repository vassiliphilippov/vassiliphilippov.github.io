Etymology

The word worrisomely comes from the common verb worry, which once gave the image of something 'gnawing' at you, like a dog worrying a bone, plus the ending -some meaning 'full of' (as in troublesome) and -ly to make it describe how something is done. So worrisomely means 'in a way that causes worry'.
