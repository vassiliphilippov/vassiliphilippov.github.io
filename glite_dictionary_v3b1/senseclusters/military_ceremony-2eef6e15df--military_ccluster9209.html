Etymology

military ceremony paints a clear picture: soldiers in uniform standing in lines, marching, saluting, and playing trumpets, where military means the soldiers and ceremony means the formal set of actions. That's why it refers to a formal event by soldiers 'to honor' people or 'to mark' an important date.
