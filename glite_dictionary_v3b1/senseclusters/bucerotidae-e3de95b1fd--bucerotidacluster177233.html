Etymology

Bucerotidae comes from the Greek word buceros, made of bous 'ox' and keras 'horn', because these birds have big curved bills that look like horns. The ending -idae shows a family or group of animals, so the name points to the horn-like bills of the birds we call 'hornbills' today.
