Etymology

belongingness comes from the verb belong ('to belong to' or 'to be owned by') plus -ing and -ness to form a noun. Here it refers to the state or quality of being owned or attached to someone or something, so think of it as the 'being owned' or 'being part of' in a legal or factual way.
