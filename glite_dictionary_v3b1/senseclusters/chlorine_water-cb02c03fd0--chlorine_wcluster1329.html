Etymology

chlorine water is simply water mixed with chlorine, so imagine water with a strong chemical added that can fade colours and kill tiny germs. People used this strong mix to bleach clothes and clean things, so the name came to mean 'a water solution of chlorine used to bleach or kill germs'.
