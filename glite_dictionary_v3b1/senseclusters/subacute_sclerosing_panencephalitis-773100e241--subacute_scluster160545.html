Etymology

subacute sclerosing panencephalitis is a medical name made of small parts: subacute means 'slow' or not sudden, sclerosing means 'hardening' or scarring, and panencephalitis means swelling of the 'whole brain'. So the name describes a slowly developing disease that causes hardening and swelling across the whole brain, which is why it refers to a long, serious brain infection that gets worse over time.
