Etymology

luminiferous comes from Latin parts lumin- 'light' (seen in luminous and luminary) and -ferous 'bearing' (seen in coniferous), so it means 'carrying or producing light'. That's why scientists once used it for the old idea of a medium that would carry light, the 'luminiferous ether'.
