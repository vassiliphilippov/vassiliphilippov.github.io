Etymology

fanaticism comes from fanatic, which originally meant someone filled with strong religious feeling and comes from the Latin fanum 'temple'. So fanaticism means extreme, often intolerant enthusiasm, and even the short word fan, as in a sports fan, comes from fanatic.
