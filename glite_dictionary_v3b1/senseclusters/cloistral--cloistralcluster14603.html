Etymology

The word cloistral comes from cloister, which in turn comes from the Latin claustrum, meaning 'a closed place', and is like the English words close and closet. Think of monks living in a quiet, walled courtyard; it's a helpful image to remember that cloistral describes a life 'shut away' from the world or very private.
