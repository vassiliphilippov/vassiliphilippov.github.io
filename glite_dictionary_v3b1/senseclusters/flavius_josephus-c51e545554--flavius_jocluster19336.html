Etymology

Flavius Josephus comes from his Jewish name Josephus and the Roman family name Flavius that he took after joining the Romans. He was a Jewish leader who later wrote first-hand accounts of the 'Jewish revolt' against Rome, so his name now stands for an important source for that history.
