Etymology

The word phanerogam comes from Greek parts phanero- meaning 'visible' and -gam meaning 'marriage' or 'union', the same -gamy you see in monogamy. That's why phanerogams are plants that show their reproductive parts, like flowers or cones, and so make 'seeds' rather than spores.
