Etymology

The phrase nimptopsical remark may come from a playful mix of silly sounds — imagine someone making a tiny noise like nimpt and then a twirling, topsy sound like topsical, so the whole line feels like nonsense. That's why a nimptopsical remark means a short, funny, 'absurd' comment that does not make much sense.
