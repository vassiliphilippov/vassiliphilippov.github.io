Etymology

The word groggy comes from the sailors' drink grog, a name for watered-down 'rum' given out by an admiral nicknamed 'Old Grog'. If sailors drank too much grog, they felt weak and unsteady, so groggy now means 'weak, unsteady, or confused'.
