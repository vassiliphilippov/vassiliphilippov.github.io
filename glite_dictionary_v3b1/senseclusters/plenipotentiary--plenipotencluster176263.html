Etymology

The word plenipotentiary comes from the Latin parts plenus ('full') and potens ('power'), and you can see the same roots in plenty ('a lot') and potent ('powerful'). So a plenipotentiary is someone given 'full' 'power' to act for their government, for example to sign treaties.
