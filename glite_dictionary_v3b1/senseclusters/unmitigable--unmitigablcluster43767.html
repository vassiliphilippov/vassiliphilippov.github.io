Etymology

unmitigable starts with un- meaning 'not' and comes from the same root as mitigate, which comes from an old Latin word meaning 'soft' or 'gentle' and is like the English word mild. So, unmitigable means 'not able to be made less serious', something that cannot be softened or reduced.
