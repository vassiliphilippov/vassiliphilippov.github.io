Etymology

cautious expectation comes from the image of someone watching a fragile new plant or a baby animal: they are full of hope but move slowly and carefully because they fear something might go wrong. So, cautious, meaning 'careful', plus expectation, meaning 'hope', describes the feeling 'a hope mixed with caution'.
