Etymology

Parliamentary democracy comes from the idea that the country's leaders are chosen by and must 'answer to' the parliament, a group of elected people. Imagine a meeting where the group 'chooses' the leader and can replace them if they do badly, so the phrase means a system where the government depends on and must 'answer to' the parliament.
