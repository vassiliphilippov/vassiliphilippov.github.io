Etymology

Crataegus tomentosa gets its name from older words: Crataegus comes from a Greek word meaning 'strong' (for the hard wood of hawthorns) and tomentosa is Latin for 'woolly', pointing to fuzzy hairs on the plant. So the name gives a simple picture — a strong-wood hawthorn with some woolly parts — which helps you remember this nearly thornless species with pear-shaped berries.
