Etymology

Alecto is the name of one of the three Furies in Greek myth, a spirit who punished people for crimes of anger. Her name probably comes from the Greek Alēktō, meaning 'unceasing' or 'relentless', so a helpful way to remember Alecto is to think of someone who never stops punishing anger.
