Etymology

The word hosteller comes from hostel plus the ending -er, where -er means 'a person who does something', and hostel goes back to Latin hospes meaning 'guest' or 'host', the same idea in host and hotel. So a hosteller is someone who looks after guests and manages a place for them, like a hotel manager.
