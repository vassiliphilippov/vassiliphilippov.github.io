Etymology

Jakob Behmen, also spelled Jakob Böhme, was a German shoemaker who had powerful visions about God and nature and then wrote books to explain them. Because his ideas mixed everyday life and deep spiritual thought and people later called this 'theosophy', he is often seen as a founder of that movement and a strong influence on later thinkers.
