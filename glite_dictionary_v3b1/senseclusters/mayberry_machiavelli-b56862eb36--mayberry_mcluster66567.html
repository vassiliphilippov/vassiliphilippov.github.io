Etymology

Mayberry Machiavelli comes from putting together Mayberry, the friendly small town from an old TV show, and Machiavelli, a man known for clever plans to get power. The picture is of a pleasant person who secretly makes plans, so the name means 'a clever schemer who seems ordinary and friendly'.
