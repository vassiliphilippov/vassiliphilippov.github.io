Etymology

Psilophytaceae breaks down into parts: psilo- meaning 'bare' and phyte meaning 'plant', with the family ending -aceae meaning 'family'. So Psilophytaceae literally means a 'family of bare plants', a helpful image for remembering these simple, leafless plants that lived very long ago.
