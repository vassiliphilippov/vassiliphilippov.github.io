Etymology

The word barbarousness comes from the old Greek barbaros, a word that copied the sound 'bar-bar' to mean 'foreign'. You can see the same root in barbarian and barbaric, and because Greeks often thought of outsiders as 'uncivilized', barbarousness now means extreme 'cruelty' or a lack of 'humanity'.
