Etymology

The word Phytolaccaceae comes from the plant name Phytolacca plus the botanical ending -aceae, which means 'family' in plant names. The name Phytolacca starts with phyto-, a common part that means 'plant', so you can think of Phytolaccaceae as the 'family of plants' that contains Phytolacca.
