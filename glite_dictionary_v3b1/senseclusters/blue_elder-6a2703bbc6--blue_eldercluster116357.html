Etymology

Blue elder gets its name from the plant word elder, used for shrubs that make berries, and from the blue colour of its fruit, which gives a clear picture to remember. That's why Blue elder now refers to a 'shrub' or small tree with white flowers and blue berries whose fruit people use to make wines and jellies.
