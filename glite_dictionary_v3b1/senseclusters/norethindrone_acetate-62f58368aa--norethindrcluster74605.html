Etymology

norethindrone acetate gets its name from how the medicine works: it is a man-made form of the natural hormone 'progesterone', and the acetate part is a small chemical piece added so the body can use it. So the name points to a lab-made hormone used for 'birth control' and to treat menstrual problems.
