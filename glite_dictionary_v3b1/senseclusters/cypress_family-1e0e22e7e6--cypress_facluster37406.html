Etymology

The term cypress family comes from the simple idea of a 'family' of plants, meaning a group of trees and shrubs that are closely related. Think of cypress, juniper, and cedar as plant 'relatives' that share features like small leaves and cones, so cypress family means 'a group of related trees and shrubs'.
