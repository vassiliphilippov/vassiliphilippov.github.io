Etymology

The word cytoarchitectural combines cyto- meaning 'cell' and architecture meaning 'how something is built' or 'the design of a building'. So, cytoarchitectural describes how cells are arranged in a tissue, especially in the brain, and helps you think of the brain's 'layout' as a kind of cellular building.
