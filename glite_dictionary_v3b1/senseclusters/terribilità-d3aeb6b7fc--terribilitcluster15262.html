Etymology

terribilità comes from Italian and from the Latin word terrere, which means 'to frighten', and from terribilis, 'causing fear', the same root behind English terrible and terror. So terribilità describes a strong, awe-inspiring power in art or a person's manner that makes people feel respect or fear.
