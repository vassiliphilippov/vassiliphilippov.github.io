Etymology

terminological inexactitude is a formal, slightly joking way to call something a 'lie'. It treats the problem as one of words: terminological means 'about words' and inexactitude means 'not exact', so imagine someone using loose words to hide the truth, and that's why it now means a misleading or not true statement.
