Etymology

ampere-hour combines ampere, often shortened to amp, a unit for electric 'current' named after the scientist Ampère, and hour, which means 'time'. So an ampere-hour is like running one amp for one hour and it tells you how much electric charge has moved, which is why batteries use this number to show their capacity.
