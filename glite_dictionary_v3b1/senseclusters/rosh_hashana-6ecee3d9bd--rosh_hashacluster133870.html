Etymology

Rosh Hashana comes from Hebrew words Rosh and Hashana, meaning 'head' and 'year', so the name means 'head of the year'. That's why Rosh Hashana is the Jewish New Year, a time for prayer, thinking about the year, and hearing the shofar.
