Etymology

Kashmiri for the language comes from the region name Kashmir, likely named after the sage Kashyapa plus a word for 'lake' or 'land'. The -i ending shows 'from' the place, so Kashmiri means the language 'from Kashmir' and is used for the local speech of the valley.
