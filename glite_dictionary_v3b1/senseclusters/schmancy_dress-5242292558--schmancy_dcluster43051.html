Etymology

The phrase schmancy dress comes from a playful sound people add to words. People used 'fancy-schmancy' to make fun of something that was too 'fancy', and the short form schmancy came to mean very stylish in a joking way, so schmancy dress means a dress that is fancy but spoken about in a fun or slightly silly way.
