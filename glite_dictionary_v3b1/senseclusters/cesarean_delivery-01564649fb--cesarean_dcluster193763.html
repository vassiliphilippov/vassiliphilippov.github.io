Etymology

cesarean delivery may come from the old name 'Caesar' or from a word meaning 'to cut'. This gives a clear picture: cesarean (about cutting) and delivery (the birth) describe taking a baby out through a cut in the mother's belly, so the phrase means 'birth by surgery'.
