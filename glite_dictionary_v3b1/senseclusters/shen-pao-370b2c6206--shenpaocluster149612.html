Etymology

Shen-pao comes from Chinese: shen means 'spirit' or 'god' and pao is an older spelling of bao, which means 'treasure' or 'protector'. So Shen-pao is like a 'spirit-protector' or 'treasure spirit', which fits its role as a divine figure in the Taoist Trinity often linked with Lao-tse.
