Etymology

dreaminess comes from the simple word dream ('images while you sleep' or 'pleasant thoughts') plus the ending -ness meaning 'state'. So, dreaminess is the 'state' of feeling soft, relaxed and a little lost in pleasant thoughts, like living in a gentle daydream.
