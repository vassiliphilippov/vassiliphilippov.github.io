Etymology

The word unpleasingness is built from un- ('not'), pleasing from please ('to give pleasure'), and the ending -ness ('state'). So, unpleasingness is the 'state of not being pleasing', which means something is unpleasant to your senses, like a bad smell or a harsh sound.
