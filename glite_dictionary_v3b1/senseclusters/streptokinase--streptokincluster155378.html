Etymology

streptokinase comes from the bacteria name strepto- meaning 'twisted' and the part -kinase from a root meaning 'to move', as in kinetic. So streptokinase is an enzyme made by strep bacteria that 'moves' chemicals in the blood to help break up clots, which is why doctors use it to dissolve clots.
