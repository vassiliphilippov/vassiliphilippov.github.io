Etymology

estimated tax return comes from estimated (a careful 'guess' about what you will owe) and tax return (a form to report the taxes you must pay). One popular idea is that governments asked people to 'pay early' by sending a guessed amount during the year to avoid a large bill at the end, so today the phrase means the form used to report and pay those estimated taxes.
