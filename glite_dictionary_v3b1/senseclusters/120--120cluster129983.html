Etymology

120 comes from the Arabic number system we use today, where each digit shows hundreds, tens, or ones, so 120 reads as 'one hundred twenty'. Think of it as one 'hundred', the 1 in the hundreds place, plus two 'tens', the 2 in the tens place, which is why 120 means that total.
