Etymology

Prunus pumilla susquehanae comes from Latin words: Prunus means 'cherry', pumilla means 'small', and susquehanae points to the Susquehanna area. So the name paints a picture of a 'small cherry from the Susquehanna', which fits this tiny, spreading American cherry that grows on sandy shores.
