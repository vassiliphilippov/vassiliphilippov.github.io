Etymology

Sapindus drumondii begins with Sapindus, a word linked to 'soap' because the tree's fruit makes a soapy foam and people used it as a natural soap, and the second part, drumondii, is the name of the person who first collected or named the tree, so the whole name helps you remember it as the soap-producing tree.
