Etymology

Pitot-static tube combines two small tubes: the forward Pitot tube points into the wind and 'catches' the moving air, and the side static tube senses the nearby still air. By comparing what each tube feels, the device tells pilots the plane's 'airspeed', so the name reminds us it measures both moving and static air.
