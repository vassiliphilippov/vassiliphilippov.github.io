Etymology

biomicroscope comes from three parts: bio- meaning 'life', micro- meaning 'small', and -scope meaning 'to look'. Like biology, the study of life, and microscope, a tool to see small things, a biomicroscope is a tool for looking at very small living tissues, especially the eye.
