Etymology

sisal hemp comes from the fiber of the sisal plant, whose long, tough leaves are pulled and twisted into strong threads. The word hemp here is an old name for a strong plant fiber, so sisal hemp means a tough plant fiber 'used for making rope, twine, and mats'.
