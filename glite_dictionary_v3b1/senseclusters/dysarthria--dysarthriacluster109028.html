Etymology

dysarthria comes from Greek parts dys- meaning 'bad' or 'difficult' and -arthria, which is linked to making clear sounds and is the same root behind English articulate. So dysarthria means 'difficulty speaking clearly', which matches the medical idea of weak or damaged speech muscles.
