Etymology

abjectly comes from the Latin parts ab- meaning 'away' and ject meaning 'to throw', so the original idea is 'thrown away'. Think of reject, which also has ject and means 'to throw back'; that's why to act abjectly is to behave as if you are 'thrown away' — very low, hopeless, or extremely humble.
