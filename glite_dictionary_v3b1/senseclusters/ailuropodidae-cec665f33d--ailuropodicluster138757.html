Etymology

Ailuropodidae comes from Greek parts: ailuro- meaning 'cat' and -pod- meaning 'foot', with the animal family ending -idae. So it literally suggests a 'cat-foot family' and is the scientific family name for the giant panda and its relatives. A helpful memory trick is that -pod- is the same 'foot' seen in words like octopus, which makes the 'foot' part easier to remember.
