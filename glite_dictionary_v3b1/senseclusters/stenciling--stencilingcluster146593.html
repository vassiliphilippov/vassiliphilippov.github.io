Etymology

stenciling comes from the word stencil, which was an old name for a cut-out pattern used to copy a design again and again. Think of a 'cookie cutter'—you put the cut-out down and paint or ink over it, so stenciling is the act of making the same design quickly and repeatedly with a template.
