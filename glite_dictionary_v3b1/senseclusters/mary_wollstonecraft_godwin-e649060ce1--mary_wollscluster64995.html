Etymology

Mary Wollstonecraft Godwin comes from her parents' names: her mother Wollstonecraft and her father Godwin. She grew up with strong ideas about 'women's rights' and later became Mary Shelley, the writer of 'Frankenstein', so the full name reminds us of both her family background and her famous writing.
