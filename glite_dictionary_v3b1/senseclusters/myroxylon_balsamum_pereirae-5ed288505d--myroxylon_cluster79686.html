Etymology

Myroxylon balsamum pereirae is a name that shows a simple picture: balsamum means a sticky, sweet-smelling resin and pereirae honors a man named Pereira who studied the tree. So the name helps you remember it is the tree that makes the fragrant resin used in perfumes and flavoring.
