Etymology

Thematic Apperception Test is named for how it works: thematic relates to the main 'theme' of a story and apperception is about how someone 'understands' an image. People are shown pictures and asked to make a story, so the test finds a person's 'hidden' thoughts and feelings from the themes and the way they understand the images.
