Etymology

reprobation comes from the Latin parts re- ('against' or 'not') and probare ('to test' or 'to approve'), and it shares the same root as everyday words like prove and approve. So reprobation literally means 'not approved', which helps explain its modern sense of strong disapproval or moral blame from other people.
