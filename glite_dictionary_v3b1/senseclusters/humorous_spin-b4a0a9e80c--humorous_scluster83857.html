Etymology

humorous spin joins humorous, meaning 'funny', with spin, a word for a turn or 'twist' that changes how a story looks. One popular idea is that journalists talked about putting a 'spin' on facts, so a humorous spin is a funny twist on something, which is why it means an interpretation meant to be 'light-hearted'.
