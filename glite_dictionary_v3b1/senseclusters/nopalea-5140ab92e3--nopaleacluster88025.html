Etymology

Nopalea comes from the Spanish word nopal, which in turn comes from the Nahuatl word nōpal and means 'prickly pear'. So Nopalea is the name for a group of prickly pear cactuses from Mexico and Central America, which explains their flat, edible pads and bright red flowers.
