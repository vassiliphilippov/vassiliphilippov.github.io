Etymology

Spanish chestnut may come from the simple fact that sellers and cooks long ago sold the sweet chestnut nuts that came from Spain and nearby lands, so people began to call that kind 'Spanish'. So today Spanish chestnut means that particular 'tree' or edible 'nut'.
