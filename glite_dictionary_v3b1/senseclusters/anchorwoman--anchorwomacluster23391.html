Etymology

The word anchorwoman is made from anchor and woman, where anchor is the same word as the heavy object that keeps a ship in place and in broadcasting means the person who 'holds' a news show together. So, an anchorwoman is a female presenter who keeps the broadcast steady and connects reports.
