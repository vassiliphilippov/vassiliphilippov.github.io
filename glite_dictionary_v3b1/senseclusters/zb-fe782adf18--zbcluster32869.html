Etymology

The word Zb is short for zettabit, using the SI prefix zetta which means 'one thousand exabits' or '10^21 bits'. Think of the letter Z at the end of the alphabet to remember it is a very, very large amount, so one Zb is used to measure extremely large volumes of data.
