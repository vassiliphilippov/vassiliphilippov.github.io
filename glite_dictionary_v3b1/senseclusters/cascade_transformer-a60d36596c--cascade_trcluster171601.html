Etymology

The noun cascade transformer comes from the picture of a cascade — a small waterfall where water drops from one step to the next — and a transformer, a device that changes electricity. When transformers are placed 'one after another' like the steps of a waterfall, their voltages add up, so the name reminds you they are connected to make a 'higher voltage' source.
