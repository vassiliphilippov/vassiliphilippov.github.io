Etymology

The word cajole comes from the French cajoler, which once meant 'to chatter like a jay' and 'to flatter'. A helpful way to remember it is to picture someone talking nicely like a noisy bird until you agree, which is why to cajole someone is to persuade them with gentle praise or repeated requests.
