Etymology

McGuffey Eclectic Readers were a series of 19th-century American school books used to teach children to read. They mixed short stories, poems, and moral advice, so 'eclectic' means taken from many places, and because teachers used them for both 'reading' and 'moral lessons', the name now refers to that famous school reader series.
