Etymology

four-letter Anglo-Saxon word grew from the older polite phrase four-letter word, because people would say the number of letters instead of printing a rude word. The word 'Anglo-Saxon' points to short old English words often used as curses, so the phrase means 'an obscene or offensive word'.
