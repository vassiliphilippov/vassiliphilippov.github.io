Etymology

self-satisfaction is simply self + satisfaction; self means 'your own' and satisfaction comes from satisfy, which means 'to please' or 'to fill a need'. So self-satisfaction is the feeling of being pleased with yourself, often without seeing your faults, and this can stop you from wanting to improve.
