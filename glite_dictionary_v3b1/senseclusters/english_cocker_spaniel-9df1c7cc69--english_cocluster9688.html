Etymology

English cocker spaniel gets its name from its job and home: spaniel was an old name for dogs that worked with hunters, cocker comes from the bird 'woodcock' these dogs often hunted, and English shows where the breed was developed. That's why the name tells us it is a small English hunting dog.
