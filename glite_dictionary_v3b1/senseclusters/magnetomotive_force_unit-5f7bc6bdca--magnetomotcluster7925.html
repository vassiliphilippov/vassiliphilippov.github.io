Etymology

magnetomotive force unit gives a simple picture: magnetomotive means a 'push' for magnets, force is a push, and a unit is a measure. Think of a pump pushing water through a pipe — this unit measures the 'push' that drives the magnetic field through a circuit, so it means 'a unit used to measure magnetomotive force'.
