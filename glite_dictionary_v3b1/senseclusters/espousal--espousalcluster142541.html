Etymology

espousal comes from the verb espouse, which goes back to the old Latin root spons- meaning 'promise' or 'betrothal' and is the same root as spouse. So espousal originally meant a wedding or engagement — the act of promising to be a partner — which is why it can mean the ceremony of becoming married.
