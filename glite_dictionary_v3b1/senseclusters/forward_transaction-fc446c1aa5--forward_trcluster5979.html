Etymology

forward transaction comes from a simple trading picture: imagine a farmer and a baker agreeing now to sell wheat later at a fixed price. The word forward points to delivery in the 'future' and transaction is the sale, so the phrase means an agreement that 'locks in' a price for future delivery.
