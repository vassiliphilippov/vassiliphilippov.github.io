Etymology

The word stiffly comes from the simple word stiff, which once meant 'strong' or 'firm' in an old German word, plus the ending -ly that makes it describe how something is done. So to move or speak stiffly is to do it 'rigid' or 'not relaxed', and that is exactly how we use the word today.
