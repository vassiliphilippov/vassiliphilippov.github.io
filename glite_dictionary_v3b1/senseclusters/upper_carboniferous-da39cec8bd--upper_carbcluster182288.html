Etymology

Upper Carboniferous uses two simple ideas: Upper shows it is the 'later' or higher rock layers, and Carboniferous means a time when many plants turned into 'coal'. So the name refers to the later part of the Carboniferous period, when warm swamp forests grew and later formed large coal deposits.
