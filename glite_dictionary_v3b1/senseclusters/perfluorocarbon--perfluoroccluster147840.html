Etymology

The word perfluorocarbon comes from the parts per-, meaning 'completely', fluoro from the element fluorine, as in fluoride, and carbon. So a perfluorocarbon is a chemical made only of carbon and fluorine, which makes it very stable and able to last a long time in the air.
