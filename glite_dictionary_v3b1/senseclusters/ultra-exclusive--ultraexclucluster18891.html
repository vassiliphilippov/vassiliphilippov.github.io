Etymology

ultra-exclusive combines ultra- meaning 'beyond', as in ultraviolet, with exclusive, from the same root as exclude meaning 'to shut out'. So, ultra-exclusive describes something that goes 'beyond' usual limits to 'shut out' most people and allow only a very small group.
