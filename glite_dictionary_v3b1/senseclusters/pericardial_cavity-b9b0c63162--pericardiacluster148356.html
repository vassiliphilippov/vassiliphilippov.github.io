Etymology

The term pericardial cavity names the small 'space' between the two layers of the sac around the heart where a little 'fluid' sits. Pericardial refers to the heart's sac and cavity means a hollow space, and because that hollow holds fluid so the heart can move easily, the phrase means the fluid-filled space that lets the heart beat smoothly.
