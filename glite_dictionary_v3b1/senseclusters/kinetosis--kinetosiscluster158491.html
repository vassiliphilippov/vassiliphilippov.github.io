Etymology

kinetosis comes from Greek parts kine- and -osis, with kine- from kinesis meaning 'movement' and -osis meaning 'a condition'. So, like the word kinetic, which means 'moving', kinetosis is a 'movement condition', that is, motion sickness that makes you feel dizzy or sick.
