Etymology

handcrafted cheeseware creates the image of a maker shaping boards, plates and small tools by hand for cheese; handcrafted means made by hand and cheeseware means dishes or utensils for cheese. So, the phrase refers to handmade dishes and tools used to 'serve' or 'prepare' cheese.
