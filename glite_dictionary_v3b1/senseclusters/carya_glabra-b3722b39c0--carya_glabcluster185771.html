Etymology

Carya glabra is a scientific name that gives a simple picture: Carya comes from an old Greek word for 'nut' and glabra is Latin for 'smooth', so the name suggests a smooth hickory with nuts. That's why the name fits this hickory species, known for its hard wood and bitter nuts.
