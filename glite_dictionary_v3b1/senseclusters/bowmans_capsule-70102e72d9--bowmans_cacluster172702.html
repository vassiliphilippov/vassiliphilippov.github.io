Etymology

Bowman's capsule is named after a doctor from the 1800s, William Bowman, and the word capsule simply means a small 'container' or 'case'. The name gives a clear picture: it is a thin, cup-like sac that sits around the kidney's tiny blood 'filter' and helps form urine.
