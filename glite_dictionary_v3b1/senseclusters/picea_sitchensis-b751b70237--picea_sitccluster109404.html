Etymology

Picea sitchensis is a scientific name that combines Picea, a Latin word for 'spruce', and sitchensis, meaning 'from Sitka', the coastal part of Alaska where this tree grows. So, the name points to the Sitka spruce, a large evergreen with sharp needles and light, strong wood.
