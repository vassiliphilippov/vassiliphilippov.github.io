Etymology

Amaranthus hypochondriacus may come from old Greek and Latin words: Amaranthus means 'unfading' and hypochondriacus is a word once linked to the chest or old remedies. The 'unfading' image helps you remember this tall, showy plant with long, bright flower spikes and useful leaves and seeds, because the flowers seem to keep their colour for a long time.
