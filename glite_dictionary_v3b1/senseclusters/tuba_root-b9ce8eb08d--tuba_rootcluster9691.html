Etymology

tuba root is the name of a vine whose swollen roots were crushed and put into rivers to make fish weak and float so people could catch them easily. Because the same root also killed insects, it became a 'natural insecticide', and today tuba root refers to a plant whose roots contain a poison used for fishing and to kill insects.
