Etymology

insolubly comes from the prefix in- 'not' and the root solv 'to loosen' or 'to solve', the same root you see in solve and solution. So insolubly means 'in a way that cannot be loosened, solved, or separated', which is why it describes things that cannot be fixed or separated.
