Etymology

Do someone a lemon may come from sellers who gave buyers a lemon, meaning something that looks fine but is bad inside, like a rotten fruit or a faulty car. If someone 'did you a lemon' they gave you a bad deal, so today the phrase means 'to trick or cheat' someone and leave them worse off.
