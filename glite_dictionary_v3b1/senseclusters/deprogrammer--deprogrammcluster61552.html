Etymology

deprogrammer comes from the parts de- 'remove' and program 'a set of instructions', like a computer program. A helpful way to remember the meaning is to think of a person who 'removes a program' from someone's mind, so a deprogrammer helps reverse cult beliefs or other indoctrination.
