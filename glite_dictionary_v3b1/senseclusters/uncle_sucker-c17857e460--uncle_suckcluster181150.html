Etymology

Uncle Sucker likely comes from two simple ideas: uncle meaning a friendly family member and sucker meaning old slang for someone easily tricked. One popular story says tricksters used the warm 'uncle' image to make a person feel safe, so Uncle Sucker came to mean 'a person who is easily deceived'.
