Etymology

The word peregrinate comes from the Latin peregrinari, meaning 'to travel abroad', and from peregrinus, meaning 'foreigner'. Think of someone walking into new lands like a foreign traveller; that's why to peregrinate is to journey or wander through a place, often on foot.
