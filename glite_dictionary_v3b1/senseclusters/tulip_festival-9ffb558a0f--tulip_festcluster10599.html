Etymology

tulip festival comes from the image of towns planting beds of bright tulip flowers and holding a public festival to show them each spring. One clear story is that Ottawa's event began after the Netherlands sent tulip bulbs to thank Canada, so today a tulip festival means 'an event to celebrate and display tulip flowers'.
