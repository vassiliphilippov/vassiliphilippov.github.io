Etymology

Muammar al-Qaddafi may come from Arabic: Muammar means 'long-lived' and al-Qaddafi shows he came from the Qaddafa tribe. So the full name tells you his background, and the 'long-lived' meaning is an easy way to remember him as Libya's long-time leader.
