Etymology

glooming comes from the simple word gloom, which long ago meant 'darkness' or 'sadness' and is related to the common words gloomy and glum. The ending -ing makes it 'full of' or 'showing' that dark feeling, so glooming describes a place or mood that is dark, sad, or a little scary.
