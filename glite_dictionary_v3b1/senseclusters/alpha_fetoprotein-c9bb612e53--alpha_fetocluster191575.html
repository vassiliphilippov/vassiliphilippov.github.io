Etymology

alpha fetoprotein gets its name from the protein found in the fetus, the unborn baby, and from the old label alpha scientists used when they first separated blood proteins. Because it comes from the baby, unusual levels in the mother's blood can point to certain 'fetal' problems, which is why doctors test for it to check for birth defects.
