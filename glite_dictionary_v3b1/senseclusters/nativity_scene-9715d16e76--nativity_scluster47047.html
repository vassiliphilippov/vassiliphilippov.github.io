Etymology

The phrase nativity scene comes from nativity meaning the 'birth of Jesus' and scene meaning a small setting. People long ago made little models showing baby Jesus in a stable with Mary, Joseph, shepherds and animals, and that's why nativity scene today means a model or display put out at 'Christmas'.
