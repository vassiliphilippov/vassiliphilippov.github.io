Etymology

wool-dyed yarn comes from the simple craft of dyeing wool before it is spun into yarn; imagine bundles of wool soaked in a pot of dye so the fibers drink the color and the spun yarn keeps a deep, even shade. So, wool-dyed yarn means 'yarn that has been colored using dye applied to wool fibers'.
