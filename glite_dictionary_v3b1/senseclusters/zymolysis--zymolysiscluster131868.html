Etymology

zymolysis comes from the Greek parts zyme, meaning 'leaven' or 'ferment', and -lysis, meaning 'breaking' or 'loosening'. Think of zyme as in enzyme and -lysis as in analysis, so zymolysis is the breaking down of substances by fermentation, for example turning sugar into alcohol.
