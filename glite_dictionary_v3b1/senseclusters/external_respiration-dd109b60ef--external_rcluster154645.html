Etymology

external respiration comes from a simple image: external means 'outside' and respiration means 'breathing'. It refers to the breathing that moves air between the outside and the lungs—bringing in oxygen and sending out carbon dioxide—so it means 'taking in oxygen and getting rid of carbon dioxide'.
