Etymology

dandle probably comes from old baby talk that copies the soft sound or gentle motion of rocking a child, like a little repeating noise for up-and-down movement. A helpful way to remember dandle is to think of it as gently 'rocking' or 'bouncing' a baby on your knee, which is exactly what the word means.
