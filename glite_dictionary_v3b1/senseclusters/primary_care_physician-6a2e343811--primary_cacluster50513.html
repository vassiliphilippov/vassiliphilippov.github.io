Etymology

Primary care physician comes from the idea that the word primary means the main or 'first' doctor, care is everyday health help, and physician is a medical doctor. Think of this doctor as the front door who sees you first and sends you to other doctors when needed, so the phrase means 'the first doctor who looks after and arranges your health care'.
