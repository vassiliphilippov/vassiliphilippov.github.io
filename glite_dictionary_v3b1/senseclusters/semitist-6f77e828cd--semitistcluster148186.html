Etymology

The word Semitist comes from Semite, which comes from the Bible name Shem, thought to be an ancestor of many Middle Eastern peoples. The ending -ist means 'person who studies', as in artist or scientist, so a Semitist is someone who studies Semitic languages or cultures.
