Etymology

Cardamine pratensis comes from older Greek and Latin words: Cardamine is a Greek word for a cress-like plant and pratensis means 'meadow' in Latin, so the name can be read as 'meadow cress'. People also call it 'cuckooflower' or 'lady's smock' because it blooms in wet meadows in spring when the cuckoo sings, which helps you remember this pale, delicate flower.
