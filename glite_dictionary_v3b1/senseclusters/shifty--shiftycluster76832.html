Etymology

shifty comes from the word shift, which means 'to move' or 'change', plus the ending -y. A helpful way to remember the dishonest meaning is to think of someone who 'shifts' their eyes or story to avoid the truth, so shifty means 'not honest' or 'untrustworthy'.
