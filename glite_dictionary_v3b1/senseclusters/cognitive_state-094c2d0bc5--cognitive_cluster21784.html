Etymology

cognitive state comes from cognitive meaning 'about thinking or the mind' and state meaning 'condition'. Imagine the mind like the weather, clear or foggy, to picture someone's current mental condition, so cognitive state means 'the current condition of a person's thinking, memory, or attention'.
