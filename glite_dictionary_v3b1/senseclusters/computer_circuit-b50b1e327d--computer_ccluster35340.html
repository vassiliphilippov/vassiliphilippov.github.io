Etymology

computer circuit comes from the picture of tiny 'paths' or 'roads' for electricity inside a computer, where the word circuit means a loop or path the electricity follows. That's why a computer circuit is a small part of a computer that sends electricity to do a job like processing information, storing data, or handling input and output.
