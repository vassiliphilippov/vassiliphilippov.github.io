Etymology

Clove oil comes from the small dried flower bud called a clove and the liquid oil that is pressed or heated out of it. People long used the strong-smelling oil to ease 'toothache', to add 'flavor', and to give a nice 'fragrance', so Clove oil now means a spicy-smelling oil used for those purposes.
