Etymology

The word bowelless comes from bowel, an old word for the body's 'guts' and for deep feelings as in the phrase 'bowels of mercy', and the ending -less, which means 'without'. So, bowelless means 'without guts' or 'without pity', which is why we call someone ruthless.
