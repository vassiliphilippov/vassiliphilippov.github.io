Etymology

The word bumpkinly comes from bumpkin, a name for a simple country person, plus the ending -ly that makes it a describing word. A helpful way to remember bumpkinly is to think of someone who might 'bump' into things, so the word means 'simple' or 'awkward' in style or behavior.
