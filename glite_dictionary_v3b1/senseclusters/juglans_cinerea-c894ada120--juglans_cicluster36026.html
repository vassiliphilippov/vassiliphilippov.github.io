Etymology

Juglans cinerea comes from old Latin words: Juglans meant 'Jupiter's walnut' and cinerea meant 'ash-coloured', pointing to the nut and the pale wood or bark. So the name gives a simple picture of a walnut tree with light-brown wood and edible nuts, which fits the butternut today.
