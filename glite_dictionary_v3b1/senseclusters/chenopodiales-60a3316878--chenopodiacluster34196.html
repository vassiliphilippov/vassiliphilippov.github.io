Etymology

Chenopodiales comes from Greek parts chen meaning 'goose' and pod meaning 'foot', because some leaves look like a goose's foot. The ending -ales marks a plant order, so the name tells you it is a group of 'goose-foot' plants, including the goosefoot family.
