Etymology

ethanediol breaks into parts you can read: ethane shows it has two carbon atoms, and di- means 'two' while -ol is like alcohol and means 'alcohol'. So ethanediol is a small chemical with two alcohol groups, which helps explain why it is a sweet, poisonous liquid often used as antifreeze.
