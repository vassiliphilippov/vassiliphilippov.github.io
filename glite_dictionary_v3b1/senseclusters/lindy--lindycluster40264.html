Etymology

lindy comes from the famous pilot Charles Lindbergh, whose nickname was 'Lindy', and after his solo flight people joked about his 'hop' across the Atlantic and called a lively new dance the Lindy Hop. So lindy now means an energetic swing dance with quick 'hops' and showy moves.
