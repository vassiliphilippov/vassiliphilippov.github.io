Etymology

Mickey Mouse ring: The noun Mickey Mouse ring comes from the famous cartoon mouse Mickey Mouse. Toy makers and jewelers made small rings with his face as a fun gift for children, so today it means 'a ring decorated with the cartoon character Mickey Mouse'.
