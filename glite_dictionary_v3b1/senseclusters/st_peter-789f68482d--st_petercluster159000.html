Etymology

St. Peter comes from a follower of Jesus whom Jesus called Peter, which means rock, to show he would be a firm leader. One popular story says Jesus gave him the 'keys of heaven', so people saw him as the leader on earth and the first 'Pope', which is why many churches are named for St. Peter.
