Etymology

Schmidt camera is named after its inventor, Bernhard Schmidt, who built a telescope that worked like a camera. He put a thin glass plate at the front so it could take wide, clear pictures of the night sky, and that is why the name links Schmidt with 'camera' meaning 'a device for taking photos'.
