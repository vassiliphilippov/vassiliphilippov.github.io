Etymology

The word rewa-rewa comes from the Māori name for a native New Zealand tree and was used by local people for the tall tree with bright red flowers and valued wood. A helpful way to remember it is to imagine the repeating sound of the name as rows of red flowers along the trunk, which is why rewa-rewa today means the New Zealand 'honeysuckle' and its useful wood.
