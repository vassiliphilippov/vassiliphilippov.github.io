Etymology

Notropis cornutus is a scientific name made from Greek/Latin roots; the genus name Notropis is the group name and the species word cornutus means 'horned'. The 'horned' part likely refers to the tiny horn-like bumps male fish get in the breeding season, so the name points to this common shiner's small, horn-like feature.
