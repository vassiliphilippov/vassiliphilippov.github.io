Etymology

The name giant chinkapin may come from an old Native American word for a small tree that makes chestnut-like 'edible nuts', and giant simply shows this type is larger. So the name gives the image of a bigger chinkapin tree with shiny leaves and nuts you can eat, which is why it refers to that evergreen tree.
