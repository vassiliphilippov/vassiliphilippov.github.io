Etymology

The name yellow-crowned night heron comes from a simple picture: this heron has a bright yellow patch on its head that looks like a tiny crown, and it hunts mostly at night, so people named it for both its color and its habit. So the name tells you the bird is 'active at night' and has a 'yellow crown'.
