Etymology

Epiglottitis comes from three parts: epi- meaning 'above', glottis the part of the throat that helps the voice and breathing, and -itis meaning 'inflammation', as in tonsillitis. So, epiglottitis is the swelling of the small flap above the voice box, which is why it causes a very sore throat and trouble swallowing or breathing.
