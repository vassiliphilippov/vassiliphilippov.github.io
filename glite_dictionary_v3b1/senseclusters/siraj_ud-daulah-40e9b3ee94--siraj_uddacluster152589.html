Etymology

Siraj ud-Daulah comes from Arabic: Siraj means 'lamp' and ud-Daulah means 'of the state', so imagine a leader as a lamp meant to light and protect his country. He was the 'last independent Nawab of Bengal' who tried to resist British control, so the name helps you remember his role as the final local ruler defending his land.
