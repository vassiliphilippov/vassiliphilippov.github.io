Etymology

penicillinase comes from penicillin, the antibiotic named after the mold Penicillium, and the ending -ase, which marks enzyme names. So, penicillinase is an enzyme that 'breaks down' penicillin, which is why bacteria that have it are 'resistant' to that antibiotic.
