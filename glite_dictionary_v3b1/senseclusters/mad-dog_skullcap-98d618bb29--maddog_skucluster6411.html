Etymology

mad-dog skullcap may come from an old belief that a kind of skullcap was used to help people bitten by a 'mad dog' or those with 'nervous' fits. The plant's small, cap-like flowers gave it the name skullcap, and because people used it for those problems the name stuck for a herb now used for 'seizures' and 'anxiety'.
