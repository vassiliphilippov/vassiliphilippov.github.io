Etymology

The word leprosarium comes from the root lepro-, the same root as leper, from Greek lepra meaning 'scaly', and the ending -arium meaning 'place for', like in aquarium. So a leprosarium is a 'place for' people with leprosy, a hospital where they were cared for and kept apart.
