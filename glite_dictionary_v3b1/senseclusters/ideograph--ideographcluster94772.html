Etymology

ideograph comes from two parts: ideo- meaning 'idea' and -graph meaning 'to write', the same -graph you see in autograph or photograph. So an ideograph is a written sign that shows an idea or thing directly, which is why the word means a symbol that stands for a thought or thing.
