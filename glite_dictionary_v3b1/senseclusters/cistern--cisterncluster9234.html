Etymology

Cistern comes from the Latin cisterna, from an old Greek word kiste meaning 'box' or 'sac', and this same idea also gave English cyst ('a sac or bladder'). So a body cistern is a natural 'space' or 'box' that holds fluid like lymph or cerebrospinal fluid.
