Etymology

The name pumice stone comes from pumice, a bubbly, very light rock formed when hot lava cools and holds many tiny holes so it looks like dried foam. Because the surface is rough, people used these pieces as a stone to rub away hard skin or dirt, so today pumice stone means 'a light, rough rock used for cleaning and smoothing'.
