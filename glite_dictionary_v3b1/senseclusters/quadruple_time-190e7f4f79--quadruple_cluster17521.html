Etymology

quadruple time is a musical phrase that means a rhythm with four 'beats' in each bar. Here quadruple means four and time means the rhythm, so imagine marching or dancing while you count 1-2-3-4 and step on the strong first beat; that's why it describes music made in groups of four beats.
