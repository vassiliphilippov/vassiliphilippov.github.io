Etymology

immune suppressant drug says what it does: a drug that uses a suppressant to lower the body's immune response. Think of it as turning down a loud alarm so it does not attack a new organ or the body's own cells, and that's why the term means a medicine used to 'weaken the immune system' to 'prevent rejection' or treat autoimmune problems.
