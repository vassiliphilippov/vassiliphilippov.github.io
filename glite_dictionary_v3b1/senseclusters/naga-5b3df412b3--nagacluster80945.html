Etymology

The word Naga is the name for groups of people and the family of languages they speak in northeast India and western Myanmar. A helpful way to remember it is that one old story says the name came from a plains word for 'naked' used by people from the lowlands to describe hill tribes, and another links it to the ancient Indian word nāga meaning 'snake' in local myths, and in both cases the name came to mean these people and their languages.
