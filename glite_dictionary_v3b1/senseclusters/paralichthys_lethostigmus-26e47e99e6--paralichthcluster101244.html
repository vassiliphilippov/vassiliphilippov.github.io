Etymology

Paralichthys lethostigmus probably comes from old Greek parts: para meaning 'beside' and ichthys meaning 'fish', while lethostigmus may mean a 'hidden spot'. That's why the name fits this flat flounder — it lies on its side with both eyes on one side and often has small marks, so the name helps you picture a 'side fish with a hidden spot'.
