Etymology

Vaccaria hispanica may come from old Latin words: Vaccaria from 'vacca' meaning 'cow', because people once used the plant or its seeds to feed cattle, and hispanica means 'from Spain'. So the name explains why this small wild flower is linked to cows and is also called 'cowherb' today.
