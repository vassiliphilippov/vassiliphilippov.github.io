Etymology

Bishop Ulfilas was a church leader in the 300s who created a simple Gothic alphabet and used it to translate the Bible so the Gothic people could read it in their own language. That's why he is remembered for 'translating the Bible into Gothic' and 'inventing the Gothic alphabet'.
