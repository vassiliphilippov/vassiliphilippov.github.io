Etymology

The word adrenocorticotrophin is made of three parts: adreno- like adrenal and adrenaline meaning 'near the kidney', cortico- from cortex meaning 'outer layer', and -trophin meaning 'to feed' or 'help grow'. So, the name means a substance that 'feeds' or tells the adrenal cortex to make hormones, which is exactly what this hormone does.
