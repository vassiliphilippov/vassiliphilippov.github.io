Etymology

Aruru is a name from ancient Mesopotamia for a mother and earth goddess in old Sumerian stories, often linked with other mother figures like Ki. She is famous for making humans from clay, so thinking of Aruru as a 'mother' and a 'creator' helps explain why she stands for birth, fertility, and the land.
