Etymology

unsmotherable passion gives the image of a fire that keeps burning even when someone tries to cover it. The verb 'smother' means to cover to stop a flame or to stop breathing, so unsmotherable means 'cannot be stopped' and passion is a very strong feeling, which is why the phrase means a feeling you cannot hold back.
