Etymology

abrading stone is the name for a simple tool made from a rock that people rub on other things. The word abrading means rubbing away and stone is the rock, so picturing someone rubbing a rock on wood or bone helps you remember it was used 'to grind', 'to smooth', or 'to sharpen'.
