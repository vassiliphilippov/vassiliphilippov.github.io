Etymology

Arabian Desert (Arabian Peninsula): The name Arabian Desert for the vast area on the peninsula simply means the dry land of 'Arabia', where Arab people and tribes lived for centuries. That's why Arabian Desert now refers to the large, sandy, dry region that covers much of the peninsula.
