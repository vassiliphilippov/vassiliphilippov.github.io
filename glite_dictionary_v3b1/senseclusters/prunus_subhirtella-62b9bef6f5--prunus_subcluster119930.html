Etymology

Prunus subhirtella is a Latin plant name that paints a small picture: Prunus is the cherry/plum group and subhirtella means 'slightly hairy', a note about the tree's tiny, soft buds and branches. So the name helps you remember it is a small, ornamental cherry tree with delicate pink flowers.
