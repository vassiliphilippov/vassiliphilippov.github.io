Etymology

take the shine out of comes from the simple image of a shiny, polished object losing its brightness; if you rub or scratch it, you take the shine out and it becomes dull. So, saying something 'takes the shine out of' a party or performance means it makes the event feel 'less enjoyable or impressive'.
