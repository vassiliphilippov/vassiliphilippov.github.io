Etymology

Bubble dance comes from old cabaret and burlesque shows where a solo performer used large round balloons like big bubbles to hide and then reveal her body while she danced. So the bubble part refers to the balloons and the dance part to the solo performance, which is why the phrase means 'a solo dance using large balloons to conceal and show the dancer'.
