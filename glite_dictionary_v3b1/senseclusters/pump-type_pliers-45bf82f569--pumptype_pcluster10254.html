Etymology

One simple idea is that the tool pump-type pliers gets its name from the way the handles and jaws slide into different positions, an action a bit like pumping up and down. So the name helps you remember that these pliers are 'adjustable' and can 'grip' many sizes of nuts, bolts, and pipes.
