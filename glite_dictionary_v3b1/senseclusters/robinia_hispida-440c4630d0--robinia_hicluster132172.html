Etymology

Robinia hispida takes its name from a gardener and a Latin word: Robinia honors a gardener called Robin, and hispida means 'bristly' or 'hairy'. That's why the name brings to mind the plant's bristly stems and helps you remember this small shrub with pink flowers.
