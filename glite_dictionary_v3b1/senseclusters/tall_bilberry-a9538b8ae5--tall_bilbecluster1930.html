Etymology

tall bilberry is named by joining tall and bilberry to picture a bilberry bush that grows higher than usual. Because it is a 'high-growing' shrub with edible bluish-black berries and is the main wild plant people used to develop cultivated blueberries, the name points to both its height and its fruit.
