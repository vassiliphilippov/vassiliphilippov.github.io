Etymology

The word hydrochlorothiazide breaks into hydro- 'water', chloro- 'chlorine', and -thiazide, the name of a group of medicines that help the body get rid of extra water. So, hydrochlorothiazide is a drug that makes you pass more water and helps lower blood pressure.
