Etymology

snow bunny may come from the picture of a small rabbit on the slopes: snow for the ski mountains and bunny for a cute animal that hops and looks pretty. People began using it for someone, often a woman, who spends time on ski slopes for fun and to look fashionable, so it now means 'a person who enjoys skiing and dresses fashionably for the slopes'.
