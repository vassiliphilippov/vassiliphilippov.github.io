Etymology

vena canaliculi cochleae comes from Latin and literally names a small vein in the tiny canal of the cochlea, the spiral part of the inner ear. It combines vena ('vein'), canaliculi ('small channel' or 'little tube'), and cochleae ('cochlea'), so the phrase describes a vein running through the cochlea's little tube and therefore means a vein that drains blood from the inner ear.
