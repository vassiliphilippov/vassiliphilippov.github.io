Etymology

Butyrate comes from a root that meant 'butter' in old Greek and Latin, so the word is linked to butter and the buttery acid it names. The ending -ate shows it is a chemical form, so butyrate means the salt or ester form of that 'butter' acid found in some foods and made by bacteria in the gut.
