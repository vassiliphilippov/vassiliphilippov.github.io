Etymology

Fere phenomenon is named after a French doctor, Charles Féré, who first noticed tiny electric changes on people's skin when they felt 'stress' or 'anxiety'. So today Fere phenomenon means those small electric changes on the skin that show someone is feeling stressed or anxious.
