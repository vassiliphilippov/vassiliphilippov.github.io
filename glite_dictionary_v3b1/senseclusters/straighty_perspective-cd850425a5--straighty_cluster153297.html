Etymology

straighty perspective comes from a simple image: imagine looking down a long, straight road. The word straighty comes from the idea of 'straight' meaning one direction and perspective means a view. So the phrase paints a view that only sees 'straight' people and misses LGBTQ+ lives, which is why it means a viewpoint that ignores people who are not straight.
