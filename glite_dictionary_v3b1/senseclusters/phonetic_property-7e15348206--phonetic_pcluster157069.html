Etymology

phonetic property shows a simple idea: phonetic points to a spoken 'sound' and property means a 'quality' of that sound, like whether it uses the voice, where in the mouth it is made, or if it is high or low in pitch. So, phonetic property means a specific characteristic of a speech sound that helps us tell sounds apart.
