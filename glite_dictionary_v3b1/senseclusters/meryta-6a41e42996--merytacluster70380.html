Etymology

Meryta is the scientific name for a group of evergreen trees that grow on ocean islands and are often called 'puka' by local people. A helpful way to remember it is to picture a coastal 'puka' with large leaves standing as a windbreak, so Meryta refers to those Pacific coastal trees.
