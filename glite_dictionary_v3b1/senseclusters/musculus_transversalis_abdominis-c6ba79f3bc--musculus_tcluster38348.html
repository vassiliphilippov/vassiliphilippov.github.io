Etymology

musculus transversalis abdominis comes from old Latin words: musculus meant 'little mouse' because a moving muscle looked like a small mouse under the skin, transversalis meant 'across', and abdominis meant 'belly'. So the name gives the picture of a thin muscle running across your belly, which helps you remember it is the deep abdominal muscle.
