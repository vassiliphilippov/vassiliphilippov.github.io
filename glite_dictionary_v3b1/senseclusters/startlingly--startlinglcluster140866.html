Etymology

Startlingly comes from the verb startle, which is built on the familiar word start meaning 'to jump' or be suddenly surprised, plus the ending -ly that makes it tell us how something happens. So Startlingly means 'in a way that makes you jump', which is why it describes something 'very surprising'.
