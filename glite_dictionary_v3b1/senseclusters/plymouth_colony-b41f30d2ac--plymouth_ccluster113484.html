Etymology

Plymouth Colony comes from the English town of Plymouth and the word colony, which means a new settlement. One clear picture is the group called 'Pilgrims' sailing from 'Plymouth' on the 'Mayflower' and landing to start that small colony in 1620, so Plymouth Colony means the settlement the 'Pilgrims' started then.
