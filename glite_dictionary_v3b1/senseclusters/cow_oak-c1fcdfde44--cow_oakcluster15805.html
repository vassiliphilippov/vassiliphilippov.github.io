Etymology

The name cow oak may come from a simple farm picture: one popular story says farmers let their cows eat or rest under a large oak, so people called that kind of 'tree' the 'cow oak'. Because the wood is strong and flexible, the name stayed for this oak used for baskets and chair seats.
