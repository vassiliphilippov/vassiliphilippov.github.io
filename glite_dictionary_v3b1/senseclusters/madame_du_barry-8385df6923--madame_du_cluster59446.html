Etymology

Madame du Barry was a real woman who rose from a poor background to become King Louis XV's main mistress and a powerful 'favourite' at court. She was later put to death during the French Revolution, so her name often brings to mind someone who had great royal favour but then suffered a sudden, dramatic fall from power.
