Etymology

Mameluke soldier comes from long ago in Egypt, where rulers took young people from other lands, made them slaves, and trained them to be fighters called Mamelukes. Because these former slaves became skilled and trusted soldiers and even rulers, the name Mameluke soldier now means that special group of trained fighters.
