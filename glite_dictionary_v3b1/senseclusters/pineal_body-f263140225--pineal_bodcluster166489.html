Etymology

Pineal body is named for its shape: pineal comes from Latin for 'pine-cone' and the small body looks like a tiny pine cone deep in the brain. It makes melatonin and helps control the 'sleep-wake cycle', so the name helps you remember this tiny gland that sets the body's daily rhythms.
