Etymology

bunny boiler comes from a famous movie where a woman boiled a pet rabbit; the pet was the bunny and the pot was the boiler. The shocking scene showed an extreme, dangerous reaction to a breakup, so 'bunny boiler' now means someone who is 'obsessive' or 'dangerous' after a relationship ends.
