Etymology

motherliness comes from the simple word mother plus the endings -ly (meaning 'like') and -ness (meaning 'quality' or 'state'), so it literally means the 'quality of being mother-like'. That's why motherliness describes the warm, caring, and tender behavior we expect from a mother.
