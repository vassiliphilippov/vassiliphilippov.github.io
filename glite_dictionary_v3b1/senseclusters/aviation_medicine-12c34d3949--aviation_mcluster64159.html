Etymology

aviation medicine joins the words aviation and medicine. Aviation means flying in planes or rockets and medicine means care for health. Think of doctors who check pilots' breathing, ears, and how the body handles high altitude and fast speed, so it means 'the study and care of the health and safety of people who fly or go into space'.
