Etymology

Lithane comes from the Greek lithos meaning 'stone', the same root as lithium, and the ending -ane gives it a chemical-style brand name. So Lithane is easy to remember as a 'stone' chemical, a white powder used in glass and ceramics and, as a medicine, to help steady mood swings.
