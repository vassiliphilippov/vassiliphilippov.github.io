Etymology

Chimerism comes from chimera, a Greek myth creature made of parts of different animals like a lion, a goat and a snake, so the word suggests 'a mix'. In biology, chimerism means one body has cells or tissues from two different genetic sources, which fits the idea of parts from different origins.
