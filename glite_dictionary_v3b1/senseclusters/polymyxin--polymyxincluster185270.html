Etymology

The word polymyxin comes from the Greek parts poly- 'many' and myx- 'mucus', and it was named after the slime-like bacteria that make it. So polymyxin is an antibiotic made by soil bacteria, which helps explain why it is a natural but sometimes toxic medicine used for serious infections.
