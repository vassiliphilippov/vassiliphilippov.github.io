Etymology

a bit hokey comes from an older, playful phrase hokey-pokey, once the name for cheap street shows and a silly dance. Because hokey-pokey felt fake and simple, saying something is 'a bit hokey' means it is 'a little silly', 'too sentimental', or 'not very serious'.
