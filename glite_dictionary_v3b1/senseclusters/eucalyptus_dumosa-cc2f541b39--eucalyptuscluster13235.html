Etymology

Eucalyptus dumosa comes from the part dumosa, which means 'bushy' or 'shrubby' in Latin. That's why the name fits: this small eucalyptus grows as a dense, low bush in dry parts of Australia, so the name helps you remember its shape and habitat.
