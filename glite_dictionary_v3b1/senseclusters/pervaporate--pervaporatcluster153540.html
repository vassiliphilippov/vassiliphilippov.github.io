Etymology

pervaporate comes from the parts per- meaning 'through' and vapor meaning 'steam' or 'gas', like in vapor or evaporate. So to pervaporate is to make a liquid turn into vapor and pass 'through' a membrane, which is exactly the idea behind this separation process.
