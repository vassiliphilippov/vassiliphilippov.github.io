Etymology

mixed-blood is simply mixed + blood, where 'blood' has long been used to mean 'family' or 'ancestry'. So the word named a person whose family lines were mixed—often used in history for people with both Indigenous and European roots—and today it still means someone with parents or ancestors from different racial groups.
