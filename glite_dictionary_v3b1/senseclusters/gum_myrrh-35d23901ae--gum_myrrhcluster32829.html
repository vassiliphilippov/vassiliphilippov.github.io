Etymology

The noun gum myrrh comes from the sticky resin that flows from certain trees: gum names the sticky sap and myrrh is the resin people collected and burned for its smell in perfumes and religious ceremonies. So today gum myrrh means a fragrant resin used as incense and in perfume.
