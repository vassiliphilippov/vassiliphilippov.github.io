Etymology

The word disputatiously comes from the common verb dispute, which means 'argue', plus the ending -ous meaning 'full of' and -ly meaning 'in a ... way'. Think of someone 'full of arguments' — they are disputatious, so to act disputatiously is to speak or behave in an argumentative, challenging way.
