Etymology

Pervaporation comes from the parts per- meaning 'through', vapor meaning 'steam' or 'gas', and -ation to make a process. It literally suggests 'vapor passing through', which fits the method of turning part of a liquid into vapor that moves through a membrane to separate or concentrate a solution.
