Etymology

vile comes from the Latin vilis, which means 'cheap' or 'worthless'. A helpful way to remember it is to think of something so low in value that you feel disgust or moral anger toward it — that's why vile now means extremely unpleasant or morally bad, and it is the same root behind vilify ('to say someone is worthless').
