Etymology

Platanaceae comes from the tree name Platanus, which comes from the Greek platanos meaning 'broad' or 'wide' because these trees have large, flat leaves and wide crowns. So Platanaceae is the family of 'plane' trees, and remembering platanos as 'broad' helps you link the name to their big leaves.
