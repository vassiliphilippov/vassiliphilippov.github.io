Etymology

Boobus Americanus may come from a cartoonist who made a fake Latin-style name that sounds like a species label. It joins Boobus (like 'boob', a foolish person) and Americanus (meaning American), so the phrase paints a picture of the 'average American' who seems simple or easily fooled.
