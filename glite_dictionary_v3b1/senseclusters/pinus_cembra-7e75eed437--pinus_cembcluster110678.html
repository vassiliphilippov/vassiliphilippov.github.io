Etymology

Pinus cembra takes its name from Latin and a local place name: Pinus means 'pine' and cembra probably comes from the Cembra valley in Italy where the tree grows. So the name is like saying 'pine from Cembra', which helps you remember it is a mountain pine that makes edible cembra nuts.
