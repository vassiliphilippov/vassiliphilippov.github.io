Etymology

The word farcically comes from farce, a kind of silly play, and that comes from the Latin word farcire, which means 'to stuff'. A helpful way to remember it is to imagine a play 'stuffed' with crazy scenes, so to act farcically means to act in a ridiculous, over-the-top way.
