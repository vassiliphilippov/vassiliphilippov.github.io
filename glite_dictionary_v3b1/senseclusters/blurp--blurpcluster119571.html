Etymology

Blurp as a verb comes from the same silly sound idea and means to make a sudden little noise or utterance. Like blurt, 'to say something suddenly', or burp, 'a sudden sound', to blurp is to let out a quick often accidental noise, which is why people blurp when they are nervous or surprised.
