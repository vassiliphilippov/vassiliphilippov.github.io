Etymology

karakul comes from Central Asia and is linked to Turkic parts qara 'black' and kul 'lake', a name often used for places there. That's easy to remember because this sheep is famous for the soft, curly, black fur of its newborn lambs, so the 'black' part connects to the animal's valuable hide.
