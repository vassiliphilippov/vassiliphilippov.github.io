Etymology

Ilex glabra is the scientific name for the plant commonly called inkberry holly. One popular story says its very dark berries were once used to make a black dye like 'ink', and the parts Ilex as a kind of 'holly' and glabra meaning 'smooth' describe the plant, so the name helps you remember its shiny leaves and dark berries.
