Etymology

Monotropaceae comes from the plant genus Monotropa, which begins with Greek mono- meaning 'one'. A helpful way to remember this family is that many of its plants have a single, lone flower and are not green but get food through fungi, so the name points to their 'one' or solitary, ghostly appearance.
