Etymology

Wild bergamot gets its name from a simple image: the plant grows in the wild (wild) and its leaves smell a bit like the bergamot orange (bergamot). One popular story says people used those fragrant leaves to make 'tea' and 'medicine', so the name helps us remember it is a wild, fragrant herb used for 'tea' and healing.
