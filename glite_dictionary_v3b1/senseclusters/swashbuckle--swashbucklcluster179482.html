Etymology

The word swashbuckle comes from two older parts, swash meaning 'to strike with a sword' or 'to make a loud swishing sound', and buckle meaning the metal buckle or small shield worn on a belt. It gives the image of a showy swordsman striking an opponent's buckle to show off, so to swashbuckle now means to act daringly and in a bold, showy way like a heroic fighter.
