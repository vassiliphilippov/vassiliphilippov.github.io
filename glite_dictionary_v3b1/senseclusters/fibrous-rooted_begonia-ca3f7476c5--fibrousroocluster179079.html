Etymology

fibrous-rooted begonia: The name fibrous-rooted begonia comes from the plant's roots: fibrous and rooted show it has 'many thin, thread-like roots' rather than one thick root like a potato. So gardeners use this name to mean a begonia that grows from thin roots, and that is why the phrase describes this type of plant.
