to chargeback

0.00/m6.0
To return money to the person, account, or department responsible
verb US / ˈtʃɑrdʒˌbæk /

Examples

To chargeback expenses
The finance team will chargeback the software subscription to the marketing department next month.
If the expense was made in error, we can chargeback the amount to the correct account.
The company decided to chargeback the travel costs to the employee's department.
I charged the payment back yesterday.
The receipt shows that I charged it back last week.

Synonyms

  • pay back
    14/m1.6
    Gives money to someone you borrowed from to settle the debt
    vschargeback: moves a payment to the responsible account instead of giving money to a lender
  • to repay
    3/m1.7
    Gives money to the lender after borrowing or accepting funds
    vschargeback: moves a previous payment to the responsible account rather than settling a loan
  • to reimburse
    3/m5.3
    Gives money to someone to cover costs or losses they had
    vschargeback: moves the cost to the responsible account instead of giving money to the person

Etymology

chargeback as a verb is built from charge ('a cost') and back ('return'), so to chargeback is to move a cost back to the person, account, or department responsible. This explains why you can chargeback a wrong payment or transfer a cost to the correct place.
