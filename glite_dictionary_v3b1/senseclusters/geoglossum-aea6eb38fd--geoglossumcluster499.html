Etymology

Geoglossum comes from the Greek parts geo- meaning 'earth' and glossa meaning 'tongue', so it really means 'earth tongue'. Imagine a dark, tongue-shaped fungus coming up from the ground, and you can see why these fungi are called earth-tongues.
