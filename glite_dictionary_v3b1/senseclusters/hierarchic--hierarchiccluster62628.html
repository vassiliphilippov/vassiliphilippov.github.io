Etymology

hierarchic comes from Greek parts hieros 'sacred' and archos 'ruler' and is related to hierarchy, with the archos part like the 'ruler' you see in monarch. It first named the church's ordered rulers, so today hierarchic means arranged in ranked levels of power or importance.
