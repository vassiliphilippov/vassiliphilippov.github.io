Etymology

order Lechanorales comes from the main genus name Lechanora plus the usual scientific ending -ales. One popular story says Lechanora comes from a Greek word for a shallow bowl. Many of these lichens make 'cups that hold spores', so the name refers to the group of lichens that have those cups.
