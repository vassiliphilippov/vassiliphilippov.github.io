Etymology

leafy-stemmed is made from the simple words leaf ('a flat green part of a plant') and stem ('the main stalk'), with -ed showing 'having' or 'covered with'. So imagine a stem full of leaves; that image explains why leafy-stemmed means 'having a stem that bears many leaves'.
