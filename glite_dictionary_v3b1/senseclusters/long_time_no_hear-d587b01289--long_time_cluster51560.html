Etymology

long time no hear may come from people using very simple English when they spoke with others who used a different language, much like the older friendly line long time no see but with hear to talk about messages. So it paints the picture of not having 'heard' from someone for a long time, and we use it to mean 'I haven't heard from you in a long time'.
