Etymology

Sand reed comes from a simple picture: a reed that grows in sand near the sea, with long stems and spreading roots that hold the sand in place. So, because the plant helps 'stabilize dunes' and its roots were used in 'traditional medicine', the name points to both where it grows and how people used it.
