Etymology

Thylacinus cynocephalus is a scientific name made from Greek words: thylacinus meaning 'pouched' and cynocephalus meaning 'dog-headed'. This picture of a pouch-bearing, dog-like animal is why the name refers to the Tasmanian tiger (the thylacine).
