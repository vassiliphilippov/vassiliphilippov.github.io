Etymology

dichromacy comes from Greek parts di- 'two' and chroma 'color', so it means 'two-color'. Think of monochrome ('one color') to remember the chroma part, and that helps explain why dichromacy is a condition where someone can match colors using only two color types instead of the usual three.
