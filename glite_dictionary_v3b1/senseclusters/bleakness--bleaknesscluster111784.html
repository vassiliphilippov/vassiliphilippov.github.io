Etymology

The word bleakness comes from an old Norse word bleikr, which meant 'pale' or 'shining', and you can see the same idea in bleach, which makes things pale. So bleakness describes a cold, empty, or lifeless feeling or place, like a pale, empty field with no colour or warmth.
