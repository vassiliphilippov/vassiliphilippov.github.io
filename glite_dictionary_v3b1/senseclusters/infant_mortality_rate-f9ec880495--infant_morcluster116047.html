Etymology

infant mortality rate comes from three plain words: infant (a baby), mortality (death), and rate (how often something happens). People began counting how many babies died before their first birthday per 1,000 births to see how healthy a place was, so the phrase now means 'the number of babies who die before their first birthday'.
