Etymology

Mujahedeen comes from Arabic and is the plural of mujahid, which means 'one who struggles' and comes from jihad, 'struggle' or 'effort'. So mujahedeen literally means 'those who struggle', which is why the word is used for Muslim fighters who take part in a jihad.
