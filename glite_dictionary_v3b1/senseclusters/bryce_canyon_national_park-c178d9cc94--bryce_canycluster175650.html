Etymology

Bryce Canyon National Park gets its name from a settler named Ebenezer Bryce, and locals called the rocky area a canyon, so the name 'Bryce's canyon' stuck. Today the park keeps that name and is famous for colorful cliffs and tall, thin rock pillars called 'hoodoos', which is why people visit the canyon.
