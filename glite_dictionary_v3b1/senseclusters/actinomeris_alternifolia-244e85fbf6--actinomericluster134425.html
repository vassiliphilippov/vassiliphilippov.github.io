Etymology

Actinomeris alternifolia is a scientific name that may come from older Greek and Latin words that describe the plant. The part Actinomeris points to the bright, ray-like yellow petals and alternifolia means the leaves sit one by one along the stem ('alternate leaves'), so the name helps you remember it has showy yellow rays and leaves in a line.
