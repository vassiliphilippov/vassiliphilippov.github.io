Etymology

frankenfruit hybrid comes from the famous story of Frankenstein: the part franken makes you think of something made by people and a bit scary, and hybrid means a mix of two plants. So a frankenfruit hybrid is a fruit mixed or changed in a way that feels 'unnatural' or 'artificial', and the name shows people's worry about it.
