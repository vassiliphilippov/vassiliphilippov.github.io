Etymology

Solidago missouriensis gets its name from Latin: Solidago means 'to make whole' because people once used goldenrod as a healing plant, and missouriensis means 'from Missouri'. So the name helps you remember it is a goldenrod used in folk medicine and that it grows in Missouri and nearby parts of North America.
