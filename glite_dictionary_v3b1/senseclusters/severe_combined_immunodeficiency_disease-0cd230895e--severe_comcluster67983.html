Etymology

severe combined immunodeficiency disease literally tells the problem: 'severe' means very serious, 'combined' shows more than one part is affected, and 'immunodeficiency' means 'not enough immune protection'. So the name reminds us that the body's defenses are very weak, which is why people with it get dangerous infections.
