Etymology

registered bond comes from the simple idea of writing the owner's name on the paper: registered means the owner's name is recorded and bond is the paper that proves a loan. Because the name is written down, the bond is safer if lost and can be passed to someone else only when the registered person signs it, so it means a bond whose owner is officially recorded and must approve any transfer.
