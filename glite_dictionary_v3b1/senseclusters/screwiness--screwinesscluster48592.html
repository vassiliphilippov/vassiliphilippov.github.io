Etymology

screwiness comes from the common word screw, a metal fastener that 'turns', and the ending -iness meaning 'the state of'. Think of a screw's twist, like when you use a screwdriver, so screwiness is a kind of 'twistiness' that makes a person or story feel odd, amusing, or unexpected.
