Etymology

antigenicity comes from antigen, which is built from anti- 'against' and -gen 'to produce' and was thought of as an 'antibody maker', plus the ending -icity meaning 'the quality of'. So antigenicity is the 'ability' of a substance to cause the immune system to react.
