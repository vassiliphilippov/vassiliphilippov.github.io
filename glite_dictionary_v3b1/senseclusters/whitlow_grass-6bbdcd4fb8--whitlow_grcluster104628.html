Etymology

whitlow grass may get its name from an old home cure: people used the plant to treat a painful finger sore called a 'whitlow'. Because the plant was linked to that sore, the name puts whitlow (the sore) and grass (a small plant) together, which is why today the tiny white‑flowered weed is called whitlow grass.
