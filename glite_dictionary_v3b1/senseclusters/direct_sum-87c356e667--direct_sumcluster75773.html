Etymology

The term direct sum gives a simple picture: take one item from each of two separate boxes and join them side by side; the joining is the sum and it is done in a direct way. So a direct sum in math means 'to add' two separate sets by pairing one item from each so every new item comes from both parts, not from mixing them.
