Etymology

The phrase mere trumpery comes from street sellers who offered shiny, cheap goods meant to fool buyers; trumpery were showy trinkets that looked valuable but were useless, and mere means 'only' or 'just'. That's why mere trumpery is used for things that are only showy and 'worthless'.
