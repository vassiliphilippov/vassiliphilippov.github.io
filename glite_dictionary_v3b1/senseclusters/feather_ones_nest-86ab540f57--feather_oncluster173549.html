Etymology

feather one's nest may come from the picture of a bird gathering soft feathers to line its nest and make a warm, comfortable home. So when someone 'feathers their nest' they quietly add money or benefits to make their life nicer, which is why the phrase means 'to use your job or position to get money or benefits for yourself'.
