Etymology

language system comes from the image of a machine where each part must work together: language is the words and sounds people use, and system is a group of parts that work as one. So, when those parts follow the 'rules' and fit together, the whole 'set of parts and rules' makes a working language, which is why language system means 'the set of parts and rules that make up a language'.
