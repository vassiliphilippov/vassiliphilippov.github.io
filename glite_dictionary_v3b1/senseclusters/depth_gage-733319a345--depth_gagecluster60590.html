Etymology

The noun depth gage gives a clear picture: a thin rod is pushed into a hole until it touches the bottom and a scale on the tool shows how far down it goes. So depth means how deep something is and the gage is the tool that 'measures' it, which is why the phrase means 'a tool for measuring depth'.
