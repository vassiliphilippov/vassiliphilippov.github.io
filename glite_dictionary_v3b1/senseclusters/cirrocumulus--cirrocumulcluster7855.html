Etymology

The word cirrocumulus comes from two Latin parts: cirro- meaning 'curl' or 'high' and -cumulus meaning 'heap' or 'pile'. So cirrocumulus clouds are high, small patches that look like little curled heaps or ripples in the sky, which explains the name.
