Etymology

Circular plane comes from the tool's curved bottom: circular points to the round shape and plane is the hand tool that smooths wood. Because the bottom is curved like part of a circle, the tool can 'follow' and 'shape' inward or outward curves in wood, so the name tells you what the tool does.
