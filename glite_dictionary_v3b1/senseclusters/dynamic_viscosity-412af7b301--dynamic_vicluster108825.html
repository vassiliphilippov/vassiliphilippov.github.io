Etymology

The term dynamic viscosity starts with viscosity, a word from the idea of 'stickiness' in a liquid, and adds dynamic, a word about movement or force. Think of stirring honey: because it is sticky you must push harder, so dynamic viscosity measures how much a fluid resists motion when you push it, that is, its 'resistance to flow'.
