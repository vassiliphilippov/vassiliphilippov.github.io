Etymology

The word nightmarishness begins with nightmare, which came from an old idea that a 'mare' was an 'evil spirit' that could sit on people and give them bad dreams. The endings -ish ('like') and -ness ('state or quality') turn it into a noun, so nightmarishness means the 'state' of being like a nightmare — very frightening or deeply unpleasant.
