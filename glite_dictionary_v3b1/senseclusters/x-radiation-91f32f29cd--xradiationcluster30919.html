Etymology

X-radiation starts with X, the letter used in math for something 'unknown', because scientists first met a new kind of ray they could not yet explain. The radiation part means 'rays' or 'shining energy', like the verb radiate, so X-radiation literally means the 'unknown rays' now used to make pictures of the inside of the body.
