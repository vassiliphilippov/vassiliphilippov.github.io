Etymology

Pure hopium comes from a joke that mixes hope and opium, imagining a made-up drug that gives a high, addictive feeling of hope. With pure meaning 'not mixed', the image is someone on only that hope, so the phrase means strong, 'unrealistic' optimism that ignores the facts.
