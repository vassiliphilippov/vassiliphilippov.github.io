Etymology

disorderly conduct comes from court language that joins the idea of disorderly as 'not following order' and conduct as 'behavior'. Imagine a noisy crowd shouting, fighting, or blocking a street, and that image of public chaos shows why it means 'behavior that disturbs public order'.
