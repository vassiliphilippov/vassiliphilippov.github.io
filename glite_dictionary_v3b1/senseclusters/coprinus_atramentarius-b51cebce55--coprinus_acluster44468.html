Etymology

Coprinus atramentarius is often called the 'inky cap' because its cap melts into a black, inky liquid when the spores mature. The two-name form Coprinusatramentarius helps you remember this: 'atramentarius' is an old word for 'ink', so the name points to the mushroom becoming 'inky'.
