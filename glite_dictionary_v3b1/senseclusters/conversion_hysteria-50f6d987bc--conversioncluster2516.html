Etymology

The term conversion hysteria comes from the idea that strong feelings are turned into body problems, where conversion means 'to change into' and hysteria was an old word for extreme emotional reaction. People imagined inner distress becoming real symptoms like numbness or paralysis, so the phrase means emotional trouble showing up as physical symptoms with no clear medical cause.
