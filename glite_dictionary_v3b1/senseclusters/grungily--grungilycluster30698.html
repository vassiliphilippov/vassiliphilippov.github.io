Etymology

The word grungily comes from grunge, a word that originally meant 'dirt' or 'grime' and later described a rough music and fashion style that looked messy and worn. Think of grime — real dirt — as a memory hook: to act grungily is to do something in a 'dirty', 'shabby', or run-down way.
