Etymology

banyan tree is famous for its strange growth: long roots hang down from the branches and take root in the ground, so one tree becomes many trunks and a wide, shady canopy. That's why the banyan tree is often thought of as 'one tree that spreads like a small forest' or 'a big shady place'.
