Etymology

Global Positioning System gets its name from a simple idea: many satellites around the world send exact time signals to a receiver, and the receiver works out its exact place by comparing those signals. The words Global, Positioning, and System point to a 'world-wide' way to find your 'position', so the name literally says it helps you find where you are on the globe.
