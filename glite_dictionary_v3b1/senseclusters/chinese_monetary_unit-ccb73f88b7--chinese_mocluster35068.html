Etymology

Chinese monetary unit is a simple name for the main money used in China. The parts Chinese, monetary, and unit mean 'China', 'money', and 'one amount'. It usually refers to the 'yuan' also called the 'renminbi', and because 'yuan' once meant 'round' from old round coins and 'renminbi' means 'people's money', the phrase points to China's basic unit of money.
