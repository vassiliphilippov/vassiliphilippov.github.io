Etymology

Lützen names the place of a decisive 1632 battle in the Thirty Years' War where the Swedish king Gustavus Adolphus was killed. A helpful way to remember Lützen is to link it with the idea of a 'loss'—so the name brings to mind a costly, decisive fight.
