Etymology

common carline thistle is the name for a spiky plant with a flat, yellowish flower head, and the word carline is an old name people used for this kind of thistle. Because people long ago used the dry flower heads as charms or simple medicines, the image of a spiky, sun-like head helps us remember this plant that grows on dry, chalky hills.
