Etymology

calabash pipe comes from the use of a dried, hollow calabash gourd as the bowl of the pipe, with a curved stem attached to the gourd. That's why calabash pipe means 'a smoking pipe with a big gourd bowl and a curved stem', often giving a mellow smoke.
