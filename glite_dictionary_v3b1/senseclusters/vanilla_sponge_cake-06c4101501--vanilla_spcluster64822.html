Etymology

vanilla sponge cake is named for what you taste and how it feels. The word vanilla points to the sweet vanilla flavor from the bean, and sponge shows the cake is light and soft with many small holes like a kitchen sponge, so the name tells you it is 'a light, airy cake' flavored with vanilla.
