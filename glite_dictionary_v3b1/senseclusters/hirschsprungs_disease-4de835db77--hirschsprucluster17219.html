Etymology

Hirschsprung's disease gets its name from the Danish doctor Harald Hirschsprung, who first described this bowel problem in newborns. He saw that a part of the bowel had no nerve cells and could not push out stool, so the condition was 'named after' him and today the name refers to that birth‑condition that causes a blockage.
