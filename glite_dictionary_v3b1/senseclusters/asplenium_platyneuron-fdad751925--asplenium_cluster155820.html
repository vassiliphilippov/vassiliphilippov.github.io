Etymology

Asplenium platyneuron comes from old Greek words that give a simple picture of the plant. The first part, Asplenium, was linked to the 'spleen' because people long ago used some ferns as medicine, and platyneuron means 'broad nerve', which points to the fern's clear central stalk and veins, so the name helps you picture a narrow green fern with a shiny black stalk.
