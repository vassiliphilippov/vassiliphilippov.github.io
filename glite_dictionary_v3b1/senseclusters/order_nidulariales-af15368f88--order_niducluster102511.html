Etymology

order Nidulariales comes from a Latin word meaning 'little nest' because these fungi make tiny cup-like fruiting bodies that look like small 'bird's nests' holding little 'eggs'. That's why the name refers to the group of fungi known as 'bird's nest fungi'.
