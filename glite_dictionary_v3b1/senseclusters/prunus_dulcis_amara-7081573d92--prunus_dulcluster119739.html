Etymology

Prunus dulcis amara gets its name from old Latin words. Prunus names the almond tree, dulcis means 'sweet' and amara means 'bitter'. The name gives the picture of an almond tree with sweet flowers but bitter, poisonous nuts, which explains why people only use its oil after careful processing.
