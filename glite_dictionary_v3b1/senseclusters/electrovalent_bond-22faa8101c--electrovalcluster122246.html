Etymology

The term electrovalent bond comes from electro, meaning 'electric', and valent, meaning 'power to join'. Picture one atom giving an electron to another; one becomes positive and the other negative and they stick together by electric attraction, so the name means 'a bond formed when one atom transfers an electron to another and they are held together by electric attraction'.
