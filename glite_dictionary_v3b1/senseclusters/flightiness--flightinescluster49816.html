Etymology

The word flightiness comes from flight (from 'to fly') and the ending -iness that makes a word meaning a 'quality'. Imagine a bird that suddenly takes flight and flies away; flightiness is like that — being quick to change your mind or act on sudden ideas instead of staying steady.
