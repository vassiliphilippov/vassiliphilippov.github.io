Etymology

Fragaria chiloensis comes from Latin and a place name: Fragaria is the Latin word for 'strawberry' and chiloensis means 'from Chiloé', an island in Chile where the wild plant grows. So the name literally says 'strawberry from Chiloé', which helps explain why this wild species is a source of the garden strawberries we eat today.
