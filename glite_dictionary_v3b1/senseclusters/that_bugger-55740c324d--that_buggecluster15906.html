Etymology

that bugger is an informal, slightly rude name for someone who annoys you; one easy way to remember it is to think of the small pest 'bug' that bothers you, so a bugger feels like 'the person who bugs you'. That's why people use that bugger to mean 'an annoying or troublesome person'.
