Etymology

scenic artist: The noun scenic artist comes from the words scenic (from the 'scene' on stage) and artist. Theatre painters used to make large painted backgrounds and stage settings, so a scenic artist is literally an artist who 'paints or designs scenery', which is why today it means someone who makes scenery for stage, film, or TV.
