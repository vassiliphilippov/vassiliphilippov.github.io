Etymology

Beaufort scale is named after Sir Francis Beaufort, a sea officer who made a simple number system that linked what sailors saw on the sea and on a ship's sails to how strong the wind was. That clear picture of numbers and sea conditions is why the term means 'a way to measure wind strength'.
