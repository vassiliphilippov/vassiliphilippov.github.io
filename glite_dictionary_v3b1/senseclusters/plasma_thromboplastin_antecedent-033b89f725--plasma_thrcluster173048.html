Etymology

The name plasma thromboplastin antecedent can help you remember what it does: plasma is the liquid part of blood, thromboplastin points to making a clot, and antecedent means 'before'. So it is a blood protein that works early to help form a clot, and if it is low a person can have too much bleeding.
