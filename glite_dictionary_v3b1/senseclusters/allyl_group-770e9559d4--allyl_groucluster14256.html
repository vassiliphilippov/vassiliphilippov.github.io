Etymology

The noun allyl group may come from an old word for garlic, because early chemists found these kinds of chemical pieces in garlic and onions. The phrase gives a clear picture: allyl is a three-carbon 'reactive piece' that attaches to a larger molecule and group shows it is that attached part, so allyl group means that reactive bit on a molecule.
