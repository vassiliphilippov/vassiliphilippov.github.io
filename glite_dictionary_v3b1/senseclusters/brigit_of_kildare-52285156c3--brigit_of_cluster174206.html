Etymology

Brigit of Kildare comes from a real early Irish woman, Brigit, who led a religious community at Kildare and became famous for her kindness and stories of little miracles. Because of this she became Ireland's 'patron saint', so the name today refers to that beloved saint and to churches and celebrations in her honor.
