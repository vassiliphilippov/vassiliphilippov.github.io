Etymology

The word glucocorticoid breaks into parts: gluco- meaning 'sugar', cortic- from cortex meaning 'outer layer', and -oid meaning 'like'. So a glucocorticoid is a hormone made in the outer part of a gland that helps control blood sugar and reduce swelling.
