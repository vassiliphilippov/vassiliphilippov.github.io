Etymology

The word Chlamydosaurus comes from Greek parts chlamyd- 'cloak' and -saurus 'lizard'. So it literally means a 'cloak-lizard' because this animal has a big frill that opens like a cloak around its neck, and -saurus is the same 'lizard' you see in dinosaur.
