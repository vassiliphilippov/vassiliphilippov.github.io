Etymology

caduceus comes from the Greek word kērykeion, which means 'herald's staff' or the stick a 'messenger' carries, and it belonged to the messenger god Hermes, so it is pictured with two snakes and wings. That's why the caduceus looks like a winged staff with two snakes and is often used as a medical symbol, even though the true healing sign has only one snake.
