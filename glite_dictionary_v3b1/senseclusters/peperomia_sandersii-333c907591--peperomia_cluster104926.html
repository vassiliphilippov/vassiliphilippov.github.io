Etymology

Peperomia sandersii probably comes from a person named Sanders. The first word Peperomia is the small pepper-like plant group and the second part sandersii means 'Sanders's plant', so imagine a gardener called Sanders holding the silvery, thick leaves to remember this houseplant.
