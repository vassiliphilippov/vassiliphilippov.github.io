Etymology

Tweedledum and Tweedledee comes from a children's book by Lewis Carroll, where two nearly identical characters, Tweedledum and Tweedledee, act like mirror images of each other. Because they look and behave the same, the phrase now means 'two people who are very similar' or 'hard to tell apart'.
