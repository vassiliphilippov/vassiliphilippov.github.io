Etymology

hyperalimentation begins with hyper- meaning 'over' or 'too much', as in hyperactive, and ends with aliment meaning 'food' or 'to feed'. So hyperalimentation is literally giving extra or full 'food' directly into the body, often into a vein when someone cannot eat.
