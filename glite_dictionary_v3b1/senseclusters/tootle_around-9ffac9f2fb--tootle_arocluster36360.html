Etymology

tootle around may come from tootle, the soft 'toot' sound of a small horn when someone drove or rode slowly and wandered about. So the picture of a little car or bike making light 'toot' sounds while moving without hurry explains why it means 'to move or travel in a slow and relaxed way'.
