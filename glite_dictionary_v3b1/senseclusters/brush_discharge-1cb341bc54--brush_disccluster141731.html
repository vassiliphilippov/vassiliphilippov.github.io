Etymology

brush discharge comes from the way electric sparks spread out like the bristles of a brush, so the bright threads look like hairs from a brush. The word discharge means the 'flow' of electricity, so the term describes a spark or 'glow' that forms many thin, brush-like streaks between high-voltage points.
