Etymology

beauty parlor comes from the simple idea of a small shop or room where people go to get their hair and face made beautiful. The words beauty and parlor show a place for treatments like hair, skin, and makeup, so it means 'a shop where hairdressers and beauticians provide hair, skin, and cosmetic treatments'.
