Etymology

The phrasal verb settle upon comes from the simple image of something coming to rest on top of another thing, like dust or a bird that settlesupon a branch or table. When people 'settle upon' a choice, the idea is that the decision has landed and now stays, so it means 'to decide' or 'agree' after thinking.
