Etymology

cryptogenic comes from the Greek parts crypto- meaning 'hidden' and -genic meaning 'origin' or 'born'. Like cryptic ('hidden') and genesis ('beginning'), cryptogenic describes something whose origin is 'hidden' or unknown.
