Etymology

Brassica oleracea gemmifera is a descriptive plant name: the parts Brassica and oleracea show it is like a small cabbage, and gemmifera means 'bud-bearing', referring to the many tiny bud-like heads on the stem. That's why the name points to the little cabbage heads we eat as Brussels sprouts.
