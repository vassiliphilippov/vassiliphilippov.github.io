Etymology

The noun thirty-second part comes from the simple idea of dividing a whole into thirty-two equal pieces. Imagine cutting a pie into thirty-two thin slices: one tiny slice is a thirty-secondpart, so the phrase means 'one of thirty-two equal parts'.
