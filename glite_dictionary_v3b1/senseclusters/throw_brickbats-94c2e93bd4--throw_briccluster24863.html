Etymology

throw brickbats comes from the image of angry people literally throwing broken pieces of brick at speakers or public figures to show dislike, because a brickbat was a small, sharp piece of brick used like a weapon. That's why today throw brickbats means 'to criticize or attack someone harshly'.
