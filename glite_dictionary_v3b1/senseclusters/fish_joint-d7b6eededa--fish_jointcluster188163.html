Etymology

fish joint comes from a simple image: early metal plates used to bolt two rails looked a bit like a flat 'fish', so workers called the plate a fish and the place where it joined the rails a joint. So fish joint now means the spot where two rails or beams are bolted together 'to join them end to end'.
