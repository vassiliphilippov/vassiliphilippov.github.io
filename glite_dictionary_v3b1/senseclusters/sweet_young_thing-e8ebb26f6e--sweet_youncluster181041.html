Etymology

sweet young thing began as a simple image: sweet meant pleasant or gentle, young showed youth, and thing could just mean a person, so it painted a picture of a pleasant, young person. That's why today it means 'an attractive and youthful person' and can sound caring or like someone is 'talking down to' them.
