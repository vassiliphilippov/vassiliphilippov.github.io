Etymology

Lactuca serriola comes from Latin words that describe the plant. Lactuca comes from the word for 'milk' because the plant makes a white, milky sap, and serriola means 'small saw' because its leaves have saw-like edges, so the name describes a milk-sap plant with saw-edged leaves.
