Etymology

battle fatigue comes from a simple image: the words battle and fatigue mean fighting and great tiredness, so soldiers worn down by long fighting lost strength in body and mind. That's why the phrase now means serious 'mental' and 'emotional' problems soldiers get after fighting for a long time.
