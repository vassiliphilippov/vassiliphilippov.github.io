Etymology

The word amphigory comes from the French amphigouri and probably from Greek parts amphi- meaning 'around', seen in amphibian and amphitheatre, and -gory meaning 'speech' or 'talk'. So an amphigory is like 'mixed-up talk' or nonsense writing, which is why the word now means a playful or meaningless piece of writing.
