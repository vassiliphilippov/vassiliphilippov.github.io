Etymology

The word peculiarly comes from peculiar, which comes from the Latin word peculium meaning 'private property' and is linked to pecu- for 'cattle' because animals were once a sign of wealth. Because it first meant something 'belonging to one person' and therefore different from others, peculiarly can mean 'in a strange or unusual way'.
