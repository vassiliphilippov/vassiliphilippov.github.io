Etymology

Cantharellus cibarius may come from old words meaning 'little cup' and 'food', with Cantharellus pointing to the cup shape and cibarius to being edible. These yellow mushrooms really look like small golden cups and have a pleasant apricot-like smell, so the name fits the tasty mushroom people cook with.
