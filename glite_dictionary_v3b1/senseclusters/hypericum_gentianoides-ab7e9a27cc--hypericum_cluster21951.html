Etymology

Hypericum gentianoides comes from two parts: Hypericum, a name for St John's-wort, and gentianoides, meaning 'gentian-like', so the name gives a simple picture of a small St John's-wort that looks like a gentian and matches this tiny yellow-flowered plant.
