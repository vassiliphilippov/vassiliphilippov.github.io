Etymology

The term heating efficiency comes from the literal idea of heating, making a place warm, and efficiency, meaning how much energy is used rather than wasted. Imagine a heater like a kettle: if most energy warms the room it has high heating efficiency, but if much escapes it is low, so the term means how well a system turns energy into 'usable heat'.
