Etymology

Menticirrhus littoralis may come from old Latin words: mentum meaning 'chin' and cirrhus meaning 'small whisker', while littoralis means 'of the shore'. So the name gives a picture of a small shore fish with a chin whisker and helps you remember it is a silvery whiting that lives near the Gulf and southern Atlantic coasts.
