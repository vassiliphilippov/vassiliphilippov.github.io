Etymology

The noun Reconstruction Period comes from the idea of rebuilding after the Civil War: reconstruction means 'to build again' and period means 'a time'. After the war, leaders worked to rebuild the southern states and bring them back into the United States, so the phrase refers to those years of rebuilding and rejoining.
