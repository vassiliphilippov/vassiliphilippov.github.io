Etymology

Afghani language may come from using Afghani as a simple label meaning 'from Afghanistan' and then adding language. Because Afghanistan has more than one main language, 'Pashto' and 'Dari', people started to call either one 'Afghani language' as a quick but not very precise name, so today it usually means one of those languages in everyday speech.
