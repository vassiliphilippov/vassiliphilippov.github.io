Etymology

Allamanda cathartica may be named after a plant collector called Allamand, while cathartica comes from a word meaning 'cleansing' because people once used this plant as a strong medicine to make the body empty. So the name links the person who studied it and its old medicinal use, which helps you remember this fast-growing, golden-flowered climbing shrub.
