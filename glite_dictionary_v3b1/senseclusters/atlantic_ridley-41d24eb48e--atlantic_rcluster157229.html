Etymology

Atlantic ridley combines a place and an old turtle name. Atlantic tells you the ocean where it lives and ridley is a traditional short name for this small, grey sea turtle (seen in 'Kemp's ridley'), so the name simply means a small sea turtle from the Atlantic.
