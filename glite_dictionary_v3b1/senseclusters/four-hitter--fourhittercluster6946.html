Etymology

four-hitter is a baseball word made from four and hitter, and it means a game where the pitcher allows only 'four' hits by the other team. Think of it like a no-hitter or a 'one-hitter', where the number tells how many hits were allowed, which is why four-hitter describes a game with just four hits.
