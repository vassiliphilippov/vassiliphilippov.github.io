Etymology

bentonitic comes from bentonite, a kind of clay named after the town Benton in Montana where it was first found, so imagine a clay from Benton that 'soaks up water' and swells. So, something bentonitic is 'of or like bentonite', a very absorbent clay often used to seal and block water.
