Etymology

hyperbilirubinemia of the newborn is the medical name for when a new baby has too much bilirubin, a yellow substance, in the blood. New babies' livers are often not ready to remove this substance, so they can look 'yellow', and that is why the name refers to this common, usually mild condition in newborns.
