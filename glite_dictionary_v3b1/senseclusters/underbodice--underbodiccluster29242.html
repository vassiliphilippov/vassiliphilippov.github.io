Etymology

underbodice is a simple compound of under and bodice, where under means 'below' and bodice comes from a word for the 'body' and means a close-fitting top. So an underbodice is a short, sleeveless layer worn 'below' the bodice or clothes as an extra undergarment for comfort and modesty.
