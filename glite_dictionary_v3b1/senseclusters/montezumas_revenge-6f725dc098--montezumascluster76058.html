Etymology

Montezuma's revenge comes from a story about Montezuma, an old ruler in Mexico. One popular story says his spirit takes revenge on visitors who eat new or unsafe food, so the phrase now jokingly means 'diarrhea' or 'stomach trouble' that travelers get.
