Etymology

Book of Ezekiel is named for the prophet Ezekiel, who had vivid visions—like the famous valley of dry bones—about the 'fall' and later 'return' of Jerusalem. So the Book of Ezekiel is the part of the Old Testament that collects his 'prophecies'.
