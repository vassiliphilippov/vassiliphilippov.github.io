Etymology

The dessert Bavarian cream may come from Bavaria, a region in Germany; one popular story says cooks long ago mixed a smooth cream custard with gelatin so it became firm and chilled it in a mold. So Bavarian cream means 'a rich, chilled custard dessert'.
