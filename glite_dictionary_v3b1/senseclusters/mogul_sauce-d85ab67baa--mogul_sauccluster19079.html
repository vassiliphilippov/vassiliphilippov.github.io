Etymology

The noun mogul sauce may come from the name mogul, used for the Mughal rulers of India, who were known for rich, spicy cooking. English makers used Indian spices to make a dark sauce, so the name stuck and today mogul sauce means 'a dark, spiced sauce served with meat'.
