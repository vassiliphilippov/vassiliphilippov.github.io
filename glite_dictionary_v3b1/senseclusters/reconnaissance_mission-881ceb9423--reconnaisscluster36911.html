Etymology

Reconnaissance mission comes from soldiers or scouts who went ahead of the main group to look at the area and the enemy, then tell others what they saw. The word reconnaissance is the act of looking and mission is a trip with a purpose, so it now means 'a mission to gather information about the enemy'.
