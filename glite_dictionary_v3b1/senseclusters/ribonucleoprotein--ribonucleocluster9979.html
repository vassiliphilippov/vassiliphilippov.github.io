Etymology

ribonucleoprotein is made of parts that mean 'RNA' and 'protein': ribo- and nucleo- point to the RNA or the cell's 'nucleus', and protein is the same word we use for body 'protein'. So, a ribonucleoprotein is a molecule made of RNA and protein found in cells or viruses.
