Etymology

Viburnum opulus is the scientific name for the 'guelder rose', a shrub with flat groups of white flowers and bright red berries that stand out in autumn. The name parts Viburnum and opulus may come from old Latin words for this kind of shrub, so picturing the white flower groups and the bright red berries gives an easy memory hook for the name.
