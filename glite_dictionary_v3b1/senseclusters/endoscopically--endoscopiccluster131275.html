Etymology

endoscopically comes from the Greek parts endo- 'inside' and -scope 'to look', like the parts in telescope and microscope. It means 'to look into hidden or small spaces with a long thin camera or tool', so an engineer who checks pipes endoscopically inspects their insides without taking the whole thing apart.
