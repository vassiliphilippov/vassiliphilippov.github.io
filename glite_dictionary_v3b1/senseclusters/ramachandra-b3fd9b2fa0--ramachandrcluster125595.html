Etymology

Ramachandra is a name made of Rama and Chandra, where Chandra means 'moon'. So Ramachandra can be remembered as 'Rama the moon' — a shining, delightful hero — which fits his role as the virtuous, celebrated figure of the Ramayana and an incarnation of Vishnu.
