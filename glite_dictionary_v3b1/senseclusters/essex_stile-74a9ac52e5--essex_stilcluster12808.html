Etymology

Essex stile may come from the area of Essex, where nightclubs and TV often showed people with big hair, heavy makeup and lots of sparkle. The image of that loud, shiny look, the stile people copied, is why Essex stile means 'a bold, glamorous, sometimes flashy way of dressing'.
