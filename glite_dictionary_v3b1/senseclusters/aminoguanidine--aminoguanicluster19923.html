Etymology

aminoguanidine combines amino- and guanidine, with the amino- part like in ammonia meaning 'with nitrogen', and guanidine coming from 'guano', 'bird droppings', where related substances were first found. So the name tells chemists it has those parts, which is why it is a useful chemical for making other compounds in synthesis.
