Etymology

The term tunica conjunctiva palpebrarum comes from Latin words that describe its job: tunica means 'cover', conjunctiva means 'joined together', and palpebrarum means 'of the eyelids'. So the phrase paints the image of a thin 'cover' joined to the inside of the eyelids, and that's why it names the soft lining that protects the eyelid surface and helps keep the eye moist.
