Etymology

Battle of Panipat comes from a huge fight near the town of Panipat in 1761, when the Afghan leader Ahmad Shah Durrani defeated the Marathas. The strong picture of armies meeting at Panipat and the Marathas' great 'defeat' explains why the name marks a major 'turning point' in Indian history.
