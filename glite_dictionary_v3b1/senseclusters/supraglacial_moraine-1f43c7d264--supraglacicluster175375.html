Etymology

supraglacial moraine shows a simple picture: a line or small mound of rocks, soil and plant bits sitting on the top of a glacier. The part supraglacial means 'above' or 'on' the ice and moraine is a heap of debris, so the phrase names the debris that fell onto or was carried across the glacier surface.
