Etymology

The bird called rock pigeon got its name from where it lives: on cliffs and rocky places, so rock refers to its home and pigeon names the bird. It is the wild bird that most 'domestic' or city pigeons come from, which is why many park pigeons are descendants of the rock pigeon.
