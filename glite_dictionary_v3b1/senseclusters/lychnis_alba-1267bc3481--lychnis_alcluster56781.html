Etymology

Lychnis alba may come from an old word Lychnis meaning 'lamp' and the Latin word alba meaning 'white', because its pale flowers open in the evening and the puffed cup under each flower looks like a tiny lantern. So the name helps you remember it is a white, night-opening plant.
