Etymology

minor scale: The musical term minor scale comes from calling some notes minor (literally 'smaller' or 'lower') than in a major scale, so the important notes are lower. Those lower notes give a darker, more 'sad' sound, which is why the minor scale is used to make music feel sad or serious.
