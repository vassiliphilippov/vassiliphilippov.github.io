Etymology

itsy-bitsy comes from playful baby talk that makes a small word sound cuter by repeating it; the familiar little word bit (a tiny piece) became bitsy, and saying it twice makes the idea feel even smaller. So itsy-bitsy means 'very small' or 'tiny' and is used to make something sound extra small.
