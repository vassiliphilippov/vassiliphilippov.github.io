Etymology

polka probably comes from Czech. One popular story says it comes from the Czech word půlka meaning 'half', because the dance uses a quick 'half-step', and another says it was named after a 'Polish woman' who helped make it famous. That's why polka means a lively Central European dance and the music for that dance.
