Etymology

mercury-in-glass clinical thermometer names a medical thermometer that has liquid mercury inside a thin glass tube. When you put it under the tongue or in the armpit, the heat makes the mercury move up the tube to show the 'body temperature', so the name tells you it uses mercury and glass to 'measure body temperature'.
