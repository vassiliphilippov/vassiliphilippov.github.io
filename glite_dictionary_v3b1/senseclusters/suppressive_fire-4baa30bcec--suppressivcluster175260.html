Etymology

suppressive fire comes from the simple image of guns firing constantly at an enemy so the enemy keeps their heads down and cannot move. The word suppressive means 'keep down' and fire means shooting, so the phrase came to mean shooting to 'stop' or 'limit' the enemy's actions.
