Etymology

minor diatonic scale gets its name from its parts: diatonic means a scale of seven notes that move step by step, and minor is an old word for 'smaller' that points to smaller steps between some notes. In this scale the step between the 2nd and 3rd notes and between the 5th and 6th notes is smaller, and that smaller step gives the scale its 'minor' or 'sad' sound, which is why musicians often use it for sad music.
