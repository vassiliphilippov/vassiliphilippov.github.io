Etymology

The expression mere pabulum comes from Latin: pabulum meant 'food' or 'fodder' and mere meant 'just' or 'only', so imagine being fed plain animal feed instead of real, nourishing food. That's why mere pabulum now describes writing or speech that is shallow, bland, or without real substance.
