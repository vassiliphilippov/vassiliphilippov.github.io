Etymology

intra-arterial comes from the parts intra- meaning 'within' and arterial from 'artery', the blood vessel that carries blood from the heart. So intra-arterial describes something that happens 'inside an artery', which is why doctors use it for treatments or measurements placed directly in that vessel.
