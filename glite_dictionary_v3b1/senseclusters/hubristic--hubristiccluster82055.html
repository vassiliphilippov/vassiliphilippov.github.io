Etymology

Hubristic comes from the Greek word hubris, which means 'excessive pride' or 'arrogance' and was used for people who acted as if they were above the gods. So, to call someone hubristic is to say they have too much pride or self-confidence, and that this pride can lead them to take risky or foolish actions.
