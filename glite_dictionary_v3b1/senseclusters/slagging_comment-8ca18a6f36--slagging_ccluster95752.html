Etymology

slagging comment comes from the slang word slag, which means 'to criticize or insult', and comment meaning a remark. The word slag also named the waste left after making metal, so calling someone 'slag' is like calling them 'rubbish'. That's why a slagging comment is a remark meant to criticize or insult.
