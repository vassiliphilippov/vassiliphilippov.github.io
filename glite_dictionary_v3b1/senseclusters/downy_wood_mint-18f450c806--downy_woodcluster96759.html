Etymology

The compound name downy wood mint describes the plant: downy means 'soft hairs', wood shows it grows 'in the woods', and mint tells you it is a mint plant with a 'minty smell'. So the name gives a clear picture of a soft, hairy herb with mint-scented flowers near trees.
