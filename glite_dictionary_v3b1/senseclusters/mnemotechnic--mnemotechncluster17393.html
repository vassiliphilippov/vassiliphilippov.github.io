Etymology

The word mnemotechnic comes from the Greek parts mneme ('memory') and technē ('art' or 'skill'), and you can see the same idea in the word mnemonic and in technique. So a mnemotechnic method is like a 'memory art', a skill or tool that helps you remember things.
