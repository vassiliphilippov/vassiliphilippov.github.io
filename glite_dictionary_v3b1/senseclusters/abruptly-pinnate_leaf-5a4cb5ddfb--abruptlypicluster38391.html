Etymology

abruptly-pinnate leaf gives a clear picture: a feather-like pinnateleaf that stops suddenly and ends with a 'pair' of small leaflets instead of a single 'tip'. So the name helps you remember it means a leaf that finishes 'abruptly' at the end with two leaflets, not one.
