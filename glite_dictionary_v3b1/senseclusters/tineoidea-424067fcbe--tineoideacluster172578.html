Etymology

Tineoidea comes from the old Latin tinea, meaning 'moth' or a small worm that eats cloth, and the ending -oidea, like the -oid in humanoid, meaning 'like' or 'form'. So, Tineoidea names a group of moths, including clothes moths, that are 'moth-like' and often eat fabrics.
