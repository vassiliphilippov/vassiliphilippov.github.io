Etymology

Wintera colorata comes from Latin naming: the word colorata means 'colored' and points to the plant's red and yellow blotches on its leaves, and Wintera is the name given by plant scientists. So the full name makes a simple picture — a New Zealand shrub with brightly marked leaves.
