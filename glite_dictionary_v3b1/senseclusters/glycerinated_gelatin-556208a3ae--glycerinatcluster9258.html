Etymology

glycerinated gelatin is named for its parts: gelatin, a soft jelly, and glycerin, which keeps it moist and smooth. Because this mix makes a soft jelly that melts or softens at body temperature, it became the common base for ointments and suppositories, so the name means 'a jelly-like base used in medicines'.
