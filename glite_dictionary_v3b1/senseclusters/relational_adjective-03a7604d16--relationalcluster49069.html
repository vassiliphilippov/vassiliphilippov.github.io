Etymology

The term relational adjective comes from grammar and gives a simple image: adjective is like a label, and relational means the label shows a relation or 'type' of the noun rather than a quality. So relational adjectives like chemical in chemical reaction tell the kind of reaction, and that is why the term means an adjective that shows or limits the noun's type.
