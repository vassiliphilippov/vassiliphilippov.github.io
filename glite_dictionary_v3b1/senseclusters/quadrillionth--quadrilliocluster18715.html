Etymology

quadrillionth comes from quadri- 'four' joined to the large-number family like million, naming the number 'one quadrillion'. As a noun, a quadrillionth is 'one part of a quadrillion', like a 'millionth' but much smaller — one out of a quadrillion equal parts.
