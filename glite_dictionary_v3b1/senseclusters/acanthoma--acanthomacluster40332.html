Etymology

The word acanthoma comes from Greek parts acanth- meaning 'thorn' and -oma meaning 'tumor' or 'swelling'. Think of a 'thorn-like' bump on the skin — that image helps remember that an acanthoma is usually a non-cancerous skin tumor.
