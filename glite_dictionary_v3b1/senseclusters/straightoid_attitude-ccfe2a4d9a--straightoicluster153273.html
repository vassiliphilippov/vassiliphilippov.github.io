Etymology

straightoid attitude comes from joining straight ('heterosexual') with the ending -oid, which means 'like' or 'similar to', creating the image of being 'like' straight. So it describes a way of thinking that favors heterosexual ideas and often ignores or leaves out other sexual orientations.
