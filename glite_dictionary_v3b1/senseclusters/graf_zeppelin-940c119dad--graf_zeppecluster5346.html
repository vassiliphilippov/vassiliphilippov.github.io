Etymology

Graf Zeppelin was named to honor Count Ferdinand von Zeppelin; Graf is the German word for 'count' and Zeppelin was his last name because he built the first large airships. That's why Graf Zeppelin refers to a famous large German airship known for long passenger journeys.
