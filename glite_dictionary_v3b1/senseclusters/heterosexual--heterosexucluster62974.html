Etymology

heterosexual comes from Greek parts hetero- 'different' and -sexual 'relating to sex', the same hetero- you see in heterogeneous which means 'different kinds'. So when we use heterosexual as an adjective it describes someone who is sexually attracted to the 'opposite' sex.
