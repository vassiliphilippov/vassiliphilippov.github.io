Etymology

Sloppiness comes from the word slop, meaning 'wet, messy liquid', and people used the idea of a 'mess' for feelings that are too much. A helpful way to remember this sense is to imagine emotions so 'runny' and overdone they make a mess, so sloppiness can mean excessive or not sincere feeling.
