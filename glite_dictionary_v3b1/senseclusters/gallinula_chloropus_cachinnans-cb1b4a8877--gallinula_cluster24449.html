Etymology

Gallinula chloropus cachinnans comes from old Latin and Greek words that describe the bird: Gallinula means 'little hen', chloropus means 'green foot', and cachinnans may come from a word meaning 'laughing' because of the bird's loud calls. So the name points to a small, hen-like water bird with greenish legs and a noisy call, which helps remember this North American subspecies.
