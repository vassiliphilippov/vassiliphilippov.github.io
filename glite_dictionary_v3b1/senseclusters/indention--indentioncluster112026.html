Etymology

indention comes from indent, which is made of in- ('in') and dent ('tooth' or 'notch'), because people once cut tooth-like edges in paper so two parts would fit together. So an indention is the small blank 'notch' at the start of a line, like a tiny visual 'dent' that shows a new paragraph.
