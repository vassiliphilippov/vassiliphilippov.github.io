Etymology

Lathyrus hirsutus comes from old words: Lathyrus refers to a kind of 'pea' and hirsutus means 'hairy', because the plant's stems and leaves have small hairs. That's why the name fits — it is a pea-family plant with a weak, hairy stem often grown as a cover or pasture crop.
