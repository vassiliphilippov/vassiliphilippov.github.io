Etymology

Pseudotsuga menziesii comes from two parts: Pseudotsuga means 'false hemlock' because its short needles look like a hemlock but it is a different tree, and menziesii is named for the man Menzies who first collected it. So the name points to the tree we call the 'Douglas fir' today and helps you remember a tall evergreen that looks like a hemlock but is its own species.
