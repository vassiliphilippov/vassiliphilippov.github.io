Etymology

Echeneis naucrates comes from old names: Echeneis meant 'ship-stopper' and naucrates meant 'ship-ruler', because people once believed the small remora could hold on to a ship and 'stop' it. Today the name simply refers to the little fish that often rides attached to sharks and other large sea animals, so the old idea of 'holding on' explains the modern meaning.
