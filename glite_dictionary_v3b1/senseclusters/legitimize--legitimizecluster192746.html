Etymology

legitimize comes from the old Latin word legitimus, which is built on lex meaning 'law' and the ending -ize meaning 'make', and you can see the same idea in the simple word legal. So, to legitimize something is to 'make' it 'lawful' or officially acceptable, which is why the word means to make something legal.
