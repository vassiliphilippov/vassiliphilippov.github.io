Etymology

X-ray machine comes from the name X-ray, where the 'X' showed these rays were first thought to be 'unknown', and machine means a device that makes them. These devices send invisible X-ray rays through objects or bodies so people can 'see inside', which is why the name means a machine that makes X rays for imaging.
