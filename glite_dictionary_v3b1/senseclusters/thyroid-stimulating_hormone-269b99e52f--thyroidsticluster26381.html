Etymology

thyroid-stimulating hormone is a name for a small chemical messenger from a gland at the base of the brain that tells the thyroid to make more hormones. The word stimulating shows its job: it 'wakes up' or pushes the thyroid into action, and hormone means a body messenger, so the phrase literally describes what it does.
