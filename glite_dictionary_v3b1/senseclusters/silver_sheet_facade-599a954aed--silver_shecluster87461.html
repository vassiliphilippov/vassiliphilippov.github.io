Etymology

silver sheet facade comes from the simple image of a building wrapped in thin, shiny metal: the silver look, the thin sheet, and the facade as the building's 'front'. So it now means the building's outer surface made of reflective silver-colored panels, used for a modern look and to reflect sunlight.
