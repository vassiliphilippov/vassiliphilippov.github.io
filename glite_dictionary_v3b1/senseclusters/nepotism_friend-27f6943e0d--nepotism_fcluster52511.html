Etymology

nepotism friend comes from the old word nepotism, which began when powerful people gave jobs to their 'nephews' or other family members. The image is of a friend getting a job because of that family link, not because of skill, so it now means 'a person who benefits from special treatment because of family ties or a close connection'.
