Entoloma comes from Greek parts ento- meaning 'within' and -loma meaning 'edge' or 'fringe'. A helpful way to remember it is to picture a mushroom with a rolled or inner edge—many Entoloma species show that feature, so the name points to a shape that helps mushroom experts identify them.
