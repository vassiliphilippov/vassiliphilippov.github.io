Etymology

olio meaning a miscellaneous mixture comes from the Spanish word olla 'pot' and the dish olla podrida ('rotten pot'), a stew of many different ingredients; from that image of mixed things, olio began to mean any 'mixture' or collection. So when you call something an olio, you are saying it is a mixed pot of different items.
