Etymology

Solar halo comes from a simple picture: tiny ice crystals high in the sky bend the sun's light into a bright round 'ring of light' around the sun. That's why the word solar means 'sun' and halo means 'circle of light', so the whole phrase refers to that bright ring you can see 'around the sun'.
