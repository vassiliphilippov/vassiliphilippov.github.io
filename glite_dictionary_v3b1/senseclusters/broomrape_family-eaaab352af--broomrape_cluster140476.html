Etymology

broomrape family may come from an old name for a plant that grew on broom bushes plus a word like rape that once meant 'to steal'. So the name gives a clear image: members of the broomrape family do not make their own food but 'live on other plants' and 'steal' water and food, which is why it refers to those parasitic, leafless herbs.
