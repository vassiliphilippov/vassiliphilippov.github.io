Etymology

Acacia melanoxylon gets its name from the tree group Acacia and the word melanoxylon, which comes from old Greek words meaning 'black wood'. Picture a tall Australian acacia with very dark, hard wood used for furniture and musical instruments, and you can see why the name points to the tree and its valuable wood.
