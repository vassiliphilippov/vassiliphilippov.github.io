Etymology

The word polythalamia comes from poly- meaning 'many' and thalam- meaning 'room' or 'chamber', and you can see poly- in polygon ('many sides') and thalam- in thalamus, which is an 'inner chamber' of the brain. So polythalamia means having 'many chambers', which fits the shell structure of some tiny organisms.
