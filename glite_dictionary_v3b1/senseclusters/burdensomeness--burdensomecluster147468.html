Etymology

burdensomeness builds on the common word burden, which means 'a load' or something hard to carry, plus the ending -some meaning 'full of', as in troublesome, and -ness meaning 'a state'. So, burdensomeness is the state of being full of burden, which explains why it describes something that causes hardship or is difficult to bear.
