Etymology

hydroperoxide breaks into hydro- meaning 'water' and peroxide, which points to 'oxygen' in an extra form. You can see hydro- in familiar words like hydrogen or hydroelectric. So, a hydroperoxide is a chemical that has a small 'water' part, a hydrogen and an oxygen, together with extra oxygen, which explains why these compounds can form in oils and react easily.
