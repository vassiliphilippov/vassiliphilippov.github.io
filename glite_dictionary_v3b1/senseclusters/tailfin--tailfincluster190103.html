Etymology

tailfin comes from the parts tail and fin, where tail means 'the back part' and fin means 'a flat part like a fish fin'. Car tailfins were small fins added to the back of cars as a decorative feature, copied from airplane and jet designs to make cars look modern in the 1950s.
