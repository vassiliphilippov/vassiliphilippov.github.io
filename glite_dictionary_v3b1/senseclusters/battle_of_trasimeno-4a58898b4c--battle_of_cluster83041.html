Etymology

battle of Trasimeno comes from a real fight in 217 BC near Lake Trasimeno where Hannibal hid his soldiers on the hills and surprised the Roman army in the fog. That sudden 'ambush' caused a great 'defeat' for Rome, so the name now refers to that famous loss.
