Etymology

henequen comes from the Spanish word henequén and before that from a Yucatec Maya name for an agave plant in Yucatán, Mexico. People grow this plant for its long leaves because they give a strong 'fiber' used to make 'rope' and 'twine', so the plant itself became known as henequen.
