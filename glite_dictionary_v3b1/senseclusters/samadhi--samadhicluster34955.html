Etymology

samadhi is also used in South Asia for a shrine or tomb and comes from the same Sanskrit parts sam- 'together' and -adhi 'to put or hold'. Think of it as the place where a teacher is 'put to rest', which is why samadhi can mean the memorial built over a saint's burial.
