Etymology

inadvertency comes from the parts in- 'not' and a root meaning 'to turn the mind to', the same idea you see in advertise, which tries to turn people's attention to something. So inadvertency literally means 'not turning your mind to' something, and that's why it means being careless or failing to do what is needed.
