Etymology

Jeremy Bentham was an English thinker in the late 1700s who said we should judge laws and actions by how much happiness they bring, an idea called 'utilitarianism'. Think of him as a man who wanted to put pleasure and pain on a simple scale to measure what is best, and that's why his name is linked to the idea of making the most happiness for the most people.
