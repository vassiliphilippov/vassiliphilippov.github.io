Etymology

musculus rhomboideus major is a Latin name that gives a clear picture: musculus means 'muscle', rhomboideus means 'diamond-shaped', and major means 'larger'. The name points to a big, diamond-shaped muscle in the upper back that pulls the shoulder blade toward the spine, so the name fits its job.
