Etymology

Chateaubriand is the name of a French writer, and cooks named a thick, center cut of beef after him as a special roast. A helpful way to remember it is to think of chateau, 'big house', because the name sounds grand, and that helps you recall that a Chateaubriand is a large, tender steak often served for two.
