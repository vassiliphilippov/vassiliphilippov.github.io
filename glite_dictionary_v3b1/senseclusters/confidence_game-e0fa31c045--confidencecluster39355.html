Etymology

The noun confidence game comes from a simple image: a trickster first wins your confidence ('to trust' them) by being friendly or helpful, and then uses that trust to steal or 'to cheat' you. That's why a confidence game now means a dishonest plan that tricks people by gaining their trust.
