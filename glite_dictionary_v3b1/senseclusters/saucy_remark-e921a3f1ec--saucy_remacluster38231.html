Etymology

saucy remark may come from thinking of saucy as like 'sauce' that gives food a sharp, spicy taste and remark as a comment. So a 'saucy remark' is a comment that is cheeky or 'boldly disrespectful', because it adds a sharp, surprising edge like sauce adds flavor.
