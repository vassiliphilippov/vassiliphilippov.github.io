Etymology

phosphorylase starts with phosphoryl, a part related to phosphorus (a name linked to 'light'), and ends with -ase, which means 'enzyme'. A phosphorylase adds or moves a phosphate group so the cell can break down molecules, so the name tells you it is an enzyme that works with phosphate.
