Etymology

salamander comes from the old word salamandra, used for a creature people believed could 'live in fire'. Because people sometimes found real amphibians in wet logs thrown on fires, the animal kept that fiery name, even though real salamanders are soft, wet animals that live on land and return to water to breed.
