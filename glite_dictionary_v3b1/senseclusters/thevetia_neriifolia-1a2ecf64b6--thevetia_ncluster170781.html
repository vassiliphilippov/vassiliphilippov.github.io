Etymology

Thevetia neriifolia may come from the name Thevetia, after a 1500s French explorer André Thevet, and the Latin neriifolia, meaning 'leaves like Nerium' the oleander. So the name points to a shrub with shiny, oleander-like leaves and bright flowers, which helps you remember it is a tropical plant whose parts are very poisonous.
