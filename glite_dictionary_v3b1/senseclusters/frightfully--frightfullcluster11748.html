Etymology

The word frightfully comes from fright ('fear' or 'a sudden scare') plus the adverb ending -ly, so at first it meant 'in a frightening way'. Over time, especially in British English, people began to use it as a strong word like 'very', so now frightfully often just means 'very' or 'extremely'.
