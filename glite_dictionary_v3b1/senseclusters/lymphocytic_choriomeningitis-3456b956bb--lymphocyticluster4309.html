Etymology

lymphocytic choriomeningitis gets its name from its parts: lymphocytic means many white blood cells called lymphocytes, and choriomeningitis names swelling of the thin coverings around the brain, the 'meninges'. So the full name describes an infection, often from house mice, that causes swelling of those brain coverings with many lymphocytes, or white blood cells, which is why it refers to this viral illness.
