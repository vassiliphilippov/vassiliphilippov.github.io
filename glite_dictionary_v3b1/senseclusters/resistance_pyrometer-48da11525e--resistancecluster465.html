Etymology

The noun resistance pyrometer comes from how the tool measures heat: a pyrometer is a device for very high temperatures and it reads a thin wire's changing resistance as the wire gets hot. So the name literally means 'a temperature meter that uses resistance', which explains why it 'measures high temperatures'.
