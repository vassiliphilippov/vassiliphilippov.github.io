Etymology

French ultramarine blue comes from the 1800s when French chemists learned to make the rich blue of the rare stone lapis lazuli in a factory. Because this new blue was made in France it became known as Frenchultramarine, and today the name means 'a deep blue pigment made in a factory as a cheaper alternative to the natural ultramarine'.
