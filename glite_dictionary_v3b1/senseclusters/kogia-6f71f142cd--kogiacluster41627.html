Etymology

Kogia is a scientific genus name for a group of small sperm whales that live in deep oceans. A helpful way to remember Kogia is to imagine a tiny, secretive whale that can release a dark, ink-like cloud when scared, so the name stands for a 'small deep-sea sperm whale' that hides itself.
