Etymology

guided missile cruiser combines two clear images: a cruiser is a large warship that travels long distances, and a guided missile is a rocket that can steer itself to a target. So a guided missile cruiser is a big warship that carries missiles that can be guided, which is why it means 'a large ship armed with guided missiles'.
