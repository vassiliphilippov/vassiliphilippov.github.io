Etymology

The noun ceremonial gong comes from the simple image of a large metal disc, the gong, being struck during a ceremonial event to 'announce' the start or a change. The loud, clear sound 'marks' the moment, so a ceremonial gong means an instrument used to draw attention to important moments or 'transitions'.
