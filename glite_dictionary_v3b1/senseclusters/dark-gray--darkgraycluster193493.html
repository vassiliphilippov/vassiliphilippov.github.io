Etymology

dark-gray is simply two words joined: dark meaning 'little light' and gray meaning 'the color between black and white'. So dark-gray means a deeper, less shiny shade of gray, closer to black, which is why we call a suit, car, or cloud dark-gray when it looks heavier or more serious.
