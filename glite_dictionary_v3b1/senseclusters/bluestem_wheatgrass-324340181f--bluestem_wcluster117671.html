Etymology

bluestem wheatgrass: The name bluestem wheatgrass comes from the plant's look: its stems are often blue-green (bluestem) and its leaves look like young wheat (wheatgrass). Because people found it good for 'grazing' and for 'protecting the soil', the name now refers to this useful forage grass.
