Etymology

Erechtites hieracifolia may come from old words meaning 'hawk-like leaves' — the part hieraci links to hawkweed and -folia means 'leaves', so the name paints a picture of the plant's leaf shape. Its common name 'American burnweed' comes from how it quickly grows in burned or disturbed ground, so the name fits its habit of rapidly taking over empty soil.
