Etymology

The word smugness comes from the adjective smug, which used to describe someone 'neat and comfortable' and then came to mean a person quietly pleased with themselves. The ending -ness, as in happiness, turns an adjective into a quality, so smugness is the state of being 'too satisfied with yourself'.
