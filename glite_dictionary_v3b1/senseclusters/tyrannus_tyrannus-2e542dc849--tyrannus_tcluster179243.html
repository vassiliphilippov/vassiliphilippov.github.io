Etymology

Tyrannus tyrannus comes from the Latin word tyrannus, which means 'tyrant'. Because these kingbirds are very bold and chase other birds away from their nests, the name gives a clear picture of a small bird acting like a 'tyrant', and that is why the name fits their behavior.
