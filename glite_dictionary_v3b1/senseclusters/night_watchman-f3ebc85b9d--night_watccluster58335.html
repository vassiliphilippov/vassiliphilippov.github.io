Etymology

Night watchman may come from old towns where a man walked the streets at night with a lantern, looking for fires or thieves. The words night and watchman give the clear picture of someone who 'watches' at night to 'guard' a place, so today the phrase means 'a man whose job is to guard a building or area at night'.
