Etymology

Aberdonian comes from the city name Aberdeen plus the ending -ian, which means 'from' or 'belonging to', like in Canadian. The place name Aberdeen begins with aber, an old word for a 'river mouth', so Aberdonian simply means someone or something 'from' that river mouth city in northeast Scotland.
