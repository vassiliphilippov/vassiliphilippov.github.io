Etymology

Rosa chinensis is a plant name made from simple Latin words: Rosa means 'rose' and chinensis means 'from China'. Because this shrub really came from China and helped make many modern garden roses, the name tells us it is a Chinese rose and an ancestor of today's garden varieties.
