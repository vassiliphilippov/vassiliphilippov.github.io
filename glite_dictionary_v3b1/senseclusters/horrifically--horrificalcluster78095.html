Etymology

Horrifically has also stretched from 'very frightening' to a strong way to say 'very' or 'extremely' in everyday speech, so people say things like 'horrifically expensive' to mean 'very expensive'. That's why horrifically can be used both for real horror and just to show a very large amount.
