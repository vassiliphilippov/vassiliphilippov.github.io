Etymology

Just sus comes from the slang sus, short for 'suspicious', used in online games where players point at someone who looks guilty or acts odd. With just meaning 'only', saying just sus is a quick way to say someone or something is 'only suspicious', not proven guilty, which is why it means 'simply suspicious'.
