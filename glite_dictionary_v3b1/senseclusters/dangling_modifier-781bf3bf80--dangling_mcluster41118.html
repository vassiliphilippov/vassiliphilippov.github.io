Etymology

dangling modifier comes from the picture of a small tag that is dangling and not clearly attached to the word it should 'modify', the modifier. Imagine a name tag hanging from the wrong coat so you do not know who it belongs to. That's why a dangling modifier means a word or phrase that seems to change the wrong thing and can 'confuse' the reader.
