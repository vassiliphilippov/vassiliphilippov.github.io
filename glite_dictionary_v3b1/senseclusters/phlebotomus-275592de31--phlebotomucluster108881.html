Etymology

Phlebotomus comes from Greek parts phlebo- 'vein' and -tome 'to cut', so it means 'vein cutter'. Think of a tiny fly that 'cuts' the skin to reach blood, which helps you remember why the name fits these small, bloodsucking sand flies.
