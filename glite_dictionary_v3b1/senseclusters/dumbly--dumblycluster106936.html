Etymology

dumbly comes from the old word dumb, which meant 'silent' or 'unable to speak'. Later dumb also came to be used as 'stupid'—perhaps because being speechless looked like not understanding—so to do something dumbly can mean to do it in a 'stupid' or unthinking way.
