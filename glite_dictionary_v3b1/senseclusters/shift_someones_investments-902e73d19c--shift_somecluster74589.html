Etymology

The phrase shift someone's investments comes from the image of moving boxes on a shelf: shift means to move and investments are where you put your money. So when you shift someone's investments you move their money from one kind of investment to another to lower risk or try to earn more, which is why it means 'to move money from one investment to another'.
