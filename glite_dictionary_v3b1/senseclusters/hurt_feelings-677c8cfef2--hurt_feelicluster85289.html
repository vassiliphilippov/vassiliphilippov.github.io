Etymology

The phrase hurt feelings may come from using hurt (like body pain) for emotions: imagine someone steps on your foot and it hurts, so when words or actions 'step on' your emotions they 'hurt' your feelings. That's why hurt feelings means 'emotional pain or being upset because of someone's words or actions'.
