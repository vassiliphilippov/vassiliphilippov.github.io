Etymology

Pedilanthus bracteatus gets its name from older Greek and Latin words: Pedilanthus means 'slipper flower' because the small blooms look like tiny slippers, and bracteatus means 'with bracts', the leaf-like parts that sit under the flowers. So the name helps you picture the waxy shrub with its slipper-shaped blooms.
