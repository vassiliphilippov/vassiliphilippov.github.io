Etymology

The word scornfully comes from scorn, which meant 'open dislike' or 'mock', and the ending -fully, meaning 'full of' or 'with'. A helpful way to remember it is to imagine someone turning up their nose and laughing; scornfully means acting with strong dislike and no respect.
