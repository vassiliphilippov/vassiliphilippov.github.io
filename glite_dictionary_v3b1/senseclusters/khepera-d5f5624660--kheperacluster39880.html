Etymology

The word Khepera comes from an ancient Egyptian word kheper meaning 'to become' or 'to come into being', and it was linked to the scarab beetle that seemed to roll the sun into the sky each morning. So Khepera is the god of the morning sun and of new life, because his name and the beetle image both show the idea of things being born or made each day.
