Etymology

sizzlingly comes from the sound word sizzle and the ending -ly, which makes it tell how something happens. The root sizzle copies the hot frying sound, like hiss or fizz, so people use it for things that are very hot or very exciting, meaning 'in a very hot or exciting way'.
