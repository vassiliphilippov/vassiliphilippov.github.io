Etymology

Sphaeralcea fasciculata may come from old Greek and Latin words, with Sphaer meaning 'round' and fasciculata meaning 'small bundles'. That's why the name points to the shrub's round flower parts and the way its many mauve flowers grow in tight clusters, so the words help you picture the plant.
