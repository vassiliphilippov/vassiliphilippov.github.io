Etymology

stuffed egg shows a simple food picture: a hard-boiled egg is cut in half and the yellow yolk is mixed with mayonnaise and spices. That yolk mix is put back into the white, so the egg is stuffed, which is why the name means a halved egg 'filled' with the seasoned yolk.
