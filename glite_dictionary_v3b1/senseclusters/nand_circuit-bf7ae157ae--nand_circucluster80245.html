Etymology

NAND circuit comes from joining NOT and AND to show it is the opposite of an AND. Think of two switches in a row: an AND lets power flow only when both are on, while a NAND is the opposite — it is 'false' only when both inputs are 'true', so the name reminds you it means 'not-and'.
