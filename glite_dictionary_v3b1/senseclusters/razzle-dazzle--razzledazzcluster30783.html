Etymology

razzle-dazzle comes from two playful parts: razzle (like 'razz', to tease or fool) and dazzle ('to blind or impress'). A helpful way to remember it is to picture a bright, showy move that both impresses the crowd and confuses the opponent, which is why razzle-dazzle means a flashy, confusing display.
