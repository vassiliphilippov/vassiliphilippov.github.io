Etymology

choppily comes from the simple verb chop, which means 'to cut', and from the adjective choppy, meaning 'full of short, rough movements or breaks'. So, to do something choppily is to do it in short, uneven, broken parts—like vegetables cut into different sizes or a boat moving over rough water.
