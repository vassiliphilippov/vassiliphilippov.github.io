Etymology

Haverhill fever is named after the town of Haverhill, Massachusetts, where a large outbreak happened when people drank milk that had a rat germ. Because the sick people had a high 'fever', rash, and joint pain, doctors began to call this rat-linked illness Haverhill fever, so today the name means that specific form of rat-bite fever.
