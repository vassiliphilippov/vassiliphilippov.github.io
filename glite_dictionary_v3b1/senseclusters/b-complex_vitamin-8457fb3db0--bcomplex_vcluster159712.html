Etymology

B-complex vitamin comes from early scientists who first called these nutrients vitamin B and thought there was just 'one vitamin', but they later found many different B vitamins together and used complex to mean a group. That's why B-complex vitamin today means the whole group of B vitamins that help the body make energy.
