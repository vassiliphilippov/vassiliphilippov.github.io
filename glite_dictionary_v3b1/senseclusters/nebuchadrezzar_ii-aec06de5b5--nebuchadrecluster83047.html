Etymology

Nebuchadrezzar II is a name from old Babylon that includes the god Nabu, and one popular meaning is 'Nabu, protect my son'. So the name was a royal prayer and now it names the powerful king who destroyed Jerusalem and sent the Israelites away to Babylonia.
