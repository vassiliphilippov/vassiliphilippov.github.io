Etymology

sensory faculty comes from the words sensory (about the body's senses, like sight or hearing) and faculty (an old word for a natural 'ability'). So it paints a picture of each sense as a built-in power, and that's why we use sensory faculty to mean an ability such as sight, hearing, or touch.
