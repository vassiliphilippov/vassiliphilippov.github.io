Etymology

sarcomatoid comes from sarcoma, which in turn comes from the old Greek sarx meaning 'flesh', plus the ending -oid meaning 'like', as in humanoid or asteroid. So sarcomatoid simply means 'like a sarcoma' — that is, tissue that looks like a tumor of the 'flesh', which is why doctors use it for cells that appear sarcoma-like.
