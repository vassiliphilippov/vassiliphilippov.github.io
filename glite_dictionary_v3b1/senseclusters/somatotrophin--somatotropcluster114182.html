Etymology

somatotrophin breaks into somato- meaning 'body', as in somatic, and -trophin meaning 'to feed' or 'nourish', as seen in atrophy where tissue wastes when it is not fed. So, somatotrophin is like a 'body-feeding' hormone, a chemical that helps the body grow.
