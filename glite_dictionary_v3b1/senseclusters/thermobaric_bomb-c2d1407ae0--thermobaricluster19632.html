Etymology

thermobaric bomb gets its name from the parts thermo, which means 'heat', and baric, which means 'pressure'. It spreads a cloud of fuel and then burns it, making a very hot blast and a strong pressure wave, so the name reminds us it causes both 'heat' and 'pressure'.
