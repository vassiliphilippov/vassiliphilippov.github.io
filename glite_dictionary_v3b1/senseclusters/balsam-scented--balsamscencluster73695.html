Etymology

The word balsam-scented starts with balsam, an old word for a sweet, sticky sap from fragrant trees used as a 'balm' or 'perfume', and the second part, scented, means 'having a smell'. So balsam-scented simply means 'smelling like balsam', a warm, soothing tree scent.
