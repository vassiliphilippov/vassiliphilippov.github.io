Etymology

potential difference comes from a simple picture: potential is like stored energy and difference is how much more one point has than another. Imagine water high on a hill and low below. The height gap makes water flow, and in an electric circuit the 'difference' gives a 'push' measured in 'volts' that makes 'current' move.
