Etymology

Karen haircut may come from internet jokes about a woman called 'Karen' who often asks to 'speak to the manager'; the short, angled haircut shown in many of those jokes became the clear image people use. So, the phrase now refers both to that hairstyle and to a woman acting entitled or demanding.
