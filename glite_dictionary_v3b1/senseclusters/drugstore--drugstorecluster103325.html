Etymology

drugstore comes from the two parts drug ('medicine') and store ('shop' or 'place that keeps things'). A helpful way to remember it is that 'drug' long ago was used for goods and medicines, so a drugstore is a shop where you can get medicines and many everyday items like cards, snacks, or shampoo.
