Etymology

The slang act like an asshat may come from the silly image of a person wearing their ass as a hat, a rude, absurd picture that makes someone look foolish and unpleasant. That's why the phrase is used to say someone is 'behaving in a foolish, obnoxious, or contemptible manner'.
