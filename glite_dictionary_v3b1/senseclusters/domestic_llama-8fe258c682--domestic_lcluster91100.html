Etymology

domestic llama comes from a local mountain language word 'lama' that Spanish speakers turned into llama. People in the high mountains long ago tamed these animals to carry loads and give wool, so calling them domestic shows they are the 'tamed' farm animals used as 'pack animals' and for wool.
