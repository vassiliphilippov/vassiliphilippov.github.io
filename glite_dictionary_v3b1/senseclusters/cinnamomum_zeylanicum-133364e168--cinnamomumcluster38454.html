Etymology

Cinnamomum zeylanicum is a scientific name that tells a simple story. Cinnamomum is the old word for the cinnamon tree and zeylanicum means 'from Ceylon', the old name for Sri Lanka, so the full name literally labels the cinnamon tree of Sri Lanka.
