Etymology

Cola acuminata makes big brown kola nuts that people in West Africa chewed to feel more awake because the nuts contain caffeine. Soft drink makers once used extracts from those nuts to flavor early 'cola drinks', so the tree's name became the source of the modern word 'cola' and the idea of a caffeinated drink.
