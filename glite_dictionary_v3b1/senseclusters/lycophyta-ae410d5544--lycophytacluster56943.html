Etymology

Lycophyta comes from Greek parts lyco- meaning 'wolf' and -phyta meaning 'plants', so it literally says 'wolf-plants'. These ancient club mosses have very small, scale-like leaves and make spores, so imagining a small 'wolf-plant' helps you remember this old group of leafed plants.
