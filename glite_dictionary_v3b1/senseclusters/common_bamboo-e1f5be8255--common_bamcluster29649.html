Etymology

common bamboo names a bamboo people saw often: common means 'seen often' and bamboo is the tall, fast-growing plant with thin green-and-yellow stems. Because this plant grows quickly and was used for fences, building and privacy screens, the phrase came to mean the species that is widely grown and useful.
