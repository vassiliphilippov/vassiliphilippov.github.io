Etymology

scuffle (tool) probably gets its name from the idea of pushing or dragging a blade through the soil, and it is like the word shuffle, which means to move by dragging. Because you push or 'shuffle' the tool across the ground to loosen weeds, it became known as a 'scuffle'.
