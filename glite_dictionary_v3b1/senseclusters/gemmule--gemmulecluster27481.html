Etymology

gemmule comes from the Latin gemma meaning 'bud' or 'jewel' and the small ending -ule, so it literally means 'little bud'. Darwin used gemmule for tiny imagined particles that carry heredity, so thinking of them as many small 'buds' makes it easy to see why he chose this name.
