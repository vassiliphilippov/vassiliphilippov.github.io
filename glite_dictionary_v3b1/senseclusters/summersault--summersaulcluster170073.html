Etymology

summersault looks like the season 'summer', but the parts tell a different story: the ending sault comes from an old word for 'leap', and the same idea is in assault, which once meant 'to leap on' someone. So a summersault is literally an 'over-leap', a full turn that sends your feet over your head.
