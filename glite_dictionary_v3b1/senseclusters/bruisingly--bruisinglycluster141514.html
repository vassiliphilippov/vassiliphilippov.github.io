Etymology

The word bruisingly comes from the simple verb bruise, which originally carried the idea 'to crush' or 'to hurt' and so came to mean hitting or damaging something strongly. So if something is done bruisingly, it is done in a way that causes real physical or emotional pain, like a hard blow or a very harsh criticism.
