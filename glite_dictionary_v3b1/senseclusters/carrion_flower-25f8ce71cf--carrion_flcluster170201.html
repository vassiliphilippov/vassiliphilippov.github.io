Etymology

carrion flower has large, star-shaped flowers that smell like carrion, that is 'rotting meat'. The strong smell tricks flies into visiting and carrying pollen to other plants, so the name shows how the flower uses a 'rotting meat' smell to attract insects and make seeds.
