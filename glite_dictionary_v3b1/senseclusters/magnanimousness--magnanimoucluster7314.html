Etymology

magnanimousness comes from the Latin parts magnus ('great') and animus ('soul' or 'spirit'), literally a 'great spirit'. You can see magnus in magnify and animus in animate, and that 'great spirit' image helps, so magnanimousness means a generous, noble heart, especially toward someone less powerful.
