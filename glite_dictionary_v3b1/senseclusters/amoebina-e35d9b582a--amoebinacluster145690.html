Etymology

Amoebina comes from amoeba, a word that means 'change' or 'changeable', plus the ending -ina which marks it as a group name. So Amoebina is a group of tiny single-celled creatures that change shape and move with 'false feet', and the name reminds you of their shifting bodies.
