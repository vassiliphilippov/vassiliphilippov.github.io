Etymology

coronary bypass surgery comes from a simple image: when a heart's blood vessel (the coronary) is blocked, doctors make a new path or 'bypass' using a healthy vessel so blood can flow past the blockage. So, coronary bypass surgery means 'an operation to restore blood flow to the heart'.
