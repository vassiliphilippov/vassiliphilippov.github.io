Etymology

kaleidoscopic comes from kaleidoscope, a tube that shows constantly changing, beautiful shapes, and that word was made from Greek parts kalos 'beautiful', eidos 'shape', and -scope 'to look'. So, kaleidoscopic means full of constantly changing, beautiful patterns.
