Etymology

Gomphotheriidae comes from the Greek parts gomphos ('nail' or 'peg') and therion ('beast'), with -idae showing an animal family. The name gives a clear image of their nail-like, peg-shaped tusks, so Gomphotheriidae are the extinct elephant-like 'beasts' known for big tusks and shovel-like lower jaws.
