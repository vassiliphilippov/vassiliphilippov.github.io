Etymology

ready-to-wear comes from a simple idea: clothes that are already 'ready to wear', and the fashion world borrowed the French phrase prêt-à-porter which means 'ready to wear'. Think of clothes on a shop rack that you can buy and wear right away. So ready-to-wear as a noun means clothing made in standard sizes and sold ready for people to wear, not made to order.
