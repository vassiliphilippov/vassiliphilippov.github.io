Etymology

Bidens coronata comes from old Latin words: bi- meaning 'two', dens meaning 'tooth', and coronata meaning 'crowned'. Imagine the plant: its seeds often have two small 'teeth' or hooks that catch on fur, and the round yellow flower looks like a little 'crown', so the name points to the plant's seed shape and flower today.
