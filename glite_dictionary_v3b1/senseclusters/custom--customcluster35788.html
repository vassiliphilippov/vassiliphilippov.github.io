custom

2/m3.4
The habit of regularly buying from the same shop
noun US / ˈkʌstəm /

Etymology

custom meaning regular business or patronage comes from the idea of a habit or what people are 'used' to doing, and it is related to the word customer; so a shop's custom is the steady trade from people who usually buy there.
