Etymology

sprigger probably comes from the simple word sprig, which means 'small branch' or 'stem'. A helpful way to remember it is that a sprigger strips those small stems from tobacco leaves and ties the leaves into bundles, so the name comes from working with 'sprigs' of the plant.
