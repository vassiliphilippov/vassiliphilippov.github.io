Etymology

soft and ploppy may come from the sound and feel of something soft that makes a wet 'plop' when you press or drop it, like ripe fruit or wet clay. The word soft shows the gentle feel and ploppy comes from the 'plop' sound, so the phrase means 'soft and squishy, a bit wet'.
