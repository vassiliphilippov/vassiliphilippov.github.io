Etymology

Chimaeridae comes from the Greek Chimaira, the same root as the English chimera, which means a 'mixed creature' from myth. Scientists used this name for the family because these strange, deep-sea fishes have odd, ghost-like features, so the name helps explain why they are sometimes called ghost sharks.
