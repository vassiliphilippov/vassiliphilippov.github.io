Etymology

King Ferdinand refers to Ferdinand II of Aragon, the king who married Isabella and helped join Castile and Aragon into a single Spain. He backed Columbus's 1492 voyage and helped start the Spanish Inquisition, so King Ferdinand usually means that 'ruler' from the late 1400s who helped create a united Spain.
