Etymology

Aztec marigold comes from the bright orange marigolds the Aztec people in Mexico used in festivals and ceremonies. Because the flowers were seen as guides for the dead and symbols of the 'sun' and 'life', people grew them for special events, so the name now refers to the large, showy plant people grow to decorate gardens.
