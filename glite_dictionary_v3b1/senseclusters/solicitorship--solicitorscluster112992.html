Etymology

solicitorship comes from solicitor, a word that once meant someone who 'asks' or 'urges' for others and today also means a kind of 'lawyer', plus the ending -ship, which shows an 'office' or 'position' like in friendship. So solicitorship is the 'office' or 'position' of a solicitor.
