Etymology

Senna alexandrina, with Senna and alexandrina pointing to the plant and its link to Alexandria, comes from a shrub whose dried leaves long ago were brewed as a strong 'laxative' tea that helped people empty their bowels. So, the name now brings to mind a plant used 'to relieve constipation'.
