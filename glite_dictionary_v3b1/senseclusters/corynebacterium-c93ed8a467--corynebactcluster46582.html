Etymology

Corynebacterium comes from the parts koryne, meaning 'club', and bacterium, meaning 'rod' or 'staff', and the second part is the same root as in bacteria. That's a helpful picture: these are bacteria that look like club-shaped rods under a microscope, and some kinds can cause disease such as diphtheria.
