Etymology

destroyer escort: The name destroyer escort comes from the words destroyer and escort, where a destroyer is a warship and to escort means to go with and protect. In World War II these ships acted like a 'bodyguard' for convoys, keeping them safe from submarines, so the name now means 'a small warship used to protect fleets or convoys'.
