Etymology

Haply comes from the old word hap, which means 'chance' or 'luck', plus the ending -ly meaning 'in a way'. A helpful way to remember it is that haply means 'by luck' or 'perhaps', and it is related to happy, which once meant 'lucky', so the origin explains why haply now means 'maybe' or 'by chance'.
