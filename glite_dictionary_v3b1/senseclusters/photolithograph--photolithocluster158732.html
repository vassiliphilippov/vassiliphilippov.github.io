Etymology

photolithograph combines photo- meaning 'light' with litho- meaning 'stone' and -graph meaning 'to write', just like in photograph and lithograph. A photolithograph is a print made by using light (a photographic process) to prepare a stone or plate for printing, so the name helps you remember it is a 'light-made' stone print.
