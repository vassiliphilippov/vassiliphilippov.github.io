Etymology

Palestinian Arab is a compound name made from the words Palestinian, from the land Palestine, and Arab, meaning people who speak Arabic and share Arab culture. It points to someone from Palestine with Arab family origins, so it means 'a person who comes from the Arab population that originally lived in Palestine'.
