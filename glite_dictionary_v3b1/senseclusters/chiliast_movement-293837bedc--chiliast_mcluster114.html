Etymology

chiliast movement may come from the word chiliast, which comes from a word meaning 'thousand', so early followers pictured a literal 'thousand'-year 'golden age' of peace. That's why chiliast movement refers to a religious group that expects a coming thousand-year time of peace and goodness.
