Etymology

ciliated protozoan is a compound name for a 'single-celled animal' that has tiny hairlike parts called cilia. The word ciliated points to the tiny hairs and protozoan points to the 'single-celled animal', so the name helps you remember that this tiny creature uses its hairs to move and eat.
