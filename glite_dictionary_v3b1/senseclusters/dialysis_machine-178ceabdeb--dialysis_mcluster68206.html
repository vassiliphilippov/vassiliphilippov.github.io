Etymology

The noun dialysis machine comes from dialysis, a word for 'filtering' blood, and machine, meaning a device that does the work. One helpful picture is blood flowing through a large coffee filter that keeps the good parts and lets waste pass away, so the dialysis machine 'cleans' the blood for people whose kidneys do not work.
