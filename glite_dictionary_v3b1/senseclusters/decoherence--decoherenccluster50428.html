Etymology

decoherence comes from the prefix de- meaning 'remove' or 'not' and coherence, which is about parts 'sticking together' and is the same idea as cohere. So, decoherence is when things stop 'sticking together', and in quantum physics this makes a system lose its connected, quantum behavior and act like ordinary objects.
