Etymology

ghoulishness comes from the Arabic word ghūl, which meant 'a monster that eats the dead' and later became the English word ghoul. So, because a ghoul is linked to graves and dark places, ghoulishness describes an eerie, grotesque quality that feels like a haunted scene.
