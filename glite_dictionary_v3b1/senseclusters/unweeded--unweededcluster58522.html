Etymology

unweeded is made of un- 'not' and weed 'a wild plant', so it originally meant 'not cleared of weeds'. Think of a garden left full of weeds where it is hard to find the good plants — in the same way an unweeded report or database still has unwanted items, which is why we use unweeded for collections not cleared of unnecessary things.
