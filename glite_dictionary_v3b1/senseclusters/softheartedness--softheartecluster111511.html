Etymology

Softheartedness comes from soft ('gentle'), heart ('feelings' or 'the center of feeling'), and the ending -ness ('quality'). So, softheartedness is the 'quality of having a gentle heart', which is why it means being kind and easily moved to feel sorry for others.
