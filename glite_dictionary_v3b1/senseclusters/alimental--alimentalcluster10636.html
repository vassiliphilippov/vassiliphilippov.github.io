Etymology

alimental comes from the Latin word alimentum, which means 'food' or 'nourishment', and you can see the same root in alimentary, meaning 'relating to food'. So alimental describes something that gives 'nourishment' or helps feed the body, which is why we use it for helpful foods, grains, or soups.
