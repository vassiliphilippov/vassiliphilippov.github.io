Etymology

Psetta maxima is the Latin name for the fish 'turbot' and may come from old words for a flat fish: Psetta names the flat, round fish and maxima means 'the biggest'. So the name gives a clear picture of a very large flat fish, which is why it refers to the large brownish 'turbot' eaten as food.
