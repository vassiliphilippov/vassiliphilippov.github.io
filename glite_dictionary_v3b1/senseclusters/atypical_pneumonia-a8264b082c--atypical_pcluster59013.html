Etymology

atypical pneumonia comes from doctors noticing lung infections that did not look like the usual kind and often had different germs or symptoms. Because these cases were called 'not typical', doctors used the name atypical pneumonia to show it is an unusual kind of pneumonia that may need different treatment.
