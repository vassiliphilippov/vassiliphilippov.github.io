Etymology

cholesterosis cutis comes from the parts cholesterosis (from the word 'cholesterol') and cutis (Latin for 'skin'). The name gives a clear image: cholesterol building up in the skin and making yellow bumps, so today it means a skin condition with cholesterol deposits.
