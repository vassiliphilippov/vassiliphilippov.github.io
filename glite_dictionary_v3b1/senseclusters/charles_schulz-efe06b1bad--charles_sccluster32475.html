Etymology

Charles Schulz was an American cartoonist who drew the comic strip Peanuts, creating simple, lasting characters like Charlie Brown and the imaginative dog Snoopy. He made short, everyday pictures about small moments and big feelings, and because people loved those drawings his name is known as the 'creator' of Peanuts.
