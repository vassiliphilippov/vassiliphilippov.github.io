Etymology

vasoconstrictor is made of vaso- meaning 'vessel', a tube that carries blood, and constrictor meaning 'one that tightens' or 'squeezes'. So a vasoconstrictor is something that 'squeezes' blood vessels, making them narrower and often raising blood pressure.
