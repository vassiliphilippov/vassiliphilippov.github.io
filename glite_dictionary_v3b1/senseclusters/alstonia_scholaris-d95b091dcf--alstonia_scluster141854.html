Etymology

Alstonia scholaris is a scientific name made of a person's name and a Latin word: Alstonia is named for the plant scientist Alston, and scholaris means 'scholar'. One popular idea is that the tree was used by medical 'scholars', so the name links the man and the idea of 'scholar' and that is why this name refers to the tree today.
