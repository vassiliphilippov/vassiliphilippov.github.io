Etymology

The word howlingly comes from howl, which means 'to make a long, loud cry like a wolf or a strong wind', and the ending -ly turns it into a word that shows how something is done. So, when you say something is howlingly funny or wrong, you mean it is very extreme, loud, or exaggerated, like a sound that 'howls'.
