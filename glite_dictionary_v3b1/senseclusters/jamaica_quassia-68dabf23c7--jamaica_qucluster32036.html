Etymology

Jamaica quassia The extract Jamaica quassia is the bitter stuff taken from the tree's wood, and its name comes from 'quassia', linked to a healer, Graman Quassi, who used the bark as medicine. That strong 'bitter' image explains why the extract is used as a 'tonic' and to keep insects away today.
