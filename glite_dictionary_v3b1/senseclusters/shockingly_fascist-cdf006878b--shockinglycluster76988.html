Etymology

Shockingly fascist comes from the word fascist, linked to an old Italian picture of a tied bundle of sticks used as a sign of one strong ruler. With shockingly to make it extreme, this phrase paints the image of very harsh control, so it means 'extremely authoritarian or oppressive'.
