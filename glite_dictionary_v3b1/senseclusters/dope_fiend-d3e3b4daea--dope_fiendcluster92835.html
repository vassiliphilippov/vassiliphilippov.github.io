Etymology

dope fiend links dope, meaning drugs, and fiend, meaning an evil spirit or a person with a very strong, uncontrollable desire. People used the image of a 'fiend' attacking because the drug craving feels very strong and harmful, so dope fiend came to mean someone 'addicted' to drugs.
