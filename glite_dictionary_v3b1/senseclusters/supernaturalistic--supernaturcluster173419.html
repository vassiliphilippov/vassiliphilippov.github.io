Etymology

supernaturalistic comes from the parts super- ('above' or 'beyond') and natural ('nature'), with the ending -istic meaning 'relating to', much like artistic or realistic. So supernaturalistic describes beliefs or ideas about things 'beyond nature', such as spirits or forces outside normal science.
