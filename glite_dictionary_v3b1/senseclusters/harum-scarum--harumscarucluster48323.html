Etymology

Harum-scarum was originally a playful pair of rhyming words to suggest chaos, and later people used it to name a wild person. A helpful picture is a 'hare' racing around without care, which is why a harum-scarum is 'a person who acts recklessly, impulsively, or irresponsibly'.
