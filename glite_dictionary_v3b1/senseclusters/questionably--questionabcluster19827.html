Etymology

questionably comes from the common word question, which means 'to ask' or 'to doubt', plus the ending -able meaning 'able to be' and -ly meaning 'in this way'. So questionably literally means 'in a way that can be questioned', which is why we use it to say something is doubtful or suspicious.
