Etymology

khokhlushka spirit may come from a Slavic word for a lively Ukrainian woman or folk dancer, so picture bright dresses, quick steps and loud singing at a village party. The khokhlushka image of dancing together with spirit for mood is why the phrase means a 'lively and joyful atmosphere'.
