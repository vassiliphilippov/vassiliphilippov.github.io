Etymology

The phrase voice in the wilderness comes from the picture of a lone voice calling out across a wide, empty wilderness where no one can hear it. One popular story comes from the Bible about a lone messenger whose warnings were not heard, so today the phrase means 'a person or message that others ignore'.
