Etymology

vestibular apparatus comes from a word for a small 'entrance' called vestibule and from apparatus meaning a set of parts, so imagine a tiny room in your inner ear full of little sensors. These sensors move when your head turns and tell your brain which way is up, so the phrase means the ear parts that control balance.
