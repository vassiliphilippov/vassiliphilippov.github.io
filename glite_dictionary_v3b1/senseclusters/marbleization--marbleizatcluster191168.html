Etymology

marbleization comes from marble, the hard, shiny stone with dark lines, and the ending -ization, which means 'the process of'. So marbleization is the process of giving something the same 'lined' or 'patchy' look as marble, which is why chefs talk about the thin lines of fat in beef or artists talk about streaks in a painted column.
