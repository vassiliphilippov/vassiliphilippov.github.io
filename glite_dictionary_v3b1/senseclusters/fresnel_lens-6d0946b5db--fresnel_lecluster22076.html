Etymology

Fresnel lens is named after the French scientist Augustin-Jean Fresnel, who made a lens from many thin, flat rings instead of one thick piece of glass. Those rings let the lens be much thinner while still 'focusing' light a long way, so the name now means this special, lightweight lens used in lighthouses and projectors.
