Etymology

wolf bean may come from the idea that this plant grows 'wild' and strong, so people compared it to a wolf and its long pods to a bean. Because it feeds animals and helps hold soil on hills, the name stuck, and today wolf bean means this hardy forage plant used to stop erosion.
