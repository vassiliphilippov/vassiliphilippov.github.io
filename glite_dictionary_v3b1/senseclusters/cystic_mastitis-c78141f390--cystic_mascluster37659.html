Etymology

cystic mastitis comes from its two parts: cystic (from 'cyst', a small fluid-filled sac) and mastitis (from words meaning 'breast' and 'inflammation'). The image is of fluid-filled lumps making the breast sore and swollen, so the phrase now means 'breast inflammation with cysts'.
