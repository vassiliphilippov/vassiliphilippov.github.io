Etymology

delirium tremens literally names the main symptoms: delirium means wild 'confusion' and tremens means 'shaking'. It was used for people who became very confused and shook after stopping heavy drinking, so today it means a very serious state where a person is confused and shakes after they stop drinking alcohol.
