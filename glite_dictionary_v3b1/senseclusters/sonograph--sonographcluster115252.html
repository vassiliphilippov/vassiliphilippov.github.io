Etymology

The word sonograph begins with sono- meaning 'sound' and ends with -graph meaning 'to record', like in photograph. So a sonograph is a machine that uses very high sound waves and their echoes to make pictures of the inside of the body, which is why doctors use it to look at organs and monitor a fetus.
