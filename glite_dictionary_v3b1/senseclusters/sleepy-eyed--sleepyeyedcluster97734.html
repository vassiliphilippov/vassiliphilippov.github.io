Etymology

The word sleepy-eyed is made by joining sleepy ('wanting to sleep') and eye ('the part of your face you see with'), so it literally means having eyes that look drowsy. A helpful image is a cat with half-closed eyes on a sunny windowsill because that shows the modern meaning.
