Etymology

Zonal pelargonium comes from two parts: zonal, for the dark 'zone' or band on the leaf, and pelargonium, an old name that meant 'stork' because the plant's seed looks like a stork's beak. So the name points to a geranium with a wide colored band on its leaves and bright flowers.
