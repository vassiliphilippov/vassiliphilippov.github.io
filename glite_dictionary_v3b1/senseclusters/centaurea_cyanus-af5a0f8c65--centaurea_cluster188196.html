Etymology

Centaurea cyanus is a Latin name with a simple picture: Centaurea comes from a myth about a centaur who, one story says, used the plant to 'heal' wounds, and cyanus means 'blue', so the name fits the bright 'blue' flower we call the 'cornflower'.
