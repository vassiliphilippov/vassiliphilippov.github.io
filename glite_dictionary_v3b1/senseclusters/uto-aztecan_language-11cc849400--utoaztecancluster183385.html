Etymology

Uto-Aztecan language comes from joining Uto (from the Ute people in the western United States) and Aztecan (from the Aztecs in Mexico). Language experts noticed the languages from these areas had many similar words, so the name shows it is a 'family' of related languages spoken from the American Southwest into Mexico.
