Etymology

wild carrot gets its name because it is the wild form of the garden carrot. Its root looks like a small, tougher carrot and its flat white flower head looks like lace. One popular story says the other name 'Queen Anne's lace' comes from a tale that a woman pricked her finger while making lace and a tiny red dot appeared in the flower's center, so the names help you remember it is the wild relative of the carrot.
