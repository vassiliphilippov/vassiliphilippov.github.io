Etymology

The word mytilid comes from the scientific name Mytilus, which comes from the Greek mytilos meaning 'mussel', and the ending -id means 'belonging to'. So a mytilid is a mussel of that family, the dark, elongated shellfish you often find attached to rocks and pilings.
