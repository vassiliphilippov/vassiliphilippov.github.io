Etymology

Potemkin facade comes from one popular story about a rich man named Potemkin who built fake villages and a decorative facade along a road so the visiting ruler would see only beauty; these sights were just a show that hid real poverty. So, Potemkin facade now means 'a fake front' that 'hides the truth'.
