Etymology

visual impairment comes from two simple words: visual meaning 'seeing' and impairment meaning something that makes that ability worse. Imagine a camera lens with a smudge that makes the picture blurry or dark, so it cannot see clearly. So, visual impairment means 'partial or total loss of vision'.
