Etymology

The word chlorambucil begins with chloro-, a part that means 'chlorine' or 'green' and makes the name sound like a chemical, and the whole word points to a medicine. A helpful way to remember it is to think of a chlorine-containing drug that fights cancer, which fits its use as an anti-cancer medicine.
