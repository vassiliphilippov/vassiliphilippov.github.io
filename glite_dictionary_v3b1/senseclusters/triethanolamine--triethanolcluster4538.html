Etymology

triethanolamine breaks into tri- 'three', ethanol 'alcohol', and amine 'like ammonia'. Because it has three alcohol parts and an ammonia-like part, it can mix oil and water, change acidity, and help protect metal, which is why it is used in cosmetics, detergents, and industry.
