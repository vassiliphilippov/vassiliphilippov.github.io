Etymology

LGBTQIA comes from the 'first letters' of the groups lesbian, gay, bisexual, transgender, queer or questioning, intersex, and asexual. Think of it like 'USA' where each letter stands for a word: LGBTQIA is a short name that brings together people with different sexual orientations and gender identities and helps include many groups under one label.
