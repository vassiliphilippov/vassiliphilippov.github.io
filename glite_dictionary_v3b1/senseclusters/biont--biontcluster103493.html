Etymology

The word biont comes from Greek parts bio- meaning 'life' and -ont meaning 'being' or 'thing that exists', the same root you see in biology. So a biont is literally a 'unit of life', which is why scientists use it for an individual living organism, from a single cell to a whole animal.
