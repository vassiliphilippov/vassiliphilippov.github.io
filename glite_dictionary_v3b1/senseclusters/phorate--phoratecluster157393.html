Etymology

phorate is formed like many chemical names: it echoes phosphate, a name for a phosphorus chemical, and it ends in -ate, a common ending for a chemical 'salt' or 'ester'. So, phorate is a phosphorus-based pesticide, and thinking of phosphate and the -ate ending helps you remember it is a strong, toxic chemical used to kill insects.
