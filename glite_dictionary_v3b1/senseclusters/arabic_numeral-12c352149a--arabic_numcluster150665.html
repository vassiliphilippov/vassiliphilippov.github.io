Etymology

The term Arabic numeral comes from how the number signs reached Europe. One popular story says the digits 0–9 began in India and were taught to Europeans by Arab traders and books, so people called them Arabicnumerals, and today Arabic numerals mean the everyday digits we use to write numbers.
