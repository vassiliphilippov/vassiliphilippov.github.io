Etymology

The Composite order is a Roman column style that mixes the leafy top of Corinthian columns with the spiral 'scroll' ornaments of Ionic columns, so you can picture a column wearing both acanthus leaves and volute scrolls. This is why the name means 'made of parts' and today it refers to a column design that combines both decorations.
