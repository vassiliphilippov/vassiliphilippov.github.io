Etymology

Guru Nanak is made of guru ('teacher') and his name Nanak, and one famous story says he went into a river for three days and came out with a strong message that all people are equal. So the name Guru Nanak reminds people of the travelling teacher who began Sikhism and taught meditation and equality.
