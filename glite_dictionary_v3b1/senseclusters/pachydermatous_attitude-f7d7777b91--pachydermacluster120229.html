Etymology

pachydermatous attitude comes from comparing a person to thick-skinned animals like elephants, because pachydermatous means 'thick-skinned' and attitude is a way of behaving. So a pachydermatous attitude brings to mind someone who does not feel hurt or show sympathy, which is why it means 'insensitive'.
