Etymology

The plant name southern maidenhair comes from a clear picture: maidenhair refers to the fern's thin, hair-like leaves that look soft like a young woman's hair, and southern shows where it often grows. So the name helps you remember it is a delicate fern with thin, shiny black stalks.
