Etymology

Sconcheon arch is an arch built in the thickness of a wall to support the part of the wall above a window or door. Imagine a curved stone bridge hidden inside the wall: the sconcheon is the wall slot and the arch is the curved piece that 'holds up' the weight above the opening, so the name tells you it is an arch that supports the wall over an opening.
