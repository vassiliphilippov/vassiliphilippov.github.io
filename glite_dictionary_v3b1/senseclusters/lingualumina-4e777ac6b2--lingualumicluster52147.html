Etymology

Lingualumina comes from Latin parts lingua ('tongue' or 'language') and lumina ('light'), so it can be remembered as a 'language of light'. That's why the name fits a made-up or artificial language that tries to be clear and easy for everyone to understand, often used in stories and science fiction.
