Etymology

cumfrey is linked to an old plant name, symphytum, which comes from words meaning 'to make grow together' because people used the herb to help 'knit' broken bones and wounds, and they even called it knitbone. So, the name fits: cumfrey is a fast‑spreading, healing herb often used as a groundcover in shady gardens.
