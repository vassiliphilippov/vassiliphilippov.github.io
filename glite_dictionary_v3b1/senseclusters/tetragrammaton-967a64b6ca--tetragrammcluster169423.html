Etymology

Tetragrammaton comes from the Greek parts tetra 'four' and gramma 'letter', and the gramma part is like the 'gram' in telegram, which relates to writing. So it literally means 'four letters' and refers to the four Hebrew letters that stand for God's sacred name, which Jewish tradition often avoids speaking aloud.
