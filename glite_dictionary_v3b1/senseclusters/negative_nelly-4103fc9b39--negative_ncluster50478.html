Etymology

negative Nelly comes from putting the word negative next to the name Nelly to make a short, rhyming label. One popular idea is that speakers chose the name because the rhyme is easy to remember and creates a picture of someone who always sees the bad side, so it means 'a person who is habitually pessimistic'.
