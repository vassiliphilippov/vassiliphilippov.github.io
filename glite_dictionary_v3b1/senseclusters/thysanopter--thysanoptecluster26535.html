Etymology

thysanopter comes from the Greek parts thysan- meaning 'fringe' and -pter meaning 'wing', the same 'pter' you see in helicopter. So a thysanopter is literally a 'fringe-wing' insect, a simple image to remember the tiny thrips with fringed wings that can damage plants.
