Etymology

lysinemia comes from the name of the amino acid lysine and the ending -emia, which means 'blood condition' and appears in a common word like anemia. So lysinemia literally names a problem with lysine in the blood, which helps you remember it is a disorder where the body cannot break down or use the amino acid lysine properly.
