Etymology

The name Rubus cissoides comes from old Latin and Greek words: Rubus was used for 'bramble' or 'blackberry', and cissoides means 'like ivy'. The name fits the plant because it has strong, trailing stems that scramble over other plants, so it looks and grows like an ivy-covered bramble.
