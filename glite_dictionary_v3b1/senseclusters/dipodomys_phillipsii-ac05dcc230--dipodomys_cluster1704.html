Etymology

Dipodomys phillipsii is the scientific name for Phillips's kangaroo rat. The part Dipodomys comes from words meaning 'two' and 'foot', which creates a picture of its long hind legs and how it hops, and phillipsii honors a person named Phillips, so the name tells us it is a hopping rodent named for Phillips.
