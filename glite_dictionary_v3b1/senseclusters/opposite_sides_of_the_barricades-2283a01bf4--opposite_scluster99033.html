Etymology

opposite sides of the barricades comes from images of street protests, where two groups stand on different sides of a line of barricades facing each other. So, the clear picture of people separated and ready to fight explains why the phrase means being in 'opposing groups' or 'opposite positions' in a debate or dispute.
