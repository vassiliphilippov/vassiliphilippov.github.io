Etymology

The word fid also came to mean a peg or bar used in a ship's rigging to hold or fasten parts together, because sailors used the same small piece of wood or metal to support masts and secure ropes. So a fid today can mean a wooden or metal bar that supports, fastens, or separates parts of a ship's rigging.
