Etymology

The word methanal is a chemical name made of two parts: meth- as in methane, meaning 'one carbon', and -al, which marks an 'aldehyde', a simple reactive chemical group. So, methanal is the smallest aldehyde, a tiny, strong-smelling gas also called formaldehyde that can kill germs but is poisonous, which is why it is used as a disinfectant and preservative.
