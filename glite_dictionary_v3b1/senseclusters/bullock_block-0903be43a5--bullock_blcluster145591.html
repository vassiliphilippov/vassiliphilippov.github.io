Etymology

bullock block may come from the idea of a bullock because the wooden block high on a ship had to carry heavy loads like a bull's strength. It was a pulley fixed at the top of the mast to help sailors lift and move ropes, so the name came to mean 'a pulley at the top of a mast used to handle ropes'.
