Etymology

dossal comes from the Latin dorsum, which means 'back'. You can see the same root in dorsal, as in the phrase 'dorsal fin', which also points to the 'back'. It was originally a cloth hung at the 'back' of an altar, so a dossal today is the decorative hanging placed behind the altar.
