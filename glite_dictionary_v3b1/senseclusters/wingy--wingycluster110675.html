Etymology

wingy comes from wing ('a part used for flying') plus the ending -y ('having' or 'full of'), so it means 'having wings' or 'wing-like'. A helpful way to remember the informal meaning 'odd' or 'quirky' is to imagine something that 'takes flight' from the usual — an idea or style that flies off in its own direction, which is why we can call it wingy.
