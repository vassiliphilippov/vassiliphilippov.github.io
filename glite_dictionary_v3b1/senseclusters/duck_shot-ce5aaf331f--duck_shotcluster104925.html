Etymology

Duck shot comes from hunting: hunters put many small lead pellets in shotgun shells to hit ducks in flight, so the parts duck and shot literally name those pellets. That's why duck shot now means the small lead pellets used in shotgun shells for 'hunting ducks' and other water birds.
