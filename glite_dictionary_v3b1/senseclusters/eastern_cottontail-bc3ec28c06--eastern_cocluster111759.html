Etymology

eastern cottontail comes from a simple picture: the rabbit's small, fluffy white tail looks like a puff of 'cotton', so people called it a cottontail, and eastern shows where it lives in North America. That's why the name means a common rabbit with a white, cotton-like tail found in the east.
