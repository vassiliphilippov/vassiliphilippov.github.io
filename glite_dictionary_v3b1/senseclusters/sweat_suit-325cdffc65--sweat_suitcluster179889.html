Etymology

Sweat suit comes from the idea of a matching outfit that makes you sweat, with suit meaning a set of clothes. People wore these warm sets to 'exercise' or 'warm up', so the phrase now means a loose, comfortable set of clothes for 'exercise' or to 'relax'.
