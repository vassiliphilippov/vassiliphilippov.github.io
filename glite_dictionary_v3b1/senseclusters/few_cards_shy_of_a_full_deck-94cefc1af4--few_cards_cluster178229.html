Etymology

few cards shy of a full deck may come from the picture of a deck of playing cards that is missing a few cards and so is not a complete deck. People used this image to say someone's mind is 'not quite complete' or a little 'off', so the phrase means 'not very intelligent' or 'slightly mentally unstable'.
