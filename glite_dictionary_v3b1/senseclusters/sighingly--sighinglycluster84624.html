Etymology

sighingly comes from the simple word sigh, a long soft 'breath' people make when they feel sad, tired, or relieved, plus the endings -ing and -ly that turn it into how something is done. So, to do something sighingly is to do it with that quiet, breathy feeling of disappointment or relief.
