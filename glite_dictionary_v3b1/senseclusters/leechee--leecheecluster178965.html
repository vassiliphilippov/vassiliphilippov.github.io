Etymology

The word leechee comes from the Chinese name lìzhī and the Cantonese form lai chi, which sound like 'lee-chee' in English, and English borrowed that name for the small red fruit with a thin shell and sweet white flesh. So remembering that leechee is the original Chinese name helps explain why the word refers to that particular sweet fruit.
