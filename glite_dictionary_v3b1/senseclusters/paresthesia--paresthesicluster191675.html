Etymology

paresthesia comes from Greek parts para- ('abnormal' or 'beside') and aisthesis ('sensation' or 'feeling'). So paresthesia literally means an 'abnormal sensation', which is why it describes tingling, prickling, numbness, or 'pins and needles' in the skin.
