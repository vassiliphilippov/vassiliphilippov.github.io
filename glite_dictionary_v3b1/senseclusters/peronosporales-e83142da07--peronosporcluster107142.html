Etymology

Peronosporales comes from the fungus genus Peronospora plus the scientific ending -ales meaning 'order', and the root spora is the same as the English word spore, a 'tiny cell that can make a new fungus'. So, the name tells you this order is a group of spore-making fungi that often live as plant parasites like downy mildew.
