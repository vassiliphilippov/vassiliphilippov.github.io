Etymology

Brassica kaber comes from Brassica, the name given to cabbage and mustard plants, and the second word kaber marks this particular wild mustard with bright yellow flowers. Because it grows fast and crowds grain fields, it is often called a 'weed' or 'pest', so the name now refers to a common trouble-making field plant.
