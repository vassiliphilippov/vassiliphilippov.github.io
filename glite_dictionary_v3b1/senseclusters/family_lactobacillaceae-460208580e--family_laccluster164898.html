Etymology

family Lactobacillaceae comes from the parts lacto and bacillus, which mean 'milk' and 'small rod'. These are rod-shaped bacteria often found in milk and used to make yogurt, so the name helps you remember they are the 'milk' rods that change milk into yogurt.
