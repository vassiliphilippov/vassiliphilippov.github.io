Etymology

The phrase Lappish language comes from the old name Lapp, a word outsiders used for the Sami people. One popular story says Lapp may come from a word for 'patch' or 'piece of cloth', perhaps from how their clothing looked to neighbours. That's why Lappish language means 'the language spoken by the Sami people'.
