Etymology

The word tessellation comes from the Latin tessella, meaning 'small tile', and long ago from a Greek word for 'four' because tiles were often four-sided. Think of a floor of tiny tiles that fit together with no gaps or overlaps, which is why a tessellation is a pattern of shapes closely fitted together.
