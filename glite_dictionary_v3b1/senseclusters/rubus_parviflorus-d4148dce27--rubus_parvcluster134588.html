Etymology

Rubus parviflorus comes from Latin words: Rubus means 'bramble' or berry bush and parviflorus means 'small-flowered'. The name matches the plant's look—a bramble with small white flowers and thimble-shaped berries—so the name helps you remember what the plant looks like.
