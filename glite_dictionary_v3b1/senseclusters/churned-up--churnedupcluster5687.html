Etymology

churned-up comes from churn, the wooden bucket people used to make butter where they 'stir' or 'beat' cream hard until it changes into butter. So to be 'churned-up' is to be stirred or shaken into a rough, messy state, which is why we say a field, the sea, or a feeling can look or feel 'churned-up'.
