Etymology

Oxidizing agent comes from a simple image: something that pulls tiny particles called 'electrons' away from another thing, where oxidizing is the pulling and agent is the one that does it. So an oxidizing agent 'takes electrons' or 'gives oxygen' to make the other thing change, which is why we call it that today.
