Etymology

Anne Brontë was the youngest Brontë sister who wrote honest, quiet stories about women's lives. She lived on the moors and wrote books like 'Agnes Grey' and 'The Tenant of Wildfell Hall', so her name is remembered as an English writer known for realistic, early feminist themes.
