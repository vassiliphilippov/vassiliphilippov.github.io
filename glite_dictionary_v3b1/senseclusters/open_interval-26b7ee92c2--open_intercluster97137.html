Etymology

The term open interval uses a simple picture: imagine a interval, a stretch of numbers between two points, where the ends are open like small gaps, so the points at the ends are 'not included' while every number inside is included. That's why an open interval means all the numbers between the 'endpoints' but not the endpoints themselves.
