Etymology

Stevens' law is named after psychologist S. S. Stevens, who noticed that when you turn up a sound or a light, the way it feels does not grow in a straight line — sometimes a big increase in real strength gives only a small change in how we 'feel' it. That's why Stevens' law helps predict how the real strength of a sound, light, or touch relates to the 'feeling' it gives.
