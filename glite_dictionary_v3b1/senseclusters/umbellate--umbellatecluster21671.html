Etymology

Umbellate comes from the Latin word umbella, meaning 'little shade', and it shares the same root as umbrella. Picture a small umbrella: the flower stalks spread out from one point like the ribs of an umbrella, so umbellate describes a cluster of flowers arranged in that umbrella-like way.
