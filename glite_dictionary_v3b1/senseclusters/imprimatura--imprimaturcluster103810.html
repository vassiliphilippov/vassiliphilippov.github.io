Etymology

imprimatura comes from Italian and Latin imprimere, meaning 'to press' or 'to print', and the same root is in English print and imprint. An imprimatura is the first thin wash of colour that gives the canvas its first 'impression', so the name helps you remember it sets the painting's overall tone.
