Etymology

Ethelred comes from Old English parts æthel meaning 'noble' and ræd meaning 'advice' or 'counsel'. So the name means 'noble counsel', which makes Ethelred the Unready easier to remember because his nickname actually meant 'no good counsel', the opposite of his name.
