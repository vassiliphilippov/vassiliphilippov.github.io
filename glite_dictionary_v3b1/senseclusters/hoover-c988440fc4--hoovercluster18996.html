Etymology

Hoover comes from the name of Mr. Hoover, whose company made very popular electric vacuum machines in Britain. Like other brand names such as Kleenex or Xerox, the name became the common word for a 'vacuum cleaner', so people often say they will 'borrow a Hoover'.
