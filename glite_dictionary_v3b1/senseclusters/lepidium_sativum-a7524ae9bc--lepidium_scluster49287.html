Etymology

Lepidium sativum is the plant's scientific name; the second word sativum comes from a Latin word meaning 'sown' because gardeners would sow its tiny seeds. The plant is the common 'garden cress' with peppery leaves used as a 'salad green', so the name reminds us how it is grown and eaten.
