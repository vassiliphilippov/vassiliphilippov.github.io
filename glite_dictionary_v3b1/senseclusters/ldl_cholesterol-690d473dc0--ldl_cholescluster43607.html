Etymology

LDL cholesterol gets its name from tiny blood carriers called LDL that carry cholesterol like delivery trucks. If these trucks leave sticky loads on the walls of blood vessels and cause blockages, doctors call it 'bad' cholesterol and worry when levels are high.
