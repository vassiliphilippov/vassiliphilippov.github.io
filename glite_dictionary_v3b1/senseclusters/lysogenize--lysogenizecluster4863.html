Etymology

lysogenize comes from the parts lyso- meaning 'break' (as in lysis), gen- meaning 'produce' (as in generate), and the ending -ize meaning 'make'. So, to lysogenize is when a virus 'joins' a bacterium and becomes part of its DNA instead of immediately breaking the cell open.
