Etymology

The word renormalization starts with re- meaning 'again' and the part normal meaning 'usual' or 'rule'. Think of it like re-setting a scale or re-calibrating a machine so numbers fit the correct rule, which is why in science it means adjusting values to remove errors or impossibly large results.
