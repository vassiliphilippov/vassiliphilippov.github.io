Etymology

Bakhtinian comes from the name Bakhtin and the ending -ian, which means 'related to' or 'from', like in Newtonian or Italian. So a Bakhtinian approach uses Mikhail Bakhtin's ideas, for example his focus on many voices and dialogue in literature.
