Etymology

rectifier diode The name rectifier diode comes from rectifier, which means 'make straight', and diode, a small part that lets electricity flow in only one direction. So it is like a one-way gate that turns 'alternating current', electricity that moves back and forth, into 'direct current', electricity that goes one way.
