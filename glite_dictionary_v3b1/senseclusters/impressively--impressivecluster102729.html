Etymology

impressively comes from impress, which is made of in- ('in') and press ('to push'). Think of a strong stamp pressed into wax that leaves a clear mark — to impress someone is to 'leave a mark' on their mind, so doing something impressively means doing it in a way that makes people admire you.
