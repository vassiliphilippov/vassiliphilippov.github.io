Etymology

Poshness comes from the word posh, which means 'luxury' or 'upper-class style', and one popular story says P.O.S.H. stood for 'port out, starboard home', the best cabins on old ships, a helpful way to remember comfort and wealth. The ending -ness means 'quality', so poshness is the 'quality of being elegant, stylish, or upper class'.
