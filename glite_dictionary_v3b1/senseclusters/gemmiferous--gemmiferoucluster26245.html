Etymology

The word gemmiferous comes from Latin gemma meaning 'bud' and the ending -ferous meaning 'bearing', like in coniferous which is 'cone-bearing'. So gemmiferous describes plants or fungi that 'bear buds' or small gemmae that can grow into new individuals without sexual reproduction.
