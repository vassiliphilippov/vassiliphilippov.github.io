Etymology

beta-adrenergic blocker literally says what it does: beta is a type of receptor that senses 'adrenaline', adrenergic connects to that adrenaline signal, and blocker means it stops the signal. Imagine a roadblock that stops fast 'adrenaline' messages to the heart, so the heart beats more slowly and blood pressure goes down, which is why the drug is used to 'slow the heart' and 'lower blood pressure'.
