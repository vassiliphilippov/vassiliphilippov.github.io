Etymology

slow-witted is made from slow ('not quick') and wit ('mind' or 'intelligence'), so it means a person whose mind works slowly. A helpful way to remember it is to think of witty ('quick with ideas') as the opposite, so slow-witted means 'not quick to understand or think'.
