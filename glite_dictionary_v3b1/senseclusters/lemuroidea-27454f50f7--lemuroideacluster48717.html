Etymology

Lemuroidea comes from lemur, a name from old Latin for a 'ghost' or 'spirit' because these night animals looked ghost-like, and the ending -oidea means 'like' or 'group' in biology. So Lemuroidea means the 'group like lemurs' and is used for the big group that includes lemurs.
