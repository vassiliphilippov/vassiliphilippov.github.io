Etymology

cumulonimbus cloud comes from two old words: cumulus meaning 'a heap' and nimbus meaning 'a rain cloud', so the name gives the picture of a tall heap of cloud full of rain and storm. That's why cumulonimbus cloud now means a large, tall storm cloud that often brings heavy rain and lightning.
