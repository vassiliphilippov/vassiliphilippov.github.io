Etymology

Luschka's tonsil is a medical name from a doctor, Luschka, for the tonsil that sits at the back of the nose. Because this small piece of tissue can grow and make it hard to breathe, the name now refers to the 'adenoid' that can cause 'breathing difficulties'.
