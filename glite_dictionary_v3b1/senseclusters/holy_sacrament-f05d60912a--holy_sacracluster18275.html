Etymology

Holy Sacrament comes from an old church idea that a simple object can become a sign of God's presence in a special ceremony. The word Holy means sacred and Sacrament was used for a 'holy sign', so today Holy Sacrament means the religious ceremony where bread and wine are made holy to remember Jesus.
