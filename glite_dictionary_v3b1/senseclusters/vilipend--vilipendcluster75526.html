Etymology

vilipend comes from the Latin parts vilis ('cheap') and pendere ('to hang' or 'to weigh'), and it is related to the English word 'vile'. A helpful image is hanging a 'cheap' label on someone or something, which is why to vilipend is to treat them as of little worth.
