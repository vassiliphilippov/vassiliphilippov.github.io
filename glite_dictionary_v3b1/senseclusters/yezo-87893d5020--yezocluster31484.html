Etymology

Yezo comes from the older Japanese name Ezo, which was used for the 'northern island' and the Ainu people who lived there, and 19th-century European writers often spelled it Yezo or Yesso. So, when you see Yezo on old maps, it refers to the island we now call Hokkaido.
