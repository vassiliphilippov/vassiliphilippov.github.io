Etymology

heresiarch comes from heresy, from a Greek root meaning 'choice', and arch, the same part in monarch meaning 'ruler'. So a heresiarch is literally a leader of a chosen, different belief, which is why the word means someone who leads or spreads ideas that the main group does not accept.
