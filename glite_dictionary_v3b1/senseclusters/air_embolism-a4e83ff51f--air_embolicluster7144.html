Etymology

The noun air embolism comes from a simple image: tiny air bubbles act like a plug inside a blood vessel, and embolism is the word for that kind of plug, so the bubbles 'block' the blood flow. That's why the term means 'air in the blood' that can stop circulation and cause serious harm.
