Etymology

The word mussel may come from the Latin word musculus, which means 'little mouse', because people thought the shellfish looked like a tiny mouse hiding in its shell, and the same root also gives English muscle. A helpful way to remember mussel is to picture that small mouse-shaped shellfish, which explains the name.
