Etymology

hyperbolic geometry comes from Greek parts: hyper means 'beyond' and the rest means 'to throw', so imagine a space pushed beyond the usual flat space. That's why the name describes a kind of geometry where lines and angles behave differently from 'normal' geometry, for example triangles can have less than 180 degrees.
