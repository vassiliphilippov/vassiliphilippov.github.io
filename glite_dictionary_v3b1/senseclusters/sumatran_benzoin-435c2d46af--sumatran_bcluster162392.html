Etymology

Sumatran benzoin comes from the island of Sumatra and the word benzoin names a sticky, sweet-smelling gum that people collect from certain trees. People have long used this gum in 'perfumes' and 'medicine', so the name now means 'a fragrant resin used in perfumes and medicines'.
