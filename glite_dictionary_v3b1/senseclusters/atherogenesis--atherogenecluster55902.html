Etymology

atherogenesis comes from Greek parts athero- meaning 'porridge' and -genesis meaning 'creation' or 'origin', the same idea you see in genesis or generate. Picture 'porridge' building up on the inside of an artery — that's why atherogenesis means the making of fatty deposits on artery walls.
