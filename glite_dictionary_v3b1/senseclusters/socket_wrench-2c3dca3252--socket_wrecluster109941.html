Etymology

socket wrench comes from its parts: the socket is like a hollow 'cup' that fits over a nut, and the wrench is the handle that turns it. Picture sliding a small metal cup onto a bolt and turning the handle, so it means 'a tool used to turn nuts and bolts'.
