Etymology

Poncirus trifoliata takes its name from how the tree looks: trifoliata means 'three-leaved' because many leaves come in groups of three, and Poncirus names a small, thorny citrus-like tree. Because it has strong thorns and small, sour, 'inedible' fruit, people used it for living 'hedges' and as 'rootstock' to join other citrus trees, so the name helps you remember a small, spiny tree used for fences and grafting.
