Etymology

Kabbalah comes from the Hebrew qabbalah, which means 'that which is received' and comes from a root meaning 'to receive'. A helpful way to remember it is to think of Kabbalah as special teachings 'received' and passed down from teachers, which is why the word now means a mystical tradition.
