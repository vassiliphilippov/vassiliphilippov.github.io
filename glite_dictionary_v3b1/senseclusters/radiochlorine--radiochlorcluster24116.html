Etymology

radiochlorine joins radio- meaning 'ray' or 'radiation' and chlorine, the chemical whose name comes from a word for 'green', as in chlorophyll. So, radiochlorine means a form of chlorine that gives off radiation and is used as a radioactive version of the element in science and medicine.
