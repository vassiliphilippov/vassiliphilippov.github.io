Etymology

United Kingdom of Great Britain and Northern Ireland comes from old kingdoms being joined: Great Britain is the large island with England, Scotland and Wales, and Northern Ireland is the part on the island of Ireland. So the long name simply says it is a 'country made of different parts'.
