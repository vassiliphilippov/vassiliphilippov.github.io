Etymology

The idiom release the kraken comes from old sea stories about a giant sea monster called the kraken, which sailors said would smash ships if it was let loose. A famous movie line 'release the kraken' made the image even stronger, and that's why today people use the phrase to mean 'unleash' something very 'powerful' or 'chaotic'.
