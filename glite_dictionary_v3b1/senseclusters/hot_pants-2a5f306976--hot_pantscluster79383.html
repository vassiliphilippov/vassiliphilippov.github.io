Etymology

hot pants came from 1960s and 1970s fashion when designers made very short, tight shorts that showed off the legs. People called them hot pants because hot meant 'sexy' or very fashionable and pants simply meant shorts, so the name came to mean 'very short, tight-fitting shorts'.
