Etymology

distastefully comes from the prefix dis- meaning 'not' and the word taste, which means both the 'sense of flavor' and your 'sense of what is good'. So to act distastefully is to act in a way that shows 'dislike' or 'poor taste', which is why it means doing something offensive or unpleasant.
