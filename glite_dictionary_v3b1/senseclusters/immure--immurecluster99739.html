Etymology

The word immure comes from Latin parts in- 'in' and murus 'wall', so it originally meant 'to put into a wall' or 'to wall in'. You can see the same root in mural ('a wall painting'), and that image of being closed in by walls explains why immure now means to enclose or imprison someone.
