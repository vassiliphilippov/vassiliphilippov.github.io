Etymology

semi-sweet chocolate gets its name from the parts semi and sweet: semi means 'partly' and sweet shows it has sugar. So the name tells you it is only partly sweet, with less sugar than regular sweet chocolate but more than bitter chocolate, which is why bakers often choose it.
