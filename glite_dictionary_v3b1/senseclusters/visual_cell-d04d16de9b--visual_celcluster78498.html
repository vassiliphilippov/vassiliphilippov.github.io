Etymology

visual cell comes from the parts visual, which means 'seeing', and cell, a tiny unit, so the phrase gives the image of a small unit that 'sees' light. These small units at the back of the eye catch light and send messages to the brain, so visual cell means a light-sensitive cell that helps us see.
