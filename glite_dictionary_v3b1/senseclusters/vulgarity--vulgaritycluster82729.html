Etymology

vulgarity comes from the Latin word vulgus, which means 'the common people' or 'the crowd'. So vulgarity moved from meaning 'common' to meaning behaviour or language that is rough, crude, or not polite, because it was seen as the style of the crowd rather than something polished.
