Etymology

riptide breaks into rip (like the verb 'to tear' or a fast, strong pull) and tide ('the movement of the sea'). Think of it as a 'tear' or sudden gap in the water that pulls you away from the beach, which is why a riptide is a dangerous current that can carry swimmers out to sea.
