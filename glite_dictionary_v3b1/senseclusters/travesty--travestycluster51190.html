Etymology

travesty comes from old French and Italian words that meant 'to dress' or 'to clothe', from Latin vestire 'to clothe', and is related to the English word vest. The idea was putting a wrong or silly costume on something, so to travesty (verb) is to present or treat something in a false, distorted, or ridiculous way.
