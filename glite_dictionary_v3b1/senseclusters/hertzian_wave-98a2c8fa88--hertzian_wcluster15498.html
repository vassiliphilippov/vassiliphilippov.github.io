Etymology

The term Hertzian wave comes from the name of the scientist Heinrich Hertz, who showed that quick electrical sparks make invisible waves that travel through the air. Because these waves were first studied by Hertz, people called them Hertzian waves, which is why the phrase was used for what we now call a 'radio wave'.
