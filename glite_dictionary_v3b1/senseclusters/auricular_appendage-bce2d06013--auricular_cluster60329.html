Etymology

auricular appendage gets its name from its shape: auricular comes from a word meaning 'little ear' and appendage means a 'small extra part'. So, it describes the small ear-shaped pouch that sticks out from the upper front part of the heart, which is why doctors check it for blood clots or sometimes remove it.
