Etymology

Love muscle is a playful 'slang' name that links love (romance or sex) and muscle (a strong arm or chest). People picture a strong arm used to attract or impress someone, so the phrase refers to a well-built bicep or chest and is also used jokingly for a man's 'penis', which is why it has those meanings today.
