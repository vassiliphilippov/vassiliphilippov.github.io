Etymology

mei comes from the East Asian character 梅, which means 'plum' and names the fragrant plum blossom seen in Chinese and Japanese art. So mei refers to that small ornamental plum tree with white or pink flowers and little yellow fruit, and that is why it stands for beauty and renewal in gardens.
