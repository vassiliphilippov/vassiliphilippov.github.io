Etymology

Orbignya phalerata is a tall palm from northern Brazil with feather-like leaves and very hard, round nuts. The nuts can be squeezed to make 'oil' and can be carved like real ivory, called 'vegetable ivory', so the name helps you picture a palm used for both 'oil' and 'vegetable ivory'.
