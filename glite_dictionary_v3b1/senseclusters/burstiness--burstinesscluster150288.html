Etymology

The word burstiness comes from the common word burst, which means 'a sudden, short event or increase', and the ending -ness, which means 'the quality of'. So burstiness is the 'quality of happening in sudden bursts instead of at a steady rate', which fits things like internet traffic, heavy rain, or order spikes.
