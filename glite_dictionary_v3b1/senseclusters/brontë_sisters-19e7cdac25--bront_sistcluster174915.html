Etymology

Brontë sisters comes from their family name Brontë and the fact that three real sisters, Charlotte, Emily, and Anne, wrote famous novels together. They lived in a small house on the Yorkshire moors and at first used male pen names. Today Brontë sisters refers to those three 19th-century English novelists.
