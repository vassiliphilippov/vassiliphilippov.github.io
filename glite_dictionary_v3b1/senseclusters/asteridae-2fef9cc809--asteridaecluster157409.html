Etymology

Asteridae comes from aster, the name of a star-shaped flower and a Greek word meaning 'star', plus the ending -idae, a common ending for a 'family' or group. So Asteridae simply means the 'aster' family — a large group of flowering plants that includes asters and many common garden flowers.
