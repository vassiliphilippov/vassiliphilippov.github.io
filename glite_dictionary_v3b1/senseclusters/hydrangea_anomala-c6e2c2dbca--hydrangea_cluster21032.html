Etymology

Hydrangea anomala may come from old words hydor and angeion, which mean 'water' and 'jar', because the plant's seed pods look like tiny jars. 'Anomala' means 'different', and this climbing hydrangea grows in a different way by sending roots and climbing walls, so the name hints at both the jar-like pods and the plant's odd way of growing.
