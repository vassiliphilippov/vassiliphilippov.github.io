Etymology

Chrysanthemum balsamita comes from older words: chrysanthemum means 'gold' and 'flower', and balsamita points to its 'balsam' or sweet smell. So the name paints the picture of a golden, balsam-scented flower, which fits why people used it in salads, teas and potpourri.
