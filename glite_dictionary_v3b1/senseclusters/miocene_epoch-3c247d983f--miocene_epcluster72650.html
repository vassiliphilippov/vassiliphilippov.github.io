Etymology

Miocene epoch was named in the 1800s from two Greek words, meiōn and kainos, meaning 'less' and 'new'. Geologists used this name to show the time was 'less recent' than the later Pliocene, so today Miocene epoch refers to the period about 23 to 5 million years ago when many modern mammals and grasslands first appeared.
