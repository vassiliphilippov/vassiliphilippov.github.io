Etymology

zikkurat comes from the old Mesopotamian word ziqqurratu, which meant 'temple tower' or 'raised place'. So think of a tall, stepped tower made of stacked levels, and you will remember a zikkurat as the rectangular, terraced temple mound of ancient cities.
