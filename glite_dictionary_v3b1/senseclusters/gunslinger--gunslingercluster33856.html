Etymology

gunslinger is made from gun and slinger, where slinger comes from the idea 'to sling' meaning 'to throw' or 'send quickly', so you can picture someone who fires a gun very fast. That's why a gunslinger is a person, often in the American Old West, known for being skilled and ready to use guns.
