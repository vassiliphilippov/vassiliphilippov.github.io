Etymology

Chinese gooseberry for the plant comes from the same image: the climbing vine was Chinese and it made fuzzy fruits like a gooseberry, so the name came to mean the vine that grows the fruit, even though people now usually call the fruit 'kiwi' or 'kiwifruit'.
