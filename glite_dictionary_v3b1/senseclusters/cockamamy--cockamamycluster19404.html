Etymology

The word cockamamy comes from American slang and was formed as a playful, made-up word for something unlikely or silly. A helpful way to remember it is to picture a rooster, called a cock, offering a ridiculous plan. So, cockamamy means 'absurd' or 'ridiculous'.
