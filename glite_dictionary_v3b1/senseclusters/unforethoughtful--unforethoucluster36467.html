Etymology

unforethoughtful is made of un- meaning 'not', fore- meaning 'before', as in before or forecast, and thoughtful meaning 'careful thinking'. So unforethoughtful describes someone or something that did not think ahead or plan, which is why it means 'lacking forethought'.
