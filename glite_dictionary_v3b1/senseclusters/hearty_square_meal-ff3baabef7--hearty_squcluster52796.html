Etymology

hearty square meal comes from the older phrase square meal, which one popular story says meant a solid, full plate of food—picture a square plate piled high. Adding hearty gives the image of warm, strong food that really fills you up, so it means 'a meal that is large, nourishing, and satisfying'.
