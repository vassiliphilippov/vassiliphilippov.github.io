Etymology

Fowl cholera comes from two simple words: fowl (meaning birds) and cholera (an old name for a severe gut illness that causes bad diarrhea). Farmers and animal doctors used that human word for a similar, deadly disease in chickens, so today the name means 'a contagious, often fatal bird disease'.
