Etymology

microchemical is made of micro- ('small') and chemical (like 'chemistry'), so it literally means 'small chemistry'. Think of common words such as microphone or microscope, where micro- means 'tiny', and that's why microchemical describes chemical tests or reactions done with very tiny amounts of a substance.
