Etymology

glass snake gets its name from the animal's fragile tail that can break off like glass when a bird or other animal grabs it. People call it a snake because it looks long and has no legs, but it is actually a 'legless lizard' that can 'shed its tail' to escape, so the name links that clear image to the animal's meaning today.
