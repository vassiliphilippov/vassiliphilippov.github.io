Etymology

nicely comes from the adjective nice, which long ago came from Latin nescius meaning 'not knowing' and at first meant 'foolish', but over time it changed to mean 'pleasant' or 'kind'. So to do something nicely is to do it in a 'pleasant' or 'agreeable' way.
