Etymology

Lycopersicum comes from Greek parts lyco- meaning 'wolf' and persicum meaning 'peach', so it once meant 'wolf-peach'. Because early Europeans called the tomato a 'wolf-peach', scientists used Lycopersicum as the old scientific name for the tomato plant, which is why you still see it in older books.
