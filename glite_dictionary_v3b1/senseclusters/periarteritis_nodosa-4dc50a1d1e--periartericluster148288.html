Etymology

periarteritis nodosa is a medical name made from older words: peri- means 'around', arteritis means 'inflamed artery', and nodosa means 'having small lumps'. So the name gives the picture of small lumps forming around arteries, which explains why it describes a disease that can block blood flow.
