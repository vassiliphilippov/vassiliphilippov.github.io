Etymology

religiousness in the sense of great carefulness comes from the same root religio (the old Latin word people link to the idea 'to bind' someone to duties), and that idea of being 'bound' can help you remember a strict, careful attitude. That's why religiousness can also mean doing things with strong care and exact attention, as if following a steady practice.
