Etymology

The phrase complete bullocks may come from the rude phrase complete bollocks. That rude word once meant 'testicles' but later became slang for 'nonsense', and people began using the animal word bullocks, meaning young bulls, as a softer form, so complete bullocks now means 'nonsense'.
