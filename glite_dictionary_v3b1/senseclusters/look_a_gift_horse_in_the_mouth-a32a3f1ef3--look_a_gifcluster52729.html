Etymology

look a gift horse in the mouth comes from the old habit of checking a horse's teeth at its mouth to see its age and value. If someone gave you a horse, looking in its mouth was rude because it showed you did not trust or like the gift, so today the phrase means don't 'question its value' and accept gifts gratefully.
