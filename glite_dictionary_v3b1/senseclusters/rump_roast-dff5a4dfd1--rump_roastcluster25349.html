Etymology

rump roast comes from the butcher's name for a piece of meat. Rump is the animal's 'back part' near the hind legs and roast means meat cooked over heat, so the phrase pictures meat from the back that is roasted, which is why it means 'a cut of beef or veal often cooked by roasting'.
