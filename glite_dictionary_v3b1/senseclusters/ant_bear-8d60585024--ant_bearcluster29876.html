Etymology

ant bear was also used by early visitors in Africa as a simple name for the aardvark because it digs and uses a long tongue to 'eat termites' and other insects. People thought it looked like a burrowing bear that ate ants and termites, so the picture of ant + bear helps explain why the name can mean the aardvark too.
