Etymology

The term fixing agent comes from the idea of fixing, meaning to make something stay in place, and an agent, a helper that does the job. So a fixing agent is like a helper substance that 'locks' color or an image so it does not wash away or fade, which is why it means a material used to make things 'permanent'.
