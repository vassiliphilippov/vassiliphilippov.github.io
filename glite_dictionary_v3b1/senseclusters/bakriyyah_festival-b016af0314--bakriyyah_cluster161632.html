Etymology

Bakriyyah festival may come from the old word bakri meaning 'goat'. One popular story says villagers used to gather for markets, feasts and special customs around goats, and over time these gatherings grew into a larger local celebration. That's why the name now refers to a community festival with dances and traditional events.
