Etymology

boulevardier comes from the French word boulevard, meaning 'wide street' or 'promenade', plus the French ending -ier, meaning 'person who spends time there'. So a boulevardier is literally a 'boulevard person' — a man who walks, socialises, and enjoys life on fashionable city boulevards, which explains the modern meaning.
