Etymology

Nizhnyi Novgorod may come from two Russian words: Nizhnyi meaning 'lower' and Novgorod meaning 'new town'. It was a 'new' settlement lower on the river than another Novgorod, so the name paints the simple image of a 'lower new town' and helps explain why the city by the Volga and Oka is called Nizhnyi Novgorod.
