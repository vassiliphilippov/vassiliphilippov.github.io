Etymology

buxomly comes from buxom plus -ly. The older word buxom once came from an old word meaning 'to bow' and meant 'obedient', but people later linked it to bosom ('breast') and the sense shifted to 'full-bosomed' or 'curvy', so buxomly means moving or looking 'in a curvy, full-bosomed way'.
