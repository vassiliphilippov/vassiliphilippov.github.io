Etymology

paraphrenic schizophrenia may come from old Greek words meaning 'beside' and 'mind', shown in the parts para and phrenic, so you can picture strange beliefs sitting beside a person's normal thoughts. That's why the name fits: people often seem normal but hold lasting 'false beliefs'.
