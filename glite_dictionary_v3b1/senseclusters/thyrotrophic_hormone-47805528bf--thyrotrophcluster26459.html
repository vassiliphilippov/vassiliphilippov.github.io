Etymology

Thyrotrophic hormone comes from parts meaning thyro (the thyroid gland) and trophic ('to feed' or 'to help'), so imagine a message that feeds or supports the thyroid. So the name fits: it is a hormone from a small gland at the base of the brain that tells the thyroid to work.
