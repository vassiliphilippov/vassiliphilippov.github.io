Etymology

The word mylodontid comes from the scientific name Mylodon, which uses Greek parts myl- 'mill' and odont- 'tooth' because the animal had wide grinding teeth. So, a mylodontid is an extinct mammal, like a ground sloth, named for those strong teeth seen in fossils.
