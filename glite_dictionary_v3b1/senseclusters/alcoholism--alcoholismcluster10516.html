Etymology

alcoholism comes from the common word alcohol plus the ending -ism, which means 'a condition of'. The word alcohol itself comes from the Arabic al-kuhl, which first meant 'the kohl', a fine makeup powder, and later came to mean 'a pure, distilled substance' or strong drink, so alcoholism is the condition of being unable to control drinking.
