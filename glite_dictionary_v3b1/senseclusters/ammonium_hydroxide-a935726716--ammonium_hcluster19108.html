Etymology

The chemical name ammonium hydroxide simply tells you what it is: ammonium comes from 'ammonia', a strong-smelling gas named long ago after the temple of Ammon, and hydroxide shows it comes from the water side of the chemical. So the name gives a clear picture, a watery form of ammonia, which is why ammonium hydroxide means an ammonia solution used for cleaning and in labs.
