Etymology

Parlor grand piano comes from the idea of a parlor as the main sitting room in a house and the grand piano as the large, rich-sounding instrument, so a smaller version made to fit the parlor kept the name. That's why parlor grand piano means a 'small' grand piano meant for use in a 'home' or small room.
