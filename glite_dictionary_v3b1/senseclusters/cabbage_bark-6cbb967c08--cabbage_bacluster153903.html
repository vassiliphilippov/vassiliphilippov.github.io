Etymology

cabbage bark probably got its name because the tree's bark smells like cabbage and comes off in thick, cabbage-like layers, a clear image people remembered. Because of that strong smell and very tough bark, people used the wood for building and the bark in old medicines, so the name now points to the tree known for its smelly, useful bark.
