Etymology

Popular Front for the Liberation of Palestine – General Command is a type of political name: Popular Front means a group of people and parties working together, Liberation says their goal is 'to free' Palestine, and General Command shows the group is led by military commanders. So the name tells you it is a political and armed group with a military-style leadership.
