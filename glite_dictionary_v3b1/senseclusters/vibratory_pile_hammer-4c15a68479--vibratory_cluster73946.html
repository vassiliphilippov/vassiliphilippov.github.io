Etymology

The name vibratory pile hammer gives a simple picture: vibratory means it shakes quickly, pile is a long post put into the ground, and hammer is a tool for driving things. Because this machine shakes a pile so it sinks into or comes out of the ground, the name came to mean a tool that 'drives or removes piles by vibration'.
