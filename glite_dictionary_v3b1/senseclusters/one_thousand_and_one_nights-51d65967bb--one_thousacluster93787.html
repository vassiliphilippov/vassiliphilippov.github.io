Etymology

One Thousand and One Nights comes from an old story about a woman named Scheherazade who told a different tale to a king every night for 1,001 nights so she would not be killed. That strong image of many nights of storytelling is why the title now means the famous book of many Middle Eastern tales, also called 'Arabian Nights'.
