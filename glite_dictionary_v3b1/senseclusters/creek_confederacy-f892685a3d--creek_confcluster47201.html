Etymology

Creek Confederacy got its name when English people called the Muskogee nation Creek, because many of their towns sat beside creeks, small rivers. Those towns and tribes joined in a confederacy, a 'group' that worked together, so the name means the alliance of tribes that led parts of the southeastern United States before they were forced to move to Oklahoma.
