Etymology

Brandy snifter gets its name from how the glass is used: the short stem and wide bowl let you cup the glass to warm the brandy, and the narrow top traps the aroma so you can 'sniff' it. That's why a brandy snifter is the special glass for enjoying the smell and taste of brandy.
