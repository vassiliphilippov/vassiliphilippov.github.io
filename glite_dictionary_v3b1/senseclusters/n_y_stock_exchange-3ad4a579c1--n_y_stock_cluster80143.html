Etymology

N. Y. Stock Exchange is a short name for the New York Stock Exchange, named for the place in New York where people meet to buy and sell 'stocks'. One popular story says the first traders met under a large 'buttonwood' tree on Wall Street, and that image helps explain why the name means the main place for trading 'stocks' today.
