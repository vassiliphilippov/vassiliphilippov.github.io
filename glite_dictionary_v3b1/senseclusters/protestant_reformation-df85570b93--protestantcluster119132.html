Etymology

Protestant Reformation comes from the words Protestant (people who 'protested' the Church) and Reformation (a push for 'change'). One famous image is Martin Luther nailing his list of complaints to a church door. So the phrase means the 16th-century movement that publicly protested the Roman Catholic Church and led to new Protestant churches.
