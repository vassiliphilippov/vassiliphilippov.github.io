Etymology

The noun sprocket wheel is a thin wheel with small, tooth-like parts called sprockets that catch and pull a chain as it turns. Because those 'teeth' hold the chain, the sprocket wheel is the part that 'drives' other wheels and belts on bikes and machines, so the name tells you its job today.
