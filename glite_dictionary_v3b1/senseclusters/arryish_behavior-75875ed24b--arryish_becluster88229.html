Etymology

'Arryish behavior may come from the nickname 'Arry, a way people used to say 'Harry' when they left off the 'H' sound. One popular story says a loud, rough man called 'Arry in old London pubs acted noisy and rude, so people used 'Arryish behavior to mean 'boisterous, unsophisticated, or slightly vulgar'.
