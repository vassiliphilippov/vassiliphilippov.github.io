Etymology

Ochnaceae comes from the plant genus Ochna and the Latin family ending -aceae, which means 'family' in plant names. So Ochnaceae simply names the 'Ochna family' of tropical evergreen trees and shrubs with thick, shiny, parallel-veined leaves.
