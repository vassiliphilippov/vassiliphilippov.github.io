Etymology

Nepenthaceae comes from the Greek word nepenthe, meaning 'without grief', a mythic potion that removes sorrow, with -aceae added to show it is a plant family. The genus Nepenthes was named for that idea because the plants have pitcher-shaped leaves like strange potion cups, and that image helps you remember this family of carnivorous plants that trap insects.
