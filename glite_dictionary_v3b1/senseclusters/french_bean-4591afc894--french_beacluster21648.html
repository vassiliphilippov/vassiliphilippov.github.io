Etymology

French bean comes from French cooks and gardeners who grew and used a long, slim green bean they called 'haricot vert'. English speakers liked the French name and began to call the same vegetable French beans. So French shows the link to France and bean is the vegetable, and today the phrase means the long, thin green pod people eat.
