Etymology

The chemical name nitrogen trichloride literally names its parts: nitrogen, plus tri (meaning 'three') and chloride, so it is one nitrogen joined to three chlorine atoms. It is a yellow, strong‑smelling chemical that was once used to bleach flour but proved dangerous, which is why it is no longer used.
