Etymology

The word mmmmm comes from a natural humming sound people make with a closed mouth to show 'pleasure' or enjoyment, especially when tasting or smelling something nice. It is like the simple sounds hum or mmm, so when you see mmmmm it tells you someone is enjoying what they taste or smell.
