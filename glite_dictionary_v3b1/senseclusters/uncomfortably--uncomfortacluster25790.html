Etymology

uncomfortably comes from un- ('not') + comfort ('ease' or 'relief'), and the ending -ably shows how something is done. Think of comfort like a soft chair or warm blanket and un- as making it the opposite, so uncomfortably means 'not comfortably', describing physical pain or a feeling of unease.
