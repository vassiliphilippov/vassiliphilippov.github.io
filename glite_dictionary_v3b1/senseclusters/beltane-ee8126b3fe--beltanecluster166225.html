Etymology

Beltane likely comes from old Irish parts Bel (a fire god) and teine 'fire', so it originally meant 'Bel's fire' or a 'bright fire'. People lit large bonfires on May 1 to welcome summer, which is why Beltane is a festival of fires marking the start of summer.
