Etymology

soft-pedal the issue comes from pianos: pressing the soft pedal makes the sound softer and quieter, as if the music is being hidden. So to soft-pedal something is to make an issue seem less important or to avoid it, which is why it means 'to treat a problem or topic as less important than it really is'.
