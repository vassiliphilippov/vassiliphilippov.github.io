Etymology

Brassica napus napobrassica is the scientific name for the vegetable often called rutabaga or swede. The name rutabaga probably comes from old Swedish words for 'root' and 'bag', so imagine a big, round 'bag' of 'root' in the ground, and that image explains why the word refers to this thick, edible root.
