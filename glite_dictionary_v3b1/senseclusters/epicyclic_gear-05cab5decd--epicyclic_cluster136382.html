Etymology

epicyclic gear gets its name from the image of small planet gears turning as they move around a central sun gear, like planets moving around the sun. So the name fits: these gears both spin and travel around the centre, letting a machine change speed or power, and the phrase describes a gear that 'revolves around' a central gear to do that.
