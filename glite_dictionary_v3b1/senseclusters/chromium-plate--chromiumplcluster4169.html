Etymology

chromium-plate combines chromium, from the Greek root chroma meaning 'color' and seen in the everyday word chrome, with plate, meaning 'a thin metal layer'. So to chromium-plate something is to cover it with a shiny chromium layer for protection and a bright, mirror-like finish.
