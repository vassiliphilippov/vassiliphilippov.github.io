Etymology

smallmouth black bass: The name smallmouth, black and bass comes from how the fish looks — it has a smallmouth (a mouth that does not reach past the eye) and a darker, 'black' colour, and bass is the common word for this kind of fish. So the name tells you it is a 'type of freshwater fish' often found in lakes and rivers and liked by people who fish.
