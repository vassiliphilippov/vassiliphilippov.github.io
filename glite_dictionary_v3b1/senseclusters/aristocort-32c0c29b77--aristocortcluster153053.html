Etymology

Aristocort looks like a brand name made from aristo- meaning 'best' and cort, the same part you see in 'cortisone' and other steroid names that come from the body's 'cortex' or outer layer. A helpful way to remember it is that the name suggests a 'strong' or 'effective' steroid, which fits its use as an anti-inflammatory medicine.
