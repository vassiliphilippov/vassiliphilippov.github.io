Etymology

sort of comes from the word sort, which means 'kind' or 'type', and the small word of that links it to what follows. Saying something is sort of a thing is like pointing to a group and meaning it belongs a little to that kind but not completely, so the phrase came to mean 'to some degree, not completely'.
