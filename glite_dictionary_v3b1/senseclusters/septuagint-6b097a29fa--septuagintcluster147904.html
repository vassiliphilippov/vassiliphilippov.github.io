Etymology

The word Septuagint comes from the Latin septuaginta, which means 'seventy', and from the old abbreviation LXX for 70. A popular story says about seventy Jewish scholars made the Greek translation of the Hebrew scriptures, so the name means 'work of the seventy' and that is why Septuagint refers to the ancient Greek Old Testament.
