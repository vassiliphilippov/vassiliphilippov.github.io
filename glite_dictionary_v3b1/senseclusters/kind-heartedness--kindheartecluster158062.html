Etymology

Kind-heartedness comes from putting kind ('friendly' or 'good') and heart ('feeling' or 'care') together, and the ending -ness makes it a word for a 'quality'. So, it literally means having a caring heart, which is why we use it for being kind, caring, and sympathetic toward others.
