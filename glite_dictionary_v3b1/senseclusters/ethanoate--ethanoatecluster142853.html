Etymology

ethanoate is a chemical name made of ethan- (meaning 'two-carbon', as in ethane) and the ending -oate, which chemists use for salts or esters of an acid. So ethanoate is the same idea as acetate and means a salt or ester containing the 'CH3COO' group, the part related to acetic (vinegar) acid.
