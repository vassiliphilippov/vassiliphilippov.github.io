Etymology

Amphiprion comes from Greek parts amphi- meaning 'both' and prion meaning 'saw', so a helpful way to remember it is to picture a small fish with little saw-like fins on both sides. That's why this scientific name is used for the bright clownfish that live with sea anemones.
