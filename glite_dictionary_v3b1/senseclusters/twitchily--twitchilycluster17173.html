Etymology

twitchily comes from the verb twitch, which means a quick, small pull or jerk, plus the adverb ending -ly. Think of a muscle or a nervous hand that gives a quick little pull; that's why twitchily means 'in a nervous or restless way, with small, sudden movements'.
