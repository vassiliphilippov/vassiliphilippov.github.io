Etymology

The scientific name Peromyscus maniculatus may come from old words, with Peromyscus for 'mouse' and maniculatus for 'little hand', so imagine a small, quick mouse with tiny, hand-like paws. That's why the name fits the 'deer mouse', a small, active mouse often found in woods.
