Etymology

The name Epinephelus fulvus comes from Latin used in scientific names: Epinephelus marks it as a 'grouper' and fulvus means 'tawny' or 'brownish'. So the name gives a picture of a dusky, brownish grouper with dark spots and reddish fins near the reef.
