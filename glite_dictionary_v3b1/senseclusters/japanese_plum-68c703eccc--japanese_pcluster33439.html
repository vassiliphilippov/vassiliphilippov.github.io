Etymology

Japanese plum names an evergreen tree from China and Japan, so imagine a glossy tree full of small, fuzzy yellow fruits that look like tiny plums. That's why the name means 'an evergreen tree native to China and Japan, grown in warm regions and producing small, fuzzy, yellow fruit with a large stone'.
