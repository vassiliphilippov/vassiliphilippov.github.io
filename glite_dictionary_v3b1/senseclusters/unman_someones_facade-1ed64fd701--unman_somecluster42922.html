Etymology

unman someone's facade creates a clear image: imagine taking off a mask or knocking off armor from someone who looks brave. unman means to make a person feel less strong and facade is the front they show. So it means to reveal the hidden 'weakness' or 'doubt' behind someone's 'brave' show.
