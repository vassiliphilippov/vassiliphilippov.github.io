Etymology

thermonuclear bomb is made of thermo, which means 'heat', and nuclear, which is about an atom's 'centre', so imagine a bomb that uses extreme heat to force tiny parts of atoms to join together. That joining gives off a huge amount of energy, so a thermonuclear bomb means a weapon far more powerful than a normal atomic bomb.
