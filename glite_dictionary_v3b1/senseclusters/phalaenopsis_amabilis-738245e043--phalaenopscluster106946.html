Etymology

Phalaenopsis amabilis gets its name from old words: Phalaenopsis comes from a Greek word for 'moth' because the open flowers look like delicate moths, and amabilis is a Latin word meaning 'lovely'. So the name gives the image of a 'lovely moth', which fits the moon orchid's soft pink-and-white blooms.
