Etymology

lemon-wood is simply the common words lemon and wood put together; people called certain trees this because the wood sometimes smells or looks a little like a 'lemon'. So lemon-wood now means a kind of evergreen tree with hard, strong wood that is useful for making furniture and other durable items.
