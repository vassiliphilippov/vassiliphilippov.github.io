Etymology

Ginkgopsida begins with ginkgo, the name of the tree that comes from a Japanese/Chinese word meaning 'silver apricot'. The ending -opsida is a scientific part that means a 'class' or group of plants, so Ginkgopsida means the ginkgo 'class', the plant group that contains the ginkgo tree.
