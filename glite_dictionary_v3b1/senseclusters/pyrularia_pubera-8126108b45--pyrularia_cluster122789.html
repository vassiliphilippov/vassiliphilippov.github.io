Etymology

Pyrularia pubera probably comes from Latin words that describe the plant: pyrularia from 'pyrus' meaning 'pear' for its soft, pear-like fruit, and pubera from 'puber' meaning 'hairy' for its fuzzy stems or fruit. That's why the name points to a shrub with pulpy fruit that is often a little hairy.
