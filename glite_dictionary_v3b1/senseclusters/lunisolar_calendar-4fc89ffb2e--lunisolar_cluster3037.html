Etymology

Lunisolar calendar comes from joining lunar and solar, where lunar means the moon and solar means the sun. The lunar part gives the 'months' by the moon's cycles and the solar part keeps the 'years' in step with the sun and the seasons, so the calendar matches both months and seasons.
