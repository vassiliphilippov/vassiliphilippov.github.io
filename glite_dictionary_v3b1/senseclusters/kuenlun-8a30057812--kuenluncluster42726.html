Etymology

The word Kuenlun comes from the Chinese name 昆仑, which appears in old Chinese stories as a great, sacred mountain. So thinking of Kuenlun as a famous high mountain helps you remember that the Kuenlun range is a long line of high peaks and a natural barrier in western China.
