Etymology

pharaoh's ant may come from old reports that these small, pale ants were first noticed near the tombs of pharaohs or in goods that travelled from Egypt. Because they travel easily with people and hide inside buildings, the name pharaoh's ant stuck, and today it means a tiny 'household pest'.
