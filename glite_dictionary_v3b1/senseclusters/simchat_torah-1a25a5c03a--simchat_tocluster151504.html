Etymology

Simchat Torah comes from Hebrew: Simchat means 'joy' and Torah means 'holy book'. On this day people dance and sing as they finish the yearly reading of the Torah and begin it again, so the name shows the joyful festival that celebrates the end and the new start of the readings.
