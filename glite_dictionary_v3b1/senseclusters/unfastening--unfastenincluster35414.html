Etymology

The word unfastening comes from un- meaning 'not' or 'reverse' and fasten meaning 'to make firm' and is related to fast. A helpful way to remember it is to imagine 'undoing what holds something tight', so an unfastening is the action of loosening or undoing what holds something in place.
