Etymology

Mount Garmo may come from a simple local name used by people living in the Pamir Mountains to point to that tall, lonely peak. Imagine villagers pointing at the high, snowy mountain and saying 'Garmo'—that picture stuck, so today Mount Garmo is the name for that very high mountain near the border.
