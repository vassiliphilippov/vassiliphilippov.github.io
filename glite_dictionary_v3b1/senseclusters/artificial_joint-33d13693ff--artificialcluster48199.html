Etymology

artificial joint: The name artificial joint combines artificial (made by people) and joint (where two bones meet). Picture a metal hinge put in to 'replace' a broken door part — the same idea is a man-made part put into the body to 'replace' a damaged hip or knee, so it means a joint put in by surgery.
