Etymology

simply sinful comes from the idea that enjoying something very rich or luxurious can feel like a small 'sin', and the word sinful paints a picture of pleasure that feels a little wrong. That's why people call a dessert or treat 'simply sinful' when it is very tasty and hard to resist.
