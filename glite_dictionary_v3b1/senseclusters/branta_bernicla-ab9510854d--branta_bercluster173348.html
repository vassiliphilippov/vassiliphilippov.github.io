Etymology

Branta bernicla may come from old names sailors used for coastal geese: Branta linked to a word meaning 'burnt' because of their dark bellies, and bernicla linked to 'barnacle' because people saw these geese near shells. So the name points to a small, dark-bellied goose that lives by the sea.
