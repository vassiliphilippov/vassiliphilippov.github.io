Etymology

torch song comes from the image of a burning torch and the old idea 'to carry a torch' for someone, which means to keep a secret, burning love. Singers used these songs to show deep, often 'unreturned love', so today a torch song means a sad, emotional song about 'longing' or 'unreturned love'.
