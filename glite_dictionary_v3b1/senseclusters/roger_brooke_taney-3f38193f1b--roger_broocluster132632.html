Etymology

Roger Brooke Taney was a U.S. judge in the 1800s who served as 'Chief Justice' and is best known for the 'Dred Scott decision'. In that ruling he wrote that enslaved people and their children were not citizens, and because of this one shocking decision his name is often linked with that dark moment in American history.
