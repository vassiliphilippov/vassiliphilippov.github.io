Etymology

The word zonked likely comes from the slang verb zonk, which meant 'to hit' or 'to stun', and from the idea of being 'knocked out'. A helpful way to remember it is to imagine tiredness hitting you so hard you fall asleep, which is why zonked means extremely tired, asleep, or out of it.
