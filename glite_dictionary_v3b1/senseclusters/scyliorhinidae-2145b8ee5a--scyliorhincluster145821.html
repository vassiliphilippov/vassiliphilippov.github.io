Etymology

Scyliorhinidae comes from the old Greek name Scyliorhinus plus the family ending -idae, and Scyliorhinus mixes a word for 'dog' and rhino- meaning 'nose', as in 'rhino' in 'rhinoceros'. So the name gives the image of 'dog-nosed' small sharks and helps explain why this family is the group of catsharks that live on the sea floor.
