Etymology

The expression uh-oh, Spaghetti-O likely comes from the small warning uh-oh being turned into a playful rhyme by adding the silly food name Spaghetti-O, maybe from the canned snack or just for the funny sound. By turning uh-oh into a rhyme, the phrase makes a little problem feel light and joking, so people use it for mild 'surprise' or 'concern'.
