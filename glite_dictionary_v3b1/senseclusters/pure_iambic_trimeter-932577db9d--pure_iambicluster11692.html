Etymology

pure iambic trimeter creates a clear sound image in poetry: iambic is a repeating 'short-long' beat and trimeter means three pairs, so the line has six beats. The word pure means 'no changes' are allowed, so the line keeps that exact 'short-long' rhythm, and that is why the phrase refers to a very strict rhythmic line.
