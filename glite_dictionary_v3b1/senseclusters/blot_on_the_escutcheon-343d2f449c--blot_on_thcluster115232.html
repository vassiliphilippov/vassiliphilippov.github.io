Etymology

The phrase blot on the escutcheon comes from old family shields: a blot is a dark mark and an escutcheon is the shield that shows a family's symbols, and a mark would spoil the shield's look. So a 'blot on the escutcheon' means a 'stain' on someone's 'reputation' or record that hurts how people see them.
