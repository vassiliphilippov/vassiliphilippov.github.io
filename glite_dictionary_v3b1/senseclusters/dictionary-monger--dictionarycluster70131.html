Etymology

dictionary-monger combines dictionary ('a book of word meanings') with monger ('seller'), a word you also see in fishmonger and warmonger. So a dictionary-monger is someone who 'sells' or pushes definitions on others, which means a person who is too eager to correct or argue about words.
