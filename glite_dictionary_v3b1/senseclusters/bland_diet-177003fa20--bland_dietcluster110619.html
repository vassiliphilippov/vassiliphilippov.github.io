Etymology

bland diet comes from the idea that bland means plain and not spicy and diet means what you eat. Doctors often tell people to eat simple foods like rice, toast, or boiled vegetables so the stomach can rest, so the phrase now means 'a diet of mild foods that won't irritate the stomach'.
