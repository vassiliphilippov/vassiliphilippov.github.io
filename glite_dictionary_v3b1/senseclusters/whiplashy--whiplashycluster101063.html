Etymology

whiplashy comes from whiplash, made of whip ('a quick strike') and lash ('a sudden, strong move'), and the ending -y meaning 'having the quality of'. So whiplashy describes something that feels sudden, sharp, or jerky, like the quick snap of a whip.
