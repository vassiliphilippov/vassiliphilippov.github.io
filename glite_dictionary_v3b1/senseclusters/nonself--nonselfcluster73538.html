Etymology

nonself comes from the prefix non- meaning 'not' and self meaning 'the person', and it is used to translate the Buddhist word anatta meaning 'no-self'. So in Buddhism nonself means there is no fixed, unchanging 'I', which is why the idea helps people let go of attachments.
