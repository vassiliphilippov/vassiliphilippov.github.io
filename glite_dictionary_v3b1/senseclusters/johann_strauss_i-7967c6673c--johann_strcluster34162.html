Etymology

Johann Strauss I led dance bands in early 1800s Vienna and helped make the 'waltz' very popular, creating the lively dance music people still know today. He was the father of Johann Strauss II, so his name is remembered as the first Strauss who started the waltz craze at Viennese balls.
