Etymology

Anthurium scherzerianum gets its name from older words: Anthurium combines the ideas of 'flower' and 'tail' because the plant has a long, tail-like spike, and scherzerianum honors the collector Scherzer. So the name gives a simple picture of the bright red plant with a twisted, tail-like bloom.
