Etymology

accommodationism comes from accommodate ('to make room' or 'adjust') plus the ending -ism ('a belief or policy'). Think of someone moving furniture to make space and avoid a fight: they give a little to keep the peace. So, accommodationism here means a policy of compromise to prevent conflict.
