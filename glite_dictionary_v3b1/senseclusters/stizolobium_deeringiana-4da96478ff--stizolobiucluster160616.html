Etymology

Stizolobium deeringiana may come from the way plant names are made: Stizolobium points to the vine's long, hairy seedpods, and deeringiana is 'named after' a person called Deering who first described or introduced the plant. So the name helps you remember a hairy-podded vine linked to that person.
