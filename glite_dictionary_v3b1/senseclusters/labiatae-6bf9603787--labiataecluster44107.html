Etymology

Labiatae comes from the Latin word labia, which means 'lips', because many flowers in this group have two parts of the flower that look like small 'lips'. So, Labiatae was the old name for the mint family and helps you remember plants like mint, basil, and sage by their lip-shaped flowers.
