Etymology

epikeia comes from an old Greek word that meant 'reasonableness' or 'fairness', made from epi- ('on' or 'in addition') and a root meaning 'fitting' or 'appropriate'. So, epikeia is the idea of adding fairness and flexibility on top of a strict law to reach a just outcome.
