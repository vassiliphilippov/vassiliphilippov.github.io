Etymology

Camassia leichtlinii takes its first part, Camassia, from the word 'camas', the sweet bulbs that Native people dug and ate, so you can picture people digging up round, tasty bulbs. The second part, leichtlinii, honors a plant collector named Leichtlin who helped bring the plant to gardeners. So the full name helps you remember this blue-flowered meadow plant.
