Etymology

A helpful way to remember the name Zestril is to see it as zest ('energy' or the peel of a lemon) plus a common drug ending like -pril or -ril. That's why Zestril is an easy name to link to the medicine lisinopril, a drug doctors use for high blood pressure and after heart attacks.
