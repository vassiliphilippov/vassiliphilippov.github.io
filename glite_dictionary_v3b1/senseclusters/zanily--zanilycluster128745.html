Etymology

zanily comes from zany, which comes from the Italian comic helper Zanni, a funny servant in old stage plays that behaved in silly ways. The ending -ly turns it into a word that shows how something is done, so to act zanily is to act 'silly' or 'comical', like a playful clown.
