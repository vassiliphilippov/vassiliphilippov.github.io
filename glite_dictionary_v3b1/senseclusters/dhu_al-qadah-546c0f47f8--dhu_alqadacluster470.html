Etymology

Dhu al-Qadah comes from Arabic words that mean 'the one of sitting'. Dhu means 'the one who has' and al-Qadah comes from a word for 'sitting' or 'staying in camp'. One popular story says people would 'sit' in their camps and stop fighting during this month, so Dhu al-Qadah came to mean a sacred month when warfare is 'forbidden'.
