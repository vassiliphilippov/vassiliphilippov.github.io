Etymology

Pipistrellus subflavus may come from old words meaning 'little bat' and 'somewhat yellow'. Pipistrellus points to a small bat and subflavus points to a pale yellow colour, so the name gives a simple picture of the small, tri-coloured 'bat' it names today.
