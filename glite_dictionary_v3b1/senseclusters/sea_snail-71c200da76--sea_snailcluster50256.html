Etymology

sea snail gets its name from how this small fish looks and acts: it has a round, tadpole-like body and special fins under its belly that work like a suction cup, so it clings to rocks like a land snail. That's why sea snail now means a small fish that sticks to surfaces with 'sucker' fins.
