Etymology

deuteranopia comes from Greek parts deuter- meaning 'second' and -opia meaning 'vision', the same -opia in myopia. So, it names a color problem caused by a weak or missing 'second' type of color-sensing cell in the eye, the one for green, and that is why people with deuteranopia have trouble telling red and green apart.
