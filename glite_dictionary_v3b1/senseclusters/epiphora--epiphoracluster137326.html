Etymology

Epiphora comes from Greek parts epi- 'on' or 'at' and phora 'to carry', and the same 'phor' appears in metaphor. So epiphora is like 'carrying' the same words to the ends of sentences, which is why it means repeating a word or phrase at the end to make a strong effect.
