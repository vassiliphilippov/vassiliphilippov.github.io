Etymology

brokenheartedness comes from the parts broken ('damaged'), heart ('love' or 'feeling') and the ending -ness (which makes a state). So, brokenheartedness is the state of having a 'broken heart' — deep sorrow after losing or losing touch with someone you love, much like heartbreak.
