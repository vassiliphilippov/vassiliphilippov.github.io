Etymology

will-o'-the-wisp comes from folk tales of a little light seen over wet ground; people called it a wisp ('bundle of straw' torch) and thought a will or wile made it a 'trick' or ghost. So a will-o'-the-wisp is a pale, flickering light that seems to lead travellers away.
