Etymology

The word intermittence comes from the parts inter- meaning 'between' and mittere meaning 'to send', and the mit part is the same you see in familiar words like transmit or permit. So, think of things being 'sent between' times that stop and start, which is why intermittence means 'stopping and starting' or happening at 'irregular' times.
