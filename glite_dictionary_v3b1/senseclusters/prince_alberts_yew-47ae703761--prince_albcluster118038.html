Etymology

Prince Albert's yew is named after Prince Albert, who helped make formal Victorian gardens popular, and gardeners used his name for this small yew because its elegant, hanging branches fitted that garden style. So, the name reminds you that it is a 'small ornamental yew' with partly drooping branches.
