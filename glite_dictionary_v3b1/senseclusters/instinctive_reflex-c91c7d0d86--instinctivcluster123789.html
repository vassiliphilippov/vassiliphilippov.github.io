Etymology

instinctive reflex comes from simple images like a baby closing its hand around a finger or a knee that jerks when tapped; both are fast, natural movements. That's why an instinctive reflex, with instinctive as a natural urge and reflex as a quick movement, means a movement that happens 'without thinking' — a natural, automatic response.
