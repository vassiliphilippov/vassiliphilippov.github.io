Etymology

fluoxetine comes from parts of its chemical name: fluor refers to 'fluorine' (the same root you see in fluoride), and the ending -ine is a common chemical ending. So the name sounds like a medicine made in a lab, which fits because fluoxetine is an antidepressant that works by changing brain chemicals to help improve mood.
