Etymology

Harakat al-Jihad al-Islami al-Filastini is made of parts: Harakat meaning 'movement', al-Jihad and al-Islami meaning 'Islamic Jihad' or 'Islamic struggle', and al-Filastini meaning 'Palestinian'. That's why the name is a memory hook: it calls the group a Palestinian movement for 'Islamic' armed struggle, so today the phrase means a militant organization.
