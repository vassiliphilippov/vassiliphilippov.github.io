Etymology

The noun explosive trace detection describes checking for tiny bits of an explosive, where trace means a very small piece and detection means finding it with a machine. Imagine a detective looking for a fingerprint; finding a tiny 'trace' tells staff there may be danger, so the phrase means 'a way to find very small amounts of explosives'.
