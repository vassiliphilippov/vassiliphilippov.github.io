Etymology

constructive fraud uses constructive in the sense of 'built' and fraud as 'deceit'. Imagine someone who, by what they do or do not say, 'builds' a wrong picture that causes another person to make a bad choice — even if there was no wish to 'deceive', the law treats the result as fraud, so the phrase means being misleading without intent.
