Etymology

haplobiontic comes from parts that mean 'single' and 'life': haplo- 'single' and bio- 'life', with -tic making it an adjective. So a haplobiontic living thing spends most of its life in a 'single' life phase, which is why the word describes organisms with mainly one haploid stage.
