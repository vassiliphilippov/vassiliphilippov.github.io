Etymology

dish the dirt comes from the image of someone serving secrets like food: dish means to put food on a plate and dirt means dirty or bad information. One popular story says people and writers began to 'dish' up others' private problems like a meal, so today it means 'to spread gossip or reveal personal secrets'.
