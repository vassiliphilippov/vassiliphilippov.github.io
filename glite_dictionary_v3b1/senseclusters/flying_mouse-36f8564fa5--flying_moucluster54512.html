Etymology

flying mouse comes from a simple picture: this small animal has a thin skin flap between its legs that lets it glide, so it looks like a tiny mouse that seems to fly. That's why people put flying and mouse together, because the words show its small size and the way it seems to 'fly' from tree to tree.
