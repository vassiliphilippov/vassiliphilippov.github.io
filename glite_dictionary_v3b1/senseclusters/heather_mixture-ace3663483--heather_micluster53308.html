Etymology

Heather mixture comes from the plant heather, which grows on hills with many tiny flowers in mixed, soft colors, and cloth makers mixed small bits of different yarn to copy that speckled, blended look. So today heather mixture means yarn or fabric with a 'soft', 'greyish' or 'blended' mix of colors.
