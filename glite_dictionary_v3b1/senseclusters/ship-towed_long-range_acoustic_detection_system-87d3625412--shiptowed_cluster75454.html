Etymology

ship-towed long-range acoustic detection system comes from how the device works: the ship-towed gear is pulled behind a ship, the acoustic parts use sound, and the long-range idea means they listen far away to detect objects. So the phrase means 'a ship system pulled behind the ship that uses sound to find objects far away under water'.
