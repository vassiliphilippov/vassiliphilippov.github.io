Etymology

Chelonethida comes from Greek parts, with chelon- meaning 'tortoise' and -ida meaning 'group'. A helpful way to remember it is to think of these tiny false scorpions as looking a little like small, hard-shelled animals because of their flat bodies and pincers, so the name fits this group that has pincers but no stinger.
