Etymology

congenital pancytopenia breaks into congenital, meaning 'born with', and the parts pan-, cyto-, -penia, which together mean 'not enough of all cells'. Imagine a baby born with too few of every type of blood cell, and you can see why the term means 'an inherited shortage of all blood cells'.
