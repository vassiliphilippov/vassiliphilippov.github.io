Etymology

Guanyin comes from Chinese words Guān 'to observe' and Yīn 'sound', so the name means someone who 'hears the sounds' or 'hears the cries' of people in trouble. That's why Guanyin is known as a figure of great compassion and mercy who listens and helps those who suffer.
