Etymology

vestibular vein is made of two words. vestibular comes from 'vestibule', meaning 'entrance' and used for the inner ear, and vein means a blood vessel. So it paints the picture of a small blood vessel by the ear's balance organ that carries blood away, which is why it means a vein that drains those balance parts.
