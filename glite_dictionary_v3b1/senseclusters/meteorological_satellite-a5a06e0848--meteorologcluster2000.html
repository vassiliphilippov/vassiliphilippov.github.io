Etymology

meteorological satellite comes from combining meteorological, which means 'about the weather', and satellite, a machine that goes around the Earth. Picture a machine high above the clouds watching storms and sending data to people on the ground, so the phrase means 'a satellite that watches and sends information about Earth's weather'.
