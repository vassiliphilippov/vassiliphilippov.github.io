Etymology

bevatron is made from BeV (an old name meaning 'billion electron volts', a unit of energy) and the ending -tron, which you see in machine names like electron and cyclotron. So a bevatron is literally a machine for reaching 'BeV' energies — a particle accelerator that pushes protons to very high energy.
