Etymology

The word Mollah (same as mullah) comes from the Arabic word mawlā, which means 'master' or 'guardian' and was used in Persian and other Muslim languages for respected religious leaders. That's why a Mollah today is a Muslim teacher or leader who guides the community and leads prayers.
