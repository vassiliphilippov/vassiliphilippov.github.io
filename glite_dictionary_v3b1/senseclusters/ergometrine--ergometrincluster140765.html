Etymology

ergometrine comes from the name of the fungus ergot and the Greek root metra, which means 'womb' or 'uterus', plus the chemical ending -ine. So, ergometrine is literally an ergot-derived drug that works on the 'womb' to make it contract and stop bleeding, which explains its medical use.
