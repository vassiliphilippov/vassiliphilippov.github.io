Etymology

Phoenicophorium comes from Greek parts: phoenic- from an old word for 'palm' and shared with phoenix, and -phorium meaning 'bearing' or 'carrying'. So the name means something like 'palm-bearing', which fits because it is a genus of palm trees from the Seychelles.
