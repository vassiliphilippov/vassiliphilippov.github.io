Etymology

humbuggery comes from humbug plus -ery, and humbug originally meant a 'trick' or 'deception'. A helpful way to remember it is to think of humbug as something called 'nonsense' or a fake show, so humbuggery is dishonest or fake behavior meant to fool people.
