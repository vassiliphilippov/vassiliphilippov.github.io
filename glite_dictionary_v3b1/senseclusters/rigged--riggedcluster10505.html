Etymology

rigged comes from the simple verb rig, which means 'to fit out' or 'to set up', and is the same root behind rigging, the ropes and sails on a ship; imagine a boat being 'fitted out' with masts and lines. So, a rigged vessel is one that has the necessary ropes, sails, and other gear to sail.
