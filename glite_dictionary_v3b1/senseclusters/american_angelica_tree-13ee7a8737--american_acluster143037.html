Etymology

American angelica tree may get its name from the old herb angelica, a plant with white flower clusters that people long ago called 'angelic' because it looked beautiful and was used to heal. That's why a small, thorny plant with similar white flowers came to be called this, so today American angelica tree means the small, thorny shrub with clusters of white blooms.
