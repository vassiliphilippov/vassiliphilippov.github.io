ante meridiem

0.00/m6.3
Words written after a clock time to show it is before midday
adverb US / ˌæn.teɪ məˈrɪd.i.əm /

Etymology

ante meridiem comes from Latin words ante and meridiem, where ante means 'before' and meridiem means 'midday'. So, it is used with times to mean 'before midday', which is why we write a.m. for hours before 12:00.
