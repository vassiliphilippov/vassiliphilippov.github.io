Etymology

electron gun may come from the simple image of a gun that 'shoots' tiny particles called electrons. So, like a gun sending a bullet, the device 'shoots' and focuses a narrow 'beam' of electrons to make images in old TVs and microscopes, which is why the term means a tool that emits and directs electrons.
