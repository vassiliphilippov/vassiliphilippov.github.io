Etymology

true Mackem comes from a nickname for people from Sunderland. One popular story says that in old shipyards workers would say make 'em to mean 'make them' about the ships they built, and that sound turned into 'Mackem', so today true Mackem means 'a real person from Sunderland' and a proud local fan.
