Etymology

redbird cactus may come from the plant's bright red flowers that look like little birds sitting on a low cactus. Even though the flowers look pretty, the plant is very poisonous and was used in traditional medicine 'to make people vomit', so the name helps people remember this dangerous but useful shrub.
