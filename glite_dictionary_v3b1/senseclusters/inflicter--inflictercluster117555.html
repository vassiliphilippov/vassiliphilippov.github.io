Etymology

Inflicter comes from inflict, which joins in- 'into' and a root that meant 'to strike', the same root you see in conflict and afflict. So an inflicter is someone who 'strikes' harm or pain onto another person or thing, in other words someone who causes something unpleasant or painful.
