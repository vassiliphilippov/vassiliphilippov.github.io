Etymology

otaku lifestyle comes from the Japanese word otaku, which once meant 'your home' and later described people who spent a lot of time at home on hobbies. One popular story says that picture of someone living with anime, manga, and games turned into the idea of an 'otaku lifestyle' — a life centered on those fan activities.
