Etymology

solemnization comes from an old Latin word sollemnis, which meant 'ceremonial' or 'official', and you can see the same root in the English word solemn, meaning 'serious'. So, solemnization is the formal, public carrying out of a ceremony, with the serious rituals that make it official.
