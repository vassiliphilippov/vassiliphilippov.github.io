Etymology

jumping plant louse is the name for a very small insect that lives on plants, feeds on their sap, and uses strong back legs to jump away. The name gives a clear picture: a 'louse' meaning 'small pest' on a 'plant' that 'jumps', so the words describe what the insect is and does today.
