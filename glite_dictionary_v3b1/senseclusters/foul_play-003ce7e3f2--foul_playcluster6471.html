Etymology

Foul play likely comes from putting together foul (meaning dirty or unfair) and play (an action in a game). Imagine a referee shouting 'foul!' when a player cheats or hits someone; that clear image of a wrong or violent action is why foul play now means 'unlawful, dishonest, or violent behavior' that suggests a crime.
