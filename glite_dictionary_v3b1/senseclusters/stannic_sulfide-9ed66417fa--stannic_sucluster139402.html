Etymology

Stannic sulfide is a name made from two parts: stannic (from an old word for 'tin') and sulfide (a compound of 'sulfur'), so the name points to a tin-and-sulfur substance. It forms bright yellow crystals, so it has been used as a 'pigment' to give lacquer and ceramics a warm yellow color.
