Etymology

agent-in-place begins with agent, from an old Latin word meaning 'to do', and is the same root as act and action, so an agent is 'one who acts'. The parts in and place mean 'inside' and 'location', so an agent-in-place is someone put 'inside' an organization to act quietly and get information.
