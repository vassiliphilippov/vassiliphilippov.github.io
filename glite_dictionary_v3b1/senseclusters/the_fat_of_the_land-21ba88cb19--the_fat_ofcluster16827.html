Etymology

The phrase the fat of the land may come from old farming images: the fat meant rich, fatty animals and crops, and the best part of a farm was the land that made them. So, to live on the fat of the land means to enjoy the richest, best things a place can give, like a 'reward' for hard work or good luck.
