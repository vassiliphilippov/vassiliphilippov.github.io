Etymology

Cassia auriculata gets its name from two parts: Cassia, an old name for plants with useful, scented bark, and auriculata, a Latin word meaning 'little ear' because parts of its flowers or leaves look like tiny ears. That's why the name describes this Indian shrub with bright yellow flowers and bark used in tanning and traditional medicine.
