Etymology

The term foley sound is named after sound artist Jack Foley, who in old movies re-created noises live while watching the picture so the sound matched the action. He made everyday sounds like footsteps, doors and cloth to make the film feel real, and that's why today 'foley sound' means a sound specially made and recorded to 'match what you see on screen'.
