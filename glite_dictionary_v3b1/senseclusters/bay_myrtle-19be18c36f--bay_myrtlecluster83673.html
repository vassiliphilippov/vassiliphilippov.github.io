Etymology

bay myrtle may come from the two plants it reminds people of: its leaves smell like bay and its shape and berries look like a myrtle bush. Early settlers used the waxy berries to make 'candles' and the strong smell for cooking and home remedies, so the name came to mean a small scented shrub used for wax and scent.
