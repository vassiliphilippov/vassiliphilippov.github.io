Etymology

stink like a mackerel comes from the very strong smell of the fish called a mackerel when it is left too long and goes bad. That's why saying stink or stink like a mackerel means something is 'very smelly' or that a situation 'seems suspicious', because the bad smell makes the problem obvious.
