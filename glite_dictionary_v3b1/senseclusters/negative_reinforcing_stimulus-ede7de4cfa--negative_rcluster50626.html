Etymology

The term negative reinforcing stimulus comes from simple scenes where someone takes something away after a behaviour. Here negative means 'taken away', reinforcing means it changes how someone acts, and stimulus is the thing removed, so the phrase names something whose removal helps 'decrease' an unwanted behaviour.
