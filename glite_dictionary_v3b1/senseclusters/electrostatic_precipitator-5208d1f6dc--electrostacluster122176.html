Etymology

electrostatic precipitator gets its name from how the machine works: it gives tiny dust particles an electric charge so they stick to metal plates and fall out of the gas. The word electrostatic means 'electric charge' and precipitator comes from a word meaning 'to fall', so the name describes 'a device that removes dust from gas'.
