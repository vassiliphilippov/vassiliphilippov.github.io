Etymology

Branchiopoda comes from Greek parts branchi- meaning 'gills' and -poda meaning 'feet', and you can see -poda in the word tripod which means 'three feet'. These small water animals have many leg-like parts that often work as gills, so the name literally says 'gill-feet'.
