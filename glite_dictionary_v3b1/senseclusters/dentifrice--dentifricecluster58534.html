Etymology

The word dentifrice comes from Latin parts that mean 'tooth' and 'to rub'. Dent- means 'tooth', the same root as in dentist and dental, and -frice comes from a word meaning 'to rub', so a dentifrice is a paste you use to rub and clean your teeth.
