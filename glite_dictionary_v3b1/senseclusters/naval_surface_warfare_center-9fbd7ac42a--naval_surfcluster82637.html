Etymology

Naval Surface Warfare Center is a literal name made of four parts: naval (about ships), surface (the part of the sea where ships sail), warfare (fighting), and center (a place where people work). So it refers to a U.S. organization that 'supports' the Navy with science and engineering for surface ships and their weapons.
