Etymology

Francisco José de Goya y Lucientes began as a court painter making polite royal portraits, but later he created dark, powerful pictures and prints that showed war and human fear. That sharp change is the memory hook: it helps explain why people call him both the last 'Old Master' and the first 'modern' artist.
