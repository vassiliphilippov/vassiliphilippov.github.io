Etymology

order Pycnogonida gets its name from Greek words: pycno- meaning 'thick' and -gonida meaning 'knee', a vivid image of their strong leg joints. These long-legged sea creatures are often called 'sea spiders', so the name helps you picture their heavy-looking leg joints and remember the group.
