Etymology

strap hinge gets its name from the way it looks: two long, narrow pieces of metal, like straps, join to make a hinge so a door or lid can turn. The word strap points to the long metal piece and hinge is the moving part, so the name simply describes a hinge that lets something 'open' or 'close'.
