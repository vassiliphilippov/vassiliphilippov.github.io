Etymology

soft-soap comes from the two simple words soft and soap, giving the image of gently washing someone to make them 'soft' and easy to handle. A helpful way to remember it is to think of using kind but 'fake' praise to 'soften' a person, which is why soft-soap means to try to persuade someone by flattery.
