Etymology

witlessness comes from the old English root wit, which meant a 'quick mind' or 'knowledge', plus the ending -less, which means 'without'. Think of witty, which means 'clever', so witlessness is literally being 'without wit', and that explains why it means lacking cleverness or intelligence.
