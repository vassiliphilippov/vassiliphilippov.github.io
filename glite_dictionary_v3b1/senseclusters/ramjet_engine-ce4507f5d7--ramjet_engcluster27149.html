Etymology

ramjet engine comes from the words ram and jet: the fast aircraft 'rams' air into the engine so the air is 'squeezed' and burned to make a hot 'jet' of gas that 'pushes' the aircraft. That's why a ramjet engine has no moving parts and must already be moving very fast to work.
