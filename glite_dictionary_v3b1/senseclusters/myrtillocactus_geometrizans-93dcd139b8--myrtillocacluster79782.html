Etymology

Myrtillocactus geometrizans comes from two Latin parts: Myrtillo- meaning 'small berry' and geometrizans meaning 'making shapes' or 'geometric'. So the name paints a simple picture of a cactus with little berry-like fruit and a tall, very geometric branching shape, which is why the name fits the plant today.
