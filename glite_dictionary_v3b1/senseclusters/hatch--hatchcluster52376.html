hatch

9/m1.9
The time when young animals come out of eggs
noun US / hætʃ /

Etymology

hatch (the event of young leaving an egg) is the same word as the verb 'to hatch' and simply names the moment the egg 'opens' or 'breaks out', so the noun points to the event when the young actually come out.
