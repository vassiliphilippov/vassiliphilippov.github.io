Etymology

The word thumbnuts is a playful insult made from thumb ('the big finger') and nuts ('a foolish or crazy person'), so it creates a silly image of someone who messes things up with their thumbs. So, calling someone a thumbnuts means they acted foolishly or like an incompetent person.
