Etymology

The idiom bawl one's eyes out comes from a strong picture: bawl means to cry loudly and eyes out makes you imagine tears pouring from the eyes. One popular story says people used this image to show someone crying so much it looked like their eyes were pouring out, so it now means 'to cry very loudly and for a long time'.
