Etymology

Armed Islamic Group comes from the French name Groupe Islamique Armé, used when a group of armed fighters formed in Algeria in 1992. The words armed (carrying weapons), Islamic (connected to Islam) and group (a number of people) create the clear image of a group using weapons with an Islamic aim, so the name refers to that organisation.
