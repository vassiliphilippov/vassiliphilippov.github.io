Etymology

sweet granadilla comes from Spanish: granadilla is a little form of granada, the word for 'pomegranate', because the fruit has many small seeds that look like a tiny pomegranate. The word 'sweet' shows this passionfruit is very sweet and fragrant, so the name means a small, sweet, pomegranate-like fruit.
