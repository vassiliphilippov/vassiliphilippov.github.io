Etymology

phantasy world comes from the idea of a whole phantasy, an imagined story, and world, a place, that a person makes inside their mind. So when someone is said to be in a phantasy world, they are thinking the imagined place is real, meaning 'a world that exists only in someone's imagination' or 'unable to accept the truth'.
