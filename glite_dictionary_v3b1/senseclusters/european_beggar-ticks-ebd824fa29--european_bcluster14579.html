Etymology

The name European beggar-ticks comes from the plant's hooked seeds that cling to clothing and animal fur as people walk past. The parts beggar and ticks show the image: the seeds grab on like a 'beggar' holding tight or like a small 'tick', so the name helps you remember the seeds 'stick' to passersby.
