Etymology

The word Antirrhinum comes from Greek parts anti- and rhino-, with rhino- meaning 'nose' and found in the word rhinoceros. So the name refers to the snapdragon's nose- or face-like flowers that open and close, helping you remember these bright, oddly shaped blooms.
