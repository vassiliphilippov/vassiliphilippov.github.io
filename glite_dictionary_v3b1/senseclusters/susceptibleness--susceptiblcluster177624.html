Etymology

Susceptibleness comes from a root that means 'to take' — the same -cept- you find in accept, and the ending -ible means 'able'. So susceptibleness is the quality of being able to 'take on' feelings, ideas, or illness, which is why it means 'easily affected or influenced'.
