Etymology

megacardia comes from two Greek parts: mega- meaning 'large' and -cardia meaning 'heart'. Think of mega as 'very big' and cardio as in 'cardio exercise', so megacardia literally means a 'large heart', which is why doctors use it for an abnormally enlarged heart.
