Etymology

somniferous comes from two Latin parts: somni- from 'somnus' meaning 'sleep' and -ferous meaning 'bearing' or 'carrying', like in transfer ('to carry across'). So, something somniferous literally 'carries sleep', which is why it means 'causing sleep' today.
