Etymology

Ossetian as a name for the people or the language comes from the region Ossetia, a word passed into English through local and Georgian speech about the Alans, the group's ancestors. The ending -ian means 'from', like in Italian, so Ossetian refers both to the people from Ossetia and to their language.
