Etymology

Geranium molle gets its name from old words: geranium comes from a Greek word meaning 'crane' because the plant's seed pod looks like a crane's beak, and molle is Latin for 'soft', which describes its soft leaves. So the name helps you picture this small wild geranium as a plant with a beak-like seed pod and soft leaves.
