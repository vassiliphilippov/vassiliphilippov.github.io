Etymology

small loan company comes from small shops or offices that gave quick, smallloans of cash to people banks would not serve. Because they made tiny, fast loans to those left out, the phrase now means 'a company that lends small amounts, often to people who cannot get bank loans and sometimes at higher interest'.
