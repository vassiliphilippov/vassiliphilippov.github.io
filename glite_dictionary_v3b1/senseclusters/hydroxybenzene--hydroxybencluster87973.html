Etymology

hydroxybenzene comes from two parts: hydroxy (from Greek for 'water', used in chemistry to name an 'OH' group) and benzene (the name for a common ring-shaped chemical). So hydroxybenzene means a benzene with an hydroxy 'OH' group — this is the chemical called 'phenol', which explains why it is a small but toxic substance used in manufacturing and disinfectants.
