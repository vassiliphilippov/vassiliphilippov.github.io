Etymology

corked comes from cork, the bark used to stop bottles, which comes from the old Latin word cortex meaning 'bark'. A helpful way to remember the tasting meaning is that a bad cork can spoil a wine's smell and taste, so a wine that tastes wrong is called 'corked'.
