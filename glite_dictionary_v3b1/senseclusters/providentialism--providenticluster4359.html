Etymology

Providentialism comes from the parts pro- 'before' and videre 'to see', and is related to the simple word provide, so it carries the idea of 'seeing ahead'. A helpful image is of God or a higher power that 'sees ahead' and guides events, so providentialism is the belief that life events are guided by that care.
