Etymology

Gay-Lussac is the name of a French chemist, Joseph Louis Gay-Lussac, who studied gases and helped first isolate boron in the early 1800s. So when you see Gay-Lussac on a law or experiment, it means the idea comes from that scientist and is about how gases behave.
