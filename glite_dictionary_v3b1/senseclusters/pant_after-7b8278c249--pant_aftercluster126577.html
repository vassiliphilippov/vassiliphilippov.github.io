Etymology

pant after gives a simple picture: imagine a dog or a runner with its tongue out and chest moving fast right after hard work. The part pant shows the hard breathing and after shows it happens following the effort, so pant after means 'to breathe hard and quickly because you have just exercised or are excited'.
