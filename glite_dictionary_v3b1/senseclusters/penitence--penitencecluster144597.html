Etymology

penitence comes from the Latin word paenitentia, from paenitere meaning 'to be sorry' or 'to regret'. It is the same root as penitent and penance, so think of someone who is sorry and wants to make things right — that is why penitence means the deep regret you feel for something wrong you have done.
