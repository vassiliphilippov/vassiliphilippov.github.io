Etymology

tantalizingly comes from the old Greek story of Tantalus, a king who was punished by standing under fruit and water that moved away whenever he tried to take them, a strong image of something very close but he could not reach. So, tantalizingly, from tantalize, means 'in a way that teases' or 'tempts' and describes something attractive but hard to get.
