Etymology

hamartia comes from an old Greek verb hamartanein, which means 'to miss the mark' or 'make a mistake'. A helpful image is a hero aiming at a goal but missing — that inner 'mistake' or character error leads to their downfall, so hamartia means the tragic flaw that causes a hero to fail.
