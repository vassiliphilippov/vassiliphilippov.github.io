Etymology

Jesum Crow may come from people changing the holy name Jesus Christ so they would not swear, turning it into a safer-sounding shout. They kept the sharp sound by using the ordinary word crow instead of 'Christ', and so Jesum Crow became an old-fashioned exclamation used 'to express surprise' or disbelief.
