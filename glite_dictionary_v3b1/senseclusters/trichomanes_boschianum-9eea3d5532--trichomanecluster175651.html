Etymology

Trichomanes boschianum may get its name from the Greek root trich- meaning 'hair' and from the name Bosch, which the species part boschianum honors. The fern's very thin, hair-like fronds are delicate and almost see-through, so the name gives a clear picture to help you remember this small, fragile plant.
