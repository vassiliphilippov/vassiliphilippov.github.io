Etymology

Refinance a mortgage comes from the simple idea of replacing an old mortgage with a new one, like swapping an old deal for a better deal. People do this to get a lower interest rate or smaller monthly payments, so refinance a mortgage means 'to replace your home loan to get better terms'.
