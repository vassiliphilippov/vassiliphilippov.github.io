Etymology

rice-grain fritillary comes from its tiny flowers that look like a rice-grain and from fritillary, a word used for plants or butterflies with a 'spotted' or 'checkered' pattern. So the name helps you picture this small, green-and-purple 'bell-shaped' flower.
