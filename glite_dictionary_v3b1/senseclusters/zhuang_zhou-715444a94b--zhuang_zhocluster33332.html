Etymology

Zhuang Zhou is the name of a Chinese thinker from about the 4th century BCE who is often called Zhuangzi. One famous story says he dreamed he was a butterfly and then could not tell if he was a man dreaming he was a butterfly or a butterfly dreaming he was a man. That clear image explains why his writings are a main part of 'Taoist' ideas about change and what is real.
