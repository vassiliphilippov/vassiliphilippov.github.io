Etymology

Neohygrophorus angelesianus is made of parts that paint a picture: neo- ('new'), hygro- ('moisture'), -phorus ('bearer') and angelesianus ('from Los Angeles'). Because this small mushroom grows where snow melts and has wet, wine-colored gills, the name helps you remember a 'new moisture-bearer' fungus found near the mountains of Los Angeles.
