Etymology

Nahuatl language gets its name from the people called the Nahua; one popular story says Nahuatl comes from a native word meaning 'clear speech' or 'good sound', so the name points to their way of speaking. That's why the name refers to the language spoken by the Nahua people of Mexico today.
