Etymology

common bog rosemary comes from the plant's look and home. People called it bog rosemary because its needle-like leaves reminded them of kitchen rosemary, and they added common when this was the usual kind found in local bogs. So the name points to a 'plant' that looks like rosemary and lives in wet bogs.
