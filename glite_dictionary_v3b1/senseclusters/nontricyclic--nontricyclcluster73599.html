Etymology

nontricyclic comes from the parts non- 'not', tri- 'three', and cyclic 'circle' or 'ring', like in tricycle. So it literally means 'not three-ring', and it names an antidepressant that is not the older 'three-ring' (tricyclic) type and therefore works in a different way.
