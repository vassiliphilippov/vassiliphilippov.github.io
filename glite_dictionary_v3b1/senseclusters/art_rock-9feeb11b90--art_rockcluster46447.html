Etymology

art rock: The noun art rock comes from musicians who treated rock music as art: they mixed ideas from jazz and classical and used new, unusual sounds to make albums for careful 'listening' rather than for dancing. That's why today art rock means rock music that focuses on creative ideas and 'listening' instead of dancing.
