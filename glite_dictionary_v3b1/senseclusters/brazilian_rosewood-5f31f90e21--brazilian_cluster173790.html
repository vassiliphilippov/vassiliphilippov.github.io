Etymology

Brazilian rosewood comes from two simple facts: the trees are Brazilian (they grow in Brazil) and the wood was called rosewood because it has a dark red color and, when cut, a sweet, rose-like smell. That's why the name means a rare, valuable 'wood' used for fine furniture and musical instruments.
