Etymology

The noun counter conditioning comes from the simple image of using one kind of training to work against a learned reaction. The word counter means 'against' and conditioning means 'teaching a response', so you teach a new calm reaction to a thing that once caused fear, and that is why the term means 'learning a different response'.
