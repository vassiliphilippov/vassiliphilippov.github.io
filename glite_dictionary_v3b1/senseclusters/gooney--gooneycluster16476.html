Etymology

The word gooney probably comes from sailors' slang gooney bird, a name for the albatross because the bird looks clumsy when it walks on land. The middle part goony meant 'silly' or 'clumsy', so it's easy to remember that a gooney is a large albatross sailors often saw at sea.
