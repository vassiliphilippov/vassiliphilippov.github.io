Etymology

electric light comes from the simple picture of a light that works by electric power: early bulbs used electricity to make a thin wire glow inside a glass bulb. Seeing a dark room suddenly lit by this glowing bulb made people call the device an 'electric light', so today the phrase means 'a lamp that produces light using electricity'.
