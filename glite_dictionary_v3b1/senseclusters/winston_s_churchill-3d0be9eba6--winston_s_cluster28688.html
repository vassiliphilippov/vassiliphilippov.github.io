Etymology

Winston S. Churchill brings to mind a determined wartime leader often pictured with a hat and a cigar, giving strong speeches to encourage people. He led Britain through World War II and won a Nobel Prize for his writing, so his name now stands for 'determined leadership' and 'powerful speaking'.
