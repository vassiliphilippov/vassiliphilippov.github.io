Etymology

electric arc comes from the image of a bright, curved bridge of electricity that jumps between two metal points. The word electric means power from electricity and arc means a curved line or bridge, so an electric arc is 'a bright flow of electricity' that makes strong light and heat, like the spark you see in welding or lightning.
