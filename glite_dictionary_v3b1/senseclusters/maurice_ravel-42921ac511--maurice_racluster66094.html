Etymology

Maurice Ravel was a French composer best known for the piece 'Boléro', which builds from one simple, repeating rhythm as more instruments join and the music grows. Thinking of that steady beat slowly getting louder helps you remember Ravel and his gift for clear, bright orchestral sound.
