Etymology

Italian sonnet comes from Italian poets like Petrarch, who made a clear two-part shape: an eight-line block called the octave and a six-line block called the sestet. The octave often shows a 'problem' and the sestet gives an 'answer', so the form is remembered as a sonnet that turns from question to reply.
