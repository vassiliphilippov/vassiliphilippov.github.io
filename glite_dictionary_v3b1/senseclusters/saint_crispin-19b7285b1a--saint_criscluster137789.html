Etymology

Saint Crispin comes from a popular story about two brothers who made shoes and quietly taught people about their Christian faith while they worked, and who were later killed for those beliefs. Because they were shoemakers, people began to call Saint Crispin the 'patron saint' of 'shoemakers', so his name is used to honor the craft and dedication of shoemakers.
