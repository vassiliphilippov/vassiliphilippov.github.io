Etymology

calumniatory comes from the Latin word calumnia, which meant 'false charge' or 'trick', and it is the same root as the English noun calumny. A helpful way to remember calumniatory is to think of a false claim made to hurt someone's name, and that is why it means statements that are false and meant to damage a person's reputation.
