Etymology

The word AIAI comes from the Arabic phrase Al-Ittihad al-Islami, which means 'Islamic Union' or 'Islamic Unity'. The letters AIAI are the first letters of those Arabic words, and the name 'Islamic Union' shows the group wanted to bring people together under a religious cause, which helps explain why it acted as an armed group.
