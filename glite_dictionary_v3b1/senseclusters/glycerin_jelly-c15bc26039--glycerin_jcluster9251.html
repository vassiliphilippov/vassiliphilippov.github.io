Etymology

glycerin jelly comes from the way the substance looks and works: a clear, soft mix of glycerin and gelatin that acts like a sticky jelly and holds tiny samples under a microscope. Because this jelly keeps the sample moist and in place, labs use it to 'put samples on a slide' and 'preserve' them, so the name refers to that specific mounting mixture.
