Etymology

The idiom cool story bro began as an online joke where people answered a long or silly tale with the flat words cool, story, bro. Because the reply sounds polite but shows no real interest, it came to mean 'to dismiss' or 'not impressed', so today cool story bro is used sarcastically to show boredom.
