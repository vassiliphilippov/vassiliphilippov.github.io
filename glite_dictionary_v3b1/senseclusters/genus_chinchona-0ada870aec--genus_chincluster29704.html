Etymology

genus Chinchona comes from a story about the Countess of Chinchón in Peru: she was said to be cured of a bad 'fever' by the tree's bark, so people named the tree Chinchona after her. That bark later gave the medicine 'quinine', so today genus Chinchona means the group of trees whose bark was used to treat 'fever' and 'malaria'.
