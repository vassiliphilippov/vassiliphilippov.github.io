Etymology

The word fucose comes from the name of a brown seaweed, Fucus, and the chemical ending -ose meaning 'sugar', so you can think of it as a 'seaweed sugar'. That's why fucose is a sugar first found in seaweeds and now found on cell surfaces where it helps cells communicate.
