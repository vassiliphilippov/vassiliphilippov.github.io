Etymology

The word servomotor comes from the parts servo (from a Latin word meaning 'servant' or 'helper', like the English word serve) and motor (from a word meaning 'move' or 'mover'). So a servomotor is a motor that 'serves' to control movement exactly, which is why we use it in robots and machines that need precise positions.
