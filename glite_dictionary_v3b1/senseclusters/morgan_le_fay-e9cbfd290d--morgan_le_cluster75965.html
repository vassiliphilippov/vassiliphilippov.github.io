Etymology

Morgan le Fay may come from an old way of saying Morgan plus le Fay, where le Fay means 'the fairy'. One popular story says people called her a 'fairy' because she used magic in the tales, so the name helps explain why she is a powerful enchantress in Arthurian stories.
