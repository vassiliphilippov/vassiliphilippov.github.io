Etymology

sulky demeanor comes from sulk, which means to sit apart and show unhappiness, and demeanor, which means how someone looks or acts. Think of a person with crossed arms and a downturned mouth. This image shows a 'gloomy or moody attitude', so the phrase describes someone who looks unhappy and refuses to join in.
