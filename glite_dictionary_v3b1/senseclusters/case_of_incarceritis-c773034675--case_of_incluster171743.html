Etymology

case of incarceritis is a funny phrase that mixes incarcerate (the word meaning 'to put in prison') and the medical ending -itis (a word for an 'illness'). The image is like catching a small 'illness' of being locked in, so people use it to say they feel 'stuck', 'trapped', or 'bored'.
