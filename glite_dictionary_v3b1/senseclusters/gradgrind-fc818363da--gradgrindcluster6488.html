Etymology

Gradgrind comes from Dickens' character Gradgrind in the novel Hard Times and his name includes the familiar word grind meaning 'to press or crush' or 'to work something hard'. So imagine facts being 'ground' into students' heads with no room for feeling, which is why Gradgrind means rigidly practical and unimaginative.
