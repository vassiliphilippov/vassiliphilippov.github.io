Etymology

The word swordsmith is made of sword and smith: a sword is a 'blade' and a smith is a 'metal worker' who shapes metal by hammering, as in blacksmith. So a swordsmith is someone who makes or repairs swords, which you can remember by picturing a person hammering a blade over hot coals.
