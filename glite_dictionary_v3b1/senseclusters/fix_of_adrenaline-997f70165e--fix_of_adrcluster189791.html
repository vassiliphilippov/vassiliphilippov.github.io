Etymology

Fix of adrenaline comes from drug slang where fix meant a 'small dose' and adrenaline is the body's quick 'rush' chemical. So saying you want a fix of adrenaline creates the image of taking a quick dose of excitement, which is why it means 'a sudden burst of excitement or energy'.
