Etymology

Stratiotes comes from the Greek stratiōtēs, which means 'soldier'. People called this plant the 'water soldier' because its long, sharp leaves look like little swords and stand up in rows like soldiers, so the name helps you remember it is a stiff, soldier-like water plant.
