Etymology

Stratford-on-Avon comes from old place-name parts: Strat- from a word for 'street', -ford meaning 'a shallow crossing in a river', and Avon which is an old word for 'river'. So the town's name means 'the street by the river crossing', and saying 'River Avon' is like saying 'River River'.
