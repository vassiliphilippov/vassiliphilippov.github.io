Etymology

Retama raetam comes from old Hebrew and Arabic names like rotem or retam, words people used for a desert 'broom' shrub that grows in dry places. That's why the name now means the small, white‑flowered desert shrub often mentioned in the Bible and sometimes called a 'juniper'.
