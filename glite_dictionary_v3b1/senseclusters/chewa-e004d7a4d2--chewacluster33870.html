Etymology

Chewa comes from the name the people use for themselves in a Bantu language. The language is often called Chichewa, where the Chi- part means 'language of', so Chewa names both the people and their language and helps you remember they are a group from Malawi, eastern Zambia, and northern Zimbabwe.
