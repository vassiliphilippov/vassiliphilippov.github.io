Etymology

Hippotragus niger comes from older Greek and Latin words: hippo meaning 'horse', tragus meaning 'goat', and niger meaning 'black'. The name gives a clear picture — a large, dark antelope with a horse-like head and strong horns that curve back, so the scientific name describes how the animal looks.
