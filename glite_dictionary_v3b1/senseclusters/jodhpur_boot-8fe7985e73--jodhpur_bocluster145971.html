Etymology

Jodhpur boot may come from the city Jodhpur in India, where riders wore short leather boots with a side strap and buckle for polo and horse riding. The short shape and strap helped the boot stay on and let the ankle move easily, so today jodhpur boots means 'a short riding boot'.
