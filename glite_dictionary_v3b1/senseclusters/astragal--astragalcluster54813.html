Etymology

astragal comes from the old Greek word astragalos, which meant 'ankle bone' or 'small bone', and people once used such bones as dice. The little rounded shape of that bone gave its name to a narrow decorative strip or edging on furniture and frames, so an astragal today is a small, bead-like molding.
