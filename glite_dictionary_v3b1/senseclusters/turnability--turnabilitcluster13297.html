Etymology

turnability comes from the simple word turn, which means 'to move or change direction', plus the ending -ability, which means 'can be done' or 'quality of'. So, turnability is the 'ability to turn' — how easily something can change direction, which is why people use it for cars, wheelchairs, and machines.
