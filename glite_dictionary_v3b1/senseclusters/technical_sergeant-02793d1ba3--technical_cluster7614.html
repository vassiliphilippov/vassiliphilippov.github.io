Etymology

technical sergeant comes from the words technical (skilled with machines and tools) and sergeant (a leader of soldiers). The image of a leader who manages and teaches technical work explains why the term now means 'a noncommissioned officer who supervises technical tasks and ranks below a master sergeant'.
