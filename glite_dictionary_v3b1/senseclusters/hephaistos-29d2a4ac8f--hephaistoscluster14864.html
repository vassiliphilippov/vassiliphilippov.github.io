Etymology

Hephaistos is the Greek name of the god of fire and making things from metal, the craftsman who works at the forge and makes tools, weapons, and armor. He is the Greek version of the Roman god Vulcan, whose name appears in volcano, so thinking of heat, hammering, and a glowing forge helps explain why Hephaistos is the god of metalworking.
