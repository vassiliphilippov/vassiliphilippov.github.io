Etymology

espousal comes from the verb espouse, from the old Latin root spons- meaning 'promise', and it shares this root with spouse. So when you 'espouse' an idea, or talk of its espousal, you are 'promising' your support or 'marrying' the idea, which is why the word also means adopting a cause.
