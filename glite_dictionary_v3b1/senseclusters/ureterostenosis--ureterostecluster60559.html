Etymology

Ureterostenosis breaks into three parts: uretero- meaning 'the tube that carries urine from the kidney to the bladder', steno- meaning 'narrow', and -osis meaning 'a condition'. So ureterostenosis is a condition where that tube becomes too narrow and can block the flow of urine.
