Etymology

Italian ryegrass mixes the parts Italian meaning 'from Italy' and rye plus grass meaning a 'grass' that looks 'like rye'. That's why today Italian ryegrass refers to a fast-growing grass farmers plant for 'hay', 'lawns', and to make the soil better.
