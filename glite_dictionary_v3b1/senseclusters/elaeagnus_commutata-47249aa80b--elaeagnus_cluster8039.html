Elaeagnus commutata is a plant name that comes from old words pointing to its shiny, silvery fruit and leaves. The part Elaeagnus is like the common name 'silverberry' and commutata may mean 'changed', so the name helps you remember this shrub with pale, silvery leaves and berries.
