Etymology

The noun forest goat likely comes from a simple picture: forest for its home and goat for its goat-like shape and sure-footed movement among trees. So the name helps you picture a large, shiny-coated, agile animal with long horns that lives in the woods, which is why it now means 'that kind of animal'.
