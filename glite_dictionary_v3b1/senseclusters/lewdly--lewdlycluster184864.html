Etymology

Lewdly comes from the old English word lewd, which originally meant 'not learned' or 'lay', meaning someone not a priest, and was used for people seen as 'uneducated' or 'coarse'. Because being 'uneducated' was thought of as rough or improper, lewd shifted to mean 'vulgar', so lewdly now describes acting in a 'sexually indecent' way.
