Etymology

Potamogetonaceae comes from Greek parts potamos 'river' and geiton 'neighbor', and the ending -aceae marks a plant 'family'. You can remember potamos from hippopotamus ('river horse'). So, Potamogetonaceae is the plant family of pondweeds, the 'river neighbors' that grow in ponds and slow streams.
