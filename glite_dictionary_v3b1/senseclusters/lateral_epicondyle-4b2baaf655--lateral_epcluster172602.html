Etymology

lateral epicondyle literally describes a 'bump on the side of a joint': lateral means 'side' and epicondyle means 'on the joint'. So the name helps you picture the bony bump on the outer elbow where muscles and ligaments attach, which is why doctors use it for that exact spot.
