Etymology

litotes comes from the Greek litos, meaning 'simple' or 'plain'. A helpful way to remember it is that litotes is a 'plain' way of speaking that says the negative of the opposite—like 'not bad' to mean 'good'—so the origin explains why it means saying something in a mild or less strong way.
