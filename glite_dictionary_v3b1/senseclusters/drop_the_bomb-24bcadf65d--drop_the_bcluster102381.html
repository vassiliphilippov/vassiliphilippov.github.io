Etymology

The phrase drop the bomb comes from the picture of someone dropping a bomb, causing a sudden, loud explosion that shocks people. One popular idea is that speakers used that image to talk about big, unexpected news, so to 'drop the bomb' now means to make a surprising or 'shocking' announcement.
