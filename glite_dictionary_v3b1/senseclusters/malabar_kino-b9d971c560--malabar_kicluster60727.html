Etymology

Malabar kino names the dark, sticky resin that oozes from certain trees on the Malabar coast of India, where Malabar is the place name and kino means tree resin. Because this red or black sap was used in 'medicine' and for 'tanning' leather, the name now refers to that useful resin.
