Etymology

arrant comes from an old French word errant, from the verb errer meaning 'to wander'. Later, being 'wandering' became a way to describe someone obviously bad or loose in behaviour, so arrant came to mean 'complete' or 'utter' in a bad way, as in 'arrant nonsense'.
