Etymology

Battle of Buena Vista is named after the place called Buena Vista, which in Spanish means 'good view' because the fighting took place near high ground with a wide view of the valley. So the name refers to that location and now means the 1847 battle when U.S. forces defeated Mexican forces under Santa Anna.
