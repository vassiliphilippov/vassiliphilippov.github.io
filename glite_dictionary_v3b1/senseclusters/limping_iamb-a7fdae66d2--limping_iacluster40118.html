Etymology

Limping iamb paints a picture of a walking step that is off: an iamb is a light then strong beat, but in a limping iamb the last foot is heavier and becomes a spondee or trochee. That's why poets use it to make a line feel 'uneven' or to 'draw attention' to the ending.
