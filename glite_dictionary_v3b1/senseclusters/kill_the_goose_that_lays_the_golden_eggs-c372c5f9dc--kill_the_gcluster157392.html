Etymology

The saying kill the goose that lays the golden eggs comes from an old story about a farmer who had a goose that laid a golden egg each day. He grew greedy and killed the bird to get all the gold at once, so he lost his steady source of money, which is why the phrase means to 'ruin a steady source of money or benefit'.
