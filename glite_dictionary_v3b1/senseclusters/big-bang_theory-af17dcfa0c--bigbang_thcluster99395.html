Etymology

big-bang theory The name big-bang theory was given by a scientist named Fred Hoyle who used the picture of a very small point making a huge explosion — a 'bigbang' — to help people imagine that the universe began very small and very hot and then spread out. So, the phrase means the idea that the universe started with one great explosion and has been expanding since.
