Etymology

miasm comes from the Greek word miasma meaning 'pollution' or 'stain', and people long ago believed a 'dirty' vapor could spread illness. So a miasm today is an unpleasant, unhealthy air or vapor, like a 'polluting' fog that makes a place feel sick.
