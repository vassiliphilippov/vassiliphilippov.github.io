Etymology

odd-pinnate combines pinnate, from the Latin pinna meaning 'feather', with odd, meaning 'uneven' or 'single'. So an odd-pinnate leaf looks like a feather with a single leaflet at the tip, which gives it an odd total number of leaflets.
