Etymology

The word cancerweed is made of cancer and weed. The part cancer comes from a Latin word meaning 'crab' and later was used for the disease 'cancer'. A helpful way to remember the name is that people once used this plant as a herbal remedy for lumps or growths, so the name connects the plant to the disease and to its traditional use.
