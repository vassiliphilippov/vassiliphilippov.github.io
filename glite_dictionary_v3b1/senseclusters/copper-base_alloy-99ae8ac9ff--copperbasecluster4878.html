Etymology

copper-base alloy comes from the clear image of making a new metal where copper is the base and other metals are mixed in. Imagine starting with a piece of copper and adding tin or zinc so the new metal keeps copper as its main part; that's why the term means 'an alloy whose main metal is copper'.
