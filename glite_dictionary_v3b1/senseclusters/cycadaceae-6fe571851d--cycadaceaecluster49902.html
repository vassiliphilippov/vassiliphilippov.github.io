Etymology

Cycadaceae comes from the plant name Cycas, a Greek word meaning 'palm', and the ending -aceae, which means 'family' in plant names like Rosaceae, the 'rose family'. So, Cycadaceae are the 'palm-like' family of plants, which helps explain why they look like palms today.
