Etymology

linebacker blitzing paints a clear picture on the football field: a linebacker is a defender who suddenly charges forward in a fast 'blitz' to reach the quarterback or the player with the ball. So, linebacker blitzing means that quick, aggressive rush by defenders to 'put pressure on' the play and try to stop it.
