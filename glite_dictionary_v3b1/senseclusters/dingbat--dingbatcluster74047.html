Etymology

dingbat began as a playful name for a small, odd object or a little printed ornament — basically a 'thing' with no real name. A helpful way to remember it is to imagine someone as a small, silly 'thing': people started calling odd or empty-headed people dingbats, which is why today it means a silly person.
