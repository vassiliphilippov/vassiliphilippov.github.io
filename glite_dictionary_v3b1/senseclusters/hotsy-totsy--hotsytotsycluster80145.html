Etymology

hotsy-totsy probably began as a playful rhyme built on the simple word hot, which can mean 'fashionable' or 'exciting', plus a cute repeat like -sy, similar to child rhymes such as 'itsy-bitsy'. So, hotsy-totsy came to mean something that is 'just right', stylish, or impressive.
