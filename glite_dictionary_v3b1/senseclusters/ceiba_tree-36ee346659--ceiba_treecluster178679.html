Etymology

Ceiba tree is the name of a very large tropical tree with a thick trunk and seed pods full of soft, silky 'kapok' fibers. People used those fibers to fill pillows and mattresses, and the tree's wide branches gave shade for gatherings, so the Ceiba tree came to be seen as a useful and often sacred tree.
