Etymology

Prokayotae comes from the Greek parts pro- meaning 'before' and karyon meaning 'kernel' or 'nut', like the little 'core' of a cell. So Prokayotae names bacteria and similar tiny organisms whose cells do not have a true nucleus, meaning they are 'before' or without that clear 'core'.
