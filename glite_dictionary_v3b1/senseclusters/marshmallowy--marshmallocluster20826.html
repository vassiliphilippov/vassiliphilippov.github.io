Etymology

marshmallowy comes from the sugary candy marshmallow, whose name joins marsh (a 'swamp') and mallow (a 'plant') for the plant used in old recipes. That's why marshmallowy can also mean overly sweet or sentimental, as if something tastes or feels like a very sugary treat.
