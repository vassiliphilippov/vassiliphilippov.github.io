Etymology

checkered lily: The plant name checkered lily comes from its nodding bell-shaped flowers, which show a clear square, chessboard-like pattern on the petals. The word checkered describes that look and lily tells you it's a flower, so the name means a 'lily' with a checkered 'pattern'.
