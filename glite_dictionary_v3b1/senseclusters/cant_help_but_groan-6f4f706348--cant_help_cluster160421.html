Etymology

can't help but groan paints a simple picture: you cannot stop yourself from making a long unhappy sound. When something is very annoying or disappointing, people naturally groan, so saying you can't help but groan means you are unable to stop that sound because you are 'annoyed' or 'frustrated'.
