Etymology

setaceous comes from the Latin root seta, meaning 'bristle', plus the ending -aceous, meaning 'made of' or 'like'. So setaceous means 'bristly', like the stiff hairs on a caterpillar or the bristles of a brush, which helps you remember its current meaning.
