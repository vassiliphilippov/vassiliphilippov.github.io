Etymology

Raffia farinifera gets its name from the common name raffia for the long palm fibres and the Latin word farinifera, from 'farina' meaning 'flour', because parts of the plant can look a little powdery. So the name helps you remember a palm with very long, feather-like leaves whose strong fibres are used to make ropes, baskets and mats.
