Etymology

Dentaria diphylla comes from Latin and describes how the plant looks: Dentaria means 'tooth' for its toothed roots and diphylla means 'two leaves' because the plant often has a pair of large leaves. So the name is an easy picture to remember the 'two-leaved toothwort'.
