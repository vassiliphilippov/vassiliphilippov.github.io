Etymology

endoergic reaction comes from two word parts: endo- meaning 'inside' and -ergic meaning 'work' or 'energy'. Imagine the reaction pulling energy into itself so energy moves 'in' rather than out. That's why the term means a nuclear reaction that 'absorbs' energy.
