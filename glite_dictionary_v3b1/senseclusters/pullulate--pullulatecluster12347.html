Etymology

pullulate comes from the Latin pullulare, from pullus meaning 'young'. Think of words like pullet or poultry that also come from the same root and remind us of young animals; the basic idea is 'producing young' or 'sprouting'. So to pullulate today is to multiply, sprout, or be full of life and activity.
