Etymology

Lashkar-e-Tayyiba comes from Persian/Urdu and Arabic: lashkar means 'army', -e- is a small link meaning 'of', and Tayyiba means 'good' or 'pure'. So the name literally means 'army of the pure', which is the group's chosen name.
