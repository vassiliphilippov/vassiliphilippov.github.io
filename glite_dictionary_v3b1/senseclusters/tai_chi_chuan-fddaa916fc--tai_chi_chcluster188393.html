Etymology

Tai chi chuan comes from Chinese words: Tai chi stands for the idea of two opposite forces working together and chuan means 'fist'. Long ago, fighters used slow, flowing 'fist' movements to train balance and calm the mind, so today Tai chi chuan usually means the slow exercises that improve 'balance' and 'relaxation'.
