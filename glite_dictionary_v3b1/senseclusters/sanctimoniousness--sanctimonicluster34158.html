Etymology

Sanctimoniousness comes from the Latin root sanctus meaning 'holy' and the idea of showing that holiness. Think of someone acting like a 'saint' to make themselves seem more moral than others, so sanctimoniousness means a show of being better or purer than others rather than true goodness.
