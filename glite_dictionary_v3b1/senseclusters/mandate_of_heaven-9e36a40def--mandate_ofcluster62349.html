Etymology

Mandate of Heaven comes from an old Chinese belief that Heaven was a 'higher power' that gave an emperor the 'right to rule' only if he was good. If the emperor became cruel or weak, Heaven would take that 'right' away, so losing the Mandate helped explain why rulers could be removed.
