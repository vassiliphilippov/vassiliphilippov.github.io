Etymology

Phyllostachys bambusoides comes from old Greek and Latin parts: phyllo meaning 'leaf', stachys meaning 'spike', and bambusoides meaning 'like bamboo'. This simple picture of long leaves and a bamboo-like stem helps explain why the name is used for a large, strong bamboo species today.
