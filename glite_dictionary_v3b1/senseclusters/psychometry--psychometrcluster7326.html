Etymology

psychometry also has a second meaning in the world of psychics: it uses the same roots psyche 'mind' and -metry 'measure', and people used the word for the supposed power to learn about an object or its owner by touching it. A helpful way to remember this meaning is to imagine someone trying to 'measure' an object's 'soul' with their hands.
