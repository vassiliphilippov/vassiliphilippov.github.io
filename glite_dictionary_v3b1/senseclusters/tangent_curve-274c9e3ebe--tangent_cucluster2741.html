Etymology

The noun tangent curve comes from the word tangent, which means 'to touch', and curve, a bending line. The name fits because the graph shows how the tangent value changes with the angle — imagine a wavy line that climbs very high and then falls near certain angles like cliffs, so tangent curve means the picture (graph) of y = tan x.
