Etymology

Hyoscyamus muticus may come from old Greek words: Hyoscyamus combines words for 'pig' and 'bean' because its seeds look like beans pigs might eat, and muticus means 'blunt' or 'short', describing the plant's shape. So the Latin name gives a simple picture of this poisonous herb that people have used in medicine.
