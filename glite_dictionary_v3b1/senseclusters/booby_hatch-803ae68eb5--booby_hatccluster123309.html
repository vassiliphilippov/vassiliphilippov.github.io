Etymology

Booby hatch may come from booby, an old word for 'a fool', and hatch, a small door or simple 'hutch'. Imagine a little building where foolish people were kept, and that image led to Booby hatch meaning 'a place for the mentally ill', often said jokingly or as an insult.
