Etymology

KR is a short form of Khmer Rouge, the name of a Cambodian communist group; Khmer means the people of Cambodia and Rouge is French for 'red', the color often used by communist movements. So KR refers to that group which ruled Cambodia in the 1970s and caused the deaths of many people.
