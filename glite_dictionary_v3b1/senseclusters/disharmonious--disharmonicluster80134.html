Etymology

disharmonious comes from the prefix dis- meaning 'not' and harmony meaning 'agreement' or 'pleasant sound'. Think of an orchestra with instruments out of tune that clash instead of playing together — that's why disharmonious describes people, colors, or sounds that do not agree or that sound bad together.
