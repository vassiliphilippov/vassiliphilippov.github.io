Etymology

Vedalia is a scientific genus name given to a small Australian ladybug and was likely named after a person or place linked to its discovery. A helpful way to remember Vedalia is to think of it as the little beetle 'used to fight pests', which is why the name now means a useful pest-control ladybug.
