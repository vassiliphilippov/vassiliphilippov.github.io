Etymology

The word bloodcurdling comes from the image of blood 'curdling', from curdle meaning 'to turn into lumps', like milk that goes bad. So a bloodcurdling cry or story is so terrifying it feels as if your blood is turning to lumps, which is why the word means extremely frightening.
