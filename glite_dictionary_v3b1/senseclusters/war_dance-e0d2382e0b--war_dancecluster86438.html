Etymology

war dance comes from real dances that warriors did before going into battle or after a victory. In the phrase, war is the fighting part and dance is the dancing with stomping, shouting, and showing weapons to make themselves brave and frighten the enemy. So, war dance now means a special dance done 'before battle' or to 'celebrate victory'.
