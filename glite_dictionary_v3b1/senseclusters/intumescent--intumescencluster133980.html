Etymology

The word intumescent comes from Latin parts in- meaning 'in' and tumescere meaning 'to swell', so it literally means 'swelling up'. A helpful link is the familiar word tumor, which also comes from the same root and means a swelling, so intumescent is easy to remember as 'becoming swollen' or 'puffed up'.
