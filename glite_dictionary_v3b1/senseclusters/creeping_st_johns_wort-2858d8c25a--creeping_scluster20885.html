Etymology

The name creeping St John's wort may come from the plant's way of growing and when it flowers. The word creeping shows it spreads along the ground, and St John's wort refers to a plant that often blooms around 'St John's Day', so the name tells you it's a low, spreading plant with bright yellow flowers.
