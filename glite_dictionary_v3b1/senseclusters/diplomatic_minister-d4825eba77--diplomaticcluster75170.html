Etymology

diplomatic minister may come from the simple picture of a leader sending a trusted helper to speak in another country: diplomatic means about talks between countries and minister means a helper who acts for someone else. So a diplomatic minister is an official who goes to 'represent' their government but 'rank' below an ambassador.
