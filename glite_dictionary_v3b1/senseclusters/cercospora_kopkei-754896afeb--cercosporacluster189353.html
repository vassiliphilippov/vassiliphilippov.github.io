Etymology

Cercospora kopkei gets its scientific name from a Greek word and from a person's name. The genus name Cercospora means 'tail seed' because its spores look like tiny tails, and the species name kopkei honors a scientist named Kopke, so the full name points to the fungus that causes yellow spots on sugarcane.
