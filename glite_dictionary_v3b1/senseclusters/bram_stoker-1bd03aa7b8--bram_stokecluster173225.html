Etymology

Bram Stoker worked in a London theatre and liked collecting old ghost and folk stories, and he used those spooky tales and the drama of the stage to write 'Dracula'. That's why Bram Stoker's name now stands for the writer of 'Dracula' and helped shape the modern idea of the 'vampire'.
