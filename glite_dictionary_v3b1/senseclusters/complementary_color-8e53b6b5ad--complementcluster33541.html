Etymology

Complementary color comes from the simple idea that two colors sitting opposite each other on the color wheel are complementary, meaning they 'complete' each other. Placed side by side they make each other look brighter, and when mixed as light they make white, so 'complementary' explains why these pairs balance each other visually.
