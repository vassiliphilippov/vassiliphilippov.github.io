Etymology

Lager beer takes its name from the German word 'lagern', and lager means 'to store'. Brewers used to keep beer in cool caves or cellars to rest slowly, and that cold storage made the beer clean and crisp. That's why lager beer today means beer that is aged cold for a smooth, fresh taste.
