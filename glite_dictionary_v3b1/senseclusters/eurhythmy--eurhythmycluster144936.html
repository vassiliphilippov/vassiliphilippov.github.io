Etymology

eurhythmy comes from Greek parts eu- meaning 'good' or 'well' and rhythmos meaning 'rhythm' or 'movement'. So eurhythmy literally means 'good rhythm' and refers to moving the body in time with music to show or teach musical rhythm.
