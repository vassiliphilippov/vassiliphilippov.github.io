Etymology

Epipaleolithic comes from Greek parts: epi- meaning 'after', paleo- meaning 'old', and -lithic meaning 'stone'. So it means 'after the Old Stone Age', which fits the middle Stone Age when people began moving from hunting and gathering to more settled life.
