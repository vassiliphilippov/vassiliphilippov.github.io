Etymology

inhalation anesthetic comes from two simple words: inhalation meaning 'to breathe in' and anesthetic meaning 'a drug that makes you sleep or feel no pain'. Imagine a patient with a mask breathing a gas that makes them go to sleep. That's why the term means a substance you 'breathe in' to make you sleep for surgery.
