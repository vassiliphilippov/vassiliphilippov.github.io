Etymology

flocculent comes from the Latin floccus, meaning 'tuft of wool', so it describes something that looks like small soft tufts. Think of woolly clumps or 'fluff' on a lamb or in clouds — that's why flocculent means 'soft, fluffy, or woolly'.
