Etymology

The noun calamus oil comes from the root of the calamus plant, a reed with a strong scent. People long ago crushed the root to make a scented oil for perfumes and medicine, so the phrase simply means 'oil made from the calamus plant' and explains why it refers to a fragrant ingredient today.
