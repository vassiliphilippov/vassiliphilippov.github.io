Etymology

electro-acoustic transducer is a name made from electro (electric), acoustic (sound) and transducer (a device that changes one thing into another). Think of it as a small bridge that lets 'sound' become 'electrical signals' and lets 'electrical signals' become 'sound', so the name explains why it means a device that converts between 'sound' and 'electrical signals'.
