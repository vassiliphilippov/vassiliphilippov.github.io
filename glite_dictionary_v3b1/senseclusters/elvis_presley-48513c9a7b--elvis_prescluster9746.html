Etymology

Elvis Presley was an American singer who became famous in the 1950s for his strong voice, bold hip movements, and bright stage clothes, and people called him the 'King of Rock and Roll' because he changed popular music. So today Elvis Presley makes people think of a famous, energetic rock star.
