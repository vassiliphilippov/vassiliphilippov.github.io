Etymology

dumb as a doornail may come from the old image of a heavy doornail hammered so flat it could not move or make a sound. So, because dumb used to mean 'silent' or 'not able to speak', the picture of a nailed-down, quiet thing became a way to say someone is completely unresponsive or very foolish.
