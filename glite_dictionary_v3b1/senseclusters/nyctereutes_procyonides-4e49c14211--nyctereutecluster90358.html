Etymology

Nyctereutes procyonides comes from Greek words: Nyctereutes means 'night wanderer' and procyonides means 'raccoon-like'. So the name gives the image of a dog-like animal that moves at night and looks like a raccoon, which is why we call it the 'raccoon dog' today.
