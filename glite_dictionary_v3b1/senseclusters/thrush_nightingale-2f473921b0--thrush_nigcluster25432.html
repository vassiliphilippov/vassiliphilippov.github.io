Etymology

thrush nightingale comes from how the bird looks and sounds: it is a kind of nightingale that is larger and more like a thrush, so people used both names to show its thrush-like size and nightingale song. That's why thrush nightingale today means a large nightingale with a rich song found in eastern Europe.
