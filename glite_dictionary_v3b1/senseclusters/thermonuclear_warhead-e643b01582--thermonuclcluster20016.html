Etymology

Thermonuclear warhead joins thermo, which means 'heat', and nuclear, which means 'atoms', with warhead, the front part of a missile that carries a bomb. So it pictures the missile 'head' carrying a bomb that uses heat from atoms to make a huge explosion, which is why it means 'a very powerful bomb fitted to a missile'.
