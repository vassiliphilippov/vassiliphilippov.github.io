Etymology

greater celandine comes from an old story: celandine is linked to the bird 'swallow' because the plant's bright yellow flowers appear when swallows return in spring. The word greater was used to show it is bigger than the 'lesser' celandine, so the name helps you picture a large yellow flower and remember the plant.
