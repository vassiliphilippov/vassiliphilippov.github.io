Etymology

The name Mameluke dynasty comes from the word Mameluke, which once meant 'owned' or 'slave' because the rulers began as slave soldiers who later took power. So the name reminds us that these rulers were former 'slave' soldiers who 'ruled' Egypt and Syria.
