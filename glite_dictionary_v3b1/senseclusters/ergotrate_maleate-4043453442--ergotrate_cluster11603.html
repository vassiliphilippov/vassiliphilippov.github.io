Etymology

Ergotrate Maleate may come from ergot, a fungus that grows on rye, and maleate, the kind of acid used to make the medicine. Long ago people noticed ergot substances made the 'womb' tighten, so doctors used these drugs after childbirth to make the uterus contract, and that is why the name links the fungus source with the drug's use today.
