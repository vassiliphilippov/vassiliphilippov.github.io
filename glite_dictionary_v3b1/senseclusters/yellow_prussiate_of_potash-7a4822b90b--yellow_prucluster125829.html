Etymology

yellow prussiate of potash comes from prussiate, a word linked to the blue pigment 'Prussian blue', and from potash, an old word for 'potassium'. Because this yellow salt was used to make dyes and pigments, the name stuck, and today it refers to a yellow potassium salt used in pigments and to stop powders from clumping.
