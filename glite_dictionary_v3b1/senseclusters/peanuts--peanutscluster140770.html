Etymology

The word peanuts comes from the tiny nut peanut, which is small and often cheap, so people began using peanuts to mean a 'very small amount of money' or tiny pay. A helpful way to remember it is to think of being paid only enough to buy a few peanuts. That's why when someone is paid 'peanuts' it means they earn almost nothing.
