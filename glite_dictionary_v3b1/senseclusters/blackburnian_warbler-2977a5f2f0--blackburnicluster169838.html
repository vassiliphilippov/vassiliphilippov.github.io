Etymology

One popular story says the bird Blackburnian warbler was 'named after' the English collector Blackburne, who sent examples of the bright bird to early scientists. Early scientists often gave new birds the names of the people who found or sent them, so the name simply honors the person who helped them find this colorful little bird.
