Etymology

Diapensiaceae comes from the plant name Diapensia plus the family ending -aceae, so it means 'a family of plants'. A helpful way to remember Diapensia is that it probably uses old Greek parts meaning 'five', because these plants often have five-part flowers, and that link helps explain the name for this small group of evergreen alpine plants.
