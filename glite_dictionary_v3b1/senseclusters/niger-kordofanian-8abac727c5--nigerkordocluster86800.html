Etymology

Niger-Kordofanian comes from two place names: Niger, the great West African 'river' and country, and Kordofan, a 'region' in central Sudan. It was used to name a group of languages spoken across those and other parts of sub-Saharan Africa, so the name reminds us where these languages come from.
