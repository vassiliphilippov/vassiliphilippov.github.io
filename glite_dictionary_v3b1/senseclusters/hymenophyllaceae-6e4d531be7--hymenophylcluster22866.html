Etymology

The word Hymenophyllaceae comes from Greek parts: hymen meaning 'membrane' and phyll meaning 'leaf', plus the family ending -aceae used for plant groups. These ferns have very thin, 'membrane-like' leaves, so the name helps explain their delicate, filmy look.
