Etymology

overleverage starts with over- meaning 'too much' and leverage, from lever, a long bar used 'to lift' heavy things, and in finance leverage means using 'borrowed money' to try to lift your gains. So, to overleverage is to use too much borrowed money and risk falling or losing everything if things go wrong.
