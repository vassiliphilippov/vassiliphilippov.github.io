Etymology

Luo comes from the name of the Luo people in parts of Africa and simply means 'the language of the Luo people'. A helpful way to remember it is to think of English, which also names the people and their language, so Luo refers to that group's speech in regions such as Adamawa in Nigeria and parts of Cameroon.
