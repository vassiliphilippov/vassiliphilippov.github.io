Etymology

Hibiscus moscheutos is the scientific name for the plant called the 'swamp rose mallow'; the first part, Hibiscus, marks it as a mallow and the second part, moscheutos, links it to its showy, rose-like flowers and marsh home. So the name helps you remember a big, rose-like mallow that grows in wet, marshy places.
