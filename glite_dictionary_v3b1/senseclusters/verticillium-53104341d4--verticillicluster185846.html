Etymology

Verticillium comes from the Latin root verticillus, which means 'small whorl' or 'little circle'. A 'whorl' is a circle of parts around a stem, like petals or leaves in a ring. That's why the name fits: this fungus makes tiny whorled branches with spores at the tips, so the word describes how it looks and helps you remember it as a plant disease fungus.
