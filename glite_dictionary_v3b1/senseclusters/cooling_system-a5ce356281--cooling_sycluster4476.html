Etymology

cooling system shows a clear picture: cooling means 'taking away heat' and system means 'a group of parts' working together, like fans, pipes and a radiator that move heat away from an engine or a room. So, cooling system means the equipment that removes heat to keep a machine or place safe and comfortable.
