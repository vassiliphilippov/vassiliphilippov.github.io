Etymology

morbidness comes from the Latin word morbus, which means 'disease' or 'sickness'. So morbidness is like a 'sick' interest — an unhealthy or disturbing liking for bad or sad things, which is why the word now means having an unhealthy interest in unpleasant subjects.
