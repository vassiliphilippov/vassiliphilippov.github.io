Etymology

The term terminable interest comes from the words terminable, meaning 'can end', and interest, meaning a person's right in property. Imagine a lamp on a timer: the light, the right, goes out when the time is up, so a terminable interest is a right that 'ends' at a set time or when a certain event happens.
