Etymology

catholic meter comes from a 17th-century idea to set a single length for everyone: catholic meant 'universal' and meter meant a unit of length. Burattini suggested using a pendulum that swung in 'one second', so the catholic meter was the length of that pendulum and was meant to be a common standard.
