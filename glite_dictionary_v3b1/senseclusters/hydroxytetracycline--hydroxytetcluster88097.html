Etymology

hydroxytetracycline begins with hydroxy-, which you can remember as 'with water', and is built on tetracycline, a common antibiotic; the tetra- part means 'four' and -cycline is like 'cycle' meaning 'ring'. So hydroxytetracycline is a form of tetracycline that has an extra 'water' part attached, which is why it is a specific antibiotic used to treat infections.
