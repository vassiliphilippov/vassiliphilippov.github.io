Etymology

The term gastric mill names a thick, strong pouch in some animals' stomach; gastric means 'stomach' and mill means a grinder. This pouch often has hard plates or small stones that crush and 'grind' food, so the name gives the picture of a little mill and explains why it means a part that breaks food before digestion.
