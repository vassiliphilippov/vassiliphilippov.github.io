Etymology

cape jasmine may come from early plants sent to Europe from the 'Cape of Good Hope' in South Africa. People felt its large, sweet white flowers looked and smelled like true jasmine, so they put cape and jasmine together and also called it 'gardenia', which is why cape jasmine now names this fragrant evergreen shrub.
