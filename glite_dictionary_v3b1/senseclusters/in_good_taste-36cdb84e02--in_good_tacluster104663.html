Etymology

The phrase in good taste comes from the idea of taste as the sense that tells us if food or other things are pleasant. Imagine a host tasting food and choosing calm decorations and gentle jokes; those choices are in good taste, so today the phrase means something is 'proper', 'polite', or 'stylish'.
