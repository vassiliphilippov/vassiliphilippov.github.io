Etymology

Amazingness comes from the verb amaze, which you can remember by thinking of the word maze, because a maze can 'confuse' and surprise you. The endings -ing and -ness turn it into 'the state' or 'the quality' of being amazed, so amazingness means 'the quality of causing great surprise'.
