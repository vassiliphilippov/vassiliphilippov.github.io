Etymology

Leyte is the name of an island in the Philippines and is used for the 1944 battle fought there. That battle began the Allied effort to retake the Philippines and saw the first use of Japanese kamikaze attacks, so Leyte often stands for a 'turning point' in the Pacific during World War II.
