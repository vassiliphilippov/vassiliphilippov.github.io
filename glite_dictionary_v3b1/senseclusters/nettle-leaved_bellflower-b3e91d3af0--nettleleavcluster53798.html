Etymology

nettle-leaved bellflower comes from a simple image: the plant has rough leaves like a nettle and bell-shaped flowers, so the parts nettle-leaved and bellflower tell you how it looks. People once used it 'to treat sore throats', so the name also helps you remember its old use.
