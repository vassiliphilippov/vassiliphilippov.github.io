Etymology

Panax pseudoginseng comes from two parts: Panax comes from a Greek word meaning 'all-heal' because plants in this group were used as medicine, and pseudoginseng literally means 'false ginseng' because it looks like true ginseng but is a different species. So the name tells us it is a ginseng-like plant valued for healing and circulation.
