Etymology

The phrase rightful possessor comes from law and a simple picture: rightful means having the 'right' (a legal right) and possessor is the one who holds something. Imagine someone holding a key and a paper that proves it is theirs; because they hold it and have the proof, they are the rightful possessor, so the phrase means 'the person legally allowed to own or hold the property'.
