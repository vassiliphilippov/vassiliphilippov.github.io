Etymology

windowing comes from the word window, meaning 'an opening' or a small view. In data and signal work, windowing means cutting the data into small parts or 'windows' so each piece can be looked at or processed. So, windowing lets engineers focus on one small part at a time to reduce noise and find patterns.
