Etymology

unrepresentative swill uses swill in its old meaning of cheap, dirty food for pigs, so you can picture a bucket of scraps. Calling information 'unrepresentative swill' gives the picture of dirty leftovers that do not show the real picture, so it means 'misleading or useless' information.
