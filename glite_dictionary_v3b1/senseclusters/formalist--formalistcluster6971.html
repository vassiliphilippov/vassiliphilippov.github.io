Etymology

The word formalist comes from formal, which comes from a root meaning 'form' or 'shape', plus the ending -ist meaning 'a person'. Think of a formalist as someone who loves the 'shape' and order of things, so in art they focus on structure and rules rather than the subject or feeling.
