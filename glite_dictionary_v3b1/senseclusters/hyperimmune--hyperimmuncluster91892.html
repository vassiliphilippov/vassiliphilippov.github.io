Etymology

Hyperimmune comes from the Greek part hyper- meaning 'over' or 'too much', as in hyperactive, and immune meaning 'protected' or 'safe from disease'. So a hyperimmune preparation is a medicine with 'extra' antibodies given to provide quick protection.
