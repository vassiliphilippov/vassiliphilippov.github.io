Etymology

Juglans regia may come from an old Latin phrase meaning 'Jupiter's nut'. One popular story says Romans called the walnut the nut of the god 'Jupiter', and that name turned into Juglans while regia means 'royal'. So the name points to a prized walnut tree, known for good nuts and fine wood.
