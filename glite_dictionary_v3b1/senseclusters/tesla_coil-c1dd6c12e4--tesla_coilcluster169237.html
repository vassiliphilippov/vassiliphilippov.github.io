Etymology

Tesla coil is named after the inventor Nikola Tesla, who used a wrapped wire called a coil to make bright, lightning-like sparks in his shows. Because these coils produced 'high voltages' and dramatic sparks, the name now means a device that makes very strong electric sparks.
