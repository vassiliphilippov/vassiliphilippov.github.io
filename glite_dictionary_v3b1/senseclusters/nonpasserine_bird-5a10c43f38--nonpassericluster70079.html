Etymology

The term nonpasserine bird comes from the parts non- (meaning 'not') and passerine (the sparrow-like, perching birds you often see on branches). So, nonpasserine bird means 'not' a perching, sparrow-like bird and helps you remember these birds often have different feet, nests, or brighter colors.
