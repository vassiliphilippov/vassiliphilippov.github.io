Etymology

The phrase ancient astronaut hypothesis creates a picture: the word ancient means very old and astronaut means a space traveller, so people imagine space visitors who came long ago. So the phrase is used for the 'unproven idea' that 'aliens' 'visited' Earth in 'ancient times' and helped shape early human cultures.
