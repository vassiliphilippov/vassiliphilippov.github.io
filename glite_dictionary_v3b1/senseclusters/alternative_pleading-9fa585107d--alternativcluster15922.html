Etymology

alternative pleading comes from a simple picture: alternative means more than one choice and pleading is a lawyer's written story to the court. One popular image is a lawyer laying out two different stories and saying 'use either one', so the lawyer keeps the client's case covered no matter which facts turn out to be true.
