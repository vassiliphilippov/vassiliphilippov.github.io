Etymology

The noun Aplectrum hyemale gets the common name putty root because its small, sticky underground roots look like pieces of putty. The second word, hyemale, is a Latin word meaning 'of winter', and that fits because the plant's single leaf and flowers appear in the cooler months, so the name points to both its sticky roots and its winter habit.
