Etymology

Warren G. Harding was the 29th President of the United States who ran in 1920 promising a 'return to normalcy' after the war. His time in office included big scandals like the Teapot Dome, where people in his government took bribes, so his name is often linked to 'corruption' or a troubled presidency.
