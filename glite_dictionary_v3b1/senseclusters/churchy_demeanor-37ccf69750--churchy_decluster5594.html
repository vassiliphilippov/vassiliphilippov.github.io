Etymology

churchy demeanor may come from the simple image of someone acting as if they are in church. Here churchy means 'church-like' and demeanor means 'the way you act', so picture a serious face, folded hands and a look that judges others. That's why it now means 'overly pious' or 'self-righteous'.
