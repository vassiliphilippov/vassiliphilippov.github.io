Etymology

The noun fibrovascular bundle gives a clear image: fibro as 'fibers' or tiny ropes, vascular as 'vessels' or little straws, and bundle means they are tied together. These ropes give the stem strength and the straws carry water and food, so the term means the plant's tubes that support it and move substances.
