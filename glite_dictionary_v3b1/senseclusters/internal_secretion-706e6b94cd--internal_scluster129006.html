Etymology

Internal secretion comes from the simple image of glands inside the body secretly sending out a substance: internal means inside the body and secretion means something sent out. Picture tiny factories pouring a chemical into the blood to send a message to other parts, so the term means a substance glands release into the bloodstream to 'act on' other tissues.
