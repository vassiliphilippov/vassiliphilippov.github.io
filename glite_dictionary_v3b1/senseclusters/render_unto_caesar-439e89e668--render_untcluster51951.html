Etymology

render unto Caesar comes from a story in the Bible where Jesus was asked about paying taxes and pointed to a coin with Caesar's picture, saying 'give to Caesar what is Caesar's'. So the clear image of handing back a coin explains why the phrase means 'to give what is due to those in charge or the government'.
