Etymology

The cocktail gin rickey may be named after a Washington man called Rickey. One popular story says a bartender mixed gin with lime and fizzy water for him, and people began to call the drink by his name, so today gin rickey means a cold, refreshing drink of gin, lime, and fizzy water.
