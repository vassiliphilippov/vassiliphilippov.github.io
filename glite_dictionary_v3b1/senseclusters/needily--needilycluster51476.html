Etymology

Needily comes from the word need ('strong want') and the ending -ly that makes it describe how someone behaves. Think of a child who keeps asking for attention or comfort; they act needily because they show a strong wish for help or affection. So, needily means behaving in a way that asks for attention, help, or comfort.
