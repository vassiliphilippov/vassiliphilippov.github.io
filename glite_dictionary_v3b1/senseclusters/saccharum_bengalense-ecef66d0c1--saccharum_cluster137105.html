Etymology

Saccharum bengalense is a tall, tough grass whose long, strong stems were often twisted into 'ropes' or woven into 'baskets', so imagine people braiding long stalks to make useful, hard-wearing items. That's why the plant is known for its strong, useful fiber, and the second word bengalense points to its origin near Bengal.
