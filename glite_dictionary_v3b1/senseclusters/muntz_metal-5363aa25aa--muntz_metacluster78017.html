Etymology

Muntz metal is named after George Muntz, an English maker in the 1800s who created a cheaper, strong brass to cover ship hulls. Sailors used it 'to protect' hulls from sea worms and damage, so the name now means this particular strong brass used for ship parts and other fittings.
