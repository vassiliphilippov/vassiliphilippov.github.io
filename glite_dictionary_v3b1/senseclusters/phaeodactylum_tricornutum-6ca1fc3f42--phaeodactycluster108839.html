Etymology

Phaeodactylum tricornutum gets its name from old Greek parts: phaeo 'dark', dactylum 'finger' and tricornutum 'three-horned', because the tiny algae often look dark and have finger- or three-point shapes under a microscope. So the name helps you picture this small sea alga that is often grown as food for young shellfish and fish.
