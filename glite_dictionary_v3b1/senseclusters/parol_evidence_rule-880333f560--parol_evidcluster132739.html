Etymology

The phrase parol evidence rule comes from the old word parol, which meant 'spoken', and evidence, which meant 'proof'. The image is simple: only what is written on the contract counts, so what people say later cannot change it, which is why the rule means 'spoken statements cannot change a written contract'.
