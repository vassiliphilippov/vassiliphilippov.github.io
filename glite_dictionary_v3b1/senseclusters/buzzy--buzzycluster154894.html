Etymology

buzzy comes from the simple word buzz, a word that copies the low sound bees make, and the ending -y which means 'full of' or 'having'. So a buzzy place is full of energy, talk, and attention—like a busy hive of bees—which is why lively cafés or events are often called 'buzzy'.
