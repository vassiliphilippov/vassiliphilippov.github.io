Etymology

bang-up performance comes from the image of a loud bang with up added to make that bang bigger. One popular story says a 'bang' was a striking hit and up showed it was extra good, so a bang-up performance feels like a powerful, attention-grabbing moment and means 'excellent' or 'very impressive'.
