Etymology

Rorschach test is named after Swiss doctor Hermann Rorschach, who showed people strange inkblot pictures and asked them what they saw. The idea is that what someone sees in the blots can 'reveal' hidden thoughts or feelings, so the test came to mean a way to learn about a person's personality.
