Etymology

Steatornithidae comes from Greek parts steato- meaning 'fat' or 'oil' and ornith- meaning 'bird', plus the family ending -idae. So the name literally says 'oil birds', which fits these night birds that eat oily fruit and use sound to find their way in dark caves.
