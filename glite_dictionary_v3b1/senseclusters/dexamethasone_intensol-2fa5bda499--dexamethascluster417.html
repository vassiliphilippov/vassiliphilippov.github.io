Etymology

Dexamethasone Intensol combines the medicine name dexamethasone, a steroid that calms swelling, with the brand word Intensol. One popular idea is that Intensol comes from 'intense' and 'solution', creating the image of a strong liquid that works fast, which is why the name suggests a powerful form used to 'reduce inflammation'.
