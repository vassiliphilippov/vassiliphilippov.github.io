Etymology

humbug probably comes from two small parts, hum and bug, where bug once meant a 'ghost' or 'goblin' that could scare people. A helpful way to remember it is to think of a 'ghost trick', so a humbug is a 'trick' or sham—false talk meant to deceive people.
