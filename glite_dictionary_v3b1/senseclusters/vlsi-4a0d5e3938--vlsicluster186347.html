    0.02/m6.1

    vsVLSI: focuses specifically on designing chips with thousands or millions of components

Etymology

VLSI comes from Very-Large-Scale Integration, meaning 'putting parts together', and by extension it also names the field of making and designing those complex chips that pack many tiny parts into one device.
