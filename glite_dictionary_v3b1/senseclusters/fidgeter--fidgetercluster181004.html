Etymology

fidgeter comes from the verb fidget plus the ending -er, where -er means 'one who'. A helpful way to remember fidget is to imagine a small, nervous 'move' or little 'fit' you cannot stop, so a fidgeter is someone who moves restlessly or cannot stay still.
