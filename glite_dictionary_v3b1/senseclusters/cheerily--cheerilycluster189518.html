Etymology

cheerily comes from cheer plus the ending -ly; you can see cheer in cheerful and in the phrase 'cheer up'. Originally cheer meant someone's 'face' or 'mood' and later came to mean joy or a shout of support, so to act cheerily is to do something 'in a happy or friendly way'.
