Etymology

chlorobenzylidenemalononitrile is a descriptive chemical name made of parts you can recognise: chloro- ('chlorine'), benzylidene from benzene ('ring'), and malononitrile which has nitrile ('nitrogen group'). So the long name simply lists the parts of the molecule, and those chlorine and nitrile parts help explain why this compound is a strong, eye- and lung‑irritating tear gas.
