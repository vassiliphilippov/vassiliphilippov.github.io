Etymology

The word sententiousness comes from the old Latin root sent- meaning 'to feel' or 'to think', the same root you see in sentence and sentiment. So sententiousness is the habit of giving short, serious moral sayings or opinions—like turning a feeling into a short 'sentence'—which is why it often sounds self-important.
