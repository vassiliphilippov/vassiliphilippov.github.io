Etymology

perceptual constancy comes from the idea that perceptual means 'what you notice' and constancy means 'staying the same'. Imagine seeing a friend from far away or in dim light and still knowing it is the same person, so perceptual constancy is when our senses keep things looking the same even if light, distance, or angle change.
