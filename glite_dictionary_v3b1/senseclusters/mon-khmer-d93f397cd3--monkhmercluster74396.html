Etymology

Mon-Khmer comes from the names of two peoples and their languages: Mon, spoken in parts of Myanmar and Thailand, and Khmer, spoken in Cambodia. So Mon-Khmer means a 'branch' of related languages from Southeast Asia, named after those two groups.
