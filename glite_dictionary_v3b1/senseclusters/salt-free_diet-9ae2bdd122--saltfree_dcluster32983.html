Etymology

The term salt-free diet comes from the simple idea of taking the common seasoning salt out of your food so the diet is 'without salt'. Doctors began to advise people with high blood pressure to eat this way because salt makes the body hold water and can raise blood pressure, so today the phrase means eating 'without salt' for health.
