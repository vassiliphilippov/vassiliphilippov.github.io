Etymology

sickle alfalfa: The plant name sickle alfalfa comes from its curved seed pods that look like a small sickle. The word alfalfa is the common name for a plant grown as 'feed' for animals, so the whole name helps you remember it is a feed plant with sickle-shaped pods.
