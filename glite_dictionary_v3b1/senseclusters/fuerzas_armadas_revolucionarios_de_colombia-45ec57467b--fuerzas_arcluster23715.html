Etymology

Fuerzas Armadas Revolucionarios de Colombia literally breaks into Fuerzas ('forces'), Armadas ('armed') and Revolucionarios ('revolutionaries'), giving the clear image of an armed group fighting for political change. That's why the name refers to the guerrilla group that fought in parts of Colombia.
