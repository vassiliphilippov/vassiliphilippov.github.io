Etymology

Pilgrimage to Jerusalem comes from the long journeys people made to the holy city of Jerusalem, where believers walked for days to pray at holy sites. Because pilgrims traveled far and with strong faith, the phrase means 'a religious journey' taken out of 'faith' to visit a holy place.
