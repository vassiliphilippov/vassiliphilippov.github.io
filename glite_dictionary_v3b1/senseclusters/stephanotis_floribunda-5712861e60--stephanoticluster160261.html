Etymology

Stephanotis floribunda may come from old words that describe the plant's flowers: Stephanotis comes from a Greek word for 'crown' because the white, waxy flowers look like little crowns, and floribunda is a Latin word meaning 'many flowers'. So the name helps you remember that this climbing plant is known for many showy, fragrant flowers.
