Etymology

Phaseolus angularis comes from older words: Phaseolus 'bean' and angularis 'angled', so the name paints a picture of a small bean with a pointed or slightly angled shape. That's why this name helps you remember it is the red adzuki bean used in sweet foods and ground into flour.
