Etymology

The plant name multiflora rose comes from Latin words multi meaning 'many' and flora meaning 'flowers'. So it literally means 'many-flowered' and creates the image of a rose bush covered in many small blooms, which is why gardeners plant it as a 'hedge' or to help grow other roses.
