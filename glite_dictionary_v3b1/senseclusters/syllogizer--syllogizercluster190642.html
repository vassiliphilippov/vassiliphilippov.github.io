Etymology

syllogizer comes from syllogism, an old Greek word meaning 'putting reasons together', and the ending -izer, which can mean 'a tool or person who does something'. So a syllogizer can be a piece of software or an AI that 'puts reasons together', which explains the computing and teaching use.
