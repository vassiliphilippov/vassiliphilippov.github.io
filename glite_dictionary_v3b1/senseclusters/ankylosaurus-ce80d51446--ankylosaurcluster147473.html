Etymology

Ankylosaurus comes from Greek parts: ankylos meaning 'fused' or 'stiff' and -saurus meaning 'lizard', the same -saurus you see in Tyrannosaurus. So, its name means 'fused lizard', a good image to remember the dinosaur's heavy armor and club-like tail to protect itself.
