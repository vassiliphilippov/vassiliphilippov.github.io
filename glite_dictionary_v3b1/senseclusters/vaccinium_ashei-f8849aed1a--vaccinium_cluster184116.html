Etymology

Vaccinium ashei comes from the old Latin word Vaccinium, which means 'berry', and the second part ashei is the name of a person, Mr. Ashe, who first described the plant. So the name simply says 'Ashe's berry', which helps you remember it is a shrub grown for edible berries.
