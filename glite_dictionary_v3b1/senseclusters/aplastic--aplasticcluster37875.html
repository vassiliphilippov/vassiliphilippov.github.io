Etymology

aplastic comes from the Greek parts a- 'not' and plastic 'able to be shaped', the same root as the word plastic. A helpful way to remember the emotional sense is to think of a person who cannot be changed or moved: if they are aplastic, they are 'not responsive' and show little reaction.
