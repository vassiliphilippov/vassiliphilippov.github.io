Etymology

occupational therapy comes from the word occupation, which here means everyday jobs or activities, and therapy, meaning treatment. Therapists use real tasks like dressing, cooking, or simple work to rebuild skills, so the name means using everyday activities as treatment to help people 'live independently'.
