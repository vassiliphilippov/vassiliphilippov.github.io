Etymology

civic pride comes from the image of people treating their 'city' like their own home and doing simple acts such as cleaning streets, planting flowers, or decorating buildings to make it look better. Here civic means 'city' and pride is the proud, caring feeling, so the phrase means being proud of and responsible for your city.
