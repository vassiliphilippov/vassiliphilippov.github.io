Etymology

Petromyzon comes from Greek parts petro- 'rock' and -myzon 'to suck', so it can be thought of as a 'rock-sucker'. This is a helpful image because lampreys have a round, sucker-like mouth and often cling to rocks or other fish, which explains the name.
