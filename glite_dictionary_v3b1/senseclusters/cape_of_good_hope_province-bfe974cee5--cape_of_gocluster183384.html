Etymology

Cape of Good Hope Province takes its name from the famous place Cape of Good Hope, a rocky point sailors reached after a long, dangerous sea trip where the words 'good hope' showed hope for a safer route. The word Province means the large area around that cape, so the name tells you it was the region near the Cape of Good Hope.
