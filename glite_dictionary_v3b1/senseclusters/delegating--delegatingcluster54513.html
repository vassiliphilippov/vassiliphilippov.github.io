Etymology

delegating comes from the Latin parts de- ('from' or 'away') and legare ('to send' or 'to appoint'), and it helps to think of it as 'sending' a task to someone else. So, delegating means giving responsibility to another person to act in your place, which is why managers delegate work to their team.
