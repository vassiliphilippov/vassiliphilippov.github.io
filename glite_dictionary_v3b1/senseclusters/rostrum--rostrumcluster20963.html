Etymology

rostrum comes from the Latin word rostrum meaning 'beak' and also the pointed front of a ship; in ancient Rome speakers stood on a platform that was decorated with captured ship beaks, so the platform itself became known as the rostrum. That's why today a rostrum is a raised platform used to show or let someone speak.
