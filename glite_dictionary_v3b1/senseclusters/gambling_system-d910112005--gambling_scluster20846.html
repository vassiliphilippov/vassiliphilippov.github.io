Etymology

gambling system comes from the simple image of a plan you can follow: gambling means betting and system means a set of steps or rules, like a recipe or a small machine. People hope that by following such a plan they will 'win', so the phrase means 'a set of rules or a method to try to increase your chances when betting'.
