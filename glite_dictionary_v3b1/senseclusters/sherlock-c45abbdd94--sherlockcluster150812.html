Etymology

Sherlock comes from the name of the famous fictional detective Sherlock Holmes, created by Sir Arthur Conan Doyle. Because Holmes was great at solving mysteries, people now call anyone who figures things out a 'Sherlock' as a playful name for a clever investigator.
