Etymology

Methamphetamine hydrochloride comes from the parts methyl (a small chemical piece added to amphetamine) and hydrochloride (the salt that turns the drug into a stable crystal). So the name shows it is a 'man-made' 'stimulant' drug in a 'crystal' form that can be used in medicine but is often abused.
