Etymology

lying under oath comes from the old practice in court where a person would put a hand on a holy book or raise a hand and promise 'to tell the truth'. To lie while under that promise, breaking the oath, was seen as very serious and was punished, so today lying under oath means making false statements after you have promised 'to tell the truth'.
