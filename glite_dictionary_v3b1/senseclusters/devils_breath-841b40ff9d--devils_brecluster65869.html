Etymology

devil's breath may come from the picture of the devil blowing a hot, dangerous breath, like a burning wind or a rotten smell that fills a place. That's why people use devil's breath to mean 'a very strong, unpleasant smell', 'a hot, dangerous wind', or 'a cold, scary feeling'.
