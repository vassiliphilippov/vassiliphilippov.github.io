Etymology

The word Japayuki-san likely combines Japan and the Japanese verb yuku meaning 'to go', plus the polite -san meaning 'Mr/Ms'. A helpful way to remember it is to think of a 'Japan-go' person — a Japanese woman who travels abroad to work, which is why the term is used for hostesses or entertainers working in other countries.
