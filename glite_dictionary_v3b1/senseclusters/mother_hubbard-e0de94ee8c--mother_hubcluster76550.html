Etymology

Mother Hubbard comes from the old children's rhyme 'Old Mother Hubbard'. Because the story shows a plain old woman, people used her name for a simple, loose dress worn at home, so Mother Hubbard now means a high-necked, unbelted dress that falls straight from the shoulders.
