Etymology

beta orbiter comes from two images: beta, from talk where 'alpha' means the leader and 'beta' means a quieter man, and orbiter, something that goes round a planet. The picture is of a quieter man who circles someone from a distance, always offering attention but never becoming a partner, so the phrase means 'a man who supports or admires a woman from the sidelines without romance or sex in return'.
