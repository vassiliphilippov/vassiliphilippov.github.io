Etymology

Mycenaean culture is 'named after' the ancient city of Mycenae, where big palaces, tombs, and rich objects were found. Because so many important finds came from that place, people used the city's name for the whole way of life and people at that time, so today Mycenaean culture means the Bronze Age civilization centered on Mycenae.
