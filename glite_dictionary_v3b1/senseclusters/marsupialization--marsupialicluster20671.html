Etymology

marsupialization comes from the Latin word marsupium, meaning 'pouch', and shares the same root as marsupial, animals like kangaroos that carry young in a pouch. In medicine it means making a small 'pouch' or open space so a cyst or abscess can drain and heal, which is why the name fits the procedure.
