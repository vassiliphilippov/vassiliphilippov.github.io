Etymology

minimal brain damage comes from the simple image of very small damage to the brain, with minimal meaning 'very small'. Doctors used this name when they thought such tiny brain problems caused children's behavior and learning troubles, so the phrase came to mean that condition, though today it is considered 'outdated'.
