Etymology

The word miscegenational comes from Latin parts miscere 'to mix' and gens 'clan' or 'race'. You can remember miscere by thinking of the English word 'mix' and gens by thinking of 'gene' or 'generation'. So, miscegenational describes the mixing of different racial groups, often by marriage.
