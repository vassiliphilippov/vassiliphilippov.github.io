Etymology

bilge keel names a long, thin fin attached along a ship's hull; bilge is the lower side where the bottom meets the side and keel means a fin. The fin works like a small wing in the water and stops the ship from rocking side to side, so a bilge keel is used to 'reduce rolling'.
