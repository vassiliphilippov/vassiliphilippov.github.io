Etymology

spirit gum comes from old stage and makeup work where the word spirit meant 'alcohol' and gum meant a sticky 'resin'; the two were mixed to make a liquid glue that could stick to the skin. So today spirit gum means the adhesive used to attach false hair or theatrical prosthetics.
