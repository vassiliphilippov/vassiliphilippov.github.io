Etymology

fourth ventricle comes from thinking of the brain as a house with small rooms: a ventricle meant a small hollow or 'little belly', and the fourth is the room near the spinal cord where fluid passes. So, the fourth ventricle is the small space in the brain where the fluid around the brain flows toward the spinal cord.
