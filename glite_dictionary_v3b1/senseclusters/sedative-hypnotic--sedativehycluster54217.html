Etymology

sedative-hypnotic comes from two parts: sedative, from the old Latin sedare meaning 'to calm', and hypnotic, from the Greek hypnos meaning 'sleep', just like the English words sedate and hypnosis. So a sedative-hypnotic is a medicine that 'calms' you and helps you 'sleep'.
