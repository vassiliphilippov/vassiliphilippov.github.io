Etymology

crinkleroot is formed from two plain English parts: crinkle meaning 'small folds or wrinkles' and root meaning 'the part of a plant under the ground'. The name describes the plant's wrinkled, scaly roots, which helps you remember this North American herb with a strong, spicy taste.
