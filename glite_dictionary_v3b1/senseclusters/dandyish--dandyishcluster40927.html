Etymology

The word dandyish comes from dandy, a name for someone very proud of their clothes and way of acting, and the ending -ish, the same as in stylish, which means 'like' or 'characteristic of'. So dandyish means 'like a dandy', showing careful, sometimes showy, style in dress and manners.
