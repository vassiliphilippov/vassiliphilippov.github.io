Etymology

nightmarishly comes from nightmare, which is made of night and mare, because long ago people imagined a mare was an evil spirit that sat on you at night and gave you 'bad dreams'. So nightmarishly means 'in a way like a nightmare', very frightening or extremely unpleasant.
