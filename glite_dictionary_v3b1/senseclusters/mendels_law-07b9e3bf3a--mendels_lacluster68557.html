Etymology

Mendel's law comes from Gregor Mendel, a scientist who grew peas and watched traits like tall or short and green or yellow being passed from parents to children. He found simple, repeatable patterns and called them a 'law', so today Mendel's law means the basic rules that tell how traits are passed from parents to children.
