Etymology

suction blower names the two actions the machine does: suction (it 'sucks' or pulls air and grain in) and blower (it then blows or pushes the mix along). Think of a big vacuum and a fan working together to move grain through pipes, and that picture explains why the name means a device that moves material by pulling and blowing.
