Etymology

The term upper mantle comes from the idea of the Earth wearing a thick cloak: mantle means a covering and upper shows it is the top part of that covering under the crust. So, the name tells us it is the top layer beneath the Earth's crust and explains why this part moves slowly and helps the Earth's plates to shift.
