Etymology

hemidemisemiquaver comes from three parts that mean 'half'—hemi-, demi-, and semi-—plus quaver, a word for a short musical note. So the name shows a note cut in half three times, which is why a hemidemisemiquaver is a very short sixty-fourth note.
