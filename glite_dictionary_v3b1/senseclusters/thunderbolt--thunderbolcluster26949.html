Etymology

thunderbolt comes from the two parts thunder and bolt, where bolt used to mean a 'shaft' or 'arrow' shot from the sky; imagine a bright arrow of lightning followed by a loud crash, which is why a thunderbolt is a flash of lightning followed by thunder.
