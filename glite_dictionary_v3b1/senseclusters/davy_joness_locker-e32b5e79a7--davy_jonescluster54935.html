Etymology

Davy Jones's locker may come from old sailors' stories about a sea spirit called Davy Jones who kept drowned sailors in his locker like a chest at the bottom of the ocean. That's why the phrase now means the 'bottom of the sea' or the place where lost ships and sailors rest.
