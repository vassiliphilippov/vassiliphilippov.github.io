Etymology

Sundacarpus combines Sunda and carpus. Sunda is the name of islands and a region in Southeast Asia, and carpus comes from a Greek word meaning 'fruit', so the name points to a plant from the Sunda area and helps you remember it as a Southeast Asian tree.
