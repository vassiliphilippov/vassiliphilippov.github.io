Etymology

physiological jaundice of the newborn names the common yellowing of a baby's skin and eyes soon after birth. The word jaundice means yellowing, physiological points to the body's normal process, and in a newborn the immature liver cannot clear the yellow pigment fast, so the phrase usually means a 'harmless' and 'temporary' yellowing in babies.
