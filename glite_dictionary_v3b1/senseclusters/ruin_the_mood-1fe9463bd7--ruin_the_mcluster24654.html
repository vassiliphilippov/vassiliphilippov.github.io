Etymology

ruin the mood paints a clear picture: imagine someone at a happy party who spills a drink on the cake or makes a sad remark, and the good feeling suddenly stops. Here ruin means to break and mood means the good feeling, so to ruin the mood means 'to spoil the pleasant atmosphere'.
