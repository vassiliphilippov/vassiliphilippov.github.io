Etymology

The word gyrfalcon comes from the Old Norse name gyrfalki, where the second part is the same as English falcon and the first part, gyr, likely points to 'grey' or 'pale'. A helpful way to remember it is that the gyrfalcon is a very large northern falcon often white or grey, so the old name fits the bird's colour and home.
