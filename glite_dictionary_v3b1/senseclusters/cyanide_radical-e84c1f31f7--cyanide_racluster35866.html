Etymology

Cyanide radical comes from old chemistry words: cyanide came from a word for 'dark blue' because early chemists saw a blue dye, and radical once meant 'root' or a small 'group' of atoms. So the name points to the little atomic 'root' -CN that acts as one piece inside many chemicals, which is why we use the term for that chemical group.
