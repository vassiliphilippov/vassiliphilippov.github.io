Etymology

cattail millet gets its name from the plant's thick, tail-like spikes that look like a cattail and a cat's tail. Because the grain of this tall millet grows on those tail-like spikes, people began to call the crop 'cattail millet', so the name now means a grass grown for 'food' and 'animal feed'.
