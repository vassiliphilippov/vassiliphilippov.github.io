Etymology

unprovided comes from un- meaning 'not' and provide, which comes from old Latin parts pro- 'before' and videre 'to see', so it originally meant 'to see ahead' or get ready. So, unprovided means 'not given what is needed beforehand', which is why we use it for people or things that lack supplies or preparation.
