Etymology

tousled comes from the verb tousle, which means 'mess up' hair, so imagine fingers or wind making hair untidy. A helpful way to remember it is to think of tussle ('a small fight') where things get pushed and become messy, so tousled hair looks like it has been moved around and is not neat.
