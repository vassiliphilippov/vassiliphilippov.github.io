Etymology

unmercifully comes from un- meaning 'not', plus merciful (from mercy) meaning 'kindness' or 'compassion', and the ending -ly which turns it into a word that describes how something is done. So, to act unmercifully is to act 'without mercy' — cruelly or very harshly.
