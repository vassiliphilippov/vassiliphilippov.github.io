Etymology

Malcolmia maritima may be named after a person called Malcolm, and the second word maritima means 'by the sea', because the plant often grows on sandy coasts. So the name gives the image of a small, fragrant garden flower linked to Malcolm that lives near the sea, which helps you remember it and its common name 'Virginia stock'.
