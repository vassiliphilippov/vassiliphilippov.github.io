Etymology

Xyridaceae comes from the plant name Xyris (the yellow-eyed grasses) and the ending -aceae, which shows a plant 'family'. So it means the family of Xyris plants, flowering grasses often found in marshes and boggy places and known for their small yellow 'eyes'.
