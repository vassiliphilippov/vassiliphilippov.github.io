Etymology

cocaine addiction links the drug cocaine, first made from the coca plant, and the word addiction, which names when a person keeps using something and cannot stop. Imagine someone returning to a small white powder again and again even when it hurts them; that's why cocaine addiction means 'a condition where someone cannot stop using cocaine despite harmful consequences'.
