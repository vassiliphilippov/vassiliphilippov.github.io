Etymology

Prunus padus comes from old Latin words: Prunus was the Latin word for 'plum' or 'cherry', and padus probably came from the ancient name of a river where the tree grew. So the name shows a small cherry tree by a river and explains why it refers to the bird cherry with small bitter fruits.
