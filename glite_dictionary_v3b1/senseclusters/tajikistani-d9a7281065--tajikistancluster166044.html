Etymology

The word Tajikistani comes from the country name Tajikistan, which combines Tajik (the people) and -stan meaning 'land', like in Pakistan or Afghanistan. The ending -i shows 'from' or 'relating to', so Tajikistani means 'relating to Tajikistan'.
