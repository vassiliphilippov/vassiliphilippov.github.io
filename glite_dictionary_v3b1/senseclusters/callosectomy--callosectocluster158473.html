Etymology

callosectomy comes from callos-, from the name corpus callosum, the thick band that joins the two halves of the brain, and -ectomy, which means 'cutting out' and appears in appendectomy. So a callosectomy is a surgery to cut that band and stop messages between the two sides of the brain.
