Etymology

The word parergon comes from Greek parts para- 'beside' and ergon 'work', like the para- in parallel, which means 'side by side', and the ergon in energy, which means 'work'. So a parergon is a small or extra 'work' that sits beside a main work, such as a decorative border or added notes.
