Etymology

Social Democratic Party comes from three simple ideas: Social (helping people and the community), Democratic (rule by the people), and Party (a political group). It began when workers wanted fairer lives and used elections to change things, so the name now shows a group that seeks 'social' change by 'democratic' means.
