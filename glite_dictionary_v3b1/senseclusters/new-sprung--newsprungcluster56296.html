Etymology

The word new-sprung is made of new and sprung, from spring which means 'to jump up' or 'appear suddenly'. So new-sprung pictures something that has just 'sprung' into being, like fresh flowers popping up after rain, and that helps you remember it means 'just appeared' or 'newly formed'.
