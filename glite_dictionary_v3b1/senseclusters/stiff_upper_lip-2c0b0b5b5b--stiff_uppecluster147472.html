Etymology

The idiom stiff upper lip comes from a simple image: people keeping their upper lipstiff and still so they do not show their feelings. One popular story says this idea grew in Britain, where soldiers and important people tried to look steady in hard times. That's why stiff upper lip means 'to remain calm and not show emotions'.
