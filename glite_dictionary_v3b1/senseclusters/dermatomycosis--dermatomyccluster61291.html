Etymology

dermatomycosis breaks into parts: dermato- meaning 'skin', myco- meaning 'fungus' like a 'mushroom', and -osis meaning 'condition'. So it literally means a 'fungal condition of the skin', which is why the word refers to a skin infection caused by fungi.
