Etymology

Cuscuta gronovii starts with Cuscuta, an old word for 'dodder' plants and it brings to mind thin, orange-yellow threads that twist around and hang on other plants. The second part, gronovii, honors a scientist named Gronovius who studied the plant, so the full name reminds us that this little plant lives by twining around and taking from other plants.
