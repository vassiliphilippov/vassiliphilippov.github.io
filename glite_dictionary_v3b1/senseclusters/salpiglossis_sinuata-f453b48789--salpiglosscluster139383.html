Etymology

Salpiglossis sinuata may come from old Greek words: salpi meaning 'trumpet' and glossis meaning 'tongue', because the flower looks like a small trumpet with long, tongue-like parts. Sinuata means 'wavy', which fits its ruffled petal edges, so the name gives a clear image of the plant's trumpet-shaped, tongue-like, wavy flowers.
