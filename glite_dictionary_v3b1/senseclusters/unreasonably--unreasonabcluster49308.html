Etymology

unreasonably is built from un- meaning 'not' and reason meaning 'a good cause' or 'sensible thinking' (the same root as reasonable), and -ly turns it into a word that describes actions. So, to act unreasonably is to act 'without good reason' or in a way that is not fair or sensible.
