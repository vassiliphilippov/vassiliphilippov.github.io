Etymology

laparocele comes from Greek parts laparo- meaning 'abdomen' or 'side' and -cele meaning 'swelling' or 'hernia'. So a laparocele is literally an 'abdominal swelling', which explains why it means a hernia where body parts push through the belly wall.
