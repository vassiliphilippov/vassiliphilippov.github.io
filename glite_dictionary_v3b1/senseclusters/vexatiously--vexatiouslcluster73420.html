Etymology

The word vexatiously comes from the Latin verb vexare, which meant 'to trouble' or 'to shake', and it gave English the word vex, meaning 'to annoy'. So to do something vexatiously is to act in a way that 'annoys' or 'causes trouble', often on purpose.
