Etymology

duodenal smear is a medical name that describes the action: duodenal means the first part of the small intestine and a smear is a thin sample spread on a glass slide. Doctors look at that slide under a microscope to find germs or damaged cells, so the name shows why the test helps 'diagnose' problems.
