Etymology

Hyperoodon ampullatus may come from old Greek and Latin words: hyper ('above') + odon ('tooth') and ampullatus ('bottle-shaped'). Its big, round forehead looks like a bottle, so the name fits the animal we call the 'northern bottlenose whale'.
