Etymology

Angelica sylvestris gets its name from two old words: Angelica comes from a story that an angel showed the plant as a 'medicine', and sylvestris means 'of the woods' because the herb grows wild in woods and damp meadows. So the name helps us remember that this is a healing plant found in the woods.
