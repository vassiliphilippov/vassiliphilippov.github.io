Etymology

Delphic oracle comes from ancient Greece, where a priestess at Delphi spoke for the god Apollo and gave short, mysterious answers that needed explanation. Because those answers were often like riddles, people now use Delphic oracle for any statement that is 'mysterious' or 'hard to understand'.
