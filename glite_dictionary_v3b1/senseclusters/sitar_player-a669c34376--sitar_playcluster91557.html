Etymology

sitar player describes someone who plays the long Indian instrument called the sitar. One popular story says the word sitar comes from Persian words for 'three' and 'string', so imagine a musician plucking a long instrument with many strings — that image explains why sitar player means 'a musician who plays the sitar'.
