Etymology

chocolate egg: The name chocolate egg comes from the old Easter idea of giving real eggs as a sign of new life, and later candy makers began to make eggs from chocolate so people could eat them. So a chocolate egg is a sweet shaped like an egg and usually given as a 'gift' at Easter.
