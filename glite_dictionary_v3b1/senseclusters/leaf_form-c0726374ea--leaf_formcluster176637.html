Etymology

leaf form The noun leaf form simply describes the visible shape of a plant's leaf, and the word form here means 'shape'. Early plant watchers grouped plants by these clear shapes, such as heart-shaped, needle-like or compound leaves, so leaf form came to mean the shapes used to 'identify' and 'classify' plants.
