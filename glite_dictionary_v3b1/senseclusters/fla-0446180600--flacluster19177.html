Etymology

Fla. is the short written form of Florida, and that name comes from Spanish la Florida which means 'flowery' or 'full of flowers' because an early explorer named it in spring. So Fla. stands for the warm, flower-rich state of Florida and is used as its standard abbreviation in writing.
