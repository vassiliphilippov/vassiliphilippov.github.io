Etymology

The phrase go the way of may come from the picture of something following the same path as a thing that has already disappeared, like the dodo. One popular story says people talked about things that 'went the way of the dodo', meaning they were gone, so go the way of now means 'to disappear' or 'to become old-fashioned'.
