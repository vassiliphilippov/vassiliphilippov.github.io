Etymology

The phrase strong calling paints the picture of a loud, clear calling you cannot ignore, and the word strong shows how powerful that pull feels. Long ago people used 'calling' for a feeling or voice that pointed someone to a job or life path, so a strong calling means a powerful inner urge to follow a particular career or purpose.
