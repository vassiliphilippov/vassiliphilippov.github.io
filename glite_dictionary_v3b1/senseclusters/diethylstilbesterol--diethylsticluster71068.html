Etymology

diethylstilbesterol is a chemical name you can read in parts. Di- means 'two', ethyl is a 'small chemical part', and stilbestrol shows it acts like the female hormone 'estrogen', so the name tells you it is a man-made estrogen medicine that was once given to women but later found to be risky.
