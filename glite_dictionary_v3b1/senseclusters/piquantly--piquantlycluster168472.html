Etymology

The word piquantly comes from the French piquant, from an old word meaning 'to prick' or 'sting', like a small sharp poke. Think of the familiar word pungent, which also means 'sharp' in taste, so piquantly means 'in a pleasantly sharp, spicy, or exciting way'.
