Etymology

ruckus is an American slang word that probably grew from the short word ruck, which people used for a crowd or a noisy push, and the sound of the word feels rough and loud. A helpful way to remember it is to imagine a messy, noisy 'ruck' or tumble, which is why ruckus means a noisy commotion or disturbance.
