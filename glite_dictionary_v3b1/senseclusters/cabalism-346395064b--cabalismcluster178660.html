Etymology

The word Cabalism comes from the Hebrew word Kabbalah, which means 'receiving' and was used for a tradition of reading religious texts to find hidden meanings. So Cabalism refers to the mystical teachings that 'receive' or uncover those hidden messages, and it is related to the English word cabal, which also suggests small secret groups.
