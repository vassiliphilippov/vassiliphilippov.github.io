Etymology

The noun prisoner of war censorship comes from the practice in war camps where guards opened and read letters sent by prisoners of war. They often cut out or blacked out words and stamped the mail as censorship, and this image of guards checking and stopping parts of messages explains why the phrase means 'watching and limiting messages to and from prisoners'.
