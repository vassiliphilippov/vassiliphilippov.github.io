Etymology

baked blush gets its name from the way the color is literally baked on clay tiles to make the little powder blush: liquid pigments are heated until they dry into a smooth, marble-like powder. That's why this product is 'long-lasting' and 'vibrant' and is used to 'color the cheeks'.
