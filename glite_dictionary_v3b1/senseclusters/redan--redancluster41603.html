Etymology

redan comes from a French idea meaning 'bent back' or 'turned in', which gives a strong picture of a V-shaped part of a wall that points out but folds so defenders can fire along both sides. That's why a redan is a V-shaped fort that protects soldiers and lets them shoot in two directions.
