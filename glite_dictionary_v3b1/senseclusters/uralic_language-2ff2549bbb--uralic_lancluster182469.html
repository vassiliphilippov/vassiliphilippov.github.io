Etymology

Uralic language comes from the name of the 'Ural Mountains', because many of these languages were first noticed near that mountain range. Early language scholars saw that Finnish, Hungarian and Estonian had similar words and grammar and lived in that area, so calling them Uralic meant they were 'a group of related languages', which is why Uralic language refers to those languages today.
