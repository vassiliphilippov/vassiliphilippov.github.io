Etymology

The word anaplasia comes from the Greek parts ana- meaning 'back' or 'again' and -plasia meaning 'formation' or 'shape', the same idea in plastic which means 'able to be shaped'. So anaplasia describes cells that 'go back' to a simpler form and lose their normal structure, which is why doctors use it for cancer cells that grow faster and look abnormal.
