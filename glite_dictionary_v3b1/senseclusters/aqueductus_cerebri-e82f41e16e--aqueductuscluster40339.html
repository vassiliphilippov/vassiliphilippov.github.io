Etymology

aqueductus cerebri comes from Latin: aqueductus means 'water channel' and cerebri means 'of the brain'. Think of a tiny pipe carrying fluid between two small spaces in the brain; that image explains why the term names the narrow canal that lets 'brain fluid' flow.
