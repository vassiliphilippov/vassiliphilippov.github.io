Etymology

The word mingle-mangle comes from the common verb mingle ('to mix') joined with a rhyming partner mangle, making a playful double word that sounds messy and mixed up. A helpful way to remember it is to picture a jumble of different things, which is why mingle-mangle means a confused mixture.
