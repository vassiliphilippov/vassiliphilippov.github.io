Etymology

Sorbus torminalis may come from old Latin words for a type of 'service' tree that people in Europe knew for its small, speckled brown fruit. Villagers often waited until the fruit was softened by frost to eat it or make a sweet drink, so the name now refers to this European tree with edible, speckled fruit.
