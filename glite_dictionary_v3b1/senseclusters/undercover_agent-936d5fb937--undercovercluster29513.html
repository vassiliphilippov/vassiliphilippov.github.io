Etymology

Undercover agent comes from the picture of someone working under a cover — hidden like a person under a blanket or wearing a disguise so they are not seen. An agent is a person working for the police or government, so an undercover agent hides their true identity to gather information, which is why it means 'a person who works in secret'.
