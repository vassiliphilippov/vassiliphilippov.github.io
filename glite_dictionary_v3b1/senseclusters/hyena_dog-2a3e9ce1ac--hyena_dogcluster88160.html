Etymology

hyena dog probably comes from early people who thought this animal looked like a mix of a hyena and a dog. Because it moved and hunted like a dog but had different colors and strange calls, the name stayed for the large wild dog of Africa, the 'African wild dog' or 'painted wolf'.
