Etymology

cirrocumulus cloud The name cirrocumulus cloud comes from two parts: cirro meaning 'high' and cumulus meaning 'small heap' or 'pile'. These clouds appear as small white patches or ripples high in the sky, so the name paints a picture of their height and little, piled shapes.
