Etymology

suborder Myxinoidei is the name scientists use for the group of eel-like hagfishes. The word Myxinoidei comes from a Greek word meaning 'slime', so imagine slimy, jawless fish, and that picture helps explain why this name refers to these hagfishes rather than to lampreys.
