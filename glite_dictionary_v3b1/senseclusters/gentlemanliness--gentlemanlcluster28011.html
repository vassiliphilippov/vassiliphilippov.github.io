Etymology

The word gentlemanliness comes from gentleman, which is made of gentle and man, plus the ending -liness; gentle once meant 'kind' or 'well born', and a gentleman was a polite, respectful man. So gentlemanliness is the quality of being polite, respectful, and well-mannered.
