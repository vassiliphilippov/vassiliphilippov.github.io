Centaurea imperialis may come from Centaurea, from the 'centaur', a half-man, half-horse figure, and from imperialis, meaning 'royal'. So the name gives the image of a royal, centaur-linked flower and helps you remember it is a beautiful, fragrant plant with rose-pink blooms.
