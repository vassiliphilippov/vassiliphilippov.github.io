Etymology

gladdon iris may come from an old word meaning 'small sword', because the part gladdon likely referred to the long, narrow leaves that look like little blades behind the iris flower. So the name helps you remember this purple iris: it has long, blade-like leaves and often gives off a 'bad smell'.
