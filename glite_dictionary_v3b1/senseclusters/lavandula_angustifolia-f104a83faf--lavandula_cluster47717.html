Etymology

Lavandula angustifolia may come from old words: Lavandula from 'to wash' and angustifolia from 'narrow leaves'. People used this small shrub with purple flowers in baths and perfumes, so Lavandula angustifolia, called 'English lavender', is known today for its strong, calming scent.
