Etymology

Radio broadcast uses the word broadcast, which comes from farming: people would cast, meaning 'to throw', seeds broad, meaning 'far and wide', across a field. So a radio broadcast is like throwing sound across a wide area with radio waves, which is why it means 'a program sent out by radio'.
