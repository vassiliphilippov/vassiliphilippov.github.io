Etymology

Mesembryanthemum edule may come from old Greek and Latin words. The part Mesembryanthemum means 'midday flower' because these plants often open their flowers around noon, and edule means 'edible', which points to the plant's tasty fruit, so the name tells us it is a 'midday flower' with edible fruit.
