Etymology

The word a-flutter comes from the old English prefix a- meaning 'in' and the verb flutter, which is a quick, light movement like a bird's wings or a butterfly in your stomach. So being a-flutter is being 'in' that quick, nervous movement, meaning you feel excited or nervous.
