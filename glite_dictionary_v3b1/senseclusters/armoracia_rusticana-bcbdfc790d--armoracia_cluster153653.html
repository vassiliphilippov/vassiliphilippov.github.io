Etymology

Armoracia rusticana is the Latin name for the spicy plant we call 'horseradish'. One popular story says people compared its big, rough root to a strong horse and used radish for 'root', so the name gives a clear picture of a large, sharp root and that's why it refers to the hot, eye-watering condiment today.
