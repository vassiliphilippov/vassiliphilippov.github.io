Etymology

shrew-sized uses the tiny mammal shrew and the ending -sized, from size meaning 'how big something is', so it means 'as small as a shrew'. A helpful way to remember it is like cat-sized or pocket-sized: the word tells you the object's size by comparing it to a shrew.
