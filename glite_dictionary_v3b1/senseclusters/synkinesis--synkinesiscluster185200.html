Etymology

synkinesis comes from Greek parts syn- meaning 'together' and kinesis meaning 'movement', the same root you see in kinetic. So synkinesis describes when one body part moves 'together' with another without meaning to, which is why an eye or hand may twitch when a nearby muscle moves after nerve injury.
