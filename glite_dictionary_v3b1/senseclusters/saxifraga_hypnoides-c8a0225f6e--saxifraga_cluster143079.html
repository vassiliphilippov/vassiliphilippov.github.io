Etymology

Saxifraga hypnoides gets its name from old Latin and Greek roots. The part Saxifraga means 'rock-breaker' because these plants grow in cracks and seem to split stone, and hypnoides means 'moss-like' because it makes low, soft mats, so the name fits this small, mat-forming mountain plant.
