Etymology

devilfish comes from joining devil (meaning 'dangerous or strange') and fish ('sea animal'). Sailors used the name for the large manta ray because its wide, wing-like body and horn-like front fins looked strange and a little frightening, so the name now helps you remember this very big ray.
