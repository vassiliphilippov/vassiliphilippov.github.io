Etymology

Dressing sacque comes from the words dressing and sacque, where sacque was a word for a 'bag'. It was a loose, bag-like jacket women slipped on while 'getting dressed' to stay warm, so the name explains the 'loose-fitting jacket worn while dressing' meaning.
