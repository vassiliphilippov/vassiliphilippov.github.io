Etymology

Lonas annua comes from Latin: annua means 'one year', so the plant grows and blooms in a single season. The genus name Lonas may come from an old word for soft, fuzzy leaves, and the bright yellow, daisy-like flowers on this small shrub make the name easy to remember.
