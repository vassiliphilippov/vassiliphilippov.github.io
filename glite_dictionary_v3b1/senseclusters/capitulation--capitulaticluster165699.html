Etymology

capitulation comes from the Latin word capitulum, meaning 'little head' or 'heading', and from caput 'head'. Those 'headings' were short points that listed conditions, and in war they became the lists an army accepted when it gave up, so capitulation means the act of surrender under agreed terms.
