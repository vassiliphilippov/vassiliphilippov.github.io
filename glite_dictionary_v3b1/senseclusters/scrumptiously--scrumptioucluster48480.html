Etymology

scrumptiously comes from scrumptious, a playful word meaning 'very tasty'. A helpful way to remember scrumptious is to think of it as a mix of sumptuous ('rich' or 'luxurious') and the lively sound scrum, so scrumptiously means 'in a very tasty way'.
