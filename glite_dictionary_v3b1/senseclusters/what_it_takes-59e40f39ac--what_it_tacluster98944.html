Etymology

The phrase what it takes comes from the image of someone checking their tools and materials before starting a job. Imagine a builder making sure they have a hammer, nails and wood — if they do, they 'have what it takes' to finish the work, so the phrase now means 'necessary qualities' or the skills to 'succeed'.
