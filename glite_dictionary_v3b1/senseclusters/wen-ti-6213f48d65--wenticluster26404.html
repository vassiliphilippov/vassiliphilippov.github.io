Etymology

Wen-Ti comes from Chinese words wen meaning 'writing' or 'culture' and ti meaning 'emperor' or 'god', so the name is like a 'writing emperor'. Think of a king of books who guides students and writers, which is why Wen-Ti is the god of literature and is honored by scholars.
