Etymology

The word mycoplasma comes from Greek parts meaning 'fungus' (myco-) and 'formed' (-plasma). Early observers called them this because the tiny, blob-like cells looked fungus-like, and that image helps you remember that mycoplasma are very small bacteria that often have no cell wall.
