Etymology

arrest meaning 'to hold attention' also comes from the idea of 'stopping' in Latin restare 'to stay'. A strong sight can 'stop' your eyes, just like a pause, so it 'arrests' your attention. That's why to arrest someone's gaze is to catch and hold it.
