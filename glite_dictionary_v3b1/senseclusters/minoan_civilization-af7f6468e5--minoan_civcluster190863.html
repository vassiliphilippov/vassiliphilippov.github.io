Etymology

Minoan civilization was named by an archaeologist who linked the large palaces on Crete to the old story of 'King Minos' and his 'labyrinth'. That's why the name makes you think of the island's palaces, colorful wall paintings and early writing, and today it means the advanced Bronze Age culture from Crete.
