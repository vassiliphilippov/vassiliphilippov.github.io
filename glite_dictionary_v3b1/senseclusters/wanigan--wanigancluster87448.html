Etymology

Wanigan probably comes from an Ojibwe word waanigan meaning 'container' or 'box'. Think of it as a small, portable 'wagon' or box that holds food and tools by the river, which is why a wanigan is a movable shelter or boat used to store supplies or to serve as a camp kitchen.
