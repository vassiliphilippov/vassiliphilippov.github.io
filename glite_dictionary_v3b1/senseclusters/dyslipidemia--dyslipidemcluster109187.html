Etymology

dyslipidemia breaks into parts: dys- meaning 'bad' or 'abnormal', lipid meaning 'fat', and -emia meaning 'blood', like in anemia. So dyslipidemia literally means 'abnormal fats in the blood', which is why doctors use it for a condition with unhealthy levels of blood fats.
