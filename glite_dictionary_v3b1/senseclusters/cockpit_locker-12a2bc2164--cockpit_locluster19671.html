Etymology

The noun cockpit locker is the box under the seats in a boat's cockpit. The locker part shows it is a storage box for ropes and life jackets, so imagine lifting the lid under a seat to grab a rope, and that picture explains why it means a 'storage space' under the cockpit seats.
