Etymology

billion floating point operations per second paints a simple picture: imagine a counter doing one billion tiny math operations per second, and floating point means numbers with a moving decimal point so the computer can handle very big or very small values. So, the phrase simply tells 'how fast' a computer can do many math steps in one second.
