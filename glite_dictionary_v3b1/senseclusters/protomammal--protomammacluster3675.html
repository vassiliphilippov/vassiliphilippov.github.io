Etymology

protomammal breaks into proto- meaning 'first', as in prototype, and mammal, from a root meaning 'breast', because mammals feed their young with milk, so you can think of it as an early 'first' mammal. So, a protomammal is an early, now extinct animal that shows mammal features and stands at the start of the mammal family.
