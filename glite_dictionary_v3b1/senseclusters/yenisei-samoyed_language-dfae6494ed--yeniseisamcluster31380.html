Etymology

Yenisei-Samoyed language gets its name from the big northern river Yenisei and the old word Samoyed that people used for some northern groups, so imagine a cold river and small villages by its banks. So the name helps you remember it was the language of people by the Yenisei in northern Siberia and is now 'extinct'.
