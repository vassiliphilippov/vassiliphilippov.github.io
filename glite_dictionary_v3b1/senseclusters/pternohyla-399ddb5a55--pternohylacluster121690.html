Etymology

Pternohyla is a scientific name made from Greek parts: ptern- (linked to Greek pteron, meaning 'wing', as in pterodactyl) and -hyla (used for 'tree frogs'). A helpful way to remember Pternohyla is to think 'winged tree frog', and that name marks a group of tree frogs found in Central America even though many of them live by burrowing in the ground.
