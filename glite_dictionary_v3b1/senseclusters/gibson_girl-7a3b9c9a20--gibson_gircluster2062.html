Etymology

Gibson Girl comes from artist Charles Dana Gibson, who drew the same picture of a confident girl with high piled hair and a stylish long dress in magazines around 1900. Because these drawings showed one 'ideal' look again and again, the name Gibson Girl came to mean 'an ideal American young woman' of that time.
