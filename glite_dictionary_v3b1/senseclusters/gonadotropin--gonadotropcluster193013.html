Etymology

gonadotropin comes from two parts: gonad meaning 'reproductive gland' and -tropin meaning 'to turn' or 'to stimulate', like in tropic plants that turn toward light. So, a gonadotropin is a hormone that 'turns on' or stimulates the gonads (ovaries or testes), which is why it helps control reproduction.
