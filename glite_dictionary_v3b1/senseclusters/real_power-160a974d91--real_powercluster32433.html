Etymology

Real power is the part of electricity that actually makes a machine or heater run — the energy that 'does work' — not the energy that only goes back and forth in the wires. Imagine pushing a swing: pushes that move it forward are like real power, while pushes that only move it back and forth cancel each other, so real means 'doing work'.
