Etymology

autocutie moment may come from joining auto, meaning 'automatic', and cutie, meaning a 'cute' person or thing. If a phone or app catches a tiny, sweet scene by itself, that small, accidental moment is an autocutie moment, so the phrase means a short, especially adorable moment that makes people smile.
