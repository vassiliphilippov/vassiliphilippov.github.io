Etymology

Floozy probably began as a playful nickname, like Flo plus the ending -sy, so it sounded like a small, familiar name for a woman. A helpful way to remember it is to think of a woman called 'Flo' with a flashy or 'loose' reputation, which is why floozy now means a woman people see as sexually promiscuous.
