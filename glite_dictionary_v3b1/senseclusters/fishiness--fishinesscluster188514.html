Etymology

The word fishiness comes from the simple word fish plus the ending -ness, originally meaning the quality of 'smelling like fish'. A helpful way to remember the other meaning is this: if something 'smells' wrong people call it fishy, so fishiness now means both a fish-like smell and a feeling that something is 'suspicious'.
