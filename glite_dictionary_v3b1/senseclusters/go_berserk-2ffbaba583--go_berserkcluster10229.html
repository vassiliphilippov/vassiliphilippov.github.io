Etymology

The phrase go berserk may come from old Norse warriors called 'berserkers' who fought in a wild, uncontrolled way and sometimes wore bear skins. Here go means 'become', and because berserk described that wild fighting, go berserk now means 'to become violently angry or out of control'.
