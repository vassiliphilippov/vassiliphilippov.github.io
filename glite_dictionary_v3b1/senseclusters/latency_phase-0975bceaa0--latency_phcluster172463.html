Etymology

latency phase comes from Freud's idea that some sexual feelings are put 'hidden' for a time. The word latency means 'hidden', and the phase is the quiet time before puberty when those feelings are turned into schoolwork, play and friendships, so the phrase means the period when sexual interest is less active.
