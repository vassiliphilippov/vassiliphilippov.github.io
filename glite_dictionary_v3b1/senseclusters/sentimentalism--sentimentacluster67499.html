Etymology

sentimentalism comes from sentiment, which goes back to Latin sentire 'to feel', and the ending -ism, which means a 'tendency' or 'system'. So sentimentalism is literally a 'tendency to feel', which explains why it means showing too much emotion, often tender sadness or missing the past.
