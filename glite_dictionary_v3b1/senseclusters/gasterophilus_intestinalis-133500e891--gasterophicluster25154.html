Etymology

Gasterophilus intestinalis gets its name from old Greek and Latin parts: gastero- meaning 'stomach', -philus meaning 'lover', and intestinalis meaning 'of the intestine'. The name gives a clear picture — the fly's larvae live in a horse's stomach and intestines, so it helps you remember they are stomach parasites.
