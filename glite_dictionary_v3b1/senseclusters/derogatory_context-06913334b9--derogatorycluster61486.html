Etymology

derogatory context: The phrase derogatory context puts together derogatory (showing 'disrespect') and context (the words or situation around something). Imagine a cruel caption under a photo that makes the person look bad — that surrounding situation is a 'derogatory context', so the phrase means an environment that makes words or actions disrespectful.
