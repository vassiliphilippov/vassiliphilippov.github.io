Etymology

Pinus torreyana is a scientific name made of two parts: Pinus, the Latin word for 'pine', and torreyana, which is named after the botanist John Torrey. So the name simply marks this species as the 'Torrey pine' and helps you remember it as the pine linked to Torrey.
