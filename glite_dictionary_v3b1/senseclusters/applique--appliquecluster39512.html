Etymology

applique comes from the old French verb appliquer, meaning 'to apply' or 'to attach', and shares the same root as English apply. So an applique (as a noun) is a small piece of fabric 'applied' to a larger cloth, like a cloth sticker, which is why it is used as decoration.
