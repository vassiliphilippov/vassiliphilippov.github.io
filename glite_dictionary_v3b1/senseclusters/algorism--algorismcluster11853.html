Etymology

The word algorism comes from the name Al-Khwārizmī, a Persian scholar who wrote about the new 'Arabic numerals', the numbers 0–9, and over time his name came to mean the method of calculating with those numbers. It shares the same root as algorithm, so algorism means the system of using 'Arabic numerals' for counting and arithmetic.
