Etymology

strawberry daiquiri gets its name from putting crushed strawberries into a classic daiquiri. One popular story says the original daiquiri was a simple mix of rum, lime and sugar from a place called Daiquiri in Cuba, and when people blended the fruit with ice the name came to mean the sweet, frozen 'cocktail' we order today.
