Etymology

Prunus serrulata is a scientific name from Latin: Prunus was the old word for 'cherry' and serrulata means 'little saw', referring to the tiny saw-like teeth on the leaves. So the name simply describes the Japanese cherry tree known for its showy spring flowers.
