Etymology

posterior horn comes from a clear picture: posterior means 'back' and horn describes a small horn-shaped part of the spinal cord. Because this little 'horn' sits at the back and receives signals from sensory nerves, posterior horn now refers to the back part of the spinal cord that takes in 'senses' like 'touch'.
