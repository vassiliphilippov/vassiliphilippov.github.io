Etymology

keratosis blennorrhagica is a medical name that literally describes the skin picture: keratosis means 'hard or scaly skin' and blennorrhagica refers to a 'mucous-like discharge'. So the name is a memory hook that points to the scaly rashes often on the palms and soles and helps doctors link them to Reiter's syndrome.
