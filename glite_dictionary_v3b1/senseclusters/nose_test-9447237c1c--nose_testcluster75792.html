Etymology

nose test comes from the simple habit of using your nose to check something by smell. People would sniff food or drink to see if it was 'fresh' or 'safe', and that quick sniff became a 'test', so today nose test means a fast check by smell or by a quick 'gut feeling' about whether something seems right.
