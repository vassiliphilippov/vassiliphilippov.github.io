Etymology

Damnably comes from the strong word damn, from an old Latin word meaning 'to condemn', with the endings -able and -ly to make it an adverb. So saying something is damnably difficult means it is very bad or annoyingly bad, as if it 'deserves condemnation', which is why we use it to mean 'very' in a negative way.
