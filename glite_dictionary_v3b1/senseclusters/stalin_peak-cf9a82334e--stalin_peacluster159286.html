Etymology

Stalin Peak got its name in Soviet times when people often named big places after Stalin, the Soviet 'leader', and peak just means the top of a mountain. That's why the mountain was called Stalin Peak, but after Tajikistan became independent it was renamed Ismoil Somoni Peak.
