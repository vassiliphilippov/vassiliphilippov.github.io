Etymology

glomalin comes from a soil fungus name, Glomus, plus the protein ending -in, and Glomus comes from a Latin word meaning 'ball', as in globe. So glomalin is a 'protein' made by that 'ball' fungus that helps soil particles stick together into clumps, which is why it improves soil structure.
