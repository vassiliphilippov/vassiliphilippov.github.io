Etymology

The word precipitin comes from the verb precipitate, which means 'to make fall out', and the ending -in which is used for proteins. Think of precipitate like 'precipitation' or rain that makes things fall — a precipitin makes its antigen 'fall' out of a liquid, which is exactly what this antibody does.
