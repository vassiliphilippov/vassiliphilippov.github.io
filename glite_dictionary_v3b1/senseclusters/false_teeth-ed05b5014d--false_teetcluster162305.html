Etymology

False teeth comes from the simple idea of putting false, meaning not real, teeth made of bone, ivory, or later metal and plastic into the mouth when someone lost their own teeth. People often took them out and put them in a glass of water, so the phrase now means 'artificial teeth' used to replace missing natural ones.
