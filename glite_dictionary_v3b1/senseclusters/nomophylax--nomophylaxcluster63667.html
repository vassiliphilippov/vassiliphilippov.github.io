Etymology

The word nomophylax comes from the Greek parts nomos 'law' and phylax 'guard'. It literally means 'guardian of the law', so a nomophylax was a high official or scholar who explained and enforced rules, especially in the church. You can also see the root nomos in the word autonomy, which means 'self-rule'.
