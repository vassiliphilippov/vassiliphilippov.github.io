Etymology

CABG is a short form of coronary, artery, bypass, and graft. Think of a 'bypass' as a road that goes around a blocked street and of a 'graft' as a piece used to join or replace something like in gardening or a skin graft. So CABG is a surgery that uses a graft to go around a blocked heart artery and restore blood flow.
