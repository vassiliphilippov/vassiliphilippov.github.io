Etymology

dryland blueberry is a literal name: dryland + blueberry paints the picture of a blueberry that grows on dry, sandy ground instead of in wet bogs. So the name helps you remember it is a 'small bush' that makes 'dark, sweet berries' and prefers dry soil.
