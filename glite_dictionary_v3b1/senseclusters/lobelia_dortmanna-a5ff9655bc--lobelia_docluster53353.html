Etymology

Lobelia dortmanna has two name parts: Lobelia honors a long-ago botanist called Lobel, and dortmanna probably points to the person or place who first sent the plant to scientists. So, thinking of a botanist Lobel and a lake or person Dortmann helps you remember this small blue 'water plant' that holds its flowers above the water.
