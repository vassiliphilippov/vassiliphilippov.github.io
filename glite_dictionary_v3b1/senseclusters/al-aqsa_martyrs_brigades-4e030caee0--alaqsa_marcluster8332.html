Etymology

al-Aqsa Martyrs' Brigades takes its name from the al-Aqsa mosque in Jerusalem and the words martyrs and brigades. The name shows the idea of people ready to 'die for a cause' (martyrs) and a 'group of fighters' (brigades), so an armed group used it to show they fight for that holy place and the name now refers to an armed organization.
