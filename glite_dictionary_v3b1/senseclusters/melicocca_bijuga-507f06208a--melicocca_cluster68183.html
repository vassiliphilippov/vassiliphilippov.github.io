Etymology

The name Melicocca bijuga may come from old word parts: meli meaning 'honey' and bijuga meaning 'two joined', because the tree produces small, sweet fruits that often grow in pairs. That's why the name fits the fruit we now call 'Spanish lime' or 'mamoncillo'.
