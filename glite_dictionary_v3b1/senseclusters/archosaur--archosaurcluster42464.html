Etymology

archosaur comes from Greek parts archo- 'ruler', like the 'arch-' in archenemy, and -saur 'lizard'. So it literally means 'ruling lizard', which helps explain why this group included the dominant reptiles, such as the ancestors of dinosaurs and crocodiles.
