Etymology

Cornish fowl comes from Cornwall in England: Cornish shows where the bird was first bred and fowl simply means a domestic bird. Farmers in that area bred a short, heavy chicken for 'meat', so the name now refers to that compact, meaty breed used for roasters and crossbreeding.
