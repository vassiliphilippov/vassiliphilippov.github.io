Etymology

xenotransplantation begins with xeno- meaning 'foreign' and ends with the familiar word transplantation, which comes from transplant meaning 'to move tissue or an organ into someone else'. So it literally means 'transplanting from a foreign species', which is why it refers to moving animal organs or tissue into humans.
