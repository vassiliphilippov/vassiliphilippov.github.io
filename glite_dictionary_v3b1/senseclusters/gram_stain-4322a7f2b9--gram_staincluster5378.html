Etymology

The noun Gram stain is named after scientist Hans Christian Gram, who used a purple stain to color bacteria so they looked different under a microscope. Bacteria that keep the purple stain are called 'Gram-positive' and those that do not are called 'Gram-negative', so the test helps tell which kind of bacteria is present.
