Etymology

unadjustable is built from un- meaning 'not' and adjustable, from adjust meaning 'to make right' or 'put in the right place'. You can see the same root in the word just, which is about what is 'right'. So unadjustable means 'not able to be changed to fit', which is why we use it for things that cannot be changed to suit different needs.
