Etymology

Irish heritage comes from the simple image of family things and stories that come from Ireland: the word Irish points to the country and heritage is what is 'passed down' by parents and grandparents, like music, food, and traditions. So Irish heritage means the cultural and ancestral background 'from Ireland'.
