Etymology

duodenal ulcer The noun duodenal ulcer names a 'sore' called an ulcer in the first part of the small intestine, the duodenum. Think of the duodenum as a 'door' where food first enters the small intestine and the ulcer as a 'sore' that wears away the lining, so a duodenal ulcer is a sore at that 'door' and often causes pain in the upper belly.
