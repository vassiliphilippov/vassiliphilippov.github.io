Etymology

Begonia socotrana combines Begonia, the plant group named for a French plant lover Michel Bégon, and socotrana, which points to the island 'Socotra' where the plant was first found. So the name tells you it is a kind of begonia that comes from Socotra, off the coast of Yemen.
