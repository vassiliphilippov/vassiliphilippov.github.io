Etymology

The noun stinking gladwyn got its name from a simple picture: when you crush the leaves the plant gives a very bad smell, so people called it stinking and kept gladwyn as the plant name. That's why the name now means a 'type of iris' with purple flowers and leaves that give a 'bad smell'.
