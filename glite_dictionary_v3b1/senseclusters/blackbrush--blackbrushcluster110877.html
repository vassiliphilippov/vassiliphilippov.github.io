Etymology

The word blackbrush comes from two simple words, black and brush, with black meaning 'dark' and brush meaning 'a group of small bushes', giving the picture of dark, brushy plants in dry places. That's why blackbrush names a thorny desert shrub or the area where these dark bushes grow.
