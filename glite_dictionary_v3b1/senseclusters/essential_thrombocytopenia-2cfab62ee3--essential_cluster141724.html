Etymology

essential thrombocytopenia is a medical name made of two parts: essential meaning 'primary' or not caused by something else, and thrombocytopenia meaning 'too few platelets', with platelets being tiny blood cells that help stop bleeding. So the name means a main condition where the blood has too few platelets.
