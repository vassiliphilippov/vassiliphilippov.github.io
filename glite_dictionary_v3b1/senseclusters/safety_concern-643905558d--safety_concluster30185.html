Etymology

The phrase safety concern comes from joining safety, meaning being safe from harm, with concern, meaning a worry. Imagine a parent sees a broken swing and worries about danger and tells the school — they raise a 'safety concern', so the phrase means a matter that could be a 'risk' to people's health or safety.
