Etymology

catjang pea may come from the Malay word 'kacang', which simply means 'bean'. The name joins catjang (the local word for bean) and pea to show it is an edible, pod-bearing legume, so thinking 'bean pea' helps you remember it is a small plant grown for its seeds.
