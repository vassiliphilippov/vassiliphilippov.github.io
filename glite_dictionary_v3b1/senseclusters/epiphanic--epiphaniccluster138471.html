Etymology

epiphanic also comes from the Greek parts epi- 'on' and phanein 'to show' and is the same root as the Christian feast Epiphany, which celebrates the 'appearance' of Christ to the Magi. That's why epiphanic can describe things that are about or connected to that Epiphany celebration.
