Etymology

Olivier salad may come from a chef named Lucien Olivier who, long ago in Moscow, mixed a rich bowl of potatoes, meat, eggs and mayonnaise for guests in his restaurant. One popular story says people loved this 'party dish' so much that the chef's name stayed, and today Olivier salad means the potato, vegetable and egg salad often served at 'celebrations'.
