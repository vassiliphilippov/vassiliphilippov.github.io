Etymology

graphicness comes from a Greek word graph that means 'to write' or 'to draw', and you can see graph in familiar words like photograph and autograph. So, graphicness helps you imagine a very clear, strong picture in your mind, which is why it often describes a description that is 'very vivid' or 'shocking'.
