Etymology

The term vertical circle comes from sky watchers imagining a circle that stands straight up through the 'point above your head'. The word vertical shows it is upright and circle shows it goes all the way around. Astronomers used that upright circle to 'measure a star's height', so it means a large circle through the sky that goes up and down through the top point over you.
