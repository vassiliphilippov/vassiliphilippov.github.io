Etymology

chordomesoderm comes from parts that mean 'cord' and 'middle skin'. The chordo- part means 'cord', like a small 'backbone', and mesoderm means the 'middle' layer or 'skin' of an embryo, so chordomesoderm is the middle layer that becomes the cord-like notochord and helps form the early backbone.
