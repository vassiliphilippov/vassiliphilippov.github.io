Etymology

Fritillaria parviflora comes from Latin words: Fritillaria may come from a word for a 'dice box' because some fritillarias have a pattern of small squares on their bell-shaped flowers, and parviflora means 'small-flowered'. So the name tells you it is a small-flowered fritillaria with bell-shaped flowers.
