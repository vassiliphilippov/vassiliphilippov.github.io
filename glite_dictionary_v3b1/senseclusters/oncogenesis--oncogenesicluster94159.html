Etymology

oncogenesis comes from Greek parts onco- meaning 'tumor' and -genesis meaning 'origin' or 'creation'. Think of -genesis like the words generate and genetic, which are about 'making', so oncogenesis is the 'making of a tumor' — the process that turns normal cells into cancer cells.
