Etymology

The name cinnamon bun comes from its two parts: cinnamon, the brown 'sweet spice', and bun, a small 'round bread'. Imagine spreading cinnamon and sugar on dough, rolling it up and baking it. That's why the name fits the sweet, rolled pastry we eat today.
