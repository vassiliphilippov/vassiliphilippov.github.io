Etymology

Saint Thomas à Becket was the Archbishop of Canterbury who argued with King Henry II about the church's power and was killed in his church in 1170. Because many people saw his death as 'martyrdom', he was made a saint and pilgrims came to his shrine, so his name now reminds people of standing up for their religious beliefs.
