Etymology

Valenciennes lace gets its name from the town of Valenciennes, France, where makers used many small bobbins to weave open, flower-like patterns in lace. Think of people sitting with threads and bobbins making tiny flowers, and so the name came to mean 'a fine, floral bobbin lace'.
