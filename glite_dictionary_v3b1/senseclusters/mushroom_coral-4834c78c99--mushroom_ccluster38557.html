Etymology

mushroom coral is named because the coral's flat, round body looks like a mushroom cap and it often sits alone on the sea floor, not attached to rocks. So the image of a lone, cap-shaped coral helps you remember it is a 'flat, disk-shaped coral that lives alone'.
