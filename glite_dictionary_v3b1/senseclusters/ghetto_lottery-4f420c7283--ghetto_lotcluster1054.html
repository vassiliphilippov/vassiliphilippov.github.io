Etymology

Ghetto lottery may come from a joking image: someone in a poor neighborhood (the ghetto) finds a small lucky thing and feels like they won the lottery. So the phrase means an unexpected piece of 'good luck' in a tough place and is often said with a smile or a bit of irony.
