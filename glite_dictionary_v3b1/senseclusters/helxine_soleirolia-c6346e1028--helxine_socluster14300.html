Etymology

Helxine soleirolia is a tiny, creeping plant with many small, round leaves that make a soft green carpet. The first part, Helxine, was used for low, moss-like plants and the second part, soleirolia, may be the name of the person who first collected it, so the name helps gardeners remember it is a low, spreading 'groundcover'.
