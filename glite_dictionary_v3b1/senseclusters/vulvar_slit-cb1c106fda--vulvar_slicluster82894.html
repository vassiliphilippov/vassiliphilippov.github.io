Etymology

vulvar slit comes from the words vulvar, from the word 'vulva' meaning the outer female sexual parts, and slit, meaning a long, thin 'opening'. So it is a simple picture of a narrow gap between the outer folds of the vulva, meaning 'the opening between the labia'.
