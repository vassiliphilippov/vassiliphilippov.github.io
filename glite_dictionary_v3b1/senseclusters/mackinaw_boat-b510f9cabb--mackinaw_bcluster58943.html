Etymology

Mackinaw boat may come from the place name 'Mackinac' at the top of the Great Lakes, where people built and used these flat-bottom boats for fishing and carrying goods. That's why today Mackinaw boat means a small flat-bottom, pointed-at-both-ends 'boat' used on the upper Great Lakes.
