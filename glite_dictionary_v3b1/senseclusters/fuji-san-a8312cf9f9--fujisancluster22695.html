Etymology

Fuji-san names Japan's famous mountain, and the ending -san here is the Japanese word for 'mountain', not the polite 'Mr'. The origin of Fuji is unclear, but a helpful way to remember it is that people often write it with characters like 不二 ('not two') or 不死 ('not death'), which show why the mountain is thought of as unique and sacred.
