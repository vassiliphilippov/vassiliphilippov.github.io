Etymology

The noun Cornish pasty comes from Cornwall, where miners took a folded pastry, the pasty, filled with meat and vegetables as a 'portable meal'. They pinched and sealed one side so they could hold it with dirty hands and throw that edge away, so today Cornish pasty means that hand-held baked pie.
