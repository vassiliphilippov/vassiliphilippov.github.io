Etymology

Let's go, Brandon comes from a 2021 car-race interview when a crowd loudly chanted a rude line about President Biden and a reporter said they were shouting 'let's go, Brandon' to the driver Brandon Brown. So the phrase became a polite-sounding 'insult' people use to show criticism of President Biden.
