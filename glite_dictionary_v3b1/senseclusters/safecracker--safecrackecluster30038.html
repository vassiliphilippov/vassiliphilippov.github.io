Etymology

safecracker joins safe, which means 'a strong box for money and valuable things', and cracker, which means someone who 'cracks' things open. Think of a nutcracker that breaks a nut: a safecracker 'cracks' a safe to steal, so the word means a criminal who opens safes to take valuable things.
