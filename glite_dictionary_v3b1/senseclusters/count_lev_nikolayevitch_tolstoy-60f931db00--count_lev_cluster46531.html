Etymology

Count Lev Nikolayevitch Tolstoy shows his name and rank in one line: Count is a 'noble title', Lev is his first name, Nikolayevitch means 'son of Nikolai', and Tolstoy is his family name. He is the famous Russian writer of 'War and Peace' and 'Anna Karenina', so this full name tells you you are talking about that great author.
