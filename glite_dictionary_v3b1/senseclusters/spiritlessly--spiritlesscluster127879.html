Etymology

spiritlessly comes from spirit, from a Latin word meaning 'breath' or 'soul', plus -less meaning 'without'. A helpful memory is the same root in inspire, which literally means to 'breathe into', so spiritlessly describes doing something 'without breath' or energy and therefore without enthusiasm.
