Etymology

Ferdinand Victor Eugène Delacroix was a French painter known for bold paint strokes and bright color. Think of his famous picture 'Liberty Leading the People', where a woman leads a moving crowd and the strong colors and motion give a feeling of drama—this is why his name is remembered for lively, dramatic art.
