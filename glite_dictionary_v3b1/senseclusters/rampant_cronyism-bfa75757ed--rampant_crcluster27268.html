Etymology

rampant cronyism comes from the words rampant, meaning 'spreading everywhere', and cronyism, from 'crony' meaning 'a close friend'. Picture people in power filling many jobs and giving many favors to their friends so unfair help is all around, and that's why the phrase means 'widespread favoritism to friends' instead of choosing people for skill.
