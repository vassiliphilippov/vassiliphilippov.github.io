Etymology

saury may come from the old word saurus, meaning 'lizard', because the fish's long, thin body looked lizard-like to early namers, so the name fits this slender, beak-nosed fish. A helpful way to remember it is to think of the Japanese name sanma, 'autumn knife fish', which also points to the fish's long, sharp shape.
