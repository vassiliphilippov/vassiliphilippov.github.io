Etymology

dewy-eyed comes from dew ('small drops of water on plants in the morning') and eyed ('having eyes'), so it paints a picture of eyes that look fresh and a little wet like morning dew. That's why someone described as dewy-eyed seems 'innocent', 'naive', or gently 'emotional'.
