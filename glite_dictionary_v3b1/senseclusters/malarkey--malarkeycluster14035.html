Etymology

malarkey appeared in American slang in the early 1900s, and one popular idea says it comes from the Irish family name Malarkey. A helpful way to remember malarkey is to think of mal- as 'bad' so the word feels like 'bad or silly talk', which is why it means 'nonsense' or empty, insincere talk.
