Etymology

Pollyannish perspective comes from the children's book character Pollyanna, a girl who always looked for something good and even played a 'glad game' to find reasons to be happy. That image of someone who only sees the bright side explains why Pollyannish perspective means 'a view that is too optimistic' and often ignores real problems.
