Etymology

chondromalacia comes from Greek parts: chondro- meaning 'cartilage' and -malacia meaning 'softening'. So chondromalacia literally means the 'softening of cartilage', which explains why it names the condition when the cartilage in a joint, often under the kneecap, becomes soft or breaks down and causes pain.
