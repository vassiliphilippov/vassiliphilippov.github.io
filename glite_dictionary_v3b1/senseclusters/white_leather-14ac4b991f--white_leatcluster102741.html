Etymology

white leather is a name for leather that was made pale by a simple treatment of the skin, often using salts, so the material looks very light. Think white as the pale color and leather as animal skin: the phrase means leather that has been 'treated to be white' and is used for gloves, book bindings, and fine shoes.
