Etymology

Ananas comosus comes from two parts: Ananas comes from a South American word for 'excellent fruit', and comosus is a Latin word meaning 'tufted', which points to the plant's bunch of top leaves. So the name fits the pineapple — a tasty fruit with a crown of leaves.
