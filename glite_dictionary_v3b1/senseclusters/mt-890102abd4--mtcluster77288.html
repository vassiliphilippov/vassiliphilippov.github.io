Etymology

Mt is the chemical symbol for meitnerium, a man-made, radioactive element named after the physicist Lise Meitner. Like other element symbols such as He for helium, Mt uses the first letters of the name, so remembering Lise Meitner helps you recall that Mt stands for meitnerium.
