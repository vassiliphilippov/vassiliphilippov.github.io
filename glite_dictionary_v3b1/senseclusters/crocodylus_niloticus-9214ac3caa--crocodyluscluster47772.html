Etymology

Crocodylus niloticus is a scientific name that comes from old Greek and Latin words. Crocodylus is from a Greek word for 'crocodile' and niloticus means 'from the Nile', so the name shows a crocodile from the Nile and that is why it refers to the large Nile crocodile today.
