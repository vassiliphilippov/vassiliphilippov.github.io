Etymology

The word Cycadofilicales mixes the familiar plant name cycad, the root filic meaning 'fern', and the ending -ales meaning 'a group'. So the name tells you it is a group of plants that were like 'cycad-like ferns' and are now extinct, often called seed ferns.
