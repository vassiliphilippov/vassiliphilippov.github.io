Etymology

Jerusalem cricket may come from a mix of old names and a strong image: people saw its large, round head that looked a little like a small human skull and its habit of living in the soil, so Spanish speakers sometimes called it 'child of the earth'. One popular story says English speakers then gave it the odd name Jerusalem cricket, which is why the words Jerusalem and cricket are confusing, because the insect is not from Jerusalem and is not a true cricket.
