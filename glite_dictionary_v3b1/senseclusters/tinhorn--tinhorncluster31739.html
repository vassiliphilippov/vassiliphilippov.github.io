Etymology

tinhorn first named a noisy small-time man in 19th-century saloons, with tin ('cheap') and horn ('to boast' or make a loud sound) creating the image of a loud show-off. That's why a tinhorn as a noun is a person who pretends to be important or rich but really is not.
