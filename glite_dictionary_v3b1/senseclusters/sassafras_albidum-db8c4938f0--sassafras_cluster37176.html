Etymology

sassafras albidum takes its name from the common word sassafras and the Latin word albidum, meaning 'white', likely for the tree's pale wood or the light color under its leaves. Early settlers and Native Americans used the strongly smelling bark and leaves to make tea and oil, so the name now brings to mind a fragrant North American tree used for flavor and medicine.
