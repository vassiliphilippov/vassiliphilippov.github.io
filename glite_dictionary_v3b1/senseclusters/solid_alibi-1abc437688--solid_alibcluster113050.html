Etymology

solid alibi pairs solid (meaning 'strong') with alibi, an old legal word that meant 'elsewhere'. Imagine showing a ticket and friends who all say you were somewhere else; because this proof is strong, the phrase means 'a good excuse that shows you were not at the crime'.
