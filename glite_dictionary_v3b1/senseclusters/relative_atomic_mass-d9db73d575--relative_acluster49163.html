Etymology

relative atomic mass comes from the simple idea of showing an atom's mass 'relative' to a chosen standard. Scientists use one atom of carbon-12 as that standard and give it the number 12, so other atoms are measured by comparison to it. That's why the term means the mass of an atom shown as a number 'compared to' carbon-12.
