Etymology

T.B. is a short form of tuberculosis, which comes from the Latin tuberculum, meaning 'small lump' or 'swelling'. Doctors used that name because the disease often makes small lumps in the lungs, so T.B. refers to the infectious lung illness tuberculosis.
