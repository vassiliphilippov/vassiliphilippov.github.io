Etymology

heterodyne receiver comes from the idea of mixing two tones. Imagine two musical notes played together and you hear a slow 'beat' that is not in either note. Early radios did the same by 'mixing' a local tone with the incoming signal to make a new, lower tone that is easier to handle, so a heterodynereceiver is a radio that mixes signals to make a simpler one.
