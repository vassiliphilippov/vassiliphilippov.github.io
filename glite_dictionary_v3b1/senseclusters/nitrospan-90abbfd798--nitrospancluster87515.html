Etymology

Nitrospan is a trade name made from nitro and span. The nitro part points to nitroglycerin, a medicine with 'nitrogen' chemicals, and span suggests 'to stretch' or 'open', so a helpful way to remember Nitrospan is that it 'stretches' blood vessels to 'open' them and ease chest pain.
