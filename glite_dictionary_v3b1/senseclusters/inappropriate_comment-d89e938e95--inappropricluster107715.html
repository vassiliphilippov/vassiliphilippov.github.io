Etymology

The phrase inappropriate comment gives a simple picture: someone makes a comment that is clearly inappropriate for the situation. For example, joking at a funeral would upset people, so that remark is called an 'inappropriate comment', meaning a remark that is 'unsuitable' or 'offensive'.
