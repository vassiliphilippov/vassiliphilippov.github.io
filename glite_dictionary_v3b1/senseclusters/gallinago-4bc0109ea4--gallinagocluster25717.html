Etymology

The word Gallinago comes from the Latin gallina ('hen') and the ending -ago meaning 'resembling', so it can be thought of as 'hen-like'. A helpful way to remember it is to picture a small, ground-feeding marsh bird that looks a bit like a plump little hen, which is why Gallinago names a group of wading birds (snipes).
