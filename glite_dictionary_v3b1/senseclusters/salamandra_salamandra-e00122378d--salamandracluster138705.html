Etymology

Salamandra salamandra comes from old stories that link salamanders with fire, so it is often called the 'fire salamander'. People long ago found these black-and-yellow animals hiding in wet wood that was put on a fire, and they thought the creatures came out of the flames. That's why the name and its bright colours make people think of 'fire' today.
