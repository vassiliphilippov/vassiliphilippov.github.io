Etymology

messianism comes from Messiah, which comes from the Hebrew mashiach meaning 'anointed one', the same idea behind the English word anoint, which means to put oil on someone to show they are chosen. So messianism is the belief that a person or group will bring 'salvation' or a great change to the world.
