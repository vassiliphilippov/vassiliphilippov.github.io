Etymology

tinderbox of tension comes from the old box called a tinderbox, filled with dry material that can catch fire from one small spark and so is ready to burn, and tension here means the strong, nervous feeling between people. That's why a 'tinderbox of tension' describes a situation likely to suddenly 'explode' into conflict.
