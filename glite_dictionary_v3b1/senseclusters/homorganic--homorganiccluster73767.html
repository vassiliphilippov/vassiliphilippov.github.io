Etymology

The word homorganic comes from Greek parts homo- meaning 'same' and organ meaning 'instrument' or 'body part', and you can see homo- in homophone ('same sound'). So homorganic describes sounds made by the 'same' organ in the mouth, which is why it means speech sounds produced at the same place in the mouth.
