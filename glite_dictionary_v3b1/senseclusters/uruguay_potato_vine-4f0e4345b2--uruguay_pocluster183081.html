Etymology

Uruguay potato vine gets its name from three simple ideas: Uruguay (the country where the plant grows), potato (because it makes edible, potato-like tubers), and vine (because it climbs or spreads along the ground). So the name means 'a climbing plant from Uruguay with potato-like tubers'.
