Etymology

packwoman comes from pack, meaning 'bundle', and woman, so it originally named a woman who carried a bundle of goods. Think of someone with a backpack full of things who walks from place to place to sell at markets and fairs; that's why a packwoman is a woman who travels from place to place selling goods.
