Etymology

awfully comes from awe, a word that once meant 'wonder' or strong 'fear', so awful originally meant 'full of awe' in the sense of something very frightening. Over time awful came to mean 'terrible' or 'very bad', so awfully can mean 'in a very bad or unpleasant way'.
