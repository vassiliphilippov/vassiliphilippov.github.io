Etymology

The phrase wimpy stuff comes from the simple idea of wimpy (weak or soft) plus stuff (things). One popular story says 'wimpy' was used for a comic character who seemed weak, so wimpy stuff paints a picture of things that are not strong or not very good, and that is why people use it to mean 'something weak or of poor quality'.
