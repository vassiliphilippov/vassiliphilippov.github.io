Etymology

ribonucleic acid gets its name from its parts: ribo from a small sugar called 'ribose', nucleic because scientists first found it near the cell 'nucleus', the cell's control center, and acid because it acts like an 'acid'. So the name points to the molecule that carries 'genetic instructions' in cells.
