Etymology

acrylonitrile-butadiene-styrene is made by joining the three chemicals that make the plastic: acrylonitrile, butadiene, and styrene, so the name reads like a short 'recipe' and is often shortened to ABS. Each part gives a useful quality, so ABS is a tough, light plastic used for car bodies, appliance cases, and toys like LEGO.
