Etymology

agricultural export comes from the plain idea of farm goods being sent out of a country: agricultural means from farms and export means to send and sell abroad. Imagine farmers loading wheat or corn onto ships to sell in other lands — that picture explains why it means 'goods produced by farming and sold to other countries'.
