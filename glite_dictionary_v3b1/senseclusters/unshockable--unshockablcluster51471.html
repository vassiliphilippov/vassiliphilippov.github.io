Etymology

The word unshockable is made from un- 'not', shock 'a sudden surprise or hit', and -able 'able to', so it means someone who cannot be shocked or surprised. Think of a shock absorber that takes the hits: an unshockable person stays calm and is not easily offended or surprised.
