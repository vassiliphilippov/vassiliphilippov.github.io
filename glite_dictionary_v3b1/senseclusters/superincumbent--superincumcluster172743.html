Etymology

The word superincumbent breaks into super- meaning 'above' and incumbent from a root meaning 'to lie on', an idea you also see in incubate which means 'to sit on eggs' and recumbent which means 'lying down'. So a superincumbent thing is one that lies on top of something else and often presses down on it.
