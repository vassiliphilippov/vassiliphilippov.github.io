Etymology

Erysimum arkansanum may come from older plant naming: Erysimum was an old name for wallflowers, and arkansanum means 'from Arkansas'. So the name points to a wallflower-like plant from that area, and its tall orange-yellow blooms helped people picture it as the 'prairie rocket'.
