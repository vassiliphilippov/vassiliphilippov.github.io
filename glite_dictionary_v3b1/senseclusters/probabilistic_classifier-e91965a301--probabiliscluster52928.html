Etymology

The term probabilistic classifier brings a simple image: imagine a weather person who does not say just 'rain' or 'no rain' but gives a percent chance for each result. The word probabilistic points to 'chance' and classifier to something that sorts items into groups, so the name means a model that gives the chance for each class instead of 'one fixed answer'.
