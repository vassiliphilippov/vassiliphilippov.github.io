Etymology

marihuana comes from Mexican Spanish for the cannabis plant, and its exact origin is unclear but it probably comes from an Indigenous Mexican word. One popular way to remember it is to think of the Spanish names Maria and Juana joined together, and today marihuana means the cannabis plant used as a 'drug' or for 'medicine'.
