Etymology

The name divan bed comes from the word divan, a low sofa or bench from old Middle Eastern homes where people would sit in the day and sometimes sleep on it at night. So, calling something a divan bed tells you it is a low, armless couch that serves as a 'seat' by day and a bed at night.
