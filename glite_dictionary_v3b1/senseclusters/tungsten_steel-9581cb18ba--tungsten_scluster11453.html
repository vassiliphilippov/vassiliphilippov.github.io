Etymology

The name tungsten steel comes from adding the very hard metal tungsten to steel. The metal's name may come from old words meaning 'heavy stone', and because this mix keeps edges sharp and can stand very high heat, tungsten steel means a very hard, heat-resistant material used for cutting tools.
