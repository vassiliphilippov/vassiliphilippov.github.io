Etymology

Cirsium rivulare may come from old words that describe the plant: Cirsium may come from a word meaning 'swollen vein', a name once used for thistles, and rivulare means 'small stream' because this thistle often grows in damp places. So, the name helps you picture a tall, purple thistle that likes wet ground.
