Etymology

California buckthorn is named for two simple things: California shows where it grows, and buckthorn was a common name for thorny shrubs like this one with stiff branches and dark berries. It is also often called 'coffeeberry' because the dark fruit look like coffee beans, so the name helps you remember it's a native California shrub with small, dark fruits.
