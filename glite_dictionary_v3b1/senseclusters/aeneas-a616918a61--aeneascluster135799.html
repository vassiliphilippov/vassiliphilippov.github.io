Etymology

Aeneas is an old Greek name made famous by Virgil's poem the Aeneid, which tells the story of a Trojan hero and leader who travels to Italy. Because the poem shows Aeneas guiding survivors and helping to start a new people, his name is remembered as a 'leader' and a 'founder'.
