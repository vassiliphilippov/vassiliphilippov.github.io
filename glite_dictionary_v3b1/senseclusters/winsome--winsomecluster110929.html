Etymology

winsome comes from an old English word wynn, meaning 'joy' or 'pleasant', plus the ending -some, which means 'full of' as in handsome or awesome. So winsome originally meant 'full of joy', and that is why we use it today for someone who is charming, innocent, or pleasing.
