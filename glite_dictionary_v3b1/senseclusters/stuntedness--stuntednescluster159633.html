Etymology

stuntedness comes from the verb stunt, which means 'to stop' or 'to limit' growth, plus the ending -ness meaning 'the state of'. Think of a young plant that cannot grow because it lacks food or care — that image shows why stuntedness means the condition of being abnormally short or small.
