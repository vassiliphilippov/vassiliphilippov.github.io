Etymology

diaphragmatic hernia comes from the literal idea of a hernia (something 'pushing through') in the diaphragm (the main 'breathing muscle' between chest and belly). When part of the stomach pushes through a hole in the diaphragm into the chest, it can press on the lungs, so the term describes that problem.
