Etymology

Tartar sauce: one popular story says the name comes from the old French 'sauce tartare', where the word tartar was used for a strong, tangy mayonnaise. Because this creamy mix of mayo and chopped pickles was often served with fried fish, the name stuck and now means the pickle-y mayonnaise we eat with seafood.
