Etymology

Roman Catholicism comes from Roman, which means from Rome, and Catholic, an old word meaning 'universal'. Long ago, Christians who followed the leader in Rome were seen as the main, 'universal' church, and calling them Roman Catholicism shows their link to Rome and its leader, so the name now means the beliefs and organisation led from Rome.
