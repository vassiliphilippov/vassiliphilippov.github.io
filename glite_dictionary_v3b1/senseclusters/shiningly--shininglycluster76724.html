Etymology

shiningly comes from the simple verb shine, which means 'to give light'. A helpful way to remember the meaning 'very successful' is to imagine someone who 'shines' on stage and stands out like a bright light, so doing something 'shiningly' means doing it 'brilliantly' or 'very well'.
