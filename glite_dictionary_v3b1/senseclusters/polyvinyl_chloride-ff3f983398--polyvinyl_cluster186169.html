Etymology

polyvinyl chloride literally describes the material: poly means 'many', vinyl is a small piece used to build plastics, and chloride shows it contains chlorine. So the name tells you it is a plastic made of many vinyl pieces with chlorine, which is why we call this common durable plastic polyvinyl chloride.
