Etymology

common blackfish comes from what fishermen long ago saw: a small, dark whale that swims in tight groups and looks a bit like a big 'fish'. They used black for its dark colour and fish because it behaved like a fish, and 'common' showed it was the one seen most often, so the name now tells us it's a dark, small whale often led by a large male.
