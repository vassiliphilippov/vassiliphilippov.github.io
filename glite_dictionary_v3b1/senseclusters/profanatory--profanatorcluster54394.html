Etymology

The word profanatory comes from Latin parts pro- 'outside' and fanum 'temple', so it originally meant being 'outside the temple' or not sacred. You can see the same root in profane and profanity, and that's why a profanatory act shows disrespect to things people consider 'sacred'.
