Etymology

Cydonia oblonga may come from the ancient city of Cydonia in Crete, where people long ago grew the fruit we now call the 'quince'. The second word, oblonga, describes the fruit's long, pear-shaped look, so the scientific name points to the quince tree and its 'pear-shaped fruit'.
