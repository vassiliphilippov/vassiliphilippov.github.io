Etymology

precipitating comes from the Latin praecipitare, which meant 'to throw headlong' and combines prae- 'before' and caput 'head'; imagine being pushed head-first off a cliff (a precipice) — that sudden fall makes it easy to remember that to precipitate something is to cause it to happen quickly or suddenly.
