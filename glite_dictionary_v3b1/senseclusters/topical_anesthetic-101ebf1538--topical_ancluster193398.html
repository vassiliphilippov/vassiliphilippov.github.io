Etymology

topical anesthetic comes from the words topical meaning 'applied to a place' and anesthetic meaning 'a drug that stops feeling'. It is a medicine you put on the skin or inside the mouth to make just that small area numb, so it means 'a drug that makes a specific area lose feeling'.
