Etymology

A helpful way to remember freakery is to see it as freak ('a strange person or thing') with the ending -ery like in cookery, which makes a quality or collection. So freakery can mean the state or style of being very strange or playful, the kind of unusual attention people enjoy at festivals or art shows.
