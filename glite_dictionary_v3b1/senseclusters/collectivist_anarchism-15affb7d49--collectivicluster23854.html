Etymology

collectivist anarchism comes from the words collectivist, meaning group ownership, and anarchism, meaning 'no state' or no rulers. So, imagine workers sharing tools and making decisions together without a boss; that's why the term means a political idea to end the state and private ownership in favor of 'collective ownership'.
