Etymology

The noun imaginary creature comes from two simple parts: imaginary (made in the mind) and creature (a living being). People long ago made up monsters and strange animals in stories to explain things or teach lessons, so an imaginary creature means a being that is 'not real' and exists only in stories or the mind.
