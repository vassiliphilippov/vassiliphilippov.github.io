Etymology

mentally retarded comes from an old medical phrase: mentally meant 'of the mind' and retarded meant 'slowed' or 'delayed', so it was used to say someone's mental development was slower. Over time the word 'retarded' became a cruel insult, so today it is considered offensive and people say 'people with intellectual disabilities' instead.
