Etymology

Zionist movement takes its name from Zion, a name from the Bible for Jerusalem and the idea of a Jewish homeland. In the late 1800s many Jews who wanted to 'return' to that land began to organize to create a nation in Palestine, so the phrase came to mean the movement working for a 'Jewish homeland'.
