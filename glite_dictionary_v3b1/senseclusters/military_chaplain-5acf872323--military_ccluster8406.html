Etymology

The compound noun military chaplain comes from the simple picture of a religious helper walking with soldiers to lead prayers and give comfort. Here military means the soldiers and chaplain means the religious helper, so the phrase now means 'a chaplain who serves in a branch of the armed forces, providing religious support and counseling'.
