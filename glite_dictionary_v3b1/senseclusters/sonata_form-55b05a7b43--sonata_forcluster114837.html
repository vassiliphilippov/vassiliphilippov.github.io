Etymology

Sonata form treats a piece of music like a short story in three parts. The first part ('exposition') introduces the main tunes, the middle ('development') plays with and changes them, and the last ('recapitulation') brings them back. That's why sonata and form together mean the shape composers use to organise many first movements.
