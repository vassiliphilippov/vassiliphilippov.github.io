Etymology

Meconopsis betonicifolia comes from old Greek and Latin word parts: Meconopsis means 'poppy-like' and betonicifolia means 'leaves like betony'. So the name is simply a short description—a poppy with betony-style leaves—which helps explain why this is the Himalayan blue poppy.
