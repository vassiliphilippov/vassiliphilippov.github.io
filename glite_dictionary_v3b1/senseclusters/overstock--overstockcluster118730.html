Etymology

overstock comes from over meaning 'too much' and stock meaning 'goods kept to sell', so picture a shop with too many boxes on the shelves. A helpful image is piles of goods that cannot be sold, which makes it easy to remember that to overstock is to supply more items than are needed. That's why the word means filling a place with more goods than can be sold.
