Etymology

Abelmoschus esculentus may come from two parts: Abelmoschus from an old Arabic word meaning 'father of musk' because the seeds can smell a little like musk, and esculentus, a Latin word meaning 'edible'. So the name literally points to the edible okra plant, which helps you remember it refers to the okra we eat.
