Etymology

possibilism comes from the word possible ('able to be') plus -ism ('belief' or 'view'). A helpful way to remember the political sense is to think of it as the belief in doing what is 'possible'—making practical, gradual changes rather than seeking a full revolution. So, the origin explains why this political approach prefers realistic, achievable reforms.
