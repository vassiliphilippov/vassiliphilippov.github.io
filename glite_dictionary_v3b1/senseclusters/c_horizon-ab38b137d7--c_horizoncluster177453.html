Etymology

C horizon comes from the way people who study soil label the different layers with letters: the letter C marks the third, lowest layer and horizon simply means a soil 'layer'. Because this part is mostly weathered rock with little plant material, C horizon now refers to the deep layer of broken rock under the soil.
