Etymology

nasopharyngeal leishmaniasis gets its name from its parts: naso- meaning 'nose', pharyngeal meaning 'throat', and leishmaniasis, a disease from small flies that bite. Imagine those flies causing sores inside the nose and throat, and you can see why the name means a disease with sores in those places.
