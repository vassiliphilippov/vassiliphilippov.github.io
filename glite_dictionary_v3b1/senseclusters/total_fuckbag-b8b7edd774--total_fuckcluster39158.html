Etymology

Total fuckbag comes from a rude image: total makes it stronger, fuck is a strong swear, and bag suggests a container of bad stuff. One simple idea is that the phrase pictures a person as a bag full of nasty things, so calling someone a total fuckbag means 'a very bad person'.
