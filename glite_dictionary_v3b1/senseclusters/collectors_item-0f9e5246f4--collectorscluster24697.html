Etymology

collector's item comes from the simple idea of a collector, someone who gathers things, and an item, a single object; imagine someone carefully placing a rare stamp or old toy into a special case because collectors want it. That's why a collector's item means an object collectors 'value' and 'want' because it is rare or special.
