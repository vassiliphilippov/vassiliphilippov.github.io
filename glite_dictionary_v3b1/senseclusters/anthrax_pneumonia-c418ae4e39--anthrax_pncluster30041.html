Etymology

anthrax pneumonia gets its name from anthrax, an old word meaning 'coal' because some anthrax skin sores looked black like coal, and from pneumonia, a word for 'lung infection'. So the phrase describes when anthrax causes a severe infection in the lungs after tiny dust from infected animals is breathed in.
