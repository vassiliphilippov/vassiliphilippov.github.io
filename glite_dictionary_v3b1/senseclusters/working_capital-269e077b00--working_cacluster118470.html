Etymology

working capital comes from two simple ideas: working means in use for daily work and capital means the money or things a business owns. Imagine a shopkeeper using cash in the till and goods on the shelves to pay bills and buy more stock — that money is 'working' to keep the shop open, so working capital means the funds a company uses for its 'short-term' costs.
