Etymology

turning and boring mill gets its name from the two jobs it does: turning means the heavy metal piece is rotated so a cutting tool can 'shape' its outside, and boring means a tool 'makes' or enlarges a hole inside. That's why a mill with this name is a large machine for shaping and making holes in big parts by turning them.
