Etymology

hypermedia is made from hyper- ('over' or 'beyond') and media ('ways to share information', as in 'social media'). So hypermedia means many kinds of media pushed 'beyond' a single form and linked together so you can jump between them, which fits its modern use for interconnected multimedia.
