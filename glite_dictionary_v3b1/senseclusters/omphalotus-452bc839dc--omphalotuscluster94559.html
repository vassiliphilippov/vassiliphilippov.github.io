Etymology

Omphalotus comes from the Greek root omphalos, which means 'navel' or 'belly button'. These mushrooms often have a small hollow in the middle of the cap, like a belly button, so the name helps you remember their navel-like shape and fits a group that even includes species that glow in the dark, such as the jack-o'-lantern mushroom.
