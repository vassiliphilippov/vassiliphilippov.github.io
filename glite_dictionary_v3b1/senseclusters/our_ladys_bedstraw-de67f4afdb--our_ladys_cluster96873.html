Etymology

Our Lady's bedstraw may get its name from how people used the plant long ago: the stems of bedstraw were dried and used to stuff beds because they smelled nice and kept insects away. One popular story says people used this plant to make a bed for the baby Jesus for 'Our Lady', so the name came to mean this small yellow wildflower today.
