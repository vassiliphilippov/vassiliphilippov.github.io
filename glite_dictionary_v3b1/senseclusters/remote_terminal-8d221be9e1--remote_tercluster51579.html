Etymology

Remote terminal comes from the simple image of a terminal, a basic device placed remote, or 'far away', from the main computer. In early computing people used small terminals in one room to 'connect' to a big central computer in another room, so remote terminal came to mean 'a device you use to work on a computer from far away'.
