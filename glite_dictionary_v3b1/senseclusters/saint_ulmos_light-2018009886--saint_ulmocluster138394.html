Etymology

Saint Ulmo's light may come from old sailors who saw a blue glow dancing on a ship's mast and thought a saint was giving them a 'sign' of protection. That's why today Saint Ulmo's light means the blue electrical glow people sometimes see on masts or airplane wings during storms.
