Etymology

Don Budge was an American tennis player who in 1938 became the first person to win all four major singles titles in the same year: the Australian, French, Wimbledon, and U.S. championships. That complete sweep is now called the 'Grand Slam', so his name is remembered for being the first to reach that famous achievement.
