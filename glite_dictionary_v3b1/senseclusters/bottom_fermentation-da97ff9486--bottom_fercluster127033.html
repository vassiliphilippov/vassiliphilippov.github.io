Etymology

The term bottom fermentation comes from the simple image of yeast sinking to the bottom of the brewing tank during a slow, cold fermentation. Because the yeast sits at the bottom, brewers use this slow, cold method for 'lager' beers and it helps give them a 'clean, crisp' taste.
