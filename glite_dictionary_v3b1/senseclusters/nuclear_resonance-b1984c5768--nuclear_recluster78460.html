Etymology

The term nuclear resonance links nuclear, meaning the atom's tiny center, and resonance, meaning a matching note: imagine one nucleus giving off a gamma ray and another nucleus having the same exact energy so it 'resonates' and 'absorbs' the ray. So, the phrase means a nucleus takes in a gamma ray because it 'matches' the ray's energy.
