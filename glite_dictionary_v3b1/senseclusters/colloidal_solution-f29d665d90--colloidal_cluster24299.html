Etymology

colloidal solution names a mixture where very small solid particles float evenly in a liquid, like tiny fat drops in milk. The word colloidal points to the tiny pieces and solution to the liquid, so the picture of bits that 'do not settle' helps you remember it means a liquid with particles that stay spread out and do not sink to the bottom.
