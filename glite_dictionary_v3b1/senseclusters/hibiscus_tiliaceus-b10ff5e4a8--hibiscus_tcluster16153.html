Etymology

Hibiscus tiliaceus grows on warm island shores, and islanders used its light wood to make 'canoes' and its strong fibers to make 'ropes' and to seal boats. So, the name means a shrubby tree by the sea that people used for making boats and ropes and that is also grown in gardens for its pretty yellow flowers.
