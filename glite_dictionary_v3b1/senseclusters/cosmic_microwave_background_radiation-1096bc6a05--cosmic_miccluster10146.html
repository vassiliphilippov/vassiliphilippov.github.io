Etymology

Cosmic microwave background radiation is a name for the faint 'afterglow' left from the very hot early universe. Its parts paint the picture: cosmic means from the whole universe, microwave is the weak light we now detect, background shows it fills space, and radiation means energy, so this faint glow helps scientists learn about the universe's 'origin'.
