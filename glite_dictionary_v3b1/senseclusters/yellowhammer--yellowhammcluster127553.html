Etymology

yellowhammer is made of the everyday words yellow and hammer; yellow means 'yellow' for its bright feathers and hammer suggests the bird's hammer-like pecking and drumming on wood. So the name helps you remember a yellow woodpecker that seems to 'hammer' at trees.
