Etymology

crabber meaning a person who complains comes from crab, a word used for someone 'grumpy' or 'bad-tempered', plus the ending -er, meaning 'one who'. A helpful way to remember it is to think of someone who is always 'crabby'—a crabber is a regular complainer.
