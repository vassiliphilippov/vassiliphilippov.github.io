Etymology

The word dreamboat joins dream ('pleasant idea or wish') and boat ('small ship'). Early 1900s slang called someone a dreamboat if they were so perfect they seemed to 'sail' into your dreams, and that's why dreamboat now means a very attractive or desirable person.
