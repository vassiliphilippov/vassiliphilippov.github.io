Etymology

EDP is an abbreviation for electronic, data, and processing, where 'electronic' means 'using electricity and devices', 'data' means 'information', and 'processing' means 'working on' that information. So, EDP describes systems that use electronic machines to automatically handle and organize large amounts of information.
