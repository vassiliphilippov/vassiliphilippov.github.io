Etymology

G. K. Chesterton is the name of the English writer Gilbert Keith Chesterton, who often signed his work with his initials. He became famous for short, clever 'essays' and the 'Father Brown' 'detective stories' that mix quick wit with religious ideas, so his name now reminds readers of witty, thoughtful writing and small mystery tales.
