Etymology

The term holy person comes from religious life, where a person who lived simply, prayed a lot and helped others was seen as set apart for the sacred. People pictured the holyperson as pure or even with a 'halo', and they trusted them to guide or heal others, so holy person now means someone very 'spiritual' and 'good'.
