Etymology

The noun custard pie comes from old comedy shows where someone would throw a soft custard-filled pie into another person's face as a funny trick. That messy, surprising picture became the sign of clownish 'comedy', so today a custard pie usually means a pie used as a 'prop' in slapstick performances.
