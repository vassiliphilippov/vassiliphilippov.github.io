Etymology

Bombax malabarica may come from two simple images: Bombax from an old word for cotton, and malabarica pointing to the Malabar region of India where the tree grows. The tree makes silk-like, cottony fibers used for 'stuffing' pillows and mats, so the name helps you remember it is a silk-cotton tree used for 'stuffing'.
