Etymology

The word sanctioner comes from the root sanct- meaning 'holy', the same root in sanctuary and sanctify. Because laws were once treated as sacred, a sanctioner could both approve actions and impose penalties on rule-breakers, which is why the word can mean both an 'approver' and a 'punisher'.
