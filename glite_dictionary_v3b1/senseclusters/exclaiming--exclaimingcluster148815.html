Etymology

exclaiming comes from Latin parts ex- meaning 'out' and clamare meaning 'to shout'. So, to exclaim is to 'shout out' suddenly, and the same root appears in proclaim and claim, which helps explain why exclaiming means a sudden, excited cry.
