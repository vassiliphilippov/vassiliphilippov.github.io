Etymology

writ of habeas corpus comes from Latin words habeas corpus that literally mean 'you must bring the body'. Imagine a judge ordering guards to bring a prisoner into court so the court can check if the person is being held legally, which is why today it means 'an order to bring a prisoner before a judge'.
