Etymology

Thompson submachine gun was named after the man who invented it, inventor Thompson, and it was one of the first small automatic submachine guns that could fire many bullets quickly. Because it was easy to use in close fighting, both soldiers and criminals used it a lot, so the famous image of the 'Tommy gun' explains why the name now means that particular kind of gun.
