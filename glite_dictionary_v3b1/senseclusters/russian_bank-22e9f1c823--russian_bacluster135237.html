Etymology

Russian bank may get its name from the way each player has their own bank, a 'pile' of cards to manage, while both players race to 'get rid of' all their cards. The word 'Russian' was probably added because the game was popular in Russia or sounded exotic, so the name gives the image of two rival 'banks' racing to finish.
