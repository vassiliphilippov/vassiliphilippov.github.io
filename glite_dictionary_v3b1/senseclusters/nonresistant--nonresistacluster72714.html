Etymology

The word nonresistant has the prefix non- meaning 'not' and the part resist meaning 'to stand against' or 'hold back', so it literally feels like 'not able to stand against' something. That's why we call people, plants, or materials nonresistant when they easily give in to a force, disease, or influence.
