Etymology

Dwight Eisenhower was an American general who led the Allied armies in Europe during World War II and helped plan the D-Day invasion. He later became the '34th President of the United States' and promoted big projects like the interstate highways, so his name is a memory hook for strong leadership, careful planning, and national building.
