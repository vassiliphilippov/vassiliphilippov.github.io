Etymology

desoxyribonucleic acid is built from parts that describe the molecule: deoxy means 'without oxygen', ribo refers to a sugar, and nucleic acid points to a substance from the cell 'nucleus'. So the name tells you it is the long molecule in the cell's 'nucleus' that carries the 'genetic information' which tells living things how to grow and work.
