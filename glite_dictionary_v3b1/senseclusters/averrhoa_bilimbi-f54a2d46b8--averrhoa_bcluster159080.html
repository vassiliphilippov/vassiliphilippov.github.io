Etymology

Averrhoa bilimbi mixes a tribute and a local name. The first part, Averrhoa, honors the old scholar 'Averroes', and the second part, bilimbi, comes from the Malay name for the very 'sour' fruit, so the name points to the tree known for its sour, cucumber-shaped fruit used in cooking.
