Etymology

The phrase terminate with extreme prejudice comes from secret military and spy orders where officers used formal words to hide a deadly command. They used terminate to mean 'end' and extreme prejudice to show very harsh action, so the whole line quietly meant 'to kill' and today it is used to say 'to kill someone in a very harsh way'.
