Etymology

caruncular comes from the Latin caruncula, a word for a small 'piece of flesh', which in turn comes from caro meaning 'flesh'. A helpful way to remember it is to think of carnivore ('meat-eater') with the same car- root, so caruncular means 'like a small fleshy bump' on an animal or person.
