Etymology

The noun pineapple plant comes from the fruit name pine + apple because early visitors thought the fruit looked like a 'pine cone' and tasted a little like an 'apple'. So the plant is simply the one that grows that spiky fruit with a tuft of leaves on top, which is why we call it a pineapple plant.
