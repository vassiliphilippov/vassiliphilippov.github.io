Etymology

spark arrester: The noun spark arrester comes from a simple image — a metal mesh or guard that catches flying sparks and 'arrests' them, meaning 'to stop', before they leave a chimney or smokestack. So a spark arrester today is a device that keeps sparks from escaping and helps prevent fires.
