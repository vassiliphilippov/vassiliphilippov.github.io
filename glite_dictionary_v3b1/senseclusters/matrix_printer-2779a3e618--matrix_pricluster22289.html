Etymology

matrix printer gets its name from the way it prints: matrix means a 'grid', and the machine makes each letter as a pattern of 'dots' by hitting an ink ribbon with many small pins. Because the pins strike the ribbon onto the paper, these printers are noisy and can make carbon-copy sheets, so the name explains how they work today.
