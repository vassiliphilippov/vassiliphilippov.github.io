Etymology

Personal digital assistant was named to suggest a small, electronic 'assistant' you carry with you. The words personal, digital and assistant mean 'for one person', 'electronic' and 'a helper', so the name creates the image of a pocket-sized helper for contacts and schedules and now means a small handheld device for managing personal information.
