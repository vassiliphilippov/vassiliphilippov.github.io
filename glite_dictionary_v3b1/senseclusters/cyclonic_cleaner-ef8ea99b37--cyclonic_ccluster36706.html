Etymology

cyclonic cleaner The name cyclonic cleaner comes from cyclonic meaning 'like a cyclone' and cleaner meaning 'a machine that cleans'. Imagine a tiny tornado inside the machine that spins dust into a clear bin, which is why this vacuum keeps strong suction and does not need bags.
