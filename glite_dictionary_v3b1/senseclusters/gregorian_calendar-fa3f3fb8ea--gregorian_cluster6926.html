Etymology

The Gregorian calendar was introduced by Pope Gregory XIII in 1582 when he 'skipped' ten days and changed how leap years work to bring the year back in line with the seasons. That's why the Gregorian calendar is the modern system most countries use to keep dates and seasons correct.
