Etymology

The word catlinite comes from the name Catlin, an American artist who showed the stone to many people, plus the mineral ending -ite, and it is the same material often called 'pipestone' that Native Americans carved into ceremonial pipes. So, catlinite means a soft, reddish 'stone' used for carving pipes and small objects.
