Etymology

Unculturable comes from un- ('not'), culture (a word for 'to grow' or 'farm', seen in cultivate and agriculture) and -able ('can'). So land called unculturable is land you 'cannot make ready for farming', which explains the meaning 'not suitable for crops'.
