Etymology

The word nirvana was taken from the Sanskrit nirvāṇa, made of nir- 'out' and vāṇa 'blow', which makes you think of worries being 'blown out'. So people call a spa, a quiet cabin, or any place of complete calm a nirvana, meaning a state of perfect happiness and peace.
