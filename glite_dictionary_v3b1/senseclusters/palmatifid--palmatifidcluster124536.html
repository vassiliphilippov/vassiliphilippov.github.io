Etymology

palmatifid comes from Latin parts: palm- meaning 'palm' or 'hand' and -fid meaning 'split' or 'cleft'. So a palmatifid leaf is like an open hand whose 'fingers' are split almost to the base but not all the way, which is why the word describes this kind of leaf.
