Etymology

The phrase speccy glasses may come from people shortening 'spectacles' to 'specs' and then saying speccy as a friendly word for noticeable glasses. Because this paints the picture of someone proudly wearing eye-catching frames, speccy glasses now means 'stylish' or 'a fashion statement'.
