Etymology

seidhr comes from the Old Norse seiðr, a kind of 'magic' in ancient Scandinavia used to see the future in trance rituals. A helpful way to remember it is to think of a seer—someone who 'sees' the future—because seiðr often meant using ritual to look into or change what will happen.
