Etymology

crypto-fascism joins crypto- meaning 'hidden' and fascism, a form of extreme, controlling politics, and crypto- is the same start as in cryptocurrency, which shows the modern link. So crypto-fascism means secret support for fascist ideas, often hidden online or in crypto communities.
