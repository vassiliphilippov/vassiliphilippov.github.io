Etymology

Middle-ear deafness comes from a simple, physical image: tiny bones in the middle of the ear carry sound to the inner ear, and if those bones do not work the sound cannot pass. So middle-ear deafness means a 'hearing loss' caused by problems with the ear's middle bones.
