Etymology

Capparidaceae comes from the caper plant name Capparis plus the scientific ending -aceae, which means 'family', and it is easy to remember if you think of the small pickled capers used in cooking. So Capparidaceae simply means the 'family of caper-like plants', which is why it names a group of related flowering plants.
