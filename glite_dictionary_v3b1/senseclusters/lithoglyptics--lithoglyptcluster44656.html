Etymology

The word lithoglyptics comes from Greek parts litho- 'stone' and -glyptics 'carving', like the familiar hieroglyph where 'glyph' is a carved sign. So lithoglyptics is basically 'stone carving', which explains why it means the art of engraving designs or words on precious stones.
