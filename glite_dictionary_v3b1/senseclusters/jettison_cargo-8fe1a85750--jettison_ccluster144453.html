Etymology

jettison cargo comes from the literal act of throwing cargo off a ship or plane in an emergency so the ship or plane becomes lighter. Sailors and pilots would jettison heavy items to stop a ship from sinking or to help a plane land safely. So today it means 'to throw goods overboard or out of a vehicle to reduce weight in an emergency'.
