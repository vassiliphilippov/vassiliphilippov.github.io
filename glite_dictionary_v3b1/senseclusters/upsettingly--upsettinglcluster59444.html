Etymology

upsettingly comes from the word upset, made of up and set, which originally meant to 'turn over' something such as a cart. Later upset came to mean 'disturb' or 'make someone unhappy', and the ending -ly turns it into 'in a way that', so upsettingly means 'in a way that causes worry or makes someone unhappy'.
