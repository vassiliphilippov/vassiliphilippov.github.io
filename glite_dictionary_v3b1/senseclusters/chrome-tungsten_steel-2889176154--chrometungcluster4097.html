Etymology

chrome-tungsten steel: The noun chrome-tungsten steel names a type of steel made by adding shiny chrome and very hard tungsten to the metal. Picture steel with a shiny, rust-proof surface and heavy parts that stand high heat; that image helps remember it means 'strong' and 'resistant to wear and heat'.
