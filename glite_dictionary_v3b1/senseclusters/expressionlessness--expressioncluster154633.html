Etymology

expressionlessness starts with expression, which comes from parts meaning 'to press out' and is the same root as the common word press and the verb express. The endings -less and -ness give the idea 'without' and make a noun, so expressionlessness literally means 'without expression', not showing feelings on your face, in your voice, or in your actions.
