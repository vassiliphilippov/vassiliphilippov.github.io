Etymology

Archeozoic eon comes from old words meaning 'ancient life' and paints a picture of Earth in its very first days, when the crust was forming and only tiny single-celled life existed. So the name refers to that long period, about 3,800 to 2,500 million years ago, when Earth's earliest life appeared.
