Etymology

Texas leaguer comes from one popular story about a Texas player, Ollie Pickering, who in 1901 hit many soft fly balls that fell just between the infielders and outfielders. People began calling these small, 'lucky' hits 'Texas leaguers', so today Texas leaguer means a soft fly ball that drops between fielders and often becomes a base hit.
