Etymology

Sealed instrument comes from old papers that were closed and marked with a wax or pressed stamp. The fact that the paper was sealed and the instrument carried a visible stamp acted like a strong signature to prove the deal, so today sealed instrument means a formal contract shown to be 'authentic'.
