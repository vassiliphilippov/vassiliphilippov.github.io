Etymology

Cetorhinus maximus comes from old Greek and Latin words: Cetorhinus may mean 'sea monster' and maximus means 'largest'. So the name gives an easy picture of a very large, slow shark that swims near the surface and eats plankton, which helps you remember this harmless giant.
