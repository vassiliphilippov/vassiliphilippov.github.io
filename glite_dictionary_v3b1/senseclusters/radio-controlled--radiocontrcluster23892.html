Etymology

radio-controlled starts with radio, from a Latin word radius meaning 'ray', which became the name for invisible signals that travel like rays, and ends with controlled, from 'control' meaning 'to guide'. So a radio-controlled device is one you 'guide' from far away using radio 'rays'.
