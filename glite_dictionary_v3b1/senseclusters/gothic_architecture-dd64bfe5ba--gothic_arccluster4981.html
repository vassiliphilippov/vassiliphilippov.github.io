Etymology

Gothic architecture was a name given later by people who thought the tall churches from the 'Middle Ages' with sharp, pointed arches looked rough and like the work of the old 'Goths' or 'barbarians'. Because of that name, Gothic architecture came to mean the style with pointed arches, ribbed vaults, and flying buttresses, which is the meaning we use today.
