Etymology

Gymnopilus ventricosus comes from old Greek and Latin words: Gymnopilus suggests a 'naked' cap and ventricosus means 'swollen' or 'belly-like'. This image of a smooth cap above a thick, belly-shaped stalk explains why the name fits this mushroom today.
