Etymology

mythmaking joins myth, from Greek mythos meaning 'story', and making meaning 'creating', so it means 'making stories'. Think of it like a craft: someone who does mythmaking builds strong stories that shape what people believe, which is why the word means creating or promoting widely believed stories.
