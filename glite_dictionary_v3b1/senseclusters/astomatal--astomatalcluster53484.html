Etymology

astomatal comes from the Greek parts a- meaning 'not' and stoma meaning 'mouth' or 'opening', so it literally means 'no opening'. Think of a leaf with no little 'mouths' on its surface, and that is why an astomatal plant or leaf has no natural openings for gas exchange.
