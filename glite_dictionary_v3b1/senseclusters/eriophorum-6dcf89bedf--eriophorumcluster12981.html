Etymology

Eriophorum comes from Greek parts erion and phoros, where erion means 'wool' and phoros means 'bearing' or 'carrying'. So, Eriophorum is like a 'wool-bearer', which fits the plant's fluffy, white, cotton-like seed heads you find in bogs and other wet places.
