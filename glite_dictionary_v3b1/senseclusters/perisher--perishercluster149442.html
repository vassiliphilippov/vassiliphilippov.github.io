Etymology

perisher is an informal British word for a naughty or troublesome person. A helpful way to remember it is to link it to perish ('to die' or 'be ruined'): think of someone who 'spoils' calm or 'ruins' things, which is why perisher can mean a mischievous child or a disagreeable person.
