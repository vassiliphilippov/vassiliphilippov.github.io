Etymology

atomic number 17 comes from how people count tiny particles in an atom: the atomicnumber is the count of small positive particles called protons, and when there are 17, the atom is chlorine. So, atomic number 17 means 'chlorine', the yellow-green gas often used to clean and kill bacteria.
