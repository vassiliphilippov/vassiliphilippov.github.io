Etymology

Coluber comes from Latin and originally meant 'snake', so it became the scientific name for a group of fast, non-venomous snakes often called racers. A helpful way to remember it is to see the root colub- as meaning 'snake', which explains why Coluber refers to slender, quick snakes.
