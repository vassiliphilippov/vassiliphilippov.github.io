Etymology

unreplaceable starts with un- meaning 'not', comes from replace which is re- 'again' plus place 'put', and ends with -able 'can'. So it literally says 'not able to be put back again', which is why we use it for things you cannot make or find again, like a one-of-a-kind painting or a family heirloom.
