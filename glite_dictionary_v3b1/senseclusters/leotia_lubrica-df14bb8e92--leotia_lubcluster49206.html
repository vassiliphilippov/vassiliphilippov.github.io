Etymology

The fungus Leotia lubrica gets its name from its jelly-like look: small, rubbery caps that grow together like little blobs. People often call it 'jelly baby' because the tiny, soft caps look like little sweets, and the second word lubrica comes from a Latin word meaning 'slippery', which explains its wet, slimy feel.
