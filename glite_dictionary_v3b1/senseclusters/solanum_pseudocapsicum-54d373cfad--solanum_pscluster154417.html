Etymology

Solanum pseudocapsicum comes from the plant's look: the parts pseudo and capsicum mean 'false' and 'pepper', so it is like a 'false pepper' with small, red, pepper-like fruits. That's a useful memory because these bright, cherry-sized fruits look pretty like tiny peppers or cherries but are 'poisonous' and not for eating.
