Etymology

Macrocheira kaempferi is a scientific name made of two parts: macro and cheira, meaning 'large' and 'hand', which paints the image of very long, hand-like legs. The second part, kaempferi, honors a naturalist named Kaempfer, so the whole name fits the giant Japanese spider crab we know today.
