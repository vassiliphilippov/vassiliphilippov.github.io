Etymology

proselytizer comes from the old Greek word proselytos, which meant a 'newcomer' or 'convert' who had 'come over' to a new group. The first part pros- means 'toward', so a proselytizer is someone who tries to bring people 'toward' their belief — in other words, to make them 'convert'.
