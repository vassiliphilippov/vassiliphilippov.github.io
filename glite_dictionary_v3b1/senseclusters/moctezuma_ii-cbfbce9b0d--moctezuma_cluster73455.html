Etymology

The name Moctezuma II belongs to the last ruler of the Aztec Empire who met the Spanish conqueror Hernán Cortés. He was captured and killed in 1520, so his name now refers to the 'last Aztec emperor of Mexico' at the time of the Spanish conquest.
