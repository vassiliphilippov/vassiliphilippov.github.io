Etymology

hypersaccharine attitude comes from the parts hyper (too much) and saccharine (very sweet); imagine someone pouring so much sugar into a drink that it tastes fake. So a hypersaccharine attitude describes a person who is overly friendly or sweet in a way that feels 'insincere'.
