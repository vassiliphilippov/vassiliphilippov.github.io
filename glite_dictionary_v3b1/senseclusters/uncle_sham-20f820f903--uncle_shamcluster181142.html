Etymology

Uncle Sham may come from a play on the name Uncle Sam and the word Sham, which means 'fake'. The image is of a friendly uncle who pretends to be honest but is really 'fake', so people call someone Uncle Sham when they are dishonest and pretending to speak for the government.
