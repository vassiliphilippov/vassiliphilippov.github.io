Etymology

combined transport bill of lading comes from the simple idea of joining different shipping papers into one, with combined meaning it joins, transport meaning the movement of goods, and bill of lading meaning the shipping paper. So, this one paper lets a 'single carrier' take charge of the 'whole journey', which is why it shows one company is responsible when goods move by different ways like ship and truck.
