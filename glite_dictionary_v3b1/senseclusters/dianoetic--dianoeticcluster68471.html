Etymology

dianoetic comes from Greek parts that mean 'through' (dia-, as in dialogue) and 'thinking' (noe-). It literally suggests 'thinking through', so a dianoetic approach is one that reaches conclusions by careful, step-by-step reasoning rather than by intuition.
