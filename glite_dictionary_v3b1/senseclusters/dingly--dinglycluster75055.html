Etymology

dingly can also mean 'small' and a bit 'shabby', and this sense may be linked to the familiar word dingy, which means 'dirty' or 'worn'; a helpful way to remember it is a tiny, well-used cottage that feels cosy despite its wear, so 'dingly' can mean charmingly worn or slightly unpleasant in look.
