Etymology

dreadlocked comes from dread and locks, where locks means 'hair' and dread once meant 'fear' but in Jamaican and Rastafari use it can mean 'deep respect'. So dreadlocked describes hair that is 'locked' into long, twisted strands, which is why it means having hair styled as dreadlocks.
