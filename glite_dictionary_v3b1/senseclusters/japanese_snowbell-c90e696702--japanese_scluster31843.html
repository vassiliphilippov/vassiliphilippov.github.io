Etymology

Japanese snowbell gets its name from where the plant comes from and how its flowers look. The word Japanese shows it comes from Japan, and the white flowers hang like little bells and cover the branches like snow, so the name helps you picture a 'small tree' with fragrant, bell-shaped flowers.
