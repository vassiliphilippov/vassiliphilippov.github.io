Etymology

brigand comes from old Italian and French words like brigante and brigare, which mean 'to fight' or 'to cause trouble', so a brigand was originally someone who made trouble on the roads and often robbed travelers. You can also notice the link with brigade, which means 'a group', because brigands often worked together.
