Etymology

fair shake of the sauce bottle may come from a simple table scene: when people pass a bottle of sauce, each person gets a quick shake to get some from the sauce bottle. If someone did not get a fair shake of the sauce bottle, they missed an equal turn, so today the phrase means 'a fair chance' or 'an equal share'.
