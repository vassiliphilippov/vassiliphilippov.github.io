Etymology

tetrahydrocannabinol breaks into three parts: tetra- meaning 'four', hydro- meaning 'water' or 'hydrogen', and cannabinol from cannabis meaning 'hemp' or 'marijuana'. So tetrahydrocannabinol is the named chemical from the cannabis plant, and that is why this long word refers to the compound that causes most of the plant's 'high' effects.
