Etymology

The word phenoplast comes from phenol, a kind of chemical, and plast, a part that means 'moulded' and appears in the familiar word plastic. So a phenoplast is a hard, heat-resistant plastic made from phenol chemicals, which is why it is used for strong parts that must resist heat.
