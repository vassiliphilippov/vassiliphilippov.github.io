Etymology

Prosopis juliflora comes from two parts: the group name Prosopis (the mesquite group) and the second part juliflora. One common idea is that juliflora means 'July flowers' because the tree makes long clusters of flowers in the hot season, so the name helps you remember a hardy, drought-resistant mesquite that blooms in summer.
