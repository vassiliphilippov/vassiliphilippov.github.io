Etymology

The word macroglobulin comes from two parts: macro- from an old Greek word for 'large' and globulin from an old Latin word for 'round' or 'ball', like the word globe. So, think of a macroglobulin as a 'large round protein' in the blood, which fits its role in the immune system and in blood clotting.
