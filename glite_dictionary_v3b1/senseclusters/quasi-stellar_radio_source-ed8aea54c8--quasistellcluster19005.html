Etymology

The name quasi-stellar radio source comes from early space research. Scientists saw tiny bright points that looked like stars but sent out very strong radio waves. The parts quasi-stellar ('almost a star') and radio source ('thing that makes radio waves') give a clear picture: a star-like object that made powerful radio signals, and this object later became known as 'quasar'.
