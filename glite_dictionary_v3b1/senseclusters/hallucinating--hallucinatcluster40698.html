Etymology

hallucinating comes from the same Latin hallucinari, meaning 'to wander in the mind', and was first used for people who saw things that were not real. A helpful way to remember the computing use is to think of an AI 'seeing' false facts, so an AI that is hallucinating makes up information that looks real but is not true.
