Etymology

tilt angle comes from the words tilt, meaning to lean, and angle, meaning the measure of that lean. Imagine a picture frame pushed to one side; the tilt angle is how far it leans. So, the term means 'the angle at which something is tilted' and is used when people need to aim or set the position of a thing, like a rocket or camera.
