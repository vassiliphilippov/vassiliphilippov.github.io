Etymology

Galactosemia comes from the Greek root galact- meaning 'milk' and the ending -emia meaning 'blood', and the same galact- is in the word galaxy, which links to the idea of the Milky Way or something 'milky'. So, galactosemia is a condition where the sugar from milk builds up in the blood because the body cannot break it down.
