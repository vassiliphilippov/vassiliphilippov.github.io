Etymology

sinistrorsal comes from the Latin word sinister, which means 'left', plus a second part that means 'to turn' or 'to rise', as in the related word sinistrorse. So sinistrorsal literally describes a spiral that turns toward the left as it grows upward, which is why we use it for shells and vines that coil from right to left.
