Etymology

The word YHWH comes from ancient Hebrew where God's special name was written with four letters and no vowels, so the exact sound was not written down. Because the name was kept very sacred, people often said Adonai instead and English Bibles usually print it as 'the LORD', while some suggest the original sound was like Yahweh, which explains why YHWH is used as the holy name of God in the Bible.
