Etymology

shoulder girdle uses the old word girdle, which meant a 'belt', and shoulder for the top of the arm. The collarbones and shoulder blades make a belt-like frame that 'supports' and 'connects' the arms to the body, so the name helps you picture the bones that hold the arms in place.
