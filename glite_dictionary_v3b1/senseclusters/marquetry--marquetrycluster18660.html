Etymology

marquetry comes from the French word marquet, which meant 'to inlay' or set small pieces into a surface. Think of marquetry as a wooden 'mosaic'—small bits of wood, ivory, or metal are put together to make patterns or pictures, which is why marquetry means decorative inlay work on furniture.
