Etymology

unequivocal comes from un- ('not') and equivocal, which itself comes from parts meaning 'equal' (equi-) and 'voice' (voc-), like the idea of 'equal voices' so something could be read in two ways. That's why unequivocal means 'completely clear' and leaves no doubt.
