Etymology

symbol-worship comes from the parts symbol and worship: symbol is from a Greek idea of a 'sign' or 'token' (like two matching pieces used to show a connection), and worship comes from an old English idea of showing 'worth' or honor. So symbol-worship means giving honor to a sign itself instead of to what the sign stands for.
