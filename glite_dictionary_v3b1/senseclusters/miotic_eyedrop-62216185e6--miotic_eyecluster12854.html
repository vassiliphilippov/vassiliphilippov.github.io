Etymology

Miotic eyedrop is named for what it does: miotic means 'making the pupil smaller' and an eyedrop is a small drop of medicine put in the eye. By making the pupil smaller, the drop helps fluid leave the eye and so 'reduce pressure', which is why doctors use it for glaucoma.
