Etymology

hypothalamic releasing factor. The noun hypothalamic releasing factor names tiny chemicals made in a small part of the brain called the hypothalamus. The word hypothalamic shows where they come from and releasing factor shows their job: they 'cause the pituitary gland to release other hormones', so the name literally means a brain signal that makes another gland release hormones.
