Etymology

The term cognitive factor comes from the words cognitive, meaning 'thinking', and factor, meaning a thing that affects a result. Picture small parts inside the mind that push how you pay attention, remember, or decide, so a cognitive factor is a mental part like attention or memory that helps explain why someone behaves or performs in a certain way.
