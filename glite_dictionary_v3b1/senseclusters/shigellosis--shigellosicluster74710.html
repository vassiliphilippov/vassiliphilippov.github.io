Etymology

shigellosis comes from the name of the Japanese doctor Shiga, whose name was used for the bacteria Shigella, and the ending -osis means 'disease' or 'condition'. So shigellosis simply means the 'disease caused by Shigella', which explains why it names an intestinal infection.
