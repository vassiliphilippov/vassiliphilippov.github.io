Etymology

Gnetophyta comes from the plant-name Gnetum and the suffix -phyta, which means 'plants'. So, Gnetophyta literally means 'Gnetum plants', and that is why it is the name for the small, rare group of seed plants that sit between cone-bearing trees and flowering plants.
