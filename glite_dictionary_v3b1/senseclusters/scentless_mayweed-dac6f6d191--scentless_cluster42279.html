Etymology

scentless mayweed gets its name from how it looks and when it blooms: mayweed was used for small daisy-like weeds that often flower in 'May', and scentless shows this one has little or no smell. So when you see a small white daisy-like weed in fields that gives no scent, the name scentless mayweed tells you both its appearance and why it is called that.
