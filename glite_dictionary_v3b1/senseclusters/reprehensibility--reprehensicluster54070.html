Etymology

reprehensibility comes from the Latin reprehendere, which meant 'to blame' and is built on prehendere meaning 'to take', the same root you see in apprehend and comprehend. So, something 'reprehensible' deserves blame, and reprehensibility is the quality of being blameworthy.
