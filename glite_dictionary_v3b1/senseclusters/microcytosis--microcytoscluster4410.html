Etymology

The word microcytosis comes from the Greek parts micro- meaning 'small', as in microscope, cyt- meaning 'cell', like the common word cell, and -osis meaning 'condition'. So microcytosis literally means a 'condition of small cells', which explains why it names the problem of having too-small red blood cells and the anemia that can follow.
