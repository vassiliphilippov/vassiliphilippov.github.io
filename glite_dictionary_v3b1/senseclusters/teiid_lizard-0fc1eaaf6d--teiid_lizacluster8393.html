Etymology

teiid lizard comes from the scientific family name 'Teiidae', a label early scientists used for a group of long-tailed, scaly lizards from the Americas. So calling an animal a teiid (a kind of lizard) tells you it belongs to that family and usually has a long tail and big belly scales.
