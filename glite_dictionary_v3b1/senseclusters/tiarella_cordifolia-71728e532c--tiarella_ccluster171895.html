Etymology

Tiarella cordifolia may come from two parts: Tiarella, meaning 'little crown' because the small groups of flowers look like tiny crowns, and cordifolia, meaning 'heart' and 'leaf' because the plant has heart-shaped leaves. That's why the name fits this woodland plant with soft white flowers and heart-like leaves.
