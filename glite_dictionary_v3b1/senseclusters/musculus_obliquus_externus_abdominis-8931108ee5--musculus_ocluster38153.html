Etymology

musculus obliquus externus abdominis comes from Latin words that name the body parts: musculus means 'muscle', obliquus means 'diagonal', externus means 'outer', and abdominis means 'of the belly'. So the name pictures the outer slanted belly muscle that helps twist the body and tighten the belly.
