Etymology

Prunus incisa: The botanical name Prunus incisa comes from Latin: Prunus means a cherry tree and incisa means 'cut' or 'notched', because the leaves often have small notches at their tips. So the name helps you remember it is a small Japanese cherry tree with pale pink blossoms.
