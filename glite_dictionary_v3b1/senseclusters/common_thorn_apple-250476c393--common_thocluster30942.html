Etymology

common thorn apple: The name common thorn apple comes from the plant's spiky seed pods that look like a rough 'apple'. The word thorn points to the sharp spines and apple is an old word for 'fruit', while 'common' means the plant is found in many places, so the name helps you picture a widely seen plant with a spiny, apple-like fruit that is poisonous.
