Etymology

hop-o'-my-thumb comes from an old folk-tale name that plays on hop, o', and thumb, where thumb makes you think of someone 'as small as a thumb'. A helpful way to remember it is to imagine a clever little boy who 'hops' and uses his small size to trick grown-ups, which is why the name means a very small but smart character in stories.
