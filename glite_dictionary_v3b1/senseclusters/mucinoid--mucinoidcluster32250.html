Etymology

The word mucinoid comes from mucin, which comes from Latin mucus meaning 'mucus', plus the ending -oid which means 'like' or 'similar'. So, mucinoid means 'like mucin' or 'like mucus', and it describes things that look or feel jelly-like or sticky.
