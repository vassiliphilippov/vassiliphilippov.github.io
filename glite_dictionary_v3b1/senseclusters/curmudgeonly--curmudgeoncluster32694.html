Etymology

curmudgeonly means grumpy or bad-tempered, and its origin is not clear, but one popular story links it to the French phrase coeur méchant, which means 'bad heart'. So a helpful way to remember curmudgeonly is to imagine a cranky, unfriendly person with a 'bad heart' who is quick to complain.
