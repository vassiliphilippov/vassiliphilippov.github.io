Etymology

millivoltmeter breaks into three parts: milli- meaning 'one thousandth', volt meaning 'the push that moves electricity', and meter meaning 'device that measures', like in thermometer. So a millivoltmeter is a measuring tool for very small voltages called millivolts, which explains its modern use for reading tiny electrical signals.
