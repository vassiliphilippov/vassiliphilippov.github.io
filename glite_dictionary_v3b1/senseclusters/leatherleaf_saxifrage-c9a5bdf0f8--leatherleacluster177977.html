Etymology

leatherleaf saxifrage comes from two simple images. Leatherleaf describes its thick, tough leaves, and saxifrage is an old word meaning 'stone-breaker' because the plant often grows in rocky, moist places, so the name helps you picture a tough-leaved plant living on rocks.
