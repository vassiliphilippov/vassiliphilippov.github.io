Etymology

ecstasy comes from Greek parts ek- 'out' and stasis 'standing', so it literally meant 'standing outside yourself' and gives a clear image of leaving your normal state. That's why ecstasy now means a state of such intense joy or emotion that you seem to be beyond your usual self.
