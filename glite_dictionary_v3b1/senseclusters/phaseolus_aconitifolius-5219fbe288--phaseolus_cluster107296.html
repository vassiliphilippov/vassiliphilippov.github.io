Etymology

Phaseolus aconitifolius comes from old Latin words: Phaseolus means 'bean' and aconitifolius means 'leaves like aconite', a different plant. So the name is simply 'the bean with aconite-like leaves', and that image helps you remember it is a small, hardy bean grown for food and to improve the soil.
