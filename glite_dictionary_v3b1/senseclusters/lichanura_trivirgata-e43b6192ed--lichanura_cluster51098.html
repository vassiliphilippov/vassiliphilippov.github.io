Etymology

Lichanura trivirgata comes from old Latin words that describe the snake's look: tri- means 'three' and -virgata means 'striped'. Because this small, rosy boa often has three dark lines along its body, the name gives an easy picture to help you remember the species and why people call it the 'rosy boa'.
