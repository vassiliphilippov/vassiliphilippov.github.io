Etymology

guide fossil comes from the idea of a fossil acting like a guide or signpost. Because some creatures lived across large areas but only for a short time, finding their fossil in a rock is like finding a stamp that shows the rock's 'age', so scientists use these fossils to 'date' and match rock layers.
