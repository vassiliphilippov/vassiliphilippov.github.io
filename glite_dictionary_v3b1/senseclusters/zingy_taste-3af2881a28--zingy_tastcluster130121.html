Etymology

zingy taste comes from the little word zing, which sounds like a quick, sharp action or sound, and the word taste for flavour. Imagine biting a lemon and feeling a quick 'zing' in your mouth — that bright, lively feeling is why zingy taste means a 'sharp' or 'lively' flavour.
