Etymology

condensing turbine comes from the machine's simple picture: the turbine is turned by steam and then the steam cools and condenses into water in a condenser, making a low pressure behind the blades. That's why a condensing turbine means a turbine that works with a condenser to pull more steam through and 'make more power'.
