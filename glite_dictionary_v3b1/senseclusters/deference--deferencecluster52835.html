Etymology

deference comes from Latin parts de- ('away') and ferre ('to carry'), a combination that came to mean 'to yield' or 'to give up'. Think of the English verb defer, which can mean 'to accept someone else's choice' or 'to delay', so deference is politely giving way to another person's wishes.
