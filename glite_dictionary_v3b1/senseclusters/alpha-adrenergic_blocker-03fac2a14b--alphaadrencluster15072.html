Etymology

The term alpha-adrenergic blocker shows what the drug does: alpha names a kind of tiny 'lock' on cells, adrenergic means it responds to 'adrenaline' signals, and blocker means the medicine stops those signals. So imagine a key, 'adrenaline', trying to open a lock at the alpha spot but being held back, and that stopping helps muscles relax and 'lower blood pressure'.
