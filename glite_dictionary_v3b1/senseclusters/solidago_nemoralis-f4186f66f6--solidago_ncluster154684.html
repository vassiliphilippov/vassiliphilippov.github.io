Etymology

Solidago nemoralis comes from Latin words: Solidago means 'to make whole' because people once used goldenrod for healing, and nemoralis means 'of the woods'. So the name gives the picture of a healing goldenrod that grows near woods, which helps you remember this yellow-flowering species.
