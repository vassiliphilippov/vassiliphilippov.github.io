Etymology

impressiveness comes from impress, which comes from an old word meaning 'to press' or 'to make a mark'. Think of a stamp pressed into clay — it leaves a clear mark, just like something impressive leaves a strong feeling in people. That's why impressiveness means the quality of making a 'strong impression'.
