Etymology

crème brûlée gets its name from French: crème means 'cream' and brûlée means 'burnt'. The dessert is a smooth custard with sugar on top that is burned with a hot tool until it becomes a hard, crackable crust, so the 'burnt' top explains the name and the dessert today.
