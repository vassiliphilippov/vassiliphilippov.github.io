Etymology

lumbosacral plexus comes from parts: lumbo meaning 'lower back', sacral meaning 'base of the spine', and plexus meaning a 'network' or 'braid' of nerves. Imagine a small braid of nerves at the base of the spine that sends messages to the hips and legs, so it is the nerve network that controls movement and feeling in the lower body.
