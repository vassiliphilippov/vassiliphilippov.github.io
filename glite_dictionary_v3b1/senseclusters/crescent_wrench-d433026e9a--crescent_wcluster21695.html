Etymology

The tool crescent wrench gets its name from its curved, moon-shaped jaw and from a company called 'Crescent' that made the popular version. The picture of a crescent (a small curved moon) on the jaw helps you remember it is an adjustable wrench used to grip and turn nuts and bolts.
