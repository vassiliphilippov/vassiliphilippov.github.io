Etymology

Saint Jerome was a Christian scholar who translated the Bible into Latin, a version called the 'Vulgate'. One popular story says he pulled a thorn from a lion's paw, so artists often show him with a lion, and that's why he is remembered as an important 'saint' and the man who made the standard Latin Bible.
