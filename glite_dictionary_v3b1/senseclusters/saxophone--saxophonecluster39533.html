Etymology

saxophone comes from the name of its inventor, Sax, plus the Greek part -phone meaning 'sound' or 'voice', so it literally means 'Sax's sound'. Think of words like telephone or microphone to remember -phone, and that's why a saxophone is the musical instrument that makes that sound and is often heard in jazz.
