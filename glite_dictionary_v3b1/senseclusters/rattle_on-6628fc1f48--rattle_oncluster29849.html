Etymology

The phrasal verb rattle on comes from the image of a small thing that keeps making a quick, noisy sound, like coins in a box that keep rattling. The noisy idea of rattle plus the sense of continuing in on makes a picture of someone who talks without stopping about little, boring things, so rattle on means 'to talk continuously, often in a tiresome or boring way'.
