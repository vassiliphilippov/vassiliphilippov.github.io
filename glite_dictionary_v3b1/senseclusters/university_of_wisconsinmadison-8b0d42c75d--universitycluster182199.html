Etymology

University of Wisconsin–Madison gets its name from where it is: it is the state University of Wisconsin and sits in the city of Madison, a city 'named after' U.S. president James Madison. Because it was set up as the main public university for the state in that city, the name now stands for a large public university known for teaching and research.
