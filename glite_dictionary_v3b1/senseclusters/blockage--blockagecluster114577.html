Etymology

The word blockage comes from the familiar word block, which originally meant a large piece of wood or anything that 'blocks' movement, and the ending -age, which makes a noun meaning 'a thing or state'. So a blockage is something that 'blocks' a pipe, road, or tube and stops the flow.
