Etymology

Lycopodium obscurum comes from old words: lyco meaning 'wolf' and podium meaning 'little foot', because the plant's low, branched shape can look like a tiny paw on the forest floor. The last word obscurum means 'dark' and points to the shady places it likes, so the name helps you picture a dark, ground-hugging evergreen plant.
