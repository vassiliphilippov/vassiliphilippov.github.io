Etymology

The word gallinipper is an old Southern US name for a very large mosquito. A helpful way to remember it is to notice the last part nipper, which looks like 'one who nips' or 'to bite', so gallinipper feels like a big mosquito that 'nips' hard and that is why it means a very large, biting mosquito.
