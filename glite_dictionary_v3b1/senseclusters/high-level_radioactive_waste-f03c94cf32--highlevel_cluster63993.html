Etymology

high-level radioactive waste describes the leftover material from used nuclear fuel that is very radioactive and even gives off heat, so you can imagine a glowing, hot container. The word high-level shows it is a 'high' amount of radioactivity and radioactive waste is the dangerous leftover. That's why it means the 'most dangerous' kind of nuclear waste that must be stored carefully for a very long time.
