Etymology

finger-flower comes from the Latin digitus meaning 'finger', and the plant's scientific name digitalis shares the same root. Its long, tube-shaped flowers look like small fingers you could slide a finger into, so the name fits the plant's shape and reminds us that its leaves were used to make a heart medicine while also being poisonous.
