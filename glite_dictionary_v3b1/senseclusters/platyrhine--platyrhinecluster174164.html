Etymology

The word platyrhine comes from Greek parts platy- meaning 'broad' or 'flat' and -rhine meaning 'nose', the same root you see in rhinoceros. So platyrhine literally means 'broad-nosed', which is why scientists use it for monkeys or people with wide, widely separated nostrils, like many New World monkeys.
