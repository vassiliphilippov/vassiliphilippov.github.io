Etymology

George I was a German ruler who became the first Hanoverian king of Britain in 1714. He spoke little English and often stayed in his home, Hanover, so he left everyday government work to his ministers, and his rule is remembered as the start of the 'Hanoverian dynasty' and a time when ministers gained more power.
