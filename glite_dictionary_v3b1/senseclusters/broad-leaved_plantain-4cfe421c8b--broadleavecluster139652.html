Etymology

broad-leaved plantain is a simple picture name: one popular story says plantain comes from an old word for the 'sole of the foot' because the plant's flat, wide leaves look like a foot, and broad-leaved means the leaves are wide. That's why the name fits this common 'weed' you see in lawns and gardens.
