Etymology

muzzy can also mean mentally confused and likely comes from the same idea as a 'fuzzy' head or the common word muddle, which means 'confused' or 'a mess'. A helpful way to remember it is to imagine your thoughts are 'fuzzy' and 'mixed up', which is why muzzy means unable to think clearly.
