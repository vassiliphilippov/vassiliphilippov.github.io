Etymology

linking verb comes from the image of a bridge: the linking part means it joins two things and verb shows it is a word that does the joining. Words like 'be' and 'become' act like that bridge, joining the sentence's subject to a description, so the name explains why they don't show action but 'connect' the subject to more information.
