Etymology

Muhammad al-Mahdi is a name from Muslim belief for a promised leader. The part al-Mahdi means 'the guided one', and one popular story says he is hidden now and will return to set things right and bring the true religion, so the name refers to the hoped-for savior who will restore justice.
