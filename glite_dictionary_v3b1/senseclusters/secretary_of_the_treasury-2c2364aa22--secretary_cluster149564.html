Etymology

The title Secretary of the Treasury uses two simple ideas: a treasury is where the state keeps its 'money', and a secretary is a trusted person who manages important affairs. One can picture a trusted helper standing by the treasury to guard and run the money. That's why the title means the top official 'in charge' of the country's money.
