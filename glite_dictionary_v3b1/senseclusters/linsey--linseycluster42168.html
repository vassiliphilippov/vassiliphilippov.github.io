Etymology

linsey comes from the same root as linen, which means 'cloth from flax', and it is linked with wool because the fabric mixes the two fibres. So, linsey is a coarse, sturdy cloth made from linen and wool, which is why it was used for warm, hard-wearing clothes and quilts.
