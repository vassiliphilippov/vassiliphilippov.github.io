Etymology

plantain family combines plantain, a small flat-leaved herb, and family meaning 'group of related plants'. The name plantain comes from Latin 'planta' meaning 'sole of the foot' because the leaves are flat like a shoe sole and were once used on wounds, so plantain family means the group of these flat-leaved herbs.
