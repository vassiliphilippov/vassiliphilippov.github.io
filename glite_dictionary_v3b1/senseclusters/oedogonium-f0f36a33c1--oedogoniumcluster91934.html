Etymology

Oedogonium comes from Greek parts oedo- meaning 'swollen' and -gonium meaning 'seed' or 'offspring'. A helpful way to remember it is to picture small swollen 'seed' cells along the long, thin threads of this freshwater green alga, which is why the name points to its reproductive cells.
