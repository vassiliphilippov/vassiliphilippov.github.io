Etymology

The word fronded comes from frond, a simple word for a large fern or palm leaf, and from the old Latin frons meaning 'leaf'. So a fronded plant is 'covered with fronds', meaning it has many fern- or palm-like leaves, which is why the word describes plants with those big, divided leaves.
