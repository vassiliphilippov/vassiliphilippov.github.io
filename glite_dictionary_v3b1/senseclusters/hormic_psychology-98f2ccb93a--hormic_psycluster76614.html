Etymology

hormic psychology comes from an old Greek word 'horme' meaning 'impulse', and the word hormic suggests being pushed by an inner 'urge'. So, imagine someone driven toward a clear aim; that's why this view sees behavior as directed toward goals, not just automatic reactions.
