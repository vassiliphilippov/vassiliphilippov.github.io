Etymology

Denis Diderot was an 18th-century French writer who collected and edited a huge book of knowledge called the 'Encyclopédie'. Because he brought together many thinkers and helped spread ideas about reason and progress, his name is linked to the 'Enlightenment' and to modern ideas about knowledge.
