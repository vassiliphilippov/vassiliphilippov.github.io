Etymology

Deflationary spiral paints a clear picture: deflationary means 'prices falling' and spiral is a shape that keeps turning down. When prices drop, people wait to buy so demand falls and prices drop again, and the spiral keeps going down, so the phrase means continuing price falls and an economy that gets worse.
