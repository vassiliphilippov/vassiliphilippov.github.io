Etymology

The word bearishly comes from the animal bear and the ending -ish, which means 'like', so it describes moving or acting 'like a bear'. A helpful way to remember it is to picture a big, clumsy bear pushing through a crowd, which explains the meaning 'rough' or 'clumsy'.
