Etymology

Stock epithet may come from old storytellers who kept a stock, a store of ready phrases, and used a short descriptive tag called an epithet, like 'rosy-fingered Dawn'. These ready labels helped singers keep the song's beat and helped the audience remember people and places, so today stock epithet means a repeated descriptive phrase for a person or thing.
