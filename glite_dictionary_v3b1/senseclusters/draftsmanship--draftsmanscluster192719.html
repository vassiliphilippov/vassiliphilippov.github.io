Etymology

The word draftsmanship comes from draft, meaning 'a careful drawing or plan', and the ending -ship, meaning 'skill' or 'quality', and from draftsman, a person who makes precise drawings. So, draftsmanship is the skill of making clear, accurate drawings like the plans for a building.
