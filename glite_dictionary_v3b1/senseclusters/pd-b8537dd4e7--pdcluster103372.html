Etymology

Pd is the chemical symbol for the metal palladium, and it uses the capital 'P' and small 'd' from that name. The name palladium was given after the asteroid Pallas, so thinking of that space rock can help you remember the name; today Pd is the short label chemists use for this silver-white metal in jewelry and industry.
