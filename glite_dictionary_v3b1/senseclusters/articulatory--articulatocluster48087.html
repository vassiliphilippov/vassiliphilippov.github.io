Etymology

articulatory comes from the Latin root articul-, which means 'joint' or 'small part', and it is the same root as the common word articulate. Think of your lips, tongue and jaw as little 'joints' that move to shape sounds, so articulatory describes the physical parts and movements used to make speech.
