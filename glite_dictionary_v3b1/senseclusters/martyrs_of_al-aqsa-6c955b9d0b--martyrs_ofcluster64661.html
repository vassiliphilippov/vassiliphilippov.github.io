Etymology

Martyrs of al-Aqsa comes from the words martyrs, meaning 'people who die for a cause', and al-Aqsa, which is 'a famous mosque in Jerusalem'. The group used this name to show they claimed to fight for that place, so today the name identifies a militant group known for armed attacks.
