Etymology

Nevoid elephantiasis combines nevoid (a patch like a birthmark) and elephantiasis (thick, elephant-like skin), so the name gives a clear image. Imagine someone born with one arm or leg thick and heavy like an elephant's because small tubes that carry fluid are blocked; that's why the term means a swelling and skin thickening on one side from birth.
