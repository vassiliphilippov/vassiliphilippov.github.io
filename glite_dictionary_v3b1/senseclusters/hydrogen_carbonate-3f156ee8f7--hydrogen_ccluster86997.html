Etymology

hydrogen carbonate literally names its parts: a carbonate group with one extra hydrogen, so it is also called 'bicarbonate' and is the main ingredient in 'baking soda'. So the name helps you remember it is the carbonate that has one hydrogen, which explains the word's meaning in chemistry.
