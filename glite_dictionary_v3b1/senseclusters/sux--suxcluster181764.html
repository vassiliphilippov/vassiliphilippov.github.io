Etymology

sux is a slang spelling of sucks, which comes from the simple verb suck that once meant 'to take into the mouth' and later came to mean 'be bad or disappointing'. A helpful way to remember it is to imagine something so bad it 'sucks' the fun away, so sux means 'very bad'.
