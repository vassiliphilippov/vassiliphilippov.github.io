Etymology

lug wrench gets its name from what it does: the word lug refers to the 'lugs' or nuts that hold a wheel, and a wrench is a tool that turns them. So picture a tool that fits over the wheel 'nuts' to grab and turn them — that's why lug wrench means a tool for loosening or tightening wheel nuts.
