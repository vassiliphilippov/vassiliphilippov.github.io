Etymology

mental retardation comes from the words mental meaning 'mind' and retardation meaning 'slowing'. Because that picture of someone's learning being 'slow' feels negative and hurtful, the phrase is now seen as old and 'offensive', so people use the kinder term 'intellectual disability'.
