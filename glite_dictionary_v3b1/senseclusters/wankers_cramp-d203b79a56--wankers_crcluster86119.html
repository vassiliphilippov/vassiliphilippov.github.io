Etymology

wanker's cramp comes from a rude joke: a wanker is a crude word for someone who masturbates and a cramp is a painful tightness in a muscle. People used the image of a sore hand from too much use, so the phrase is now used jokingly for 'a painful hand condition from repetitive use'.
