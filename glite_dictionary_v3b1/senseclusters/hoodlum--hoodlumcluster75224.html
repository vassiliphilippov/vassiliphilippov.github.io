Etymology

hoodlum probably comes from American slang in the late 1800s, and one idea says it grew from hood meaning 'gang member' or 'criminal', though some people also link it to a family name. A helpful way to remember it is to think of a hood as a young 'troublemaker', so a hoodlum is someone who joins a gang or causes violent trouble.
