Etymology

imperii is a Latin form of imperium, a word meaning 'command' or 'empire'. You can see the same root in English imperial and the word 'empire', so imperii in old texts means 'of the empire' or 'of imperial power', which is why historians and legal writers use it to show something belongs to the empire.
