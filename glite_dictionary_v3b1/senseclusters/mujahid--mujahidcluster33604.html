Etymology

The word mujahid comes from the Arabic root jahada, which means 'to struggle' or 'to strive', and it is the same root as jihad, which means 'struggle'. So a mujahid is literally 'one who struggles', and today the word is often used for someone who takes part in a religious or armed fight.
