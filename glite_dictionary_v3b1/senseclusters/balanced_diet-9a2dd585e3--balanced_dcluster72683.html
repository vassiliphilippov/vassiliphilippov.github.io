Etymology

balanced diet brings to mind a plate or a set of scales with different foods kept even—fruits, vegetables, proteins and grains—so no one type is too much or too little. The word balanced shows the idea of equal parts and diet means the foods you eat, so the phrase means eating a mix of foods in the 'right amounts' to stay 'healthy'.
