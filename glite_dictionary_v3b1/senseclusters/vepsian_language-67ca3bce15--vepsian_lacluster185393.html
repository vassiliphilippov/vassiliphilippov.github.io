Etymology

Vepsian language takes its name from the Veps people, a small group who lived for many years by the lakes and forests of northwestern Russia. Their everyday speech, songs and stories were passed down in their villages and became known as the Vepsian language, so today the phrase means the language spoken by the Veps people.
