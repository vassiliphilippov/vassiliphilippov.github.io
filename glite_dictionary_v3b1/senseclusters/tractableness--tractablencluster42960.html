Etymology

tractableness comes from the root tract, from an old Latin word meaning 'to handle' or 'to pull', plus the ending -able meaning 'able', and you can see the same root in tractor, a machine that pulls things. So, tractableness is the quality of being easy to handle or easy to persuade.
