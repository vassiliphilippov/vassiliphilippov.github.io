Etymology

cedar chest The name cedar chest comes from making a chest out of cedar wood. Cedar gives off a strong smell and oils that keep moths away, so people stored clothes and quilts inside to protect them. So today cedar chest means 'a wooden chest used to protect clothes and linens from moths'.
