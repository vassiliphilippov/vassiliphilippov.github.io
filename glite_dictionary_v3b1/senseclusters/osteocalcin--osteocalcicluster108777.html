Etymology

osteocalcin comes from the parts osteo- meaning 'bone' and calc- meaning 'calcium', the same root as calcium. So osteocalcin literally means a bone calcium protein, which fits its job as a protein made by bone cells that helps keep bones healthy and helps the body use energy.
