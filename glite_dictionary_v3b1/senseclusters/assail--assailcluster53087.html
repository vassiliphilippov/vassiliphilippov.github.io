Etymology

assail comes from the Latin parts ad- ('to') and salire ('to jump'), so it originally meant to 'jump at' someone. You can see the same 'jump' idea in words like somersault and assault, which helps explain why assail means to attack someone quickly or strongly, with actions or words.
