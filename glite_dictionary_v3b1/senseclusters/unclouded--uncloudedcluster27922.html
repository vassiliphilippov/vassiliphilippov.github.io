Etymology

The word unclouded is made from un- meaning 'not' and cloud meaning 'a cloud' or 'something that hides or makes things unclear'. So, unclouded describes a sky or view that is 'not covered' and, by the same image, a mind, sound, or color that is clear and easy to understand.
