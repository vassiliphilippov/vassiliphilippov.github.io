Etymology

germ plasm comes from an old science idea: the word germ meant a tiny seed or cell that starts new life, and plasm meant the living material inside it. So germ plasm was the name for the living stuff in reproductive cells thought to carry 'heredity', and that is why today it refers to the part of those cells that passes traits to the next generation.
