Etymology

jazz band comes from putting jazz, a lively music style from New Orleans where players often make up music on the spot, together with band, which means a group of musicians. The image of a small group with trumpet, saxophone, piano and drums playing lively, made-up tunes is why it means 'a group of musicians who play jazz'.
