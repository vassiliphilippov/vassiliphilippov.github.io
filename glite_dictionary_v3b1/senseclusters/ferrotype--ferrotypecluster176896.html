Synonyms

  • daguerreotype
    0.1/m9.9
    Uses a silver-coated copper plate developed with chemicals to form an image
    vsferrotype: uses a thin metal sheet and a different simpler production method
  • tintype
    0.09/m9.9
    Creates a direct positive image on a thin metal sheet
    vsferrotype: means the same photograph type but uses an alternative historical name
  • ambrotype
    0.01/m9.9
    Produces a positive image on a glass plate common in the 1800s
    vsferrotype: uses thin metal rather than glass as the image support

Etymology

ferrotype comes from ferro- meaning 'iron' and -type meaning a 'print' or 'impression'. So it literally means an 'iron print', which helps you remember it is a photograph made on a thin metal plate.
