Etymology

burr marigold combines burr (a prickly seed that sticks to fur or clothes) and marigold (a yellow flower). The name paints the picture of a plant with bright yellow blooms and spiny seed heads that 'attach to fur or clothing', so it simply describes a yellow-flowered plant whose seeds stick and spread.
