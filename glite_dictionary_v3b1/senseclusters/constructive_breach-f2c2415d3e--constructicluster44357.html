Etymology

constructive breach uses constructive to mean 'made by actions' and breach to mean a 'break' in an agreement. Imagine someone quietly removes the supports of a stage until it cannot hold the show — they never said they would stop the performance, but their actions made it impossible, so the law treats that as a 'breach' before the time to perform.
