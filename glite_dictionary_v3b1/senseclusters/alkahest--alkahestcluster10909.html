Etymology

The word alkahest probably comes from the Arabic article al- ('the'), the same al- you see in algebra and alcohol, plus a second part that once meant 'to cleanse' or 'to dissolve'. Alchemists used alkahest for a supposed 'universal solvent' because they believed it could 'clean' or dissolve any substance.
