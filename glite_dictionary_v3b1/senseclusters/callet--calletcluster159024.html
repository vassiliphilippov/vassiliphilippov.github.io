Etymology

callet was once a rude name for a woman in older English and literature, probably coming from old French words for an immoral or quarrelsome woman. A helpful way to remember it is to think of callet as an old insult meaning a 'hussy' or 'troublemaker', so when you see it in old stories it has that rude sense.
