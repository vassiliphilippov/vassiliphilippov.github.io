Etymology

ungrateful also comes from un- 'not' plus grat from Latin gratus 'pleasing' or 'thankful', like in gratitude. That old idea of 'not pleasing' explains why an ungrateful task feels 'not rewarding' or 'thankless' and is unpleasant to do.
