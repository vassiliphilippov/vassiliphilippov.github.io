Etymology

Heat unit comes from the need for a simple, standard 'amount' of heat that people could compare, and the word unit shows it is one fixed amount used for measuring. For example, an early 'heat unit' was the 'calorie', the heat needed to raise a small amount of water by one degree, so today a heat unit means any standard measure of heat energy.
