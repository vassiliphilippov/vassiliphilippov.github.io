Etymology

beauty is in the eye of the beer holder is a joke on the old saying 'beauty is in the eye of the beholder' that swaps 'beholder' for beer holder, so you can picture someone holding a beer and seeing others as prettier after a drink. That's why the phrase humorously means that drinking can change what someone thinks is beautiful.
