Etymology

feel like a louse may come from calling someone a louse, a tiny, dirty insect that people dislike. Imagining yourself as that low, annoying creature makes a strong picture of being mean or shameful, so the phrase means 'to feel guilty or ashamed' after you have hurt someone.
