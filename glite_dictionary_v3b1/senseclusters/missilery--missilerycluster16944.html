Etymology

missilery comes from missile, which goes back to Latin mittere 'to send', plus the ending -ery, which makes a word about a place, a supply, or a practice like bakery or armory. So missilery is the set, the military units, or the science of weapons that are 'sent' at a target, and that explains its modern meaning as missiles, the missile branch, or missile technology.
