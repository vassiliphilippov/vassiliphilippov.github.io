Etymology

fly meaning 'move or happen quickly' comes from the image of birds or objects moving fast through the air, so time or events 'fly' because they seem to pass very fast.

Related Terms

  • antonym: drag - (of time) to pass very slowly
  • synonym: escape - to get away from a place or situation
  • synonym: flee - to run away from danger or threat
  • antonym: linger - to stay in a place longer than necessary, opposite of moving quickly away
