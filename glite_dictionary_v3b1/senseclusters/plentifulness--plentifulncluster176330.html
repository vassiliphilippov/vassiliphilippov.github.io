Etymology

plentifulness comes from the common word plenty, which itself comes from an old Latin word plenus meaning 'full'. The parts -ful mean 'full of' and -ness makes a 'state', so plentifulness is the 'state of being full' or having a large supply, which is why it means 'abundance'.
