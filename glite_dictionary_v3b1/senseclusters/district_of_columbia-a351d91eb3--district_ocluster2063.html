Etymology

District of Columbia comes from two simple parts: District means a special area, and Columbia is an old poetic name for America that may come from the explorer Columbus. When the country's leaders made a separate area for the capital, they used this name, so the phrase today refers to the federal 'district' that is home to Washington.
