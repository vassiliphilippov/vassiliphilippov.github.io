Etymology

Fritillaria micrantha gets its name from older words that describe the flower's look: Fritillaria may come from a word for a small 'dice box' because some fritillary flowers have a checkered bell pattern, and micrantha means 'small flower', so the name helps you picture a small, bell-shaped flower and explains why we call this California species that today.
