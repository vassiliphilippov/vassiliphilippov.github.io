Etymology

Corynebacterium diphtheriae gets its name from old Greek words: coryne meaning 'club' because the bacterium cells look like small clubs, and diphtheriae from 'diphthera' meaning 'leather' for the thick, leathery membrane that forms in the throat during diphtheria. So the name paints a simple picture of the germ that causes diphtheria.
