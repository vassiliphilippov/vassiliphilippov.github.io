Etymology

Hygrophorus inocybiformis comes from old Greek and Latin words. Hygrophorus puts together parts meaning 'wet' and 'to carry', and inocybe + -formis means 'shaped like an Inocybe', so the name gives a simple picture of a damp mushroom with waxy gills that looks like an Inocybe.
