Etymology

M2 comes from a simple label for parts of the money supply: M stands for 'money' and the number 2 marks the second, wider group after M1. So M2 means 'more' kinds of money—cash plus savings and other items that are almost cash, which is why banks watch it to judge liquidity and inflation.
