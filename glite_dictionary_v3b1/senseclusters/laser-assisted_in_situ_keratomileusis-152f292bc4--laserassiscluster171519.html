Etymology

laser-assisted in situ keratomileusis is a long medical name that literally tells what the operation does: laser-assisted means with a laser, in situ means 'in the original place', and keratomileusis means 'to reshape the cornea'. So the name describes a surgery where a thin flap is lifted and the cornea is reshaped with a laser to improve sight.
