Etymology

Golgi apparatus is named after the scientist Golgi, who saw a stack of thin layers that looked like a small machine under his microscope. Because apparatus means a little machine, that image of a machine that 'sorts' and 'packages' things helps you remember that the Golgi 'sorts' and 'packs' proteins and lipids to send them to the right place in the cell.
