Etymology

Davy Crockett was a real American man of the frontier and a politician long ago, famous for hunting, his coonskin cap, and for dying while fighting at the Alamo. Because many songs and stories made him larger than life, his name now stands for a 'folk hero' and the brave spirit of the 'frontier'.
