Etymology

Egyptian Islamic Jihad comes from three simple parts: Egyptian means they were from Egypt, Islamic shows they followed Islam, and Jihad is a word for 'struggle' or 'fight'. They used this name because they were a small armed group trying to replace Egypt's government, so the name tells you who they were and what they wanted.
