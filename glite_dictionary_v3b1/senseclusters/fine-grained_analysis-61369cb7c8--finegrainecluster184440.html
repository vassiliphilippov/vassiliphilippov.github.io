Etymology

The noun fine-grained analysis comes from the image of tiny grains: fine means 'very small' and grain is a tiny piece you need to look at closely. So, studying those tiny pieces gives a clear picture of a 'detailed' study, and that's why the phrase means a careful look at small differences.
