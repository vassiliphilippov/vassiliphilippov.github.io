Etymology

bop meaning 'to hit' is a sound word that copies a short, soft strike, like the quick noise 'bop' or 'pop' when something is tapped. A helpful way to remember it is to think of that small 'bop' sound, which is why to bop someone is to give a quick, often playful, hit.
