Etymology

The Via Dolorosa is the well-known path in Jerusalem that people believe Jesus walked while carrying his cross on the way to be crucified. The name comes from Latin: via means road and dolorosa means 'sorrow', so pilgrims walk it today to remember Jesus' 'suffering'.
