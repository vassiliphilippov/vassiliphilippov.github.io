Etymology

Acanthisittidae is the scientific name for New Zealand wrens and comes from the bird name Acanthisitta plus the family ending -idae, which marks a 'family'. A helpful way to remember it is to see Acanthisitta as made from Acanthis ('thorn' or a small finch) and -itta ('little bird'), so the name suggests a 'tiny bird', fitting these very small, ancient wrens of New Zealand.
