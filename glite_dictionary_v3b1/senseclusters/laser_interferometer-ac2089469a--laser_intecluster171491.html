Etymology

The compound noun laser interferometer joins laser, a very steady, bright beam of light, and interferometer, a tool that brings two light paths together. When the beams meet they make patterns like ripples, and because the laser is so steady those patterns change with even tiny moves, even over long distances, so the machine can 'measure' very small changes in distance.
