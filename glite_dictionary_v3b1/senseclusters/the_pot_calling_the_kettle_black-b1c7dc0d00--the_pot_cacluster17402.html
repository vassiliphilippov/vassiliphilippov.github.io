Etymology

The idiom the pot calling the kettle black comes from old kitchens where both the pot and the kettle stood over the fire and became black with soot. If the pot calls the kettle 'black', it looks silly because both are the same, so the phrase is used to show 'hypocrisy' when someone criticizes a fault they also have.
