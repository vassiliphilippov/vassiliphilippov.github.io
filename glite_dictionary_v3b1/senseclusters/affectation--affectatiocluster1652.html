Etymology

affectation comes from the Latin word affectare, which meant 'to put on' or 'to try to get'. Think of the English verb affect—when someone 'affects' an accent or a way of speaking they are putting it on like a costume. That's why an affectation is behaviour that is not natural but made to impress others.
