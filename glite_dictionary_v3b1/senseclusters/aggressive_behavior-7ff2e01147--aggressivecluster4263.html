Etymology

Aggressive behavior is easy to picture: aggressive means forceful or attacking and behavior is how someone acts. Imagine someone pushing, shouting, or hitting to get their way — these are examples of aggressive behavior, so the phrase means 'hostile or violent actions to harm or show power over others'.
