Etymology

The noun dust cloud names a real cloud made of tiny bits of dirt in the air, where dust means small grains and cloud means a thick, visible mass. Imagine a truck kicking up dirt that hangs like a cloud and hides the road, so dust cloud means 'a mass of dust in the air that makes things hard to see'.
