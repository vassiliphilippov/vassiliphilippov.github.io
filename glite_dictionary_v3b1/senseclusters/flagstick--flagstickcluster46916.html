Etymology

flagstick comes from the simple words flag 'a small piece of cloth used as a sign' and stick 'a thin pole'. A helpful way to remember it is to think of a 'flag on a stick' used to mark a special spot, a boundary, or a danger, which is exactly what a flagstick does outside of golf.
