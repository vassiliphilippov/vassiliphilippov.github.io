Etymology

neurological disorder combines neurological (about the brain and nerves) and disorder (a health problem). Think of your brain and nerves as the body's electrical wiring; when that wiring has a problem, a neurological disorder means a 'problem' in the 'nervous system', so it refers to a disease that affects the brain or nerves.
