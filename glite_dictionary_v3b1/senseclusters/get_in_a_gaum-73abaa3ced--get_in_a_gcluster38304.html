Etymology

get in a gaum may come from the image of your thoughts being stuck like sticky gaum or 'gum' that holds things in place, so you cannot move or think easily. That's why when your mind is stuck and you feel upset or flustered, you 'get in a gaum'—in other words, you 'become confused or upset'.
