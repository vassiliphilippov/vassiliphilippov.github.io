Etymology

cystic fibrosis comes from two parts: cystic, meaning small sacs called 'cysts', and fibrosis, meaning 'scar' tissue that makes parts hard. In this disease, thick, sticky mucus causes tiny cysts and scarring in the lungs and pancreas, so the name describes how the organs become clogged and damaged.
