Etymology

reclothe comes from the prefix re- meaning 'again' and the verb clothe, like in cloth or clothing, which means 'to put clothes on'. So to reclothe someone is to 'put clothes on' them again, and it also means to give a story or idea a new 'outfit', that is, to show it in a new way.
