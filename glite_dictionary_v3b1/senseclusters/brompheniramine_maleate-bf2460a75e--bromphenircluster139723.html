Etymology

brompheniramine maleate is a name made of parts: brom shows it has bromine, pheniramine is the active part that fights allergies, and maleate means it is put into a form that is easy to make into tablets. So the name tells you this is an 'allergy medicine' used to 'relieve allergy symptoms'.
