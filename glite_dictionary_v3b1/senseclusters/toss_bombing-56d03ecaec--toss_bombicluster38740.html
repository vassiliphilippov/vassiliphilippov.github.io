Etymology

Toss bombing gets its name from the literal action: toss (to throw) and bombing (dropping a bomb). In this method the plane flies low, then pulls up and 'tosses' the bomb so it flies in a curve toward the target while the aircraft stays away, which is why the term means this kind of bombing.
