Etymology

Brachiosaurus is often used as a general name for any very large, long-necked sauropod, and a helpful way to remember this is its name comes from Greek parts brachio- 'arm' and -saurus 'lizard'. Because these giants had long front legs and could reach high leaves, people call similar big dinosaurs 'Brachiosaurus' even when they are not the exact species.
