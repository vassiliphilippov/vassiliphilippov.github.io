Etymology

Euonymus americanus may come from old Greek and Latin: Euonymus means 'good name' and americanus means 'from America'. The shrub's bright red pods open to show orange seeds, a striking picture that helps explain why people gave it a 'good name' and named it as a plant 'from America'.
