Etymology

unmitigated breaks into the prefix un- meaning 'not' and the verb mitigate, from a Latin root mitis meaning 'soft' or 'gentle'. So unmitigated literally means 'not softened' and is used to say something is complete or absolute, often in a bad way like an 'unmitigated disaster'.
