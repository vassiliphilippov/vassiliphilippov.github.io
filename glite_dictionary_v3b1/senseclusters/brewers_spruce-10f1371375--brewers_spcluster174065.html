Etymology

Brewer's spruce may come from a plant scientist long ago called Brewer who first found this kind of spruce on mountain slopes in California and Oregon. Because he showed people the tree with its long, hanging branches, his name became the tree's name, so today Brewer's spruce refers to that particular species.
