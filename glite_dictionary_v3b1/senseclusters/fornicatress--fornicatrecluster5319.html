Etymology

fornicatress comes from fornicate, which goes back to the Latin fornix meaning 'arch' because in old Rome some prostitutes worked under arches, so the verb came to mean 'sex outside marriage'. The feminine ending -tress, as in actress, marks a woman, so fornicatress means a woman who has 'sex outside marriage'.
