Etymology

Serranus subligarius may come from old Latin naming: Serranus was used for a small 'sea bass' and subligarius could mean 'wearing a small belt', a picture of the fish's dark stripe under its belly. So the name helps you remember this small sea bass often found in shallow warm waters by its little band under the body.
