Etymology

Lucius Tarquinius Superbus comes from his family name and the nickname Superbus, which means 'proud'. According to old stories, his proud and harsh rule made the Romans drive him out, so his name is remembered as Rome's 'last king' and as a sign of 'cruel rule'.
