Etymology

Salvadora persica is often called the 'toothbrush tree' because people chew its twigs and use them like small brushes called 'miswak' to clean their teeth. That's why the name brings to mind natural oral care and the plant's use as food, since people also fed its shoots to camels on long journeys.
