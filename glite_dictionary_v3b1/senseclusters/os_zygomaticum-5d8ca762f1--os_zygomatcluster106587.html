Etymology

os zygomaticum comes from old Latin and Greek words. The Latin os means 'bone' and zygomaticum comes from a word for a 'yoke', a bar that joins two parts, so it pictures the bone that links the cheek to the skull and therefore means the 'cheekbone' today.
