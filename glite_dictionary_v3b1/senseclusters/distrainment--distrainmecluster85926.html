Etymology

distrainment comes from the same root as distrain, with dis- 'apart' and stringere 'to pull', and it makes you think of goods being 'pulled away' from a person. The root is like the English word strain, which also means 'to pull', so distrainment can mean the actual property or goods that were seized to cover a debt.
