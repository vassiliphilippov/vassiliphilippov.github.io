Etymology

Coraciidae comes from an old Greek bird name coracias, which is linked to corax, the Greek word for 'crow'. A helpful way to remember it is to think of these birds as 'crow-like' rollers, which is why Coraciidae is the name for the family of bright birds called rollers.
