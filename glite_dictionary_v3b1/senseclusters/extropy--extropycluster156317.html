Etymology

extropy was made as the opposite of entropy ('disorder'), with the prefix ex- meaning 'out' or 'beyond'. So extropy paints a picture of moving out of chaos toward more order, growth and the spread of intelligence and technology, which is why futurists use it for the idea that life can expand and improve.
