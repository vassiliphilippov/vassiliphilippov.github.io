Etymology

Country-style comes from country meaning 'the countryside' and style meaning 'a way' or 'fashion'. Think of a small farmhouse with warm wood, simple furniture, and hearty food — that's why Country-style describes things that look, taste, or feel 'from the countryside' and homey.
