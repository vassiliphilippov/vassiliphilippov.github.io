Etymology

technological revolution links technology, meaning 'new tools and machines', and revolution, meaning 'a big turn or sudden change'. Imagine a town where new machines arrive and life quickly 'turns' from farming to factories, and that's why the phrase means 'a fast, big change' in how people live and work.
