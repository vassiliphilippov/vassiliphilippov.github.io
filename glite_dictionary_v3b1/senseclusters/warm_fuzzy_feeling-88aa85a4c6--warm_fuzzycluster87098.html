Etymology

warm fuzzy feeling comes from the simple image of holding something small, warm and fuzzy, like a kitten or a soft blanket that makes you feel safe and cosy. That's why it means a quiet 'comfort' or 'happiness' you feel inside when something kind or lovely happens.
