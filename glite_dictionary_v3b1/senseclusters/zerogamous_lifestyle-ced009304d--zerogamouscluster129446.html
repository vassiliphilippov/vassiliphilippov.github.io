Etymology

zerogamous lifestyle comes from putting zero, meaning 'none', together with the part gamous, which comes from words about 'marriage' or being married, creating a picture of a zero sign over a wedding ring. So it means a way of life without 'romantic commitments' or 'exclusive relationships'.
