Etymology

singletree comes from the parts single ('one') and tree, an old word for a wooden bar or beam used on carts, so you can picture a single wooden seesaw that can move to share the pull. So a singletree is the one bar in a harness that links a horse or ox to a cart and helps balance and spread the load.
