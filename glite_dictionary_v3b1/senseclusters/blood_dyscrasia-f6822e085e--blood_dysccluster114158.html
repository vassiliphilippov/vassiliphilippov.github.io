Etymology

The medical term blood dyscrasia comes from old medicine ideas about body fluids: blood and the old word dyscrasia meaning 'bad mixture'. So it gives a simple picture of blood mixed in the wrong way, and today it means 'any abnormal condition or disorder of the blood'.
