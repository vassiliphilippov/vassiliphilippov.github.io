Etymology

cape gooseberry comes from how the small South American plant reached Europe: it was sent through the Cape of Good Hope, so people added Cape, and because its round yellow fruits looked like tiny gooseberries they added gooseberry. That's why cape gooseberry now names the plant with small orange-yellow fruits inside a thin papery husk.
