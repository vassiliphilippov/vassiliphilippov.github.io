Etymology

Tursiops truncatus is a scientific name that comes from older words: Tursiops points to 'dolphin' and truncatus means 'short' or 'cut', referring to the animal's short, rounded 'bottle-like' snout. So the name describes the common 'bottlenose dolphin' we know from the sea and aquariums.
