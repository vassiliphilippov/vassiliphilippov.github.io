Etymology

panpharmacon comes from Greek parts pan- 'all' and pharmakon 'drug' or 'medicine', and the pharm part is the same as in pharmacy. So a panpharmacon is a 'drug for all', which is why it means a cure-all people once hoped would heal every disease.
