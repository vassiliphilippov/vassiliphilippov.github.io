Etymology

Schumpeterian comes from the name of the economist Schumpeter, Joseph Schumpeter, who said that entrepreneurs and new ideas drive big changes in the economy and that new things often replace the old, an idea called 'creative destruction'. So a Schumpeterian strategy focuses on innovation and on changing or replacing old markets.
