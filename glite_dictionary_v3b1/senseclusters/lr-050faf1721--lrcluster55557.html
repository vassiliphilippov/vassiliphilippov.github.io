Etymology

Lr is the chemical symbol for the element lawrencium, a man-made, radioactive metal made in labs. The name lawrencium honors the American scientist Ernest Lawrence, and the ending -ium is the same as in familiar elements like 'helium', so Lr simply stands for the metal named for Lawrence.
