Etymology

The idiom smoking one's own dope paints a clear picture: someone literally smoking their own dope and losing clear thinking. One popular idea is that this shows a person so taken by their own ideas that they hurt themselves, so the phrase means 'acting in a way that harms yourself' or 'believing your own praise'.
