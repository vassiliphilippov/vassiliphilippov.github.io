Etymology

Wen Ch'ang is the traditional Chinese god who looks after writing, learning, and exams; students and writers would visit his temple with brushes and books and ask for success. So Wen Ch'ang became a simple symbol of help for 'writers' and 'students' when they need ideas or good exam results.
