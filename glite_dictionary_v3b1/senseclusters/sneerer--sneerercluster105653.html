Etymology

sneerer comes from the verb sneer, which has long meant to show 'contempt' by a mocking look or sound. A helpful way to remember it is to imagine someone making a short 'snort' or small laugh that shows they feel better than another person, so a sneerer is someone who makes mocking faces or remarks.
