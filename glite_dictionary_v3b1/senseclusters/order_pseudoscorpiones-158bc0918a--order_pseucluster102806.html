Etymology

order Pseudoscorpiones comes from two parts: pseudo meaning 'false' and scorpiones meaning 'scorpions'. Because these tiny, spider-like animals look like little scorpions but have no sting, the name literally says 'false scorpions' and helps explain what they are.
