Etymology

The word haplography comes from two Greek parts: haplo- meaning 'single' and -graphy meaning 'writing'. Imagine a writer who should copy a repeated word twice but writes it only once — that 'single writing' image helps you remember that haplography is the accidental leaving out of a repeated letter, word, or sequence.
