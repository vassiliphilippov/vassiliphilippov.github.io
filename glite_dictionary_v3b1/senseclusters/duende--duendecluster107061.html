Etymology

duende comes from Spanish and at first meant a small 'spirit' or goblin; the poet García Lorca used it to describe a sudden, deep feeling that seems to take hold of a performer. So, saying a singer or actor has duende means their art shows intense, moving passion, as if touched by a spirit.
