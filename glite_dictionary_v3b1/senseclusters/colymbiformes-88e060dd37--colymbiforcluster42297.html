Etymology

Colymbiformes comes from the Greek root colymbi- meaning 'diver', the same idea as the English word 'dive', and the ending -formes meaning 'shape' or 'form'. So Colymbiformes literally means 'diver-shaped birds', which fits because these grebes are expert swimmers and divers.
