Etymology

The word penetrative comes from Latin penetrare, meaning 'to go into' or 'to pierce', and it is the same root as penetrate. A helpful way to remember the thinking meaning is to imagine a mind that 'goes into' a problem to find its heart, so a penetrative person sees deep truth quickly.
