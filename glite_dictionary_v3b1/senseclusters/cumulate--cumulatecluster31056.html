Etymology

The word cumulate comes from the Latin cumulus, which means 'heap' or 'pile' and is the same root in the common word accumulate and in cumulus clouds that pile up in the sky. So, to cumulate is to gather things into a 'heap' over time, which is why it means 'to accumulate'.
