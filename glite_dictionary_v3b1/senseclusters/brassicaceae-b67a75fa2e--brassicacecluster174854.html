Etymology

Brassicaceae comes from the plant name Brassica, which meant 'cabbage' or 'mustard', and the ending -aceae that plant scientists use for 'family'. So, Brassicaceae is the 'cabbage or mustard family', which is why it names plants like cabbage, broccoli and mustard.
