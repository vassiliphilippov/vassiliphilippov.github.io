Etymology

unvarnished honesty comes from the image of wood or furniture with no varnish: varnish is a shiny coat that covers and smooths things, so unvarnished means without that cover. So unvarnished honesty means telling the truth in a 'plain' and 'direct' way, without hiding or softening it.
