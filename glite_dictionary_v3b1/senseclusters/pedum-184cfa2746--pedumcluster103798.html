Etymology

Pedum is the name of an ancient town in Latium and comes from the Latin root ped-, which means 'foot' and appears in English words like pedal and pedestrian. A helpful way to remember Pedum is to think of it as the town 'at the foot' of a hill or road, which fits where it stood in the early Roman area.
