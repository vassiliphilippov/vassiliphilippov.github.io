Etymology

heliothis moth comes from the scientific word Heliothis, which uses 'helio' meaning 'sun' and the word moth. One easy image is a moth on warm, sunny plants whose young caterpillars eat corn and tomatoes, so heliothis moth now names a moth whose young are serious crop pests.
