Etymology

The term genderist attitude comes from combining gender (being male or female) with the ending -ist (as in 'racist') and attitude (a way of thinking). This creates the image of a person who looks at others through a narrow view and treats them differently because of their gender, so it means 'a view or behaviour that discriminates against people based on their gender'.
