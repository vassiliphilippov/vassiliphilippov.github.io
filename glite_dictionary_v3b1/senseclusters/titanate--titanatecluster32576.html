Etymology

titanate as an adjective comes from titan- ('Titan', a 'giant') plus the ending -ate used in chemistry, like in sulfate, to name a 'salt' or compound. That's why calling a material 'titanate' means it is 'made with' or 'contains' titanate compounds used for coatings and durable parts.
