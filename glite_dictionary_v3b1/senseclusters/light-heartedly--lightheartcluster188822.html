Etymology

The word light-heartedly is made from light ('not heavy' or 'not serious') and heart ('feelings' or 'courage'), with -ly added to make it an adverb. Think of someone with a 'light heart'—happy and not worried—so to act light-heartedly is to behave in a cheerful, carefree way.
