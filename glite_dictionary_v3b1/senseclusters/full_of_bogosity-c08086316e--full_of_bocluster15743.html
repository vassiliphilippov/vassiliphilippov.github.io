Etymology

The phrase full of bogosity comes from the word bogus, an old slang word for 'fake'. One popular story says bogus was used for fake coins, so bogosity gives the picture of something full of fakes and nonsense, and that's why 'full of bogosity' means 'full of nonsense'.
