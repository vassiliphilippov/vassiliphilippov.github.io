Etymology

Anoa depressicornis may come from the local word Anoa for the small Sulawesi buffalo and from Latin in depressicornis that means 'pressed down' and 'horn'. So, the name gives a simple picture of a tiny wild buffalo with low, small horns, which is why it names this species.
