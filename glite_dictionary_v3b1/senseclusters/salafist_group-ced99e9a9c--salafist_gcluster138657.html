Etymology

Salafist Group comes from Salafist + Group: the word 'Salaf' means the 'earliest Muslims', so Salafist describes people who want to follow those early ways. The name was used by an Algerian organization that used violence to try to change the government, so today the phrase usually refers to that specific armed group.
