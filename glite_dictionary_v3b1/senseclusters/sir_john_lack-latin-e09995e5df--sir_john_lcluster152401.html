Etymology

Sir John Lack-Latin began as a joking, made-up title for someone who could not speak Latin. The mock parts Sir and Lack-Latin create the image of a polite 'knight' who is 'missing Latin', so the name came to mean someone playfully called 'not educated' in scholarly subjects.
