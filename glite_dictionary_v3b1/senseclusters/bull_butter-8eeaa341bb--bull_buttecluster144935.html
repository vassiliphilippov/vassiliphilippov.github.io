Etymology

The slang bull butter likely comes from the rude phrase bullshit, where people replaced the rude word shit with the harmless butter to avoid swearing. Because it keeps the idea of a 'bull' but uses a milder word, the phrase now means 'nonsense' or something not to be believed.
