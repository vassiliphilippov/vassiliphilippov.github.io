Etymology

systolic murmur comes from two parts: systolic (when the heart squeezes) and murmur (a soft, unusual sound). So it paints the image of a gentle extra noise a doctor might hear with a stethoscope while the heart is squeezing, which is why it means a sound heard during 'systole'.
