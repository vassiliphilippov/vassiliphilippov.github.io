Etymology

differentiability comes from different ('not the same') plus -ability ('can be'), via the verb differentiate ('to show a difference'). So differentiability is the idea of 'being able to show small differences', and in maths it means a function 'can have' a derivative so you can measure its small change or slope at a point.
