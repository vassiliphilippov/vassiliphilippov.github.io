Etymology

The word dimethylbenzene is made of three parts: di- meaning 'two', methyl meaning a small chemical group, and benzene meaning a ring-shaped part found in many chemicals. So the name literally says a benzene ring with two methyl groups, which is why this chemical (also called xylene) has that structure and is used as a solvent.
