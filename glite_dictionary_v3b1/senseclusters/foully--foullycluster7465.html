Etymology

The word foully comes from the old English word foul meaning 'dirty' or 'rotten' and the ending -ly that makes it an adverb, and foul is also the same word used for a bad or unfair action in sport. So, to be treated or accused foully is to be treated in a 'dirty' or 'unfair' way.
