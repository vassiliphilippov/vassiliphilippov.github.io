Etymology

boogeyman comes from an old word bogey meaning 'an evil spirit' or 'ghost' that adults used in stories to frighten children. Like the word bugbear, it names a make-believe creature used as a warning, so boogeyman today means an imaginary monster people say will scare misbehaving children.
