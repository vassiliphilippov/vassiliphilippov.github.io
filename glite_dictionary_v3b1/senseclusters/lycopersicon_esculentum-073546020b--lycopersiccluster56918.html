Etymology

Lycopersicon esculentum comes from old Greek and Latin words: Lycopersicon means 'wolf-peach' and esculentum means 'edible'. So the name gives the image of a wild 'wolf peach' you can eat, which is why it was used for the tomato plant people grow for food.
