Etymology

Microgramma piloselloides comes from old Greek and Latin parts: micro meaning 'small' and piloselloides meaning 'hair-like'. This gives a clear picture of the fern's tiny, hair-like runners that carpet tree trunks, so the name fits a fern that 'grows on trees'.
