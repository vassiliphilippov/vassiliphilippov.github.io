Etymology

expedition comes from the Latin parts ex- 'out' and ped 'foot', so it originally meant 'to free the feet' and set out quickly. You can see ped in everyday words like pedal and pedestrian, and that is why an expedition today is an organized journey a group makes to go somewhere for a specific purpose.
