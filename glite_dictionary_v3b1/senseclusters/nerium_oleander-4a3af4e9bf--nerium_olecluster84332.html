Etymology

The name Nerium oleander may come from old Greek and Latin words: Nerium from 'nerion', a plant that grew near water, and oleander from 'olea', the word for 'olive', because its long leaves look like olive leaves. So the name gives a clear picture of this evergreen shrub with long, olive-like leaves and helps you remember the showy but poisonous plant.
