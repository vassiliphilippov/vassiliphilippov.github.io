Etymology

family Sarcoptidae comes from old Greek parts sarco- meaning 'flesh' and -ptes meaning 'to scrape', because these tiny mites dig into and damage skin. So, the family Sarcoptidae today is the group of small mites that live on animals and cause skin diseases like scabies and mange.
