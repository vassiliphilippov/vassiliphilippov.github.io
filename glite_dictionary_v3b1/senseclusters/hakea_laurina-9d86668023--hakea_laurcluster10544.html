Etymology

Hakea laurina mixes a person's name and a Latin description. Hakea comes from a plant collector called 'Hake', and laurina means 'laurel-like' because the leaves look like small laurel leaves, so the name helps you picture the tall shrub with round red‑yellow flowers.
