Etymology

The word grugru probably comes from a Native American name for a small tropical palm that has a swollen, spiny trunk and edible nuts. A helpful way to remember grugru is to think of it as the local palm that gives both 'shade' and 'food', which is why the word refers to that nut-bearing tree.
