Etymology

Osteostraci comes from Greek parts osteo- meaning 'bone' and ostraci meaning 'shell' or 'shield', so the name means 'bony shield'. That's why Osteostraci is used for the armored, jawless fish that had heavy bony plates protecting their heads.
