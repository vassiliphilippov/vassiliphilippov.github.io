Etymology

Sept. 11 comes from the date September 11, 2001, when planned terrorist attacks used passenger planes to destroy the World Trade Center towers in New York and to hit the Pentagon. Because this day changed many people's lives and the world, Sept. 11 now usually means 'the attacks of that day'.
