Etymology

Bidens trichosperma gets its name from old Latin and Greek words that describe its seeds. Bidens means 'two-toothed' because the fruit has two small hooks, and trichosperma means 'hairy seed' because the seeds have tiny hairs that help them stick to animals or clothes, so the name fits this yellow wetland plant.
