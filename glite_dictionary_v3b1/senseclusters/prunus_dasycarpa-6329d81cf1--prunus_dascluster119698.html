Etymology

Prunus dasycarpa may come from the parts Prunus (meaning 'plum') and dasy- + -carpa (literally 'hairy fruit'). So imagine a small plum-like or 'apricot' tree with purple twigs and rough purple fruit, and that picture helps you remember it is a small hybrid apricot tree known for its purple twigs and not very tasty fruit.
