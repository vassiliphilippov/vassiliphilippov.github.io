Etymology

lysogenicity comes from lyso- meaning 'to loosen' or 'to break' and -genic like in genetic, meaning 'relating to genes'. Think of lysis (when a cell breaks) and genetic to remember that lysogenicity is when a virus puts its genes into a bacterium and stays quiet inside the host.
