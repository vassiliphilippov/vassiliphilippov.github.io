Etymology

Pouteria zapota may come from two parts: Pouteria is the scientific name for a group of trees and zapota comes from an old local word for a 'soft' and 'sweet' fruit. Because this tree has smooth, orange, very sweet fruit and useful wood, the name came to mean this particular tropical tree and its fruit.
