Etymology

Wumao troll comes from Chinese wumao, which means '50 cents' because one popular story says some commenters were paid fifty cents for each pro-government post. The English word troll means someone who posts online to cause arguments, so wumao troll now means 'an online user who posts to support the government, often as part of a group'.
