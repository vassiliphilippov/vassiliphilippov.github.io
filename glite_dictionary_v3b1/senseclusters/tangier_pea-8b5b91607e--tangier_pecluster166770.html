Etymology

Tangier pea likely gets its name from the city of Tangier in North Africa and the word pea, because gardeners in Europe first met a pea-like plant that came from that place. It looked like a 'sweet pea' with bright flowers but had little smell, so the name now tells us it is a North African pea with showy, scentless blooms.
