Etymology

carvel-built comes from the ship name caravel, from Portuguese caravela, meaning a 'small ship' that used planks laid 'edge to edge' to make a smooth hull. So carvel-built describes a hull made with planks meeting 'edge to edge', giving a sleek, watertight surface.
