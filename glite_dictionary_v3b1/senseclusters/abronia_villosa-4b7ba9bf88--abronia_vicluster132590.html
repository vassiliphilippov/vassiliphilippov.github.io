Etymology

Abronia villosa comes from old Greek and Latin words: Abronia suggests 'delicate' or 'graceful' and villosa means 'hairy' or 'with soft hairs'. The name gives a picture of a small, sticky desert plant with soft hairs and bright pink, trumpet-shaped flowers, so the name fits what the plant looks like today.
