Etymology

trifoliate orange The name trifoliate orange comes from its leaves: trifoliate means 'three leaflets' and orange is the fruit the tree makes. So the name paints a simple picture — a small, thorny orange tree with leaves in groups of three, which helps you remember why gardeners use it for hedges and to help grow other orange trees.
