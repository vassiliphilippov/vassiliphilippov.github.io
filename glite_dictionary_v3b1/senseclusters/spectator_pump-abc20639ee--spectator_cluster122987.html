Etymology

Spectator pump may come from the word spectator (someone who 'watches' an event) and pump (a low or medium-heeled shoe). One popular story says people going to matches and parties liked to wear smart, two-tone shoes that stood out, and that image gave the name to the two-colour, medium-heeled style we call a spectator pump today.
