Etymology

Book of Obadiah is the short Old Testament book named for the prophet Obadiah, who spoke a brief 'warning' against the nation of Edom. Imagine one messenger writing a short, strong note about a country's fall; that's why the book is named for Obadiah and why it is so short and direct.
