Etymology

Bodensee is a German name made of two parts: Boden meaning 'ground' or 'bottom' and See meaning 'lake' (like the English word 'sea'). So Bodensee simply names the lake by the land and is the German word for Lake Constance on the border of Germany, Switzerland, and Austria.
