Etymology

Tamiasciurus hudsonicus comes from older words: the first part, Tamiasciurus, mixes ideas meaning 'one who stores food' and 'tail', a picture of a squirrel that hides food and has a bushy tail. The second part, hudsonicus, points to the 'Hudson Bay' area, so the name helps you remember this red squirrel of northern forests.
