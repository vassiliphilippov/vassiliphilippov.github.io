Etymology

sulfadiazine starts with sulfa, from 'sulfur' and the common name 'sulfa' for that group of medicines, and ends with -diazine, a chemical ending that shows it is a specific chemical. So, sulfadiazine is a sulfa antibiotic used to treat bacterial infections.
