Etymology

Mellivora capensis comes from two Latin words: mellivora meaning 'honey eater' because this animal often raids beehives, and capensis meaning 'from the Cape' because it was first described in southern Africa. So the name gives a simple picture — a honey-eating badger from the Cape — which is why it refers to the honey badger today.
