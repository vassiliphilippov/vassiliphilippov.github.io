Etymology

grubbily comes from the simple adjective grubby, which likely comes from grub — either a small worm or the act of digging in the dirt. A helpful way to remember it is to imagine hands or clothes covered in soil, so to do something grubbily is to act 'in a dirty or untidy way'.
