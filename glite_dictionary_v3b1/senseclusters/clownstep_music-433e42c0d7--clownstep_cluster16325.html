Etymology

The name clownstep music comes from combining clown (funny circus sounds and jokes) with step (a quick dance beat). One popular story says DJs added 'clownish' sound effects to fast drum and bass rhythms, so the term now means a 'playful' style with bouncy beats and silly sounds.
