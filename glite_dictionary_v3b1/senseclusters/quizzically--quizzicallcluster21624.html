Etymology

Quizzically comes from the everyday word quiz, a short test or a small 'question', plus the ending -ically which turns it into a word that describes how someone looks or acts. So, to look quizzically is to show a quiet question on your face, a mix of being puzzled, curious, or slightly amused.
