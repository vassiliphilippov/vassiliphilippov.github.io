Etymology

shoofly for a device to keep flies away or for the sweet pie comes from shoo + fly, where 'shoo' is 'go away' and 'fly' is the insect. The pie, made with sticky molasses, often attracted flies, so people called it a shoofly, and a tool to keep bugs off meals was also a literal 'shoo-fly'.
