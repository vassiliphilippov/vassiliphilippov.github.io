Etymology

Punjabi comes from the region name Punjab, a Persian name made of the words panj ('five') and ab ('water'), meaning the 'Land of Five Rivers'. The ending -i means 'from' or 'of', so Punjabi simply means 'from Punjab', which is why it names the language spoken there.
