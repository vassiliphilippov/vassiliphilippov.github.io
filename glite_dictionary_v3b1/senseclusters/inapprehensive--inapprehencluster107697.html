Etymology

The word inapprehensive starts with in- meaning 'not' and the rest comes from apprehend, which gave the idea of being 'seized' or 'gripped' by a feeling, so apprehensive came to mean 'full of worry'. So inapprehensive means 'not seized by worry', calm and unafraid.
