Etymology

The word chef-d'oeuvre comes from French parts chef meaning 'head' or 'chief', like the English word chief, and oeuvre meaning 'work'. So a chef-d'oeuvre is literally the 'head' or best 'work' of an artist, which is why it means a masterpiece.
