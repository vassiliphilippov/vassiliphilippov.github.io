Etymology

India rubber may come from two simple ideas: the material was called rubber because people used it to 'rub out' pencil marks, and it was linked to India because Europeans first saw it arriving from the distant 'Indies'. That's why India rubber now means the stretchy material used for things like erasers and coats.
