Etymology

cardiac tamponade comes from the parts cardiac meaning 'heart' and tamponade meaning 'to plug' or 'to press down'. Imagine the heart with fluid around it pressing like a tight plug so the heart is 'squeezed' and cannot pump well — that's why the term means a dangerous build-up that compresses the heart.
