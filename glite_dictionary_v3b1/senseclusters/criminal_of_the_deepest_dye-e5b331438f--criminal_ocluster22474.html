Etymology

The idiom criminal of the deepest dye may come from the image of cloth soaked in very dark dye; the cloth would be permanently stained, so a criminal 'dyed' in the deepest dye is seen as deeply marked by bad deeds. That's why the phrase means 'extremely immoral or wicked'.
