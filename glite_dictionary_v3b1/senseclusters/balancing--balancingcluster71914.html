Etymology

balancing comes from an old word for a scale with 'two' pans: the prefix bi- means 'two' and it shares the same idea as balance, making two sides even. So balancing is like putting things on a scale and adjusting them until both sides 'agree', which is why it means comparing and adjusting things to make them match.
