Etymology

The term inferior thalamostriate vein is a simple location name in anatomy: inferior means 'below', thalamostriate shows it sits by the thalamus and striatum, two parts of the brain, and vein means a channel that 'drains blood'. It joins the 'basal vein', so the name tells you where it is and what it does.
