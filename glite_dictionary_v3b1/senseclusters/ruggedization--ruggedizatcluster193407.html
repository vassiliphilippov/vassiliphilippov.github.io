Etymology

Ruggedization comes from the simple word rugged ('rough' or 'strong') plus the parts -ize ('make') and -ation ('process'), so it literally means the process of making something rugged. That's why ruggedization refers to making equipment stronger so it can survive hard use and bad conditions.
