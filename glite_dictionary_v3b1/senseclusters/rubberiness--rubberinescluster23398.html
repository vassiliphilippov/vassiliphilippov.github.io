Etymology

rubberiness comes from rubber, the material that was named because people used it to 'rub' out pencil marks, plus the endings -y and -ness that mean 'like' and 'quality'. So rubberiness is the 'quality' of being like rubber — flexible, stretchy, or bouncy.
