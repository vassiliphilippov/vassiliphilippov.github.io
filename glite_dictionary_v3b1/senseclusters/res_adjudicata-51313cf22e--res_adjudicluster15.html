res adjudicata

0.00/m9.9

Etymology

res adjudicata comes from Latin: the words res and adjudicata mean 'thing' and 'decided'. Imagine a judge closing a case file and stamping it 'decided' so no one can open it again — that's why the phrase means 'a matter that cannot be raised again' in court.
