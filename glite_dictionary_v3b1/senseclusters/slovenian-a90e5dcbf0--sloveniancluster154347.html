Etymology

A Slovenian is a person from the country Slovenia, and the name comes from Slovene, which comes from an old word slovo meaning 'word' or 'speech'. So a Slovenian originally meant 'people who speak the same language', which explains why today it means a native or inhabitant of Slovenia.
