Etymology

The word diddley comes from the everyday word diddle, which can mean 'to cheat' or 'waste time', and from the slang phrase diddly-squat, where squat means 'nothing'. A helpful way to remember diddley is to think of getting 'diddley' as getting 'nothing' or only a very small amount.
