Etymology

Cottage tent gets its name from the picture of a little house inside a tent: cottage means a small 'house' and tent is a temporary 'shelter'. So a cottage tent is a tent that looks more like a small house, with walls and a roof, and therefore means 'a large tent with walls and a roof designed to accommodate a family'.
