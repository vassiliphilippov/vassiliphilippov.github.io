Etymology

The word hypocapnia comes from two parts: hypo- meaning 'under', like in hypothermia, and capnia, from a Greek word for 'smoke' that doctors use for carbon dioxide. So hypocapnia means there is 'too little' carbon dioxide in the blood, often after fast or deep breathing.
