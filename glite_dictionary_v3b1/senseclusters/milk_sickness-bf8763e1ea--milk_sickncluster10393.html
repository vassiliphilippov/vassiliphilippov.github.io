Etymology

milk sickness The name milk sickness comes from a simple, dangerous scene. People drank milk from cows that had eaten a poisonous plant called white snakeroot, and the sickness came from that milk. So the term means 'a disease caused by drinking milk from cows that ate a poisonous plant'.
