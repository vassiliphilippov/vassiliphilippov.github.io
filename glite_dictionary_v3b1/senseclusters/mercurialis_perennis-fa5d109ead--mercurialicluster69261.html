Etymology

Mercurialis perennis, often called dog's mercury, has a name from old medicine stories: one simple idea is that people used 'mercury' in remedies and adding 'dog' marked a common or 'less useful' version. Because this low, green plant spreads like a carpet in the woods and is actually 'poisonous', the name stuck to warn people.
