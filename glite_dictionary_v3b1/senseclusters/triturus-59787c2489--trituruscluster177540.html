Etymology

Triturus is a Latin-style scientific name for a group of newts. A helpful way to remember it is to think of the Latin root triturare meaning 'to rub' or 'to grind' and imagine a newt's rough skin being rubbed by water, so Triturus names these small aquatic salamanders.
