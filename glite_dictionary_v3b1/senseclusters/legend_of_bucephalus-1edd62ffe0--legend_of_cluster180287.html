Etymology

Legend of Bucephalus comes from a famous story about Alexander and a wild horse: the horse feared its own shadow, so Alexander turned it toward the sun, calmed it and rode it, 'taming' the animal. That's why the Legend of Bucephalus is told to show 'great skill' and a strong, loyal bond between a rider and his horse.
