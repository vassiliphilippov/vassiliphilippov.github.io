Etymology

order Cycadofilicales comes from parts that mean cycado- ('like a cycad') and -filicales ('like a fern'), because their fossils look like plants that mix cycad and fern parts. So, the name helps you remember this 'group of seed plants' that lived a long time ago and are now extinct.
