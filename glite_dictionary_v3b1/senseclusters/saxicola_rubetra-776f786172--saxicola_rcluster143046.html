Etymology

Saxicola rubetra may come from old Latin words: Saxicola means 'rock-dweller' and rubetra may come from a word for 'bush' or 'bramble'. That picture, a small bird sitting on rocks, fence posts or low bushes, is why the name fits this little songbird you find in meadows today.
