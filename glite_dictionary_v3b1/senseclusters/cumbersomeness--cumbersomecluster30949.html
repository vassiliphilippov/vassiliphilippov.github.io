Etymology

cumbersomeness comes from cumbersome, which has the part cumber meaning 'to burden' and the ending -some meaning 'full of', and you can see the same idea in encumber ('to put a burden on'). So, cumbersomeness is the state of being heavy, awkward, or hard to carry or use.
