Etymology

The noun uranyl radical comes from chemistry and names a small charged group made of one uranium atom with two oxygen atoms like arms. So this charged group is an 'ion' that often joins with acids to make 'salts', often bright yellow, and the term means a uranium–oxygen ion that forms salts.
