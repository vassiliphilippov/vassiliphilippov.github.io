Etymology

Caravaneer comes from caravan, a word from Persian kârvân that meant 'a group of travellers', plus the ending -eer which makes a noun for 'a person who does or belongs to'. So a caravaneer is someone who travels with or leads a caravan across deserts and long trade routes.
