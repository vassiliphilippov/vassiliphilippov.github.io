Etymology

Damascus steel comes from the old city of Damascus, where traders sold sword blades whose metal showed dark, wavy, water-like patterns in the steel. People liked these blades because they were very sharp and tough, so the name came to mean a special kind of 'steel' that is 'strong' and 'sharp'.
