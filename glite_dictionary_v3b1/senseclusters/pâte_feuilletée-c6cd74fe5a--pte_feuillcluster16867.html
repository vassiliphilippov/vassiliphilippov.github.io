Etymology

pâte feuilletée comes from the French words pâte and feuilletée, which comes from the word for 'leaf'. Bakers fold butter into the dough again and again to make many thin layers like stacked leaves, and when baked these layers puff and separate, so the name means 'a light, flaky pastry dough'.
