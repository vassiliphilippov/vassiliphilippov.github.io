Etymology

The plant name Parietaria diffusa comes from old Latin words that describe where and how it grows: Parietaria relates to the word 'wall' and diffusa means 'spread'. Because this herb often grows in cracks in walls and spreads over rocks, the name gives a clear image and helps you remember the plant.
