Etymology

Clinging vine comes from the image of a plant that seems to hug a wall or fence: the clinging part shows it 'holds on' with tiny roots or twisty stems, and the vine is the long plant that climbs. That's why the name means a plant that 'attaches itself' to a support to grow upward.
