Etymology

bollix is a slang verb that probably comes from the stronger British word bollocks, which people used for a bad mess or nonsense, so a helpful way to remember it is to imagine something 'bollocked' up. That's why to bollix something means 'to ruin' or 'to spoil' it by clumsiness.
