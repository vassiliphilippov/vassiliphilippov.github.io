Etymology

polyvinyl-formaldehyde breaks into poly- meaning 'many', vinyl (the same 'vinyl' you find in a 'vinyl record'), and formaldehyde, a common chemical used to preserve things. So the name shows it is a material made of many vinyl parts joined with formaldehyde, which is why it is a synthetic polymer used in coatings and adhesives.
