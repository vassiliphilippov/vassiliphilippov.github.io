Etymology

pyloric stenosis comes from two parts: pyloric, meaning the small opening at the end of the stomach, and stenosis, meaning a narrowing. When that opening becomes too narrow, food is 'blocked' and 'can't pass' into the small intestine, so the term describes that blocked condition.
