Etymology

gaff topsail The sailing term gaff topsail names a small triangular sail set above the gaff, a wooden pole that holds the main sail. The topsail's bottom edge lies along the gaff and its front edge runs up to the top of the mast, so the gaff topsail is the 'extra sail' that catches more wind and makes the boat go faster.
