Etymology

Pacific silver fir describes the tree's home and look: Pacific for the 'west coast' where it grows, silver for the pale, shiny undersides of its needles, and fir for the 'type of tree'. So the name points to a fir tree from the Pacific with a soft, silvery appearance.
