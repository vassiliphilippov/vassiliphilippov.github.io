Etymology

Janissary comes from the Turkish words yeni 'new' and çeri 'soldier', so it originally meant 'new soldier'. These young men were chosen and trained to be a special troop for the Ottoman ruler, which is why Janissary now means a member of that famous Ottoman infantry.
