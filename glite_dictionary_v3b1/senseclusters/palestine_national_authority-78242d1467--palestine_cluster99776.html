Etymology

Palestine National Authority comes from when a new body was set up after a 1990s peace agreement to run services in parts of the West Bank and Gaza. The words are literal: Palestine points to the land and people, National shows it serves the whole people, and Authority means a group 'to govern', so the name refers to a temporary governing body for those areas.
