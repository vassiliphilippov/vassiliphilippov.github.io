Etymology

sesquipedalian comes from the Latin parts sesqui- ('one and a half') and ped- ('foot'), the same ped- you see in pedal and pedestrian. So to be sesquipedalian is to use very long, sometimes showy words — like words 'a foot and a half' long, which can make speech or writing hard to follow.
