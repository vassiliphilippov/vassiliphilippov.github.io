Etymology

Aiee copies the short cry people make when they are surprised, hurt, or alarmed. A helpful way to remember it is to think of English cries like ow and ouch or the Spanish 'ay', because they all copy a sudden sound, so Aiee means 'a cry of surprise or pain'.
