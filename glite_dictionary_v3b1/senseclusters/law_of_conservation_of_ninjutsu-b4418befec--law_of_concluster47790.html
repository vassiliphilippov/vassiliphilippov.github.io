Etymology

Law of Conservation of Ninjutsu is a playful idea that uses the sound of a science 'law' to make a joke. It imagines a ninja's skill, ninjutsu, being shared so one ninja keeps most power while many ninjas each have less, and that's why fans say a solo fighter is 'strong alone' and a crowd is 'weak in a group'.
