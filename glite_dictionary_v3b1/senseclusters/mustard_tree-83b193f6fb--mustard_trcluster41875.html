Etymology

mustard tree was used for the small Salvadora shrub because its leaves and fruits have a sharp, mustard-like taste and its fibrous branches were used as natural 'toothbrushes' while shoots fed animals. That's why the name links the spicy taste to a useful tree in dry areas.
