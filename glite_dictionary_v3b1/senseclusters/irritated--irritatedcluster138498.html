Etymology

The word irritated comes from an old Latin word irritare, which meant 'to excite' or 'to rub' so something becomes sore. That's why irritated can mean a physical, itchy or sore feeling and also the emotional sense of being 'annoyed', like being 'rubbed the wrong way'.
