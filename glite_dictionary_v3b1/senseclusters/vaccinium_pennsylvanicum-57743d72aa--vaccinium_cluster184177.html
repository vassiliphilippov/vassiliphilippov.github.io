Etymology

Vaccinium pennsylvanicum comes from Latin words: Vaccinium was used for berry plants like blueberries, and pennsylvanicum means 'from Pennsylvania'. So the name gives a simple picture of a low blueberry shrub from the northeastern US, helping you remember it is the 'lowbush blueberry' with small dark blue berries.
