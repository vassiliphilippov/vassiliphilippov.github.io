Etymology

Bengal kino is the name for the thick, reddish sap from the dhak tree, and the word kino means a sticky juice that dries into a hard red gum. Because people used that dried red gum to 'dye' cloth and to treat wounds, the name now means that dried reddish juice used as a 'dye' and a simple 'medicine'.
