Etymology

Satchel Paige was the nickname of Leroy Paige, a famous American baseball 'pitcher' known for his fastball and a very long career. One popular story says people called him satchel because as a boy he carried a small bag called a satchel, and that simple image helps us remember him today as a great, long-lasting player.
