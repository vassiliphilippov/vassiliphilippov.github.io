Etymology

The idiom on one's coattails comes from the image of someone walking right behind a powerful person and holding the long back part of their coat, the coattails. If you held on to those coattails, you would be pulled along or noticed because of the other person's success, so today it means 'to follow closely or gain advantage from someone else'.
