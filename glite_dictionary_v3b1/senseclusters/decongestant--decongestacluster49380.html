Etymology

decongestant breaks into de- meaning 'remove' and congest meaning 'to pile up' or 'crowd together' (think of traffic congestion where the road is full). So a decongestant is a medicine that 'removes' that pile-up in your nose or chest, which is why it helps a stuffy nose clear.
