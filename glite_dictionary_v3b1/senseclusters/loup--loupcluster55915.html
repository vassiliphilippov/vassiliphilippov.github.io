Etymology

Loup for a small jeweller's magnifying glass or a half-mask comes from French loupe, a small lens, and from French loup 'wolf' for the mask. A helpful way to remember the magnifier sense is that a loup helps you 'look' closely, and the mask sense hides the eyes like a wolf's face.
