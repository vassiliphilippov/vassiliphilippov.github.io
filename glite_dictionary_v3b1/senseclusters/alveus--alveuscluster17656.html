Etymology

alveus originally meant a 'hollow' or 'basin' in Latin, and people also used it for a small wooden boat carved from a single tree trunk. A helpful way to remember is to think of a boat as a 'hollow' that carries people or goods, which is why alveus can mean a small boat.
