Etymology

adrenocorticotrophic hormone comes from three parts: adreno- (the 'adrenal' glands), cortico- (the 'cortex', the outer part of those glands), and -trophic (to 'help' or 'stimulate'). So it literally means a hormone that tells the adrenal cortex to work, and today it names the hormone made by a small gland at the base of the brain that makes the adrenal gland release stress hormones.
