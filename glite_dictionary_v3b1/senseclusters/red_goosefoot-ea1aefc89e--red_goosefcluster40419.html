Etymology

red goosefoot gets its name from a simple picture: it has red stems and leaves that look like a goose's foot, so the parts red and goosefoot describe how it looks. So the name helps you remember that it is a reddish field 'weed' that can compete with crops and may be 'poisonous' to animals.
