Etymology

Dengoid infection comes from the parts dengue and -oid, where -oid means 'like'. Doctors used this word for an infection that looks like dengue fever in its symptoms but is not caused by the dengue virus, so it means 'an illness that resembles dengue but isn't dengue'.
