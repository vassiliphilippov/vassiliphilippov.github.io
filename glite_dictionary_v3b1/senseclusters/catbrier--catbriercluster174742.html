Etymology

The word catbrier comes from two parts: cat and brier, and brier means 'thorny bush', as in the word 'briar' used in 'briar pipe'. A helpful way to remember it is that the vine has sharp stems that can scratch like a cat's claws, so the name fits its prickly nature.
