Etymology

chloroacetophenone breaks into parts: chloro- meaning 'green' and like chlorine, aceto- meaning 'vinegar', and -phenone with the '-one' ending like acetone. So the name tells you it is a specific chemical made with chlorine and acetic parts, which helps explain why chloroacetophenone is used as a tear gas that irritates the eyes and skin.
