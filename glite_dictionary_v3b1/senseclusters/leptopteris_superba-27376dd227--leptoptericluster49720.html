Etymology

Leptopteris superba comes from old Greek and Latin parts: lepto- meaning 'thin', -pteris meaning 'fern' or 'wing', and superba meaning 'splendid' or 'showy'. So the name gives the picture of a 'thin, splendid fern', which fits this New Zealand species with fine divided fronds and woolly stalks.
