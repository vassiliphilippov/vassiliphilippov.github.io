Etymology

vehemently comes from the Latin word vehemens, meaning 'violent' or 'forceful', and from vehere 'to carry'. A helpful way to remember it is to imagine strong feelings being 'carried' forward with power, so to do something vehemently is to do it with very strong feeling or force.
