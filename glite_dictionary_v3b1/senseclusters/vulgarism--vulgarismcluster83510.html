Etymology

vulgarism comes from the Latin word vulgaris, which means 'common' or belonging to ordinary people and is the same root as vulgar. A helpful way to remember it is that a vulgarism is a 'common' word that polite people call 'rude', so it means an offensive or coarse expression.
