Etymology

Lepechinia is a plant name given in honor of the Russian naturalist Lepechin. These plants belong to the 'mint family' and often have strong, pleasant smells, so thinking of Lepechinia as 'Lepechin's aromatic mints' helps you remember what the word refers to.
