Etymology

Laguncularia racemosa is the scientific name for the 'white mangrove'. The first part, laguncularia, may come from a word for a small bottle like its seed pods, and the second part, racemosa, means 'with bunches' of flowers, so the name helps you picture this coastal tree that people use for tannin.
