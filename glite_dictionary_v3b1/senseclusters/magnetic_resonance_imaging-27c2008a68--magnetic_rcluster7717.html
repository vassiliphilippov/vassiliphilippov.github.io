Etymology

The term magnetic resonance imaging comes from how the scan works: strong magnetic fields line up tiny parts of the body, a radio pulse makes them resonate, which is the resonance part, and the machine makes an image, a detailed 'picture' of organs from those signals. So the name literally describes making a 'picture' from magnetic resonance.
