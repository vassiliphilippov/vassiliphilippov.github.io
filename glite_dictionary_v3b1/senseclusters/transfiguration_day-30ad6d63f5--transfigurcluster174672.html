Etymology

Transfiguration Day comes from the Bible story of the Transfiguration, when Jesus went up a mountain and his face and clothes became very bright so his friends said he was 'changed'. So churches set a special day to remember that moment, and today Transfiguration Day is the festival that celebrates it.
