Etymology

Rhamnus californicus is a scientific name that tells a simple story: Rhamnus is an old name for 'buckthorn' and californicus means 'from California', so the name points to a buckthorn plant from California. People also call it 'California coffeeberry' because its small berries were once used like coffee, which helps you remember the plant and its fruit.
