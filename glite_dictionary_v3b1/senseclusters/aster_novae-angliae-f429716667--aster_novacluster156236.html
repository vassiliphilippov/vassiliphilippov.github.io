Etymology

Aster novae-angliae literally means 'New England star' in Latin: aster = 'star' and novae-angliae = 'of New England'. Because the flowers look like little stars and the plant comes from New England, the name helps you remember it is the purple, late-season flower gardeners often call a 'Michaelmas daisy'.
