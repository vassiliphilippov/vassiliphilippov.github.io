Etymology

self-renunciation joins self- ('the person') and renunciation ('giving up'). The nounce part is the same as in announce, from a root meaning 'to tell', so a renunciation was originally a formal 'saying you give something up', and that is why self-renunciation means giving up your own wants or needs for others.
