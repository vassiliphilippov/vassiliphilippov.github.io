-ite

Forming a noun for a person who belongs to a group, supports a leader, or lives in a place
x US / aɪt /

Examples

  • -ary

Etymology

-ite comes from an old Greek ending -ites that meant 'belonging to' or 'from'. So when we call someone a Brooklynite or an Israelite, the -ite part shows they are a member of, supporter of, or person from that place or group.
