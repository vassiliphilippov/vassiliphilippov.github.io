Etymology

chemistry laboratory combines chemistry, the study of substances and how they change, with laboratory, a place for work and experiments, creating a clear picture of people doing chemical tests. So the phrase literally names a place to do chemistry, which is why it means 'a room for experiments, research, or teaching'.
