Etymology

ammonium nitrate/fuel oil is a literal name that shows its two main parts: the fertilizer ammonium nitrate and the fuel oil used with it. Miners and construction crews used that mixture to 'blast rock', so the name directly points to its meaning as an 'industrial explosive'.
