Etymology

overrefinement combines over- ('too much') and refine ('to make pure or more exact'), so it literally means making something too 'fine' or delicate. Think of polishing a story or idea until its shape is lost; that's why overrefinement means adding unnecessary small, complex details that change the original meaning.
