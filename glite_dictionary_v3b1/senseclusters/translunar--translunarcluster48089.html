Etymology

The word translunar comes from the prefix trans- meaning 'across' or 'beyond', the same trans- as in transport, and lunar meaning 'moon', so it literally says 'beyond the moon'. A helpful way to remember its literary sense is to think of something 'beyond the moon', strange, dreamlike, and 'not of this earth'.
