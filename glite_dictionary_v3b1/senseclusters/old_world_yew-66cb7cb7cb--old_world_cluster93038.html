Etymology

The name Old World yew comes from the words Old World and yew: Old World was the name people used for Europe and Asia before the Americas were known, and a yew is a slow-growing evergreen often seen in very old churchyards. So, Old World yew means a yew species from Europe and parts of Asia and is often very old.
