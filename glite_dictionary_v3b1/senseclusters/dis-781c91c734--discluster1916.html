Etymology

Dis comes from the Roman name Dīs Pater, which meant 'rich father' and came from the Latin word dives 'rich' because people thought gold and other wealth lay under the earth. So Dis became the name of the god of the underworld, linking the idea of riches below the ground to the ruler of the world of the dead.
