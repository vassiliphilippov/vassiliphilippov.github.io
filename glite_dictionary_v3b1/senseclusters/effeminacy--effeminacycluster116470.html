Etymology

effeminacy comes from Latin parts e- 'out' and femina 'woman', so it originally meant 'made like a woman'. The same root is in feminine and female, which is why effeminacy now means showing qualities thought of as 'female' or 'unmanly', and it is often used as a negative word.
