Etymology

The scientific name Vulpes vulpes comes from Latin and simply repeats the Latin word Vulpes, which means 'fox'. Early scientists used Latin names and sometimes repeated the same word to show the main kind in a group, so Vulpes vulpes is the formal name for the red fox.
