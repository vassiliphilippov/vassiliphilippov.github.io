Etymology

Dipteryx odorata may come from old Greek and Latin words—Dipteryx meaning 'two wings' and odorata meaning 'scented'—because the tree has winged fruit that carry its seeds and very fragrant black seeds called tonka beans. That's why the name points to a tall tropical tree known for its sweet-smelling seeds used to flavor food and perfumes.
