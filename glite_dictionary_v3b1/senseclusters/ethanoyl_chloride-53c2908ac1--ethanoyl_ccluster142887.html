Etymology

ethanoyl chloride splits into ethanoyl from ethane, a tiny two-carbon unit, and chloride, meaning 'has chlorine', so picture a small ethane piece holding a chlorine atom. This picture helps: ethanoyl chloride is a small, very reactive liquid used to add that ethane piece to other molecules, and it reacts with water to give off strong fumes, so it must be handled carefully.
