Etymology

emmenagogue comes from Greek parts emmeno- meaning 'month', seen in menstruation, and -agogue meaning 'to lead' or 'bring'. So an emmenagogue is a substance that 'leads' the monthly flow, which is why it means something that brings on a woman's period.
