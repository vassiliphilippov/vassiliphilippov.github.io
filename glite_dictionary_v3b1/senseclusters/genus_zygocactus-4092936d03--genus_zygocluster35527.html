Etymology

genus Zygocactus comes from old Greek words: zygo meaning 'yoke' or 'pair' and cactus meaning 'prickly plant'. This helps you remember it because these cacti have flat stem segments joined together like pieces in a yoke, so the name points to their 'joined' stems.
