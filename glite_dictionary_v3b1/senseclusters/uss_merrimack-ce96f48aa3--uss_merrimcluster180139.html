Etymology

USS Merrimack comes from the Merrimack River in New England, because ships are often named after rivers to show strength and local ties. One famous USS Merrimack was rebuilt with iron plates and fought the USS Monitor, so the name now brings to mind a 'turning point' in sea fighting.
