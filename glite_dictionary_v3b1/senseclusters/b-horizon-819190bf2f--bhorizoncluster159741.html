Etymology

B-horizon names the soil layer below the A- layer, with B- simply showing it is the second layer. The part horizon is the same as the familiar horizon, the line where sky meets land, and it originally meant a 'boundary', so B-horizon is the lower 'boundary' in soil where minerals and clay collect.
