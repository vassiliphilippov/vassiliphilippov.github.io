Etymology

subversion comes from the Latin parts sub- meaning 'under' and vert/vers meaning 'to turn', the same root you see in convert and reverse. It suggests a hidden 'turning from under' that works against leaders or rules, so subversion can mean secretly trying to overthrow a government.
