Etymology

The word abbey-lubber comes from abbey, meaning 'monastery', and lubber, an old word for a 'lazy' or clumsy person, the same lubber you find in landlubber. Think of a monk lounging inside the abbey and avoiding work, so an abbey-lubber is simply a lazy person who shirks duties.
