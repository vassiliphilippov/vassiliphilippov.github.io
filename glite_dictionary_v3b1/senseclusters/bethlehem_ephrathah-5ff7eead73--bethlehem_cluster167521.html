Etymology

Bethlehem Ephrathah combines the name Bethlehem, which means 'house of bread', with Ephrathah, an older local name, giving the picture of a small, fruitful town. This simple image helps explain why the town near Jerusalem is known in the Bible as the 'birthplace' of Jesus and the early 'home' of David.
