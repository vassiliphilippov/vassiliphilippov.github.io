Etymology

siderophilin comes from Greek parts sidero- meaning 'iron' and -philin meaning 'loving', like the 'philo-' idea in philosophy which is 'love of wisdom'. So, siderophilin is 'iron-loving', a helpful image because this protein 'loves' or binds iron and carries it in the blood.
