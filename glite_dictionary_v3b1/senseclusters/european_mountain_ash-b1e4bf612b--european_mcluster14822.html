Etymology

European mountain ash gets its name from how the tree looks and where it grows: its many small leaflets look like a true ash and it often grows on hills, which explains the mountain part, while bright orange berries hang in clusters like small lights. One popular story says people used its branches to 'protect' homes from bad luck, so the strong picture of ash-like leaves and red berries helps you remember the name for this small tree with orange-red berrylike fruit.
