Etymology

steatite comes from an old Greek root steat- meaning 'fat' or 'tallow', so it was thought of as a 'fat' stone. It is also called soapstone because the rock feels smooth and soapy, and that soft, soapy feel explains why people use it for hearths, tabletops, and carved ornaments.
