Etymology

Lapp for the Sámi language probably comes from the place name Lappi, the Finnish name for the north, or from an old word meaning 'patch', perhaps for patchwork clothes. So remember: it once named the language of people from Lapland, but today the correct name is Sámi and Lapp is now seen as old or offensive.
