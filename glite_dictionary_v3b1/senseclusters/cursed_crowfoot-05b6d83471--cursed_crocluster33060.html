Etymology

cursed crowfoot gets its name from the plant's look and its bad reputation: the small, split leaves look like a bird's foot, so people called it crowfoot, and farmers added cursed because it spreads fast in wet fields and is hard to remove. That's why the name now describes an annual marsh herb often seen as a 'troublesome weed'.
