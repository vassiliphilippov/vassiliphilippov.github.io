Etymology

pyrrhotite comes from the Greek root pyrrhos, meaning 'flame-coloured' or 'reddish', and the mineral ending -ite which names rocks and minerals. So pyrrhotite is a brownish, sometimes weakly magnetic mineral, and the name helps you remember its warm, rusty colour and that it is a mineral.
