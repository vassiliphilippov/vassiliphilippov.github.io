Etymology

Homobasidiomycetes breaks into three parts: homo- 'same', basidio- (like the English base) 'pedestal or support', and -mycetes 'fungi'. So the name means fungi with the same kind of spore-making 'base', which is why it is used for groups that include most common mushrooms and puffballs.
