Etymology

The phrase Jiminy Cricket may come from people changing the name 'Jesus Christ' to a softer saying so they would not swear, and it was made famous by the small cricket in Disney's Pinocchio who often reacts to surprises. That's why Jiminy Cricket is used as a mild exclamation for 'surprise' or 'annoyance'.
