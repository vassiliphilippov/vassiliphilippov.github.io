Etymology

intemperately comes from in- meaning 'not' and temper meaning 'to keep things steady or under control', the same root you see in temperate and in the idea of 'temper' as self-control. So, to act intemperately is to act 'not moderate' — in other words, in an excessive or uncontrolled way.
