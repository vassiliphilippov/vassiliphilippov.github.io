Etymology

Trichoglossus comes from Greek parts tricho- meaning 'hair' and glossus meaning 'tongue'. A helpful picture is the lorikeet's brush-tipped, hair-like tongue used to drink nectar, so the name describes these colorful parrots' special tongue.
