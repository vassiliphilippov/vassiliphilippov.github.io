Etymology

Glenlivet takes its name from Scottish Gaelic: glen (from gleann) means 'valley' and Livet, the river name, may mean 'smooth' or 'flood'. So the whisky Glenlivet is named after that valley and its water, which helps explain its Scottish origin.
