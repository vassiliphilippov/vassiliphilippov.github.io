Etymology

mahogany tree probably gets its name from an old Caribbean word used by sailors and traders for the rich, reddish-brown wood called mahogany, so people began to call the tree by that name. That's why mahogany tree now means the tree that gives that 'valuable wood' used for fine furniture.
