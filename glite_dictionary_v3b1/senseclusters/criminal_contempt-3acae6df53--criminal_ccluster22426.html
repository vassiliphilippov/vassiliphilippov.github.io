Etymology

criminal contempt comes from a simple image: someone showing open contempt by refusing to obey the judge or by shouting and disrupting court. Because courts must keep order, that rude or defiant act is treated as a criminal offense and can be punished, so the phrase means 'a crime for disrespecting the court'.
