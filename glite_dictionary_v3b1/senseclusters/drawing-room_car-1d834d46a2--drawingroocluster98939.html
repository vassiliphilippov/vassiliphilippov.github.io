Etymology

The noun drawing-room car comes from the idea of a train car fitted like a house drawing-room, with soft chairs and space to sit as if you were in a living room. Because it felt like a private sitting room, passengers paid more for this 'extra comfort', so today the term means a comfortable passenger car for day travel.
