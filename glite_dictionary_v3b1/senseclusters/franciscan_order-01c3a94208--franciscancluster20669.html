Etymology

The Franciscan order was started by Saint Francis of Assisi in the 1200s. The word Franciscan comes from his name and order means a group of religious people who follow his ideas. Saint Francis taught living simply and helping the poor, so the order became known for 'poverty', 'humility', and 'charity', and today the phrase means a community that lives and works this way.
