Etymology

Bolívar comes from a place name in the Basque country and probably means 'mill valley', from the Basque parts boli 'mill' and ibar 'valley'. So the name began as a family or place name, and today Bolívar is remembered as the famous leader whose name marks many cities, coins, and monuments.
