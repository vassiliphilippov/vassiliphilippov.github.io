Etymology

be a nimrod comes from the name Nimrod, a mighty hunter in the Bible, but a popular cartoon joke changed the sense. In early cartoons, Bugs Bunny called the clumsy hunter Elmer Fudd 'Nimrod' as a joke, and people began using nimrod to mean 'a fool', so 'be a nimrod' now means to act foolishly.
