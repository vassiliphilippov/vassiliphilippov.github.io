Etymology

President Truman is the name of Harry S. Truman, who became the 33rd US President after Franklin D. Roosevelt died and had to make very hard choices at the end of World War II, including ordering the first atomic bombs. So, President Truman often brings to mind a leader who faced sudden, world-changing decisions.
