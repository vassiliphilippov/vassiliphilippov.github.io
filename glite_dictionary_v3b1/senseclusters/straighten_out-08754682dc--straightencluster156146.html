Etymology

The verb straighten out comes from the image of pulling a bent or twisted thing until it lies flat: you use straighten to make it straight and out to show it is spread or untangled. Imagine pulling a kink from a rope or a bent wire until it is smooth. That's why it means 'to make something straight or untangled'.
