Etymology

Bahasa Melayu comes from two Malay words: bahasa meaning 'language' and Melayu meaning the Malay people or the Malay lands. So Bahasa Melayu simply means 'the Malay language' and is the name used for the Malay spoken in Malaysia and neighboring countries.
