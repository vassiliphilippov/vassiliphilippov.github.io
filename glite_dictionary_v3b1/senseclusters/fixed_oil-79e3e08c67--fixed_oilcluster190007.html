Etymology

The term fixed oil comes from the idea of an oil that stays in place: the word fixed means 'held' or not moving, so unlike perfume oils that disappear into the air, these oils stay and do not 'evaporate'. That's why fixed oil means an oil that 'does not evaporate' and is used in food, cosmetics, or medicine.
