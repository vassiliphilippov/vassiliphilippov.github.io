Etymology

trenching spade is a small, narrow shovel soldiers carried to dig trenches. Imagine a soldier using the thin blade to cut a narrow ditch in hard ground; the name links trenching ('digging trenches') and spade ('shovel'), so it means the compact shovel used by infantry.
