Etymology

clunk is a word that copies a heavy, dull sound, like a toolbox falling or boots hitting wood, and is similar to clank or clomp. So, to clunk means 'to make or move with a dull, heavy sound', which is why we say a car 'clunked' when its parts hit hard.
