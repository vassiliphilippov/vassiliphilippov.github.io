Etymology

line-drive single comes from baseball: a line-drive is a very fast, low, straight hit and a single means the batter reaches 'first base'. Imagine the ball flying hard past the infield on a straight line so the batter can reach 'first base', and that image explains why the phrase means a sharp hit that lets the batter get to first.
