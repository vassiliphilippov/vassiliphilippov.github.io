Etymology

finagler comes from the informal verb finagle, which means 'to get something by clever or tricky ways'. A helpful image is someone who 'fiddles' or finds small shortcuts around the rules to reach a goal. That's why a finagler is a person who uses clever, tricky, or dishonest methods to obtain things.
