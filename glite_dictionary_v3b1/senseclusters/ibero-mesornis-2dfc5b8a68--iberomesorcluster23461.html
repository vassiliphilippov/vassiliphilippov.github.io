Etymology

Ibero-mesornis combines Ibero- meaning 'Iberia', the Spain region, meso- meaning 'middle', and -ornis meaning 'bird'. So it literally says a 'middle bird from Iberia', which is a helpful memory for an early, simple bird known from fossils found in Spain.
