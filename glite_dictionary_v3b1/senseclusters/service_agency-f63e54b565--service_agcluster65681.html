Etymology

The noun service agency combines service, meaning help for someone, and agency, a group that 'acts for' others; imagine a small office of helpers doing tasks for many clients. Because one agency helps many clients, it can do special work and often save clients money. That's why service agency means a business you hire to do certain services and pay for.
