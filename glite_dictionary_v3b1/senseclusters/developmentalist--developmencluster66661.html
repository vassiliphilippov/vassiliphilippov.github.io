Etymology

developmentalist comes from development (from develop, meaning 'grow' or 'unfold') plus the ending -ist meaning 'a person who does or supports something', as in artist or dentist. So a developmentalist is someone who studies or promotes ways to help towns and the local economy 'grow'.
