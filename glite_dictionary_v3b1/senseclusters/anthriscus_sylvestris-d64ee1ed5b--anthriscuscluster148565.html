Etymology

Anthriscus sylvestris is a scientific name that paints a small picture: Anthriscus comes from an old Greek word for parsley-like plants and sylvestris means 'of the woods' or 'wild'. So the name points to the wild, parsley-like plant we often call 'cow parsley'.
