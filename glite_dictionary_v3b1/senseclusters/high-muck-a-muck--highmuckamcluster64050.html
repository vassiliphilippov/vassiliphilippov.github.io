Etymology

The word high-muck-a-muck likely comes from an old Pacific Northwest phrase muckamuck, which meant 'important person' or 'chief', and people added high to make it 'very' important. That's why today high-muck-a-muck is a joking way to call someone who behaves like a big, self-important person.
