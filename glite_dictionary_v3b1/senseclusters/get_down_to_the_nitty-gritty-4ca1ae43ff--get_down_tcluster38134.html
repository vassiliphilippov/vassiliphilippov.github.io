Etymology

The idiom get down to the nitty-gritty comes from a picture of tiny dirty bits at the bottom: nitty (like 'nits', tiny bits) and gritty (small grains of sand or dirt). So, to get down to the nitty-gritty means 'to focus on the smallest, most important facts'.
