Etymology

The word ecumenicalism comes from the Greek oikoumenē, meaning 'the inhabited world', and it is the same root as eco- in 'ecology'. The ending -ism means 'a belief or movement', so ecumenicalism is the belief in bringing different Christian groups together into one, worldwide church.
