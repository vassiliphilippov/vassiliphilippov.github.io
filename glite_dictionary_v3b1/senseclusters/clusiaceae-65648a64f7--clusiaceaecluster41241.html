Etymology

Clusiaceae comes from the name of the botanist Clusius and the ending -aceae, which means 'family' in plant names like Rosaceae, the rose family. So Clusiaceae literally means the 'Clusius family' of plants and is used for a group of similar trees, shrubs, and vines.
