Etymology

winter aconite may come from a simple picture: winter because it often pushes up and blooms while snow still covers the ground, and aconite because its small yellow flowers reminded people of other 'aconite' plants. That's why the name helps you remember a tiny plant that appears very early, often before the snow melts.
