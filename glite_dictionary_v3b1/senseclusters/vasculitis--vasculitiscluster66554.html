Etymology

vasculitis breaks into two parts: vascul- from Latin 'vas' meaning 'vessel', like the common word vessel, and -itis meaning 'inflammation' or 'swelling'. So vasculitis is 'inflammation' of blood vessels, which is why it means swelling or damage in the vessel walls.
