Etymology

crucible steel comes from an old method of melting iron in a very strong pot called a crucible, using charcoal to clean the iron and make steel that was even all the way through. Because the metal was melted and mixed inside the pot, it came out cleaner and stronger, so crucible steel now means 'high-quality steel'.
