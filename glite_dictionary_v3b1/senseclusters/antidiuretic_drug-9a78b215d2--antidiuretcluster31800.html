Etymology

antidiuretic drug comes from the parts anti (meaning 'against') and diuretic (a medicine that makes you pass more 'urine'). Think of turning down a tap to slow the flow of water — an antidiuretic drug does the opposite of a diuretic, so it helps the kidneys make less 'urine' and you pass less liquid.
