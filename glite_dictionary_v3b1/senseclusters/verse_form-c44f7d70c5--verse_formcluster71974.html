Etymology

The noun verse form joins verse, meaning a single line of a 'poem', and form, meaning a set shape or pattern, to create a clear image: a shape that gives a poem its lines and flow. So a verse form is a type of poem with a fixed pattern of lines and rhythm, like a haiku or a sonnet.
