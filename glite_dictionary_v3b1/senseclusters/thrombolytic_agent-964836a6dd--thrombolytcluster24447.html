Etymology

thrombolytic agent comes from parts that tell the story: thrombo- means 'clot' and -lytic means 'to break', while agent shows it is a drug. So the name literally shows a medicine that 'breaks' or 'dissolves' a blood 'clot', which is why it means a drug used to open blocked blood flow.
