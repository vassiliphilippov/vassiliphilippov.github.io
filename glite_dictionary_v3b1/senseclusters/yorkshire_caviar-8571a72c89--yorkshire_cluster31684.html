Etymology

Yorkshire caviar comes from a funny idea: cooks in Yorkshire made a rich fish-egg spread and called it fancy caviar to make a simple local food sound special. That's why Yorkshire caviar now means a tasty local treat from Yorkshire, a 'fancy' dish made at home rather than the expensive caviar from abroad.
