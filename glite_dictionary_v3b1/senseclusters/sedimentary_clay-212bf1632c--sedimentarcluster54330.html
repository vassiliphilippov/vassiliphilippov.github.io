Etymology

The noun sedimentary clay comes from two simple words: sedimentary, meaning things left in thin layers, and clay, meaning soft, sticky earth. Picture fine soil and plant bits carried by water or wind that settle in those layers and get pressed together until they become smooth and sticky. So, sedimentary clay means clay 'made by layers' of material left and pressed together over time.
