Etymology

Amelanchier comes from French and was a local name for the small tree that has white flowers and sweet, edible berries. A helpful way to remember it is to think of Amelanchier as the official name for the familiar serviceberry or Juneberry, so the word points to a shrub with pretty blossoms and tasty fruit.
