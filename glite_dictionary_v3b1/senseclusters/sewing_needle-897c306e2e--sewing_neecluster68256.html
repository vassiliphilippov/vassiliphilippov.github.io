Etymology

sewing needle is a plain name: sewing shows it joins cloth and needle shows it is a small, sharp point that 'pricks' fabric. Picture that tiny point with a little hole that 'carries' the thread to 'pass it through fabric', and that is why the phrase means the thin metal tool used for sewing.
