Etymology

sodding comes from the British insult sod, a rude name for someone annoying, and from the phrase sod off. One popular idea connects sod to the old word for 'turf' or 'soil', but the common use is as an insult. So a helpful way to remember sodding is to think of it like the word 'bloody' — it is used to 'show anger' or strong annoyance.
