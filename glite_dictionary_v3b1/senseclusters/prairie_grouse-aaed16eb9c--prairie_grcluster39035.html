Etymology

The compound name prairie grouse comes from the simple image of a grouse living on the prairie. A grouse is a brown, ground bird and a prairie is wide grassland, so the name means 'a type of bird' found on these open plains, and that's why it refers to birds like the greater prairie chicken and the sharp-tailed grouse.
