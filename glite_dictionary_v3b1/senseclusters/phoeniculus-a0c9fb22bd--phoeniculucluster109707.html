Etymology

Phoeniculus comes from the Greek root phoinix meaning 'purple' and the Latin ending -culus meaning 'little'. So the name is like 'little purple one', which is a helpful picture for this genus of African woodhoopoes that have shiny, purple or metallic feathers.
