Etymology

The word gardant comes from Old French garder 'to guard' plus the ending -ant meaning 'doing' or 'watching', and it is related to English guard and guardian. So a gardant animal in heraldry is shown 'watching' the viewer, as if it is guarding or keeping an eye on you.
