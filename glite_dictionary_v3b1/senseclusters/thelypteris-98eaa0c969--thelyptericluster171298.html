Etymology

Thelypteris comes from Greek parts thely- meaning 'female' and -pteris meaning 'fern', so the name can be thought of as a 'female fern'. That's an easy memory: Thelypteris is a group of ferns often called marsh ferns, so thinking 'female fern' helps you remember it is a kind of fern that grows in wet places.
