Etymology

cryptorchidism comes from Greek parts crypt- meaning 'hidden' and orchis meaning 'testicle', and the ending -ism shows a 'condition'. So it literally means 'hidden testicle', which is why the word describes when a testicle has not come down into the scrotum.
