Etymology

Spanish psyllium: The name may come from the plant's tiny, flea-like seeds—psyllium comes from a word meaning 'flea' and Spanish points to where the plant grew. The seeds swell when soaked and form a soft bulk that helps relieve 'constipation', so that's why the plant is used as a 'mild laxative'.
