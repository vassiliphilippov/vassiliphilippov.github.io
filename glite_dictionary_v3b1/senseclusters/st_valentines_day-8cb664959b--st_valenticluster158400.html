Etymology

The holiday St Valentine's Day may come from a priest named Saint Valentine who, long ago in Rome, secretly married young couples and sometimes gave them notes of love. Because of his acts, people began to send cards and small gifts on his day, so today it means 'a day for love and gifts'.
