Etymology

Commerce Secretary comes from two words: commerce, meaning 'trade' or 'business', and secretary, once a trusted person who kept a leader's papers and letters. Because a secretary managed important business for leaders, a Commerce Secretary today is the senior government official who runs the department in charge of trade and business.
