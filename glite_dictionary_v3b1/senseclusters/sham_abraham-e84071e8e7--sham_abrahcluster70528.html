Etymology

sham Abraham may come from joining sham (a fake) and Abraham, a name that makes people think of 'Honest Abe' as a symbol of honesty. Imagine someone putting on a beard and hat like 'Honest Abe' to look honest while hiding lies, so sham Abraham means 'a false show of honesty'.
