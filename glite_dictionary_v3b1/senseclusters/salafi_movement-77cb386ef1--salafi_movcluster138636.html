Etymology

The Salafi movement gets its name from the Arabic word salaf, which means 'ancestors' or 'the early generations'. Its followers try to copy the beliefs and actions of those first Muslims exactly, so the name came to mean a movement that wants a strict return to the earliest Muslim practices.
