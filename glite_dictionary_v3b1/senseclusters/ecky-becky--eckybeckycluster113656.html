Etymology

ecky-becky is a playful rhyming slang phrase like a child's rhyme; a helpful way to remember it is that the first part ecky makes you think of icky ('unpleasant') and the sound is close to hokey ('silly'). So, calling someone ecky-becky means they are acting petty, annoying, or in an odd, silly way.
