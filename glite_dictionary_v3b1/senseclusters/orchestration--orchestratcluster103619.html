Etymology

orchestration comes from orchestra, 'a group of musicians', and a helpful way to remember the non-musical meaning is to think of a conductor arranging players on a stage; in the same way someone arranges people or events so they work together. So orchestration in everyday life means the careful arranging of parts to achieve a particular effect.
