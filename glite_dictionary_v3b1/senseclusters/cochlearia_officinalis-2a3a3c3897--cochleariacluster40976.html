Etymology

The plant Cochlearia officinalis has a name that gives a clear image. Cochlearia may come from a word for 'spoon' because its leaves are spoon-shaped, and officinalis marked plants used as 'medicine'. Sailors ate its sour leaves full of vitamin C to prevent 'scurvy', so the name helps you remember it is the old medicinal 'scurvy-grass'.
