Etymology

geological phenomenon joins the words geological, which is about the Earth's rocks and ground, and phenomenon, which means a visible event. Think of a volcano or an earthquake happening in the ground, and that clear picture shows why it means 'a natural event involving the Earth's shape or parts'.
