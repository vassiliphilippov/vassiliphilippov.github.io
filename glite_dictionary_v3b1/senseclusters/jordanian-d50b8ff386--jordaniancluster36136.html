Etymology

Jordanian comes from the country name Jordan, named after the River Jordan, whose old Hebrew name means 'to flow down' or 'to descend' as it runs into the Dead Sea. So, used as a describing word, Jordanian means 'from Jordan' or 'relating to its people and culture'.
