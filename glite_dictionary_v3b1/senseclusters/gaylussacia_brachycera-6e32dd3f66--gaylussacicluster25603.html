Etymology

Gaylussacia brachycera was named with two parts: the genus Gaylussacia honors the French scientist Joseph Gay-Lussac, and the species brachycera comes from old Greek words meaning 'short horn' because a small part of the flower or fruit looks like a tiny horn. So the name helps you remember a low shrub that grows close to the ground, with small shiny leaves and very small berries.
