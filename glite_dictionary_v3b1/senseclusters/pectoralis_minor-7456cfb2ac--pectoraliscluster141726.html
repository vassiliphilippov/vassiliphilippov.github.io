Etymology

pectoralis minor comes from Latin words: pectoralis means 'of the chest' and minor means 'smaller'. So the name pictures the smaller chest muscle under the bigger one, and that is why it refers to the thin muscle that pulls the shoulder blade down and helps raise the ribs.
