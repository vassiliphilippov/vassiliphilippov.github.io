Etymology

stilbesterol comes from a chemical name that joins stilbene (an old word that meant 'to shine') with the ending -estrol ('estrogen-like'). So the name points to a strong, man-made form of the female hormone estrogen, which helps explain why it was used in medicine and farming and why its risks are connected to hormones.
