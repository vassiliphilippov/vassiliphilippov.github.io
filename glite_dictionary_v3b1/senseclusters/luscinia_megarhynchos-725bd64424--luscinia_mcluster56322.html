Etymology

The name Luscinia megarhynchos comes from Latin and Greek words. The word Luscinia means 'nightingale', and mega- + -rhynchos mean 'large beak'. So the name makes you picture a nightingale with a large beak and helps you remember this bird famous for singing at night.
