Etymology

Sarcorhamphus comes from the Greek parts sarco- meaning 'flesh' and -rhamphus meaning 'beak'. So it literally means 'flesh-beak', a clear image of a bird that uses a strong bill to tear meat, which fits a vulture genus like the king vulture.
