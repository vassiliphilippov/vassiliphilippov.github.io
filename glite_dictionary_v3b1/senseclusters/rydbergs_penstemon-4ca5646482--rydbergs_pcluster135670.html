Etymology

Rydberg's penstemon comes from the plant group penstemon and the botanist Rydberg who first described this species in North America. Imagine Rydberg pointing at the bright blue-violet flowers and people calling it the penstemon 'named after' Rydberg, so the name simply means the penstemon he identified.
