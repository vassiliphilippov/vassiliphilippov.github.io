Etymology

The name bungee cord comes from bungee meaning a very stretchy rope and cord meaning a rope, so picture a large rubber band with hooks at each end that you stretch to hold things tight. Because it stretches and then springs back, the bungee cord is used 'to secure or fasten objects' firmly.
