Etymology

Scuffle hoe is named for the way it works: the word scuffle brings to mind short, shuffling pushes and hoe is the garden tool. You push it along the soil so the blade scrapes and cuts weeds just under the surface, so the name shows it is a hoe used by 'pushing' rather than 'pulling'.
