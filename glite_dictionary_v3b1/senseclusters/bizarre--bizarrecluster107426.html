Etymology

bizarre probably came into English from French and Italian words like bizzarro, once used for someone 'irritable' or odd, and one idea even links it to a Basque word bizar meaning 'beard'. A helpful way to remember it is to imagine a very strange face or beard you cannot forget, so bizarre now means 'strangely unusual'.
