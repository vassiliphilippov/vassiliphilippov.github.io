Etymology

The phrase act like a wanksta mixes the words wank, a rude word for a foolish person, and gangsta, short for 'gangster', a tough street person. One popular story says the image is of someone pretending to be a hard, street-smart 'gangsta' but who is really a fool, so the phrase means 'to behave as if one is tough when this is not real'.
