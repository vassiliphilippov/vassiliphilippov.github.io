Etymology

Phoeniculus comes from the Greek root phoinix meaning 'purple' and the Latin ending -culus meaning 'little'. That's why the scientific name feels like 'little purple one' and helps you remember this genus of woodhoopoes from sub‑Saharan Africa, known for their bright, shiny plumage.
