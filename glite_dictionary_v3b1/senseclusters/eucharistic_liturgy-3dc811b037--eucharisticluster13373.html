Etymology

Eucharistic liturgy comes from old Greek words: Eucharistic from a word meaning 'thanksgiving' and liturgy from a word meaning 'work for the people'. It paints the picture of a shared, thankful service where blessed bread and wine are given and eaten, so it now means the Christian ceremony that remembers the Last Supper.
