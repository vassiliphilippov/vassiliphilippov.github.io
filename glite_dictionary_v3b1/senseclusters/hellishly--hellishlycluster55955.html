Etymology

hellishly comes from hell, meaning 'a place of great pain or punishment', plus the ending -ish meaning 'like' and -ly, which makes the word tell how something is done. So, to do something hellishly is to do it in a very bad, intense, or extreme way, because the idea of 'hell' gives the word a sense of strong unpleasantness.
