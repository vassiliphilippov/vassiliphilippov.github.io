Etymology

tanklike is simply tank + -like, where tank can mean a strong metal container or a military vehicle and -like means 'similar to'. So tanklike describes something big, sturdy, or shaped like a tank, which is why we use it for heavy vats, tough vehicles, or tall water towers.
