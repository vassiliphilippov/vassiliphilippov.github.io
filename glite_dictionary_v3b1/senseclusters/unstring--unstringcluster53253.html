Etymology

unstring is made from the prefix un- meaning 'remove' or 'do the opposite' and string, the 'cord' that stretches across a musical instrument or a bow. So to unstring something is literally to take off its strings, which is why we use it for removing strings from a guitar, violin, or bow.
