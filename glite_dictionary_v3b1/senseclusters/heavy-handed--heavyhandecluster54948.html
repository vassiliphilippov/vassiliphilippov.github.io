Etymology

heavy-handed comes from joining heavy ('weighty') and handed ('with the hand'), so imagine someone with a 'heavy hand' who moves awkwardly and drops things. This picture helps: a heavy hand is clumsy or not delicate. So heavy-handed can mean clumsy or lacking skill with the hands.
