Etymology

lactase deficiency comes from lactase, the body 'enzyme' that breaks the milk sugar 'lactose', and deficiency, meaning 'not having enough'. So lactase deficiency literally means not having enough lactase, which explains why people with it cannot digest milk and often feel uncomfortable after eating dairy.
