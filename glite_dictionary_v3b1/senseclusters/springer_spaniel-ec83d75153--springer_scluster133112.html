Etymology

springer spaniel comes from the dog's old job. A springer was a dog that would 'spring' birds—make them fly out of bushes—so hunters or other dogs could catch them, and spaniel shows it is a type of hunting dog, so today the name means a lively breed made to find and fetch birds.
