Etymology

Sioux came into English from French, who shortened a name used by another Native group that meant 'little snakes' or 'enemies', so it was not the people's own name. Many of the people call themselves Dakota or Lakota, which mean 'friends' or 'allies', and today Sioux is used in English for those Native American groups.
