Etymology

dicamptodontid comes from Greek parts di- 'two', campto- 'bent', and odont- 'tooth', so the name literally means 'two-bent tooth', and the ending -id shows it is a member of that animal family, as in canid 'dog family' or felid 'cat family'. That's why dicamptodontid refers to a particular family of salamander — a member of that group often found near cold streams.
