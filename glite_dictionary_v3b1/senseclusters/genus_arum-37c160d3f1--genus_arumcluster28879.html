Etymology

genus Arum takes its name from an old Greek and Latin plant word 'aron' for the wild arum, a woodland plant with thick, potato-like roots, big heart-shaped leaves and a strange hooded flower. So genus Arum simply means the scientific group named after that familiar plant, remembered for its roots and showy leaves.
