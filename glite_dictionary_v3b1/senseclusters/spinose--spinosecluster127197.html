Etymology

The word spinose comes from the Latin root spina, meaning 'thorn' or 'spine', and the ending -ose, meaning 'full of'. It is like the English word 'spine' or 'spiny', so spinose means 'full of thorns' and is used for plants or animals with sharp spines.
