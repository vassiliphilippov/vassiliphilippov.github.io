Etymology

International System of Units is named for the idea of a single measuring 'toolbox' for the world: international means many countries, system means a set of rules, and units are the things we use to measure, like the meter, kilogram, and second. That's why countries and scientists use the 'SI' to 'measure the same way' worldwide.
