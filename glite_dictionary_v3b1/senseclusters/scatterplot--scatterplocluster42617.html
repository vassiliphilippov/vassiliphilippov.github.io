Etymology

The word scatterplot combines scatter ('to throw or spread in different directions') and plot ('a small drawing or map where you mark points'). Think of scattering seeds across a small plot of land—each dot on the graph is like a seed, so a scatterplot is a graph that shows many dots and the relationship between two things.
