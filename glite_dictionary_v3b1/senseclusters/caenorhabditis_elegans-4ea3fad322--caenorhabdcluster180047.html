Etymology

Caenorhabditis elegans comes from word parts that paint a simple picture: Caenorhabditis combines parts meaning 'new' or 'recent' and 'rod-like', and elegans means 'elegant' or 'slender'. So the name helps you picture a small, thin, graceful worm, which fits this tiny, clear roundworm scientists use in the lab.
