Etymology

The word Malacanthidae comes from the Greek parts malakos ('soft') and akantha ('spine' or 'thorn'). So, it means 'soft-spined' — a handy image for this family of marine fish, the tilefishes, which often have short spines and smoother heads, helping you remember the name.
