Etymology

Fremontodendron comes from Fremont, the explorer John Frémont, and the Greek part -dendron meaning 'tree'. You can see -dendron in rhododendron too. So it is like 'Fremont's tree' and is the name for a shrub that looks like a small tree with big yellow flowers.
