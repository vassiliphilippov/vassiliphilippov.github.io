Etymology

The word whiskered comes from whisker, an old word for the long, stiff hairs on an animal's cheeks and chin. One helpful way to remember it is to think of whisk (a quick brush or movement) and imagine those hairs brushing your face, so whiskered means 'having these noticeable facial hairs'.
