Etymology

Calendula officinalis may come from Latin: Calendula comes from the word 'calends', the 'first day of the month', because the flowers open often and the plant blooms for many months, and officinalis was a label for plants sold in old medicine shops. So the name explains why this common marigold was used for 'medicine' and prized for its long flowering.
