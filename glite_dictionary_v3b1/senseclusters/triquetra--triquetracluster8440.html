Etymology

triquetra comes from the Latin parts tri- 'three' and triquetrus 'three-cornered', so it originally meant a three-cornered shape. That's why a triquetra is a three-part knot or loop often seen in Celtic art to show unity or the idea of three, like the word 'trinity'.
