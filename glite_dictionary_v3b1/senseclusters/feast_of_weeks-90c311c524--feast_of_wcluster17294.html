Etymology

Feast of Weeks is named for counting seven weeks after Passover; Weeks here means those weeks when people brought 'first fruits' to the temple and remembered Moses receiving the Ten Commandments on Mount Sinai. So the holiday marks both the harvest and the 'giving of the Ten Commandments'.
