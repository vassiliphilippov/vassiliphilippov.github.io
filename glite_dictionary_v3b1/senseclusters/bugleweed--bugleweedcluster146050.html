Etymology

bugleweed joins bugle and weed, and a helpful way to remember it is to picture the plant's small, trumpet-like flowers as a tiny 'bugle' while 'weed' simply means a low wild plant. So, bugleweed is a low-growing herb often used as ground cover in gardens.
