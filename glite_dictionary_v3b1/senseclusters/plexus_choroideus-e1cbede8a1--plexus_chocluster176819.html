Etymology

plexus choroideus comes from words that make a simple picture: plexus means a woven 'network' and choroideus refers to a thin 'membrane' near the brain's small spaces. Imagine a small net of blood vessels that makes the liquid that bathes the brain and helps control its pressure, so the name fits the structure's job.
