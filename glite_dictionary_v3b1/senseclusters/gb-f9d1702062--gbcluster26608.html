Etymology

Gb is a short form of the unit gilbert, named for the English scientist William Gilbert who studied magnets, so think of the common name 'Gilbert' to remember it is about magnetism. A Gb measures the 'push' that creates a magnetic field, which is why engineers use it for magnetomotive force.
