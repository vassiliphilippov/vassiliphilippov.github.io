Etymology

Jacquinia keyensis comes from a scientist's name and a place: Jacquinia honors the botanist Jacquin and keyensis means 'from the Keys', the small islands where it grows. So the name is an easy memory hook for this small Caribbean shrub with thick, leather-like leaves and very hard wood.
