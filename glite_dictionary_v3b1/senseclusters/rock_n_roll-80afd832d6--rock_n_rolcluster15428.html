Etymology

Rock 'n' roll comes from older uses of the words rock and roll, where rock meant 'to move back and forth' and roll meant 'to sway or turn'. Radio hosts in the 1950s used that image of movement to name the new music that makes people dance, so the name fits the music's strong beat and dance feeling.
