Etymology

devitalization comes from the part de- meaning 'remove' and the root vital meaning 'life' or 'energy', the same root you see in vitality and vitamin. So devitalization is the act of taking away the life or energy of something, which is why we use it for cells, nerves, or even a weakened company.
