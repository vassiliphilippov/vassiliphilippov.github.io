Etymology

cyclopic comes from the Greek name Cyclops, which is made from a word for 'circle' (cyclo-) and a word for 'eye' (ops, the same root as in optic). So cyclopic describes something that has or looks like a single eye in the middle of the forehead, like the one-eyed creature of Greek myth.
