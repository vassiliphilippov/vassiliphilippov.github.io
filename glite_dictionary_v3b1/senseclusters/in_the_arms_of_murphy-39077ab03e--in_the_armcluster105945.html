Etymology

in the arms of Murphy comes from the idea of Murphy's Law, named after an engineer who said 'if anything can go wrong, it will'. Saying you are in the arms of Murphy creates the picture of being held by Murphy and his arms of 'bad luck', so it means being in a situation where many things go wrong.
