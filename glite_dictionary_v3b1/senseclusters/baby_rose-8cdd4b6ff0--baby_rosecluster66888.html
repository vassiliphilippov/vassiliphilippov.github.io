Etymology

baby rose comes from gardeners who saw this bush make many tiny flowers while growing very quickly. They used baby to show the small blooms and rose for the plant, and because the bush is often 'used as a base for new roses' the name now means a small, vigorous rose often planted for hedges or grafting.
