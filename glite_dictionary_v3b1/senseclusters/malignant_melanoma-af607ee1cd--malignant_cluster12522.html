Etymology

malignant melanoma comes from two parts: melanoma comes from an old word for 'black' because this cancer often shows as dark, odd-shaped spots on the skin, and malignant means 'dangerous' and likely to spread. So the name literally describes a dangerous dark skin growth, which is why it refers to this serious form of skin cancer.
