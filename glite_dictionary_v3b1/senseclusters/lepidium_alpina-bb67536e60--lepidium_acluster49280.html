Etymology

Lepidium alpina comes from old words: Lepidium may come from a Greek word meaning 'small scale', like a tiny seed or leaf, and alpina means 'from the Alps' or 'mountain'. So the name gives a simple picture of a small mountain plant, which is why it is used for a little herb that grows on rocky slopes.
