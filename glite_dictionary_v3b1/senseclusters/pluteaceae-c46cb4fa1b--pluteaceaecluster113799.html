Etymology

Pluteaceae comes from the Latin pluteus, meaning 'shield', because some species have caps that look like small shields, and the ending -aceae marks a plant or fungus 'family'. So Pluteaceae names this 'shield family' of mushrooms, which today are recognized by features like free gills and pink spores.
