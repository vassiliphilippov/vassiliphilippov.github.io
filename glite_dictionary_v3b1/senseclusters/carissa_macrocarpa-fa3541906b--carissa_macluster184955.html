Etymology

Carissa macrocarpa combines a personal name and a simple description: Carissa may mean 'grace' or 'beloved', and macrocarpa comes from words meaning 'large' and 'fruit'. So the name gives a picture of a thorny shrub with big, edible fruit, which is why it fits this South African plant.
