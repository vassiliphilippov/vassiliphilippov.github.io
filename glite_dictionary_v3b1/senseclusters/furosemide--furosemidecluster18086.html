Etymology

furosemide comes from two parts of its chemical makeup: furo- refers to a small ring called 'furan', and -semide comes from 'sulfonamide', a common chemical group in some medicines. So the name is a technical drug name that refers to its chemistry and helps you remember it is a medicine that makes the body pass extra water.
