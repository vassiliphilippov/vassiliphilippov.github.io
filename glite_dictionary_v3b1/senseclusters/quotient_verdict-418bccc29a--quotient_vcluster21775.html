Etymology

The noun quotient verdict comes from a simple math picture: jurors each name a number for damages, they add the numbers and divide by the number of jurors to get a quotient, which becomes the verdict. That's why the term means a decision made by averaging jurors' numbers, and why it is not allowed - it ignores each juror's true opinion.
