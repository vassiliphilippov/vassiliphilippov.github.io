Etymology

superoxide anion comes from the parts super, oxide, and anion: it is an oxygen molecule (an oxide) that has picked up one 'extra electron', so it becomes more active and is an 'anion' with a 'negative' charge. That's why the name describes a negatively charged oxygen molecule.
