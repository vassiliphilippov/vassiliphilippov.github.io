Etymology

rabbit bush likely got its name from the simple picture of rabbits hiding under or feeding on the low, bushy branches—people saw a rabbit beside a bush and the name stuck. That's why rabbit bush today means the yellow-flowered shrub often used to make 'yellow dye'.
