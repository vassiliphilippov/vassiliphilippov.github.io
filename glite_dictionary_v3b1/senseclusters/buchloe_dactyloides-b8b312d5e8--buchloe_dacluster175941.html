Etymology

Buchloe dactyloides may come from old words meaning Buchloe 'ox or cow' and dactyloides 'finger-like'. People likely used this name because the grass grew where large grazing animals like the 'buffalo' fed and its thin blades looked like little fingers, so the name links to the modern common name 'buffalo grass'.
