Etymology

erythroblastosis fetalis comes from old word parts: erythro meaning 'red', blast meaning 'young cell', and fetalis meaning 'of the fetus'. This image helps remember the condition: it describes a baby with many immature red blood cells because the mother's immune system attacks the baby's red cells, so the baby has too few healthy red blood cells.
