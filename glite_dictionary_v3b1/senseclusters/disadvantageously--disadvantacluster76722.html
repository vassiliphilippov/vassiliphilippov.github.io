Etymology

disadvantageously comes from the prefix dis- meaning 'not' or 'the opposite of', the word advantage meaning 'help' or 'benefit', and the ending -ly which makes it an adverb. So, it means doing something 'in a way that is not helpful' or 'unfavorable', like an action that makes things worse for someone.
