Etymology

teakettle is just the two words tea and kettle put together: a kettle is a pot used 'to boil' water and tea is the hot 'drink' you make with that water. So a teakettle is literally a kettle for making tea, which is why we use it to heat water and often hear it whistle when the water is ready.
