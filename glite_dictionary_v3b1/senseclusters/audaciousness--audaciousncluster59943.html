Etymology

The word audaciousness comes from the old Latin verb audēre, meaning 'to dare', and is the same root behind audacity, so think of it like the simple English word 'dare'. So audaciousness is the quality of being bold or fearless, and sometimes it can mean being rude or disrespectful.
