Etymology

Matteuccia struthiopteris gets its name from how the plant looks: its tall, curved, feathery leaves look like an 'ostrich' feather. One part, struthiopteris, comes from words meaning 'ostrich' and 'fern', so the name helps you remember it is the fern with ostrich-like leaves.
