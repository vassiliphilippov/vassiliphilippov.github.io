Etymology

ratchet wheel comes from the image of a simple machine: a wheel with teeth like a saw and a small pawl, a catch, that lets the wheel turn one way but stops it from going back. So, a ratchet wheel today means a wheel that allows movement in 'one direction' and prevents backward motion.
