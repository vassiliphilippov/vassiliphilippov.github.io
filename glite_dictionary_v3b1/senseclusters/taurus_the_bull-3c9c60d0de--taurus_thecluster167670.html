Etymology

Taurus the Bull comes from a very old picture in the sky: people long ago saw a group of stars that looked like a bull, so they called the sign Taurus. Bulls are strong, steady and often stubborn, so this image helps explain why the sign is linked to 'patience' and 'determination'.
