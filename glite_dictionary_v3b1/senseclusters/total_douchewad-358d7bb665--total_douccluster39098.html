Etymology

total douchewad probably comes from joining two rude words: douche (a harsh cleaning device or shower) and wad (a small lump or bundle). The odd picture of a useless, unpleasant lump makes the insult stick, so it is used to mean 'an extremely rude, obnoxious person'.
