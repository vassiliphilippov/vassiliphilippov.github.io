Etymology

hocus-pocus began as playful nonsense that sounds like a small magic 'spell'. One popular story says it copied the Latin phrase hoc est ('this is') often said in church, so it sounded like secret words, and that's why hocus-pocus now means showy tricks or nonsense used to fool people.
