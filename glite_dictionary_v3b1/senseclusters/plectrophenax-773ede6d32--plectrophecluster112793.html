Etymology

Plectrophenax is a scientific name for a group of small birds like the snow bunting and comes from Greek parts plektron 'spur' and phenax 'imposter'. A helpful way to remember it is to think of a bird that 'tricks' you by blending into the snow, because the snow bunting's white feathers can hide it, so the name points to this group's look.
