Etymology

mallee hen gets its name from the bird's home and its type: mallee is the dry Australian bush where it lives, and hen was used because it is a female, ground bird that looks a little like a chicken. So the name means 'an adult female bird from the mallee', and that helps you remember it is a large ground bird known for building big nesting mounds.
