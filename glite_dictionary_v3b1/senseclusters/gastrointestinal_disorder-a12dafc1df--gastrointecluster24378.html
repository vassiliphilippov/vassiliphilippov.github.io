Etymology

gastrointestinal disorder comes from Greek words: gastro meaning 'stomach' and intestinal meaning 'intestines', with disorder meaning 'problem'. So the phrase names a 'problem' of the stomach and intestines, which is why today it means an illness that affects those organs.
