Etymology

ketoacidosis-resistant diabetes gets its name from the phrase parts: ketoacidosis, a serious state where the body makes acids because it cannot use sugar properly, and resistant, meaning that problem usually does not happen. So the name helps you remember this is a milder kind of diabetes where the dangerous acid build-up usually 'does not happen' and can often be managed with diet and pills.
