Etymology

Jemaah Islamiyah comes from Arabic words: Jemaah meaning 'group' and Islamiyah meaning 'Islamic'. The name gives the simple image of a group gathered for Islam, and that is why those words were used as the name of a militant group in Southeast Asia.
