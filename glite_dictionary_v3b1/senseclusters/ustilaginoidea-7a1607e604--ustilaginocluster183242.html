Etymology

Ustilaginoidea comes from the smut-fungus name ustilagin- (a root linked to a Latin word for 'burn' because the spores look dark) and the ending -oidea meaning 'like' or 'group', as in asteroid. So Ustilaginoidea is the name for a group of fungi 'like' smut that cause plant diseases.
