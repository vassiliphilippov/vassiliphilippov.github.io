Etymology

pratt is British slang for someone who behaves in a silly or foolish way. A helpful way to remember it is to link pratt to the older word prat, meaning 'bottom', which you see in pratfall, a silly fall onto your 'bottom', so calling someone a pratt is like calling them a 'bottom' who acts foolishly.
