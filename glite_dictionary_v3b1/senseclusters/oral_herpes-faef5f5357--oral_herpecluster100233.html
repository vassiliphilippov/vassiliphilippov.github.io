Etymology

oral herpes starts as a literal label: oral means about the 'mouth' and herpes comes from an old word meaning 'to creep', a picture of blisters that can appear and come back around the mouth. So oral herpes refers to a viral infection that causes sores or blisters around the mouth and can spread to other people.
