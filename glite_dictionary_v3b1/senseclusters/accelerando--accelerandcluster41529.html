Etymology

accelerando comes from Latin accelerare 'to make faster', the same root as accelerate, formed from ad- 'to' and celer 'swift', with the Italian ending -ando that shows an action. So an accelerando in music is 'an increase in speed' or a part that is played 'gradually moving faster'.
