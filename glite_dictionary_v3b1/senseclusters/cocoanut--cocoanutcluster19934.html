Etymology

The word cocoanut comes from the Portuguese and Spanish word coco, which meant 'head' or 'a funny face' because the three dark spots on the shell looked like eyes and a mouth. English added nut because it is a large nut, so the name points to the hard, round tropical fruit with a hairy shell.
