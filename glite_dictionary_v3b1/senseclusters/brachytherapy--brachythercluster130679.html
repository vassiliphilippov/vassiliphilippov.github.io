Etymology

brachytherapy comes from two Greek parts: brachy- meaning 'short' and therapy meaning 'treatment'. So, the name shows it is a 'short' or close treatment, because the radioactive source is placed inside or very near the tumor so the radiation goes mostly to the cancer.
