Etymology

Rocky Mountain bristlecone pine points to both place and a clear feature: these small pines grow on high, rocky slopes in the Rocky Mountains and their cones have short, stiff bristles. Because they grow very slowly and survive cold, windy weather, they can be 'long-lived' and live for many centuries.
