Etymology

Becket probably began as a simple family name from two images: the old French bec meaning 'beak' or the old word beck meaning 'brook' or 'small stream'. So you can remember Thomas Becket, the archbishop of Canterbury who was killed for defending the church, by picturing a little 'beak' or a tiny 'brook' next to his story.
