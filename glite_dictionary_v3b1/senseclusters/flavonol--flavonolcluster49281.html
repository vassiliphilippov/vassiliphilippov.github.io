Etymology

flavonol begins with flav-, from Latin meaning 'yellow', and ends with -ol, a common chemical ending for alcohol parts of chemicals. So a flavonol is a yellow plant compound often found in fruits and vegetables, which explains why it can give pale or yellow colors and why scientists study it for its antioxidant effects.
