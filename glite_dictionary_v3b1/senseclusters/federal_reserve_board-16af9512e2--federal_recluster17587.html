Etymology

Federal Reserve Board is named from three simple words: federal, meaning the national government, reserve, meaning money kept for safety, and board, meaning a small group who meet to make decisions. Because this small group meets to control the nation's kept money and set interest rates, the name shows why it is the body that decides the country's 'money' rules.
