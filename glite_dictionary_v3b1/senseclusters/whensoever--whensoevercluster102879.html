whensoever

0.00/m9.4
At any time you want
adverb US / ˌwɛnsoʊˈɛvər /

Examples

Whensoever you wish
You may enter the garden whensoever you wish, day or night.
The invitation allows guests to arrive whensoever it pleases them.
He promised to help her whensoever she needed assistance.
You can call me whensoever you need help.
Please visit whensoever you feel like it.
He is ready to assist whensoever you require it.

I will be here whensoever you need me!
Can you remember whensoever we last met?

Synonyms

  • anytime
    30/m1.0
    Used for any time when something is convenient or possible
    vswhensoever: is broader, not limited to times that are convenient or possible
  • anywhen
    0.02/m9.7
    Expresses happening at any time without extra conditions
    vswhensoever: is the same meaning but uses different wording
  • when
    0.01/m1.0
    Refers to a specific time or occasion for something to happen or be scheduled
    vswhensoever: is broader, applying to any time rather than a single planned occasion
  • whenever
    0.00/m1.0
    Means any time and also every time a certain event occurs
    vswhensoever: is used more generally for any time rather than repeated occurrences

Etymology

whensoever is an old way of putting together when and ever, meaning 'at any time', so it simply means 'whenever' or 'at whatever time'.
