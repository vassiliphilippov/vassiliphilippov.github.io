Etymology

Baruch ben Neriah literally breaks into Baruch ('blessed'), ben ('son of') and Neriah ('lamp of the Lord'). He was the scribe who helped the prophet Jeremiah write down his messages, so think of the name as 'the blessed son of the Lord's light' to remember his role as Jeremiah's writer.
