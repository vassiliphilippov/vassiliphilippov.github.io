Etymology

The insult son of a bitch comes from a simple, rude picture: calling someone's mother bitch (a female dog) was a strong way to show dislike, so to call a person a son of a bitch meant they came from a hated or shameful mother. That's why today the phrase is used as a very rude 'insult' for someone seen as nasty, annoying, or very bad.
