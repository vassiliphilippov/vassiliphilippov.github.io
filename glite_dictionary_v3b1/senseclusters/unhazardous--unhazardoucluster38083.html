Etymology

unhazardous starts with un- meaning 'not' and has hazard as its root, a word that once named a risky 'game of dice' from Arabic 'al-zahr' and later came to mean 'danger' or 'risk'. So unhazardous simply means 'not dangerous' or 'safe'.
