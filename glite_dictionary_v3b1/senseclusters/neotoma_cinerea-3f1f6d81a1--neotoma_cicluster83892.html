Etymology

The species Neotoma cinerea is the scientific name for the 'bushy-tailed woodrat'. The second word cinerea comes from Latin for 'ash-coloured', so the name points to its grey fur and helps you picture a small rat with a big bushy tail that likes to collect shiny things.
