Etymology

The word mooneye comes from an old belief that the moon could cause a horse's sore eye, so the name is literally moon + eye meaning 'moon' and 'eye'. That's why mooneye means a sore or swelling in a horse's eye that keeps coming back and can sometimes cause blindness.
