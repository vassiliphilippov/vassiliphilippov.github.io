Etymology

The word pettifogger comes from petty ('small') and fog ('mist' that hides), and it was used for a lawyer who used small, confusing tricks. A helpful way to remember it is to think of someone making a 'fog' of words over 'small' points, so a pettifogger today is a person who argues over tiny details or uses dishonest tricks.
