Etymology

blooper comes from the small, silly sound bloop plus the ending -er that turns a sound into a thing. A helpful way to remember it is to imagine a quick, funny 'bloop' when someone makes an awkward slip, which is why a blooper is an embarrassing mistake often seen in films or on live TV.
