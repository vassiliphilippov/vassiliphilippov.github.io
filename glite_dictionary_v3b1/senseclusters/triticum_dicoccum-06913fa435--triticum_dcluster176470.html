Etymology

Triticum dicoccum comes from old plant naming. The parts Triticum and dicoccum mean 'wheat' and 'two grains' because each seed head often holds two seeds, and that simple image helps explain why the name refers to this old, hardy wheat now used for bread and animal feed.
