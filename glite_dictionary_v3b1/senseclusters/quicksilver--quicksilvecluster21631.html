Etymology

quicksilver comes from old words meaning 'living' (quick) and 'silver' (silver), so it literally meant 'living silver' and gives a striking image of the metal mercury as a heavy, shiny liquid that moves like something alive. That's why quicksilver is an old name for the element mercury.
