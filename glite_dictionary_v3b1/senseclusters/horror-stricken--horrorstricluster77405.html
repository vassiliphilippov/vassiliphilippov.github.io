Etymology

horror-stricken comes from horror, from an old Latin word horrere meaning 'to shudder', and stricken, from strike meaning 'to hit'. So, to be horror-stricken is like being 'hit' by a feeling that makes you 'shudder', which is why it means extremely shocked or terrified.
