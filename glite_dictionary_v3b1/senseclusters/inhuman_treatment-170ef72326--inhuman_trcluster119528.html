Etymology

inhuman treatment comes from a strong image: inhuman means acting as if someone is not even human, and treatment is how you treat them. One popular idea says the picture of a person being ignored, hurt, or left without food shows why the phrase means 'to cause great pain and suffering in a cruel way'.
