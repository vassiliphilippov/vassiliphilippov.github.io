Etymology

Balsamorhiza comes from the parts balsam and rhiza, where balsam means 'a sweet-smelling substance' and rhiza means 'root'. So the name literally means 'balsam-root' and that helps you remember this plant because it has large, fragrant roots that were used for medicine.
