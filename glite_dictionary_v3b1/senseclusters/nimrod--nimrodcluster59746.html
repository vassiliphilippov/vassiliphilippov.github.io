Etymology

nimrod comes from the Bible name Nimrod, who was called a 'mighty hunter'. A later cartoon joke used the name to mock a clumsy hunter, and people began using nimrod to mean a 'foolish' or 'incompetent' person, so the modern insult grew from that ironic twist.
