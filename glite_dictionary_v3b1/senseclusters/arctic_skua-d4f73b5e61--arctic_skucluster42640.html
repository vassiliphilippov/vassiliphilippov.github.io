Etymology

arctic skua gets its name from two simple ideas: arctic means the cold north, and skua may come from a word used by northern fishermen for a bold, 'thieving' sea bird. Because these birds often chase other seabirds and steal their fish, the name gives a clear image of a fierce sea bird that 'robs' others.
