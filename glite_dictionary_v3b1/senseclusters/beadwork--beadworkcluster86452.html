Etymology

beadwork can also mean a beaded edge or molding, from bead ('small round shape') and work ('decoration' or 'crafted detail'). Think of a row of tiny round 'beads' along the edge of a chair or cabinet, and you can see why beadwork means the decorative beaded edging on furniture or buildings.
