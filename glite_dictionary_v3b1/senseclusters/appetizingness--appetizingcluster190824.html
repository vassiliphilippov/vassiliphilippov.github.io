Etymology

appetizingness comes from appetite, which means 'desire to eat', and the endings -ing and -ness turn it into 'the quality of making someone want to eat', and you can also see the same root in appetizer, a small dish that wakes your appetite. So, appetizingness is the quality of making you want to eat.
