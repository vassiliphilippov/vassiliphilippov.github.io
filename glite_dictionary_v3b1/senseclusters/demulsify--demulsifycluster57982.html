Etymology

demulsify is built from de- ('remove' or 'reverse') and emulsify ('to make an emulsion', a mix of two liquids such as oil and water). So to demulsify is to 'undo' that mix and let the liquids 'separate', like breaking mayonnaise back into oil and water.
