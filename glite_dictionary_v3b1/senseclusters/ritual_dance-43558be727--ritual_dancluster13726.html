Etymology

ritual dance simply combines ritual, a set of actions in a ceremony, and dance, meaning movement to music. In many cultures long ago, people used dances in sacred ceremonies to tell stories, ask spirits for help, or honour ancestors, so the phrase came to mean 'a dance performed as part of a ceremony'.
