Etymology

abstrusity comes from Latin parts ab- 'away' and trud- 'push', so it originally meant something 'pushed away' or 'hidden'. That's why abstrusity means the quality of being hard to understand — the idea is that the meaning is 'hidden' from view, like something you cannot easily reach or see, and you can see the same root in intrude ('push in').
