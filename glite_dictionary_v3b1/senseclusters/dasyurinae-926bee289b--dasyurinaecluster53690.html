Etymology

Dasyurinae comes from Greek parts dasy- meaning 'hairy' and oura meaning 'tail', plus the scientific ending -inae meaning 'subfamily'. The name gives the image of animals with shaggy tails, which helps explain this group of small, meat-eating pouched animals like quolls and the Tasmanian devil.
