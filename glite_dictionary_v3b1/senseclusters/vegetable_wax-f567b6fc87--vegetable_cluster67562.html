Etymology

vegetable wax is the wax that comes from plants, with vegetable meaning 'from plants'. Imagine people scraping a shiny layer from palm leaves or fruit skins, for example carnauba, to make polish, candles, or skin creams, and so the name means 'a wax made from plants'.
