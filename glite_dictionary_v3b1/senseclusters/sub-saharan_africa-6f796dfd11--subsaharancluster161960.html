Etymology

Sub-Saharan Africa is a simple place name: Sub- means 'below' and Saharan comes from the Sahara Desert, so it literally names the part of Africa 'below' the Sahara. That's why we use it today to mean the countries and people south of the Sahara, not those in North Africa.
