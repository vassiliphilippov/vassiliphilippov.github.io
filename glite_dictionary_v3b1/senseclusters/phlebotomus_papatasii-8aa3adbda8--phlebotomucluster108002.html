Etymology

Phlebotomus papatasii likely comes from old Greek word parts plus a species name used by early researchers. Phlebotomus combines parts that mean 'vein' and 'to cut', giving the image of a tiny fly that 'cuts' skin to drink blood, and papatasi names this particular sand fly. So the name points to the fly's habit of biting and spreading disease.
