Etymology

ultrapop song comes from ultra meaning 'very' or 'extreme' and pop short for 'popular music', so imagine a song turned up to the highest level with very simple words and a repeating tune that sticks in your head. That's why it means 'a pop song that is extremely catchy and widely appealing'.
