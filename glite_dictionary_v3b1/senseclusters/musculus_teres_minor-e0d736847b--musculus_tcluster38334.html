Etymology

musculus teres minor: The body name musculus teres minor comes from Latin words: musculus means 'little mouse' because the muscle under the skin looked like a tiny mouse when it moved, teres means 'rounded', and minor means 'smaller'. So the name paints a picture of a small, rounded shoulder muscle and helps you remember that musculus teres minor is the little muscle that turns the arm out and keeps the shoulder steady.
