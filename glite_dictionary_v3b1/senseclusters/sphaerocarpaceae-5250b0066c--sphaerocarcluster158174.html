Etymology

Sphaerocarpaceae breaks into sphaero- 'sphere' or 'round', carp- 'fruit', and the family ending -aceae. So it means a 'round-fruited family' and is the name for a group of liverwort plants that often have round, fruit-like parts, which helps you remember the word.
