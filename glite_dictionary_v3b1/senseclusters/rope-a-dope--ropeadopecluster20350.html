Etymology

rope-a-dope comes from boxing and became famous after Muhammad Ali used it in the 'Rumble in the Jungle', when he leaned on the rope to let his opponent tire himself out. The dope part is old slang for 'fool', so rope-a-dope is a trick of 'pretending to be weak' to make the other person waste energy, which is why we now use it to mean outsmarting someone by appearing passive.
