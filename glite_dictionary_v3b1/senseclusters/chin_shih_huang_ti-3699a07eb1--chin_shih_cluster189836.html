Etymology

Ch'in Shih Huang Ti's name shows his role: Ch'in was his state and Shih Huang Ti means 'First Emperor'. He gave himself this title after he joined many small kingdoms into one country and made common rules and walls, so the name reminds us that he was the first ruler to unite China.
