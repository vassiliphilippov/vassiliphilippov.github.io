Etymology

Pantalone comes from an old Italian name, from the saint Pantaleon, and it became the name of a comic stage figure who wore tight trousers called pantaloons. So Pantalone now means the familiar silly, stingy old man from those plays, and this is why English also used 'pantaloon' for an old-fashioned, foolish man.
