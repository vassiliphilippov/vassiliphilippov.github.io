Etymology

The noun brain truster comes from the 1930s name brain trust, made of brain (clever people) and trust (people you rely on); it named a small group of advisers the president 'trusted' for ideas. So a brain truster is someone who gives expert advice and is 'trusted' to help make big decisions.
