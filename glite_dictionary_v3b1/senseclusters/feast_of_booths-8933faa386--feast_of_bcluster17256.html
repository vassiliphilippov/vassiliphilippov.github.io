Etymology

Feast of Booths is a Jewish festival called Sukkot that remembers when the Israelites lived in simple huts for 40 years in the desert. People still build temporary shelters, or booths, to 'remember' that journey and to 'give thanks', so the holiday means a time of joy and thanks.
