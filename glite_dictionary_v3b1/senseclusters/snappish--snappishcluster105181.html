Etymology

The word snappish comes from snap, a short, sharp sound or quick action, and the ending -ish, which means 'somewhat' or 'tending to'. Think of someone 'snapping' when annoyed; their words are short and sharp, so snappish means being quick to speak in an annoyed or rude way.
