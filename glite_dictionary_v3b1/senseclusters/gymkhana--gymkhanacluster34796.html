Etymology

gymkhana comes from South Asia; its part khana means 'house' or 'place' in Persian and related languages and was used for clubs and meeting places in British India. So a gymkhana became the name for a sports meeting, often with horse games and races, which is why today it means a riding event with skill competitions.
