Etymology

Erse comes from old Gaelic names for Ireland like Éire and Erin, which mean 'Ireland' or 'Irish'. So Erse came to mean the Gaelic language spoken in Ireland and later in parts of Scotland, which is why it refers to Irish or Scottish Gaelic.
