Etymology

Caesalpinia coriaria comes from a person's name and a Latin word. The first part, Caesalpinia, honors the Italian botanist Cesalpino, and the second part, coriaria, means 'for leather' because people used the tree's pods to tan and make leather, so the name points to what the tree was used for.
