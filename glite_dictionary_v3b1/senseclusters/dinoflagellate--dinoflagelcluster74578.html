Etymology

dinoflagellate comes from two parts: dino- meaning 'whirling' and flagellate from a word for 'whip' and flagellum meaning 'little tail'. These tiny sea cells often spin and move with whip-like tails, so the name helps you remember their spinning motion and tail-like flagella.
