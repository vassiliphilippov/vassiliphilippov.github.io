Etymology

The scientific name Hibiscus esculentus comes from Latin and can be read as 'edible hibiscus': Hibiscus is the same plant group as the garden hibiscus and esculentus means 'edible'. So the name points to the plant we call 'okra', because it is a hibiscus-like plant with green pods people eat.
