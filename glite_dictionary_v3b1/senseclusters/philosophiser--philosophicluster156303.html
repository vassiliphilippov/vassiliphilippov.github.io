Etymology

philosophiser comes from Greek parts philo- meaning 'love' and sophia meaning 'wisdom', and these are the same roots in philosopher and philosophy. So a philosophiser is someone who 'loves wisdom' and likes to think deeply about ideas and the meaning behind things.
