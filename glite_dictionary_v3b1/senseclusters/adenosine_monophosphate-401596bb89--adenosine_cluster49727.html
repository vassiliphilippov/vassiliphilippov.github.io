Etymology

adenosine monophosphate is named from its parts: adenosine (a small molecule) and monophosphate meaning 'one phosphate'. Think of it as a tiny battery with only one power unit. Cells can change it into ADP or ATP to give or store energy, so the name points to a small molecule important for cellular energy.
