Etymology

The word Raphidae comes from the genus name Raphus plus the scientific ending -idae, which marks an animal 'family' and can be thought of like a last name that groups related animals. It was the name for a former family of extinct, flightless island birds, including the dodo, so Raphidae points to that group of birds.
