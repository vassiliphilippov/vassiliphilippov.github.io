Etymology

tropical year gets its name from the word tropical, which comes from an old word for 'turn' because people saw the Sun seem to 'turn' at the tropics. The tropical year is the time it takes the Sun to come back to the same point in the 'seasons', so the name shows this year is tied to our seasons.
