Etymology

bubbler comes from the word bubble and the ending -er, so it originally meant 'one that makes bubbles'. In parts of the United States, especially Wisconsin, a public drinking fountain was called a bubbler because the water often 'bubbles' up from the spout, and so the word now means a place to get a drink.
