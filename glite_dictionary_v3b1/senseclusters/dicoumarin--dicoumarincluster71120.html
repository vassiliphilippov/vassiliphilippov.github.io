Etymology

dicoumarin comes from the prefix di- meaning 'two' and coumarin, a plant chemical first found in the tonka bean, so the name means 'two coumarin parts'. So, think of it as two plant units joined together — this compound was noticed in spoiled sweet clover and is known for preventing blood from clotting.
