Etymology

The word muezzin comes from the Arabic mu'adhdhin, which means 'the one who calls' and is formed from a verb meaning 'to call'. So a muezzin is the person who calls or announces the prayer from the mosque tower, which is why you hear their voice across the neighbourhood at prayer times.
