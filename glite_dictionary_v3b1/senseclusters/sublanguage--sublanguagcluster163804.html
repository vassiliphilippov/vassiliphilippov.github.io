Etymology

sublanguage is built from sub- meaning 'under' or 'part of' and language meaning 'words and grammar', and the sub- is the same in familiar words like submarine. So a sublanguage is a smaller, special part of a language used by a field or group, which is why medical or programming language can seem different from everyday speech.
