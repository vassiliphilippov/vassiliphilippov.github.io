Etymology

Cicuta verosa may come from old Latin words. The first part, Cicuta, was used for a water plant that could poison people, and verosa means 'true', so the name gives a clear picture of a real poisonous marsh plant and explains why it refers to a tall, deadly herb found in wet places.
