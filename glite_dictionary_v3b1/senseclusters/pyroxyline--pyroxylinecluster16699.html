Etymology

The word pyroxyline comes from Greek parts pyro- meaning 'fire' and xylon meaning 'wood'. That image helps because pyroxyline is a very flammable treated form of cellulose, the substance found in wood and cotton, so the name is like 'fire-wood' and explains why it was used in collodion, plastics, and lacquers and why it needed careful storage.
