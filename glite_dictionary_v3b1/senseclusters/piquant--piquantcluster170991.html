Etymology

Piquant comes from the old French verb piquer, which means 'to prick' or 'to sting', and is like the English word pique which means 'interest'. A 'piquant' remark gives a small, pleasant 'sting' to the mind, so it means lively, clever, or pleasantly surprising.
