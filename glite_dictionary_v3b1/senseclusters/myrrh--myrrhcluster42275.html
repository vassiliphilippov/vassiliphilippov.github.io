Etymology

myrrh comes from old Arabic and related words like murr, meaning 'bitter', because people long ago used that strong, sticky resin and found its taste and smell sharp. So, myrrh today still means the fragrant, slightly bitter resin that was burned as incense, used in perfumes and medicine, and even given as a sacred gift.
