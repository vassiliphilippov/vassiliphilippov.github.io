Etymology

bimli hemp likely comes from a local East Indies name for a tough plant, with bimli as the local word and hemp the familiar fiber plant. Traders and farmers kept that name for the plant that gave 'strong fibers', so today bimli hemp means a valuable plant used to make rope and cloth.
