Etymology

Regnellidium diphyllum is named after the plant scientist Regnell (that is Regnellidium) and the part diphyllum, which comes from words for 'two' and 'leaf'. So the name gives a simple image of a plant with split, two-part leaves, which helps explain and remember this small aquatic fern.
