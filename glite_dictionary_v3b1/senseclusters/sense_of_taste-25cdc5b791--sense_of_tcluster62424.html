Etymology

The noun sense of taste combines the word sense, meaning an ability, and taste, meaning what your tongue notices. Imagine a lemon on your tongue and you quickly know it is 'sour', so the phrase means the ability to tell flavors like 'sweet', 'sour', 'bitter', or 'salty'.
