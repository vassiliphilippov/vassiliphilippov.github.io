Etymology

calabura is a Spanish name used in many tropical places for the Muntingia tree that gives small, sweet red fruit and has bark used to make strong, silky rope. A helpful way to remember it is that the name sounds a bit like Spanish calabaza ('gourd'), but you can think of calabura as the little 'cherry' tree of the tropics.
