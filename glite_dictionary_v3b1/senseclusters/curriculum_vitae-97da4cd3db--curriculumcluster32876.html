Etymology

The noun curriculum vitae comes from the Latin words curriculum, meaning 'course', and vitae, meaning 'of life', so it originally meant 'course of life'. Think of it as a list that runs through your education, skills and jobs, and that's why the phrase now names the document people send with job applications.
