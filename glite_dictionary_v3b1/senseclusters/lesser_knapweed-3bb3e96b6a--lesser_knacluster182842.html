Etymology

lesser knapweed may come from an old word for knap meaning a small rounded 'knob', because the plant's tight, round flower heads look like little bumps. The word lesser means 'smaller' and weed shows it is a wild plant, so the name says 'a smaller wild plant with knobbly flower heads'.
