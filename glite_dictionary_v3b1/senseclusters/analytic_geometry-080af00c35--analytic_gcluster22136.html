Etymology

The compound noun analytic geometry comes from René Descartes, who joined analytic, meaning thinking with numbers, and geometry, meaning the study of shapes, by using a number grid to draw lines and curves. So, analytic geometry means 'using algebra and a coordinate grid to study shapes'.
