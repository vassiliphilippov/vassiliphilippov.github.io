Etymology

Prosecuting attorney comes from two simple ideas: prosecuting comes from an old word meaning 'to follow' a case, and attorney means a person chosen 'to act for' someone. So a prosecuting attorney is literally someone who follows a criminal case for the government, which is why it means an official who brings charges.
