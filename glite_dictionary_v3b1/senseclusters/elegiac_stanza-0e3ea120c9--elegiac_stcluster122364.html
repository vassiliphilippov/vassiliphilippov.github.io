Etymology

elegiac stanza comes from the word elegiac, which comes from 'elegy' meaning a sad song or poem, and stanza, which means a group of lines. It is a short, four-line pattern poets used to show 'loss' or 'mourning', so the name helps you remember it is for sad poems.
