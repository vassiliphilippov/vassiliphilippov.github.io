Etymology

Aesculapian tradition comes from Aesculapian, a word linked to Aesculapius, the ancient god of medicine whose symbol was a staff with a snake. That image stood for doctors and their old healing ways, so today Aesculapian tradition means a system of 'medicine' or 'healing' based on those old practices.
