Etymology

The word curvilineal comes from two parts: curvi-, from Latin curvus meaning 'bent' and like English curve, and -lineal, from Latin linea meaning 'line' and like line or linear. So curvilineal describes a shape or path that follows a 'bent' or curved line rather than a straight one.
