Etymology

Chimonanthus praecox comes from old Greek and Latin words: Chimonanthus joins chimon ('winter') and anthus ('flower'), and praecox means 'early'. So the name paints the picture of an 'early winter flower', which fits this shrub that produces fragrant yellow blooms in the cold months.
