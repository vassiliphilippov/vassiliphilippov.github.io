Etymology

neritid gastropod comes from neritid, based on the old name 'Nerita' used for small sea snails, and gastropod, a word that literally means 'stomach-foot' because the snail moves on a broad foot under its body. So the name helps you remember it is a 'sea snail' with a short spiral shell that can close itself with a hard plate.
