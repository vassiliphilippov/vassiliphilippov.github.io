Etymology

coronary thrombosis is a medical term that comes from the parts coronary, meaning the arteries that circle the heart, and thrombosis, meaning a clot. Imagine a plug blocking one of the heart's small tubes — that's why the term means 'a blood clot blocking a heart artery' and can cause a 'heart attack'.
