Etymology

Staffordshire terrier comes from Staffordshire, an area in England where people bred strong bull-and-terrier dogs long ago; the word terrier shows the dog 'type' and Staffordshire shows the 'place'. That's why the name now means a muscular, loyal American dog breed.
