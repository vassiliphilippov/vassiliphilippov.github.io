Etymology

The noun parabolic reflector comes from a curved dish, where parabolic means the bowl shape and reflector is something that bounces rays. The dish makes rays meet at a single spot called the 'focus' or sends them out as a straight beam, so a parabolic reflector is used to focus light, sound, or radio waves.
