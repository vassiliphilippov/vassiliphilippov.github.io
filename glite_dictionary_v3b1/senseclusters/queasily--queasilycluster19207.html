Etymology

queasily comes from queasy, which means 'sick' or 'uneasy', and the ending -ly turns it into 'in a sick or uneasy way'. A helpful image is someone holding their stomach and smiling nervously — they move or look queasily, which is why the word describes a feeling of nausea or discomfort.
