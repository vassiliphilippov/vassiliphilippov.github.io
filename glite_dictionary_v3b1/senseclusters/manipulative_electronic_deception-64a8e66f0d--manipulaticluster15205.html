Etymology

manipulative electronic deception creates a simple picture: manipulative means changing, electronic means electronic signals, and deception means tricking someone, so imagine sending false signals to a radar to hide a real ship. That's why it means 'to hide or alter electronic signals in order to mislead an enemy or prevent the enemy from detecting sensitive operations'.
