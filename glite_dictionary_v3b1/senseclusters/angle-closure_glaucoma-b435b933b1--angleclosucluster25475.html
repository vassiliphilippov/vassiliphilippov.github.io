Etymology

Angle-closure glaucoma comes from a simple picture: the front of the eye has a small angle where fluid normally drains. If the colored part, the iris, moves to close that angle, the fluid is trapped and 'eye pressure' rises quickly, so the name refers to this sudden and serious type of glaucoma.
