Etymology

Paki-bashing joins the rude short form Paki, which comes from the word 'Pakistani', and bashing, from the verb 'to bash' meaning 'to hit or attack'. So, Paki-bashing means violent attacks, abuse, or discrimination aimed at people from Pakistan.
