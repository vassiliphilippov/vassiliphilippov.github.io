Etymology

Great balls of fire is an old exclamation whose words great, balls, and fire paint a loud picture of huge burning balls, like fireworks, and a popular 1950s song helped make the phrase famous. That's why people say Great balls of fire today to show strong 'surprise' or 'excitement'.
