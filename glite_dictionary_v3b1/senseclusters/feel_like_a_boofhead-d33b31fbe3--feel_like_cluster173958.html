Etymology

feel like a boofhead comes from Australian slang; a boofhead is a playful word for a clumsy or silly person. One popular story says boof copies the soft 'bump' sound of a small accident, so a boofhead gives the image of someone who seems to have 'bumped' their head, and that's why feel like a boofhead means 'to feel foolish' after a mistake.
