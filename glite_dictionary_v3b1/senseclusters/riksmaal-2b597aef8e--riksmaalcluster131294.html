Etymology

Riksmaal comes from the Norwegian parts rik ('state' or 'kingdom') and mål ('speech' or 'language'), so it literally means 'the state's language'. A helpful way to remember this is that the name explains its role: Riksmaal was the official written standard of Norwegian before it was renamed Bokmål.
