Etymology

reentering polygon names a many-sided shape that folds back into itself: some corners point inward instead of outward. Here reentering suggests the sides 're-enter' the shape and polygon means a many-sided figure, so the term means a polygon with one or more angles larger than a straight line, more than 180 degrees.
