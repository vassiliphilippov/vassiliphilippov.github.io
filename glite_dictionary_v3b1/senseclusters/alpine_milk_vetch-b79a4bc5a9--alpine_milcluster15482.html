Etymology

alpine milk vetch is a simple name that tells you about the plant: alpine means it grows in high mountains, milk comes from an old use as food for cows that helped them give more milk, and vetch is the common name for small pea-like plants. So the name points to a mountain plant once used to help make 'milk'.
