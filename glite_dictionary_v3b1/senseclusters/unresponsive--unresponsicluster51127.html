Etymology

The word unresponsive comes from the parts un- 'not' and respond 'to answer', like the word response, so a helpful way to remember it is to imagine someone who will not 'answer' with words or interest. That's why unresponsive describes a person who does not reply, react, or show interest in questions or requests.
