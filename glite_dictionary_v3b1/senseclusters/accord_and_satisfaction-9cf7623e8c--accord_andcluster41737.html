Etymology

accord and satisfaction comes from the simple idea of a deal: one person makes an accord (an agreement) and the other accepts a smaller payment as satisfaction (a payment that ends the debt). So, if the person owed money accepts that smaller payment, they 'give up' the right to ask for more and the debt is finished.
