Etymology

savings bank trust comes from the idea of a savings bank (a safe account for money) and a trust (one person keeping money for someone else). In this arrangement a person keeps control of the account while alive and the money goes to the named 'beneficiary' after their death, so the term means 'an account held for the person who will get the funds'.
