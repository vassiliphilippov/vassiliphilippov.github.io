Etymology

unscrupulousness comes from un- meaning 'not' and scruple, a word for a hesitation about what is 'right and wrong' that originally meant a 'small sharp stone' that makes you stop. So unscrupulousness is the quality of having no hesitation about right and wrong, acting without shame.
