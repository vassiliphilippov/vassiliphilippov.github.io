Etymology

Anthocerotaceae comes from the plant name Anthoceros, which is made of Greek parts anthos 'flower' and ceros 'horn' because the plant's spore-making part looks like a small horn. The ending -aceae shows it is a plant family, so Anthocerotaceae is the family of 'horn-flower' plants, the hornworts.
