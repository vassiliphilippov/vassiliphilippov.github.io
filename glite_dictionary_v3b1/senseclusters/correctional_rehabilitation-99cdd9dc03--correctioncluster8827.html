Etymology

Correctional rehabilitation comes from two simple words: correctional meaning 'to correct' bad behavior and rehabilitation meaning 'to help someone rebuild skills and life'. Picture a prison classroom where people learn jobs and get help to change; that's why the phrase means the programs that help people who committed crimes 'reform' and rejoin society.
