Etymology

white walnut gets its name from trees with lighter-coloured wood and nuts than other walnuts, where the word white points to the lighter colour and walnut names the tree. People also used the husks to make a 'light brown' dye and ate the nuts, so the name helps you remember a walnut with lighter wood and edible nuts.
