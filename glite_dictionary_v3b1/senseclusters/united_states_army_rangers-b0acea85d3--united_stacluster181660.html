Etymology

United States Army Rangers comes from the old word ranger, meaning someone who 'ranges' — moves over a wide area to scout or patrol. Long ago, small groups of rangers moved fast through forests to find the enemy, so today the Rangers are elite soldiers trained for hard, high-risk missions.
