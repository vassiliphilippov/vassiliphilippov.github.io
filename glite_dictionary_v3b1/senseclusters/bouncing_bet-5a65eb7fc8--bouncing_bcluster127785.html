Etymology

bouncing Bet gets its name from the plant's soap-like leaves. One popular story says people would rub or beat the leaves and they made suds or bubbles that seemed to bounce, while Bet was a common woman's name, so the friendly name stuck and now the phrase means a plant used as a 'natural soap'.
