Etymology

The phrase sharp sting comes from the clear image of something pointed, like a bee's sting or a nettle, quickly pricking the skin. The word sharp shows it is sudden and strong and the word sting shows the quick prick, so sharp sting now means 'a sudden, intense pain'.
