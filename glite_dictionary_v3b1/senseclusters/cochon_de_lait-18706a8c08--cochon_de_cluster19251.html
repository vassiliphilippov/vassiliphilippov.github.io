Etymology

cochon de lait literally means 'milk pig'; cochon is pig and lait is milk. It refers to very young piglets still drinking their mother's milk, and because these tender, milk-fed piglets were often roasted whole for special meals, the phrase now means a whole young pig prepared for roasting.
