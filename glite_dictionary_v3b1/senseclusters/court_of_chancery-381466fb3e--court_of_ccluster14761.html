Etymology

The court of chancery gets its name from the chancellor, a royal official who worked in the chancery, an office full of records and seals where people asked for special help. People went there when the regular courts were too strict and they wanted a fair decision, so the court became the place for 'equity' or 'fairness' rather than strict law.
