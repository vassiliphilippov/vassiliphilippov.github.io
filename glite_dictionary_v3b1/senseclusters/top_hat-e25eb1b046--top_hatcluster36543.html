Etymology

top hat grew from a simple image: a very tall, flat top on a man's hat, because in the 1800s men wore high, stiff hats of silk or felt to look important at 'formal occasions'. That's why today top hat means 'a tall, formal hat' and is the kind of hat you still see at weddings, galas, or in magic shows.
