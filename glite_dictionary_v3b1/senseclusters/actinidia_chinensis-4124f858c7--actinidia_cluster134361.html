Etymology

Actinidia chinensis is the scientific name for the climbing vine that gives us the 'kiwifruit'. The first part, Actinidia, is the plant group name and the second part, chinensis, means 'from China', so the name literally says 'kiwifruit plant from China', which helps you remember it is the kiwifruit vine.
