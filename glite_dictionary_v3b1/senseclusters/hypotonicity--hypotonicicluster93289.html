Etymology

Hypotonicity comes from hypo- meaning 'less' and tonic meaning 'tone' or 'tension', the same 'tone' you know in the word tone. So in medicine it means 'less' muscle tone — the muscles feel softer or looser than normal, which is why doctors call it hypotonicity.
