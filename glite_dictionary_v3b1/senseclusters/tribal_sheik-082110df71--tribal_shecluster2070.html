Etymology

tribal sheik comes from the words tribal, from the word 'tribe', and sheik, an Arabic word for an older man who led a group. Picture an elder sitting in a tent while villagers come to him for advice and decisions, and that's why tribal sheik means 'the leader' or 'chief' of a tribe or village.
