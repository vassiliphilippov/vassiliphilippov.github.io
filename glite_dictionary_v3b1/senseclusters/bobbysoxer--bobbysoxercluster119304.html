Etymology

bobbysoxer comes from the short ankle socks called bobby socks that many teenage girls wore in the 1940s and 1950s. The -er ending means 'one who', so a bobbysoxer was literally 'one who wears bobby socks' and came to mean a young fan of popular music and fashion.
