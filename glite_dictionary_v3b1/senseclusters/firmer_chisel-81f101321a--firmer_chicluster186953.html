Etymology

firmer chisel probably comes from the word firmer meaning 'more firm' or 'stronger' and chisel the cutting tool, so picture a solid, straight blade you can push without it bending. That's why firmer chisel refers to a chisel with a thin, flat blade used for careful, precise shaping of wood.
