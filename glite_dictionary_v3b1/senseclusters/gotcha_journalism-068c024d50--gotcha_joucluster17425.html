Etymology

gotcha journalism comes from the casual word gotcha, short for 'got you', something you say when you have caught someone making a mistake. The image is a reporter waiting to surprise a guest and shout 'gotcha', so the term means reporting that tries to catch people off guard and expose their mistakes.
