Etymology

ZFLOPS combines the SI prefix zetta, meaning '10^21' or 'one sextillion', with FLOPS, short for 'floating-point operations per second'. So, ZFLOPS names a computer speed that can do one sextillion floating-point operations every second, much like kilo tells you there are 1,000 of something.
