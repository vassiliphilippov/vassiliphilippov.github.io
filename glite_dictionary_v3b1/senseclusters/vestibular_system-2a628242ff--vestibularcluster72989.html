Etymology

vestibular system comes from the old word 'vestibule', meaning a small entrance or hall, and the parts vestibular and system point to a group of parts in the inner ear. Inside the ear, tiny fluid-filled chambers and little stones move when you turn or tilt, and those movements tell the brain about your 'balance', so the name links those 'little halls' to the idea of keeping steady.
