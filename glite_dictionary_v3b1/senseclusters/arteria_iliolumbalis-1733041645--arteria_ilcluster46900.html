Etymology

arteria iliolumbalis is a Latin name made of parts: arteria means 'artery', a blood vessel, ilio points to the ilium or pelvis, and lumbalis means 'lower back'. So the name shows an artery near the pelvis and lower back that 'carries blood to' pelvic muscles and bones.
