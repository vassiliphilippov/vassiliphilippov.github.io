Etymology

tree of heaven may come from an old Chinese name that called it a tree from heaven, because it grows very fast and reaches high into the sky. Because it can live in poor soil and even polluted city places, people saw it as 'heaven-sent', so the name helps us remember this tough, fast-growing tree.
