Etymology

coprostanol comes from the Greek root copro- meaning 'dung' and the ending -stanol, which shows it is a fat-like chemical related to cholesterol. So coprostanol is a 'dung' form of cholesterol made during digestion, and that is why scientists test for it to find human sewage.
