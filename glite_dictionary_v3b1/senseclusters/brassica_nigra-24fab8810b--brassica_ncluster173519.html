Etymology

Brassica nigra is a scientific Latin name: Brassica means a cabbage-like plant and nigra means 'black', giving the image of a mustard plant with dark seeds. That's why the plant is called 'black mustard' and is grown for its strong dark seeds used to make table mustard.
