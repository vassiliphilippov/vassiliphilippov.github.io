Etymology

derogative comes from the parts de- ('down' or 'away') and rogare ('to ask' or 'propose'), and it once carried the idea of 'taking away'. So a derogative remark 'takes away' or lowers someone's respect, which is why it means a comment that belittles someone, and you can spot the same rog root in interrogate ('to ask').
