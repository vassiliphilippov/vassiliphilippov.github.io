Etymology

hare's-foot bristle fern comes from the look of the plant's creeping stems, which are covered in soft hairs. The part hare's-foot suggests these hairs look like a rabbit's paw and bristle points to the small stiff hairs, so the name helps you picture the fern's 'soft, hairy underground stems'.
