Etymology

Catharacta skua may come from older words: Catharacta meaning 'waterfall' or a fast rush, and skua a local word for a 'bold sea bird'. People imagined the bird diving and grabbing food, so the name fits this large brown seabird known for being aggressive and stealing food.
