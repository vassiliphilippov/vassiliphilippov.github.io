Etymology

the Flood in the Halo games is a dangerous alien infection that spreads fast and turns living things into parts of itself; the name uses the image of a rising tide of water to show how it 'overwhelms' everything. So in Halo, the Flood means a fast, all-covering infection that takes over worlds.
