Etymology

equivocal comes from Latin parts aequi- meaning 'equal' and voc- meaning 'voice', the same root you see in vocal. So, it originally meant 'equal voice', and today equivocal describes words or statements that can be read in more than one way, making them unclear or sometimes meant to mislead.
