Etymology

modern-day fakir comes from the old word fakir, which named a poor holy person who lived with almost nothing, often sitting on the ground, praying for a long time, or doing hard acts to show their faith. So calling someone a modern-day fakir today means they are seen as 'living simply' or acting like a modern holy person.
