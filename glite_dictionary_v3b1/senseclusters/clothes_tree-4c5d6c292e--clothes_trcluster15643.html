Etymology

The noun clothes tree comes from its shape: a stand with pegs that look like the branches of a tree, where people hang clothes, hats, or umbrellas. So the picture of a small tree for hanging items helps you remember why it means 'a freestanding upright pole with pegs or hooks for hanging clothes, hats, or umbrellas'.
