Etymology

unseeded is made of un- meaning 'not' and seed meaning 'seed', like the small plant part you put in the ground. In sports, to 'seed' someone is to place them in the draw because they are expected to do well, so an unseeded player is not given that ranking and is seen as less likely to win.
