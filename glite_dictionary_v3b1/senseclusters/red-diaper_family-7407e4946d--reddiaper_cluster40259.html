Etymology

red-diaper family comes from the image of red, the color linked to 'communist' politics, and diaper, a baby's cloth, so it gives the picture of a child born into a 'red' home. So, it means 'a family whose parents hold strong leftist or communist beliefs and raise their children with those ideas'.
