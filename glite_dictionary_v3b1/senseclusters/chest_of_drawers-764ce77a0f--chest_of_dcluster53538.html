Etymology

The compound noun chest of drawers comes from a simple idea: a chest was a large box and drawers were the sliding boxes inside it. So the name gives a clear picture of a box with sliding parts used 'to store clothes', which is why it means 'a piece of furniture with drawers for storing clothes'.
