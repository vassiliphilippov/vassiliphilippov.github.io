Etymology

order Actinaria combines order, meaning a 'group' of related animals, and Actinaria, from an old Greek word for 'rays'. The name comes from the many tentacles of sea anemones that spread out like rays, so order Actinaria means the group of animals with ray-like tentacles, the sea anemones.
