Etymology

The name tineoid moth may come from the old word tinea, used for tiny moths that 'eat cloth', so imagine a small, dull moth nibbling a sweater. That's why tineoid moth now refers to small, plain-colored moths that often have chewing mouths instead of a long straw-like mouth.
