Etymology

Latimeria chalumnae is named for Marjorie Courtenay-Latimer, the museum worker who first showed scientists the fish, and for the nearby 'Chalumna' River where it was found, so Latimeria honors the finder and chalumnae points to the place. That's why the name refers to the rare living 'coelacanth' that was long 'thought extinct' until 1938.
