Etymology

The noun little yus comes from old Slavic alphabets, where a small letter called yus showed a 'nose' vowel, a vowel made partly through the nose. It was called 'little' to tell it apart from a larger nasal letter called 'big yus', so today little yus means that old letter and the nasal sound it marked.
