Etymology

Monophysite comes from the Greek parts mono- 'one' and physis 'nature'. Think of monologue which means 'one speech' and physical which means 'nature' or 'body' to help you remember. So, as an adjective it describes a belief or church that says Christ has only one, divine nature, not both divine and human.
