Etymology

linger too long comes from the simple picture of someone who stays behind after others leave, like missing the last bus because you lingered at the stop. The word linger means 'to stay' and too long means 'for longer than is a good idea', so the phrase means 'to remain in a place or situation longer than is a good idea'.
