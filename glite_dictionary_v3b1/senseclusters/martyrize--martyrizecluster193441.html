Etymology

martyrize comes from the word martyr, which originally came from a Greek word meaning 'witness' and later described someone who suffers or dies for a belief, and the ending -ize means 'to make'. So to martyrize someone is to treat or hurt them as if to make them a martyr, often to punish or frighten others.
