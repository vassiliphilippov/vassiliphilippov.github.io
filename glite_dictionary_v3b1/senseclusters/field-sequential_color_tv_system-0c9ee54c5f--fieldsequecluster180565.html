Etymology

field-sequential color TV system gets its name from how early color TVs sent pictures: the set sent each color as a separate field, and the word sequential simply means 'in order' — red, then green, then blue — so the screen and our eyes mixed them into a full image. That's why the name literally describes 'fields one after another' and explains how the system worked.
