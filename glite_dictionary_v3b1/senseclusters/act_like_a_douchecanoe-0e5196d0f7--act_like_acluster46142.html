Etymology

act like a douchecanoe may come from joining a rude insult and a small boat: douche (a rude person) and canoe (a small boat), which creates a silly picture of someone being loud or annoying in a tiny boat. That silly image helps explain why it means 'to behave in a foolish, obnoxious, or very irritating way'.
