Etymology

tortrix comes from the Latin root torqu- meaning 'to twist' and the ending -trix meaning 'female who does something'. That's why the name fits: the tortrix caterpillar twists and rolls leaves to make a shelter, so it is like a 'leaf-twister' that can damage crops and fruit.
