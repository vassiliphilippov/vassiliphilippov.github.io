Etymology

diffusiveness comes from Latin parts dif- 'apart' and fundere 'to pour', so think of something 'poured out' and spread everywhere, the same idea you see in diffuse. That's why diffusiveness can mean how much something (like data, light, or a substance) is spread out over a large area.
