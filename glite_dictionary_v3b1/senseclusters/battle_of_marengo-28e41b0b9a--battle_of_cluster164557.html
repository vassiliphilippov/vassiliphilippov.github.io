Etymology

Battle of Marengo was fought near the village of Marengo in northern Italy in 1800, when Napoleon's army beat the Austrians. Because this quick and important victory gave France control of the area, the name Battle of Marengo is remembered as a 'turning point' in Napoleon's campaign.
