Etymology

Demogorgon is a name from medieval stories for a mysterious and terrifying god of the underworld. A helpful way to remember it is to see it as a mix of demon ('evil spirit') and Gorgon ('terrible monster'), so the name brings to mind a hidden, frightening power.
