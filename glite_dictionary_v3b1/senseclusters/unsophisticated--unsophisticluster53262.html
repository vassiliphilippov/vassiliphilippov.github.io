Etymology

unsophisticated starts with un- meaning 'not' and ends with sophisticated, which comes from an old Greek root sophos meaning 'wise' and appears in words like philosophy. So unsophisticated describes someone who is 'not worldly or polished', which is why it means lacking experience or refinement.
