Etymology

The noun wild orange names a small tree with sweet-smelling flowers and orange-like fruit that is usually bitter and 'not for eating'. The name comes from the simple picture: wild because it grows on its own and orange because the fruit looks like an orange, and farmers often use it to help grow other orange trees.
