Etymology

Decumaria is a plant name you can remember by its parts: think of decumb- (as in 'decumbent', meaning 'to lean' or 'to lie down') and the ending -aria which often means 'connected with' in names. So Decumaria suggests a plant that leans on and clings to walls or trees with sticky roots.
