Etymology

The word burningly comes from the simple verb burn, which means 'to be on fire' or feel a strong heat. The ending -ing makes it 'full of' that feeling and -ly turns it into a word that describes how something is done, so doing something burningly is doing it 'in a very strong or intense way'.
