Etymology

Hatiora gaertneri is a scientific plant name made of two parts: Hatiora the group name and gaertneri which means 'of Gaertner'. One popular story says the cactus was named for a gardener or plant collector called Gaertner, so think of it as 'Gaertner's Hatiora' and remember its coral-red spring flowers.
