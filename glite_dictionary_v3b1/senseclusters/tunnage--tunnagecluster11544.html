Etymology

tunnage comes from the old word tun, which meant a 'large barrel' for wine, and ports used to charge a fee for each tun a ship carried. Over time that idea moved to tonnage (and the word ton as a measure of size), so tunnage came to mean a 'tax' or charge based on a ship's size.
