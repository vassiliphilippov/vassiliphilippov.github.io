Etymology

triangulator comes from tri- 'three' and angle, like triangle, with -ator meaning 'one who'. A triangulator can be a person who uses points or ideas from different sides to find a position or gain advantage, so the word shows someone who 'builds' or uses triangles to reach a goal.
