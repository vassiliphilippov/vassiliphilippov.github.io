Etymology

Rameses II may come from Egyptian words that include the god Ra and a word meaning 'born of', so his name can be thought of as 'born of Ra'. He was a famous 'pharaoh' meaning 'king' who built huge temples and led armies, so today his name reminds us of a powerful Egyptian ruler.
