Etymology

ferrocyanic acid comes from two parts, ferro meaning 'iron' and cyanic from 'cyan', a dark blue color from a dye used long ago. Chemists named the acid made from iron-containing cyanide salts ferrocyanic acid, so the word tells you it is an acid from those blue iron salts even though the acid itself is unstable.
