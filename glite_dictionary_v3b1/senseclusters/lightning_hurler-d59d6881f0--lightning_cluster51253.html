Etymology

Lightning Hurler paints the picture of someone throwing bolts from the sky: lightning is the bright bolt and a hurler is one who throws. In Roman stories, Jupiter was seen as the god who did this, so calling him the Lightning Hurler means he 'brings lightning' and shows his great power.
