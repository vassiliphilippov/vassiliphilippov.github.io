Etymology

Iambic trimeter comes from the words iamb, a two-syllable beat that goes weak then strong like 'da-DUM', and trimeter, meaning three of those beats in a line. So, iambic trimeter means a line of poetry with three 'da-DUM' beats, giving a quick, steady rhythm.
