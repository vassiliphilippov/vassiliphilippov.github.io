Etymology

Psidium littorale is the scientific name for a small tropical shrub that makes sweet, yellow fruit often called 'strawberry guava' or 'Cattley guava'. Think of Psidium as the guava group and littorale as meaning 'by the shore', so the name helps you remember a little South American guava tree that bears fragrant fruit.
