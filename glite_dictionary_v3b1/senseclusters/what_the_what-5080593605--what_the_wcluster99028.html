Etymology

what the what is a modern, playful way to show strong 'surprise' or 'confusion'. One popular idea is that it comes from people starting to say 'what the...' like 'what the hell' and then stopping and repeating what to show they are shocked or unsure, so the double what feels like an echo. So, the phrase is used when someone is very surprised or cannot believe something.
