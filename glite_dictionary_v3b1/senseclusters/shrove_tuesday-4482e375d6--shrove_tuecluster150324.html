Etymology

The name Shrove Tuesday comes from the old word shrove, from 'shrive' meaning 'to confess', because people went to church to confess before 'Lent'. It was also the last day to use up eggs, milk and butter before the fast, so many people made pancakes, which is why we still eat pancakes on that day.
