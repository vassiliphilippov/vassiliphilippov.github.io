Etymology

The word curviness comes from the simple word curve meaning 'bend' and the ending -ness meaning 'quality', so it describes the quality of having smooth, rounded shapes. That's why we speak of the curviness of a sculpture or a person's body to mean the rounded, bending lines they have.
