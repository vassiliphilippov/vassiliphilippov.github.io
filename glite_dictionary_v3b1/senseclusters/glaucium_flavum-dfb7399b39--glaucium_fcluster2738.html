Etymology

Glaucium flavum comes from old words that describe how the plant looks: Glaucium refers to 'blue-green' leaves and flavum means 'yellow' for its bright flowers. So the name gives a simple picture—a seaside plant with grey-blue leaves and yellow blooms, which is why people call it Glaucium flavum today.
