Etymology

flip shit creates a sudden, messy image: flip means to turn over quickly and shit adds a rude, strong feeling. One popular story says it comes from older sayings like 'flip your lid' about a head suddenly snapping, so the phrase means 'to lose your temper' very suddenly.
