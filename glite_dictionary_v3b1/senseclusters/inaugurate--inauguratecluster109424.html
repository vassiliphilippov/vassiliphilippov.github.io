Etymology

inaugurate comes from Latin ritual words: in- meaning 'in' and augurare meaning 'to take omens' or 'to ask the gods', because Romans used priests to read signs before starting something important. So to inaugurate today is to officially begin or introduce something, often with a ceremony.
