Etymology

anti-bullying campaign paints a clear picture: anti means 'against', bullying is the hurtful behaviour, and a campaign is people working together, so imagine teachers and students putting up posters and standing between a bully and a victim to 'stop' bullying. So, the phrase means an organized effort to prevent and deal with bullying.
