Etymology

Vitamin B1 comes from early scientists who called important nutrients vitamins and gave numbers to those in the B group, so the first was named B1 and later called thiamine. That's why Vitamin B1 now means the specific nutrient the body needs to make energy and to prevent the disease 'beriberi'.
