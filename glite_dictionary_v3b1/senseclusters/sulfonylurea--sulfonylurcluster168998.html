Etymology

sulfonylurea comes from two parts: sulfonyl, which is related to 'sulfur', and urea, a simple chemical often found in 'urine'. So, think of sulfonylurea as a medicine made of a sulfur-like part and a urea-like part, which helps you remember it is a group of drugs that lower blood sugar in people with type 2 diabetes.
