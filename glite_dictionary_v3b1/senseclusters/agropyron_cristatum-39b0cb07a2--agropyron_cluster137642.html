Etymology

Agropyron cristatum comes from old words where Agropyron means 'field wheat' and cristatum means 'crested', so the name gives the image of a wheat-like grass with a small crest. Because this grass is strong and helps hold soil, people plant Agropyron cristatum to feed animals and to stop soil loss, so the name helps you remember it is a hardy field grass that protects the land.
