Etymology

The word di-iodotyrosine breaks into di- 'two', iodo from iodine which means 'violet', and tyrosine, a kind of amino acid first found in cheese. So, di-iodotyrosine is a tyrosine with 'two' iodine atoms attached, which explains its role in making thyroid hormones.
