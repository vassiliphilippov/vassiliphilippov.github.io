Etymology

mastic tree gets its name from the sticky resin called mastic that the tree makes. People in the Mediterranean scraped the bark and collected small drops that looked like 'tears' and chewed them like gum, so today mastic tree means the evergreen shrub that produces aromatic resin used in food and as chewing gum.
