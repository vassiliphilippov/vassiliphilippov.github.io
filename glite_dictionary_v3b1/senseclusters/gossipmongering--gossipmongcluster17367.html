Etymology

gossipmongering comes from gossip, which used to mean a 'close friend' but later came to mean talking about people's private lives, and monger, as in fishmonger, meaning someone who 'sells' something. So gossipmongering is like 'selling' gossip — spreading personal or shocking stories about others, often in a harmful or careless way.
