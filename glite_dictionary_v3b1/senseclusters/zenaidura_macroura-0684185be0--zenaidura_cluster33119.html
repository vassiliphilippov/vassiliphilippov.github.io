Etymology

Zenaidura macroura was named for a woman and for its 'long tail': Zenaidura from 'Zenaida' and macroura meaning 'long tail'. People call it the 'mourning dove' because its soft, sad coo sounds like someone mourning. So the name helps you remember a gentle, long-tailed bird with a mournful call.
