Etymology

The noun capacitor mike comes from the small electric part called a capacitor inside the microphone, made of two metal plates with a thin moving plate between them that moves when sound hits it. That's why a capacitor mike is 'sensitive' and used to pick up quiet, clear sounds for high-quality recordings.
