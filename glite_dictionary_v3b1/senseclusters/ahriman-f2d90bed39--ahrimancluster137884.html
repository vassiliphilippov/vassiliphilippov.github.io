Etymology

Ahriman comes from an ancient Persian name Angra Mainyu, which breaks into angra meaning 'destruction' and mainyu meaning 'spirit'. So Ahriman literally means a 'destructive spirit', which explains why he is the dark, evil enemy in Zoroastrian stories.
