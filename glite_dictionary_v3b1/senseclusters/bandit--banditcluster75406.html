Etymology

bandit comes from the Italian word bandito, which once meant 'sent away' or 'put outside the law'. A helpful way to remember it is to think of the English words ban ('forbid') and band ('group'): a bandit is someone outside the law who often joins a band of robbers, which explains the modern meaning 'armed robber'.
