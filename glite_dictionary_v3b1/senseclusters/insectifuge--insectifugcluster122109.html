Etymology

insectifuge comes from the parts insect- ('insect') and -fuge ('to flee' or 'drive away'). Think of a fugitive, a person who runs away, because it uses the same 'flee' idea. So an insectifuge is something that makes insects 'flee' or go away, which is why it means a chemical that repels insects.
