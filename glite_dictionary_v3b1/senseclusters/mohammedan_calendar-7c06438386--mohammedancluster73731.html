Etymology

Mohammedan calendar comes from older English that links the name Muhammad with the word calendar. It was used to mean the 'moon-based calendar' used by 'Muslims', which begins in the year he left Mecca (622), so the name points to that Islamic year system. Today people usually say 'Islamic calendar' instead of this older name.
