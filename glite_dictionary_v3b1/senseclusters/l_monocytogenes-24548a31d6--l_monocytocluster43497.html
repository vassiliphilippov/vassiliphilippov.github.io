Etymology

L. monocytogenes gets its name from the group Listeria, named for surgeon Joseph Lister, and from the parts mono ('one') + cyto ('cell') + genes ('making'). It was named because this bacterium often affects a type of single white blood cell called a monocyte, so the name points to the bacteria and how it acts in the body.
