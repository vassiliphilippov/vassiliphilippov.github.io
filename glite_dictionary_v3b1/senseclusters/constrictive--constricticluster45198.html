Etymology

constrictive comes from the parts con- ('together') and strict- from an old Latin word meaning 'to pull tight', the same idea you can see in the simple English word string. So a constrictive job or rule 'pulls things tight' and limits freedom or choices, which is why we use it for things that feel limiting.
