Etymology

Islamic Group of Uzbekistan is a literal name: Islamic shows the group's religion-based aim, Group means an organized band of people, and Uzbekistan is the country where they operate. Formed in the 1990s, they became an 'armed group' known for attacks and drug smuggling, so the name today points to a violent organization active in that region.
