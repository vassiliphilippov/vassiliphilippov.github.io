Etymology

The word bestowment comes from bestow plus the noun ending -ment, and bestow originally meant to 'put in place' or 'give', sharing a root with stow, as in 'stow away' meaning to put something somewhere. So a bestowment is the act of giving or presenting something, like an award or gift.
