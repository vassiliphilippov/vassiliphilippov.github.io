Etymology

The name noncommissioned officer comes from the idea of a 'commission', an official paper that gives someone the right to be an officer. If a leader came up from the regular soldiers without that paper they were called non-commissioned, so today a noncommissioned officer is a leader promoted from the ranks who has authority but not a formal 'commission'.
