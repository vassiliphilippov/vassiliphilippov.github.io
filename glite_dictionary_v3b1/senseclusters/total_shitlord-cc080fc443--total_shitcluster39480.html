Etymology

total shitlord comes from internet talk that joins the rude word shit meaning something bad with lord meaning someone who acts like they are in charge, so the image is of a person who rules over rude or nasty behaviour and total makes it stronger. So, it is used to call someone 'an extremely obnoxious or arrogant person'.
