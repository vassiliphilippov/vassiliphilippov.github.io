Etymology

Pennisetum americanum may come from Latin words that give a clear picture: Pennisetum refers to a 'feather' because the tall flower spikes look soft and feathery, and americanum was a label meaning 'American'. So the name helps you remember a tall grass with cattail-like spikes that people grow for grain and animal feed.
