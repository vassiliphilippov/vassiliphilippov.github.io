Etymology

addlebrained comes from the old word addle, which meant 'to make rotten' or 'to make confused', and brain for the mind. Think of 'addled eggs'—an addlebrained person has a 'mixed up' or 'rotten' brain, so the word means someone who is confused or foolish.
