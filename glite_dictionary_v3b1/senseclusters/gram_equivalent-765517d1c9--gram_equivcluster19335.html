Etymology

gram equivalent comes from old chemistry: a gram is a unit of weight and an equivalent is the amount that 'matches' a fixed amount of another substance, like 8 grams of oxygen or 1.008 grams of hydrogen. So, a gram equivalent is the mass that will 'react' or 'combine' with that fixed amount, helping chemists know how much of a substance is needed.
