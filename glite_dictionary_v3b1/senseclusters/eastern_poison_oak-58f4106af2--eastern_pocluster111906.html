Etymology

Eastern poison oak gets its name from two clear pictures: its leaves look like an oak tree's leaves, and its oil is like poison, causing an itchy rash. The word 'eastern' shows it grows in the eastern United States, so the name means 'a plant that causes an itchy skin reaction'.
