Etymology

booster cable is a thick insulated wire used to connect two car batteries so one can 'boost' the other. The word booster means to give extra power and cable means a heavy wire, so imagine a strong cable between two cars sending power to a dead battery to 'start' the engine, which is why the name tells you its job.
