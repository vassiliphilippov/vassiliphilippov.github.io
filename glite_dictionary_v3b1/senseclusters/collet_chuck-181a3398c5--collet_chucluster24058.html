Etymology

collet chuck comes from two parts: a collet is a small cone that squeezes around a round object, and a chuck is a holder. So a collet chuck is literally a cone-in-a-holder used to 'clamp' round pieces tightly, which is why the name means 'a clamp that holds round pieces so a machine can work on them accurately'.
