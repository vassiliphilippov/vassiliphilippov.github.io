Etymology

Callorhinus ursinus may come from old Greek and Latin words: callo- and -rhinus meaning a 'beautiful nose', and ursinus meaning 'bear-like'. The image of a seal with a noticeable nose and thick, bear-like fur helps explain why this name refers to the northern fur seal.
