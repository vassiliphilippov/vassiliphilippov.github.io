Etymology

regressive leftism combines regressive, which means 'moving backwards', and leftism, which means 'left-wing' politics, so imagine people on the left taking steps back instead of forward. One popular use says the phrase describes ideas that say they want progress but actually push society 'backwards', so it is used to criticize those views as harmful.
