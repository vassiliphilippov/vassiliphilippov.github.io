Etymology

resident manlet likely comes from joining resident (the regular person in a place) and manlet — a joking form of 'man' with the small ending '-let', like in 'piglet', to show someone small. Picture the small, well-known person in a group; that's why the phrase means 'the noticeably short person in a group', often used as a light joke.
