Etymology

The noun lampoon artist comes from the old word lampoon, which one popular story says began as rude songs or verses that made fun of important people. So an artist who makes sharp, mocking pictures became known as a lampoon artist, and that's why the phrase means someone who 'mocks' or 'criticizes' people with funny but harsh art.
