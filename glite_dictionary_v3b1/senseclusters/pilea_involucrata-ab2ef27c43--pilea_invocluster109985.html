Etymology

Pilea involucrata may get its name from Latin words that describe how it looks: Pilea suggests a small 'cap' or soft top and involucrata means 'wrapped' or 'covered'. Those words make a picture of the plant's velvety leaves and the small leaf-like parts that hide its flower clusters, so the name helps you remember this species.
