Etymology

circumflex vein comes from old Latin parts: circum meaning 'around' and flex meaning 'bend', so the name paints a simple picture of a vein that bends or curves around a nearby artery or joint. That's why circumflex vein is used for veins that run in a curved path next to the matching artery.
