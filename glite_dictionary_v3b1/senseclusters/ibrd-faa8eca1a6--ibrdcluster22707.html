Etymology

The word IBRD is made from the first letters of International, Bank, Reconstruction, and Development, where International means 'between countries', Reconstruction means 'to rebuild', and Development means 'to grow or improve'. That's why the IBRD is the World Bank institution that gives loans to help countries rebuild and grow.
