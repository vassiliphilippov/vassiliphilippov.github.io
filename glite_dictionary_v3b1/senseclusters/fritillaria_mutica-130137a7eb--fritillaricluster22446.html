Etymology

Fritillaria mutica comes from old Latin words that describe how the flowers look. The part fritillaria may come from a Latin word for a small dice box because the petals can show a checkered 'pattern', and mutica means 'blunt' or not pointy, so the name helps you picture the green-and-purple, bell-shaped flower.
