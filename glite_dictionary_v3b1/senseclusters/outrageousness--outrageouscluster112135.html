Etymology

outrageousness comes from the word outrage, which once meant something 'beyond' normal limits, like the prefix ultra- that also means 'beyond'. So, something outrageous goes 'beyond' what people accept, and outrageousness is the quality of being very 'shocking' or 'excessive'.
