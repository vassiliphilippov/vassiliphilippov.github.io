Etymology

Zauschneria californica has a name that tells a little story: Zauschneria is 'named after a person' and californica means 'from California'. Its bright, tube-shaped red flowers look like small fuchsias, so people call it the 'California fuchsia', and that image helps you remember the plant and its name.
