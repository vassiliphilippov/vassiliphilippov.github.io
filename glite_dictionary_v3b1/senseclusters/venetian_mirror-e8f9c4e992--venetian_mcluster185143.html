Etymology

Venetian mirror may come from the famous glassmakers of Venice in the 1500s who made very clear glass and backed it with shiny metal to make a bright, almost perfect mirror. That's why today a Venetian mirror means a highly decorated, fine mirror that gives a 'clear reflection'.
