Mirabilis uniflora comes from Latin words: Mirabilis meaning 'wonderful' and uniflora meaning 'one-flowered'. So the name paints a picture of a single, lovely flower that opens in the late afternoon, which explains why it refers to a fragrant plant that blooms each evening.
