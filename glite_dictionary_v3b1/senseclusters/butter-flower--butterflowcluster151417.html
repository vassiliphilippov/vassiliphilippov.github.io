Etymology

butter-flower comes from the bright yellow color of the bloom: the butter part refers to 'butter' because the flower looks like a small bit of butter, and the flower part simply names it, much like the familiar word buttercup. So the name helps you remember these small yellow Ranunculus as little 'butter' flowers.
