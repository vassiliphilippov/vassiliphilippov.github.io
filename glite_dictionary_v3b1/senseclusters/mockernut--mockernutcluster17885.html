Etymology

Mockernut comes from the simple parts mock and nut, where mock means 'to make fun of', and a helpful way to remember it is that the nut's very hard shell seems to 'mock' anyone who tries to crack it. So the name fits the hickory tree, which has a hard-shelled nut you can eat.
