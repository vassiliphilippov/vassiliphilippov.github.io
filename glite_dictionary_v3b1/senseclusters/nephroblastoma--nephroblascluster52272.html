Etymology

nephroblastoma breaks into three parts: nephro- meaning 'kidney', blast meaning 'young cell' or 'sprout', and -oma meaning 'tumor'. So it means a 'tumor of young kidney cells', which is why nephroblastoma is a kidney cancer that often appears in young children.
