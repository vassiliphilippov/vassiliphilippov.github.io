Etymology

peptize comes from the Greek root pept- meaning 'to digest' or 'to cook down'. In chemistry it means to break a solid into tiny pieces and spread them through a liquid, so thinking of 'digesting' the solid helps remember why peptize means making the solid stay mixed in the liquid.
