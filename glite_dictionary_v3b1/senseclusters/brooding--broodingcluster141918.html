Etymology

brooding also means to think sadly or worry again and again, and this sense comes from the image of a bird brood 'sitting' over its eggs, staying close and quiet. So to brood about a problem is to sit with dark thoughts, which is why brooding describes a gloomy, thoughtful mood.
