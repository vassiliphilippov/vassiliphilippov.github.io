Etymology

The plant name sweet flag comes from two simple words: sweet for the plant's pleasant root smell and flag from an old word for a 'reed' or long leaf. Because this marsh plant has long leaves and a sweet-smelling root, sweet flag now means that aromatic wetland plant.
