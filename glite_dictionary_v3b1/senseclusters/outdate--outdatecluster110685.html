Etymology

Outdate comes from the parts out- meaning 'beyond' or 'more than', and date meaning a 'time' or 'day' on a calendar, so it originally meant to be 'beyond the date' of something. That's why if ruins outdate a castle, the ruins are from an earlier time and are older than the castle.
