Etymology

Betula alleghaniensis comes from Latin words: Betula means 'birch' and alleghaniensis means 'from the Allegheny', the mountain area where this tree grows a lot. So the name tells you it is a birch from that region and helps explain why people also call it the 'yellow birch'.
