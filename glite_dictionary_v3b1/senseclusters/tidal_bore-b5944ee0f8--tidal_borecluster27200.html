Etymology

The noun tidal bore comes from a simple image: tidal means the sea's tide and bore means a strong wave that pushes up a river. Imagine a wall of water moving against the river's usual flow and surprising people, so the tidal bore means 'a high, often dangerous wave that rushes upstream'.
