Etymology

refrigerator cookie comes from a simple kitchen trick: cooks roll the dough into a log and chill it in the refrigerator, then slice and bake the pieces when they want fresh cookies. So the name links the refrigerator and the cookie, and it means a cookie that is made ahead and 'ready to bake' when needed.
