Etymology

The word pretexting comes from pre- 'before' and pretext, which originally meant a 'cover' made by a root that meant 'to weave' or 'to cover', so a pretext is a 'cover' put in front of the truth. So pretexting is making a false cover or excuse to hide your real purpose and get information.
