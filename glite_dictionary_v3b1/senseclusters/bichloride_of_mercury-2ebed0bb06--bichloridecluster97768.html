Etymology

bichloride of mercury comes from old chemical naming: bi- means 'two' and chloride means 'a part that has chlorine', so the name paints a picture of mercury with two chlorine parts. Because this white compound was used to kill germs and protect wood, the name now refers to a very poisonous preservative.
