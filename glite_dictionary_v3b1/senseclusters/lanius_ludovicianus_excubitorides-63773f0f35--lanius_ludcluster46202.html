Etymology

Lanius ludovicianus excubitorides may come from the Latin word Lanius, meaning 'butcher', because this shrike kills and often sticks its prey on thorns or barbed wire like a butcher hanging meat. The rest of the name (ludovicianus, excubitorides) names the local type, so the full name helps you remember the bird's 'butcher' habit.
