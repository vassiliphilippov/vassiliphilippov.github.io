Etymology

The phrase mercantile system comes from mercantile, a word for merchants, and system, meaning a planned way of doing things. Imagine rulers tightly controlling merchants and colonies to bring gold and silver into the royal chest, and you can see why it means 'a government-controlled trade policy to make the country rich'.
