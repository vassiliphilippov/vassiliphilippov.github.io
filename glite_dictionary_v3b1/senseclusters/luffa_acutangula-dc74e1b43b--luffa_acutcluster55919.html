Etymology

Luffa acutangula gets its name from Latin words that describe the plant. The first part, Luffa, is the common name for sponge gourds and the second part, acutangula, means 'sharp-angled' and refers to the fruit's clear ridges, so the name points to a climbing plant grown for edible young fruits and for its fibrous, sponge-like mature gourds.
