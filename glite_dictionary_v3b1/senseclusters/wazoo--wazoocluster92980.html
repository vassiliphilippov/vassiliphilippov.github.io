Etymology

wazoo is a playful American slang name for the 'rear end'. It is likely a made-up, silly word that sounds like the little instrument kazoo, so a helpful way to remember it is to think of a funny name for your back, which also appears in the phrase 'up the wazoo' meaning 'a lot'.
