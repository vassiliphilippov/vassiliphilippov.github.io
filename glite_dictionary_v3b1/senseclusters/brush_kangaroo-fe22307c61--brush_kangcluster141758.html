Etymology

The noun brush kangaroo comes from a simple picture: brush means 'thick bushes' and kangaroo is the animal, so a brush kangaroo is a kangaroo that lives in bushes and low trees. That's why the name refers to small or medium kangaroos often found in forests and thick bushes.
