Etymology

The noun borscht circuit probably comes from holiday hotels in the Catskills where Jewish guests often ate borscht and performers moved from hotel to hotel on a circuit. So, today borscht circuit means 'a group of resorts' known for Jewish guests and for helping many comedians start their careers.
