Etymology

The noun phenolic glycoside comes from two parts: a phenol (the active chemical) joined to a sugar. Imagine a plant hiding a strong chemical inside a sugar wrapper that it can 'open' when needed, and that picture helps explain why the name means a phenol attached to a sugar.
