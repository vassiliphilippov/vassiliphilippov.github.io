Etymology

nucleolus organizer comes from the picture of a small manager on a chromosome: the word nucleolus names the cell part and organizer suggests it 'organizes' the pieces needed to build that part. So the name refers to the chromosome region that 'organizes' and helps make the nucleolus after the cell divides.
