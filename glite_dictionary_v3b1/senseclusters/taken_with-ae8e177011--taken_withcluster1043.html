Etymology

Taken with may come from the old sense of taken meaning 'captured' or 'grabbed' and the word with showing what does the taking. Imagine an idea or person literally grabbing you so you feel held by them. That's why it now means being 'strongly attracted to' or 'very fond of' someone or something.
