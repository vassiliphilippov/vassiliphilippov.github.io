Etymology

The plant Dipsacus fullonum gets its name from two older words: Dipsacus comes from a Greek word for 'thirst' because the cup-like leaves hold rainwater, and fullonum comes from the Latin 'fuller' because cloth workers used the dried flower heads to brush wool. So the name remembers both the plant's water-holding cups and its use in cloth making.
