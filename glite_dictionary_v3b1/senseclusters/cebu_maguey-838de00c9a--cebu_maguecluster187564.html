Etymology

Cebu maguey gets its name from the island Cebu and the plant maguey, a plant with thick leaves whose long fibers were pulled out, dried and twisted to make strong rope. That's why Cebu maguey means the 'hard natural fiber' used for coarse twine, sacks and fishing nets.
