Etymology

Ice for a frozen dessert comes from the same cold idea as ice meaning 'frozen water' — people kept sweets on ice to make them very cold, and the cold treat became called an ice. A helpful way to remember it is to think of a fruity, icy snack like a simple, frozen portion of ice cream.
