Etymology

ebbing comes from the sea word ebb, which means the tide moving away from the shore. Picture water slowly going back after high tide — like the phrase ebb and flow — and you have a neat image for the meaning 'a gradual decrease' in strength, support, or energy. So, ebbing is used for things that slowly fall or weaken.
