Etymology

Fountain of Youth comes from old stories about a magic spring where people who drank or washed in the water became young again. The words fountain and youth give a simple picture of a water source that returns youth, and one famous tale says explorers looked for it to gain 'immortality', so today we call anything that makes someone feel younger a Fountain of Youth.
