Etymology

rabbit-eared bandicoot is named for its long, rabbit-like ears: the parts rabbit and eared create a clear picture of a bandicoot with big, rabbit-style ears. So the name helps you imagine this small Australian marsupial that is often active at night and is easily remembered by its large ears.
