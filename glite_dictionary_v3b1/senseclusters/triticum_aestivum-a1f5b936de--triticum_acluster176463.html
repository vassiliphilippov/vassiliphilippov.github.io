Etymology

Triticum aestivum is a scientific name from Latin that literally means 'summer wheat', with Triticum for wheat and aestivum for 'of summer'. Imagine golden fields ripening in the warm months. That image helps explain why the name refers to the common wheat grown for grain and bread.
