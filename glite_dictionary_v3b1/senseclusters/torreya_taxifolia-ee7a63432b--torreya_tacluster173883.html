Etymology

The name Torreya taxifolia comes from a plant scientist named John Torrey and from Latin words that describe the leaves. Torreya honours John Torrey and taxifolia means 'yew-leaved', from 'yew' and 'leaf', so the name helps you picture a small tree with shiny, yew-like leaves.
