Etymology

The compound noun slide action comes from the sliding part of a gun: the slide moves back and forth in the action, like a small drawer, to 'eject' a used shell and 'load' a new one. That's why slide action now means a gun mechanism that uses this sliding movement to move shells for the next shot.
