Etymology

The word lacunar comes from the Latin lacuna, meaning 'gap' or 'hollow', and you can also see the related English word lacuna ('a missing part'). Think of a small hollow or sunken panel in a ceiling, and that is why a lacunar is a decorative recessed panel.
