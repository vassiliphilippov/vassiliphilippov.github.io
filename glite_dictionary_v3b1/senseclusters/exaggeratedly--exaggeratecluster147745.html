Etymology

exaggeratedly comes from exaggerate, which breaks into ex- 'out' and a root meaning 'to heap up', like piling more on to make something seem bigger. So to speak exaggeratedly is to say something in a way that makes it seem larger or more important than it really is, and the -ly ending turns it into a word that tells how something is done.
