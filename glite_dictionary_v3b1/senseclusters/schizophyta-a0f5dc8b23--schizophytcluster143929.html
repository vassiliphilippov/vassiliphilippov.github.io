Etymology

Schizophyta comes from Greek parts schizo- meaning 'split' and -phyta meaning 'plant'. It was used for simple, algae-like living things, for example blue-green algae, that often live as single cells that split, so the name helps you remember them as 'splitting plants' and today they are usually called Cyanophyta or cyanobacteria.
