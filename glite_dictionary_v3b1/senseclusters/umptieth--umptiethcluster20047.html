Etymology

The word umptieth comes from the playful number umpteen, which people use to mean 'many', and the ordinal ending -ieth, as in thirtieth. So umptieth is a humorous way to say the 'last' in a long, unclear series, like saying you have heard something for the 'manyth' time.
