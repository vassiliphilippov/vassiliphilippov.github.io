Etymology

square-tailed comes from the two clear English words square ('a shape with four equal sides or a straight edge') and tail ('the back part of an animal'). So square-tailed describes a tail that looks cut straight across, like the end of a small square, which makes it easy to spot birds or butterflies with that shape.
