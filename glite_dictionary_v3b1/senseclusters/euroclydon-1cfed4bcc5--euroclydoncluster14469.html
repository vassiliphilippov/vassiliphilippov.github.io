Etymology

Euroclydon comes from Greek parts Euro- meaning 'east wind' and -clydon meaning 'wave' or 'surge', so the word literally names a violent sea storm. That's why in the Bible a Euroclydon is the powerful wind that drove ships off course in the Mediterranean.
