Etymology

superoxide dismutase is named for what it does: it takes dangerous oxygen molecules called superoxide and changes them into normal oxygen and a milder chemical so they do less harm to the cell. The name parts superoxide, the harmful oxygen, and dismutase, a word for an enzyme that changes chemicals, help you remember it 'protects' cells from damage.
