Etymology

The word osteoarthritic begins with osteo- meaning 'bone' and arthr- meaning 'joint', and the ending -itic means 'relating to a disease'. So, osteoarthritic describes something to do with bone and joint problems, which is why it refers to the pain and stiffness of osteoarthritis.
