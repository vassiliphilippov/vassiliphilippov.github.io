Etymology

The word cantankerous comes from an old English dialect form contankerous, which meant 'argumentative' or 'difficult'. A helpful way to remember it is to imagine someone who 'can't answer us' in an argument, grumpy and ready to argue, which is why cantankerous now means bad-tempered and hard to deal with.
