Etymology

Conditioned reflex comes from famous experiments by the scientist Ivan Pavlov, who rang a bell every time he fed dogs so they began to drool at the sound of the bell. The word conditioned means 'made by training' and reflex is an automatic reaction, so the phrase means 'a learned response' to a new signal.
