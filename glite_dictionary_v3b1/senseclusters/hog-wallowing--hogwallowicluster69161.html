Etymology

hog-wallowing comes from hog (which means 'pig') and wallow (which means 'to roll in mud or water'). It gives the clear image of a pig happily rolling in the mud, so when a person is hog-wallowing they are rolling or lying about in mud in a messy, carefree way.
