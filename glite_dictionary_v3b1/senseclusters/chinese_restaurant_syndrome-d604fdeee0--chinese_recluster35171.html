Etymology

Chinese restaurant syndrome came from news reports in the 1960s about people getting headaches, tingling and sweating after eating at some Chinese restaurants. Reporters blamed 'MSG', so the phrase came to mean the 'group of symptoms' people thought were caused by that food.
