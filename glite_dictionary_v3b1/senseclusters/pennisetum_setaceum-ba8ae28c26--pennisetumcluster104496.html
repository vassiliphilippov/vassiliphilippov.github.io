Etymology

Pennisetum setaceum comes from Latin words: penni- meaning 'feather' and setaceum meaning 'bristle' or 'hair'. The plant's long, soft flower plumes look like little feathers or a small fountain of hair, so the name fits this ornamental 'fountain grass'.
