Etymology

tetrahydrofuran literally describes the chemical: tetra- means 'four', hydro- relates to 'water', and furan names a small five-sided 'ring' of atoms in the molecule. So the name tells chemists about its structure and helps explain why it is a strong solvent used in labs and industry.
