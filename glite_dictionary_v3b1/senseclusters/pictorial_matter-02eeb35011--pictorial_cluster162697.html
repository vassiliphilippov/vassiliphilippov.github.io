Etymology

pictorial matter comes from two simple words: pictorial meaning 'made of pictures' and matter meaning 'material' or 'content'. Imagine pages filled with pictures and photos added to a book or magazine, so today pictorial matter means the 'illustrations or photos' used to decorate or explain a text.
