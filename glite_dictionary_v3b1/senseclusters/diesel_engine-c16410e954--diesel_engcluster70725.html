Etymology

Diesel engine was named after the inventor Rudolf Diesel, who built a new kind of engine that runs on heavy oil. The parts diesel and engine give a clear picture: it is an engine that runs on 'diesel' fuel, so today the phrase means an engine that uses that fuel.
