Etymology

Lac wax comes from lac, the sticky resin made by tiny insects, plus wax, a hard substance. When people make shellac from that resin with alcohol, a hard 'leftover' wax does not mix with the alcohol and comes away. That's why lac wax today means the hard wax left from shellac which resists water and alcohol.
