Etymology

luminosity comes from the Latin lumen meaning 'light' and the ending -osity meaning 'a quality', and it shares the same root as luminous and luminary. So when scientists or artists talk about a star's or a painting's luminosity, they mean its brightness — how much light it gives or reflects.
