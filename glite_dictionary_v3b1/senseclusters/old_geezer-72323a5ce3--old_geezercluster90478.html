Etymology

old geezer may come from British slang where geezer simply meant a man or fellow, often one who seemed a bit rough or funny, and old shows age. So the phrase paints the picture of a familiar, slightly odd older man and is used as a casual or slightly disrespectful way to say 'an elderly man'.
