Etymology

Samaveda comes from Sanskrit parts sama meaning 'melody' or 'song' and veda meaning 'knowledge' or 'sacred text'. So the name means 'knowledge of melodies', which explains why the Samaveda is a collection of hymns and tunes used in ancient Indian rituals.
