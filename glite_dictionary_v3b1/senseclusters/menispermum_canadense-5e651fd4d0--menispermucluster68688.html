Etymology

Menispermum canadense gets its name from its curved seed: Menispermum comes from old words meaning 'moon' and 'seed', and canadense means 'from Canada'. That's why people call it the 'Canadian moonseed', a simple picture to remember its moon-shaped seed and its grape-like but poisonous fruit.
