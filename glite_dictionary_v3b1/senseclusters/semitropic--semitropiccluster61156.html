Etymology

semitropic is made of semi- meaning 'half' or 'partly' and tropic which is the same root as tropical, meaning 'of the tropics' or very warm regions near the equator. So semitropic means 'partly tropical', referring to places just outside the tropics that have similar warm, mild weather.
