Etymology

Cucurbita pepo melopepo may come from old words for plants that look like small melons. The parts Cucurbita (a word for 'gourd'), pepo ('melon' or 'pumpkin') and melopepo ('melon-like pepo') give the picture of round, edible squash. So the name points to the kind of summer squash, like zucchini and pattypan, that we pick young and eat.
