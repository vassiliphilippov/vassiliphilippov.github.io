Etymology

Holy Grail comes from an old Christian story about a special cup that Jesus used at the Last Supper, and later tales said brave knights searched for this holy cup in a great quest. Because the cup was seen as powerful and sacred, the phrase names that holy object 'a sacred or miraculous cup'.
