Etymology

The word premiss comes from Latin parts prae- meaning 'before' and mittere meaning 'to send'. It originally meant something 'sent before' the main idea, and you can see the same 'to send' root in the word mission. So, a premiss is a statement you accept first and then use to reach a conclusion.
