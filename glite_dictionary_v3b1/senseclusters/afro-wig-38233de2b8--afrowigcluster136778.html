Etymology

Afro-wig comes from Afro, a short word for the round, tightly curled hairstyle that became popular in the 1960s and shows pride in hair 'from Africa', and wig, a piece of 'artificial hair'. So, an Afro-wig is a wig made to look like an Afro.
