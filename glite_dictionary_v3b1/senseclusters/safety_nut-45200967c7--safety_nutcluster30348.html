Etymology

The name safety nut comes from a simple idea: it is an extra nut that is screwed on after the first nut to hold that first nut tight. By stopping the first nut from coming loose, the extra nut makes the fastening 'safe', so a safety nut means 'a nut used to stop another nut from loosening'.
