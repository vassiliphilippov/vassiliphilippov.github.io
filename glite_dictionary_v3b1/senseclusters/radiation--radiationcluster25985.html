Etymology

Radiation comes from the Latin radius, meaning 'ray' or 'spoke', and helps us picture things that fan out from a center. In the brain, terms like 'optic radiation' name nerve fibres that spread out like 'rays' connecting different parts, so the root fits this arrangement.
