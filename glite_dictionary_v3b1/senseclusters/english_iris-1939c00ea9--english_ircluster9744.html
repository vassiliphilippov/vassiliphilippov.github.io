Etymology

English iris comes from the two words English and iris, and iris points to the flower's many colors like a 'rainbow'. Since gardeners in England often grew this iris from a bulb, they called it the English iris, so the name brings to mind an elegant garden flower with large, colorful blooms that are not yellow.
