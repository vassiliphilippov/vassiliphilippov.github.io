Etymology

The word madrasa comes from the Arabic word darasa, which means 'to study', and the ma- part means 'place', so madrasa literally means 'place of study'. Today the word is used in many languages, including Urdu and Hindi, to mean a Muslim school where religious and sometimes other subjects are taught.
