Etymology

hard solder is named because the solder is hard to melt and needs much more heat than soft solder. Because it melts hotter and often contains metals like copper, it flows into the joint and makes a very strong, lasting connection between metal pieces, which is why people use it for strong, durable repairs and jewelry.
