Etymology

divert comes from the Latin parts dis- 'away' and vertere 'to turn', so it means 'turn away'. A helpful way to remember the meaning 'entertain' is to think of it as 'turning' someone's mind away from worries, so something that diverts you gives you a pleasant break.
