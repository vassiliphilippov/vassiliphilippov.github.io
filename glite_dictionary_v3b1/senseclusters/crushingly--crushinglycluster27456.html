Etymology

crushingly comes from the simple word crush, which means 'to press or break something with great force', and the ending -ly turns it into 'in a ... way'. Imagine a heavy weight pressing down until something breaks, so when news or defeat comes crushingly it hits very hard and feels overwhelming or devastating.
