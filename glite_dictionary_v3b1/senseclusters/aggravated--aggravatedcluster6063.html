Etymology

aggravated comes from the old parts ad- 'to' and gravis 'heavy', so it first meant 'made heavy' or 'made worse'. A helpful way to remember the emotional meaning is that when something gets worse it can make you angry, so aggravated can also mean 'made more angry'.
