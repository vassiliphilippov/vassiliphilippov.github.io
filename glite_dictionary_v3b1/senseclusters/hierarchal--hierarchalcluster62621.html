Etymology

The word hierarchal comes from Greek parts hier- 'sacred' and arch 'rule', because it first meant the 'rule of priests' and you can see arch in monarch to remember 'ruler'. So, a hierarchal system is one that is arranged in clear levels or ranks of authority.
