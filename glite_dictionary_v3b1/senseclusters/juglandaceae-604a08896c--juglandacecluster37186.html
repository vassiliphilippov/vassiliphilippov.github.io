Etymology

Juglandaceae comes from the Latin Juglans 'walnut' and the plant ending -aceae 'family'. A helpful way to remember it is to think of Juglans as 'Jupiter's acorn', an old story, so the word simply means the 'walnut family', the group of trees like walnut, hickory, and pecan that make edible nuts.
