Etymology

The phrase vibrant personality starts with vibrant, a word that makes you think of bright color or quick movement, like something that seems to 'vibrate' with life, and personality meaning a person's character. So a vibrant personality means someone whose character feels 'lively' and 'full of energy', which is why we use it for very enthusiastic people.
