Etymology

characteristic curve comes from the simple picture of a bent line on a graph: characteristic means a device's typical 'behavior' and the curve is the line that shows how one value changes with another. So a characteristic curve is the graph engineers use to see a device's particular 'behavior' as things like voltage or temperature change.
