Etymology

radiation therapy comes from the idea of using powerful rays to treat disease: radiation means high-energy rays like X-rays and therapy means 'treatment'. Doctors aim those rays to destroy or damage cancer cells, so the phrase means a medical 'treatment' that uses energy rays to kill or weaken them.
