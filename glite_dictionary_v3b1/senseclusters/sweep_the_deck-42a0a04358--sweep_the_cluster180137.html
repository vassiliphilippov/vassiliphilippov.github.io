Etymology

sweep the deck comes from life on ships: sailors used brooms to sweep the wooden deck clean of salt, water and loose ropes. Clearing the deck kept the ship safe and ready, so today sweep the deck means 'to clean' or 'tidy up' a deck or outdoor area.
