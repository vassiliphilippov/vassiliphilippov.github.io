Etymology

The word Rheiformes comes from the bird name rhea and the ending -formes, which means 'shape' or 'form'. Like in uniform, -formes means 'in the shape of'. So Rheiformes are birds 'in the shape of' a rhea, meaning large, flightless birds like ostriches and emus.
