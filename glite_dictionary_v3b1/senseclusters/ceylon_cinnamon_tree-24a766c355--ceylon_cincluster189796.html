Etymology

Ceylon cinnamon tree: The name Ceylon cinnamon tree comes from the old name Ceylon for Sri Lanka, where this tree grows. People there peel the thin, spicy bark and roll it into sticks called 'true cinnamon', so the name tells you both the place and the real source of the spice.
