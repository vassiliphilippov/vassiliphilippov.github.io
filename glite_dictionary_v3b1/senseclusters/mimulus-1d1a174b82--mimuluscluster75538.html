Etymology

Mimulus the homeopathic remedy is named after the flower Mimulus, whose Latin name comes from mimus 'actor' plus -ulus 'little', so 'little mimic'. A helpful way to remember this remedy is to link it to the plant name and the idea of a small, clear emotion, since it is used for specific, known fears.
