Etymology

The word abstrusely comes from two parts, ab- 'away' and trudere, the same root you see in intrude and protrude, meaning 'to push'. So, to speak abstrusely is like 'pushing away' the clear meaning, which is why it means 'in a way that is hard to understand'.
