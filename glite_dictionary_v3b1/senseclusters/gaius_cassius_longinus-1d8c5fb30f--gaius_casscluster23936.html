Etymology

Gaius Cassius Longinus comes from old Roman naming: Gaius is a common first name, Cassius is the family name, and Longinus likely began as a nickname meaning 'the tall one'. He is remembered as a leading planner of the plot 'to assassinate' Julius Caesar, so the picture of a tall Roman senator who helped plan Caesar's death can help you remember him.
