Etymology

Eucalyptus maculata may come from Greek and Latin words: Eucalyptus means 'well-covered' because of the cap over the flower bud, and maculata means 'spotted' for its patchy bark, so the name points to how the tree looks and explains the common name 'spotted gum'.
