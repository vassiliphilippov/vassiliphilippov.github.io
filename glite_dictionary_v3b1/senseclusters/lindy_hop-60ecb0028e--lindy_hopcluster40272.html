Etymology

Lindy hop comes from the 1920s American dance scene. One popular story says dancers named the move after pilot Charles Lindbergh, calling it Lindy, and added hop because the dance has quick jumps and lively steps. That's why Lindy hop means 'an energetic partner dance' with fast, playful moves.
