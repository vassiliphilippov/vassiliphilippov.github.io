Etymology

The noun hospital attendant shows a clear picture: an attendant who 'attends' to people and jobs inside a hospital, doing simple tasks like bringing luggage, changing sheets, and guiding visitors. So a hospital attendant means 'a worker who cares for patients and helps with general tasks but does not give medical treatment'.
