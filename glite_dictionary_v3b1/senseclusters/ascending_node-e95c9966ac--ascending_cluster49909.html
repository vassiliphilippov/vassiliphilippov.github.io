Etymology

ascending node is the spot in an orbit where an object moves from the south side to the north side of Earth's path around the Sun. The word ascending means 'going up' and node means 'point', so the name gives a simple image of the orbit 'going up' across Earth's path and explains why we use it to describe an orbit's direction.
