Etymology

Cepheid comes from the constellation Cepheus and the well-known star Delta Cephei, where this kind of variable star was first noticed. So a Cepheid is a star 'like' Delta Cephei that brightens and dims in a regular pattern and helps astronomers measure distances in space.
