Etymology

The word ill-conceived starts with ill- meaning 'bad' and the part conceive meaning 'to form an idea' (or 'to become pregnant'). Think of a plan that is 'badly born' or badly formed, and that's why ill-conceived means not carefully planned or badly thought out.
