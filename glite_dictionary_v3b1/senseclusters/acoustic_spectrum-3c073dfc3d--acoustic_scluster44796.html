Etymology

acoustic spectrum may come from the image of a rainbow for 'sound', because acoustic means 'sound' and spectrum means a range like the colours in a rainbow. So an acoustic spectrum is like a picture that shows which high and low sounds are strong or weak, and that's why it helps us tell instruments apart or find damage.
