Etymology

ricey comes from the common word rice used in slang about showy cars, plus the ending -y that turns a noun into an adjective; a helpful way to remember it is to think of 'rice' as a label for flashy but cheap-looking modifications. So ricey means 'flashy but not faster', describing a car that looks loud but gives little real performance.
