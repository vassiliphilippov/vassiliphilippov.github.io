Etymology

Agglutinogen comes from parts that mean 'glue' and 'to produce': agglutin- from a word for 'glue' and -gen meaning 'to produce'. So an agglutinogen is a substance on blood cells that makes them 'stick' or 'clump' and causes the body to make the antibodies that do the clumping.
