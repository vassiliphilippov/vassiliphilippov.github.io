Etymology

The word motorization comes from motor, meaning 'engine', and the ending -ization, which means 'the process of making'. Motor comes from a word meaning 'to move', so motorization is the process of 'making things move with engines' and that is why it now means adding motors or cars to machines, places, or whole societies.
