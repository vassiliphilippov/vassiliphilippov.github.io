Etymology

sheet bend comes from sailing: sailors called the rope that held a sail a sheet, and the old word bend meant 'to tie'. So the name describes a knot used to tie a sheet to another rope, and that's why the sheet bend means a knot for joining two ropes, even when they are different sizes.
