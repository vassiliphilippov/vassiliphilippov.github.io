Etymology

Triatoma comes from Greek parts tri- ('three') and -atoma ('parts' or 'cut'). A helpful way to remember it is that the name probably points to a body feature in three pieces, so Triatoma is the scientific name for these blood-sucking 'kissing bugs'.
