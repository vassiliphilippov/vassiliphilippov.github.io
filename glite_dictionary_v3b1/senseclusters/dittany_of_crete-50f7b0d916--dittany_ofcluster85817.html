Etymology

Dittany of Crete is a small plant from the rocky island Crete with soft leaves and a pleasant smell that people long used as a healing herb. One popular story says people picked its leaves to put on cuts or to make tea to help healing, so the name now makes people think of a little plant used for 'healing' and 'medicine'.
