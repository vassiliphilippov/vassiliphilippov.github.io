Etymology

The name orthoboric acid comes from two parts: ortho- and boric, where ortho- is a Greek word meaning 'straight' or 'correct' and boric refers to the element boron. So orthoboric acid means the 'regular' or usual form of boric acid, which helps you remember it is the common form used in medicine and industry.
