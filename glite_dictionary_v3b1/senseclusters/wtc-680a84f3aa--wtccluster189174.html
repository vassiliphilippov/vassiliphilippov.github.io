Etymology

WTC is a short form of World Trade Center, where World means 'all countries', Trade means 'business', and Center means 'main place'. The name became famous because the twin towers in New York were destroyed on September 11, 2001, so today WTC often makes people think of the buildings and the memorial at that site.
