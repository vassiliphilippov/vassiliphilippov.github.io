Etymology

bad taste in someone's mouth comes from the simple image of eating something bitter or rotten so the bad taste stays in your mouth, leaving a lasting unpleasant feeling. That's why we use it to say someone has a lasting 'feeling of dislike' or 'disapproval' after an event.
