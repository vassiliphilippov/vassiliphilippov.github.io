Etymology

Acinonyx jubatus may come from old Greek and Latin words that describe the cheetah. The first part, Acinonyx, may mean 'fixed claw' because cheetahs' claws are more visible than in other cats, and jubatus comes from Latin 'juba' meaning 'mane' for the short mane of young cheetahs, so the name points to the animal's visible claws and small mane.
