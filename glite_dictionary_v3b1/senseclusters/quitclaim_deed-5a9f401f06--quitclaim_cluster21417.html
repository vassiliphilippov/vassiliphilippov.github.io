Etymology

quitclaim deed comes from the words quit and claim: quit means 'give up' and claim means 'right' to a property. A quitclaim deed is a simple paper where someone gives up any claim they have to a house or land, but it does not promise that their ownership is clear. That's why people use it to transfer what they have without a guarantee.
