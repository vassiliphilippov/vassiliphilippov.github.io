Etymology

polyvinyl resin comes from its parts: poly- meaning 'many', vinyl a name for a small building block used to make plastics, and resin meaning a sticky material like plastic. So the name gives a simple image of many vinyl pieces joined into a resin, which is why it refers to the strong plastic used for pipes, flooring, and coatings.
