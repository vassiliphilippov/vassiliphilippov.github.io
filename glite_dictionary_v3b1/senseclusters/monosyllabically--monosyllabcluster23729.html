monosyllabically

0.02/m9.1

Etymology

monosyllabically comes from mono- meaning 'one' and syllable meaning 'a unit of sound' in a word, so a monosyllable is a 'one-syllable' word; that's why to speak monosyllabically is to give very short answers or use single-syllable words like 'yes' or 'no'.
