Etymology

Challengeable is made from challenge, meaning to question or 'call out', and the ending -able, meaning 'can be', so it literally means something that 'can be questioned'. Originally, to 'challenge' often meant to 'call someone to court' or to formally oppose, which is why challengeable is used for legal or official decisions you can dispute.
