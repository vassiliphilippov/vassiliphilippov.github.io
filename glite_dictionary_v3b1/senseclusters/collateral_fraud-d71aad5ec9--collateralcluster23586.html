Etymology

The legal phrase collateral fraud comes from the idea of a dishonest act happening at the side: collateral means 'on the side' and fraud is a trick that hides important facts from someone. That's why collateral fraud means a hidden, side trick that stops a person from knowing their 'rights' or getting a 'fair hearing'.
