Etymology

floriferousness comes from Latin parts flor- meaning 'flower' and -ferous meaning 'bearing' or 'carrying', like in transfer ('to carry across'). So floriferousness means the quality of 'bearing many flowers', which explains why we use it for plants that produce lots of blooms.
