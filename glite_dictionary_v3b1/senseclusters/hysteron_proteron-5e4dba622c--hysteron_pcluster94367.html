Etymology

hysteron proteron comes from old Greek words hysteron meaning 'later' and proteron meaning 'earlier'. It names a way of speaking that puts what should come last first—like saying 'I put on my shoes and socks'—so the final action stands out, which is why it means 'reversing the normal order'.
