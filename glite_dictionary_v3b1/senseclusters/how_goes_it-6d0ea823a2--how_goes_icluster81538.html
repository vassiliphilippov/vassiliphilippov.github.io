Etymology

how goes it comes from older ways of speaking English where goes means 'moves' or 'is happening' and it stands for your life or situation. One popular image is someone asking if a small cart was rolling, and that picture turned the question into a short, friendly way to ask 'how are you' or 'how are things going'.
