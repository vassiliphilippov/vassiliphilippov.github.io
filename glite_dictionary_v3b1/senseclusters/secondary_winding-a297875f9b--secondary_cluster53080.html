Etymology

secondary winding is the coil of wire on a transformer that receives the electricity made by the first coil, the primary winding. Imagine two loops of wire wrapped on the same metal core: when the primary winding is powered, the other coil 'catches' that energy, so the secondary winding is the coil that provides the transformer's output electricity.
