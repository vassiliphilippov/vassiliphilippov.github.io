Etymology

Adiantum capillus-veneris gets its name from old words that describe the fern's look. Adiantum comes from a Greek word meaning 'not wet' because the leaves do not stay wet, and capillus-veneris means 'hair of Venus' because the thin black stems and tiny leaf parts look like delicate hair, so the name helps you picture this fragile fern.
