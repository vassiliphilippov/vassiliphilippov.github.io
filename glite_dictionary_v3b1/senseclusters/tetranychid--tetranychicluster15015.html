Etymology

tetranychid comes from Greek parts: tetra- meaning 'four' and -nychid referring to a 'spider' or 'spinner'. So a tetranychid is a tiny, spider-like mite with four pairs of legs that spins fine silk on plants, which is why these mites make webbing on leaves and can damage plants and fruit trees.
