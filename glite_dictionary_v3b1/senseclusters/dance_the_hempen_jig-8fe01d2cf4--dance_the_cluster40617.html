Etymology

The phrase dance the hempen jig may come from the terrible image of a person hanging on a hemp rope: hempen means made of hemp and a jig is a lively dance. As the person swung or moved on the rope it could look like a strange dance, so the phrase came to mean 'to be hanged'.
