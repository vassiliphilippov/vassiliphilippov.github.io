Etymology

Anolis carolinensis gets its name from two parts: Anolis, a word that may come from an old Caribbean name like 'anoli' for small lizards, and carolinensis, which means 'from Carolina'. So the name tells you this is the small green lizard, the green anole, that comes from the Carolina region.
