Etymology

Abul-Walid Mohammed ibn-Ahmad Ibn-Mohammed ibn-Roshd is a traditional Arabic name that reads like a family line: Abul-Walid means 'father of Walid' and the ibn parts such as ibn-Ahmad, Ibn-Mohammed, ibn-Roshd mean 'son of' someone. He was a famous philosopher from Spain in the 1100s, often called 'Averroes', and the long name helps you remember he was the real scholar who wrote important explanations of Aristotle's ideas.
