Etymology

The noun duffel coat comes from a strong wool cloth called duffel, named after the town of Duffel in Belgium. Coats made from that heavy cloth often had a hood and wooden toggle fastenings, so duffel coat came to mean the warm, hooded coat with toggles that we wear today.
