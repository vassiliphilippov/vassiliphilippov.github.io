Etymology

methylthionine chloride gets its name from its parts: methyl (a small chemical piece), thionine (an old dye name), and chloride (a salt). That name helps you remember it is a blue-green dye used to stain tissues, to work as an antiseptic, and even as an 'antidote' for cyanide poisoning.
