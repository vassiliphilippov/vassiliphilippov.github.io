Etymology

widdle mess comes from baby talk, where little children say widdle instead of 'little' when they point to a tiny spill or toys on the floor, so the words widdle and mess create the picture of a very small mess. So, widdle mess means a 'small' or 'minor' mess and is used in a playful way.
