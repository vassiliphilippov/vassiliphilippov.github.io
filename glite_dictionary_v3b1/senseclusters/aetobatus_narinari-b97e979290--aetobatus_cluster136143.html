Etymology

Aetobatus narinari is a scientific name that gives a simple picture: Aetobatus comes from old words for 'eagle' and 'ray' because the fish's long nose and wide fins look like an eagle in flight. The second word narinari may come from a local name for the animal, so the whole name helps you remember this large, spotted 'eagle ray' found in warm seas.
