Etymology

electric hammer comes from putting together electric ('powered by electricity') and hammer ('a tool for hitting nails or breaking hard material'), so it paints the picture of a hammer with a motor. That's why it means 'a hammer powered by electricity' used to hit nails or break hard materials.
