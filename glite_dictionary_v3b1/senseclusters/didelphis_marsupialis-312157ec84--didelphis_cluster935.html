Etymology

Didelphis marsupialis comes from old Greek and Latin words: Didelphis means 'two wombs' and marsupialis means 'pouch'. That picture of an animal with a pouch for its babies helps you remember this name is for an opossum, a marsupial that carries its young in a pouch.
