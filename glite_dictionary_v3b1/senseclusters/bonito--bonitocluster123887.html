Etymology

bonito comes from the Spanish and Portuguese word bonito, which means 'pretty' or 'good'. So people called the fish bonito because of its shiny, 'pretty' skin or because it was 'good' to eat, and today bonito still names this mackerel-like fish used in cooking.
