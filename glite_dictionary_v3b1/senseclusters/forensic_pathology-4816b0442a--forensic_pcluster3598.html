Etymology

forensic pathology comes from two parts: forensic, meaning 'connected with the court', and pathology, meaning the study of disease and dead bodies. Imagine a doctor who studies a dead body and then speaks in court to explain how the person died, so that is why forensic pathology means 'medical work in legal investigations'.
