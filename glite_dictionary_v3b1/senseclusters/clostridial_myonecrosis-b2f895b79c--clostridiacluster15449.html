Etymology

Clostridial myonecrosis breaks into parts: clostridial from germs called Clostridium, myo meaning 'muscle', and necrosis meaning 'death'. So the name literally says 'muscle death' caused by those germs, which is why it means a very fast, often deadly infection of muscle.
