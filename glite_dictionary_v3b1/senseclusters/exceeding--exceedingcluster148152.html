Etymology

The word exceeding comes from parts that mean 'out' (ex-) and 'to go' (-ceed), so it originally meant 'to go beyond'. You can see the same -ceed in common words like proceed and recede, which helps explain why exceeding means 'beyond the usual' or 'very great'.
