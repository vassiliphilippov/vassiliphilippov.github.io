Etymology

tough-minded comes from the everyday words tough ('strong' or 'not easily hurt') and mind ('thinking' or 'feelings'), so the phrase literally means a strong way of thinking. That's why a tough-minded person deals with facts and problems realistically and stays determined instead of being moved by emotions.
