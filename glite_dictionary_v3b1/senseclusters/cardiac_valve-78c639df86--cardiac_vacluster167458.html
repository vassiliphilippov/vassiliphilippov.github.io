Etymology

cardiac valve combines cardiac, meaning 'heart', and valve, meaning a small door. Imagine a tiny door in the heart that opens to let blood go forward and closes to stop it going backward, so a cardiac valve controls 'one-way' blood flow between the heart's chambers and vessels.
