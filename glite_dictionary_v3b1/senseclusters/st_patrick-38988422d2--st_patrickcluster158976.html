Etymology

St. Patrick was a church teacher long ago who, one popular story says, used a three-leaf shamrock to show how the Father, Son and Holy Spirit are one. That's why he is remembered as the 'patron saint' of Ireland and why people celebrate 'St. Patrick's Day' every year.
