Etymology

Processional march comes from the words processional, meaning a formal group moving in a line, and march, meaning a steady marching beat. Imagine a slow line of people in a ceremony walking while music keeps a steady step; that's why it means 'a piece of music with a marching rhythm' played during a procession.
