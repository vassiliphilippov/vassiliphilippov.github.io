Etymology

z comes from the old Greek letter zeta, which itself comes from an even older sign that meant 'weapon' or 'sword'. So z is the symbol for the 'z' sound and the last letter of the alphabet, and you can remember it by words like zebra or by its name 'zee' in American English or 'zed' in British English.
