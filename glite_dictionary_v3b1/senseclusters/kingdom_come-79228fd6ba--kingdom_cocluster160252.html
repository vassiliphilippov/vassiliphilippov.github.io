Etymology

kingdom come probably comes from an old Christian prayer that said 'thy kingdomcome', meaning the 'afterlife' or a future world. One popular story says that to send someone to kingdom come meant sending them to that next world or destroying them, so today it often means 'to die' or be ruined completely.
