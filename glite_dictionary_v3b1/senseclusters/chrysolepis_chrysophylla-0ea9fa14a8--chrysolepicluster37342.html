Etymology

Chrysolepis chrysophylla comes from Greek words: chryso- meaning 'gold' and -phylla meaning 'leaf', because the tree's leaves are shiny yellow-green and look golden underneath. That's why this small Pacific Coast evergreen, with glossy leaves and edible nuts, has a name that points to its 'golden leaf'.
