Etymology

Jerusalem thorn may come from travellers who thought this spiny bush looked like the thorny plants in stories about Jerusalem. The name joins Jerusalem with the sharp branches (the thorn), and that simple image of a spiny, yellow-flowered shrub explains why we call this plant a 'Jerusalem thorn' today.
