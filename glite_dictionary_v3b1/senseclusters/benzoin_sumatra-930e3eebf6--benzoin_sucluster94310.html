Etymology

The name benzoin Sumatra comes from the sticky resin called benzoin that comes from trees on the island of Sumatra, where people cut the bark so the sap 'bleeds' out and hardens into fragrant lumps. That's why benzoin Sumatra refers to a fragrant 'resin' used in 'perfume', 'incense', and 'medicine'.
