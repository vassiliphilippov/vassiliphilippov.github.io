Etymology

Tagetes erecta is the scientific name for the tall, bright marigold often used in Mexican ceremonies. The name helps you remember the plant: Tagetes is the marigold group and erecta means 'upright', so picture a tall, straight orange-yellow flower — this image explains why the name refers to the tall, showy marigold.
