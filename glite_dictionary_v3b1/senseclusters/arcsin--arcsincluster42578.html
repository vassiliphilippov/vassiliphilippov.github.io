Etymology

arcsin is made of two parts, arc and sin. The arc part means 'curved part of a circle', like an 'arch', and sin comes from sine, the math function that gives a value from a triangle. So arcsin is the angle on the circle that has that 'sine' value, in other words the angle whose sine is a given number.
