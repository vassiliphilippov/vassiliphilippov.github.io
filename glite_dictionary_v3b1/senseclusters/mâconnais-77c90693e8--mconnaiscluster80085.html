Etymology

**Mâconnais** comes from the French town **Mâcon** and the ending **-nais**, which means 'from' or 'of' a place. So a **Mâconnais** is a wine 'from Mâcon' in Burgundy. Like **Champagne** or **Bordeaux**, the name tells you the place the wine comes from.
