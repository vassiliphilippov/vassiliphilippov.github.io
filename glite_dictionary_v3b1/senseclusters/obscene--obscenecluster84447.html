Etymology

obscene comes from the old Latin word obscenus, which meant 'repulsive' or 'indecent'. One popular way to remember it says ob- means 'away' and scaena means 'stage', so something kept 'off the stage' was hidden because it shocked people, and today obscene describes sexual or moral things that make people uncomfortable.
