Etymology

clean bomb got its name in early atomic tests when scientists tried to make a cleanbomb that did not leave dangerous dirt. Because these devices left much less radioactive 'fallout' than normal bombs, people called them 'clean', so today clean bomb means 'a bomb that produces very little radioactive contamination'.
