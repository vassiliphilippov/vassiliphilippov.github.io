Etymology

hatefulness comes from the simple word hate, which means 'strong dislike', plus -ful meaning 'full of', as in helpful, and -ness meaning 'state or quality', as in kindness. So hatefulness means the state of being 'full of hate', which is why it describes someone or something full of hatred.
