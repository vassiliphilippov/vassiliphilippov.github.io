Etymology

popcorn pisser may come from the image of someone at a movie whose popcorn is spilled when they jump at every scare, or who becomes a pisser by laughing or getting so scared they 'wet themselves' a little. So the phrase means someone who is 'easily frightened' or who reacts with 'strong excitement'.
