Etymology

Beefsteak geranium may get its name from the plant's large, flat leaves that look like a piece of raw 'beef' or a 'steak'. The word beefsteak points to that meat-like look and geranium was a garden name for similar houseplants, so the name now means a houseplant that is actually a 'begonia' and is known for big, colorful 'leaves'.
