Etymology

Scutellaria lateriflora comes from Latin words: scutella meaning 'small dish' and lateriflora meaning 'side flower', because its tiny cup-shaped flowers sit at the side of the stem like little caps, which gave it the common name 'skullcap'. So this image helps remember that people long used the herb to 'calm nerves' and reduce spasms.
