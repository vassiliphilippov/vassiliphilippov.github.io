Etymology

toying comes from the simple word toy, which originally meant a 'small, playful object' and whose verb form meant 'to play' or 'to treat lightly'. So to 'toy' with someone's feelings is like treating them as a plaything, which explains the modern sense of playful, teasing behavior that can flirt or show sexual interest.
