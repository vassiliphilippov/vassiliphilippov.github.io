Etymology

Rickettsiaceae comes from the scientist's name Ricketts plus the scientific family ending -aceae, which means 'family'. So Rickettsiaceae names the family of tiny organisms first linked to Dr. Ricketts, and that is why the word refers to a group of disease-causing microbes.
