Etymology

.22-caliber comes from the word caliber, which came from an Arabic word qalib meaning 'mould' and an Italian form calibro about the size a mould makes. The '.22' means 0.22 inch across the barrel, so .22-caliber simply tells you the gun or bullet's size.
