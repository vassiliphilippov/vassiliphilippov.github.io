Etymology

Notornis mantelli comes from two name parts: Notornis and mantelli. One popular story says Notornis uses old words for 'southern bird' and mantelli honors the scientist Mantell who studied New Zealand animals, so the full name reminds us of the large, flightless bird known as the takahē.
