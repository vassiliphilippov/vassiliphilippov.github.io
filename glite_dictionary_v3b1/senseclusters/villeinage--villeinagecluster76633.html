Etymology

villeinage comes from old words for a farm or village: villa meaning 'farm' (the same root as village) and old French vilain meaning 'country person', plus -age meaning 'state'. So villeinage was the 'state' of people tied to a manor, the serf's legal condition, the land they held, and the services they owed to a lord.
