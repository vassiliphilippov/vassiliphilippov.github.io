Etymology

Veronicastrum virginicum comes from the small flower Veronica plus the part -astrum meaning 'like', and virginicum meaning 'from Virginia'. So the name gives the image of a tall plant that looks like a big Veronica and comes from eastern North America, which fits its long flower spikes and native home.
