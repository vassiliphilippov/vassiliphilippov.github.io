Etymology

dispose in the sense 'get rid of' grew from the idea of 'putting aside' (Latin dis- 'apart' and ponere 'to put'), which led to the phrase 'dispose of' meaning 'put away' or 'throw away'. That's why to dispose of something is to get rid of it.
