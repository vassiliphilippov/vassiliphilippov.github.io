Etymology

mischief-maker comes from mischief, which once meant 'bad luck' or 'harm', and maker, 'someone who makes', so it literally means someone who makes trouble. A helpful memory is to see the mis- part like in mistake meaning 'wrong', which shows a mischief-maker is a person who creates playful or real trouble.
