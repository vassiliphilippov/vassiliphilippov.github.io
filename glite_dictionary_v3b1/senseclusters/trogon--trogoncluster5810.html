Etymology

The word trogon comes from the Greek trōgōn, from trōgein 'to gnaw', because these birds often gnaw holes in trees to make nests. So, think 'gnaw' to remember a trogon: a brightly colored tropical forest bird with a long tail that makes its home in holes.
