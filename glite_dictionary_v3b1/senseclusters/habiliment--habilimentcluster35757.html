Etymology

habiliment comes from the Latin root habil- meaning 'fit' and the ending -ment meaning 'a thing'. Think of habit, which once also meant a piece of clothing like a monk's habit, so habiliment is a 'thing' that fits you and means clothing or dress for a special occasion.
