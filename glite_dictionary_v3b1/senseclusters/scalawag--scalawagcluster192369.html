Etymology

scalawag came into American use after the Civil War as a harsh name for white Southerners who worked with Northern politicians during Reconstruction. It probably comes from older slang scallywag, meaning 'rascal' or 'scoundrel', so the word was used to show they were seen as untrustworthy or acting for their own gain.
