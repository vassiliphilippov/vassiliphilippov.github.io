Etymology

How-d'ye-do began as a spoken phrase like 'how do you do?', and a helpful way to remember it is to imagine that surprised talk becoming the name for a fuss. It is like the simple word to-do, which means 'fuss' or 'trouble', so a how-d'ye-do is an awkward or troublesome situation.
