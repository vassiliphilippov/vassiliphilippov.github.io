Etymology

Cleridae comes from the group name Clerus used by scientists and the scientific ending -idae, which means 'family' in animal names. Think of Canidae for dogs or Felidae for cats. Cleridae simply means the 'Clerus family' of beetles, which is why it names a family of checkered beetles that eat other insects.
