Etymology

common St John's wort may come from the plant's bright yellow flowers that often bloom around St John's Day, when people would pick it for special customs, and wort is an old word for 'plant' or 'herb'. That's why the name links the shrub to its long use as a healing 'plant' or simple 'medicine' for mood and wounds.
