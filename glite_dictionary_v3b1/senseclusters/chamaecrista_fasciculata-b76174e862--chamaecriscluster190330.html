Etymology

Chamaecrista fasciculata combines old words: Chamaecrista that suggests 'close to the ground' and fasciculata that means 'a small bundle or cluster'. This makes a clear image because the plant grows low with tight clusters of yellow flowers and leaflets that fold when touched, so the name matches the plant's look and behavior.
