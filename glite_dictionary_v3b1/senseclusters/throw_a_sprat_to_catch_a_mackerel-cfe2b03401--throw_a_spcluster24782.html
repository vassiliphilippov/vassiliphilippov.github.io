Etymology

throw a sprat to catch a mackerel comes from fishing: a sprat is a very small fish thrown as bait to bring in a larger mackerel, so fishermen would 'throw a sprat' to catch the bigger fish. So the saying means 'to give up a small thing to get something much larger'.
