Etymology

progressive vaccinia is named from two parts: progressive meaning 'keeps getting worse' and vaccinia referring to the virus used in the old smallpox 'vaccine'. In people with a weak immune system the small vaccine sore can keep growing and not heal, so the name describes this serious, growing infection after vaccination.
