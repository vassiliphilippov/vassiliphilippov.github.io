Etymology

Unintimidating comes from the prefix un- meaning 'not' and the root timid meaning 'fearful', so it literally says 'not making someone feel afraid'. Think of it as the opposite of intimidate, which means 'to make someone feel timid', so an unintimidating person is easy to approach.
