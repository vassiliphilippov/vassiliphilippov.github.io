Etymology

jammy stroke of luck paints a sweet picture: one popular story says jammy comes from the idea of finding a soft, sweet spot of jam inside a cake — a small, unexpected treat — and stroke of luck is a sudden lucky event. So a 'jammy stroke of luck' means a pleasant surprise that happens 'by chance'.
