Etymology

ultramarine blue gets its name from words that mean 'beyond the sea', with ultra meaning 'beyond' and marine meaning 'sea'. The deep blue pigment was made from a rare stone called lapis lazuli that came from far away and was shipped across the sea, so the name now refers to that rich, vivid blue color.
