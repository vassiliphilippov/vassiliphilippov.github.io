Etymology

The term Kronecker delta is named after the mathematician Leopold Kronecker and works like a simple on/off rule. It gives 1 when two numbers are the same and 0 when they are different, so it acts like a tiny switch that says 'yes' for equal items and 'no' for unequal ones.
