Etymology

The phrase conspiracy of silence comes from the image of a group secretly agreeing to keep quiet; conspiracy is a secret plan and silence is 'not talking'. That's why it means an agreed choice to avoid speaking about something, often to protect people or 'hide' wrongdoing.
