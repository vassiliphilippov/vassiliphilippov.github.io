Etymology

dashed hope comes from an old meaning of dash, 'to strike' or 'to break'. Imagine a plan with a quick hard line through it or a small boat hit by a wave, and that image of something smashed explains why dashed hope means a hope that is 'destroyed' or 'disappointed'.
