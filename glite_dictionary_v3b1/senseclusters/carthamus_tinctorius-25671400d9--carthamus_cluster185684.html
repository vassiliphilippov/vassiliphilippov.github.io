Etymology

Carthamus tinctorius gets its name from old words about dyeing, and the second part, tinctorius, means 'used for dyeing'. People long ago used the bright red-orange flowers to make color for cloth and to make oil, so the name helps you remember the plant was used for dye and useful oil.
