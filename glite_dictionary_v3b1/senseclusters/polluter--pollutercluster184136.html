Etymology

polluter comes from the verb pollute, which in turn comes from an old Latin root polluere meaning 'to make dirty'. You can also see the same root in pollution. So a polluter is someone who 'makes dirty' the air, water, or land, which is why we use the word for people or groups that cause environmental harm.
