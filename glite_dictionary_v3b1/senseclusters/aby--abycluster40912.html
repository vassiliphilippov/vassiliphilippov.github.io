Etymology

The word aby is an old verb meaning 'to make amends' or 'to pay the penalty' for a wrong. A helpful way to remember it is to think of a- + buy: you 'buy' back your mistake by paying or doing something to set it right, so to aby is to atone for a wrongdoing.
