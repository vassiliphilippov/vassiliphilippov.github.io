Etymology

Otolaryngology is made of three parts: oto- 'ear', laryng- 'voice box' or 'throat', and -ology 'study'. So otolaryngology is the medical study of the ear, nose, and throat, which is why doctors in this field treat hearing, breathing, and voice problems.
