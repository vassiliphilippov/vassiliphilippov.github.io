Etymology

consecration comes from Latin parts con- 'together' and sacrare 'to make sacred', from sacer 'sacred', the same root as sacred and sacrifice. So when we speak of someone's consecration to a goal, it means they have given their life or time as if it were 'sacred' to that purpose, showing deep and formal dedication.
