Etymology

Spodoptera exigua may come from old Greek and Latin: spodo meaning 'ash', ptera meaning 'wings', and exigua meaning 'small', so the name gives a picture of a small, ash-coloured moth. Its young are called 'beet armyworms' because they move over crops like a little army, so the name now refers to this moth species and its crop‑damaging larvae.
