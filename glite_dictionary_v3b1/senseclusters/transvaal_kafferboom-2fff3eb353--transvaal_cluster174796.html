Etymology

Transvaal kafferboom comes from the place name Transvaal and the Afrikaans word kafferboom, where boom means 'tree' and kaffer was an old word, now seen as offensive, that settlers used for local people. So the name once meant 'the tree near the local people' and today it simply names the South African tree with bright red flowers.
