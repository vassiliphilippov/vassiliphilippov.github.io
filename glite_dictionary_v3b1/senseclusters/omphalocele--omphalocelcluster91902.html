Etymology

omphalocele comes from Greek parts omphalo- meaning 'navel' or 'belly button' and -cele meaning 'hernia' or 'bulge'. So omphalocele literally means a 'navel hernia', which explains why it names the birth defect where a baby's organs bulge through near the belly button.
