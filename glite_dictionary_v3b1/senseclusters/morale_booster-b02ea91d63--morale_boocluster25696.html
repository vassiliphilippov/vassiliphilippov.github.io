Etymology

morale booster combines morale (the group's energy or 'spirit') and booster (something that gives a small push up). Imagine someone giving a quick push to lift a heavy box or bringing treats to cheer a team — that's why a morale booster means a small thing that raises a group's 'confidence' or 'spirit'.
