Etymology

The medical term lateral epicondylitis comes from doctor words: lateral means the outer 'side' of the elbow, epicondyle is the bony 'bump' there, and -itis means 'inflammation'. So it literally names an inflamed tendon at the outside of the elbow, usually from overuse, which is why it means pain on the outer elbow.
