Etymology

Santa Sophia comes from the words Santa and Sophia, meaning 'holy' and 'wisdom', so imagine a great shining 'house of wisdom' under a huge round roof. That's why Santa Sophia today is the name people use for the famous old building in Istanbul that was a church, then a mosque, and now a museum.
