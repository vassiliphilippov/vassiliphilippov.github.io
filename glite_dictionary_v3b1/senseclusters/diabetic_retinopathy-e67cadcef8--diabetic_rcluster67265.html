Etymology

diabetic retinopathy breaks into diabetic (about 'diabetes') and retinopathy (a disease of the retina). High blood sugar slowly damages the tiny blood vessels in the eye, like small pipes that leak or get blocked and make dark spots or blur your 'vision', so the name means eye damage caused by 'diabetes'.
