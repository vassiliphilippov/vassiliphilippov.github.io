Etymology

Federal Aviation Administration describes its job: Federal means the national government, Aviation means airplanes and 'air travel', and Administration is the office that makes and enforces rules. Picture officials watching the sky and writing safety rules for pilots; that's why the name means the US agency that keeps air travel safe.
