Etymology

oyster agaric may come from the mushroom's look and the words in its name: the soft, fan-shaped caps look like small oyster shells and they grow in layered clusters on dead wood. The word agaric was an old name for gilled mushrooms, so together the name points to a gilled mushroom that looks like an 'oyster', which is why it refers to this edible forest mushroom.
