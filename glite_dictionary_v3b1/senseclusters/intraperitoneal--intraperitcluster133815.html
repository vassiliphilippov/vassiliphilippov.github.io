Etymology

Intraperitoneal breaks into two parts: intra- meaning 'within' and peritoneum meaning the thin 'lining' around the belly organs. So, intraperitoneal describes something given or found 'inside' the space made by that lining, for example a medicine placed directly into the belly cavity.
