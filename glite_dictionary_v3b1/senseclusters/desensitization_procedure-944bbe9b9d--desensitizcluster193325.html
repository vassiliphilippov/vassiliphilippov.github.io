Etymology

desensitization procedure comes from the idea of making someone less sensitive: desensitization means 'making less sensitive' and procedure means a planned method. Imagine facing something scary in small steps until it feels less scary, so the phrase means a treatment where a person faces their fear slowly until the fear gets smaller.
