Etymology

irrecoverably comes from the negative part ir- meaning 'not' and recover meaning 'to get back', with -ly turning it into a word that describes how something happens. So if something is lost irrecoverably, it cannot be got back or fixed, like a file destroyed after a hard drive crash.
