Etymology

hackmatack comes from a Native American name for a local tree and is the same tree often called tamarack in English; people remembered it for its 'strong wood' used for tools and craft. So hackmatack simply names a North American tree, which helps explain why the word refers to this poplar with broad, heart-shaped leaves.
