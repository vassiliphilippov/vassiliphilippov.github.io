Etymology

puckerbush probably comes from its two simple parts: pucker (because the sour or strong berries make you 'pucker' or 'tighten your lips') and bush ('a small tree or shrub'). That's why the name fits this evergreen shrub with hard waxy berries and a strong smell, whose fruit was used long ago for making candles.
