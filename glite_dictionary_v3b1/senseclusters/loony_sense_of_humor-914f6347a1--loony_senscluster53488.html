Etymology

Loony sense of humor comes from the word loony, which means a bit 'crazy' and was once linked to the old idea that the moon made people act strange. So a loony sense of humor creates the image of someone who likes very silly or odd jokes, and that is why it means a taste for strange, playful comedy.
