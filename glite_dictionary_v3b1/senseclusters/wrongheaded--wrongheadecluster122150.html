Etymology

The word wrongheaded is a simple combination of wrong ('not correct') and head or headed ('head' as 'mind' or the direction you are going), similar to the familiar word headstrong. So it gives the picture of a person whose 'head' is set the wrong way, and means someone who keeps to wrong or misguided ideas.
