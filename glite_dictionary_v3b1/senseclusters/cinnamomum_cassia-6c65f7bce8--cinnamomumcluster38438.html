Etymology

Cinnamomum cassia is the name of a Chinese tree whose dried bark is sold as a common, strong-smelling 'type of cinnamon'. Because the bark is thicker and tastes sharper than 'true cinnamon', the name now usually refers to the stronger cinnamon people buy in many shops.
