Etymology

Fagus sylvatica purpurea literally describes the tree: Fagus is the beech, sylvatica means 'from the woods', and purpurea means 'purple'. Gardeners gave this name to a beech grown for its shiny purple leaves, so the name tells you it is a purple beech.
