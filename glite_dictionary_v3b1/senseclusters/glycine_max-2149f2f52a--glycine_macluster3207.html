Etymology

Glycine max may come from old Greek and Latin words: Glycine from a Greek word meaning 'sweet' because some related plants had sweet roots, and max from Latin meaning 'big' or 'great'. So the name points to the main, important soybean and helps you remember it is the large soybean grown for food.
