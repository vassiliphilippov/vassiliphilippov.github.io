Etymology

kinesthetically comes from Greek roots kinesis ('movement') and aisthesis ('feeling' or 'sensation'). It is related to the English word kinetic, so doing something kinesthetically means using your body and its movement to learn or understand, in other words 'by movement' rather than just by looking or listening.
