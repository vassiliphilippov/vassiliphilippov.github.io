Etymology

The noun hydrocyanic acid comes from hydro meaning 'water' and cyanic meaning 'cyan' (a blue chemical), so the name literally points to cyanide in water. Because it is hydrogen cyanide mixed in water, the name tells people it is a very poisonous 'acid'.
