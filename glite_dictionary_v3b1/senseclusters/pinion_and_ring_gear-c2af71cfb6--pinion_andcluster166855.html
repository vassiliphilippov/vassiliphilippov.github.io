Etymology

Pinion and ring gear is the name for a small gear, the pinion, whose teeth fit into the teeth of a large circle, the ring gear, so a small wheel turns a big ring to move power from one axle to another. That's why pinion and ring gear refers to that pair of gears that work together to 'send turning power' between axles, often at a 'right angle'.
