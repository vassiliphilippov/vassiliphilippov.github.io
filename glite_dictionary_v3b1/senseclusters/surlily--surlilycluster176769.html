Etymology

surlily comes from surly plus the ending -ly, so it means 'in a rude, unfriendly way'. Long ago surly was spelled sirly and meant 'like a sir', or proud, but its meaning later changed to 'rude', maybe because it began to sound like sour, and that is why surlily describes someone speaking or acting badly.
