Etymology

Penstemon dolius starts with Penstemon, from Greek for 'five stamens', the five little parts inside the flower. The second word dolius may come from a Latin word for 'barrel', a simple picture for the short, rounded clusters of violet and blue blooms, so the name helps you remember this low-growing plant.
