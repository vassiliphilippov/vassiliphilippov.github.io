Etymology

systemic lupus erythematosus comes from three parts: systemic means 'through the whole body', lupus comes from an old Latin word for 'wolf' because early doctors thought the face rash looked like a wolf bite, and erythematosus means 'red' for the typical rash. So the name reminds us the disease causes red rashes and can affect many parts of the body.
