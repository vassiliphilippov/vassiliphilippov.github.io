Etymology

The word tepidarium comes from the Latin tepidus 'lukewarm' and the ending -arium 'place for', so it suggests a 'place of mild warmth'. A helpful way to remember its modern use is to think of a meeting that is only a little warm — a tepidarium of conversation means a lukewarm atmosphere with little enthusiasm.
