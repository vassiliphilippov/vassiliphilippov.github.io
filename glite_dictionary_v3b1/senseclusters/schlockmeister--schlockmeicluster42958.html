Etymology

schlockmeister comes from schlock, a Yiddish and German word for 'cheap, low-quality goods', and meister, a German word meaning 'master' and like the English word 'master'. So a schlockmeister is literally a 'master of junk', which is why we use it for someone who makes or sells low-quality goods, often to make money.
