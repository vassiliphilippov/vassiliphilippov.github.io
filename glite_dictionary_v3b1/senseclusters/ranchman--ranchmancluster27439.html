Etymology

ranchman comes from the parts ranch and man, and ranch comes from the Spanish rancho, meaning 'a small farm or camp'. So a ranchman is a 'man of the ranch', like a farmer who works on a large cattle farm, which is why the word means a man who owns or works on a ranch.
