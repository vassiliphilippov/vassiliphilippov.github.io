Etymology

private foundation comes from the idea of a foundation as a base or support and the word private meaning it is started and paid for by one person, family, or company. Imagine a family building the base of a house with their own money — because the help comes mostly from that single private source, a 'private foundation' today means a charity supported mainly by one private source.
