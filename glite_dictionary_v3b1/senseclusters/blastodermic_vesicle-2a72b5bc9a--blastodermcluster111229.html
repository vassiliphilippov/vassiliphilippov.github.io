Etymology

blastodermic vesicle may come from Greek and Latin words: blastoderm meaning 'sprout' and 'skin' and vesicle meaning 'small sac'. So the name gives a clear picture of a tiny, skin-like sac of early embryo cells, which is why it refers to that early stage of development.
