Etymology

Paul Gauguin was a French painter who left Europe to live in Tahiti and other islands, and he painted people, places and bright, unusual colours from island life. Because he used strong colours and simple shapes to show those South Pacific scenes, people now remember him as a key artist of bold colour and island-inspired work.
