Etymology

Measuring unit may come from the simple idea of using one fixed thing—like a person's foot or a small stone—as the basic unit people used to measure objects. Because everyone compared things to the same measuring 'standard quantity', the phrase now means a fixed 'standard quantity' used to measure length, weight, or time.
