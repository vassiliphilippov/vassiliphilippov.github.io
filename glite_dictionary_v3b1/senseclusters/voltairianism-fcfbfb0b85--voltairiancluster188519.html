Etymology

Voltairianism comes from the name Voltaire, a famous French writer who used reason, fought against religious intolerance, and believed in a 'distant God'. The ending -ism means 'belief or system', as in feminism, so Voltairianism refers to the set of ideas from Voltaire: questioning and doubt, belief in a 'distant God', and a strong wish for free speech and tolerance.
