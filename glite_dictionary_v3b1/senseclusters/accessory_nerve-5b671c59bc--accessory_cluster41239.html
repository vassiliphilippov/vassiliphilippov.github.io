Etymology

The term accessory nerve uses accessory to mean a small 'helper' and nerve for the body 'wire' that sends signals to muscles. Because this nerve comes from both the brain and the spine and works with other nerves to move the neck and shoulders, it is seen as the 'helper' nerve that supports those movements.
