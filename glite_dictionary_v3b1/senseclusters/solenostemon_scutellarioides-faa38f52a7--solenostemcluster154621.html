Etymology

Solenostemon scutellarioides is a scientific name that describes the plant's look: Solenostemon points to tube-like flower parts and scutellarioides says it looks like a related small-flowered plant. Most gardeners use the short name 'coleus' and remember the plant for its bright, colorful leaves.
