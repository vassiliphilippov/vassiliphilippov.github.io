Etymology

The word yellow-orange is simply formed from yellow and orange, so it means a color that sits 'between yellow and orange'. A helpful way to remember it is to think of the fruit orange, which often has a yellow shine in sunlight, so yellow-orange describes an orange colour with a clear touch of yellow.
