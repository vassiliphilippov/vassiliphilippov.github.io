Etymology

ethinyl joins eth-, the 'two-carbon' part you see in words like ethanol, with -inyl, like vinyl, which means 'a small group attached'. So ethinyl as an adjective means 'containing or relating to that two-carbon group', and chemists use it to name molecules that have that part.
