Etymology

cobra chicken is a funny nickname for the 'Canada goose'. People started saying cobra chicken because the bird's long neck and loud hiss look a bit like a small cobra, and calling it a chicken makes the name playful, so the phrase now refers to that aggressive, long-necked bird.
