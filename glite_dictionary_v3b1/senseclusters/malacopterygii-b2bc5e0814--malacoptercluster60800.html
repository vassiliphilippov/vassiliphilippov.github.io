Etymology

Malacopterygii comes from Greek parts malaco- meaning 'soft' and -pterygii meaning 'fin', and the 'pter' part is the same you see in pterodactyl meaning 'wing'. So Malacopterygii are 'soft-finned' fishes, which fits because their fins are supported by flexible rays instead of hard spines.
