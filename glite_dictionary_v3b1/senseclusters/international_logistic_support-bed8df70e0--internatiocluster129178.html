Etymology

international logistic support paints a picture of a line of trucks, ships and planes carrying food, fuel and equipment across borders. The word logistic is about moving supplies and support means 'help', so the image of countries sending transport and supplies to each other explains why the phrase means arrangements for countries to help each other with transport and supplies, often in military or disaster work.
