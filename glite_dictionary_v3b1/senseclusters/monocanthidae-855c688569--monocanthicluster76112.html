Etymology

Monocanthidae breaks into parts: mono- 'one', acanth 'spine', and the family ending -idae 'family'. The mono- part is the same 'mono' you see in monotone, meaning 'one'. So, the name points to these filefishes often having a single, strong spine, which is why it is used for this family of fish.
