Etymology

baobab comes from old African and Arabic names for the tree, known in Arabic as bu hibab, meaning 'fruit of many seeds'. That's an easy memory hook: the baobab has a huge trunk and large fruit full of seeds and soft, edible pulp, so the name fits what the tree is like today.
