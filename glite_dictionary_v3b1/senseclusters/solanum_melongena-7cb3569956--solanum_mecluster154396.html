Etymology

Solanum melongena is the Latin name for the eggplant. The first part, Solanum, was a name for nightshade plants. The second part, melongena, may come from an old word for a round fruit like a 'melon', because early farmers thought the eggplant looked like a small melon, so the name points to the plant and its fruit.
