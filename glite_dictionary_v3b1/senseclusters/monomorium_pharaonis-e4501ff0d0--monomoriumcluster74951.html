Etymology

Monomorium pharaonis is the scientific name for the tiny ant often called the 'Pharaoh ant'. The word pharaonis may come from 'Pharaoh' because early naturalists linked the ant with Egypt, and that image helps you remember this small red house pest that sneaks into kitchens and hospitals.
