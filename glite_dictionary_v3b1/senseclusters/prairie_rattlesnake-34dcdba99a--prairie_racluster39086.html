Etymology

The noun prairie rattlesnake comes from two simple images: prairie is the wide grassland where it lives, and rattlesnake points to its tail that makes a loud rattle. That rattle is used as a 'warning', so the name means a venomous snake of the prairie that warns others before it bites.
