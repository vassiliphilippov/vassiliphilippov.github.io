Etymology

make mincemeat out of comes from the kitchen image behind make and mincemeat: cutting meat into tiny pieces called mincemeat. So when someone is 'made mincemeat out of', they are crushed or beaten badly, which is why the phrase means 'to defeat thoroughly'.
