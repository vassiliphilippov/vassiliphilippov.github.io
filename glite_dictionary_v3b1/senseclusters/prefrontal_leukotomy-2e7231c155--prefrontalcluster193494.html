Etymology

The term prefrontal leukotomy comes from the literal image of cutting connections at the front of the brain: prefrontal means the front part of the brain and leukotomy refers to making cuts in those nerve connections. Doctors did this operation to try to 'treat mental illness', but because it cut important nerve links it often changed a person's thinking and personality.
