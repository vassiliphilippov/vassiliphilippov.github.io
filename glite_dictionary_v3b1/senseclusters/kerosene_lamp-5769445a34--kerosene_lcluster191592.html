Etymology

kerosene lamp is a name for a lamp that uses kerosene, a liquid 'fuel' burned to make 'light'. Imagine a glass lamp with a wick soaked in kerosene that gives a steady flame, and that picture explains why the name means a lamp that runs on kerosene.
