Etymology

Petromyzontidae comes from Greek parts meaning 'stone' and 'to suck': petro- 'stone', myzon 'to suck', plus the family ending -idae 'family'. So, Petromyzontidae are lampreys, jawless fish with a round, sucking mouth that often attaches to rocks or other fish.
