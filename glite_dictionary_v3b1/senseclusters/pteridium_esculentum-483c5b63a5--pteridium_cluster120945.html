Etymology

Pteridium esculentum comes from old scientific words: Pteridium for a fern and esculentum meaning 'edible', which gives the picture of people gathering young fern fronds to eat. So the name helps you remember it is a fern that grows in mild, wet parts of southeastern Asia and does not survive harsh winters.
