Etymology

sapa comes from Latin sapa, which meant 'boiled grape juice' and became the Italian name for a thick, sweet syrup. A helpful way to remember it is to think of tree 'sap' — a sticky juice — because sapa is a thick, sweet juice used to flavor cakes and fruit.
