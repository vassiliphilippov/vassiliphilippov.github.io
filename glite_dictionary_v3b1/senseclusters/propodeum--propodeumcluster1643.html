Etymology

The word propodeum comes from Greek parts pro- meaning 'before' and pod meaning 'foot', so you can think of it as the part 'before the foot' on an insect's body. That's why the propodeum is the first abdominal segment joined to the thorax, sitting just behind the insect's middle legs.
