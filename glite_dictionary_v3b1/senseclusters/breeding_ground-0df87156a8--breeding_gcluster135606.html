Etymology

breeding ground comes from the image of animals gathering to have young on a safe patch of land, with breeding meaning making babies and ground meaning land. So when people call a place a 'breeding ground' today, they mean it is 'a place that helps something grow or spread', like insects, disease, or new ideas.
