Etymology

electrostatic unit comes from early scientists who measured how strongly two electric charges push or pull each other; they made a standard amount — a unit — from that force and named it after electrostatic effects. That's why it refers to a unit defined by the 'forces between charges' and is now mostly of historical interest.
