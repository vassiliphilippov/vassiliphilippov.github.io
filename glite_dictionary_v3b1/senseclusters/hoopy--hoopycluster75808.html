Etymology

hoopy was made popular by writer Douglas Adams in his book 'The Hitchhiker's Guide to the Galaxy' as slang for someone 'very cool' and 'together'. A helpful way to remember hoopy is to think of someone who is 'hip' and 'in the loop', so hoopy means being skilled, prepared, and confident.
