Etymology

dwarf daisy The plant name dwarf daisy comes from its look: dwarf means very small, and the tiny yellow flower heads really look like little daisy flowers. The plant often grows low to the ground with woolly leaves to keep water, so the name tells you it is a 'small, daisy-like' plant that survives in dry places.
