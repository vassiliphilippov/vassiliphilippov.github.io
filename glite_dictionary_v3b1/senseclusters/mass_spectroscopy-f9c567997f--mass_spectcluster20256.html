Etymology

The noun mass spectroscopy joins mass (the weight of tiny charged pieces) and spectroscopy (making a pattern, like a rainbow, so you can read different parts). In the lab, scientists turn molecules into charged pieces and spread them into a pattern to read their weights, so the term means 'to find the masses of small charged particles'.
