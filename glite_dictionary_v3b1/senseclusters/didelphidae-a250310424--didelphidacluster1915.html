Etymology

Didelphidae comes from Greek parts di- 'two' and delphys 'womb', and the ending -idae marks an animal family. Early scientists used the name because female opossums have a double womb, so Didelphidae is the 'family of animals with two wombs', which explains why it refers to opossums.
