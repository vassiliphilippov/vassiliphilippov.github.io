Etymology

Foaming bath gets its name from the clear image of a product that makes foam in the bath: you add small crystals, powder, or liquid to the water and lots of bubbles appear and smell nice. That's why it means 'a product added to bathwater to make bubbles, add scent, and soften the water'.
