Etymology

barbecue fork comes from two simple words: barbecue (cooking food over an open fire) and fork (a tool with two long prongs). Imagine holding a long fork to 'lift' and 'turn' meat on a hot grill — that picture explains why a barbecue fork is long and used for those jobs.
