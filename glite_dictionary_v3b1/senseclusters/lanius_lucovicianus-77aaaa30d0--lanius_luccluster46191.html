Etymology

Lanius lucovicianus is the scientific name for a small shrike with a broad black band across its eyes. The first part, Lanius, comes from an old word meaning 'butcher' because these birds often catch small animals and stick them on thorns. So, picturing a little masked 'butcher' bird helps you remember this species from the southeastern United States.
