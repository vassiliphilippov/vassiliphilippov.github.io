Etymology

protoplasmic comes from the parts proto- meaning 'first' and plasma meaning 'something formed', as in the familiar word prototype and the word plasma. So, it points to the 'first formed' living, jelly-like substance inside cells, and protoplasmic means 'relating to' that living jelly.
