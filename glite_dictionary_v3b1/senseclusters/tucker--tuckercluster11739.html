Etymology

tucker is used in the phrase 'tuckered out' to mean 'to make someone very tired'. A helpful way to remember it is to think of being so worn down by work or activity that you are completely 'tuckered', and the word likely comes from older uses of tuck about hard work or pulling, which is why it now means 'exhaust'.
