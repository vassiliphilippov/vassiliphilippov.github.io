Etymology

The exclamation oh my gosh comes from people wanting a softer way to say 'oh my God' so they replaced 'God' with the milder word gosh. Because gosh sounds like 'God' but is more polite, people use oh my gosh to show surprise, shock, or disbelief without upsetting others.
