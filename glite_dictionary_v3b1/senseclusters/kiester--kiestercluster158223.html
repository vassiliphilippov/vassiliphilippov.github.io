Etymology

kiester for the buttocks likely comes from an older word for a 'box' or 'chest' and is related to the German word kiste meaning 'box'. A helpful way to remember it is to imagine the 'rear' as a small box at the back of the body, which is why people began using kiester to mean buttocks.
