Etymology

aflare comes from the prefix a- meaning 'in' or 'on' and the word flare, which suggests 'flame' or 'to spread out'. So, think of a flag or a scarf 'aflare' streaming and spreading in the wind, which is why aflare means 'spreading out or streaming as if blown by the wind'.
