Etymology

D.A. stands for Doctor of Arts, a kind of doctor degree. The word doctor comes from an old Latin word docere meaning 'to teach', and arts here means 'study' or 'skills', so a D.A. is a doctor degree that focuses on teaching and practical work in a subject rather than on research.
