circular measure

0.00/m8.6

Etymology

circular measure may come from a simple image: the words circular and measure mean you measure an angle by the part of a circle it takes, like how much of the circle's edge is used. That's why 'circular measure' now means measuring angles in 'radians', where one full turn around the circle equals '2π'.
