Etymology

The word Mohammedanism is built from the name Muhammad and the ending -ism, so it once meant 'followers of Muhammad'. That sense is misleading and often offensive because the faith is called Islam, which means 'submission' to God, and its people are called Muslims, so today we use Islam or Muslim instead.
