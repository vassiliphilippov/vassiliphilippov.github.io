Etymology

crystallized comes from crystal, from an old Greek word krystallos meaning 'ice' or 'clear rock'. When fruit is covered in sugar the sugar makes tiny crystals on the surface, so the fruit becomes like little crystals — that is why crystallized means 'preserved with sugar'.
