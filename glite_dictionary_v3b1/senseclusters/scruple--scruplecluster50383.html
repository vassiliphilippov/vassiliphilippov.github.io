Etymology

scruple meaning a moral doubt comes from the Latin scrupulus 'small sharp stone'. Imagine a pebble in your shoe that makes you stop and think; that's why a scruple is a feeling of doubt or unease about doing something right or wrong, and the same idea is in the word scrupulous.
