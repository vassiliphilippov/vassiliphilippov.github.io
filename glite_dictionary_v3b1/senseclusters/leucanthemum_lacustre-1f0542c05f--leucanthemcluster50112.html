Etymology

Leucanthemum lacustre is a scientific name made from Greek and Latin words: Leucanthemum means 'white flower' and lacustre means 'of the lake' or 'living in marshes'. So the name gives a simple picture of a white, daisy-like plant that grows in wet places.
