Etymology

Pieris rapae is the scientific name for the 'cabbage white' butterfly; the pale white insect often flies over cabbage patches and lays eggs on the plants, so its name links its white wings to the vegetable it loves. That's why Pieris rapae today means the small white butterfly that feeds on cabbages.
