Etymology

anathematize comes from the Greek parts ana- 'up' and tithenai 'to place', and is related to anathema, which meant something 'set apart' either for God or as cursed. So to anathematize someone is to officially set them apart as wrong or 'cursed', which is why it means to formally condemn or curse.
