Etymology

Rangifer caribou mixes a Latin scientific name with a local animal name: Rangifer links it to reindeer, and one popular story says caribou comes from a Native word meaning 'to scrape' because the animals scrape snow to find food. So the name helps you remember it is a reindeer-like animal that lives in forests and scrapes snow to feed.
