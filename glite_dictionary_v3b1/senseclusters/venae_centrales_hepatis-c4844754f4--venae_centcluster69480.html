Etymology

venae centrales hepatis literally breaks into venae ('veins'), centrales ('central'), and hepatis ('of the liver'), so it names the small veins in the middle of each liver unit. Imagine the liver as many tiny round rooms with a drain in the center that collects blood and sends it out, and that clear picture explains why the term means the central veins that receive blood.
