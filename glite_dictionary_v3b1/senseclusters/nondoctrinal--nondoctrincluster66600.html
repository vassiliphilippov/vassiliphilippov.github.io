Etymology

The word nondoctrinal comes from non- meaning 'not' and doctrine meaning 'teaching' or 'official belief', and doctrinal means 'about those teachings'. So nondoctrinal means 'not based on official teachings or fixed beliefs', which is why it describes ideas or approaches that avoid strict doctrine.
