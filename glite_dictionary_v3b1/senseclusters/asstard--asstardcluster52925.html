Etymology

asstard is a rude blend of ass, a word for 'donkey' or a rude term for a stupid person, and the insulting ending -tard, which comes from a hurtful slur meaning 'slow'. So asstard is used to call someone extremely foolish or obnoxious, and it is strongly offensive.
