Etymology

The noun notorious jacker comes from the adjective notorious, meaning 'well known for bad things', and jacker, from the verb jack meaning 'to steal' or take by force. Picture a thief so famous that everyone knows his face; that image explains why the phrase means 'a person well known for stealing'.
