Etymology

Wankel rotary engine is named after its inventor, Felix Wankel, and makes power by turning instead of by pistons that move 'up and down'. Imagine a triangle-shaped part spinning inside a round ring, and that's why the engine is small and gives a smoother run than regular piston engines.
