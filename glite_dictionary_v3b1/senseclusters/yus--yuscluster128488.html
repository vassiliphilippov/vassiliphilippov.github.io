Etymology

The word yus was the old name for letters that marked a 'nasal' or 'through the nose' vowel sound in early Slavonic alphabets like Cyrillic and Glagolitic. A helpful way to remember it is to imagine a little 'nose' sound, and that's why yus refers to the special nasal vowel letter seen in Old Church Slavonic texts.
