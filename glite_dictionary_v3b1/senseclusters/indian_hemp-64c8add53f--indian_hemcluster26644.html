Etymology

Indian hemp also came to mean the cannabis plant from India that gave both strong fibers and a sticky resin used as medicine or for pleasure called 'bhang' or 'hashish'. The image of the same plant making rope and a resin helps explain why the term can mean a plant used for rope or for its drug uses today.
