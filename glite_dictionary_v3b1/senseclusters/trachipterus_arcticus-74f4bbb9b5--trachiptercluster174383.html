Etymology

Trachipterus arcticus may come from old words meaning 'rough fin' for Trachipterus and 'northern' for arcticus. This gives an easy picture of a long, ribbon-like silver fish with a noticeable fin living in deep northern waters, so the name points to how the fish looks and where it lives.
