Etymology

Symphalangus syndactylus is the scientific name for the 'siamang' and comes from old words meaning 'joined fingers'. The parts symphalangus and syndactylus point to the partly webbed second and third toes that look joined and help the animal hold branches, so the name refers to this trait.
