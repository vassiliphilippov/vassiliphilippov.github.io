Etymology

ruinously comes from ruin, an old word meaning 'a fall' or 'collapse', and you can picture ruins — broken buildings or destroyed things. With -ous and -ly it means 'in a way that causes ruin', so ruinously describes something so expensive or damaging it makes things fall apart.
