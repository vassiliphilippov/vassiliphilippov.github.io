Etymology

Kyphosus sectatrix may come from its name parts: Kyphosus from a Greek word for 'hump' (the fish's rounded back) and sectatrix from Latin for 'follower', because these fish often swim near and follow boats. That's why the name fits a species found around Bermuda and Florida that is known to follow ships.
