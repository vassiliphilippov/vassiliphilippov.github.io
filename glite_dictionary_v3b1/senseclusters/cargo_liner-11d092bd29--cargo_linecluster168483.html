Etymology

cargo liner comes from the words cargo (goods) and liner (a ship that sails on a set 'line' or route). Picture a ship like a bus that follows the same path and carries goods on a 'regular schedule', and that is why cargo liner means a large ship that carries cargo on fixed trips.
