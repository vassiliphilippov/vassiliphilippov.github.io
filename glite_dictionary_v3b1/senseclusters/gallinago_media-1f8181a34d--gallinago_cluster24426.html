Etymology

Gallinago media is the scientific name for the 'great snipe'. The name combines Gallinago, the snipe group, and media, the species word, so imagine a heavy, dark snipe flying and making a loud display over wet meadows to attract a mate; that picture helps you remember that Gallinago media means this larger, darker snipe today.
