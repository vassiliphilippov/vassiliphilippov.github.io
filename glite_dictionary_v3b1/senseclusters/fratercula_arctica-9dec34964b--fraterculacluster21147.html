Etymology

Fratercula arctica comes from old Latin words: Fratercula means 'little brother' or 'friar' because the puffin's black-and-white feathers look like a monk's coat, and arctica means 'northern' because the bird lives in cold northern seas. That's why this name is the scientific name for the 'Atlantic puffin', a seabird with a bright, colorful beak found in the northern Atlantic.
