Etymology

Campanula persicifolia comes from old Latin words that describe the plant: Campanula means 'little bell' and persicifolia means 'peach-leaved'. That picture of tiny bell-shaped flowers and leaves like a peach helps explain why this name fits the blue or white bellflower.
