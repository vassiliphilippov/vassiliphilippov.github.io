Etymology

triceps brachii: The name triceps brachii comes from Latin: triceps means 'three-headed' and brachii means 'of the arm'. Picture a muscle with three parts on the back of the upper arm that pull together to straighten the elbow, so the name shows why it 'extends the forearm'.
