Etymology

Gabonese Republic comes from the country's name Gabon, which one popular story says comes from a Portuguese word for 'cloak' because early sailors thought the wide river mouth looked like a cloak. When the country became independent in 1960 it used the 'republic' name, so Gabonese Republic simply means the nation of Gabon as a republic.
