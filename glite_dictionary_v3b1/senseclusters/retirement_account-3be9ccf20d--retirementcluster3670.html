Etymology

Retirement account comes from the idea of a special account where you keep money for when you retire, or 'stop work'. Imagine a locked box or piggy bank labelled 'retirement' where you add money bit by bit so you can use it 'later', and that's why the phrase means a savings plan to use after you leave work.
