Etymology

water program comes from joining water, which shows what is being worked on, and program, which means a planned set of actions. Imagine a town making a step-by-step plan to clean a river, fix pipes and save drinking water. That picture shows why the phrase means 'an organized plan to improve or manage a body of water'.
