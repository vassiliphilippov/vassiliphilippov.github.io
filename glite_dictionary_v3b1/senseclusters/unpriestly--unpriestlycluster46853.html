Etymology

The word unpriestly is made from un- meaning 'not', the noun priest meaning 'a religious leader', and -ly which makes a word describe something, just like in unhappy. So unpriestly means 'not like a priest' or 'not fitting a priest', which is why it describes behaviour that seems wrong for someone in that role.
