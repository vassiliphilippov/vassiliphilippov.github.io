Etymology

sandarac comes from an old Mediterranean name for a fragrant tree and its sap, later used in Europe as sandarach and probably from Arabic sandarūs. So when sandarac refers to wood, think of a hard, fragrant timber taken from that North African tree and prized for strong, fine work.
