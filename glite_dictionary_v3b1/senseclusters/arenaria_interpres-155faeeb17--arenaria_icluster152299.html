Etymology

Arenaria interpres comes from Latin: Arenaria means 'of the sand' and interpres means 'messenger' or 'interpreter', because people saw the bird flip stones and seaweed on the shore to find food. So the name points to its habit of turning things on the beach, which is why people call it the 'turnstone'.
