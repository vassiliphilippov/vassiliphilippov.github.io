Etymology

The word flatteringly comes from flatter, which once meant 'to stroke' or 'smooth' someone with words, like 'stroking' them with praise to make them feel good. So, to speak flatteringly is to give praise that makes someone seem more attractive or impressive, often by exaggerating.
