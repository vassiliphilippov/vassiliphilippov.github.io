Etymology

Mentha rotundifolia is a plant name that literally describes the herb: Mentha means 'mint' and rotundifolia means 'round leaf', so it points to a mint with small, round leaves. People also call it 'apple mint' because the stems smell a little like apples, and that is why it is used in teas and gardens.
