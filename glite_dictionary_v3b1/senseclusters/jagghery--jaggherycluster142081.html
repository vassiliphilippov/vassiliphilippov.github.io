Etymology

jagghery comes from an old Sanskrit word guda, which means 'sweet' or 'sugar' and was used in India for blocks of unrefined cane or palm sugar. So jagghery still means the brown, unrefined sugar used to sweeten dishes in South Asian and African cooking.
