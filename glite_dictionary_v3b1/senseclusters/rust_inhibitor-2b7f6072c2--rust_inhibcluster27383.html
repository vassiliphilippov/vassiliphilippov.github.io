Etymology

rust inhibitor comes from the simple image of a protective coat for metal: the part rust is the red damage that appears on metal and the part inhibitor is something that stops it, so a rust inhibitor acts like a shield or oil that keeps water and air away. That's why it means 'a substance that prevents or slows rust'.
