Etymology

Hynerpeton bassetti comes from the place Hyner in Pennsylvania where its fossils were found and from the name bassetti honoring the person who helped discover them. The part -peton means 'creature', so the name helps you remember it was an early animal with legs that lived long ago and was found at Hyner.
