Etymology

Bradley method is named after Dr. Robert Bradley, who taught that a woman's partner should be her main 'coach' during labor. Think of a couple practicing breathing, relaxation and nutrition with their coach called Bradley, and you can see why the name means a way to prepare for 'natural childbirth' together.
