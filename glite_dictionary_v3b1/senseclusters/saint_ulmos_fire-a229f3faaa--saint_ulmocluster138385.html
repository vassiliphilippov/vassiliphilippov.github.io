Etymology

Saint Ulmo's fire comes from old sailors who saw a blue or violet light on the tips of a ship's masts during storms and thought a saint was protecting them. One popular story says the name links Saint Ulmo, a protector of sailors, and the bright fire they saw, so today the phrase means 'a blue electrical glow' on pointed objects like masts or plane parts.
