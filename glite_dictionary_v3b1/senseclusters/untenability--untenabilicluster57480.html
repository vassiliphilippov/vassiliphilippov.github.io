Etymology

untenability comes from the prefix un- 'not' and tenable, from the Latin verb tenere 'to hold', and it shares the same root as tenant and retain. So untenability means something that cannot be 'held' or defended, which is why we use it for ideas, claims, or plans that cannot be kept or justified.
