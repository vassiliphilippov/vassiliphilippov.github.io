Etymology

indecent is formed from the prefix in- meaning 'not' and decent, which comes from an old word meaning 'fitting' or 'proper'. So indecent literally means 'not proper', and we use it for behaviour, language, or clothes that people find offensive or not polite, often with a sexual meaning.
