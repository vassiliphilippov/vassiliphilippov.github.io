Etymology

cubic boron nitride is named for what it is: the elements boron and nitride, where nitride means a 'form of nitrogen', are arranged in a box-like cubic crystal. Because this cube-like structure is 'very hard', it is used to cut and grind tough materials, so the name helps explain its use today.
