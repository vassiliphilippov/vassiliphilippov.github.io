Etymology

officinalis comes from the Latin officina, which meant a 'workshop' or 'storeroom' and later a 'medicine shop'. So when a plant name includes officinalis, it shows the plant was kept in old medicine shops and used for healing, which is why the name marks plants with medicinal use.
