Etymology

sinusoidal projection comes from the map's shape: the equator and parallel lines are straight while the other meridians bend in smooth, wavy curves like a 'sine' wave. Because of this shape, the map 'keeps the correct size' of places, so mapmakers use it when they need true areas, especially near the tropics.
