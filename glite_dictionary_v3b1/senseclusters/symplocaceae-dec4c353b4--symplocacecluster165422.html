Etymology

Symplocaceae comes from the plant name Symplocos, from Greek words that mean 'together' or 'woven'. The ending -aceae marks a plant 'family', so Symplocaceae is the family of plants related to Symplocos, and you can remember the 'sym-' part from symphony ('sounds together').
