Etymology

The noun ang moh may come from a word in a Chinese dialect that meant 'red hair'. ang means 'red' and moh means 'hair', a simple image locals used for pale or fair-haired Europeans. That's why today ang moh is used in Singapore and Malaysia to mean 'foreigner' or a 'Western person'.
