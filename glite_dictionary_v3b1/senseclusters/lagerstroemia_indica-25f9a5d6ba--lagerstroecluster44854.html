Etymology

The scientific name Lagerstroemia indica is named after a Swedish plant collector, Magnus Lagerström, who collected these plants in eastern India and sent them to Europe. The second word indica means 'from India', so the name shows both the person and the place and helps you remember this crape myrtle comes from India.
