Etymology

warm atmosphere comes from the simple picture of a room that is warm — with soft heat, gentle light and cozy air — and an atmosphere that makes people feel safe. One popular image is friends talking comfortably in that room, so the phrase came to mean 'a friendly and welcoming environment'.
