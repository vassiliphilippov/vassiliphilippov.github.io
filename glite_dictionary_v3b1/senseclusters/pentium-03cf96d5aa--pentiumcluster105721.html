Etymology

Pentium is also used to name a whole personal computer when it has a Pentium chip inside, a bit like calling a car by its engine type; a helpful way to remember this is to think of the computer as being named after its 'engine' the Pentium processor. So when people say a PC is a Pentium, they mean it uses an Intel Pentium chip.
