Etymology

Cimicifuga americana: The plant name Cimicifuga americana comes from Latin roots cimex and fugare, where cimex means 'bug' and fugare means 'to drive away'. So the name literally means 'American bug-bane' and was used for a North American plant people thought would keep insects away.
