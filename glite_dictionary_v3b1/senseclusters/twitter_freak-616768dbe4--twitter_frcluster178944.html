Etymology

Twitter freak comes from the website Twitter, which is named after short bird 'tweets', and the word freak, meaning someone who is obsessed. Imagine a person who keeps 'tweeting' like a busy bird and follows every trend, so it means 'a person who is extremely enthusiastic or obsessed with using Twitter'.
