Etymology

pico- was chosen for the metric system from Spanish and Italian words for 'a very small bit', similar to Italian piccolo ('small'). That's why pico- means 'one trillionth' (10^-12) and a pico-second is 'one trillionth' of a second, marking very tiny amounts.
