Etymology

oversaturation comes from over- ('too much') and saturate ('fill completely'), like a sponge that cannot take any more water. A helpful image is a website full of ads or a field with too much fertilizer; when there is too much, things stop working well. So, oversaturation means there is more of something than can be handled or used.
