Etymology

Remonstrate comes from the Latin parts re- 'again' and monstrare 'to show', and it shares the same root as demonstrate and demonstration, which can mean a public show or protest. So, to remonstrate is to 'show again' your objection or complaint — to argue or protest strongly.
