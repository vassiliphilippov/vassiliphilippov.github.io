Etymology

wondrously comes from the simple word wonder meaning 'a feeling of surprise and pleasure', with -ous meaning 'full of' and -ly meaning 'in a way'. So, to do something wondrously is to do it 'in a way full of wonder', that is, amazingly or very beautifully.
