Etymology

The phrase oasis in the city comes from the image of an oasis in a desert: a small green place with water and shade surrounded by dry land. When people call a park or coffee shop an oasis in the city, they mean it is a calm, restful spot that gives relief from noise and busy streets, so it now means 'a peaceful place' in a busy urban area.
