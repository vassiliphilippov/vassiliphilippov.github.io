Etymology

proclaimer comes from the Latin parts pro- 'forth' and clamare 'to shout', the same root you can see in words like claim. Picture a person loudly sharing their beliefs in a town square 'shouting forth' their message, so a proclaimer is someone who publicly announces or preaches ideas.
