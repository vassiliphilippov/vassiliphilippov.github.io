Etymology

thresh comes from an old English verb threscan meaning 'to beat', and farmers used it for beating grain so the seeds fall out. Think of people hitting grain on a hard floor or a machine called a thresher doing the same job. That's why thresh means to separate seeds from a grain by beating.
