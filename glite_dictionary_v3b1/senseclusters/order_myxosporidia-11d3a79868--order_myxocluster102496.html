Etymology

order Myxosporidia gets its name from two parts, myxo and sporidia, which come from words meaning 'slimy' and 'spores'. It is a 'group' of tiny, single-celled 'parasites' that make tough 'spores' and often live in fish, so the 'slimy + spores' image helps you remember what they are and do.
