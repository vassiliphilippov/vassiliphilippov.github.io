Etymology

observer's meridian comes from a simple picture: imagine a straight north-south line in the sky that goes through the exact point above you, linking the observer and the meridian. When the sun crosses this line at 'local noon', people used it to tell time and find their place on Earth, so today the term means the north-south line that matches where the observer stands.
