Etymology

inwardness comes from inward (made of in 'inside' + ward 'toward') plus the noun ending -ness meaning 'a state or quality'. So inwardness is the idea of turning attention 'inside' yourself, which explains why it means a focus on your own thoughts and feelings or thinking about yourself.
