Etymology

Sayeret Mat'kal comes from Hebrew words: Sayeret means 'reconnaissance' or 'scouting unit' and Mat'kal means 'general staff', the army's top leaders. So the name paints a picture of a small, top team that scouts for the army leaders, and that is why it refers to Israel's elite special forces who do secret missions and hostage rescues.
