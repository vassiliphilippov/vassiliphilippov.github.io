Etymology

The word read-out comes from the verb read ('to look at and understand words or numbers') and the word out ('out' or 'spoken so others can hear or see'), so it means information that is 'read out' by a machine. Think of a screen or meter that shows numbers for you to read—that display or the numbers themselves are the read-out.
