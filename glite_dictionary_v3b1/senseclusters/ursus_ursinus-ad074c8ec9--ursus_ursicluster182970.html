Etymology

The name Ursus ursinus may come from Latin: ursus meaning 'bear' and ursinus meaning 'bear-like'. So the name is like saying 'bear-like bear', a simple picture to help you remember it refers to the sloth bear, the shaggy, long-snouted bear that eats insects.
