Etymology

anaerobic exercise is literal: anaerobic means 'without oxygen' and exercise means activity. Think of a sprinter or someone lifting very heavy weights, working all-out for a few seconds so their muscles burn because the body can't get oxygen fast enough, and that's why anaerobic exercise describes short, high-power efforts that 'build strength' rather than long, steady workouts.
