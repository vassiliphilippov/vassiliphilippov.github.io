Etymology

The word adenomatous comes from the Greek root aden- meaning 'gland', shown in the medical word adenoma meaning 'gland tumor', and the ending -ous meaning 'full of'. So, adenomatous means 'having gland-like tissue', which is why doctors use it for growths or tumors made of gland cells.
