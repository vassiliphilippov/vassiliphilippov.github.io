Etymology

nongonococcal urethritis comes from two parts: nongonococcal meaning 'not caused by gonorrhea' and urethritis meaning 'inflammation', which is swelling and pain in the urethra, the tube that carries urine. So, it refers to an inflammation of that urine tube that is not caused by gonorrhea, often from other infections like chlamydia.
