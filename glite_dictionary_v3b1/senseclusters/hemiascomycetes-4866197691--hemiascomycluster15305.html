Etymology

Hemiascomycetes comes from parts meaning hemi- 'half', asco 'sac', and mycetes 'fungi'. So the name means 'half-sac fungi' and helps you remember that this group, including many yeasts and some plant parasites, do not make the full sac structures that other fungi have.
