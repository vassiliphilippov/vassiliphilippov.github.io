Etymology

B vitamin gets its name from early scientists who found a helpful group of 'vitamins' and simply gave the group the letter B as the second set they discovered. Later they found the group had several different ones like B1, B2 and B12, so today B vitamin means any one of that family that helps the body make energy and stay healthy.
