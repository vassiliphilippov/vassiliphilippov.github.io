Etymology

air reconnaissance comes from the early days when soldiers used balloons and then airplanes to look down on enemy camps and gather information. The parts air and reconnaissance literally mean 'from the air' and 'to scout', so today it means 'to observe or collect information about enemy positions from the air'.
