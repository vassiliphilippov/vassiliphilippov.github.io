Etymology

respiratory alkalosis comes from two parts: respiratory meaning 'breathing' and alkalosis meaning 'blood that is too alkaline'. Imagine breathing very fast and blowing out too much gas from your body so the blood becomes less acidic. So the term means 'a condition when fast or deep breathing makes the blood too alkaline'.
