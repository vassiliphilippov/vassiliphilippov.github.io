Etymology

Laurentian Plateau gets its name from the nearby St. Lawrence area, Laurentian, and the word plateau, which means 'high, flat land'. Long ago, huge sheets of ice moved over the region and left a wide, rocky high area, so today the name refers to that large high region in eastern and northern Canada.
