Etymology

blood-oxygenation level dependent functional magnetic resonance imaging literally describes how it works: when a part of the brain is active it needs more blood-oxygenation, and the magnetic resonance imaging machine detects those level-dependent changes. So the name means a kind of MRI that shows 'brain activity' by following oxygen changes in the blood.
