Etymology

spin a story may come from the image of someone turning wool on a wheel to make thread and then weaving it into cloth. Like turning thread into cloth, to spin a story means to twist small ideas into a whole, often exaggerated tale, so it now means 'invent or tell an exaggerated story'.
