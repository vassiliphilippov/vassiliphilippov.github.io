Etymology

irreligiousness breaks into three parts: ir- meaning 'not', religious meaning 'connected to religion', and -ness meaning the 'state' of something. The ir- is the same 'not' you see in irregular, so that part is easy to spot. So, irreligiousness is the 'state' of being 'not religious', or not having religious belief.
