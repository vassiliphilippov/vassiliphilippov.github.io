Etymology

The phrase featherless biped comes from an old story about philosophers. One famous story says a thinker defined humans as featherless, meaning no feathers, and as a biped, meaning an animal with two legs. A student plucked a chicken and called it that, so today the phrase is a playful way to mean 'human being'.
