Etymology

Tabernaemontana divaricata was named after a 16th-century botanist who used the name 'Tabernaemontanus'. The species word divaricata means 'spreading apart' in Latin and gives a clear image of the shrub's wide branches and open, wavy white flowers, so the name fits the plant.
