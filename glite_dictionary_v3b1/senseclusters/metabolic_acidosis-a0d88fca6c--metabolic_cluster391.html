Etymology

metabolic acidosis combines metabolic meaning 'the body's chemical processes' and acidosis meaning 'too much acid'. Picture the body's fluids slowly filling with acid because those processes make too much or cannot remove it, so the phrase means 'too much acid in the body' and explains why it is a health problem.
