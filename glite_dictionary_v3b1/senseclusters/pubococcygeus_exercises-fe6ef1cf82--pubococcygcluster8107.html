Etymology

pubococcygeus exercises are simple moves for the pubococcygeus muscle, a band that runs from the pubic bone to the tailbone and works like a small 'hammock' under the bladder and other organs. By tightening that 'hammock' with short squeezes, people can improve 'bladder control' and pelvic health, so the name shows what the exercises do.
