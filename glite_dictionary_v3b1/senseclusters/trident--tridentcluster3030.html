Etymology

The word trident comes from the Latin parts tri- ('three') and dent ('tooth'), so it literally means 'three-toothed'. A helpful way to remember it is that tri- is the same 'three' you see in triangle and dent is the same 'tooth' in dentist, which explains why a trident is a spear with three prongs and a symbol of sea gods.
