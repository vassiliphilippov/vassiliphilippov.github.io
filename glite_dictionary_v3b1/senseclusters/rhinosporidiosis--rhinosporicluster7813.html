Etymology

Rhinosporidiosis breaks into parts: rhino- meaning 'nose', spore meaning 'tiny cell', and -osis meaning 'disease'. So it literally means a 'nose spore disease', which fits because it is a long-lasting infection that makes growths in the nose and often comes from stagnant water.
