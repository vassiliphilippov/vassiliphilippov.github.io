Etymology

European larch simply points to the tree's type: European shows where it grows and larch is the old name people used for this tall tree with soft needles. The word larch may come from the old Latin 'larix', and because this tree loses its needles in autumn it stood out from other trees that keep their needles, so the name came to mean the European kind of larch.
