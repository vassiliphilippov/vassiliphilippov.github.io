Etymology

iridosmine comes from the names of the two metals iridium and osmium, and iridium comes from iris, which means 'rainbow' and appears in the word iridescent. Because iridosmine is a natural mix of these very hard metals, the name helps you remember it is a tough metal mix that does not rust and is used to make pen nibs and needles.
