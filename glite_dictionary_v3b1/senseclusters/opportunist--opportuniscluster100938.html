Etymology

opportunist comes from the Latin word opportunus, the same root as opportunity, and originally meant 'favorable' or 'coming into a harbor' from ob- 'to' and portus 'harbor'. So an opportunist is someone who jumps on a 'favorable' chance to gain personal advantage.
