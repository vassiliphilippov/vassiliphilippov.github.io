Etymology

fungal infection The compound noun fungal infection joins the words fungal and infection. Fungal comes from the word 'fungus', meaning mould or small mushrooms, and infection means something enters the body and causes illness, so picture mould or tiny mushroom-like growth on the skin to remember it means a 'disease' caused by fungus.
