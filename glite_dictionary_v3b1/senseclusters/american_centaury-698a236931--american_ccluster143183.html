Etymology

American centaury gets its name from the older plant name centaury, which comes from a story about a wise centaur who used wild plants to heal people. When a similar pink marsh plant was found in America, people called it American centaury because it looked like the original, so the name shows it is a similar, 'healing' kind of plant.
