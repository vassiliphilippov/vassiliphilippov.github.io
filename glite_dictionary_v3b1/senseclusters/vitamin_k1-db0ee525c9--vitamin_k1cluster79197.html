Etymology

vitamin K1 may come from the letter K, chosen by early scientists from a German word for 'clotting', and the number 1 marks the plant form found in green leaves. So imagine a green leaf with 'K1' on it to remember that vitamin K1 helps 'blood clotting' and keeps bones healthy.
