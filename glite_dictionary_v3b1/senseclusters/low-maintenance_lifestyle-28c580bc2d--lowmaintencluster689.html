Etymology

low-maintenance lifestyle comes from the idea of low-maintenance things that need little care, like a plant that rarely needs water or a car that rarely needs repairs. So a low-maintenance lifestyle means a way of living that is 'simple and easy' and needs little time, effort, or money.
