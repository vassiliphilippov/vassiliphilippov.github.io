Etymology

malacostracan comes from Greek parts malakos and ostrakon, which mean 'soft' and 'shell'. Think of a 'soft-shell crab' — that image helps because malacostracan is the name for crabs, lobsters and shrimp, animals that shed their shells and can be soft for a time.
