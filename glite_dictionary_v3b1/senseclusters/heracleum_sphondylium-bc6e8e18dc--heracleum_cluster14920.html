Etymology

Heracleum sphondylium may come from Greek: Heracleum is named for the hero 'Heracles' because the plant is tall and strong, and sphondylium may come from a word for 'vertebra' or 'knot' that points to its thick, jointed stems. So, the name gives the image of a big, sturdy plant, which fits the tall, rough plant we know today.
