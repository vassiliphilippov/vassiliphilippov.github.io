Etymology

Frederick William I was King of Prussia from 1713 to 1740 who made the army very strict and strong. Imagine a king like a strict drill sergeant who trained soldiers to be perfectly ordered and ready for war, and that image explains why he is remembered for reforming and strengthening the Prussian army.
