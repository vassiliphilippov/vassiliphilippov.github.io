Etymology

retail market comes from old market scenes where sellers would cut large goods into small pieces and sell them directly at stalls, so retail suggests selling in small amounts and market is the place to buy. So today retail market means the place or industry where goods are sold straight to 'consumers' rather than to 'producers' or other sellers.
