Etymology

bosom of a jacket comes from the old use of bosom for the front of the chest and the warm, inner part of a jacket. Imagine tucking a small letter close to your heart inside the jacket where it is safe and out of sight, and that's why the phrase means 'the inner pocket or place to hide small things'.
