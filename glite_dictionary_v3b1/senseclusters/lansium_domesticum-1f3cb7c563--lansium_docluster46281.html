Etymology

Lansium domesticum probably comes from the local word 'langsat' for the fruit and the Latin word domesticum meaning 'of the home'. So the name gives a clear image: a common tree people grow near their homes that bears the sweet 'langsat' berries, and that is why it names this edible tree today.
