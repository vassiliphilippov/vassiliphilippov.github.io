Etymology

colorful blinkie comes from the early internet, where people put small, bright pictures that flashed on their profiles to 'decorate' them. The word blinkie comes from blink (to flash) with a little '-ie' ending, so it now means a small, moving, colorful picture used to make a page stand out.
