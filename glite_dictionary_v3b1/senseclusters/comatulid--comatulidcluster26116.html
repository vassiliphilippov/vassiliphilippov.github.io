Etymology

The word comatulid comes from an old root coma meaning 'hair', the same idea behind the word comet, which once meant a 'long-haired' star. So a comatulid is a sea animal with many feather-like 'hairs' for arms, and that 'hairy' look helps you remember its feathery arms and how it swims freely.
