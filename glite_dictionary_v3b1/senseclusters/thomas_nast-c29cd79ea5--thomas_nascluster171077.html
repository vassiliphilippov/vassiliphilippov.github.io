Etymology

Thomas Nast was a 19th-century American cartoonist who drew clear, powerful pictures, like Uncle Sam and the elephant and donkey, to explain politics to many people. Because his drawings made these images famous, his name now stands for the person who created many of today's 'political symbols'.
