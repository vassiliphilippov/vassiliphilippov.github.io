Etymology

amobarbital comes from the drug family name barbital, seen in barbiturate, which were drugs used to bring 'sleep' and 'calm'. The amo- part marks a particular chemical form of that family, so amobarbital is a specific barbiturate used as a sedative and to help control seizures.
