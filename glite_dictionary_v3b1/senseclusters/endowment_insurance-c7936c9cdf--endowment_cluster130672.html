Etymology

endowment insurance comes from endowment (money set aside like a 'gift') and insurance (protection). Imagine paying small amounts that build into one clear 'lump sum' you receive at the end of a set time, or that same money goes to your family 'if you die' during that time, so the name now means a policy that pays a fixed sum at the end or on death.
