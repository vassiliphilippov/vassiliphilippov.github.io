Etymology

virginals (the small keyboard instrument) likely gets its name from the old word virgin, from virgo, which means 'maiden' or 'young woman'. A helpful way to remember this is that the instrument was often played by young women and has a pure, delicate sound, so the name links to 'virgin' and the idea of a light, simple tone.
