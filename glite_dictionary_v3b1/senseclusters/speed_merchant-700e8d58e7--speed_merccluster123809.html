Etymology

speed merchant may come from a playful image: a merchant sells things, so calling someone a speed merchant makes it sound as if they 'sell' or trade in speed, a picture of a driver who loves going fast. That's why it means 'a person who drives very fast' or 'is passionate about speed'.
