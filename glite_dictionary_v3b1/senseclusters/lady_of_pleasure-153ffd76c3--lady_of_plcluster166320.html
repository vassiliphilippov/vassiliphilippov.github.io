Etymology

lady of pleasure is an old polite phrase used instead of a rude word for a woman who worked as a 'prostitute'. People joined lady (a polite word for a woman) with pleasure (enjoyment) to soften the idea, and that image of a woman tied to 'pleasure' is why the phrase means a woman who sold sexual services.
