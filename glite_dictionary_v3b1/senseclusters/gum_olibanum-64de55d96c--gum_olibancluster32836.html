Etymology

Gum olibanum is named for the sticky, milky sap people collect from certain trees; gum means the resin and olibanum may come from an Arabic word for 'milk' because the drops look white. People burned this resin as 'incense' in ceremonies and to make rooms smell nice, so today the name means the aromatic substance used in those 'rituals'.
