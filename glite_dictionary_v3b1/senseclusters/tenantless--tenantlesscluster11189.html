Etymology

The word tenantless breaks into tenant and -less. Tenant comes from the Latin word tenere meaning 'to hold', the same idea you see in retain, so a tenant is someone who 'holds' or lives in a place, and -less means 'without', so tenantless means 'without someone renting or living there'.
