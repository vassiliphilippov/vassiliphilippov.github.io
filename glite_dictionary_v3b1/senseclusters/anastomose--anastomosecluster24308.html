Etymology

Anastomose comes from Greek parts ana- 'again' and stoma 'mouth' or 'opening', so think of joining two 'mouths'. Picture linking two straws or tubes so liquid can flow between them, and that's why to anastomose means to join tubes like blood vessels or ducts to make one continuous tube.
