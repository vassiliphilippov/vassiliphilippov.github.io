Etymology

The noun cottonmouth moccasin comes from the snake's habit of opening its mouth to show a white, cotton-like lining, which is the cottonmouth part, and moccasin is a local name for this water snake. When it shows that white mouth it is warning, so the name is used for a 'dangerous water snake'.
