Etymology

gynoecium comes from Greek parts gyno- meaning 'woman' and oikos meaning 'house', and the oikos part is the same root you see as 'eco' in eco-friendly. That's a useful picture: the gynoecium is the 'woman's house' of the flower where the female parts sit and seeds form.
