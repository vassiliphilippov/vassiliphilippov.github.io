Etymology

The noun Cartesian coordinate system is named after the French thinker René Descartes and gives a simple picture: two number lines cross at a point called the origin, making the x and y directions. By counting steps along those lines you can 'locate' any point, so the name now means 'a way to find positions on a plane'.
