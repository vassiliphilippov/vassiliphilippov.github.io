Etymology

The word Miltown is a brand name for the drug meprobamate, a medicine that calms anxiety and relaxes muscles. A helpful way to remember the name is to see Miltown as sounding like mild + town, giving the image of a 'mild' or 'calm' place, which fits the drug's calming effect.
