Etymology

Dewey decimal classification comes from Melvil Dewey, a librarian who in the late 1800s made a simple system to sort books by number. He gave each subject a three-digit code and extra numbers after a dot, so books looked like they had address numbers on their spines — that's why the phrase means 'a way to organize books' in libraries.
