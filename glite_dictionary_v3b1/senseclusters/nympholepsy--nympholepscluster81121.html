Etymology

nympholepsy comes from Greek parts nymph (a myth figure of perfect beauty) and -lepsy meaning 'seizure', like in epilepsy. Think of being 'seized' by a nymph — carried away with a strong, often impossible longing for an ideal, which is why nympholepsy means wanting something you cannot reach.
