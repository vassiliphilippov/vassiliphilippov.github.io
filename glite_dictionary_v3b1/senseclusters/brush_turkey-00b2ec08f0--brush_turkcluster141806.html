Etymology

brush turkey is a name that comes from how people saw the bird: it lives in the brush, meaning thick bushes, and looks a little like a turkey. The clear image of a large bird scratching in the bushes and building big mounds helps explain why brush turkey means 'a large ground bird that lives in bushy areas'.
