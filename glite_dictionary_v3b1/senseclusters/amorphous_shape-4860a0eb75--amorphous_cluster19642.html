Etymology

amorphous shape comes from the simple image of a soft blob, like a cloud or a puddle, that has no clear edges and keeps changing. The word amorphous means 'without a clear form' and shape is the form, so this image explains why the phrase means 'a shape that lacks a clear structure or definition'.
