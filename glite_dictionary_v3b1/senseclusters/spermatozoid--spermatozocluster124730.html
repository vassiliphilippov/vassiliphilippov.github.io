Etymology

Spermatozoid comes from older Greek parts spermato- meaning 'seed' and -zoid meaning 'like' or 'form', and it is related to the English word sperm which also means 'seed'. So a spermatozoid is a 'seed-like' moving cell that swims to reach and fertilize an egg.
