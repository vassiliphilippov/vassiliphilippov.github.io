Etymology

The word Falconiformes comes from falcon meaning the bird and the ending -iformes, which comes from a word for 'form' or 'shape', so you can remember it as 'in the shape of a falcon'. That's why Falconiformes is the name for the group of birds of prey, like falcons, hawks and eagles, that share similar hooked beaks and strong talons.
