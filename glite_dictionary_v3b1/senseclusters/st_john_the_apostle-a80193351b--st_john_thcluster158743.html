Etymology

St. John the Apostle comes from Christian stories that say John was one of Jesus's twelve close followers. He is called 'the beloved disciple' because he is shown leaning on Jesus at the Last Supper, and people later believed this close friend wrote the Gospel, some letters, and the Book of Revelation, so the name refers to that disciple and traditional author.
