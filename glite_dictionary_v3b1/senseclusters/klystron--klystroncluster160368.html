Etymology

The word klystron comes from a Greek root klyzein meaning 'to wash' and the ending -tron meaning 'device', as in electron. One helpful way to remember it is to think of the tube 'washing' or pushing a stream of electrons into tight groups to make and amplify microwave waves, which is what a klystron does.
