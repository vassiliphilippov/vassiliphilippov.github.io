Etymology

Gooseneck barnacle gets its name from its long, curved stalk that looks like a goose's neck, so the part gooseneck describes the shape. The barnacle uses that stalk to attach itself to rocks, floating wood or boat hulls, so the name helps you picture and remember this sea animal.
