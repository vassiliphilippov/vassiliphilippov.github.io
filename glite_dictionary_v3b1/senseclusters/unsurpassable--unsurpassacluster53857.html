Etymology

unsurpassable breaks into familiar parts: un- meaning 'not', surpass meaning 'to go past' (think of the simple word pass), and -able meaning 'can be'. So something unsurpassable cannot be 'gone past' or beaten, which is why it means the best and impossible to improve.
