Etymology

The word sensualize comes from the root sens- meaning 'sense' or 'feeling' and the ending -ize meaning 'to make'. So to sensualize in art or writing is to make an idea appeal to the senses — to make it feel touchable, vivid, or strongly sensory for the reader or viewer.
