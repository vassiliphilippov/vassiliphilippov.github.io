Etymology

rheumatoid spondylitis comes from old medical words: rheumatoid refers to 'rheumatism', a condition with joint pain, and spondylitis combines the words for 'spine' and 'inflammation'. So the name literally means inflammation of the spine, which explains the stiffness and loss of movement.
