Etymology

genus Pothos combines the Latin word genus meaning 'a group' and the Greek word Pothos, which may come from a word for 'longing' because the plant's long vines seem to reach out for support. So, the name helps you remember it is a group of climbing plants whose vines 'reach' and cling to trees.
