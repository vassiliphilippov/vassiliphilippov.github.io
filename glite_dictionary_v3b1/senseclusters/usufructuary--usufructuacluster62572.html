Etymology

usufructuary as an adjective comes from the same Latin parts usus ('use') and fructus ('fruit' or 'benefit'), giving the image of 'using the fruit' of something. That's why usufructuary describes a right or interest that lets you use and enjoy property and its benefits without being the owner.
