Etymology

The word contemplation comes from Latin parts con- ('with') and templum ('a marked or sacred space') where people would stand to watch the sky, so it originally meant quietly watching or thinking in a special place. That's why contemplation now means long, quiet thinking or looking thoughtfully at something.
