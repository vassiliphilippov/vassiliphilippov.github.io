Etymology

The term electrical elastance comes from the image of a spring that pushes back when you stretch it, with electrical showing it is about electric charge and elastance meaning 'stiffness'. So, it describes how much a material 'opposes' or resists 'holding' electric charge, the opposite of capacitance.
