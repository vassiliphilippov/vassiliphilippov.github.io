Etymology

Japanese angelica tree may come from where the plant grows and how it looks: Japanese because it comes from Japan, angelica because its large, umbrella-like flower clusters looked 'angelic' or like the herb called angelica, and tree because it grows like a small tree. That's why the name helps you remember it as a showy, tree-like plant often grown in gardens.
