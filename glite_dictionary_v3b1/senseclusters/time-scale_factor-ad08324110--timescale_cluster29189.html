Etymology

The term time-scale factor comes from the simple image of a speed dial for time: imagine a film projector with a number that shows how much you speed up or slow down the action, with time and scale showing what is measured and factor meaning the multiplying number. So the time-scale factor is the number that tells how much 'faster' or 'slower' a simulation runs compared to real life.
