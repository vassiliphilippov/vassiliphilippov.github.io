Etymology

The noun laundry stiffener comes from the old habit of adding starch to wet clothes so collars and cuffs dried hard. The parts laundry and stiffener are literal: laundry is the washed clothes and a stiffener is something that makes them stiff, so the phrase means 'a substance added to laundry to make fabrics stiffer'.
