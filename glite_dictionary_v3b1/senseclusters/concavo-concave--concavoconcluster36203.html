Etymology

Concavo-concave comes from Latin parts con- meaning 'together' and cavus meaning 'hollow'. Think of two small hollows like the English words cave or cavity, or the inside of a bowl on both sides. So a concavo-concave surface curves inward on both sides, as in some lenses or mirrors.
