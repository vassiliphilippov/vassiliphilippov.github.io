Etymology

The name true flycatcher comes from birdwatchers and scientists who wanted to mark the 'real' flycatchers in one bird family. The word true means 'real' and flycatcher shows a small bird that sits and darts out to catch insects in the air, so the name tells you it really catches flying insects.
