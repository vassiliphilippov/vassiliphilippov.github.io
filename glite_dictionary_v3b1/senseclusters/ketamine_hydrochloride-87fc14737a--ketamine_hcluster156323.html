Etymology

ketamine hydrochloride is a chemical name: ketamine comes from words for its basic chemical parts and hydrochloride shows it is made into a stable 'salt', so it becomes a white powder or liquid that is easy to give as medicine. Because of this stable form, doctors and vets use ketamine hydrochloride as an 'anesthetic' and 'tranquilizer' to make people or animals sleep safely for procedures.
