Etymology

hemosiderosis breaks into three parts: hemo- meaning 'blood', sider- meaning 'iron', and -osis meaning 'condition'. So hemosiderosis describes a condition where iron from blood 'builds up' in the body's tissues, which is why doctors use it for that medical problem.
