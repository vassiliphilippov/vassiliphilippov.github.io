Etymology

empyrean comes from a Greek word meaning 'in fire', because people long ago imagined the highest heaven as a bright, pure fire. The root pyr means 'fire', the same root in English pyre, so empyrean as an adjective means 'heavenly' or like the highest, shining part of the sky.
