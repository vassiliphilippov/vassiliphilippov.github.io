Etymology

pure muppetry comes from the TV show 'The Muppets' and the word muppet, a silly puppet people began using to mean a foolish person. So muppetry became a word for silly or stupid behavior and 'pure' means 'completely', which is why pure muppetry means 'behavior that is extremely foolish'.
