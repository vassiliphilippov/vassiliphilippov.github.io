Etymology

The word anamorphism is made of ana- 'up' or 'again' and morph 'form', the same 'morph' in metamorphosis, so imagine a picture that looks wrong until you move to the right place or use a mirror. That is why anamorphism names an image or painting that appears normal only from a certain angle or with a special view.
