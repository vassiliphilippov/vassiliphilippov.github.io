Etymology

The term Aristotelian logic comes from the Greek thinker Aristotle, who showed how to put ideas into short, step-by-step arguments called syllogisms, simple two-step arguments. So, Aristotelian logic means a way of thinking that uses these short steps to 'reach a conclusion' from given facts.
