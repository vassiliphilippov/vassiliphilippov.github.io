Etymology

The Moscow Kremlin gets its name from the word Kremlin, a Russian word that means a 'fortress' or 'citadel', a walled place with towers where rulers lived and worked. Because this strong, walled complex sits at the heart of Moscow and is where the country's leaders meet, Moscow Kremlin also stands for the 'Russian government'.
