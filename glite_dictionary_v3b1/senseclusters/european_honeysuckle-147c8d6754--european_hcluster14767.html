Etymology

European honeysuckle gets its name from people sucking the sweet nectar from its long, tube-shaped flowers. The parts honey, for the sweet taste, and suckle, an old word for 'to suck', explain the name, so today it refers to a shrub known for honey-sweet flowers and bright fruit.
