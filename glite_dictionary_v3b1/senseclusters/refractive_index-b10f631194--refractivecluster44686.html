Etymology

refractive index comes from the idea of 'refraction', which is light bending when it goes into something like water. For example, a straw looks bent in a glass. The word refractive points to that bending and index means a number that compares how fast light moves in empty space and in the material, so the term tells how much the material slows and bends light.
