Etymology

wear out shows the idea of using up a person's energy until they cannot go on. Imagine carrying a heavy bag all day or a child playing without stopping; the activity slowly wears you down, and the parts wear meaning 'use' and out meaning 'completely' explain why it means 'to make someone very tired'.
