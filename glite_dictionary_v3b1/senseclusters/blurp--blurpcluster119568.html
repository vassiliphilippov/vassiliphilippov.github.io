Etymology

Blurp is a playful sound word that copies a short silly noise used as a quick reaction. A helpful way to remember it is to think of burp, 'a sudden sound', and blurt, 'to say something suddenly', so blurp is used as a quick comic interjection for mild surprise or embarrassment.
