Etymology

George Armstrong Custer was a United States Army general who is best known for his defeat and death at the Battle of the Little Bighorn, where he and a small group of soldiers were surrounded. The strong image of his 'last stand' — a small force overwhelmed by a larger enemy — is why his name is remembered and often used when people talk about a famous defeat or a risky decision.
