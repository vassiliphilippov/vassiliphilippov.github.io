Etymology

lycaenid comes from the old scientific name Lycaena, a Greek name once used for small butterflies and linked to lykos 'wolf', a surprising image that can help you remember these tiny, showy insects. So, a lycaenid is one of the small, brightly colored butterflies in that group.
