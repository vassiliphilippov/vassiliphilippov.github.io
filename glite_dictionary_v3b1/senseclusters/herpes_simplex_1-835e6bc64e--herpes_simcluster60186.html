Etymology

herpes simplex 1 gets its name from older words: herpes comes from a Greek word meaning 'to creep' because the sores can spread along the skin, and simplex means 'simple' to show it is the common, milder form. That's why the name refers to the virus that usually causes cold sores around the mouth.
