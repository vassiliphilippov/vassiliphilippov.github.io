Etymology

The word singerie comes from the French singe ('monkey') and the ending -erie (a kind of thing), so it originally meant 'monkey-thing'. Think of the English verb 'ape', which means 'to copy'; singerie now means a silly or mocking imitation of human behavior, like people 'aping' each other.
