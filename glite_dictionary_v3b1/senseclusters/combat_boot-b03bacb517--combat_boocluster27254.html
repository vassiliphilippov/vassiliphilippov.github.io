Etymology

combat boot comes from the simple image of a strong boot used in combat, meaning 'fighting' by 'soldiers'. Because these boots were heavy and lace-up to protect the ankle, today combat boot means a tough, lace-up shoe first made for soldiers and now often worn for 'work' or 'fashion'.
