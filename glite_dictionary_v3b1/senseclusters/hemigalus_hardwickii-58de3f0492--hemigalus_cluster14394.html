Etymology

Hemigalus hardwickii comes from old words and a person's name: Hemigalus means 'half-weasel', giving the image of a small, thin animal, and hardwickii shows it was named after a man called Hardwicke. So the name helps you remember this small, slender banded palm civet from Southeast Asia.
