Etymology

transversus abdominis comes from Latin words: transversus meaning 'across' and abdominis meaning 'of the abdomen'. The name fits the muscle's image — a flat band that runs like a belt across the front and sides of the belly, so it squeezes the inside organs and helps 'support the spine' and keep the body stable.
