Etymology

arteria ethmoidalis comes from old Latin and Greek words: arteria means 'artery' and ethmoidalis refers to the ethmoid bone, a small 'sieve-like' bone just behind the nose. That's why the term means the artery that runs to and gives blood to the area around the nose.
