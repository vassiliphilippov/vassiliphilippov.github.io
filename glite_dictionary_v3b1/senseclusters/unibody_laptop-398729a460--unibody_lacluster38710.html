Etymology

The noun unibody laptop comes from how the case is made: the word unibody means 'one body', because the laptop's case is formed from a single solid piece instead of many parts. That one-piece build makes the laptop look smooth and feel stronger, so 'unibody laptop' means a laptop made from one piece for better strength and style.
