Etymology

infectiousness is also used for feelings or ideas that spread quickly; a helpful way to remember this is that infect originally meant 'to make into' something, so an 'infectious' laugh can 'make' other people laugh. So infectiousness here is the quality of causing emotions or ideas to 'spread' from person to person.
