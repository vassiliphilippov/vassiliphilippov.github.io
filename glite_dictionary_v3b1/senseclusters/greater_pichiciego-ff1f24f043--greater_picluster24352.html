Etymology

greater pichiciego comes from Spanish parts pichi, meaning 'small', and ciego, meaning 'blind', and the word greater shows this is the larger type. The image of a small, nearly blind armadillo that lives underground with pink armor helps explain why the name refers to a rare burrowing armadillo that is hard to see in the wild.
