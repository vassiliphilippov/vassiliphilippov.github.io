Etymology

Satureja calamintha glandulosa comes from Latin parts that describe the plant: Satureja is a kind of savory herb, calamintha means 'like mint', and glandulosa means 'full of small glands' that give a strong smell. So the name paints a picture of a minty, savory plant with many little scent glands, which explains its strong aroma and use in cooking.
