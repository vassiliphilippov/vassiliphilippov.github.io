Etymology

beat someone's brains out uses a very strong image: beat (to hit) and brains (the mind in the head), as if someone were hit so hard their 'brains' were badly injured. That's why it can be a real threat, 'to hit someone violently', and by exaggeration it is also used to mean 'to work or think very hard'.
