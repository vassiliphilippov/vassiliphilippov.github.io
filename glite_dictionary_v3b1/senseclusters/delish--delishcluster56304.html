Etymology

delish is a short, informal form of delicious, a word that comes from an idea of 'delight' or 'great pleasure'. So delish keeps that meaning and is used to say something is 'very tasty' or pleasing to the taste.

Surface Forms

  • delish - positive (base)
  • delisher - comparative
  • delishest - superlative
