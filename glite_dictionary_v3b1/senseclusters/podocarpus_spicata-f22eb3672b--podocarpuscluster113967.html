Etymology

Podocarpus spicata may come from old Greek words podo meaning 'foot' and carpus meaning 'fruit', because its small seed cones sit on a short stalk like tiny feet. The spicata part suggests a 'spike' shape of the cones, so the name paints a simple picture of an evergreen tree with small, footed, spiky fruits to help you remember it.
