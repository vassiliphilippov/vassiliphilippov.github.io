Etymology

The word iontotherapy combines ion 'charged particle' and therapy 'treatment'. The ion part comes from an old Greek word meaning 'to go', because ions are tiny particles that move in an electric current, so iontotherapy is a treatment that uses electricity to send medicinal ions into the body.
