Etymology

Datura comes from an old Indian name, dhattūra, which reached Europe and English through Spanish. People once called the plants 'thorn apple' because of the round fruit, and since many Datura species are poisonous and can cause strong, dream-like visions, the name is linked to both traditional medicine and danger.
