Etymology

synovial fluid may come from an old word for 'egg white' because doctors long ago thought the shiny, slippery liquid in joints looked like raw egg white. The word synovial comes from that old word and fluid means 'liquid', so synovial fluid refers to the thick, slippery liquid that helps joints move smoothly.
