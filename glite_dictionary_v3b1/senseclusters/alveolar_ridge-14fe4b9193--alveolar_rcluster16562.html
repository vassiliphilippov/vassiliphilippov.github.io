Etymology

alveolar ridge comes from the idea of a raised edge in the mouth: alveolar links to a 'small hollow' that holds tooth roots and ridge means a raised edge, so the alveolar ridge is the bony line that holds the tooth sockets and helps make some speech sounds when the tongue touches it.
