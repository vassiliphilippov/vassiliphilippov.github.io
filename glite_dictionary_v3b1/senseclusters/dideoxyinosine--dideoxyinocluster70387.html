Etymology

dideoxyinosine breaks into di- ('two'), deoxy ('without oxygen'), and inosine, a natural molecule in cells. So it is an inosine that is 'missing two oxygens', and that small change helps the drug stop the HIV virus's copying machine, which is why dideoxyinosine is used as an antiviral medicine.
