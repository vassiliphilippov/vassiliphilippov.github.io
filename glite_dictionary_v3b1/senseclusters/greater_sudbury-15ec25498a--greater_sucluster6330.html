Etymology

Greater Sudbury comes from the old town name Sudbury and the word Greater, showing it is the larger area formed when nearby towns joined. One popular idea says Sudbury may come from words meaning 'south' and 'fort', so you can picture a 'southern fort', and that link helps explain the city's name today.
