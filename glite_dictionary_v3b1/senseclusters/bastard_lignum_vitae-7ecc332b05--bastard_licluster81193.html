Etymology

The noun bastard lignum vitae likely comes from early settlers who found a small evergreen that looked like the true lignum vitae but was a different type of tree. They used bastard to mean 'false' or 'not the real one', so the name points to a similar, very hard wood that is not the original lignum vitae.
