Etymology

confession of judgment comes from a simple, literal idea: the person who owes money writes a confession, a 'written admission of debt', so the lender can get a judgment from the court without a normal trial. So, it means a written agreement that lets a creditor obtain a court decision against a debtor without a full court case.
