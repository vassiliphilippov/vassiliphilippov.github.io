Etymology

William Tecumseh Sherman was a United States general in the Civil War who is best known for capturing Atlanta and leading a destructive 'March to the Sea'. Picture his soldiers destroying farms, railways and towns to cut the South's supplies, so his name is remembered for that harsh way of fighting and the Union victory.
