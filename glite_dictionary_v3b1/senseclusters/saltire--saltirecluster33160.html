Etymology

Saltire comes from old French and the Latin word saltare, which means 'to jump', and the same jumping root is in the English word somersault. Think of two diagonal lines crossing like legs in a leap — that X-shape is why a saltire is the X-shaped cross you see on flags like Scotland's.
