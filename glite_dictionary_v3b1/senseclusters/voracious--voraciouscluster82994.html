Etymology

The word voracious comes from the old Latin vorare, meaning 'to swallow' or 'to devour'. Think of someone who 'devours' food or books, and remember it has the same root as devour and the -vore in carnivore, so voracious means very hungry or having a huge appetite for food, ideas, or new experiences.
