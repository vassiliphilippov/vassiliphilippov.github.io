Etymology

sunnily comes from sun and the adjective sunny, where sunny means 'bright' and 'cheerful', and the ending -ly turns it into an adverb. So to do something sunnily is to do it in a 'bright' or 'cheerful' way, like a sunny day that lifts your mood.
