Etymology

tone-deaf to others' feelings comes from music: someone who is tone-deaf cannot hear or match a musical note, so they miss the sound. Imagine that person in a conversation — they cannot 'hear' other people's feelings, so the phrase means 'unable or unwilling to understand other people's emotions'.
