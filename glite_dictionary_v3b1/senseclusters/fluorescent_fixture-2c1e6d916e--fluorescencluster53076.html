Etymology

fluorescent fixture comes from a simple picture of a long glass tube that glows when electricity passes through it, which is the fluorescent part, and a metal or plastic holder that keeps the tube in place and gives it power, which is the fixture part. So the phrase means a lighting unit that 'holds' and 'powers' a glowing 'lamp'.
