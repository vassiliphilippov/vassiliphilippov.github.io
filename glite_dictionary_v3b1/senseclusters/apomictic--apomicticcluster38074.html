Etymology

The word apomictic comes from Greek parts apo- ('away from') and mixis ('mixing'), and you can see the same idea in the English word mix. So, an apomictic plant makes seeds 'without fertilization' — that is, the seed is made 'without mixing' with another plant, producing a copy of the parent.
