Etymology

septicemia comes from Greek parts septi- meaning 'rot' or 'infection' and -emia meaning 'blood'. So septicemia literally means 'infection in the blood', which is why it can make the whole body very sick, and you can remember the parts from the word septic and from anemia for the -emia part.
