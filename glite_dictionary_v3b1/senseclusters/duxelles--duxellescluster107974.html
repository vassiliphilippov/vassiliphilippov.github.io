Etymology

The word duxelles comes from French and probably from the name d'Uxelles, which means 'of Uxelles' and points to a person or place. One popular story says a cook or noble called d'Uxelles made this finely chopped mushroom mix, so the name stuck and now means that mushroom filling used in cooking.
