Etymology

Trichosurus vulpecula is a scientific name that comes from old Latin and Greek words: Trichosurus means 'hairy tail' and vulpecula means 'little fox', a picture of its bushy tail and fox-like face. So the name points to the common brushtail possum and helps you remember this animal.
