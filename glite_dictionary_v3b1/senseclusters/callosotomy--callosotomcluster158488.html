Etymology

The word callosotomy comes from parts you can see: calloso- (like callus, meaning 'hard' or 'thick') and -otomy (meaning 'cutting'). So a callosotomy is the 'cutting of the hard band' that connects the two sides of the brain, which is why it is a surgery used to stop severe seizures.
