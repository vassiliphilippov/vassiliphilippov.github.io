Etymology

Japanese black pine names a tree from Japan with dark bark and long needles. The parts are literal: Japanese shows where it comes from, black points to its dark look, and pine is the type of tree, so the name simply describes a garden tree people use for decoration and for planting near the sea because it resists salt and pollution.
