Etymology

plexus mesentericus comes from Latin and gives a clear image: plexus means a braided 'network' and mesentericus refers to the 'mesentery', the thin tissue that holds the 'intestines'. Imagine a braid or net of nerves wrapped in that tissue supplying the gut, and that's why the term means 'a network of nerves that supplies the intestines'.
