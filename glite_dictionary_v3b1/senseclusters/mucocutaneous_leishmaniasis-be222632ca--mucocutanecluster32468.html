Etymology

mucocutaneous leishmaniasis comes from its parts: mucocutaneous joins mucous, the wet linings inside the mouth and nose, and cutaneous, the skin, while leishmaniasis is the disease name. So the name tells you it causes 'sores on the skin and mucous membranes'.
