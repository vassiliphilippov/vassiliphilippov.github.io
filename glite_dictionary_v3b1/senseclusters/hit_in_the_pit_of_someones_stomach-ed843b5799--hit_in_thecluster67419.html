Etymology

hit in the pit of someone's stomach comes from the image of a sudden hit or punch to the empty place just below your chest, the pit of your stomach, where you feel a sharp, sick feeling. So when bad news hits you like that, you feel sudden 'fear' or 'shock' in your body.
