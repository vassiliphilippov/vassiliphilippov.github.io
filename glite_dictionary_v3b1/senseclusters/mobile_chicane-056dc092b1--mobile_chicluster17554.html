Etymology

mobile chicane comes from a simple picture: a chicane is a row of barriers or a quick zigzag in the road that forces drivers to slow, and mobile means it can be moved. So a mobile chicane is a movable, temporary barrier used to 'slow down' or 'control traffic'.
