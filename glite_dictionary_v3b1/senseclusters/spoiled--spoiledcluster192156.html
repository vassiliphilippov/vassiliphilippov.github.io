Etymology

The word spoiled comes from an old Latin verb spoliare, which meant 'to strip' or 'to ruin' and later became the English verb spoil meaning 'to damage'. So when food is spoiled, it has been 'ruined' or has gone 'bad' and is not safe to eat.
