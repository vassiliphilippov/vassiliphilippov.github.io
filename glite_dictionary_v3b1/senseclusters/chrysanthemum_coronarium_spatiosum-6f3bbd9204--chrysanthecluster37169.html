Etymology

Chrysanthemum coronarium spatiosum gets its name from older words that describe the plant: chrysanthemum means 'gold flower', coronarium points to plants used for 'crowns', and spatiosum suggests 'spreading'. So, the name fits a wide, leafy plant whose tender, edible leaves are grown and eaten in many Asian dishes.
