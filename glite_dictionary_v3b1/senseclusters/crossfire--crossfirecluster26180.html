Etymology

The word crossfire comes from the same parts, cross ('to meet or go across') and fire ('to shoot'). A helpful way to remember the second meaning is to imagine words 'shooting' back and forth from both sides, so being in the crossfire means being caught in a fast back-and-forth attack of opinions.
