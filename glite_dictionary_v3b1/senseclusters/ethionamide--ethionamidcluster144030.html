Etymology

The word ethionamide breaks into ethio- and -amide. A helpful way to remember it is to think of ethio- like the eth- in ethanol, a familiar chemical name, and -amide as a common chemical ending, so ethionamide is the name of a particular medicine used to treat tuberculosis.
