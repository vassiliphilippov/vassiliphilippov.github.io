Etymology

perforated eardrum simply describes the thin skin inside the ear called the eardrum that has a small hole, the perforated part. Because the hole lets sound escape and lets water or germs enter, it means 'a hole or tear in the eardrum' that can cause 'hearing loss' and infections.
