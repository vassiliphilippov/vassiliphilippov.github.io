Etymology

Pinot Chardonnay may come from a wine maker idea to make a lighter, crisper wine by using Chardonnay grapes in a style like Pinot. That's why the name links Pinot and Chardonnay and helps you remember it is a 'dry white table wine' similar to 'Chablis'.
