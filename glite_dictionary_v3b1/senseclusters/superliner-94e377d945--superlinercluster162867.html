Etymology

Superliner comes from super- meaning 'above' or 'very' and liner as in 'ocean liner' or 'airliner', meaning a regular passenger vehicle. So a Superliner is an 'extra large' liner — the name Amtrak uses for its large, double-decker long-distance train car.
