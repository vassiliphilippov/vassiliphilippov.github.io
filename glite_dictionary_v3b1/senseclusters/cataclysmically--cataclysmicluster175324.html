Etymology

cataclysmically comes from the noun cataclysm, built from the Greek part kata- ('down') and a root that means 'wash' or 'flood', giving the image of a great flood washing things away. So when something happens cataclysmically, it happens like a huge flood — it causes very large, violent change or damage.
