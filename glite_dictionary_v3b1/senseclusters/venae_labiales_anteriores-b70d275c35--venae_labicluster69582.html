Etymology

The term venae labiales anteriores literally names venae meaning 'veins', labiales meaning 'of the labia', and anteriores meaning 'front'. Imagine small tubes at the front of the outer lips that carry blood away, so the name refers to the veins that drain blood from the 'labia majora'.
