Etymology

The word kaleidoscopical comes from Greek words kalos 'beautiful', eidos 'shape', and skopein 'to look'. Think of a kaleidoscope, which makes ever-changing colorful patterns, so kaleidoscopical describes something that keeps changing in bright, complex patterns.
