Etymology

Scotch malt whiskey is a name that tells you where and how it is made: Scotch means 'from Scotland', malt means the drink is made from barley that was soaked and dried to make 'malted barley', and whiskey is the strong alcoholic drink. That's why the phrase means 'whiskey distilled in Scotland from malted barley' and helps you remember its pure barley taste.
