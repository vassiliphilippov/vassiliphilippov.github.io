Etymology

Virginia cowslip gets its name from the place Virginia and the old flower name cowslip. People long ago called small primrose-like flowers 'cowslips' because they often grew in fields where cows ate the grass, so the name helps you remember it is a wild primrose-like plant from Virginia with pretty blue flowers.
