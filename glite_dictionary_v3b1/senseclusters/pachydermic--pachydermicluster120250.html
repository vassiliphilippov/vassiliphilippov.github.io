Etymology

pachydermic comes from old Greek parts pachy- meaning 'thick' and -derm meaning 'skin', the same idea behind pachyderm, the name for animals like elephants, rhinos, and hippos with very thick skin. So pachydermic means 'relating to' or 'like' those thick-skinned animals.
