Etymology

Pineus pinifoliae may come from Latin words for 'pine' and 'pine leaves', with Pineus naming the pines and pinifoliae naming the leaves called 'needles' that the insect eats, so the name tells us it is a small insect that lives on and feeds on spruce and pine needles.
