Etymology

blastogenesis comes from the Greek parts blasto- meaning 'bud' or 'sprout' and -genesis meaning 'origin' or 'creation', like in genesis. So it literally means 'creation by budding', which is why the word describes asexual reproduction where a new organism grows as a bud from the parent.
