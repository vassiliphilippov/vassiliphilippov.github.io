Etymology

spiritless comes from spirit, an old word that meant 'breath' or 'life', plus the ending -less, meaning 'without'. You can see the same root in inspire, which is like 'breathing' life into someone. So, spiritless paints the picture of being 'without breath' and therefore without energy or enthusiasm.
