Etymology

cycloserine comes from the parts cyclo- meaning 'circle' or 'ring' and serine, which is the name of an amino acid, so the name points to a ring-shaped amino acid. This helps you remember it is a specific chemical drug, known as an antibiotic used to fight tuberculosis.
