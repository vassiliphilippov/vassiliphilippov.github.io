Etymology

environmental sustainability joins environmental meaning nature and sustainability meaning keeping things going to create a simple image: people only take what they need from the land, like a farmer who picks some fruit but leaves seeds so the trees grow again. That's why it means 'using resources in a way that does not harm nature and that lets the planet keep providing for the future'.
