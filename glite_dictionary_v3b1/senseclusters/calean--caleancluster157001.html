Etymology

calean comes from the Persian word qalyan, which was a name for a 'water pipe' used across the Middle East and South Asia. It is the same idea as the familiar hookah, and because the smoke passes through water the calean cools and smooths the smoke, which is why the word means that kind of pipe.
