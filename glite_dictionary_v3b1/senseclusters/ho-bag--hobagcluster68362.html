Etymology

ho-bag is rude slang formed by joining ho (short for 'whore', an offensive word for someone who sells sex) and bag, a common word for a container. A helpful way to remember it is that this harsh label mixes the sexual insult ho with the ordinary bag, so it is used to call someone seeking sexual attention and is considered offensive.
