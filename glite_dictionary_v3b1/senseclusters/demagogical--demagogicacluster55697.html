Etymology

demagogical comes from the Greek parts demos ('the people') and agogos ('leader'), and you see the same demo in democracy ('rule by the people'). So a demagogue was a leader who spoke directly to the people, often using strong feelings and fears, and demagogical now describes speech that appeals to emotion rather than calm reason.
