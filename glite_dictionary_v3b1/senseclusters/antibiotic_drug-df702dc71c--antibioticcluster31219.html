Etymology

antibiotic drug comes from the parts anti (meaning 'against') and biotic (from a word for 'life'). One popular story says Alexander Fleming found mould on a plate that made a clear ring where bacteria had died. So antibiotic drug means a medicine that fights bacteria, 'to kill or stop bacteria'.
