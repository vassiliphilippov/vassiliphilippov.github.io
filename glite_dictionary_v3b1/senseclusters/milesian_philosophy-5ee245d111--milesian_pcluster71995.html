Etymology

Milesian philosophy gets its name from the ancient Greek city of Miletus: Milesian means 'from Miletus' and philosophy means 'love of wisdom'. Thinkers like Thales in Miletus tried to find natural reasons for what happens in the world instead of using stories about gods, which helped them 'explain nature'. So Milesian philosophy refers to this early school that aimed 'to explain nature' and the world by simple, natural ideas.
