Etymology

The noun tax dodge comes from dodge, which means to move quickly to avoid something, and tax, the money people pay to the government. Imagine someone moving money or papers like dodging a ball to avoid paying taxes; that's why tax dodge means 'an illegal way to avoid paying taxes'.
