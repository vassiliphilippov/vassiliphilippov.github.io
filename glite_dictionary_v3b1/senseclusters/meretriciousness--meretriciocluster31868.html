Etymology

meretriciousness comes from an old Latin word meretrix meaning 'prostitute', and it came to mean being showy to get attention but without real value. A helpful way to remember it is to think of cheap, shiny jewelry that looks like gold but is fake, so meretriciousness means flashy but worthless.
