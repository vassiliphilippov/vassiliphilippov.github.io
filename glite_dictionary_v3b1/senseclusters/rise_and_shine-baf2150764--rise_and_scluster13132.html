Etymology

The phrase rise and shine may come from a simple morning picture: you rise, meaning you get out of bed, and then you shine, meaning you look bright and happy. Parents and teachers often used it as a cheerful call to wake children and make them ready for the day. So it now means 'wake up and start the day in a cheerful, energetic way'.
