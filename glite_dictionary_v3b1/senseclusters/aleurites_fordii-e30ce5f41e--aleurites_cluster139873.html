Etymology

Aleurites fordii may come from the Greek word 'aleuron' meaning 'flour', because the tree's leaves and seeds can look soft and powdery, and fordii shows it was 'named after' a person called Ford. So the name helps you remember this Chinese tree whose seeds are pressed to make tung oil.
