Etymology

sardonicism comes from the old word sardonic, which people long ago linked to Sardinia and a plant that supposedly caused a bitter, twisted laugh called a 'sardonic grin'. So sardonicism means a cruel, mocking kind of humor or smile, like a bitter 'grin' that hides true feelings.
