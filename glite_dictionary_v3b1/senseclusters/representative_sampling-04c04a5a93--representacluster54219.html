Etymology

Representative sampling comes from the picture of taking a few items that look like the whole group, for example taking a handful of marbles that keeps the jar's color mix. So Representative means 'showing the whole' and Sampling means 'taking a few', which is why the phrase means 'to choose small groups that show the whole group'.
