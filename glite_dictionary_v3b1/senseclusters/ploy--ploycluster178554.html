Etymology

The word ploy comes from an old Latin root plicare meaning 'to fold' or 'to bend', which passed into old French as a word for a 'turn' or 'twist'. So a ploy is like a small 'bend' or clever turn in a plan — a trick used to change a situation and get an advantage.
