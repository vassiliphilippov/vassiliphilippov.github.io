Etymology

The word tragacanth comes from the Greek parts tragos 'goat' and akantha 'thorn' because the sticky gum comes from thorny plants that goats were often seen near. So the name helps you remember it is a sticky plant gum used to thicken or bind medicines, paints, and dyes.
