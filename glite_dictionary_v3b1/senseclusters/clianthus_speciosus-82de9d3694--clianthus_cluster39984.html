Etymology

Clianthus speciosus may come from old Greek and Latin words that praise its flowers, and speciosus means 'showy' or 'beautiful', so the name points to its bright, eye-catching red blooms. That's why the name fits this sprawling shrub with scarlet, black-marked flowers.
