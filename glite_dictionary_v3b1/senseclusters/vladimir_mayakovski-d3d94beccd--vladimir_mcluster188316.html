Etymology

Vladimir Mayakovski was a Russian poet who used loud, dramatic readings and made his lines look like big posters in public. Imagine him shouting poetry in the street and painting bold words on walls; that picture helps you remember why he is seen as a 'bold' and 'leading' voice in early Soviet writing.
