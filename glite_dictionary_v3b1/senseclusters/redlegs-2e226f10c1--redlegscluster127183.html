Etymology

Redlegs was used by the Cincinnati baseball team in the 1950s to avoid a political link to Reds, because people then feared communism. A helpful way to remember it is that the name literally shows red and legs, meaning 'red legs' like the team's red socks, so the name stayed about the uniform rather than politics.
