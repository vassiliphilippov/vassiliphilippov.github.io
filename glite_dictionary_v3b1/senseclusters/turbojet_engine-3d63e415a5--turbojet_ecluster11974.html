Etymology

Turbojet engine gets its name from two parts: turbo, a fast, spinning fan, and jet, a fast stream of air or gas. Picture that spinning fan making a hot, powerful stream that pushes the plane. So the name means 'a jet engine that uses a spinning fan to make a high-speed stream to push an aircraft'.
