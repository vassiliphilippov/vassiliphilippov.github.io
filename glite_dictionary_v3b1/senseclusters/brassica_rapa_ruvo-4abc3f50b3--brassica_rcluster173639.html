Etymology

Brassica rapa ruvo comes from old Latin words and a place name: Brassica means a cabbage-like plant, rapa means 'turnip', and ruvo likely comes from Ruvo, a town in Italy where farmers grow this vegetable. So the name is a simple label — a turnip-type cabbage linked to Ruvo — which helps you remember it is a leafy edible plant used for its shoots.
