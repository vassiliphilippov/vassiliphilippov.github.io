Etymology

English-weed likely combines English ('from England') and weed ('a wild plant you do not want in your garden'). A helpful way to remember the name is to picture a bright yellow plant that came with or reminded people of English gardens and that spreads quickly, so people call it a garden 'weed' in South Africa.
