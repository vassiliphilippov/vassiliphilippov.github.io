Etymology

X-ray diffraction comes from a simple picture: very small rays called X-rays hit the neat rows of atoms in a crystal and are bent and spread out, which is diffraction, making a pattern of spots. Scientists study that pattern to find where the atoms are, so the phrase means 'to find the crystal's structure'.
