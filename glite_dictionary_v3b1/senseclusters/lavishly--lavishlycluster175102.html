Etymology

lavishly comes from an old word linked to lavare, which means 'to wash' and was used for a strong 'flood' of water, so imagine someone being 'washed' in gifts. So, to spend lavishly is to give or use a lot—almost like 'flooding' someone with presents or rich decoration.
