give (someone) the boot

0.6/m7.4
To stop someone from working at their job, usually because of bad work or behavior
verb US / ɡɪv ðə buːt /

To give the boot

The board has given him the boot after the scandal.

Etymology

The idiom give (someone) the boot may come from the simple image of using a boot to kick someone out of a room or workplace. That's why the phrase now means 'to fire' someone.
