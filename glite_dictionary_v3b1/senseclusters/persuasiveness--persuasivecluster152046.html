Etymology

persuasiveness comes from Latin parts per- ('through' or 'very') and suadere ('to urge' or 'advise'), and it shares the same root as the verb persuade. So, persuasiveness is the skill of 'urging' ideas into someone's mind — the power to convince or win them over.
