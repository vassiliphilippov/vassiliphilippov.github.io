Etymology

Scottish maple gets its name because people saw this maple often growing in Scotland, and its pale, flaky bark and broad five-part leaves made it easy to notice. So, Scottish maple today means that particular 'kind of maple' tree with pale grey, peeling bark and yellow leaves in autumn.
