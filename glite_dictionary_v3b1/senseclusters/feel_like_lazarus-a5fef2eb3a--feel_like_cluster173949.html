Etymology

feel like Lazarus comes from a Bible story where Jesus brought a man named Lazarus back to life, so the strong image of someone rising from death helps you imagine a sudden return to health or strength. That's why saying feel like Lazarus means 'to feel revived or full of new energy' after illness or a hard time.
