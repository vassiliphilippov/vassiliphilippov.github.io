Etymology

resulting trust comes from the idea that resulting means 'coming from the facts' and trust means someone holding property for another. Imagine someone gives property but really meant the original owner to keep the benefit and did not make it official; if a court sees this, it will treat the property as held for that person, so today resulting trust means a trust a court creates when the parties intended one but did not make it official.
