Etymology

Dunkirk spirit comes from 1940, when many small civilian boats sailed to Dunkirk to rescue trapped soldiers. Because ordinary people worked together and showed quiet courage, Dunkirk spirit now means a group's 'determination' and ability to 'pull together' in hard times.
