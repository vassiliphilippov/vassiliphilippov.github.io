Etymology

nasopharyngitis breaks into naso- 'nose', pharyng- 'throat', and -itis 'inflammation'. So it means inflammation of the area behind the nose and above the throat, and you can remember -itis from a word like tonsillitis, which is why doctors sometimes use it for the common cold.
