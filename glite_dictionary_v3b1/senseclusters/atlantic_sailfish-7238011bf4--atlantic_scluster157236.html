Etymology

Atlantic sailfish is named for how it looks: it has a very tall, sail-like top fin, the sail, and a long pointed bill, and it lives in the Atlantic Ocean. So the name makes a clear picture and explains why it refers to this fast, sail-finned 'fish' today.
