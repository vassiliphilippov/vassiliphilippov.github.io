Etymology

military greeting comes from old actions where knights lifted the front of their helmet so people could see their face, and later soldiers raised a hand to show they had no weapon. One popular story says this clear, honest movement became a formal sign of 'respect', so a greeting in the military now means a formal, respectful gesture like a salute.
