Etymology

craze meaning a short, very popular trend is linked to crazy, which means 'wild' or 'mad'; a craze makes many people act in a sudden, excited way, so the word now describes a fast, intense fashion.

  • synonym: mania - an excessive enthusiasm or desire; an obsession
