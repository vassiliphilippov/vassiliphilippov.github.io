Etymology

Tinfoil hat comes from the clear image of someone folding shiny tinfoil into a small hat to block 'mind control' or secret signals. One popular story says people joked that wearing such a hat would stop the brain being watched, so today calling someone a 'tinfoil hat' means they seem to believe 'conspiracy theories' or look 'paranoid'.
