Etymology

check (one's) eyelids for leaks is a funny saying that imagines your eyelids like the roof of a house and a small leak as a sign they are closing from tiredness. One popular joke says that if your eyelids 'leak' you need to rest, so the phrase means 'to take a short nap'.
