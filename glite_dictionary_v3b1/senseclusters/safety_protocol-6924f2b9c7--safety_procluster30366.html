Etymology

safety protocol links safety, meaning keeping people safe, and protocol, meaning a clear plan of steps. Imagine workers who always follow the same short plan, check the tools, put on gloves, then start the machine. So safety protocol means 'a set of rules or procedures' people follow to avoid accidents.
