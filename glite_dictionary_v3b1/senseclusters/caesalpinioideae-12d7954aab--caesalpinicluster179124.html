Etymology

Caesalpinioideae comes from the plant name Caesalpinia, which was named for the Italian plant scientist Cesalpino, and the ending -oideae meaning 'like' or 'a group'. So Caesalpinioideae is the subfamily of plants 'like' Caesalpinia, a group of trees and shrubs in the pea and bean family.
