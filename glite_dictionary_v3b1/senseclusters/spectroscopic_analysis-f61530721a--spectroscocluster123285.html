Etymology

Spectroscopic analysis comes from the simple image of splitting a material's light into colours, like a rainbow, and reading the pattern those colours make. Different materials make different light patterns, so looking at the pattern tells you what something is made of, which is why the phrase means 'examining a material by studying the pattern of light it makes'.
