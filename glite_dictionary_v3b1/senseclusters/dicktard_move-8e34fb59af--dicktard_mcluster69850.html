Etymology

The slang dicktard move comes from joining two rude words, dick, meaning a rude person, and tard, a short, offensive insult for someone acting very stupid, which creates a strong image of a very foolish action. So the phrase is used for 'an extremely foolish act' or 'a decision that shows very poor judgment'.
