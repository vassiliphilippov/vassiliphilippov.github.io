Etymology

sesquipedalia comes from Latin parts sesqui- 'one and a half' and ped 'foot'. It originally meant 'a foot and a half long', so a helpful way to remember it is to think of sesquipedalia as 'very long words', and you can see the same ped in familiar words like pedal and pedestrian.
