Etymology

American Stock Exchange gets its name from its early days as the 'New York Curb Exchange', when traders stood on the street curb to shout and trade shares of smaller companies. That image of curb trading later became an organized market in New York, so today American Stock Exchange means a New York stock market for companies not listed on the big NYSE.
