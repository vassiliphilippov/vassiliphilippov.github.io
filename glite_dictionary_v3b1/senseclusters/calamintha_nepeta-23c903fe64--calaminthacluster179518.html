Etymology

Calamintha nepeta gets its name from old words for mint; the first part, Calamintha, may come from a Greek word for a 'mint' plant, and the second part, nepeta, links it to the 'catmint' group or a place name. So the name is a handy memory: it points to a small, strongly scented mint herb often used in teas and gardens.
