Etymology

contempt of cop comes from putting contempt (showing strong disrespect) together with cop to name when an officer feels insulted. One popular story says police used the phrase for rude or refusing-to-obey behavior that led them to punish or arrest someone, so it now means 'disrespecting a police officer' and often causes trouble with police.
