Etymology

plainclothes comes from the simple English parts plain ('ordinary') and clothes ('clothing'), so it literally means 'wearing ordinary clothes instead of a uniform'. That's why we call someone plainclothes when they dress to blend in, especially if they are a police officer trying not to be recognized.
