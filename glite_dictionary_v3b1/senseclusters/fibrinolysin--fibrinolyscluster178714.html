Etymology

fibrinolysin comes from fibrin (a word meaning 'fiber' or 'thread') and lysin (from a root meaning 'to loosen' or 'break down', as in analysis). So, fibrinolysin is a substance that 'breaks down' the fibrous material that holds a blood clot, which is why it helps dissolve clots.
