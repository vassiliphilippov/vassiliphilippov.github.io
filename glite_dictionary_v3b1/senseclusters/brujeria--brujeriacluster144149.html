Etymology

A helpful way to remember the informal use of brujeria is to think of Spanish bruja 'witch', because the word is used for a clever trick or skill that looks like 'magic'. That's why people sometimes call an amazing fix or recipe 'brujeria' when it seems too surprising to explain.
