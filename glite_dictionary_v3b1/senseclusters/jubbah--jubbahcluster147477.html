Etymology

jubbah comes from the Arabic word jubba, which meant a 'long robe' or 'outer garment'. A helpful way to remember it is to picture a long, loose robe worn over other clothes for prayers or special ceremonies, which is exactly why jubbah means a long, loose outer garment today.
