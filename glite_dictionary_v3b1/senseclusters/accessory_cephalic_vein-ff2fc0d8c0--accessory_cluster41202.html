Etymology

accessory cephalic vein names an 'extra' vein on the thumb side of the forearm. The word accessory means 'extra' and cephalic vein is the main surface vein there, so this 'accessory cephalic vein' is the small vein that runs along the forearm and joins the main one near the elbow.
