Etymology

adversa comes from Latin parts ad- ('to' or 'against') and vers- ('turn'), so it originally meant 'turned against' or 'opposite'. That's why adversa is used in law and science to point to something 'unfavorable' or 'contrary', and you can see the same root in English adverse and adversary.
