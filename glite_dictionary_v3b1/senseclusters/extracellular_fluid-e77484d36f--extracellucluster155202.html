Etymology

The term extracellular fluid comes from its parts: extra meaning 'outside', cellular meaning 'cells', and fluid meaning 'liquid'. Think of cells as little houses and the extracellular fluid as the liquid in the streets and in the blood that carries salts and proteins, so the phrase means the liquid found 'outside' the body's cells.
