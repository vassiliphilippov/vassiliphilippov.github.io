Etymology

hemerobiid fly comes from old Greek parts hemero meaning 'day' and bios meaning 'life', a phrase used for insects thought to live only a short time. These small, brown lacewing flies are delicate and their young eat pest insects, so the name helps you remember a tiny, short-lived fly that helps control garden pests.
