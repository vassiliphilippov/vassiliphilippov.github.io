An ending added to verbs to make nouns that show actions or results

Examples

The suffix ment
Adding the suffix ment to 'encourage' creates the noun 'encouragement'.
The word 'agreement' is formed by attaching ment to 'agree'.
Many English nouns, like 'movement', use the suffix ment to show an action or result.

Etymology

ment comes from the Latin ending -mentum, which turned verbs into nouns that mean the 'result' or the 'action' of something. You can see this in movement, agreement, and encouragement, so adding ment to a verb today makes a noun showing the action or result of that verb.
