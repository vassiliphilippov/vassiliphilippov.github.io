Etymology

Fast of Av is a Jewish day that remembers the destruction of the Jerusalem temples long ago. The word Fast means 'to go without food' and Av is the name of the month, and on the ninth day of Av people do not eat or drink and pray, so the name means a day of mourning and remembrance.
