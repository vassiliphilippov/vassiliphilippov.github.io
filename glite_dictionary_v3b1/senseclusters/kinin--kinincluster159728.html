Etymology

kinin comes from an old Greek root kinein meaning 'to move', the same idea you see in kinetic. In the body, kinin proteins make blood vessels wider and help send pain signals, so thinking of them as small 'movers' explains why they cause swelling, vessel widening, and pain.
