Etymology

The word santorum was created in 2003 from the last name of U.S. senator Rick Santorum as part of an online protest. A writer asked people to use santorum for 'a frothy mix of lube and poo' so the name would become linked to that meaning, which is why the word now has a shocking, controversial sense.
