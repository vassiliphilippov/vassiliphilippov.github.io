Etymology

carcinomatosis comes from parts that mean 'crab' (carcino-), 'tumor' (-oma) and 'condition' (-osis). The 'crab' picture is the same idea behind the word cancer, so carcinomatosis helps you remember a condition where cancer has spread widely through the body.
