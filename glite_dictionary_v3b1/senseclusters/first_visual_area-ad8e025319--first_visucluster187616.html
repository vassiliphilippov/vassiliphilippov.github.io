Etymology

The term first visual area comes from a simple image: imagine a relay where the eyes send signals to the very first station in the back of the head. The words first, visual, and area show it is the 'first' brain 'area' that begins to process what we 'see', so the name means the brain region that first receives and starts to process what we see.
