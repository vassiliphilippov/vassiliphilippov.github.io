Etymology

Roneograph comes from the brand name Roneo plus the ending -graph, which means 'to write' or 'print' and you see it in words like photograph. So a Roneograph is the Roneo machine that 'writes' or 'prints' many copies, which fits its use as a stencil duplicator.
