Etymology

Guided Bomb Unit-28 literally tells you what it is: Guided Bomb shows the bomb is steered to its target, and Unit-28 is the 'model number' for this version. So the name means a heavy laser-guided bomb made to reach an exact spot and go deep into the ground before it explodes.
