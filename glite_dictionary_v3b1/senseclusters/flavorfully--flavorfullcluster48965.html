Etymology

The word flavorfully comes from the noun flavor ('taste' or 'smell') plus -ful ('full of') and -ly (makes it an adverb). So when used for speech or writing, flavorfully means doing something 'with vivid, interesting detail', making a story or description feel rich and alive.
