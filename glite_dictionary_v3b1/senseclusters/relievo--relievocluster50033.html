Etymology

relievo comes from Italian parts re- and levare, where levare means 'to lift' and re- means 'again', so the idea is 'lifted up'. Think of a relievo as a picture or design 'lifted' out of a flat surface, like the English word relief, and that's why it names sculpture that stands out from its background.
