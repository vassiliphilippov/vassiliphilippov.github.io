Etymology

Vasectomize comes from the parts vas meaning 'vessel' or 'tube' and -ectomy meaning 'cutting out', with -ize to make it a verb. So to vasectomize someone is to cut or close the small sperm tube called the vas to stop sperm and make a man unable to have children.
