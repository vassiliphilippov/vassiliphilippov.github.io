Etymology

Naval Air Warfare Center Weapons Division is a name that explains its job: Naval means 'the navy', Air Warfare means 'fighting in the sky', and Weapons Division means 'the part that makes and tests weapons'. So the name shows it is the navy group that studies and makes air and missile systems.
