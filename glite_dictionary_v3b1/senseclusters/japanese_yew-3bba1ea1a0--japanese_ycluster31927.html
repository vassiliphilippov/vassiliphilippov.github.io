Etymology

Japanese yew simply names a type of yew from Japan and nearby China that gardeners liked for its dark, shiny leaves and because it was easy to shape. Gardeners carried it to Europe and planted it as a tough evergreen for hedges, so today Japanese yew means this shrub often used in gardens.
