Etymology

Adaptive radiation paints a clear picture: one original species spreads out like rays from a single point (the idea behind radiation) and each group quickly changes to fit new places or ways of living (the idea of adaptive). That's why adaptive radiation means 'many new species' coming from one ancestor as they adapt to different places or ways of living.
