Etymology

rum mill may come from the image of a mill that makes or changes things; imagine news being put into a small factory and coming out different. One popular story says that when people pass news through that 'mill' it becomes 'gossip' or 'rumors', which is why the phrase means a place that makes or spreads gossip.
