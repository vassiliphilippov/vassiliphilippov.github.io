Etymology

family Cicadellidae comes from the Latin word cicada, meaning a buzzer, plus the ending -idae, which shows it is a 'family' group. So the name gives a clear image: a 'family' of small, buzzy insects that hop on leaves and often harm crops, which is why we use it for the leafhoppers today.
