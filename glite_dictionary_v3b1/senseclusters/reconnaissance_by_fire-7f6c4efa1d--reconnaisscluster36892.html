Etymology

reconnaissance by fire comes from a simple battlefield image: soldiers would shoot into a likely hiding place to force anyone inside to 'shoot back' and so show their position, with reconnaissance meaning looking for the enemy and fire meaning shooting. So the phrase means 'using gunfire to test and reveal where the enemy is'.
