Etymology

The term improper fraction describes a fraction where the top number, the numerator, is equal to or larger than the bottom number, the denominator. Because it is not a 'proper' part less than one, the word 'improper' shows the fraction is one or more, so the name means a fraction with a numerator equal to or greater than the denominator.
