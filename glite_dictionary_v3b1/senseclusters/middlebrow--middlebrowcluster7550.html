Etymology

middlebrow is a simple blend of middle and brow, where brow means 'forehead' and was used in words like highbrow to suggest being very intellectual and lowbrow to suggest the opposite. So a middlebrow person has tastes 'in the middle', not very highbrow and not very lowbrow.
