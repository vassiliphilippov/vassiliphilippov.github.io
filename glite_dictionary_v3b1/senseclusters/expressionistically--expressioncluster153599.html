Etymology

The word expressionistically comes from expression (from ex- 'out' and press 'to push'), so think of it as 'pushing feelings out'. It also links to expressionism, an art style that uses bold, exaggerated ways to show strong 'emotion'. So to act expressionistically is to do something in a loud, dramatic style to show feeling.
