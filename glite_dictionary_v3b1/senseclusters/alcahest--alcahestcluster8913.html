Etymology

alcahest comes from the name alchemists gave their dream substance, starting with the Arabic article al- 'the' and with the rest tied to alchemy, so it was like calling it 'the dissolver'. That's why alcahest refers to a mythical universal solvent — a substance imagined to 'dissolve' any material.
