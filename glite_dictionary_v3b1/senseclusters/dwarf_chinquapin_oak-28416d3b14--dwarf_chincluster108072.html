Etymology

The name dwarf chinquapin oak comes from the tree's size and its nut. It is called dwarf because it stays small and bushy, and chinquapin because its sweet, chestnut-like acorns reminded people of the chinquapin nut, so the name means a small oak that makes those sweet nuts.
