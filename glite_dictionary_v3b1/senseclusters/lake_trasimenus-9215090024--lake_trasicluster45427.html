Etymology

Lake Trasimenus is remembered for a famous event in 217 BC when the general Hannibal hid his soldiers in the hills beside the lake and suddenly attacked a marching Roman army. That strong image of soldiers appearing from the hills explains why the name brings to mind a deadly 'ambush' and is linked to that battle today.
