Etymology

farolito comes from the Spanish word farol meaning 'lantern' plus the small ending -ito meaning 'little', so it literally means a 'little lantern'. The older word farol goes back to the idea of a big guiding light (like a lighthouse), which helps explain why farolitos are small lights used to guide and decorate at Christmas.
