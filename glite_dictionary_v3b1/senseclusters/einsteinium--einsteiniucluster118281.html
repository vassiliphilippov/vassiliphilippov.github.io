Etymology

Einsteinium is named after the famous scientist Einstein, and the ending -ium shows it is a chemical 'element'. It was first found in the debris of a hydrogen bomb and is made in labs by hitting plutonium with neutrons, so Einsteinium is a rare, man-made, highly radioactive element.
