Etymology

Peramelidae comes from the genus Perameles plus the scientific ending -idae, which means 'family' for animals and appears in names like Canidae for dogs and Felidae for cats. It is the name for the family of bandicoots, marsupial animals with a 'pouch', so the name simply tells us these related animals belong to the same family.
