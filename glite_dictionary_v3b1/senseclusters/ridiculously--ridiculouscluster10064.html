Etymology

ridiculously comes from the Latin root ridere, which means 'to laugh', and from ridiculous, which originally meant 'worth laughing at'. So the ending -ly turns it into a word that tells how something happens, meaning it is 'so unreasonable or absurd that it is almost laughable'.
