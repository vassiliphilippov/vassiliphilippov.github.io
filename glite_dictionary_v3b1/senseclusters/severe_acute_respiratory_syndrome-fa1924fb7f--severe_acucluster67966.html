Etymology

The medical name severe acute respiratory syndrome was chosen to describe the illness: severe means 'very serious', acute means 'sudden', respiratory means 'related to breathing', and syndrome means 'a group of symptoms'. So the name tells you it is a sudden, serious disease that affects breathing.
