Etymology

unbreathable is made from un- ('not'), breathe ('to breathe') and -able ('able'). A helpful way to remember the figurative use is to imagine a room so tense you feel you cannot breathe, so unbreathable can mean 'so oppressive or unpleasant that it is hard to stay'.
