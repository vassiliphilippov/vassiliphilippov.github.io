Etymology

turps is a short, informal form of turpentine, the strong-smelling liquid painters use to thin paint and clean brushes. Turpentine originally comes from the name of a tree, the 'terebinth', because people made the liquid from the tree's sap, so turps keeps that meaning as a paint thinner.
