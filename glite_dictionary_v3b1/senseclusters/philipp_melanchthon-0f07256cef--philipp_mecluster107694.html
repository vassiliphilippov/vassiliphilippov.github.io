Etymology

Philipp Melanchthon comes from his original German surname Schwarzerdt, which he changed to a Greek form that means 'black earth'. He was a German scholar and a close friend of Martin Luther who helped lead the Reformation and improve schools, so his name today reminds people of that church and education leader.
