Etymology

eelpout comes from eel and pout, with eel meaning 'a long, snake-like fish' and pout suggesting a fish with a downturned mouth or chin 'whiskers'. So the freshwater eelpout is an eel-shaped fish you can spot by its 'pouting' mouth and chin whiskers.
