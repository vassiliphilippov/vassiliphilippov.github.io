Etymology

The word pleonexia comes from Greek parts pleon- meaning 'more' and -exia meaning 'having or taking', so a helpful way to remember it is to imagine someone who always wants 'more' and keeps taking more than their fair share. So pleonexia means a strong wish to have more than is fair.
