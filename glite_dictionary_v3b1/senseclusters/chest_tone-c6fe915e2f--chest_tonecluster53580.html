Etymology

The term chest tone comes from the clear feeling you get when you sing or speak low notes: the sound seems to vibrate in your chest and the tone feels like it comes from there. So, 'chest tone' now means the 'lower' and 'full' part of the voice used to make notes sound warm and strong.
