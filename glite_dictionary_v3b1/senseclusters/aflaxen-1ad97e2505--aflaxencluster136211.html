Etymology

Aflaxen is a brand name formed from the drug name naproxen and the familiar plant flax, which makes linen. A helpful way to remember it is to picture a 'flax' plant joined with a pain medicine, so that's why Aflaxen refers to a naproxen drug used to reduce pain and inflammation.
