Etymology

battle of Tertry comes from a real fight near the small village of Tertry in northern France in 687, where Pepin of Herstal defeated his rivals. When Pepin won, his family took real power from the old kings, so the name battle of Tertry now marks that 'turning point' in the kingdom's history.
