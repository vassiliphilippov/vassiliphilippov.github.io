Etymology

cozily comes from cozy, also spelled cosy, a short English word that once meant 'comfortable' or 'snug' and likely came from old dialect words for 'to wrap' or 'to pamper'. So, to do something cozily is to do it in a warm, comfortable, and relaxed way.
