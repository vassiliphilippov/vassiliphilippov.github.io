Etymology

permanganic acid is named from its parts: 'per-' meaning 'extra oxygen', 'manganic' from the metal manganese, and 'acid' meaning 'a sour chemical'. That's why the name points to a deep purple, oxygen-rich acid seen only in solution and used to make permanganate salts.
