Etymology

varsity letter comes from a school tradition: members of the main sports team, the varsity, were given a big cloth letter with the school's initial to sew on a jacket or sweater. Because the cloth letter showed a student's place on the team, the phrase now means an 'award' for strong participation in a school's sport or activity.
