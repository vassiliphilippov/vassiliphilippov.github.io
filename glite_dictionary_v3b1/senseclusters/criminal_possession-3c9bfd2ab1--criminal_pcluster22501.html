Etymology

criminal possession comes from two simple words: possession meaning 'to have or control something' and criminal meaning 'illegal'. Imagine someone holding a stolen phone — they are in possession of it, and because the item is 'illegal' to have, that situation is called 'criminal possession', which is why the phrase means being charged for having illegal property.
