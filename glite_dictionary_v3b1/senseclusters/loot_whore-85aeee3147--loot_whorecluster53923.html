Etymology

The slang noun loot whore probably comes from joining loot, meaning game treasure, with whore, an insulting word for someone who will do anything for gain. The image is of a player grabbing all the treasure for themselves, so the term means 'a player who is obsessed with taking in-game loot even if it hurts the team'.
