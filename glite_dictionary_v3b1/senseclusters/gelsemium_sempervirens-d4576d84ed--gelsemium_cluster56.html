Etymology

Gelsemium sempervirens comes from old plant names. Gelsemium may come from the Italian word for 'jasmine' because the yellow flowers smell sweet, and sempervirens means 'always green', so the name gives the image of a sweet-smelling evergreen vine. That's why the name fits this poisonous vine with fragrant yellow flowers.
