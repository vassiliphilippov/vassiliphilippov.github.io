Etymology

osage orange may come from the Osage people who lived where the tree grows and from the tree's round, bumpy fruit that looks like a rough orange. Native people used the tree's hard, orange-colored wood to make strong bows, so the name connects the people and the orange-like fruit to the tree we call osage orange today.
