Etymology

Schmidt telescope is named after Bernhard Schmidt, who built a telescope with a mirror and a special curved glass 'corrector' that reduced image distortions and let him photograph a wide area of sky. So, today a Schmidt telescope means a telescope made with that special corrector to give wide, clear pictures of the night sky.
