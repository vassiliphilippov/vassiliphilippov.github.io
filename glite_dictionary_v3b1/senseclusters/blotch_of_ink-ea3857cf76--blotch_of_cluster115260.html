Etymology

blotch of ink comes from the simple image of spilled ink: when someone drops a bottle or smudges a pen, a dark, uneven blotch of ink spreads over the paper or clothes. That's why it means 'a visible spot or stain of ink that can spoil writing or the look of a page'.
