Etymology

A helpful way to remember the sea animal man-of-war is to imagine its float looking like the sail of a small warship, so sailors compared it to a 'man-of-war' ship. That's why the man-of-war, often called the 'Portuguese man-of-war', is a floating creature with long, stinging tentacles.
