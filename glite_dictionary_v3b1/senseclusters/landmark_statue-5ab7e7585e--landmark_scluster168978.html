Etymology

The phrase landmark statue comes from the simple idea of a tall, easy-to-see statue that everyone uses as a 'landmark' in a town or city. People used such statues to find their way or to meet, so a landmark statue today means a well-known, easily recognized statue you can use as a 'reference point'.
