Etymology

pappose comes from the plant word pappus, which in turn comes from a Greek word pappos meaning 'old man' or 'grandfather'; imagine a tiny white beard on a seed like a dandelion. That's why pappose means having feathery bristles or tufts that help seeds travel on the wind.
