Etymology

Illicium anisatum may come from Latin words: Illicium meaning 'to lure' and anisatum meaning 'like anise', because its star-shaped fruit looks like the spice 'anise'. So, although it looks like a cooking spice, Illicium anisatum is poisonous and must never be eaten.
