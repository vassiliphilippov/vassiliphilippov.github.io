Etymology

Scardinius erythrophthalmus is the scientific name for the rudd, a small European freshwater fish. The name's second part, erythrophthalmus, comes from old Greek words meaning 'red' and 'eye', and this points to the fish's red fins and eyes, so the name helps you remember which fish it is.
