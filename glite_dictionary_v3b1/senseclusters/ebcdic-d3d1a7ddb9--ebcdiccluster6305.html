Etymology

EBCDIC is the name of an old IBM character code. The letters come from the long name Extended Binary Coded Decimal Interchange Code, which you can think of as 'a way to code' letters and numbers for big mainframe computers, and that's why EBCDIC refers to that specific way of showing text.
