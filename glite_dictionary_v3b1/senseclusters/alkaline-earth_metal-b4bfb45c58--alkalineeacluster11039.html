Etymology

alkaline-earth metal: The name alkaline-earth metal comes from early chemists: they called certain mineral powders 'earth' because they came from the ground, and used alkaline to say these metals make a 'basic' substance when they react. So the name helps you remember that these are metals from the earth that form 'basic' (not acidic) oxides today.
