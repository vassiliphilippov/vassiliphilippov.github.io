Etymology

Muhammad Ali of Egypt was an Albanian-born Ottoman soldier who rose to become the powerful 'ruler' of Egypt in the early 1800s. He built a modern army, changed farming and industry, and made Egypt more independent from the Ottomans, so people often call him the 'founder of modern Egypt'.
