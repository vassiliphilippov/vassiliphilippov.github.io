Etymology

considerably comes from consider, which is built from the Latin parts con- and sidere meaning 'with' and 'star', because Romans sometimes looked at the stars when they wanted to think carefully. A helpful way to remember it is that something considerable was 'worthy of being thought about' or 'important', so considerably means 'to a large degree' or 'much'.
