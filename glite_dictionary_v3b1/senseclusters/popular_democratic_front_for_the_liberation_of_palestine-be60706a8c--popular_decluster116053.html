Etymology

Popular Democratic Front for the Liberation of Palestine uses words that show its purpose: Popular means 'of the people', Democratic means 'for the people to rule', Front is a group formed to act together, and Liberation means 'freeing' Palestine. So the name tells you it is a group claiming to work for the Palestinian people and their freedom.
