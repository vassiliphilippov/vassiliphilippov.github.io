Etymology

The name family Scarabaeidae comes from the old word 'scarab' for the round dung beetle that was important to the ancient Egyptians. That's why Scarabaeidae is the name for the group, a 'family', of scarab or 'dung beetles' that roll dung or eat dead plants and animals.
