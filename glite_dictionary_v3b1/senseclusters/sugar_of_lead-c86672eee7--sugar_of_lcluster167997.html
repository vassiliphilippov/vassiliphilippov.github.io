Etymology

sugar of lead got its name because the white, crystal-like powder of lead tasted like sugar, so people called it 'sweet' even though it was 'poisonous'. That's why the term means a poisonous white compound once used to make shiny coatings on pottery, in dyeing and in old cosmetics.
