Etymology

proscriptive comes from the Latin parts pro- and scribere, where scribere means 'to write' and the same root appears in scribe and script. In the past, rulers would 'write down' the names of people to be banned or punished, so proscriptive means 'officially banning or excluding' people or groups, often for political reasons.
