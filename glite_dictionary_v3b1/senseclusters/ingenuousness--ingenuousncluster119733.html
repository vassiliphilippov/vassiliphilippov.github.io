Etymology

ingenuousness comes from an old Latin word ingenuus meaning 'freeborn' and 'honest', because people born free were seen as open and sincere. So ingenuousness now means being open, honest, and innocent, and a helpful way to remember it is that it sounds like genuine ('honest').
