Etymology

Asian tiger mosquito gets its name from where it came and how it looks: Asian because it first came from Asia, and tiger because its black-and-white stripes look like a tiger. That's why the name gives a clear picture of a small, striped mosquito that now spreads to many places and can carry disease.
