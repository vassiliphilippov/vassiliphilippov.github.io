Etymology

Mauretanian comes from the name of the ancient North African kingdom Mauretania, which in Latin came from Maurus meaning 'person from that land'. Think of the English word Moor, which shares the same root, so Mauretanian means 'relating to Mauretania', its people, or its culture.
