Etymology

Agonus is the scientific name for a group of small, bottom-dwelling sea fish. A helpful way to remember it is to link it to the old Greek root agon- meaning 'contest' or 'struggle', the same root in agony, so you can picture these hardy fish fighting to survive and see why the name fits the genus.
