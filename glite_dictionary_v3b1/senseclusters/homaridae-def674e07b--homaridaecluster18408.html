Etymology

The word Homaridae comes from the old Latin name Homarus, which means 'lobster', and the family ending -idae that means 'group'. So Homaridae simply means the group of lobsters, such as the American and European lobster, and explains why the name is used for these large-clawed lobsters people eat.
