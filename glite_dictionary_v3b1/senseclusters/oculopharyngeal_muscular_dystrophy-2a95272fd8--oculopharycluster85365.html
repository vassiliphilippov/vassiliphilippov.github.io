Etymology

Oculopharyngeal muscular dystrophy is named from word parts that point to the body: oculo means 'eye', pharyngeal means 'throat', and muscular dystrophy means 'weak muscles'. So the name shows it is a muscle disease that first affects the eyelids and the muscles used for swallowing.
