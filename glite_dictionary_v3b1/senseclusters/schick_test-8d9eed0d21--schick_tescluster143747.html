Etymology

Schick test was named after Dr. Schick and used a tiny amount of diphtheria toxin put just under the skin to see if the body would react. If the arm showed no reaction, the person was 'immune'; if it did, they were not. So Schick test now means that simple skin check for 'diphtheria' immunity.
