Etymology

freight liner comes from the words freight, meaning 'goods', and liner, a ship or train that runs on a regular 'line' or 'route'. The image is of a long train or ship that follows a fixed, fast route between factories and ports, so today freight liner means a long-distance, regular service or train for carrying 'goods' quickly.
