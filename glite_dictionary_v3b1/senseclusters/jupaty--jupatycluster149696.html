Etymology

The word jupaty comes from an indigenous Brazilian name for a tall palm with very large, tough leaves. A helpful way to remember it is that the jupaty's long, strong stems and leaves are used for roofs and fences, so the name is tied to a plant that gives 'shelter' and building material.
