Etymology

The noun sardine oil comes from a simple idea: it is the oil taken from the small fish sardine. Factories used to make oil from many sardines to make 'soap' and to keep machines running, so the name now means 'oil taken from sardines' used as a 'lubricant' or to make 'soap'.
