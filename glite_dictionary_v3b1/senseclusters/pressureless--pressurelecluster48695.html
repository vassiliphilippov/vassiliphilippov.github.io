Etymology

pressureless comes from pressure, built on the idea 'to press' meaning 'to push' or 'force', and the ending -less meaning 'without'. So pressureless can mean 'without' the push or force that makes you hurry or feel stressed, which is why it describes calm, relaxed situations.
