Etymology

aphrodisiacal comes from the Greek goddess Aphrodite, whose name is linked to 'love', and it is formed like the common word aphrodisiac. So, something aphrodisiacal is thought to make people feel 'sexual desire' or more attracted to each other, which is why the word is used for foods, scents, or other things that raise attraction.
