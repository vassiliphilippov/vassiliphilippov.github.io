Etymology

system of numeration began as a simple idea: people needed an ordered way to show numbers, so they used marks on bone or their ten fingers to 'count'. The word system means an ordered way and numeration means showing 'numbers', so the phrase now means a clear method or symbols for 'counting' and writing 'numbers'.
