Etymology

The word Pickeringia is a plant name made from the surname Pickering plus the Latin ending -ia, which means 'belonging to' or 'connected with'. So Pickeringia simply means 'Pickering's plant', like gardenia, and that is why the genus name shows the person the plant honors.
