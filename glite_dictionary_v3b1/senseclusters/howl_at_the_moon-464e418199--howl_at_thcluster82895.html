Etymology

Howl at the moon comes from the image of wolves at night, when a wolf will howl up at the bright moon, making a loud, long cry. That picture of noisy, emotional crying is why we use the phrase to mean 'make a loud, emotional cry' or 'express strong feelings' like frustration or longing.
