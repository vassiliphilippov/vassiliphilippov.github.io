Etymology

paralipsis comes from Greek parts para- meaning 'beside' and leipein meaning 'to leave out', so it originally means 'to leave something to the side'. It is like the word ellipsis, which also means 'leaving something out', and that's why paralipsis is a way of pointing to something by pretending not to mention it.
