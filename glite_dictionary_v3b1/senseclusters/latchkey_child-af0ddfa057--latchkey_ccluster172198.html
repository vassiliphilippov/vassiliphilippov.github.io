Etymology

latchkey child The phrase latchkey child comes from a time when many children wore a house key on a string so they could let themselves in when their parents were at work. One popular story says the child would open the door latch and stay alone after school, so the phrase now means a child who is 'on their own' or 'without adult supervision' after school.
