Etymology

The law of areas comes from the picture of a line from the sun to a planet sweeping out equal areas like equal pie slices in equal times; when the planet is closer the slice is long so the planet must move faster, and when it is farther the slice is short so it moves slower. That's why the law means planets 'move faster when closer to the sun and slower when farther away'.
