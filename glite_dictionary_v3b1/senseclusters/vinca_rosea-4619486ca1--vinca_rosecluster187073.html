Etymology

Vinca rosea gets its name from Latin: Vinca may come from a word meaning 'to bind' because the stems can wrap around, and rosea means 'pink' or 'rosy'. So the name gives the image of a pink plant that can wrap around things, which helps you remember its bright, long-lasting flowers.
