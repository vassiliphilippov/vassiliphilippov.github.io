Etymology

challenge someone's palate paints a picture of a chef offering bold, new tastes so the palate, the roof of your mouth and your sense of taste, has to work harder and notice different flavours, while the challenge is the test. So, it now means 'to introduce food or drink that tests or stimulates a person's sense of taste'.
