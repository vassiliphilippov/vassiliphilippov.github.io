Etymology

supratentorial lesion comes from a simple image: the part supra means 'above' and tentorium was named for a thin 'tent-like' layer inside the skull that sits over the lower brain. So a supratentorial lesion is a damaged area 'above the tent' in the upper part of the brain, and that's why doctors use the term for a tumor or injury there that can affect thinking or cause seizures.
