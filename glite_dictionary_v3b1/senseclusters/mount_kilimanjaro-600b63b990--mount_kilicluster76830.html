Etymology

Mount Kilimanjaro combines Mount meaning 'mountain' with Kilimanjaro, a local name that, one popular story says, comes from kilima meaning 'hill' and a word for 'white', so the name paints a picture of a tall, white-topped mountain. That's why the name fits Africa's highest, snow-capped peak.
