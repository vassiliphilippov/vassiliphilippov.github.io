Etymology

Anomiidae comes from the genus Anomia, made from Greek parts a- and nomos meaning 'without' and 'rule', which gives the idea 'irregular'. So these 'saddle oysters' have thin, irregular shells that cling to rocks, and the name helps you remember their uneven shape.
