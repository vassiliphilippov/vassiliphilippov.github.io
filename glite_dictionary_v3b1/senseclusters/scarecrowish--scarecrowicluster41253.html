Etymology

The word scarecrowish comes from scarecrow, a straw figure put in fields to 'scare' birds, plus the ending -ish, which means 'like' or 'in the style of'. So something scarecrowish looks or moves like a scarecrow, often very thin and wearing old, loose clothes.
