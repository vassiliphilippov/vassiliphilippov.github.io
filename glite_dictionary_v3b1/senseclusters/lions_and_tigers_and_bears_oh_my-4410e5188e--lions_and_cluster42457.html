Etymology

lions and tigers and bears, oh my comes from the famous film 'The Wizard of Oz', where Dorothy and her friends walk into a dark forest and nervously call out those scary animals as they go. That's why people say it now to show 'surprise', 'fear', or 'being overwhelmed', often in a playful way.
