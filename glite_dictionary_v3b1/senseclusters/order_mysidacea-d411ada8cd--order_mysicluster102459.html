Etymology

order Mysidacea comes from Greek words meaning 'shrimp-like', so Mysidacea literally points to small, shrimp-like animals. That's why the name means a 'group' of tiny, shrimp-like creatures often called 'opossum shrimps', because the females carry their young in a pouch, a clear image to remember the group.
