Etymology

The term Khoikhoi language comes from the people’s own name for themselves; Khoikhoi is often said to mean 'real people' or 'men', so the phrase simply names the language of that group. Many of these languages are known for their click sounds, a clear and unusual feature that helps you remember 'Khoikhoi language' today.
