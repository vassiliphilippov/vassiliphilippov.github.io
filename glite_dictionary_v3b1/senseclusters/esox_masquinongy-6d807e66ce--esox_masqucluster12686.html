Etymology

Esox masquinongy comes from two parts: Esox is an old word for 'pike', and masquinongy likely comes from a Native American word like 'maskinongé' meaning 'great fish' or 'big pike'. So the name gives the simple image of a large pike-like fish, which explains why it refers to the big predatory 'muskellunge'.
