Etymology

Western honey mesquite gets its name from what people noticed: Western because it grows in the dry American West, honey because its long seed pods are very sweet like 'honey', and mesquite from the Spanish name for the shrub. So the name tells you it is a thorny western tree with sweet pods people used as 'feed' for animals.
