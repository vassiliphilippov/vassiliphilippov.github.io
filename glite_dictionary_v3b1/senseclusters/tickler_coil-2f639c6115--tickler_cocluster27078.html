Etymology

tickler coil comes from the idea of 'tickle' as a light touch: the word tickler suggests a small, gentle touch and the coil is a small loop of wire that sends a tiny signal back into a radio circuit. That little 'tickle' makes weak signals stronger or helps the circuit keep working, so the term now means a small coil used to give 'feedback' in radios.
