Etymology

sponge gourd names a climbing plant whose fruit is a gourd people eat when it is young, but if it is left to dry the inside becomes stringy and sponge-like. So the name joins sponge and gourd and explains why the dried fruit is used for 'washing' and why the plant is grown for both food and natural sponges.
