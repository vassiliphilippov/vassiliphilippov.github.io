Etymology

shriveled comes from the idea of something getting much smaller and wrinkled, like a flower or fruit left in the hot sun. Think of it like the familiar word shrink plus the idea of a 'wrinkle', so shriveled describes something that has 'shrunk' and become 'wrinkled'.
