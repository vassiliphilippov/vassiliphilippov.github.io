Etymology

pitch contour comes from the idea of a visible shape of sound: pitch, which is how 'high' or 'low' a sound is, moves along a line or contour like hills and valleys. So the phrase helps you picture how a voice or melody rises and falls and why that pattern can change the 'meaning' or feeling of a sentence or song.
