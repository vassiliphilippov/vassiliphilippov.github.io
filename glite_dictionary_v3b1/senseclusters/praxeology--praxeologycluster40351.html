Etymology

praxeology comes from the Greek root praxis, meaning 'action', and the ending -logy, meaning 'study', so it literally means the 'study of action'. Think of the common word practice (the same root): praxeology studies why people act and how they choose, which is why it is used in economics and philosophy.
