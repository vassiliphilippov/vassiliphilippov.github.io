Etymology

The term operating expense comes from the words operating and expense, where operating means 'running' a business and expense means 'money spent'. Imagine a shop paying rent, electricity and wages to keep working and open, and these regular payments are the costs that keep it running. So, operating expenses are the everyday costs of running a business.
