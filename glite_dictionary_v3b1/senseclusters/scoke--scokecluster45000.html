Etymology

scoke is a local name for a tall, coarse plant with dark, drooping berries, and its exact origin is unclear. A helpful way to remember scoke is to think of smoke, because the hanging dark berries can look like puffs of black smoke, and that image links to the plant and its 'poisonous' nature.
