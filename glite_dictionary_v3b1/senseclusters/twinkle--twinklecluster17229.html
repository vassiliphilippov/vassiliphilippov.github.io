Etymology

twinkle comes from an old English verb twincan, which meant something like 'shine quickly' and was probably a word made to copy a quick flash. Think of a small bright 'spark' in someone's eyes, like a 'wink' or a 'blink', and that's why twinkle means a lively, merry look.
