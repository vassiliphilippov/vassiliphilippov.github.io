Etymology

quiddity comes from the Latin root quid meaning 'what' and originally meant 'whatness'. A helpful way to remember the argument sense is to imagine someone asking a tiny, picky 'what?' that pulls attention away from the main point, which is why a quiddity can mean a subtle or evasive objection.
