Etymology

The word magnetograph is made from magnet (a metal object that 'attracts' iron) and -graph (which means 'to write', like in photograph). So a magnetograph is a device that 'writes down' or records magnetic changes, which is why scientists use it to measure the Earth's or the Sun's magnetic field.
