Etymology

thermosetting resin gets its name from thermo (heat) and setting (becoming fixed), so it literally means a sticky resin that 'sets' when heated. Think of baking a cake: once it is cooked it becomes solid and you cannot turn it back into batter, so a thermosetting resin hardens when heated and 'cannot be reshaped'.
