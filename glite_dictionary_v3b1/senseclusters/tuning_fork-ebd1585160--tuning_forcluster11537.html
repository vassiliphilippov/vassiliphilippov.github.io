Etymology

The noun tuning fork comes from a small metal tool shaped like a two-pronged fork that, when struck, makes a steady musical tuning note. Musicians use that clear sound to 'tune' instruments and doctors sometimes use the vibration to test 'hearing', so the name shows the tool's purpose.
