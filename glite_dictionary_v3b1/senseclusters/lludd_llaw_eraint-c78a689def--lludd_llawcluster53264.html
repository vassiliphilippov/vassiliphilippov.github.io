Etymology

Lludd Llaw Eraint literally means Lludd with llaw 'hand' and eraint 'silver' in old Welsh. One popular story says he had a silver hand or metal glove to fight enemies, so the strong image of a shining hand helps explain why he is remembered as a 'protector' and 'warrior god'.
