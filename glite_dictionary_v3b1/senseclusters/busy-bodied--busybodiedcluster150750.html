Etymology

busy-bodied comes from the older noun busybody, made of busy ('active' or 'always doing things') and body ('person'). So a busy-bodied person is like someone whose 'body' is always 'busy' in other people's business, meaning they 'meddle' or 'interfere' and often annoy others.
