Etymology

flotsam and jetsam comes from old sailors: flotsam was pieces of a broken ship or its load that floated after a wreck, and jetsam were items sailors threw into the sea to save the ship. That's why both words were about bits of trash at sea, and today the phrase means 'unwanted or leftover things'.
