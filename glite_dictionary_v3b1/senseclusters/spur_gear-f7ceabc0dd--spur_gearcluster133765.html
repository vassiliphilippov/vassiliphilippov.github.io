Etymology

spur gear may come from the small pointed spur on a rider's boot, because the gear's straight teeth stick out like those points. Those teeth push the next gear on the same flat level, so spur gear now means a gear with straight teeth that transfer 'turning motion' between two rods that turn side by side.
