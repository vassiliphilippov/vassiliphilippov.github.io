Etymology

pyrazine can be remembered by its parts: the first part pyra- sounds like 'pyro' or 'fire', because many pyrazines form when foods are roasted, and the ending -azine is a common ending for chemicals that contain nitrogen. So pyrazine often means a small ring chemical that gives the roasted, nutty, or earthy smells used in foods and perfumes.
