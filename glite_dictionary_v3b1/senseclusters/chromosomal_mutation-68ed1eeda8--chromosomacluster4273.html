Etymology

The term chromosomal mutation comes from how scientists saw dark, thread-like chromosomes in cells that carry the bits of heredity called 'genes'. A mutation means a 'change', so the phrase pictures a change in those thread-like parts, which is why it means a change in an organism's 'genes'.
