Etymology

Puppet show comes from simple scenes where a hidden person moved a small figure, the puppet, to act and speak for the crowd, creating a little play or the show. Seeing a small figure move like a person is a strong image, so the phrase came to mean 'a performance in which puppets are used to tell a story'.
