Etymology

onomatopoeic comes from Greek parts onoma 'name' and poiein 'to make', so it literally means 'making a name'. The poiein part is the same root in the words 'poet' and 'poem', which are about 'making' words. That's why an onomatopoeic word sounds like the thing it names, for example 'buzz', 'splash' or 'crash'.
