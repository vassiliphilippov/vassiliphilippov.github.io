Etymology

Cheilanthes comes from Greek parts cheil- 'lip' and -anthes 'flower'. The curled edge of the fern's leaf looks like a little 'lip' that hides the spore area, so these plants are called 'lip ferns' and the name helps you remember their small, folded leaves.
