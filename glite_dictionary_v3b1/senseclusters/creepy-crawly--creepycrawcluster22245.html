Etymology

Creepy-crawly as an adjective comes from the same words creep and crawl, with creep meaning 'to make you feel afraid' and crawl meaning 'to move along the surface'. So a creepy-crawly feeling is like your skin being 'crawled' on, which is why it makes you feel uneasy.
