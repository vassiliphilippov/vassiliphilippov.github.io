Etymology

The compound mental disturbance joins mental, which means the mind, and disturbance, which means something that breaks the calm. Think of a quiet room where a sudden noise makes you jump and lose clear thinking; a mental disturbance is when the mind is shaken in the same way, so it describes a 'problem with thoughts or feelings'.
