Etymology

insinuation comes from Latin in- 'into' and sinuare 'to bend', a root you see in sinuous meaning 'curvy', so imagine a thought that 'slides in' around the edge. So an insinuation is a quiet or indirect hint that 'slips into' a conversation instead of being said clearly.
