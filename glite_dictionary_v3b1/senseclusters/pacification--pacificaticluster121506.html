Etymology

pacification comes from the Latin root pax meaning 'peace' and the verb pacify meaning 'to make peace'. So when a government speaks of a pacification of a region, it literally means 'making it peaceful', which is why the term is used for efforts (even military ones) to end resistance and restore order.
