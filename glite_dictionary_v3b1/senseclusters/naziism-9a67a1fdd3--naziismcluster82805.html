Etymology

Naziism comes from Nazi, a short form of the German Nationalsozialist, which literally means 'National Socialist'. A helpful way to remember it is that National means 'of the nation' and socialist is about ideas for society, so Naziism is the set of extreme, racist ideas and policies that led to rule by a dictator.
