Etymology

bork is slang that means to damage or break something so it stops working. A helpful way to remember it is that it sounds like 'broke', and some people link it to judge Robert Bork or the Muppet 'Swedish Chef' who says 'bork bork bork'. So, to bork something is to make it 'broken' or unusable.
