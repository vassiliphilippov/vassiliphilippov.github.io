Etymology

The phrase unsuccessful person comes from a clear picture of someone whose tries do not reach the goal; the word unsuccessful means 'not reaching success' and person is the one doing the trying. So, unsuccessful person means 'someone who repeatedly fails or does not achieve their goals'.
