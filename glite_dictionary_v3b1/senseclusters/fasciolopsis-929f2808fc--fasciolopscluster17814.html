Etymology

Fasciolopsis comes from Latin and Greek parts meaning 'ribbon' and 'appearance': fasciola means 'ribbon' or 'band' and -opsis means 'appearance'. So the name means 'looks like a small ribbon', which fits these flat, ribbon-like worms that live in the intestines.
