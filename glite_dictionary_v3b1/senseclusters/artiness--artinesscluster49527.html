Etymology

artiness comes from the simple word art, which goes back to Latin ars meaning 'skill' or 'craft', and the ending -ness meaning 'state'. A helpful way to remember the negative sense is to think of someone adding 'too much art' to their style to look special — that can seem fake or showy, so artiness can mean a showy, not natural style.
