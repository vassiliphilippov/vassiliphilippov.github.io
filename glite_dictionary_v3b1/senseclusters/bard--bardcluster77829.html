Etymology

bard (verb) often seen as barding, comes from the same old French word barde meaning 'horse armour' or 'decorative cloth', and it simply means 'to cover' a horse with that cloth or armour. That's why to bard a horse is to dress it in decorative cloth or armour for display or battle.
