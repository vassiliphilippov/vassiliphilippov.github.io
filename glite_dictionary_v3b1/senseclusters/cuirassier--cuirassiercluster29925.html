Etymology

cuirassier comes from the French word cuirasse, meaning 'breastplate', and cuirasse is related to cuir, French for 'leather', because early chest armor was made of leather. A cuirassier was originally a soldier on a horse who wore a cuirass, so today the word means a horse soldier in heavy armor.
