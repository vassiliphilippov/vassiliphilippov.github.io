Etymology

Phytomastigina comes from the parts phyto- meaning 'plant' and mastig- meaning 'whip', which brings to mind a tiny plant with a whip-like tail. So, Phytomastigina are plant-like single-celled organisms with a whip-like tail and chlorophyll, and that is why they are often treated as a kind of tiny algae.
