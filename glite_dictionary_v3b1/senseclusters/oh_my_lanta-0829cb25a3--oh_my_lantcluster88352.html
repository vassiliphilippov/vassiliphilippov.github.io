Etymology

Oh my lanta is a playful, softened version of 'Oh my God'; one popular idea is that people swapped the strong word for the silly-sounding 'Lanta' so it sounds fun rather than serious. That's why the phrase is used to show 'surprise' or 'shock' in a light, joking way.
