Etymology

shanghai comes from the name of the Chinese city Shanghai and a cruel trick on 1800s docks, where thieves would kidnap men and put them on ships bound for Shanghai. So, to shanghai someone came to mean 'force' them to 'join a ship's crew' or do work against their will.
