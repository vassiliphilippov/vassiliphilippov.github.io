Etymology

The word Spencerian comes from the name Spencer because Platt Rogers Spencer created a famous style of cursive, and the ending -ian means 'one who is connected to' or 'one who does'. So a Spencerian is someone who writes in or teaches the graceful Spencerian handwriting style.
