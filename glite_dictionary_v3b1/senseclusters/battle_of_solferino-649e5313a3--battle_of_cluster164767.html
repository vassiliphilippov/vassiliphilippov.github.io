Etymology

Battle of Solferino is named after the town of Solferino in northern Italy, where French and Austrian armies fought in 1859. Henry Dunant saw many wounded soldiers after the battle and this 'inspired' him to help create the Red Cross, so the battle is remembered as a 'turning point' for modern aid and for Italy's unification.
