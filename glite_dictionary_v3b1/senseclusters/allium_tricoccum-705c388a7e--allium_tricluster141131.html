Etymology

Allium tricoccum is a plant name that comes from old Latin words: Allium means 'garlic' or 'onion' and tricoccum may mean 'three small parts', a picture of the plant's tiny bulbs or seeds. That's why the name helps you remember it is a wild garlic-like plant whose leaves and bulbs are eaten.
