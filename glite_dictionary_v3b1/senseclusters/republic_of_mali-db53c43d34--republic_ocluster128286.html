Etymology

Republic of Mali takes its name from the old Mali Empire, a powerful kingdom famous for rich kings, gold and long trade routes including the city of Timbuktu. After independence from France in 1960, the country kept the name Mali and added 'Republic' to show it was now an 'independent state'.
