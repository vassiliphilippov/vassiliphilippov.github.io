Etymology

Aldosteronism comes from the hormone aldosterone, which is built from the parts aldo- and -sterone, like the familiar word steroid. A helpful way to remember it is that the hormone makes the body 'hold' salt and water, so aldosteronism is the condition of having too much of that hormone, often causing high blood pressure.
