Etymology

Carbonarism comes from the Italian group Carbonari, a name that means 'charcoal burners' and is related to carbon ('coal'). Imagine people meeting in dark places near charcoal ovens — the name shows they were secret groups of workers, so Carbonarism means the ideas and organization of that secret revolutionary movement.
