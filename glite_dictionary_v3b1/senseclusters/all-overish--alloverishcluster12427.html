Etymology

all-overish comes from the parts all + over, which mean 'everywhere', plus the ending -ish, which means 'somewhat' or 'like', so it suggests being 'somewhat all over' or scattered and unfocused. That's why all-overish describes feeling unwell, uneasy, or when a person or place seems disorganized.
