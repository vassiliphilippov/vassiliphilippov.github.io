Etymology

The plant name rhizomatous begonia says how the plant grows: rhizomatous means it has long, creeping stems that send out roots and new shoots, and begonia is the plant type. These creeping stems let the plant 'spread' across the ground and make beautiful leaves, so the name tells you it is a begonia that 'spreads' by rhizomes.
