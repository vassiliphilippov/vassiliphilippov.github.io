Etymology

T-bar lift gets its name from the real T-shaped bar riders use: the metal bar looks like the letter T, and skiers hold the bar or rest it behind them while they are 'pulled uphill'. That's why the phrase refers to the ski lift that uses a T-shaped bar to move people up the slope.
