Etymology

disconcertment comes from the parts dis- meaning 'not' or 'away' and concert meaning 'harmony' or a musical show like a concert. So disconcertment is the uneasy, embarrassed feeling you get when the 'harmony' is broken and something suddenly surprises or confuses you.
