Etymology

cutesy-poo comes from the simple word cute plus playful, baby-like endings -sy and -poo, like in cutie or sweetie-poo. A helpful way to remember it is that people made the word as a joking, childlike way to make something extra sweet, so cutesy-poo means 'excessively cute' or 'silly in a cute way'.
