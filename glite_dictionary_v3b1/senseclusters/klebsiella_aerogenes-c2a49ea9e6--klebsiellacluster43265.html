Etymology

Klebsiella aerogenes is a name made from a scientist's name and a word that means 'air-producing': Klebsiella honors Dr. Klebs, and aerogenes means 'makes gas'. That's a useful image because these bacteria often make gas in lab tests and live in people and the environment, so the name fits a bacteria that can sometimes cause infections.
