Etymology

road to Damascus comes from a Bible story about Saul, who on the road to Damascus saw a bright light, heard a voice, and suddenly changed his beliefs. The picture of someone stopped on a road and completely changing their view is why the phrase means 'a sudden and important change in beliefs' today.
