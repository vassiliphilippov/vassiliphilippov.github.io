Etymology

palingenesis comes from the Greek parts palin meaning 'again' and genesis meaning 'birth' or 'origin'. So the word is a natural way to say 'rebirth' or 'renewal' — used for biological regrowth or for the idea of something being born again in philosophy or myth.
