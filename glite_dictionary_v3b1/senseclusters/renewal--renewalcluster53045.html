Etymology

renewal comes from the parts re- 'again' and new 'not old', which gives a simple picture for 'land renewal' — making used or wasted land 'new again' for parks, homes, or farms. So land or urban renewal is the process of turning empty or damaged areas into useful, 'new again' places.
