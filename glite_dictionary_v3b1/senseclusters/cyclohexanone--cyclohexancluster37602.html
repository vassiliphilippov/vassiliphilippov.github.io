Etymology

cyclohexanone comes from three parts: cyclo- meaning 'ring' (like 'cycle'), hex- meaning 'six' (like 'hexagon'), and -one meaning 'a certain chemical type'. So, cyclohexanone is a chemical that has that 'one' type on a ring of six carbon atoms, and the name tells you its shape and makeup.
