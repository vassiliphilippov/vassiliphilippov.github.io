Etymology

environmental business analysis paints the picture of a careful study of a company's surroundings: environmental means the outside factors around a business, business means the company, and analysis means a close look. By checking rules, markets and risks near the company, this study shows 'threats' and 'opportunities' and helps managers decide what to do.
