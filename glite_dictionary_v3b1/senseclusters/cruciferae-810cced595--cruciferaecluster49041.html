Etymology

Cruciferae comes from Latin parts crux 'cross' and -ferae 'bearing', the same root you see in crucifix, which also shows a 'cross'. The flowers of these plants have four petals arranged like a small cross, so the name means 'cross-bearing' and helps you remember cabbage, broccoli and mustard.
