Etymology

Peritrate is a modern drug name that includes the part -nitrate, a hint to nitrate medicines that 'open' blood vessels — think of nitroglycerin, a well-known heart medicine. So, seeing Peritrate as a kind of 'nitrate' helps you remember it treats angina or 'chest pain' by improving blood flow to the heart.
