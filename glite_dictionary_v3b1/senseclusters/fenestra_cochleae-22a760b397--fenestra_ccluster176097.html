Etymology

fenestra cochleae is a Latin name that literally means the 'window' of the cochlea. The word fenestra means 'window' and cochleae points to the cochlea, the small snail-shaped part of the inner ear, so the name gives a clear image of a tiny round 'window' that connects the middle ear and the cochlea.
