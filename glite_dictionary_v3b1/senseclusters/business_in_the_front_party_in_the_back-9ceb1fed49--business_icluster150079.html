Etymology

business in the front, party in the back may come from a popular 1980s hairstyle called the mullet, where the hair is short at the front and long at the back. The short front looked neat like 'business', while the long back looked fun like a 'party', so the phrase now describes something that seems formal in one part and relaxed in the other.
