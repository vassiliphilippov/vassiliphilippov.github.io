Etymology

Madeira winter cherry gets its name from its bright, cherry-like berries that often appear in winter and from the island of Madeira, where the plant became well known. Because the berries look like small cherries but are 'poisonous', the name helps you remember it is a pretty houseplant with dangerous fruit.
