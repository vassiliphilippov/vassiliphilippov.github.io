Etymology

sabra comes from the Hebrew word tzabar, meaning 'prickly pear' or 'cactus', and the plant is rough and spiky on the outside but soft inside. So a sabra is a native-born Israeli who may seem tough or sharp on the outside but is warm and caring inside.
