Etymology

barrio comes from the Spanish word barrio, which meant 'neighborhood' and comes from the Arabic barrī, meaning 'of the land' or 'outside'. So a barrio was originally a part of town outside the center, and that's why today it means a local district often associated with Spanish-speaking communities.
