Etymology

Eupatorium purpureum is often called 'Joe-Pye weed' and one popular story says it was named after a man called Joe Pye who used the tall plant as a folk remedy for fevers. The Latin parts Eupatorium and purpureum point to the plant group and its purple flowers, so the name reminds you of a tall purple flower once used as a home medicine.
