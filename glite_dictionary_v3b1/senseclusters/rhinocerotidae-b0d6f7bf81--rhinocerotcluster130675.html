Etymology

Rhinocerotidae comes from Greek parts: rhino- meaning 'nose', keras meaning 'horn', and -idae meaning 'family'. So Rhinocerotidae is the 'nose-horn family', which helps explain why this name is used for the group of large, horned animals we call rhinos.
