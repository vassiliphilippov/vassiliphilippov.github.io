Etymology

Hector Berlioz was a French composer who wrote very dramatic, dream-like music, for example the piece 'Symphonie fantastique', and he used a very large orchestra to create wild images. That's why his name is remembered today for bold, emotional Romantic music that changed how people thought about orchestral sound.
