Etymology

Coil notebook comes from the coil, a spiral of wire or plastic that holds the notebook pages together like a small spring. Because that coil lets pages 'turn easily' and 'lie flat' or fold back, people call a notebook a coil notebook when they mean one that is easy to write in and use.
