Etymology

discalceate comes from dis- meaning 'without' and calceus meaning 'shoe', and is like Spanish 'descalzo' which means 'barefoot', so it literally means 'without shoes'. That's why discalceate describes monks or friars who go barefoot or wear only sandals to show humility and poverty.
