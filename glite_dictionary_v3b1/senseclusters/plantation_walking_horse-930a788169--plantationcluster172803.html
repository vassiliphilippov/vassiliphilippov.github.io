Etymology

The compound noun plantation walking horse likely comes from the old Southern 'plantations' where people needed a horse that could travel all day with a smooth, fast 'running walk'. The name links the place plantation and the way it moves walking, so it now means a breed known for a comfortable, steady way of walking and long endurance.
