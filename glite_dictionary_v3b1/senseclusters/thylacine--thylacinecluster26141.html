Etymology

thylacine comes from a Greek root thylakos meaning 'pouch'. That is a helpful memory hook because the thylacine was a Tasmanian animal that carried its young in a 'pouch', and its common name, the Tasmanian tiger, reminds you of the stripes on its back.
