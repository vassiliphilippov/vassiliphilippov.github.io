Etymology

Hyaloid membrane comes from Greek words meaning 'glass-like' for hyaloid and 'skin' for membrane, so it paints the image of a thin, clear skin around the eye's jelly. That's why the name means the clear layer that holds the vitreous and separates it from the retina.
