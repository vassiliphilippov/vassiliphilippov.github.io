Etymology

maharishi uses the same parts maha 'great' and rishi 'sage', and so it is used as a title for a great teacher. A helpful way to remember famous figures like Maharishi Mahesh Yogi is that the title means 'great sage', which explains why it was used as a name for a spiritual leader.
