Etymology

binge-eating syndrome comes from the words binge and eating, where binge means doing something a lot in a short time. Imagine someone eating a lot of food in one sitting and then feeling 'guilt' and 'shame', so the term means the problem of eating too much again and again and feeling bad afterward.
