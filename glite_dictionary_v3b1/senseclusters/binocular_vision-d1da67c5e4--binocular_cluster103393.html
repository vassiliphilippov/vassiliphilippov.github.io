Etymology

binocular vision comes from a simple idea: binocular means 'two eyes' and vision means 'seeing', so it is seeing with both eyes together. When both eyes work together they let you see more around you and help your brain 'judge how far away' things are, so the term means seeing that helps you judge distance.
