Etymology

no way to treat a lady comes from old polite ideas about how a lady should be treated. It gives a simple picture: saying there is 'no way' to treat her means the behaviour is 'rude' or 'disrespectful', so we use the phrase to say someone's actions toward a woman are not acceptable.
