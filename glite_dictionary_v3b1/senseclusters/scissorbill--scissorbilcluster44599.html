Etymology

The word scissorbill looks like scissor + bill, with 'scissor' meaning 'to cut' and 'bill' meaning 'beak'. A helpful way to remember it is to picture a bird with a cutting beak that looks silly and easy to catch, and that's why a scissorbill is slang for a foolish person who is easily tricked.
