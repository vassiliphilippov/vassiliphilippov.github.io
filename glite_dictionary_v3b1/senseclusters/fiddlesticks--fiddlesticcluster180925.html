Etymology

fiddlesticks is an old, mild exclamation that likely comes from fiddle (a small violin) plus sticks, giving a sense of something small or silly. That's why people use fiddlesticks to show 'nonsense', 'surprise', or 'annoyance' instead of stronger language.
