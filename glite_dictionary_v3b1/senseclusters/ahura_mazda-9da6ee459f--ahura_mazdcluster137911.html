Etymology

Ahura Mazda comes from very old Persian words meaning 'Wise Lord': Ahura means 'lord' and Mazda means 'wisdom'. So the name paints a picture of a wise, guiding god, and that's why Ahura Mazda is the supreme god of light, goodness, and truth.
