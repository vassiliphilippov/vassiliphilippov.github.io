Etymology

lateral cerebral sulcus comes from its parts: lateral meaning 'side', cerebral meaning 'brain', and sulcus meaning 'groove'. It is a deep groove on the side of the brain that separates the frontal and temporal lobes, so the name directly tells you what it is.
