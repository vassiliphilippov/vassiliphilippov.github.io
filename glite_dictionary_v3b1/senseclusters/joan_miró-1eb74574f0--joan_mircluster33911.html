Etymology

Joan Miró was a Spanish painter who made bright, simple shapes like stars, eyes and lines that look like drawings from dreams or a child's imagination. He loved the sea and wide sky, and his loose, playful lines and colors made his work feel free and surprising, so today his name often means 'bright, imaginative art'.
