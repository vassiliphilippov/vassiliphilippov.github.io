Etymology

explosive unit comes from the idea of counting how much power a blast has by comparing a fixed explosive amount called a unit, like a standard block of TNT. People used a standard blast as a 'unit' so they could say a big explosion was the same as several of those blocks, and that's why explosive unit now means a measure of the force of an explosion.
