Etymology

Go walkabout comes from a traditional practice among Aboriginal Australians, where young men would leave home and spend a long time walking in the bush to connect with the land and learn about themselves. Because they walked without a set plan, go walkabout now means 'to travel or wander freely', often to clear your mind or 'find yourself'.
