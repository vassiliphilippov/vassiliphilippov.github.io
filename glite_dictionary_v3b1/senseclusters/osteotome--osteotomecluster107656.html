Etymology

osteotome comes from Greek parts osteo- meaning 'bone' and -tome meaning 'to cut', the same idea you see in lobotomy ('cutting a lobe'). So an osteotome is literally a 'bone cutter', which is why surgeons use it to cut or shape bone.
