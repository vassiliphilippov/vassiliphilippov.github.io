Etymology

horselaugh comes from the simple English words horse and laugh, and it creates an image of a laugh that sounds like a horse 'neighing' or making a loud, rough noise. So, a horselaugh is a loud, rough laugh that reminds people of a horse, which is why the word means a laugh like a neigh.
