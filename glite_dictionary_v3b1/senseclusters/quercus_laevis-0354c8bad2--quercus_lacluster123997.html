Etymology

Quercus laevis is the Latin name for a small tree often called the 'turkey oak'. The part Quercus means 'oak' and laevis is the second part of the name; its leaves have bristle-tipped lobes like a turkey's toes, so the name helps you picture and remember this dry, sandy-soil oak.
