Etymology

Cryptococcus gattii comes from Greek and a scientist's name: crypto means 'hidden' and coccus means 'round', and gattii honors a researcher named Gatti. So the name gives a simple picture: a small, round fungus that is hard to see and can 'cause infection' in people and animals.
