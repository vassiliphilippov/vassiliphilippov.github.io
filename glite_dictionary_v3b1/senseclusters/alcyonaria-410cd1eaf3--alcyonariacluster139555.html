Etymology

Alcyonaria comes from the Greek root alkyón, meaning 'kingfisher'. In Greek myth the kingfisher brought calm seas, called 'halcyon days', and early scientists used this name for soft corals that live in warm, calm waters. So, think of Alcyonaria as 'kingfisher' corals of the sea, which helps you remember they are marine soft corals with many tentacles.
