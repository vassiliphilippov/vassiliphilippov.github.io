Etymology

striated muscle cell comes from the clear stripes you can see along a long muscle cell under a microscope; striated means 'striped' and muscle cell says it is a cell in muscle. Because these long, striped cells can contract to make the body move, the name refers to cells that make 'voluntary movement'.
