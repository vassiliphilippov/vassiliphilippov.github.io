Etymology

dubby may be remembered by its soft sound like dull or dozy, words that give a 'heavy' or 'slow' feeling after little sleep, and a helpful way to remember it is to imagine moving slowly and foggy in the head. So dubby means feeling tired, sleepy, or sluggish.
