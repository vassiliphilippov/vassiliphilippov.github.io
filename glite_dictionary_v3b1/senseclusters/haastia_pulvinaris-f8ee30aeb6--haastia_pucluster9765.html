Etymology

Haastia pulvinaris comes from a scientist named Haast and the Latin word pulvinaris, which means 'cushion-like'. This gives a clear picture: the plant grows in low, dense 'cushions' of tawny leaves on alpine rocks, so the name helps you remember it is a cushion-forming ground cover.
