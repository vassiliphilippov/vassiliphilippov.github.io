Etymology

Caesalpinia sepiaria is named in two parts: Caesalpinia honors Andrea Cesalpino, an Italian doctor who studied plants, and sepiaria comes from a Latin word for 'hedge' because the thorny shrub was often used as a living fence. That's why the name helps you remember it is a spreading, thorny shrub used as a hedge with bright yellow flowers.
