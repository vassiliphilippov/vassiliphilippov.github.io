Etymology

Hindustan comes from Persian parts Hindu ('people of the Indus') and -stan ('land' or 'place'), and you can see the same -stan in names like Pakistan and Afghanistan. So Hindustan originally meant 'land of the Hindus' and is used for the region of India where Hindu culture is strong.
