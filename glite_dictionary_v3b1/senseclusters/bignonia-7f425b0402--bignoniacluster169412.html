Etymology

Bignonia was named after a French man called Bignon, and the ending -ia is often added to plant names to mean 'of' or 'for' a person. A helpful way to remember it is to think of Gardenia, another plant named for a person, so Bignonia really means the plant 'of Bignon' and is not related to the word 'big'.
