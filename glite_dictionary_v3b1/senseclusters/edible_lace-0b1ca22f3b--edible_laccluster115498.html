Etymology

edible lace comes from the simple idea of making food look like delicate cloth. Bakers create thin patterned sheets from sugar or other ingredients that look like real lace, and because they are edible you can eat them, so the name means 'a decorative, lace-like sheet used to decorate cakes and desserts'.
