Etymology

The noun Arabian Nights' Entertainment comes from the old story of Scheherazade, a clever storyteller who told the king a new tale every night and stopped in the morning so he would want more and 'spare her life'. That's why the name joins Arabian, Nights', and Entertainment and now means a famous book of old stories from the Arab world.
