Etymology

Express luxury liner combines express ('very fast'), luxury ('very comfortable'), and liner ('a big passenger ship that runs on a fixed route'). Picture a shining palace on the sea racing between cities, so it means 'a large passenger ship designed for fast travel and equipped with luxurious accommodations and amenities'.
