Etymology

sargasso weed names the brown seaweed that floats in large patches in the Sargasso Sea; the parts sargasso (the sea) and weed (a water plant) show the literal image. So it means a type of 'floating seaweed' that forms mats, supports sea life, and can even tangle ships.
