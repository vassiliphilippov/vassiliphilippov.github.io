Etymology

turkey leg names the turkey's leg, the large lower part of the bird that people roast and eat. Because people often hold and eat this roasted turkey leg like a drumstick at fairs and holiday meals, the phrase now means 'a cooked piece of the bird' or simply 'a drumstick'.
