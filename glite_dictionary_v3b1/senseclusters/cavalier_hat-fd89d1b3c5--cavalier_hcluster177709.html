Etymology

The noun cavalier hat comes from cavalier, the name for the horse-riding supporters of the king in the 1600s who often wore soft, wide-brimmed felt hats with long feathers. Because those men were known for that look, the words cavalier and hat became the name for that style, so today it means 'a soft wide-brimmed 17th-century hat often with a plume'.
