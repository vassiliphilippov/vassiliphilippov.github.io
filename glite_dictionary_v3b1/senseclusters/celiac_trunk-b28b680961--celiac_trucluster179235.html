Etymology

celiac trunk The noun celiac trunk comes from two parts: celiac, meaning 'belly', and trunk, like a tree's main stem. Picture a tree trunk that splits into big branches going to the stomach, liver and spleen, and the artery does the same, so the name helps you remember it is the main blood route for the belly organs.
