Asiatic shrew mole uses Asiatic (from Asia), shrew, and mole to paint a simple picture: a tiny animal from Asia that looks a bit like a shrew but digs and lives like a mole as it hunts for insects. So the name tells you it is a small, 'burrowing' and 'insect-eating' mammal.
