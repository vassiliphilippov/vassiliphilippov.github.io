Etymology

Egyptian cat, with Egyptian meaning 'from Egypt' and cat meaning the animal, comes from old Egypt where people kept and often treated cats as special animals. That's why Egyptian cat now means 'a domestic cat from Egypt' or a cat that looks like those famous, graceful animals.
