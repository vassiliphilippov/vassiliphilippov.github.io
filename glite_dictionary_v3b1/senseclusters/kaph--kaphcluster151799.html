Etymology

The word kaph is the Hebrew name for the eleventh letter and comes from a word meaning 'palm' or 'open hand', because the old picture for it looked like a hand. The same letter later became Greek kappa and our letter K, so kaph still often stands for the 'k' or 'kh' sound.
