Etymology

radial keratotomy comes from the words radial and keratotomy: radial means 'like the spokes of a wheel' and keratotomy means 'cutting the cornea'. Surgeons made small cuts in the clear front of the eye that looked like spokes so the cornea could change shape, so the operation 'corrects nearsightedness'.
