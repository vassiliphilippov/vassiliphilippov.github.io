Etymology

Flowering onion is a plant name that comes from onion bulbs that send up a tall stem topped by a round ball of small flowers. Flowering shows it makes flowers and onion shows it comes from an onion bulb, so the name simply means 'an onion that makes flowers' and explains why gardeners grow it for its pretty blooms.
