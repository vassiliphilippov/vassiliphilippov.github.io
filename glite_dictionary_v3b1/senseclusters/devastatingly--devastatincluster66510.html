Etymology

devastatingly comes from devastate, which once meant 'to make empty' or 'destroy' and is related to vast, the English word 'vast' meaning very large or empty. A helpful way to remember the modern use is to think of something so strong it stuns people, so when someone is 'devastatingly' charming or handsome it means they have an extremely powerful effect.
