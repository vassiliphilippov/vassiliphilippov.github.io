Etymology

commercial finance company combines commercial (about business) and finance company (a firm that lends money), so picture a factory owner or shopkeeper going to a firm that only helps other businesses with cash. That's why it means 'a company that gives loans to businesses, especially manufacturers and wholesalers'.
