Etymology

The term ecclesiastical law comes from ecclesiastical, meaning 'church', and law, meaning 'rules'. Long ago, church leaders made special rules to run services and daily life, and these 'church rules' became a separate system of law. So ecclesiastical law today means the rules made by Christian churches to govern their organization and work.
