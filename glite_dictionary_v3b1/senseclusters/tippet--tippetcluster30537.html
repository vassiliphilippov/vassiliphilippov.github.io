Etymology

tippet comes from an old French word for a 'small cloak' or hood, and the ending -et shows it was a little or short piece. A helpful way to remember it is to imagine a small 'tip' of fur or cloth you drape over your shoulders, which is why a tippet is a short fur scarf or shoulder cape.
