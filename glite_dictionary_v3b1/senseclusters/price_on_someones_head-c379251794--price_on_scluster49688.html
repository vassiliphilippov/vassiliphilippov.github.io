Etymology

price on someone's head may come from old times when towns or rulers put a money price on a criminal and sometimes asked for the criminal's head as proof of capture. Hunters would bring the person or proof to claim the money, so the image of money on a person's head came to mean 'a reward for catching or killing someone'.
