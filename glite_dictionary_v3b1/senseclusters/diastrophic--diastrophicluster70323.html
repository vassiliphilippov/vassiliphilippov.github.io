Etymology

diastrophic comes from Greek parts dia- meaning 'through' and stroph meaning 'turning'. In geology, a 'diastrophic' change is when the Earth's crust bends or 'turns'—this sense of large deformation explains why the word describes folds, faults, and mountain building.
