Etymology

hygroscopic comes from two old parts: hygro- meaning 'moist' or 'wet' and -scopic (like scope) meaning 'to look' or 'show'. A helpful image is a 'moisture watcher' that pulls water from the air, which is why hygroscopic describes a material that tends to absorb moisture.
