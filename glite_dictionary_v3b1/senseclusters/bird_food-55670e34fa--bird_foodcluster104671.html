Etymology

The name bird food is very literal: bird + food shows it was simply the food people mixed and left for birds. People began making special mixes of seeds and nuts to put in feeders or on the ground so wild or pet birds could eat, especially in winter. That's why bird food means 'a mixture of seeds or other food specially prepared or given to birds'.
