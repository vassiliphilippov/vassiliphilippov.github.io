Etymology

Macroclemys temmincki comes from old Greek words and a scientist's name: Macro means 'large' and clemys was used for 'turtle', while temmincki honors the scientist Temminck. So the name gives the idea of a very big turtle, which fits the alligator snapping turtle's huge size and heavy, prehistoric look.
