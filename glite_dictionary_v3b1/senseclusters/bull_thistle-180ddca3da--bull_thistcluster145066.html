Etymology

bull thistle may get its name from the plant's large size and very sharp spines that remind people of a bull's horns. Because the word bull suggests strength and the word thistle means a prickly plant, the name stuck and today bull thistle means a common purple-flowered 'weed' with tough, pointy leaves.
