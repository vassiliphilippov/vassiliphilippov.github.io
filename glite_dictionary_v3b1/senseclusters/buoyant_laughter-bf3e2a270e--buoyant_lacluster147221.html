Etymology

The phrase buoyant laughter uses the word buoyant, meaning 'able to float', to paint a picture of laughter that rises like a bright balloon or keeps people afloat. So, buoyant laughter means 'cheerful, lively, and uplifting' because it makes people feel lighter and happier.
