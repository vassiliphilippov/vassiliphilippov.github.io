Etymology

Crotalus tigris may come from old words that describe the snake's look. The first part, Crotalus, means 'rattle' because of the small rattle on its tail, and the second part, tigris, means 'tiger' for its dark bands like a tiger, so the name points to the snake's rattling tail and striped pattern.
