Etymology

Xanthorrhoeaceae comes from the plant name Xanthorrhoea, which breaks into Greek parts xanthos 'yellow' and -rrhoea 'flow'. The name likely points to the yellow sap or gum that flows from some of these plants, so Xanthorrhoeaceae is the family of those yellow-sap flowering plants.
