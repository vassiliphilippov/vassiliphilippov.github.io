Etymology

niggardness comes from an old Norse word hnuggr meaning 'stingy' and from the older English word niggard meaning 'not willing to give'. Although it sounds like a racial slur, it is not related to that word, and today it simply means a strong unwillingness to spend or share, so you can remember it as the opposite of 'generous'.
