Etymology

clawlike comes from the simple word claw (the curved, sharp nail on a bird or animal's foot) and the ending -like, which means 'similar to'. So clawlike means 'looking like a claw' or 'having the shape of a claw', which is why we use it for fingers, talons, or objects that are sharp and curved.
