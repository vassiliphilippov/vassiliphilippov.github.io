Etymology

Kobus leche may come from a local word 'lechwe' for the water-loving antelope, with Kobus as the group name and leche from that local word. Picture a tawny animal with long back legs splashing through shallow marshes, and that image explains why the name refers to this antelope that lives on wet grassy plains.
