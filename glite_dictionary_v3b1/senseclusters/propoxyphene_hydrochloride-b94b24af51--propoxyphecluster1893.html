Etymology

propoxyphene hydrochloride comes from two parts: propoxyphene, the active molecule that eases pain, and hydrochloride, the chemical form that makes it stable as a medicine. So the name simply points to this specific mild pain drug used to relieve 'mild to moderate pain'.
