Etymology

tamale pie may come from taking a Mexican tamale and turning it into a baked pie-style dish by spreading the tamale filling in a pan instead of wrapping it. Imagine spicy meat and corn spread like a pie and topped with cornbread, and that picture explains why the name now means 'a baked dish with the taste of tamales'.
