Etymology

The word maintenance comes from Latin parts manu- meaning 'hand' and -tenere meaning 'to hold', like in manual. A helpful way to remember this sense is to think of 'holding' someone's life by giving food or care so they can keep living, which is why maintenance here means sustaining life.
