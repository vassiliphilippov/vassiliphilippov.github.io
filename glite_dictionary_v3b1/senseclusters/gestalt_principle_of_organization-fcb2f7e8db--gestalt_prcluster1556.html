Etymology

Gestalt principle of organization comes from the German word Gestalt, meaning 'shape' or 'whole', and organization, meaning how parts are put together. It gives a simple picture: our mind prefers whole forms, so it naturally groups similar or close items into a single pattern, and that's why the principle explains how we see unified shapes in design.
