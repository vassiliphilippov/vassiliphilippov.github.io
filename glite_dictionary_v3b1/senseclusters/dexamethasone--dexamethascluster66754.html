Etymology

dexamethasone is built from parts: dex- from a root meaning 'right' (like dexterous), meth- for a small chemical part, and -asone the ending used in steroid names (as in cortisone). So, the name points to a steroid medicine, which helps you remember it is a drug used to reduce inflammation.
