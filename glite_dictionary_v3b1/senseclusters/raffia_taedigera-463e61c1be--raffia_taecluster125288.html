Etymology

Raffia taedigera gets its name from Raffia — the long, strong fibers taken from the palm's leaf stems — and taedigera as the species name. Picture long, ribbon-like stems used to tie roofs and weave baskets; that image shows why this tall 'palm' is known for its strong 'stems' used in building and weaving.
