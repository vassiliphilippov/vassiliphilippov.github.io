Etymology

chlorohydric acid comes from two parts: chloro- (from 'chlorine', a greenish gas) and hydric (from 'hydrogen' or water). The name points to hydrogen chloride mixed with water, so it is another name for 'hydrochloric acid', a very strong, damaging acid.
