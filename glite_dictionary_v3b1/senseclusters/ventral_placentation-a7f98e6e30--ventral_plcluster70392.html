Etymology

ventral placentation uses the parts ventral, which means 'front' or 'belly', and placentation, which means where the plant's 'seeds' are placed, and it creates a clear image: imagine the ovary as a small pouch with seeds attached along its inner 'belly' side. That's why the term means 'seeds attached to the ventral wall'.
