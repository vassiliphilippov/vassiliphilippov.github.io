Etymology

The name typhoid bacillus comes from two simple images: typhoid was the word for a serious fever and sickness, and bacillus is an old Latin word meaning 'little rod', used for rod-shaped germs. So the name tells you it is 'the little rod-shaped germ that causes typhoid fever'.
