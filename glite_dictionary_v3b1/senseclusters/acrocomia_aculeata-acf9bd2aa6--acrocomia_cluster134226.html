Etymology

Acrocomia aculeata gives a clear picture: a tall tropical palm with a thick, spiny trunk and clusters of edible nuts. The second word, aculeata, means 'spiny', so the name helps you remember the tree by its sharp spines and useful nuts, which is why it is often called the 'macaw palm'.
