Etymology

Spodoptera frugiperda comes from old Greek and Latin words: Spodoptera combines words for 'ash' and 'wing', and frugiperda means 'fruit-destroyer'. So the name gives a clear image of a grey-winged moth whose young eat and ruin crops, which is why it is the name of a major crop pest.
