Etymology

The phrase wrongful conduct comes from courts and everyday use where people put together wrongful, meaning 'wrong' or 'illegal', and conduct, meaning how someone behaves. Think of a judge or boss pointing to someone's actions and saying they are 'wrong'; that's why the phrase now means behaviour that breaks moral or civil rules.
