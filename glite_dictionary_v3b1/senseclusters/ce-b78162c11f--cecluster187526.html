Etymology

The word Ce is the chemical symbol for the metal cerium, which was named after the small planet Ceres, itself named for the Roman goddess of farming. A helpful way to remember Ce is to think of the word 'cereal', which links to Ceres, and that is why Ce refers to this grey metal used in things like lighter flints.
