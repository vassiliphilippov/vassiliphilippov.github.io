Etymology

Sennacherib comes from an old Mesopotamian name, Sîn-aḥḫē-erība, built around the moon god Sîn and a word for 'brothers'. It means something like 'the moon god Sîn has given (or replaced) brothers', so you can remember it as a king's name that praises the god and says he had the god's help or a new family member.
