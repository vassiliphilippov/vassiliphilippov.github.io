Etymology

beat the shit out of paints a strong, rude image: beat means to hit and shit is used here to show doing it completely and with force. One popular way to see it is hitting someone so hard they are badly hurt or clearly defeated, so it now means 'to attack someone very violently' or 'to defeat someone completely'.
