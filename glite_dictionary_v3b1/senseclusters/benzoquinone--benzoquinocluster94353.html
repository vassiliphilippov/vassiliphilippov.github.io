Etymology

benzoquinone joins benzo (from 'benzene', a common ring-shaped chemical) and quinone (a name for molecules that can 'accept electrons'). So the name helps you remember it is a benzene-type compound that often has a yellow color, is used in dyes, and works in biology by taking electrons.
