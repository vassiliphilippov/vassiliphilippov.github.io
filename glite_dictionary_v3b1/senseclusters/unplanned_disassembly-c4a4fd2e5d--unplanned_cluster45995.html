Etymology

The noun unplanned disassembly comes from engineers' joke: disassembly normally means taking something apart on purpose and unplanned turns that into a wild picture of a machine suddenly falling into pieces. So, people use it to describe a sudden, catastrophic 'breakdown' of a device.
