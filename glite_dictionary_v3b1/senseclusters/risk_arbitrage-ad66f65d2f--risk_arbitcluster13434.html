Etymology

The term risk arbitrage comes from the trading idea of arbitrage, where you buy one stock and sell another to make a profit, and the word risk, because you are really making a 'bet' that a 'takeover' will finish. So risk arbitrage means buying into a deal to try to earn money, but it can lose a lot if the deal fails.
