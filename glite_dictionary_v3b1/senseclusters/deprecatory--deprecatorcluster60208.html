Etymology

deprecatory comes from the old Latin verb deprecari, which meant 'to pray against' or 'to beg to keep something away'. Over time that idea of 'asking against' became 'showing dislike', so a deprecatory remark is one that shows disapproval or makes something seem unimportant.
