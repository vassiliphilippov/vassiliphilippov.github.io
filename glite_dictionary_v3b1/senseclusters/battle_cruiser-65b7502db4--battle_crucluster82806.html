Etymology

battle cruiser joins battle and cruiser, where battle means 'big fight' and cruiser was a 'fast ship'. Navies built these ships with very large guns but lighter armor so they could go faster, so the name shows a warship made for heavy fighting that relies on speed instead of heavy protection.
