Etymology

CD-ROM drive gets its name from CD-ROM and drive. A CD-ROM is a small shiny disc whose letters mean 'compact disc' and 'read-only memory', meaning you can read but not change its data, and the drive is the machine that spins the disc and reads what is on it, so the whole phrase means 'a device that reads information from CD-ROM discs'.
