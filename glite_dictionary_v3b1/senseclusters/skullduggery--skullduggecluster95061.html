Etymology

skullduggery probably comes from an old Scottish word like 'sculduddery' meaning 'trick' or 'mischief', but its exact history is not certain. A helpful way to remember it is to imagine sneaky, hidden tricks, so skullduggery means secret, dishonest behaviour.
