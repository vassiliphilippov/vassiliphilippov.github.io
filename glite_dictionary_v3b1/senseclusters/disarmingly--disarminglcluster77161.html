Etymology

disarmingly comes from dis- meaning 'remove' and arm meaning 'weapon' or 'defence'. To disarm someone is to take away their weapons, and when we use it about behaviour it means to take away suspicion, so disarmingly describes a way of acting that makes people feel safe and less defensive.
