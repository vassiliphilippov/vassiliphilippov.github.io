Etymology

order Myxobacteriales comes from the name of bacteria called Myxobacteria; the part myxo- means 'slime' because these bacteria move in slimy layers and come together to form little fruit-like groups. The word 'order' simply means a biological 'group', so the name tells you it is a group of slime-making bacteria.
