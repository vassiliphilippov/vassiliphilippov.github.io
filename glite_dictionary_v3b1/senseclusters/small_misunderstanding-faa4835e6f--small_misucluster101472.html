Etymology

The phrase small misunderstanding comes from a simple image: two people hear or read something slightly wrong and for a short time they have different ideas. Because the problem is small (only a tiny error) and a misunderstanding (a wrong idea), it now means 'a minor mix-up' that is not serious.
