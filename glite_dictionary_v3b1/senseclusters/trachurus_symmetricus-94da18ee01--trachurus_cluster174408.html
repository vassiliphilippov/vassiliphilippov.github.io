Trachurus symmetricus may come from old Greek and Latin words. The part Trachurus may mean 'rough' and 'tail' because the fish has a rough-looking tail area, and symmetricus means 'even' or 'balanced', which points to the fish's regular body shape, so the name fits this Pacific jack mackerel.
