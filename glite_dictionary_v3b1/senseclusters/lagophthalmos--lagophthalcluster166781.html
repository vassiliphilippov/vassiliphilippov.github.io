Etymology

lagophthalmos comes from Greek parts lagōs 'hare' and ophthalmos 'eye', and the same ophthalmos root is in ophthalmology 'study of the eye'. People once thought hares sleep with their eyes open, so lagophthalmos means being unable to fully close the eyelids.
