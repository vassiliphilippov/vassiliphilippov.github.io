Etymology

kitambilla comes from local names in Sri Lanka and southern India, likely Sinhala or Tamil, for a maroon-purple fruit with tart, sweet pulp. So, think of it as a South Asian 'gooseberry' to link the word to the fruit often used in jams and preserves.
