Etymology

The word careen comes from an old word for the ship's keel 'the bottom part of a ship', carina, and first meant to tip a ship onto its side for cleaning. From that image of a ship leaning over, careen came to mean moving quickly and out of control, which is why we now say a car or person can 'careen' across the road.
