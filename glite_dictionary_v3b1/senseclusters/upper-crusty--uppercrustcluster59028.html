Etymology

upper-crusty comes from the phrase upper crust, where upper means 'top' and crust is the 'outer part of bread' that sits on top; people used this bread image to talk about the richest or most social group. So upper-crusty describes someone who acts snobbish, as if they belong to that 'top' class.
