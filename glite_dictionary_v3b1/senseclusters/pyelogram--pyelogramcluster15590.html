Etymology

pyelogram comes from the Greek parts pyelo- meaning 'basin', the part of the kidney that collects urine, and -gram meaning 'a record' or 'something written', as in telegram. So a pyelogram is a 'record' or picture of the kidney's basin, which is why doctors use it as an X-ray to check the kidneys and the tubes that carry urine.
