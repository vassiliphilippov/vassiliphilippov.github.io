Etymology

northern storm petrel comes from sailors' stories: these tiny sea birds often appear when bad weather comes ('storms') and seem to 'walk' on the water with quick steps, like the tale of St. Peter walking on the sea. So storm and petrel link the bird to storms and that walking-on-water image, and 'northern' just shows where it lives.
