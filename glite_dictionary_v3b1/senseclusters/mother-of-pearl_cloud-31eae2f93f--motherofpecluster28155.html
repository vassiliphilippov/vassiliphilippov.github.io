Etymology

mother-of-pearl cloud gets its name from the shiny inside of some shells called mother-of-pearl, which show soft rainbow colors. High, thin clouds can glow with the same pearly, changing colors when the sun is just below the horizon, so the phrase means a high cloud that looks 'bright' and 'pearly'.
