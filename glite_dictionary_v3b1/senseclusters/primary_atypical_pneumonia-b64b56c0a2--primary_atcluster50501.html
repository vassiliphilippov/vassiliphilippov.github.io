Etymology

primary atypical pneumonia is a medical name made of three parts: primary meaning 'first' or main, atypical meaning 'not usual', and pneumonia meaning 'lung infection'. So the name helps you remember it is a lung infection that looks different from usual pneumonia and often affects young people.
