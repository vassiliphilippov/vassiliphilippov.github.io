Etymology

The word surreptitiousness comes from the Latin verb surripere, which means 'to steal secretly' and combines sub- 'under' with rapere 'to take'. A helpful way to remember it is to imagine quietly 'snatching' something without being seen, so surreptitiousness means the quality of being secretive or hidden.
