Etymology

appealingness comes from the verb appeal, which originally meant 'to call' or 'to call to someone', so something 'appealing' is like a thing that 'calls to you' and draws your interest. Adding -ing and -ness turns that into 'the quality of being attractive', which is why appealingness means how attractive or pleasing something is.
