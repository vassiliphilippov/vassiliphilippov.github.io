Etymology

systema alimentarium comes from Latin words that mean a 'system' for 'food': systema means 'system' and alimentarium comes from the Latin for 'food'. Think of the mouth, stomach and intestines as a kitchen line that breaks down and moves food so the body can 'digest' and use it, which is why the phrase names the body's digestive system.
