Etymology

williwaw probably began as a sailors' word and even sounds like the wind itself, as if it imitates a sudden blast. It was used for a sudden, violent cold gust that falls down from coastal mountains, especially in polar regions, so a williwaw means a quick, powerful downrush of wind, like a 'gust' or 'blast'.
