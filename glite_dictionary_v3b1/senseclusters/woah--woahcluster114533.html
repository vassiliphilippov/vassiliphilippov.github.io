Etymology

woah is a modern spelling of the old shout whoa and it is also like the exclamation wow, so a helpful way to remember it is to think of a quick breath or sudden reaction that stops you for a moment. So, people use woah when they are 'surprised', in 'amazement', or do not 'believe' what they see.
