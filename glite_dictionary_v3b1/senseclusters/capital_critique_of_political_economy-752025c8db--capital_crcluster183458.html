Etymology

Capital: Critique of Political Economy is Marx's title that shows the book's aim: Capital means money, property, and the power money gives, and Critique of Political Economy says he will protest the usual ideas about 'political economy' — in other words, how society deals with money, work, and value. That's why the title tells readers the book studies and argues against the way capitalism works.
