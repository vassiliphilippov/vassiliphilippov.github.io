Etymology

Algol comes from the Arabic name al-ghūl, meaning 'the ghoul', and from ra's al-ghūl meaning 'the ghoul's head', the same root as English ghoul. Because the star becomes dim and bright, people thought of it as a blinking demon's eye, so the name matches the star's changing light.
