Etymology

Once more into the breach comes from a famous speech in Shakespeare's play where soldiers are told to go again through a broken part of the city wall. The image of marching back into the breach, the gap in the wall, explains why the phrase today is used to encourage someone to 'try again' or face a hard challenge one more time.
