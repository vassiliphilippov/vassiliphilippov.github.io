Etymology

Lobotes comes from an old Greek root lobos meaning 'lobe' or 'rounded part', the same idea as the English word lobe (as in 'earlobe'). These fish have large rounded fins that can look like three extra 'tails', so the name helps explain why they are called tripletails.
