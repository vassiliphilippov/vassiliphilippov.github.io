Etymology

Anthemis nobilis probably comes from old words: Anthemis for the flower and nobilis meaning 'noble', because people long ago prized its apple-scented leaves and small white flowers. That's why it became a valued, calming herb used in teas and oils and is often called 'Roman chamomile' today.
