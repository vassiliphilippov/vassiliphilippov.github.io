Etymology

tourtiere comes from the French word tourte, which once meant a small bird called a 'turtledove' and later also meant a 'pie' or the cooking dish tourtière. That's why tourtiere today is a rich meat pie in Quebec — the name keeps the old link to pies and the dish used to cook them, often at Christmas.
