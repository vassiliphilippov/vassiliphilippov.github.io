Etymology

The verb zarking around may come from the silly sound zark children make when they pretend to be buzzing robots or insects, and the word around for moving here and there. One popular story says kids would run and shout 'zark' as they played, so zarking around came to mean 'to behave in a silly or playful way' or to waste time.
