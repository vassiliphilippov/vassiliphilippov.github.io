Etymology

The word devilment begins with devil, meaning an 'evil spirit' or a naughty person, and ends with -ment, a part that turns something into a noun meaning an 'action' or state. So, devilment is like 'devilish' or mischievous action — playful or naughty behaviour meant to tease or annoy others.
