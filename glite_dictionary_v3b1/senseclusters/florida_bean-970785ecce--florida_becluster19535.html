Etymology

The plant name Florida bean probably comes from farmers in Florida who planted this climbing bean in warm fields to feed animals and to improve the soil. That's why today Florida bean means a long-vined plant with purple flowers and hairy pods used for 'animal grazing' and 'soil improvement'.
