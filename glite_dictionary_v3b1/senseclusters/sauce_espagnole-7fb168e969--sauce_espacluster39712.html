Etymology

sauce Espagnole comes from French cooking and its name literally means 'Spanish sauce'. Cooks called this rich brown sauceEspagnole because Espagnole means 'Spanish' in French and the recipe used tomatoes and spices people linked to Spain, so the name stuck — that's why today it refers to the classic brown sauce made from browned vegetables, stock, and a little tomato.
