Etymology

Desperate criminal comes from stories of the Wild West and the Spanish word 'desperado', used for outlaws who had 'no hope' and nothing to lose. A desperate person would take big risks, so a criminal like that acted boldly and dangerously, and that's why desperate criminal means 'a bold and dangerous outlaw' today.
