Etymology

Yurak-Samoyed comes from two parts: Yurak, an old name for the people now called Nenets, and Samoyed, an old Russian name for some Arctic peoples that is now seen as 'outdated'. So Yurak-Samoyed simply means the 'language of those northern people', which is why it names a Uralic language once spoken in Siberia.
