Etymology

Tyrannus vociferans comes from Latin words: Tyrannus meaning 'tyrant' and vociferans meaning 'noisy'. Observers called this bird a 'tyrant' because it boldly defends its nest and 'noisy' because it makes loud calls, so the name fits the grey bird with a yellow belly.
