Etymology

imbibition comes from the Latin parts im- 'in' and bibere 'to drink', and it is the same idea as imbibe — literally 'drink in'. So, think of a seed or a sponge 'drinking' water: when a material soaks up liquid and swells, that process is called 'imbibition'.
