Etymology

as bleak as Mordor comes from Mordor, the dark, empty land in the famous book 'The Lord of the Rings'. Mordor is shown as ash, bare rock and no life, so saying something is as bleak as Mordor means it is 'very empty, dark, or hopeless'.
