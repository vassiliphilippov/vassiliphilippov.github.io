Etymology

Volvocales comes from the algal name Volvox, which comes from the Latin volvere meaning 'to roll', plus the scientific ending -ales that marks a group. Many of these green algae form round colonies that spin in ponds, so the name helps you remember their rolling, ball-like groups.
