recluse

0.01/m7.2

adjective US / rɪˈkluːs /

Examples

Etymology

recluse as an adjective comes from the same Latin parts re- 'back' and cludere 'to shut', much like close or closet. So to be recluse is to live apart or keep yourself 'shut away' from others.
