Etymology

The name vitreous humor may come from old words for 'glass' and 'liquid', because vitreous means 'glass-like' and humor means 'liquid'. This creates a simple image of a clear, jelly-like liquid inside the eye, so the name now means the clear gel between the lens and the back of the eye.
