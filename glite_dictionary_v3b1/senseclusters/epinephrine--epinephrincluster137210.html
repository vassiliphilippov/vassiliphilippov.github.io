Etymology

The word epinephrine comes from Greek parts epi- 'upon' and nephros 'kidney', because the hormone is made by glands that sit on top of the kidneys. You may know it as adrenaline, and that helps explain its meaning: it is the quick hormone that makes the body 'get ready' for action and can save lives in severe allergic reactions.
