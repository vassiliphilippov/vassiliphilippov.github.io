Etymology

Kantianism comes from the name Kant and the endings -ian ('belonging to') and -ism ('a system or belief'), like in Marxism or Christianity. So Kantianism means the set of ideas that belong to the philosopher Immanuel Kant, which is why we use it for his philosophical system.
