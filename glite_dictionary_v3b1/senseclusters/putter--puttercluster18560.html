Etymology

The verb putter (to do small, unimportant tasks) comes from the everyday verb put ('to push') and is influenced by the similar word potter ('to work slowly on small jobs'). A helpful way to remember it is to picture moving small things about without a clear plan, which is why to putter is to spend time idly doing little tasks.
