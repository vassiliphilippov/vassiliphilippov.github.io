Etymology

spendthriftiness comes from the older word spendthrift. That word combines spend ('to use money') and thrift ('saving' or 'savings'), so imagine someone who 'spends' their 'thrift' and uses up their savings. So, spendthriftiness is the habit of spending money carelessly.
