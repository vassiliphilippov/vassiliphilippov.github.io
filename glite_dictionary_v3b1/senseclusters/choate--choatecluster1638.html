Etymology

The word choate comes from the older word inchoate, which meant 'not fully formed', and people dropped the beginning in- to make choate, a simple opposite meaning 'fully formed' or 'complete'. That's why in law a choate right or lien is one that is 'complete' and can be enforced in court.
