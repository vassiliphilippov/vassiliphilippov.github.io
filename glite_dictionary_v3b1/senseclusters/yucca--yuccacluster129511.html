Etymology

The word yucca comes from a Caribbean Indigenous word yuca, which Spanish used for the plant 'cassava'. Early European speakers sometimes used the same name for different plants, and that's why yucca now refers to the sharp, pointed plant with stiff leaves and white flowers.
