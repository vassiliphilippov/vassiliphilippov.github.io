Etymology

Yagi aerial is named after the Japanese engineer Hidetsugu Yagi and is an antenna with a long main rod and several short side rods that all point the same way, like a comb guiding radio waves. So a Yagi aerial is used to 'pick up signals' from one direction and get reception from far away.
