Etymology

Primula elatior gets its name from Latin words: primula means 'first' because it blooms early in spring, and elatior means 'taller' because the plant stands a little higher than some other primroses. So the name points to a primrose that flowers early and is a bit taller, which is why gardeners often call it the 'oxlip'.
