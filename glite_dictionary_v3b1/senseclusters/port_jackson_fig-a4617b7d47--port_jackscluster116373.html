Etymology

Port Jackson fig may come from the place Port Jackson, the old name for the harbour at 'Sydney', where early visitors found large fig trees with long, hanging roots and wide tops. Because people often saw these trees near that harbour, they named the plant after the place, which is why the name points to that location.
