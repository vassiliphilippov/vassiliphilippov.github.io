Etymology

Onobrychis viciaefolia comes from old words that describe the plant: Onobrychis roughly means 'donkey chew' and viciaefolia means 'vetch-like leaves'. So, because animals eat it and bees love its flowers, the name points to its use as 'animal fodder' and a good source of 'nectar'.
