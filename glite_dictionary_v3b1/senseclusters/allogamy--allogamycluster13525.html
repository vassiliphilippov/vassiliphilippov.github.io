Etymology

allogamy comes from Greek parts allo- meaning 'other' and -gamy meaning 'marriage', the same root you see in monogamy and polygamy. So allogamy is like a 'marriage' between different plants — fertilization by pollen from another plant of the same kind, which gives more variety in their offspring.
