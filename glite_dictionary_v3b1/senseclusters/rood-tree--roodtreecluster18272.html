Etymology

Rood-tree comes from the Old English word rood meaning 'cross' and the everyday word tree meaning 'tree'. So a rood-tree is literally a 'cross-tree', a wooden cross often shown in churches, which is why it means a picture or model of the cross on which Jesus was crucified.
