Etymology

The noun clot buster comes from a simple picture: the word buster means 'one who breaks', so a clot buster is a medicine that 'busts' a clot, a lump of blood that blocks a blood vessel. That's why the term means 'a drug used to break up blood clots and let blood flow again'.
