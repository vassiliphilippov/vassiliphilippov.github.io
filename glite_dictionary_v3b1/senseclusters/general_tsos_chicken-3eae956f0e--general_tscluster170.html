Etymology

The dish General Tso's chicken is named after a real Chinese general, General Tso, but the food itself was made much later by chefs in Taiwan and the United States. One popular story says chefs used his name to make the dish sound traditional, so today General Tso's chicken means 'a sweet and spicy fried chicken' served in many Chinese-American restaurants.
