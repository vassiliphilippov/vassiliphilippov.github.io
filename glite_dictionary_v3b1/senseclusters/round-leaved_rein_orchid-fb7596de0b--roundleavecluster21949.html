Etymology

round-leaved rein orchid comes from a simple picture: the plant has two round leaves at its base, round-leaved, and a single tall flower stalk that people thought looked like a rein, a leather strap used to guide a horse. So the name tells you how the plant looks and helps you remember this small orchid.
