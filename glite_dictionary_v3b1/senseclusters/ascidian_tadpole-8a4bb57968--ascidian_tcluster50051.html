Etymology

ascidian tadpole is named for its look: a young ascidian, a sea-squirt, in a swimming stage that has a tail like a tadpole with a stiff rod like a tiny spine. So the name points to this 'tadpole' shape and refers to the free-swimming larval stage before it settles and becomes an adult.
