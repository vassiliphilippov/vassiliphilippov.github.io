Etymology

CD4 T cell comes from the CD4 protein, which sits on the cell's surface like a small ID tag, and from T cell, the kind of immune cell. Because cells with the CD4 tag 'help' other immune cells fight infection, the name reminds us both of the marker and the cell's job.
