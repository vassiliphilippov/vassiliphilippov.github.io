Etymology

serosanguineous comes from two parts: sero-, from serum meaning 'clear yellowish fluid', and sanguine, from a word for 'blood'. So, serosanguineous describes a liquid that has both that clear fluid and blood, like the liquid you see coming from a healing wound.
