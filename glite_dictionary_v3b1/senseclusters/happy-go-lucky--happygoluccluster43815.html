Etymology

happy-go-lucky comes from putting three simple words together: happy ('feeling good'), go ('move') and lucky ('fortunate'), so picture someone who just moves through life smiling and trusting things will work out. That's why happy-go-lucky means 'carefree' and not worried about the future.
