Etymology

Scotch laburnum may get its name because the tree laburnum was often grown in Scotland, so people added 'Scotch' to show it was 'Scottish'. Its bright, hanging yellow flowers made it a popular garden tree, and that is why the name now means a decorative shrub with long yellow blooms.
