Etymology

The noun router plane comes from woodworking: it is a small plane with a narrow cutter that 'routes', or 'hollows out', a 'groove' in wood. The name joins router for the hollowing action and plane for the smoothing tool, so router plane means a plane used to make smooth, level grooves.
