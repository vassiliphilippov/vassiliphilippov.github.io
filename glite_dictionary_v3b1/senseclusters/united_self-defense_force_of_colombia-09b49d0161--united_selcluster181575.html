Etymology

United Self-Defense Force of Colombia comes from local armed groups that joined together to protect their towns from attacks by rebels. The words United, Self-Defense, and Force show they were a joined 'armed group' claiming to 'defend' people, but today the name is used for a powerful group blamed for fighting and drug crimes.
