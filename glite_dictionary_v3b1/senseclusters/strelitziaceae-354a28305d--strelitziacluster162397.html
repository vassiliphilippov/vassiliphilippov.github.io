Etymology

Strelitziaceae comes from the plant name Strelitzia, named for the place Strelitz, plus the botanical ending -aceae, which means 'family' for plants. So, Strelitziaceae really means the 'family of Strelitzia', which is why it names the plant family that includes the bird of paradise.
