Etymology

stimulant drug comes from a simple image: long ago a 'stimulant' was like a stick used to poke animals to make them move, so it came to mean something that 'wakes up' or 'speeds up'. A drug is a substance, so a stimulant drug is a substance that temporarily 'wakes you up' or 'speeds your body or mind up'.
