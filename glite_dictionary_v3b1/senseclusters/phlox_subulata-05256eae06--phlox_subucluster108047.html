Etymology

The name Phlox subulata comes from older words: Phlox means 'flame' because its flowers look bright and subulata means 'needle-shaped' because its leaves are thin and pointy. So the name helps you remember it as a low plant that makes a carpet of bright, flame-like flowers and has needle-shaped leaves.
