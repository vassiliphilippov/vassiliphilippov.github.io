Etymology

United Society of Believers in Christ's Second Appearing comes from the group's belief: they were a united society of believers who expected Christ's 'second appearing', or his return, to happen soon. People later called them 'Shakers' because in their meetings they often shook and moved a lot, and the long name tells us they were a group of Christians who lived together and waited for Christ's return.
