Etymology

Chamaecyparis lawsoniana mixes a Greek plant word and a person's name. The part Chamaecyparis comes from words meaning 'on the ground' and 'cypress', and lawsoniana honors a man named Lawson who helped introduce the tree to gardeners, so the full name is like calling it 'Lawson's cypress'.
