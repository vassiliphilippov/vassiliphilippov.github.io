Etymology

Platypoecilus comes from Greek parts: platy- meaning 'flat' or 'broad', as in platypus, and -poecilus meaning 'varied' or 'many-coloured'. So the name shows a broad, colorful little freshwater fish, which fits the small aquarium 'platys'.
