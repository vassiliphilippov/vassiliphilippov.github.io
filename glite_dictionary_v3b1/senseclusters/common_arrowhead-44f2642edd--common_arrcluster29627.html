Etymology

common arrowhead is a water plant whose leaves are shaped like an arrowhead, the pointed tip of an arrow. Because the shape is so clear and the plant grows in many wet places, people called it common arrowhead, with 'common' meaning 'widespread', so the name tells you both what it looks like and that it is often found there.
