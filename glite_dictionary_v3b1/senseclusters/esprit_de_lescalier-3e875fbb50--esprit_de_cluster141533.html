Etymology

esprit de l'escalier comes from a simple picture: someone leaves a party and only as they walk down the stairs do they think of the perfect reply. Here esprit means 'wit' and l'escalier means 'the staircase'. That's why it now means 'a clever remark or comeback that comes too late'.
