Etymology

Ceylon gooseberry gets its name from two simple ideas: Ceylon was the old name for 'Sri Lanka' and gooseberry shows that the fruit looks small and tart like a gooseberry. So the name helps you remember it is a small, bushy tree with sour, purple berries from Sri Lanka.
