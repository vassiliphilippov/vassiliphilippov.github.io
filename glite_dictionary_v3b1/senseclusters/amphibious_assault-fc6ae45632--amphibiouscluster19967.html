Etymology

The phrase amphibious assault comes from the word amphibious, meaning 'able to operate on both land and water', and assault, meaning 'attack'. Imagine soldiers moving from ships across the water onto a beach to attack, and that clear picture is why it means 'a military attack by troops brought ashore by naval ships'.
