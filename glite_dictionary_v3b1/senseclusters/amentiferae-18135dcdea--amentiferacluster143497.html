Etymology

Amentiferae comes from Latin amentum 'catkin', a small hanging flower, and the part -ferae meaning 'bearing' or 'to carry', like in English transfer 'to carry across'. So Amentiferae means 'plants that bear catkins', which is why it was the name for trees such as willows and birches.
