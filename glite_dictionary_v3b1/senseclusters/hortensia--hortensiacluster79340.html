Etymology

hortensia is a name used for the hydrangea and most likely comes from a woman's name like Hortense, which gardeners and botanists once used for the plant. A helpful way to remember it is that it looks like horticulture from Latin hortus meaning 'garden', so hortensia is easy to link to a big, showy garden flower.
