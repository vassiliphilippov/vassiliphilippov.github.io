Etymology

trade rat gets its name from a small, bushy-tailed rodent that loves to carry shiny bits and other objects back to its nest. One popular story says people watched it taking and keeping things like it was 'trading' or 'collecting' treasures, so the word trade was added to rat to mean a rodent that steals and stores small items.
