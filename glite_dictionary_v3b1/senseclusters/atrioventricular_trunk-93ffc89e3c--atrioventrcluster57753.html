Etymology

Atrioventricular trunk paints a clear picture: a main trunk that carries an electrical message from the upper chambers (atria) down to the lower chambers (ventricles). So, because this bundle really 'sends a signal' that makes the ventricles 'contract', the name explains its job in the heartbeat.
