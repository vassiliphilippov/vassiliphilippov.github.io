Etymology

Casuarina equisetifolia may come from names that paint a picture of the tree. The part Casuarina comes from a word for the big bird 'cassowary' because the thin branches look like its feathers, and equisetifolia means 'horsetail leaves' because the leaves are long and thin like a horse's tail, so the name helps you picture this tropical tree with thin, feathery leaves.
