Etymology

hyperpolarize starts with hyper- meaning 'too much' and uses polarize from polar meaning 'poles' or 'opposite ends', so think of hyperactive and the phrase 'polar opposite' to see the parts. So to hyperpolarize a group is to push people far to opposite sides, making compromise very hard.
