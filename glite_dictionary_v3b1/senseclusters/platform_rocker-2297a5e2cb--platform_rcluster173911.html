Etymology

platform rocker The noun platform rocker comes from the simple image of a rocker, a rocking chair, set on a flat platform so only the chair rocks while the base stays still. So today platform rocker means 'a rocking chair mounted on a fixed platform', which lets it 'rock smoothly without the floor moving'.
