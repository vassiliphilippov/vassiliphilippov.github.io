Etymology

The word corakan comes from local names for the finger millet plant in South Asia, where people have used it for a long time. A helpful way to remember it is to think of it as a kind of millet, a small 'grain' used for flatbreads and porridge. So, corakan means the cereal that grows in dry weather and is eaten as a staple food in parts of Asia and Africa.
