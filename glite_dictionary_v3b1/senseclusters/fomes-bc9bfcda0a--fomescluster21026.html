Etymology

Fomes comes from the Latin word fomes, which means 'tinder', the dry material used to start a fire. These fungi grow as hard, wood-like shelves on tree trunks and one famous species was used as real tinder, so the name helps you remember their dry, shelf-like growth on dead wood.
