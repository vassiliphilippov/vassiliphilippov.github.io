Etymology

Argyrotaenia citrana may come from old words meaning argyro 'silver', taenia 'band' and citrana from 'citrus'. The moth often has a pale band on its wings and its caterpillars eat oranges and other citrus, so the name gives the picture of a 'silver-banded citrus' moth.
