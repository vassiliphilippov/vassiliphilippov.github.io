Etymology

The term electric dipole moment comes from a simple picture: two opposite charges, one positive and one negative, placed a short distance apart like a tiny plus and minus on a stick. The word dipole means 'two poles' and moment is like 'strength times distance', so the term measures how strongly separated charges will respond to an electric field.
