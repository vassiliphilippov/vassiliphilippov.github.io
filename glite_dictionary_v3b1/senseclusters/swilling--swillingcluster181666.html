Etymology

The word swilling comes from the old English word swill, which meant 'wash' or the dirty liquid 'slop' used to feed animals, and this sense shifted to pouring or drinking large amounts of liquid. A helpful way to remember it is to think of it like an extreme swallow — taking down lots of liquid fast, so swilling means drinking greedily or too much.
