Etymology

Battle of Little Bighorn is named after the small Little Bighorn River in Montana where, in 1876, General Custer attacked a large group of Sioux and Cheyenne and was defeated. That's why the name reminds people of a dramatic 'last stand' and a major 'defeat' in U.S. history.
