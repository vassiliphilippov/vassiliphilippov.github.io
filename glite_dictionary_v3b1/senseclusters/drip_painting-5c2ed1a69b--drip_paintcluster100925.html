Etymology

drip painting comes from the simple, literal act of letting paint drip or pour onto a flat canvas instead of painting carefully with a brush. The picture to remember is an artist moving around a canvas on the floor while drops of paint fall and spread, so the phrase now means 'a painting' made by dripping paint.
