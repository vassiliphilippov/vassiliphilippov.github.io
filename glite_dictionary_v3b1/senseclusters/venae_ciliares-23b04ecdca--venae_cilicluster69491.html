Etymology

venae ciliares comes from Latin: venae means 'veins' and ciliares comes from 'cilia', the word for 'eyelashes' or tiny hairs near the eye. The name gives a simple picture of small veins by the ciliary, eyelash-like part of the eye, so it refers to the small veins that drain blood from the ciliary body.
