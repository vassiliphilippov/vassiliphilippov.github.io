Etymology

Kurdistan Workers Party says who the group was for and what it wanted: Kurdistan means the 'land of the Kurds', Workers shows it began as a movement for 'working people', and Party means a 'political group'. So, the name points to a Kurdish political movement that later became an armed organization involved in conflict.
