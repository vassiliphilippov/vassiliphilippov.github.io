Etymology

reinvest dividend can be pictured like a gardener who uses the fruit of a tree to plant new seeds. The dividend is the 'fruit' — a small payment from your investment — and to reinvest it means you buy more shares with it instead of taking it as 'cash', so your investment can grow over time.
