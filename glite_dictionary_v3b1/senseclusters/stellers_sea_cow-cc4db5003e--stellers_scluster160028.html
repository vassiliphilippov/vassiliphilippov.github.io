Etymology

Steller's sea cow was named after the naturalist Georg Wilhelm Steller, who first described the animal in the 1700s. People called it a sea cow because it was a very large, slow mammal that ate seaweed like cows eat grass, so the name now means the large, now extinct marine mammal first described by Steller.
