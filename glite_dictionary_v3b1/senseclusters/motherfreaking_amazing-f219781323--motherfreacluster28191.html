Etymology

motherfreaking amazing comes from people using a very strong swear for emphasis and then making it less strong, creating a cleaner form of motherfucking amazing by using freaking instead. So people say motherfreaking amazing to mean 'extremely impressive' while avoiding a very offensive word.
