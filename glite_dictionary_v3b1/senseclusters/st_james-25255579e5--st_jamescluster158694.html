Etymology

St. James is the name of James, one of Jesus's twelve 'apostles' who was later called a 'saint'. Because people built a shrine for him at Santiago and many 'pilgrims' walked there, St. James now refers to that apostle and the saint remembered in the New Testament 'letter' and by those who make the pilgrimage.
