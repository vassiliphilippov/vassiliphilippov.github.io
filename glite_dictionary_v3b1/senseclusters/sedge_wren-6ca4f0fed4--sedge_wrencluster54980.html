Etymology

The name sedge wren comes from two words: sedge, a kind of tall, grass-like plant that grows in wet places, and wren, a small songbird. Because the bird lives and sings in the tall wet grasses called 'sedges', the name simply means a 'wren' that lives in 'sedges'.
