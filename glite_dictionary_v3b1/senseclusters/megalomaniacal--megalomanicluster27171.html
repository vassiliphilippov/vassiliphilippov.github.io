Etymology

megalomaniacal comes from Greek parts megalo- meaning 'great', like mega, and mania meaning 'madness' or 'a very strong obsession', like 'maniac'. So a megalomaniacal person is 'obsessed with being great' and often wants too much power or thinks they are more important than others.
