Etymology

carbon process comes from an old photo method where people coated paper with a thin, sticky layer mixed with dark pigment made from carbon, so the actual picture was the pigment stuck to the paper. That's why the term carbon process means this special 'printing method' known for rich, 'long-lasting' images.
