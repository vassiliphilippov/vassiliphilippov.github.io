Etymology

Alfred Lord Tennyson is the full name of the famous English poet Alfred Tennyson, who was later given the title 'Lord' and served as 'Poet Laureate' for Queen Victoria. He wrote well-known Victorian poems like 'In Memoriam' and 'The Charge of the Light Brigade', so the name now points to that important 19th-century poet.
