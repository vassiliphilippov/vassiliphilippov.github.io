Etymology

The word asphyxiation comes from Greek parts a- meaning 'not' and sphyxis meaning 'pulse' or 'throb'. So it originally described 'no pulse' when breathing stops, and that is why asphyxiation means not having enough oxygen to breathe or being suffocated.
