Etymology

The name Clement VII shows two things: Clement means 'merciful' in Latin and VII means he was the seventh pope with that name. He was pope from 1523 to 1534 and is remembered for refusing to cancel Henry VIII's marriage, a choice that helped lead England to break away from the Catholic Church.
