Etymology

Camorra probably comes from an old Italian and Spanish word camorra that meant 'brawl' or 'quarrel'. A helpful way to remember the word is to think of a 'brawl'—these groups began as fighting gangs, so the name came to mean the secret, violent criminal society it is today.
