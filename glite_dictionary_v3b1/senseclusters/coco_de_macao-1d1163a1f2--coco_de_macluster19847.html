Etymology

coco de macao may come from linking coco ('coconut') and macao ('monkey'), because the palm makes hard, coconut-like nuts that monkeys were often seen opening in the trees. That's why the name fits a palm whose hard nuts give oil and a 'vegetable ivory' used for carving.
