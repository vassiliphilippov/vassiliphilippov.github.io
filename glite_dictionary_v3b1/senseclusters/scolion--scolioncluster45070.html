Etymology

scolion comes from an old Greek word skolios meaning 'crooked' or 'bent'. A helpful way to remember it is to imagine guests at a banquet taking turns to sing lines so the song 'bends' from person to person, which is why a scolion is a short, often improvised song sung by guests at a party.
