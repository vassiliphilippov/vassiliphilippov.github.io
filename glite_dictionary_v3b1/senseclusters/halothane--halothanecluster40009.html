Etymology

halothane comes from the chemistry prefix halo-, used for elements like chlorine and fluorine, and the ending -thane, from ethane, a simple 'gas'. So halothane is an ethane molecule with those atoms added, which is why it is a nonflammable gas doctors use to put patients 'to sleep' for surgery.
