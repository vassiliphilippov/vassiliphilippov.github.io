Etymology

diacetylmorphine is made of parts: di- 'two', acetyl from 'acetic' which you can think of like 'vinegar', and morphine. The morphine part is named after Morpheus 'god of dreams' because morphine makes people sleepy and dreamy. So diacetylmorphine is a chemically changed form of morphine with two acetyl parts and is the drug known as heroin, a very strong and addictive substance.
