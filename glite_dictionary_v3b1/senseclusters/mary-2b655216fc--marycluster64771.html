Etymology

Mary comes from the old Hebrew name Miriam, and you can see it today in forms like Maria and Marie; it may mean 'beloved' or 'wished-for child', though some older ideas give it the sense 'sea of bitterness'. So Mary became the famous name of Jesus's mother and is used as a special, caring name.
