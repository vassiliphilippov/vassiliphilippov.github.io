Etymology

lash-up comes from the verb lash, which means 'to tie' or 'fasten', and up, which here means 'together' or 'finished'. Imagine quickly tying parts with rope or tape to make a temporary fix — that is why a lash-up is a quick, rough arrangement made from whatever you have.
