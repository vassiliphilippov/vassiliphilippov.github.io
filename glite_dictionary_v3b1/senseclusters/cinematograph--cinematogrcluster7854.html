Etymology

Cinematograph comes from parts that mean 'movement' and 'writing' — cinema ('to move') and graph ('to write' or 'record'), like the graph in photograph. So a cinematograph was a machine that recorded or showed 'moving pictures', which is why it is used for an early film projector.
