Etymology

guelder rose may come from the Dutch region Gelderland, so guelder is a place name, while rose was once used for showy garden flowers. The name creates the image of a pretty shrub with white flower clusters and bright red berries, so it helps explain why the plant is used as an 'ornamental' shrub today.
