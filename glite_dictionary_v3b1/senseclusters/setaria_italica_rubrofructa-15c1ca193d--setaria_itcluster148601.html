Etymology

Setaria italica rubrofructa comes from older words that describe the plant. Setaria comes from a word for 'bristle' because the plant has long bristly spikes, and rubrofructa means 'red fruit' for its orange to reddish grains, so the name gives the image of a bristly millet with red grains.
