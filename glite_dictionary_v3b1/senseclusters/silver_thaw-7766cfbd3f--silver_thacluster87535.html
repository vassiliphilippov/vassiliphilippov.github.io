Etymology

The weather term silver thaw comes from the sight of rain that freezes on trees and roads and makes them shine like silver. The word silver shows the shiny ice and thaw shows the weather change, so the phrase means 'freezing rain' that leaves a 'coating of ice' and makes travel dangerous.
