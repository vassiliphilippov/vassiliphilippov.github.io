Etymology

The word macadamize comes from macadam, the road method named after a Scottish engineer called McAdam who covered roads with layers of small broken stones. So to macadamize a road is to 'pave' it in that style by pressing in small stones and sometimes using tar, which later gave us the word 'tarmac'.
