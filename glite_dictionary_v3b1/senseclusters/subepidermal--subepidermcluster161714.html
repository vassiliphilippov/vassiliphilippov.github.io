Etymology

subepidermal breaks into sub- 'under', like in submarine, and epidermal, from epi- 'on' and derma 'skin', the same root as epidermis, meaning the outer skin. So, subepidermal simply means 'under the outer skin', which is why doctors use it for things just below the skin surface.
