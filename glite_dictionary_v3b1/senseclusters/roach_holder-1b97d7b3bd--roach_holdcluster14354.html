Etymology

Roach holder comes from a simple picture: the tiny leftover end of a smoked joint, called a roach, is hard to hold, so people used a small metal holder or tweezers to hold it without burning their fingers. So today roach holder means 'a small tool used to hold the end of a marijuana cigarette so you can smoke the last bit'.
