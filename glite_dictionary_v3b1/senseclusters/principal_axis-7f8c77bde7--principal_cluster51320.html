Etymology

The term principal axis comes from imagining a straight road through the center of a lens or mirror. Principal means 'main' and axis means a straight line, so light that travels on that road goes straight to the focus without bending. That's why 'principal axis' refers to the main line that shows how light passes through a lens or mirror.
