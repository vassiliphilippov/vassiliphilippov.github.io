Etymology

effeteness comes from Latin parts ex- ('out') and fetus ('to bring forth' or 'offspring'), so it originally meant something that had already produced and was used up. Imagine a field that has given its crops and is now empty and weak—this picture explains the idea. So, effeteness means being 'worn out' or no longer strong.
