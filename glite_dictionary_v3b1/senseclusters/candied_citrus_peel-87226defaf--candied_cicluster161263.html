Etymology

candied citrus peel comes from a simple kitchen practice: thin strips of orange or lemon peel are cooked in sugar until they become soft and sweet. So, candied citrus peel today means those sweet strips of peel kept in sugar and used to flavor or decorate cakes and other treats.
