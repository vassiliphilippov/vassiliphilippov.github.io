Etymology

Saurischia comes from the Greek parts sauros and ischion, which mean 'lizard' and 'hip', so it means 'lizard-hipped'. That's why Saurischia refers to dinosaurs with a lizard-like hip, including meat-eating theropods and the large, long-necked sauropods.
