Etymology

Mycobacterium africanum may come from the word parts myco (meaning 'fungus-like') and africanum ('from Africa'), so the name makes you picture a fungus-like bacterium found in Africa. That's why the name refers to a species of bacteria that can cause 'tuberculosis' in people.
