Etymology

irresponsiveness comes from the parts ir- 'not', responsible (from an old root meaning 'to answer' or 'to respond'), and -ness which makes a noun. A helpful way to remember it is that being 'responsible' meant being able to 'answer' for your actions, so irresponsiveness is the quality of not accepting responsibility or not 'answering' for what you do.
