Etymology

Dalbergia latifolia comes from two parts: Dalbergia, a name that honors a person called Dalberg, and latifolia, Latin for 'broad leaves'. So the name points to a tree with 'broad leaves' and helps explain why this large Indian tree is known for its dark, hard wood used in furniture and musical instruments.
