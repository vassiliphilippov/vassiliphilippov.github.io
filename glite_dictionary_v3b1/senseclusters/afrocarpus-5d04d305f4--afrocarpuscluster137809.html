Etymology

Afrocarpus joins Afro- meaning 'Africa' with -carpus from a Greek word meaning 'fruit', a common ending in plant names. So, Afrocarpus points to a fruiting plant from Africa and helps you remember it is a group of African evergreen trees often called yellowwood.
