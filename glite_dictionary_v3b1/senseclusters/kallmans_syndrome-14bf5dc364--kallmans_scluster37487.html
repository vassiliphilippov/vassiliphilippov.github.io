Etymology

Kallman's syndrome is named after Dr. Franz Kallmann, who first described people born with a very poor sense of smell and 'delayed puberty'. Because he linked those two signs, the name now means the condition of being born with a poor sense of smell and not developing normal sexual changes at puberty.
