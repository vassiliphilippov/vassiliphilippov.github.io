Etymology

The word apocarpous comes from the Greek parts apo- meaning 'away' and karpos meaning 'fruit'. So an apocarpous flower has its fruit parts each 'away' from the others — the carpels are separate instead of joined, which is why the word describes flowers like buttercups and roses.
