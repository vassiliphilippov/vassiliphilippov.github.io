Etymology

vetturino comes from the Italian vettura, which means 'carriage' or 'vehicle' and comes from an old root meaning 'to carry', the same root behind the English word vehicle. So a vetturino is literally someone who 'carries' people — a driver you hire to take you in a horse-drawn carriage in Italy.
