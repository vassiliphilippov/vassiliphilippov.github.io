Etymology

eau de cologne comes from the French words eau and cologne, meaning 'water' and the city of Cologne. It was the name for a light perfume first made there, and because eau showed the scent was weaker than strong perfumes, today eau de cologne means a lightly scented liquid you can splash on your skin.
