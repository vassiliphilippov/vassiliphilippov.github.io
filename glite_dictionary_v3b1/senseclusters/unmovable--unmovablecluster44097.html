Etymology

unmovable begins with un- meaning 'not' and uses move meaning 'to change place', so it literally says 'not able to be moved'. A helpful image is a heavy stone or a person who will not change their mind — both stay 'fixed in place', which is why unmovable means something that cannot be moved or changed.
