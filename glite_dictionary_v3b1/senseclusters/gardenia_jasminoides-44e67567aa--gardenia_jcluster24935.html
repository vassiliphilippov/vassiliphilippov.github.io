Etymology

Gardenia jasminoides was named after a real person, the naturalist Alexander Garden, so Gardenia honours his name, and jasminoides means 'like jasmine' because its white, sweet-smelling flowers and scent remind people of jasmine. That's why the name fits this evergreen shrub with shiny leaves and fragrant blossoms.
