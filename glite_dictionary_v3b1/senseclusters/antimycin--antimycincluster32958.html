Etymology

antimycin comes from anti- meaning 'against' and myc meaning 'fungus', with the common chemical ending -in; the myc part is the same idea you see in mycology, the study of fungi. So, antimycin literally means a substance 'against fungi', which explains why it stops fungal growth and is used in research.
