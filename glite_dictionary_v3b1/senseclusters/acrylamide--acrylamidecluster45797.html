Etymology

acrylamide joins acryl, from 'acrylic' as in acrylic paint or plastic, and amide, from 'ammonia' meaning 'related to ammonia'. So acrylamide is a specific chemical with an acrylic-like part and an 'amide' group, which helps you remember it is an industrial compound that can also form in foods cooked at high heat.
