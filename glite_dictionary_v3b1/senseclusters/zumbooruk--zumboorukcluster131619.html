Etymology

zumbooruk comes from Persian and Arabic roots: zambur meaning 'wasp' plus the small ending -ak, so it literally means 'little wasp'. A helpful way to remember the word is to picture a small cannon on a camel like a 'little wasp' — small, fast and able to sting the enemy, which explains its modern meaning.
