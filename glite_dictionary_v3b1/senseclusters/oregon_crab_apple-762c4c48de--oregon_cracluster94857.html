Etymology

Oregon crab apple gets its name from the small wild apples that grow in the Oregon area. Long ago people used the word 'crab' to mean 'small' or 'sour', and apple is the fruit, so a crab apple is a tart wild apple, which explains why the name refers to a hardy little tree with small, sour fruit.
