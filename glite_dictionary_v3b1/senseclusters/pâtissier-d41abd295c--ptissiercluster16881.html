Etymology

The word pâtissier comes from French pâtisserie, which comes from pâte meaning 'dough' or 'paste' and is like the English words 'paste' and 'pasta'. So a pâtissier is literally someone who works with dough to make cakes and sweet pastries, which explains the modern meaning.
