Etymology

sense of wormwood comes from the bitter plant wormwood, which has a very sharp, unpleasant taste and was used in old stories and the Bible as a sign of sorrow. So, saying someone feels a sense of wormwood paints a picture of deep 'bitterness' or 'regret'.
