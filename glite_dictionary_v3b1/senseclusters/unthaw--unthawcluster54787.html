Etymology

unthaw comes from the first part un- plus thaw, but unlike many un- words it does not mean the opposite; it is a nonstandard way to say 'to thaw' or 'defrost'. A helpful way to remember this is to think of unthaw as 'undoing the freeze', so when someone says unthaw they mean to warm something until it becomes soft or liquid again.
