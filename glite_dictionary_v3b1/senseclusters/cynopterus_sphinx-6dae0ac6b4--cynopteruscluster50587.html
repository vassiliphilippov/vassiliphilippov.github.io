Etymology

Cynopterus sphinx may come from old Greek words in Cynopterus meaning 'dog' and 'wing', and from sphinx because the bat's short nose and round face looked a little like a small dog or a mysterious statue. So the name points to this short-nosed, fruit-eating bat today.
