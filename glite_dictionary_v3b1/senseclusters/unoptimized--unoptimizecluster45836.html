Etymology

The word unoptimized starts with un- meaning 'not', and the rest comes from optimize, which is related to a root meaning 'best' and appears in the familiar word optimal. So unoptimized means 'not made to work as well as possible', which is why unoptimized programs often run slower or use more memory.
