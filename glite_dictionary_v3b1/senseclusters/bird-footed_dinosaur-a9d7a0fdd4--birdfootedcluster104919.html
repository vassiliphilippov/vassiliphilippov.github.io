Etymology

bird-footed dinosaur comes from a simple picture: a dinosaur with bird-like feet — strong, three-toed back legs and smaller front legs that helped it 'walk' and 'run'. So, the name points to dinosaurs that moved on powerful back legs and not on their short front legs.
