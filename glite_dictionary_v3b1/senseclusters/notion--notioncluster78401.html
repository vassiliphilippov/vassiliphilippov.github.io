Etymology

notion for small sewing items probably grew from the common idea meaning of the word, where a 'notion' is a small thing or idea. A helpful way to remember this use is to imagine all the little bits you keep for a project—buttons, pins and thread—are the tiny 'notions' you need, which is why these small items are called 'notions' today.
