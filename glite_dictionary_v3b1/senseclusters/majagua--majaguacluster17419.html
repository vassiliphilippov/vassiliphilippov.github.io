Etymology

majagua comes from a native Caribbean name that Spanish speakers used for a tough tropical tree. A helpful way to remember majagua is to think of it as the local name for a tree with 'strong wood' and 'tough fibers', which is why majagua means a tropical tree valued for its wood and fibers.
