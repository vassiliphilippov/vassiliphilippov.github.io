Etymology

Martin Luther was a German monk who famously nailed his list of complaints, the 'Ninety-five Theses', to a church door to protest the church selling forgiveness. This bold, public act started a big change in the church, so Martin Luther is remembered as the man who helped begin the Protestant churches.
