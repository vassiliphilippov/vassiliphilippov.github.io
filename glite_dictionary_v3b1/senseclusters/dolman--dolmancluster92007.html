Etymology

dolman comes from the Turkish word dolaman (from a verb like dolamak) meaning 'to wrap', and it originally named a wrapped garment worn over the shoulders; so the military dolman is a short, close jacket that sits like a wrapped piece of clothing and that is why the name fits its style.
