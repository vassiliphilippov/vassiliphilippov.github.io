Etymology

The term tenement house comes from the old word tenement, which meant 'a holding' or property someone kept and was used for buildings divided into many rented rooms. Imagine a big, old house full of small rented apartments where many families live close together; that's why tenement house now means a crowded, often run-down apartment building.
