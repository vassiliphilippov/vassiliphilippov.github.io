Etymology

family Lophosoriaceae comes from the fern genus Lophosoria, which combines lophos ('crest' or 'tuft') and sori ('spore clusters'). The ending -aceae marks a plant 'family', so the name paints a picture of small tree ferns with tufted groups of spores and thus refers to this small family of tree ferns.
