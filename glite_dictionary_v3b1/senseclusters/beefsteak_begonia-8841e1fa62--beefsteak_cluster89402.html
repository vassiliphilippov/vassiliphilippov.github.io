Etymology

beefsteak begonia may come from the way its large, thick leaves look. People thought the round, fleshy leaves with red undersides looked like a piece of beefsteak meat, so they used that picture when naming the begonia. That's why the name now means a begonia grown for its big, colorful leaves.
