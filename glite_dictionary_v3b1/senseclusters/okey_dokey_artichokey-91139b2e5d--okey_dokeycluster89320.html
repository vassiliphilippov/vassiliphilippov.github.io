Etymology

okey dokey, artichokey comes from a playful rhyme people made by adding artichokey (a silly form of the vegetable artichoke) to the simple okey dokey to sound funny. That's why the phrase feels light and cheerful and is used to say 'okay' or to 'accept' something in a joking, friendly way.
