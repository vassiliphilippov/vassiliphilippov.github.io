Etymology

The compound cocktail lounge comes from the words cocktail, meaning a mixed alcoholic 'drink', and lounge, meaning a 'room' to rest and relax. That image of a comfortable room where people sit and sip mixed drinks explains why it now means 'a bar in a hotel or restaurant where cocktails are served and people relax'.
