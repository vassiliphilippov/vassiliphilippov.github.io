Etymology

Metrazol shock therapy was a medical treatment that used the drug Metrazol to make patients have violent shaking and a short coma. Doctors called this physical shock because they hoped the strong body reaction would help the mind, so the name simply describes using Metrazol to cause a 'shock'.
