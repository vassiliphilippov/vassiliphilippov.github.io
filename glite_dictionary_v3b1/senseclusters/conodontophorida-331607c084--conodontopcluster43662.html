Etymology

The word Conodontophorida comes from parts that mean cono- 'cone', -odont- 'tooth', and phor- 'to carry'. So it is like 'cone-tooth carriers', which helps explain why this extinct group is known mainly from tiny cone-shaped, tooth-like fossils.
