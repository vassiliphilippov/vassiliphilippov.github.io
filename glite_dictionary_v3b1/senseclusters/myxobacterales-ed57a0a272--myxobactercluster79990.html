Etymology

Myxobacterales is built from myxo- meaning 'slime', bacter- meaning 'bacteria', and the ending -ales that marks an 'order' or group of living things. So the name literally says 'a group of slime bacteria', which fits because these bacteria move together and form complex fruiting bodies.
