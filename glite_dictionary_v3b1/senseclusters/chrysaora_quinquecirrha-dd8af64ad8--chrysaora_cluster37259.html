Etymology

Chrysaora quinquecirrha comes from old Greek and Latin words that describe the jellyfish: Chrysa- means 'golden', quinque- means 'five', and -cirrha suggests 'curl' or 'tentacle'. So the name creates the image of a golden jellyfish with five long curling tentacles, which helps you remember it is the Atlantic sea nettle.
