Etymology

U. S. Army Special Forces: The name comes from soldiers chosen for unusual missions — special means they do rare, hard jobs and forces means a group of soldiers. They are often called 'Green Berets' because they wear a green hat, and that image helps explain why the name means top soldiers trained for difficult missions.
