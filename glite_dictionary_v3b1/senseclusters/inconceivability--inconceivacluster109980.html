Etymology

inconceivability is built from in- ('not'), conceive ('form an idea' or 'imagine', as when you conceive a plan), and -ability ('the quality of being'). So inconceivability means the 'quality of not being able to imagine' something, which is why we use it for things that seem impossible to believe.
