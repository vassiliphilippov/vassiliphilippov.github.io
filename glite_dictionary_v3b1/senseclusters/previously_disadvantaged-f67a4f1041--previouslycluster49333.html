Etymology

previously disadvantaged creates a clear picture: imagine a race where some runners started late or had heavy bags, so they were behind the others. Those people were disadvantaged before, so they are called previously disadvantaged. So the term means people who 'in the past' had social or money disadvantages and may now get extra support.
