Etymology

The word ELA comes from the first letters of three Greek words, Epanastatikos, Laikos and Agonas, which mean 'revolutionary', 'people's' and 'struggle'. So the name ELA literally says 'people's revolutionary struggle', which explains why the group used it as their name when they fought the military government.
