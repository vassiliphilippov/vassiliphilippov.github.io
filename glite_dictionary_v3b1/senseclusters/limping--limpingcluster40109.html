limping

0.1/m4.4
A problem walking because of an injury to a leg or foot
noun US / ˈlɪmpɪŋ /

Etymology

Limping comes from the simple verb limp, which used to mean 'to walk with difficulty' or 'to drag one leg'. So, limping means walking in a painful or uneven way.
