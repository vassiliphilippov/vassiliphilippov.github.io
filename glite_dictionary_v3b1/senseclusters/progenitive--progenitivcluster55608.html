Etymology

progenitive (noun) comes from the same roots pro- 'forth' and gen- 'birth' and is like the common word progenitor, which means 'ancestor' or 'parent'. So a progenitive is an earlier family member who started a family line, which is why it means an ancestor in old or literary use.
