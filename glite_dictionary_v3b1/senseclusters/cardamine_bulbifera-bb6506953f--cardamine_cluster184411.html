Etymology

Cardamine bulbifera comes from Latin words that describe the plant. The first part, Cardamine, is an old word for a kind of 'cress' and the second part, bulbifera, means 'bearing bulbs' because this plant makes small bulb-like growths on its stems, so the name tells us it is a cress that produces little bulbs.
