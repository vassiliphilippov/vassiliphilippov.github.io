Etymology

The word wrongheadedly comes from the simple parts wrong ('not correct') and head ('mind' or 'direction'), plus -ly to make it an adverb. A helpful image is someone whose head points the 'wrong way' — so to act wrongheadedly is to act because of a wrong or stubborn idea.
