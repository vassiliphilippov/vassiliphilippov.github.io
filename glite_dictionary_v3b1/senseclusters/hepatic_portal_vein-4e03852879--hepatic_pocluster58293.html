Etymology

hepatic portal vein comes from words where hepatic means 'liver' and portal means 'gate'. It is like a blood 'gate' that carries nutrients from the stomach and intestines straight to the 'liver' to be processed, so the name describes the short vein that carries blood from the digestive organs to the liver.
