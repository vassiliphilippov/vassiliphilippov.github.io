Etymology

The word zombielike comes from zombie + -like, and zombie came into English from West African and Haitian words for a 'dead' or 'spirit' person who could be made to move again. A helpful way to remember it is that -like means 'similar to', so zombielike means 'similar to a zombie', that is, 'lifeless', 'automatic', or 'without energy'.
