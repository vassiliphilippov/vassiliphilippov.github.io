Etymology

The plant name bristly oxtongue comes from the leaves: they are long and flat like an ox's tongue, which is the oxtongue, and they are covered in stiff hairs, which is the bristly part. So the name helps you picture this tough 'weed' with tongue-shaped, 'spiny' leaves and yellow flowers.
