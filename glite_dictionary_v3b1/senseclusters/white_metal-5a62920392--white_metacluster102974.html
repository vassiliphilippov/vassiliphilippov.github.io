Etymology

The noun white metal likely comes from the metal's pale, shiny look, so people called the soft, silvery material whitemetal. This metal is a mix of metals used as a thin lining in 'bearings' and other parts that rub together, so the phrase now means 'a silvery mix of metals used to stop machine parts from wearing out'.
