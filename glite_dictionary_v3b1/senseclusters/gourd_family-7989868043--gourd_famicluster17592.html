Etymology

gourd family comes from gourd, the hard, squash-like fruit people once used as cups or bottles, and family, meaning a 'group' of related plants, so the phrase gives a clear picture of similar plants together. That's why gourd family today means the group of vine plants like pumpkins, cucumbers, and melons that are all kinds of gourds.
