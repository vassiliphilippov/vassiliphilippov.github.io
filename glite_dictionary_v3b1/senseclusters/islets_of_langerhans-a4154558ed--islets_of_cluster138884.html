Etymology

islets of Langerhans: The name islets of Langerhans compares small groups of cells to tiny islets, or little 'islands', and uses the scientist's name Langerhans because he first found them. These little 'islands' in the pancreas make 'insulin' and other 'hormones', so the name helps you remember they are separate cell groups that control blood sugar.
