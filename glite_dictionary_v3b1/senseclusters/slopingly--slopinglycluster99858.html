Etymology

The word slopingly comes from slope, which means 'a hill' or 'to lean', plus the ending -ly that makes it an adverb. Think of a roof or a row of books set on a small hill — if they are placed 'slopingly' they are at an angle or slanted, which is exactly what the word means today.
