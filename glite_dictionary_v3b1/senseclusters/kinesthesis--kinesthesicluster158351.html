Etymology

The word kinesthesis comes from Greek parts kine- meaning 'movement' and -esthesia meaning 'feeling' or 'sensation', so it literally means the 'feeling of movement'. Think of kinetic (movement) and anesthesia ('without feeling') to remember that kinesthesis is the body's sense of where and how its parts move.
