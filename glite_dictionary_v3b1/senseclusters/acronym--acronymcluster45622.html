Etymology

The word acronym comes from Greek parts acro- meaning 'tip' or 'top' and -onym meaning 'name', so it is a 'name made from the tips' of words. You can see -onym in common words like synonym and antonym. That's why an acronym is made from the first letters of a phrase and spoken as a single word.
