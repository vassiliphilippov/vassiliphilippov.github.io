Etymology

Chickasaw National Recreation Area is named for the Chickasaw people who once lived in the region, and 'national recreation area' is an official term for a public 'park' set aside for people to enjoy nature. So the name tells you it is a natural park linked to the Chickasaw people and made for outdoor fun.
