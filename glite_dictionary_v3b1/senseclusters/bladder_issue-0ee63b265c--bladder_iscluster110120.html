Etymology

bladder issue comes from the simple image of the bladder as a small balloon that holds urine and the word issue, meaning 'a problem' or 'trouble'. If the balloon leaks, hurts, or cannot hold much, you have a bladder issue, so the phrase means 'a problem with peeing or the bladder'.
