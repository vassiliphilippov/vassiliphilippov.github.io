Etymology

Diomedea nigripes comes from a classic name plus a simple Latin description: Diomedea is a name from old Greek stories, and nigripes literally means 'black foot'. That clear image of a bird with black feet helps you remember this species—the black-footed albatross—so the name tells you what the bird looks like.
