Etymology

Pallas Athena comes from a story where Athena had a friend named Pallas and, during a practice fight, she accidentally killed her and kept the name 'Pallas' to honour her. So the name Pallas Athena reminds us she is the goddess of 'wisdom' and the city's 'protector'.
