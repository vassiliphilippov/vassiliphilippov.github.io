Etymology

defilade comes from the French défilade and is linked to the English word file, meaning a single line, and to the idea of a 'narrow pass'. Imagine soldiers moving in a line behind a ridge so they are out of sight; that's why defilade means arranging cover or positions to protect troops from enemy fire.
