Etymology

The word unclip combines un- ('remove' or 'reverse') and clip ('to fasten' or 'to hold with a clip'), so it simply means to take a clip off. Think of un- like in unlock or untie, which do the opposite, and that helps you remember that unclip frees something by removing its clip.
