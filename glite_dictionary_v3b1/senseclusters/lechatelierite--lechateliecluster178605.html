Etymology

lechatelierite is named after the French chemist Le Chatelier, and the ending -ite is a common word ending that means 'a mineral'. So lechatelierite literally means 'Le Chatelier's mineral' and refers to the clear natural 'glass' made when sand is fused by lightning or a meteor strike, which explains its modern meaning.
