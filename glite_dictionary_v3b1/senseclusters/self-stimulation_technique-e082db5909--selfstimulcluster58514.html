Etymology

self-stimulation technique names a simple method you use on yourself to feel calmer. Think of self as 'you do it', stimulation as a small touch or movement like squeezing a stress ball or tapping, and technique as a method. That's why the phrase means a way to 'calm down' or 'relieve stress'.
