Etymology

exoergic reaction comes from Greek parts exo meaning 'out' and erg meaning 'work', so it paints the picture of energy moving out of a system. Imagine a kettle giving off heat and steam; the energy leaves the kettle, so an exoergic reaction is a reaction that releases energy.
