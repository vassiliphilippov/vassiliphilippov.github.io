Etymology

Platycladus orientalis comes from Latin words: Platycladus meaning 'flat branch' and orientalis meaning 'from the east'. That image helps you remember the plant: it has flat, plate-like leaves and comes from Asia, so the name points to this eastern evergreen people plant in gardens.
