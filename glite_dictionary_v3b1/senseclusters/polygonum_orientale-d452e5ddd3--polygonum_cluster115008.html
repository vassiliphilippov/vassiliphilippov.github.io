Etymology

Polygonum orientale comes from Latin words that mean 'many joints' and 'eastern'. The name creates a clear image: Polygonum points to the plant's many joints on its stems and orientale shows it came from eastern Asia, so the scientific name describes how the plant looks and where it came from.
