Etymology

copper oxide comes from a simple picture: when copper meets 'oxygen' it forms a new layer or substance that can be red, black, or green. So copper oxide literally means a substance made of copper and oxide, and oxide is a word for something joined with 'oxygen', which is why the name describes what it is today.
