Etymology

Plantago lanceolata comes from old Latin words that describe how the plant looks: Plantago comes from 'planta' meaning the sole of the foot because the flat leaves lie close to the ground, and lanceolata means 'spear-shaped' because the leaves are long and narrow. So the name helps you picture a low plant with long, spear-like leaves.
