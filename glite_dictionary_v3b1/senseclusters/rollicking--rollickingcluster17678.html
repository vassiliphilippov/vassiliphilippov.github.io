Etymology

rollicking comes from rollick, a playful old word that probably mixes roll ('rolling') and lark ('playful fun'). So, to be rollicking is to feel lively and joyful, as if you are 'rolling' with excitement and having a playful adventure, which is why it means 'lively and full of fun'.
