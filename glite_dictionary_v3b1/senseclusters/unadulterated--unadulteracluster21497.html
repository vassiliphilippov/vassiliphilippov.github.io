Etymology

Unadulterated comes from un- ('not') and adulterate ('to mix in something bad'), so it simply means 'not mixed or changed'. That's why we can use unadulterated to mean 'complete' or 'total'—for example, 'unadulterated joy' is joy with nothing holding it back.
