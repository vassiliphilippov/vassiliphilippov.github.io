Etymology

Monofocal lens implant comes from parts that mean 'one focus': mono means 'one', focal means 'focus', and implant means something put into the body. It is a lens put into the eye with a single focus, so it helps you see clearly at one distance, usually for far vision, and that is why people often need reading glasses for close work.
