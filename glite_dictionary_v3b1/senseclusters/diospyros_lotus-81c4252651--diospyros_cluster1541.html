Etymology

Diospyros lotus may get its name from old Greek words: Diospyros can mean 'divine fruit' or 'fruit of Zeus', and lotus links it to the sweet 'lotus' fruit from old stories. So, the name gives a clear image of a small, sweet, much-loved fruit, which fits this persimmon tree today.
