Etymology

conduction anesthesia gets its name from conduction, which means 'to carry', like electricity moving along a wire. When an anesthetic is placed near a nerve it travels along the nerve and 'blocks feeling' in the area. That's why conduction anesthesia means using an anesthetic near a nerve to make one part of the body numb.
