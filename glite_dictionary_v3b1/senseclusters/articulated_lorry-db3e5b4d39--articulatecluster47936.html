Etymology

articulated lorry comes from articulated meaning 'joined' and lorry meaning 'truck'. Imagine a vehicle where the front part and the 'trailer' bend at a joint like an elbow, and that picture explains why it means a large vehicle with a 'cab' and a 'trailer' used to carry goods.
