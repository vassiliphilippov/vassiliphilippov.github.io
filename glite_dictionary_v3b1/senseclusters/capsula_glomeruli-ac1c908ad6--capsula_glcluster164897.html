Etymology

capsula glomeruli is a Latin name for the thin two-layered cover that sits around a tiny ball of blood vessels in the kidney. Think of the capsula as a thin cup or bag wrapping the glomeruli, a little ball of vessels, so it 'surrounds' and 'collects' the fluid the kidney filters.
