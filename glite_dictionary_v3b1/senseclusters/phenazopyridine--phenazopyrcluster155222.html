Etymology

phenazopyridine is a long chemical name made of three parts: phen- (a simple carbon ring), azo (a kind of link often used in dyes that gives strong colour), and pyridine (a small ring with one 'nitrogen' atom). So the name simply describes the drug's parts and gives a helpful memory: it can turn urine bright orange.
