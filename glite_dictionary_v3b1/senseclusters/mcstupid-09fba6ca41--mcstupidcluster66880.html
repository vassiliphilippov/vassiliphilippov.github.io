Etymology

The word McStupid is a joking name made by putting the label Mc- before stupid to mean someone who acts very foolish. The Mc- part comes from the Gaelic Mac meaning 'son of', and because we also see Mc- in names like McDonald's it now works like a funny title, so McStupid feels like calling someone the big or 'official' kind of stupid.
