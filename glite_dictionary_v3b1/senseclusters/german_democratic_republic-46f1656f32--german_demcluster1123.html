Etymology

German Democratic Republic comes from the country set up in 1949 in the part of Germany controlled by the Soviet Union after World War II. It used the words German, Democratic, and Republic to sound like a normal 'democratic' country, but it was the official name for 'East Germany', so today the name refers to that former country that existed from 1949 to 1990.
