Etymology

Athiorhodaceae is easiest to remember by its parts: thio 'sulfur', rhod 'red' or 'rose', and the ending -aceae 'family'. So you can think of them as the 'red sulfur family', which fits because they are a group of small sulfur bacteria often found in water.
