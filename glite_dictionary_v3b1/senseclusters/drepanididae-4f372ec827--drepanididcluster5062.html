Etymology

Drepanididae comes from the Greek root drepan- meaning 'sickle' and the family ending -idae meaning 'family'. These Hawaiian honeycreepers often have thin, curved, sickle-like beaks, so the name helps you remember them as the 'sickle family' of birds.
