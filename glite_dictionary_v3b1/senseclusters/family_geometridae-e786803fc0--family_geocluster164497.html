Etymology

The name family Geometridae comes from old Greek words: geo meaning 'earth' and metron meaning 'measure'. Their caterpillars move in a looping, inching way so they look like they are measuring the ground step by step, which is why they are often called 'measuring worms' or 'inchworms' today.
