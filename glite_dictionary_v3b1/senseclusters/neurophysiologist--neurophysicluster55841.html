Etymology

The word neurophysiologist has three parts: neuro- meaning 'nerve', physio- meaning 'body' or 'natural', and -logist meaning 'one who studies'. So a neurophysiologist is someone who studies the body's nerves, which is why they look at how the nervous system works and measure its electrical activity.
