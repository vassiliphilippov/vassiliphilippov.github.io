Etymology

nitrochloromethane breaks into three clear parts: nitro meaning 'nitrogen', chloro meaning 'chlorine', and methane meaning a 'simple gas' like natural gas. So the name tells you it is a gas with nitrogen and chlorine parts, which is why nitrochloromethane is a strong irritant and has been used as a tear gas.
