Etymology

sinlessness comes from the simple word sin, meaning 'wrongdoing', plus the parts -less meaning 'without' and -ness which makes a name for a state or quality, as in careless or happiness. So, sinlessness literally means 'the state of being without sin', which is why it describes innocence or moral purity.
