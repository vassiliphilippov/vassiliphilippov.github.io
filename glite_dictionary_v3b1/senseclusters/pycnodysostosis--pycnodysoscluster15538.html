Etymology

pycnodysostosis breaks into parts: pycno- 'dense', dys- 'bad' or 'difficult', and ostosis 'bone', as in the common word osteoporosis. So the name means a 'bad dense bone' disorder, which fits a condition with unusually dense but brittle bones and short stature.
