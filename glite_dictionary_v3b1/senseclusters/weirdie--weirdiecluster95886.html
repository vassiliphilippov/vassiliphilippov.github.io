Etymology

weirdie comes from weird + the friendly ending -ie; weird once meant 'fate' but later came to mean 'strange' because of old stories about the supernatural, and the -ie makes it a small or personal form like 'doggie' or 'selfie'. So a weirdie is someone who behaves in an unpleasantly odd way.
