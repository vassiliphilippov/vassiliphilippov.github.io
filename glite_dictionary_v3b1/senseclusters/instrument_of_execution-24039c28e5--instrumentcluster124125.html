Etymology

instrument of execution comes from a simple, literal idea: instrument means a tool and execution means 'to put someone to death' by law. So the phrase names the tools used to carry out a death sentence, such as the guillotine or electric chair, and that is why it means 'a device used to carry out a death sentence'.
