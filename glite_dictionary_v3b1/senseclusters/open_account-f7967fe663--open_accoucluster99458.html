open account

0.2/m4.3
An agreement to get goods or services now and pay later

Etymology

The phrase open account comes from old shop record books. A shopkeeper would open an account by writing a customer's purchases and leave it 'open' until the customer could 'pay later'. So today open account means 'buy now, pay later'.
