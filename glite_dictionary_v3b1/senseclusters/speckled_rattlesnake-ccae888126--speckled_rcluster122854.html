Etymology

The name speckled rattlesnake paints a clear picture: speckled refers to the small spots that help the snake 'hide' among rocks, and rattlesnake is a snake with a noisy tail that acts as a 'warning'. So the name simply describes a spotted snake that blends into its home and warns with a rattle.
