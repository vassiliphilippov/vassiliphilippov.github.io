Etymology

Spanish Guinea comes from Spain's rule over a region of western Africa called 'Guinea'. Putting Spanish before Guinea showed it was the part of 'Guinea' controlled by Spain, which is why the territory had that name until it became Equatorial Guinea in 1968.
