Etymology

distomatosis comes from Greek parts di- meaning 'two', stoma meaning 'mouth', and -osis meaning 'disease'. It names the illness caused by liver flukes, parasites that have two mouth-like suckers, so the word points to the parasites' two 'mouths' and the disease they cause.
