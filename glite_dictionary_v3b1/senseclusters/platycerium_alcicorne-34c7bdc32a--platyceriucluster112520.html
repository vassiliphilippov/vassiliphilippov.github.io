Etymology

Platycerium alcicorne comes from old Greek and Latin words: platy means 'broad' and alcicorne means 'elk horn' or 'horn', because the fern's wide, forked leaves look like big antlers. That's why the name suits this staghorn fern often grown as a decorative plant.
