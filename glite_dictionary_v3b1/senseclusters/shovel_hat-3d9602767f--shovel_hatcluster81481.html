Etymology

shovel hat comes from the hat's front, which sticks out like the flat blade of a shovel, so you can easily picture its shape. It was often worn by priests, so the name came to mean 'a stiff, broad-brimmed hat with a projecting front, worn by clergymen or used for sun protection'.
