Etymology

Hamitic language comes from the Bible name 'Ham', who people long ago thought was the ancestor of many African peoples, so scholars used his name to label some north African languages. That's why the term came to mean a group of African languages once thought related to 'Semitic' languages, and why it is now little used by language experts.
