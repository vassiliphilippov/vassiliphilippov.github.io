Etymology

pepperwood comes from the simple parts pepper and wood because people noticed the tree's leaves and fruit had a 'pepper-like' or 'spicy' smell and its wood was very hard. So, the name helps you remember a tree with a spicy scent and strong wood often used for furniture.
