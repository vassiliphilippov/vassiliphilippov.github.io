Etymology

chromatography column comes from a simple image: early chemists saw colored bands form like stripes as a mixture moved down a tall tube, so chromatography (literally 'color writing') and column (a vertical tube) were put together. That's why chromatography column now means 'a vertical tube used to separate a mixture into parts'.
