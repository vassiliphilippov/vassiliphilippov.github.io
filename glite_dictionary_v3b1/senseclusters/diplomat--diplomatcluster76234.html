Etymology

The word diplomat comes from the Greek word diploma, which meant a 'folded paper' that showed a person's official power. So, because these people had to handle delicate and sometimes tense talks while protecting their country's interest, a diplomat now also means someone skilled at keeping good relations and negotiating calmly.
