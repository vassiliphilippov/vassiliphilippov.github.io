Etymology

fluoroboride comes from fluoro-, from the element fluorine as in fluoride, and -boride, from boron plus the -ide ending like in oxide or chloride. So a fluoroboride is a chemical compound where boron and fluorine are joined, which is exactly what the modern word means.
