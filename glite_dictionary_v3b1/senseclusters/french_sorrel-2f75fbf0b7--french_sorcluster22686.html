Etymology

French sorrel is named for its taste and its cooking history: sorrel comes from an old word for 'sour' because the leaves have a sharp, lemon-like flavor, and French points to the way this variety was used a lot in French cooking. So the name reminds you it is a tart, edible leaf used in salads and sauces.
