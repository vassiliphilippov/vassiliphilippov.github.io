Etymology

Bird pepper probably comes from birds eating the tiny, hot fruits and then spreading the seeds, or from the little red fruits looking like a 'bird's eye'. So the name connects bird, who eats and spreads the seeds, and pepper, the spicy fruit, which helps you remember this small, very hot chili.
