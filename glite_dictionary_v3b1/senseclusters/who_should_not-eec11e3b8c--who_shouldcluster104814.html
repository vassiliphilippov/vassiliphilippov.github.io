You say this after praising yourself to seem modest or unsure

Etymology

who should not comes from a short, almost joking question people add after praising themselves. Imagine someone saying 'I did well' and then adding who should not as if saying 'I shouldn't say this', so that small question softens the praise and shows 'modesty'.
