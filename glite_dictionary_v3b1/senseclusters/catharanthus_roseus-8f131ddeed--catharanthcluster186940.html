Etymology

Catharanthus roseus is a Latin plant name. The first part, Catharanthus, comes from old words meaning 'pure' and 'flower', and roseus means 'pink', so the name gives the image of a 'pure pink flower' and helps you remember this pink periwinkle that is grown in gardens and used in medicine.
