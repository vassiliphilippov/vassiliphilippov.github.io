Etymology

nonreducing is made of the common non- meaning 'not' and reduce meaning 'make smaller' or 'remove'. In chemistry, to 'reduce' is a special change where a molecule gains tiny particles called electrons, so a nonreducing substance does not do that change and is 'not able to take part in reduction reactions'.
