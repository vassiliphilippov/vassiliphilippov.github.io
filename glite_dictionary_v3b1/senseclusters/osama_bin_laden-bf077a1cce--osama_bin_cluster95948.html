Etymology

Osama bin Laden is an Arabic-style name: Osama is a personal name meaning 'lion', bin means 'son of', and Laden is his father's name. Because he founded and led al-Qaeda and was behind the large attacks on September 11, 2001, the name Osama bin Laden now strongly refers to that terrorist leader in news and history.
