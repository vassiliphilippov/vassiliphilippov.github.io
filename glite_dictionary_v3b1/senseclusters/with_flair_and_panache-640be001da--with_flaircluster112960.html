Etymology

The phrase with flair and panache joins two strong images: flair means a natural, quick talent, and panache was once a bright feather on a soldier's hat that showed bold style. So, saying someone did something with flair and panache paints a picture of confident, showy skill, which is why it means 'with stylish confidence'.
