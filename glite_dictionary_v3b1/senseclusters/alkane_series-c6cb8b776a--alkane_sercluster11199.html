Etymology

alkane series names a group of very simple chemicals called alkanes, made only of carbon and hydrogen with single bonds. They form a series because each molecule has one more carbon atom and two more hydrogen atoms than the last, like a row of houses where each house has one more room, so the name helps you remember they are similar molecules in a regular list.
