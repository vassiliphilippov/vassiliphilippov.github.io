Etymology

antiarrhythmic begins with anti- meaning 'against' and uses rhythm, meaning a 'regular beat' like in music or the heart. So an antiarrhythmic is a medicine that works 'against' an irregular heart 'beat', which is why it helps prevent or treat abnormal heart rhythms.
