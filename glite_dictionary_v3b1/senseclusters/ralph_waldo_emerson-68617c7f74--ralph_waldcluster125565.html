Etymology

Ralph Waldo Emerson was an American writer and speaker in the 1800s who loved nature and wrote short, powerful essays. One clear picture is him walking alone in the woods and urging people to trust themselves—this idea, called 'transcendentalism' or 'self-reliance', is why he is remembered as a leading thinker whose essays and talks still inspire people today.
