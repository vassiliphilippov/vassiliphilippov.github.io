Etymology

air pump is named for what it does: air is what moves and the pump is the tool that pushes it, so imagine pushing a handle and forcing air into a bicycle tyre or a balloon. Because it literally moves air in or out, the phrase means 'a device used to move air into or out of an object or space'.
