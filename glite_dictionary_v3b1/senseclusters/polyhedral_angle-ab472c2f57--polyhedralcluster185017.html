Etymology

polyhedral angle comes from the picture of a solid corner, like the corner of a dice or a pyramid, where several flat faces meet at a single point. The word polyhedral means 'many faces' and angle means the 'space' between those faces, so the phrase names the three-dimensional 'corner' formed by them.
