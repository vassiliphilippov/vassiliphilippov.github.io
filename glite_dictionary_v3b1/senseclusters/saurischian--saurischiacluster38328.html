Etymology

saurischian comes from Greek parts saur- meaning 'lizard' and -ischian meaning 'hip'. So a saurischian is 'lizard-hipped' and the name describes dinosaurs with crocodile-like hips. You can remember saur- because it appears in dinosaur and Tyrannosaurus.
