Etymology

montan wax may come from the word montan meaning 'of the mountain' or 'from mines' and wax meaning the substance. It was first made from lignite, a type of brown coal, and other minerals dug from the ground, so the name gives a clear image of a hard wax 'from the earth' and explains why we use it for polishes and industrial products.
