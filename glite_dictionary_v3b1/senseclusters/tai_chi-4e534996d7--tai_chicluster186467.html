Etymology

t'ai chi comes from an old Chinese idea of balance between two opposite forces, shown as yin and yang, and it grew from slow, controlled martial-arts moves sometimes called 'shadow boxing'. That's why t'ai chi today is a slow, calm exercise people use for balance, relaxation, and health.
