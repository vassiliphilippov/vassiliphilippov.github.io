Etymology

The old name extragalactic nebula comes from a simple picture: a nebula was a fuzzy cloud of stars and extragalactic meant outside our galaxy. So fuzzy patches seen beyond the Milky Way were called extragalactic nebula, which is why the phrase once meant what we now call a 'galaxy'.
