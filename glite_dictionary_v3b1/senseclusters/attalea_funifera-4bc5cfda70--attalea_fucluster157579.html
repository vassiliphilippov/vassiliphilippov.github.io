Etymology

Attalea funifera may come from old Latin words: the second part funifera joins funis ('rope') and fera ('to carry'), so it literally means 'rope-bearing'. Because the palm gives very strong fibers used to make ropes, mats, and brushes, the name gives a simple picture that links the tree to its use.
