Etymology

Carcinoma comes from Greek parts carcino- meaning 'crab' and -oma meaning 'tumor'. Ancient doctors thought some growths had veins that looked like crab legs, so they used the idea of a 'crab' for these illnesses and the same root also appears in the word cancer. So, carcinoma now means a cancerous tumor that starts in the cells that line the body.
