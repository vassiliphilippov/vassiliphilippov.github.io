Etymology

The compound name nasturtium family starts with nasturtium, a word from old words meaning 'nose' and 'twist' because the plant's sharp, peppery smell makes your nose want to twist. The word family means a 'group' of related plants, so nasturtium family tells you this group of bright, often edible garden plants is all from the same kind of plant.
