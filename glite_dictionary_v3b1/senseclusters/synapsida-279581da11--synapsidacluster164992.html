Etymology

Synapsida comes from Greek parts syn- meaning 'together' (the same root as synchronize) and -apsida meaning 'arch' or 'opening'. So the name points to their skull shape with a single opening, a feature that helps explain why these ancient reptiles are seen as the ancestors of mammals.
