Etymology

tetanus antitoxin is named from its parts: tetanus, the disease, and antitoxin, which means 'against the toxin', because early doctors used antibodies from animals to fight the tetanus poison. That's why the term now means a medicine of antibodies given for 'short-term protection' after a dangerous wound.
