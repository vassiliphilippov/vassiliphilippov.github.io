Etymology

incalculably begins with in- meaning 'not' and uses calculate, a word that comes from an old word for a 'small pebble' people used to count. So if something is incalculably large or important, it is 'not able to be counted' or measured, which is why the word means 'extremely' or 'immeasurably'.
