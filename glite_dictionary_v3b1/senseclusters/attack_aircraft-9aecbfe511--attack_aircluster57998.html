Etymology

attack aircraft combines attack and aircraft, creating a clear picture of a military plane that attacks from the air. Imagine a fast plane flying low to hit enemy ground targets or give close help to soldiers; that's why it means 'a plane designed to attack enemy targets, often at low altitude'.
