Etymology

granular pearlite comes from how the metal looks under a microscope: pearlite was named because it looks 'pearl-like' with light and dark stripes, and granular means the hard parts turn into small round grains instead of thin layers. So granular pearlite describes a tiny, 'grainy' structure in steel that forms after very slow cooling, and this appearance helps engineers judge how hard and strong the metal will be.
