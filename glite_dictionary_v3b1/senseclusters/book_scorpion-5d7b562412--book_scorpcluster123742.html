Etymology

book scorpion gets its name from a simple picture: the creature is very small, looks 'scorpion-like', and lives among old books and papers, so the parts book and scorpion explain its name. That's why today book scorpion means a tiny creature like a scorpion you may find in books that usually eats small pests and does not harm the books.
