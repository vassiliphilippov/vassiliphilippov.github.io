Etymology

deconsecration comes from the parts de- meaning 'remove' and consecrate meaning 'make sacred', which is the same root as the English word sacred; so a helpful way to remember it is as taking the 'sacred' label off. That's why deconsecration is the official act of removing a place's or object's sacred status.
