Etymology

Common meter comes from old hymns and ballads. People called it common because many songs used the same line rhythm, and meter means the pattern of beats, so with its repeated long-short-long-short lines the words could be sung to many different 'melodies', which is why it means the familiar line pattern used in hymns and ballads.
