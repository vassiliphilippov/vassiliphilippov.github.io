Etymology

carpe diem comes from the Roman poet Horace, who used carpe meaning 'pluck' or 'seize' and diem meaning 'day', giving the image of someone picking a ripe fruit to enjoy now. So carpe diem today tells people to 'make the most of the present' and take chances now.
