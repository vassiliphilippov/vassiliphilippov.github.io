Etymology

Anaphylactic antibody is the name for an antibody that can cause 'anaphylaxis', a sudden, severe allergic reaction. Imagine tiny defenders in the body that accidentally set off a loud alarm, causing swelling and trouble breathing, and that image explains why the name links the antibody to the serious reaction.
