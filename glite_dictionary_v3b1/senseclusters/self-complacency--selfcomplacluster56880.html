Etymology

The word self-complacency comes from self- and complacency, where complacency comes from com- ('with') and placere ('to please'). So self-complacency is being 'pleased with yourself' — like feeling so happy with your work that you stop looking for problems, which is why it means too much satisfaction and not seeing faults.
