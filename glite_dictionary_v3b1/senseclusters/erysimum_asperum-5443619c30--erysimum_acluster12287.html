Etymology

Erysimum asperum is a Latin plant name that helps you picture the plant: its common name is 'prairie rocket' because the tall orange-yellow flowers stand up like little rockets across the grass. The two parts tell you about the plant—Erysimum is the old name for these bright-flowered plants and asperum means 'rough', a simple hint at its slightly rough leaves.
