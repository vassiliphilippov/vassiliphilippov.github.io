Etymology

lyre-flower probably comes from the shape of its blooms: the curved, heart-shaped petals look a little like a lyre, 'a small harp', and flower means 'a plant bloom'. So, the name helps you picture a garden plant with drooping, deep-pink, heart-shaped flowers.
