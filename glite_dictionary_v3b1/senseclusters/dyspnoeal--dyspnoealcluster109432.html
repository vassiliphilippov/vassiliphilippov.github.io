Etymology

dyspnoeal comes from the Greek parts dys- meaning 'difficult' and pnoe meaning 'breath', so a helpful way to remember it is 'difficult breath'. So, it describes someone who has trouble breathing, just like during an asthma attack or after hard exercise.
