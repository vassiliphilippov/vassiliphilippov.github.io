Etymology

Agamogenesis breaks into three parts: a- meaning 'without', gamo- meaning 'marriage', and -genesis meaning 'birth' or 'origin', so it literally means 'birth without marriage'. So, agamogenesis is reproduction that happens without the joining of sex cells, which is why it refers to asexual reproduction.
