Etymology

The noun mat board comes from the two words mat and board. A mat is a flat border that sits around a picture and a board is a stiff piece of material, so a mat board is a firm board with a cut-out window that 'frames' and 'supports' artwork, which is why it means 'a stiff, flat board used to mount or frame artwork'.
