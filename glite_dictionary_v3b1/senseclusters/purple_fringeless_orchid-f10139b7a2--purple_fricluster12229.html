Etymology

purple fringeless orchid is a name that comes from how the flower looks: it has purple petals and, unlike similar plants called 'fringed orchids', no fringe on the petal edges, so it is fringeless. So the name points to a purple orchid without the usual fringe, helping people tell this species apart.
