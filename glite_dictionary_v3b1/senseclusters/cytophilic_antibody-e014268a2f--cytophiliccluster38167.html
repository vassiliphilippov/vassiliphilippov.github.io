Etymology

cytophilic antibody comes from Greek parts: cyto- meaning 'cell' and -philic meaning 'loving'. So the image is 'cell-loving'—an antibody that 'sticks to' cells because part of it acts like glue, and that is why the term means an antibody that binds to certain cell types.
