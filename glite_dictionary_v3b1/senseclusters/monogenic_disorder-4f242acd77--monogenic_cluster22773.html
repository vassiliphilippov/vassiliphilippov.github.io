Etymology

The term monogenic disorder comes from the parts mono- meaning 'one' and -genic meaning 'from a gene', with 'disorder' for a health problem. Think of one broken brick in a wall: that single faulty gene can cause the whole illness, so the phrase means a disease caused by a change in one gene.
