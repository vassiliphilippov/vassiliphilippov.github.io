Etymology

The word styrax comes from the old Greek word stýrax, which named a tree and its 'resin', a sticky, sweet-smelling substance, and the name may go back to a Middle Eastern language. That's why styrax today means a small tree known for its fragrant, bell-shaped flowers and its aromatic resin.
