Etymology

The name Elanoides forficatus may come from Greek and Latin words that describe the bird's shape: Elanoides means 'kite-like' and forficatus means 'scissor-shaped' or 'forked', which gives a clear picture of its long, deeply forked tail. That's why the name fits the graceful 'swallow-tailed kite' we know today.
