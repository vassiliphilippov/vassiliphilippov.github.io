Etymology

The Foreign Intelligence Surveillance Court is a special, mostly secret court that decides when the government can do surveillance, or 'watching', of foreign people or groups to gather intelligence, or 'important safety information'. That's why the court's name explains its job: it can approve secret searches or wiretaps to get that information.
