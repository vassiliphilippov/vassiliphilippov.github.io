Etymology

genus Octopus comes from old Greek words: the parts octo and pous mean 'eight' and 'foot', so the name gives the literal image of an 'eight-foot' animal. 'Genus' means a group of similar species, so genus Octopus is the main group name for the common eight-armed octopuses.
