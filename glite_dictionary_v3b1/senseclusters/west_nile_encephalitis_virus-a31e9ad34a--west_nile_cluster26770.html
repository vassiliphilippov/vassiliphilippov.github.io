Etymology

West Nile encephalitis virus gets its name from where it was first found and what it does: West Nile is a region in Uganda where doctors first found it, encephalitis means 'inflammation of the brain', and virus means a 'tiny germ' that causes disease. So the name tells us it is a germ from West Nile that can cause a brain infection.
