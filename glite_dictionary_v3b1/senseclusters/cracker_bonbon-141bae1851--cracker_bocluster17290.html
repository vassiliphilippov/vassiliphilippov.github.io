Etymology

The noun cracker bonbon may come from joining the English word cracker, a paper tube that pops when pulled, and the French word bonbon, which means 'candy'. When you pull a cracker bonbon, it pops open and a small gift or sweet falls out, so the name shows it is a fun 'party surprise' with something to enjoy.
