Etymology

Guillaume de Grimoard was a French church leader who became the 'pope' Urban V and tried to bring the pope's home back from Avignon to Rome; imagine him traveling between the two cities to move the seat of the church. He was later declared a 'saint' in 1870, so his name now reminds people of that effort to return the papacy and of his holy reputation.
