Etymology

Myrsinaceae comes from the plant name Myrsine, the old Greek word for 'myrtle', plus the ending -aceae which means 'family' in plant names. So Myrsinaceae is basically the 'myrtle family', a name for a group of related tropical trees and shrubs.
