Etymology

Liberation Tigers of Tamil Eelam name shows what the group was about: Liberation means 'freedom', Tigers is the strong animal image for fighters, and Tamil Eelam is the Tamil 'homeland' they wanted. That's why the name refers to the fighting group that tried to create a separate Tamil state.
