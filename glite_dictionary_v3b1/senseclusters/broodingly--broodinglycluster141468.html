Etymology

broodingly comes from the verb brood, which once described a bird 'sitting on eggs' to keep them warm; a helpful way to remember the thinking sense is to imagine a person 'sitting on' heavy thoughts like a hen on her eggs. So, to look broodingly means to show deep, serious, or gloomy thought.
