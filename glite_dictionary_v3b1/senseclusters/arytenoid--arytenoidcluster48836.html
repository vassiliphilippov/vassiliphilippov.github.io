Etymology

The word arytenoid comes from the Greek word arytaina meaning 'ladle' and the ending -oid meaning 'like'. So an arytenoid is a small, 'ladle-shaped' cartilage at the back of the throat where the vocal cords attach, which helps produce the voice.
