nib

0.00/m5.8
A hard outer tip of a bird's beak used for pecking or breaking food

Etymology

nib originally meant a bird's 'beak', from the old word neb, so the word still fits the hard, pointed tip of a bird's beak. That's why we call that small, horny tip a nib.
