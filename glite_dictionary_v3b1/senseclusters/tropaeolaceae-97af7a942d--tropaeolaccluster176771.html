Etymology

Tropaeolaceae comes from the plant genus Tropaeolum, which in turn comes from the Latin tropaeum meaning 'trophy'. People thought the round leaves and bright flowers looked like small shields or trophies, so Tropaeolaceae is the family name for those trophy-like garden plants, such as nasturtiums.
