Etymology

Penstemon newberryi takes its name from Penstemon, a word from Greek meaning 'five stamens' because each flower has five little parts that make 'pollen', and newberryi honors the explorer Newberry who collected the plant in the American West. So the name points to the flower's shape and the person who found it.
