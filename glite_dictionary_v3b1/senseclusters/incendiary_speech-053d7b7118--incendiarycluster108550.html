Etymology

The phrase incendiary speech comes from the image of words as fire: incendiary means 'that starts fires' and speech is what someone says. One popular picture is a speaker throwing a lit match into a crowd, so the 'words' can light anger or trouble, and that's why the phrase means 'to cause strong feelings or trouble'.
