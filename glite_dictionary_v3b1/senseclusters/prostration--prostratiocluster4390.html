Etymology

The word prostration comes from the Latin parts pro- meaning 'down' and sternere meaning 'to stretch', like the English word 'stretch'. So it literally means being 'stretched down' on the ground, which is why we use it for a sudden collapse or complete physical or mental tiredness.
