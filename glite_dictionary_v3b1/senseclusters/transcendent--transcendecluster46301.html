Etymology

transcendent comes from Latin parts trans- ('across' or 'beyond') and scendere ('to climb'), and the -scend part is the same as in ascend ('to climb up'); imagine 'climbing beyond' a border. So, a transcendent experience is something that goes above or beyond ordinary limits.
