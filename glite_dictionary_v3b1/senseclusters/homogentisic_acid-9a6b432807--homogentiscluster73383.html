Etymology

homogentisic acid gets its name from two parts: homo (showing one extra part) and gentisic (a related acid), so the word paints it as a close chemical 'cousin' of gentisic acid. It appears as a middle acid when the body breaks down the amino acids tyrosine and phenylalanine, so the name points to its role as a 'middle' or 'in-between' step in that process.
