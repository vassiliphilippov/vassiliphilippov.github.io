Etymology

muscle spasm comes from the clear image of a muscle suddenly tightening on its own: the spasm is a quick, painful twitch like a rope pulled tight. So, 'muscle spasm' means a sudden tightening of a muscle that happens 'without meaning to' and often causes pain.
