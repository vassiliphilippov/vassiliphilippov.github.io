Etymology

stupid-head is a simple compound of stupid ('silly' or 'not smart') and head ('person' or 'mind'), so it pictures someone's head as being silly; a helpful way to remember it is to think of calling someone's head foolish. It is a childish, playful insult used to mean a silly or foolish person.
