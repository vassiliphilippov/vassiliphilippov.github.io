Etymology

Silene comes from a name in Greek myth, Silenus, a wild follower of the god of wine. Think of these as bright, 'wild' garden flowers—often with sticky stems that 'catch' small insects—so the old name helps you remember the showy, sometimes sticky plants called Silene.
