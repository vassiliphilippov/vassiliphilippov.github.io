Etymology

Richard Lovelace was an English poet in the 1600s who supported the king and was once put in prison; while jailed he wrote the famous lines 'Stone walls do not a prison make' in his poem 'To Althea, from Prison'. That's why people remember him as a proud, loyal poet who wrote about freedom and love even in hard times.
