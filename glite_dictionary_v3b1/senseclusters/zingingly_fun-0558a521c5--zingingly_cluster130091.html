Etymology

The adjective zingingly fun may come from the short word zing, which copies a sharp, bright sound or a quick, exciting movement. So when something is zingingly fun, think of a little spark or quick zing that fills the moment with energy and surprise, which is why it means 'extremely lively and exciting'.
