Etymology

Splutter in disbelief comes from the image of someone so shocked they try to speak but make short, broken sounds and small spits instead of clear words. Those noisy attempts are splutter, and being in disbelief means you cannot believe something, so splutter in disbelief means 'to speak in a confused or shocked way'.
