Etymology

acoustic delay line comes from its parts: acoustic means 'sound' and a delay line is a 'line' that makes a signal take longer. In this device an electric signal is changed into sound that travels through a tube or block and so the signal is held back for a short time, which is why the name means 'delay using sound'.
