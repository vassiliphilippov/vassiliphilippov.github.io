Etymology

methicillin comes from the chemical part methyl and the well-known antibiotic penicillin. A helpful way to remember it is that methyl means 'a small chemical group', so methicillin is basically penicillin with a small chemical change made to fight bacteria that were not killed by the original penicillin.
