Etymology

Senna alata mixes the plant group name Senna with the Latin word alata, which means 'winged', a useful image because the leaves have wide, wing-like edges. People used the leaves' juice to treat skin problems like 'ringworm', so the name reminds us of both the winged leaves and its use as a skin medicine.
