Etymology

breechcloth joins breech ('lower body' or 'buttocks') and cloth ('piece of fabric'), so it literally means 'cloth for the lower body'. You can also see the same root in the old word breeches ('pants'), which helps you remember it is a garment for the hips and waist. That's why a breechcloth is a simple strip of fabric worn around the hips or waist as a traditional garment.
