Etymology

Bowdlerization comes from the name of Thomas Bowdler, who in the early 1800s made versions of plays with 'offensive' parts removed so they were 'safe for families'. So bowdlerization now means cutting or changing parts of a text to make it less offensive, often making the work feel less true to the original.
