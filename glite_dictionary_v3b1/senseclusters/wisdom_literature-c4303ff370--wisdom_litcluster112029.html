Etymology

wisdom literature comes from a simple picture: ancient teachers put together short sayings, poems, and stories that shared wisdom, or 'advice on how to live', in books — the literature. So today wisdom literature means those old biblical books, like Proverbs and Ecclesiastes, that offer moral advice and life lessons.
