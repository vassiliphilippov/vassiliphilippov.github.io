Etymology

cockhole is a rude insult made from cock ('rooster', and in crude speech a 'man's private part') and hole ('opening'). A helpful way to remember it is to see it as a strong, harsh label for someone selfish or unpleasant, which is why cockhole is used as a vulgar insult.
