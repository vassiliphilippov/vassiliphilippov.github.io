Etymology

polecat may come from old names that mixed the idea of poultry, meaning 'chickens', and cat, so people thought of it as a 'chicken-cat' that raided coops. That's why the name fits a small animal like a weasel that farmers blamed for stealing chickens and for the strong, unpleasant smell it can spray when threatened.
