Etymology

The name the Venerable Bede comes from the title venerable, which means 'respected', and from Bede, the monk who wrote early English history. Picture an old monk in a quiet room writing books and people calling him 'venerable' because they trusted his knowledge, so the name shows he was a respected teacher and historian.
