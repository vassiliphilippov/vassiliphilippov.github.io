Etymology

The word metagenesis comes from Greek parts meta- meaning 'change' or 'after' and genesis meaning 'birth' or 'origin'. So think of it as a 'change of birth' — that is why it describes a life cycle where a plant or animal switches between different generations, like jellyfish moving from a polyp to a medusa form.
