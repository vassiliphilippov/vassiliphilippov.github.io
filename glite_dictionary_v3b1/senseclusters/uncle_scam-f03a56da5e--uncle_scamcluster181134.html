Etymology

Uncle Scam may come from a simple joke on the name Uncle Sam, the well-known figure for the country, where scam ('a trick') replaces Sam. This makes the image of someone who seems like a friendly uncle but is actually dishonest, so the name means 'a person who deceives others'.
