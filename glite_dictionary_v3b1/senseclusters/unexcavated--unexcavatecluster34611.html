Etymology

The word unexcavated begins with un- meaning 'not' and comes from ex- 'out' plus cavare 'to hollow', the same root you see in cave and cavity. So unexcavated means 'not dug out' or 'not explored', like an area of ground or a tomb that has not been opened yet.
