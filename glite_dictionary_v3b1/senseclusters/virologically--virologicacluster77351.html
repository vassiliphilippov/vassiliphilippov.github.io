Etymology

The word virologically comes from virology, which is virus plus the ending -logy meaning 'study', and you can see the same -logy in biology. A helpful way to remember it is to imagine scientists watching tiny troublemakers in a lab, because virologically means 'in a way that is about the study of viruses'.
