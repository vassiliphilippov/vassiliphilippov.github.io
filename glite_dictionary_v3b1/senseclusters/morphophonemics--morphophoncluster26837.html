Etymology

The word morphophonemics comes from the parts morpho- meaning 'form' and phon- meaning 'sound', as in metamorphosis, which means 'change of form', and phonics, which is about 'sound'. So, it reminds you that this field studies how small parts of words change the way they are pronounced in different places.
