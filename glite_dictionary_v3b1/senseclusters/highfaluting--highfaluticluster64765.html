Etymology

highfaluting joins high, meaning 'lofty' or 'showing importance', with a sound like flaunt, which means 'to show off', so a helpful way to remember it is to imagine someone acting very 'high' and showing off. That's why highfaluting means being pompous or pretending to be more important than you are.
