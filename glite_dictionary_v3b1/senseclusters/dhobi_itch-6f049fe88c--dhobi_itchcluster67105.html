Etymology

dhobi itch comes from dhobi, a word for a washer who worked with wet clothes, and itch for the skin problem. Wet clothes and damp skin often caused fungal rashes in the groin, so dhobi itch now means 'a fungal infection that affects the groin and inner thighs'.
