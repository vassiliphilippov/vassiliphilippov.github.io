Etymology

polyneuritis comes from three parts: poly- meaning 'many', neur- meaning 'nerve', and -itis meaning 'inflammation', as in arthritis. So, polyneuritis is an 'inflammation' of many 'nerves', which is why it causes pain, weakness, or numbness in different parts of the body.
