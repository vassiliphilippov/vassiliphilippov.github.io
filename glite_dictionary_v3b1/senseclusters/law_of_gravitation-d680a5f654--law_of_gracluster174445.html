Etymology

law of gravitation comes from Isaac Newton's famous story about an apple falling. He saw the apple fall and realised the same invisible gravitation 'pull' that makes apples fall to Earth also keeps the moon moving, so he described a rule saying every object 'pulls' on every other object. That's why the phrase means that any two objects 'attract' each other.
