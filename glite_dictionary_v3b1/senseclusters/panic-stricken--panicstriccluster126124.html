Etymology

panic-stricken comes from panic, named after the Greek god Pan, who was said to cause sudden fear with a loud shout, and stricken, from 'to strike' meaning 'hit'. So panic-stricken means being 'struck' by 'sudden fear', which is why it describes people who act in a wild or desperate way.
