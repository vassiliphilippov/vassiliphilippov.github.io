Etymology

The idiom off one's nut comes from nut meaning a person's 'head' and from the simple picture of a small metal nut that holds parts together; when the nut is off, the parts are loose and do not work right. That's why someone 'off their nut' is said to be acting 'crazy' or not thinking clearly.
