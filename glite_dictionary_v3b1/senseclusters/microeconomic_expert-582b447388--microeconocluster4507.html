Etymology

microeconomic expert gives a clear image of someone using a magnifying glass to study tiny parts of the economy, because micro means 'small', economic means 'about money and markets', and expert is a specialist. So a microeconomic expert is a person who studies 'small' areas like individual shops, companies and consumers instead of whole countries.
