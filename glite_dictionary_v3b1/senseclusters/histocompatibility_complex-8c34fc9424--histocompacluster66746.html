Etymology

histocompatibility complex comes from parts meaning histo- 'tissue', compatibility 'a good match', and complex 'a group'. So, it paints a simple picture: a group of genes that help tissues 'match' each other, which is why the term refers to the genes that decide if a donated organ will be accepted or rejected.
