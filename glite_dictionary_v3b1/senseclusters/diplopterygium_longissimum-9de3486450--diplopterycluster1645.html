Etymology

Diplopterygium longissimum comes from old word parts: diplo- meaning 'double', -pterygium meaning 'wing', and longissimum meaning 'very long'. The name paints a picture of a fern with long, wing-like leaves often in pairs that spread into large patches, which fits this species that makes very long fronds and covers wide areas.
