Etymology

switchback comes from the two parts switch and back, meaning 'change direction' and 'back', because roads and tracks literally 'switch back' on themselves as they climb a steep hill. That's why a switchback is a road, path, or railway that makes sharp zigzag turns to go up or down a slope.
