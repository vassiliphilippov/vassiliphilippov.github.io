Etymology

salacity comes from the Latin salax meaning 'lustful', which comes from salire 'to leap' — imagine someone 'leaping' after desire, and you can see the same idea in the word salacious. So salacity means a strong interest in sexual things or lewd behaviour, which is why it describes obscene actions.
