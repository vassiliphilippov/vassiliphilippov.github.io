Etymology

indolent comes from the Latin parts in- 'not' and dolere 'to feel pain'. A helpful way to remember the 'lazy' meaning is to think of someone who is 'not moved by pain' and so avoids effort, which is why indolent describes someone who is usually lazy and avoids work.
