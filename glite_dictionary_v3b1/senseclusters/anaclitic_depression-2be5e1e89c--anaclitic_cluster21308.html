Etymology

The term anaclitic depression comes from a Greek word for anaclitic meaning 'to lean on' and depression meaning 'deep sadness'. Imagine a baby who must lean on its mother for care, and when that support is gone the baby becomes very sad and may stop developing, so the phrase now means severe depression in infants who lose their main caregiver.
