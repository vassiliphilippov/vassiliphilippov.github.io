Etymology

Chateau Migraine is a joking phrase that mixes Chateau (a French word for a wine house) and migraine (a very bad headache). It paints the picture of a fancy wine 'chateau' that gives you a terrible 'hangover', so the phrase means a very bad 'headache' or 'hangover' after drinking.
