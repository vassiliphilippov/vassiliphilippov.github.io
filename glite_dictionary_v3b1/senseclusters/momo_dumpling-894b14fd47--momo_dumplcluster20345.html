Etymology

momo dumpling likely comes from the Tibetan word momo, which meant a small 'steamed' dough cake; cooks in Tibet and Nepal filled these little cakes with meat or vegetables, and the name stuck to the food. So today momo dumpling means the stuffed dumpling many people eat as a snack or at festivals.
