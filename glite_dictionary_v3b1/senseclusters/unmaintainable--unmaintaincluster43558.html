Etymology

unmaintainable is made from un- 'not', maintain 'keep' and -able 'able to', and you can think of maintain like manual which links to 'hand' and 'hold'. So it can mean 'not able to be held up', which is why we use it for ideas or legal positions that cannot be defended or kept as true.
