Etymology

The word sensitize comes from the old root sens-, from sentire, meaning 'to feel', and the ending -ize meaning 'make'. So, to sensitize a person or group is to make them more able to 'feel' or notice an issue, or to make the body react to a substance, which is why it means 'make sensitive' or 'raise awareness'.
