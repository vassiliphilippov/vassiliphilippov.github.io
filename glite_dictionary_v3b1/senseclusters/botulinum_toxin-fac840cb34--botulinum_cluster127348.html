Etymology

botulinum toxin: The name botulinum comes from an old Latin word for 'sausage' because the bacteria were first linked to food poisoning from spoiled sausages. Because this toxin can stop muscles from working, doctors now use tiny amounts of botulinum toxin in medicine to relax muscles for treatment and cosmetic use.
