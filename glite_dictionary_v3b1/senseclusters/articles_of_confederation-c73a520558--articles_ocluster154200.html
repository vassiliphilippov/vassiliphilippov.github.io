Etymology

Articles of Confederation comes from the words articles, meaning the written rules each state agreed to, and confederation, meaning a loose joining of independent 'states'. Imagine each state signing its own paper and being tied together by a loose rope—connected but keeping most power—so the document served as the first US 'constitution' but created a 'weak central government'.
