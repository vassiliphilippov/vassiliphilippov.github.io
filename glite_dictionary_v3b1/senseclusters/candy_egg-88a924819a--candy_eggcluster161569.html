Etymology

The noun candy egg comes from the simple image of a sweet (candy) made in the shape of an egg. People give these chocolate 'eggs' as a 'treat' at 'Easter' and hide them in egg hunts, so the phrase now means a small sweet shaped like an egg often given at 'Easter'.
