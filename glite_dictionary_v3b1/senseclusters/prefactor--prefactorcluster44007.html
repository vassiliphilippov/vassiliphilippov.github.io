Etymology

The word prefactor comes from two parts: pre- meaning 'before' and factor meaning 'a number that multiplies' or 'something that changes a result'. So a prefactor is the number or expression that sits in front of a formula and multiplies it, which is why changing the prefactor changes the whole result.
