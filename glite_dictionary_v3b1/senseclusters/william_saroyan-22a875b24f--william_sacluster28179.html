Etymology

The writer William Saroyan grew up in an Armenian immigrant family and became famous for warm, simple plays and short stories about everyday people. Imagine him sitting in a café, watching a busy street and turning small moments into touching stories, and that's why his name is linked to stories of immigrant life and ordinary people.
