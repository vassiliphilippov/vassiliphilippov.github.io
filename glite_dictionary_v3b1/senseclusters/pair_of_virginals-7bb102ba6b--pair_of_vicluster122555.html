Etymology

pair of virginals may come from a simple image: virginals were small, box-like keyboards often played by 'young women', and people in older speech sometimes used pair to name a single boxed object. That's why pair of virginals now refers to that legless, rectangular keyboard from the 16th and 17th centuries.
