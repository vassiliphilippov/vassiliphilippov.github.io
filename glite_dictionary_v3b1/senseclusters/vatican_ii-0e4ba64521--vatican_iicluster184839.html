Etymology

Vatican II, made of Vatican and II, comes from the Second Vatican Council, a large meeting of Catholic bishops from 1962 to 1965 that made important changes like using local languages in Mass and encouraging churches to work together. So today Vatican II usually means that meeting and the 'spirit of reform' it started.
