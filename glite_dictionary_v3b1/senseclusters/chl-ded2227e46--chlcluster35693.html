Etymology

Chl is a common abbreviation for chlorophyll, the green pigment in plants that helps them make food. The full word comes from Greek parts chlor- 'green' and -phyll 'leaf', so chlorophyll literally means 'green leaf', which is why Chl points to the green material that captures light for the plant.
