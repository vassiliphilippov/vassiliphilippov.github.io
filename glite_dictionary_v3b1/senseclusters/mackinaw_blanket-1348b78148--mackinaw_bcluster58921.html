Etymology

Mackinaw blanket is named after the Mackinaw area by the Great Lakes, where trappers and soldiers used a thick wool cover with a red-and-black plaid. One popular story says people began to call that heavy blanket a 'Mackinaw' because it kept them 'very warm', so today Mackinaw blanket means a thick, warm wool blanket.
