Etymology

technical grade comes from old factory labels. The word technical meant 'for machines or industry' and grade meant 'level of quality', so a 'technical grade' product was fine for factory use but 'not pure enough for food or medicine', which is why today it means a chemical good for industrial work but with small impurities.
