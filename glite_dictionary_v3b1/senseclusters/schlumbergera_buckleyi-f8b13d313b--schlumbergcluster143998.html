Etymology

Schlumbergera buckleyi may come from two people's names: Schlumbergera after a plant collector called Schlumberger and buckleyi after someone called Buckley. The long Latin name works like a label that 'honors' those people and helps gardeners tell this winter‑flowering house cactus from other plants.
