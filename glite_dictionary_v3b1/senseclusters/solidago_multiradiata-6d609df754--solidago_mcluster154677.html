Etymology

Solidago multiradiata may come from Latin words that describe the plant: Solidago means 'to make whole' because goldenrod was used as a simple medicine, and multiradiata means 'many-rayed', like a small golden sun made of many thin flower rays. So the name helps you picture a healing goldenrod with lots of radiating flowers on mountain slopes.
