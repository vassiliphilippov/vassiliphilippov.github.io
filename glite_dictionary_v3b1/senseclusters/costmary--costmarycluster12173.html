Etymology

costmary is thought to mean something like 'Mary's herb', joining cost (an old word for a 'strong-smelling root') and Mary, because people long ago used it to scent cloth and to flavor food. So, the name helps you remember that costmary is a fragrant herb used in cooking, tea, or potpourri.
