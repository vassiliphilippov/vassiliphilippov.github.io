Etymology

orthogonality comes from Greek parts ortho- meaning 'straight' or 'right' and -gon meaning 'angle', like the common word angle. So orthogonality means being at a 'right' angle, or 'perpendicular', and it also describes things that are 'independent' or do not affect each other.
