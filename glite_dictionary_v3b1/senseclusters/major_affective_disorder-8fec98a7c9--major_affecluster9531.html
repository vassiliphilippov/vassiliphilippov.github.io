Etymology

The term major affective disorder comes from the words major, meaning 'serious', affective, meaning 'feeling' or 'mood', and disorder, meaning a health problem. Doctors used these words to show it is a serious problem of 'feeling' or 'mood' rather than a physical illness, so today it means a severe emotional or mood disorder.
