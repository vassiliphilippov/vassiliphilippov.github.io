Etymology

DIP switch gets its name from a small block of switches made with two rows of metal pins; DIP stands for 'dual in-line', meaning the two lines of pins, and switch is the tiny on/off control, so the name points to its shape and use. That's why a DIP switch means a tiny switch you set by hand on electronic boards to choose options.
