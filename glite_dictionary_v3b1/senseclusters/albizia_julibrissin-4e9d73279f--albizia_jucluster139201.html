Etymology

Albizia julibrissin gets its common names 'silk tree' and 'mimosa' because its pink flowers have long, silky flower threads that look like bits of silk. Its thin, feathery leaves also fold up like a sleeping plant, so the name helps you remember the pretty tree with soft, silk-like blooms.
