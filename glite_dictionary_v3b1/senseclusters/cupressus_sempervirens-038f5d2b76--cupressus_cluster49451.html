Etymology

Cupressus sempervirens is a Latin name made of two parts: Cupressus meaning 'cypress' and sempervirens meaning 'always green'. So the name paints the picture of a tall, narrow cypress that keeps its leaves all year and explains why people plant it as a neat border.
