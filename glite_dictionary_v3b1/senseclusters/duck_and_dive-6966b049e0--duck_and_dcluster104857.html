Etymology

The phrase duck and dive comes from two quick actions: duck (lower your head) and dive (move down or under). Picture someone bending and slipping through gaps to avoid a crowd or danger, and that's why it means 'to move quickly to avoid something' or 'to evade a difficult situation'.
