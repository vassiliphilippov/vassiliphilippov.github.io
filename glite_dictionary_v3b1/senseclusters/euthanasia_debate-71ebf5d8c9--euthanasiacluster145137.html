Etymology

euthanasia debate names the public discussion about assisted dying. The word euthanasia comes from words meaning 'good death' and debate means a public discussion. So the phrase brings to mind people arguing about when a 'good death' should be allowed, which is why it means a public argument about what is right and what the law should say.
