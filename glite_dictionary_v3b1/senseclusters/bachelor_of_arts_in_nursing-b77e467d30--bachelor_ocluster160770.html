Etymology

Bachelor of Arts in Nursing comes from older uses of the words: Bachelor meant a young learner who had finished basic training, Arts meant school subjects, and Nursing meant caring for sick people. So the name shows someone has completed a 'first university degree' in both the study and the practical skills needed 'to be a nurse'.
