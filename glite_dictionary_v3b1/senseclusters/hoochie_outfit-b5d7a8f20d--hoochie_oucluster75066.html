Etymology

The phrase hoochie outfit comes from the old phrase hoochie coochie, the name for a sexy dance at fairs where dancers showed a lot of skin. So hoochie outfit paints a picture of a hoochie, a sexy performer, wearing an outfit made to get attention, which is why it means 'clothing that is revealing or meant to look sexy'.
