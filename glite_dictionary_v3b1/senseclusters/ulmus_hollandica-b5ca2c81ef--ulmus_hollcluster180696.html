Etymology

Ulmus hollandica comes from Latin: Ulmus is the Latin word for 'elm' and hollandica means 'from Holland' or 'Dutch'. The name gives a clear picture of a type of elm with a Dutch link, so it is easy to remember this is a European hybrid shade tree.
