Etymology

flagellated protozoan names a tiny, single-celled creature. Here flagellated means it has one or more long, whip-like tails called 'flagella' that it waves to swim, and protozoan means a single-celled, animal-like organism. So the phrase points to a tiny swimmer that can sometimes 'cause disease'.
