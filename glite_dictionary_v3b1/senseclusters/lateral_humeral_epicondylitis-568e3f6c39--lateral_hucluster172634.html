Etymology

lateral humeral epicondylitis comes from older medical words: lateral means 'outer', humeral refers to the 'upper arm bone', and epicondylitis means 'pain and swelling at a bony bump by the elbow'. So the name shows pain on the outer side of the elbow after repeated arm use, which is why it is the formal name for 'tennis elbow'.
