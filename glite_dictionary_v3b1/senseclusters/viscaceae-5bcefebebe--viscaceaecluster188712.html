Etymology

Viscaceae comes from the Latin viscum, meaning 'mistletoe' or the sticky substance from its berries, and the ending -aceae, a common part used to name plant families like Rosaceae, the 'rose' family. So Viscaceae literally means the 'mistletoe family', which fits because these plants are mistletoes that often live on other trees and have sticky berries.
