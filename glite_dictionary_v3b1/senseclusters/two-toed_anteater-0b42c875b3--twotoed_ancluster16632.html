Etymology

two-toed anteater gets its name from a clear image: an anteater with only two-toed front feet, where you can see two big 'toes' or claws. That's why the name tells you what kind of anteater it is—a small animal from South America that uses those claws and a long tongue to eat 'ants' and 'termites'.
