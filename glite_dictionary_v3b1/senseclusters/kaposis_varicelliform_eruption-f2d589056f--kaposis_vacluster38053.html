Etymology

Kaposi's varicelliform eruption gets its name from Dr. Moritz Kaposi and the word varicelliform, which literally means 'like chickenpox'. So, the name creates the image of a sudden skin eruption with many blisters, and that's why it refers to 'a rare, serious skin infection' often seen in people with eczema.
