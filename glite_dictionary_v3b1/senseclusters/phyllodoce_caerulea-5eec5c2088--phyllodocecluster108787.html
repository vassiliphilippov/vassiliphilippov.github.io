Etymology

Phyllodoce caerulea is a scientific name made from an old Greek name and a Latin color word: Phyllodoce was a sea-nymph in Greek stories and caerulea means 'blue' in Latin. So the name gives a simple picture of a delicate, blue-flowered plant, which helps you remember this small evergreen shrub with tiny leaves that grows in high mountain areas.
