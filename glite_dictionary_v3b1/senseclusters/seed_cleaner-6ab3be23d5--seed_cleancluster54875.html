Etymology

The name seed cleaner comes from farming and shows what it does. Farmers first used sieves and wind to separate good seed from dirt, straw and broken pieces, so a machine that did this was called a cleaner of seeds. That's why seed cleaner today means a machine that 'removes impurities' from seeds.
