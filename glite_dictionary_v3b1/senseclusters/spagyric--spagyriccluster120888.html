Etymology

spagyric comes from two Greek parts, spao ('to draw out') and ageiro ('to gather'). The word paints a simple picture: alchemists would 'draw out' the useful parts of a plant, clean or change them, and then 'gather' them back together, so spagyric describes methods related to making medicines by alchemy.
