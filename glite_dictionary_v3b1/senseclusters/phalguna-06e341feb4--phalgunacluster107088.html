Etymology

Phalguna comes from Sanskrit, where the older form Phālguna is tied to the bright full-moon time and to spring flowers, so it suggests being 'full' or 'flowering'. So, Phalguna is the late winter/early spring month in the Hindu calendar, a time for festivals like Holi.
