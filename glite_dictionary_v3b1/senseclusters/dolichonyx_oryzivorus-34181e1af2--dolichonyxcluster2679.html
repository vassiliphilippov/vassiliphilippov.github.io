Etymology

Dolichonyx oryzivorus comes from old Greek and Latin words: Dolichonyx means 'long' and 'claw' and oryzivorus means 'rice-eater'. So the name gives a simple picture of a long-clawed bird that eats in rice fields during migration, which helps you remember this migratory songbird.
