Etymology

Negara Brunei Darussalam is the official name of Brunei and is made of three words: Negara means 'country', Brunei is the place name, and Darussalam comes from Arabic and means 'abode of peace'. So the name literally says 'the country of Brunei, the abode of peace', which is why it is used as the formal name for the nation.
