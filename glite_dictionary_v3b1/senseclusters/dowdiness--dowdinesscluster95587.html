Etymology

dowdiness comes from the adjective dowdy, meaning 'unfashionable' or 'shabby', and it may be linked to the old word dud for 'old clothes'. So, think of worn 'duds' or shabby clothes as a memory: dowdiness is the quality of looking dull or out of style.
