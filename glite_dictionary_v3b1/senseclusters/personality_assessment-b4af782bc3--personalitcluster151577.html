Etymology

personality assessment The name personality assessment comes from a simple idea: personality is a person's usual thoughts and behavior, and assessment is a careful check or score. When someone answers questions, the test 'measures' different parts of their personality, so the phrase means 'a test or questionnaire to evaluate a person's personality'.
