Etymology

oat cell carcinoma gets its name from how the cancer cells look under a microscope; the cells are very small and a little long, so they look like tiny oats, and doctors used that image to call them oat and cell. That's why oat cell carcinoma refers to a fast, serious 'type of lung cancer'.
