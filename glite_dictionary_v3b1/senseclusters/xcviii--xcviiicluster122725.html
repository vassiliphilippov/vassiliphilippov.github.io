Etymology

xcviii is a Roman numeral made from letters: X means '10', C means '100', V means '5', and I means '1'. The part XC is '100 minus 10' so it stands for '90', and VIII is '8', so together xcviii is the number '98' and is used for chapter numbers, legion names, or years on monuments.
