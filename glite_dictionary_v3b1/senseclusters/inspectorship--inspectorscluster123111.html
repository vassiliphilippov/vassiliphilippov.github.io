Etymology

Inspectorship comes from the parts in- and spect, which mean 'into' and 'to look'. You can see the same spect in the word spectacle, a thing to 'look' at. So an inspector 'looks into' places or problems, and an inspectorship is the official job or rank of the person who does those inspections.
