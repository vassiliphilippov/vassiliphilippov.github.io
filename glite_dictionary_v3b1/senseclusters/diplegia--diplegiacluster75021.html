Etymology

The word diplegia comes from Greek parts di- meaning 'two' and -plegia meaning 'a strike' or 'paralysis', and you see the same -plegia in paraplegia and hemiplegia. So, diplegia means paralysis of two matching parts of the body, usually both legs.
