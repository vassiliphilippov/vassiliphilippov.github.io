Etymology

Sauvignon blanc comes from French words: sauvignon from a word for 'wild' and blanc meaning 'white'. Farmers used the name for a grape that grew like a wild vine, and the wine kept the name, so today Sauvignon blanc means the white grape and the dry white wine made from it.
