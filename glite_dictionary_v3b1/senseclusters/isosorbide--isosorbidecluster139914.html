Etymology

isosorbide comes from the prefix iso- meaning 'same' and sorbitol, which is a 'sugar from fruit'. It is basically a changed form of that sugar used as a medicine to help blood flow in the heart, so isosorbide is used to prevent chest pain and treat heart failure.
