Etymology

high beam comes from car headlights where a beam is a strong line of light and high shows the light was aimed up to reach far ahead. That is why high beam means the bright, far-reaching headlight setting used to 'see far', but it can 'blind' other drivers so you must switch it off when someone is coming.
