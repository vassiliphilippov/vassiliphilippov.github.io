Etymology

wop is a rude, offensive name for someone of Italian background and probably comes from the Italian/Neapolitan word guappo, which meant a bold, swaggering man like a 'thug' or 'dandy'. It became a hurtful term for Italian immigrants in the United States, and a wrong story says it came from the letters WOP meaning 'without papers', but the true link is to guappo, so the word is still very insulting today.
