Etymology

superior conjunction comes from words used by sky watchers: conjunction means 'to line up' and superior means 'farther from' Earth. Picture the planet, the Sun, and Earth in one straight line with the planet on the far side of the Sun so it is hidden, and that image explains why the term means a planet aligned on the opposite side of the Sun from Earth.
