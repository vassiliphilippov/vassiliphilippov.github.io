Etymology

Ptyas mucosus may come from old words where Ptyas meant 'snake' and mucosus meant 'slimy', giving the picture of a long, smooth snake. Because this large, not-poisonous ratsnake is shiny, often hisses, and lives near people and farms, that picture helps explain what we mean today by Ptyas mucosus.
