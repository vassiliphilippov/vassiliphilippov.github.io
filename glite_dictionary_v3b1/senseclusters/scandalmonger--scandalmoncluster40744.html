Etymology

The word scandalmonger joins scandal, meaning public 'shame' or bad news, and monger, meaning a 'seller' or dealer, the same monger you see in fishmonger. So a scandalmonger is like someone who 'sells' gossip, which is why it means a person who enjoys spreading hurtful stories about others.
