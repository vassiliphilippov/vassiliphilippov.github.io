Etymology

lipfern comes from the two simple parts lip and fern, and a helpful way to remember it is to think of lip as meaning 'edge'. Many plants called lipfern have a curved edge on their leaves where their spores sit, so lipfern means a fern with a 'lip'-like edge that often grows on rocks in dry places.
