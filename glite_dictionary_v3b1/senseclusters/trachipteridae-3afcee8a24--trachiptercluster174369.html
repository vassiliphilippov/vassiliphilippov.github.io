Etymology

Trachipteridae comes from Greek parts trachy- 'rough' and -pter- 'fin', with -idae showing it is a 'family' of animals. So, Trachipteridae is the 'rough-fin' family, a group of long, ribbon-like sea fishes, and the name helps you picture their long, fin-like bodies.
