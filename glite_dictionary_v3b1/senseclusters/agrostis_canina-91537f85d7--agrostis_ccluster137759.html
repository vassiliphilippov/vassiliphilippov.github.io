Etymology

Agrostis canina comes from older words: Agrostis means 'field' or 'grass' and canina means 'of a dog'. People may have called it 'dog grass' because it often grew where dogs walked or its fine hairs looked like a dog's fur, so the name came to mean this particular species of grass.
