Etymology

labetalol hydrochloride combines two parts: labetalol (the active drug) and hydrochloride (the common acid form that helps the medicine stay stable and be taken easily). The ending -olol in labetalol appears in many heart medicines, so the name is a clue that labetalol hydrochloride is used 'to lower high blood pressure'.
