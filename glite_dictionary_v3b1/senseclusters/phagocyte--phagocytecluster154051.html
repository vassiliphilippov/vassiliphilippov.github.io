Etymology

phagocyte comes from the Greek parts phago- meaning 'to eat' and -cyte meaning 'cell', so it is a 'cell that eats' germs and dead cells in the body. That's why phagocytes protect us by swallowing and breaking down bacteria and worn-out cells.
