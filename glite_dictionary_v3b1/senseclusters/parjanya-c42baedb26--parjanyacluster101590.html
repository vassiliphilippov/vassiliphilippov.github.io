Etymology

Parjanya comes from Sanskrit and means 'rain' or 'thunder', so it was the name of the god who brings storms and life-giving rain. A helpful way to remember Parjanya is to think of him as the bringer of water to the fields, which is why people prayed to him for crops and relief from drought.
