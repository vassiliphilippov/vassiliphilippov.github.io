Etymology

restiveness comes from restive, a word once used for a horse that would not move and linked to the Latin root stare meaning 'to stand'. A helpful way to remember it is to think of rest, which means 'not moving', turning into nervous energy, so restiveness is the state of being 'restless' or hard to control.
