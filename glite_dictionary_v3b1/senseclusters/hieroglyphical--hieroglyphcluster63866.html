Etymology

hieroglyphical comes from Greek parts hieros 'sacred' and glyphein 'to carve', and it is the same root as hieroglyph, meaning a 'sacred carving' or picture sign. So something hieroglyphical looks like those carved picture symbols and is often hard to read.
