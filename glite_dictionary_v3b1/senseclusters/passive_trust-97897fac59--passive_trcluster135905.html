Etymology

passive trust comes from a simple image: a person who only holds a box and does nothing else. Because the person is passive, meaning not active, and the trust is simply holding property for someone else, the phrase describes 'a trust' where the 'trustee' has no duties and only keeps the assets for the 'beneficiary'.
