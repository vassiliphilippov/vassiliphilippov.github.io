Etymology

Uniat comes from a root meaning 'union', the same idea you see in union and unite. It was used for Eastern Christian groups who made a 'union' with the Pope in Rome while keeping their own worship and traditions, which is why the word means a church that accepts Rome but keeps its own ways.
