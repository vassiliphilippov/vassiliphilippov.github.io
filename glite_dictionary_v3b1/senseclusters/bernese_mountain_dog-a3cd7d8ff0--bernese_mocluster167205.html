Etymology

The name Bernese mountain dog comes from Swiss farmers. In the canton of Bern, people used big, strong dogs in the mountains to pull carts and help on the farm, so Bernese means 'from Bern' and mountain dog shows their place and job. That's why the name now means 'a large, gentle Swiss working breed'.
