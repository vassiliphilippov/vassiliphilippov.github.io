Etymology

Ethyl acetate gets its name from two parts: ethyl (from alcohol) and acetate (from the old word for 'vinegar'). Made from an alcohol and a vinegar-like acid, it has a sweet, fruity smell and is used as a solvent in flavorings and nail polish remover.
