Etymology

Privet andromeda may come from combining the hedge word privet, which suggests 'privacy', with andromeda, a name from an old Greek story that suggests 'beauty' and white robes. The picture of a white shrub with many small, bell-shaped flowers used as a living hedge helps explain why the name means that kind of bush.
