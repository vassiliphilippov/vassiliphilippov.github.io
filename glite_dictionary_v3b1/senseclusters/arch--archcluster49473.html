Etymology

arch as an adjective comes from an old root meaning 'chief' or 'extreme', the same idea you see in archenemy and archbishop. A helpful way to remember this is that an arch smile or remark is playfully 'very' sly or teasing, so it is mildly naughty in a clever way.
