Etymology

The word lucre comes from the Latin lucrum, meaning 'gain' or 'profit', and the same root appears in lucrative. Over time lucre picked up a negative feel and is often used for money gained in a dishonest or morally wrong way, as in the phrase 'filthy lucre'.
