Etymology

Leonid Brezhnev was the leader of the Soviet Union from 1964 to 1982, and his long time in power and the slow economy of those years made his name stand for the 'Era of Stagnation'. Many people also remember him for his thick eyebrows and long speeches, which give a clear image to help you recall who he was.
