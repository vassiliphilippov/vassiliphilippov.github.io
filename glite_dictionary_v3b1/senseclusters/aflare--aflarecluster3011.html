Etymology

aflare combines the prefix a- meaning 'in' or 'on' with flare, a word linked to 'flame' or a sudden 'burst of light'. So, imagine eyes or a skyline 'aflare' as if filled with light or fire, and you can see why aflare means 'lit up by or as if by fire; radiant'.
