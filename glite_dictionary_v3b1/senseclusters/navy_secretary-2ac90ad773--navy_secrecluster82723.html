Etymology

Navy Secretary is a title that comes from the clear image of a secretary (an official who runs a department) for the navy (the country's ships and sailors). So, it was used for the person who was 'the head of the Navy department', a role that existed until the Navy became part of the new Department of Defense in 1947.
