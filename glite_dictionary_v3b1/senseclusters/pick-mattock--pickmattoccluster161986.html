Etymology

The word pick-mattock simply joins two tool names: pick (a pointed tool for 'breaking' rock) and mattock, an old English word for a digging tool like a small 'axe' or 'hoe'. So a pick-mattock is one tool that both 'picks' and 'chops' to break hard soil or rocky ground.
