Etymology

sigmoidoscopy breaks into two parts: sigmoid (from the Greek sigma, meaning 'S-shaped') and -scopy (from a root meaning 'to look', like scope in 'microscope'). So, a sigmoidoscopy is a medical exam that looks into the S-shaped lower part of the colon.
