Etymology

mountain azalea gets its name from a simple picture: an azalea (a low, pink-flowering bush) growing on a mountain, where it spreads into flat mats among the rocks. So, the name means a 'small evergreen shrub' that grows in 'high mountain areas' and is often used in rock gardens.
