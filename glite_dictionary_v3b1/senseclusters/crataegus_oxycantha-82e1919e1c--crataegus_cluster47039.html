Etymology

Crataegus oxycantha comes from old Greek words that mean 'strong' and 'sharp thorn'. The part Crataegus shows the tree's hard wood and the part oxycantha shows its sharp thorns, so the name paints a picture of a small, tough tree covered in thorns.
