Etymology

Nervism comes from the Latin root nervus meaning 'nerve' or 'sinew' and the ending -ism meaning 'a theory or belief', and it shares the same root as the English words nerve and nervous. So, nervism is the idea that the nervous system controls behavior and all bodily processes.
