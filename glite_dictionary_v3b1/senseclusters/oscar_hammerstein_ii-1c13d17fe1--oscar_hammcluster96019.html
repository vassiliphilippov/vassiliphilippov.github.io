Etymology

Oscar Hammerstein II was an American writer who wrote the words for many famous musicals with composer Richard Rodgers. The II shows he was the 'second' Oscar in his family, so think of him as the second Oscar who helped create hits like 'Oklahoma!' and 'The Sound of Music', which is why his name is now known for great musical lyrics.
