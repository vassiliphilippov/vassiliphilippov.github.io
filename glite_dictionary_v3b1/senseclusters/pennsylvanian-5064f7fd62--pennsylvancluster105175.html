Etymology

Pennsylvanian as the name for a prehistoric period comes from Pennsylvania because researchers first studied the coal-rich rocks there. The sylvania part comes from Latin silva meaning 'woods' or 'forest', and the period had huge swamp forests that later made coal, so the name reminds us of those ancient forests and coal formation.
