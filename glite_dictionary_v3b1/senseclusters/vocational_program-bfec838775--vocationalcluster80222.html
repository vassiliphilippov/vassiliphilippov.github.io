Etymology

vocational program The phrase vocational program comes from the idea of learning a vocation, meaning a 'job' or 'calling', through a planned program of practical work. Imagine a workshop where students learn tools and real tasks, and that's why it means a course that trains you for a specific job rather than general school study.
