Etymology

The phrase capture attention comes from the idea of capture meaning 'to catch' and attention meaning what you notice. Imagine a bright poster catching your eye like a net catching a bird; once your eye is caught, your interest stays, so the phrase means 'to attract and hold someone's interest'.
