Etymology

ad prosequendum comes from Latin and was used when a court chose someone to bring a legal case for another person. The parts ad ('for') and prosequendum ('prosecuting') give the picture of a person sent 'for prosecuting' on behalf of a child or an incapacitated adult, so it means a person appointed to act for someone in court.
