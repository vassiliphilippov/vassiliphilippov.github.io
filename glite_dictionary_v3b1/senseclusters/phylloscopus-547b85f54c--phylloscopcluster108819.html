Etymology

The word Phylloscopus comes from Greek parts phyllo- meaning 'leaf' and scopus meaning 'watcher', the same root you see in scope. So a Phylloscopus is a 'leaf-watcher' — a small bird that moves through leaves looking for insects, which explains the name.
