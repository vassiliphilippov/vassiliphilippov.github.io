Etymology

aponeurotic comes from Greek parts apo- 'away from' and neuron 'sinew', and a 'sinew' is like a tendon. Imagine a tendon that spreads out into a flat sheet, so aponeurotic means 'like a wide, tendon-like layer that connects muscles'.
