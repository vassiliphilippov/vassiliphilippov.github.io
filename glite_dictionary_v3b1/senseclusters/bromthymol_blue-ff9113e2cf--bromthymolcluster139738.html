Etymology

bromthymol blue comes from the parts brom, from the element 'bromine', and thymol, a substance first found in the herb thyme, plus the word 'blue' for the dye. It is a blue dye that turns yellow in 'acid' and blue in 'base', so the name helps you remember it is a color-changing indicator.
