Etymology

noli-me-tangere comes from Latin and literally means 'do not touch me', from noli 'do not' and tangere 'to touch'. Because tangere is the same root as in tangible ('touchable'), doctors used noli-me-tangere for a painful or dangerous ulcer or growth they would not or could not touch, which is why it names an untouchable lesion.
