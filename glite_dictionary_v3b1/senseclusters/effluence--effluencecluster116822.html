Etymology

effluence comes from the old Latin verb effluere, made from ex- 'out' and fluere 'to flow', so it literally means 'to flow out'. Think of English words like flow and fluid; an effluence is something that flows out, such as waste or water leaving a pipe, which is why the word is used for outflow and pollution.
