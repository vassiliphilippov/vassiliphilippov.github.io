Etymology

President Eisenhower refers to Dwight D. Eisenhower, a U.S. Army general who led the Allied forces in World War II and then became the country's 34th President. Imagine a uniformed general moving into the White House—that picture helps you remember that President Eisenhower means the U.S. leader who served from 1953 to 1961.
