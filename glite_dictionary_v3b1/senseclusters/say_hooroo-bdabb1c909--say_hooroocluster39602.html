Etymology

say hooroo comes from the Australian farewell hooroo, a short, friendly shout people used when they were leaving. One popular story says hooroo grew from a quick spoken 'hurry' or playful sound made at parting, and that lively shout is why say hooroo means 'to say goodbye'.
