Etymology

ineluctable comes from two parts: in- meaning 'not' and eluctare meaning 'to struggle out', so it gives the image of something you cannot struggle away from. A helpful way to remember it is to think of reluctant, which also has the idea of resisting, and that is why ineluctable means 'impossible to avoid' or 'inescapable'.
