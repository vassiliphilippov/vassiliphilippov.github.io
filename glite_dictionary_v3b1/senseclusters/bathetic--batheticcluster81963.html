Etymology

The word bathetic comes from the Greek root bath- and the older word bathos, which mean 'depth'. People later used bathos to describe a sudden fall from serious to silly, so bathetic describes something that tries to be deeply emotional but feels fake, overdone, or even unintentionally funny.
