Etymology

cleric comes from an old word meaning 'chosen' or 'set apart' for a special role, and long ago that often meant people who could read and worked for the church. That's why cleric today means a member of the clergy, and it is related to the word clerk, which also began as a name for a church worker who 'could read'.
