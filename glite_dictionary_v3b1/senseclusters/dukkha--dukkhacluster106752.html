Etymology

dukkha comes from old Indian languages like Pali and Sanskrit, where the part du- means 'bad' and the whole word suggests something 'hard to stand' or 'difficult to bear'. So, imagine a heavy load or a painful feeling that is hard to carry; this is why dukkha means 'suffering', 'dissatisfaction', or 'unease'.
