Etymology

Unpleasure is formed from the negative prefix un- meaning 'not' and pleasure meaning 'enjoyment', like in unhappy. So unpleasure is a simple way to say something is 'not pleasurable', an unpleasant feeling or thing that causes discomfort or annoyance.
