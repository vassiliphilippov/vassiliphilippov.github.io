Rhodie spirit may come from the friendly nickname Rhodie for students and the word spirit meaning 'enthusiasm' and 'togetherness'. Imagine people wearing school colours, cheering at games and helping each other — that lively picture explains why the phrase means a strong 'sense of community' at university events.
