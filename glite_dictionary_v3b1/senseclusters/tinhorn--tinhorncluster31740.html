Etymology

tinhorn originally meant a small, unimportant gambler in old saloons, and the pieces tin ('cheap metal') plus horn (a loud instrument or boast) help you picture a flashy but weak player. So a tinhorn today often means a small-time or inexperienced gambler who talks big but has little real skill.
