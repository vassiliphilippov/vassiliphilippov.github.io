Etymology

Existentialist philosophy may come from the focus of existentialist thought on 'existence' and from philosophy as deep thinking about life. Imagine a person standing alone, having to choose what their life means, and this picture explains why the idea says people are free and must make their own meaning and take responsibility for their choices.
