Etymology

Cozy hoodie comes from two simple ideas: cozy meaning warm and snug, and hoodie meaning a sweatshirt with a hood. Imagine pulling the hood up and wrapping yourself in soft fabric like a small warm shelter, so it's easy to see why the phrase means 'a warm and comfortable sweatshirt for relaxing'.
