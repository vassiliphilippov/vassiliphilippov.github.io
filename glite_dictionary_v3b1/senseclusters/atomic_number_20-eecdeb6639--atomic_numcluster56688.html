Etymology

atomic number 20 comes from counting the tiny positive particles called 'protons' inside an atom; calcium atoms have exactly 20, and that exact count is what makes the atom the element calcium. So, atomic number 20 means the 'element' calcium, known for strong bones and a bright flame when it burns.
