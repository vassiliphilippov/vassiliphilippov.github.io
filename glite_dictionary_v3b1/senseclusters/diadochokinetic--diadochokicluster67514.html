Etymology

diadochokinetic comes from Greek parts: diadocho- 'one after another' and -kinetic 'movement'. The -kinetic part is the same as in the common word kinetic ('movement'), so diadochokinetic literally means making movements one after another, which is why it describes the ability to make quick, back-and-forth movements of muscles or body parts.
