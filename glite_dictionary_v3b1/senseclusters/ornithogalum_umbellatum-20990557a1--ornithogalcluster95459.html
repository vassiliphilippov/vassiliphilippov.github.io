Etymology

Ornithogalum umbellatum may come from old Greek words: ornitho meaning 'bird' and galum meaning 'milk', so people called it 'bird's milk' for its bright white flowers. The word umbellatum refers to the round 'umbrella' shape of its flower clusters, so the name helps you picture the small white, star-shaped plant we mean today.
