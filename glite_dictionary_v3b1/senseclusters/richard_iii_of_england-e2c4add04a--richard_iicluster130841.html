Etymology

Richard III of England was a 15th-century king who took the throne from his young nephew and is linked to the mystery of the missing Princes in the Tower. He was defeated and killed by Henry Tudor at Bosworth Field in 1485, and that's why he is remembered as the last king of his royal family and a famous, often debated figure in history and drama.
