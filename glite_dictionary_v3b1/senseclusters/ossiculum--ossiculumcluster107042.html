Etymology

ossiculum comes from the Latin os 'bone' and the ending -culum that makes something small, so it literally means 'little bone'. That's why an ossiculum is one of the very small bones, like the tiny bones in the middle ear. You can see the same root in ossify, which means 'to turn into bone'.
