Etymology

diverticulitis comes from diverticulum, a Latin word for a 'small pouch' that 'turns aside', and from the verb divert, 'to turn', plus the ending -itis, which means 'inflammation' as in tonsillitis. So diverticulitis is inflammation of those small pouches in the digestive tract.
