Etymology

Scleranthus comes from Greek parts scler- 'hard' and -anthus 'flower', the same -anthus you see in chrysanthemum. So it literally means 'hard flower', a useful image for these small, tough plants that grow in dry, rocky ground and are often hard to pull up.
