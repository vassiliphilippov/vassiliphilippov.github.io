Etymology

anthropocentrically comes from an old Greek root anthropo- meaning 'human' and centric meaning 'center', plus the ending -ly that makes it describe how something is done. So, to act anthropocentrically is to put humans at the 'center' or to treat humans as the most important, which is why the word means 'in a human-centered way'.
