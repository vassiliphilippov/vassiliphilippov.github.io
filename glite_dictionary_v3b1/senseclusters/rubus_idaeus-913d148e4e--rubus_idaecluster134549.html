Etymology

Rubus idaeus comes from Latin words: Rubus means 'bramble', a wild bush with berries, and idaeus likely means 'from Mount Ida', an old mountain where people said raspberries grew. So the name pictures a bramble 'from Mount Ida', which helps you remember it is the red raspberry plant.
