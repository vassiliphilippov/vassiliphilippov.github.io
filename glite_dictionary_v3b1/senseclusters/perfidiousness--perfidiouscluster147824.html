Etymology

perfidiousness comes from the Latin parts per- and fid, where fid means 'faith' and appears in familiar words like fidelity and 'faithful', while per- gives a sense of 'against' or 'away'. So perfidiousness is the quality of turning against faith—betrayal or being untrustworthy.
