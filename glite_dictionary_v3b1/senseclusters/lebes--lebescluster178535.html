Etymology

The word lebes comes from the ancient Greek word λέβης, which meant a large bronze 'cauldron' or 'kettle' used for cooking and religious ceremonies. Imagine a heavy metal bowl standing over a fire, and you can see why a lebes refers to that kind of ancient cooking or ritual pot.
