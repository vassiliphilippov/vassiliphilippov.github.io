Etymology

American spicebush may be named for its strong, warm smell: the leaves and bark give a spicy scent, so people thought of it as a spice that grows on a bush. Early settlers even used the leaves as a 'spice' or to make a 'tea', so the name helps you remember that the plant smells and tastes spicy.
