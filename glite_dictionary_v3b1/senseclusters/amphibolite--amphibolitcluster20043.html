Etymology

The word amphibolite comes from the mineral amphibole plus the ending -ite, which means 'rock' or 'stone'. The amphibole part has the Greek root amphi- meaning 'both', the same root as amphibian ('on land and water'), so amphibolite is a 'rock' mostly made of amphibole minerals.
