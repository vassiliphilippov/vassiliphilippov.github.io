Etymology

poriferous comes from the simple word pore 'small hole' and the Latin part -ferous 'bearing' or 'carrying', the same ending you see in coniferous, which means 'cone-bearing'. Think of a sponge that 'carries' lots of tiny holes, and that's why poriferous describes something full of small openings.
