Etymology

YHVH is the sacred four-letter name of God in ancient Hebrew, written without vowels so its exact sound is not known. Many people guess the pronunciation as Yahweh or Jehovah, or they use 'the LORD' out of respect, which explains why many Bible translations replace YHVH with 'the LORD'.
