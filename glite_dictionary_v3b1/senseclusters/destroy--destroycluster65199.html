Etymology

The word destroy is also used for killing animals for safety or mercy, and it comes from parts that mean 'remove' (de-) and 'build' (stru-), so it carries the idea of taking something away or ending it. That's why officials sometimes 'destroy' sick animals to stop danger or suffering.
