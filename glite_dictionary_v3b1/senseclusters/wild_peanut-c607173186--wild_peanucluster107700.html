Etymology

Wild peanut gets its name from a simple picture: a low vine that makes small, peanut-like pods hidden under the soil. The word wild means it 'grows without being planted' and peanut shows the pods look and taste like garden peanuts, so the name tells you it is a wild plant with edible, peanut-like pods.
