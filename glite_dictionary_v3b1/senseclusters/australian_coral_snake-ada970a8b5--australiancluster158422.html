Etymology

Australian coral snake likely gets its name from the word coral, because the snake's bright red, black and white bands look like pieces of coral. Those bright bands are a natural 'warning' that the snake is 'venomous', so the name helps explain this small Australian snake's striking, dangerous-looking colors.
