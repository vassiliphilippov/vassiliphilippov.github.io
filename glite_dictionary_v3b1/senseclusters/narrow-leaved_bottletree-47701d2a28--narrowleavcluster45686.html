Etymology

narrow-leaved bottletree: The name narrow-leaved bottletree comes from the tree's look: a thick, bottle-like trunk gave the name bottletree, and its long, thin leaves explain narrow-leaved. So the name helps you picture a tree with a 'bottle' trunk that can store water and narrow leaves.
