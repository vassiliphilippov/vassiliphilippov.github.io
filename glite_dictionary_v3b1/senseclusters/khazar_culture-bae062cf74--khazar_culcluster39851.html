Etymology

Khazar culture gets its name from the Khazars, a Turkic people who lived on the open plains and built a busy trading state between the Black Sea and the Caspian Sea. Picture horse riders, crowded markets and people of many faiths mixing their customs — those common habits and beliefs are the 'ways of life' the name Khazar culture now describes.
