Etymology

trip to the woodshed comes from times when parents or teachers would take someone on a short trip to the private woodshed to give a quick punishment or scolding. One popular story says that this clear image of a private, telling-off visit is why the phrase now means 'an occasion when someone is punished or strongly reprimanded, often privately'.
