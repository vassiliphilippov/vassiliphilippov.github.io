Etymology

The word promiscuously comes from the Latin parts pro- and miscere, which give the idea of 'mixed together', like the English word 'mix'. So, to act promiscuously is to do things without choosing carefully or all mixed together, which is why it can mean having many casual partners or behaving in a messy, unplanned way.
