Etymology

Hottentot's fig may come from old European settlers in South Africa who used Hottentot for the local people and called the plant a fig because its round, soft fruit looked like a small fig. So the name links the people near where the plant grew and the fig-like fruit, which is why we use this name for the coastal plant today. Note: the word Hottentot is an old term and can be seen as offensive today.
