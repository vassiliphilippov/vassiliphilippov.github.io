Etymology

authoritarian regime comes from two parts: authoritarian (from 'authority', meaning a strong ruler) and regime (meaning 'rule' or a way of governing). Imagine one leader or a small group standing over everyone, making all the decisions and not listening to the people. That's why it means a government where power is held by a leader or small elite who are 'not accountable' to the public.
