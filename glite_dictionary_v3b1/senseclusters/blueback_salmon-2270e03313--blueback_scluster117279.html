Etymology

The name blueback salmon comes from how the fish looks: at sea it has a shiny blueback, and when it returns to rivers to lay eggs its flesh turns bright red. Because the adults 'die' after laying eggs and the red meat is valued as food, fishermen also call this fish the 'sockeye', which helps explain its life cycle and why people eat it.
