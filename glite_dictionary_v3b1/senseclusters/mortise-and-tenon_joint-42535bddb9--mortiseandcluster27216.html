Etymology

The noun mortise-and-tenon joint comes from old woodwork: a tenon, a short wooden tongue, fits into a mortise, a matching hole, so the two pieces lock together like a plug in a socket. That's why the name means a very 'strong' joint used in furniture and buildings.
