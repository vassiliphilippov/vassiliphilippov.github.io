Etymology

Urban typhus comes from the way the disease appeared in crowded city areas where fleas on rats bit people and passed tiny germs that caused fever and a rash. Urban means 'city' and typhus is an old word for a serious 'fever', so the name means a fever illness spread by fleas in cities.
