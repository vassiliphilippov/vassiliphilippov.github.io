Etymology

Babar the Elephant began as a little tale a mother told her children about a young elephant who leaves the jungle, goes to the 'city', learns human ways and even wears a suit. The artist Jean de Brunhoff drew that story into books, so Babar the Elephant became the famous elephant who has adventures and later becomes a 'king'.
