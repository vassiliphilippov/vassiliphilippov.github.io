Etymology

Simuliidae comes from the scientific genus Simulium, a Latin name for a group of 'small biting flies', plus the family ending -idae which means 'family' in biology. So, Simuliidae simply names the family of blackflies and similar biting flies, which is exactly how the word is used today.
