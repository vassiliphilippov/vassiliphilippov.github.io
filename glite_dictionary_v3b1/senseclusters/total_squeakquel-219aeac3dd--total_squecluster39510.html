Etymology

total squeakquel is a playful blend of squeak, meaning a small, weak sound, and sequel, a follow-up movie. One popular story says the idea of a follow-up that only gives a little 'squeak' instead of a full new story shows it is 'unoriginal' or 'poorly made', so the phrase now means 'a sequel that is unoriginal or poorly made'.
