Etymology

German short-haired pointer: The name German short-haired pointer comes from three simple parts: German because the breed started in Germany, short-haired for its close coat, and pointer for the dog's habit of standing very still and 'pointing' its nose and body toward birds. That's why the name tells you it is a hunting dog known for being clever and good at many tasks.
