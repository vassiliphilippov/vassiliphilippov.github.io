Etymology

codlins-and-cream comes from the parts codlin and cream, where codlin was an old word for a 'small apple' and cream is the white dairy food. A helpful way to remember the name is to picture the plant's pale centre and reddish outer petals as a little apple with cream, which explains why this flower has that name.
