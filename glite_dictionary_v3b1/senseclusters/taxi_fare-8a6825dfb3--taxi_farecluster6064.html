Etymology

The compound noun taxi fare comes from joining taxi (a car you hire) and fare (a payment for a journey). Long ago people paid a 'fare' for coach or boat trips, and the same idea moved to taxis, so taxi fare means 'the money you pay for a taxi ride'.
