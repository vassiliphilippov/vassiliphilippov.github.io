Etymology

genus Agaricus comes from an old Greek word agarikon that meant a kind of 'mushroom'. So the parts genus ('group') and Agaricus ('mushroom') together name the group of common mushrooms with gills, which is why the term refers to the familiar edible and related kinds today.
