Etymology

pink-slime journalism comes from the cheap processed meat known as pink slime, a pale paste added to ground beef to make it look like more meat and hide what is really inside. So calling news 'pink-slime journalism' gives the image of articles that look like real reporting but are low-quality and misleading, which is why it means 'misleading, attention-seeking news'.
