Etymology

The word amenorrhea comes from Greek parts: a- meaning 'without', meno- meaning 'month' as in menstruation and menopause, and -rrhea meaning 'flow', the same ending as in diarrhea. So amenorrhea literally means 'no menstrual flow', which is why it names the absence of periods.
