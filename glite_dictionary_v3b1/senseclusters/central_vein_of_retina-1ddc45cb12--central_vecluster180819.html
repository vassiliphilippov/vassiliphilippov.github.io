Etymology

central vein of retina is a literal name: central means 'middle', vein is a blood vessel, and retina is the layer at the back of the eye that senses light, so imagine one main 'river' in the middle that drains blood from the retina. That's why it means 'the main vein that carries blood away from the retina'.
