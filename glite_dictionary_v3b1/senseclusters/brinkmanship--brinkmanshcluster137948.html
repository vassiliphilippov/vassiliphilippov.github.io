Etymology

brinkmanship comes from brink meaning 'edge' and the ending -manship, as in showmanship, meaning 'skill' or 'art'. So the word paints a picture of the 'skill of standing on the edge' — often in politics — pushing a risky situation to the limit to make the other side give in.
