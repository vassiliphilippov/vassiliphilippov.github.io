Etymology

Anna Pavlova was a famous Russian ballerina known for her small, expressive movements and for the solo 'The Dying Swan', which looked like a graceful swan dying on stage. Because she toured the world and showed this delicate style to many people, her name now often brings to mind 'grace' and classic ballet.
