Etymology

The place name Dasht-e Lut comes from Persian words: dasht meaning 'plain' or 'desert' and -e meaning 'of', so it means 'the desert of Lut'. That simple, literal name fits the land because it is a very hot, empty plain with strange salt and sand shapes.
