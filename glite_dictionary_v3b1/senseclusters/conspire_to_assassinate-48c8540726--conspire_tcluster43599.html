Etymology

The phrase conspire to assassinate comes from a vivid image: conspire once meant people 'breathing together', close and whispering, and assassinate comes from old words for secret killers. Together they paint a picture of people quietly planning a murder, so the phrase means 'to secretly plan together to kill'.
