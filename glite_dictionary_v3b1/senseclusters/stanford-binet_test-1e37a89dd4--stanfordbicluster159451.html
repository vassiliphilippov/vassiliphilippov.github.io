Etymology

Stanford-Binet test is named after the French psychologist Alfred Binet, who made the first school test to find children who needed help, and after Stanford University, where Lewis Terman changed and spread the test. That's why the name refers to a formal 'intelligence test' used to measure 'IQ'.
