Etymology

The word menehune comes from Hawaiian stories about small, secret people who build fishponds and walls at night. One idea links menehune to an older Polynesian word manahune, meaning 'common people', so you can remember them as hidden workers who quietly made things, which is why the word now means those little night builders.
