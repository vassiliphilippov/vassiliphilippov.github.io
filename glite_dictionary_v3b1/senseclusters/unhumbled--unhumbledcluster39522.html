Etymology

unhumbled is made of the prefix un- ('not') and humble, which comes from an old Latin word meaning 'ground' or 'low'. Think of being 'low to the ground' — to be humbled is to be made low or modest, so unhumbled means 'not made low' and not feeling ashamed or defeated.
