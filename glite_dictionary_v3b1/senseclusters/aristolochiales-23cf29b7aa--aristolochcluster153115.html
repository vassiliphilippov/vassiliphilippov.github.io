Etymology

Aristolochiales comes from the plant name Aristolochia, which is made of the Greek parts aristos 'best' and lochia 'childbirth' because people once used these plants to help women during birth. The -ales ending marks a group of related plants, so Aristolochiales is the group that includes the birthwort family.
