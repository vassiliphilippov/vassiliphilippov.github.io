Etymology

Lochaber ax comes from the Lochaber area of Scotland and refers to a long-handled battle ax used by Highland fighters. Its long handle and heavy hooked blade were used to pull riders off horses, so the picture of that long pole with a big blade helps you remember it means 'a long-handled battle-ax' today.
