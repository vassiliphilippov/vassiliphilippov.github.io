Etymology

prairie gentian is a name made of two parts: prairie for the open grassland where it grows, and gentian for a group of blue, bell-shaped flowers whose name long ago came from a king who used the plant as 'medicine'. So, the name tells you it is a blue 'wildflower' of the prairie.
