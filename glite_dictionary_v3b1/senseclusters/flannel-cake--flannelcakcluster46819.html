Etymology

flannel-cake comes from flannel ('soft cloth') and cake, and a helpful idea is that people either wrapped thin pancakes in a flannel to keep them warm or thought the cakes were soft like the cloth. So a flannel-cake is a thin, flat 'pancake' cooked on a griddle.
