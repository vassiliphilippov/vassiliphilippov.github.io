Hygrophorus borealis comes from the parts Hygrophorus (from old words meaning 'wet' and 'to carry') and borealis (Latin for 'northern'). So think of it as a 'moist-carrying' mushroom from the north, which helps you remember it is a damp-loving, pale mushroom often found in northern forests.
