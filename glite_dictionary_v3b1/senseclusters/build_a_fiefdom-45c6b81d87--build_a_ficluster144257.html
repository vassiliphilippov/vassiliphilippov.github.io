Etymology

build a fiefdom comes from the days when a lord was given land and power to rule as his own fiefdom. To build a fiefdom meant gathering people and money that answered only to that lord. So today build a fiefdom means 'to create a personal area of power or control', for example inside a company where one person acts like a little ruler.
