Etymology

The word beebalm comes from the simple parts bee and balm, where 'balm' means a soothing, fragrant plant or small healing salve. So beebalm is a fragrant herb that bees love and gardeners often grow for its bright flowers and leaves that can make a sweet herbal tea.
