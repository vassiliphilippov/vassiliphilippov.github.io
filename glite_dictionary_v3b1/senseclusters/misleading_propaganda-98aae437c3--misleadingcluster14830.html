Etymology

misleading propaganda combines propaganda, meaning organized messages a group sends out with posters, leaflets, or loudspeakers, and misleading, meaning giving the wrong idea. Picture a loudspeaker repeating a false story to many people; that image shows why the phrase means 'information spread to deceive'.
