Etymology

Eucalyptus camaldulensis gets its name from Greek and a place in Italy. The word Eucalyptus means 'well-covered' because the flower buds have a small cap that falls off when they open, and camaldulensis may come from 'Camaldoli', an Italian garden where the tree was grown, so the full name points to the river red gum tree that grows by rivers and has red, strong wood.
