Etymology

nonconformity is made of non- 'not' and conformity, where con- means 'together' and form means 'shape', so it means 'not matching the shape'. Imagine two plans or pieces that do not fit together like mismatched puzzle pieces, and you have nonconformity as a lack of agreement or match between things.
