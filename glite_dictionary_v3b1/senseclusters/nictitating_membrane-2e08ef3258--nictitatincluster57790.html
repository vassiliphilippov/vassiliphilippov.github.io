Etymology

nictitating membrane: The term nictitating membrane comes from a word meaning 'to wink'; the clear membrane is a thin third eyelid that sweeps across the eye like a quick blink. That's why the phrase means the thin, protective eyelid that moves to 'protect' and 'moisten' the eye while the animal can still see.
