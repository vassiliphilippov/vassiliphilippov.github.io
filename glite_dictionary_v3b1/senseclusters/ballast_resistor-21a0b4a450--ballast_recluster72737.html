Etymology

The name ballast resistor comes from the idea of a ship's ballast, a heavy weight that keeps the boat steady, and the electrical resistor, a part that slows the flow of electricity. Together they act like a small weight inside a circuit that helps keep the current 'steady' when heat or other changes try to make it rise or fall.
