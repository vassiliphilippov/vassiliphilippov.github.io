Etymology

The word garibaldi names a loose, high-necked blouse and comes from the Italian leader Garibaldi, whose volunteer soldiers famously wore bright 'red shirts' in the 19th century. So a garibaldi blouse copied the look of those shirts and became a popular, showy style.
