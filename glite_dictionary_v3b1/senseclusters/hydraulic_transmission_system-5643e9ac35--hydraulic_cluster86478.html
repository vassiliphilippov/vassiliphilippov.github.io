Etymology

hydraulic transmission system comes from a simple picture: hydraulic means using a 'liquid', transmission means 'sending', and system is a group of parts working together. Think of liquid pushed through pipes to move machine parts like people passing boxes. The moving liquid 'sends' power, so the phrase means a system that uses liquid under pressure to send force.
