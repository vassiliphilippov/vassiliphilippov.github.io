Etymology

Laputa was invented by Jonathan Swift as the name of a floating island in Gulliver's Travels, so think of it as a place that 'floats away' from ordinary life. A helpful way to remember Laputa is to picture clever people so lost in theory that their island rises above everyday problems, which is why the name stands for impractical, out-of-touch thinking.
