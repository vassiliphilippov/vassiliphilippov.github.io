Etymology

toxic behavior uses toxic, a word for 'poison', and behavior, meaning actions. Imagine someone pouring poison into a shared cup so it hurts everyone who drinks; this clear image shows why toxic behavior means 'harmful or poisonous actions' that hurt people emotionally.
