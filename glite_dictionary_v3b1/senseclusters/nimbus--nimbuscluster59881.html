Etymology

nimbus: The word nimbus comes from the Latin word nimbus, meaning 'cloud' or 'rain', and it became the name for a dark rain cloud—think of a heavy 'cloud' ready to pour. So, a nimbus is a rain cloud that brings wet weather.
