Etymology

musicalness comes from the simple word music plus the ending -ness, which means 'quality'. The word music originally meant the 'art of the Muses', the old Greek goddesses of song and art, and is related to the English word muse, so musicalness means having the 'quality of music' or a sound that feels like music.
