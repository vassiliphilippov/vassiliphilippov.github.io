Etymology

Atlantic Standard Time comes from using the same official clock in places by the Atlantic Ocean: Atlantic points to the ocean and Standard Time means the area's 'official time'. So the name now refers to the local clock used in Puerto Rico, Bermuda and the Canadian Maritimes, which is four hours behind the world's main time.
