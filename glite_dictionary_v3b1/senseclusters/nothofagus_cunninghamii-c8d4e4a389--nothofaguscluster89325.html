Etymology

Nothofagus cunninghamii comes from old Greek words and from the name of a botanist. The first part, nothos + fagus, means 'false beech' because the tree looks like a beech but is different, and cunninghamii honors the plant collector Allan Cunningham, so the name tells us what the tree looks like and who it remembers.
