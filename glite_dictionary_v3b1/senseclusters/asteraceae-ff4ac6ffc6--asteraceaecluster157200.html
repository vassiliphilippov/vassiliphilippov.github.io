Etymology

Asteraceae comes from the Greek word aster meaning 'star' and the Latin family ending -aceae used for groups of plants. So Asteraceae is like the 'star family' of flowering plants, which helps explain why daisies, sunflowers and dandelions in this family have flower heads that look like little stars.
