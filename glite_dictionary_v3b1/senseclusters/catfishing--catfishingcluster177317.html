Etymology

catfishing for online trickery comes from the fish catfish and a story in a popular documentary. The film used the idea of a 'catfish' keeping other fish lively, so a helpful way to remember it is that a 'catfish' online keeps a relationship 'alive' with a fake identity, and therefore catfishing means creating a fake online person to deceive someone.
