Etymology

The expression sucktastic attitude is a joking mix of suck and -tastic. Here suck means 'to be bad' and -tastic (from 'fantastic') adds the idea 'very', so the image is of someone being very bad or annoying, and that's why it describes a very poor or annoying attitude.
