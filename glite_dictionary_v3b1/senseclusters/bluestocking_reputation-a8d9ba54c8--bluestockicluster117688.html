Etymology

bluestocking reputation comes from 18th century meetings called the Blue Stockings, where guests sometimes wore plain blue stockings and spent the evening talking about books and ideas. That picture of people in blue stockings known for reading made 'bluestocking' mean someone 'well-educated' or 'intellectual', so the phrase means a reputation for learning.
