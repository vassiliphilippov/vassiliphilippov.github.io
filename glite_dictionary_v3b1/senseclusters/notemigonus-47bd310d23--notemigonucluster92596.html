Etymology

The word Notemigonus is a scientific name made from old Greek parts and is used for the golden shiner, a small shiny freshwater fish from North America. A helpful way to remember it is to see note- meaning 'back' and -gonus meaning 'angle' or 'bend', so you can picture a slim fish with a shiny back and understand why the name fits this common bait fish.
