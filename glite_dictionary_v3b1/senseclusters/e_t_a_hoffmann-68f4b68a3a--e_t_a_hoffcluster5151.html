Etymology

E. T. A. Hoffmann comes from his full name: E. T. A. are the first letters of Ernst Theodor Amadeus and Hoffmann is his family name. He wrote strange, musical stories that mixed art and the 'supernatural', and that image is why we remember him for 'fantastic and supernatural tales', such as the story that inspired the Nutcracker ballet.
