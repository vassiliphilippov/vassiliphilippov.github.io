Etymology

quiche-eater joins quiche, a French egg 'tart' whose name comes from an old German word meaning 'cake', and eater, so it literally means 'someone who eats quiche'. People often use it jokingly to mean someone seen as trendy, fussy, or a bit too proud of their food.
