Etymology

Ringer's solution is named after a 19th-century scientist, Sydney Ringer, who found a mix of salts that kept frog hearts beating outside the body. Because his salt mix kept tissues alive, the name now refers to the liquid doctors and labs use 'to restore fluids' and 'to keep tissues alive' during treatment and experiments.
