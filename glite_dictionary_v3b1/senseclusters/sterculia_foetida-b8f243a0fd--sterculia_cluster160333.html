Etymology

Sterculia foetida: The plant name Sterculia foetida comes from old Latin words that point to its strong smell. The first part, Sterculia, comes from Sterculius, a Roman god linked with dung, and the second part, foetida, means 'stinking', so the name warns about the tree's foul-smelling flowers and pods.
