Etymology

The Clifford trust is named after a person or court case called Clifford and describes a way to put income into a trust for someone who pays tax at a lower rate. People kept the income with that person for ten years or more to lower the original owner's tax, so the term means 'a trust set up to reduce tax by shifting income'.
