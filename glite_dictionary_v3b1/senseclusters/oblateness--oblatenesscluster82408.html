Etymology

The word oblateness comes from the parts ob- (meaning 'toward' or 'against') and Latin latus (meaning 'broad' or 'wide'), and it is the noun form of oblate. So oblateness describes being a bit 'flattened' at the poles and 'wider' around the middle, which is the same idea of 'wide' you see in latitude.
