Etymology

The word bye-bye comes from the short word bye, a friendly form of 'goodbye', and is made by repeating the word like simple child talk such as mama, so it sounds gentle and easy to say. So, bye-bye is used when leaving someone, often to comfort children or to make a parting feel friendly.
