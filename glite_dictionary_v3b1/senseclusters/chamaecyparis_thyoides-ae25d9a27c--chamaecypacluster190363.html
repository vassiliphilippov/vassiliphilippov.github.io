Etymology

Chamaecyparis thyoides comes from old name parts meaning chamae 'on the ground', cyparis 'cypress', and thyoides 'like a cedar', so the picture is a cypress-like tree that grows low in wet ground. That's why the name fits this Atlantic white cedar, a slow-growing evergreen with strong, fragrant wood.
