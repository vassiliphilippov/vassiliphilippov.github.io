Etymology

The word uninspirable breaks into un- meaning 'not', inspire meaning 'to breathe life or new ideas into someone', and -able meaning 'able to'. So uninspirable literally means 'not able to be breathed into', which helps remember that someone uninspirable cannot be easily excited or motivated.
