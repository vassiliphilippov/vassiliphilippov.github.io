Etymology

tail lamp: The name tail lamp comes from the simple picture of a lamp at the tail, the 'rear' of a vehicle. In early times, horse carriages and early cars had small lamps hung at the back to show other drivers at night, so today tail lamp means the red light at the rear that shows a vehicle is there.
