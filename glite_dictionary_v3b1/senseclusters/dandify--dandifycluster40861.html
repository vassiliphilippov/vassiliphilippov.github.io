Etymology

The word dandify comes from dandy, a word for a man who loves fine clothes and showy style, plus the ending -ify, as in beautify, meaning 'make' or 'become'. So, to dandify someone is to 'make' them look like a dandy, dressing them in very fashionable or showy clothes.
