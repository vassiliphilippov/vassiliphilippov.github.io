Synonyms

  • suppressor
    0.2/m8.8
    Stops the visible effect of another hereditary unit often for mutant versions
    vsmodifier: alters effects broadly rather than only hiding visible traits
  • modifier gene
    0.00/m7.2
    Names a hereditary unit that changes another hereditary unit's effect
    vsmodifier: is the shorter label for the same concept
  • epistatic gene
    0.00/m9.6
    Masks another hereditary unit's effect at a different locus to block trait expression
    vsmodifier: alters effects more generally rather than only masking at another locus
  • suppresser
    0.00/m8.8
    Uses an alternate spelling for the same inhibiting hereditary unit
    vsmodifier: has identical meaning but uses the other standard spelling

Etymology

modifier comes from the root mod meaning 'measure' or 'way', and is related to the verb modify 'to make change'. So in genetics a modifier is something that changes the effect or strength of another gene.
