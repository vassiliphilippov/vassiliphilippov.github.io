Etymology

The word boi started online as a playful spelling of boy and is used like a quick shout to show 'surprise', 'disbelief', or 'amusement'. You can remember it from internet memes like 'dat boi' — it works as a short, joking reaction, which is why people now say boi as an exclamation.
