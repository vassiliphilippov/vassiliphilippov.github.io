Etymology

generousness comes from the Latin root genus meaning 'birth' or 'family', the same root you see in generation and gene. At first it meant 'noble by birth', so being generous was like having a noble, open heart that gives freely, and that is why generousness means the habit of giving time, money, or help to others.
