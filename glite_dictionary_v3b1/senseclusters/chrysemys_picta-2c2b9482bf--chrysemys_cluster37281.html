Etymology

Chrysemys picta gets its name from old Greek and Latin words: Chrysemys meant 'golden turtle' and picta meant 'painted', because this turtle has bright yellow and red marks on its shell. So the name is a simple picture of the animal and explains why people call it the 'painted turtle' today.
