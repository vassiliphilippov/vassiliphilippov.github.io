Etymology

The word Uta is a scientific genus name given in the 1800s to a group of small side-blotched lizards. The exact origin of the name is not clear, so a helpful way to remember Uta is to treat it as a short name for these little desert lizards and to think 'small side-blotched lizard' when you see it.
