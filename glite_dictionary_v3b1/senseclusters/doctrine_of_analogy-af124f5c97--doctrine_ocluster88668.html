Etymology

doctrine of analogy comes from a simple image: doctrine is a teaching and analogy is a comparison, like using a small toy car to show a real car. That picture helps remember that when people use human words about God they point to a likeness but the real thing is much bigger and different, so the phrase says any 'similarity' to God is limited by a greater 'difference'.
