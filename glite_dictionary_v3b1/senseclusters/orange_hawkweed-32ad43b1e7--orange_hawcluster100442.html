Etymology

orange hawkweed may come from an old belief about hawks: people thought hawks ate the plant or fed it to their young, so they called it a hawkweed, using 'weed' to mean a plant people did not want. The word orange points to its bright orange flowers, so the full name now means a bright, often unwanted wildflower.
