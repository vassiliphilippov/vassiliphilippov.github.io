Etymology

golden polypody gets its name from a clear picture: golden for its yellowish leaves and polypody which suggests 'many feet', because each leaf splits into many little lobes that look like tiny feet. That's why the name fits a fern with large, deeply divided leaves often found on rocks or grown indoors.
