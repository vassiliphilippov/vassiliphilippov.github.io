Etymology

lymphadenoma is built from lymph 'clear body fluid', aden 'gland' as in adenoid, and -oma 'swelling' or 'growth'. So a lymphadenoma is a swollen or enlarged lymph gland, which is why doctors use this word for an abnormal lymph node.
