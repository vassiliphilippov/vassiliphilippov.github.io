Etymology

Lophodytes cucullatus comes from old Greek and Latin words: Lophodytes combines 'lophos' meaning 'crest' and 'dytes' meaning 'diver', and cucullatus means 'hooded'. The name points to the male duck's round crest that looks like a little hood, so it explains the common name 'hooded merganser'.
