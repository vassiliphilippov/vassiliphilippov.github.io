Etymology

aerophilous comes from the parts aero- meaning 'air' and -philous meaning 'loving', so it means 'air-loving'. That's why an aerophilous organism needs or depends on free oxygen or open air. You can also see aero- in words like aeroplane and aerobic, which helps remember the 'air' part.
