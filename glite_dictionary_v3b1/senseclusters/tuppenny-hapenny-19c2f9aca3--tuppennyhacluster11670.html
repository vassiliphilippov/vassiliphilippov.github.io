Etymology

tuppenny-ha'penny mixes old British coin names tuppenny 'two pennies' and ha'penny 'half a penny'; both are very small amounts of money. Because they are tiny coins, calling something tuppenny-ha'penny is a way to say it is cheap, unimportant, or of little value.
