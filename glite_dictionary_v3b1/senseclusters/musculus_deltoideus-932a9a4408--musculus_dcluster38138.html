Etymology

musculus deltoideus comes from Latin and Greek and means a 'triangle muscle'. The word musculus means 'muscle' and deltoideus refers to the Greek letter delta because this muscle is triangle-shaped and sits over the 'shoulder', which is why it helps lift and move the arm.
