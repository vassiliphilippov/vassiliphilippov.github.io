Etymology

The name 17 November comes from a famous event in Greece: on 17 November 1973 students at the Athens Polytechnic rose up against the military government and the army attacked the protest. A violent left-wing group later chose that date as its name to remember the protest, so today 17 November refers to that terrorist group.
