Etymology

malignantly comes from the root mal- meaning 'bad' and from malign meaning 'harmful', and you can see mal- in words like malfunction and malicious. So malignantly means 'in a harmful or dangerous way', which is why doctors use it for tumors that grow and spread quickly.
