Etymology

Calophyllum inophyllum comes from old Greek words: Calophyllum meaning 'beautiful leaf' and inophyllum meaning 'oil leaf'. The tree has shiny leaves and its nuts make a useful oil used in traditional medicine and for building boats, so the name points to both its glossy leaves and oily seeds.
