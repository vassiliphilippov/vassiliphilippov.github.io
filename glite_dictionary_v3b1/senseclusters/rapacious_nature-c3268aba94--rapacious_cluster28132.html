Etymology

rapacious nature comes from the word rapacious, which paints a picture of an animal that 'grabs' or snatches everything, and nature here means a person's usual character. So the image of an animal taking all it can helps explain why the phrase means 'extremely greedy' or 'always trying to take more', especially of money or natural resources.
