Etymology

memorial park comes from the idea of making a 'cemetery' look and feel like a green park with lawns, trees, gardens and quiet paths, while adding a memorial area for names or plaques. That's why a memorial park today is a peaceful place where people go to 'remember' loved ones rather than a busy graveyard.
