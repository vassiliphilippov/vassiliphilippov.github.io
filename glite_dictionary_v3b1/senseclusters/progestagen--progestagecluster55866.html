Etymology

The word progestagen comes from pro- meaning 'for' and gest- as in gestation meaning 'to carry' or 'pregnancy'. So a progestagen is 'for carrying' a pregnancy, which is why these hormones help prepare and support the uterus for pregnancy or are used in birth control and hormone treatment.
