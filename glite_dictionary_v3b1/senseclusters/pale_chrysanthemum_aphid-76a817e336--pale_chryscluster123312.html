Etymology

pale chrysanthemum aphid comes from a simple image: the insect is light in color, pale, lives on chrysanthemum flowers and is an aphid, a tiny bug that drinks the plant's 'sap'. So the name tells you it is a small 'pest' that feeds on chrysanthemums and can damage the plants.
