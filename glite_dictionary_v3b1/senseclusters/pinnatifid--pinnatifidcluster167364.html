Etymology

The word pinnatifid comes from Latin parts pinna meaning 'feather' and -fid meaning 'split'. It describes a leaf that is 'split like a feather' into deep lobes toward the leaf's middle, so the leaf looks feather-like but stays one piece rather than separate leaflets.
