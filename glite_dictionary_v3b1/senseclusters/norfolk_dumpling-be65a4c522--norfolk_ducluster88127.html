Etymology

Norfolk dumpling comes from simple home cooking in Norfolk: cooks made small balls of plain dough and boiled or steamed them in a pot, so the soft dumplings were a regular 'side dish' to meat and stew. That's why the name still means the soft, steamed or boiled dumpling people eat today.
