Etymology

The phrase pitiful performance comes from the word pitiful, meaning something that makes people feel pity or sorry, and performance, meaning a show or action; imagine a show so weak that the audience feels sorry for the actors. So, pitiful performance means 'very poor' or 'disappointing'.
