Etymology

Feast of Dedication comes from a time long ago when Jews cleaned and made the Temple in Jerusalem holy again after a victory, and they lit the Temple lamp even though only a small jar of oil was left and it kept burning for eight nights. That's why people remember the event by lighting a menorah each night during the eight-day festival, also called 'Hanukkah'.
