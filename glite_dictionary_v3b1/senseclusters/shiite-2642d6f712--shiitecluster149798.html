Etymology

Shi'ite comes from the Arabic word Shīʿa, which means 'followers' or 'party' and first named the people who followed Ali as the leader after Muhammad. So, a Shi'ite today is someone who belongs to the branch of Islam that sees Ali as the leader after Muhammad.
