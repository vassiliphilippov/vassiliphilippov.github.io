Etymology

corposant comes from sailors who linked the strange glowing light to a saint; it likely comes from Italian words corpo 'body' and santo 'holy', or from the name St. Elmo. So, calling that electric glow a corposant is like saying it was a 'holy body' or the saint's protective fire on a ship.
