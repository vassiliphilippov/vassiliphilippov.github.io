Etymology

capriciousness comes from the word caprice (from Italian capriccio), which meant a 'sudden change' or 'whim'. A helpful way to remember this is a popular story that links capriccio to capo ('head') and riccio ('hedgehog'), so imagine your hair suddenly standing up like a hedgehog — so capriciousness means being unpredictable and likely to change suddenly without reason.
