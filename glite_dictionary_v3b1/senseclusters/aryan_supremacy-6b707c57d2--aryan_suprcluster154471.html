Etymology

Aryan supremacy comes from 19th-century ideas when the word Aryan, used for certain ancient peoples, was turned into a claim that they were better and supremacy means being above others. That claim was later used by racist groups to say one race is best, so today the phrase means believing that Aryan people are 'superior' to other races.
