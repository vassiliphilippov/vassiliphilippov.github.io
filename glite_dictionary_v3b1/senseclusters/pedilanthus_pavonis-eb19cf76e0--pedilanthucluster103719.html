Etymology

The plant name Pedilanthus pavonis comes from old Greek and Latin words. Pedilanthus means 'slipper flower' because the bloom looks like a small slipper and pavonis means 'of the peacock', referring to its bright, showy colours, so the name helps you picture this waxy shrub with slipper-shaped, peacock-like flowers.
