Etymology

Douay-Rheims Version is named after two towns, Douay and Rheims, where English Catholic scholars lived in France while they made an English Bible from the 'Latin Vulgate'. Because they translated and printed the book there, the name came to mean the English 'translation' used by many Catholics.
