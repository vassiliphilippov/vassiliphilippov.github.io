Etymology

spaghettini comes from Italian and is related to spaghetti, which comes from spaghetto meaning 'little string'. The ending -ini makes it 'even smaller' or 'thinner', so spaghettini is the thinner kind of spaghetti and that is why it cooks faster and feels lighter.
