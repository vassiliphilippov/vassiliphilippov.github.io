Etymology

discombobulated was invented as a playful, joking opposite of a pretend verb combobulate, so it feels a little silly and made-up. A helpful way to remember it is to see dis- as 'not' and think of compose as 'calm', so discombobulated means 'confused' or 'not in control'.
