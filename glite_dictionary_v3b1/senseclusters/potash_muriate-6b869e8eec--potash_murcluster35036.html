Etymology

potash muriate comes from two old words: potash, the salt people made by pouring water through wood ashes in a pot, and muriate, a word for a salt linked to the old name 'muriatic' for a strong acid. So the name literally points to a potassium salt, which is why potash muriate means potassium chloride, a compound used in medicine and as a fertilizer.
