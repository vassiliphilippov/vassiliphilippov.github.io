Etymology

informer’s privilege paints a simple image: an informer is someone who tells the police in secret and a privilege is a special right or shield. It is like a shield that lets the government 'refuse to give' the informant's name in court to keep that person safe, so the phrase means the right to protect a secret source's identity.
