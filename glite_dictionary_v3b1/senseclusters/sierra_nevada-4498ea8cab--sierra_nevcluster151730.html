Etymology

Sierra Nevada is a Spanish name that comes from sierra meaning 'mountain range' or 'saw' and nevada meaning 'snow-covered'. Spanish explorers used this picture of white, saw-like peaks when they named the tall, snowy mountains in California, so the name fits the range you see today.
