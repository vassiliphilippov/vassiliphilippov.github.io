Etymology

Oaxacan comes from the place name Oaxaca, a region in southern Mexico, and Oaxaca itself comes from a native word that meant 'place of the huaje tree'. So, Oaxacan means 'from or about Oaxaca', which is why we use it for the region's food, art, and culture.
