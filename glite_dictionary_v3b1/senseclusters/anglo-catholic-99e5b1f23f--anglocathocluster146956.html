Etymology

Anglo-Catholic joins Anglo- ('English') and Catholic ('of the Catholic Church'). So an Anglo-Catholic is an English Anglican who keeps Catholic-style ceremonies and sacraments, and the word means support for the High Church tradition within Anglicanism.
