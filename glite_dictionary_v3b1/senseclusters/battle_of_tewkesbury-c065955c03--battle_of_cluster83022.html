Etymology

battle of Tewkesbury was the final big fight of the Wars of the Roses in 1471, fought near the town of Tewkesbury, where Edward IV defeated the Lancastrians. Because this one battle killed or captured many leading opponents, it is remembered as the moment that 'ended' major Lancastrian resistance.
