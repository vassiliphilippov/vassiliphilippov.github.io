Etymology

social ostracism may come from a long-ago practice in Greece where people wrote a name on a broken pot and voted to send that person away. That image of using a broken pot to choose someone to be 'left out' helps explain why social ostracism today means the group 'excludes' or 'leaves someone out' as a kind of punishment.
