Etymology

regular hexagon is a six-sided shape where all six sides and corners are the same; picture a honeycomb cell or a shape divided into six equal triangles to see it clearly. The word hexagon means 'six sides' and regular means 'all the same', so the name tells you it has 'equal' sides and corners.
