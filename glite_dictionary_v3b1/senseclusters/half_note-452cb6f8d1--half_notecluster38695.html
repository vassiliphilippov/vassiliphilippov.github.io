Etymology

half note comes from the simple image of a half of a note: imagine a 'whole' note cut into two equal pieces like a pie, and each piece lasts half the time. That's why a half note today means the note that lasts 'half' as long as a whole note and usually takes two 'beats' in 4/4 time.
