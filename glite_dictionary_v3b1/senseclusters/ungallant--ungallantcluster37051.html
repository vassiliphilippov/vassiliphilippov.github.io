Etymology

ungallant is un- ('not') plus gallant, a word that came to mean 'polite' and 'brave', like a courteous knight, so a helpful image is someone who lacks that polite, chivalrous manner. So ungallant means 'not polite or chivalrous', which fits rude or discourteous behavior.
