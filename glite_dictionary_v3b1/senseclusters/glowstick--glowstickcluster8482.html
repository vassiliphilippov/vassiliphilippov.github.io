Etymology

glowstick comes from the simple parts glow ('shine softly') and stick ('a small thin piece of wood or plastic'), so it is literally a 'stick' that 'glows'. You make a glowstick light by cracking or bending it to mix the liquids inside, so it gives quick, temporary light at parties and other events.
