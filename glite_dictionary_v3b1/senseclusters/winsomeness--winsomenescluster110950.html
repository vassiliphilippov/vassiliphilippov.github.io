Etymology

Winsomeness comes from the old English root wynn meaning 'joy' or 'pleasure' and the part -some meaning 'full of'. So, winsomeness is being 'full of charm' or a sweet, innocent kind of attractiveness, which is why we use it for someone who is simply delightful.
