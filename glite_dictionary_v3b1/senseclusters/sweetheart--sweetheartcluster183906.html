Etymology

sweetheart comes from the two simple words sweet and heart, meaning 'sweet to the heart' or someone pleasing to the feelings; long ago it named a loved person and later was used for a charming, attractive woman, which is why a film or story may call a glamorous woman a 'sweetheart'.
