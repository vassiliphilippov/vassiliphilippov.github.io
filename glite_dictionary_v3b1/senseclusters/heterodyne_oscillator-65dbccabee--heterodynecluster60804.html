Etymology

The noun heterodyne oscillator comes from an old radio trick called heterodyne, which is simply mixing two different signals. Imagine two musical notes played together: you hear a new slow 'beat' made from their difference, and the oscillator makes one steady signal so the mix creates new signals that are easier to tune. So a heterodyne oscillator is used to 'shift' radio signals into a range a receiver can handle.
