Etymology

bluing comes from blue and -ing, where blue means the color 'blue' and -ing shows an action or result. A helpful way to remember it is that a little blue cancels yellow and makes white look brighter, so bluing is the blue liquid or treatment added to laundry or hair to give a whiter look.
