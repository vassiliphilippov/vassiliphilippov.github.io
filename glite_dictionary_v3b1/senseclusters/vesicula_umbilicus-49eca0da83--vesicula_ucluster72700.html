Etymology

vesicula umbilicus comes from the Latin words vesicula meaning 'little sac' and umbilicus meaning 'navel'. It names a tiny sac near an embryo's navel that carries blood until the heart starts, so the phrase now means a temporary early blood-carrying structure.
