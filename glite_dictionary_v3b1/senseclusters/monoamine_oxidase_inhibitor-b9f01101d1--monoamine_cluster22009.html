Etymology

Monoamine oxidase inhibitor is a name that tells the function: monoamine are mood chemicals in the brain, oxidase is the helper that breaks those chemicals down, and an inhibitor 'stops' that helper. So, by 'stopping' the oxidase, the medicine lets mood chemicals 'increase', which is why it is used to treat depression.
