Etymology

test-tube baby comes from the literal picture of a test-tube holding an embryo and a baby whose life began in a glass tube in a lab. Doctors fertilized the egg outside the mother's body and then put the embryo into her womb, so the phrase means 'a baby conceived in a lab and then placed in the mother's body'.
