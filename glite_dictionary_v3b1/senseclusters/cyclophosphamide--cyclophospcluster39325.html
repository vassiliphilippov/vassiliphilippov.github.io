Etymology

The word cyclophosphamide is built from cyclo- meaning 'circle' (like cycle), phosph- for 'phosphorus', and -amide a chemical ending from 'ammonia'. So, the name shows it is a chemical medicine with a ring and phosphorus, which helps explain why it can slow the immune system and be used to treat autoimmune disease or prevent transplant rejection.
