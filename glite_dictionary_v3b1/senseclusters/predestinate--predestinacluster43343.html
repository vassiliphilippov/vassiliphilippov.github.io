Etymology

Predestinate as an adjective comes from pre- 'before' and destine, the same root as destiny, meaning 'fate' or 'to set', so a predestinate event is 'set before' it happens and seen as fixed ahead of time. That's why we call a role or meeting predestinate when it seems decided and cannot be changed.
