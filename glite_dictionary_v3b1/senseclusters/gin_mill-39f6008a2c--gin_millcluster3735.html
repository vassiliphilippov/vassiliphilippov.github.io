Etymology

Gin mill comes from a simple picture: gin is a strong drink and mill was used like a factory or shop that 'made' or sold gin, so the place looked like a little factory for gin. That's why gin mill came to mean a 'bar' or 'tavern' that is cheap or a bit rough.
