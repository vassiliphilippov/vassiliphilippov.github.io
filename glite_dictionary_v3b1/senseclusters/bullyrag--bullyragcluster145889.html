Etymology

The word bullyrag joins bully (a person who 'pushes or frightens' others) and rag (a verb meaning 'to tease or scold'), so you can picture a bully teasing or bossing someone. That's why to bullyrag someone today means to intimidate or harass them with constant scolding, threats, or bossiness.
