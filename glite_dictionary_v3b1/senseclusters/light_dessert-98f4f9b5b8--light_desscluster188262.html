Etymology

light dessert uses light in the simple sense 'not heavy' and dessert for the sweet food at the end of a meal. Think of a small bowl of fruit or a scoop of sorbet that feels easy on your stomach compared with a rich cake, and that's why it means a dessert that is easier to digest or lower in calories.
