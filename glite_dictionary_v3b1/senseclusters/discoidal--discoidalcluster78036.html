Etymology

discoidal comes from disc, which means 'a flat, round shape' and is like the everyday word 'disk', and the ending -oid means 'like' or 'shape of'. So discoidal simply means 'shaped like a disc', which is why we use it for flat, round things such as tablets, fossils, or some blood cells.
