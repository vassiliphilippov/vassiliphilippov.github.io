Etymology

Clout nail comes from an old word clout meaning a patch or flat piece of cloth, because the nail's wide, flat head works like a small patch to hold thin metal without tearing. That's why clout nail means 'a short nail with a large, flat head' used to attach thin sheet metal to wood.
