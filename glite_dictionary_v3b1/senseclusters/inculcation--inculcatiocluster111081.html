Etymology

inculcation comes from Latin roots in- 'into' and calcare 'to tread' or 'press down'. Think of it as 'pressing' an idea into someone's mind by repeating it, which is why inculcation means teaching or fixing an idea, attitude, or habit by constant instruction.
