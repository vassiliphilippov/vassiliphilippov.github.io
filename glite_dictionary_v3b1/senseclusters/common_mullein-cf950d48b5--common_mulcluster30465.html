Etymology

common mullein is a plant with tall, dry stalks and soft, woolly leaves. Ancient Greeks and Romans used mullein stalks as 'torches' to give 'light', so the strong picture of a burning stalk helps explain why common mullein means that tall, woolly plant you can spot in fields today.
