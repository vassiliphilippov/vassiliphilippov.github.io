Etymology

yikes on bikes likely comes from people taking the shout yikes and adding a rhyming, funny tag on bikes to make the line more dramatic; imagine someone seeing something shocking while bikes rush past and shouting even louder. That silly picture is why people use it to show 'surprise', 'alarm', or 'disbelief'.
