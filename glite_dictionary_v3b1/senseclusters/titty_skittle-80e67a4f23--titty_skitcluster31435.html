Etymology

titty skittle may come from mixing a playful word titty, meaning a cute, tiny thing, with the candy name skittle, known for small, colorful sweets. Picture a little bright tablet or bead that looks cute and tiny, and that's why the phrase means 'a small colorful sweet'.
