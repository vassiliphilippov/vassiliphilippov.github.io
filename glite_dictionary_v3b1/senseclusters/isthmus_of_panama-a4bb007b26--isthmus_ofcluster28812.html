Etymology

Isthmus of Panama comes from the word isthmus, which means a thin strip of land at Panama that acts like a 'bridge' between Central and South America and separates the Caribbean Sea from the Pacific. Picture a narrow land path with water on both sides, and that's why the phrase means a land link between two larger lands.
