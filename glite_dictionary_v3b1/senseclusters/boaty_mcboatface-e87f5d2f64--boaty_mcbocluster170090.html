Etymology

Boaty McBoatface came from a 2016 public online vote in Britain, when someone jokingly put the silly words Boaty and McBoatface together and the name got a lot of attention online. That's why people now use 'Boaty McBoatface' as a funny name for a boat or other vehicle.
