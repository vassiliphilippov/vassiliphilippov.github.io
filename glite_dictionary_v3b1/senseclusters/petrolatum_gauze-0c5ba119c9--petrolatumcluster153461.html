Etymology

petrolatum gauze comes from the simple idea of thin gauze cloth soaked with petrolatum, a greasy jelly that stops the dressing from sticking to the skin. Because the cloth is greasy, it 'protects' the wound and 'keeps the area moist', so the term means a dressing used to protect wounds and help them heal.
