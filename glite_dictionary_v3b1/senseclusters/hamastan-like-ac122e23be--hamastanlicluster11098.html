Etymology

The word Hamastan-like comes from the name Hamas, the suffix -stan meaning 'land' as in Pakistan, and the English ending -like meaning 'similar to', so it gives the image of 'Hamas-land' and means a place that looks politically or socially like an area controlled by Hamas.
