Etymology

widow-maker trail comes from the old phrase widow-maker, used for things that can kill you—like a heavy dead branch that falls or a sudden steep drop. People began to call a very dangerous path a widow-maker trail because it could leave a spouse alone, so today it means a trail that is especially dangerous and could cause someone 'to die'.
