Etymology

buffer zone The noun buffer zone comes from the idea of a soft buffer, a cushion or space, put between two zones or groups. Imagine a strip of trees or empty land that keeps two sides apart and so 'reduces conflict' and 'protects' them, which is why a buffer zone means an area that 'separates' and 'protects'.
