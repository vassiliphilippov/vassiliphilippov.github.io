Etymology

drilling mud is a very literal compound: drilling (making a deep hole) and mud (a thick watery mix). Workers pump this muddy liquid down the hole to cool the drill, carry out pieces of rock, and keep the hole walls open, so today drilling mud means the special fluid used to help and protect the drill and the well.
