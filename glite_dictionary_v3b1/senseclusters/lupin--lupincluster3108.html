Etymology

lupin comes from the Latin lupus, which means 'wolf'. People once called the plant a 'wolf's bean'—one idea says this was because its seeds are bitter or because it seemed to take food from the soil—and that name stuck, so we still call these tall, colorful flowers lupin today.
