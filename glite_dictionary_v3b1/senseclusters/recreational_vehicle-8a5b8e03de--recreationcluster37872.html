Etymology

recreational vehicle gives an easy image: imagine a car with a bed and a small kitchen inside for fun road trips. The words recreational ('for fun') and vehicle ('a road vehicle') explain why it means 'a large motor vehicle with living space and amenities for travel and camping'.
