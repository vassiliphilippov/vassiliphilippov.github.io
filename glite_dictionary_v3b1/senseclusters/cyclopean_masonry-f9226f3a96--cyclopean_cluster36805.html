Etymology

The term cyclopean masonry comes from cyclopean, a name linked to the Cyclopes, the giant one-eyed creatures in old stories. People thought only giants could move the huge, rough stones used in masonry walls, so the phrase now means 'walls made of very large, irregular stones fitted together without mortar'.
