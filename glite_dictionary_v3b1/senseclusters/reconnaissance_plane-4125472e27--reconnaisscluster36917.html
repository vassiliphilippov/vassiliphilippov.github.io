Etymology

reconnaissance plane comes from the French word reconnaissance meaning 'to look closely' and the word plane for the aircraft. Imagine a plane flying high like an eye in the sky, watching the ground to find and report 'information' about enemy positions, so the name means a plane used to gather information.
