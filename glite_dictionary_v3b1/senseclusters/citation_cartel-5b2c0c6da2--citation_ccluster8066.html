Etymology

citation cartel comes from the image of a small group of journals or researchers who quietly agree to cite each other's papers to raise their citation numbers. Citation means a reference in a paper and cartel is a group that makes secret deals, so the phrase means 'a group that cheats by swapping citations to make their work look more important'.
