Etymology

crotch dumpling may come from a funny image of a small child or pet sitting in someone's lap like a soft dumpling at the crotch, the place where the legs meet. That's why it is used as a playful name for someone who is cuddly and a little 'awkward' or for a 'small child on a lap'.
