Etymology

Litmus paper is thin paper covered with litmus, a dye that changes color when you dip it into a liquid. Picture a small strip turning red in vinegar or blue in soapy water. That clear color change is the memory hook, so litmus paper is used to tell if something is 'acid' or 'alkaline'.
