Etymology

Harkat-ul-Mujahidin comes from Arabic words used in Urdu; Harkat means 'movement', al- means 'the', and Mujahidin means 'fighters' or 'those who struggle'. So the name literally says 'movement of the fighters', which explains why it is used for an armed militant group.
