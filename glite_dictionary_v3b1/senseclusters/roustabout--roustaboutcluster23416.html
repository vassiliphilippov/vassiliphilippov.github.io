Etymology

roustabout comes from the two parts roust and about, where roust (like the common word rouse) means 'to wake or force to move' and about means 'around'. So a roustabout is someone who is made to move around to do hard, physical work, which is why today it means an unskilled worker on ships, in circuses, or at oil fields.
