Etymology

The name Lepisma saccharina may come from old words: Lepisma means 'scale' and saccharina means 'sugar'. This gives a picture of a small, shiny bug with tiny scales that likes sweet or starchy things, which is why the name is used for the house insect people call 'silverfish'.
