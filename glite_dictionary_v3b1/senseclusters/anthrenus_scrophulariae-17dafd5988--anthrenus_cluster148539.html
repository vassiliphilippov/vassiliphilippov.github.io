Etymology

Anthrenus scrophulariae may come from old naming habits that link insects to plants. The first part, Anthrenus, marks a small round beetle and the second part, scrophulariae, points to the plant 'Scrophularia' where it was often found, so the name helps you picture this tiny carpet beetle that can damage natural fibres in houses and museums.
