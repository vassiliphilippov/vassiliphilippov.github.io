Etymology

The word run-of-the-mine is a variation of the familiar phrase run-of-the-mill. In that phrase, run means what a place produces regularly and a mill was a place that made simple everyday goods. So a run-of-the-mine is like ore that is common, which is why it means 'ordinary' or 'not special'.
