Etymology

fundamental frequency comes from the simple image of a vibrating string: the lowest and strongest vibration—the fundamental—gives the main tone, and frequency is how often the string moves back and forth. So, fundamental frequency is the lowest or main 'frequency' that decides a sound's 'pitch'.
