Etymology

gaucheness comes from the French word gauche, which means 'left'. Long ago, being 'left' was often seen as clumsy, so gauche came to mean 'awkward', and the ending -ness makes it the 'quality' of being awkward. So gaucheness today means being socially clumsy or lacking grace.
