Etymology

overwhelm comes from over- meaning 'too much' and the old word whelm meaning 'to cover' or 'sink', giving the image of a wave of feeling washing over you. So, to be overwhelmed by grief or kindness is to be 'covered' by strong emotions and unable to think clearly.
