Etymology

Oleoresin capsicum comes from words that mean oleo (oil), resin (a sticky substance), and capsicum (pepper). So it describes the oily, sticky extract of peppers that carries the pepper's heat, which is why people use it both as a strong seasoning and in pepper spray to 'irritate' the eyes and skin.
