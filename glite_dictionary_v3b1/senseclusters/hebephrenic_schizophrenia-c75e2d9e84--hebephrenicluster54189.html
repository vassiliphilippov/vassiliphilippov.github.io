Etymology

hebephrenic schizophrenia may come from old Greek words: hebe meaning 'youth' and phren meaning 'mind'. So the name gives the image of a 'young mind' acting childlike and mixed up, which is why it describes the kind of schizophrenia that often starts in the teenage years and includes childish or inappropriate behaviour.
