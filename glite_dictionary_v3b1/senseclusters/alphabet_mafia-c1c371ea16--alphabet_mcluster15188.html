Etymology

The phrase alphabet mafia mixes two clear images: alphabet for the many letters used for LGBTQ+ groups, and mafia for a secret, powerful gang. People started using it as a joke or to criticise the idea that those groups have hidden 'influence', so today it means a humorous or critical name for an imagined powerful LGBTQ+ 'group' in media, politics, or business.
