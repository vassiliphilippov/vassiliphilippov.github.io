Etymology

Meuse-Argonne Offensive gets its name from the two places where the fighting happened: the Meuse River and the Argonne Forest, and 'offensive' means a big 'attack'. So the name refers to the major American-led 1918 campaign that pushed German armies back and helped end the war.
