Etymology

The word polynuclear breaks into poly- meaning 'many' and nuclear from nucleus meaning 'core' or 'center', as in polygon for many sides or nuclear energy for a central part. So polynuclear literally means 'many cores', which is why it describes something with more than one nucleus or central part.
