Etymology

disenfranchisement comes from the parts dis- 'not' and franchise 'a right', and franchise is related to the idea of being 'free', so a helpful way to remember it is to think of someone losing their 'franchise' or freedom to take part. So disenfranchisement means the act of taking away someone's rights, especially the right to vote.
