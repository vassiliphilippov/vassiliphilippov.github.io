Etymology

yakuza comes from a Japanese name for a losing card hand made of the parts ya- '8', ku- '9' and za '3'. Gamblers with that bad hand were called 'yakuza', and over time the name moved from those gamblers to the criminal groups, so yakuza now means a member of Japanese organized crime.
