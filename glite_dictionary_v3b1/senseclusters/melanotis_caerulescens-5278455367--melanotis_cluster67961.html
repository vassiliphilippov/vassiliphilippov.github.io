Melanotis caerulescens may come from old Greek and Latin words: Melanotis meaning 'black-eared' and caerulescens meaning 'bluish'. Imagine a bird with dark patches by its ears and bright blue feathers, and that image explains the name and helps you remember this blue Mexican mockingbird.
