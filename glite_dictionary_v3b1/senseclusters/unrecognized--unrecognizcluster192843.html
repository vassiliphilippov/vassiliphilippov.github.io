Etymology

unrecognized begins with un- 'not' and comes from recognize, which once meant 'to know again' as re- means 'again' and the rest means 'to know'. So, in this sense, someone unrecognized is 'not seen as someone people know' or 'not identified', for example when a person in disguise is not noticed by fans.
