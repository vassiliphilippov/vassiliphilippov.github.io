Etymology

trojan horse comes from an old story: Greek soldiers hid inside a big wooden horse that looked like a gift, and the people of Troy brought it inside their city walls. That's why a 'trojan horse' today means something that looks helpful but actually hides danger, like a program that secretly harms your computer.
