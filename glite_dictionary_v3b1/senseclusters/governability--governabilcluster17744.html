Etymology

governability comes from the verb govern plus the parts -able and -ity. The verb govern goes back to an old Latin word gubernare meaning 'to steer', like steering a ship, and the same root appears in government and governor. So governability is the quality of how easily a country or system can be 'steered' or managed by those in charge.
