Etymology

Mostaccioli comes from Italian mosto, which means 'fresh grape juice' or 'must', because the name was first used for foods made with grape must or sweet sauces. That's why mostaccioli became the name for a small, tube-shaped pasta often used in baked dishes with rich sauce.
