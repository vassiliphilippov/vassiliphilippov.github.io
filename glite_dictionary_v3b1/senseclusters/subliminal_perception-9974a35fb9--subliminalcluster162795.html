Etymology

subliminal perception comes from older parts sub (meaning 'under') and limen (meaning 'threshold' or 'door'). Imagine a message staying below your mind's door so you do not notice it, but your brain still reacts, and that's why it means 'not noticing something but still reacting to it'.
