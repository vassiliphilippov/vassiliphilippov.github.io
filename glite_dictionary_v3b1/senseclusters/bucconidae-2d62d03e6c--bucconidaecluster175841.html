Etymology

Bucconidae comes from the genus name Bucco, which itself comes from the Latin bucca meaning 'cheek' or 'mouth'. Because these birds look puffy around the head and bill, the name helps you remember that Bucconidae are the puffbirds with big, fluffy faces.
