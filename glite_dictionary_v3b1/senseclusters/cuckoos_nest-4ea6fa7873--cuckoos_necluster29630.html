Etymology

cuckoo's nest may come from calling someone a cuckoo because the bird's name was used to mean 'crazy', and nest meant the place where they lived. The picture of a nest of cuckoos became a way to talk about a place for people seen as 'crazy', so the phrase came to mean 'a psychiatric hospital' and is now often seen as rude.
