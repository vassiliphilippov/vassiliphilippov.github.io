Etymology

The noun supple willow comes from the image of willow branches that bend easily without breaking; the word supple means 'bendy' and the willow is the tree, and basket makers used these soft branches for weaving. That's why supple willow means a willow or branch that is 'flexible' and good for bending into shapes.
