Etymology

Algonkin language gets its name from the Algonkin people who lived along rivers in parts of Canada and the northeastern United States. One popular story says the name may come from a word used by nearby groups to mean 'those who speak a different language', so today Algonkin language simply means the language spoken by the Algonkin people.
