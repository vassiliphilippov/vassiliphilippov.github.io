Etymology

Greeting card comes from the simple idea of a card that carries a greeting, a picture and a short written note sent for a special day. Because these small cards were used to send 'good wishes' or kind words, the term now means a decorated card sent to share a personal message on holidays, birthdays, or other occasions.
