Etymology

The term peritoneal cavity names a small 'space' inside the belly between two thin linings called the peritoneum, and the word cavity simply means a hollow or 'space'. Think of it like a folded bag with a little slippery fluid so the organs can 'move' easily; that's why it means 'the small space in the abdomen that contains a little fluid to help organs move'.
