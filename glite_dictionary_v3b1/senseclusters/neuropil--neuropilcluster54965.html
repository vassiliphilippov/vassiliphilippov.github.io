Etymology

neuropil combines neuro-, the same root as neuron, meaning 'nerve', and -pil, a part that suggests a 'dense mass' or 'felt' of tiny threads, so think of it as a 'felt' of nerve threads, which matches its meaning as a dense network in the brain.
