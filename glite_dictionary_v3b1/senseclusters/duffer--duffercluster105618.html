Etymology

The word duffer probably comes from an older word duff or the verb to duff, which once meant 'to hit badly' or 'worthless stuff'. A helpful way to remember it is to picture someone who 'duffs' a shot and keeps missing, so a duffer today means a clumsy or incompetent person, often in sport.
