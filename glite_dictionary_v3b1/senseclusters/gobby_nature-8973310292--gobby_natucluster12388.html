Etymology

The phrase gobby nature may come from the old word 'gob' meaning 'mouth', and the word gobby came to mean 'big-mouthed'. Imagine a person with a big 'gob' who talks loudly and often; that image explains why gobby nature means 'talkative, outspoken, and often loud'.
