Etymology

Populus trichocarpa is a scientific name made from the parts Populus, the poplar tree, and trichocarpa, which means 'hairy fruit' because the tree's seed pods are full of fine hairs that float like cotton. So the name gives a clear picture of a poplar with fluffy seeds and explains why scientists use that name today.
