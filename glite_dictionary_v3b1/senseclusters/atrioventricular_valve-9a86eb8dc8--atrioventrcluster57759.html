Etymology

The noun atrioventricular valve names a small gate between the heart's upper and lower chambers: atrioventricular links atrium, the upper chamber, and ventricle, the lower chamber, and valve means a gate. This gate lets blood move from the upper chamber to the lower one but stops it going back, so the name helps you remember it 'lets blood flow one way'.
