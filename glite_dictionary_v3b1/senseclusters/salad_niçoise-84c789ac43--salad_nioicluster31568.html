Etymology

salad Niçoise comes from the town of Nice in France, where fishermen made a simple salad using local fish like tuna and anchovies, with olives and hard-boiled eggs. That's why today 'salad Niçoise' usually means a cold salad with tuna or anchovies, olives, tomatoes and eggs dressed in olive oil.
