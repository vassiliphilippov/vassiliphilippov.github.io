Etymology

implicate in its criminal sense comes from the Latin in- ('in') and plicare ('to fold'). Imagine folding a person into a story or plan: to implicate someone is to 'fold them in' to the wrongdoing, which is why the word means to show or suggest their involvement. You can also see the same root in complicate or duplicate, where things are 'folded' together.
