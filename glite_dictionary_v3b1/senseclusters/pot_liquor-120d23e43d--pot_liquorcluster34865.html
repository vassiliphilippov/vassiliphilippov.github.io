Etymology

pot liquor comes from the simple kitchen picture of the tasty liquid left in a cooking pot, where liquor once meant 'liquid' or 'broth'. People would save that flavorful liquid to make 'soup' or add taste, so pot liquor now means the cooking liquid used for flavor and nutrients.
