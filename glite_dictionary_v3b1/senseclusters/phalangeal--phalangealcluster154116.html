Etymology

phalangeal comes from the old Greek word phalanx, which meant a 'line of soldiers' and was also used for a 'finger'. So phalangeal means 'relating to the phalanges', the bones of the fingers or toes, and you can remember it by picturing those bones as soldiers standing in a row.
