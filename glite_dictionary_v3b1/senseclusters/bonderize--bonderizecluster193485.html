Etymology

bonderize comes from a trade name for a phosphate coating process and it sounds like the common word bond, because the treatment makes a tight, protective layer that 'bonds' to metal. So, to bonderize is to coat metal with a phosphate solution to 'prevent rust' and help it last longer.
