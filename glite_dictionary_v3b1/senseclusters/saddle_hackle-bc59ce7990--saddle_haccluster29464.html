Etymology

saddle hackle comes from the long, narrow feathers on a rooster or hen: the word saddle means the bird's 'back' and hackle means the shiny 'feathers' in that area. People who make fishing flies and crafters used those feathers, so saddle hackle now means those long back feathers used for tying flies and decoration.
