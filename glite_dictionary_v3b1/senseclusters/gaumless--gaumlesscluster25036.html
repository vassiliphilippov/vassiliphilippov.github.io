Etymology

The word gaumless likely comes from an old northern English word gaum meaning 'to stare' or 'be foolish', plus the ending -less meaning 'without'. A helpful way to remember it is to think of someone who gawks and seems 'without' sense or energy, which is why gaumless means slow-witted or sluggish.
