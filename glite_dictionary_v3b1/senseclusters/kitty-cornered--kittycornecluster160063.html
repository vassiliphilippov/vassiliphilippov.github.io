Etymology

kitty-cornered comes from an older phrase cater-corner (also heard as catty-corner), where the cater part comes from an old word for 'four'. Imagine a square with four corners — things on the opposite corner are 'diagonally across', so kitty-cornered means 'diagonally across'.
