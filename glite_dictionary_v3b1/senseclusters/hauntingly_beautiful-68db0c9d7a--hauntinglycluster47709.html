Etymology

hauntingly beautiful comes from the image of something like a ghost or a memory that stays in your mind. The word haunting suggests a ghost or memory that 'stays in your mind', and beautiful means lovely, so together the phrase means 'so lovely and strange that it keeps coming back to you and feels a little sad or mysterious'.
