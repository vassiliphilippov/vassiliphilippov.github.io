Etymology

Purkinje's tissue is named after a scientist called Purkinje who found special thin fibers in the heart. Imagine them as fast wires that carry the heart's electrical signal. That's why Purkinje's tissue now means the network of fibers that send signals to make the lower chambers called 'ventricles' squeeze.
