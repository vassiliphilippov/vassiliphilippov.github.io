raised hand

0.02/m2.5
Lifting a hand to show you want to speak or answer
noun US / ˈreɪzd hænd /

Examples

A raised hand signal
During the vote, several students showed their support with a raised hand.
If you have a question, please signal with a raised hand rather than speaking out.
The teacher looked around and called on the student with the raised hand.
Can I see a raised hand if you agree?
The instructor noticed a raised hand in the back of the room.
The teacher encouraged everyone to keep a raised hand if they had something to say.
He didn't see any raised hands in the audience.
The raised hand signaled that she wanted to contribute.
Did anyone else see a raised hand during the meeting?
A raised hand can indicate a desire to speak.
I saw a raised hand from the back of the class.
The raised hands showed the level of agreement among participants.

Etymology

Raised hand comes from classrooms and meetings where a person lifts their hand into the air to show they want 'to speak', 'to answer', or 'to vote'. So, the clear picture of a lifted hand explains why a raised hand means a signal that someone wants to speak or show agreement.
