Etymology

Pedilanthus tithymaloides gets its name from words that picture the flower as a little shoe: Pedilanthus is from a word for 'slipper' because the bloom looks like a tiny shoe, and tithymaloides means 'like Tithymalus', a similar plant. So, the name helps you remember the plant's zigzag stems and bright, shoe-shaped flowers.
