Etymology

blackdamp comes from the common words black ('dark') and damp ('wet' or 'moist'). Miners used it for a dark, heavy air in a mine that feels like wet fog and has no oxygen to keep people alive, so blackdamp means the deadly mine air that can make people stop breathing.
