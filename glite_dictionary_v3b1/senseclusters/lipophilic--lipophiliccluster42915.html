Etymology

lipophilic comes from Greek parts lipo- meaning 'fat' and -philic meaning 'loving'. It literally means 'fat-loving', so a lipophilic substance likes fats and can mix with or stay in fatty tissue, which is why some vitamins and drugs collect in body fat and can pass through cell membranes.
