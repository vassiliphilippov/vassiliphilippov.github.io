Etymology

operational damage comes from the image of a tank or vehicle being hurt while out on a mission: operational means 'during an operation' and damage means 'hurt to equipment'. So, operational damage means the loss or hurt that happens to equipment while it is used in the field, not from accidents or when it is being fixed.
