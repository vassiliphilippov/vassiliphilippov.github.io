Etymology

The word perceivable comes from the Latin verb percipere, made of per- 'completely' and capere 'to take', and it shares that 'take' root with words like capture. So, something perceivable is something you can 'take in' with your eyes, ears, or mind, which is why it means 'able to be noticed'.
