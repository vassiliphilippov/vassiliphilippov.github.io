Etymology

Asmara has a local story that its name comes from the Tigrinya phrase Arba'at Asmara, which is often said to mean 'the four joined' after four villages came together. So you can remember Asmara as 'the place that joined', which helps explain why it grew into one city and is now the capital of Eritrea.
