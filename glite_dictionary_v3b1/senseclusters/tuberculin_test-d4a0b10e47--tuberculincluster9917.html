Etymology

tuberculin test is named after tuberculin, a substance made from the germs that cause tuberculosis, and the word test. Doctors inject a little tuberculin under the skin to see a small bump or 'reaction', and that 'reaction' shows if someone has been infected with 'tuberculosis'.
