Etymology

absurd comes from Latin parts ab- 'away from' and surdus 'deaf', and the root surdus is like the English word deaf. It first meant 'out of tune' or 'not in harmony', so we now use absurd to describe something 'ridiculous' or wildly 'unreasonable'.
