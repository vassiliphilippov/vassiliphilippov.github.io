Etymology

obsessivity comes from Latin parts ob- and sedere, where sedere means 'to sit' and ob- adds the idea 'against', so obsess once meant 'to sit on' or 'press upon' someone. That's why obsessivity describes a strong habit of thinking about one idea, as if it keeps 'sitting on' your mind.
