Etymology

hotchpotch comes from an old French word hochepot, meaning 'shaken pot' and from hoche 'to shake' plus pot 'pot'. It originally named a mixed dish of meat and vegetables, so imagining a pot being stirred and shaken helps you remember it means a thick soup or stew.
