Etymology

Piper cubeba may come from its parts: Piper is the old Latin word for 'pepper' and cubeba was a trader's word for a 'small berry'. Because the little fruits look and taste like pepper, traders called them that and the name stuck, so today Piper cubeba refers to the pepper-like spice made from those berries.
