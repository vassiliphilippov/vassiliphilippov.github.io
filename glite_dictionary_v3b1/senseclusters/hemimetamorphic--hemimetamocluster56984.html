Etymology

The word hemimetamorphic comes from Greek parts hemi- meaning 'half' and metamorphosis meaning 'change of form'. So a hemimetamorphic insect goes through only a 'half' change from young to adult, not the big change you see in a butterfly, which is why the young often live in water and do not look like the adults.
