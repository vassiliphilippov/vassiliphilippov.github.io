Etymology

order Hypermastigina comes from old Greek words: hyper meaning 'many' and mastigina meaning 'whip', because these tiny single-celled organisms have many whip-like tails that help them move. That's why the name describes a group of small organisms that use these whips to move and to help wood-eating insects digest their food.
