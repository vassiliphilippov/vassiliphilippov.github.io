Etymology

place-kicking comes from the two simple actions you do: you place the ball, which means 'to put' it on the ground, and you kick it, which means 'to strike' it with your foot. So place-kicking literally names the sport action of kicking a ball after it has been put on the ground, used to score goals or extra points.
