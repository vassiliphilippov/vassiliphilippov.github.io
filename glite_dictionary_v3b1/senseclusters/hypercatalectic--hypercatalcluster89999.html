Etymology

Hypercatalectic breaks into hyper- and catalectic, where hyper- means 'too much', as in hyperactive, and catalectic is a poetry term meaning 'missing its last beat'. So a hypercatalectic line has 'too many' beats, an extra syllable at the end, which makes the line feel longer or gives extra emphasis.
