Etymology

St Patrick's Day may come from a story where Saint Patrick used a small three-leaf plant called the shamrock to explain the idea of three persons in one God. Because of that image, people began to wear the shamrock and green on his day, and today St Patrick's Day is a time to celebrate Irish people and culture with parades and parties.
