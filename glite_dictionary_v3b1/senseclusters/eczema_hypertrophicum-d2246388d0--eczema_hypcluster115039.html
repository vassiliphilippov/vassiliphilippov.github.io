Etymology

The term eczema hypertrophicum joins eczema, meaning red, itchy skin, with hypertrophicum, a word that means 'too much growth'. So the name makes a clear picture: eczema where the skin has become thick and shows deep skin lines, which is why it means a 'thickened' kind of eczema.
