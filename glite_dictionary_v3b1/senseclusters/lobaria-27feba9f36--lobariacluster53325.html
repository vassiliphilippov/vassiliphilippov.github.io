Etymology

Lobaria comes from the Latin root lobus, which means 'lobe' or 'a rounded leaf-like part' and points to the lichen's leaf-like, lobed shape. That's why Lobaria are called leafy lichens you can find on old trees, and why their presence helps show a forest's health.
