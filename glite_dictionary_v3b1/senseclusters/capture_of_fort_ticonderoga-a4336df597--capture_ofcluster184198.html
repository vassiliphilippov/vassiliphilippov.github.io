Etymology

Capture of Fort Ticonderoga refers to a famous 1775 event when American soldiers surprised and took the British fort at Ticonderoga, taking its guns and supplies. The word capture here means 'to take by force', so the image of men carrying away cannons helps explain why the action is called a capture.
