Etymology

The noun bulb syringe comes from how the tool looks and works: a soft rubber bulb and a narrow syringe tip. You squeeze the bulb, put the tip in the nose or ear, then release to 'suck' fluids out or squeeze again to 'push' water in, so the name reminds us it moves liquid in or out.
