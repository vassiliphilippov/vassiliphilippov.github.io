Etymology

Proboscidea louisianica comes from the part Proboscidea, a word meaning 'long nose', and louisianica, meaning 'from Louisiana', because its long, curved seed pods look like a beak or a claw. That's why people often call it 'devil's claw' and why the name fits the plant today.
