Etymology

rattling comes from the verb rattle, a word that copies the quick, sharp sound of things hitting each other, like coins in a tin or a baby's rattle. Because that sound feels strong and lively, people began to use rattling to mean 'very' or 'really' in friendly speech, so a 'rattling good' meal is a really good meal.
