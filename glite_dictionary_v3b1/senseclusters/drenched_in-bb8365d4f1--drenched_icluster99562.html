Etymology

drenched in comes from imagining something soaked in water, like clothes left out in heavy rain. The word drenched means soaked and in shows what covers it, so when we say a voice is drenched in emotion or a city drenched in color, we mean it is 'completely covered' or 'full of' that quality.
