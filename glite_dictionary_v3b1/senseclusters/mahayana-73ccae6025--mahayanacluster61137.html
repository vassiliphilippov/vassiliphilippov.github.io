Etymology

Mahayana comes from the Sanskrit parts maha meaning 'great' and yana meaning 'vehicle' or 'way', so it literally means 'Great Vehicle'. That's why Mahayana Buddhism teaches a wide path meant to carry many people to become enlightened through compassion and helping others.
