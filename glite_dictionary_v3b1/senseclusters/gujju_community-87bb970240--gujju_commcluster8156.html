Etymology

Gujju community comes from a friendly short form Gujju for people from Gujarat, India, and community meaning a group of people. People used Gujju in everyday speech, so groups of Gujaratis abroad became called the Gujju community, which is why it means 'people from Gujarat who live in other places'.
