Etymology

hiya doin' comes from people shortening 'how are you doing?' into a quick, friendly question, with hiya as a casual 'hi' for 'you' and doin' for 'doing'. Picture someone leaning in and asking hiya doin'?, and you get why it now simply means 'how are you?'.
