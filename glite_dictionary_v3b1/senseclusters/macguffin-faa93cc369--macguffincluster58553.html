Etymology

MacGuffin was made famous by director Alfred Hitchcock as a playful name for any object or goal that starts a story and makes people act. Think of it like a pretend Scottish name with parts Mac- and guffin for something everyone chases, so a MacGuffin is a thing that 'keeps the plot moving' even though the thing itself often has little real importance.
