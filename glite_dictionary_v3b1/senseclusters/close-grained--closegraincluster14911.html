Etymology

close-grained combines close (meaning 'near' or 'tight') and grain (meaning 'small particles' or 'fibers'), so it gives a clear picture of tiny parts packed together. That's why close-grained describes wood or stone whose grains are very near each other, making it dense and smooth.
