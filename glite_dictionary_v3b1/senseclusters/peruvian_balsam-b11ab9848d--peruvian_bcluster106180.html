Etymology

Peruvian balsam comes from a tree in Peru and nearby lands whose bark is cut to collect a sticky, fragrant 'sap'. Because this sweet-smelling sap was used by healers and in 'perfume' and 'medicine', the name now means the aromatic substance taken from that tree.
