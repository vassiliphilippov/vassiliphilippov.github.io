Etymology

classical hemophilia gets its name from Greek parts haima (meaning 'blood') and philia (meaning 'love' or 'fondness'), so the word literally points to a strong link with blood. 'Classical' was added to mark the original, common form that runs in families and is now called hemophilia A, so the name helps remember that this is a lifelong bleeding disorder caused by a missing blood protein.
