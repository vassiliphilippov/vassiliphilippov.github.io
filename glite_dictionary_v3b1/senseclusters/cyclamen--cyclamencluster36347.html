Etymology

The word cyclamen comes from the Greek root kyklos, the same root as cycle, and means 'circle' because the plant grows from a round underground tuber. So, thinking of a small 'circle' under the soil helps you remember cyclamen as the houseplant with round leaves and nodding flowers.
