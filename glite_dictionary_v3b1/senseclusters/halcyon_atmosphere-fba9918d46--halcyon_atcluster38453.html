Etymology

The phrase halcyon atmosphere comes from an ancient Greek story about the halcyon (a small sea bird) that calmed the sea while it made a nest, creating quiet, sunny 'halcyon days'. Because those days were calm and safe, people used 'halcyon' to mean 'peaceful', so halcyon atmosphere now means a calm, peaceful place.
