Etymology

The noun summer squash comes from how people harvest and eat this vegetable: farmers pick the soft, young squash during the warm summer months. Because these fruits are 'picked while young' and usually 'eaten fresh' instead of stored for winter, the name now means a type of squash eaten while still soft.
