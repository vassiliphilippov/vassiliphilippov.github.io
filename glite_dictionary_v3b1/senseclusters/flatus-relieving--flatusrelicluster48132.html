Etymology

flatus-relieving begins with the Latin flatus, which means 'a blow' or 'breath' and came to mean 'gas', and the second part relieving means 'making less' or 'easing'. So flatus-relieving describes a medicine or treatment that helps gas leave the body and reduces the pain or bloating it causes.
