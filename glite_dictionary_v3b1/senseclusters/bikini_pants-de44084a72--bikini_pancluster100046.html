Etymology

bikini pants: The name bikini pants comes from the small two-piece swimsuit called the bikini. Because these pants look like the swimsuit's lower piece, the 'bikini bottom', people started using the name for small, tight underpants, so it means 'small, tight-fitting underwear similar to a bikini bottom'.
