Etymology

The phrase consent decree comes from two words: consent (an 'agreement') and decree (a judge's 'order'). Imagine two sides shaking hands and a judge signing their deal so it becomes a court order everyone must follow. So, consent decree means an 'agreement' the court approves and enforces, often 'without admitting guilt'.
