Etymology

Shitposty remark comes from the internet slang shitpost, made from shit meaning 'rubbish' and post meaning 'online message'. Imagine someone throwing a messy, silly message into a chat just to get a reaction, so shitposty remark means 'a deliberately silly or provocative comment'.
