Etymology

The phrasal verb connect with comes from the simple image of connect meaning 'to join' and with meaning 'together', like a bridge or a cable that lets ideas and feelings travel between people. So, when you connect with someone you understand or feel close to them, which is why it means 'to feel close to someone'.
