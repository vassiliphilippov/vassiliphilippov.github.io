Etymology

Fraxinus quadrangulata comes from Latin words: Fraxinus means 'ash' and quadrangulata means 'four-angled', because the young twigs can look almost square. That's why the name fits this tree often called the blue ash, known for its blue-tinged bark and very hard brown wood.
