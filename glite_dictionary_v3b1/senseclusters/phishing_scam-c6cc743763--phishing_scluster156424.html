Etymology

phishing scam comes from the idea of phishing being like fishing: a fisherman uses bait on a hook to catch fish, and a scammer sends fake emails or messages as bait to 'catch' people and their passwords. That's why phishing scams mean 'fake messages that try to steal personal information'.
