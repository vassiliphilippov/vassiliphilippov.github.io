Etymology

Nepotistically comes from the root nepot-, from Latin nepos meaning 'nephew', and is related to the English word nephew. It comes from a time when people in power often gave jobs to family, so to act nepotistically is to show 'favoritism to relatives' by giving them jobs or chances.
