Etymology

brewer's yeast comes from the simple idea that brewers used this special yeast to make beer. Because this tiny yeast turns sugar into alcohol and bubbles, people began to call it 'brewer's yeast', and that is why the name now means a yeast used for brewing, baking, or as a supplement.
