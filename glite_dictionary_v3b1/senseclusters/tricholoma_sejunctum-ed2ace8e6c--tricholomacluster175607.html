Etymology

Tricholoma sejunctum may come from old Greek and Latin parts that describe the mushroom's look: tricho- means 'hair', -loma means 'edge', and sejunctum means 'separated'. This fits the dark, hair-like center and the yellow, more separated edges of the cap, so the name helps you picture the mushroom.
