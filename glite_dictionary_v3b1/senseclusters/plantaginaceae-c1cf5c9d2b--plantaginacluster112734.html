Etymology

Plantaginaceae comes from the plant name Plantago, English 'plantain', and the ending -aceae, which names plant families. Plantago comes from a Latin word meaning 'sole' because the leaves are flat like the bottom of a foot. So Plantaginaceae means the 'plantain family', which fits because it groups the small herbs and common weeds called plantains.
