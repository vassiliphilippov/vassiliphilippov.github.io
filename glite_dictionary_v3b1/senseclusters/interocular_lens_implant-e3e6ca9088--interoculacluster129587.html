Etymology

interocular lens implant comes from its parts: interocular meaning 'inside the eye', lens meaning a clear piece that focuses light, and implant meaning something put into the body. It is a clear artificial lens put into the eye to replace a cloudy natural lens, so the phrase means 'an artificial lens placed inside the eye' to restore sight.
