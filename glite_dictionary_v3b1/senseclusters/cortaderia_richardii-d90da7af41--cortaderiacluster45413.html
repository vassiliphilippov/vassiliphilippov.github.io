Etymology

Cortaderia richardii may come from the Spanish word Cortaderia linked to 'to cut' because the grass has long, sharp leaves, and the second part, richardii, was 'named for' a person called Richard who studied plants. That's why the name fits a tall, garden grass with feather-like plumes.
