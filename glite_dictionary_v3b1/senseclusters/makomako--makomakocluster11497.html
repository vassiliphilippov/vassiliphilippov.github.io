Etymology

makomako comes from Māori and is the native name for a New Zealand shrub whose many berries were traditionally made into wine. A helpful way to remember it is the repeated mako in the name, like the clusters of small berries or the birds that eat them, so the word brings to mind this berry-bearing tree.
