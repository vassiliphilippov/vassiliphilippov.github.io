Etymology

apoplectic outburst comes from the medical word apoplectic, once used for someone having a stroke who looked very red and rigid, and the word outburst, meaning a sudden loud explosion of feeling. So the picture is of a person so angry they seem to explode or lose control, and that is why it means 'a sudden, extreme burst of anger'.
