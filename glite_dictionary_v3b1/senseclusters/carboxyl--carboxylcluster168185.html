Etymology

carboxyl comes from the parts carbo- 'carbon', ox- 'oxygen', and the ending -yl 'group'. So used as an adjective, carboxyl means 'having' or 'relating to' the carbon-and-oxygen group -COOH, which changes a molecule's behavior, often making it more acidic.
