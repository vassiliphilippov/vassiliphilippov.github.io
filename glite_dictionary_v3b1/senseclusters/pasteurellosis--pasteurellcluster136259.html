Etymology

pasteurellosis comes from the name Pasteur, the scientist Louis Pasteur, plus the ending -osis meaning 'disease'. You can also see Pasteur's name in the word pasteurize, so pasteurellosis means the 'disease' caused by the Pasteurella bacteria.
