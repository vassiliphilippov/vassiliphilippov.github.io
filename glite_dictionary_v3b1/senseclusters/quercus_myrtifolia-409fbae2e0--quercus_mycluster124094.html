Etymology

Quercus myrtifolia comes from Latin words: Quercus means 'oak' and myrtifolia means 'myrtle-leaved' because its leaves look like those of a myrtle. So the name paints the picture of an oak with myrtle-like leaves and fits this small evergreen shrub.
