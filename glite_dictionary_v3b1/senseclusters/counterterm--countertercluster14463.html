Etymology

Counterterm comes from the parts counter ('against' or 'opposite') and term ('word' or 'part'). A helpful way to remember this meaning is to think of counter as in counteract or counterbalance: a counterterm is a word or expression that stands 'against' another, so it gives the opposite or a negation.
