Etymology

The word clear-thinking combines clear, meaning 'easy to see' or 'not cloudy', and thinking, meaning 'using your mind', so it creates the picture of a mind like a clear window that sees ideas plainly. So, a clear-thinking person can make calm, logical decisions without confusion.
