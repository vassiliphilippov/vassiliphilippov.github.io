Etymology

The noun inverted pleat comes from sewing. A pleat is a 'fold' in fabric and inverted means the folds turn to the 'inside', so the 'crease' shows on the outside while the extra fabric hides inside. That's why an inverted pleat gives a neat outside line but still adds space and movement to the garment.
