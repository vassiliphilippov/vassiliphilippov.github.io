Etymology

Fechner's law is named after the scientist Gustav Fechner, who noticed that our sense of something grows much more slowly than the physical change. Imagine turning up a dim lamp: to feel each equal step of brightness you must make the light much stronger each time, so the rule explains why 'sensation' increases slowly compared to the 'stimulus'.
