Etymology

The word brewage starts with the familiar verb brew, which means 'to make a drink by steeping, boiling, or fermenting', and it adds the ending -age, which makes a noun meaning 'the thing made'. So, a brewage is simply a drink made by brewing, for example a beer or traditional ale, rather than a drink made by distilling.
