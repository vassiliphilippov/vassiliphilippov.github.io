Etymology

soutache comes from the same French word for the braid and also describes the craft of sewing that braid around beads to form shapes; imagine a little 'cord' stitched in curves to make flowers or patterns. That's why soutache can mean both the technique and the small pieces of jewellery or embroidery made with it.
