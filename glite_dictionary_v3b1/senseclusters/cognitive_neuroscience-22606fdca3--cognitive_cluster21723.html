Etymology

cognitive neuroscience comes from joining cognitive, meaning the 'mind' or 'thinking', and neuroscience, meaning the study of the 'brain' and nerves. Picture scientists watching the brain while a person thinks, so the phrase means the study of how the brain makes 'thinking' and 'behaviour' happen.
