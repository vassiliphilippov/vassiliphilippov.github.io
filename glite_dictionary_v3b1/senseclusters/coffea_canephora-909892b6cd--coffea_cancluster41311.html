Etymology

Coffea canephora may come from the word Coffea, meaning coffee, and an old Greek word canephora meaning 'basket-bearer', a picture of branches full of berries that could fill a basket. That image helps you remember that this species makes many strong beans used for 'robusta' coffee and is hardy, so it is grown widely.
