Etymology

The word quick-frozen comes from quick meaning 'fast' and frozen meaning 'turned to ice', so it describes food frozen very fast. Freezing food quickly helps 'lock in' the taste and nutrients, which is why quick-frozen vegetables and fish often taste fresher.
