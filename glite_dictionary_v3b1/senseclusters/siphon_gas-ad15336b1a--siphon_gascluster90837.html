Etymology

The verb siphon gas comes from the simple image of using a long tube or siphon: you put one end into the fuel tank and suck at the other end so the gas runs out into a container. That's why siphon gas means 'to transfer' fuel from one place to another, and often 'to steal' fuel when it is taken without permission.
