Etymology

Spiranthes cernua comes from Greek and Latin words: Spiranthes means 'spiral flower' and cernua means 'nodding'. The name gives a clear picture — the creamy white flowers wrap around the tall stem in a spiral and often bend like a small nod, so the name helps you remember how the orchid looks.
