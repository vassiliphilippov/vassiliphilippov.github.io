Etymology

herding cats uses the picture of herding, which means driving animals together, and cats, which are independent and will not follow orders. One popular idea is that people used this funny, strong image to describe how impossible it feels to make everyone do the same thing, so today it means 'trying to manage a group that is difficult to control'.
