Etymology

polyneuritic psychosis comes from old Greek parts: poly- meaning 'many', neuritic meaning 'nerve', and psychosis meaning 'mind sickness'. The image of many damaged nerves and a sick mind helps explain why the term refers to the dementia that can follow many years of heavy drinking, where there is a 'loss of recent memory' while older memories stay.
