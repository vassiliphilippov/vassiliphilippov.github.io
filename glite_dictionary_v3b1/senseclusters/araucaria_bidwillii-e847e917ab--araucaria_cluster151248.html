Etymology

Araucaria bidwillii, often called the bunya-bunya tree, is famous in Australia for its very large cones full of edible seeds. One popular story says Aboriginal people gathered and roasted these seeds at big feasts, so the strong image of a huge tree with heavy cones and tasty nuts helps you remember that it is a large conifer with edible seeds.
