Etymology

philosophical doctrine joins philosophical (ideas about life and thinking) and doctrine (a taught rule). Think of a teacher writing down the main rules for a group of thinkers; those written rules become the 'doctrine' that guides the philosophy, so philosophical doctrine means 'a set of beliefs' in that philosophy.
