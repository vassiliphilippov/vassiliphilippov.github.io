Etymology

The word inelegant begins with in- meaning 'not' and the part elegant comes from a word that meant 'chosen' or 'carefully picked', similar to the idea in elect. So, inelegant is 'not chosen' and today it describes something that is not graceful, stylish, or in 'good taste'.
