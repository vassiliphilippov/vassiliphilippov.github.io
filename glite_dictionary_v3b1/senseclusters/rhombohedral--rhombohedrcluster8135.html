Etymology

rhombohedral comes from the same root as rhombus, which first meant a 'spinning top' and later a diamond-shaped figure, plus -hedral from hedron meaning 'face'. So, a rhombohedral crystal has faces like tilted diamonds, which is why the word means 'shaped like a rhombohedron'.
