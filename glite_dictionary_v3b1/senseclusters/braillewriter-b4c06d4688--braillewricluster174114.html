Etymology

Braillewriter comes from Braille, named for Louis Braille, a blind French teacher who made a 'dot' system for reading, and writer, meaning something that 'writes'. So a Braillewriter is like a small typewriter that makes raised 'dots' you can feel, which is why it produces Braille text.
