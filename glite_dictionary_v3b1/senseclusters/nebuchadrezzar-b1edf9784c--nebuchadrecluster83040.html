Etymology

Nebuchadrezzar comes from an old Babylonian name Nabû-kudurri-uṣur, which was like a short prayer to the god Nabû asking him to 'protect' the king or his son. So the name means being under Nabu's protection, a fitting idea for a powerful ruler.
