Etymology

The phrase guilt feelings paints a picture of carrying something heavy inside: guilt is the sense of having done wrong and feelings are the painful emotions you hold, like a weight on your chest. One common image is that this weight makes you sad or restless, so 'guilt feelings' means 'remorse' or 'responsibility' for something you did.
