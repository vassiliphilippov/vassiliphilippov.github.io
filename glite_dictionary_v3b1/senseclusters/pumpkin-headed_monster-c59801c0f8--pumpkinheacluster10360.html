Etymology

The noun pumpkin-headed monster comes from Halloween pictures of carved pumpkins used as heads. Imagine a creature with a glowing, pumpkin-shaped head called pumpkin-headed and the body of a monster, and that's why the phrase means a fictional creature with a pumpkin head.
