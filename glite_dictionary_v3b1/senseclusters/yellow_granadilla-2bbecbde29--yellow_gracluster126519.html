Etymology

yellow granadilla may come from Spanish granadilla, a small form of the word 'granada' which means 'pomegranate' or 'grenade' because the many seeds inside look like a tiny pomegranate or grenade. The word yellow marks the fruit's colour, so the name simply means the yellow, seed-filled fruit prized for its sweet pulp.
