Etymology

burster comes from burst ('a sudden release') plus -er ('that which'), and the name fits the sky as well as machines. In astronomy a burster is an object that sends out sudden, intense flashes of X-rays or gamma rays, so the word describes its quick, powerful bursts of radiation.
