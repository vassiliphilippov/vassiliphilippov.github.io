Etymology

The word nitty-gritty likely comes from two simple words, nit and grit, which suggest 'small' and 'tiny hard bits', and one popular story links this to dirty bits in old ships but we are not sure. Think of clearing away the tiny bits to reach what matters, so nitty-gritty means the 'most important details'.
