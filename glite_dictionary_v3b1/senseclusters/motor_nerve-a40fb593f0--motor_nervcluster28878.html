Etymology

motor nerve combines motor (a 'mover') and nerve, giving a clear picture of a pathway that makes things move; imagine a wire that carries an order from the brain to a muscle. So, a motor nerve is a nerve that 'carries orders' to muscles, which is why it means 'a nerve that makes muscles act'.
