Etymology

Leo Tolstoy comes from two parts: Leo, from a word meaning 'lion', and Tolstoy, a Russian family name that long ago meant 'thick' or 'stout'. Picture a strong lion with a heavy coat. This simple image helps you remember a great Russian writer known for big, serious novels and moral ideas.
