Etymology

lag screw may come from old building work where big screws were used to hold the 'lagging', the heavy cover, on hot pipes and boilers, so the name joined lag and screw. That's why lag screw now means a heavy-duty screw you drive with a wrench to hold heavy wood or metal in place.
