Etymology

Merdeka celebration comes from the word Merdeka, which in Malay and Indonesian means 'freedom' or 'independence' and was often shouted by crowds when a country won its freedom. So a Merdeka celebration is the public festival people hold to mark that 'independence' and remember the moment the nation became free.
