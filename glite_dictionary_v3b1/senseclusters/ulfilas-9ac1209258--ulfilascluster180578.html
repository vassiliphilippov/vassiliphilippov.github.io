Etymology

Ulfilas is a name from old Germanic parts wulf meaning 'wolf' and -ila meaning 'little', so the name means 'little wolf'. That image makes it easy to remember Ulfilas as the small 'wolf' bishop who translated the Bible into Gothic and helped create the Gothic alphabet.
