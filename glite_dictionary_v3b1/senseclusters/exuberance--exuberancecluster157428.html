Etymology

Exuberance comes from Latin parts ex- meaning 'out' and uber meaning 'rich' or 'fruitful', so it gives the image of joy or energy 'overflowing' out of someone. That's why exuberance means lively, cheerful energy that seems to spill out and spread to others.
