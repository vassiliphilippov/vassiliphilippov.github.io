Etymology

Inclined comes from Latin parts in- 'into' and clinare 'to bend', so it originally meant 'bent in' or 'leaning'. Think of a ramp or roof that 'bends' up from the flat ground; that sloping surface is an inclined plane, which is why we call any sloping surface inclined.
