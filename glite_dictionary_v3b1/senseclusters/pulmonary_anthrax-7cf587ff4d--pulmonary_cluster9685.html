Etymology

pulmonary anthrax is a medical name for an infection of the 'lungs' by anthrax. The word pulmonary comes from an old word for 'lungs', so imagine tiny spores being 'breathed in' and attacking the lungs, and that's why it means anthrax picked up by breathing in the spores.
