Etymology

Antonio Lucio Vivaldi was an Italian priest with bright red hair who played the violin and wrote lively music. He is best known for a group of violin concertos called 'The Four Seasons' that sound like musical pictures of spring, summer, autumn and winter, so his name today means a famous composer of violin music.
