Etymology

rock-and-roll comes from two simple words, rock and roll, which were used in early blues to mean 'to move' or 'dance' and which also described a ship 'rocking and rolling'. In the 1950s in the United States the name was used for a new, energetic music, so the word helps you remember music that makes people move and dance.
