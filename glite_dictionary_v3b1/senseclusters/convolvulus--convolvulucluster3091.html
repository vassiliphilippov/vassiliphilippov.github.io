Etymology

convolvulus comes from Latin parts con- 'together' and volvere 'to roll', so the name means 'to roll together'. This gives a clear picture: the plant twists and wraps itself around fences and other plants, which is why convolvulus refers to twining or trailing vines like bindweed.
