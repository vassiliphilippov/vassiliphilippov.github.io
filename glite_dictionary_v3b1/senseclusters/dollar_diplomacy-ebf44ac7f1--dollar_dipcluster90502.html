Etymology

dollar diplomacy puts dollar, meaning money, together with diplomacy, meaning talks with other countries; one popular story says US leaders in the early 1900s used loans and investments to influence other governments instead of using soldiers. So, dollar diplomacy means 'using a country's money to gain influence in other countries'.
