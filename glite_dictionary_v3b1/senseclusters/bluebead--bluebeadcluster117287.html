Etymology

bluebead comes from putting blue and bead together, with bead meaning a 'small round piece', so it first named little blue glass beads. It was later used for a plant whose bright blue berries look like tiny 'blue beads', especially in North America, which is why bluebead now means either a small blue bead or a plant with blue berry-like fruits.
