Etymology

Worcester sauce comes from the English city of Worcester, where, long ago, two men mixed vinegar, molasses and spices and left the mixture to age in barrels. One popular story says this long aging made the sauce dark and very flavorful, so today Worcester sauce means a 'dark, tangy' sauce used 'to flavor food'.
