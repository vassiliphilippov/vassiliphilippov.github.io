Etymology

Sophora tetraptera is the scientific name for a small tree with bright yellow flowers and very hard wood. The second part, tetraptera, may come from Greek meaning 'four wings', so imagine the plant's seed pods or flower parts with four thin, wing-like parts — that's why the name points to this feature and helps you remember the tree.
