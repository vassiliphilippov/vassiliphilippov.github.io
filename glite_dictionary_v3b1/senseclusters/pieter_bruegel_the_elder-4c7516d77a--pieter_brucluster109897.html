Etymology

Pieter Bruegel the Elder was a painter from what is now Belgium who painted busy village scenes and wide landscapes full of tiny people, giving a clear picture of everyday country life. He is called the Elder to show he is the older Pieter Bruegel, so his name now means the original artist famous for detailed pictures of 'country life'.
