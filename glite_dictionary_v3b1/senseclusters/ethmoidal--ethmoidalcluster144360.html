Etymology

The word ethmoidal comes from ethmoid, from the Greek ethmos meaning 'sieve', because the ethmoid bone has many small holes like a sieve. So, ethmoidal means 'relating to the ethmoid bone or the ethmoid sinuses' in the skull and nose.
