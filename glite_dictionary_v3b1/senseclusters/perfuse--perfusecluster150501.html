Etymology

perfuse comes from the Latin parts per- 'through' and fundere 'to pour', the same root you see in words like diffuse and profuse. A helpful image is light or color being 'poured through' a room or material, which explains why the word now means to spread or fill something with a liquid, colour, or feeling.
