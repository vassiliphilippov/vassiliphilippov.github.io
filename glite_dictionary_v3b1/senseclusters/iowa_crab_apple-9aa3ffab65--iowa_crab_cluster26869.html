Etymology

Iowa crab apple likely comes from combining Iowa, the place where the tree was often seen, and crab apple, meaning a 'small, sour apple'. So the name gives a clear image of a small wild apple tree tied to Iowa, which helps you remember this species with fragrant pink flowers.
