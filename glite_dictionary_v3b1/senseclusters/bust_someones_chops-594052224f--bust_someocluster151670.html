Etymology

The idiom bust someone's chops likely comes from chops, a word for the mouth or jaw. One popular story says to bust someone's jaws meant to hit them, and later the phrase was used for attacking someone with words instead of fists. So, today bust someone's chops means 'to tease, nag, or criticize'.
