Etymology

Spirits of wine comes from old distillers who heated wine and caught the clear vapour, the 'spirit' that rose from it. People thought this rising liquid was the true, strong part of the wine, so they called it spirits, and that's why today spirits of wine means 'very strong alcohol' used in labs and hospitals.
