Etymology

silver-leaved nettle The plant name silver-leaved nettle comes from a simple picture: its leaves are pale and shiny, so silver and leaved, and people called the rough, common weed a nettle. So today the name means a wild, weedy plant with silvery leaves that people once used to 'curdle milk'.
