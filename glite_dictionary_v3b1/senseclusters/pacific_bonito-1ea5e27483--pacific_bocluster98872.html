Etymology

Pacific bonito takes its name from Pacific, the ocean where it lives, and the Spanish word bonito, which means 'pretty' and is used for small, striped tunas. Fishermen named this fast, dark-fleshed fish for its stripes and where it lives, so the name simply points to the bonito found in the Pacific and often used for canning.
