Etymology

Decorative finial comes from the picture of a small ornament placed at the very end or top of a building, post, or piece of furniture. The word finial is linked to the idea of an 'end' or 'finishing touch', and decorative shows it is for looking good, so the phrase now means a small decorative piece at the top or end.
