Etymology

The noun physical value comes from the idea of an item's 'real' cost: physical means the actual object and value means how much it would cost to make or buy it again. People often count that cost after taking away what the item lost from age and use, so 'physical value' means the real amount to replace an item rather than its 'market price'.
