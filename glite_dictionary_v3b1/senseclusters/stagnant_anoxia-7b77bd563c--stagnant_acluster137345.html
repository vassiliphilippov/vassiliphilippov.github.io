Etymology

The phrase stagnant anoxia comes from the image of still water that does not get fresh air: stagnant means very still and anoxia means 'no oxygen'. So when blood in small vessels moves very slowly or stops, parts of the body get 'no oxygen', and the phrase describes that lack of oxygen caused by poor circulation.
