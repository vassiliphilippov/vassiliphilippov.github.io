Etymology

penguin may come from a sailor word, with one idea linking it to Welsh pen gwyn meaning 'white head' and another linking it to Portuguese pinguim meaning 'fat'. Sailors first used the name for a different big northern bird and later for the black-and-white seabird, which is why penguin now means that short, heavy sea bird that cannot fly.
