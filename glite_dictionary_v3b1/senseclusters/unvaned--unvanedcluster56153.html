Etymology

unvaned is made from un- meaning 'not' and vane, a 'small fin or feather' that helps keep an arrow straight, like a weathervane that shows wind direction. So unvaned means an arrow 'without' those guiding vanes, which is why unvaned arrows often fly badly.
