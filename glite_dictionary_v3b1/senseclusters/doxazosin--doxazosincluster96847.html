Etymology

doxazosin is a drug name you can remember by splitting it into doxa- and the ending -zosin, which it shares with medicines like prazosin and terazosin. These -zosin drugs work to 'relax' blood vessels, so they lower blood pressure and can ease symptoms of an enlarged prostate.
