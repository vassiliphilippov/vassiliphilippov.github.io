Etymology

vena phrenica comes from Latin words: vena meaning 'vein' and phrenica meaning 'diaphragm', the main breathing muscle under the lungs. It literally names the vein that carries blood away from that muscle, so today it means 'either of the two veins that drain the diaphragm'.
