Etymology

Folk devil comes from the simple image of ordinary people (folk) and an evil figure (devil) joined together: a town or the media picks someone and treats them like a 'devil' to blame for trouble. That's why folk devil means 'a person or group the public sees as a threat and wrongly blames for social problems'.
