Etymology

diestrus comes from two parts: the prefix di- (like in 'divide' or 'double', meaning 'two' or 'between') and estrus (meaning 'heat', the time when an animal is ready to mate). So, diestrus is the 'between heats' time, the quiet period when an animal is not sexually active.
