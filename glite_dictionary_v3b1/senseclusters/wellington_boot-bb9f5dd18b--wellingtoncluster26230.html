Etymology

Wellington boot is named after the Duke of Wellington, who asked for a simple knee-high leather boot to wear in wet, muddy fields, and people began to call that new style a 'Wellington'. When rubber versions were made later, the name stayed, so today Wellington boots mean knee-length waterproof boots for wet and muddy conditions.
