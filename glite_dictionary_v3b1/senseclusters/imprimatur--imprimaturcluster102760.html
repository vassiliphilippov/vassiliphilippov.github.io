Etymology

The word imprimatur comes from the Latin imprimere, meaning 'to press', and is the source of English words like print and imprint. Long ago a real 'stamp' was put on books to show they could be published, so an imprimatur today is an official 'stamp' of 'approval' or 'permission' to publish.
