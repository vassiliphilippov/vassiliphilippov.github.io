Etymology

Oil tycoon paints a simple image: oil, the black fuel, and tycoon, a word for 'a very rich and powerful person', so imagine someone sitting on barrels of oil or owning many oil rigs. That's why oil tycoon means 'a wealthy and powerful person who owns or controls big oil businesses'.
