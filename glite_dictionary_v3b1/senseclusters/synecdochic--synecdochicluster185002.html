Etymology

The word synecdochic comes from Greek and the prefix syn- means 'together', as in synchronize, giving the idea of things 'taken as one'. That's why a synecdochic expression uses a 'part' for the 'whole' or the 'whole' for a 'part', like saying 'wheels' when you mean a whole car.
