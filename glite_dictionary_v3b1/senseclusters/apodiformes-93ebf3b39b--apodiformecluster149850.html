Etymology

Apodiformes comes from Greek parts a- ('without') and pous ('foot'). These birds, like swifts and hummingbirds, have very small legs and spend most of their time flying, so the name paints a picture of animals that seem almost 'without feet', which is why it fits this group of small, fast birds.
