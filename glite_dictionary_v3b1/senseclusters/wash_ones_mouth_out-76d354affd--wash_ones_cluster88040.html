Etymology

wash one's mouth out may come from a simple old punishment where a child who said a rude word had to wash their mouthout with soap; the strong image of cleaning the mouth for bad language makes the phrase easy to remember. That's why today it means 'to scold or punish someone, often jokingly, for saying bad words'.
