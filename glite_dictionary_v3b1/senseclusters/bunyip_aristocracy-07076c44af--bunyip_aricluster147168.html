Etymology

bunyip aristocracy comes from the Australian mythical animal called the bunyip and the word aristocracy for nobles. One popular story says people pictured a made-up 'bunyip' dressed like a noble to mock those who 'pretend to be important', so the phrase means a fake or snobby group who act like rich or important people.
