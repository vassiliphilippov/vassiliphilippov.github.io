Etymology

Active subwoofer uses active to mean 'powered' and subwoofer to mean a speaker for deep, low sounds. Imagine a bass box with its own little amplifier inside so it can work by itself, and that's why an active subwoofer today means a subwoofer with a 'built-in amplifier'.
