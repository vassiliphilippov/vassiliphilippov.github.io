Etymology

innate reflex puts together innate, meaning 'born' or 'built-in', and reflex, meaning a quick body action. Imagine a baby who turns its head and sucks when something touches its cheek — this built-in action happens 'without learning', so innate reflex means an 'automatic, instinctive' reaction.
