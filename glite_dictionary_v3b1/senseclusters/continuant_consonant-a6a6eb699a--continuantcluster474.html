Etymology

The term continuant consonant comes from the idea of a continuant (something that keeps going) and a consonant (a speech sound). Imagine air moving through a small gap in the mouth so the sound does not stop, and you have the picture behind the term. So, it means 'a consonant sound where the mouth is narrow but not fully closed, so the air flows continuously'.
