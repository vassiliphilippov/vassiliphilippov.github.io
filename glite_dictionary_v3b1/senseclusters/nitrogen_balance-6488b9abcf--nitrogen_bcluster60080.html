Etymology

Nitrogen balance comes from a simple image: imagine a scale that compares nitrogen coming into the body or soil and the balance of what goes out. So the balance shows if the body or soil is 'healthy' because it has enough nitrogen, or 'not enough' when more nitrogen leaves than comes in.
