Etymology

exchange premium: The noun exchange premium comes from a simple image: when you make an exchange, a shop or bank asks for a small premium on top. Imagine handing over money at an airport and paying a little 'extra' above the normal rate; that 'extra' is the exchange premium, which is why the term means the added 'cost' of converting money.
