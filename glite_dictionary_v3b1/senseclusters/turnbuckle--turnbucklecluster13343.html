Etymology

turnbuckle comes from the parts turn and buckle, where turn means 'to rotate' and buckle is like the buckle on a belt that 'fastens' things. So a turnbuckle is a metal fitting you turn to pull, tighten, or shorten ropes and wires, which is exactly what the tool does today.
