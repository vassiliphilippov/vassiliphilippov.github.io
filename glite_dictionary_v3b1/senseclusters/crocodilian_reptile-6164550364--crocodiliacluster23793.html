Etymology

crocodilian reptile comes from the parts crocodilian (meaning 'like a crocodile') and reptile (a 'cold-blooded animal that crawls'), creating a clear picture of a large, scaly hunter. So the name points to 'an animal that lives in water and on land and hunts prey', such as crocodiles, alligators, caimans, and gharials.
