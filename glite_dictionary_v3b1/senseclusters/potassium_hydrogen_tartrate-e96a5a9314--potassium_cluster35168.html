Etymology

potassium hydrogen tartrate is better known as 'cream of tartar'. One popular story says it comes from tiny white crystals called tartrate that form inside old wine barrels, and people scraped these crystals and used them in cooking. That's why potassium hydrogen tartrate today is the white salt cooks use to help baking powder work and to keep egg whites firm.
