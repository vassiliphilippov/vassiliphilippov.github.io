Etymology

The phrase make a schmutter may come from a Yiddish word like schmutz, which means 'dirt' or 'smear'. Imagine someone smearing dirt on a shirt — they have 'made a schmutter', so the phrase came to mean 'to mess up' or 'to do something badly'.
