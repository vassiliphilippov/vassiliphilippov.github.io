Etymology

Contemplative comes from the Latin verb contemplari, meaning 'to look' in a special place called a templum ('temple'). People who were called contemplatives spent long, quiet hours in such places to pray and think, so today a contemplative is someone who spends a lot of time in quiet, often for religious reasons.
