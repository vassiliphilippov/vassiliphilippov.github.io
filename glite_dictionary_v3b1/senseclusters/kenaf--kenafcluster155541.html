Etymology

kenaf comes from old local names for a hemp-like plant used in North Africa and Asia, and its scientific name cannabinus shows a link to 'cannabis' or hemp. So kenaf refers to the tall plant people grow for its strong 'fiber', which is used for rope, cloth and paper.
