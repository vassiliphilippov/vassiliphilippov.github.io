Etymology

The word maladjusted is made of mal- 'bad' or 'wrong' and adjust 'to set right' or 'to fit', the same idea you see in malfunction or when you adjust a machine. So a maladjusted object or person is 'not properly adjusted' for its situation and will not work or fit well.
