Etymology

Asplenium nigripes may come from old words: Asplenium was linked to the 'spleen' because people once used similar ferns to treat that part of the body, and nigripes means 'black foot', probably describing a dark stem or base on the plant. So the name gives a simple picture of a delicate fern with a dark 'foot', which helps you remember this rare species.
