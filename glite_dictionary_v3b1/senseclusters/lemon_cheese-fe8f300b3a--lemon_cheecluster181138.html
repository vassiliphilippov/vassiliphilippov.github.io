Etymology

The name lemon cheese comes from old home cooking where people made a thick, creamy mix from lemon juice, eggs, butter and sugar. Because the mix cooled and became firm, like cheese, they called it cheese, so lemon cheese came to mean a sweet 'spread' you put on toast.
