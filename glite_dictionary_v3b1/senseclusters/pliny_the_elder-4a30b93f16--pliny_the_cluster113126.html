Etymology

Pliny the Elder was a Roman writer who made a very large book called 'Natural History' describing plants, animals and minerals. He is also remembered for going to watch the eruption of Mount Vesuvius and dying while observing it, and the name 'Elder' marks him as the older Pliny, so people remember him both for his book and for dying as a careful observer.
