Etymology

rattlebox is a simple compound of rattle and box, named for the plant's dry seed pods that shake and make a 'rattle' like a little box of seeds. The plant's scientific name Crotalaria also comes from a Greek word for 'rattle', so the name points to the same noisy pods.
