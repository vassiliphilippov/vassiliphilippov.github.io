Etymology

dovetail plane: The noun dovetail plane names a small hand tool, a plane, used to cut the tail-shaped grooves for a dovetail joint. Because a dovetail joint looks like a bird's tail and 'locks' two boards together, the tool's name shows it is used to make pieces fit tightly and hold well.
