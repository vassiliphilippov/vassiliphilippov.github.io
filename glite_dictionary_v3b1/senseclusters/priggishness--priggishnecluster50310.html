Etymology

priggishness comes from the simple word prig, an old English term for someone who acts 'too proper' or who thinks they are morally better than others, with the endings -ish and -ness that make it 'the quality of being' that way. So, priggishness means the annoying habit of behaving overly correct and self-important.
