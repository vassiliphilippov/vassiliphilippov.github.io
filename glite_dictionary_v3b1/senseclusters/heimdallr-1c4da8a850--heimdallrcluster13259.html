Etymology

Heimdallr may come from old Norse parts: heim meaning 'home' or 'world' and -dallr a part thought to mean 'bright' or 'watcher'. So a helpful way to remember him is as the light-bringing, watchful guardian of the gods' home, which fits his role as the god of dawn and the guard of Asgard.
