Etymology

The word 165 is spoken as one hundred sixty-five, built from one ('1') plus hundred ('100') and the parts sixty and five. A helpful way to remember it is to imagine 'one' full 'hundred' with an extra 'sixty' and an extra 'five', so together they make one hundred sixty-five of something.
