Etymology

The word bitchily comes from bitch, a word that once meant a 'female dog' and later became an insult for a cruel or unpleasant person. Adding -y made bitchy ('like a bitch') and -ly turns it into an adverb, so to speak bitchily is to speak in a mean, hurtful way.
