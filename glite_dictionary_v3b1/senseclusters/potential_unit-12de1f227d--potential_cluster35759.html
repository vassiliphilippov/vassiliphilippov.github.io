Etymology

The noun potential unit comes from the simple idea of potential as stored 'ability to do work' and unit as a way to measure things. Think of a battery as water behind a dam: a potential unit measures the push each tiny piece of electric charge would get, so it means 'energy per charge' compared with a chosen point.
