Etymology

Calliphoridae comes from Greek parts: calli- meaning 'beautiful', -phor- meaning 'to carry', and the animal family ending -idae. So think of them as 'beautiful carriers', which fits because these blowflies often have shiny, metallic bodies and are known for carrying larvae and arriving early at dead animals.
