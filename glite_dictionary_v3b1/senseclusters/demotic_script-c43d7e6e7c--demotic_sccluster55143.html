Etymology

Demotic script comes from the word Demotic, which meant 'of the people', and script means 'writing'. It was a simple, fast handwriting used by writers and ordinary people for daily letters and records instead of the slow, formal picture writing, so the name explains why it means the everyday Egyptian writing.
