Etymology

family Callitrichaceae gets its name from small water plants called Callitriche, which have very thin, hair-like leaves that spread under the water. One popular idea says the parts calli- and -trich mean 'beautiful hair', so the name helps you remember this family is made of little freshwater plants with fine, hair-like leaves.
