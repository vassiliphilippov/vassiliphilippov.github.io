Etymology

Protirelin is a medical name built from parts: pro- meaning 'for' or 'forward' and -relin which you can remember as 'release'. So, protirelin is a hormone that tells the pituitary to release the thyroid-stimulating hormone, which explains its use in tests and treatment.
