Etymology

The word hemicrania comes from Greek parts hemi- meaning 'half' and cranium meaning 'skull' or 'head', the same pieces you see in hemisphere and the word cranium. So hemicrania is like a 'half-head' pain, a severe headache that usually affects one side of the head.
