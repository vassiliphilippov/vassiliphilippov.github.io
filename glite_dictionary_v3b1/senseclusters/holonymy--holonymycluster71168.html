Etymology

The word holonymy comes from Greek parts hol- meaning 'whole' and -onym meaning 'name', like in holistic and synonym. So a holonym is the 'whole' that names its parts, for example 'tree' names branches and leaves, which is why holonymy means the link between a whole and its parts.
