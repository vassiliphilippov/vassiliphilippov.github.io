Etymology

The word Latimeridae comes from the name Latimeria, which was named for Marjorie Latimer who found the living coelacanth, and the ending -idae marks an animal 'family'. So, Latimeridae means the 'Latimer' family of lobe-finned fishes, which is why the name refers to the coelacanth group.
