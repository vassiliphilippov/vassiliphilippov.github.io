Etymology

overexcitability breaks into over- meaning 'too much', excite meaning 'to stir up', and -ability meaning 'the quality of being'. So picture a nerve or muscle that reacts too easily, like a wire that sparks at the smallest touch, which is why doctors use the word for unusually strong nerve or muscle responses such as spasms or seizures.
