Etymology

Thomas Aquinas was an Italian church thinker from the 1200s who tried to bring 'faith' and 'reason' together. He used clear, step-by-step ideas from the old Greek thinker Aristotle and offered what he called the 'five ways' to argue for God's existence, so his ideas are still taught today.
