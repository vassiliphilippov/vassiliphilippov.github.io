Etymology

Linaria vulgaris is often called 'butter-and-eggs' because its flowers are bright yellow and orange, like butter and egg yolk. That simple image of yellow-and-orange blooms helps you remember that Linaria vulgaris is a common wildflower and weed that spreads along roads and in gardens.
