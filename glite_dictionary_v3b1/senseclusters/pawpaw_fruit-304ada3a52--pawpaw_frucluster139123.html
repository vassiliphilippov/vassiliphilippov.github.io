Etymology

The noun pawpaw fruit may come from the spoken Spanish and Caribbean word 'papaya', which English ears heard as a repeated 'paw-paw' sound and turned into pawpaw. That's why pawpaw fruit today means the same sweet, orange-fleshed tropical fruit often called 'papaya'.
