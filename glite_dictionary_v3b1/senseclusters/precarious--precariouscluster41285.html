Etymology

precarious comes from the Latin verb precari and its root prex, which mean 'to ask' and 'prayer'. A helpful way to remember it is to think of something 'left to prayer' or 'left to chance', so it is unsure and likely to fall or fail, and therefore means risky, unstable, or not safe.
