Etymology

Vicomte Ferdinand Marie de Lesseps was a French diplomat and engineer in the 1800s who was in charge of building the Suez Canal. Imagine a long canal cutting between the Mediterranean and the Red Sea; because he planned and led that big canal, his name is remembered for 'connecting two seas' and for changing how ships travel.
