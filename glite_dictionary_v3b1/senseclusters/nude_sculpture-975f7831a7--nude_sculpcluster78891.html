Etymology

nude sculpture comes from an old art idea of making a sculpture that shows the nude human body. Ancient Greek and later European artists studied the 'naked' body to learn its shape and beauty, and they made these statues for art and teaching. So, nude sculpture means 'a statue representing a naked human figure'.
