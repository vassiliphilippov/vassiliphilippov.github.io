Etymology

nortriptyline is a medicine name made of parts: nor- often means 'without' a small part in drug names, and triptyline contains tri- meaning 'three', like in tricycle. A helpful way to remember it is that nortriptyline is a 'tricyclic' antidepressant, a drug with three connected rings in its shape used to treat depression, so the name shows both its drug family and the small change that makes it different.
