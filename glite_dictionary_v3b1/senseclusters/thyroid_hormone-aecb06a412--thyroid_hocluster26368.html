Etymology

thyroid hormone gets its name from the small, shield-shaped gland in the neck: the word thyroid comes from an old Greek word for 'shield', and hormone means a chemical 'messenger'. These hormones tell the body's cells how fast to use energy, so they control the body's 'speed' or activity.
