Etymology

rum nose may come from an old, simple picture: people saw that men who drank a lot of rum often had a red, swollen nose, so the phrase came to mean a nose that shows heavy drinking. That's why rum nose means a reddish, enlarged nose often thought to show a 'heavy drinker'.
