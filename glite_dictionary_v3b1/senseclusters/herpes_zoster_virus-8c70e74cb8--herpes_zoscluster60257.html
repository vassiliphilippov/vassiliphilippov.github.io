Etymology

herpes zoster virus comes from old Greek words: herpes meaning 'to creep' and zoster meaning 'belt'. Doctors used these words because the virus makes a painful rash that often appears as a belt across the body and seems to creep along the skin, so the name links to the rash we call 'shingles'.
