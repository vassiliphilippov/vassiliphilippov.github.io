Etymology

Achillea gets its name from the Greek hero Achilles, because stories say he used the plant yarrow to 'heal' soldiers' cuts and wounds. So, the name helps you remember that Achillea is a group of scented, healing herbs like yarrow that people have used in traditional medicine.
