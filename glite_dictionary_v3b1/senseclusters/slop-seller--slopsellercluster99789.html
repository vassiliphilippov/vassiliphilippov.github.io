Etymology

The word slop-seller comes from slop, an old Dutch word for loose, cheap clothes meaning 'loose' and 'cheap'. A slop-seller was someone who sold ready-made rough clothes to sailors and workers, so the term came to mean a dealer in cheap, ready-made clothing.
