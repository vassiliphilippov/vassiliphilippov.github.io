Etymology

Tapirus indicus combines Tapirus, from a native word 'tapiira' for the animal, and indicus, a Latin word meaning 'from the Indies' or 'Asian'. So the name paints a simple picture of the tapir 'from Asia', which helps you remember the black-and-white species found in Malaysia and Sumatra.
