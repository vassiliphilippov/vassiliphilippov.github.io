Etymology

thorn in the flesh comes from the Bible, where Paul said he had a thorn in his flesh — a small sharp thing that kept hurting him. So the picture of a tiny sharp thorn that keeps hurting explains why the phrase means 'a constant problem that keeps bothering you'.
