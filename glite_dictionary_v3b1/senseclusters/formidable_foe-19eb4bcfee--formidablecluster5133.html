Etymology

formidable foe comes from an old sense of formidable, meaning something that causes fear, and foe, meaning enemy. The picture is an enemy who seems very powerful and frightening, so people feel afraid and find them hard to beat, which is why it means 'a strong, hard-to-defeat opponent'.
