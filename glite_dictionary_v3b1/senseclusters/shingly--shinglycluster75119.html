Etymology

shingly comes from shingle, a word for a 'small stone' or 'pebble', plus -y, which means 'full of' or 'covered with', so it describes ground 'covered with small stones'. Think of a beach of pebbles that crunch under your feet, which explains why a shingly path is hard to walk on.
