Etymology

Aggregation comes from Latin parts ad- meaning 'to' and grex meaning 'flock' or 'group', so it originally meant 'adding to a flock'. Think of small things coming together like sheep joining a flock — that helps explain why an aggregation is a collection of items brought into one group.
