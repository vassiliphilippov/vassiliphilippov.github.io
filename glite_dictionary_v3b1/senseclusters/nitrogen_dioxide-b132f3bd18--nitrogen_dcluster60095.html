Etymology

The compound nitrogen dioxide is named from its parts: nitrogen and dioxide, where the 'di-' part means 'two', so imagine one nitrogen wearing two oxygen 'hats' to remember it. That's why the name tells you it is a gas made of one nitrogen plus two oxygens and why it refers to the brown, harmful air pollutant.
