Etymology

Marcus Antonius was a Roman general and close supporter of Julius Caesar who became famous for his love affair with Cleopatra and for being defeated by Octavian at the sea battle of Actium. Because of this dramatic story, his name today refers to that leader and to the events that helped bring about the 'end of the Roman Republic'.
