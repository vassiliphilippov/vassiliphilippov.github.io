Etymology

Malope trifida combines Malope, a word for mallow-like plants, and trifida, a Latin word that means 'split into three'. That name gives a clear picture: each flower is surrounded by three large, heart-shaped leaf parts, so the name helps you remember the plant by its three-part look.
