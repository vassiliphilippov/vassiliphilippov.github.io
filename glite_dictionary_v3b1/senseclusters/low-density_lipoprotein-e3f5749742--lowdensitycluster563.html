Etymology

The noun low-density lipoprotein comes from its parts: lipoprotein is a little package that carries fat and protein in the blood, and low-density means the package is not tightly packed. Imagine slow, loose carriers that leave fat on the inside of blood vessels, so people call it 'bad cholesterol' and high levels are linked to heart disease.
