Etymology

electroweak interaction is named because it joins two forces: electro (the force behind 'electricity' and light) and weak (the small force that causes some kinds of radioactivity). Scientists found that at very high energy they act as one force, so the name means the single interaction that covers both 'electric' and 'weak' effects.
