Etymology

The noun extreme unction comes from the old church practice of a priest rubbing oil on a person at the very end of life. The word extreme meant 'last' and unction meant 'anointing' or 'rubbing with oil', so the picture of that final blessing explains why the phrase means the sacrament given to someone who is dying.
