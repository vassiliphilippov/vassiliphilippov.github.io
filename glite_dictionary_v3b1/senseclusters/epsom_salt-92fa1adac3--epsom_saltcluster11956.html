Etymology

Epsom salt gets its name from the town of Epsom in England, where a bitter mineral spring left white crystals that looked like salt. People used the water and crystals to soothe sore muscles and help constipation, so today Epsom salt means the mineral magnesium sulfate used in baths and as a mild laxative.
