Etymology

hyperaldosteronism comes from the prefix hyper- meaning 'too much' and the hormone name aldosterone, which is made by the adrenal glands near the kidneys and helps control blood pressure and potassium. So hyperaldosteronism is when the body makes 'too much' aldosterone, which explains the high blood pressure and low potassium in this condition.
