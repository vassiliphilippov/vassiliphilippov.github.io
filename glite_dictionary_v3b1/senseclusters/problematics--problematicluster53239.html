Etymology

problematics comes from the word problem, which goes back to a Greek idea of 'something thrown forward' — picture an obstacle thrown into your path. The ending -atics, like in mathematics, shows a field or group of things, so problematics means the set of complex difficulties or tricky questions in a subject.
