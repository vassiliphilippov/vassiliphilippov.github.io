Etymology

The word batholith comes from the Greek words bathos 'depth' and lithos 'stone', so it literally means 'deep stone'. You can see the same lithos in monolith, which means 'single stone', and this helps you remember the 'stone' part. So a batholith is a very large mass of rock that formed deep under the earth.
