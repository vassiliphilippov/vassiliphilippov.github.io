Etymology

malodorous comes from two parts: mal- meaning 'bad' and odor meaning 'smell', so it literally means 'bad-smelling'. Think of mal- as in malfunction and odor as in 'odor' or 'odour', which helps explain why malodorous describes something that smells awful.
