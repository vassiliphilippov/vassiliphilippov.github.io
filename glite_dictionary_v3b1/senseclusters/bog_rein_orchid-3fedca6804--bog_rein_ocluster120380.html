Etymology

bog rein orchid may come from where and how the plant grows: it lives in bogs, or 'very wet ground', and its tall flower spike looks like a thin rein or strap. So the name helps you remember it is an orchid with many sweet-smelling white flowers on a long spike that grows in wet places.
