Etymology

heat of vaporization comes from its literal parts: heat (the energy you give) and vaporization (the act of turning a liquid into a gas). Think of boiling water: you keep adding heat but the temperature stays the same until the water becomes gas. So, the phrase means the exact amount of heat needed to 'vaporize' a liquid.
