Etymology

Vascularize comes from the root vas meaning 'vessel', like the everyday word vessel, and the ending -ize means 'to make'. So, to vascularize is to 'make' or give tissue blood vessels, which is why doctors use it for supplying an organ or graft with a blood supply.
