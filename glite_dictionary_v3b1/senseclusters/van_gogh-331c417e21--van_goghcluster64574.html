Etymology

van Gogh comes from the real Dutch artist Vincent van Gogh, who painted with strong colors and thick, visible brush strokes. Because of famous works like 'Starry Night', saying 'van Gogh' today makes people think of that artist and of art with bright color and deep feeling.
