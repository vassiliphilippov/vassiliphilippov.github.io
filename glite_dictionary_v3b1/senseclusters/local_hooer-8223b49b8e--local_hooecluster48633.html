Etymology

local hooer probably comes from the old word hooer, a softer way to say 'whore', combined with local, meaning someone from the town. People used the phrase to paint a clear picture — a person in the town thought to sleep with many others — so it came to mean 'a person in a locality who is considered promiscuous'.
