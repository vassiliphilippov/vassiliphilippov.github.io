Etymology

Begonia feastii combines the group name Begonia and the second part feastii, which probably comes from the surname 'Feast' of the person who first found or described the plant. Its round, colourful leaves are like a little 'feast' for the eyes, so the name helps you remember it is a showy plant grown for its looks.
