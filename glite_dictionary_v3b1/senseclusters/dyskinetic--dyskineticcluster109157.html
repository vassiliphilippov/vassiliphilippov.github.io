Etymology

dyskinetic comes from the parts dys- meaning 'bad' or 'difficult' and kinesis meaning 'movement', and you can see the same idea in the word kinetic ('movement'). So, dyskinetic describes 'bad' or uncontrolled muscle movements, which is why doctors use it for involuntary movement problems.
