Etymology

The word Antwerpen has a memorable origin. One popular story says it comes from Dutch words hand ('hand') and werpen ('to throw') after a hero cut off a giant's hand and threw it into the river, and you can still see a statue of the thrown hand in the city. The name also includes a part like English wharf ('dock'), which reminds us that Antwerpen is an important port.
