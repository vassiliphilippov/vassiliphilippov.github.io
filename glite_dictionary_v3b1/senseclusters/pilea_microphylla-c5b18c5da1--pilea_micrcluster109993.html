Etymology

Pilea microphylla is a small, stingless plant that often releases pollen in a sudden little 'pop' or 'burst', a surprising action gardeners notice. The bold part microphylla hints at its very small leaves, so the image of tiny leaves plus a quick pollen 'pop' helps you remember this plant.
