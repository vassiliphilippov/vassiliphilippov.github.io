Etymology

scutching machine comes from the old way called scutching, when workers would beat and scrape flax or cotton to free the soft fibers from the hard parts of the plant. The machine did that same beating and scraping faster, so the name stuck and today scutching machine means 'a machine that separates the fibers from their hard parts'.
