Etymology

hog sucker: The name hog sucker comes from the fish's big, pig-like mouth, which suggests hog, and its wide lips that cling to rocks like a sucker. So the name helps you remember it is a freshwater 'fish' with a large mouth and sucker-like lips that feed on stream bottoms.
