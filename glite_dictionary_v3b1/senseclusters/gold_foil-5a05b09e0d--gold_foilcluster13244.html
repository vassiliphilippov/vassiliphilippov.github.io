Etymology

gold foil comes from the practice of hammering real gold into very thin foil sheets that look like 'thin metal paper' you can use to cover objects. Because people used these delicate sheets to decorate frames, art, or even to cover teeth and use in experiments, the phrase now means those very thin sheets of gold used for decoration and science.
