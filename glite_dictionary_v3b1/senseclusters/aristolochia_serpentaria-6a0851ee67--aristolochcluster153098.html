Etymology

Aristolochia serpentaria gets its name from the second word serpentaria, which points to snakes. One popular story says herbalists thought the root looked like a small snake and used the plant to treat 'snakebites'. That's why it is also called 'Virginia snakeroot' and why the name links the plant to snakes.
