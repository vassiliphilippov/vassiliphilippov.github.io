Etymology

Euclidean axiom comes from the Greek mathematician Euclid; Euclidean refers to his style of geometry and axiom means a simple, obvious rule. These clear rules were used as building blocks in geometry, so Euclidean axiom now means 'one of the basic assumptions' used in Euclidean geometry.
