Etymology

The name white cypress probably comes from the tree's pale bark or light-coloured wood and because it looks like a cypress, so white shows the colour and cypress shows the kind of 'tree'. That's why the name refers to a slow-growing, hardy 'tree' often used as a windbreak or in gardens.
