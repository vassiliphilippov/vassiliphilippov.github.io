Etymology

The term Alpine scurvy may come from images of mountain travellers who, after long trips with little fresh food, showed red skin, stomach trouble and confusion that people compared to scurvy. That's why Alpine scurvy now means 'a disease from a lack of certain nutrients' that causes skin, digestion and mental problems.
