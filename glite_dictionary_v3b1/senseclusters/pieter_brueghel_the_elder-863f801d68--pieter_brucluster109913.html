Etymology

The painter Pieter Brueghel the Elder got the Elder because he had a son with the same name, so people used the Elder to show he was 'the older artist'. He became famous for painting busy village scenes filled with many small people, so when you see Pieter Brueghel the Elder it means the original, well-known artist who painted lively peasant life.
