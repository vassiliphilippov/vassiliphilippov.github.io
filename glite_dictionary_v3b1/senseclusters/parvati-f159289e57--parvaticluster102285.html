Etymology

Parvati comes from the Sanskrit name Pārvatī, which means 'daughter of the mountain' and comes from parvata 'mountain'. Because she is the mountain's daughter, people see her as a caring mother who brings family blessings and plenty, which is why Parvati is worshipped as a goddess of love, devotion and abundance.
