Etymology

cabin cruiser comes from two words: cabin meaning 'a small house on a boat' and cruiser meaning 'a boat that travels for pleasure'. The image is a motorboat with a little house on it that can travel, so cabin cruiser means 'a motorboat with a cabin for living'.
