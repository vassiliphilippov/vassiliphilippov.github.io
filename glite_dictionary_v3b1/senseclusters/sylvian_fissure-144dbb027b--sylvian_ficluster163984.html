Etymology

Sylvian fissure is named after a 17th-century doctor, Franciscus Sylvius, and the word fissure literally means 'deep groove'. So, because Sylvius described the deep crack on the side of the brain that separates the front and side parts, the name helps you remember it is the deep groove that divides those brain areas.
