Etymology

megacycle comes from the parts mega- and cycle. The mega- part means 'large' and in measurement it means 'a million', and cycle means 'a single turn or wave', like in bicycle where it means 'wheel'. So, a megacycle is 'a million cycles per second', which is why radios and electronics used this unit.
