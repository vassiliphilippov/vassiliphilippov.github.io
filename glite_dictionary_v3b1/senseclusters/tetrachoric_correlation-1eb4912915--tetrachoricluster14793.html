Etymology

tetrachoric correlation may come from tetra and choric, giving the image of a table split into 'four' boxes: when you turn two answers into 'yes' or 'no', you get four cells. It estimates the hidden link behind those two 'yes/no' splits, so it helps us guess how strongly the original things are related from only two groups.
