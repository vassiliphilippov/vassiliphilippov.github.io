Etymology

fibrocystic breast disease comes from its parts: fibro (firm breast tissue) and cystic (small fluid pockets). This picture explains why the breasts can feel 'lumpy' and sometimes 'hurt', so the name describes a common condition that is usually 'not cancer'.
