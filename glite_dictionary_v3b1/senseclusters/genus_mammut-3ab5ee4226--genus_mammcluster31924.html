Etymology

genus Mammut gets its name from a word early people used for very large, elephant-like animals they found in the ground. One popular story says explorers heard a local word like 'mamut' for the big buried bones, so scientists later used Mammut as the name for the group of extinct animals we call 'mastodons' today.
