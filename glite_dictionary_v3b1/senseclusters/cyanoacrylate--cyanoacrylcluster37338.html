Etymology

Cyanoacrylate comes from two parts: cyano-, which means 'blue' and reminds us of cyan, and acrylate, the same root as acrylic, as in acrylic paint. So, the name points to the chemical type, and cyanoacrylate is the technical name for the very fast, strong 'super glue' used to join things or sometimes to close small wounds.
