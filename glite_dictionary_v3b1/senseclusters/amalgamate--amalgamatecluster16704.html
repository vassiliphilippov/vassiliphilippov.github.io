Etymology

amalgamate comes from amalgam, a word for a 'mixture' or soft paste used in old chemistry, from Arabic al-malgham meaning 'the mixture'. So to amalgamate is to mix parts together into one single thing, which is why we use it for joining companies, groups, or substances.
