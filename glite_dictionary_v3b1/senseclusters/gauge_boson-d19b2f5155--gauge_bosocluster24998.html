Etymology

The term gauge boson combines gauge, a word for a tool or rule for 'measuring', and boson, a group of particles named after the scientist Bose. Physicists used 'gauge' to mean a special rule about how forces work, so a gauge boson is a particle that follows those rules and carries a force between other particles.
