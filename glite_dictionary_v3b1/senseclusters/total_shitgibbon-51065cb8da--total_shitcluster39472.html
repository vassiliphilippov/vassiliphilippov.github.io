Etymology

total shitgibbon may come from joining the rude word shit, meaning 'very bad' or 'rubbish', with gibbon, a small ape that can look loud and silly. Imagine a messy, noisy ape acting rude — that's the picture people use, so calling someone total shitgibbon says they are 'foolish' or 'obnoxious'.
