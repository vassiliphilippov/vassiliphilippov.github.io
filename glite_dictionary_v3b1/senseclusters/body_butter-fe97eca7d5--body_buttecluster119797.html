Etymology

The compound noun body butter was named because these products are very thick and smooth, like real butter, and they are made for the body. One popular idea is that calling it 'butter' gives the image of a rich, 'thick cream' that helps keep skin soft, so body butter today means a heavy, nourishing skin cream.
