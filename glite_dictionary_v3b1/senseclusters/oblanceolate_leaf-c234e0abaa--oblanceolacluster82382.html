Etymology

oblanceolate leaf comes from the parts ob- and lanceolate, where ob- means 'reverse' and lanceolate comes from the word 'lance', a spear. Imagine a spear turned the other way so the leaf is wider at the tip and narrower at the base, and that's why the term describes a leaf broader near the tip.
