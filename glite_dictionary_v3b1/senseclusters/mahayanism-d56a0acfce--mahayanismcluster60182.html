Etymology

Mahayanism comes from Mahayana, a Sanskrit word made of maha- 'great' and yana 'vehicle'. Think of it as a 'great vehicle' that aims to carry many people to become buddhas, which is why Mahayanism emphasizes the bodhisattva path and helping all beings.
