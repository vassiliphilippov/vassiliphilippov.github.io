Etymology

The word terbinafine is a modern medicine name; a helpful way to remember it is to notice the ending -fine, seen in some drug names, and the start terbina-, which sounds like 'turpentine', a strong natural oil once used against germs and fungi. So, terbinafine is an antifungal drug used to treat skin and nail 'fungus'.
