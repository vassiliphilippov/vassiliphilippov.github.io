Etymology

witnesseth is the old-fashioned form of witness plus the ending -eth. The -eth ending is like today's 's' in 'witnesses', and witness comes from wit meaning 'knowledge', so witnesseth means 'proves' or 'shows as proof', which is why it appears in legal papers.
