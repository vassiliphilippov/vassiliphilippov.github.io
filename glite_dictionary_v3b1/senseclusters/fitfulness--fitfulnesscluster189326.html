Etymology

fitfulness comes from fit, which here means 'a sudden short burst', as in a 'fit of coughing' or 'fits and starts', and the parts -ful and -ness make it 'the state of having many fits'. So, fitfulness means doing something in sudden, irregular bursts rather than steadily.
