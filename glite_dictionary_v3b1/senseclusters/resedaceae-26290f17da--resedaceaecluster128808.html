Etymology

Resedaceae comes from the plant name Reseda, a Latin word meaning 'to soothe', because mignonette and similar herbs were long used for their calming smell and skin uses. The ending -aceae is the usual way to name a plant family, as in Rosaceae, so Resedaceae simply means 'the Reseda family' of small, often fragrant herbs.
