Etymology

The term pinko faction comes from a color image: pinko uses 'pink' to show someone leans a little toward the 'left' (not fully 'red' or 'communist'), and faction means a small group inside a party. So pinko faction was used as an insult for a group with 'left' or 'communist' ideas.
