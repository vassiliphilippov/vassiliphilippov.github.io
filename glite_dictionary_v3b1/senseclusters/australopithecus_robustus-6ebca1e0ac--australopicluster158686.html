Etymology

Australopithecus robustus is a scientific name made of two parts: Australopithecus meaning 'southern ape' and robustus meaning 'strong'. Fossils with big teeth and powerful jaws were found in southern Africa, so the name shows it was a heavily built, ape-like species that lived long ago.
