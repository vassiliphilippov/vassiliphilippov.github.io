Etymology

The noun Pterocles indicus comes from old Greek words that point to Pterocles meaning 'wing' and indicus meaning 'from India'. Imagine a strong-winged sandgrouse flying far to find water in dry land; that picture helps you remember this bird is a winged species from India that lives in arid places.
