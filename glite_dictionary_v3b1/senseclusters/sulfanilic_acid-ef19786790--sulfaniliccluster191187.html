Etymology

sulfanilic acid comes from the chemical parts behind the name: sulfa points to a sulfur-containing 'acid' group and anilic comes from the word 'aniline', the simple organic chemical it is made from. So the name literally says it is an 'aniline' with a sulfa acid group, which fits its role as an acid used in dyes and lab tests.
