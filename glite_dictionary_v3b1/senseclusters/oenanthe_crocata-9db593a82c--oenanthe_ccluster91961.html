Etymology

The plant name Oenanthe crocata may come from old Greek and Latin words: Oenanthe is linked to a word for 'wine' because some related plants smell like wine, and crocata means 'yellow' for its yellow juice. Because that yellow sap and the thick root are deadly if eaten, the name points to a dangerous, poisonous plant.
