Etymology

organic brain syndrome comes from doctors using the words organic, brain, and syndrome: organic means 'physical' or part of the body, brain is the organ, and syndrome is a 'group of symptoms'. So the phrase means a mental problem caused by a physical injury or disease of the brain.
