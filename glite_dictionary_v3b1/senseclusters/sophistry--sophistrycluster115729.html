Etymology

The word sophistry comes from Greek roots like sophist and sophia, which mean 'wise' or 'wisdom', and the same root appears in philosophy ('love of wisdom'). Long ago a sophist was a teacher who sometimes used clever arguments to trick people, so sophistry now means clever-sounding but false or misleading reasoning.
