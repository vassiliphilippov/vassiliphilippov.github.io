Etymology

edicule comes from the Latin aedicula, a small form of aedes, which means 'building' or 'temple'. Think of it as a tiny 'house' or 'shrine' inside a larger building, and the same root appears in edifice, so an edicule is a small architectural frame that shelters a statue or relic.
