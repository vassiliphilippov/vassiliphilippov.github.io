Etymology

Japanese poinsettia may come from people comparing this bright plant to the familiar poinsettia, while adding Japanese to suggest a 'decorative' or 'exotic' look. So the name helps you remember it is a showy, poinsettia-like plant rather than meaning it comes from Japan.
