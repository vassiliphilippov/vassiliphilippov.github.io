Etymology

fetoscope has two parts: feto- ('unborn baby') and -scope ('to look' or 'observe'), the same -scope in telescope and microscope. So, a fetoscope is a tool to check or examine the unborn baby, here by placing it on the mother's belly to listen to the heartbeat.
