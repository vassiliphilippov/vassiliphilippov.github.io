Etymology

restorativeness comes from the simple verb restore, which means 'to bring back' or make someone healthy again, and from the word restorative, which means 'helping someone recover'. So, restorativeness is the quality of bringing back health, energy, or calm, which is why we use it for sleep, a walk, or soothing tea.
