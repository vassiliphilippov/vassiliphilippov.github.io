Etymology

Kemal Pasha was the name people used for Mustafa Kemal Atatürk. The word Pasha was an old Ottoman title, like a 'general' or 'governor', and Kemal is his name. He led the country after the empire fell and set up a modern, non-religious government, so the name now reminds people of the leader who made those big changes.
