Etymology

The word Pitot comes from the French engineer Henri Pitot, who invented a small L-shaped tube to measure the speed of water. Think of a little 'straw' held against the flow of air or water: a Pitot tube measures how fast the fluid moves, so the name now means a tool for measuring fluid speed.
