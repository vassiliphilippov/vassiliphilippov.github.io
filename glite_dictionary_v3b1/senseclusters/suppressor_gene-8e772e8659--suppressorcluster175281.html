Etymology

suppressor gene gives a clear image: think of a tiny guard or brake inside a cell—suppressor means to hold back and gene means a small instruction. That's why a suppressor gene 'stops' or 'reduces' another gene's harmful effects, so if it is lost harmful growth can happen.
