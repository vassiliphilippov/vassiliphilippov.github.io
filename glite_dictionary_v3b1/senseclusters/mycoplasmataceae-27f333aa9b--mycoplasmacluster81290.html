Etymology

Mycoplasmataceae breaks into parts: myco- meaning 'fungus', plasma meaning 'formed', and the ending -aceae meaning 'family'. These tiny organisms were once thought to be fungus-like because of their shape, so the name helps you remember they are a 'family' of unusual, formed microbes.
