Etymology

breach of trust with fraudulent intent is a legal phrase that paints a simple picture: someone is given money, keys, or goods (the trust) but then breaks that promise (a breach) and plans to cheat (the fraudulent intent) by taking or selling them. So, it means 'to steal property you were trusted with'.
