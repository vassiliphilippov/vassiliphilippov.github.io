Etymology

ionization chamber gets its name from what the device does: radiation makes tiny charged bits inside a gas-filled chamber, and the device detects those charges. So the parts ionization (making 'charged particles') and chamber (a closed space) explain why the phrase means 'a tool that measures radiation'.
