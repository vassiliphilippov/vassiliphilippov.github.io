Etymology

chicane comes from French where it meant a 'trick' or a 'small problem' in a game, and card players used it for difficult hands; in bridge a chicane is a hand with no trumps, which makes play tricky. So, the name links the idea of a 'trick' or awkward position to this special kind of hand.
