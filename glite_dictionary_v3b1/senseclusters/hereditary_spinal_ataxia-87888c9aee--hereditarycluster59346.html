Etymology

hereditary spinal ataxia is a name made from simple parts: hereditary means 'runs in the family', spinal refers to the spine, and ataxia means 'loss of coordination'. So the term describes a condition passed down in families that affects the spine and causes trouble walking and poor coordination.
