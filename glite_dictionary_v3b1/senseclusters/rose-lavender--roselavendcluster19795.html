Etymology

rose-lavender comes from the names of two flowers, rose and lavender, where rose suggests a soft 'pink' and lavender suggests a gentle 'pale purple' and a light scent. So rose-lavender describes a color that mixes the 'pink' of a rose with the 'pale purple' of lavender.
