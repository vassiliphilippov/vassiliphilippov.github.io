Etymology

offshore drilling rig gives a clear picture: offshore means 'away from the shore, at sea' and a drilling rig is a big machine that drills into the ground. So imagine a huge platform and heavy machines standing out on the water, drilling down through the sea floor to 'get oil or gas', which is why the phrase means a large platform at sea used to get those resources.
