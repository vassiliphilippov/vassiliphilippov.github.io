Etymology

covenant of salt may come from an old practice of sprinkling salt on agreements or offerings to seal them. Because salt keeps food from going bad, the image of salt made a promise seem 'lasting' and 'unbreakable', so the phrase came to mean a promise that would last and show strong 'loyalty'.
