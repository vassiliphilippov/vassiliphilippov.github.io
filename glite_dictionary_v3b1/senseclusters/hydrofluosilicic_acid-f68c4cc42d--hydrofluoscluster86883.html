Etymology

hydrofluosilicic acid comes from the name parts hydro-, fluo-, and silicic: hydro- points to 'water', fluo- to 'fluorine', and silicic to 'silicon' (the element in sand). So the name literally says an acid of fluorine and silicon in water, which fits why this is a strong, corrosive acid used in water treatment and industrial chemistry.
