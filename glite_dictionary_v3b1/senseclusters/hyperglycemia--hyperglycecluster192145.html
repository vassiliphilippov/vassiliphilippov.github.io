Etymology

The word hyperglycemia breaks into hyper- meaning 'over' or 'too much', as in hyperactive, and glyc- meaning 'sweet' or 'sugar', seen in glucose. So hyperglycemia means 'too much sugar in the blood', which is why it names the condition of high blood glucose.
