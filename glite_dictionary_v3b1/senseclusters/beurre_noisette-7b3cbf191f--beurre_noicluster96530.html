Etymology

beurre noisette comes from French cooking: you heat beurre, or butter, until the little bits in it turn light brown and smell like noisette, which means 'hazelnut'. So beurre noisette refers to butter cooked until it is brown and 'nutty' in taste, used as a sauce or to add extra flavor.
