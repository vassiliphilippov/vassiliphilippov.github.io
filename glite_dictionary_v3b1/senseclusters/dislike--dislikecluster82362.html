Etymology

dislike as a noun names the feeling of 'not liking' someone or something and comes from dis- ('not') + like ('to like'), so a dislike is simply that 'not liking' feeling.

Related Terms

  • degree higher: aversion - a strong dislike or disinclination
  • degree higher: hatred - intense or passionate dislike
  • antonym: like - a feeling of approval or enjoyment
