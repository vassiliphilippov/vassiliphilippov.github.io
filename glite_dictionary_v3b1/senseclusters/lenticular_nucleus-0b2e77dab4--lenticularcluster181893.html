Etymology

lenticular nucleus comes from Latin words: lenticular from a word for a 'lentil' or small 'lens', and nucleus meaning 'core'. The name creates a simple picture; this part of the brain looks like a small lens or bean, so it refers to that lens-shaped 'core' involved in controlling movement.
