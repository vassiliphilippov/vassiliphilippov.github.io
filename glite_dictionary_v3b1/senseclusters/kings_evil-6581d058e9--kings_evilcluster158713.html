Etymology

king's evil comes from the old belief that the king could heal a swollen neck disease called 'scrofula' by laying on hands and touching the sick. Because people thought the evil (the illness) was linked to the king's touch, the disease became known as the king's evil. So today, king's evil refers to that old disease, 'scrofula'.
