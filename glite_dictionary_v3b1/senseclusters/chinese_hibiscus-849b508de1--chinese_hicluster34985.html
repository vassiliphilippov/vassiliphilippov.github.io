Etymology

Chinese hibiscus: The name Chinese hibiscus comes from where the plant was first known in Europe; Chinese means 'from China' and hibiscus is an old word for big, showy flowers. So Chinese hibiscus tells you it is a hibiscus plant 'from China' that has large, bright blooms.
