Etymology

Foraminifera comes from the Latin parts foramen meaning 'hole' and -fera meaning 'to carry'. So the name is like 'hole-bearers' and helps you picture these tiny sea creatures with shells full of small holes, which is why their fossils help geologists date rocks.
