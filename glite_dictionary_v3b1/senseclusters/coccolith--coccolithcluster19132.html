Etymology

coccolith comes from the Greek parts kokkos ('grain' or 'berry') and lithos ('stone'), so it literally means a tiny round 'stone' or 'plate'. So, a coccolith is a very small round 'plate' made by certain algae, which is why the word is used for the tiny calcium plates found in ocean sediments.
