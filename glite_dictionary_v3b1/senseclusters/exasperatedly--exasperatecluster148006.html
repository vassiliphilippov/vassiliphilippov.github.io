Etymology

exasperatedly comes from exasperate, which joins ex- meaning 'completely' and asper meaning 'rough', so it originally meant 'to make rough' or to wear down someone's patience. So, to act exasperatedly is to show strong annoyance as if your patience has been rubbed away.
