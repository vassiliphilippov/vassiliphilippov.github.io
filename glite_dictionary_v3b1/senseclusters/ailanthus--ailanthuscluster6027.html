Etymology

ailanthus comes from an old Asian name often written as ailanto, which people have called the tree and which is said to mean 'tree of heaven'; a helpful way to remember it is to picture this fast‑growing, tough street tree reaching up like a 'heavenly' tree, which matches its rapid growth and adaptability.
