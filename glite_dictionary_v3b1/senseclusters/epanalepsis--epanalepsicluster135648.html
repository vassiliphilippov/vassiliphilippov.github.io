Etymology

The word epanalepsis comes from Greek parts epan- meaning 'again' and -lepsis meaning 'taking', so it means 'taking up again' and is like the idea 'repeat'. That's why epanalepsis names a kind of repetition where the same word or phrase appears at the beginning and at the end of a sentence to add emphasis.
