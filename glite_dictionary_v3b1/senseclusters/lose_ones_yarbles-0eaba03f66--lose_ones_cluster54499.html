Etymology

lose one's yarbles is probably a playful twist on 'lose one's marbles'. One popular story says yarbles was a cheeky word for small round things and sometimes for testicles, so the image of losing them feels like losing what keeps you steady, and that's why it means 'to become mentally unstable'.
