Etymology

The noun heterocercal fin comes from Greek parts hetero- and -cercal, which mean 'different' and 'tail'. The phrase gives a simple image: a tail with a bigger top than bottom, so today heterocercal fin names the kind of tail, as in sharks, that helps the fish lift itself while it swims.
