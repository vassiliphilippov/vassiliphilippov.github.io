Etymology

Cushing's syndrome comes from the American surgeon Harvey Cushing, who first showed that a set of signs was caused when a small gland in the brain made too much of the hormone 'cortisol'. Because he linked common problems such as weight gain, a round face and weak muscles to one cause, the name now tells doctors that the condition is caused by too much 'cortisol'.
