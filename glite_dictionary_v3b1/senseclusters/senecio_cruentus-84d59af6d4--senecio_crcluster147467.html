Etymology

Senecio cruentus comes from old Latin words: Senecio means 'old man' because related plants have white, beard-like hairs on their seed heads, and cruentus means 'blood-red' for its bright red flowers. So the name helps you picture a daisy-like plant with white, hairy seeds and red blooms.
