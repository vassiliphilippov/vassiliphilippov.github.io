Etymology

agricultural practice paints a simple picture: agricultural means farming and practice means a regular way of doing things, like a farmer plowing fields, sowing seeds, or rotating crops. So the phrase now refers to the 'methods' or 'techniques' farmers use to grow crops or look after animals.
