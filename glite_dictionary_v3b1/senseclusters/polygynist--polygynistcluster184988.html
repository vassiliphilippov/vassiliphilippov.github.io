Etymology

Polygynist comes from Greek parts poly- meaning 'many' and gyn- meaning 'woman', so a polygynist is a man who has two or more wives. You can remember poly- from polygon ('many' sides) and gyn- from gynecology ('care of women'), which helps explain the word's meaning.
