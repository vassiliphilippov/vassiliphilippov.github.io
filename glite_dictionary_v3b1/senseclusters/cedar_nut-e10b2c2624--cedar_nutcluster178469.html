Etymology

cedar nut likely comes from people calling some pine trees a kind of cedar and naming the small edible seeds from those trees nuts; one popular story says traders sold these seeds as a tasty 'snack' and for 'cooking', so the name stuck and now cedar nut means the edible seed used in food.
