Etymology

hard cider gets its name from the simple idea that apple juice, called cider, was left to turn into alcohol and the word hard was added to show it was 'alcoholic' rather than a 'sweet' apple drink. That's why hard cider means an alcoholic drink made by fermenting apple juice.
