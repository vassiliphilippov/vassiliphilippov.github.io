Etymology

The compound name lobster butter comes from a simple kitchen idea: cooks mix real lobster or cook lobster shells in melted butter to make a rich, lobster-flavored spread. So, lobster butter today means butter blended with lobster meat or lobster flavor and used as a 'spread' or to 'finish' dishes.
