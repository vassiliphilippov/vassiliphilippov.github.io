Etymology

The word cyclosporine comes from parts meaning cyclo- 'circle' and spore 'spore', because the medicine was first found in a spore-making fungus and its active part is ring-shaped. So the name points to a fungal, ring-shaped drug that lowers the immune response to help prevent organ rejection after a transplant.
