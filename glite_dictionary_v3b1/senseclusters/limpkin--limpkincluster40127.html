Etymology

limpkin is easy to remember if you break it into limp (to 'walk unevenly') and the little ending -kin (meaning 'small'). A helpful way to remember the bird is to picture a small, long-billed wader that looks a bit awkward on land and gives a loud, mournful call, so the name fits its ungainly walk and sound.
