Etymology

enceinte comes from French and from the Latin parts in- 'in' and cingere 'to gird' or 'to surround'. Think of a belt or band around the belly — so enceinte originally meant 'encircled' and then came to mean 'pregnant', which helps you remember its modern meaning.
