Etymology

Adipogenic comes from adip(o) meaning 'fat' and -genic meaning 'producing', the same idea you see in 'generate' or 'genesis'. So adipogenic means 'coming from fat tissue', which is why doctors use it for growths or tumors that start in fat cells.
