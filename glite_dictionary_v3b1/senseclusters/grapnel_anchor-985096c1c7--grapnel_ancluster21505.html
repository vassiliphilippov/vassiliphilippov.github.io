Etymology

Grapnel anchor gets its name from its shape and action: it is a small anchor with several hooked arms, like a tiny grappling hook that 'grabs' rocks or weed on the sea bottom. That's why a grapnel anchor is used to catch the bottom and hold small boats in place.
