Etymology

Shavuot comes from the Hebrew word shavua, which means 'week', so Shavuot means 'weeks' because the holiday comes after seven weeks of counting the Omer. That's why Shavuot marks the end of that count and remembers the giving of the Ten Commandments to Moses on Mount Sinai.
