Etymology

endogamy comes from the Greek parts endo- meaning 'within' and -gamy from gamos meaning 'marriage', and you can see the same -gamy in monogamy and polygamy. So, endogamy means marrying only 'within' your own group, which helps keep a community's traditions and family ties.
