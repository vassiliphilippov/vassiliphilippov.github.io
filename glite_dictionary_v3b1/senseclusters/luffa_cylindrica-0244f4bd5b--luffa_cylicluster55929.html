Etymology

Luffa cylindrica may come from an old Arabic word for 'sponge' because when the gourd dries its inside becomes a stringy sponge used to scrub. The second word, cylindrica, points to the long, tube-like shape of the fruit, so the name helps you remember that this gourd becomes a natural 'sponge'.
