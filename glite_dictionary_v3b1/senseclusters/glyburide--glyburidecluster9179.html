Etymology

glyburide combines the scientific prefix gly-, which means 'sugar' and appears in words like glucose, with the drug ending -buride, so the name hints that it is a medicine for blood sugar. That's why glyburide is used to help lower blood sugar in people with type 2 diabetes.
