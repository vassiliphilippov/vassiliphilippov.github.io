Etymology

Ammonium chloride gets its name from two parts: ammonium, from the old name 'sal ammoniac' for a salt found near the temple of Amun in Egypt, and chloride, meaning a salt that contains chlorine. So the name gives the simple picture of a white 'salt' made from the ammonium part and chlorine, which fits why ammonium chloride is a white salt used in medicine and in some batteries.
