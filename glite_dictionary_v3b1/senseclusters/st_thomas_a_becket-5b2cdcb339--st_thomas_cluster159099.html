Etymology

St. Thomas a Becket was Thomas Becket, a 12th-century Archbishop of Canterbury who was killed in his cathedral after he stood up to King Henry II. People believed he had 'died for the church' and called him a 'saint', so many pilgrims visited his shrine and his name is still used today.
