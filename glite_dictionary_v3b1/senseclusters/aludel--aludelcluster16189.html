Etymology

aludel comes from Arabic roots, with the familiar Arabic article al- meaning 'the', and the name was used for a special jar used by alchemists, just like the word alchemy also keeps that al-. So an aludel is literally 'the alchemist's jar', which is why the word means a pear-shaped vessel used for distillation.
