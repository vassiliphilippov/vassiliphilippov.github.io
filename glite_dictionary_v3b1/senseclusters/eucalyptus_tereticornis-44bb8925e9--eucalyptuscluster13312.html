Etymology

Eucalyptus tereticornis gets its name from old Greek and Latin words. The word Eucalyptus means 'well-covered' and points to the small cap that covers the flower buds, and tereticornis means 'rounded horn', describing the round, horn-like shape of the buds or fruit, so the full name describes the tree's covered, horn-shaped buds.
