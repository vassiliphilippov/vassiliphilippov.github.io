Etymology

Dalmatian pyrethrum is named for where it grows and what it does: Dalmatian shows it comes from Dalmatia, and pyrethrum comes from an old word meaning 'fire' because people used the plant like 'fire' to attack insects. That's why today the name refers to a plant used to repel or kill insects.
