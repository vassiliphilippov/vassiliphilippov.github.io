Etymology

genius loci comes from Latin and literally means the 'spirit' of a place, with genius meaning 'spirit' and loci meaning 'of the place'. Imagine a quiet old library or a busy market where the place itself seems to have a mood, and that's why the phrase means the special atmosphere of a place.
