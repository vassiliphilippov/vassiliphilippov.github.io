Etymology

Hibiscus sabdariffa may come from old local words for its sour, red cups; Hibiscus names the flower and sabdariffa may be a local name for the edible cup. Those bright, cup-like parts are used to make 'red tea', jams and a strong fiber, so the name points to this useful tropical plant.
