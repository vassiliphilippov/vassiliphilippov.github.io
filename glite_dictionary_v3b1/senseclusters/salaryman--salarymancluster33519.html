Etymology

salaryman comes from the English words salary ('regular pay') and man, and it was used in Japan to name a company employee who earns a fixed wage. A helpful way to remember it is to think 'a man who gets a salary', which explains why salaryman refers to the loyal office worker who often works long hours in Japan today.
