Etymology

Boy's Own tale comes from old magazines like the 'Boy's Own Paper' that printed short stories of brave boys, treasure hunts and daring rescues. Because those pages showed brave, exciting action, the phrase now means 'an exciting, old-fashioned adventure story'.
