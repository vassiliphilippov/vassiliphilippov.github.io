Etymology

Burhinidae comes from the scientific name Burhinus, which is made from Greek words bous 'ox' and rhinos 'nose', plus the family ending -idae. So it means 'ox-nosed', a simple image to help you remember these large wading birds called stone-curlews with strong, thick beaks that live in warm places.
