Etymology

genital wart is a plain, literal picture: wart is the common word for a small, rough 'bump' on the skin, and genital shows it is on the 'sex organs' or the area near the anus. So genital wart simply means a small skin growth that appears on or near the genitals, often caused by a common virus.
