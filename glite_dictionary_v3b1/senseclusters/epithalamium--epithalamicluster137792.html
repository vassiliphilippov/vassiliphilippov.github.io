Etymology

epithalamium comes from Greek parts epi- 'upon' and thalamos 'bridal chamber', a bit like the English word 'bedroom'. In ancient times people sang these poems outside the bridal chamber, so an epithalamium is a poem to celebrate the bride and groom.
