Etymology

greedy bankster comes from the made-up word bankster, which mixes the words 'banker' and 'gangster' to paint a picture of a banker acting like a criminal. The word greedy adds the idea of someone who wants more money than is fair, so the phrase is used for a banker seen as dishonest or exploitative, meaning 'a dishonest or corrupt banker'.
