Etymology

sardonically comes from the Greek word sardanios and a story about Sardinia: people said a local plant caused a bitter 'grin' or 'laugh' before death. So, to speak sardonically is to use a bitter, mocking smile or voice, which is why the word means showing mockery or bitter sarcasm.
