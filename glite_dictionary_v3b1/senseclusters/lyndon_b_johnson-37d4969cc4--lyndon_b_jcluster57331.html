Etymology

Lyndon B. Johnson was the 36th President of the United States and is often called 'LBJ' from his initials. He became president after John F. Kennedy was shot in 1963 and signed big laws like the 'Civil Rights Act', so his name usually brings to mind that leader and those changes.
