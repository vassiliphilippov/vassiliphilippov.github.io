Etymology

The plant cape forget-me-not is named for the Cape area of South Africa, where it comes from, and the old flower name forget-me-not, used for small blue flowers people gave to ask someone to 'remember' them. So the name points to a bright garden flower from the Cape that reminds people to 'remember'.
