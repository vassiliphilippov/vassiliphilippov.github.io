Etymology

Callithump is a playful made-up word from the Greek root kalli- meaning 'beautiful' and the English word thump meaning 'a loud hit'. A helpful way to remember it is to think of a 'beautiful thump' as a funny name for a noisy, lively parade, so callithump means a loud, rowdy celebration.
