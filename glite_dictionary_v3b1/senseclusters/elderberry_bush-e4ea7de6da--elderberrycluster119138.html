Etymology

elderberry bush is a literal name: it is a bush that grows elderberries, white flower clusters that turn into small dark berries. For a long time people used those berries and flowers to make 'syrup' and simple 'medicine', so the phrase points to a shrub known for its edible, helpful fruit.
