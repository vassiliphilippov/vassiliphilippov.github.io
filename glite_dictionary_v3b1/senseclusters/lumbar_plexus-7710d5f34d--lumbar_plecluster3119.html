Etymology

The noun lumbar plexus combines lumbar (meaning 'lower back') and plexus (a word for a braided 'network' of nerves). So imagine a small net of nerves in your lower back sending messages to your legs, and that's why lumbar plexus means the nerve network that serves the lower body.
