Etymology

proctoscopy comes from Greek parts procto- meaning 'rectum' and -scopy meaning 'to look'. So a proctoscopy is literally 'looking' into the rectum with a scope, like a microscope or telescope, which explains why it is a medical exam to see the rectum and the end of the colon.
