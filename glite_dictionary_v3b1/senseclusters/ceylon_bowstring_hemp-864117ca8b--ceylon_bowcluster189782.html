Etymology

Ceylon bowstring hemp gets its name from where it came from and how people used it: 'Ceylon' was the old name for Sri Lanka, and its strong fibers, like hemp, were used to make bowstrings and ropes. So the name reminds you it is a tropical plant grown for tough fiber and as a houseplant.
