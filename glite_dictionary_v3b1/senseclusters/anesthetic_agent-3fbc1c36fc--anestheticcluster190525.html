Etymology

The compound noun anesthetic agent comes from parts that mean 'without feeling' (anesthetic) and 'a thing that does it' (agent), which gives the clear image of a substance that makes part of the body stop feeling. That's why it refers to a drug used to numb or stop pain during medical procedures.
