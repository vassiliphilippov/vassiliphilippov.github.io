Etymology

The word Gavialidae comes from the Indian name gharial, a river crocodile with a very long thin snout. Gharial itself comes from the Hindi word ghara meaning 'pot' because male gharials have a pot-like bump on their nose, and the ending -idae marks an animal family, so Gavialidae means the family of these long-snouted crocodiles.
