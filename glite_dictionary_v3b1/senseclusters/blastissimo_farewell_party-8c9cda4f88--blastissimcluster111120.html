Etymology

blastissimo farewell party comes from joining the English word blast, meaning 'a lot of fun', with the Italian ending -issimo, meaning 'very', which creates a picture of a party that is extra big and exciting. So, the phrase means 'a very lively or extravagant goodbye party'.
