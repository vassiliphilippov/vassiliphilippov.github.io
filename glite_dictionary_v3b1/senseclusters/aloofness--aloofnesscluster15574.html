Etymology

aloofness comes from the sailing word aloof, made of a- 'on' and loof 'wind side', which described a ship kept 'away' from the shore—think of a boat staying at a distance. So, aloofness is the habit of keeping people or feelings at a distance, being emotionally or socially reserved.
