Etymology

MEMS is an abbreviation made from micro-, electro-, mechanical, and systems, with micro- meaning 'small' and mechanical meaning 'moving parts', so the name points to tiny machines with electrical parts. That's why MEMS as a noun means the technology or the little devices that sense, move, or control things at a very small scale.
