Etymology

AAA stands for abdominal aortic aneurysm, a name that tells you the problem: abdominal means 'belly', aortic means 'of the aorta', the body's main artery, and aneurysm means 'bulge'. So an AAA is a dangerous bulge in the aorta inside the belly that can burst and needs immediate medical care.
