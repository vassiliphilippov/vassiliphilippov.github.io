Etymology

The word Castanopsis comes from Greek parts castan- meaning 'chestnut' and -opsis meaning 'appearance' or 'look'. It was given to these trees because they remind people of chestnut trees, so Castanopsis is a helpful name meaning 'chestnut-like' that links the name to the tree's appearance.
