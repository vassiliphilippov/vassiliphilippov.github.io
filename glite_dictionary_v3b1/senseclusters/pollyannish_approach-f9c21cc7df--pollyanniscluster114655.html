Etymology

Pollyannish approach comes from the story character Pollyanna, a girl who always tried to find something good in every situation. Because Pollyanna often ignored problems by only seeing the bright side, a 'Pollyannish approach' now means being 'too optimistic' or 'ignoring negative things'.
