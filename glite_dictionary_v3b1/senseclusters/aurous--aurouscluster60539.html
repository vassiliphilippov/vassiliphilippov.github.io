Etymology

The word aurous comes from Latin aurum, which means 'gold', plus the English suffix -ous, meaning 'full of' or 'having'. In chemistry, -ous is used for the lower form of a metal, so aurous means 'containing gold' in its lower form, where the gold has a single positive charge, unlike auric, which is the higher form.
