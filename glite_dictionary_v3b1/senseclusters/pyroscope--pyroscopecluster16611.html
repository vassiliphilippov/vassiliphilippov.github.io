Synonyms

  • optical pyrometer
    0.00/m8.8

  • radiation pyrometer
    0.00/m8.9

  • resistance pyrometer

Etymology

Pyroscope comes from the Greek parts pyro- meaning 'fire' and -scope meaning 'to look', the same roots you see in telescope and microscope. So a pyroscope is like a 'look at fire' tool, which fits because it measures very high temperature by watching the color of light from a hot object.
