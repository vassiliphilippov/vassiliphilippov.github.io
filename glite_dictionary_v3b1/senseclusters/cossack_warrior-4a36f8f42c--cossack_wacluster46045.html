Etymology

The compound Cossack warrior comes from Cossacks, free soldiers who lived on wide grasslands and fought on horseback, known for riding fast and fighting bravely. The image of a lone horseman with a sword explains why Cossack + warrior now means a skilled, 'brave' horse soldier.
