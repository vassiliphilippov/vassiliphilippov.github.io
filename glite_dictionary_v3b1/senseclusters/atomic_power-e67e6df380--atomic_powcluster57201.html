Etymology

atomic power comes from the words atomic (about the atom, a very small piece of stuff) and power (energy). When scientists learned how to split an atom, a very large amount of energy came out, and people began using that energy to make electricity. So, atomic power now means 'electricity made by using the energy from atoms'.
