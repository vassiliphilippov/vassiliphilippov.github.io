Etymology

Vigna angularis is the scientific name for the small bush that gives the 'adzuki beans' used in Chinese and Japanese sweets. The first part Vigna names the plant group and the second part angularis means 'angled' and may refer to the plant's slightly angled seed pods, so the name helps you remember this plant is the source of those edible beans.
