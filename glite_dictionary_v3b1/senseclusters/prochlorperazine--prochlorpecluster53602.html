Etymology

prochlorperazine is a chemical name made of three parts: pro- (a common prefix meaning 'for' or 'before'), chlor- (from 'chlorine', showing a chlorine atom in the molecule), and -perazine (the name of the drug family). So, the word prochlorperazine tells you it is a specific man-made medicine in that family, which fits its use to treat serious mental illness and to stop nausea and vomiting.
