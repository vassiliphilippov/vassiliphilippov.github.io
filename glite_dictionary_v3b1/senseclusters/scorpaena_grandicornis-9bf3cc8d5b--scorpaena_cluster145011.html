Etymology

Scorpaena grandicornis gets its name from old words: Scorpaena comes from a word for 'scorpion' because the fish has sharp, stinging spines, and grandicornis means 'large horn' because it has big horn-like spines. So the name helps you remember that this sea fish is a scorpionfish with venomous, horn-like spines.
