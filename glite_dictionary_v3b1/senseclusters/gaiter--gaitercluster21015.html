Etymology

The word gaiter comes from an old French word for a leg covering, and a helpful way to remember it is to think of gait 'way of walking' because gaiters cover the lower part of the leg where you walk. So, gaiters protect your lower leg and keep mud, snow, and dirt out of your shoes or boots.
