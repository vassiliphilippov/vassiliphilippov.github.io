Etymology

Toxicodendron quercifolium comes from two parts: Toxicodendron meaning 'poison tree' and quercifolium meaning 'oak-leaf'. The name paints a simple picture — a poisonous shrub with oak-like leaves — and that fits because touching it can cause a painful skin rash.
