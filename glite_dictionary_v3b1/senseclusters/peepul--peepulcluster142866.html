Etymology

The word peepul comes from the Hindi name pipal and the older Sanskrit pippala, meaning 'sacred fig'. This is the same kind of 'fig' tree you may know, and because it is tied to temples and stories like Buddha finding enlightenment under one, peepul now refers to that large, holy fig tree.
