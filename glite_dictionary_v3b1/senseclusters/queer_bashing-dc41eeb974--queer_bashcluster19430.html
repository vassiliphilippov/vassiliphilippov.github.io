Etymology

The noun queer bashing joins queer, a rude word for LGBT people, and bashing, from the verb 'to bash' meaning 'to hit', creating the image of someone being physically attacked for being called 'queer'. That's why the phrase means violent attacks or abuse aimed at people because of who they love or their gender identity.
