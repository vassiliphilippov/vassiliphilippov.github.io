Etymology

Platanus wrightii is the scientific name for a medium-sized tree with big, deeply lobed leaves. The first word, Platanus, comes from a word meaning 'broad' because of the wide leaves, and the second word, wrightii, shows it was 'named after' a person called Wright who helped find or study the tree, so the name points to a broad-leaf tree linked to Wright.
