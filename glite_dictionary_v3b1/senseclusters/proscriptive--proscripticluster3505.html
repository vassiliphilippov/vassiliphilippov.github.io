Etymology

proscriptive comes from the Latin parts pro- and scribere, where scribere means 'to write' and the same root is in script and scribe. Long ago, officials would 'write' lists of actions that were not allowed, so proscriptive now means 'forbidding or restricting' actions or behavior.
