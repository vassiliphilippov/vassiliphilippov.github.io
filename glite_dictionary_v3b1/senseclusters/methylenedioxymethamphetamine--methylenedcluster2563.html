Etymology

methylenedioxymethamphetamine is a chemical name made of parts: methylene (a small chemical piece), dioxy (literally 'two' and 'oxygen'), and methamphetamine (the strong stimulant often called meth). So the long name tells you it is a man-made version of meth with extra oxygen parts, which is why it is a drug that gives strong energy and changes mood and feelings.
