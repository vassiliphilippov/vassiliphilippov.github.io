Etymology

overstrain comes from the parts over- meaning 'too much' and strain meaning 'to stretch' or 'pressure'. Imagine a rope pulled too tight or a muscle used too hard; that picture shows why overstrain as a noun means 'too much strain' that can cause damage or tiredness.
