Etymology

The word advertizement comes from Latin parts ad- meaning 'to' and vertere meaning 'turn', and you can see the same idea in advertise and advert. So, an advertizement is something that 'turns' people's attention toward a product, service, or event, which is why it is a public notice or announcement.
