Etymology

oxycodone starts with oxy-, from 'oxygen' to show an extra oxygen, and ends with -codone, from 'codeine', a pain medicine from opium. So, oxycodone is a stronger, oxygen-modified relative of codeine, which is why it is a powerful, doctor-prescribed pain medicine used to relieve moderate to severe pain.
