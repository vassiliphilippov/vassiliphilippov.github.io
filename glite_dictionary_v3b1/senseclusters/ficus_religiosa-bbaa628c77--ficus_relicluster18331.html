Etymology

Ficus religiosa, the fig tree, is famous because one popular story says Siddhartha Gautama sat under this tree and reached 'enlightenment', so people called it the 'sacred fig' or 'Bo tree'. That's why the tree is often planted near temples, is seen as holy, and is known for living a long time.
