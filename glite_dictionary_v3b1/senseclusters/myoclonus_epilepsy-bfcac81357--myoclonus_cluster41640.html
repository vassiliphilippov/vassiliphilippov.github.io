Etymology

myoclonus epilepsy combines myoclonus (sudden, brief muscle jerks) and epilepsy ('a condition with seizures'). The name paints a clear picture: people have quick, shock-like jerks together with seizure problems and often worsening memory or thinking, so the term refers to this kind of epilepsy that often runs in families.
