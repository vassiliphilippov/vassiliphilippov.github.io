Etymology

shapelessly comes from the parts shape + -less meaning 'without' and the adverb -ly meaning 'in a way'. So, to do something shapelessly is to do it 'in a way without a clear shape', like a sweater hanging loose or dough spreading on a tray, which explains the word's meaning today.
