Etymology

2nd lieutenant comes from the idea of being the 'second' officer who can 'stand in for' a leader. The word lieutenant meant someone who takes another person's place, so a 2nd lieutenant was the junior officer who helped the captain, and today it means the lowest officer who begins to lead soldiers.
