Etymology

jazz fusion comes from musicians mixing the free playing of jazz with the loud, electric sounds of rock and new keyboards in the 1970s, so the word fusion here means sounds melting together like a musical stew. That's why jazz fusion means a style that 'blends' jazz and rock and uses electric instruments.
