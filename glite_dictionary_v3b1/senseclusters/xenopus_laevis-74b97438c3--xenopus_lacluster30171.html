Etymology

Xenopus laevis is a scientific name made from old Greek and Latin words: Xenopus means 'strange foot' because of its clawed toes, and laevis means 'smooth' for its skin. That name refers to the African clawed frog scientists use in labs, so when you see Xenopus laevis think of a smooth, clawed lab frog.
