Etymology

tie-in comes from the simple verb tie ('to fasten') plus in ('into' or 'with'), so it literally means something that 'ties in' two parts together. Think of a metal plate or rope that you use to tie an extension to a wall or to join cables — that is why a tie-in is a connector or fastener used to join things.
