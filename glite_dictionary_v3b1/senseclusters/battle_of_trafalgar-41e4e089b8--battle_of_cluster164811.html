Etymology

The Battle of Trafalgar is named after Cape Trafalgar, a rocky point on the coast of Spain where, in 1805, British ships met the French and Spanish fleets. Admiral Nelson's ships broke the enemy line and won a great 'sea victory', so the name now refers to that famous win at sea.
