Etymology

hypophosphoric acid comes from the parts hypo-, which means 'less', and phosphoric, from 'phosphorus'. The name gives a simple image: it is a different kind of phosphoric acid with fewer oxygen atoms and two phosphorus atoms in each molecule, so chemists use this special name to show it is not the usual phosphoric acid.
