Etymology

Gum turpentine comes from the sticky gum, a kind of tree sap that runs from pine trees, and the strong-smelling oil 'turpentine' that is made from that sap. Because painters used this liquid to 'thin' paint and clean brushes, the name came to mean the fluid from pines used as a 'solvent'.
