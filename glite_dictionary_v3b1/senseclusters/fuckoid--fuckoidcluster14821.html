Etymology

The word fuckoid comes from the rude word fuck and the ending -oid, where -oid means 'like' or 'shape of', as in humanoid 'human-like'. So fuckoid is a strong insult for someone 'like' a fuck, meaning a very rude or obnoxious person, which is why people use it for someone who ruins a party.
