Etymology

aspis comes from ancient Greek aspis, where it was the name for a dangerous, often venomous snake and it gave English the shorter word asp. So aspis still refers to those deadly snakes, like the ones in stories about Cleopatra or the vipers of southern Europe and North Africa.
