Etymology

algarroba bean names the long, brown pod of the carob tree, full of sweet pulp and small beans. For a long time people fed the pods to animals and used the sweet pulp as a 'chocolate substitute', so the name means the pod used for 'animal feed' and as a sweet cooking ingredient.
