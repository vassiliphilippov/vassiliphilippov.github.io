Etymology

neurasthenia comes from Greek parts neur- meaning 'nerve' and -asthenia meaning 'weakness', so it originally meant 'weak nerves'. That's why the word was used for long-term tiredness and mental exhaustion that people once thought came from worn-out nerves.
