Etymology

yearningly comes from the verb yearn, which meant 'to desire' and is like the simple word long, as in 'to long for'. A helpful way to remember it is to imagine wanting something for a long time, almost like for a 'year', so to look or speak yearningly is to show a deep, lasting desire.
