Etymology

MRTA is a short name made from the first letters of the Spanish words Movimiento, Revolucionario, Túpac Amaru, and it means 'Túpac Amaru Revolutionary Movement'. The group took the name of the Andean rebel leader Túpac Amaru, so MRTA points to a Peruvian rebel group that wanted to fight the government.
