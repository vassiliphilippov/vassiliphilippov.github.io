Etymology

The name Tarsius syrichta may come from two simple ideas: tarsius from a word for the long ankle bones that help it 'jump', and syrichta from a word for its sharp, high 'call'. So the name shows a small, big-eyed primate that jumps on long feet and makes high calls.
