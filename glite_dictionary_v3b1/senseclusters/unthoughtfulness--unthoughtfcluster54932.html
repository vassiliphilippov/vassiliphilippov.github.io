Etymology

unthoughtfulness comes from simple parts you know: un- ('not'), thought ('thinking'), and -ness ('state' or 'quality'), and you can see the opposite idea in thoughtful. So, unthoughtfulness is the 'state of not thinking' or not considering things carefully before you act or speak.
