Etymology

Theophrastaceae is the plant family named after the ancient Greek writer Theophrastus, who studied plants and is often called the 'father of botany'. The ending -aceae marks a plant family, as in the Rosaceae, the rose family, so the name tells you this is a family of plants linked to Theophrastus.
