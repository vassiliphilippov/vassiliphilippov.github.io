Etymology

fumblingly comes from the verb fumble, which means 'to feel or search clumsily', plus the ending -ly that turns it into an adverb. Think of someone searching for keys with cold, shaking hands—that image explains why fumblingly means 'in a clumsy, awkward, or hesitant way'.
