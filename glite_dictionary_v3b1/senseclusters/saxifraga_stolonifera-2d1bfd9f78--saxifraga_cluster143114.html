Etymology

Saxifraga stolonifera may come from Latin words that describe the plant: Saxifraga means 'rock-breaker' because these small plants often grow in cracks in stones, and stolonifera means 'with runners' because it spreads by thin stems that run along the ground. So the name fits the plant today — a small garden plant that grows in rocky spots and makes new plants by sending out runners.
