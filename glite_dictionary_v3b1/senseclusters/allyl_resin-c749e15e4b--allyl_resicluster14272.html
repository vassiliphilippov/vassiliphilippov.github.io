Etymology

Allyl resin comes from the small chemical part allyl, first found in oil from garlic and onions, and the word resin, which means a sticky substance that hardens. So the name helps you picture a sticky material made from the allyl chemical, which explains why it is used as a strong 'adhesive' when cured.
