Etymology

Intermittent cramp uses two simple words: intermittent means 'coming and going' and cramp is a 'sudden tight muscle pain'. So the term paints the image of a muscle that tightens and then relaxes again and again, and that is why it means 'periodic muscle twitching or painful cramps'.
