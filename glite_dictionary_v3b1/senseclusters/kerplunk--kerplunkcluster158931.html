kerplunk

0.00/m9.9
An exclamation said to copy a sudden heavy splash or thud
interjection US / kərˈplʌŋk /

Examples

Kerplunk! It fell
The rock landed in the pond with a loud kerplunk that made everyone laugh.
As the book slipped off the shelf, it hit the floor with a sudden kerplunk.
He dropped his keys into the metal bucket and they fell with a distinct kerplunk.
Wow! Kerplunk!
Kerplunk! The water splashed everywhere when the rock hit.
Look out! That bucket is going to kerplunk!
Wow! That was a big kerplunk!
I didn't expect that kerplunk to be so loud!

Etymology

As an exclamation, kerplunk simply copies the sound of a big drop or splash, much like the small word plunk. So people say kerplunk to show a sudden 'heavy splash or thud'.
