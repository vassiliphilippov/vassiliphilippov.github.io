Etymology

The word dodginess comes from dodgy, which likely comes from the verb dodge, meaning 'to avoid' or 'move quickly away'. One helpful way to remember it is to imagine someone who keeps 'dodging' questions or rules and so seems risky and 'not trustworthy', so dodginess is the state of being suspicious or unreliable.
