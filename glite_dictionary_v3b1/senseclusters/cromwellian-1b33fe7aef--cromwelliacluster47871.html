Etymology

Cromwellian comes from the name Cromwell, after Oliver Cromwell, the English leader in the 1600s who was known for very 'strict' and 'plain' rules and way of life. So, to call something Cromwellian is to say it is severe, 'strict', or 'austere' in style, rules, or behaviour.
