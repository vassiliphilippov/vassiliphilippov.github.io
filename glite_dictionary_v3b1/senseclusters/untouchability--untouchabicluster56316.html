Etymology

untouchability comes from three parts: un- 'not', touch 'to make contact', and -ability 'the state of being'. A helpful way to remember it is that it literally means 'not touchable', and it was used especially in India for the practice where some people were kept away and not allowed to mix with others, so it means social exclusion.
