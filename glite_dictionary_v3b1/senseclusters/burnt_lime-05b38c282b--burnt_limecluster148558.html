Etymology

burnt lime comes from the simple picture of making it: people 'burn' or heat a white rock called limestone until it turns into a dry powder, so the words burnt and lime mean the lime made by burning. That's why burnt lime today means the white material used in mortar and cement that makes heat when mixed with water.
