Etymology

lightweight aerated concrete gets its name from the words lightweight and aerated: lightweight means 'lighter' and aerated means 'filled with air', so the concrete looks like a hard sponge with many tiny air pockets or light bits. Because it is lighter and keeps heat, builders use it to make lighter, warmer buildings.
