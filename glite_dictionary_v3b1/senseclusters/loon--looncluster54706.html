Etymology

loon as an insult means a foolish or 'crazy' person, and a helpful way to remember this is to think of the bird's wild, strange call and odd behaviour; people began using the bird's name to mean someone acting crazy or foolish. So calling someone a loon is a way to say they seem 'crazy' or very silly.
