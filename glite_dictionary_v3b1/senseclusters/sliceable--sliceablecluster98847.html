Etymology

sliceable comes from the common word slice, which means 'a thin part cut from a whole', and the ending -able, which means 'can be'. A helpful way to remember this sense is to think of a report or data as a cake you can cut into pieces: if it is sliceable, you can divide it into clear parts for analysis.
