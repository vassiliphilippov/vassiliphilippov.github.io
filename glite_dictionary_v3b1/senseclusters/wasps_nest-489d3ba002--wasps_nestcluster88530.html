Etymology

wasp's nest comes from the real home built by wasps: the insect wasp chews wood into a paper-like material and builds a hanging nest where many wasps live. So, wasp's nest means the actual 'home' of wasps and reminds us it can hold many insects and be dangerous if disturbed.
