Etymology

odiferous comes from Latin parts: odor meaning 'smell' and -ferous meaning 'carrying' or 'full of', where the 'fer' is like in transfer ('to carry'). So odiferous literally means 'carrying a smell', which is why we use it for things that give off a strong or noticeable smell.
