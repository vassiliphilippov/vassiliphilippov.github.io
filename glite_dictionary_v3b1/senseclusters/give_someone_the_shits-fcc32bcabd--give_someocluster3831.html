Etymology

give (someone) the shits may come from the literal idea of shits as a bad stomach problem that makes you feel sick and uncomfortable. So when something causes that strong, unpleasant feeling, people say it gives them 'the shits', meaning it makes them 'very upset' or 'angry'.
