Etymology

The word nonspecifically breaks into non- meaning 'not', specific meaning 'exact' or 'one particular thing', and -ally which makes it describe how something happens. So nonspecifically means 'not in a specific way'. Think of it as 'not choosing one exact target', which is why it describes something general that affects many things rather than one.
