Etymology

Bluffer comes from bluff, which originally meant 'to brag' or 'to puff yourself up'. Think of someone puffing up to look stronger or more sure than they really are. That's why a bluffer is a person who pretends to have skills, knowledge, or intentions they do not actually have.
