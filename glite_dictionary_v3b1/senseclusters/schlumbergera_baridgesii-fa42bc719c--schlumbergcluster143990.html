Etymology

Schlumbergera baridgesii may come from the name of an old plant collector, Schlumbergera, with the second part, baridgesii, likely honoring the person who found or first grew the plant. So the name works as a simple 'label' that remembers those people and now tells us which winter-flowering cactus it is.
