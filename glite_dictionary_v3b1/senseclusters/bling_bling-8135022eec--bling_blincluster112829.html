Etymology

bling bling comes from hip-hop and from a copy of a quick, shiny sound: the short word bling copies a jewel's sparkle or a tiny bell, and repeating it makes the idea feel bigger. That's why bling bling means 'expensive, flashy jewelry' people wear to get attention.
