Etymology

loco is Spanish slang that means 'crazy' and is used in English as an informal word for odd or wild behavior. A helpful way to remember it is that it is related to local and location ('place'), so being 'loco' is like being 'out of place' or not acting normal, which explains the meaning 'crazy'.
