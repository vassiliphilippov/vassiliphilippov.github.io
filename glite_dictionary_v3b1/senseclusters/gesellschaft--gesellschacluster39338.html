Etymology

gesellschaft comes from Gesell 'companion' and -schaft like English -ship, but in sociology it was used to describe modern life where relations are formal and goal-focused rather than close and personal. So the old idea of 'companions' helps explain why gesellschaft now means impersonal, often work-based social ties.
