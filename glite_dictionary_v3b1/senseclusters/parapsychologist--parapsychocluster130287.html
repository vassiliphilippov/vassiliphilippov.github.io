Etymology

parapsychologist comes from three parts: para- meaning 'beyond', psych- meaning 'mind', and -ologist meaning 'one who studies', like in biologist. So a parapsychologist studies mind-related events that are 'beyond' normal experience, such as telepathy and clairvoyance.
