Etymology

regreen is built from the prefix re-, like in redo, meaning 'again', and green, meaning 'plants' or 'environmentally friendly'. So to regreen a place means to make it green again by planting trees or using greener methods, which is why people use it for both restoring nature and making things more eco-friendly.
