Etymology

yellow ageratum joins yellow and ageratum, where ageratum comes from an old Greek word meaning 'not aging' because these flowers keep their color for a long time. That's why the name fits a small shrub with bright, daisy-like yellow blooms that 'stay fresh' for many days.
