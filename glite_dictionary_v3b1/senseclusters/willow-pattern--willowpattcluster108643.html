Etymology

willow-pattern comes from an English name for a Chinese-style blue-and-white 'design' that shows a tall willow tree, a river and a small bridge, and English pot makers turned that picture into a repeating pattern on plates and teapots. So a willow-pattern piece of china is simply a cup, plate or teapot decorated with that willow-tree scene.
