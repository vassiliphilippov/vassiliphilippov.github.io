Etymology

Hepatocarcinoma breaks into hepato- meaning 'liver' and carcinoma meaning 'cancer'. The carcinoma part comes from a Greek word for 'crab', a helpful image to remember how the disease can hold on, so hepatocarcinoma is 'liver cancer'.
