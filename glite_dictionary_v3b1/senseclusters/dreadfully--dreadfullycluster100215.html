Etymology

The word dreadfully comes from dread, meaning 'great fear', plus -ful, which means 'full of', and -ly, which turns a word into an adverb. So it originally meant 'in a way full of fear', and today dreadfully can mean 'very' or 'extremely', or sometimes 'in a frightening way'.
