Etymology

The noun head linesperson comes from the sideline scene in American football: the linesperson watches the yard lines and the measuring chains, and the head is the one in charge. So, the head linesperson is the official who keeps track of the 'yardage' and leads the chain crew, which is why the name fits the job.
