Etymology

mushroom-shaped cloud gets its name from the sight of a huge cloud that looks like a giant mushroom, with a tall stem of smoke and a round top. That's why the phrase describes the big cloud that rises after a 'powerful explosion', especially a 'nuclear bomb'.
