Etymology

clocks is a common name for a small field weed, and it probably comes from the way the plant's round seed heads and long curled seed tails look or move like a tiny clock. A helpful way to remember clocks is to picture the seeds turning and scattering like the hands of a clock, which explains the name used for this weed today.
