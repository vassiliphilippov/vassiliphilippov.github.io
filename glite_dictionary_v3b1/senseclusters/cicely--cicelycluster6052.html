Etymology

Cicely is the name of a sweet-smelling herb often called sweet cicely. A helpful way to remember it is to think of the word 'sweet' — the plant has a 'sweet' and 'anise-like' flavor and aroma, which is why cooks use it to add a gentle sweetness to food.
