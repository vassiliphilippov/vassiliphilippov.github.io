Etymology

The word simplicity comes from simple (Latin simplex, 'single' or 'not complex'), and the idea of being 'not complex' can also describe a person who is innocent or easy to fool. A helpful way to remember this sense is to think of a 'simple' mind that does not expect tricks, so simplicity can mean innocence or being easily deceived.
