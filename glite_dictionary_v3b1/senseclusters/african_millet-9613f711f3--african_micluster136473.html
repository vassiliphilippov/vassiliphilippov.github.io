Etymology

African millet may come from early traders who found similar small-seed grasses in Africa and used the word millet for them. Think of many tiny, hard seeds like beads, and this image helps you remember it is a hardy cereal whose flour makes flatbreads and is used as a 'staple food' in dry regions.
