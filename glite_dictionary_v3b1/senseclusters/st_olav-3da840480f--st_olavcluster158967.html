Etymology

St. Olav comes from a real king named Olav who lived around the year 1000 and worked to bring Christianity to Norway. After he died in battle, people said miracles happened at his grave, so he was 'made a saint' and became Norway's 'patron saint', which is why towns still celebrate him today.
