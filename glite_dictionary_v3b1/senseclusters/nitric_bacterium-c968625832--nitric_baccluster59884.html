Etymology

Nitric bacterium comes from the words nitric, which refers to 'nitrate', and bacterium, a tiny living cell, so the name gives a clear picture of a small organism that makes 'nitrate'. That's why the name means a bacterium that turns 'nitrite' into 'nitrate' and helps soil and water by keeping the nitrogen cycle working.
