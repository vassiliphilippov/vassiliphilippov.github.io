Etymology

The name sodium cyanide combines sodium (the metal) and cyanide, a word taken from Greek for the dark 'blue' color because early scientists worked with a deep blue substance. So the name simply means the metal plus the cyanide part, and today sodium cyanide is known as a very strong 'poison' often used in mining.
