Etymology

plantlike flagellate: The term plantlike flagellate comes from how these tiny living things look and move. They are called plantlike because they can make food from light, and flagellate refers to 'flagella', tiny whip-like tails they use to swim, so the name means a 'single cell' that is 'like a plant' but can move with tails.
