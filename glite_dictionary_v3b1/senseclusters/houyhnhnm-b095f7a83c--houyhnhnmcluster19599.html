Etymology

The word Houyhnhnm was invented by Jonathan Swift for a race of intelligent horses in his book Gulliver's Travels. These calm, reasonable horses rule the wild Yahoos and live by strict order, so Houyhnhnm now refers to one of Swift's fictional, thinking horses and their ideal society.
