Etymology

The phrase basketball shot comes from the game's early days, when players tried to throw a ball into a hanging basket that was often a real peach basket. The word shot simply means the throw, so the image of aiming and trying to 'score' by getting the ball into the basket is why the phrase means an attempt to score.
