Etymology

revolutionariness comes from revolution, a word built on the idea of a 'turn' that later meant a big 'change' in how things are done, the same idea you see in revolve. The ending -ary means 'related to' and -ness makes a noun for a 'state', so revolutionariness is the state of being like a revolution, making large, often sudden change.
