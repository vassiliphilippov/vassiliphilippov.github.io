Etymology

hurdy-gurdy is a playful name that copies the instrument's steady sound; people once said a double word like 'hurdy-hurdy' to echo the constant 'drone' made when a wheel rubs the strings. So a hurdy-gurdy is a string instrument with a crank and a wheel that keeps a continuous background sound under the tune.
