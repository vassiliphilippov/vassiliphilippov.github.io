Etymology

devil-may-care comes from an old saying that sounds like telling the devil 'you can take care of it', so the speaker leaves the worry to someone else and shows no concern. A helpful way to remember it is that it means 'not caring' about risks or results, so a devil-may-care person is carefree and a little reckless.
