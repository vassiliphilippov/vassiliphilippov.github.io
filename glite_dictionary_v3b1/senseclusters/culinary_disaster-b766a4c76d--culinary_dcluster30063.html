Etymology

The phrase culinary disaster combines culinary, meaning 'cooking', and disaster, meaning a 'big mistake' or 'mess', and paints a clear picture of a kitchen where a dish is burned, spoiled, or ruined. That's why it means 'a severe failure in cooking'.
