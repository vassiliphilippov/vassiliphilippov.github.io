to announce

2/m3.9
To show that something will happen soon, often by a sign you notice
verb US / əˈnaʊns /

Etymology

announce also kept the idea of 'telling before' from the Latin nuntiare, so it can mean to give a sign that something will happen soon. That's why we say thunder 'announces' a storm — it tells us the storm is coming.
