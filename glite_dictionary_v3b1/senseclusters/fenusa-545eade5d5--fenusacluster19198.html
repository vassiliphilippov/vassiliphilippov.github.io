Etymology

Fenusa is a scientific name for a group of small sawflies whose young insects live as leaf miners inside birch leaves. A helpful way to remember Fenusa is to think of fen meaning 'marsh' and imagine tiny miners eating inside leaves, which links the name to its meaning as a birch leaf-mining insect.
