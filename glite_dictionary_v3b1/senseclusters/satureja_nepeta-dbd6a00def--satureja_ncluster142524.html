Etymology

Satureja nepeta may get its name from the old word Satureja, linked to 'satyr', a wild man from old stories who liked strong-smelling herbs, and from nepeta, which connects it to 'catmint'. That's why the name gives the image of a small, very aromatic herb used in cooking and herbal tea.
