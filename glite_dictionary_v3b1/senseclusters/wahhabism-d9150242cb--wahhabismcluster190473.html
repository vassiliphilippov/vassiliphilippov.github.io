Etymology

The word Wahhabism comes from the name of an 18th-century Arabic teacher, Muhammad ibn Abd al-Wahhab, where Wahhab is from an Arabic word meaning 'to give', and the English ending -ism means 'a movement'. So Wahhabism means the religious movement based on that teacher's ideas and refers to a strict, conservative form of Sunni Islam.
