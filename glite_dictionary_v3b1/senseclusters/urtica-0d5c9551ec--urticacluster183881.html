Etymology

Urtica comes from the Latin word urtica, which means 'nettle' and is related to the old Latin verb urere meaning 'to burn', because nettles can 'burn' or sting your skin. So the name Urtica fits this group of stinging plants, some of which give strong fibers used to make cloth and rope.
