Etymology

Ailanthus silkworm is named from the tree ailanthus and the word silkworm. Imagine a bright green caterpillar living on the ailanthus tree, eating its leaves and spinning silk, so the name means 'the large green larva that feeds on ailanthus trees and produces silk'.
