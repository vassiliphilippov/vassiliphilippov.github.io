Etymology

Jasminum officinale may come from the old Persian word 'yasmin' for the sweet-smelling flower and from the Latin name officinale, a tag used in old medicine shops to mean 'used in medicine'. So the name pictures the common jasmine people used for perfume, tea, and remedies.
