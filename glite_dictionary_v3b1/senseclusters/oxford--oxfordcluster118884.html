Etymology

oxford for the low, laced shoe comes from students at Oxford University who wore a style called Oxonian, and that name was shortened to oxford. The place name ox + ford means 'ford for oxen', and that's why an oxford is a smart, formal lace-up shoe named after the city and its students.
