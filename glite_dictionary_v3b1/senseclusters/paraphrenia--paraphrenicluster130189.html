Etymology

paraphrenia comes from Greek parts para- ('beside' or 'abnormal') and phrēn ('mind'), and it uses the same 'phrenia' you see in schizophrenia. So it names a mental condition where a person develops long-lasting false beliefs while their personality and social skills often remain fairly normal.
