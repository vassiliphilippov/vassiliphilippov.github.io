Etymology

exaggerator comes from the verb exaggerate, which has the parts ex- 'out' and agger 'heap' or 'pile'. Think of someone 'piling on' details until a story looks much bigger than the truth, so an exaggerator is a person who makes things seem larger or more important than they really are.
