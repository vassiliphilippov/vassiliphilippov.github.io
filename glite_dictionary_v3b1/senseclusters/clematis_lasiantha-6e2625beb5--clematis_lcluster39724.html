Clematis lasiantha may come from old Greek words: Clematis meaning 'vine' and lasiantha from words meaning 'hairy' and 'flower', giving the image of a climbing plant with many small, soft flowers. That's why the name fits this climbing plant with clusters of creamy-white blooms.
