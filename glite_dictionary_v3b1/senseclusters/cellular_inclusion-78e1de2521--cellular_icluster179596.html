Etymology

cellular inclusion gives a simple picture: a small body or dark spot is literally 'included' inside a cell, so cellular means 'of the cell' and inclusion means something put inside. You can see these tiny spots under a microscope, so the term now means a small body 'inside' cells that can show 'disease' or cell activity.
