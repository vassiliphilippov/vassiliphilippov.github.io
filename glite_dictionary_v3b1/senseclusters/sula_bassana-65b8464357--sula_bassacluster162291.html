Etymology

Sula bassana is the Latin name for the big white seabird called the 'northern gannet'. The part Sula was an old word for diving sea birds and bassana may come from Bass Rock, a famous cliff where many of these birds nest, so the name brings to mind a large white bird that dives and nests on cliffs.
