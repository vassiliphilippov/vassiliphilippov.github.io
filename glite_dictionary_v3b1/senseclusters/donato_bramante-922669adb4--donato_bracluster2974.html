Etymology

Donato Bramante was an Italian 'architect' who built a small round temple called the 'Tempietto' and drew the first plans for 'St. Peter's Basilica' in Rome. His use of old Roman shapes helped start the 'High Renaissance' in architecture, so his name now stands for the beginning of that style.
