Etymology

interlingual rendition comes from its parts: inter- meaning 'between', lingual meaning 'language', and rendition meaning 'version'. Imagine someone writing the same ideas again in another language so the same 'meaning' is kept, and that is why it means a written 'version' of a text in a different language.
