Etymology

purau comes from Pacific island languages where this coastal tree is common and is the local name for the same plant English calls the 'beach hibiscus'. A helpful way to remember it is that island people used the tree's light wood and strong fibres for canoes and rope, so purau today means that useful coastal tree.
