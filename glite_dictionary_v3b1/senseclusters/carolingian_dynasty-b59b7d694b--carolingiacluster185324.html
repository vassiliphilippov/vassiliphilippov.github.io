Etymology

Carolingian dynasty: The name Carolingian dynasty comes from the old Latin name for Charles, 'Carolus'. So Carolingian means 'of Charles' and dynasty means 'a ruling family'. That is why the phrase refers to the Frankish royal family started by Charlemagne's father that ruled much of Western Europe.
