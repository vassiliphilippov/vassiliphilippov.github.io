Etymology

New adventure comes from the image of explorers starting a fresh journey into unknown places, where new shows it is just beginning and adventure is a risky, exciting trip. So calling something a new adventure gives the picture of stepping into the unknown, which is why it means 'an exciting or unfamiliar experience'.
