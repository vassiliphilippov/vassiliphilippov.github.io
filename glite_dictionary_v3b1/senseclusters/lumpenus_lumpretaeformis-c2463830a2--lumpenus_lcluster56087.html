Etymology

Lumpenus lumpretaeformis is the scientific name for the snakeblenny. The fish has a long, thin body that wiggles like a snake, so the name parts Lumpenus and lumpretaeformis give a clear picture of a 'snake-like' fish living in cold northern waters, which helps you remember what kind of animal it is.
