Etymology

The word Ragnarok comes from old Norse parts ragna- meaning 'of the gods' and -rök meaning 'fate' or 'final events'. So Ragnarok literally means the 'fate of the gods', which is why it names the prophesied final battle that brings the end of the world, and a helpful way to picture it is as the 'twilight of the gods'.
