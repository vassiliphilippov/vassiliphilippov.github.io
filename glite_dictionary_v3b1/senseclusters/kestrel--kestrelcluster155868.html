Etymology

kestrel comes from an old French name for a small hawk, which is a helpful picture because a kestrel is a little falcon often seen 'hovering' in the air while it watches for prey. So the origin reminds you that a kestrel is a small bird of prey that stays in one place in the air to hunt.
