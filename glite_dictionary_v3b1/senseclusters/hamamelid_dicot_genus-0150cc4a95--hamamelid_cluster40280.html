Etymology

hamamelid dicot genus combines plant-name parts: hamamelid links to the witch-hazel plants, dicot means 'two seed leaves', and genus means a 'group' of related species. So the term is used to name a group of mainly woody flowering plants (like witch-hazel) that often have separate male and female flowers in hanging clusters.
