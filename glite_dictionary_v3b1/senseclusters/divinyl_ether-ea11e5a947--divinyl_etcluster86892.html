Etymology

divinyl ether — The chemical name divinyl ether describes how the chemical is built: divinyl means 'two vinyl parts' and ether names a compound where those parts are joined by an oxygen, like two small arms connected by an oxygen 'bridge'. So the name points to a specific substance: a very light, highly flammable liquid that was once used as an 'anesthetic' to make people sleep for surgery.
