Etymology

The word rhizopodan comes from Greek parts rhizo- meaning 'root' and -pod meaning 'foot', because these tiny single-celled creatures move and feed with root-like extensions called pseudopods, or 'false feet'. So a rhizopodan is a creature that reaches out with these root-like 'feet' to move and eat.
