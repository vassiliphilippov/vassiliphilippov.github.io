Etymology

obelus comes from the old Greek word obelos, meaning 'spit' or 'pointed stick', and it is the same root as obelisk. Ancient editors used a small pointed mark to 'point out' lines they thought were doubtful, and that mark later became the division sign and a mark for questionable text.
