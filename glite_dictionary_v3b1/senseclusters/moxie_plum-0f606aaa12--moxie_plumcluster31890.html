Etymology

moxie plum may come from the word moxie meaning 'brave' or full of spirit and the small white seeds or fruit that look a little like a tiny plum. Picture a tough little shrub holding tiny, plum-like seeds in cold places, and that image explains why the name refers to a hardy, slow-growing shrub.
