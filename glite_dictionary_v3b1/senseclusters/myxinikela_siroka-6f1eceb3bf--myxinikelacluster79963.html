Etymology

Myxinikela siroka may come from the part Myxinikela, a word for 'slime', and the part siroka, a place or name, so the name gives the image of a long-ago hagfish that made slime. Fossils show it looked like modern hagfishes, so the name helps us remember it was an ancient 'jawless' fish.
