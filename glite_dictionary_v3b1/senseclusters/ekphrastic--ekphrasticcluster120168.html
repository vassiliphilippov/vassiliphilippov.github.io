Etymology

ekphrastic comes from Greek parts ek- 'out' and phrasis 'speech', and it shares the same root as phrase. That original idea of 'speaking out' helps: ekphrastic writing 'speaks out' a painting or sculpture, giving a clear description of the work of art.
