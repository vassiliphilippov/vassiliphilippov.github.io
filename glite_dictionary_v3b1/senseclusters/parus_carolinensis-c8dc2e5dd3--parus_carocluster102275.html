Etymology

Parus carolinensis comes from Latin words: Parus was the old word for a small bird, a 'tit', and carolinensis means 'from Carolina'. So the name literally says the 'tit from Carolina', which is why it refers to the 'Carolina chickadee' found in the southern United States.
