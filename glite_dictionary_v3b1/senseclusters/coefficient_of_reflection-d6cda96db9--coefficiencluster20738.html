Etymology

The term coefficient of reflection comes from a simple image: imagine shining light or throwing a ball at a wall and seeing how much is sent back. Here coefficient means a 'number' and reflection is what is 'bounced' off a 'surface', so the phrase names the number that shows how much incoming light or energy is reflected.
