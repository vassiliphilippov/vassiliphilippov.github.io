Etymology

The word inexcusably breaks into three parts: in- meaning 'not', excuse meaning 'to forgive or explain', and -ly which makes it 'in a way'. So inexcusably means 'in a way that cannot be forgiven or justified', which is why we use it for actions that have no acceptable reason.
