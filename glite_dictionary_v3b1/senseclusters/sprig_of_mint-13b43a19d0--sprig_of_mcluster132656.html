Etymology

sprig of mint shows a simple picture: a small green sprig, a tiny stem of mint, placed on a drink or on a plate. People add it for a fresh smell and a light 'flavor' or 'garnish', so the phrase means a small mint stem used to decorate or flavor food and drinks.
