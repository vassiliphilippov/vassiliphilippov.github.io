Etymology

Tindale is a family name from a place name: it joins Tyne, a river name, and dale 'valley', so it originally meant 'valley by the Tyne'. Today Tindale refers to William Tindale, the English scholar who translated the Bible into English, so the name now reminds us of that translator.
