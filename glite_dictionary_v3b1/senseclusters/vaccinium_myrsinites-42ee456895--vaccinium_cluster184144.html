Etymology

Vaccinium myrsinites may come from two old words: Vaccinium (a group of berry plants) and myrsinites meaning 'like myrtle', which gives the image of a myrtle-like shrub with berries. So the name fits the plant today, a shiny evergreen shrub with bluish-black berries.
