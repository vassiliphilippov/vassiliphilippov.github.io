Etymology

Pecten magellanicus may come from Latin: Pecten means 'comb' and creates the picture of a scallop's shell with comb-like ridges, and magellanicus links the animal to the seas sailed by the explorer Magellan. So the name helps you remember this is a large scallop from cold Atlantic waters that is valued for its edible muscle.
