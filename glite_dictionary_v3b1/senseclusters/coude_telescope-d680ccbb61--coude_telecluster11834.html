Etymology

The noun coude telescope gets its name from the French word coude, meaning 'elbow', because its mirrors bend the path of light like an elbow bends an arm and send the light to a single 'fixed spot'. This keeps instruments still at that 'fixed spot', so the telescope is ideal for long exposures and precise instruments like a spectrograph.
