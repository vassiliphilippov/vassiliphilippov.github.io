Etymology

showy goldenrod combines showy (meaning very bright or eye-catching) and goldenrod (a plant with long stems of yellow flowers), creating a clear picture of the plant. Because this species has tall, bright yellow flower stems that really stand out, the name tells you it is a goldenrod that is especially 'showy'.
