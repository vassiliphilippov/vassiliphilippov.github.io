Etymology

four-flush comes from poker: a flush is five cards of the same suit, so having only four cards is not the real thing and a four-flush was a way to 'pretend' you had a strong hand. So, a four-flush now means a person or claim that is a 'bluff' or a show of false wealth or confidence.
