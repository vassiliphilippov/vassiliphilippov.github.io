Etymology

lamebrain comes from the two simple words lame ('not working well' or 'weak') and brain ('mind' or 'head'), so imagine a brain with a 'limp' that does not work properly. That's why lamebrain is a rude slang word for someone who seems foolish or makes silly mistakes.
