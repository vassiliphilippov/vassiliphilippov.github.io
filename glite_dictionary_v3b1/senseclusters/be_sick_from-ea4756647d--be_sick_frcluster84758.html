Etymology

be sick from paints a clear picture: be sick means to feel ill or vomit and from points to the cause, like motion, a bad smell, or food. Imagine someone on a boat who feels the waves and then 'is sick from' the motion. So, it means 'to feel sick or to vomit because of' something.
