Etymology

The word sidestick is made of side ('the left or right part') and stick ('a thin pole or handle'), so imagine a stick placed at the side of your seat. A sidestick is a control stick at the side of the seat, like a joystick at your side, and that is why pilots use it to steer an aircraft.
