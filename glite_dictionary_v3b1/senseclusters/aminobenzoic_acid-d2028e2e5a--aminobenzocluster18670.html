Etymology

aminobenzoic acid simply describes its parts: an amino group, a small group with nitrogen, attached to benzoic acid, a simple ring-shaped acid. Chemists named it because the chemical is made by putting an amino on benzoic acid, so the name tells you what the chemical is.
