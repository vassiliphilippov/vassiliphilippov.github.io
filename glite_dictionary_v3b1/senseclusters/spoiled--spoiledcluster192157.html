Etymology

The word spoiled comes from the same root as the word spoils, like in the phrase 'the spoils of war', and the Latin spoliare, meaning 'to plunder' or 'to ruin'. So a spoiled child is one whose character has been 'ruined' by too many gifts or too much attention.
