Etymology

The word steep-sided comes from the simple parts steep ('rising sharply' or 'very slanted') and side ('side' or 'edge'), so it paints a picture of a hill or cliff with very sharp sides like a wall. That's why steep-sided means a slope, cliff, or valley with very steep or nearly vertical sides.
