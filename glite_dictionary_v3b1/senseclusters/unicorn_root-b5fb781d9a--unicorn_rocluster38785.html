Etymology

One popular story says the name unicorn root comes from the plant's root shape: the long, pointed root looked like a single horn, so people thought of unicorn and root. Because horns were seen as strong and healing, the bitter root was used 'to cure stomach pain' and the name now refers to a plant used as medicine.
