Etymology

kweek comes from the Dutch and Afrikaans word kweek, which means 'to grow' or 'to raise'. It is used for a tough, spreading grass because the plant 'grows' and spreads fast, so the name helps you remember that kweek is a fast-growing, spreading turf.
