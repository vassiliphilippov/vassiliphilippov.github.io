Etymology

John Paul II chose the name of two earlier popes, John and Paul, and the 'II' shows he was the second pope to use that combined name. Born Karol Wojtyła in Poland, he was pope from 1978 to 2005 and is remembered for visiting many countries and speaking for peace and human rights.
