Etymology

tonish comes from the French phrase bon ton, which means 'good style' or 'good manners' and was used for fashionable high society, and it is like the English word tone, meaning 'style' or 'manner'. So to be tonish is to look or act 'stylish' in an upper-class, slightly old-fashioned way.
