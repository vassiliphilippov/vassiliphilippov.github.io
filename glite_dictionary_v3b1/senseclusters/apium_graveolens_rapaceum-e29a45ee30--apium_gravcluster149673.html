Etymology

Apium graveolens rapaceum comes from old Latin words: Apium meant 'celery', graveolens meant 'strong-smelling', and rapaceum described a root like a 'turnip'. So the name paints a simple picture of a strong-smelling celery with a large, edible root, and that is why it names the vegetable we call 'celeriac' today.
