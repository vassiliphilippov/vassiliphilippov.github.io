Etymology

first law of thermodynamics comes from early work on heat and steam engines, where people noticed that the fuel's energy did not disappear but changed into motion or stored heat. So the law is a simple rule: 'energy cannot be created or destroyed, only changed', which is why the phrase means the total energy in a system stays the same.
