Etymology

Shimchath Torah comes from the words Shimchath, meaning 'joy' or 'rejoicing', and Torah, the Jewish holy book. On this day people celebrate finishing the yearly reading of the Torah and immediately begin it again, often dancing with the scrolls, so the name explains the festival of 'joy with the Torah'.
