Etymology

sextillion comes from the Latin part sext- meaning 'six' and the familiar number million, so it is like the 'sixth' step in the million naming series. That's why a sextillion is a very large number — in American use it is written as 1 followed by 21 zeros, while in older British use it can mean 1 followed by 36 zeros.
