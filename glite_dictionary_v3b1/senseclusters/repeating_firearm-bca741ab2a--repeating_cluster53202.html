Etymology

repeating firearm gets its name from the idea of 'repeating' shots. Unlike old guns that fired once and had to be loaded again, a repeatingfirearm holds several rounds so the shooter can fire one after another without reloading, which is why the name means a gun that can shoot several times in a row.
