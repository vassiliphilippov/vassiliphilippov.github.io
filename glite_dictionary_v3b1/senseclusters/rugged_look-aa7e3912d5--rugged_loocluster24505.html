Etymology

rugged look comes from comparing a person's face or style to rough, rocky land: rugged means rough and strong, like an old rock, and look means appearance. So when someone has stubble, lines, or sun-dark skin people say they have a rugged look, meaning 'rough, strong, and often attractive appearance'.
