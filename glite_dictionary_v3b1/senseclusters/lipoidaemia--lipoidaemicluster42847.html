Etymology

lipoidaemia comes from Greek parts lipo- ('fat') and -aemia ('blood'), so it literally means 'fat in the blood'. Think of lipid (a fat) and anemia (a blood condition) to remember that lipoidaemia is when there is too much fat in the blood.
