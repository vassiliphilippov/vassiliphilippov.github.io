Etymology

Doctorly comes from doctor + -ly, and doctor comes from the Latin word docere meaning 'to teach'. So to be doctorly is to act like someone with special knowledge or authority, like a teacher or a medical doctor, which is why a doctorly voice sounds confident and helpful.
