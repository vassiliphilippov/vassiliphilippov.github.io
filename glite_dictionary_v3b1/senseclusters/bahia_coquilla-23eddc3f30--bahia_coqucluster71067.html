Etymology

bahia coquilla may come from the place name Bahia in Brazil, where the palm grows, and from coquilla, a word for a hard little shell or nut. Because the tree has strong, shell-like fibers, the name now refers to the palm whose fibers are used for 'ropes', 'mats' and 'brushes'.
