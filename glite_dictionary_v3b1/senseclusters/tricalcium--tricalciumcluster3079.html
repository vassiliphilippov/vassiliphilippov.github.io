Etymology

The word tricalcium comes from the prefix tri- meaning 'three', like in triangle or triple, and calcium, the element name from Latin 'calx' meaning 'lime'. So, tricalcium as an adjective describes something that has 'three' calcium atoms or parts, which is why we say tricalcium phosphate or tricalcium silicate.
