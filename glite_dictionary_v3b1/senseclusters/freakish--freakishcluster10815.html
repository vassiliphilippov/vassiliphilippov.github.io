Etymology

freakish comes from the simple word freak, which long ago could mean a sudden change or a strange person and later came to mean 'odd' or 'unexpected'. The ending -ish means 'like' or 'somewhat', so freakish literally means 'like a freak' and is used for something very strange or unusual.
