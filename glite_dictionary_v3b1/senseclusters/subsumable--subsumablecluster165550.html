Etymology

subsumable comes from the parts sub- 'under' and sumere 'to take'. So, if something is subsumable it can be 'taken under' or included in a larger group, which is why we use the word when one thing fits inside a bigger category, like how 'assume' or 'consume' come from the same 'take' root.
