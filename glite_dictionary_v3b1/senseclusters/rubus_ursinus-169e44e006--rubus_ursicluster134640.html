Etymology

Rubus ursinus comes from Latin words: rubus meaning 'bramble' or 'blackberry' and ursinus meaning 'of bears'. Because wild bears eat these sweet berries, the name gives a clear image — a 'bear's blackberry' — and so it refers to this species.
