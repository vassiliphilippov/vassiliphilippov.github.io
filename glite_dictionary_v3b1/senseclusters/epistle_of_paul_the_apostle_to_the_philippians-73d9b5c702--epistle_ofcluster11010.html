Etymology

Epistle of Paul the Apostle to the Philippians is a letter Paul sent to the Christians in the town of Philippi; epistle simply means 'letter' and Philippians means the people from Philippi. Because it was a personal, encouraging message, the book is still read today for its ideas about 'joy' and 'humility'.
