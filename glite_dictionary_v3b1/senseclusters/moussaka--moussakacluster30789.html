Etymology

moussaka comes from Turkish musakka, which comes from the Arabic musaqqaʿa meaning 'chilled' or 'moistened'. Long ago it may have been a cool, moist vegetable dish, and that old idea helps you remember today's moussaka as a layered, saucy bake of eggplant and meat.
