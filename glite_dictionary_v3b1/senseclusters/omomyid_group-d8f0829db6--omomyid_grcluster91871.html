Etymology

omomyid group names a set of very small, big-eyed primates that lived 30–50 million years ago; picture tiny, night-active animals jumping through tree branches and eating fruit and insects. That image helps you remember they were 'nocturnal' relatives of tarsiers and early monkeys, and why their fossils help us learn about primate evolution.
