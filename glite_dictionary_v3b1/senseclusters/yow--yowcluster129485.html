Etymology

yow is a short, sharp sound people make, like ow for 'pain' but with the push of a yell or the start of a yowl. A helpful way to remember it is to think of adding a quick 'y' to 'ow', so yow is used as a sudden cry for 'surprise', 'excitement', or 'pain'.
