Etymology

tentacled comes from the Latin word tentaculum, meaning a 'feeler', which is from tentare meaning 'to feel' or 'to reach out'. So a tentacled creature has arm-like parts that reach out to touch or grab things, a bit like how to extend is to 'reach out'.
