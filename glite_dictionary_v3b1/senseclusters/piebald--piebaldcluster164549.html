Etymology

Piebald as a noun means an animal with irregular patches of two or more colors. The word comes from pie, the bird 'magpie' known for black-and-white feathers, and bald, an old word meaning 'spotted' or 'patched', so a piebald is an animal that looks like a magpie in its colors.
