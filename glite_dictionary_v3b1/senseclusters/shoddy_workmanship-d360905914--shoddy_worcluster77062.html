Etymology

shoddy workmanship comes from an old cloth trade: shoddy was cheap fabric made by shredding old rags, and workmanship means the work done. Because that fabric was weak and fell apart, people started using shoddy workmanship to mean 'work that is badly made' or 'of poor quality'.
