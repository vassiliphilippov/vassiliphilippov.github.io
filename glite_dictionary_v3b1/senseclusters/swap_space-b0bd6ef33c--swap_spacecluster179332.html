Etymology

swap space comes from the idea of swap, meaning 'to exchange', and space, meaning an area on the disk. Imagine your computer's fast memory as a small desk and the disk area as a box where you put extra papers; when the desk is full you move papers to the box and later bring them back. That's why swap space means the disk area used as 'virtual memory' when physical memory is full.
