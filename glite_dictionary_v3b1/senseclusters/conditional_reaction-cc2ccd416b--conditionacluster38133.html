Etymology

conditional reaction comes from classic learning experiments like Ivan Pavlov's, where a dog learned to drool when it heard a bell paired with food. Here conditional means 'only after a certain signal' and reaction means 'a response', so the phrase means a response that happens only when a specific signal is present.
