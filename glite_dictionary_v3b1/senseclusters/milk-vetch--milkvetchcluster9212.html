Etymology

milk-vetch comes from the parts milk and vetch, where vetch means a 'pea-like' plant often used as animal 'feed'. A helpful way to remember it is to think of these pea-family plants, such as Astragalus, that grazing animals eat and that farmers linked with milk, so milk-vetch names those wild plants you find in fields.
