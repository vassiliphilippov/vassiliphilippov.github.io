Etymology

corroborate comes from Latin parts cor- (a form of 'com-', meaning 'together') and roborare (from 'robur', meaning 'strength' or 'oak'). Think of the common word robust, which comes from the same root and means 'strong'. So to corroborate something is to make it 'stronger' by giving evidence or support.
