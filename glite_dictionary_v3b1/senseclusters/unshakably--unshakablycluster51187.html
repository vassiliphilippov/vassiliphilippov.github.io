Etymology

unshakably is made from un- ('not') and shake ('to move or be disturbed'), with -able and -ly turning it into a word that tells how something is done. A helpful image is a tree with deep roots that does not move in a storm — unshakably means 'not able to be shaken', so it describes someone or something steady, sure, and unchanged.
