Etymology

girl Friday may come from the character Friday in the old story 'Robinson Crusoe', where Friday was a loyal 'helper' to the main character. People later said 'man Friday' for a trusted helper, and the 1940 film 'His Girl Friday' made the female form well known, so today girl Friday means a woman who helps with many tasks.
