Etymology

Congress of Industrial Organizations comes from a simple picture: leaders brought many unions together in one congress, a meeting, so unions that organized all workers in a factory — the industrialorganizations — could 'join' and act as one group. That's why the name refers to a large group of unions for factory workers and later 'merged' with the American Federation of Labor.
