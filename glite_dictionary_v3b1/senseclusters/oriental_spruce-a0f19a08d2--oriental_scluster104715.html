Etymology

The noun oriental spruce comes from the parts oriental and spruce, where oriental meant 'from the east' and spruce is an evergreen tree. People first used this name after finding a dark, drooping spruce in the eastern mountains, so the name tells you it's a spruce 'from the east' often grown for its graceful branches.
