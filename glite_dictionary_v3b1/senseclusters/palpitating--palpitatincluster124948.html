Etymology

The word palpitating comes from the old Latin verb palpitare meaning 'to flutter' or 'to beat', and from palpare meaning 'to touch'. The same root is in palpable ('able to be felt'), so palpitating describes a quick, trembling beat or movement you can feel in the body.
