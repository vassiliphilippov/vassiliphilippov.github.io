Etymology

hussar comes from the Hungarian huszár, the name for a fast, light horse soldier from Hungary. A helpful way to remember it is that huszár may come from Hungarian húsz 'twenty', a story that links the word to old groups of twenty people and so to the idea of small, swift horse units — which is why hussar means a speedy, lightly armed soldier on horseback.
