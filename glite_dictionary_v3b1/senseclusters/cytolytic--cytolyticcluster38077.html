Etymology

The word cytolytic comes from Greek parts cyto- meaning 'cell' and -lytic meaning 'to break' or 'destroy'. So, something described as cytolytic breaks down or kills cells, which is why scientists use the word for treatments, proteins, or viruses that destroy cells.
