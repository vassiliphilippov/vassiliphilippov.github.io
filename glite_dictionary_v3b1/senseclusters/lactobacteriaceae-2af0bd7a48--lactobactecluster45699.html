Etymology

Lactobacteriaceae breaks into parts: lacto- meaning 'milk', bacteri- meaning 'bacteria', and -aceae which marks a family. So, they are the 'milk bacteria family': bacteria that often change milk into lactic acid and help make yogurt and cheese, though some can also cause disease.
