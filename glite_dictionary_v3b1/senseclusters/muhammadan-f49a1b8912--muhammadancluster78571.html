Etymology

Muhammadan comes from the name Muhammad plus the ending -an, which means 'from' or 'belonging to', like in American. It originally meant 'relating to Muhammad or to Islam', but today it is old-fashioned and can be seen as offensive, so people usually say Muslim or Islamic.
