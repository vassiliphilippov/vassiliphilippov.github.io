Etymology

Grias names a tropical tree best known for the 'anchovy pear'. A helpful way to remember Grias is that its name may come from a Greek word for 'old woman', so imagine the fruit's wrinkled skin or the tree's rough bark, and that picture links the name to the pear-like fruit today.
