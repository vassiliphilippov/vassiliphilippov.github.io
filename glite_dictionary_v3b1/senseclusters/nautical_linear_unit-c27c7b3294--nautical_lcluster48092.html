Etymology

Nautical linear unit comes from sailors who used simple lengths, like a ship's rope (a 'cable') or a small part of the Earth, to measure distance at sea. Here nautical means 'from the sea', linear means a straight distance and unit means a fixed measure, so the phrase now means a sea measure used for 'navigation'.
