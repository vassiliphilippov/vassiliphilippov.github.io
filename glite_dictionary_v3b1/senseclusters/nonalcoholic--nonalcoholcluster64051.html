Etymology

nonalcoholic combines non- meaning 'not' with alcoholic, which comes from alcohol, a word that came into English from Arabic al-kuhl and later meant 'the spirit in drinks'. So nonalcoholic means 'not containing alcohol', used for drinks and products with no alcohol.
