Etymology

The word aminopherase breaks into parts: amino ('related to amino acids'), the root pher ('to carry', as in transfer), and the enzyme ending -ase (as in lactase). So, aminopherase is an enzyme that 'carries' an amino group from one molecule to another, which explains its role in amino acid metabolism.
