Etymology

Piper nigrum comes from old Latin words: piper meaning 'pepper' and nigrum meaning 'black'. It is a climbing plant whose small berries called peppercorns turn dark when dried to make the black and white pepper we use in cooking, so the name refers to the plant that gives 'black pepper'.
