Etymology

abradant comes from the same root as abrade and abrasive, from an old word radere meaning 'to scrape' or 'wear away'. Think of sandpaper or a grinding wheel scraping a surface—an abradant is anything that does that, so it wears down, smooths, or polishes by friction.
