Etymology

pellagrin comes from the Italian words pelle 'skin' and agra 'bitter' or 'rough', because the illness makes the skin red and damaged. That's why pellagrin as an adjective describes someone or something affected by pellagra, a disease from lack of niacin (vitamin B3).
