Etymology

Cryptogramma crispa may come from Greek parts: crypto- meaning 'hidden', -gramma meaning 'writing' or 'mark', and crispa meaning 'curled'. So the name gives the image of a fern with curled, parsley-like leaves and hidden tiny dots of spores, which helps explain why this Latin name refers to that curled fern today.
