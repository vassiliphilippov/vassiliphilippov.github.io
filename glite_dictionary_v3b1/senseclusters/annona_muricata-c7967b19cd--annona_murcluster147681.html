Etymology

Annona muricata may come from two parts: Annona is from a native Caribbean word for 'custard apple' and muricata is a Latin word meaning 'spiny'. So the name gives a clear picture — a prickly green fruit with soft, custard-like white flesh, which is why we know it today as soursop.
