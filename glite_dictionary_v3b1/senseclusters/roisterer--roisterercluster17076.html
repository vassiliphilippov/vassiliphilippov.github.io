Etymology

Roisterer comes from the old verb roister, which meant to make loud, rough fun. A helpful way to remember it is to think of someone who 'roars' with laughter and is like the word boisterous, so a roisterer is a noisy, cheerful person who celebrates in a wild, unruly way.
