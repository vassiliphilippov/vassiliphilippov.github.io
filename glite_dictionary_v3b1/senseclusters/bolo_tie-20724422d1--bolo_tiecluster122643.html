Etymology

bolo tie comes from the American West, where people wore a simple leather cord with a decorative slide at the neck instead of a cloth tie. One popular idea says bolo comes from the word 'bola' (a throwing cord) and, because it is a cord worn like a 'necktie', the bolo tie today means a leather or cord neckpiece fastened with an ornamental clasp.
