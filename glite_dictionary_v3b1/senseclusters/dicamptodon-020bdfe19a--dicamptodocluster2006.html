Etymology

The word Dicamptodon comes from Greek parts di- 'two', campto 'bent', and -don 'tooth', so it literally means 'two-bent-tooth'. This name points to the shape of its large teeth and helps you remember that Dicamptodon is a big salamander often found near cold streams.
