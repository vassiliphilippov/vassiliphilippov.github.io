Etymology

mixotrophic comes from mix ('mixed') and trophic (a root meaning 'to feed' or 'nourish'), so it names a living thing that uses more than one way to get food. So a mixotrophic organism can use sunlight like a plant and also eat or absorb other food, which is why it means getting energy both from photosynthesis and from consuming other organisms.
