Etymology

unfavorableness is made of simple parts: un- meaning 'not', favor meaning 'support' or 'a good opinion', and -ness meaning 'the state of something'. So unfavorableness is the 'state of not being favorable' — a situation or condition that is not helpful and makes success less likely.
