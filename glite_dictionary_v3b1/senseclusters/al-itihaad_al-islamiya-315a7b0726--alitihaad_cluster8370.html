Etymology

al-Itihaad al-Islamiya comes from Arabic: al-Itihaad means 'union' or 'unity' and al-Islamiya means 'Islamic'. So the name gives the image of groups joining together under Islam, which explains why a movement used this name to show it aimed to unite people for an Islamic cause.
