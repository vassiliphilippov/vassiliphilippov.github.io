Etymology

mouse-tooth forceps is named for the tiny, sharp 'teeth' on the tips of the tool — mouse-tooth shows these little tooth-like points and forceps means the tool. Because those small teeth hold soft tissue without slipping, the term now means a pair of forceps used to 'grasp delicate tissue' during surgery.
