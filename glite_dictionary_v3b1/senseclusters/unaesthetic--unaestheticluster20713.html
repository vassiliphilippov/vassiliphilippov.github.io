Etymology

unaesthetic is made from un- ('not') and aesthetic ('related to beauty or the senses'), so it describes something that does not look or feel pleasing. A helpful way to remember it is to think of curtains or colours that look bad — they are unaesthetic, meaning 'not attractive'.
