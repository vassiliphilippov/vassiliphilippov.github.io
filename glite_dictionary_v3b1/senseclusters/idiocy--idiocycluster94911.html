Etymology

idiocy comes from the Greek root idios, which meant 'own' or 'private', and from this came the word idiot that first meant a private person who did not take part in public life. Over time idiot came to mean a very foolish or uneducated person, so idiocy now refers to extreme stupidity and is considered offensive and outdated.
