Etymology

teff grass comes from teff, a very small-seeded grass that farmers in Ethiopia have grown for food and for animal feed. Imagine thin plants with tiny, dust-like seeds that are ground into flour to make 'injera' or cut as hay; that image explains why the name means a grass grown for edible little grains and animal feed.
