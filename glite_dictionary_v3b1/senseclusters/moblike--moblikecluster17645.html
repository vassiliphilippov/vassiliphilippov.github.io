Etymology

moblike comes from mob, which is linked to the Latin word mobile meaning 'movable' or a 'crowd that moves', and the ending -like which means 'similar to'. So to call behaviour moblike is to say it is 'like a crowd that moves and acts without order', which explains why it means 'disorderly' or 'lawless'.
