Etymology

Jules Alfred Huot de Goncourt is remembered as one half of the Goncourt brothers who wrote books together with his brother Edmond de Goncourt. Imagine two brothers sitting side by side and sharing the same notes—that image helps explain why his name stands for a French writer who 'co-wrote' important works and kept famous diaries.
