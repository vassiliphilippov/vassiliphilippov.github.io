Etymology

woodwaxen is made of two parts: wood and waxen. A helpful way to remember it is that waxen comes from an old word meaning 'to grow', so woodwaxen is a small shrub that 'grows in woods' and has bright, 'waxy' yellow flowers once used for dye.
