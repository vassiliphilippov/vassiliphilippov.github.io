Etymology

psychogram comes from Greek parts psycho- meaning 'mind' and -gram meaning 'something written', the same ending you see in telegram and diagram. So a psychogram is like a 'written picture' of a person's mind, which fits its use as a chart or record of personality and likely behavior.
