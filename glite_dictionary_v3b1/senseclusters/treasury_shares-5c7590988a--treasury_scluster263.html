Etymology

treasury shares comes from the simple image of a company putting its own shares into its own treasury or safe after it buys them back. Because they sit in the company's safe, these shares are 'not active in the market' and 'cannot vote', so the phrase now means shares the company holds itself rather than shares owned by outside investors.
