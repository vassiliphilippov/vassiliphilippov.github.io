Etymology

Rhamnaceae comes from the old plant name Rhamnus, the Greek word for 'buckthorn', a thorny shrub with small fruits. The ending -aceae shows it is a plant 'family', so Rhamnaceae means the 'family of buckthorns', which fits because many plants in this group are thorny and have small, often medicinal fruits.
