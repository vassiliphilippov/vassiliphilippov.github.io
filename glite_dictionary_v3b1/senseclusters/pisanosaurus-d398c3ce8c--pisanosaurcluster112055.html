Etymology

Pisanosaurus is formed from Pisano (named after the person or place linked to its discovery) and saurus, a Greek word meaning 'lizard'. So the name means 'Pisano's lizard', which helps you remember it is a very early dinosaur from the Late Triassic of Argentina.
