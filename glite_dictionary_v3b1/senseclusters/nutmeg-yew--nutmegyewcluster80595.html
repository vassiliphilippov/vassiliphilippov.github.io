Etymology

The word nutmeg-yew comes from the two English words nutmeg and yew: the tree's fruit looks like nutmeg ('a spice') and the tree itself is a yew ('an evergreen'). So the name tells you it is a yew with fruit that reminds people of nutmeg, even though the fruit is not edible and has a strong turpentine flavor.
