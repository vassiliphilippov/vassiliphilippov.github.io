Etymology

renal colic links renal ('kidney') and colic ('a sudden, severe cramp'). Picture a small stone moving down the tube that carries urine from the kidney and making a sudden, sharp cramp. So renal colic means the severe, cramping pain you get when a kidney stone moves.
