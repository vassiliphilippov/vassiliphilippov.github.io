Etymology

spikenard comes from spike + nard, where spike points to the plant's long flower and nard was an old name for a plant from India known for its 'sweet smell'. So spikenard means the sweet-smelling oil or ointment made from that plant, which is why it was a precious perfume in ancient ceremonies.
