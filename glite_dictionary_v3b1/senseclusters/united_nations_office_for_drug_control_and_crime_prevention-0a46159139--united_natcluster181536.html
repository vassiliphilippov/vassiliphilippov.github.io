Etymology

United Nations Office for Drug Control and Crime Prevention was named as one UN office with two jobs: one part worked on stopping illegal drugs and the other worked on stopping crime, so imagine two desks in one office. That's why the name means 'a former UN agency that worked worldwide to control drugs and prevent crime'.
