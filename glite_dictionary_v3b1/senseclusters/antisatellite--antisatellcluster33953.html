Etymology

The word antisatellite comes from anti- meaning 'against' and satellite meaning 'an object that goes around a planet', and you can see anti- in words like antivirus. So an antisatellite system is literally 'against' satellites, which is why it means weapons or technology made to destroy or disable them.
