Etymology

The word Entolomataceae comes from the mushroom genus Entoloma plus the family ending -aceae, which means 'family', like Rosaceae the rose family. Entoloma is built from Greek parts ento- 'inside' and loma 'edge' or 'fringe', a helpful way to remember the gills and edges under the cap, so Entolomataceae is the family of those pink-spored, gilled mushrooms.
