Etymology

oceanic bonito names a sea fish: bonito is a mackerel-like fish whose meat is dried, smoked and shaved into thin flakes called 'katsuobushi'. That's why the name refers to a fish often prepared as dried flakes used to make 'dashi' soup stock.
