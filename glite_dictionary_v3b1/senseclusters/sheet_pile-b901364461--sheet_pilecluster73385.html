Etymology

The noun sheet pile comes from the image of long, flat metal or wooden sheets placed side by side and driven into the ground to make a tight pile or wall. So a sheet pile is literally a row of boards or metal that 'holds back' soil or water, which is why the term means 'to stop soil or water from moving into an area'.
