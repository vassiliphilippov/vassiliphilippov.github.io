Etymology

family Caproidae names a group of sea fish often called 'boarfishes'; the word Caproidae comes from a word for 'boar' because these fish have round, deep bodies and sharp spines that reminded people of a boar's head. So the phrase means a 'family' of these deep-bodied, spiny sea fish and helps you remember which fish belong together.
