Etymology

revolutionary group comes from the idea of a 'revolution' as a big turning or change in how a country is run. Imagine many people turning a large wheel together to move the old system aside; a revolutionarygroup is a set of people who try to make that major political change, so the phrase means an organization formed to carry out a revolution.
