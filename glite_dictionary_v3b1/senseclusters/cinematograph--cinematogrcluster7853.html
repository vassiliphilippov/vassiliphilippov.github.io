Etymology

Cinematograph comes from parts that mean 'movement' and 'writing' — cinema (from Greek for 'move') and graph (from Greek for 'write'), the same graph you see in photograph. So a cinematograph was a place for showing 'moving pictures', which is why it became an early word for a cinema.
