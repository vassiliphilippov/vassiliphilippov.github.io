Etymology

retem comes from an old Hebrew name for a desert shrub mentioned in the Bible and meant a 'broom' or small woody plant like a juniper. Think of the common English word 'broom', which is both a shrub and a cleaning tool, to remember retem as a sheltering plant of dry lands.
