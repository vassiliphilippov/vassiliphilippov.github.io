Etymology

The word sticklerism comes from stickler, originally someone who 'sticks' to a rule or point, like the idea behind stick in the phrase 'stick to'. A helpful way to remember it is to imagine a person glued to the rules, so sticklerism means being too strict or inflexible about following rules.
