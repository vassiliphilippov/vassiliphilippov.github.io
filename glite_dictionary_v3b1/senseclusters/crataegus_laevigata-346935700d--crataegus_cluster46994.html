Etymology

Crataegus laevigata may come from old Greek and Latin words: Crataegus means something like 'strong wood' because hawthorn wood is very hard, and laevigata means 'smooth' or 'with smooth leaves'. So the scientific name describes the 'Midland hawthorn' and helps explain why this name is used for the thorny shrub today.
