Etymology

Sansevieria zeylanica comes from two parts: Sansevieria, a name that honors an Italian noble, and zeylanica, which means 'from Ceylon', the old name for Sri Lanka. That's why the name tells you it is a Sansevieria linked to Sri Lanka and helps you remember its thick, strong, rope-like leaves often called 'bowstring hemp'.
