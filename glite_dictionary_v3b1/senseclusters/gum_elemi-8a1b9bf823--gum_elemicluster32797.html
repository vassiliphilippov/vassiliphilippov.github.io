Etymology

gum elemi names the sticky resin people collect from certain trees. The word gum shows it is like sticky 'sap', and elemi was a name old traders used for this fragrant resin. Harvesters cut the bark and the sap hardens into a fresh, citrus-smelling lump, so gum elemi today means this fragrant resin used in incense and varnish.
