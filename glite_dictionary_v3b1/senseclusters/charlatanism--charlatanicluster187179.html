Etymology

The word charlatanism comes from the Italian ciarlatano, from ciarlare meaning 'to chatter', which is like the English word chatter; a charlatan was someone who talked a lot to sell fake cures and trick people. So charlatanism, with -ism meaning 'practice', is the practice of using showy talk and lies to pretend to have real knowledge.
