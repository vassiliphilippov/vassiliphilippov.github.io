Etymology

Oxytropis lambertii may come from old Greek words oxy meaning 'sharp' and tropis meaning 'keel' or 'boat-shaped', because the flower has a pointy, boat-like petal. The second part, lambertii, is a name that 'honors' a person named Lambert, so the whole name paints a simple picture of a plant that grows in small clumps with pointy, boat-shaped flowers and remembers who studied it.
