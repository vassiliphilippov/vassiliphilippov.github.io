Etymology

Ike is a short, friendly nickname for Dwight D. Eisenhower; it was used like calling a Robert 'Bob' and became famous in the 'I Like Ike' election campaign. So Ike now usually means the United States general and 34th President, Dwight D. Eisenhower.
