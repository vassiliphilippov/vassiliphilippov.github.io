Etymology

water speedwell is named for where it grows and an old belief: water because it lives at the edges of streams and ponds, and speedwell because people once thought the plant could bring 'speed', meaning a quick recovery, and make someone 'well'. So the name paints a simple picture of a small blue-flowered plant by the water that was once thought to help people get better quickly.
