Etymology

Handsomeness comes from the older word handsome, which originally meant 'easy to handle' and was formed from hand (something you use to hold) plus the ending -some meaning 'having'. Over time handsome changed to mean 'good-looking', so handsomeness now means the quality of being 'good-looking' or attractive.
