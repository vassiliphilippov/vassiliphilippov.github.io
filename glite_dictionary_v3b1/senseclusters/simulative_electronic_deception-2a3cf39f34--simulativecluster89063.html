Etymology

simulative electronic deception is like turning on fake radio or radar signals so an enemy sees things that are not really there. The word simulative means 'to copy', electronic is 'using signals', and deception means 'to trick', so the phrase means 'using electronic tricks to mislead an enemy about your real abilities'.
