Etymology

cloth cap: The noun cloth cap comes from the simple, flat hat that workers often wore, made of cloth, a soft fabric, with a small stiff front so the cap kept rain and sun out. That's why cloth cap means 'a flat cap made of wool or similar fabric, with a stiff peak'.
