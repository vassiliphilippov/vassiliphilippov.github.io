Etymology

The word loud-voiced is made from two simple English words: loud meaning 'strong or noisy' and voice meaning 'the sound you make when you speak'. So loud-voiced literally describes someone 'having a loud or powerful voice', which is why it means a voice that is unusually loud or powerful.
