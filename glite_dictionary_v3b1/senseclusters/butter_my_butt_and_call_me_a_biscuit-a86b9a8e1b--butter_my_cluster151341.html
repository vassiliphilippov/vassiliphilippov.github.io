Etymology

butter my butt and call me a biscuit comes from a silly old American joke. Imagine being so surprised you would let someone butter your butt and call you a biscuit, a very silly and unlikely picture that shows you do not believe it. That's why people say it to show 'surprise' or 'disbelief'.
