Etymology

Flowering cherry is a name for a cherry tree or bush that is grown for its pretty spring flowers. The words flowering, meaning full of flowers, and cherry, meaning the tree, give the image of a tree covered in white or pink blooms, so the name means a cherry grown 'for decoration'.
