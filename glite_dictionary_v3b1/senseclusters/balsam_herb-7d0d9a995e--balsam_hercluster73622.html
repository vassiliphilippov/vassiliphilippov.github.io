Etymology

balsam herb takes its name from the word balsam, which means a sweet, perfume-like smell, and herb, a plant used for cooking or scent. Because the plant has a strong, pleasant smell and is used in teas, salads, and potpourri, the name now tells us it is a fragrant herb used for 'flavor' or 'scent'.
