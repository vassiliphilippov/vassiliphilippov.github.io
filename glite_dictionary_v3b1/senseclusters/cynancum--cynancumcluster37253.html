Etymology

cynancum comes from old Greek parts cyn- meaning 'dog' and -anchum meaning 'to strangle', because people once thought some of these climbing plants could poison or choke dogs. That's why the name fits a tropical, climbing plant whose roots were even used in old medicine to 'make people vomit'.
