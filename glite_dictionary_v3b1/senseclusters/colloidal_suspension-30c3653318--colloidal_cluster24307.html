Etymology

colloidal suspension gives a simple image: colloidal means very tiny solid bits and suspension means they are 'suspended' in a liquid, like fat drops in milk that float in the water but do not dissolve, so the phrase means a mix where small particles 'stay mixed' rather than dissolving.
