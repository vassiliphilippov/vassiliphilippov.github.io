Etymology

Hypotonicity comes from the parts hypo- meaning 'less' or 'under' and tonic meaning 'tone' or 'tension', like the common word tone. In the sense of solutions, it means there is 'less' pushing force to stop water, so water moves into cells and they can swell, which is why we talk about a solution's hypotonicity.
