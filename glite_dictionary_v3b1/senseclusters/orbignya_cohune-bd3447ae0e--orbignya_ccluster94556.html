Etymology

Orbignya cohune may come from the scientist Orbigny and the local word cohune for the palm's large nut, so imagine Orbigny pointing at a heavy nut to help you remember it. That is why the name refers to a tropical palm with big, oil-rich nuts used for cooking, skin products and a hard 'vegetable ivory'.
