Etymology

Dalmatia pyrethrum gets its name from where it grows and what people used it for: Dalmatia is a coastal region in the Balkans, and pyrethrum is the white-flowered plant local people crushed to kill insects. People would grind its flowers and leaves into a powder for gardens and clothes, so the name is linked to its use as a 'natural insecticide'.
