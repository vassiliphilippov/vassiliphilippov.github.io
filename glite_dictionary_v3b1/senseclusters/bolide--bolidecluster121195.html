Etymology

The word bolide comes from an old Greek word bolis, meaning 'missile' or 'thrown object', and it has the same idea as ballistic about things that are shot or thrown. So a bolide is like a space 'missile' that burns very brightly and can explode in the air, which is why we use the word for a very bright meteor that may explode in the atmosphere.
