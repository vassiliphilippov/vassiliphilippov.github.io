Etymology

Gunpowder Plot gets its name from a real plan in 1605 to use gunpowder to blow up the Houses of Parliament and kill King James I. Guy Fawkes was found guarding barrels of gunpowder under the building on November 5, so Gunpowder Plot today means that failed 'plan' and is remembered each year with fireworks and bonfires.
