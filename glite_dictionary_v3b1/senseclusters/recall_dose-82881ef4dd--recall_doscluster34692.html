Etymology

recall dose may come from the simple idea of 'call back' plus 'dose': recall means 'call back' and dose is one amount of medicine. A second dose 'reminds' the body to make stronger protection after the first shot, so that's why a recall dose means an extra shot given to make the first one work better.
