Etymology

biserial correlation comes from bi- meaning 'two' and serial meaning 'series' or 'list'. It gives a simple picture: it measures the link between a long list of numbers and a two-part 'yes or no' list, so the name helps you remember it shows the relationship between a variable with many values and a variable with just two choices.
