Etymology

crinkle-root comes from the simple English parts crinkle ('small folds' or 'wrinkles') and root ('underground part of a plant'). The plant has crinkled leaves and a strong, scaly root that people used to add flavor or for medicine, so the name tells you how the plant looks and why its root was important.
