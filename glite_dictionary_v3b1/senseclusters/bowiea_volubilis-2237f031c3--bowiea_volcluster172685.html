Etymology

Bowiea volubilis may come from a plant collector's name, with Bowiea from James Bowie and volubilis a Latin word meaning 'twining'. The name gives a clear picture: the plant grows from a large bulb and sends bright green, leafless stems that twist around supports, so the name helps you remember how it looks.
