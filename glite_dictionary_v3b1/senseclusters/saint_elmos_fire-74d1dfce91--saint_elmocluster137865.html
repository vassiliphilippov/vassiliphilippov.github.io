Etymology

Saint Elmo's fire may come from old sailors who saw a blue glow on ship masts and wires during storms and thought it was the light of Saint Elmo watching over them, so they called the glow a 'fire' even though it was not a real flame. Today Saint Elmo's fire means the bright electric glow you can see on pointed objects like masts or wings during a storm.
