Etymology

The name Dáil Éireann comes from Irish words: Dáil means 'assembly' and Éireann means 'of Ireland'. The name was chosen for the new Irish parliament in 1919 to show it was Ireland's own assembly, so today Dáil Éireann means 'the lower house of Ireland's parliament'.
