Etymology

cowpen daisy probably comes from farmers who saw bright yellow daisy flowers growing beside a cowpen, meaning 'a pen where cows are kept'. So the parts cowpen and daisy make a simple picture and explain why the name means 'a daisy-like plant that often grows near cows'.
