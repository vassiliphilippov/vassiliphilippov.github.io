Etymology

contaminating comes from the old Latin verb contaminare, which meant 'to make unclean' and combines con- 'with' and a root meaning 'to touch', a bit like the idea behind contact. So something contaminating is making a place or thing impure by 'touching' or mixing in harmful or unwanted substances.
