Etymology

gas phlegmon uses a clear image: gas refers to the pockets of gas that form in the body from bacteria and phlegmon was an old word for a spreading, painful sore. So the name shows it is a very fast, severe 'infection' that fills tissues with gas and can destroy them quickly.
