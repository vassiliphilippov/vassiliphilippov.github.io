Etymology

bacteriologist comes from a Greek root bacteri- that meant 'small rod' because early scientists saw tiny rod shapes when they looked with a microscope, and the ending -ologist means someone who 'studies', just like in biologist. So a bacteriologist is a person who studies bacteria.
