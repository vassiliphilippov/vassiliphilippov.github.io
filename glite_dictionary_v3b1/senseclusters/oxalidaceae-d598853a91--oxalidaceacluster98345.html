Etymology

Oxalidaceae gets its name from the plant name Oxalis, which comes from the old Greek word oxys meaning 'sharp' or 'sour' because the leaves taste sour, and the ending -aceae marks a plant 'family', so Oxalidaceae is simply the wood-sorrel or 'sour-plant' family of herbs.
