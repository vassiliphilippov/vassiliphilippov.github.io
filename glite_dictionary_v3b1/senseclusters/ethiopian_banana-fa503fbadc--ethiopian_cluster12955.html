Etymology

Ethiopian banana gets its name from how it looks and where it grows: it has big, banana-like leaves and comes from Ethiopia, even though its fruit is not eaten. That's why the name now means this Ethiopian plant whose large leaves are used to wrap food and whose young flower shoots people do eat.
