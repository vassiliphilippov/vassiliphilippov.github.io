Etymology

obelus comes from Greek obelos, 'spit' or 'pointed stick', and was used by ancient readers as a sharp mark beside text. This dagger-like sign (†) came to show a footnote or to mark that someone had died, which is why an obelus can mean a note mark or a death mark.
