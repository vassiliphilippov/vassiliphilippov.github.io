Etymology

powder method comes from the simple image of grinding a crystal into a fine powder, so many tiny crystal pieces point in many directions. When X-rays hit that powder they make a clear pattern that lets scientists 'identify' the mineral, so the name means a method to find what a crystal is by its X-ray pattern.
