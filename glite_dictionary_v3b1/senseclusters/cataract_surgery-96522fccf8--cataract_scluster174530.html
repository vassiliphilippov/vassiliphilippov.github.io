Etymology

cataract surgery comes from the word cataract, which once meant a waterfall or a curtain of water and gave people the picture of a curtain or cloud over the eye when sight was blocked. In cataract surgery the cloudy lens is taken out and often replaced so light can pass again, so the phrase now means the operation that fixes a 'cloudy' eye and helps to 'restore sight'.
