Etymology

Autoclaved concrete comes from how the blocks are made: wet concrete is put into a large machine called an autoclave, which is like a big 'pressure cooker' using heat and pressure. That's why the term means a type of concrete made this way, which becomes strong, lightweight, and gives good insulation and resistance to fire and mold.
