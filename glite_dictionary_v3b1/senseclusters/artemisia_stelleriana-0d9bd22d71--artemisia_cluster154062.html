Etymology

Artemisia stelleriana is a plant name made of two parts: Artemisia, a name linked to the goddess Artemis or an ancient queen, and stelleriana, which 'honors' the explorer Georg Steller who collected coastal plants. So the name helps you remember a silvery, seaside plant tied to Artemis and a sea explorer.
