Etymology

Pepper shrub may get its name because its little black fruits look like 'pepper' seeds and its leaves are often 'peppered' with red and yellow spots. That's why pepper refers to the spotted look or pepper-like berries and shrub means a small bush, so the name describes a small evergreen bush with colorful, spotted leaves and tiny black fruits.
