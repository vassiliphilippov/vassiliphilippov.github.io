Etymology

magnetic bubble memory comes from how the device works: it stores information as tiny round magnetic areas called 'bubbles' on a thin layer of magnetic material. So the name joins magnetic, meaning using magnets, bubble, meaning the small round areas, and memory, meaning 'data storage', which explains why the term means a device that keeps information in tiny magnetic 'bubbles'.
