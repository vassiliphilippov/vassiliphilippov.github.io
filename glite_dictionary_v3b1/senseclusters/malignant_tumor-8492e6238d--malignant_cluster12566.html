Etymology

malignant tumor combines malignant (meaning 'bad' or 'harmful') and tumor (a lump of cells); imagine a harmful lump that keeps growing and sends out parts like roots that move into other areas, so it means a 'cancerous' growth that can 'spread' to other parts of the body.
