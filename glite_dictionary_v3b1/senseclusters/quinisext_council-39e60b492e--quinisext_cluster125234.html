Etymology

Quinisext Council gets its name from old words for 'five' and 'six', with quini meaning 'five' and sext meaning 'six', because this one meeting acted like both the fifth and the sixth church councils and made many rules. So the Quinisext Council refers to the special 692 church meeting that set important rules.
