Etymology

inferior thyroid vein literally tells where the vein is and what it does: inferior means 'lower', thyroid is the neck 'gland', and vein is a blood vessel. So this vein takes blood from the lower part of the thyroid and goes into a larger neck vein, which is why its name matches its meaning.
