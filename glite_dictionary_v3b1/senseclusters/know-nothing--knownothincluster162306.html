Etymology

know-nothing began in the 1800s in the United States when members of a secret political group would reply 'I know nothing' to questions, and the phrase stuck as a name for someone seen as uninformed. The parts know and nothing mean 'to know' and 'nothing', so calling someone a know-nothing today is an insult meaning they are ignorant or refuse to learn.
