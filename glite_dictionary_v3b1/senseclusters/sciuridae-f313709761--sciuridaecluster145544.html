Etymology

Sciuridae comes from the old Greek word skiouros, built from skia 'shadow' and oura 'tail', and from the Latin sciurus, which gave us the English word 'squirrel'. A helpful way to remember it is to picture a squirrel's big bushy tail making a shadow, because Sciuridae is the family name for all true squirrels.
