Etymology

geomantic comes from geo- meaning 'earth' and -mantic meaning 'reading signs', and geo- is the same root you see in geography. So geomantic literally means 'reading the earth', which is why it describes rituals that look at patterns in the ground to find spiritual meaning or choose a site.
