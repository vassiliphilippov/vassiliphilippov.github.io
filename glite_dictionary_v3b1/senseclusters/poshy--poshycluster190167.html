Etymology

poshy comes from posh + -y, where posh means 'stylish' or 'upper-class' and -y means 'like' or 'full of'. One popular story says posh came from 'port out, starboard home', but a helpful way to remember poshy is to think 'like posh', that is 'fancy' or 'for people with money'.
