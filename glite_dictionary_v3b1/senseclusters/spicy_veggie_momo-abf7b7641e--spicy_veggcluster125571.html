Etymology

spicy veggie momo comes from the mountain kitchens of Nepal and Tibet. The word momo means a small 'dumpling', and with spicy and veggie it shows a dumpling filled with hot, seasoned vegetables, so the name tells you it is a spicy vegetable dumpling often steamed or fried and eaten as 'street food'.
