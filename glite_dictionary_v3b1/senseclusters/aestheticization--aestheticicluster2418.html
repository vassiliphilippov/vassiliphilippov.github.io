Etymology

aestheticization comes from aesthetic meaning 'beauty' or 'artistic feeling' and the ending -ization meaning 'the act of making'. Think of it as 'turning something into beauty', which explains why it means focusing on how things look or treating them as art, sometimes ignoring practical or moral aspects.
