Etymology

thoughtlessness breaks into three parts: thought meaning 'an idea or paying attention', -less meaning 'without', and -ness which makes a noun. So thoughtlessness means 'the quality of not thinking about other people or the results of your actions', which is why it describes a lack of care or consideration.
