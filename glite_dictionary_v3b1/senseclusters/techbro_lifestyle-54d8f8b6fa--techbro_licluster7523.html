Etymology

techbro lifestyle may come from combining tech (short for 'technology') and bro (a word for a young man who likes to show off and look important), which gives a clear picture of young startup founders wearing hoodies, driving fast cars, and working long hours. That's why the phrase describes a way of living linked to wealthy, work-focused young people in the tech world.
