Etymology

The word hypersensitized comes from three parts: hyper- meaning 'too much', sens- meaning 'to feel', and -ized meaning 'made or become'. Think of hyper- like in hyperactive and sens- like in sensitive, so hypersensitized means 'made too sensitive' and reacts more than normal.
