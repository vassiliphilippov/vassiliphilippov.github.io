Etymology

rockabye comes from the old nursery rhyme 'rock-a-bye baby' and is made of rock meaning 'to move gently back and forth' and the soft ending -bye often heard in lullabies and goodbyes. So a rockabye is a gentle song you sing while rocking a child to sleep.
