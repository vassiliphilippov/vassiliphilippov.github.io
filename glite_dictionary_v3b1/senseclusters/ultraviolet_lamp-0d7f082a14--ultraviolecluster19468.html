Etymology

ultraviolet lamp gets its name from the kind of light it makes: ultraviolet is light just past the color violet in a rainbow, and a lamp is a thing that gives light. So, an ultraviolet lamp means 'a lamp that gives ultraviolet light', the kind that can make some paints glow or help kill germs.
