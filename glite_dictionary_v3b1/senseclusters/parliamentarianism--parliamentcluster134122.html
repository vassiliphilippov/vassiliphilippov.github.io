Etymology

parliamentarianism comes from parliament, a word from an old verb meaning 'to speak', because a parliament is a place where people meet and speak together, and the ending -ism means 'a system or practice'. So parliamentarianism can mean the system where elected representatives in a parliament form and control the government.
