Etymology

enlighten comes from the parts en- 'make' and light 'what makes things bright', the same idea behind the everyday words light and lighten. Think of it as 'putting light into the soul' or turning on a lamp in a dark room, so to enlighten someone spiritually is to give them inner light or deep understanding.
