Etymology

hydatidiform mole comes from hydatid (meaning 'water-filled sac') and mole (meaning 'mass'), because the tissue in the womb turns into many small fluid-filled cysts that look like a bunch of grapes. That's why the name describes this abnormal pregnancy growth that usually ends the pregnancy.
