Etymology

industrial-strength comes from industrial (from 'industry', meaning 'work in factories') and strength ('power' or 'being strong'), giving the idea of something made for heavy, factory use. So, something called industrial-strength is much more powerful or durable than a normal household product and meant for very hard jobs.
