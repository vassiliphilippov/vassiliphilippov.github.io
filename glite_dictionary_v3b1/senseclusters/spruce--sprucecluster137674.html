Etymology

spruce (to make neat) comes from an older use of spruce meaning items 'from Prussia' and later 'smart' for fine clothes, so to 'spruce up' is to make someone or something look smart and tidy. A helpful way to remember it is to think of cleaning or dressing to look well put together.
