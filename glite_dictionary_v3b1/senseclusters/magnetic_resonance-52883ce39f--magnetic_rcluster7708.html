Etymology

magnetic resonance combines magnetic, which is like a tiny magnet, and resonance, which is like something that 'rings' when you tap it. One simple image is tiny particles in the body acting like little magnets that 'ring' and send back a signal when we nudge them in a magnetic field, and that 'ringing' signal is why the term now means the effect used to make pictures of the inside of the body.
