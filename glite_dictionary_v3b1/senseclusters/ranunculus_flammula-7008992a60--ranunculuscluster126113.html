Etymology

Ranunculus flammula comes from old Latin words: Ranunculus means 'little frog' because these buttercups often grow at the water's edge where frogs live, and flammula means 'little flame' for the plant's small bright yellow flowers, so the name fits this wetland, yellow-flowering plant.
