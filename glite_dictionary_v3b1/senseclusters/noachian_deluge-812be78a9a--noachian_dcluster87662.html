Etymology

Noachian deluge comes from the Bible story of Noah, where a very large flood covers the whole world and only Noah's boat, the ark, survives. The word Noachian refers to Noah and deluge means flood, so the phrase now means 'a very large flood' or is used for any huge disaster.
