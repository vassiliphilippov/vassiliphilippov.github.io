Etymology

wood spirit may come from old chemical work: people heated wood and collected the clear, strong liquid that came out, calling it a spirit because it seemed like the wood's hidden essence. That's why wood spirit now means the dangerous alcohol 'methanol', in other words a 'poisonous alcohol' used as a solvent or fuel.
