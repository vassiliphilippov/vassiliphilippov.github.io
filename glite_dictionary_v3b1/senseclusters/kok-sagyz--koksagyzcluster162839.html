Etymology

kok-sagyz comes from Kazakh parts kok meaning 'blue/green' and sagyz meaning 'gum' or 'resin'. A helpful memory is that the plant's roots make a sticky gum like natural rubber, so kok-sagyz is the plant grown for rubber.
