Etymology

Welsh rarebit got its name as a joke: one popular story says people used to call simple melted cheese on toast 'Welsh rabbit' to tease the Welsh, and later the name changed to rarebit. Because it is melted cheese served on hot toast, the name now means this tasty 'cheese on toast' dish.
