Etymology

rozzer is British slang for a 'police officer' or 'cop'. Its exact origin is unclear, but one popular idea is that it began as a nickname like Roz, so a helpful way to remember rozzer is to picture a police officer called Roz — that is why the word means a police officer in informal speech.
