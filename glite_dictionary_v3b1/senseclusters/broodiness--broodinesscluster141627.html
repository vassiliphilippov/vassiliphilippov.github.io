Etymology

broodiness comes from the word brood, which meant 'a group of young animals' and the verb 'to brood' meaning 'to sit on eggs'. A bird that broods is quiet and still, so people began to use 'brood' for being deep in thought or gloomy, and that's why broodiness can mean a pensive or gloomy mood.
