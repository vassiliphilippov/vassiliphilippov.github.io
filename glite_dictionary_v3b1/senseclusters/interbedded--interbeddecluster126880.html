Etymology

The word interbedded combines inter- meaning 'between' and bed meaning 'a layer', as in a 'river bed' or a 'flower bed'. Think of different 'beds', like mattresses or layers of soil, placed between each other, so interbedded means layers of different materials arranged between one another.
