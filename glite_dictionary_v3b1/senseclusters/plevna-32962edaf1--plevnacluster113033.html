Etymology

Plevna is the older name used in Turkish and older English for the Bulgarian town Pleven. The town name comes from an old Slavic part plev- meaning 'chaff' or 'straw', so a helpful way to remember Plevna is as a farming place that later became famous for the 1877 siege.
