Etymology

anesthetize comes from Greek parts an- meaning 'without' and aesthesis meaning 'feeling' or 'sensation', and it shares a root with aesthetic, which is about feeling or perception. So, to anesthetize someone is to make them 'without feeling', which is why it stops pain during medical procedures.
