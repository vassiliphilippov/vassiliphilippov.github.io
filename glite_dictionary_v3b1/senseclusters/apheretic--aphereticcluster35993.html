Etymology

The word apheretic comes from the Greek word aphaeresis, which means 'a taking away' and breaks into a- 'away' and phaer- 'to take'. So apheretic describes a sound or syllable that is 'taken away' from the start of a word, as in 'squire' from 'esquire'.
