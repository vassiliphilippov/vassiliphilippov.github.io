Etymology

felicitously comes from the Latin words felix and felicitas, which mean 'happy' or 'good fortune'. Think of the English word felicity, which means 'great happiness' or a 'pleasing choice'. So, to do something felicitously is to do it in a pleasing and very suitable way.
