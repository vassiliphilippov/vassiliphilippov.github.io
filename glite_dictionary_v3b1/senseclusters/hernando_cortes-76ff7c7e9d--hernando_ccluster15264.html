Etymology

Hernando Cortes was a Spanish soldier who sailed to Mexico in the early 1500s and, with a small force and help from local peoples who opposed the Aztecs, captured the great city of Tenochtitlan. Because of this event, his name is remembered as the man who brought the 'Aztec Empire' under Spanish rule.
