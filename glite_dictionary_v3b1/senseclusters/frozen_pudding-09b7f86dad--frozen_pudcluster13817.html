Etymology

frozen pudding likely comes from a simple kitchen idea: cooks would take a creamy pudding and let it become frozen, sometimes adding nuts or a little alcohol so the texture stayed nice, to make a firm, cool treat. That's why the name means a 'chilled dessert' of custard or cream served frozen.
