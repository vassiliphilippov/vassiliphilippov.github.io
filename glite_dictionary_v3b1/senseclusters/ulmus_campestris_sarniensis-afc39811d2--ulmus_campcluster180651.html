Etymology

Ulmus campestris sarniensis comes from Latin words: Ulmus means 'elm', campestris means 'of the fields', and sarniensis means 'from Sarnia', the old name for the island of Jersey. So the name gives a simple picture of an elm from the fields or Jersey, which helps you remember this special English elm grown in gardens.
