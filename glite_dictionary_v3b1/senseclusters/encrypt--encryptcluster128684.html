Etymology

The word encrypt comes from two parts: en- meaning 'in' and crypt meaning 'hidden', the same root you see in cryptic and crypt. So to encrypt something is to put it 'in' a 'hidden' form, changing it into a secret code so people who should not see it cannot read it.
