Etymology

The term pulmonary congestion comes from a simple image: pulmonary means 'lungs' and congestion is like a traffic jam, a place that is too full or blocked. So pulmonary congestion means the 'lungs' are full of fluid or blood, which causes 'breathing trouble'.
