Etymology

Schaffneria nigripes is a scientific name whose first part, Schaffneria, may honor a botanist named Schaffner, and whose second part, nigripes, comes from Latin meaning 'black foot', a picture of dark stalks or a dark base on the fern. That's why the name helps you remember this fern as the one with a 'black foot'.
