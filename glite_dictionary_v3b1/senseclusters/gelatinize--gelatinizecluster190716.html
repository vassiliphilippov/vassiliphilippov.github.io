Etymology

The word gelatinize comes from gelatin, which in turn comes from the Latin verb gelare meaning 'to freeze' or 'to make stiff'. Think of the common word gel or 'jelly' — to gelatinize is to 'make into gel' or 'become jelly-like', which is why we use it for sauces or starch that thicken into a jellyy texture.
