Etymology

pertinaciously comes from Latin parts per- ('thoroughly') and ten- ('to hold'), the same root you see in tenacious and tenacity. Imagine someone who 'holds on' to an idea and refuses to give up, so to act pertinaciously is to work stubbornly and persistently until you succeed.
