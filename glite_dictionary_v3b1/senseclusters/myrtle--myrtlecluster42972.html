Etymology

myrtle comes from the old Greek word myrtos, meaning 'fragrant shrub', and people long ago used its shiny leaves and flowers in garlands and perfume. That's why myrtle today names a small evergreen shrub with glossy leaves and sweet-smelling flowers.
