Etymology

Crotalus adamanteus may come from old words that describe the snake: Crotalus means 'rattler', like something that shakes and makes a sound, and adamanteus means 'diamond-like' for the dark diamond marks on its back. So the name points to the large rattlesnake known for its loud rattle and diamond-shaped patterns.
