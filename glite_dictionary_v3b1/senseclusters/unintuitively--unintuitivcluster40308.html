Etymology

unintuitively breaks into un- meaning 'not' and intuitive, from intuition meaning 'to understand without thinking' or a 'gut feeling'. So if something is done unintuitively, it is done in a way that does 'not' match your first, natural guess and therefore feels hard to understand.
