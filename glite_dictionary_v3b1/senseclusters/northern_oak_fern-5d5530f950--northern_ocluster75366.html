Etymology

northern oak fern gets its name from a simple picture: it is a fern that grows in the north and has leaves shaped like small oak leaves. So 'northern' tells where it lives, 'oak' shows the leaf shape, and 'fern' tells the plant type, which is why the name fits this yellow-green fern in rocky, cool places.
