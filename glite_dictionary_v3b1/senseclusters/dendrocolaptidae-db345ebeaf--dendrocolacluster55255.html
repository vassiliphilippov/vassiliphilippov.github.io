Etymology

Dendrocolaptidae comes from Greek parts dendro- 'tree' and kolaptēs 'pecker', with the family ending -idae. So it literally means 'tree peckers', which fits these woodcreeper birds that climb trunks and peck bark like small woodpeckers.
