Etymology

lantern-fly comes from an old idea that this bright, strange insect could give off light like a small lantern; people once thought its long snout might glow, though that is a myth. That's why the name joins lantern ('a light') and fly, and it helps you remember the insect by its bright colors and unusual head shape.
