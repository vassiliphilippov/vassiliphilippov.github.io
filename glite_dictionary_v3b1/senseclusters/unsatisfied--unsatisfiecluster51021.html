Etymology

unsatisfied begins with the prefix un- 'not' and the verb satisfy, which comes from the Latin root satis meaning 'enough' and appears in the common word satisfaction. So, to be unsatisfied is to feel that something is 'not enough'—your needs or wishes are not met, which explains the uneasy, restless feeling.
