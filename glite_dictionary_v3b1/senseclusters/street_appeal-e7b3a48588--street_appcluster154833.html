Etymology

street appeal comes from the world of selling homes: imagine a house seen from the road with tidy flowers, fresh paint and a neat front that catches the eye. The words street (what you see from the road) and appeal (how attractive it looks) create the picture, so street appeal means the attractive look of a place 'when seen from the street'.
