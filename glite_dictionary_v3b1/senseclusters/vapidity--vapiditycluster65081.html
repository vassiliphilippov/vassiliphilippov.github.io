Etymology

vapidity comes from vapid, from the Latin vapidus, and is linked to vapor, which means 'steam' or 'breath'. Think of steam as light and empty, so something 'vapid' has no strong taste or energy; that's why vapidity means the quality of being dull or lifeless.
