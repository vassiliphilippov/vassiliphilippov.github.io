Etymology

Wikipedian comes from Wikipedia, a name made from wiki ('quick' in Hawaiian) and -pedia (as in encyclopedia, meaning 'learning'). So Wikipedian as an adjective means 'of or like Wikipedia', for example its neutral style, rules, or community ways.
