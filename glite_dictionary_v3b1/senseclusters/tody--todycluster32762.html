Etymology

tody comes from the scientific name Todus that early naturalists gave to these small Caribbean birds, and that name probably came from a local Caribbean word for the bird. A helpful way to remember it is that tody sounds a bit like 'tiny' and these birds are small, bright insect-eaters from the West Indies.
