Etymology

Lavandula latifolia comes from Latin: Lavandula is linked to 'to wash' because people used lavender in baths, and latifolia means 'broad leaves'. So the name gives a clear picture of a lavender used for washing with wider leaves, and that fits the spike lavender we grow today for its strong smell and fragrant oil.
