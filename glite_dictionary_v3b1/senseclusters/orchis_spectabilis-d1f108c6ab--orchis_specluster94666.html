Etymology

Orchis spectabilis comes from an old Greek word and a Latin word: Orchis (Greek for 'testicle') because the plant's two round root bulbs look like that, and spectabilis (Latin for 'showy') because of its bright, hooded flowers. So the name tells you about both the bulb shape and the plant's showy flowers.
