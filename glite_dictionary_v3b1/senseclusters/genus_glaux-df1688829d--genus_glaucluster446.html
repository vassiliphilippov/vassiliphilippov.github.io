Etymology

Genus Glaux: The name Genus Glaux comes from an old Greek word 'glaux', which can mean 'owl' or 'pale grey'. People thought the plant's small, round, light-coloured flowers looked like an owl's eye by the sea, so the name came to be used for the coastal plant 'sea milkwort'.
