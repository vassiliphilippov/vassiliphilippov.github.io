Etymology

graphic symbol comes from graphic meaning 'picture' and symbol meaning 'a sign that stands for something'. Imagine a tiny picture on a screen, like an emoji or icon, that stands for an idea, object, or feeling, and that is why it means 'a pictorial or digital sign'.
