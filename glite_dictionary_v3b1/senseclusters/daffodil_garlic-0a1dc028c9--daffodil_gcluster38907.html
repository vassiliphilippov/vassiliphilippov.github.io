Etymology

daffodil garlic may get its name from the way it looks: it has small groups of white flowers that remind people of tiny daffodil flowers, and its leaves and bulbs smell faintly of garlic. So the name helps you remember it is a 'wild onion' with a 'mild garlic smell'.
