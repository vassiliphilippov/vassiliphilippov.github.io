Etymology

wrapped in bacon comes from a clear cooking picture: small foods are wrapped in strips of bacon so the bacon becomes crispy and adds smoky, salty flavor while the inside cooks. So, saying a dish is wrapped in bacon means 'cooked or prepared by being covered in bacon'.
