Etymology

tarsitis comes from two parts: tars-, which names the firm strip inside the eyelid, and -itis, meaning 'inflammation', the same ending you see in tonsillitis or arthritis. So, tarsitis is inflammation of the eyelid's tarsal plate, which is why it causes swelling and redness.
