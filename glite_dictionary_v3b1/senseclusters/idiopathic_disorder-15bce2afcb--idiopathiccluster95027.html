Etymology

idiopathic disorder comes from Greek words: idio- meaning 'own' or 'personal' and -pathic meaning 'suffering' or 'disease'. It describes a problem that seems to come from inside the body with no known outside cause, so it now means 'a disorder with an unknown cause'.
