Etymology

capillary vessel comes from a word meaning 'hair' because capillary describes tubes as thin as a hair and vessel means a blood tube. Picture tiny hair-like tubes where oxygen and nutrients move out of the blood into nearby cells — that's why capillary vessels means the 'smallest blood vessels' that allow this 'exchange'.
