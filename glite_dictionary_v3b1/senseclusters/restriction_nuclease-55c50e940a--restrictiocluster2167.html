Etymology

restriction nuclease gives a simple image: nuclease is like tiny scissors that 'cut' DNA, and restriction means those scissors only work at certain 'restricted' spots, like a barcode on the strand. So a restriction nuclease is an enzyme that 'cuts' DNA at specific places to make precise fragments used in labs.
