Etymology

thyroxine comes from thyroid and the chemical ending -ine, and thyroid itself comes from an old Greek word meaning 'shield' because the gland looks like a small shield at the front of the neck. So, thyroxine is the hormone made by that 'shield' gland and it helps control how fast your body uses energy.
