Etymology

The word bloodhound comes from the old phrase blooded hound, where blood meant 'well-bred' or 'of noble birth' rather than the blood you bleed. A bloodhound was a prized hunting dog, and today the name fits because these dogs are famous for their powerful nose and tracking skills.
