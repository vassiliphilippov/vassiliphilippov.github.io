Etymology

ultrasonic flaw detector gets its name from two simple parts: ultrasonic means sound too high for our ears, and a flaw detector is a tool that finds faults. Like a bat sending high sounds and listening for echoes, the device sends ultrasonic waves into a material and listens for changes, so it can find 'hidden cracks' inside.
