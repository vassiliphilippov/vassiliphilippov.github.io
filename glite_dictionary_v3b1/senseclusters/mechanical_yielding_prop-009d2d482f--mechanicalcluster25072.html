Etymology

mechanical yielding prop comes from its literal design: a metal prop whose yielding parts are inner tubes that slide so it can 'give a little' when heavy weight presses on it. That's why the name means a support that moves in a controlled way to 'absorb pressure' and protect tunnels or structures.
