Etymology

Lysimachia comes from the Greek name Lysimachos, built from lysis 'to loosen' and mache 'fight'. One story says the plant was thought to 'stop fighting', and that idea matches its English common name 'loosestrife', which makes a handy image to remember this yellow, water-loving flower.
