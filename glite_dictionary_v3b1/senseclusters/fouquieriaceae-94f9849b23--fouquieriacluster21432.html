Etymology

Fouquieriaceae comes from the plant genus Fouquieria plus the Latin ending -aceae, which means 'family' in plant names. The genus is named after a person, so the whole name means the 'Fouquieria' family, the group of plants that includes the ocotillo. That's why Fouquieriaceae refers to that small family of spiny shrubs and trees.
