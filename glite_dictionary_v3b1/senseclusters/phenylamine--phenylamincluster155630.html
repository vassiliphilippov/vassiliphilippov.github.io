Etymology

phenylamine breaks into phenyl and amine, where phenyl names the 'benzene' part as in phenol, and amine comes from 'ammonia' meaning a 'nitrogen' group. So phenylamine is an 'amine' attached to a 'phenyl' part, which explains why it is the chemical also called aniline and why it is used in dyes but is toxic.
