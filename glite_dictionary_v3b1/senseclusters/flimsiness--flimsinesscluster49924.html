Etymology

flimsiness comes from the adjective flimsy, which probably began as a way to say something was very light or 'not solid' and may be linked by sound to flim-flam, a word for a small trick. So, flimsiness means the quality of being weak, fragile, or poorly made — 'not strong' or 'not reliable'.
