Etymology

The name tenth cranial nerve simply marks it as the tenth nerve that comes from the brain. It is also called the vagus nerve from a word meaning 'wandering', because it wanders down into the chest and belly to reach the heart, lungs and stomach, which is why it helps control many organs.
