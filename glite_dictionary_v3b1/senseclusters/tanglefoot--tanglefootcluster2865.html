Etymology

The word tanglefoot joins tangle and foot, giving the picture of a 'foot caught' or 'stuck' in something. It began as a name for a sticky paste that would trap insects by tangling their feet, and later people used it as a joking name for a cheap, strong drink that can 'tangle' your feet and make you stumble.
