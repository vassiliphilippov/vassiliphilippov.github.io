Etymology

Malpighia glabra is the scientific name for a small tropical shrub called the 'Barbados cherry' that bears bright red, 'cherry-like' fruit. The second word glabra means 'smooth', so the name helps you picture the plant's shiny leaves and tasty cherry-like berries.
