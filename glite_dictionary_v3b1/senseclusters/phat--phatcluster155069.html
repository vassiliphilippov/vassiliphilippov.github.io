Etymology

The word phat came from American hip-hop as a spelling of fat, which in slang can mean 'big', 'full', or 'very good'. A helpful way to remember it is the informal letters P.H.A.T. that stand for 'pretty hot and tempting', though that is a folk idea and not the true origin. That's why phat means 'very stylish' or 'excellent' today.
