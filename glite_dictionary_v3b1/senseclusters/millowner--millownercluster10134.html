Etymology

millowner is made of mill and owner. A mill (think windmill or watermill) is a place with big stones or machines that 'grind' grain or drive other machines, and an owner is 'one who has something', so a millowner is 'one who owns a mill', today used for someone who owns or runs a factory or mill.
