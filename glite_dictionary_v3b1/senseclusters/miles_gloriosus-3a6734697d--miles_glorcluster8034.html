Etymology

The noun miles gloriosus comes from the Latin words miles ('soldier') and gloriosus ('glorious'), and it named a comic character in Roman plays who bragged loudly about battles he never fought. That picture of a showy, false hero is why miles gloriosus now means 'a braggart' or an arrogant, boastful soldier.
