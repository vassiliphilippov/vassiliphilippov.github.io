Etymology

periostitis breaks into three parts: peri- meaning 'around', osteo- meaning 'bone', and -itis meaning 'inflammation', like 'perimeter' for 'around' and 'arthritis' for 'inflammation'. So, periostitis is an inflammation 'around the bone', which is why it causes swelling and pain near a bone.
