Etymology

The word macaroon comes from the Italian word maccarone, which meant 'paste' or 'crushed' because cooks crushed almonds to make the dough, and it is related to the familiar word macaroni. So a macaroon is a small, chewy sweet made from that crushed almond or coconut paste.
