Etymology

Achras zapota may come from old local names for the tree and its fruit: the part Achras was used for similar tropical trees, and the part zapota comes from Spanish 'zapote', a native word for a soft, sweet fruit. People noticed the tree's sticky sap used as 'chewing gum' and its tasty fruit, so the name became linked to the tree we now call Achras zapota.
