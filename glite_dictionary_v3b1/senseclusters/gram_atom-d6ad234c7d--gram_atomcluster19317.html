Etymology

gram atom comes from joining gram, a unit of weight, and atom, a tiny particle. It means the amount of an element that 'weighs the same number of grams as the element's atomic weight', so one gram atom of oxygen weighs 16 grams and that simple picture helps you remember the meaning.
