Etymology

The phrase magnetic north comes from how a compass works: the north‑seeking end of the needle points to a place where the Earth acts like a big magnet. The word magnetic shows the 'magnet' force and north is the direction the needle points, so following a compass takes you toward 'magnetic north' rather than 'true north'.
