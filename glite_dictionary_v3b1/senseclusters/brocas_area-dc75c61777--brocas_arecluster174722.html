Etymology

Broca's area is named after the French doctor Paul Broca, who found a patient who could not 'speak' because a small part of the left front of his brain was damaged. Because that small area controls the movements needed for 'speech', scientists kept the name and now use Broca's area to mean the brain part that helps produce words.
