Etymology

The noun contact mike comes from how it is used: contact means it touches a surface and mike is short for microphone, so you put it on a guitar, window, or machine to pick up vibrations directly instead of sounds in the air. So, it means 'a microphone you attach to an object to record its vibrations rather than sounds in the air'.
