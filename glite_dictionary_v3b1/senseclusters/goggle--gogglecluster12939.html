Etymology

goggle comes from an old verb that meant 'to stare with wide eyes'. It is the same root as the everyday word goggles ('eye-protectors'), so imagine the round glass making your eyes look very wide — that picture helps you remember that to goggle is to look with very wide-open eyes.
