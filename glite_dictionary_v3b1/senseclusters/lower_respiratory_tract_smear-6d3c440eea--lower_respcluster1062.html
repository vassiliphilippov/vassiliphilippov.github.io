Etymology

lower respiratory tract smear names a clear image: a tiny sample taken from the lower respiratory tract, the deep parts of the lungs, and spread on a glass slide as a smear to look under a microscope. So, the term means 'a prepared slide sample from the lower lungs used to check for disease'.
