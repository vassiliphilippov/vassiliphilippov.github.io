Etymology

Northern harrier gets its name from where it lives and how it hunts: northern because it lives in the north, and harrier from a word meaning 'to harry', that is, 'to chase'. So the name makes you picture a bird gliding low over fields and marshes to hunt, which is why we call it that today.
