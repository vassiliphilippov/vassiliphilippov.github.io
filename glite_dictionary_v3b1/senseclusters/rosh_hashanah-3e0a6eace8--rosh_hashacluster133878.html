Etymology

Rosh Hashanah comes from Hebrew words Rosh meaning 'head' and Hashanah meaning 'year', so it literally means the 'head of the year'. That's why it is the Jewish festival that marks the start of the year with prayer, the blowing of the shofar, and wishes for a sweet new year.
