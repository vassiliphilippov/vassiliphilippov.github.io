Etymology

The word profoundly comes from Latin parts pro- ('forth') and fundus ('bottom'), so it originally meant something like 'very deep' or 'from the bottom'. It shares the same root as foundation, which also comes from the idea of a 'base' or 'bottom'. That's why when something affects you profoundly, it touches you 'deep down' or has a strong, lasting effect.
