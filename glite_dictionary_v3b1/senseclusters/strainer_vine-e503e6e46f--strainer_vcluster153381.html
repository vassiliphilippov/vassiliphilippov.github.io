Etymology

strainer vine gets its name from the plant's fruit: when the inside dries it becomes a fibrous natural strainer — like a kitchen 'sponge' that can 'strain' or filter liquids. Because it grows as a climbing vine, the name simply says it is a vine with a useful, strainer-like fruit.
