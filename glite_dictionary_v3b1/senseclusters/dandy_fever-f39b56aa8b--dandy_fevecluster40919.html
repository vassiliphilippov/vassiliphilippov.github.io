Etymology

dandy fever may come from the Caribbean word 'dengue', which English ears heard as dandy. One popular story says people with the illness moved stiffly and had sore joints, like an affected 'dandy', so the image of a stiff dandy helped the name stick for the fever that causes joint pain.
