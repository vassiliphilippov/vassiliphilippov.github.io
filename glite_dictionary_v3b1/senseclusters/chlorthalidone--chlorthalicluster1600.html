Etymology

The word chlorthalidone breaks into chloro- ('containing chlorine') and thalidone (the family name for a type of diuretic medicine). So, chlorthalidone is a medicine that has a chlorine atom in its chemical name and helps the body remove extra water and lower blood pressure.
