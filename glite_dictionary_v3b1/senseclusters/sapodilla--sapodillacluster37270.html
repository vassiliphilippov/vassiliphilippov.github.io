Etymology

The word sapodilla for the tree comes from the same root tzapotl via Spanish zapote and the ending -illa. The sapodilla tree gives a sticky sap called chicle, used to make chewing gum, so a helpful way to remember the tree is as the source of sweet fruit and sticky sap.
