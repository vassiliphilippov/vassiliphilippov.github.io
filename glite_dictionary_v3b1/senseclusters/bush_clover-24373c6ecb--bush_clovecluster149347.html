Etymology

bush clover comes from how the plant looks and how people used it. It grows like a small bush and has leaves like true clover, and because farmers used it as 'animal feed' and to help the soil, the name came to mean 'a plant that grows like a small bush and is used for animal feed and to help the soil'.
