Etymology

ceremoniousness comes from the word ceremony, which once meant a religious 'ritual' or a very formal public event, plus the endings -ous ('full of') and -ness (which makes a noun). So ceremoniousness is the quality of being full of ceremony, meaning acting very formal and polite like at an important ritual or official event.
