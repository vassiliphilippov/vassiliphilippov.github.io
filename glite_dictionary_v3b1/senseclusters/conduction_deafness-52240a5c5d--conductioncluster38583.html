Etymology

conduction deafness uses conduction to mean 'carrying' and deafness to mean 'hearing loss'. Imagine sound as a small car trying to travel a road into the inner ear, but the middle ear bones or fluid block the road so the sound cannot be carried through, and the person cannot hear those sounds.
