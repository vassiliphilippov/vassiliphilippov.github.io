Etymology

loose-jointed comes from the parts loose ('not tight') and joint ('where two parts meet', like a knee or a hinge). So something loose-jointed has parts that are not firmly joined and is wobbly or very flexible, which fits both a loose toy or sofa and a style of writing that does not flow well.
