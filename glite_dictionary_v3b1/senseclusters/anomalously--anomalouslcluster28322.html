Etymology

anomalously comes from Greek parts a- 'not' and a root meaning 'even', so it originally meant 'not even' or 'uneven' and now describes something that happens in a strange or 'not normal' way. A helpful way to remember it is to think of it as 'not normal', which links it to the common word normal.
