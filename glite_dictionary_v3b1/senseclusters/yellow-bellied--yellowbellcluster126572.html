Etymology

The word yellow-bellied comes from the words yellow and belly, making a picture of someone with a 'yellow' stomach. Because 'yellow' has long been used to mean 'afraid' or 'weak', yellow-bellied is a way to call someone 'cowardly' or lacking courage.
