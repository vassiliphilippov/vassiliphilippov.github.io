Etymology

The idiom find one's way out of a paper bag comes from a silly picture: imagine a person stuck inside a small paper bag who cannot find the exit. One popular image is that if someone cannot even do that, they are very bad at simple tasks, so the phrase means 'unable to handle even the easiest problems'.
