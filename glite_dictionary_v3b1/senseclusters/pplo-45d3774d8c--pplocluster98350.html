Etymology

PPLO is a short name from the phrase pleuro-pneumonia-like organisms, because these tiny germs were first linked to 'pneumonia' and so the name meant 'pneumonia-like organisms'. That's why PPLO now refers to a group of small, often antibiotic-resistant germs called mycoplasmas that can cause an unusual, 'atypical' form of pneumonia.
