Etymology

bacterial plaque names the sticky film that builds up on teeth. Bacterial means tiny germs and plaque is a soft coating. Picture a thin, sticky layer of germs and food on your teeth that can harden and cause 'tooth decay' and 'gum disease', so the phrase reminds you that this harmful film must be brushed and flossed away.
