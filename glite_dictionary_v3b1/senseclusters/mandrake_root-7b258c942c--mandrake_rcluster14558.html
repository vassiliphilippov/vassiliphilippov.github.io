Etymology

The mandrake root is the root of the mandrake plant that often looks like the shape of a tiny person, and one popular story says it would scream when pulled from the ground. Because of that strange shape and scream, people long ago believed the mandrake root had special powers and used it as 'medicine' or a charm, so it became linked to 'magic' and 'good luck'.
