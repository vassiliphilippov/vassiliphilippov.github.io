Etymology

unimpassioned breaks into un- meaning 'not' and passion meaning 'strong feeling'. A helpful way to remember it is to picture someone speaking calmly, without strong feeling. So, unimpassioned means 'not influenced by strong emotions', which is why we use it for calm, fair decisions or explanations.
