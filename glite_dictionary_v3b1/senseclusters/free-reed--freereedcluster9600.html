Etymology

free-reed is a simple descriptive name made from free and reed, where free means 'able to move' and reed is the thin strip that vibrates in wind instruments. It names a reed that vibrates 'freely' through a slot without sealing it, which is exactly how harmonicas and accordions make sound.
