Etymology

unsympathetic uses un- 'not' with sympathetic (from syn- 'together' and pathos 'feeling'), which can be read as 'not in harmony'. So an unsympathetic place or person is one that does not feel right with you, and therefore is not suited or friendly to your tastes.
