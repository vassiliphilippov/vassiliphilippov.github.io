Etymology

Coelenterata comes from Greek parts koilos 'hollow' and enteron 'intestine', so it means 'hollow gut'. A helpful image is that jellyfish, corals, and sea anemones have one large central body cavity instead of a true gut, which is why older books used that name even though scientists now divide them into different groups.
