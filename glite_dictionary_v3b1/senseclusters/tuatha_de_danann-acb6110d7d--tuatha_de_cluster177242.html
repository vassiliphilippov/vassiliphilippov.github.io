Etymology

Tuatha De Danann comes from old Irish words: Tuatha means 'people' and De Danann means 'of the goddess Danu', and one popular story says it names a magical people who once ruled Ireland. That's why Tuatha De Danann refers today to a group of powerful, magical beings — like 'gods' or 'demigods' — in old Irish stories.
