Etymology

adulteress comes from adultery plus the ending -ess. A helpful way to remember adultery is that it has the root alter, which means 'other' and shows up in words like alternate, so it names sex with an 'other' person, and -ess means 'woman', which is why an adulteress is a woman who commits adultery.
