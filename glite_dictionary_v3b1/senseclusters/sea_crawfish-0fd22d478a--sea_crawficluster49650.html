Etymology

sea crawfish: The name sea crawfish may come from early fishermen who found a lobster-like animal in the sea that looked like a small crawfish from rivers. So the name means a 'crawfish' of the sea, which explains why it refers to a large edible marine crustacean with a spiny shell and no big pincers.
