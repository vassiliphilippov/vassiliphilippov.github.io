Etymology

The expression dope slap may come from the simple image of giving a quick slap to someone acting like a dope, meaning a 'foolish person', to make them pay attention. That's why it now means a quick, often playful or annoyed hit to make someone 'wake up', 'realize' something, or show you feel foolish.
