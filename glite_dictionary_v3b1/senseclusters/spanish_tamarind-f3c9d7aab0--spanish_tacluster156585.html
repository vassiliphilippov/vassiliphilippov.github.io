Etymology

Spanish tamarind may come from a simple picture: early traders thought the tree's small, apple-shaped fruit looked like a tamarind and added Spanish to mark it as a foreign or exotic fruit. So the name now refers to a shrubby tree with edible, tamarind-like fruit.
