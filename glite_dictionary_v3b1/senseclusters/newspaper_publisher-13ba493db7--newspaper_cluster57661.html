Etymology

newspaper publisher comes from early print shops where people used a printing press to make many copies of the news. A newspaper was the printed pages and a publisher was the person or company who paid to run the press and send the papers out, so the phrase now means 'a person or company that owns and is responsible for producing and distributing a newspaper'.
