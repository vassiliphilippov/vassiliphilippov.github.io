Etymology

The word slapsticky comes from slapstick, the wooden stick comedians used to make a loud 'slap' and get a laugh, plus the ending -y meaning 'like' or 'full of'. So slapsticky means 'full of silly, clumsy physical jokes', just like slapstick comedy.
