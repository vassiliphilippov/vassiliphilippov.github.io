Etymology

kidney begonia gets its name from the plant's rounded leaves that look 'kidney-shaped' and often have a reddish underside; the bold kidney image helps you picture the leaf. The word begonia shows it is a type of houseplant, so the name points to the plant's leaf shape to help you remember it.
