Etymology

The word starer comes from the verb stare, which means 'to look with a long, fixed look', and the ending -er makes it 'one who does something', like runner. A helpful way to remember it is to picture someone holding a steady, unfriendly look. That's why a starer is a person whose gaze can make others feel uneasy.
