Etymology

Cortone Acetate may come from the name Cortone, linked to cortisone, a hormone that reduces swelling, and Acetate, the chemical part added to the medicine. It was the brand name of a steroid medicine used 'to reduce swelling', so the name points to both the drug's chemical form and its purpose.
