Etymology

juvenile rheumatoid arthritis is a medical name that links juvenile, meaning 'a young person', with rheumatoid arthritis, meaning a kind of joint swelling and pain. Picture a child with stiff, swollen knees or hands; that image explains why the phrase means 'arthritis that affects children'.
