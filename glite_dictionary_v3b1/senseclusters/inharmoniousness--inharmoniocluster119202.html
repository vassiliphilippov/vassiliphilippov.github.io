Etymology

inharmoniousness comes from the prefix in- meaning 'not' and the word harmony meaning 'agreement' or 'fitting together', often of sounds or parts. So inharmoniousness is the state of not fitting or sounding well together, which is why we use it for bad music, clashing colors, or troubled relationships.
