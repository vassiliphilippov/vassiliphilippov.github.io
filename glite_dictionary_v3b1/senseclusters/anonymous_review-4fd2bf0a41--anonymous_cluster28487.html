Etymology

anonymous review comes from two simple words: anonymous meaning 'name not shown' and review meaning a public 'opinion' about something. People began to publish reviews without their names so they could be honest or avoid trouble, so today an anonymous review means a review where the author's identity is 'not revealed'.
