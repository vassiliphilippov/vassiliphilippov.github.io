Etymology

ketosis-prone diabetes gets its name from the parts ketosis, when the body makes chemicals called 'ketones', and prone, which means 'likely to happen'. Because people with this kind of diabetes often make many ketones, they are 'likely' to have dangerous 'ketone build-up', so they usually need both diet and insulin to control it.
