Etymology

The word LET is a short name for Lashkar-e-Taiba, from Urdu/Arabic where Lashkar means 'army' and Taiba means 'pure'. So LET is made from the group's first letters and the name literally says 'army of the pure', which helps explain why it is an armed group.
