Etymology

The word whipstitch combines whip and stitch. The whip part suggests 'to move quickly' or 'to wrap around' because the thread is whipped over the fabric edge, and stitch means 'a single sewing loop', so a whipstitch is a fast loop that wraps along the edge to join or finish fabric.
