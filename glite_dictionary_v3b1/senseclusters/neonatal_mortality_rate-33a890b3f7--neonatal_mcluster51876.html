Etymology

neonatal mortality rate comes from its parts: neonatal meaning 'newborn', mortality meaning 'death', and rate meaning a 'number' or 'measure'. So it literally counts how many newborn babies die in the 'first 28 days' of life for every '1,000' live births, and that number helps show how good care for mothers and babies is.
