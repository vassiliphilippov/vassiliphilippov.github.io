Etymology

The word nervously comes from the old word nerve, which named the body's thin cords that carry feeling and movement, and the ending -ly simply makes it an adverb. When those nerves are tight or jumpy you feel 'worried' or shaky, so to act nervously is to do something while your nerves are tense.
