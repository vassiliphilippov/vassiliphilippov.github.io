Etymology

fearnaught is the name for a heavy, coarse wool cloth or a coat made from it, used to protect people from harsh weather. A helpful way to remember the word is to think of the parts fear + naught as 'fear nothing', like clothing that keeps out wind and rain, so fearnaught means a strong, protective coat.
