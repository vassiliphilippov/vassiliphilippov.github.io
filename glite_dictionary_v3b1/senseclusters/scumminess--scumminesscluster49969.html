Etymology

scumminess comes from scum, a word for the 'dirty layer' that forms on water or other surfaces, plus the ending -ness, which makes a word mean the 'state' or 'quality' of something. So scumminess is the 'state' of being like scum — either physically covered in dirt or morally low and unpleasant.
