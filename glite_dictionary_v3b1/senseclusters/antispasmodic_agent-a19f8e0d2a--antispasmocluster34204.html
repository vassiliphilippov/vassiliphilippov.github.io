Etymology

The term antispasmodic agent puts together anti- meaning 'against', spasmodic meaning a sudden 'spasm' or muscle twitch, and agent meaning a drug. Imagine a medicine acting like a calm hand on a twitching muscle, so the phrase means 'a drug that stops muscle spasms'.
