Etymology

fane comes from the Latin fanum, which means 'temple' or 'sacred place'. A helpful way to remember it is to think of the same root in fanatic, once meaning someone full of religious feeling, and in profane, which meant 'outside the temple', so a fane is simply a temple or holy building.
