Etymology

Furfural comes from the Latin furfur, which means 'bran'. Chemists first made this liquid from 'bran' and other plant bits, so the name reminds us it comes from plant material. The ending -al marks it as a chemical, which is why the name fits a plant-based liquid used as a solvent.
