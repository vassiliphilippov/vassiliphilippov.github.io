Etymology

moodiness comes from mood, an old word meaning 'mind' or 'spirit', plus the ending -ness meaning 'a state'. A helpful image is to think of someone's inner weather changing often, so moodiness is the state of often changing feelings that can make a person sulky, gloomy, or irritable.
