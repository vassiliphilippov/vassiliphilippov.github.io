Etymology

Noctiluca miliaris is a scientific name that may come from Latin: Noctiluca means 'night light' because these tiny sea creatures glow at night, and miliaris means 'millet-like' for their small, round shape. That's why the name fits the glowing organisms that cause the 'sea sparkle' you see in dark water.
