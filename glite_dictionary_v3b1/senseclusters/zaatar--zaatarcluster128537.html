Etymology

zaatar comes from the Arabic za'atar, which means 'wild thyme' or a similar 'herb'. That herb's name became the name for the common Middle Eastern mix of dried herbs, sesame and sumac that people sprinkle on bread, so zaatar today can mean both the plant and the seasoning blend.
