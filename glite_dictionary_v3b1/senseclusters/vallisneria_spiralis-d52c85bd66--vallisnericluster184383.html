Etymology

The plant Vallisneria spiralis has long, thin ribbonlike leaves that wave under the water, so people often call it 'eelgrass' because the leaves look like grass under the surface. Because it grows fast and helps make oxygen, it is a common choice for 'aquariums' and ponds to keep fish and water healthy.
