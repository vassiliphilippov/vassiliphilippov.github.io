Etymology

The name pocket rat may come from the animal's fur-lined cheek pouches that work like a small pocket for carrying seeds and bits for its nest. So, the name links the idea of a 'pocket' to a small rat that 'stores' food in its cheeks, which explains the word's meaning today.
