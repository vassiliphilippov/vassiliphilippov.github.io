Etymology

genus Porphyrula comes from the old Greek word porphyra meaning 'purple' and the small ending -ula, so it literally means 'little purple'. Scientists gave this name because the American purple gallinule and its relatives have bright purple and green feathers, so today the name refers to that group of colorful birds.
