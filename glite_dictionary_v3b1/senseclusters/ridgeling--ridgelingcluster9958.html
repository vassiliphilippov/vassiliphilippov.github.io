Etymology

ridgeling likely comes from ridge and the ending -ling, where ridge is a 'raised line' and -ling means 'small'; imagine a small 'ridge' on the horse where a testicle did not drop, and you will remember that a ridgeling is a male horse with one or both testicles not dropped.
