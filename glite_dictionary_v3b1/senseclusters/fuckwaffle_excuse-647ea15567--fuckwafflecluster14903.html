Etymology

fuckwaffle excuse comes from putting the rude word fuck and the verb waffle together, where waffle means 'to talk a lot without saying anything useful'. So the picture is someone angrily talking nonsense to hide the truth, and it now means 'a silly or fake excuse said in an annoying way'.
