Etymology

spiffily comes from the simple word spiffy, which comes from spiff meaning 'to make neat' and 'smart'. A helpful way to remember it is to think of someone who 'spiffs up' their clothes to look sharp, so spiffily means 'in a smart, fashionable way'.
