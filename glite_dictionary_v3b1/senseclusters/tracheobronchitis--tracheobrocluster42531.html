Etymology

tracheobronchitis is built from three parts: trachea ('windpipe'), bronchi ('large air tubes'), and -itis ('inflammation'). So, tracheobronchitis means inflammation or infection of the windpipe and the bronchi, which fits its modern meaning as a respiratory infection.
