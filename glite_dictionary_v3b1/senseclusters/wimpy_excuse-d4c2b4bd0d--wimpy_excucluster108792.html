Etymology

wimpy excuse may come from the word wimpy, which describes a weak, timid person, and the word excuse means a reason. Imagine that shy, weak person giving a soft, not believable reason, and you can see why a wimpy excuse means an excuse that is 'weak' or 'not believable'.
