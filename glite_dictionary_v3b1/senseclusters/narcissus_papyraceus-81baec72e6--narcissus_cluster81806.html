Etymology

Narcissus papyraceus begins with Narcissus, from a Greek myth about a man who fell in love with his own reflection and whose name became linked to daffodil flowers. The second word, papyraceus, comes from Latin and means 'paper-like' because the white, star-shaped petals are thin like paper, so the full name points to a white daffodil with delicate, papery blooms often grown indoors.
