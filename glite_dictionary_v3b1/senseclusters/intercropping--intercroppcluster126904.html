Etymology

The word intercropping combines inter- meaning 'between' or 'among' and crop meaning 'plants', so it gives a clear picture of different plants grown 'among' each other. That's why intercropping means growing two or more crops together in the same field to help the soil, cut pest problems, and grow more food.
