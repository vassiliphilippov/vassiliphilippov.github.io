Etymology

Snatch block comes from a pulley that can be opened so you can 'snatch' the rope into it without feeding the rope end through. The words snatch, meaning 'to grab quickly', and block, meaning the pulley, explain why the term means a pulley that opens to take a rope and lets you change the rope's direction easily.
