Etymology

Publius Vergilius Maro is often called Vergil and wrote the long poem 'Aeneid' about Aeneas's journey from Troy to the land that became Rome, and he also wrote poems about farming ('Georgics') and country life ('Eclogues'). Because his stories helped people imagine Rome's past and he was so famous, his name is now used to mean 'one of the greatest Roman poets'.
