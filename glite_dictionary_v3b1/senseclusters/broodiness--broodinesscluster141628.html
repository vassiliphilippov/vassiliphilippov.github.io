Etymology

broodiness comes from the word brood, meaning 'a group of young', and from the verb 'to brood' which means 'to sit on eggs'. A hen that is broody will stay on the nest to warm her eggs, so broodiness describes the wish to sit on eggs or a strong desire to have children.
