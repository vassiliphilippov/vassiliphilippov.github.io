Etymology

The phrase grace and poise brings together two simple pictures: grace, like smooth, beautiful movement in a dance, and poise, like quiet balance, as when someone stands calmly on a narrow beam. So the expression means 'elegant and composed', moving or acting in a calm, balanced way.
