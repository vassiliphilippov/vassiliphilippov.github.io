Etymology

Myasthenia gravis comes from older words: myasthenia, made of parts that mean 'muscle' and 'weakness', and gravis, which means 'serious'. So the name paints the picture of 'very weak muscles', and that is why it is used for a disease that causes strong muscle weakness.
