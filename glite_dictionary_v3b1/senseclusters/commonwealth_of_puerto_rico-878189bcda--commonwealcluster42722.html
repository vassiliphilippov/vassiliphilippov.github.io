Etymology

Commonwealth of Puerto Rico comes from the old idea of a 'common wealth' — a community that shares power and goods. Using the word Commonwealth showed Puerto Rico had its own local government while still tied to the United States, so the name reminds us it is 'self-governing' but 'associated' with the U.S.
