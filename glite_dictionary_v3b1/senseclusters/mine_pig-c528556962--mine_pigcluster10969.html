Etymology

The compound noun mine pig may come from the old image of hot liquid iron poured into a row of small molds that looked like little pigs, which gave the metal the name pig. The word mine shows the iron came straight 'from ore' at the mine and not from scrap metal, so mine pig means 'pig iron' made directly from ore, pure and without scrap.
