Etymology

lady's thistle may come from a simple picture: people long ago called the Virgin Mary 'Our Lady', and the plant's pale, white-marked leaves looked like a light cloak on a lady, so the thistle was called 'lady's thistle'. So today lady's thistle is the common name for this tall thistle with white-blotched leaves.
