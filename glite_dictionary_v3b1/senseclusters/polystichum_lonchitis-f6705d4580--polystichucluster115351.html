Etymology

The plant name Polystichum lonchitis comes from old words that make a clear picture: poly ('many'), stichum ('rows') and lonchitis ('spear'). So the name helps you imagine a tough, evergreen 'fern' with many rows of small spear-shaped leaves, which explains the name today.
