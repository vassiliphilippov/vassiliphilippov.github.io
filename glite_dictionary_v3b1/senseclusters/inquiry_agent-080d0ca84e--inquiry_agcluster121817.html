Etymology

inquiry agent comes from the idea of a person who makes an inquiry, meaning they ask questions to find out facts, and an agent who works for someone else. People hired these agents to go out, talk to others and follow clues, so today inquiry agent means 'a private detective' who 'investigates' for clients.
