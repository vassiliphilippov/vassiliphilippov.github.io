Etymology

esse est percipi is a Latin phrase used by the philosopher George Berkeley that links esse ('to be') and percipi ('to be perceived'). Imagine a chair that only seems to be there while someone looks at it, and you get why the phrase names the idea that something exists only when it is perceived.
