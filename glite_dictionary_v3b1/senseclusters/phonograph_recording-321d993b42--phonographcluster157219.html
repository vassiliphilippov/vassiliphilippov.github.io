Etymology

phonograph recording comes from the idea of 'writing' sound: phono means 'sound' and graph means 'to write', and early machines actually scratched a groove in a disk to 'write' music with a needle. That's why phonograph recording today means a physical disk you play to hear recorded 'sound'.
