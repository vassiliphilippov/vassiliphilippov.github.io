Etymology

isoclinic line names a map line that links places where the Earth's magnetic field makes a compass needle tilt the same amount. So, it means 'a line on a map connecting points with the same magnetic inclination', where 'magnetic inclination' is how much a compass needle points up or down, and scientists use it to compare compass tilt across different regions.
