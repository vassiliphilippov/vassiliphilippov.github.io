Etymology

fluctuant comes from the Latin root fluctu- meaning 'flow' or 'wave', the same idea you find in the common word fluid. So something fluctuant is like a soft thing that can 'flow' or move and change shape when pressed, which is why doctors use it for a fluid-filled swelling and why we also say markets or feelings can be fluctuant when they change up and down over time.
