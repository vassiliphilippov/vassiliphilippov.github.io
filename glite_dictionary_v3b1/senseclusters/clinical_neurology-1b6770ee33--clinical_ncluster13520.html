Etymology

clinical neurology comes from two parts: clinical comes from a word for 'bed' because doctors cared for patients at their beds, and neurology comes from words for 'nerve' and 'study'. So, clinical neurology means the part of medicine that looks after and treats people with problems of the nerves and brain.
