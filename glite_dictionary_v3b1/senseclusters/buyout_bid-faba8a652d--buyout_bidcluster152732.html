Etymology

buyout bid comes from a simple business image: a bid is an offer and to buy out someone is to buy all their shares so they leave the company. So a buyout bid is an offer to buy all of a person's or group's shares, which is why it means 'an offer to purchase all of an individual's or entity's shares or holdings in a company'.
