Etymology

Amaranthus cruentus gets its name from old words: Amaranthus comes from Greek meaning 'unfading' or 'never dying', and cruentus is Latin for 'blood-red'. That's why the name fits this tall plant with bright red flower spikes that seem to keep their colour and that also produce seeds people eat.
