Etymology

The noun lost treasure comes from images of pirates and explorers hiding a treasure and then the treasure became lost, with only a map or clue left behind. That picture of a hidden chest or sunken gold helps explain why the phrase means 'valuable items that are lost, hidden, or waiting to be found'.
