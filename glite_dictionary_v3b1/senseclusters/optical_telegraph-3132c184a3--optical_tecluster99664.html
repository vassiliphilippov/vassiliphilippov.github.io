Etymology

The optical telegraph was an old system that sent messages by sight: lines of towers with big moving arms or flags that people watched from tower to tower. The name comes from optical (meaning 'seen with the eye') and telegraph (meaning 'message at a distance'), so that's why it means a system for sending messages using visual signals.
