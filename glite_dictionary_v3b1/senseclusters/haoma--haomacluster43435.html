Etymology

haoma comes from ancient Iran and originally named both a vine and the 'sacred drink' made from its milky juice. It is like the Indian word soma, so picturing a green vine turned into a strong, holy drink helps you remember why haoma means a plant used to make an intoxicating drink in old religious ceremonies.
