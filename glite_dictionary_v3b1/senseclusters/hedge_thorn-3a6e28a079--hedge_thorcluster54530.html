Etymology

The noun hedge thorn comes from a simple picture: farmers planted this thorny shrub in a row to make a hedge, and its sharp thorns formed a fence that kept animals out. So the name shows the plant is used 'for hedging' as a natural 'barrier', and its plumlike fruit also attracts birds.
