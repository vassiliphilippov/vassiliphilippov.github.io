Hygrophoraceae breaks into parts: the prefix hygro- means 'moist', the root phor- means 'to carry' or 'bear', and the ending -aceae marks a family. So, Hygrophoraceae is like a 'moist-bearing' family of fungi, which fits mushrooms that often have clean, waxy gills that look moist.
