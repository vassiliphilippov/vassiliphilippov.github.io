Etymology

The word cirriped comes from Latin parts cirri meaning 'curl' or 'feathery strand' and -ped meaning 'foot', the same ped you see in pedal and pedestrian. These sea animals have feathery 'feet' called cirri that they use to catch food while stuck to rocks or ship hulls, so the name explains how they look and live.
