Etymology

Boris Pasternak was a Russian writer best known for the novel Doctor Zhivago; the book was banned in his country but secretly published abroad, and when he won the Nobel Prize in 1958 he was forced to decline it, so his name today often stands for 'great literature' and 'artistic freedom'.
