Etymology

HSV-2 stands for herpes simplex virus type 2; herpes comes from a Greek word meaning 'to creep' because the infection can come back and move along the skin, simplex means 'single', and virus originally meant 'poison'. So HSV-2 is the 'second type' of this virus and that name helps remember that it commonly causes genital herpes.
