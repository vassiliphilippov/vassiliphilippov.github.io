Etymology

The word gravidation comes from the Latin word gravidus, which meant 'heavy' and was used for 'pregnant'. The ending -ation turns that idea into a state or condition, and you can see the same root in Spanish 'grávida'. So, gravidation means the condition of being pregnant, or simply 'pregnancy'.
