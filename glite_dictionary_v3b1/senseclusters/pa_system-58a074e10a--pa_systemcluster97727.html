Etymology

P.A. system comes from P.A. for 'public address' and the word system. Imagine a person speaking into a loudspeaker so their voice is made louder and reaches the whole room; the equipment that does this became known as the P.A. system, so today it means the electronic gear that makes sound louder for public places.
