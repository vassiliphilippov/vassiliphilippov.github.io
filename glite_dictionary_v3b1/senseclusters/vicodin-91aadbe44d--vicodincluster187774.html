Etymology

Vicodin is a brand name for a prescription painkiller made from hydrocodone and acetaminophen, and the name was created by the maker rather than coming from an old language. A helpful way to remember Vicodin is to think of 'vic' like 'victory' over 'pain', so the name points to a drug that gives 'relief' from moderate to severe 'pain'.
