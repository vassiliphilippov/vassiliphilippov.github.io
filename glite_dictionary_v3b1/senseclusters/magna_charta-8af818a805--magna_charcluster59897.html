Etymology

Magna Charta comes from old Latin words: Magna means 'great' and Charta means 'charter' or a written promise. In 1215, rich landowners forced King John to accept this 'great charter' to limit the king's power and protect some 'rights', so today Magna Charta stands for limits on rulers and basic rights.
