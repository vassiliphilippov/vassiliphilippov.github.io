Etymology

Mazzini is an Italian family name that probably comes from the Italian word mazza, meaning 'club' or 'heavy stick'. A helpful way to remember Giuseppe Mazzini is to think of that 'club' image — he was a strong, forceful activist who pushed for Italian unity.
