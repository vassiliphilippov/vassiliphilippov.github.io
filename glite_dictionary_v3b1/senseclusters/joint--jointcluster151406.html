Etymology

joint: A helpful way to remember the slang joint for a rolled marijuana cigarette is to think of it as a small 'thing' people pass around or as paper 'joined' around the weed; the exact origin is not certain. In everyday use, a joint now means a rolled marijuana cigarette.
