Etymology

Flying Spaghetti Monster comes from a joke story in which a god is imagined as a flying pile of spaghetti and meatballs, a funny monster made of food. That's why people use the Flying Spaghetti Monster today as a 'parody' god to 'mock' weak arguments for God and as a 'symbol' of 'non-religious' or scientific views.
