Etymology

xxiv is a Roman numeral written with letters: X means 'ten', V means 'five', and I means 'one', and when a smaller letter comes before a larger one (as in IV) it means 'take away'. So xxiv is 'ten' + 'ten' + ('five' 'take away' 'one'), which equals twenty-four, and that's why xxiv represents the number twenty-four.
