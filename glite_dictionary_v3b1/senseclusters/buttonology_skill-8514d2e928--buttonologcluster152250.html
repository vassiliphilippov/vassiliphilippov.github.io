Etymology

Buttonology skill comes from putting the word button together with the made-up ending -ology, which people use to mean 'study of', to suggest deep knowledge of buttons. Picture a tailor with a big box of buttons who can pick and sew any button easily, and that image explains why the phrase means 'expertise in handling buttons'.
