Etymology

The word autoradiographic comes from three parts: auto- 'self' (like in autograph), radio- 'radiation' (like 'radio'), and -graphic 'writing' or 'picture' (like in photograph). So an autoradiographic image is like a 'picture made by radiation', which is why this method shows where radioactive markers are in a sample.
