Etymology

crocidolite comes from Greek parts krokid- meaning 'tuft' and lithos meaning 'stone', so it once meant 'tufted stone' because the mineral grows in thin, hair-like fibers. That's why the name refers to the blue asbestos mineral that is now known to be very dangerous to health.
