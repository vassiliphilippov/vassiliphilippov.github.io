Etymology

English-Gothic architecture comes from the way builders in England made the old Gothic style their own in the 1300s–1400s. Picture very tall churches with strong vertical lines and stone ceilings that open like a hand fan ('fan vaults'); that's why the name now means buildings with tall lines, fan-shaped ceilings, and low 'Tudor' arches.
