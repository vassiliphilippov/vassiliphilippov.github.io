Etymology

higgledy-piggledy comes from two playful rhymes, higgledy and piggledy, like simple pairs such as 'easy-peasy', which people used long ago in English to make a funny sound. It gives the image of things thrown together, so higgledy-piggledy means 'in a disorderly or chaotic way'.
