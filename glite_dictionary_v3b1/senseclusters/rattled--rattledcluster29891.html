Etymology

rattled comes from the sound word rattle, which copies the quick shaking noise of a loose object or a baby's toy. That picture of something being 'shaken' led to the meaning of feeling 'nervous', 'confused', or 'upset', so to be rattled is to feel shaken by a sudden event.
