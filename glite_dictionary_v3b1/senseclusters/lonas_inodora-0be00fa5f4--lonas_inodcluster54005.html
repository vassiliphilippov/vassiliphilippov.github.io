Etymology

Lonas inodora probably comes from Latin words that describe the plant: the part inodora means 'without smell', so people used the name for a small shrub with bright yellow, daisy-like flowers that give little scent. That's why the name helps you remember a shrub that looks like a yellow daisy but is not very fragrant.
