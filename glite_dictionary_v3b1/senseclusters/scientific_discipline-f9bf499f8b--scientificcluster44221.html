Etymology

scientific discipline joins scientific, meaning about studying nature, and discipline, a word that once meant training and now often means a 'field' of study with its own rules. Think of a school where each room is for one subject; that room is a 'discipline' of science, so the phrase means a particular branch of science like physics or biology.
