Etymology

friar preacher comes from medieval times. Members of a group called the Dominicans travelled to towns to 'preach' and teach, so a friar was a religious brother who acted as a preacher, often wearing a black cloak. That's why today friar preacher means a Dominican friar known for giving sermons.
