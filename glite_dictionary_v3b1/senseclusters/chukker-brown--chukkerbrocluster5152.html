Etymology

The word chukker-brown probably comes from the polo term chukker, meaning 'a short period' of play, and from the brown leather and horse coats you see in the game. So chukker-brown means a warm, 'snuff' or greyish-to-yellowish brown, like old leather or a faded photograph.
