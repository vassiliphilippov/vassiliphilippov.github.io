pikey behavior may come from the old word pikey, used for people who camped by a pike, a kind of main road. Seeing them there made others call them 'pikeys' and later use pikey behavior for actions they called 'bad' or 'criminal', so today the phrase is a harmful 'insult' and is 'offensive'.
