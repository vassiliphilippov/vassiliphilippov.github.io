Etymology

mock turtle was made in England to copy rich 'turtle soup' using cheaper parts of a calf so people could have a similar meal without turtle meat. The word mock means 'fake' or 'imitation', so mock turtle came to mean a dish that looks and tastes like turtle soup but has no real turtle.
