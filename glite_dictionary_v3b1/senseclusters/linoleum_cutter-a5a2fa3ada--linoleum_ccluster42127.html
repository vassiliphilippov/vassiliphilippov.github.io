Etymology

linoleum cutter comes from the simple picture of a small, sharp tool used to cut linoleum, where cutter means 'tool that cuts'. Imagine a tiny knife with a short, curved blade slicing a design from a linoleum sheet, and that is why the name means a tool for cutting linoleum.
