Etymology

Prohibition era comes from the word prohibition meaning 'to ban' and era meaning 'a time'. In the United States it refers to 1920 to 1933, when a law made alcohol illegal and people drank in secret bars called speakeasies, so the phrase means 'the time when alcohol was banned'.
