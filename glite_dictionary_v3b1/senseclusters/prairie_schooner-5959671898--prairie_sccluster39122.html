Etymology

prairie schooner got its name from a simple picture: the white canvas top of a covered wagon looked like the 'sails' of a schooner, and the wide grass of the prairie looked like a sea so the wagons seemed to 'sail' across it. That's why the name means a large covered wagon used by people moving west.
