Etymology

drabness comes from an old word drap that meant 'cloth', a plain brown cloth people used long ago. Because that cloth looked plain and colorless, drab came to mean 'dull', so drabness is the quality of being 'dull' or without style. You can see the same root in drape, which also comes from the idea of 'cloth'.
