Etymology

gamma-terpinene breaks into two simple parts: the gamma part is the Greek letter 'gamma', often used to name different forms, and the terpinene part comes from turpentine, the 'tree resin' or oily substance from plants, so the name tells you it is a plant oil compound found in citrus and used for scents.
