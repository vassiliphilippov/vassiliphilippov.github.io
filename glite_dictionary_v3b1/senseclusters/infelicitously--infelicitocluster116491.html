Etymology

infelicitously breaks into in- ('not') and felicitous, which comes from a root meaning 'happy' or 'fortunate' and is the same root as felicity. So to do something infelicitously is to do it 'not happily' or 'not suitably', which explains why it means in an awkward, inappropriate, or unfortunate way.
