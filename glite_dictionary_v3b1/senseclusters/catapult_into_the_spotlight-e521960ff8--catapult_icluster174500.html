Etymology

The phrase catapult into the spotlight comes from the image of a catapult, an old machine that suddenly throws something, and a spotlight, the bright stage light that makes a person clearly seen. Imagine being thrown into that bright light: you are suddenly noticed by everyone, so the phrase means 'to suddenly become the center of attention'.
