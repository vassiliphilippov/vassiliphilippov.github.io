Etymology

The noun low beam headlamp comes from the simple idea of a headlamp that sends a beam of light set 'low' so it lights the road without blinding other drivers. That's why a low beam headlamp is the setting used when other vehicles are nearby: it gives light forward but does not 'blind' people on the road.
