Etymology

Lagopus scoticus comes from old Greek and Latin words: lagopus joins words for 'hare' and 'foot' because the bird has furry, feathered feet like a hare, and scoticus means 'of Scotland' because it lives on Scottish moors. So, the name describes a hare-footed bird from Scotland.
