Etymology

herpes zoster comes from two old Greek words: herpes meaning 'to creep' and zoster meaning 'belt'. The name paints a simple picture of a rash that seems to 'creep' along a nerve in a belt-like band, so it refers to the disease we now call 'shingles'.
