Etymology

hemipterous insect comes from Greek roots hemi ('half') and pterous ('wing') because their front wings are hard at the base and softer at the tip, so they look like 'half' wings. That's why hemipterous insect is used for bugs like aphids and shield bugs that have a sucking mouth and partly hardened front wings.
