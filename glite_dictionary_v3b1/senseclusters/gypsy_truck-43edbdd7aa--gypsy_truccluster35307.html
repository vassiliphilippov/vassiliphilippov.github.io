Etymology

gypsy truck may come from old traveling people who moved in bright, decorated wagons. Here gypsy means a traveler and truck means the vehicle, so the picture is of a colorful moving shop that 'travels' to 'sell' goods, and that's why the phrase means a vehicle used to sell goods.
