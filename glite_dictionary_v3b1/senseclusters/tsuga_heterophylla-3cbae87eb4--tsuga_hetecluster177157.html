Etymology

Tsuga heterophylla may come from the Japanese word 'tsuga' for the hemlock tree and the parts hetero ('different') + phylla ('leaves'), so the name gives a simple image of a hemlock with 'different leaves' and helps explain why we call it the western hemlock.
