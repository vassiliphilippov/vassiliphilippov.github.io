Etymology

Claudio Monteverdi was an Italian composer around 1600 who acted like a musical bridge between the older Renaissance music and the newer Baroque style. Because he mixed the old many-voice music with more dramatic, emotional sounds and helped create the first operas, people call him the 'father of opera' and a 'bridge' between the two periods.
