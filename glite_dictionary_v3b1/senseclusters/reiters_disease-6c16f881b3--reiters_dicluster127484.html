Etymology

Reiter's disease is named after a doctor, Hans Reiter, who first described the pattern of joint, eye, and urinary problems. Doctors now more often call it 'reactive arthritis' because the body's joints and eyes become inflamed as a reaction that happens after an infection, so the newer name explains the cause.
