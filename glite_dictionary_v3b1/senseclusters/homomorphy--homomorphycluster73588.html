Etymology

The word homomorphy comes from Greek parts homo- 'same' and -morph 'shape', so it literally means 'same shape'. Think of the homo- in homogeneous 'all the same' to help you remember that homomorphy describes different animals or objects that have a similar shape even when they are not related.
