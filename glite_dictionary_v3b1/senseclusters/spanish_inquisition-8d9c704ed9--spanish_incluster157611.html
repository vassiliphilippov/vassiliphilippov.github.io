Etymology

Spanish Inquisition comes from the word inquisition, meaning an official 'questioning'. It was started by Spanish rulers in the late 1400s to find people who did not follow the Catholic faith, and because it used harsh trials and punishments the name now suggests a severe or unfair 'investigation'.
