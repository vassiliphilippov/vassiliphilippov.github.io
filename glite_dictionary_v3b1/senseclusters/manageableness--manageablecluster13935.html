Etymology

manageableness starts with manage, which originally meant 'to handle' and comes from an old word for 'hand', seen in the English word manual, and it adds -able meaning 'can' and -ness meaning 'state'. So, manageableness is the 'state' of being able to be handled, which is why it means how easy something is to manage or control.
