Etymology

The word alkalescent comes from alkali, the same root behind alkaline, which came into English from Arabic 'al-qaliy' meaning 'ashes', and the ending -escent, which means 'becoming'. So alkalescent means 'becoming alkaline' or 'slightly alkaline', which is why we use it to describe things that are only a little basic.
