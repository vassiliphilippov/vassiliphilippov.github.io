Etymology

tolazoline joins tol- (from the toluene family of chemicals) and -azoline (a common ending for a small nitrogen ring in many drugs). A helpful way to remember it is to think of a 'tol' chemical with a nitrogen ring that 'opens' blood vessels, which explains why tolazoline is used to improve blood flow.
