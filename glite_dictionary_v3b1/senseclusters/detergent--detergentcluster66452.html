Etymology

detergent comes from Latin parts de- meaning 'off' and tergere meaning 'to wipe away', so a helpful image is something that 'wipes off' dirt. So a detergent is a cleaning substance that removes dirt and grease, which is why we use it for washing clothes and cleaning surfaces.
