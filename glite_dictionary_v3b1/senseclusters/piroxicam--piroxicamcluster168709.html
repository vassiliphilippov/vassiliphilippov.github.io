Etymology

The word piroxicam is a medicine name whose ending -oxicam appears in other drugs like meloxicam, so a helpful way to remember it is as part of the 'oxicam' family of anti-inflammatory medicines. That's why piroxicam is used to treat arthritis and to reduce 'inflammation' and 'pain'.
