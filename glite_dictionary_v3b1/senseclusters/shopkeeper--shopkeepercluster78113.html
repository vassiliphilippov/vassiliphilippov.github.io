Etymology

shopkeeper is made of two simple parts: shop and keeper. A shop is a place that sells things and keeper comes from 'keep', meaning 'to hold' or 'look after' (think of a goalkeeper), so a shopkeeper is someone who 'looks after' and runs a shop.
