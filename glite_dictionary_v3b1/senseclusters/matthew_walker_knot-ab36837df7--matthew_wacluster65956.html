Etymology

Matthew Walker knot may come from a sailor or rope worker named Matthew Walker who taught this useful end knot. The knot is made by weaving the strands of a rope into a neat knot so the end becomes a firm 'stopper', and so today the name means a knot used to stop a line from slipping through an opening.
