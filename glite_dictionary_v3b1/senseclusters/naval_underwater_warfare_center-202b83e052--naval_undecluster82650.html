Etymology

Naval Underwater Warfare Center is a literal name: Naval means 'navy', Underwater means 'under the sea', Warfare means 'fighting', and Center means a place for study and testing. So, the name shows it is a navy organization that studies and tests tools and systems for 'fighting under the sea'.
