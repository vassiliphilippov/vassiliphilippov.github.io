Etymology

rapid spread gives a simple image: something moving out very fast, like a small fire jumping across dry grass or a spill running across a table. The word rapid means very fast and spread means to move out in all directions, so rapid spread describes a 'sudden or very quick increase' of something, often something bad like a disease or invasive species.
