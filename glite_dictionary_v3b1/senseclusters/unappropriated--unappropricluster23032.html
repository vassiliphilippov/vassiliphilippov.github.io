Etymology

The word unappropriated starts with un- meaning 'not' and the rest is appropriate, which comes from an old Latin root meaning 'one's own' and is the same root as property and proper. So, unappropriated describes something that has not been made someone's own or set aside, which is why it means 'not assigned or claimed'.
