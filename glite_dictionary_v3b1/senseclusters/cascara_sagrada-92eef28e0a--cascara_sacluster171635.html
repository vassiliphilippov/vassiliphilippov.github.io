Etymology

cascara sagrada comes from Spanish words cáscara 'bark' and sagrada 'sacred'. One popular story says local healers used the tree's dried bark as medicine, so people called it the 'sacred bark', and today cascara sagrada means that dried bark used as a 'natural laxative'.
