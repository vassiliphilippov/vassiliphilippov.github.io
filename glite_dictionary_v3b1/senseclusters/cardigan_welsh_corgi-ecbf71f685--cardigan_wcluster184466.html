Etymology

Cardigan Welsh corgi is named for Cardigan, a place in Wales, and for the Welsh word corgi, made of cor + gi meaning 'dwarf' and 'dog'. These small, low dogs were bred in Cardigan to herd cattle, so the name helps us remember they are a short, sturdy herding breed today.
