Etymology

The word merica is an informal, joking form of America that comes from dropping the first syllable and writing it like 'Merica. People use it to mean the 'United States' and to make a joke about loud patriotism or classic American images like flags, pickup trucks, and barbecues, so it brings to mind a playful, proud idea of the country.
