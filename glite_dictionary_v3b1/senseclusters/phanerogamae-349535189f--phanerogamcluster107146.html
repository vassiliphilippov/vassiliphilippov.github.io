Etymology

The word Phanerogamae comes from Greek parts phanero- meaning 'visible' and -gamae (from gamos) meaning 'marriage' or 'union'. In old plant systems it named plants whose parts that make seeds, like flowers, are easy to see, so it meant the group of seed-bearing or flowering plants.
