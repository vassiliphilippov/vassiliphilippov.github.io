Etymology

biotinylate comes from biotin, a small vitamin whose first part bio- means 'life', and the chemical ending -ylate, which means 'to add' or 'attach' a group. So, to biotinylate is to 'attach' a biotin tag to a molecule to make it easier to find or see.
