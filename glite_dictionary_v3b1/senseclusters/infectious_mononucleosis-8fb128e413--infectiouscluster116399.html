Etymology

infectious mononucleosis is named for what doctors see in the blood: mononucleosis means there are many white blood cells each with one large nucleus, and infectious means the illness can be 'passed between people'. So the name helps you remember that this spreading illness shows those unusual white blood cells and causes fever and swollen glands.
