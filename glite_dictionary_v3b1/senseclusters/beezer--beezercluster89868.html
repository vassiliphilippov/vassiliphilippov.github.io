Etymology

The word beezer is British slang for a large nose and, in some places, for something 'excellent'. A helpful way to remember it is to think of beezer like beak ('bird's nose'): a big 'beak' gives the image of a large nose, and that strong image helps explain why the word can also mean something that stands out.
