Etymology

American bittersweet gets its name from the yellow seed pods that split open to show bright red seeds on a climbing vine. One popular story says the seeds look 'sweet' but taste 'bitter', so the word bittersweet, a mix of 'bitter' and 'sweet', fits the plant, and 'American' simply shows it is the 'North American' kind.
