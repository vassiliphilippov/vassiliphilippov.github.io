Etymology

schmaltzy comes from schmaltz, a Yiddish and German word for melted chicken fat, literally 'fat'. Think of something greasy or too sweet — when a film, song, or speech piles on fake feeling it feels sticky, so we call it schmaltzy, meaning overly sentimental or not sincere.
