Etymology

Carolina jasmine comes from where the plant grows and how it smells. People called it Carolina for the southern US area and jasmine because the yellow flowers have a sweet smell like true jasmine, so the name shows it is a sweet-smelling yellow vine from the Carolinas that is 'poisonous'.
