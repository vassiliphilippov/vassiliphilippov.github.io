Etymology

St. Jerome was a Christian scholar long ago who made the first full Latin version of the Bible, called the 'Vulgate'. One popular story says he pulled a thorn from a lion's paw and the animal became his friend, and because he is often shown writing with a book and a lion, that strong picture helps you remember he was a hard-working Bible 'translator' and teacher.
