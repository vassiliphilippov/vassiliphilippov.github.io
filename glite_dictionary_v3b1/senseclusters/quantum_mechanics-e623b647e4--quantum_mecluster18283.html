Etymology

quantum mechanics comes from the idea that energy and matter are made of very small 'packets' called quantum, like steps instead of a smooth ramp, and mechanics means the study of how things move and act. So quantum mechanics is the study of how atoms and even smaller parts behave when their energy comes in these tiny packets.
