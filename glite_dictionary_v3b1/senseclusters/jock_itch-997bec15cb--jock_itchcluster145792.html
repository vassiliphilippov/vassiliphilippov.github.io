Etymology

jock itch comes from athletes and their clothing: jock was a word for an athlete or the 'jockstrap' they wore, and sweat and tight gear often caused an uncomfortable itch in the groin. So it now means a skin infection that makes the groin very itchy, especially in men and athletes.
