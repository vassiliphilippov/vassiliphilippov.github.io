Etymology

cultigen comes from the parts cult- meaning 'to care for' or 'till' (as in cultivate) and -gen meaning 'born' or 'produced' (as in generate). So when a plant is called cultigen it means it was 'grown by human cultivation' rather than occurring naturally in the wild.
