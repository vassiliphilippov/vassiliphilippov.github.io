Etymology

The idiom care a jackstraw may come from an old game where people used tiny straw sticks called jackstraws. One popular story says a jackstraw was so small and unimportant that to 'care a jackstraw' meant you did not care at all, so today the phrase means 'to care very little or not at all'.
