Etymology

insomnia comes from the Latin parts in- 'not' and somnus 'sleep', so it means 'no sleep'. You can also see the same root in the word somnolent 'sleepy'. That's why insomnia describes when a person regularly finds it hard to fall asleep or stay asleep.
