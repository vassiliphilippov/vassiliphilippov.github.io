Etymology

dental implant joins dental, meaning 'about teeth', and implant, meaning 'to place something into the body'. Imagine a small metal 'root' planted into the jaw like a seed, with a new tooth fixed on top, and you have the image — that's why it means 'an artificial tooth put into the jaw'.
