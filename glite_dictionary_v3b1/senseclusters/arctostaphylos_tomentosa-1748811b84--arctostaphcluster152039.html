Etymology

The plant name Arctostaphylos tomentosa comes from Greek and Latin words: Arctostaphylos mixes 'bear' and 'bunch of grapes' and tomentosa means 'woolly'. Imagine a low shrub with soft, woolly twigs and berry-like fruits a bear might eat, and you can see why the name describes this woolly California shrub.
