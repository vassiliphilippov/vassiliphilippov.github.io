Etymology

reinforcement comes from the verb reinforce (from re- 'again' and force 'strength'), so in behavior it means to make an action stronger by giving a reward or other encouragement.

Related Terms

  • degree lower: negative reinforcement - removal of an unpleasant stimulus to increase behavior
