Etymology

Touchingly comes from the simple word touch ('to feel with the hand' or 'to move someone's feelings') plus the endings -ing and -ly, which change it into a word that describes how something is done. So touchingly means 'in a way that touches the heart', like a story or speech that moves your feelings as if it gently touched you.
