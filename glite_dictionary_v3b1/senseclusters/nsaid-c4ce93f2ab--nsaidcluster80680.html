Etymology

NSAID stands for 'non-steroidal anti-inflammatory drug', where non- means 'not', anti- means 'against', and 'inflammatory' is about 'swelling' or 'pain'. So an NSAID is a medicine that fights 'swelling', 'pain', and 'fever' but is not a steroid.
