Etymology

The compound hotel clerk combines hotel, a place for travellers, and clerk, a person who writes and keeps records. Imagine someone sitting at a front desk, writing guest names and taking money, and you see why a hotel clerk today greets guests and handles 'check-ins' and reservations.
