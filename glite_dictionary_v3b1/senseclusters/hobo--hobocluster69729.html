Etymology

hobo may come from an old phrase hoe-boy, which is like 'hoe boy' and means 'farm worker' who moved from farm to farm with a hoe looking for work. A helpful way to remember it is to picture that travelling farm worker, which is why hobo means a person who travels looking for temporary work.
