Etymology

Comic opera is a name made from comic (meaning 'funny') and opera (a musical play). Long ago, writers made lighter operas with spoken lines and happy endings, so people called them comic operas to show they were 'funny' rather than 'serious', which is why today the term means an opera with a light, humorous tone, spoken dialogue, and a happy ending.
