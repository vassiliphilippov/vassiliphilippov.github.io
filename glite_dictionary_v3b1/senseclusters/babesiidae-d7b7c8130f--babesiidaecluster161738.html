Etymology

Babesiidae comes from the genus name Babesia, which was named after the Romanian doctor Victor Babeș, and the ending -idae means 'family' in biology. So Babesiidae is the 'family' of parasites named after Babeș, which explains why the word refers to a group of tiny organisms that infect red blood cells.
