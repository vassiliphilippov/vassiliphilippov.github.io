Etymology

grassy death camas got its name from a clear, sad picture: early farmers and ranchers saw animals eat the grass-like plant and 'die', so they called it death camas. The word grassy shows its grass-like leaves, and that is why the name now means 'poisonous' and dangerous to animals that eat the grass.
