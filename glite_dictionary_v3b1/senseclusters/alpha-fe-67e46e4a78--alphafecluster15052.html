Etymology

alpha-Fe combines alpha, the Greek letter meaning 'first', and Fe, the chemical symbol for iron taken from Latin ferrum meaning 'iron'. So alpha-Fe is the 'first' or basic form of iron that is stable at normal temperatures and the common magnetic iron found in steel and the Earth's crust.
