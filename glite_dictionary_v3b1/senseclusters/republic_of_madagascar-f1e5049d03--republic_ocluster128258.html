Etymology

Republic of Madagascar The name 'Madagascar' comes from old European maps and traveler stories — one popular story says a famous traveler wrote a name that sounded like 'Madagascar' and mapmakers used it for the island. After the island became independent in 1960 it called itself the Republic of Madagascar, with 'Republic' meaning a country with its own elected government.
