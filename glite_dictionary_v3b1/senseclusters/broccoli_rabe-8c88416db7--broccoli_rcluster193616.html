Etymology

broccoli rabe may come from Italian: broccoli for its small broccoli-like flower buds and rabe from the word 'rapa' meaning 'turnip', because the plant has leafy, turnip-like greens. So the name helps you remember it looks like tiny broccoli but is a leafy vegetable with a slightly 'bitter' taste.
