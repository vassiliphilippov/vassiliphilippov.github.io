Etymology

Tadzhik language comes from the people called Tadzhik, who speak a local form of Persian in Central Asia. So Tadzhik language literally means 'the language of the Tajiks' and today refers to the Persian-related speech used mainly in Tajikistan, often written in the Cyrillic alphabet.
