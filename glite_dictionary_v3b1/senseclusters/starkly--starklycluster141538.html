Etymology

starkly comes from an old German word stark, which meant 'strong' or 'stiff' and later 'plain' or 'severe', and you still hear stark in phrases like 'stark naked' or 'stark contrast'. So, to say something is starkly shown is to say it is shown in a very clear, sharp way that is hard to miss.
