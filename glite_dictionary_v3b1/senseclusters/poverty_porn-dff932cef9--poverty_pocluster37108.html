Etymology

The term poverty porn puts poverty next to porn to create a strong image of people watching poor people's suffering like a show. One popular story says critics used it for ads and programs that treat suffering as 'entertainment' or a way to get 'sympathy'. That's why the phrase means media that show poverty in a way that uses people to make viewers feel sorry or shocked.
