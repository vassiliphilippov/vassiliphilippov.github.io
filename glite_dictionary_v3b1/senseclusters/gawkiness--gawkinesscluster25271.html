Etymology

gawkiness comes from the verb gawk and the adjective gawky, which once meant 'to stare' with an open mouth and later came to mean 'to be clumsy'. So, think of someone 'gawking'—staring and moving awkwardly—to remember that gawkiness means 'awkwardness in movement or posture'.
