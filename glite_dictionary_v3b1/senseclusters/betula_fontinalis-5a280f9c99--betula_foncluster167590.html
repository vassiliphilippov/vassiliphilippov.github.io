Etymology

Betula fontinalis comes from Latin: Betula means 'birch' and fontinalis means 'of springs' or 'near water'. So, imagine a birch tree standing by a stream with brownish bark — this image helps you remember that Betula fontinalis is a birch that 'grows near streams' in the western United States.
