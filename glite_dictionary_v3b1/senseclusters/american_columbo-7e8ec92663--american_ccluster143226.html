Etymology

American columbo may come from old herbal medicine. One popular story says European herb sellers called a bitter root 'columbo' and used it as 'medicine', so when early settlers found a similar plant in North America they named it American columbo, which is why the name points to a plant known for its root and its use in 'medicine'.
