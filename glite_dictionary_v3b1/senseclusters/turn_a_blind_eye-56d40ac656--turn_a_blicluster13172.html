Etymology

turn a blind eye comes from a famous story about Admiral Nelson, who had a blind eye and put his telescope to that eye so he would not see an order to retreat. One popular story says he did this on purpose to ignore the signal, and that's why turn a blind eye means 'to deliberately ignore' or 'pretend not to notice' something wrong.
