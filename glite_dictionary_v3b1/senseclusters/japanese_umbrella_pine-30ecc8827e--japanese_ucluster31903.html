Etymology

Japanese umbrella pine gets its name from how it looks: the needles form flat clusters at the ends of branches and spread out like the top of an umbrella. People call it an umbrellapine because it is a pine tree with umbrella-like branches, and 'Japanese' shows where it comes from.
