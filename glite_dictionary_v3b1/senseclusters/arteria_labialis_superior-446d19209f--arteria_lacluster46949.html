Etymology

arteria labialis superior comes from Latin: arteria means 'artery', labialis means 'lip', and superior means 'upper', so the name gives a clear picture of an artery going to the 'upper lip'. That's why it refers to the branch of the facial artery that supplies blood to the upper lip.
