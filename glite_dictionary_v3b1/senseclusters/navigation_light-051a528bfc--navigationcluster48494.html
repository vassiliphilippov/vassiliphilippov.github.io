Etymology

Navigation light comes from the simple idea of a navigationlight fixed on a ship or plane so others can see where it is and which way it faces. Because the red and green lamps show a vehicle's 'position' and 'direction' at 'night' or in bad weather, the phrase now means the small safety lights that help others find and avoid it.
