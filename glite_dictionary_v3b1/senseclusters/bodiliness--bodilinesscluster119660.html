Etymology

The word bodiliness comes from the everyday word body and the ending -ness, which makes a noun meaning 'state' or 'quality'. So bodiliness is the 'state' of being bodily or 'physical', which is why it means having or relating to a real body rather than being only an idea or something in the mind.
