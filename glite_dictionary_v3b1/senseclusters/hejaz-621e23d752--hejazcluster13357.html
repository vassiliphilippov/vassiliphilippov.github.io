Etymology

Hejaz comes from the Arabic al-Ḥijāz, which means 'the barrier' (from the root ḥ-j-z 'to separate'). The name fits the place because the Hejaz is the strip of land and mountains along the Red Sea that 'separates' the coast from the desert, and so it became the name for this western region of Saudi Arabia where Mecca and Medina are found.
