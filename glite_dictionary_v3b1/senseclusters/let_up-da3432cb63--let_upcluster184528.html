Etymology

The phrasal verb let up comes from the image of loosening a hold: to let something up is to relax your grip so the pressure becomes smaller. Imagine you hold a rope tight and then open your hand a little, and the pull is less, so the phrase means 'to become less strong' or 'to ease'.
