Etymology

mythologization comes from myth meaning 'story' and the parts -ize 'to make' and -ation 'a process', so it literally means the process of making something into a myth. So, mythologization is when people turn a person, event, or idea into a larger-than-life story, often making it seem bigger and more symbolic than the real facts.
