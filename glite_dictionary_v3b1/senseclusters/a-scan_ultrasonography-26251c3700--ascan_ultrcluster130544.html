Etymology

A-scan ultrasonography gets its name from the simple, one-line picture the machine makes: the A-scan part refers to the line of spikes the device draws as it sends sound into the eye. Because that single-line reading shows the front and back of the eye, doctors can 'measure the eyeball' and work out the right lens power, so the name ties directly to how it is used.
