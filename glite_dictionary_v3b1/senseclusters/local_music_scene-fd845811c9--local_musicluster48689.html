Etymology

local music scene paints a simple picture: the word local points to the area, music shows what happens there, and the scene is like a small stage or place full of bands, clubs, and people who go to shows. So, local music scene is an easy way to talk about 'the community of musicians, venues, and music activity in a particular area'.
