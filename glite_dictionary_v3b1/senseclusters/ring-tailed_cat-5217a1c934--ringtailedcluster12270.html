Etymology

ring-tailed cat gets its name from its long bushy tail with black-and-white rings and from people who thought it looked like a small cat. The parts ring, tailed, and cat make the picture of a 'cat' with a ringed tail, so the name points to the tail pattern rather than to a true cat.
