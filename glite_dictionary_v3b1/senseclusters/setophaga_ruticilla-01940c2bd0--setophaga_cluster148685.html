Etymology

Setophaga ruticilla may come from older words meaning 'insect eater' for Setophaga and 'little red tail' for ruticilla. Imagine a small warbler flashing bright orange on its tail — that image explains why the scientific name and the English name 'American redstart' both point to the bird's red tail.
