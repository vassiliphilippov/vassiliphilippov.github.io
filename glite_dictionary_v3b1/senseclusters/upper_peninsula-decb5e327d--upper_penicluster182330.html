Etymology

Upper Peninsula is named for a simple picture: upper means the northern part and peninsula means land almost surrounded by water, so the name describes the northern piece of Michigan that sticks into the lakes. That's why Upper Peninsula refers to the peninsula between Lake Superior and Lake Michigan.
