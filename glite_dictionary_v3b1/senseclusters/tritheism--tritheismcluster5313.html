Etymology

tritheism comes from tri- (as in trio or triangle) meaning 'three' and theism meaning belief in 'god'. So tritheism literally means 'belief in three gods' and is used for the idea that the Father, the Son, and the Holy Spirit are three separate gods rather than one God.
