Etymology

The name coco plum tree may come from how its fruit looks: people thought the small round fruit looked a bit like a tiny coconut and a plum, so that name was given to the tree that grows near the coast. So, the name helps you remember it is a tree by the sea with 'plumlike' fruit you can eat.
