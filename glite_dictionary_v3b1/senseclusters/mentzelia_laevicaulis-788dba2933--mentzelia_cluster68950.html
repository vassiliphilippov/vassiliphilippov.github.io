Etymology

Mentzelia laevicaulis is named from two parts: Mentzelia is named after a plant scientist called Mentzel, and laevicaulis is Latin for 'smooth stem'. So the name gives a simple picture: a Mentzelia with smooth, white stems and pale yellow flowers that open in the morning.
