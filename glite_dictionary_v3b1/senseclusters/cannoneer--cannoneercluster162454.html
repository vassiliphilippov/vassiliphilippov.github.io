Etymology

The word cannoneer is made from cannon ('big gun') and the ending -eer ('person who'), so a cannoneer is a person who operates a cannon. A helpful way to remember is that cannon came from an old word for a 'reed' or 'tube' because early guns were like hollow tubes, so the name links the tube-shaped gun to the person who fires it.
