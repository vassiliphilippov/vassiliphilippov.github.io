Etymology

puff of white smoke comes from the clear image of a small, quick cloud of light smoke — a short puff of white smoke from a chimney or engine, or the white smoke released when a new pope is chosen. So, the phrase means 'a small, brief burst of white smoke' and helps you picture something that appears and then fades quickly.
