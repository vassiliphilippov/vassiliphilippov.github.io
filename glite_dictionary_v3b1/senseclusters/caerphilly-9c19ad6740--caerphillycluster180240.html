Etymology

Caerphilly comes from Welsh. It begins with caer meaning 'fort' or 'castle' and ends with the personal name Ffili, like 'Philip', so the place name means 'Ffili's fort', which fits the town's large medieval castle and explains why the cheese is named after the town.
