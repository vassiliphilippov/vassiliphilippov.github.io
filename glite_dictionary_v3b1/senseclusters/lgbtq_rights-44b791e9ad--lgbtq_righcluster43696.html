Etymology

LGBTQ+ rights comes from the letters L, G, B, T, Q and the + sign, which activists used as a simple name for people who stood together to ask for fair treatment. So today LGBTQ+ rights means 'equal treatment and protection' for lesbian, gay, bisexual, transgender, queer and other people.
