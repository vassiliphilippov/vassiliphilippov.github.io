Etymology

yellowbird comes from the two simple words yellow and bird, where yellow means 'the color yellow' and bird means 'a flying animal'. So a yellowbird is exactly a bird with bright yellow feathers, which makes the name easy to remember when you see a small yellow songbird in a garden.
