Etymology

Pieris rapae is also used for the green caterpillar stage of the 'cabbage white'; these small green larvae hide in cabbage leaves and eat holes in them, so gardeners call the larva by the same name. That's why Pieris rapae can mean the caterpillar that damages cabbage and similar plants.
