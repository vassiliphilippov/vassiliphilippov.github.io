Etymology

Grand Duchy of Moscow is a name that literally shows what it was: a big Grand area ruled by a duke, called a Duchy, with its center in Moscow. Imagine a ruler in Moscow gathering nearby towns and power, and you can see why the name means a large medieval Russian 'state' that helped form modern Russia.
