Etymology

The word Thaddaeus probably comes from an old Aramaic name like taddā' meaning 'heart' or 'breast'. A helpful way to remember is that 'heart' stands for courage and care, and that is why people pray to Thaddaeus when they face hopeless or desperate situations.
