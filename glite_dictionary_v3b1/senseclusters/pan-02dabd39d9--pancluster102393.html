Etymology

Pan is the Greek god of fields and shepherds, often shown as half-goat who plays pipes, and his name gave us the English word panic ('sudden fear') and the 'pan flute' (the pipes he played). So, his wild, musical nature explains why stories link Pan to forests, shepherds and sudden fear.
