Etymology

auf wiedersehen is a German goodbye that literally means 'until we see each other again'. It is made of auf and Wiedersehen, with Wiedersehen from wieder 'again' and sehen 'to see', so the phrase is used to say 'goodbye' while hoping to meet again.
