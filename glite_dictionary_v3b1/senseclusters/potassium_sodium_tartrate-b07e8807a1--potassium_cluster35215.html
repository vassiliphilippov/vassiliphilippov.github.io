Etymology

potassium sodium tartrate simply tells you what it is: a 'double salt' made from the metals potassium and sodium and an acid part called tartrate. The word 'tartrate' comes from 'tartar', the crust left in old wine barrels, and that full name helps explain why the compound is the mild 'laxative' found in some powders.
