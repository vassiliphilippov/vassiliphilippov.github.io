Etymology

The word rhapsodically comes from Greek parts rhaptein 'to stitch' and aeidein 'to sing', because long ago performers would 'stitch' songs and poems together into one excited speech. So, to speak rhapsodically is to talk like that, full of strong feeling and great excitement.
