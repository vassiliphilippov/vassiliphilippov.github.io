Etymology

Newton's second law of motion is named after the scientist Isaac Newton and comes from his simple idea that a stronger push makes something speed up more, while heavier things need a bigger push. Imagine pushing an empty shopping cart and a full one: the same force gives more acceleration to the empty cart than to the cart with more mass, so the law tells us how motion changes when we push an object.
