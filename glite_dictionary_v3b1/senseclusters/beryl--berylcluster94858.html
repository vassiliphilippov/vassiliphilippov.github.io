Etymology

beryl comes from the old Greek word beryllos, used for a blue-green precious stone, and that Greek word likely came from an old Indian word vaidurya meaning 'sea-green'. So, think of emerald and aquamarine, which are types of beryl, to remember that beryl is the name for a group of green and blue gems.
