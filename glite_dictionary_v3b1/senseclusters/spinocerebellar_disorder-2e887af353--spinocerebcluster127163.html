Etymology

spinocerebellar disorder comes from spino- meaning the spine and cerebellar meaning the small 'little brain' at the back of the head. When the spine and that 'little brain' are damaged, people often have trouble with 'balance' and 'coordination', so the name points to problems with movement and muscle control.
