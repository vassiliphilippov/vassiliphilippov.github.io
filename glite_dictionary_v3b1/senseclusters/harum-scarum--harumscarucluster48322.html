Etymology

Harum-scarum started as a silly rhyming phrase to show wild behavior. Think of someone like a fast 'hare' who acts without planning, so calling them harum-scarum means they are 'recklessly or cheerfully irresponsible; behaving in a wild, careless, or impetuous way'.
