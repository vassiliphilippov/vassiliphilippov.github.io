Etymology

The term lighting fixture comes from the two words lighting (the 'lights') and fixture (something fixed in place that 'holds' things). So it means a thing fixed to a wall or ceiling that 'holds' and powers 'lights', which is why today it refers to a mounted device for lamps or bulbs.
