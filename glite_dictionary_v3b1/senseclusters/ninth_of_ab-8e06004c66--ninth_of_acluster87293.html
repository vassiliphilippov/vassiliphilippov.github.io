Etymology

Ninth of Ab comes from the Jewish calendar: it is the ninth day of the month called Ab. One popular story says both Jerusalem temples were destroyed on that date, so people 'fast' and 'mourn' to remember the loss, which is why the day is a major day of mourning today.
