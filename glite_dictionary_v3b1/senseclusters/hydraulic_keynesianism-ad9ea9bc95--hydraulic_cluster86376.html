Etymology

hydraulic Keynesianism uses a simple picture: hydraulic points to water pipes, taps and valves that control a flow, and Keynesianism is the economic idea about using government action to influence demand. The image helps remember the meaning because, like turning a tap to change water flow, governments use 'government spending' and taxes to 'manage the economy' and control demand.
