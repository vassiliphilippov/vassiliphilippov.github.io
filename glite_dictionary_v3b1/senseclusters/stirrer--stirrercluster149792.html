Etymology

The word stirrer can also mean a person who causes trouble; it comes from stir as in 'stir up', which means 'to start or make stronger', plus the ending -er. Think of someone who 'stirs the pot' and mixes feelings or gossip, so a stirrer is someone who provokes or makes trouble.
