Etymology

unassailable comes from the prefix un- meaning 'not' and the root assail, from an old Latin word that meant 'to jump at' or attack, and you can see the same idea in assault. So unassailable literally means 'not able to be jumped at', which is why it describes something that cannot be attacked, questioned, or defeated.
