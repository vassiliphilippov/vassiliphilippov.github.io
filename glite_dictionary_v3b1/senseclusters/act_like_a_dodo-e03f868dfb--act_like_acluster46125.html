Etymology

act like a dodo may come from the old bird called the dodo, a heavy, clumsy bird that could not fly and often seemed slow and awkward. So, calling someone a dodo gave the image of a person doing something silly, and to act like a dodo means 'to behave in a foolish or clueless way'.
