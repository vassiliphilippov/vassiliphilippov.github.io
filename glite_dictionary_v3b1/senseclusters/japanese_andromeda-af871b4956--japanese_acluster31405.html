Etymology

Japanese andromeda may come from the Greek myth of Andromeda, because the shrub's hanging groups of white flowers looked to people like a necklace or a flowing dress. So, because the plant comes from Japan and has that picture, Japanese andromeda now means an 'evergreen garden shrub with hanging white flowers'.
