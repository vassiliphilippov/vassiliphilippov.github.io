Etymology

nonmaterialistic starts with non- meaning 'not' and the rest comes from material, which means the 'things' or 'stuff' we own. So, to be nonmaterialistic is to 'not' care much about possessions and to prefer inner life, ideas, feelings, or experiences over owning more things.
