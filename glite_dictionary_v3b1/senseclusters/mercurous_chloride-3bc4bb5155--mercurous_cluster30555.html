Etymology

mercurous chloride gets its name from its parts: mercurous (from mercury) and chloride (from chlorine), and it was better known as 'calomel', a white powder once given as a strong medicine to make people go to the toilet. That's why it was used as a 'purgative', though today we avoid it because it is 'toxic'.
