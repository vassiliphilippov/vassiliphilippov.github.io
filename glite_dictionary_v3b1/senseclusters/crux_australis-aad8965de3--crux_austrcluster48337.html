Etymology

Crux Australis comes from Latin and literally has two parts: Crux ('cross') and Australis ('southern'), because the bright stars form a small cross in the southern sky. Sailors and travellers used that cross of stars to find direction, so the name now means the 'Southern Cross' and is a symbol of the Southern Hemisphere.
