Etymology

The word stimulator comes from the Latin stimulus, meaning 'goad' or 'push', and the ending -or, meaning 'one that does'. You can also see the same root in stimulate and stimulant, so a stimulator is a person or thing that 'pushes' others to be more active or motivated.
