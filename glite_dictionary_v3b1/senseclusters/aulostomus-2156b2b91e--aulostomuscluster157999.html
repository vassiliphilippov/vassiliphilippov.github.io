Etymology

The word Aulostomus comes from Greek words aulós meaning 'pipe' or 'flute' and stoma meaning 'mouth'. So it literally means 'pipe-mouth', a helpful picture for the trumpetfish: these fish have long, tube-like snouts that look like a small trumpet or pipe.
