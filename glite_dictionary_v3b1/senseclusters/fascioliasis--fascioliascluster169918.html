Etymology

fascioliasis comes from fasciola, meaning 'little band' or 'ribbon', and the suffix -iasis, meaning 'disease' or 'infection'. So fascioliasis is the disease caused by a ribbon-like parasite called a liver fluke, and the name links the parasite's shape to the illness it causes.
