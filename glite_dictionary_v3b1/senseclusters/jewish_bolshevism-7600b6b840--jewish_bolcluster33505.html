Etymology

Jewish Bolshevism comes from a harmful claim in the early 1900s that tried to link Jewish people with the Bolshevik communist movement, and some groups used it to blame Jews for social and political change. That's why today the phrase refers to that false 'conspiracy' and is seen as an 'antisemitic' stereotype.
