Etymology

gasteromycete comes from Greek parts: gastero- meaning 'stomach' and -mycete meaning 'fungus'. The name gives a clear image: these fungi hold their spores inside the mushroom like a little stomach, which is why the word describes fungi whose spores develop inside before they are released.
