Etymology

towing line comes from a clear picture: a long rope or cable used to 'tow' a broken boat or car. It links tow meaning 'to pull' and line meaning 'rope', so the phrase pictures a rope that pulls something and today means the rope or cable used to pull a boat or vehicle.
