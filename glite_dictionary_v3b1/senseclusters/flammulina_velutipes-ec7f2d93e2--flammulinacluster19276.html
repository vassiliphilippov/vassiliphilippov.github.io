Etymology

Flammulina velutipes comes from Latin words that describe the mushroom's look: Flammulina means 'little flame' because of the orange cap, and velutipes means 'velvety stalk' because the stem feels soft and darkens with age. So the name helps you picture this mushroom you can eat.
