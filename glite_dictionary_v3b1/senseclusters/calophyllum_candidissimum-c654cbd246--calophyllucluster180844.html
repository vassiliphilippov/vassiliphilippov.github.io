Etymology

Calophyllum candidissimum may come from old Greek and Latin words: Calophyllum means 'beautiful leaf' and candidissimum means 'very white'. One popular idea is that this name describes the tree's shiny leaves and white flowers, so the name gives a quick picture of the tropical tree people use for shade and strong wood.
