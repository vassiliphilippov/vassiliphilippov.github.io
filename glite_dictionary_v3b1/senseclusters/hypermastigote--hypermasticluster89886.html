Etymology

Hypermastigote comes from hyper- meaning 'more' or 'over' and mastig- meaning 'whip', because these tiny single-celled animals have many whip-like tails to move. So, a hypermastigote is a tiny creature with many whip-like tails that lives in insect guts like termites and helps them digest wood.
