Etymology

The word Bradypodidae comes from Greek parts: bradys meaning 'slow', pod meaning 'foot', and the ending -idae meaning 'family'. So Bradypodidae is the 'slow-foot family', which fits because this family is the true sloths, slow animals that live in trees in Central and South America.
