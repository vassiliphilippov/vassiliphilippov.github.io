Etymology

The word Juglans comes from the old Latin phrase Jovis glans, which means 'Jupiter's nut' (people once thought the walnut was a special nut linked to the god Jupiter). So, Juglans is the scientific name for walnut trees today, and that link to 'Jupiter's nut' helps explain why it refers to walnuts.
