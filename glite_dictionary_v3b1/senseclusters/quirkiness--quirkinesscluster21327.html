Etymology

quirkiness comes from the old word quirk, which once meant a 'sudden twist' or 'turn', like a small bend in the road of someone's behaviour. A helpful way to remember it is to think of a little, charming twist in a person's habits, which is why quirkiness means an unusual but appealing trait and is the same idea behind quirky, meaning 'odd'.
