Etymology

R.I. is a short form for Rhode Island, a U.S. state whose name probably comes from a Dutch phrase meaning 'red island'. A helpful way to remember R.I. is that the letters stand for Rhode and Island, and you will often see this short form in addresses and lists for that small state.
