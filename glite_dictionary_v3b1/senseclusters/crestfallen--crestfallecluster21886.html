Etymology

crestfallen comes from crest meaning 'the top of the head' or 'the top of a helmet' and fallen meaning 'dropped'. So to be crestfallen is to look as if your 'crest' has fallen down — a simple picture of feeling sad and disappointed after hopes are broken.
