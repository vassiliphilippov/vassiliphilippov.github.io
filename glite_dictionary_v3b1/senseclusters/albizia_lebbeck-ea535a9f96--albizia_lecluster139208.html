Etymology

Albizia lebbeck gets its name from two parts: Albizia, after an Italian family who first brought the tree to Europe, and lebbeck, from old local names for the same plant. So the Latin name is a simple label that points to the big shade tree with round flower clusters and long seed pods that rattle, the one often called the 'siris tree' or 'woman's tongue tree'.
