Etymology

The word besprinkle is made of be- and sprinkle, where sprinkle means 'to scatter small drops or little bits' and be- gives the idea 'cover with' or 'make'. So besprinkle means to 'cover something lightly by scattering drops or little bits', like sugar on a cake or a gentle rain on a window.
