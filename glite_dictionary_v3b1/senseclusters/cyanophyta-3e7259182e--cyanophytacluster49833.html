Etymology

Cyanophyta comes from cyan ('blue') and phyta ('plants'), so it literally means 'blue plants'. That's a helpful memory: these are the blue-green organisms that make food from sunlight and produce oxygen, though scientists now usually call them cyanobacteria because they are bacteria, not true plants.
