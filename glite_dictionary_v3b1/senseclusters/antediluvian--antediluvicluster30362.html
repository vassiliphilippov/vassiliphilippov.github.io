Etymology

antediluvian comes from the Latin parts ante- ('before') and diluvium ('flood'), literally 'before the flood' and related to the English word deluge ('big flood'). Think of something so old it belongs to the time before the great flood, and that's why antediluvian means extremely old or very old-fashioned.
