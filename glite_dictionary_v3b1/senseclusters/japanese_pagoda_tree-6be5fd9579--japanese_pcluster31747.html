Etymology

Japanese pagoda tree may get its name from the way the tree's branches and flower clusters form flat, layered shapes like the roofs of a pagoda, and people often planted it near pagodas in old gardens. That's why the name is used for this kind of tree with a rounded crown and creamy-white flowers.
