Etymology

Alosa pseudoharengus comes from name parts that show a simple picture: Alosa is the fish group and pseudoharengus combines pseudo- meaning 'false' and harengus meaning 'herring'. So the name paints a fish that looks like a herring but is a different kind of shad, which explains why it is used for this small fish often salted or smoked.
