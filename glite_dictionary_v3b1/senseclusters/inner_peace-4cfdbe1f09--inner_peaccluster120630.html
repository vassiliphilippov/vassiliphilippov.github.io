Etymology

inner peace comes from the simple image of a quiet place inside you, with inner meaning 'inside' and peace meaning 'quiet' or 'calm'. People pictured a calm room in their mind where noise and worry cannot reach, so the phrase now means 'a calm, worry-free state'.
