Etymology

subduedness comes from the verb subdue, built from sub- 'under' and a root meaning 'to lead', the same root you see in reduce and conduct. It originally meant 'bring under control', so someone's subduedness is their calm, restrained, or patiently controlled behaviour in difficult situations.
