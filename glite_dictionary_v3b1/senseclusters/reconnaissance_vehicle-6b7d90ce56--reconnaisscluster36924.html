Etymology

reconnaissance vehicle comes from the idea of a 'scout' going ahead to look and bring back news, where reconnaissance means the act of looking and vehicle means a machine that carries people. So a reconnaissance vehicle is a fast, light military car sent ahead to 'scout' and gather information.
