Etymology

transversus abdominis muscle gets its name from Latin: transversus means 'across' and abdominis means 'of the belly', so the name literally says 'the muscle that runs across the belly'. So, because it lies across the belly and tightens the abdomen, it helps 'support the core' and keep the lower back stable.
