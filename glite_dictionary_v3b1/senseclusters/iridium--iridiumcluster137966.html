Etymology

iridium comes from the Latin iris, meaning 'rainbow', because some of its chemicals made many bright colors, and the same root is in iris for the eye and the flower to help you remember. So, despite the colorful name, iridium is a very hard, heavy metal used in strong parts and tools that must resist heat and wear.
