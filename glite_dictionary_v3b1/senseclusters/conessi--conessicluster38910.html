Etymology

conessi likely comes from local South Asian names for a tropical tree used in traditional medicine. Its scientific name, Holarrhena antidysenterica, even shows its purpose: anti- means 'against' and 'dysentery' is 'severe diarrhoea', so you can remember conessi as the tree used to fight stomach illness.
