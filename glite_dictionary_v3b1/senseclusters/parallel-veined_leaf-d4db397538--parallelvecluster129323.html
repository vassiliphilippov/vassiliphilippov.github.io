Etymology

parallel-veined leaf describes a leaf whose veined lines run parallel from the bottom to the tip, so the veins look like many thin lines or train tracks next to each other. That's why the term is used for leaves of plants like grasses, lilies, and orchids, where this clear pattern helps identify the leaf.
