Etymology

decouple comes from the parts de- meaning 'remove' and couple meaning 'pair' or 'join', so it literally means to 'take a pair apart'. In engineering use, to decouple is like putting a small barrier so noise or unwanted signals cannot travel from one part to another, which is why it means reducing or removing a connection.
