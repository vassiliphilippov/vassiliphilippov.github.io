Etymology

Ulysses S. Grant may have got his name from a simple mistake when he was young: he was born Hiram Ulysses Grant, but a local politician sent his name to a military school as Ulysses S. Grant, adding the S probably from his mother's maiden name 'Simpson', and Grant kept it. He later led the Union army to victory in the Civil War and became the 18th president, so today Ulysses S. Grant is the name we use for that famous general and president.
