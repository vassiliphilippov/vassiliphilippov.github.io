Etymology

yellow chestnut oak comes from how the tree looks: its nuts and leaves can remind people of a chestnut and its leaves or bark can look yellow, so people joined yellow, chestnut, and oak to name it. That's why the name means 'a medium-sized oak with chestnut-like features'.
