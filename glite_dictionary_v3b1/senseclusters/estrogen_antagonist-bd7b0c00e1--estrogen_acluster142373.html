Etymology

The term estrogen antagonist paints a simple picture: estrogen is a body hormone and an antagonist is an opponent that 'blocks' it. So an estrogen antagonist is a drug that 'blocks' estrogen and is used to treat breast cancer that needs estrogen to grow.
