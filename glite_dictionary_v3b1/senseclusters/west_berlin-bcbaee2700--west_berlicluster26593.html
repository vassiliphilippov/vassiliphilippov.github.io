Etymology

West Berlin was the name given after World War II to the western part of the city that the United States, Britain and France controlled. Because it was like an 'island' of the West inside East Germany and cut off by the Berlin Wall, the name now brings to mind the Western-controlled part of Berlin until Germany became one country again in 1990.
