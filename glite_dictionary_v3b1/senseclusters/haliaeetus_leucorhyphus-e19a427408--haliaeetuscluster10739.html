Etymology

Haliaeetus leucorhyphus comes from old Greek words: Haliaeetus means 'sea-eagle' and leucorhyphus includes 'leuco' for 'white', so the name paints the image of a 'sea-eagle with white' feathers and helps you remember it is a large fish-eating eagle.
