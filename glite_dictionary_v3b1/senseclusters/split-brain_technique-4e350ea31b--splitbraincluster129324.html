Etymology

split-brain technique comes from the literal idea of split and brain: doctors cut the main link between the brain's two halves to 'stop serious seizures'. Because the halves could no longer share information and began to act on their own, scientists used the method to watch each side work, so the name now refers to that surgery and to the way it shows separate brain functions.
