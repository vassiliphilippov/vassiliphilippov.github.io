Etymology

macerate comes from the Latin macerare, which meant 'to soften by soaking'. Think of leaving dried fruit or onions in vinegar or sugar until they become soft and juicy — that soaking is macerating, and it explains the modern meaning of 'soften or break down by liquid'.
