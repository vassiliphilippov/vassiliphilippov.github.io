Etymology

Cumbersome also comes from cumber ('to burden') and -some ('causing'), so think of something that 'weighs down' an idea. If a sentence or style 'burdens' the reader and gets in the way, it feels awkward and clumsy. That's why cumbersome can describe a style or expression that is hard to read.
