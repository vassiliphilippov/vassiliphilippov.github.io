Etymology

helter-skelter is an old rhyming pair that was made to sound hurried and messy, like two quick words stuck together. A helpful way to remember it is to think of the parts sounding like hurl or hurtle ('to rush'), so helter-skelter means 'in a disorderly or chaotic way'.
