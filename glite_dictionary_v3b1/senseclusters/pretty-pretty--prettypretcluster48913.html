Etymology

Pretty-pretty comes from repeating pretty, which means 'attractive' or 'pleasing', and the repeat makes the idea feel stronger or more showy, like decorating something with too many ribbons. So pretty-pretty means overly or superficially pretty, often in a way that looks a bit fake.
