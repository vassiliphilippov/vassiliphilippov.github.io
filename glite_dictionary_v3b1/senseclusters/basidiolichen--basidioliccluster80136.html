Etymology

basidiolichen begins with basidio-, from a Greek word meaning 'small base' or 'pedestal', and ends with lichen, the familiar name for the fungus-and-algae partnership. So a basidiolichen is a lichen that has a fungus with that 'base' structure, which helps you remember it is the kind of lichen made with a basidium-type fungus.
