Etymology

tetrabromo-phenolsulfonephthalein is really a name made of parts that describe the chemical: tetra- means 'four', bromo- comes from the element 'bromine', sulfone is related to 'sulfur', and phthalein is a kind of dye. That's why the name tells you it is a special dye with four bromine atoms and a sulfur part, and this make-up is what makes it change color in acid, so it is used as an acid-base indicator.
