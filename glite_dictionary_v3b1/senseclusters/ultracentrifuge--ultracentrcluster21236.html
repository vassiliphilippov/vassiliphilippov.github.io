Etymology

ultracentrifuge combines ultra- meaning 'beyond' or 'extreme' and centrifuge, which comes from center plus a piece meaning 'to move away'. So an ultracentrifuge is a very powerful spinning machine that pushes tiny parts away from the center so they can be separated.
