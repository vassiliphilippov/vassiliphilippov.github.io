Etymology

azulejo comes from Arabic al-zillīj, which meant 'polished stone' or 'tile' used for decoration. Many of these tiles are blue, so a helpful way to remember the word is to think of Spanish azul and English azure. That's why azulejo means a painted, tin-glazed ceramic tile used in Spain and Portugal.
