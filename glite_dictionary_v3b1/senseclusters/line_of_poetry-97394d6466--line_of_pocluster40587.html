Etymology

line of poetry comes from the old idea of a line as a 'thread' or a marked rule on the page. One popular story says writers thought of words as a 'thread' running across the paper or followed a marked 'line', so each row of words became its own part. That's why a line of poetry now means a single row of words with its own rhythm or meaning.
