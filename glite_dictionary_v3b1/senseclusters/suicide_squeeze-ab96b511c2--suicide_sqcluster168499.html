Etymology

suicide squeeze comes from baseball and gives a strong picture: the word squeeze is the soft hit that 'squeezes' the runner toward home, and suicide shows how risky the move is because the runner breaks for home immediately. So, it means 'a very risky play' where the runner on third runs for home as the batter 'bunts', hoping the soft hit lets them score.
