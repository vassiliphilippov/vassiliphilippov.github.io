Etymology

Penstemon linarioides may come from the look of the flower: Penstemon means 'beardtongue' because one inner part of the flower looks like a small, hairy tongue, and linarioides means 'like Linaria', a plant with narrow leaves. So the name helps you picture the blue-violet wildflower with a tongue-like part inside the bloom and thin leaves like Linaria.
