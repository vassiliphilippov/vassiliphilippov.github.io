Etymology

Quinidine gets its name from quinine, the well-known drug made from Spanish quina, a native word meaning 'bark'. Both medicines originally come from the bark of a South American tree, so quinidine is remembered as a bark-derived drug that doctors use to help control irregular heartbeats.
