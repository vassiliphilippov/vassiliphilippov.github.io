Etymology

gamma hydroxybutyrate gets its name from parts of the chemical: gamma shows where a small part sits, hydroxy means a tiny group with oxygen and hydrogen, and butyrate comes from a short acid called butyric acid. People call it 'GHB', and because it can make someone very sleepy or 'pass out', it is used as a sedative and is sometimes misused by attackers to make someone unconscious.
