Etymology

Loyalist Volunteer Force is a name that tells its story: Loyalist means 'people who wanted Northern Ireland to stay part of the United Kingdom', Volunteer means 'members who joined by choice', and Force shows it was an 'armed group'. That's why the name refers to a pro-UK armed group formed in 1996 and helps explain why it became known for violent attacks against Catholics and people who supported the peace process.
