Etymology

Jesuitic turn may come from the idea that Jesuitic refers to Jesuit priests who were known for clever, tricky arguing and that turn means a sudden twist in speech. So the phrase paints the picture of someone making a clever twist in their words, and that's why it means 'a complex or cunning argument'.
