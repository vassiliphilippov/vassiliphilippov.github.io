Etymology

carbonous comes from carbon, from the Latin carbo meaning 'coal' or 'charcoal', and the ending -ous meaning 'relating to' or 'full of'. So carbonous means 'relating to carbon' and is often used for compounds where the carbon has less oxygen, like carbon monoxide.
