Etymology

requirements contract comes from the simple idea that a buyer promises to buy all their requirements ('needs') from one seller under a single contract. Imagine a regular shopper who always goes to the same shop for everything they need; that image shows why the phrase means a deal where the seller must provide all the buyer's 'needs'.
