Etymology

optical glass comes from the idea of very clear glass made for things that work with light, because optical means 'about sight or light'. Craftsmen ground and tested this glass so it would bend light in a known way to make good lenses, so optical glass means 'clear glass used to make lenses and other parts that control light'.
