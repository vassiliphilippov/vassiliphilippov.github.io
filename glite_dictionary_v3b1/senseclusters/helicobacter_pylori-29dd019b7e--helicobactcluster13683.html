Etymology

Helicobacter pylori comes from parts that describe the microbe's shape and home; helico means 'spiral', bacter means 'bacterium', and pylori points to the lower part of the stomach, so the name helps you remember it is a spiral-shaped bacterium that lives in the stomach.
