Etymology

white vitriol comes from old chemistry: people called glassy salt crystals vitriol and used the color word white for the colorless zinc kind. So, picture small white, glass-like crystals — that image helps you remember it is the colorless zinc salt used to protect wood and to add a thin layer of zinc to metal.
