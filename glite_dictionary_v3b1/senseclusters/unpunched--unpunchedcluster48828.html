Etymology

The word unpunched is built from un- meaning 'not' and punch meaning 'to make a hole' or 'to hit', just like in 'punch a hole' or 'punch someone'. So unpunched describes something that has not had holes made or has not been hit, for example a clean card or an unmarked face.
