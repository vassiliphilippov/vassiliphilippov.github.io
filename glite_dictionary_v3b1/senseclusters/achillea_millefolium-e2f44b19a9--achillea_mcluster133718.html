Etymology

Achillea millefolium gets its name from Latin: mille means 'thousand' and folium means 'leaf', because the plant has many tiny, feathery leaves. One popular story says the hero Achilles used this plant to help heal wounds, which is why the name Achillea is linked to healing and why the plant, also called 'yarrow', is known for its many leaves and traditional medicine.
