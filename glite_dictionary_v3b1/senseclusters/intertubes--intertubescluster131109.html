Etymology

intertubes is a playful name for the Internet that mixes inter- (as in Internet) with tubes. Think of tubes as 'pipes' that carry information, so intertubes gives the funny image of the whole online world as a bunch of pipes and is often used to tease or make fun of people who do not know much about technology.
