Etymology

tube-shaped comes from the simple word tube, meaning a 'hollow' 'cylinder' like a straw or pipe, and the ending -shaped meaning 'having the form of'. So, tube-shaped means 'having the shape of a tube', which is why we use it for things that are long, round, and hollow to let air or water pass.
