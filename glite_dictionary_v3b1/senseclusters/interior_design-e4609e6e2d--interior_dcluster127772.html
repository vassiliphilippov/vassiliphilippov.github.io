Etymology

interior design comes from the simple idea of planning the inside of a building: interior means 'inside' and design means 'a plan'. Imagine someone drawing a room with furniture, colours and light to make it useful and beautiful, and that's why interior design today means planning and decorating the inside of homes or offices.
