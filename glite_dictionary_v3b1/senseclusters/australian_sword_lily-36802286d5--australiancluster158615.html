Etymology

Australian sword lily probably comes from how the plant looks. One popular story says its long, narrow leaves are like a sword and its flowers look like a lily, and because it grows in Australia the name gives a clear picture, so it means 'a plant with long, sword-like leaves and lily-like flowers from Australia'.
