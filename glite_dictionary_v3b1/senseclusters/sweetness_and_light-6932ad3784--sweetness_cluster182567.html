Etymology

sweetness and light comes from a simple picture: sweetness (pleasant taste) and light (warm brightness), like honey in sunshine. Writers used that image to show calm, pleasant times, so the phrase now means 'a cheerful, pleasant, and friendly attitude or situation'.
