Etymology

subversive comes from the Latin parts sub- meaning 'under' and vertere meaning 'to turn', the same root you see in words like reverse and convert. So when we call ideas or actions subversive, we mean they are meant to 'turn' a system from below and thus weaken or overthrow the existing authority.
