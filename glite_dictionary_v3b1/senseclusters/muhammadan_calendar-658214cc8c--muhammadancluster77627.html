Etymology

Muhammadan calendar may come from the fact that the system begins with the Prophet Muhammad's journey (the 'Hegira') in 622 AD, so writers called it Muhammadan (about Muhammad) and calendar (a system of months). That's why the Muhammadan calendar means the moon-based calendar used by Muslims, which starts at that event and whose months move through the seasons.
