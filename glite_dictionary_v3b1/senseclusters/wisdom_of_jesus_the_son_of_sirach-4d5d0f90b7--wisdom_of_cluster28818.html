Etymology

Wisdom of Jesus the Son of Sirach names the book and its author: Jesus (a common name then) who was the son of Sirach, and the word 'Wisdom' shows it is a collection of wise sayings. So the title means 'a book of moral lessons and proverbs', which is why people use it today to talk about those old teachings.
