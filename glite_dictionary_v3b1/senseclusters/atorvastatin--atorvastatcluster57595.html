Etymology

The word atorvastatin breaks into two parts: the first part atorva- is the specific name given to this drug, and the ending -statin marks a group of medicines that lower 'cholesterol'. A helpful way to remember -statin is to think 'stop', because these drugs help 'stop' the body from making extra cholesterol, which is why atorvastatin is used to lower blood fats.
