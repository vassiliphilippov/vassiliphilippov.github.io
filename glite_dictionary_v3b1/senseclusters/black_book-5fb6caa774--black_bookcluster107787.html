Etymology

The noun black book may come from a small black-covered book that shop owners, town leaders, or criminals used to write the names of people who caused trouble. If your name was in that hidden book, you were 'out of favor' or 'to be avoided', so today black book means a list of people who are 'out of favor' or should be avoided.
