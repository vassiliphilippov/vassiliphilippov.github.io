Etymology

Grigori Rasputin was a real man from Siberia who called himself a 'holy man' and became a close adviser to Russia's royal family after seeming to help their sick son. People thought he had strange power and a secret hold over the rulers, so today his name is used for a mysterious person who has strong, secret 'influence' behind leaders.
