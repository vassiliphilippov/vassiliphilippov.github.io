Etymology

Heavily comes from the old English word hefig 'weighty' and the ending -ly that makes it mean 'in a heavy way'. Think of a heavy weight pressing down — that image helps: when you depend 'heavily' on someone or when rain falls 'heavily', it means 'very much' or 'a lot'.
