Etymology

The word Rankine is named after the Scottish engineer and physicist William John Macquorn Rankine, who worked on heat and temperature. A Rankine is a unit on the Rankine scale, which is like Fahrenheit but starts at 'absolute zero', so zero Rankine is the coldest possible temperature.
