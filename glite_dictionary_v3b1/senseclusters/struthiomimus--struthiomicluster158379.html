Etymology

Struthiomimus comes from parts that mean 'ostrich' and 'imitator', with struthio for 'ostrich' and mimus for 'mimic'. So it literally means 'ostrich mimic', which helps you picture a small, fast, toothless dinosaur that looked and ran like an ostrich.
