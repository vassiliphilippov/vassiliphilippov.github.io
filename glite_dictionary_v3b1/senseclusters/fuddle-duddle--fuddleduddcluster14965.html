Etymology

fuddle-duddle is a playful made-up phrase, formed from fuddle and duddle, used as a 'polite' or 'nonsense' way to show annoyance instead of saying a rude word. One popular story says Canadian prime minister Pierre Trudeau made the phrase famous in the 1970s, which is why fuddle-duddle today means a dismissive or evasive remark rather than a real insult.
