Etymology

pettishness comes from pettish, which is related to petty ('small') and pet ('favored' or 'spoiled'). A helpful way to remember pettishness is to imagine a small, spoiled 'pet' who quickly gets annoyed over little things, which explains why the word means being easily irritated or bad-tempered in a childish way.
