Etymology

unretted breaks into un- meaning 'not' and retted, from retting, the process of soaking or controlled 'rotting' of plant stems to free the fibers, much like the everyday word rot. So unretted means the stems have not been through that soaking 'rotting' and the fibers are still tough.
