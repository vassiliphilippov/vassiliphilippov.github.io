Etymology

The noun nut sedge comes from the plant's small, nut-like roots called 'tubers', and sedge is a grass-like plant, so the tiny round roots look like little nuts. That's why the name fits a plant people sometimes eat for its 'edible tubers' and why it is also often called a 'weed' when it spreads into gardens.
