Etymology

Christopher Marlowe was an English poet and playwright from the late 1500s who wrote daring plays like 'Doctor Faustus'. He helped bring a new style of poetry to the stage, using lines without rhyme, so people remember him as an important early writer who influenced later playwrights such as Shakespeare.
