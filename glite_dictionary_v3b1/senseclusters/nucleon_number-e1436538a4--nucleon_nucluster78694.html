Etymology

nucleon number is like a headcount of the tiny particles in the centre of an atom: the word nucleon means the particles 'protons' and 'neutrons' and number means how many. So it tells the total of 'protons' and 'neutrons' and shows which version, or 'isotope', of the element you have.
