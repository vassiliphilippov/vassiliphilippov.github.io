Etymology

St Bernard is named after a mountain shelter at the Great St Bernard Pass, where a monk called Saint Bernard and his big dogs helped find travellers lost in snow. These strong, furry dogs would lead people to safety, so the name now refers to a large dog known for 'rescue' work.
