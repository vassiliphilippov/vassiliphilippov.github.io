Etymology

nitrocalcite joins nitro- and calcite: nitro- comes from the word 'nitre' and nitrate, meaning a salt with nitrogen, and calcite is linked to calcium and the old word 'calx', meaning 'lime'. So, nitrocalcite literally means a 'calcium salt with nitrate', which is why the mineral is calcium nitrate.
