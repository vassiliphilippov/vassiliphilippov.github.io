Etymology

Ionic medication comes from a simple picture: a small 'electric current' moves tiny charged particles, or 'ions', and the word ionic points to those ions while medication is the medicine moved into the body. So it means a treatment that uses an 'electric current' to push medicine into deeper parts of the body.
