Etymology

intemperateness comes from the prefix in- meaning 'not' and the root temper meaning 'to control' or 'keep balance', the same root you see in temperate and when you 'keep your temper'. So, intemperateness is when someone does not have that control or balance and gives in to too much food, drink, or strong feeling.
