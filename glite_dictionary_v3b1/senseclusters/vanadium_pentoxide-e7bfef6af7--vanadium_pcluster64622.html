Etymology

vanadium pentoxide gets its name from two parts: vanadium was named after the old Norse goddess Vanadis because many vanadium compounds show bright, pretty colors, and penta + oxide mean 'five' and 'oxygen atoms'. So the name tells you this is a vanadium compound with five oxygen atoms, which helps explain its formula and its yellow-red color.
