Etymology

The word pharmacognosy comes from the Greek words pharmakon meaning 'medicine' or 'drug' and gnosis meaning 'knowledge'. You can also see pharmakon in the familiar word pharmacy, so pharmacognosy is the study of medicines from natural sources like plants.
