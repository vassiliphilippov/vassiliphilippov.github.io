Etymology

The word irreconcilability comes from the familiar verb reconcile ('bring together' or 'make agree'), with the prefix ir- ('not') and the ending -ability ('ability'). So it means 'not able to be brought together', which is why we use it for ideas, plans, or people that cannot be made to agree.
