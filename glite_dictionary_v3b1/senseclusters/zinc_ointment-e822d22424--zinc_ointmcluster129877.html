Etymology

Zinc ointment comes from the simple idea of mixing zinc, a white powder, into an ointment to make a thick white paste that sits on the skin like a small shield. Because that paste protects and soothes damaged skin, the name now means 'an ointment containing zinc used to treat rashes and minor skin problems'.
