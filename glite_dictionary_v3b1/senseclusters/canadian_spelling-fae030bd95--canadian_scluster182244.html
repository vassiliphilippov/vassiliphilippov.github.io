Etymology

Canadian spelling comes from Canada's history: the word Canadian shows where this way is used and spelling means how words are written. Because Canada used to be connected to Britain and is next to the United States, writers kept some British forms like 'colour' and also used American forms like 'color', so Canadian spelling today means a mixed way of writing words.
