Etymology

leoncita comes from Spanish: it is a small form of león ('lion') with the ending -cita meaning 'little' or 'small'. So a leoncita is literally a 'little lion', a name given to this small South American marmoset because its silky fur and face make it look a bit like a tiny lion.
