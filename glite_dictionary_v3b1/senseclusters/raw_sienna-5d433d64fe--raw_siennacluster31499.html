Etymology

raw sienna is named after Siena, a city in Italy where artists dug a yellow-brown earth to make paint. The word raw means the earth was used 'as is' and not 'burnt', so the pigment kept its warm, natural color, which is why raw sienna means a natural yellowish-brown paint color.
