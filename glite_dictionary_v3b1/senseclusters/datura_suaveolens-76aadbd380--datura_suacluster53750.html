Etymology

Datura suaveolens comes from its large, trumpet-shaped flowers that open at night and give a strong, sweet smell. The Latin word suaveolens means 'sweet-smelling', so the name reminds us it is a night-blooming plant with fragrant, trumpet-like flowers.
