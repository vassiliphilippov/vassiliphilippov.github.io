Etymology

do-gooding comes from the simple parts do + good with the action ending -ing, so it literally means 'doing good' or 'helping others'. It is often used in a critical way for help that seems showy or self-important, so that's why do-gooding can mean helping others with good intentions but in a way that feels too proud or annoying.
