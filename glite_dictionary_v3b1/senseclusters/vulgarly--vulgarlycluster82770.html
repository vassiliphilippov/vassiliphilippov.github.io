Etymology

The word vulgarly comes from vulgar, which comes from the old Latin word vulgus meaning 'the common people' or 'ordinary'. Over time, 'ordinary' came to be seen as 'not polite', so vulgarly now means doing something in a 'rude' or 'crude' way.
