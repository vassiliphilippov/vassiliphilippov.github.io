Etymology

Prince Otto Eduard Leopold von Bismarck was a famous German leader who helped unite Germany. The words Prince and von show he came from an important family ('von' means 'from') and Bismarck is his family name, so the long name helps you picture a powerful noble who became the first leader of a united Germany.
