Etymology

The term candied fruit comes from a simple kitchen method: cooks heat fruit in sugar so the sugar soaks into the fruit and makes it sweet and shiny, turning the fruit into candied pieces. So candied fruit today means fruit preserved in sugar and used as a 'sweet treat' or in baking.
