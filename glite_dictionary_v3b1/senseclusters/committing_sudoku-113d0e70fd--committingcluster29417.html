Etymology

committing sudoku is a joking twist on the serious phrase committing seppuku, where the violent-sounding word is swapped for the number puzzle sudoku, making a silly, surprising picture. So people use committing sudoku to mean 'making a big, embarrassing mistake' in a playful way.
