Etymology

German pancake likely comes from German-speaking cooks who baked a thin batter in a hot oven so it 'puffed up' into a big, bowl-like shape. That clear image — a pancake that rises in the oven and then falls — explains why the name means the thick, puffy oven-baked pancake served right away.
