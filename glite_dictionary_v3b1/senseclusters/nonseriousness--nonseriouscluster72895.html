Etymology

nonseriousness comes from the parts non- meaning 'not', serious meaning 'important' or 'dangerous', and -ness which makes a 'quality' or 'state'. So nonseriousness is the 'not dangerous' or 'not severe' quality of something, as when a doctor says an injury is mild.
