Etymology

immunosuppressive drug comes from the parts immune, meaning the body's defenders, and suppressive, meaning 'to calm or reduce'. Imagine a medicine telling the defenders to stand down so they do not 'attack' a new organ or the body's own tissues, and so the phrase means a drug that reduces the immune response.
