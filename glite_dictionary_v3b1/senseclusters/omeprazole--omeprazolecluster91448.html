Etymology

omeprazole is the name of a medicine that lowers 'stomach acid' and helps with heartburn and ulcers. A helpful way to remember it is that many drugs that reduce acid share the ending -prazole, as in lansoprazole and pantoprazole, so -prazole marks this type of stomach medicine and the first part ome- simply makes the name unique.
