Etymology

sleep divorce comes from a simple image: it joins sleep and divorce to show partners who choose to sleep apart, like a small 'divorce' only about bedtime. So, it means an arrangement where a couple 'sleep in different beds or rooms' to get better rest and keep their relationship healthy.
