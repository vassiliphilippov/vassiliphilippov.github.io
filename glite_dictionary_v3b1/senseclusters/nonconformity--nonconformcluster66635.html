Etymology

nonconformity is made of non- 'not' and conformity, where con- means 'together' and form means 'shape', so it means 'not fitting the same shape'. Think of a person like a puzzle piece who refuses to fit into the group's shape, and you see why nonconformity means refusing to follow social rules or customs.
