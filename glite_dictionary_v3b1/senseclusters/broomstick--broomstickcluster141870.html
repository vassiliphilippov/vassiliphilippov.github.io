Etymology

The word broomstick comes from broom + stick, meaning a 'stick with a brush' used for sweeping. Because people made brooms from the branches of the broom bush, the same long stick became the image of witches 'flying' in stories, which is why we use the word for both the cleaning tool and the magical ride.
