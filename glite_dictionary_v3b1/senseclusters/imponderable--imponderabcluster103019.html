Etymology

The word imponderable comes from im- 'not' and ponderare 'to weigh', the same root in English ponder which means to think or 'weigh' ideas. So an imponderable as a noun is something you cannot 'weigh' or measure, and that is why it means something you cannot predict or figure out.
