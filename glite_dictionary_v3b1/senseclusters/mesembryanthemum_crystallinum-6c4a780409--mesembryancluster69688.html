Etymology

Mesembryanthemum crystallinum gets its name from its shiny leaves: the second word crystallinum means 'crystal-like' because the thick leaves are covered in tiny clear bumps that look like ice or glass. That's why the name helps you remember it as the plant with sparkling, frosty-looking leaves.
