Etymology

Mastigomycotina comes from Greek parts mastigo- meaning 'whip' and myco- meaning 'fungus', with -tina showing a 'group'. The 'whip' picture is a good memory: these fungi had whip-like tails on their moving spores and sex cells, so the name described that former group of fungi.
