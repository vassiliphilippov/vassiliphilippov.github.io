Etymology

attrite comes from an old Latin verb made of ad- 'to' and terere 'to rub', giving the picture of someone or something slowly worn down by constant pressure, and the idea is also in attrition. So, to attrite people or resources is to 'wear them down' over time until they lose strength or leave.
