Etymology

Archness comes from the adjective arch, which originally meant 'chief' or 'extreme', and you can see the same arch in archenemy and archbishop. Over time people used arch to mean 'playfully sly', as in an 'arch smile', so archness is the quality of being 'teasing and mischievous'.
