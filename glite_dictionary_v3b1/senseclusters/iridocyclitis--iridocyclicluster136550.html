Etymology

The word iridocyclitis joins three parts: irido- from iris meaning the 'colored part' of the eye, cycl- from a Greek word for 'circle' as in the English word cycle meaning the circular area near the lens, and -itis meaning 'inflammation'. So iridocyclitis means inflammation of the iris and that circular part of the eye, which is why it causes pain and problems with vision.
