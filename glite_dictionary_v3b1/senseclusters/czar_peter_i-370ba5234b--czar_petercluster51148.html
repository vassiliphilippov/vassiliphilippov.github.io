Etymology

Czar Peter I is often called 'Peter the Great' because he made very big changes to modernize Russia and bring it closer to Western Europe. He even cut his beard, wore European clothes and built the new city of St. Petersburg as a 'window to Europe', so his name now makes people think of a ruler who brings big, modern changes.
