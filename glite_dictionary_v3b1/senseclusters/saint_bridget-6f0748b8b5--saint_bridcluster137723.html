Etymology

Saint Bridget comes from an Irish holy woman, Brigid of Kildare, who lived around the fifth century and was famous for helping the poor and leading a community of women. One popular story says she made a small cross of reeds to protect people's homes, and because of deeds like that people remember her with churches, windows, and a feast day on February 1st as a sign of 'care' and 'protection'.
