Etymology

histiocytic leukemia comes from the parts histiocytic, from histiocytes that are cells living in body 'tissue', and leukemia, which means 'blood cancer'. So it names a disease where these tissue cells show up too often in the blood, which is why it means 'a cancer of certain immune cells in the blood'.
