Etymology

family Caesalpiniaceae is named after the Italian plant scientist Andrea Cesalpino, who long ago grouped certain trees, shrubs and herbs under his name; imagine him writing a book and labelling a group 'Caesalpiniaceae'. So today the phrase means 'a family or group of related plants' named for that scientist.
