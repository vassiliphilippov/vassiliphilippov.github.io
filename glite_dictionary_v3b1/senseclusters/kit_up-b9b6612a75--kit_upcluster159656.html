Etymology

The phrasal verb kit up comes from the word kit, meaning a set of clothes or tools, and the idea of putting that set 'up' on someone. Imagine a worker or soldier taking their kit and putting it on to get ready, so kit up means 'to equip or dress someone with the necessary gear'.
