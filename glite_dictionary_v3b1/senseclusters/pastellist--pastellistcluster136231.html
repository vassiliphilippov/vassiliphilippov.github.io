Etymology

pastellist comes from pastel, a 'soft colour stick' used for drawing, plus the ending -ist, which means 'a person who does something' like in artist. The word pastel itself comes from a word for 'paste' because the colour was mixed into a soft form, so a pastellist is simply an artist who makes pictures with those soft colour sticks.
