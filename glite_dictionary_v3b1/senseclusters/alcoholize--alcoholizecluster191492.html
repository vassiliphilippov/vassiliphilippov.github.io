Etymology

The word alcoholize comes from alcohol, which comes from the Arabic word 'al-kuhl' meaning 'essence' or 'spirit', and the ending -ize meaning 'to make'. So, to alcoholize food is to 'make it alcoholic', for example by soaking cherries in brandy.
