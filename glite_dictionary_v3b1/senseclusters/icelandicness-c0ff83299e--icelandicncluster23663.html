Etymology

Icelandicness comes from Iceland ('land of ice') plus the parts -ic ('from') and -ness ('state or quality'), and the last part is the same as in the familiar word kindness. So, Icelandicness means the 'state of being Icelandic' or the special qualities of Iceland and its people.
