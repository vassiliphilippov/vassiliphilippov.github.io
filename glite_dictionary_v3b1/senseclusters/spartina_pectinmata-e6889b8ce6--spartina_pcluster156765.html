Etymology

Spartina pectinmata may come from older words that create a picture: Spartina for 'rope' and pectinmata for 'comb-like teeth'. So imagine a strong marsh grass that looks like rope and has comb-like, toothy leaf edges and long bristles, and that image helps explain why the name fits this cordgrass species.
