Etymology

tenorist comes from tenor, the name for a lower, singing voice and the tenor saxophone, plus the ending -ist, which means 'one who does'. A helpful way to remember it is to think of the tenor sax as the sax with a deep, singing sound, so a tenorist is someone who plays that instrument.
