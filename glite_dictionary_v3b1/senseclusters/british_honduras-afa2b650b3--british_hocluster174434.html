Etymology

British Honduras comes from a simple picture: Spanish sailors called the coast Honduras because the sea there was very deep ('deep water'), and when Britain ruled the area they added British to show it was their colony. That's why British Honduras was the 'former name' of the country now called Belize until 1981.
