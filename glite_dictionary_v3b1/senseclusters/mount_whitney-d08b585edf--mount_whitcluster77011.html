Etymology

The name Mount Whitney comes from an 1800s scientist, Josiah Whitney, who explored and studied California's rocks; the word Mount means 'mountain' and Whitney is his family name, so people used his name for the peak. That's why Mount Whitney now means 'the tallest mountain' in the Sierra Nevada.
