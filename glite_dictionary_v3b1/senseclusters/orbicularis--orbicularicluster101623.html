Etymology

orbicularis comes from the Latin root orbis 'circle' and the little form orbiculus 'little circle', and it is related to everyday words like orb and orbit. So orbicularis as an adjective means 'round' or 'ring-shaped', which is why it describes muscles or structures that make a circular band around the eye or mouth.
