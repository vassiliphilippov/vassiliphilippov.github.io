Etymology

Chatter mark comes from the picture of something that chatters — it shakes or knocks quickly — leaving a small mark each time, like a glacier with bits of rock or a vibrating tool that makes a row of dents. That's why the term means 'a groove or dent left by repeated shaking or hits'.
