Etymology

Saint Dominic was a Spanish priest who lived around 1170–1221 and who started the Dominican Order, a group of preachers who went out to teach people. Because he was the 'founder' of that order and became famous for teaching, many churches and places were later named after him, so the name now refers to that priest and to those places.
