Etymology

Arthur Wellesley is the name of the British general also called the 'Duke of Wellington'; he led the army that beat Napoleon at the Battle of Waterloo and later became 'Prime Minister'. Think of him in his uniform on the battlefield to remember that the name refers to this famous military and political leader.
