Etymology

neostigmine is made of neo- meaning 'new' and -stigmine, a name taken from the older drug physostigmine, which was originally found in a plant. So neostigmine is the 'new' drug in that family, a man-made medicine used to boost nerve signals and help with muscle weakness and some eye problems.
