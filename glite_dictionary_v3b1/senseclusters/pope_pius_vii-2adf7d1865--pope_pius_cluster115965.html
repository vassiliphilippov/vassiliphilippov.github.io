Etymology

Pope Pius VII was an Italian pope from 1800 to 1823. One famous story is that Napoleon once took him prisoner and later forced him to go to Paris to 'crown' Napoleon as Emperor, and Pius also made an important agreement with Napoleon to restore the Church in France, so today his name is linked with both his capture and that deal.
