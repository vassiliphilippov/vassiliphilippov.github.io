Etymology

Sanitary landfill comes from the idea of making an old 'dump' more clean and safe: people would fill low land with trash (the word landfill is literal) and then cover it with soil so it would smell less and attract fewer pests, and the word sanitary showed it was meant to be clean. That's why sanitary landfill means 'a place where waste is buried and covered to reduce smell and pollution'.
