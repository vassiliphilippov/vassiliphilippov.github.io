Etymology

The word fanboyism breaks into three parts: fan ('a person who loves something'), boy ('a young, very excited person'), and -ism ('a way of thinking' or 'habit'). So, fanboyism means the habit of loving something so much that you ignore its faults and defend it without thinking.
