Etymology

acoustic modem comes from its parts: acoustic means 'sound' and modem is a device that sends data, because the unit turned digital information into real sounds. People used to put a phone handset into the modem and hear little beeps that travelled down the line, so the name explains its job of sending data over a phone as sound.
