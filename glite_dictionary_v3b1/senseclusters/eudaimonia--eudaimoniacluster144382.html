Etymology

eudaimonia comes from the Greek parts eu- meaning 'good' and daimon meaning 'spirit' or 'inner guide'. A helpful way to remember it is to think of a 'good spirit' inside you guiding you to live well, which is why eudaimonia means deep happiness and a full life where you reach your potential.
