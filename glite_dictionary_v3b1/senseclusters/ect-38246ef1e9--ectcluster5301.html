Etymology

ECT stands for electroconvulsive therapy, built from electro- ('electricity') and -therapy ('treatment'), and it was once called electroshock. That's a clear memory hook: the name literally describes a medical treatment that uses a small electric 'shock' to the brain to help some people with very severe depression.
