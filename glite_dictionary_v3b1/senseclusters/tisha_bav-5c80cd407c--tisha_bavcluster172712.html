Etymology

Tisha B'Av means 'Ninth of Av' and comes from the date on the Jewish calendar when several great tragedies happened, most importantly the destruction of the Jerusalem temples. The name shows this: Tisha is 'nine' and Av is the month, so people fast and mourn on Tisha B'Av today as a day of sorrow and remembrance.
