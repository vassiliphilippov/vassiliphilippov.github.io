Etymology

chocolate-colored begins with chocolate, the dark sweet food whose name came from a Mexican word for a cocoa drink, and the ending -colored means 'having the color of'. So chocolate-colored simply means 'the color of dark chocolate', a deep warm brown you can remember by picturing a dark chocolate bar.
