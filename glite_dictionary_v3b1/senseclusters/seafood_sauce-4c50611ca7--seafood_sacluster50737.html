Etymology

seafood sauce is a simple name made by joining seafood and sauce: cooks mixed tangy ingredients like ketchup, horseradish and lemon to make a sauce that would 'go with' fish and shellfish. That's why the phrase means 'a tangy sauce served with seafood'.
