Etymology

The word laryngotracheobronchitis is built from parts that name three airway areas plus the ending -itis: laryngo- 'voice box', tracheo- 'windpipe', broncho- 'air tubes', and -itis 'inflammation'. So it literally means inflammation of those airways—much like a mix of laryngitis and bronchitis—which explains why it causes breathing problems.
