Etymology

tritanopia comes from parts that mean 'three' and 'vision' — tri- ('three', as in tricycle) and -opia ('vision', as in myopia). It is named for the 'third' color sense (the blue one), so it describes the rare color blindness where people have trouble telling blue from yellow.
