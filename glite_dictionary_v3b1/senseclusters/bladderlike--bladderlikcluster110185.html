Etymology

bladderlike comes from bladder, a common word for a 'pouch' or 'bag' that holds liquid, and the ending -like, which means 'similar to'. So something bladderlike looks or works like a small bag of liquid, for example a plant part that stores water or a pocket that helps an animal float.
