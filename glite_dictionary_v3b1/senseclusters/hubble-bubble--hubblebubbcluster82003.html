Etymology

hubble-bubble is a playful English name for the water pipe also called a hookah; the word copies the 'bubbling' sound the smoke makes as it passes through the water, just like the common word bubble. So, to remember hubble-bubble, think of the soft 'hubble' or 'bubble' noise the pipe makes when people smoke together.
