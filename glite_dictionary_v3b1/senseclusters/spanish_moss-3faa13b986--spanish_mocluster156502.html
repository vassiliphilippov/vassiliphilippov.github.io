Etymology

Spanish moss may come from a simple image: early settlers saw its long, grey, hair-like strands hanging from trees and thought they looked like the long hair or beards of Spanish priests, so they called it Spanish and, since it grows on trees, moss. That's why Spanish moss today means the grey, hanging plant.
