Etymology

The term exocrine gland comes from Greek parts exo meaning 'outside' and crine meaning 'to release'. This picture helps: exocrine glands send their liquids through a small tube called a duct to an outside or inner surface of the body, so they 'pour out' their substances rather than putting them into the blood.
