Etymology

The word wiggly comes from the verb wiggle, a word that copies tiny, quick movements and means 'to move with small twists or by squirming'. Adding -y means 'full of', so wiggly is 'full of wiggles' and that is why we use it for things that bend, twist, or squirm.
