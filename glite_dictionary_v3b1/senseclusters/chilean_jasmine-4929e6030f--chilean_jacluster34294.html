Etymology

Chilean jasmine may come from gardeners noticing two things: Chilean points to where the vine came from and jasmine describes its flowers because they smell like 'true jasmine'. So the name tells you it is a South American vine grown for its pretty flowers and sweet scent, not a 'true jasmine'.
