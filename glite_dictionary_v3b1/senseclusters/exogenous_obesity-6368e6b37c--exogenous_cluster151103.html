Etymology

The noun exogenous obesity comes from exogenous meaning 'coming from outside' and obesity meaning 'being very overweight'. It paints a simple image of extra weight that comes from outside things like too much food and lifestyle choices, so it means weight gain caused mainly by outside factors rather than internal body problems.
