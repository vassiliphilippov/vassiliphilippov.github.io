Etymology

cyclostyle comes from Greek parts cyclo- meaning 'circle' (like cycle) and -style from stylus meaning 'point' or 'pen'. It was named for the small round cutting wheel and pointed tool that make holes in a stencil, so a cyclostyle is a device used to make copies.
