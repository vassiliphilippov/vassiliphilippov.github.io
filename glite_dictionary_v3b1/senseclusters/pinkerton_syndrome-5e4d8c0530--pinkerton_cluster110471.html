Etymology

Pinkerton syndrome may come from the old Pinkerton detective agency, whose agents watched people closely and looked for signs of betrayal. So the word syndrome is used to mean a condition where someone feels too much 'suspicion' or 'distrust' and finds it hard to trust others after bad experiences.
