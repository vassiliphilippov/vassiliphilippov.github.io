Etymology

Fritillaria lanceolata comes from Latin words that describe how the plant looks. The first word Fritillaria may come from 'fritillus' meaning 'dice box' because many species have a checkered, bell-shaped flower, and the second word lanceolata means 'spear-shaped' for its long narrow leaves, so the name helps you picture a checkered bell flower with lance-like leaves.
