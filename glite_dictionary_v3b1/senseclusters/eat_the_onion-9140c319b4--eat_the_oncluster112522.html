Etymology

The idiom eat the onion comes from the image of someone forced to eat a strong, bitter onion, which makes them cry but gets the job done. People used that picture to show you must accept something unpleasant instead of avoiding it. So, eat the onion means 'to face a difficult or unpleasant task directly'.
