Etymology

acetify comes from the root acet-, from Latin acetum, meaning 'vinegar' or 'sour', plus the ending -ify, meaning 'to make'. So, to acetify something is to turn it into 'vinegar' or make it 'sour', and that is why it means 'to make or become acidic'.
