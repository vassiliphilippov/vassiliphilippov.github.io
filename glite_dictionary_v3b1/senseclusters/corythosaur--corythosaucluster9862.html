Etymology

corythosaur comes from Greek parts corytho- meaning 'helmet' or 'crest' and -saur meaning 'lizard', the same -saur you see in dinosaur. So a corythosaur is like a 'helmet-lizard', which fits the duck-billed dinosaur with a large, helmet-like crest on its head.
