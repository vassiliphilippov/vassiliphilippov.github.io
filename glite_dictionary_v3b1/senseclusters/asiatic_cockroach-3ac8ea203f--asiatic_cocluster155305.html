Etymology

Asiatic cockroach comes from a simple idea: Asiatic means 'from Asia' and cockroach is the insect, so the name describes a cockroach that came from Asia. That's why the term now refers to the dark brown cockroach species originally 'from Asia' that is found in many parts of the world.
