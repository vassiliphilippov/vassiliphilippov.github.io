Etymology

Capparis mitchellii may come from the caper plant group (Capparis) and from the explorer Thomas Mitchell (mitchellii). Its dark purple fruit looks like a small orange, so people call it the 'wild orange', which helps you remember it is a small Australian tree with edible fruit.
