Etymology

academism comes from academy, which goes back to the Greek place name Akademos, a 'school' where Plato taught. It was used for the way art was taught in those schools—safe, traditional painting and sculpture that follow set rules—so in art academism means a formal, rule-bound style that some people see as lacking new ideas.
