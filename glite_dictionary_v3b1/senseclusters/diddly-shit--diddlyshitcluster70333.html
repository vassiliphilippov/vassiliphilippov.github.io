Etymology

diddly-shit comes from slang where diddly means 'nothing' or 'very small' and appears in phrases like diddly-squat, while the -shit part is a rude intensifier. So diddly-shit is a coarse way to say 'nothing at all' or a 'very tiny amount', often used to show strong dislike or disappointment.
