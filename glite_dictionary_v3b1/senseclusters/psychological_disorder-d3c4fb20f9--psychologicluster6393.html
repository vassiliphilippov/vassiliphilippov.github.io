Etymology

The term psychological disorder comes from parts that mean the 'mind' and 'not in order': psychological relates to the 'mind' and disorder suggests things are 'not in order', so the phrase paints the picture of the mind being out of its usual order and is used for conditions that disturb a person's thinking, feeling, or behavior.
