Etymology

Schumpeterian as a noun names a person who follows the ideas of Schumpeter, someone who believes entrepreneurs and new ideas are the main force of economic change, often summed up as 'creative destruction'. So a Schumpeterian supports innovation and sees new businesses as the key to growth.
