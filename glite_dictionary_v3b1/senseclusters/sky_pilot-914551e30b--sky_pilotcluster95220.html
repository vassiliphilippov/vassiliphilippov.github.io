Etymology

sky pilot may come from a simple image: a pilot guides you and the sky stands for heaven, so soldiers called their 'chaplain' a sky pilot because he helped guide their souls. That's why sky pilot now means a 'chaplain' who gives comfort and spiritual help to soldiers.
