Etymology

antiarrhythmic drug is made from anti- (meaning 'against') and arrhythmic (meaning 'no rhythm' or 'irregular beat'). Imagine a conductor helping a band play in time; the drug helps the heart keep a steady beat, so it means 'a drug that prevents or treats irregular heart rhythms'.
