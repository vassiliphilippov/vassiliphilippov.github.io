Etymology

Albizia saman is a scientific name made from a person's name and a local word. The genus Albizia is named after an Italian who brought similar trees to Europe. The word saman was used by local people for this wide, umbrella-like tree that became the 'rain tree' because its leaves fold and the canopy lets rain fall through.
