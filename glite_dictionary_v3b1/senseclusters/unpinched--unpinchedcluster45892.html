Etymology

Unpinched comes from the prefix un- meaning 'not' and the word pinch meaning 'to squeeze', so it literally means 'not squeezed'. So, think of shoes that do not squeeze your toes or a relaxed face with no tight lines; unpinched describes something free from tightness or tension.
