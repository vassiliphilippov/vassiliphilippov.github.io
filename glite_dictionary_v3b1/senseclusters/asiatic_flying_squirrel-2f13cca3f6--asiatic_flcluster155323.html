Etymology

The noun Asiatic flying squirrel names a small animal from Asia: Asiatic means from Asia, flying shows how it moves, and squirrel tells you it is a tree rodent. It has a stretch of skin between its legs that acts like a wing so it can glide from tree to tree, and that is why people call it 'flying' even though it does not truly fly.
