Etymology

Psychotic belief comes from doctors seeing that sometimes a person's mind makes a very strong idea that is 'not true'. Because psychotic points to the mind being out of touch with reality and belief is a held idea, the phrase means a firm idea someone keeps even when shown evidence it is 'not true'.
