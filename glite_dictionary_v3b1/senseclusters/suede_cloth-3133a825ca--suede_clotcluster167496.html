Etymology

suede cloth: The name suede cloth comes from suede, a word that one popular story says comes from the French for 'Sweden', where soft leather for gloves once came from. Because this fabric was made to look and feel like that soft leather, suede cloth now means 'a soft fabric that looks like suede leather'.
