Etymology

photoconductive cell comes from three parts: photo- meaning 'light', conductive meaning 'lets electricity flow', and cell meaning a small device. Imagine a tiny device that lets electricity flow more when 'light' hits it, so it is used to 'detect' and 'measure' light.
