Etymology

leitmotiv comes from German and literally means 'leading motive', with leit- from a word for 'to lead' and -motiv meaning 'motive' or a short musical idea. So a leitmotiv is a short musical phrase that 'leads' you to a particular person, idea, or situation in a piece of music.
