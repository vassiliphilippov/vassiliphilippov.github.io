Etymology

teach a lesson comes from the simple picture of a teacher who teaches a lesson to a student; a sharp punishment or hard experience acts like that teacher and 'teaches a lesson' to someone. That's why it means 'to give someone an experience that changes their behavior or teaches something important'.
