Etymology

mousetrap in football borrows the image of a mouse and a trap: a defender is lured into a spot and then blocked, leaving a clear path for the runner. A helpful way to remember it is to think of baiting and then trapping, so it means a play that tricks a defender and opens a lane for the offense.
