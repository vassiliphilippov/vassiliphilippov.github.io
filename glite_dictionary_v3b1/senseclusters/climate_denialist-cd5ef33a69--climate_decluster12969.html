Etymology

climate denialist combines climate, meaning the Earth's weather over many years, and denialist, meaning someone who says 'no' to clear evidence. Imagine a person waving away scientists' charts and saying 'no' — that's why the term means 'a person who refuses to accept evidence from scientists that the Earth is getting warmer'.
