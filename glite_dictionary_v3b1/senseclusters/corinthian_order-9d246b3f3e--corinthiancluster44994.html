Etymology

Corinthian order may come from the city of Corinth and a simple story about a basket of acanthus leaves. One popular story says a sculptor saw the leaves growing around a basket on a girl's grave and copied them for the tops of columns, so today Corinthian order means tall, slender columns with carved leaves used to make buildings look 'grand'.
