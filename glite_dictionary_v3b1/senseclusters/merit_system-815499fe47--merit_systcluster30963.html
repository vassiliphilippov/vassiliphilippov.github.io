Etymology

merit system comes from the idea of testing a person's merit (their skill or worth) and then following an organized system of rules to pick people for jobs. Long ago, governments often chose friends or supporters, but they began using tests and rules to choose workers, so today a merit system means hiring or promoting people 'based on ability' not on connections.
