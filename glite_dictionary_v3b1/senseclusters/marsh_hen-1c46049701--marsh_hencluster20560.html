Etymology

marsh hen comes from the two words marsh, a wet place with tall grass, and hen, a farm chicken. People used the word 'hen' for small, chicken-like birds that live in marshes, so the name paints a picture of a small bird in the reeds and now means 'a small marsh-dwelling bird'.
