Etymology

Armillaria comes from the Latin root armilla, which means 'bracelet' or 'ring'. Many of these mushrooms have a ring or band on their stem, so the name is like a 'bracelet' and helps you remember this group of mushrooms that can be eaten or can cause root rot in trees.
