Etymology

Weeder comes from the simple word weed ('a plant you do not want') plus the ending -er that means 'one who does' or 'a thing that does', like baker or runner. So a weeder is the tool that 'weeds' your garden, which is why the word refers to a hand tool for taking out unwanted plants.
