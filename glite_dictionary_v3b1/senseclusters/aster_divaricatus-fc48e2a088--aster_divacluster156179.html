Etymology

Aster divaricatus comes from old Greek and Latin words that describe the plant. Aster means 'star' for its white, star-shaped flowers and divaricatus means 'spreading' for its wide, branching stems, so the name helps you picture a spreading, star-flowered aster in the woods.
