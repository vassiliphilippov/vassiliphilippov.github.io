Etymology

The word packcloth is simply pack + cloth, with pack meaning 'to carry' and cloth meaning 'fabric'. Think of the cloth used for a backpack or a pack animal: it had to be very strong, so packcloth came to mean a heavy, coarse fabric used for making bags, tents, or covering goods.
