Etymology

yarmelke comes from Yiddish and Polish words like yarmulka that meant a small 'cap' for the head. A popular story links the word to a Hebrew phrase meaning 'fear of the king', which helps explain why this small round skullcap is worn during prayer as a sign of respect.
