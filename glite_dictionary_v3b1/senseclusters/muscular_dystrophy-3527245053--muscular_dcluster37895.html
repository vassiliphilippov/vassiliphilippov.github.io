Etymology

muscular dystrophy is a medical name made of muscular (about the body's muscles) and dystrophy (a word that means 'poor growth' or 'not getting enough nourishment'). So the name creates the image of muscles that slowly become 'weak' and 'waste away', which is why it refers to diseases that cause gradual muscle loss.
