Etymology

Kingdom of Thailand comes from the words kingdom, meaning a country ruled by a king, and Thailand, meaning 'land of the Thai people' or often 'land of the free'. The country used to be called 'Siam' until 1939, so the modern name shows both the nation and its king, and that's why it is the official name today.
