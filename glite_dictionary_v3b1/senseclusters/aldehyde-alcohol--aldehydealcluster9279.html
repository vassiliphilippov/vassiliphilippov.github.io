Etymology

aldehyde-alcohol is simply made from two parts: aldehyde, the same root you see in formaldehyde that names a reactive chemical group, and alcohol, the familiar word for molecules with an 'OH' group. So aldehyde-alcohol means 'a molecule that has both an aldehyde and an alcohol part', which explains why it is useful in making other compounds and must be stored with care.
