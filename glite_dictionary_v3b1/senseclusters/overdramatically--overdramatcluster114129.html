Etymology

The word overdramatically is made of over- meaning 'too much' and dramatic, from drama meaning 'a play' or 'a big show'. So, to act overdramatically is to show 'too much' feeling, like someone acting too much on stage, which is why it means being more dramatic or exaggerated than needed.
