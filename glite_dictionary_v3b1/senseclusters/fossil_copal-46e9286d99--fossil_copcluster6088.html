Etymology

fossil copal comes from fossil + copal and gives a clear picture: copal is tree resin and fossil shows it is old and buried, so it is resin that has partly hardened underground. That's why fossil copal means an in-between stage between 'resin' and 'amber', and why it is often used as cheaper jewelry material.
