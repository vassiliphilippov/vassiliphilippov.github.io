Etymology

action mechanism comes from two simple ideas: action (a movement) and mechanism (the parts inside a machine). Imagine a piano key that moves tiny parts to hit a string — those parts are the action mechanism, the pieces that make movement happen, so the term means 'the machine parts that pass on power and start movement'.
