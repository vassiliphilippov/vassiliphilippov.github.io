Etymology

Lathyrus tuberosus comes from old Latin words: Lathyrus was used for plants like peas and tuberosus means 'having tubers', small potato-like roots. People in parts of Europe ate these little tubers and even used the roots to flavour whiskey, so the name points to the plant's edible tubers.
