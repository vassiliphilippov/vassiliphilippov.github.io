Etymology

Prince Albert yew is a plant name that probably honors Prince Albert, the husband of Queen Victoria, who helped make garden plants popular. The word yew names the type of tree, so the full name links that person to a small, attractive 'ornamental' tree with partly 'weeping' branches that gardeners like.
