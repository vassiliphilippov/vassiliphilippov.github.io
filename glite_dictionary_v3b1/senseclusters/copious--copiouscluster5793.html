Etymology

copious comes from the Latin word copia, meaning 'plenty' or 'abundance', and you can see the same idea in cornucopia, the 'horn of plenty' that pours out food. So, think of something spilling out in large amounts to remember that copious means 'large in amount' or 'abundant'.
