Etymology

The expression blunt instrument comes from the picture of a tool that is not sharp: a bluntinstrument that hits instead of making fine cuts, like a hammer rather than a scalpel. So we call a law or plan a blunt instrument when it is a rough, forceful way to solve a problem and is 'not precise'.
