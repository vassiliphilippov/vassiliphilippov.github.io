Etymology

dawdler comes from the old verb dawdle, which probably comes from a short old word dawd meaning 'to be slow' or 'lazy'. A helpful way to remember dawdler is to picture someone 'dragging their feet' or wasting time, because that is exactly what a dawdler does.
