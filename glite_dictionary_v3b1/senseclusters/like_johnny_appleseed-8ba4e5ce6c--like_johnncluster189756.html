Etymology

The phrase like Johnny Appleseed comes from an American folk hero, Johnny Appleseed, who walked across the countryside planting apple seeds and trees. Because he left trees and help in many places, calling someone like Johnny Appleseed means they 'plant trees' or 'do good deeds' in lots of places.
