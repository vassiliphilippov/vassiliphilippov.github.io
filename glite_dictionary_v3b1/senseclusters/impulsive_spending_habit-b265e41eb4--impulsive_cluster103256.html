Etymology

The phrase impulsive spending habit comes from a simple picture: impulsive is like a sudden push to buy, spending is using money, and habit is something you do again and again. Picture someone who sees a shiny item and immediately reaches for their wallet many times, and you can see why it means 'a habit of spending money without thinking'.
