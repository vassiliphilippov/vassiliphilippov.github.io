Etymology

lesser peritoneal cavity literally names a small space behind the stomach. It combines lesser (smaller), peritoneal (from the 'peritoneum', the thin lining of the belly) and cavity ('a hollow or space'), so the phrase refers to that specific small area behind the stomach.
