Etymology

The word shebeen comes from the Irish word síbín, which meant an 'illegal or home-made drink' and the small houses where people drank it. So a shebeen today is an unlicensed or informal bar, a hidden place to drink and meet, a meaning that moved from Ireland to places like South Africa.
