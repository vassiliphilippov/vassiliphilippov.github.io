Etymology

Scombroidea comes from an old Greek word skombros, meaning 'mackerel' or 'tuna', and the last part -oidea means 'like'. So Scombroidea is the 'mackerel-like' group of sea fish, which is why it includes mackerels, tunas, bonitos, and other fast swimmers.
