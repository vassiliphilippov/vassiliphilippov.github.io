Etymology

The word impressive comes from the verb impress, made of im- and press, and press comes from an old Latin root pressare meaning 'to press'. Think of something that 'presses' into your mind or feelings so strongly you notice it, because that pressure is what makes something impressive and worthy of admiration or respect.
