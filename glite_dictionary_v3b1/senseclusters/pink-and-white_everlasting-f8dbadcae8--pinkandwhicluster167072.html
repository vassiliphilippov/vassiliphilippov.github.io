Etymology

The flower name pink-and-white everlasting comes from the plant's thin, papery pink-and-white petals that keep their color when dried. Because the petals last a very long time, people called it everlasting, meaning 'long-lasting', so the name tells us it is a flower that stays beautiful for a long time.
