Etymology

corn tash comes from the American South and names a traditional country 'dish'. People made bread from corn, wrapped it in cabbage leaves and baked it in hot ashes; the wrapped loaf was called a tash. That strong image of smoky, wrapped 'bread' explains why the name means this food today.
