Etymology

cacodyl group comes from cacodyl, an old arsenic substance famous for its terrible smell, and group here means a small part of a chemical. So the cacodyl group is the little arsenic-containing piece of a chemical, which is why chemists use the term to talk about some arsenic compounds.
