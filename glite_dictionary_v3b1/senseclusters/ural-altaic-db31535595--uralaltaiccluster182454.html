Etymology

Ural-Altaic comes from two place names, Ural and Altaic, which point to the Ural Mountains and the Altai region, so the name was used for languages from those areas. It was a label for the idea that many indigenous languages of Russia and Central Asia belonged together, but today most experts reject that grouping, so the name mainly shows a geographic link rather than a settled language family.
