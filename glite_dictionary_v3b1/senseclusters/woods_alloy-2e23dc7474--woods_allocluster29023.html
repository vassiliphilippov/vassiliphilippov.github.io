Etymology

The noun Wood's alloy is named after Barnabas Wood, a 19th-century dentist who found a metal mix that melts at a low temperature. Because it literally 'melts' easily, people used it for molds and safety fuses, so the name points to the inventor and to the alloy's low-melting quality.
