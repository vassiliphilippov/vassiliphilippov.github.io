Etymology

Cypripedium calceolus pubescens comes from old names that describe how the flower looks: its lower lip forms a pouch like a small shoe, so people call it the 'yellow lady's slipper'. The part Cypripedium points to the slipper shape and pubescens means 'hairy', a note about the plant's soft hairs.
