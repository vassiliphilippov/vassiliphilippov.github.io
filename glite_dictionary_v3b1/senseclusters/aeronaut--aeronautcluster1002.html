Etymology

The word aeronaut comes from the parts aero- meaning 'air' and -naut meaning 'sailor', the same '-naut' you see in astronaut, so it means an 'air sailor'. That's why an aeronaut is a person who sails through the sky, often by flying a balloon or airship.
