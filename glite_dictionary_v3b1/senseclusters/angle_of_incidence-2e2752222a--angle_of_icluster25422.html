Etymology

The term angle of incidence describes the angle between a ray, like a beam of light, and a line that stands straight out from the surface where the ray meets it. Imagine light hitting a mirror: the angle from the light to that straight-up line is the 'angle of incidence', and that incidence, the idea of where the ray 'meets' the surface, explains why light bounces a certain way or why a wing gives more lift.
