Etymology

ketosis-resistant diabetes is a medical name for a kind of diabetes that does not go into ketosis. Think of ketosis as when the body burns fat and makes 'ketones', and resistant shows it usually avoids that state, so the condition stays milder and can often be controlled by diet or pills rather than insulin.
