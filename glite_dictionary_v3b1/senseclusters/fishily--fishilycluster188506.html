Etymology

fishily comes from the adjective fishy, which grew from the common word fish plus the ending -y that makes something 'like' or 'full of'. At first fishy meant 'smelling like fish' or 'off', and because a strange fish smell often suggests something is wrong, fishily now means 'in a suspicious or doubtful way'.
