Etymology

Galbulidae comes from the old bird name Galbula, from Latin meaning 'yellow' or 'yellow-green', a reference to the bright colours of some jacamars. The ending -idae is the scientific way to mark a family of animals, like in Canidae for dogs, so Galbulidae simply means the family of jacamar birds.
