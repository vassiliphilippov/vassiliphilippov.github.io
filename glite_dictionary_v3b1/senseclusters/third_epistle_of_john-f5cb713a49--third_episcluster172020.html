Etymology

The Third Epistle of John is named like a short letter: Epistle means 'letter', Third shows it is the third one, and John is the writer. So the title simply means 'John's third letter' and refers to a short New Testament book thought to be written to a friend named Gaius about welcoming and helping visitors in the early church.
