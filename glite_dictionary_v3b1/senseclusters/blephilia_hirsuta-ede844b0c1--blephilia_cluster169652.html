Etymology

Blephilia hirsuta comes from older Greek and Latin words: Blephilia may come from a Greek word meaning 'eyelash' because parts of its flowers have tiny, hair-like fringes, and hirsuta is Latin for 'hairy'. So the name describes the plant's 'hairy' look and explains the common name 'hairy wood mint'.
