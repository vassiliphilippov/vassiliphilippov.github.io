Etymology

pobody's nerfect is a playful, joking, funny spelling of 'nobody's perfect', where pobody's stands for nobody's and nerfect stands for perfect. The joke is that the words are themselves 'imperfect', so the phrase gives a clear, funny picture that helps people accept small errors. That's why people say pobody's nerfect to mean 'it's okay to make a mistake'.
