Etymology

The word sycamore comes from the Greek sykomoros, made of syko- 'fig' and -moros 'mulberry', because the first sycamore known to Greeks was a kind of fig tree with fruit like a mulberry. That's why sycamore today names large trees with broad leaves and pale, patchy bark.
