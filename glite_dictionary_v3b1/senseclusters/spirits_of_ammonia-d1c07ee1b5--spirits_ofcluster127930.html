Etymology

spirits of ammonia comes from old medicine bottles: spirits meant a strong alcohol liquid and ammonia gave a very sharp smell. People would sniff a little to 'revive' someone who fainted, so the name now refers to a strong-smelling liquid used as a 'smelling salt' to wake or alert people.
