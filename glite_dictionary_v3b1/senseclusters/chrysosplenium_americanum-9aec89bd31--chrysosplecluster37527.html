Etymology

Chrysosplenium americanum may come from old Greek words: chrysos meaning 'gold' and splenium linked to the 'spleen', because people long ago used the little plant to help spleen illnesses. So the 'gold' part fits its small yellow flowers and helps you remember this yellow, water-loving herb.
