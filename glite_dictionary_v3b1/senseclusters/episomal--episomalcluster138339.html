Etymology

The word episomal comes from the Greek parts epi- meaning 'on' and soma meaning 'body', as in epidermis ('on the skin'). So episomal describes DNA that sits 'on' or apart from the cell's main chromosomes, which is why it means genetic material separate from chromosomal DNA.
