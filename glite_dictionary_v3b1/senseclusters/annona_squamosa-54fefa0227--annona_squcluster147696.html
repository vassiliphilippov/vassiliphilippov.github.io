Etymology

Annona squamosa may come from an old Caribbean word 'anón', which gives the Annona part, and from the Latin word 'squamosa', the squamosa part, meaning 'scaly'. So the name shows the sweet, creamy fruit inside a rough, scaly rind, which helps you remember this tropical tree.
