Etymology

frangipane probably comes from the Italian family name Frangipani; one popular story says a member of that family made a perfume used to 'scent' gloves, and later cooks used the family name for a sweet almond filling because of its pleasant smell. So frangipane today means a rich 'almond cream' used in tarts and pastries.
