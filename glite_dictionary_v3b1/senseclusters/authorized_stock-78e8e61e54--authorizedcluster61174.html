Etymology

authorized stock comes from the idea in a company's official papers that authorize or allow a fixed number of stock certificates. Imagine the company has a box of blank share 'tickets' and is told how many it may use, so authorized stock means the maximum number of shares a company is 'allowed by law' to issue.
