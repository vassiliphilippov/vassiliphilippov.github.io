Etymology

peacoat probably comes from a Dutch word pij that meant 'coarse wool', and sailors wore short jackets made from this cloth which they called a 'pea jacket'. So a peacoat is a heavy wool jacket originally worn by sailors, which is why it looks warm and strong today.
