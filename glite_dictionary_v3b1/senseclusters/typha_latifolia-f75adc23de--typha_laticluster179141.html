Etymology

Typha latifolia comes from Latin words that describe the plant: Typha points to the brown, stick-like seed head known as a 'cattail', and latifolia means 'broad leaves'. So the name paints a simple picture — a tall marsh plant with wide leaves and a fluffy brown head, which is why it fits this common 'cattail'.
