Etymology

wildcatter comes from wildcat, a word for an 'untamed' animal that came to mean a 'risky' venture, plus the ending -er. A wildcat well was a risky oil well drilled where no one knew there was oil, so a wildcatter is someone who drills for oil in places not known to have it.
