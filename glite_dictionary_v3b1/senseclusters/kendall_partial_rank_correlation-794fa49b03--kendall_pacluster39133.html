Etymology

Kendall partial rank correlation is named after a statistician called Kendall and joins rank (sorting items by order) with partial (taking away the effect of other things). So picture sorting two lists, taking away outside effects, and then seeing how the remaining ranks move together; that's why it means 'to measure the link between two variables while controlling for other variables'.
