Etymology

Oenothera fruticosa is a Latin plant name: Oenothera may come from an old Greek word for 'evening' because many relatives open at dusk, and fruticosa means 'bushy'. So the name gives a simple picture — a bushy primrose-like plant, which helps you remember this cheerful yellow flower that blooms in the day.
