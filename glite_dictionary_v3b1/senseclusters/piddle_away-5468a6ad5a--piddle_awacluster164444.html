Etymology

The phrasal verb piddle away comes from the idea of piddle, meaning small, unimportant actions or dribbling like tiny drops, plus away, showing something is used up. Imagine minutes leaking out in little bits while you do nothing useful; that's why piddle away means 'to waste time or resources'.
