Etymology

sutura lamboidea comes from Latin: sutura means 'seam' and lamboidea means 'like the letter lambda'. One popular idea is that the curved seam at the back of the skull looks like the Greek letter 'lambda', so the name points to the seam where the bones meet at the back of the skull.
