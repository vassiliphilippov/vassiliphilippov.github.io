Etymology

calciferol comes from the parts calci- meaning 'calcium' and -fer- meaning 'to carry', plus -ol as a common chemical ending. So the name suggests 'a substance that carries calcium', which helps explain why calciferol is a vitamin important for keeping bones strong.
