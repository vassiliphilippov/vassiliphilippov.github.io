Etymology

The word dimout comes from the simple parts dim ('not bright') and out ('off' or 'away'), so it literally means turning lights down or partly off. In wartime a dimout was a partial 'blackout' to make a city less visible to enemy planes, which is why the word now means reducing lights to hide a place.
