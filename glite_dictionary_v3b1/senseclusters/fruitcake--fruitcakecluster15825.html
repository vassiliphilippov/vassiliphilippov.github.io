Etymology

A helpful way to remember the informal use of fruitcake for a strange or crazy person is to think of the cake's mixed ingredients, many fruit and nuts, and the word nut, which people also use for someone who is 'crazy'. So calling someone a fruitcake means they behave in an odd or unusual way.
