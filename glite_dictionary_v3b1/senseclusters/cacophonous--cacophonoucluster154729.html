Etymology

The word cacophonous comes from Greek parts caco- meaning 'bad' and -phone meaning 'sound', the same phone in 'telephone' that helps you remember it is about sound. So cacophonous means 'bad sound', which is why we use it for harsh, unpleasant noise.
