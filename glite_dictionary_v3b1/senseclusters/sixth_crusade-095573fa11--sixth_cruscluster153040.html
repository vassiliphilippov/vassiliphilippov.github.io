Etymology

Sixth Crusade was the sixth of a series of Christian campaigns to the Holy Land. It is remembered because its leader, Emperor Frederick II, won control of Jerusalem by talking and making a deal in 1229 instead of fighting, so the name points to a rare crusade won by 'negotiation'.
