Etymology

bilateral symmetry comes from the simple idea of dividing a shape or animal down the middle so the two sides match like a mirror; bilateral means 'two sides' and symmetry means 'same on both sides'. That's why bilateral symmetry is used to say the 'left and right' parts are matching.
