Etymology

ketosis-resistant diabetes mellitus is a medical name that points to a type of diabetes that is resistant to ketosis, the state when the body makes many ketones (strong chemicals made when there is not enough insulin). So, the name helps you remember that this form is often milder and can usually be controlled with 'diet and pills' rather than 'insulin'.
