Etymology

Amen cadence comes from church singing: one popular story says choirs often held the word amen while music made a simple move that leads to the final chord, and people began to call that peaceful close an amen or closing cadence. So, the name explains why an amen cadence now means a calm musical 'ending' at the end of hymns.
