Etymology

mission impossible creates a strong image: a mission is an important task and impossible means it cannot be done, so the phrase feels like a job that will fail. Imagine a spy team given a task with no way to win, dangerous and very hard. That's why it means 'a task that is extremely difficult or almost impossible'.
