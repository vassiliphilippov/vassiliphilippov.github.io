Etymology

Tricholoma populinum comes from old words: Tricholoma means 'hairy edge' and populinum means 'of the poplar'. Picture a mushroom with a fringed cap growing under cottonwood trees, which is why the name fits this edible mushroom often found in clusters near those trees.
