Etymology

The noun Bosnian-Herzegovinian monetary unit is the official name for the country's money and comes from the idea of a 'convertible mark' — a 'mark' that could be changed into other money. Because the currency was tied to stronger money like the 'euro' to keep its value stable, the name stuck and today it means the basic unit of money used in Bosnia and Herzegovina.
