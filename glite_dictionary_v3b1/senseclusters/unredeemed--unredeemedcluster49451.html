Etymology

The word unredeemed comes from redeem, which originally meant 'buy back', from old Latin parts re- 'again' and emere 'to buy'. In everyday use, to redeem a coupon is to exchange it for its value, so unredeemed means 'not yet used' or 'not claimed'.
