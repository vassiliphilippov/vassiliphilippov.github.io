Etymology

Peter I is the title for Peter the Great and the I shows he was the 'first' ruler named Peter. One memorable story says he traveled to Europe in plain clothes and worked in shipyards to learn new skills, and he used those ideas to change Russia and build St. Petersburg, so Peter I is remembered as the czar who 'modernized' Russia.
