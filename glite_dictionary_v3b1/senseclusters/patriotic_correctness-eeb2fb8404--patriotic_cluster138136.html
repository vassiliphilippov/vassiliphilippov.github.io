Etymology

patriotic correctness comes from the idea of 'political correctness' and creates a clear image: imagine people being expected to show the right kind of patriotic behaviour, like wearing flags and praising the country, so they are 'correct' about national views. That's why it means 'following strict public rules about patriotism'.
