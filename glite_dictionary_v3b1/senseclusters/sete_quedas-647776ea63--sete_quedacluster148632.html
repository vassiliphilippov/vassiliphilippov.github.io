Etymology

Sete Quedas comes from the Portuguese words Sete and Quedas, which mean 'seven' and 'falls'. It named a line of seven large waterfalls on the Paraná River, and that clear picture explains why the name still refers to those famous falls even after they were covered by water when a dam was built.
