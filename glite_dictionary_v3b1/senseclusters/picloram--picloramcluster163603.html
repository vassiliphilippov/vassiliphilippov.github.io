Etymology

picloram is a name made from chemical parts: the picol- part comes from 'picolinic' (a simple acid used in chemistry) and the -ram ending is a short lab-style ending used in some product names. So picloram is the chemical name chemists gave to this weed killer, and today it means a strong herbicide used to control broadleaf weeds and woody plants.
