Etymology

paternity leave comes from copying maternity leave, where paternity means 'father' and leave means 'time off'. It paints the picture of a father taking time away from work when a baby arrives, so the phrase means 'a period of time that a father is allowed to be away from work after the birth or adoption of his child'.
