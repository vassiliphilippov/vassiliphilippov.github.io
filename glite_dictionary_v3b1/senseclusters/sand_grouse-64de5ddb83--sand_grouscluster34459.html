Etymology

sand grouse comes from the bird's home and its look: sand for the dry places it lives, and grouse because people thought it was like a 'pigeon-like' ground bird. These birds fly long distances to bring water to their chicks, so the name helps you remember it is a dry-land bird that looks and acts like a pigeon.
