Etymology

worriedly comes from worry, an old word that once meant 'to strangle' or 'to choke' and later came to mean 'to trouble' or make someone anxious, and the ending -ly turns it into a way of doing something. So, to speak worriedly is to speak in a way that shows you are troubled or anxious.
