Etymology

as sweet as a songbird may come from the clear, gentle singing of small birds. Imagine a tiny songbird making a pure, lovely tune. Calling someone as sweet as a songbird compares their voice or laugh to that pleasant sound, so it means 'very pleasant or musical'.
