Etymology

Celastrus scandens gets its name from Latin words that describe the plant: scandens means 'climbing', because the shrub climbs up trees and fences. Its yellow capsules open to show bright red seeds, a striking picture that led people to call it 'American bittersweet', so the name fits the plant you see today.
