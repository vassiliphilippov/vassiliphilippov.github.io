Etymology

ballistocardiograph comes from parts meaning 'throw' (ballisto-), 'heart' (cardio-), and 'write' (-graph). So it is a machine that 'writes down' the tiny 'pushes' the heart makes and the small movement of the body with each beat, which is why it measures the heart's force.
