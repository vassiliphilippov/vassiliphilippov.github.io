Etymology

Gnathostomata comes from Greek parts gnath- meaning 'jaw' and -stoma meaning 'mouth'. So Gnathostomata names the group of animals that have both upper and lower jaws and a mouth, like most fish, amphibians, reptiles, birds, and mammals.
