Etymology

Poecilocapsus comes from older roots: poecilo- meaning 'spotted' or 'varied' and -capsus a name part used for small bugs (you can see the same 'caps' in capsule, meaning 'box'). So, Poecilocapsus is easy to remember as a group of plant bugs that often have stripes or spots, like the four-lined plant bug.
