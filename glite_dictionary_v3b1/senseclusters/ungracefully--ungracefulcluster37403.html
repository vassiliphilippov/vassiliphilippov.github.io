Etymology

ungracefully is made of un- 'not', grace 'smooth, elegant movement', and -ly 'in this way'. Imagine a dancer who trips — that image helps you remember that ungracefully means moving 'not' in a smooth or elegant way, so it means awkwardly or clumsily.
