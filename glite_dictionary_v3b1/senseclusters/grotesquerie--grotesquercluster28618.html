Etymology

grotesquerie comes from grotesque, which itself comes from the Italian word grotto meaning 'cave', because artists once found strange, mixed figures painted in cave-like rooms. So, grotesquerie now means the quality of being oddly mixed or strangely shaped, which is why we use it for things that shock and fascinate.
