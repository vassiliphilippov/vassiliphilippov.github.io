Etymology

biohazard suit puts two ideas together: biohazard (a 'danger from living things') and suit (a 'full outfit' you wear). Imagine someone inside a sealed coverall or plastic bubble that keeps dangerous germs out, so the phrase means a protective garment you wear to stay safe from biological or chemical danger.
