Etymology

vibraphone comes from vibra- meaning 'to shake', as in vibrate, and -phone meaning 'sound', as in telephone. So the name fits: the instrument's metal bars and rotating discs make the sound 'shake' or wobble, giving its mellow, shimmering tone.
