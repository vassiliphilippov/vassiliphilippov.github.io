Etymology

Israelites comes from the Hebrew name Israel, a name that likely means 'he struggles with God'. The ending -ites marks a group of people, so Israelites simply means 'people of Israel' and refers to the ancient Hebrew nation descended from Abraham, Isaac and Jacob in the Bible.
