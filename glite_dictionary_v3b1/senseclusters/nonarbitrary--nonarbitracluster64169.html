Etymology

nonarbitrary is made from the prefix non- meaning 'not' and arbitrary meaning 'based on a person's choice or wish', the same root you see in arbitrate 'to decide for others'. So a nonarbitrary rule or decision is not a personal choice but is based on evidence, clear rules, or fair reasons.
