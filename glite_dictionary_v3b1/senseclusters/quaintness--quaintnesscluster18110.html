Etymology

quaintness comes from an old French word coint that meant 'clever' and from Latin cognitus 'known', which is related to the English word know. Over time the idea changed from 'clever' to something pleasingly old or unusual, so quaintness now means a charming, old-fashioned or attractively unusual quality.
