Etymology

Boeuf fondu bourguignon comes from French words: boeuf means beef, fondu means 'melted', and bourguignon refers to the Burgundy style. The dish is cubes of beef cooked in hot oil until very soft and then dipped in red wine sauces, so the name gives a clear image of 'melted' beef in the Burgundy way.
