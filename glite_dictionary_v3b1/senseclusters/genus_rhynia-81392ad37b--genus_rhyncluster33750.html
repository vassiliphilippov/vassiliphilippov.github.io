Etymology

genus Rhynia is a scientific name taken from the fossil site called Rhynie; genus means 'group' and Rhynia names the place. The fossils are small, leafless, branching stems with tiny spore bags at their tips, so the name points to a 'group' of very early land plants found at Rhynie.
