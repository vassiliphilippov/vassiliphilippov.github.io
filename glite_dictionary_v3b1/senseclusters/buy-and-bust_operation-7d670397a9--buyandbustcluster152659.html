Etymology

buy-and-bust operation comes from a simple, literal scene: police pretend to buy illegal drugs and then bust the seller, where 'bust' means 'to arrest'. So the phrase now describes an undercover police action in which officers pose as buyers to catch and arrest drug sellers.
