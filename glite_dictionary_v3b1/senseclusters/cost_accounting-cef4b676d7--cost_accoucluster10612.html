Etymology

cost accounting comes from the simple idea of keeping a special book for the cost of making something and doing the accounting for it. Imagine a maker writing down every amount of money spent so they can see the real price; that habit of 'recording' and 'checking' costs is why today cost accounting means keeping track of the costs of making goods or providing services.
