Etymology

rambunctiously probably comes from playful older words like rumbustious, which meant 'noisy and wild'. A helpful way to remember it is to think of ram plus bouncy, someone who 'rams' around and 'bounces' about. So to act rambunctiously is to behave in a loud, energetic, and uncontrolled way.
