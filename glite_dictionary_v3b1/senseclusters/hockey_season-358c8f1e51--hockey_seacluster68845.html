Etymology

hockey season comes from the idea of a season as a regular 'time of year' and hockey as the sport. Because hockey was often played only in colder months when ice was good, people talked about the 'hockey season' as the months set aside for games. That's why today hockey season means the yearly period when official hockey games are played.
