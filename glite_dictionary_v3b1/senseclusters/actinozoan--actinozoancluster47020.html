Etymology

actinozoan comes from Greek parts actino- meaning 'ray' and -zoan meaning 'animal'. Think of actino- as rays spreading from a center and -zoan like zoo, which is about animals. That's why actinozoan names sea animals such as corals and anemones that have tentacles or arms spreading out like rays.
