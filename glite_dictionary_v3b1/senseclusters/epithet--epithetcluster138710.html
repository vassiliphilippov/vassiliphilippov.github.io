Etymology

epithet comes from Greek parts epi- 'on' and tithenai 'to place'. A helpful way to remember the insulting sense is to think of an epithet as a nasty label someone 'places on' a person in anger. So, an epithet can mean a rude or abusive word aimed at someone.
