Synonyms

  • gangrene
    0.7/m8.7
    Describes death and decay of tissue from infection or poor blood flow
    vsmortification: is the medical synonym often used interchangeably for that same condition
  • necrosis
    0.5/m8.2
    Means localized death of cells or tissues from injury, infection, or lost blood supply
    vsmortification: is narrower, often implying infected decay rather than any cell death
  • sphacelus
    0.00/m9.9
    Describes tissue death from disease or injury and can mean the dead tissue itself

  • myonecrosis
    0.00/m9.9

    0.00/m9.9

    vsmortification: is the general term that covers many types of localized tissue death

Etymology

mortification comes from the root mort- which means 'death', the same root you see in mortal and mortuary. It originally meant 'to make dead', so in medicine mortification is the death of living tissue.
