Etymology

Musa paradisiaca is a Latin name that means 'banana of paradise': Musa is the old word for banana and paradisiaca means 'from paradise'. Early explorers thought these lush, tasty plants looked like a piece of paradise, so the name now refers to this species of banana or plantain.
