Etymology

The term letter telegram comes from an old post office service that mixed a fast telegram and a slow letter: post office workers would copy a customer's words and send them abroad at a 'cheaper' price so the message arrived the 'next day'. That's why letter telegram means a lower-cost telegram sent for delivery the following day.
