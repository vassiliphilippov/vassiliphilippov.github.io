Etymology

The name Kolkwitzia amabilis honors a plant scientist named Kolkwitz, and the Latin word amabilis means 'lovely'. So the name literally says 'Kolkwitz's lovely plant', a simple picture that helps you remember the shrub's arching branches and pink flowers.
