Etymology

Orbignya martiana is named after two people: the genus Orbignya is named for the French naturalist d'Orbigny and the species martiana is named for the botanist Martius. Picture a tall feather palm with their names on its trunk, and that image helps you remember it is the northern Brazilian palm whose hard nuts give oil and 'vegetable ivory'.
