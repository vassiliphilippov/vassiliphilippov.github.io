Etymology

The expression like a phoenix from the ashes comes from a story about a mythical bird, the phoenix, that burns itself and then rises again from its own ashes. That's why saying someone comes 'like a phoenix from the ashes' means they are 'renewed' or come back stronger after a great loss.
