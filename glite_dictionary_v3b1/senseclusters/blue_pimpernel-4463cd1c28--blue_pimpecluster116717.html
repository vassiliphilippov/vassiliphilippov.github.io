Etymology

blue pimpernel: The plant name blue pimpernel comes from its small, bright blue flowers and from an old low-growing plant called the pimpernel, so the name paints a clear image of a little blue wildflower. People once used the plant as a simple 'medicine' to help with 'cramps', which is why the name is also linked to that traditional use.
