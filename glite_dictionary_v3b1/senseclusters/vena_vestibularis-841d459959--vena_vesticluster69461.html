Etymology

vena vestibularis is a medical name from Latin: vena means 'vein' and vestibularis comes from 'vestibule', a small entrance or hall. It names the vein that drains blood from the inner ear's balance organs, the saccule and utricle, so the name tells you its job in the ear.
