Etymology

The word Plumbaginaceae comes from the plant name Plumbago, which comes from the Latin plumbum meaning 'lead'. People once called these plants 'leadwort' either because they were thought to treat lead poisoning or because some looked dull and lead-like, so Plumbaginaceae simply means the 'leadwort family'.
