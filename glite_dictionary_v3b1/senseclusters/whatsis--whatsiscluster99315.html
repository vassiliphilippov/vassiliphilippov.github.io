Etymology

whatsis is a playful shortening of the question parts what + its, like asking 'what is it' when you forget a name and similar to the informal word 'whatchamacallit'. A helpful way to remember it is to imagine pointing at something and saying whatsis, because it simply means a thing whose name you do not know.
