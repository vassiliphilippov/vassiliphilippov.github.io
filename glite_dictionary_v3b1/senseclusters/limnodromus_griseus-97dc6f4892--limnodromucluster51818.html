Etymology

Limnodromus griseus comes from old roots that give a clear image: limno means 'marsh', dromus means 'runner', and griseus means 'grey'. So the name literally says 'grey marsh runner' — a grey wading bird that runs along muddy shores, which helps you remember this short-billed dowitcher.
