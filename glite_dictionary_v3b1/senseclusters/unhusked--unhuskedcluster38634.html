Etymology

unhusked is made from the prefix un- meaning 'not' (as in unlock) and husk meaning 'outer shell' or the leaf that covers grain. A helpful image is an ear of corn still wrapped in its leaves: unhusked means the outer covering has not been removed, so the grain or seed is still inside the husk.
