Etymology

Ashcan School: The name comes from ashcan, an old word for a 'trash can', and school meaning a group of artists. One popular story says critics called them 'ashcan' as a joke because the painters showed the dirty, everyday life of the city, so the name came to mean a group of painters known for real, rough city scenes.
