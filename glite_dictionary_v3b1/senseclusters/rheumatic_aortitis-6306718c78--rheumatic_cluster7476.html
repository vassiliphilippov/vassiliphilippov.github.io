Etymology

rheumatic aortitis is a medical term for when the aorta, the heart's main artery, becomes swollen after rheumatic fever. The word rheumatic shows the cause and aortitis literally means 'inflammation' of the aorta, so the term means 'inflammation' of the main artery caused by rheumatic illness.
