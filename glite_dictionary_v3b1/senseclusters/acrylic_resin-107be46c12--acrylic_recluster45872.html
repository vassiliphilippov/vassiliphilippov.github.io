Etymology

acrylic resin names a clear plastic that starts as a sticky liquid and then hardens into a glass-like coat. The parts acrylic (a kind of material) and resin (a sticky liquid that sets hard) explain why artists pour it over paintings and why makers use it to give a glossy, protective finish.
