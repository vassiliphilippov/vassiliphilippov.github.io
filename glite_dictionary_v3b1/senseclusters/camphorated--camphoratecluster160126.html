Etymology

camphorated comes from camphor, a strong-smelling substance from the camphor tree that was used for a long time in Asia for medicine and scent, and the name came into English from older words like karpūra from Sanskrit and kafur from Arabic. So, camphorated means 'treated with camphor', which is why camphorated oils and ointments smell strong and are used for medicine.
