Etymology

surrealistically comes from surreal, which joins the prefix sur- ('above') and real ('real'), and from the art name surrealism that showed dreamlike images. Think 'above reality', so to do something surrealistically is to do it in a strange, dreamlike way, like a story or picture that feels 'not quite real'.
