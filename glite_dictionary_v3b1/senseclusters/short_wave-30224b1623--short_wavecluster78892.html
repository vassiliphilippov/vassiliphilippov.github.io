Etymology

short wave comes from early radio people who thought of radio signals as waves; because these signals had much shorter waves than the old 'long wave' signals, they called them shortwave. Short waves can bounce high in the sky and travel far, so today 'short wave' means radio used for 'long-distance' or 'international broadcasting'.
