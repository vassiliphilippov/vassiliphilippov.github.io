Etymology

Cardiospermum halicacabum gets its scientific name from old words: cardio- means 'heart' and -spermum means 'seed', because the seeds look like tiny hearts. The second part, halicacabum, may come from an old Greek name for the plant, so the full name points to the climbing vine with heart-shaped seeds and the balloon-like pods gardeners call 'balloon vine'.
