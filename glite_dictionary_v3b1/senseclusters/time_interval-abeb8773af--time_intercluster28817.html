Etymology

Time interval comes from the simple image of a clear gap between two moments, when you mark the start and the end. Think of the space between two clock ticks or two heartbeats: the first moment and the second are the ends, and the interval is the amount of time, so the phrase means 'a definite, measurable period between two events'.
