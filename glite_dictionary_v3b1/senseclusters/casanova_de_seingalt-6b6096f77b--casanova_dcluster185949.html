Etymology

Casanova de Seingalt was an 18th-century Venetian adventurer and writer who told famous stories of his many love affairs. Because he openly wrote about his romantic and sexual adventures, the surname Casanova came to mean a 'womanizer', so his name now makes people think of someone who seduces many partners.
