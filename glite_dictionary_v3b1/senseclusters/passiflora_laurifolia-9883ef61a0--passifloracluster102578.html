Etymology

Passiflora laurifolia gets its name from Latin words that describe the plant. The first part, Passiflora, means 'passion flower' because people thought the flower's many parts looked like symbols of Christ's 'passion', and laurifolia means 'laurel-leaved', so the name tells you it is a passionflower with laurel-like leaves.
