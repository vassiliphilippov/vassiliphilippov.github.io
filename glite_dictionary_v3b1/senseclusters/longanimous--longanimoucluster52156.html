Etymology

longanimous comes from Latin parts long- meaning 'long' and animus meaning 'spirit' or 'mind', and the same root is in English animate, which is about life or spirit. That's why longanimous gives the idea of a 'long-souled' person who stays calm for a long time, so it means patient and slow to get angry or fight back.
