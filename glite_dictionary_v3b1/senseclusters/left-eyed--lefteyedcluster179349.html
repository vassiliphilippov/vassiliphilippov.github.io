Etymology

The word left-eyed comes from two simple English parts: left ('on the left side') and eye ('eye'). Like in common words such as one-eyed or blue-eyed, it describes someone or something that has or uses the left eye, so it means 'having only the left eye' or 'seeing mainly with the left eye'.
