Etymology

Levi comes from an old Hebrew root L-W-H that means 'to join' or 'attached', and in the Bible Leah gave the name hoping her husband would be 'attached' to her. So the New Testament disciple called Levi can be remembered as someone 'joined' to Jesus and to the group of his followers.
