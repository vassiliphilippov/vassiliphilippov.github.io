Etymology

ballyhoo artist comes from old fairs and circuses. One popular story says ballyhoo was the loud, showy talk and stunts used by showmen to get a crowd, and an artist of this sort was someone very good at those noisy promotions, so today it means 'a person who uses exaggerated publicity to get attention'.
