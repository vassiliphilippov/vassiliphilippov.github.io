Etymology

devil's avocado may come from the cooking word 'deviled', which means spicy or very rich, mixed with the creamy green avocado, so it gives the picture of a deliciously sinful spread. That's why people call avocado devil's avocado when it is used as a creamy 'spread' or 'dip', like guacamole.
