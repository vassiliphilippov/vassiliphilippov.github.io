Etymology

gowned comes from the simple word gown, which once meant a long outer 'robe' or formal 'dress' worn for ceremonies or special jobs. So, to be gowned is to be dressed in that robe, like patients in hospital gowns or graduates at a ceremony, which is why the word means 'wearing a gown'.
