Etymology

Fragrant agrimony gets its name from the plant's strong, sweet smell. If you brush its small yellow flowers or rub a leaf, a pleasant scent rises, so the name uses fragrant to show the smell and agrimony to name the plant, meaning a 'pleasant-smelling herb'.
