Etymology

The word khadi comes from India and originally meant 'hand-spun' cloth made by village weavers, much like the English word 'homespun'. Mahatma Gandhi made khadi a sign of self-reliance during the independence movement, so today khadi still means simple, hand-made cotton cloth and a symbol of national pride.
