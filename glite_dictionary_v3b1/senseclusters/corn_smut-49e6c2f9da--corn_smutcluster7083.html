Etymology

corn smut comes from the dirty, swollen lumps a fungus makes on corn; the dark growths looked like a patch of 'smut' or dirt on the ear. So corn smut means a fungal 'disease' of corn, and in Mexico those swollen kernels are called 'huitlacoche' and eaten as a 'delicacy'.
