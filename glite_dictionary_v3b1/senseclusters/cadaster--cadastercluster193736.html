Etymology

The word cadaster comes from an old Greek word katastichon meaning 'a list' or 'a register', and it later passed into Latin and French. Think of a cadaster as a special 'catalog' or phone book for land that shows who owns each plot, its borders, and its value. So a cadaster today is the official list of land, owners and values used for taxes.
