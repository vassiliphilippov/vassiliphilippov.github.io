Etymology

Amorphophallus campanulatus has a very large, bell-like flower and a strong bad smell that attracts flies, and its scientific name comes from old words: amorphophallus meaning 'odd stalk' and campanulatus meaning 'bell-shaped'. So the name helps you remember this smelly, bell-shaped plant people grow for its edible tuber.
