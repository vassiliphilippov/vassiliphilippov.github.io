Etymology

The term orderly sergeant may come from the old role of a sergeant who literally carried and handed out orders for the officer. The picture of orderly (carrying orders) plus sergeant explains why the phrase means the company's main sergeant who acts as the officer's 'assistant' and helps 'give orders' to the men.
