Etymology

ballistic fingerprinting, where ballistic means bullets and fingerprinting means the unique marks, comes from the idea that bullets and casings get tiny, unique scratches from a gun, like a fingerprint. By comparing these marks, investigators can 'identify' or 'match' a specific gun to a crime.
