Etymology

The word akimbo probably comes from an old phrase made of a- meaning 'in' and a root like kimbo meaning 'bent' or 'crooked', creating the image of arms or legs turned out to the side. So, akimbo today means standing or sitting with limbs 'bent outward', often with hands on the hips.
