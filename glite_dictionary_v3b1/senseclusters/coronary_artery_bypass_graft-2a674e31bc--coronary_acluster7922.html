Etymology

coronary artery bypass graft comes from a simple image: when a blocked artery near the coronary, the heart, stops blood flow, surgeons bypass the block by adding a graft, a short piece of blood vessel, to make a new path for blood. So it means 'an operation to restore blood flow to the heart'.
