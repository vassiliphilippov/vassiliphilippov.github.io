Etymology

dope whore is an insulting compound that comes from combining dope (a word for 'drugs') and whore (a word for 'a prostitute'), creating the harsh image of someone who sells sex to get drugs. That's why it is used as an offensive 'insult' for a drug addict involved in prostitution.
