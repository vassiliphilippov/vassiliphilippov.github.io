Etymology

xiphium iris may come from ancient Greek words: xiphium meaning 'sword' and iris meaning 'rainbow'. That picture of long, sword-like leaves and rainbow-like flowers helps explain why the name refers to a kind of iris that grows from a bulb and has violet-purple blooms.
