Etymology

Polystichum braunii comes from Greek plus a person's name. The word Polystichum means 'many rows', a picture of the fern's leaves with rows of tiny spots or scales, and braunii means 'named for' the botanist Braun, so the name helps you remember it is Braun's 'many-row' fern that stays green in winter.
