Etymology

The word thinginess comes from the common word thing ('object' or 'item') plus the ending -iness that makes a word for a 'state' or 'quality', and it is like the casual words thingy or thingamajig. So people use thinginess playfully when they cannot remember a name and need a funny word for an unknown object.
