Etymology

withdrawal symptom: The compound withdrawal and symptom comes from a simple picture: withdrawal is something being pulled away from the body and symptom is a 'sign' the body shows. When a drug or alcohol is 'withdrawn' or 'taken away', the body reacts with signs like shaking or feeling anxious, so the phrase means the signs that appear after stopping an addictive substance.
