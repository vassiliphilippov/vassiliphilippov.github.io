Etymology

The phrase crosslight effect comes from the image of crossing light hitting a person or object from the side, so bright areas and dark shadows meet across the surface. This side light makes textures and shapes stand out, so the term now means creating 'dramatic contrast' and 'depth' in photos and on stage.
