Etymology

8vo comes from octavo, from the Latin octavus meaning 'eighth', the same 'oct-' you see in octagon and octave. It was used for a book size where a sheet is folded three times to make eight leaves or sixteen pages, so 8vo points to those 'eighth' parts and explains the name for that size.
