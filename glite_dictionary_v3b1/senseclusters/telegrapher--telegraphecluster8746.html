Etymology

telegrapher comes from Greek parts tele- meaning 'far' and -graph meaning 'write', the same pieces you see in telephone and autograph, and the ending -er means 'person who'. A telegrapher was originally the person who 'wrote' or sent messages over long distances, which is why it means someone who sends and receives messages by telegraph.
