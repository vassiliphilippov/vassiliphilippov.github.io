Etymology

The idiom all sweetness and light may come from a simple picture: a place full of sweetness, like candy or kindness, and warm light, like sunshine, so everything feels calm and pleasant. So when something is all sweetness and light today, it means it seems 'pleasant' and like there is 'no trouble'.
