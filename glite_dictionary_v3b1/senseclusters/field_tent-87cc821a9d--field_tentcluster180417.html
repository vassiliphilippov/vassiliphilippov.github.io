Etymology

field tent comes from joining the words field, meaning open land where soldiers stay, and tent, meaning a portable shelter, to describe a shelter used in the field. Because armies often used large canvas tents near their camps, the phrase now means 'a large canvas tent used outdoors by soldiers or campers'.
