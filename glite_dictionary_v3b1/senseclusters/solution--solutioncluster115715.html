Synonyms

  • aqueous solution
    0.03/m3.5
    Uses water as the solvent for dissolving substances
    vssolution: refers generally to mixtures with any solvent, not only water
  • colloidal solution
    0.01/m5.8
    Has tiny solid particles suspended throughout a continuous liquid phase
    vssolution: describes dissolved substances, not suspended particles
  • solid solution
    0.00/m7.0
    Is a homogeneous solid where substances form a single solid phase
    vssolution: applies to mixtures in liquids or other phases rather than solid crystals

Etymology

solution comes from an old Latin root solu- meaning 'to loosen', the same root you see in dissolve. In chemistry, a solution is when one substance 'loosens' into a liquid and mixes in, like sugar disappearing in tea, so the word fits the idea of a mixed liquid.
