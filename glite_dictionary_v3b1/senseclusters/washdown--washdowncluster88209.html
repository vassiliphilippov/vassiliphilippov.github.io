Etymology

The word washdown comes from the simple words wash ('clean') and down ('all the way' or 'from top to bottom'), so think of washing something 'down' until it is fully clean. That's why a washdown is a thorough cleaning of equipment, surfaces, or vehicles to remove dirt or contaminants.
