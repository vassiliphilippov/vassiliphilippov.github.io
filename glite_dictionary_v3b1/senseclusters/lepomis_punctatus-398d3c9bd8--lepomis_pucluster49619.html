Etymology

Lepomis punctatus is a scientific name made from old Greek and Latin words that describe the fish. The second part, punctatus, means 'spotted' in Latin, and Lepomis may come from a Greek word for 'scales' or 'scaled mouth', so the name paints a simple picture of a small, scaly sunfish with many spots and explains why we use this name today.
