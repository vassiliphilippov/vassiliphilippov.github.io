Etymology

Convolvulus scammonia gives a simple picture: Convolvulus shows its twisting stems that wind around other plants, and scammonia points to the thick roots that produce a resin called 'scammony'. People long used that root resin as a strong 'laxative', so the name links the plant to its medical use to make people go to the toilet.
