Etymology

The word unconstitutionality is made of un- meaning 'not', constitution which is the same root as the common word constitute and comes from an old word meaning 'to set up' or 'establish', and -ality which makes the word a noun. So, unconstitutionality means the state of 'not' following the constitution, which is why courts use it to say a law or action breaks the country's basic rules.
