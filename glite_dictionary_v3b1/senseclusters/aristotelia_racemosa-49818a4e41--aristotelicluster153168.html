Etymology

Aristotelia racemosa may come from two parts: Aristotelia probably honours the Greek thinker Aristotle, and racemosa is a Latin word meaning 'cluster', pointing to the groups of small berries or flowers on the plant. So the name gives a simple picture — a shrub with many small clustered berries, which helps explain why people eat the fruit or make wine from it.
