Etymology

sakkos comes from a Greek word meaning 'sack' or 'bag' and was first used for a loose, sack-shaped robe worn by Byzantine bishops. Think of the English word sack to remember its loose shape. So a sakkos today is a richly decorated tunic that keeps that original 'sack' form.
