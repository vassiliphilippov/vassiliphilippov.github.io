Etymology

Earl of Warwick comes from the town of Warwick and was the title given to the earl who ruled that area. One famous holder, Richard Neville (1428–1471), was so powerful in the Wars of the Roses that people called him the 'Kingmaker' because he could help put kings on the throne, so the title is linked with great influence.
