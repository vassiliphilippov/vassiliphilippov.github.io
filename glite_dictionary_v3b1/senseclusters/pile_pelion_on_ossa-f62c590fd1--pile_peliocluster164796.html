Etymology

The phrase pile Pelion on Ossa comes from a Greek myth where giants tried to stack the mountain Pelion on top of the mountain Ossa to reach the gods. This giant, impossible picture is why it means 'to try something impossible' or 'to add more trouble to a big problem'.
