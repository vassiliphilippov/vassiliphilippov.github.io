Etymology

doming comes from the word dome and the ending -ing, where dome means 'rounded roof or shape' and -ing means 'the action of'. Imagine the ground slowly pushed up by hot rock under the earth into a rounded hill, and you have geological doming, the process of making that dome-shaped rise.
