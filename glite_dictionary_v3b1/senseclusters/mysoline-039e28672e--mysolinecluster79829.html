Etymology

Mysoline is a brand name for a medicine used to treat seizures and tremor. A helpful way to remember it is to see Mys- (like myo-) meaning 'muscle' and the ending -line like many drug names, so the name suggests a medicine that helps calm muscle shaking and seizures.
