Etymology

complex absence comes from two simple ideas: absence means a short 'blank' when someone seems to lose attention, and complex means there are extra signs like muscle weakness or small involuntary movements. So the name describes a short seizure with reduced awareness plus additional symptoms, and that is why doctors use it for this kind of absence seizure.
