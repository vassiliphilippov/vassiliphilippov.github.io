Etymology

fluorescein isothiocyanate tells a simple story: fluorescein is a yellow-green dye that 'glows' and isothiocyanate is the part that 'sticks' to proteins or antibodies. That's why the name means a glowing tag used to 'label' antibodies so scientists can see them under the microscope.
