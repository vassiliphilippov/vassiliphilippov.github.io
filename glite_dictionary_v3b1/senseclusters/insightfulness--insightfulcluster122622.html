Etymology

The word insightfulness comes from the smaller word insight, made from in- 'into' and sight 'seeing', plus -ful 'full of' and -ness 'state of'. So, it is like 'seeing into' something, and insightfulness means having deep understanding and the ability to notice what is not obvious.
