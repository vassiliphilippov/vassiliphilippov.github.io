Etymology

Iraqi Mukhabarat comes from the Arabic word khabar, meaning 'news' or 'information'. So Mukhabarat literally means 'those who gather information', and the phrase is used for Iraq's 'intelligence service' or 'secret police', especially under Saddam Hussein.
