Etymology

Geranium pratense gets its name from older languages: Geranium comes from a Greek word for 'crane' because the plant's seed pod looks like a long bird's beak, and pratense is Latin for 'meadow' because it often grows in fields. So the name means the 'meadow cranesbill' and describes a tall meadow plant with blue flowers.
