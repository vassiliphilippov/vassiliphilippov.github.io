Etymology

The word swaggeringly comes from swagger, an old word that meant 'to sway' or 'strut', so imagine someone swinging their body proudly as they walk. The ending -ly makes it mean 'in a proud, showy way', which is why swaggeringly describes acting confident and trying to get attention.
