Etymology

pains comes from the same root as pain, which long ago meant 'hurt' or 'punishment'. A helpful way to remember it is to think of 'taking pains' as being ready to feel a little 'hurt' or trouble to do something carefully, so pains now means the care or effort someone gives to do a good job.
