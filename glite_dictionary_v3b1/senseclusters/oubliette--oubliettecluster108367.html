Etymology

oubliette comes from the French word oublier, meaning 'to forget', plus the small ending -ette, meaning 'little'. Think of it as a 'little forgotten place' — a secret dungeon where people were thrown and left to be forgotten, which is why an oubliette is a hidden prison cell with an opening only in the ceiling.
