Etymology

euphonious comes from Greek parts eu- meaning 'good' or 'well' and phon meaning 'sound' or 'voice', the same root you see in telephone and symphony. So something euphonious literally means 'good sound', which is why it describes speech or music that is pleasant to the ear.
