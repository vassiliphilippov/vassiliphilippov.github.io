Etymology

The word uncovering comes from the parts un- meaning 'not' or 'the opposite of' and cover meaning 'to put something over'. So uncovering is like taking the cover off to 'reveal' or 'find' something hidden, just like the similar word discover which means to 'find' what was covered.
