Etymology

paleomagnetic starts with paleo- meaning 'ancient' and ends with magnetic from magnet meaning 'related to a magnet'. So paleomagnetic is about the 'ancient magnetism' kept in rocks, which is why scientists use it to learn about Earth's past magnetic field and how continents moved.
