Etymology

procaine hydrochloride was created as a safer medical alternative to cocaine, and its name shares the '-caine' part with 'cocaine' while the brand name 'Novocaine' meant 'new caine'. Doctors used it to numb small areas during operations, so that's why the name is linked to 'a local anesthetic' and 'to numb'.
