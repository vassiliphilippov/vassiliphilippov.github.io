Etymology

Picea pungens comes from old Latin words. The first part, Picea, may come from a word for 'pitch' meaning sticky tree sap, and pungens means 'sharp' or 'pricking', pointing to the tree's stiff, sharp needles. So the name gives a simple picture of the blue spruce we know today.
