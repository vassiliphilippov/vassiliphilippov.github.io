Etymology

Stinker comes from stink 'bad smell' and the ending -er 'one that'. A helpful way to remember this sense is to imagine a person who 'gives off' a bad feeling like a smell, so a 'stinker' can mean someone who is mean, dishonest, or unpleasant.
