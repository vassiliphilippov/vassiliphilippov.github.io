Etymology

Abies balsamea may come from simple Latin words: Abies means 'fir' and balsamea points to the tree's sweet-smelling, sticky sap called 'balsam'. Early settlers used its scented needles and sap for medicine and as a Christmas tree, so the name helps you remember the tree by its smell and use.
