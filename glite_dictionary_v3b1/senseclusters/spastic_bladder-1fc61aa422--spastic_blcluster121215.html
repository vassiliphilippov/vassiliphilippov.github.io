Etymology

Spastic bladder comes from spastic (from the word 'spasm', a sudden muscle movement) and bladder (the organ that holds urine). If the bladder muscles 'spasm' they squeeze at the wrong time and cause sudden or 'involuntary' urination, so the term describes a bladder that cannot control urine, often after nerve injury.
