stuck in Schmucksville may come from a funny image of a small town called Schmucksville, where 'schmuck' means a foolish person, and being stuck in that town paints a clear picture of not being able to escape an embarrassing moment. So the phrase means feeling trapped in a 'foolish' or 'awkward' situation, often because of your own mistake.
