Etymology

Diodontidae comes from Greek parts di- 'two' and odont 'tooth', with -idae showing it is a family name. These fishes have two large joined teeth that form a beak, so the name helps you remember they are the 'two-toothed' porcupinefishes known for inflating their bodies.
