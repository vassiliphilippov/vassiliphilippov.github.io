Etymology

striped muishond may come from the Afrikaans words muis ('mouse') and hond ('dog') because people thought it was small like a mouse but had a dog-like bite. Its strong black-and-white stripes give a clear picture, so the name helps you remember it is the small, striped carnivore called the 'striped polecat'.
