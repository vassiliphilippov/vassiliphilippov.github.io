Etymology

rhomboideus major muscle comes from words that mean a 'diamond' shape and 'large', because rhomboideus means a diamond shape and major means bigger. The muscle is the larger diamond-shaped muscle that pulls the shoulder blade toward the spine, so its name describes both its shape and its job.
