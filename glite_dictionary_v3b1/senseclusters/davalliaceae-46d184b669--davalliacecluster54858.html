Etymology

The word Davalliaceae comes from the plant group Davallia, named after the Swiss botanist Davall, plus the ending -aceae, which means 'family' in plant names, as in Rosaceae, the 'rose family'. So, Davalliaceae simply means the 'family' of plants related to Davallia, which is why it names this group of ferns.
