Etymology

The phrase mercy buckets comes from a joke on the French 'merci beaucoup', because the French words sound like 'mercy buckets' in English. The silly image of saying mercy and a pile of buckets makes it playful, so people use it as a cheerful or joking way to say 'thank you very much'.
