Etymology

scrub fowl may come from the bird's home and habits: scrub refers to the low 'bushes' where it lives and fowl means a ground 'bird'. These birds build large mounds of dead leaves and dirt to keep their eggs warm, so the picture of a bird in the scrub making a warm nest explains why we call it scrub fowl.
