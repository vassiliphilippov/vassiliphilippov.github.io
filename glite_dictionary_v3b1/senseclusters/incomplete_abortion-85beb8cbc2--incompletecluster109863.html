Etymology

incomplete abortion comes from its parts: incomplete and abortion, where abortion means 'ending a pregnancy' and incomplete means 'not finished'. It gives the image of some pregnancy tissue still left in the womb, so it means 'an ending of a pregnancy' where not all the tissue has left and more treatment may be needed.
