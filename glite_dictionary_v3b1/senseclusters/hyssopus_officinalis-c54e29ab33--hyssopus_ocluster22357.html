Etymology

Hyssopus officinalis comes from old Greek and Latin words: Hyssopus was the name for a fragrant shrub and officinalis showed the plant was 'used in medicine' or sold in medicine shops. That's why the name points to a useful, aromatic herb still used for cooking, perfume, and simple remedies.
