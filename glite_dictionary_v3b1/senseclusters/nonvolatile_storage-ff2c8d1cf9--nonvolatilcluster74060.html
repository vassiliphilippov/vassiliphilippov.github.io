Etymology

nonvolatile storage: The term nonvolatile storage comes from the idea that it does not lose what is kept when the power goes off: nonvolatile means not 'volatile', or not likely to disappear, and storage is a place to keep things. Think of a locked box that keeps your papers safe when the lights go out; that image shows why nonvolatile storage means memory that 'keeps data when power is off'.
