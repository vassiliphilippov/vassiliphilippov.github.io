Etymology

branchiopod crustacean comes from old word parts: branchio- meaning 'gill', -pod meaning 'foot', and crust meaning 'shell'. Picture a small shelled animal with many leaflike legs that help it swim and breathe, and you can see why this name describes the animal today.
