Etymology

The word apatosaur comes from the Greek parts apate 'trick' and sauros 'lizard'. The apate part is like the English word 'deceive', and sauros is the same root you see in 'dinosaur'. So apatosaur means 'deceptive lizard', which is a helpful image because this very large, long-necked dinosaur was often confused with the better-known 'brontosaurus'.
