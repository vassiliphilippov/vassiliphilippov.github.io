Etymology

distrustful comes from the prefix dis- meaning 'not' and the word trust meaning 'belief or confidence in someone'. A helpful way to remember it is to think of dis- like in disagree meaning 'not', so a distrustful person is 'not trusting' and tends to doubt others.
