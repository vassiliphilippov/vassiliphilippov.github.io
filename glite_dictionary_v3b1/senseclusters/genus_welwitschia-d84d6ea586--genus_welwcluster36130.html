Etymology

genus Welwitschia comes from the name of the plant scientist Friedrich Welwitsch, who found the strange desert plant with just two long leaves—imagine him writing his own name next to the new discovery. So genus means the 'group' in scientific names and Welwitschia is the name given to that group, which is why the phrase refers to this unique plant group today.
