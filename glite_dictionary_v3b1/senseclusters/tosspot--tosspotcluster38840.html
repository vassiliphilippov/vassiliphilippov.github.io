Etymology

tosspot comes from the old image of someone who 'tossed' back pots of drink — the parts toss ('to tip or throw') and pot ('a drinking container'). A helpful way to remember it is to imagine a person quickly tipping one pot after another, which is why tosspot became an old name for a heavy drinker or a foolish person.
