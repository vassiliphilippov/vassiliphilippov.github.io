Etymology

Unstimulating breaks into un- meaning 'not' and stimulate, which comes from a word for a small 'push' or 'spark' that makes you feel interested or active. So, Unstimulating means 'not giving a push or spark', which is why something unstimulating feels boring and not exciting.
