Etymology

Jose Ortega y Gasset is a Spanish personal name: Jose is the Spanish form of 'Joseph', and y means 'and', joining his father's surname Ortega and his mother's surname Gasset. He was a Spanish philosopher, so using his full name helps people know exactly which thinker is meant.
