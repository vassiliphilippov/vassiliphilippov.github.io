Etymology

The name owlet moth may come from how these insects remind people of small owls: the word owlet means a little owl, and the word moth shows they fly at 'night' and often have dull colors or round wing marks that look like eyes. That's why the name now means a group of night-flying moths whose caterpillars often damage crops.
