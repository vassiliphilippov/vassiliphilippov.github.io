Etymology

resinoid starts with resin, the 'sticky substance' from trees used for glue and varnish, and ends with -oid, which means 'like', as in humanoid. So a resinoid is a material that is 'like' resin, a kind of plastic made by using natural or artificial resins, which explains the word's meaning.
