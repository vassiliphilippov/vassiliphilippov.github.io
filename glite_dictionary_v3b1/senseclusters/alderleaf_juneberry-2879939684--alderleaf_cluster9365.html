Etymology

alderleaf Juneberry is a plant name that tells you how it looks and when it bears fruit. The part alderleaf shows the leaves look like an alder tree, and Juneberry means it often makes sweet berries in 'June', so the name helps you picture a small tree with white flowers and purple-red fruit.
