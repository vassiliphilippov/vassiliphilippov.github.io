Etymology

The hockey term icing the puck comes from shooting the puck from your own end across the center line and the other team's goal line without anyone touching it. Because this makes the referee 'stop play', the phrase means that long, untouched shot that causes a stoppage and is often used when a team sends the puck away to relieve pressure.
