Etymology

wellspring of joy is a phrase that comes from the picture of a bubbling wellspring, a natural spring of water that keeps flowing, and the idea that joy can flow like water. So, calling someone a wellspring of joy means they are a steady 'source' of 'happiness' that keeps giving.
