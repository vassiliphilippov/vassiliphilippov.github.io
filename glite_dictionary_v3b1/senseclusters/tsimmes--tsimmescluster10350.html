Etymology

tsimmes comes from Yiddish and names a 'sweet stew' of carrots, fruit, and sometimes meat that is often served at Jewish holidays, like a special holiday 'stew'. So, tsimmes today still means that sweet, slow-cooked dish you eat at celebrations.
