Etymology

inkblot is simply the clear mix of ink and blot, and a blot is a 'spot' or 'stain' of ink. Because psychologists have long used strange ink 'spots' to see what people imagine, an inkblot now often means an irregular ink 'spot' used to test what someone sees or thinks.
