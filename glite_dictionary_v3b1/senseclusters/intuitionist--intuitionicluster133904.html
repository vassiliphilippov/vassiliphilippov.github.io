Etymology

intuitionist comes from intuition, which means a quick, direct 'feeling' or 'knowing' without long thinking, plus the ending -ist, meaning 'a person who follows a belief or practice'. So an intuitionist is someone who trusts those immediate feelings or insights—especially in philosophy and mathematics, where they often prefer direct mental 'insight' over formal proof.
