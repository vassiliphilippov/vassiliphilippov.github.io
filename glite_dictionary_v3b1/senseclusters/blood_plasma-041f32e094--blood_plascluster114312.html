Etymology

blood plasma is the pale yellow liquid part of blood. Imagine red cells floating in a clear yellow liquid — that liquid, plasma, 'holds' the cells and 'carries' proteins and nutrients, so blood plasma means the liquid that moves and keeps important parts in the blood.
