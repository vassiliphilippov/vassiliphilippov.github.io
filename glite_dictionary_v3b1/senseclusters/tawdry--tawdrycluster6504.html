Etymology

tawdry comes from the name St. Audrey and the phrase St. Audrey's lace, because long ago people sold cheap lace and small jewelry at a fair for 'St. Audrey'. That's why tawdry now means something that looks 'showy' but is 'cheap' or low quality.
