Etymology

Auxiliary storage gives a simple picture: your computer's desk is where you work and the auxiliarystorage is the filing cabinet where you keep papers you do not need right now. So, auxiliary storage means a place to save data for the 'long-term' rather than the 'main memory' used while a program runs.
