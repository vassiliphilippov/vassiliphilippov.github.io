Etymology

The word thundershower is simply two common words put together: thunder meaning 'loud sound' and shower meaning 'short rain'. So a thundershower is an ordinary brief rainstorm that comes with thunder (and often lightning), which makes it easy to remember as a quick, noisy rain.
