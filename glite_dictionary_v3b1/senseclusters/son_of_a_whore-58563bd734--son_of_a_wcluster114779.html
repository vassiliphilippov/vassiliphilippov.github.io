Etymology

son of a whore comes from the simple image of naming someone's mother a 'whore' to shame the person. Because a mother's reputation once reflected on her child's honor, this was a strong personal attack, so today the phrase is used as a very offensive insult meaning 'a dishonorable or contemptible person'.
