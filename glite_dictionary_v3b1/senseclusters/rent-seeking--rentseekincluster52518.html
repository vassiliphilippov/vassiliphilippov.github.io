Etymology

rent-seeking comes from rent, which means 'income' like the money a landlord gets, and seeking, meaning 'trying to get'. So rent-seeking is a helpful way to remember people 'trying to get' income or advantages without creating new wealth, often by using power or rules to move money to themselves.
