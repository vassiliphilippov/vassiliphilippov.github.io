Etymology

Callitrichaceae comes from the plant name Callitriche, which is made of Greek parts kalli- meaning 'beautiful' (as in calligraphy) and -trich meaning 'hair' or 'hair-like', a picture of its thin leaves. The ending -aceae means 'plant family', so Callitrichaceae is the family of those 'beautiful, hair-like' water plants.
