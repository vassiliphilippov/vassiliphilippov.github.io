Etymology

nonvolatilizable starts with non- meaning 'not' and contains volatile, which comes from an old idea of 'to fly' and now helps us think of something that can 'turn into vapor'. So nonvolatilizable means 'not able to turn into vapor' or 'not easily evaporated', which fits its use for substances that stay behind instead of becoming vapor.
