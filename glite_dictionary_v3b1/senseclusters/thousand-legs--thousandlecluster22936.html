Etymology

thousand-legs comes from the two English words thousand ('a very large number') and legs ('the parts animals walk on'), and it is used for a millipede or centipede, small long animals with many legs. A helpful way to remember it is to imagine the animal covered in legs, even though it does not really have one thousand.
