Etymology

machine stitch comes from the simple picture of a sewing machine making each stitch quickly and evenly, unlike sewing 'by hand'. Because the machine repeats the same action, the stitches are faster, more even and often stronger, so the phrase means 'a stitch made by a sewing machine'.
