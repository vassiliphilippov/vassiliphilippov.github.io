Etymology

Ficus diversifolia comes from Latin words: Ficus meaning 'fig' and diversifolia meaning 'different leaves'. So the name shows it is a small fig plant with varied leaves that can look like mistletoe, which helps you remember its shape and why people keep it as a houseplant.
