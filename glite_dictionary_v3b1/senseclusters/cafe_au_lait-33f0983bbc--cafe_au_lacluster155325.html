Etymology

cafe au lait comes from French and literally means 'coffee with milk', with the words cafe meaning 'coffee', au meaning 'with', and lait meaning 'milk'. It was originally used for hot coffee mixed with hot milk, often in equal parts, so today the phrase still names that mixed drink.
