Etymology

expressivity comes from the parts ex- 'out' and press 'to press', the same root as the common word express. It describes how clearly someone can 'press out' their feelings or thoughts, for example in voice or movement. So the origin shows why expressivity is about how strongly and clearly we show emotion.
