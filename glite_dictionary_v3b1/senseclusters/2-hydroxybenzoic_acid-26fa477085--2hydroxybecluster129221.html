Etymology

2-hydroxybenzoic acid is a plain chemical name that describes the molecule: 2 shows where the hydroxy group sits on the ring, and benzoic acid names the acid ring itself. This same substance is called 'salicylic acid' from willow bark, so the name tells you exactly which chemical is used in aspirin, dyes and skin products.
