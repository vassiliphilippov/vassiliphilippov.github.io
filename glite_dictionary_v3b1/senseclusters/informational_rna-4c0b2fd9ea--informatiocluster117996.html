Etymology

Informational RNA is like a tiny mail carrier inside a cell: informational means it carries 'information' and RNA is the part that carries the message. It brings instructions from DNA in the nucleus to the ribosome, the cell's protein factory, so the cell can build proteins, which is why scientists call it 'messenger RNA' or 'mRNA'.
