Etymology

Armored personnel carrier comes from the simple idea of a vehicle covered in metal for protection: armies put metal around a vehicle so the personnel, the 'soldiers', inside could be safe while the carrier carried them. That's why the term means 'a protected vehicle that carries soldiers'.
