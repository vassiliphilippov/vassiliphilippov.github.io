Etymology

whangdoodle is a playful made-up name likely built from whang (a loud 'bang' or 'hit') and doodle (a small, silly 'drawing'). A helpful way to remember it is to picture a noisy, odd creature in a children's story, which is why it means a fanciful imaginary animal.
