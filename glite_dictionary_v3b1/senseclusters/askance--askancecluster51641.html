Etymology

askance probably comes from old words meaning 'to the side' or 'turned'. A helpful way to remember it is to think of the a- part plus a root like skance that sounds a bit like 'skew', so to look askance is to give a 'sideways' look of 'doubt' or 'disapproval'.
