Etymology

Tonkin ski pole comes from making ski poles from Tonkin Cane, a kind of 'bamboo' from northern Vietnam that is light, strong and bendy. Because the pole's bend and strength helped skiers control turns, the name came to mean a ski pole made from that bamboo.
