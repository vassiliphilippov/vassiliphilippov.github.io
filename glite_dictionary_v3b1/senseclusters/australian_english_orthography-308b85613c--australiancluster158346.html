Etymology

Australian English orthography started when British settlers brought their spellings and, over time, local words and some American forms mixed in. Think of teachers, printers and writers choosing which forms to use for schoolbooks and newspapers; those choices became the common orthography, the 'spelling' used in Australia, so the phrase means 'the way Australians spell and write'.
