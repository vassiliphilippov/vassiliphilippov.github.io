Etymology

maple-leaved bayur may come from a simple picture: maple-leaved shows the tree's leaves look like a maple leaf, and bayur is a 'local name' for the tree. So the name helps you picture a tree with maple-shaped leaves, sweet-smelling white flowers that open at night, and red wood used for planks.
