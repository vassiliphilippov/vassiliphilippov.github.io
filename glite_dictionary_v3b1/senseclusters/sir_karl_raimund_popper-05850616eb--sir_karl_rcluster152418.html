Etymology

Sir Karl Raimund Popper was an Austrian-born British philosopher who argued that scientific theories cannot be finally proved, only 'proved wrong'. Think of his name Popper like a bubble that can pop: scientists try to pop ideas by testing them, and that image helps you remember his view that theories must be checked and can be 'proved wrong'.
