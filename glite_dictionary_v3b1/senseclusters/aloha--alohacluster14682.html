Etymology

The word aloha comes from Hawaiian and is used as both a greeting and a farewell, like 'hello' and 'goodbye'. A helpful way to remember it is to think of alo 'presence' and ha 'breath', like sharing breath or life with someone, which is why aloha carries warmth, love, and respect as well as a simple hello or goodbye.
