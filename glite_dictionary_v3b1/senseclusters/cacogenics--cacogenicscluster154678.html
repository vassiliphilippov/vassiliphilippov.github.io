Etymology

The word cacogenics comes from the Greek root kakos meaning 'bad' and the part -genics meaning 'birth' or 'origin', the same root you see in genetics. So cacogenics is the study of 'bad' or harmful factors that affect birth and the passing of traits, which explains its modern meaning about causes of decline in offspring or populations.
