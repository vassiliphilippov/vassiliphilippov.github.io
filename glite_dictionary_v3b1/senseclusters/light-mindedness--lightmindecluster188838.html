Etymology

light-mindedness comes from the parts light ('not heavy' or 'not serious') and mind ('thinking'): imagine a mind like a feather that floats rather than staying steady, so the person does not take things seriously. That's why light-mindedness means a lack of seriousness, especially when serious behavior is expected.
