Etymology

Scrubbing brush comes from two plain words: scrub, meaning 'to rub hard to remove dirt', and brush, meaning 'a tool with stiff hairs'. Imagine rubbing that small, stiff-bristled brush back and forth across a dirty floor, and that image shows why it means 'a brush for heavy cleaning'.
