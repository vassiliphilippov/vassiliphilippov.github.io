Etymology

proleptic comes from Greek parts pro- 'before' and -leptic from a root meaning 'take', and the pro- is the same 'before' you see in prologue, so it originally meant 'taken beforehand'. So a proleptic scene or hint shows something before it happens, which is why it means 'anticipating' or 'foreshadowing'.
