Etymology

Lactuca sativa asparagina comes from Latin words: Lactuca means 'lettuce', sativa means 'cultivated', and asparagina refers to 'asparagus' because this plant has long, crunchy stems that look a little like asparagus. That's why the name describes a lettuce grown for its thick edible stems rather than its leaves.
