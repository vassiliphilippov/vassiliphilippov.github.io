Etymology

emotional intelligence in technology borrows the same idea: emotional (feelings) and intelligence (being smart), so it's like teaching a computer to 'read' and 'respond' to people's feelings. That's why it now means a system's ability to notice mood from voice, face or words and to change how it acts to fit the person.
