Etymology

officiation comes from the Latin root officium, meaning 'duty' or 'service', the same root as official and office. A helpful way to remember this is that to officiate at a wedding or ceremony is to perform the person's 'duty' in charge, so officiation is the act of carrying out that formal public duty.
