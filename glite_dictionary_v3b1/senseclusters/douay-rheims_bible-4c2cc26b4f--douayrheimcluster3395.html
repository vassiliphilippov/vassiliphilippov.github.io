Etymology

Douay-Rheims Bible is named after two French towns, Douay and Rheims, where English Catholic scholars living in France made an English 'translation' of the 'Latin Bible' in the late 1500s and early 1600s. That's why the name is used for this traditional Catholic English Bible today.
