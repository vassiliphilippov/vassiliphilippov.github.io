Etymology

Rhyncostylis comes from Greek parts: rhynch- meaning 'beak' or 'snout', like rhino, and -stylis meaning 'a part of the flower'. So the name suggests a flower with a 'beak-like' part, which helps you remember these fragrant Asian orchids.
