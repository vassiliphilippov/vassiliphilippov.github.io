Etymology

febrile atmosphere comes from the image of a fever: febrile means 'feverish' — hot and restless — and atmosphere is the mood in a place. So, calling a meeting or crowd a febrile atmosphere suggests it feels hot, excited, or tense, which is why the phrase means 'a situation full of strong excitement or tension'.
