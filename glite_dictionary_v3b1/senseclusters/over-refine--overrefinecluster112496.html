Etymology

over-refine joins over- ('too much') and refine ('make purer or more exact'), so it means to make something 'too fine' or perfect. A helpful image is polishing a dish or a sentence until it loses its natural flavour — that shows why over-refining can make writing, food, or ideas weaker.
