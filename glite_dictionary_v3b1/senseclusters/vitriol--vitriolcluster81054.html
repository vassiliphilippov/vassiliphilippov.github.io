Etymology

vitriol comes from the Latin word vitrum, meaning 'glass', because old chemists called shiny crystal salts that looked like glass 'oil of vitriol'. Those salts made a very strong, burning acid, so people began using vitriol for language that 'burns' or is very 'bitter'.
