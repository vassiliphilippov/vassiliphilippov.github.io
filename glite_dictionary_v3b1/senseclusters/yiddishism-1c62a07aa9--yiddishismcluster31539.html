Etymology

Yiddishism comes from Yiddish, which means 'Jewish', plus the ending -ism, a small part that marks a 'feature' or 'practice'. So a Yiddishism is a word or expression taken from Yiddish — for example 'bagel' or 'chutzpah' — and the word names the influence of Yiddish on English.
