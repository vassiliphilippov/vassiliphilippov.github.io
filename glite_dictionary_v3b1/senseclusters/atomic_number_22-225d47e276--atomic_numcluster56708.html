Etymology

The noun atomic number 22 comes from the way scientists name atoms: the word atomic plus the number 22 shows an atom with 22 small particles inside, so the label points to the metal 'titanium'. That's why atomic number 22 means 'titanium', a light, strong metal used in medical implants and airplane parts.
