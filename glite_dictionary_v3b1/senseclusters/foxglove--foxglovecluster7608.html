Etymology

foxglove comes from the joining of fox and glove; one popular story says the bell-shaped flowers look like tiny gloves that might fit a fox's paw, and another says it may come from 'folk's glove' meaning 'fairy glove'. So the name is an easy picture: a tall plant with tube or glove-shaped flowers — pretty but often poisonous and used for heart medicine.
