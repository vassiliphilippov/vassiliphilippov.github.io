Etymology

R-2 is a street name for the sleeping drug flunitrazepam; a helpful way to remember it is to think of the 'R' from Rohypnol or an 'R' mark on the pill and the '2' as a strength or type. So, R-2 refers to that specific strong sleeping drug often used illegally.
