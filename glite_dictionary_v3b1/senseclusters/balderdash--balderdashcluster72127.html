Etymology

The word balderdash probably began as a playful, made-up word that mixed silly sounds with dash, which can mean 'a small amount' or a quick act. A helpful way to remember it is to imagine a 'dash' of 'nonsense', so balderdash means 'silly or senseless talk'.
