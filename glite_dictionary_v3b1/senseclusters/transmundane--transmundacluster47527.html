Etymology

The word transmundane comes from the parts trans- meaning 'beyond' and mundus meaning 'world', and it is related to the common word mundane, which means 'worldly'. So transmundane describes things or experiences that are 'beyond' the physical world, like supernatural visions or realities outside normal life.
