Etymology

will-o'-the-wisp comes from old stories about pale lights in marshes; wisp was a 'bundle of straw' used as a small torch and will (or wile) suggested a 'trick'. So a will-o'-the-wisp now means a false hope that leads you on but you can never reach it.
