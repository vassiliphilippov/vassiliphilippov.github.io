Etymology

camosh comes from the Native American name for the plant, similar to camas, which was known for its sweet, edible 'bulbs' that people dug up and cooked. So, remember camosh as the spring flower with blue, star-shaped blooms and an edible bulb, which is exactly what the word refers to today.
