Etymology

Direct injection diesel is a type of diesel engine where direct injection means the fuel is sent straight into the part of the engine where it burns, like pouring fuel straight on a small fire instead of into a cup first. So, because the fuel goes straight to the burning area, the engine 'uses less fuel' and makes less smoke, which is why the term describes a more efficient diesel engine.
