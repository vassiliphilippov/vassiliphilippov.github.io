Etymology

machine politician comes from the idea of a political machine — a strong, well-organized group that moves votes and favors like parts of a machine. A machine politician is seen as one of those parts, acting to keep the group's power and help its private interests, so the phrase means 'a politician controlled by a party group for personal advantage'.
