Etymology

western United States comes from the simple idea of direction: it names the states that lie to the west of the Mississippi River, the area people moved into when many Americans went 'west' in the 1800s to find land and gold. So today western United States means the part of the country on the western side, known for deserts, mountains, and wide open spaces.
