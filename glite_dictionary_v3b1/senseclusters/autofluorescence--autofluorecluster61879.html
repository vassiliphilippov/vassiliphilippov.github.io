Etymology

autofluorescence breaks into auto- meaning 'self', like in automatic, and fluorescence meaning 'a glow or light given off when light hits something', like a fluorescent lamp. So autofluorescence is the 'self-glow' of cells or tissues that give off light by themselves when you shine light on them, without adding any dyes.
