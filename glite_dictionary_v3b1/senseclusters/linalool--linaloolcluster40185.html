Etymology

linalool comes from the plant name linaloe, a word once used for fragrant plants like lavender, plus the chemical ending -ol, which means 'alcohol'. So linalool literally names a fragrant 'alcohol' found in many essential oils, explaining its use in perfumes and flavourings.
