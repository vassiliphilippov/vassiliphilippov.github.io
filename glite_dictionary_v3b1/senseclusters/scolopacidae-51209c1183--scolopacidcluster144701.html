Etymology

Scolopacidae comes from the old Latin name Scolopax, from Greek skolopax, and it originally meant a 'snipe' or 'woodcock'. So, Scolopacidae is the scientific name for the family of long-billed wading birds like the 'snipe' and 'woodcock', which makes the name easy to remember for these shore birds.
