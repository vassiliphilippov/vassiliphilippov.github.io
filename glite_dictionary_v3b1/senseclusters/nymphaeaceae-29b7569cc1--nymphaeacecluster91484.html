Etymology

Nymphaeaceae comes from the Greek name nymphaea for the water-lily, and this is related to nymph, a 'young nature spirit' or 'water spirit' because the flowers looked like delicate spirits on the water. So Nymphaeaceae is the family name for water-lilies and their close relatives.
