Etymology

Cnidoscolus urens comes from older words, with Cnidoscolus meaning 'stinging' and urens meaning 'burning' or 'stinging'. Because the plant has many tiny hairs that hurt the skin, the name is a clear reminder that this herb will 'sting' or 'burn' if you touch it.
