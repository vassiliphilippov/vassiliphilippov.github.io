Etymology

askew comes from the prefix a- meaning 'on' or 'in' and the word skew meaning 'slant' or 'tilt', the same root you see in the word skew; a helpful image is something 'on a slant'. So, askew literally means 'on a slant', which is why we use it for something not straight or level.
