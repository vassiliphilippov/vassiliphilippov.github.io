Etymology

The word sociopathy comes from two parts: socio- (as in society) meaning 'people' or 'companions', and -pathy (as in empathy or sympathy) meaning 'feeling' or 'disease'. So, sociopathy points to a problem with social feeling, which matches the idea of lacking empathy and ignoring social rules.
