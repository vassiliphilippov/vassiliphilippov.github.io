Etymology

Chamaeleonidae comes from an old Greek word chamaeleon that meant 'earth lion' (from parts meaning 'on the ground' and 'lion'), and the ending -idae shows it is a family of animals. So Chamaeleonidae names the family of chameleons, the lizards famous for changing colour and for moving their eyes independently.
