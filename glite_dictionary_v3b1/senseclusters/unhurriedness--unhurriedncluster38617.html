Etymology

unhurriedness starts with un- meaning 'not' and uses hurry which means 'to move fast' or 'haste', so it literally says 'not hurrying'. Picture someone taking their time and staying calm; that image shows why unhurriedness means a calm, relaxed pace or manner.
