Etymology

hash out comes from hash, a simple dish made by chopping meat and vegetables. Think of chopping an idea into small pieces and mixing it until it makes sense, which is what people do when they hash out a plan. So, to hash out something is 'to discuss it carefully to reach an agreement'.
