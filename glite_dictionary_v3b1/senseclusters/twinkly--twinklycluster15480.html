Etymology

twinkly comes from the word twinkle ('a small quick flash or sparkle') plus the ending -y ('full of' or 'like'). Think of 'Twinkle, Twinkle, Little Star': a twinkly smile or eye is like a tiny bright flash of happiness, which is why the word means a cheerful, sparkling look.
