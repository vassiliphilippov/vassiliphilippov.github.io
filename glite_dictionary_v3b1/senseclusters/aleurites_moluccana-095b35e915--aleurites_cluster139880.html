Etymology

Aleurites moluccana is the scientific name for the 'candlenut tree'. People on Pacific islands used its oily nuts for light — they could burn a nut, press it for lamp oil, or string the nuts like simple candles, so the name points to a tree whose nuts provide oil and light.
