Etymology

hairy as an informal word for 'dangerous' or 'frightening' comes from the idea of something that makes your hair stand up, as in the phrase hair-raising. A helpful way to remember this sense is to picture a scary moment that makes your hair 'stand on end', which is why hairy can mean risky or frightening.
