Etymology

The word SEB is a short name made from the scientific phrase staphylococcal enterotoxin B, which means a 'staph' bacteria 'toxin' of type B that causes 'food poisoning'. So SEB today refers to that specific toxin and is often talked about as a dangerous agent.
