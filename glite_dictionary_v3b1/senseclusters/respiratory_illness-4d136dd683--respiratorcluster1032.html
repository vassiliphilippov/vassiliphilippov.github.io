Etymology

respiratory illness puts together respiratory, which is about 'breathing', and illness, so it literally means a sickness that affects your breathing. Imagine a blocked nose or sore lungs that make it hard to breathe; that picture explains why the phrase means a disease of the nose, throat, or lungs.
