Etymology

tarboosh comes from the Arabic word tarbūsh, which meant the same 'cap' and was used in Ottoman lands; a helpful way to remember it is as a traditional red, flat-topped hat with a tassel, like a fez, so the origin explains why it means this kind of man's hat today.
