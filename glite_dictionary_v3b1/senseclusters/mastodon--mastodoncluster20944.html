Etymology

mastodon comes from Greek parts masto- meaning 'breast' and -odon meaning 'tooth'. Early scientists thought its large molar teeth had nipple-like bumps, so they called it 'breast-tooth', which is why a mastodon is the big, extinct, elephant-like animal known for its special teeth.
