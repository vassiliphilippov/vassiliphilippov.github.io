Etymology

Bismarck Archipelago comes from German explorers in the 1800s who named the islands after their leader, Otto von Bismarck. They used the word Archipelago to mean a 'group of islands', so today Bismarck Archipelago is the name for that group of islands northeast of New Guinea.
