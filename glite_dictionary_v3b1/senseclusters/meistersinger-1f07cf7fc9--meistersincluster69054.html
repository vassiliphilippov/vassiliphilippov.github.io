Etymology

Meistersinger comes from the German parts Meister ('master') and singer ('singer') and was the name for members of medieval guilds of poets and musicians in cities like Nuremberg who followed strict rules for writing and performing songs. So a Meistersinger was literally a 'master singer' in that musical tradition.
