Etymology

quodlibet comes from medieval Latin parts quod ('what') and libet ('it pleases'). A helpful way to remember the musical meaning is to think 'whatever pleases' — a composer mixes together whatever tunes they like, so a quodlibet is a playful mash‑up of melodies.
