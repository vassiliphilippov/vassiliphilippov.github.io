Etymology

worldly belongings comes from the simple idea of things that belong to this world rather than to the spirit, so worldly means 'of the physical world' and belongings means 'things you own'. Imagine someone leaving home and packing every small item into one bag — that image shows why the phrase means 'material possessions'.
