Etymology

nutmeg melon may come from people thinking the melon smelled or tasted like nutmeg, or from its skin which had a net-like pattern that looked a bit like ground nutmeg. So today nutmeg melon means a sweet muskmelon with green flesh and a nutmeg-like smell.
