Etymology

Have several seats is a rude way to tell someone to sit down and be quiet. One popular idea says it comes from the polite 'have a seat'; adding several and imagining someone taking many seats makes the order stronger, like sending them away. So it is used to tell someone to 'stop talking' or 'go away'.
