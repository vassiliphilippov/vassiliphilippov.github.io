Etymology

isogamy comes from Greek parts iso- 'equal' and -gamy 'marriage', the same -gamy you see in monogamy and polygamy. So isogamy is the joining of equal sex cells, which is why it means reproduction by the fusion of similar cells called 'gametes'.
