Etymology

luxury liner comes from two simple words: luxury meaning rich, comfortable things, and liner meaning a ship that 'sails a set route'. In the early 1900s, big ocean ships had fancy rooms, restaurants and music for wealthy travelers, so the phrase came to mean 'a large passenger ship with luxurious accommodations'.
