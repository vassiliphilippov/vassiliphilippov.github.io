Etymology

ancient texts comes from the simple image of very old pieces of writing: ancient means 'very old' and texts are written pieces like clay tablets or dusty scrolls found in tombs or old libraries. When experts open these fragile objects and read stories or records from long ago, we call them ancient texts, meaning 'written works from a very early period'.
