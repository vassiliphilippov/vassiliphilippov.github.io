Synonyms

  • Charles's law
    0.00/m5.7

    vslaw of volumes: expresses the same principle using density inversely related to temperature rather than volume

Etymology

law of volumes comes from a simple picture: when a gas is heated, its volume gets bigger and the gas becomes less dense, so a hot-air balloon 'rises'. So, the law means that at the same pressure, increasing temperature makes a gas's volume increase.
