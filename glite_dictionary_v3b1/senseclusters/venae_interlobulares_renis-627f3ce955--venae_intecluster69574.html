Etymology

venae interlobulares renis literally names the small veins that run between the tiny sections of a kidney: venae means veins, interlobulares means between lobules, and renis means 'of the kidney'. These veins carry blood from the kidney's outer part toward the larger renal veins, so the name describes their job — 'veins that drain the kidney'.
