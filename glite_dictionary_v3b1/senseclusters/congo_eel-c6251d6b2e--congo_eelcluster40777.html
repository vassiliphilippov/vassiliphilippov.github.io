Etymology

congo eel likely comes from two pictures: congo giving a sense of a distant, wild water place and eel showing its long, thin, snake-like body. Although it is 'not a true eel', it is a slim, eel-shaped salamander that lives in still, muddy water, so the name helps you picture its shape and home.
