Etymology

Georgia O'Keeffe is the name of an American 'painter' famous for very large, close-up paintings of flowers and for bold views of the New Mexico desert, where small details can fill the whole painting. That's why her name now brings to mind strong, modern pictures of nature and why people call her the 'Mother of American modernism'.
