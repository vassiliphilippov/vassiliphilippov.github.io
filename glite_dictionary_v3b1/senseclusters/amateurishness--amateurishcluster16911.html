Etymology

amateurishness comes from amateur, which comes from Latin amare meaning 'to love', because an amateur does something for love rather than as a paid professional. The endings -ish (as in 'childish') mean 'like' and -ness makes it a 'state', so amateurishness is the state of being 'like an amateur' and often shows a lack of professional skill.
