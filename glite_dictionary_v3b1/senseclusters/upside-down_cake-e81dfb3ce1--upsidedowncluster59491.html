Etymology

upside-down cake: The dessert upside-down cake comes from the way it is made: fruit is placed in the bottom of the pan and batter goes on top, and after baking you flip the pan so the fruit becomes the top. So the name describes the upside-down way the cake was cooked and explains why the fruit is the 'topping'.
