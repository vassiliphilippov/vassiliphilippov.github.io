Etymology

Fulmarus comes from old Norse words fúll and márr, which mean 'foul' and 'gull', because these birds can spit a strong, oily fluid that smells bad to defend themselves. So Fulmarus is a sea bird name that reminds you both of a gull and of its smelly defence.
