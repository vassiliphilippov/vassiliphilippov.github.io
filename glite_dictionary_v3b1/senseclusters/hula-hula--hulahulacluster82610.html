Etymology

hula-hula comes from the Hawaiian word hula, which means 'dance', and the repeated form gives a lively name for the dance; a helpful way to remember it is that the double word feels like the rhythmic movement of the hips and hands. So hula-hula refers to the traditional Polynesian dance that uses gestures to 'tell a story' and mark special events.
