Etymology

The noun reserve account comes from the simple idea of keeping money back: reserve means to save for later and account is the place at a bank where you keep money. Imagine putting cash in a jar for a future bill, so a reserve account is a bank account where money is set aside to cover expected costs or 'future payments'.
