Etymology

The word Gordian comes from an old story about the city Gordium and its famous Gordian knot, a knot so tangled a king said whoever could 'untie' it would 'rule' the land. Because Alexander the Great cut the knot instead of untying it, a Gordian problem now means a very tangled, difficult problem that may need a bold, simple solution.
