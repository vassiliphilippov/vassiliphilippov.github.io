how do you do

4/m6.1
A formal greeting used when meeting someone for the first time that does not need an answer
interjection US / haʊ də ju du /

Etymology

how do you do comes from an old polite question that meant 'how are you'. In very formal meetings people often answered with the same words instead of giving a real answer, so it became a short, set greeting for a first meeting.
