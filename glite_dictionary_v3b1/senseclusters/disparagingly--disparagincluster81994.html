Etymology

The word disparagingly comes from disparage, made of dis- ('away') and par ('equal' or 'rank'), the same root you see in the word par in 'on par'. So to speak disparagingly is to talk in a way that puts someone 'away from equal', that is, to show disrespect or make them seem less important.
