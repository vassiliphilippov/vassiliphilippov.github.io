Etymology

Glenlivet is a place name from Gaelic parts glen (from gleann) meaning 'valley' and Livet, the river name that may mean 'smooth' or 'flood'. So Glenlivet literally means 'valley of the Livet', which is why the area is known as a scenic valley with rivers and distilleries.
