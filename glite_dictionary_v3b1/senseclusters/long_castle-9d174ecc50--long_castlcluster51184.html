Etymology

long castle comes from chess: it names the move when the king walks two squares toward the 'queen's side' and the rook travels a longer way than in a short castle. So long points to the longer rook move and castle is the king-and-rook move, which is why 'long castle' means 'to castle on the queen's side'.
