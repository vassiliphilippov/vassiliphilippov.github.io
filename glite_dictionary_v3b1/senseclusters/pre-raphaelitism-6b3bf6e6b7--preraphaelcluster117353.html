Etymology

Pre-Raphaelitism comes from the prefix pre- meaning 'before' and the name Raphael, a famous Italian painter. Artists of Pre-Raphaelitism wanted to copy the vivid color and fine detail of paintings made before Raphael, so the name shows they looked back to earlier art styles.
