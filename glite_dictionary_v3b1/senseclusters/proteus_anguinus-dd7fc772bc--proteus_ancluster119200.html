Etymology

Proteus anguinus may get its name from the old sea-god Proteus, who could 'change shape', because the animal looks strange and different from other salamanders. The second part, anguinus, comes from a word for 'snake' and points to its long, thin body, so the name fits this pale, cave-living salamander.
