Etymology

scalded milk comes from the simple image of heating milk until it is very hot, nearly boiling, so it becomes slightly scalded. Cooks found this hot milk makes dough and custard smoother, so today scalded milk means milk heated just below boiling and then cooled for use in recipes, 'used in cooking'.
