Etymology

The phrasal verb hit out at comes from the image of someone making a strong outward strike, with hit meaning 'strike' and out meaning 'away'. So when people 'hit out at' someone with words, they are using speech like a strong blow, which is why it means 'to criticize someone or something strongly and publicly'.
