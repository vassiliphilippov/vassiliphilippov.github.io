Etymology

The word numberplate comes from two simple English words, number and plate, where number means the car's identifying 'number' and plate means a flat 'plate' of metal. So a numberplate is literally a flat metal plate that shows a vehicle's 'registration number', which is why we use it to identify cars; in American English the same item is often called a 'license plate'.
