Etymology

The noun pine sawyer comes from the beetle's habit of making long holes in pine wood. People called it a sawyer, a word for someone who saws wood, because the beetle's young seem to 'saw' through the tree and leave long tunnels. That's why the name refers to a large beetle that damages pine trees.
