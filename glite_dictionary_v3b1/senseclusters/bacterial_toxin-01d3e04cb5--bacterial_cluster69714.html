Etymology

bacterial toxin shows a simple picture: bacterial means it comes from bacteria and toxin means their poison. Imagine tiny bacteria releasing a powerful 'poison' that can hurt your body and 'make you sick', so the phrase means a poisonous substance made by bacteria.
