Etymology

The Gallipoli Campaign is named after the Gallipoli peninsula, a narrow piece of land by the sea where Allied ships and soldiers tried to land in 1915. The image of troops attacking a thin beach and being stopped by strong Turkish defenders helps explain why the name now suggests a 'failed military attempt' and a heavy loss.
