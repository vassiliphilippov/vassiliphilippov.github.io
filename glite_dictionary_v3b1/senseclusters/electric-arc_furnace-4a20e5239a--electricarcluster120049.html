Etymology

The noun electric-arc furnace is named for how it heats metal: a strong electric arc, a bright, very hot spark that jumps between metal rods, makes the heat inside and melts the metal. That's why the name means a furnace that uses an electric 'arc' as the heat source to melt scrap metal and 'make steel'.
