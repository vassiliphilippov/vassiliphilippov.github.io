Etymology

chlortetracycline comes from three parts: chloro- meaning 'green' (as in chlorine or chlorophyll), tetra- meaning 'four', and -cycline related to 'cycle' or 'ring'. So the name once told scientists that this is a tetracycline with a chlorine added, and today chlortetracycline means that specific antibiotic used to treat infections.
