Etymology

American pit bull terrier gets its name from its history: American because it was bred in the United States, pit from the small 'pits' where dogs were once kept for fighting, and bull and terrier from the kinds of dogs used for strong work and hunting small animals. That's why the name today brings to mind a strong, active dog breed.
