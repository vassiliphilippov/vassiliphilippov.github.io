Etymology

The noun fragmentation bomb puts together fragmentation, which means 'breaking into pieces', and bomb, which means an explosive. Its metal case is made to break into many sharp pieces that fly out to hurt people and damage things, so the name means 'a bomb to cause injury or damage over an area'.
