Etymology

trifluoromethane breaks into tri- 'three', fluoro (from fluorine, the same root as fluoride in toothpaste) and methane 'a simple gas'. So the name means a methane gas with three fluorine atoms attached, which is why it refers to the gas CHF3 used for cooling and in industry.
