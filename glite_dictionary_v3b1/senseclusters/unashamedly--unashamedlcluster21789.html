Etymology

The word unashamedly is built from un- meaning 'not', ashamed meaning 'feeling shame', and the ending -ly that makes it describe how something is done. Imagine someone speaking proudly with their head up — they act 'not feeling shame'. So, to do something unashamedly is to show no embarrassment about it.
