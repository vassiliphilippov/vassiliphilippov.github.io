Etymology

divine revelation comes from the image of divine meaning 'from a god' and revelation meaning something shown or told to people. Imagine a bright light, a voice, or a messenger that 'reveals' a hidden truth, and that's why the phrase means 'a message or knowledge from a higher power'.
