Etymology

Roman pace comes from how Roman soldiers measured distance. A pace was the length from the heel of one foot to the next time that same heel hit the ground, and because soldiers used this step for marching and to mark roads, one Roman pace became a standard unit of about 4.85 feet, which is why the term means that specific old measure.
