Etymology

Psittacula krameri comes from a Latin word and a person's name: Psittacula is from the Latin for 'parrot' with a small ending, so it means 'little parrot', and krameri honors a scientist named Kramer who first described the bird. So the full name literally means 'Kramer's little parrot', which helps explain why it refers to the green, ring-necked parakeet we see in many cities.
