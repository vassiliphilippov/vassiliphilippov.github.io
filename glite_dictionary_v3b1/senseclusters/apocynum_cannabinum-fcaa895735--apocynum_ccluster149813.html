Etymology

Apocynum cannabinum gets its name from people noticing its hemp-like stems; the part cannabinum points to 'hemp' because its long fibers were used to make 'rope' and 'nets'. It was also called 'dogbane' or 'Indian hemp' and used in simple medicine, so the name helps us remember its tough fiber and traditional healing uses.
