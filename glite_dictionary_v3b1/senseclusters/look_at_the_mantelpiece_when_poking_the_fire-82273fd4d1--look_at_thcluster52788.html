Etymology

look at the mantelpiece when poking the fire paints a simple image: while you are poking the fire to keep it burning, you keep looking at the mantelpiece with its pictures and small objects instead of finishing the task. That's why it means 'to distract yourself by focusing on something irrelevant'.
