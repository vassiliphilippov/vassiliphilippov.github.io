Etymology

stamp collector comes from the simple idea of saving the small paper pictures you find on letters — the stamps — and the person who keeps them, the collector. People would carefully put stamps into books to look at and learn from, so a stamp collector is someone who collects and studies stamps as a 'hobby'.
