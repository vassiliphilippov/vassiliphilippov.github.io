Etymology

Saint Matthew the Apostle is the name for Matthew, one of Jesus' twelve followers who became a holy person; the words Saint and Apostle show he is seen as a 'saint' and a 'apostle' or special messenger. He is often shown with a book because people believe he wrote the 'Gospel' that tells Jesus' life, so the title links him to both being a holy man and the writer who told Jesus' story.
