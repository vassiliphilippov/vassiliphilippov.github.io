Etymology

al-Qa'ida comes from Arabic parts al- (meaning 'the') and qa'ida (meaning 'base' or 'foundation'). So the name literally means 'the base', which helps you remember it is the name of an international group that served as a central 'base' for fighters and operations known for organising attacks.
