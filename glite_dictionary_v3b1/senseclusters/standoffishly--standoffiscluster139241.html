Etymology

standoffishly comes from the parts stand + off, which together mean 'stand apart', with -ish meaning 'somewhat' and -ly making an adverb, so a helpful picture is someone who literally stands away from a group. So, to act standoffishly is to behave in a 'distant' or 'unfriendly' way, like a person who keeps their distance at a party.
