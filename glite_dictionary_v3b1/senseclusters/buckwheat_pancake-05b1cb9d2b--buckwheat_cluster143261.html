Etymology

Buckwheat pancake comes from making a thin pancake with flour from the buckwheat plant, which is not 'wheat' but a plant that gives small seeds. Long ago cooks poured the thin batter on a hot pan to make flat cakes for breakfast, so today it means 'a thin, flat cake made from buckwheat flour'.
