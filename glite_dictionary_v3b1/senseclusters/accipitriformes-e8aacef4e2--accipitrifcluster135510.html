Etymology

Accipitriformes comes from the Latin root accipitr- meaning 'hawk' or 'bird of prey' and the suffix -formes meaning 'shape' or 'form', a common ending for groups of animals. So Accipitriformes literally means the 'hawk-like' order of birds, which explains why it includes hawks, eagles, vultures and kites.
