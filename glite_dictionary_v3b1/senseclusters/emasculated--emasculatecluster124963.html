Etymology

Emasculated comes from the same Latin parts e- ('away') and masculus ('male'), the same root in the word masculine, and by extension it began to mean 'made less male' or 'weakened'. So to feel emasculated is to feel less strong or less masculine, as if you have lost power or confidence.
