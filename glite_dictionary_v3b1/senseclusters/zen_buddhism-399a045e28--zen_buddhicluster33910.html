Etymology

Zen Buddhism gets its name from Zen, a word meaning 'meditation' or 'quiet sitting', and from Buddhism, the teachings of the Buddha. Because it grew from the practice of sitting quietly to reach direct 'insight', that's why Zen Buddhism today focuses on meditation and simple, present-moment experience.
