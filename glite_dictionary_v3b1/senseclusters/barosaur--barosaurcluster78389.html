Etymology

The word barosaur comes from Greek parts baro- meaning 'heavy' and -saur meaning 'lizard', the same -saur you see in dinosaur. So thinking of a 'heavy lizard' helps you remember a barosaur was a very large, long-necked dinosaur that could reach high into trees.
