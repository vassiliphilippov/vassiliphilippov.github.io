Etymology

Jehovah comes from the Hebrew holy name written as the four letters YHWH. Early readers added the vowel sounds of Adonai, which means 'Lord', to help say the name, creating the spoken form 'Jehovah'. So Jehovah is used as a name for God in the Old Testament and in many translations.
