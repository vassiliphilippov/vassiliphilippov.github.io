Etymology

proton magnetic resonance imagines tiny proton particles as little magnets inside a strong magnetic field; when radio waves hit them they take in energy and send it back as a small 'signal'. That's why the phrase names the physical effect used to study molecules and to make medical 'images' like MRI.
