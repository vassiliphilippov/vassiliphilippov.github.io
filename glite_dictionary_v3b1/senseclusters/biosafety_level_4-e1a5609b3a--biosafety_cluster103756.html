Etymology

biosafety level 4 names the highest safety category for labs. The word biosafety means protection against very dangerous living germs, and level 4 shows it is the top level with the strictest rules, strongest suits and most careful work, so it refers to the extra-strong precautions used when scientists handle viruses that have no vaccine.
