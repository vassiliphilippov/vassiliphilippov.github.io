Etymology

genus Rickettsia comes from genus, a word for 'a group of related species', and Rickettsia, the name given to honor the scientist Howard Ricketts who studied these bacteria. So it means 'a group of related bacteria named after Ricketts', and today it is used for bacteria that can cause diseases like typhus and Rocky Mountain spotted fever.
