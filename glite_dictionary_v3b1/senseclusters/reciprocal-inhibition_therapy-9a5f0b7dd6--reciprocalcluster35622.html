Etymology

reciprocal-inhibition therapy is named because reciprocal means 'each other' and inhibition means 'to stop', creating the image of two opposite reactions that cannot happen together, like a seesaw where when one side goes up the other goes down. So therapists teach a calm response that 'stops' the anxious response, and that is why the therapy helps reduce fear.
