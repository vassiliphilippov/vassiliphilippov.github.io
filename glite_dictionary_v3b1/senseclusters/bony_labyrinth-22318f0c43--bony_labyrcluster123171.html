Etymology

The name bony labyrinth paints a simple picture: bony means made of bone and labyrinth means a 'maze', so it is a hard, maze-like set of passages inside the skull. This bone 'maze' holds and helps 'protect' the delicate parts of the inner ear, so the phrase refers to the ear cavity that contains those structures.
