to propose

2/m3.4
To suggest someone for a job or official position
verb US / prəˈpoʊz /

Examples

To propose a candidate
At the meeting, someone proposed him for the position of club president.
The committee decided to propose her as a candidate for the board of directors.
They plan to propose Dr. Wilson for a national science award next year.
I would like to propose a new candidate for the position.
She proposed him for the award last year.
Did you propose anyone for the job?
She has proposed several candidates for the role.
John proposed a new candidate for the board.

Etymology

propose comes from the parts pro- 'forward' and pose 'to put', so to propose someone is to 'put them forward' as a choice for a job or honor.
