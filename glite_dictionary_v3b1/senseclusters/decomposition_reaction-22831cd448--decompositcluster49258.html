Etymology

decomposition reaction comes from the idea that decomposition means 'breaking apart' and reaction means a chemical change. Imagine a tower of blocks falling into separate pieces — that picture shows how one substance breaks into two or more simpler ones, so the term means 'a reaction in which a single compound breaks down into simpler substances'.
