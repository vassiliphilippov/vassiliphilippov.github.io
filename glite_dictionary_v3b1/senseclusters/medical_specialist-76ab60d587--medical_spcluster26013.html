Etymology

medical specialist comes from the idea of a doctor who focuses on one area of medicine: medical means about medicine and a specialist is someone who studies one thing closely. Imagine a doctor who studies the heart only, and you can see why the phrase means 'a doctor with extra training and expert knowledge in one area of medicine'.
