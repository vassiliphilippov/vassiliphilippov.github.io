Etymology

The word somesthesis comes from Greek parts soma 'body' and esthesia 'feeling', the same esthesia in anesthesia ('no feeling'). So somesthesis literally means 'body feeling' and that is why it refers to all bodily sensations like touch, temperature, pain, and the sense of body position.
