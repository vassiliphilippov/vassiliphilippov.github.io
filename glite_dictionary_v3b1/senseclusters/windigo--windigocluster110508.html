Etymology

windigo comes from Algonquian languages (for example Ojibwe and Cree) and from the word wiindigoo, which was used for an 'evil spirit' or 'one who eats humans'. This image of a hungry, dangerous spirit helps explain why windigo names a terrifying monster in northern stories.
