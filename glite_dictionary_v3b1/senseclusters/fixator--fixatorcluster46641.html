Etymology

fixator comes from an old Latin word fixare meaning 'to fasten' and the ending -ator that makes it 'a thing that does' the action. So, a fixator is something that holds or fixes a part firmly in place, which is why in the body a fixator is a muscle that keeps a joint steady during movement.
