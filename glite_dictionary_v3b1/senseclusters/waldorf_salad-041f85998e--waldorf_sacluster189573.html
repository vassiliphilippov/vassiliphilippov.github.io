Etymology

Waldorf salad was first served at the Waldorf-Astoria hotel in New York, where cooks mixed chopped apples, celery and nuts with mayonnaise for hotel guests. One popular story says the hotel's name became the dish's name, so today Waldorf salad means that apple-and-celery 'salad' with walnuts or raisins.
