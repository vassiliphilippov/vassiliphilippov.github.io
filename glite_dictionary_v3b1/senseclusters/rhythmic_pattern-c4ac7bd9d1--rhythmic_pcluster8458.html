Etymology

The noun rhythmic pattern comes from the image of repeated beats, like a steady heartbeat or marching feet where rhythmic shows the beat and pattern shows the repeating order. So, it means the regular order of 'stressed' and 'unstressed' syllables that gives a poem its rhythm.
