Etymology

love-in-idleness has the clear parts love and idleness, and one popular story says a drop of the flower's juice could cause 'love'; Shakespeare even used it as a magic flower in 'A Midsummer Night's Dream', so the name helps you remember this small wild pansy is tied to love and the old idea of a love potion.
