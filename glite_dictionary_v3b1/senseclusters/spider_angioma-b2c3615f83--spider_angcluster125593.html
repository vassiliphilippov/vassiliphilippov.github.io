Etymology

The noun spider angioma comes from how the skin mark looks: a small red center with thin blood lines spreading out like a spider's legs, and angioma is a medical word for a small blood-vessel spot. So the name means 'a small blood vessel on the skin that looks like a spider', which helps doctors describe this mark.
