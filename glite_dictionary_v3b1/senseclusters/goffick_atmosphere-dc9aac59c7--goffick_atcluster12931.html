Etymology

Goffick atmosphere comes from a playful idea of an over-the-top 'Gothic' look: imagine a Goffick style with too many candles, heavy curtains and loud dramatic sighs that feel more showy than scary. That's why it now means a gloomy, overly dramatic mood that is often used to make fun of real 'Gothic' darkness.
