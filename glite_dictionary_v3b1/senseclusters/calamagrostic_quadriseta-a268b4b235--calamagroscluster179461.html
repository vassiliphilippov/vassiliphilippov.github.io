Etymology

Calamagrostic quadriseta may come from older words meaning a 'reed-like grass', calamagrostic, and 'four bristles', quadriseta, so imagine a tall reed with four stiff hairs at the top. That's why the name fits a tall, reedlike grass often cut for 'hay'.
