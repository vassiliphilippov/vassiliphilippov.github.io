Etymology

The phrase venial sin comes from a Latin word meaning 'forgivable'; venial means easy to forgive and sin means a wrong. Church people used it for small wrongs that do not break your relationship with God, like a light stain that can be cleaned rather than a deep wound. So, venial sin means a small, forgivable wrongdoing, not a 'mortal' or very 'serious' one.
