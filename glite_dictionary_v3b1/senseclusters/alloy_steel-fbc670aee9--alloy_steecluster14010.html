Etymology

alloy steel gets its name from alloy, a 'mix' of metals, and steel, iron with carbon. Think of it like a recipe: adding chromium or nickel changes ordinary steel to make it 'stronger' or better at 'resisting rust', so the name shows it is steel mixed with other metals.
