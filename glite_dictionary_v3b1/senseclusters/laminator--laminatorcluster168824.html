Etymology

laminator comes from the Latin root lamina, meaning 'thin plate' or 'layer', with the ending -ator meaning 'one who'. You can see the same root in laminate, so a laminator is literally 'one who adds layers', which explains a machine or person that covers items with a protective sheet.
