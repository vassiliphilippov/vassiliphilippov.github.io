Etymology

Sir Roger Bannister is the English runner who in 1954 became the first person to run a mile in less than four minutes, the famous 'four-minute mile'. Imagine a clock dropping below four minutes as he crossed the line, like breaking an invisible barrier, so his name now stands for that daring athletic moment.
