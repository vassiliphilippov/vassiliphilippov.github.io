Etymology

passionflower family: The plant name passionflower family comes from a story by early Christian missionaries who saw parts of the showy passionflower as signs of Christ's 'passion', for example a thread-like crown that looked like a crown of thorns and centre parts that looked like nails. That's why people gave this name to the whole family of plants.
