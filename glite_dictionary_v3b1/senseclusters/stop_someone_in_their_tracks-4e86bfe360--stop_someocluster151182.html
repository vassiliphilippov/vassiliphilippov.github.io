Etymology

stop (someone) in (their) tracks comes from a simple picture: imagine a person walking along a path or a railway and suddenly freeze, so they literally stop on the tracks. That's why it means 'to make someone suddenly stop what they are doing or thinking', usually because they are surprised or shocked.
