Etymology

The word lease-lend comes from joining the two simple words lend and lease, where lend means 'to give for a short time' and lease means 'to rent for a time'. It was used for the World War II program of sending supplies to allied countries without selling them, so the name shows the idea of giving or renting goods to help an ally.
