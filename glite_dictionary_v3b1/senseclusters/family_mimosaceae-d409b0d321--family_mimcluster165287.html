Etymology

family Mimosaceae comes from the plant name Mimosa, known for leaves that fold when touched, plus the ending -aceae, a Latin ending that means a plant 'family'. So, family Mimosaceae simply names the group of plants related to Mimosa, many of which are spiny shrubs with leaves that close when touched.
