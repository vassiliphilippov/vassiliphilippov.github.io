Etymology

pit nutter comes from car racing. The word pit is the place where cars stop for quick work like new tires and fuel, and nutter is a British slang word for someone who is a bit crazy about something. So a pit nutter means 'an extremely enthusiastic fan' who is obsessed with the pits and every race detail.
