Etymology

potassium bitartrate gets its name from a crust called 'tartar' that formed inside old wine barrels; people scraped this crust and turned it into the white powder called 'cream of tartar'. So, the name now means the baking ingredient used to 'stabilize egg whites' and improve texture.
