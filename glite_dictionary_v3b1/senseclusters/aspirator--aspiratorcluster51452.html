Etymology

aspirator comes from the Latin word aspirare, which means 'to breathe toward' and shares the same root as inspire and respire. An aspirator 'breathes' air in, so it pulls or sucks air or gas through a liquid to make a vacuum, which is why labs use it for filtration.
