Etymology

Diospyros kurzii mixes a Greek word and a person's name: Diospyros may come from Greek for 'divine fruit', and kurzii is 'named after' the botanist Kurz. So the name gives a simple picture — a notable 'divine' fruit tree found and named by Kurz — which helps you remember this large Asian tree known for its hard, striped wood.
