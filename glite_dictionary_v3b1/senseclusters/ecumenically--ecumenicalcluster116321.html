Etymology

The word ecumenically comes from the Greek oikoumenē meaning 'the whole inhabited world', and from the root oikos meaning 'house' or 'home', like in the word economy. So when used about religions, to act ecumenically is to work in a way that brings different faiths together and encourages cooperation.
