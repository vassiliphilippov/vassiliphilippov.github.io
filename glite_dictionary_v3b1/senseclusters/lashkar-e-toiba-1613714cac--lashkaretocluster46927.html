Etymology

Lashkar-e-Toiba breaks into simple parts: Lashkar means 'army' in Urdu and Persian (like the English word 'army'), the -e- means 'of', and Toiba (from Arabic Tayyiba) means 'pure' or 'good'. So the name literally means 'army of the pure', which is why this name is used by the Islamist militant group.
