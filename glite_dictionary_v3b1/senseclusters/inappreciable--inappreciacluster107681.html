Etymology

The word inappreciable comes from the prefix in- meaning 'not' and appreciable, from appreciate meaning 'to value' or 'to notice'. So, inappreciable literally means 'not able to be valued or noticed', which is why we use it for something so small or unimportant that you cannot really see or feel it.
