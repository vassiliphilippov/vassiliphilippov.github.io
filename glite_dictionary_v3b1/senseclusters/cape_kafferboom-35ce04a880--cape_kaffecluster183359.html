Etymology

Cape kafferboom comes from old Afrikaans words: boom means 'tree' and 'Cape' names the Cape region, while 'kaffer' was an old word used by settlers and is now considered 'offensive'. So the name simply described a tree from the Cape, which is why it refers to this South African tree.
