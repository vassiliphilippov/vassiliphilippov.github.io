Etymology

Av is the name of a Hebrew month and probably comes from the Hebrew word av meaning 'father', and it is also like the old Babylonian month name Abu. A helpful way to remember it is to think of Av as a 'father' name that stayed in the calendar, which is why the summer month still has this ancient name today.
