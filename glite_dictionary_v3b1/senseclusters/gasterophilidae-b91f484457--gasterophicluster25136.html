Etymology

Gasterophilidae comes from Greek parts: gaster meaning 'stomach', phil meaning 'lover', and the ending -idae meaning 'family'. So it is like 'stomach-lovers', because the flies' larvae, or baby flies, live in the stomachs of horses.
