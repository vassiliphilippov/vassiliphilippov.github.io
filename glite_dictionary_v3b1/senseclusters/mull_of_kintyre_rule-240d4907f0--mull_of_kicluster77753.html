Etymology

Mull of Kintyre rule may come from a British media guideline named after the Mull of Kintyre, where editors imagined a slanted line across a picture to decide what to hide. That's why the phrase now means that parts of an image should not show above a diagonal line, or 'not extend above a diagonal line'.
