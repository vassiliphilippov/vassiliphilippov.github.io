Etymology

The word megasporophyll comes from three parts: mega- meaning 'large', sporo- meaning 'spore', and -phyll meaning 'leaf'. So a megasporophyll is literally a 'large-spore-leaf', a leaf-like part that carries the large female spores in plants that do not have flowers.
