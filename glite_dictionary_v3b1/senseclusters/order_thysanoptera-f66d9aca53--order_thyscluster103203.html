Etymology

order Thysanoptera is a scientific name for tiny insects called 'thrips'. The Greek parts thysan- meaning 'tassel' or 'fringe' and -ptera meaning 'wings' point to their thin wings with long hairs, so the name helps you remember this group's 'fringed wings'.
