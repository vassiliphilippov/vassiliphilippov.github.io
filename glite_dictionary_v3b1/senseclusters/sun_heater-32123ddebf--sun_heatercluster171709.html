Etymology

Sun heater The noun sun heater comes from how the small electric heater looks and feels: bright halogen bulbs give a warm, sun-like light and the unit often turns to spread that warmth around the room. That's why people call a portable electric heater a sun heater — it shines and heats like a little 'sun'.
