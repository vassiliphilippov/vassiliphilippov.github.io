Etymology

pheniramine comes from chemistry parts: phen- (from phenyl, a common 'ring' found in many medicines) and -amine (a chemical ending meaning a nitrogen 'group'). So, the name points to its chemical make-up and helps remember that pheniramine is an antihistamine drug used to treat allergy symptoms.
