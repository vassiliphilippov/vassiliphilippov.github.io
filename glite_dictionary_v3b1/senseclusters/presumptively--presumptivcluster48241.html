Etymology

The word presumptively comes from presume, which has pre- meaning 'before' and a root that meant 'to take', so to presume is to 'take' something as true before you have full proof, much like 'assume'. So if something is done presumptively, it is treated as true by reasonable assumption until evidence shows otherwise.
