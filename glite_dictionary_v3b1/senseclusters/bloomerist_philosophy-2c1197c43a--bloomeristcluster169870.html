Etymology

Bloomerist philosophy comes from the name of 19th-century reformer Amelia Bloomer and the loose trousers called bloomers that some women wore to move more freely. Those women wanted more freedom to teach, work, and take part in public life, so the phrase came to mean an educational idea that supports 'women's rights' and wider roles for women.
