Etymology

The word Tylenchidae comes from the scientific genus Tylenchus plus the animal-family ending -idae, which means 'family' in scientific names and appears in names like Canidae for dogs. So Tylenchidae is the 'family' of small worm species related to Tylenchus, many of which live on plant roots and can damage crops.
