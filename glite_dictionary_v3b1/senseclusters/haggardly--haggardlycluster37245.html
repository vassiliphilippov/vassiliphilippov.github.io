Etymology

haggardly comes from haggard, an old word for someone or a bird that looked 'wild' or 'worn', often used about hawks that had been captured and looked very tired. So, think of a tired, rough-looking hawk to remember that haggardly means acting or looking very tired, worried, or worn out.
