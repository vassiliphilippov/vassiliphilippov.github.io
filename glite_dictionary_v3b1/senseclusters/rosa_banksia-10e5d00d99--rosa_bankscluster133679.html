Etymology

One popular story says the plant name Rosa banksia comes from Lady Banks, the wife of a famous plant collector who helped bring this climbing rose from China to England. The name links Rosa (rose) with banksia (from the name Banks), and the easy picture to remember is a wall covered in many small yellow or white flowers—so the name now refers to this common 'garden plant' that climbs walls.
