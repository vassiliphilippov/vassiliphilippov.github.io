Etymology

post-rotational nystagmus names what happens to the eyes: post = 'after', rotational = 'turning', and nystagmus = 'eye movement'. Imagine you spin and then stop; your body still feels the turn, so your eyes make small, slow movements in the opposite direction, and that is why the phrase means 'eye movements after turning'.
