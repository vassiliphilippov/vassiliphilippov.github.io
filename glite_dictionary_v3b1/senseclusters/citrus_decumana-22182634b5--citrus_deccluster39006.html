Etymology

Citrus decumana is a scientific name made from the Latin words Citrus and decumana, and decumana may come from a Latin word meaning 'large'. The name creates a simple image — a tree that makes very big, grapefruit-like fruits — so it helps you remember that 'Citrus decumana' refers to the pomelo.
