Etymology

Third-degree burn comes from a medical way of naming burns by 'degrees' or levels of damage: first-degree is mild, second-degree causes blisters, and third-degree destroys the deep skin layers. So third-degree burn means 'a very serious burn that can destroy skin, may make the area numb, and needs medical care'.
