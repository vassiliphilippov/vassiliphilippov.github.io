Etymology

acromphalus comes from Greek parts acro- 'high' or 'top', as in acrobat, and omphalos 'navel'. So it means a navel that 'sticks out' or is pushed up, which is why doctors use it for a bulging belly button that can be an early sign of an umbilical hernia.
