Etymology

Brocade comes from the Italian broccato, meaning 'decorated', and from an old word for a 'small point' or 'bud' that 'sticks out', like a tiny decoration such as a brooch. So brocade is a rich fabric with raised, showy patterns, often in gold or silver.
