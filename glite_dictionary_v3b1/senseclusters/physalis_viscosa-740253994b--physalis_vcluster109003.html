Etymology

Physalis viscosa gets its name from old Greek and Latin words that describe the plant's look and feel. The first part, Physalis, comes from a Greek word meaning 'bag' or 'bladder' and points to the paper-like husk that makes each fruit look like a tiny lantern, and the second part, viscosa, comes from a Latin word for 'sticky' referring to the plant's sticky parts. So the name simply describes the plant's 'bag'-like husk and its 'sticky' quality.
