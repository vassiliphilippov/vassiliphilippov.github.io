Etymology

Lo/Ovral is a brand name you can remember by parts: Lo- sounds like 'low' and Ovral comes from 'ovary' or 'ovulation', so it hints at a lower-dose medicine that works on the ovaries to prevent ovulation. That's why Lo/Ovral is used as a birth-control pill.
