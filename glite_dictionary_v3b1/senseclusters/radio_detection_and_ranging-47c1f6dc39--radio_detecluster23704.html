Etymology

radio detection and ranging names exactly what the system does: it sends out radio waves and listens for an 'echo' to do the detection and to measure 'distance' in the ranging. Imagine shouting in a dark room and using the echo to find how far away the walls are — that image shows why the phrase means a system that finds and tells how far away objects are.
