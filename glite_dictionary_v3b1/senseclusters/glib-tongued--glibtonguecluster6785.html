Etymology

The word glib-tongued comes from glib, which once meant 'slippery' or 'smooth', and tongued, from 'tongue' meaning 'to speak'. So a glib-tongued person has a 'smooth' or 'slippery' way of speaking that sounds charming but is often not sincere.
