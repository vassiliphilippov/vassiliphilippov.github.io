Etymology

cooking stove comes from the simple image of a stove as a heated box or metal surface where people keep a fire, and cooking which means 'to cook' or prepare food. So a cooking stove is literally a heated thing used 'to cook' meals, and that is why it means the kitchen appliance for making food.
