Etymology

intoxicate shares the root tox with toxic, and that root meant 'poison'. So when we say someone is 'intoxicated' by joy or success, it creates the image of being 'filled' or overwhelmed by a very strong feeling, almost like a powerful 'poison' that takes over your mood.
