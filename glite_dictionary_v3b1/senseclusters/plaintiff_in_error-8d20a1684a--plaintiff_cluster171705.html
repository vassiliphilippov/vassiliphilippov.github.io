Etymology

plaintiff in error comes from old court language where error simply meant a 'mistake' in a lower court's decision. If someone thought the judge had made that mistake, they became the plaintiff who asked a higher court to correct it, so today plaintiff in error means the party who appeals a lower court's ruling.
