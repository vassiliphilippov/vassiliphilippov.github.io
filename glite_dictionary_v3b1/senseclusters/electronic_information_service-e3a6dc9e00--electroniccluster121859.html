Etymology

Electronic information service is a modern compound made from electronic, meaning it uses computers or electricity, information, meaning facts or data, and service, meaning something that gives help or access. Think of an online library or database sending articles and data to your computer, and that image explains why it means 'a system that provides information you can access electronically'.
