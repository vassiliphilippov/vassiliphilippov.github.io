Etymology

neurolysis comes from neuro- meaning 'nerve' and -lysis meaning 'loosening' or 'breaking down', the same -lysis you see in dialysis. So, in medicine it can mean the deliberate 'breaking down' or removal of nerve tissue to relieve pain.
