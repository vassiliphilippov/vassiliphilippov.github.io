Etymology

gamma aminobutyric acid gets its name from its shape: the amino group sits on the third or gamma link of a small four-part chain called butyric acid. That name points to the molecule itself — often called GABA — which is a brain chemical that calms nerve cells and 'reduces nerve activity'.
