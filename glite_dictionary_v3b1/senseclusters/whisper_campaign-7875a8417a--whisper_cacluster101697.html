Etymology

whisper campaign comes from a simple image: whisper is a very quiet way to speak and campaign is a planned effort. Imagine people quietly sharing negative stories as part of a plan so the harm spreads slowly; that's why it means 'a secret attempt to spread rumors or negative information to harm someone's reputation'.
