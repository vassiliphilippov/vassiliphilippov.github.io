Etymology

Geococcyx californianus comes from old Greek and Latin words: geo meaning 'earth', coccyx meaning 'cuckoo', and californianus meaning 'from California'. So the name helps you picture a cuckoo-like bird that lives and runs on the ground in the California region, which is why it refers to the fast, ground-running greater roadrunner today.
