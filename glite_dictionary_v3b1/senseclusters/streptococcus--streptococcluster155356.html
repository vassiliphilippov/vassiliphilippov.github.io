Etymology

streptococcus comes from Greek parts strepto- meaning 'twisted' and -coccus meaning 'berry'. That gives a simple image: these bacteria are round like small 'berries' that join in a twisted chain, so the name streptococcus tells you about their round shape and how they form chains and can cause infections.
