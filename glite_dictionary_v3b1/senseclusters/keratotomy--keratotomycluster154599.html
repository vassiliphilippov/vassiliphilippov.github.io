Etymology

keratotomy comes from two Greek parts: kerato- meaning 'horn' and -tomy meaning 'cutting', and kerato- is the same root as keratin, which is found in hair and nails. So a keratotomy is literally a surgical 'cut' in the cornea, the clear front of the eye, used to change its shape and improve vision.
