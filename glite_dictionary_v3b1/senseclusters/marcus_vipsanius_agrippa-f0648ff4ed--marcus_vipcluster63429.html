Etymology

Marcus Vipsanius Agrippa is the name of a Roman general and ship builder who was Octavian's close friend. He led and fixed the ships that won the great sea battle at Actium, so his name is remembered for helping 'defeat Antony and Cleopatra' and bring Octavian to power.
