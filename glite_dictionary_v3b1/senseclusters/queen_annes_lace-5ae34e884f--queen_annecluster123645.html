Etymology

Queen Anne's lace is named for the way its flat group of small white flowers looks like a piece of lace, and one popular story says 'Queen Anne' pricked her finger while making lace, leaving a tiny red dot in the flower's center. That's why the wild plant, also called 'wild carrot', got the name.
