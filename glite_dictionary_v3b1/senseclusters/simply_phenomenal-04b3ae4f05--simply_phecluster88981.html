Etymology

simply phenomenal uses phenomenal, which comes from the word 'phenomenon' for a rare event you can see, like a bright comet or the northern lights. So, calling something simply phenomenal gives the clear picture that it is plainly and totally amazing, which is why it means 'extremely impressive'.
