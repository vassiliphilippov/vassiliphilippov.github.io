Etymology

The word squirmingly comes from the verb squirm, a word that feels like a worm twisting to get free and gives a clear picture of awkward, restless movement. With -ing and -ly it means 'in a way that shows discomfort or nervousness', so someone who shifts or wriggles is waiting or speaking squirmingly.
