Etymology

estragon names the herb also called tarragon and comes from old words that compare it to a dragon, with Latin dracunculus meaning 'little dragon' and similar Arabic names like tarkhūn. So, think of a little 'dragon' when you taste estragon: its strong, peppery flavor makes it a bold herb in cooking.
