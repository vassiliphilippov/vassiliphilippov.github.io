Etymology

gastric antacid is made of two parts: gastric (a word for 'stomach') and antacid (literally 'against acid'). So the name gives a clear picture — a medicine that goes to the stomach and works 'against acid', which is why it means a substance that neutralizes stomach acid and relieves heartburn.
