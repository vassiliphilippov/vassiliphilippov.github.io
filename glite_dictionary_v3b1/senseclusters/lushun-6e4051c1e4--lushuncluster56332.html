Etymology

Lushun comes from the Chinese name Lüshun, written 旅顺, and it was often called Port Arthur by foreigners. The Chinese characters mean 'travel' and 'smooth', so imagine ships moving smoothly into the harbour — that's why Lushun is known as an important port.
