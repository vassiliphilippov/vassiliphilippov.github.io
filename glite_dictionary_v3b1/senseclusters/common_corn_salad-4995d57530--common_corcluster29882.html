Etymology

common corn salad gets its name from the way it grew: the small plant often popped up in fields of corn (an old word for 'grain') and people picked its little leaves for salad. So, 'common' shows it was found a lot, and the name explains why it means the small leafy plant we eat as a 'salad green'.
