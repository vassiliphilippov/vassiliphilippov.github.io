Etymology

Third Council of Constantinople — the name comes from a large meeting of church leaders: the word Third shows it was the third important meeting, Council means a meeting, and Constantinople is the city where it took place. In 680–681 the leaders met and decided that Jesus has both God's will and a human will, so today the name refers to that specific meeting and its decisions about what the church believes.
