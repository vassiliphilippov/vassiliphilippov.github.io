Etymology

commencement exercise comes from the idea that commencement means 'beginning' rather than an end. Long ago, receiving a degree was seen as the start of a person's new life and exercise was a word for a formal public event, so the phrase means the formal ceremony that marks the start of a graduate's next stage.
