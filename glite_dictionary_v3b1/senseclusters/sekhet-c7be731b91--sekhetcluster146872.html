Etymology

The name Sekhet is linked to an old Egyptian word meaning 'power', and it is like the better-known goddess Sekhmet, whose name comes from sekhem 'power'. Think of a fierce lioness full of the sun's heat — that image explains why Sekhet is a sun goddess of both healing and destruction.
