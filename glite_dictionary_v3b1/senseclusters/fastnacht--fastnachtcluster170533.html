Etymology

The word fastnacht comes from two German parts, fast ('to fast', to not eat) and nacht ('night'), so it means the 'night before the fast'. That's why in Pennsylvania Dutch communities a fastnacht is a special doughnut eaten on Shrove Tuesday, the day before Lent.
