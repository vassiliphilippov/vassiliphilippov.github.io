Etymology

ipratropium bromide gets its name from the bits used to make the drug: the part ipra comes from an isopropyl chemical group and tropium links it to the old drug atropine, while bromide shows it is the drug's salt form. So the long name is a chemical label that tells you it is a medicine used 'to open the airways'.
