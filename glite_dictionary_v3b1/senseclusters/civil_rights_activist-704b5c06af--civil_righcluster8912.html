Etymology

civil rights activist comes from the words civil, meaning about 'citizens', rights, meaning basic freedoms, and activist, meaning someone who 'acts' to make change. Picture people marching, speaking and organising to win fair treatment, and you see why the term means a person who works actively for equal rights.
