Etymology

The medical name carpal tunnel syndrome comes from a real carpal 'wrist' tunnel made by bones and a tight band where an important nerve runs and can be squeezed. When that nerve is pressed, people feel 'pain, numbness or weakness' in the hand, so the name explains the problem.
