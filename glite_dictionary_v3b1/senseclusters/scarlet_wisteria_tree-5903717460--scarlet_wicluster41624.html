Etymology

Scarlet wisteria tree gets its name from its bright red flowers—the word scarlet means red—and from the way its long hanging groups of flowers look like the plant wisteria. The word tree shows it is a tree rather than a climbing plant, so the name helps you picture a tree with red, hanging blossoms.
