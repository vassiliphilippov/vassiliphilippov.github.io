Etymology

wild rye names a grass that is literally a wild form of rye growing naturally in fields, along rivers, and on hills. Imagine tall, untended rye bending in the wind, helping to keep the soil and giving 'food for animals', so the name points to a wild grass used for 'animal food' and to 'keep soil in place'.
