Etymology

Hypericum perforatum gets its common name 'St John's wort' because it often blooms around 'St John's Day' in late June, and 'wort' is an old word for a healing plant. The second word perforatum means 'perforated' and refers to the tiny oil dots on the leaves that look like small holes when you hold them to the light, so the name helps you remember this yellow medicinal plant.
