Etymology

Fabricator comes from the root fabric- meaning 'to make' or 'build', the same root as fabricate and fabric. Imagine a workshop where a maker makes metal parts or pieces; a fabricator is a person, company, or factory that 'makes' or assembles products, so the word means a maker of parts.
