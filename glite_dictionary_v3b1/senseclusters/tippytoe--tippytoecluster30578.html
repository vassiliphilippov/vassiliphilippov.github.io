Etymology

tippytoe comes from the simple image of standing on the very end of your toes: tip ('end' or 'point') plus toe, and it is like the common word tiptoe. The extra -y makes the word sound small or playful, so to tippytoe is to walk quietly on your toes to avoid making noise.
