Etymology

Pink paper daisy gets its name from the flower's bright pink color, its thin, dry petals that look like paper, and its small daisy-like shape. Because these 'paper' petals dry well and keep their color, gardeners often dry the flowers, so the name explains both how it looks and how people use it.
