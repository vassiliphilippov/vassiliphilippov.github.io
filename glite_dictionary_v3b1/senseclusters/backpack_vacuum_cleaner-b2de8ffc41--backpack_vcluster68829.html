Etymology

The name backpack vacuum cleaner comes from the simple image of a vacuum cleaner worn on your back like a backpack, so the machine sits in a pack while you walk. That's why a backpack vacuum cleaner means a portable vacuum you wear on your back to 'move more easily' and be 'less tiring' while cleaning.
