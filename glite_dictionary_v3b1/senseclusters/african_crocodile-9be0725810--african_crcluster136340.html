Etymology

African crocodile is a name that uses African for the place and crocodile for the animal, so it simply labels a crocodile that lives in Africa. Picture a large crocodile lying on warm river stones with most of its body in the water, ready to bite, and that image helps you remember it means 'a big, dangerous crocodile from Africa'.
