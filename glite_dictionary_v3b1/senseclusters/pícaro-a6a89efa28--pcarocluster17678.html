Etymology

pícaro comes from Spanish and names a clever rogue or rascal, a common figure in classic stories who lives by quick tricks. A helpful way to remember it is to picture a street-smart trickster who 'picks' his way through life, which is why a literary pícaro is a clever rogue who survives by wit.
