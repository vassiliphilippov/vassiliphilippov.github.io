Etymology

apricot sauce comes from the simple idea of cooking apricots into a smooth sauce; cooks often use apricot preserves or a fruit puree to make a glaze or dip. That's why apricot sauce now means 'a sweet sauce made from apricots', often used with meats or desserts.
