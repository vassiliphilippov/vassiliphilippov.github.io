Etymology

hit the waves: The phrasal verb hit the waves comes from the image of a surfboard or small boat going out onto the sea; the verb hit and the noun waves show moving into the water to ride the surf. That's why it now means 'to go surfing' or 'to head out onto the sea for fun'.
