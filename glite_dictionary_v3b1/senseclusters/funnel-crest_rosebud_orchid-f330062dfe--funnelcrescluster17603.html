Etymology

Funnel-crest rosebud orchid may come from how the flower looks: one part of the bloom forms a small funnel, a raised line looks like a crest, and the whole bloom looks like a tiny rosebud. So the name helps you picture a small orchid with 'pink' funnel-shaped flowers.
