Etymology

cor anglais literally means cor 'horn' and anglais 'English' in French, even though the instrument is not a true horn and is not really English. One popular story says the name may come from an old word meaning 'angled' because early instruments had a bent tube, and that's why today cor anglais means the lower, mellow instrument similar to an oboe.
