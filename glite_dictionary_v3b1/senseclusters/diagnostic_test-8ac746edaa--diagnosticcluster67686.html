Etymology

diagnostic test: The compound diagnostic meaning 'to find the cause' and test meaning a check came together to name a tool that looks for problems. Imagine a detective checking clues to find what is wrong, and that's why it means a medical or scientific procedure used to find the cause of a disease or problem.
