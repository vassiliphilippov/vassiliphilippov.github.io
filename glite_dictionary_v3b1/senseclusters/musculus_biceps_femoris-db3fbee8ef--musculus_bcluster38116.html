Etymology

musculus biceps femoris comes from Latin and literally means 'two-headed muscle of the thigh'. The parts musculus ('muscle'), biceps ('two heads') and femoris ('of the thigh') explain why it is the muscle at the back of the thigh that helps 'bend the knee' and 'turn the leg outward'.
