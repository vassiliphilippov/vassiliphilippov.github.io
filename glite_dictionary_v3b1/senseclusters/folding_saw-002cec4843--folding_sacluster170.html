Etymology

folding saw is a simple, literal name: the word saw and the idea of folding explain it, because the blade folds into the handle so the sharp part is hidden. Because the blade tucks away, the tool is easy to carry and 'safe' to store, so the term means a small, 'portable' saw used for camping and light work.
