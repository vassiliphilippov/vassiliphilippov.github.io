Etymology

The phrase bitch goddess comes from joining bitch, a rude word for a harsh or bossy woman, and goddess, a woman with great power who is admired. Imagine a strict, strong woman whom people both fear and admire, and you can see why the phrase means 'a powerful, assertive woman who is often regarded as domineering or intimidating'.
