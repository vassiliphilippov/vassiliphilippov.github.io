Etymology

abcoulomb comes from the prefix ab- meaning 'absolute' and the unit name coulomb, named after the scientist Coulomb. So an abcoulomb is a larger electrical charge equal to ten coulombs, which helps you remember it as a big version of the familiar coulomb.
