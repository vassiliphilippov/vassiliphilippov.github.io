Etymology

Bandura likely comes from the Ukrainian word bandura, which is a traditional 'stringed instrument' similar to a 'lute' or 'guitar'. It probably began as a nickname or job name for someone who played or made the instrument, so you can remember the name Bandura as 'the lute player'.
