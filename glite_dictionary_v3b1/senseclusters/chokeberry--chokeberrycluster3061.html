Etymology

chokeberry comes from the parts choke ('to have trouble breathing') and berry ('a small fruit'). A helpful way to remember the berry is that it is very tart and can make your mouth feel tight or make you cough, so the fruit earned the name for its strong, almost 'choking' taste.
