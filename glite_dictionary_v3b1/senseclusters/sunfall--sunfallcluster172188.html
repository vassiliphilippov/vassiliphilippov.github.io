Etymology

sunfall comes from the simple parts sun and fall, where sun means 'the bright star that gives light' and fall means 'to drop'. Imagine the sun literally dropping from the sky in a myth, and that image helps you remember this sense of sunfall as a legendary event that brings great disaster.
