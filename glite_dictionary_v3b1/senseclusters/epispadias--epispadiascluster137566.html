Etymology

The word epispadias comes from Greek parts epi- meaning 'on' and spadias meaning 'slit' or 'opening', and epi- is the same 'on' you see in epicenter and epidermis. So epispadias describes a condition where the tube that carries urine opens 'on' the top of the penis instead of at the tip.
