Etymology

care a lottle is a playful, funny change of the words care, a, and lottle, joking with the old phrase 'care a little' by mixing 'little' and 'lot' into a tiny-sounding word. So it gives the image of only a very small 'lottle' of care, which is why it means 'to care only a little'.
