Etymology

The word agamous starts with a- meaning 'not' and comes from gamos, a Greek word for 'marriage' or 'joining', the same root you see in monogamy. So agamous literally means 'without joining', which helps you remember it describes reproduction that happens without the joining of male and female sex cells.
