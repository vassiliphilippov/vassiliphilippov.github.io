Etymology

banking industry may come from a simple market image: long ago moneylenders sat at a wooden bench called a 'banco', so banking meant the work done at those benches. So, when many of these 'banks' and their work were joined, people called the whole industry the banking industry, meaning all the 'banks' and their 'business', which is why today it refers to the whole area of banks and services.
