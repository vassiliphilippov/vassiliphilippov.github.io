Etymology

daisy fleabane comes from two simple ideas: the tiny white flowers look like a small daisy, and people long ago thought the plant would 'drive away' fleas, so they called it fleabane. So the name helps you remember it is a small daisy-like wildflower often found in fields and along roadsides.
