Etymology

sleeping accommodation comes from putting sleeping, meaning 'to rest', and accommodation, meaning 'a place to stay', together. So it gives a clear picture of a space made mainly for sleeping, which is why it means a room or area for sleeping in hotels, trains, or other short-term places.
