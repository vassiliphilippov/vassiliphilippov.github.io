Etymology

effusiveness comes from the Latin parts ex- 'out' and fundere 'to pour', so it gives the picture of 'pouring out'. That's why an effusive person seems to 'pour out' their feelings or warm words, a meaning you can spot in the related English word diffuse ('spread out').
