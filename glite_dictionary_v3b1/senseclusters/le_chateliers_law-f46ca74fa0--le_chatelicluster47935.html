Etymology

Le Chatelier's law is named after a French chemist, but the easiest image is a simple balance: if you push one side, the balance moves to push back. So when a chemical system is changed by temperature, pressure, or the amount of substances, it 'pushes back' by shifting in a way that partly cancels the change, which is why the law helps predict how reactions will move.
