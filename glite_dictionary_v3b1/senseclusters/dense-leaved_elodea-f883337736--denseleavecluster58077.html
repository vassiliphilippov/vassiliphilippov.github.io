Etymology

dense-leaved elodea is a simple name: dense-leaved means the leaves are packed closely together, and elodea is the common name for a water plant. Picture a thick green underwater bush that helps 'add oxygen to the water' in fish tanks — that's why the name refers to a deep-green plant used in aquariums.
