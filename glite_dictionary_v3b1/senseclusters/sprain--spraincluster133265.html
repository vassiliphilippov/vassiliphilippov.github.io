sprain

1/m3.9
An injury to a joint when the strong bands that hold it stretch or tear, causing pain and swelling
noun US / spreɪn /

Etymology

sprain comes from an old word like press, which means 'to press' or 'to twist', so a sprain is when a joint is suddenly forced or twisted and becomes painful.
