Etymology

echo sounding comes from the simple idea of sending a sound into the water and listening for the echo that comes back. The sounding is the measuring: by timing how long the echo takes, people can find the 'depth' or where things are under the water, so echo sounding means a method to measure depth or locate underwater objects.
