Etymology

The phrase homing device comes from the idea of pigeons that always find their way 'home'. The word homing means moving toward 'home' and device means a small machine. So a homing device is a small machine that makes a missile or vehicle move toward a target, which is why the name means 'a machine that finds its target'.
