Etymology

ditch weed comes from the simple picture of cannabis plants growing wild in the sides of roads and field ditches and being treated like ordinary weeds no one wants. Because these plants grow without care and are often weak, the phrase means 'wild marijuana' or 'low-quality cannabis'.
