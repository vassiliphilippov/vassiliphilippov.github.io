Etymology

Brevoortia is a scientific name taken from the surname Brevoort, the person it was named for. The ending -ia often means 'belonging to' in scientific names, so Brevoortia literally means 'the fish of Brevoort', which helps you remember it is the name for a group of small, oily fish.
