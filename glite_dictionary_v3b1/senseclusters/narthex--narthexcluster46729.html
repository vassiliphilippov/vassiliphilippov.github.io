Etymology

narthex comes from the Greek narthēx, the name of a long plant called 'giant fennel' whose stiff stem was used like a 'stick'. Early churches had a long covered porch at the front where people could wait, and that porch took the name, so narthex now means the entrance hall or porch of a church.
