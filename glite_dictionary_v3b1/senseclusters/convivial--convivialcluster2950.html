Etymology

convivial comes from Latin parts con- 'with' and vivere 'to live', and the Latin convivium meant 'a meal together'. The same viv root is in revive, which means 'to bring back to life'. So, convivial describes people or places that enjoy being 'with' others and lively company, meaning cheerful and sociable.
