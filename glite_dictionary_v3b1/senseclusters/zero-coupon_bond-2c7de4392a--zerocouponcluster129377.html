Etymology

zero-coupon bond comes from old paper bonds where a coupon was a small slip you clipped to get interest and zero shows there are none. Because there are no coupons the bond pays 'no regular interest' and is sold for less now with the full amount paid back later, so the name helps you remember how it works.
