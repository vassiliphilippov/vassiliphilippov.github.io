Etymology

Ro is the name of a made-up language created to avoid ordinary words and to sort ideas by meaning into an abstract system. A helpful way to remember Ro is to think of it like a neat 'row' of boxes for ideas, because the language gives each idea its own place and so feels more like a system of meanings than a list of borrowed words.
