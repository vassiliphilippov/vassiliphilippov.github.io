Etymology

The noun white thistle gets its name from the plant's sharp prickles and its large pale blooms: the bright white flowers stand out while the stems and leaves are very prickly. So white shows the flower color and thistle shows the prickly plant, which is why people call this tropical 'prickly weed' a white thistle.
