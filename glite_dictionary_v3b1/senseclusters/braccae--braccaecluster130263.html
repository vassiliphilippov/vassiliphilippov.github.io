Etymology

The word braccae comes from an old Celtic word for loose 'trousers' that the Romans borrowed from the people they met. That's why braccae means the loose pants worn by Celts and some Roman soldiers, and the same root lives on in English breeches, which helps you remember these were old-style trousers.
