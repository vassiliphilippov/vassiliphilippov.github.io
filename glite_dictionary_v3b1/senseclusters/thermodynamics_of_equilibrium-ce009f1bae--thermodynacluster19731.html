Etymology

thermodynamics of equilibrium paints a simple picture: a hot cup of tea cooling until it matches the room's temperature, when heat stops moving and everything is in balance. Thermodynamics is the study of heat and energy and equilibrium is that 'not changing' balanced state, so the phrase means the study of systems in a steady, balanced condition.
