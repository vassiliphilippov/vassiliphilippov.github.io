Etymology

The word chron comes from the Greek chronos, meaning 'time', and you see the same root in familiar words like chronicle and synchronize. In geology a chron is a period of Earth's history marked by one direction of the planet's magnetic field, so the name reminds us it is a unit of 'time'.
