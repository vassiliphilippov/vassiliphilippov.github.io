Etymology

bark yourself comes from an old saying, 'Why keep a dog and bark yourself?'. The picture of someone making the noise the dog should make—having to bark while the dog sits—shows doing a job yourself when you could have someone else do it, so it means 'to do something yourself instead of letting others help'.
