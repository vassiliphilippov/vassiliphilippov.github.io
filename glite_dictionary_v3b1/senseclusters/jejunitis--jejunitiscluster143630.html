Etymology

jejunitis comes from the root jejun-, from a Latin word meaning 'empty' because the middle part of the small intestine was often found empty, plus the ending -itis, which means 'inflammation' and appears in words like tonsillitis. So jejunitis means 'inflammation of the jejunum', the middle section of the small intestine.
