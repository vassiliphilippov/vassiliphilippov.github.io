Etymology

Odd-job man comes from the simple picture of a man who does many small, different jobs. Imagine someone who comes to paint a fence, fix a gate or mend a leak, so the idea of odd meaning 'various' and job plus man explains why it means 'a man who does small repairs and jobs'.
