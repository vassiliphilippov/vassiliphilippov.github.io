Etymology

Phytelephas macrocarpa is a palm with hard, white nuts that look like little pieces of elephant ivory. The name includes Phytelephas, from words for 'plant' and 'elephant', and macrocarpa, meaning 'big fruit', so the image of an 'elephant plant' helps you remember that its seeds are used like 'ivory' today.
