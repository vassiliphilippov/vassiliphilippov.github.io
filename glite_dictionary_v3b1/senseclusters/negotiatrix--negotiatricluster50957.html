Etymology

The word negotiatrix is the female form of negotiator, coming from the Latin root negotium ('business') and the ending -trix ('female doer'). In Latin the parts ne- ('not') and otium ('leisure') meant 'not free time', so a negotiatrix is a woman who works at making deals, which is why it means 'a female negotiator'.
