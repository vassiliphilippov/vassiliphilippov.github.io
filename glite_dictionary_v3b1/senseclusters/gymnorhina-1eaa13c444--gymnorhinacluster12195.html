Etymology

Gymnorhina comes from ancient Greek parts gymno- meaning 'naked' and -rhina meaning 'nose', the same rhino part you see in rhinoceros. So the name means 'naked nose' and refers to the bird's bare skin around its beak, which helps you picture the Australian magpie.
