Etymology

noble-mindedness combines noble (meaning 'high' or 'honourable') and mind (meaning 'thoughts' or 'attitude'), so it shows a person whose thoughts are 'high' and good. That's why noble-mindedness means having strong moral ideals and acting with fairness and generosity.
