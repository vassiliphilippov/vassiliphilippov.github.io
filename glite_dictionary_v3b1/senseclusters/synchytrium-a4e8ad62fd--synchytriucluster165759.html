Etymology

Synchytrium comes from Greek parts: syn- meaning 'together' (the same syn- you see in 'sync') and chytrium from a Greek word for a 'little pot'. Think 'little pots together' — this image helps you remember that Synchytrium is a group of fungi with small, pot-like bodies, which is why scientists gave it that name.
