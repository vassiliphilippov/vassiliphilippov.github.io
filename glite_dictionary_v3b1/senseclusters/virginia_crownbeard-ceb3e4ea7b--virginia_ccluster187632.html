Etymology

Virginia crownbeard may come from the way its white flower clusters sit like a small crown at the top of a tall stem, while the long, hair-like leaves or bristles look like a beard. The word Virginia points to the area where the plant is common, so the name helps you remember it as a 'tall herb with clusters of white flowers'.
