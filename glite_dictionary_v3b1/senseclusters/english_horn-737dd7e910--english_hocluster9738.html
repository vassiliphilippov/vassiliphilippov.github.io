Etymology

English horn may sound like an instrument from England, but it is not a horn and not from England; one popular story says the name came from an old word meaning 'bent' because the instrument's tube is curved, so people began to call it 'English horn'. This story helps explain why the name refers to a longer, lower kind of oboe with a deep, warm sound.
