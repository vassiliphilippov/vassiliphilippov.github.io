Etymology

waxy appearance comes from the smooth, shiny surface of candle wax, so imagine waxy skin like a candle, glossy and a little stiff, and the appearance looks like wax. So when people use it, they mean the skin or surface seems 'smooth' or 'glossy' like wax.
