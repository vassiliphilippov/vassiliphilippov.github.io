Etymology

gringa for the Mexican cheese-and-pork tortilla probably comes from Spanish gringa ('foreign woman'), from gringo ('foreigner'). One popular story says people called this wheat-flour taco 'gringa' because the flour and style looked 'American', which helps explain the food name.
