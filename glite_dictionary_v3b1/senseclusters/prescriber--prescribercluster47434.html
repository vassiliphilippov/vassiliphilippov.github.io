Etymology

prescriber comes from the parts pre- ('before') and scribe ('write'), the same root you see in script and prescription. A helpful way to remember this sense is to think of a prescriber as someone who 'writes ahead' the rules or instructions others must follow, so it means a person or group that sets standards.
