Etymology

The noun multifocal lens implant comes from the idea of a single artificial lens put inside the eye that has several focus areas, like many small lenses in one piece. Here multifocal means 'many focus points', lens is the clear piece that helps the eye focus, and implant means it is put into the eye, so it helps a person see both 'near' and 'far' objects.
