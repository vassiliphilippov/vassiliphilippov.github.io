Etymology

The noun Astroloma humifusum may come from old Greek and Latin words: Astroloma from a word for 'star' because the flowers look like small stars, and humifusum from a word for 'on the ground' because the shrub grows low and spreads. That's why the name describes a low, spreading plant with star-like flowers.
