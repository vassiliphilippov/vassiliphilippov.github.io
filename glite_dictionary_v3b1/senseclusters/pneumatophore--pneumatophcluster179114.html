Etymology

pneumatophore comes from the Greek root pneuma meaning 'breath' and the ending -phore meaning 'bearer'. So a pneumatophore is a 'breath-bearer', an air-filled root that carries air to help a plant breathe, and you can remember pneuma from pneumonia, which is a lung illness about breathing.
