Etymology

stagecoach comes from the parts stage, which once meant a 'stop' on a route, and coach, a 'vehicle' for people. A stagecoach was a horse-drawn coach that went from 'stop' to 'stop', changing horses at each stage, which is why it ran on regular routes between towns before trains.
