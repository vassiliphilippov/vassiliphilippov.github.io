Etymology

third law of thermodynamics says that a perfect crystal, a solid where every atom sits in order, has almost no 'entropy' or 'disorder' as the temperature gets close to absolute zero because the tiny motion that makes disorder stops. So the law explains why 'entropy' goes to zero at very low temperature.
