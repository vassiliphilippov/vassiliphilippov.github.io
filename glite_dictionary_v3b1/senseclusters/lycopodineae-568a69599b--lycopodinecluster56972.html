Etymology

Lycopodineae comes from the plant name Lycopodium, which is made of the Greek parts lykos and podion, meaning 'wolf' and 'little foot'. Think of the plants' small, spreading stems like a 'wolf's foot', and that image helps explain why this name is used for the group of clubmoss plants.
