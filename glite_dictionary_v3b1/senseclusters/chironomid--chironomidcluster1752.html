Etymology

chironomid comes from the scientific name Chironomus, which comes from Greek parts cheir- 'hand' and -nomos 'arrange', and a helpful way to remember this is to imagine the little fly moving its front legs like tiny hands. So, chironomid today means a small non-biting midge, a kind of fly, that lives near water.
