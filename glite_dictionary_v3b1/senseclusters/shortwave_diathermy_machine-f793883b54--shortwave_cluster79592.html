Etymology

shortwave diathermy machine comes from the parts shortwave, diathermy, and machine. One simple story is that doctors use short radio waves to warm muscles deep under the skin — diathermy literally means 'heat through' — so the name means a 'machine' that makes 'deep heat' inside the body.
