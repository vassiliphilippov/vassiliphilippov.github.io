Etymology

The name fuel-air bomb comes from how it works: it first spreads fuel into the air, then the bomb lights that fuel cloud so it burns and explodes with a huge blast. So, the image of a burning fuel cloud explains why the term means 'a weapon that makes a very powerful explosion over a wide area'.
