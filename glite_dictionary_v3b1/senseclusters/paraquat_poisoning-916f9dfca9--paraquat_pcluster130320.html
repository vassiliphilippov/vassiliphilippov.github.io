Etymology

paraquat poisoning comes from the name of a strong farm chemical, paraquat, which is a powerful weed killer. If someone swallows or breathes in this chemical it attacks the body's organs like the lungs, liver and kidneys, so the phrase means 'poisoning caused by paraquat'.
