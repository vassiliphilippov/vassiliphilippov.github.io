Etymology

The word laccase comes from lacc-, a root meaning 'lac' or 'resin', the natural material used to make lacquer, plus the ending -ase meaning 'enzyme'. So laccase is an enzyme that breaks down organic materials, which is why enzymes called laccases from fungi and plants are used to treat wastewater and remove dyes.
