Etymology

postage stamp comes from the idea of a small piece of paper that shows the mail fee was 'paid'. The word postage means the cost to send a letter and stamp comes from marking or sticking a small paper to an envelope, so a postage stamp is the little paper proof you stick on mail to show payment.
