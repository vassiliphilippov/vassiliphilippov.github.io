Etymology

polyunsaturated fatty acid is a literal name: poly means 'many', unsaturated refers to having two or more 'double bonds' in the fat's chain, and fatty acid is the type of fat. So the phrase simply says it is a fat with many double bonds, which is why we use it for the healthy fats found in fish and some vegetable oils.
