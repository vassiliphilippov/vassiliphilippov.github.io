Etymology

vaginal smear comes from the simple action of collecting and spreading cells: doctors take cells from the vaginal lining and gently smear them on a glass slide to look at them under a microscope. So, the phrase means 'a sample of cells taken from the vagina to test for infection or abnormal cells'.
