Etymology

The phrase abstract nonsense comes from jokes by some mathematicians who used it for very general, idea-only arguments. They named the high-level part abstract and mock-called it nonsense because it felt far from real examples, so today it means ideas that are 'too theoretical' or 'meaningless'.
