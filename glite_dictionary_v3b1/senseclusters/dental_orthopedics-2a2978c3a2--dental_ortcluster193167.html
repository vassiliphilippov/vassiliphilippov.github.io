Etymology

dental orthopedics puts together dental (about 'teeth') and orthopedics (about straightening 'bones'), so imagine braces or small supports that gently guide the jaw and teeth into the right place. That's why dental orthopedics means preventing or correcting irregular 'teeth' and 'jaws'.
