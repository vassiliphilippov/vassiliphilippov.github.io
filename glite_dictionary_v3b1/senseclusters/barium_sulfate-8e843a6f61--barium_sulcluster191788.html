Etymology

The name barium sulfate comes from its two parts: barium, a heavy metal, and sulfate, a kind of salt that makes a very white powder. Because this heavy white powder 'shows up on X-rays', doctors use it to make the inside of the body visible, so the name tells us what it is and why it is used.
