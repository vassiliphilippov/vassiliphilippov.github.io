Etymology

cinnamene is named from cinnamon, the spice, plus the chemical ending -ene, which means 'a type of hydrocarbon'. So a helpful way to remember it is that cinnamene is a cinnamon-like oily chemical and the '-ene' shows it is a hydrocarbon used as a building block for plastics.
