Etymology

sneakiness comes from the verb sneak, which comes from an old word meaning 'to creep' or 'move quietly'. A helpful way to remember it is to imagine someone moving like a 'snake', quiet and hidden, so sneakiness means the habit of being sly and hiding what you do.
