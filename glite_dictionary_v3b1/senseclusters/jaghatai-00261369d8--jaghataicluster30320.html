Etymology

Jaghatai comes from the name of Genghis Khan's son Chagatai, so it originally meant 'Chagatai's language' and was used as a written Turkic language for books and poems long ago in Central Asia. That's why many modern Central Asian languages, like Uzbek, still have words and grammar from Jaghatai.
