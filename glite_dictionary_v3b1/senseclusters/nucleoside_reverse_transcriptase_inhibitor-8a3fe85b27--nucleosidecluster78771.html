Etymology

nucleoside reverse transcriptase inhibitor gives a simple picture: small blocks called nucleosides are used by a copying machine, reverse transcriptase, to build the virus's DNA, and an inhibitor is like a bad block that stops the machine. So the name refers to drugs that 'stop' that copying machine and keep the virus from making new copies.
