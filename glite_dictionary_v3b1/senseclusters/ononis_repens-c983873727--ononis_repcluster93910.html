Etymology

Ononis repens comes from old words: Ononis links to a Greek word for 'donkey' because donkeys ate the plant, and repens is Latin for 'creeping' because the plant spreads along the ground. The English name 'creeping restharrow' gives a clear picture too: its tough roots could stop a harrow (a farm tool), so the name helps you remember a low, spreading, tough plant.
