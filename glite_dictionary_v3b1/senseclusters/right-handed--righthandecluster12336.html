Etymology

right-handed for turning or spirals uses the same parts right ('turning to the right or clockwise') and hand ('your hand'), and a helpful way to remember it is to curl the fingers of your right hand because the way your fingers point shows the direction of a 'right-handed' spiral; that's why a 'right-handed' screw tightens when you turn it clockwise.
