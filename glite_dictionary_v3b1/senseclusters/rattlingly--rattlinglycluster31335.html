Etymology

rattlingly comes from the common verb rattle plus the endings -ing and -ly, so it literally means 'in a rattling way'. Think of something moving fast and making short, sharp noises, like an old truck on gravel, and that's why rattlingly describes very fast, energetic, noisy action.
