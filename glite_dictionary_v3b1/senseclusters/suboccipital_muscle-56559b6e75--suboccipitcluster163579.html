Etymology

The noun suboccipital muscle paints a simple picture: sub- means 'under' and occipital means the 'back of the skull', so these are the small muscles right under the base of the skull. They help turn and hold the head, so when these muscles are tight you may feel neck pain or have trouble moving your head.
