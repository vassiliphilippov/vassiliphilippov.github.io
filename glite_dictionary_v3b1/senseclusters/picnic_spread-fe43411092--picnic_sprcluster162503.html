Etymology

The noun picnic spread comes from the words picnic and spread. Imagine a blanket with plates, sandwiches and fruit laid out so everyone can help themselves: that laid-out food is a picnic spread, which is why the phrase means 'an assortment of food arranged and laid out for a picnic'.
