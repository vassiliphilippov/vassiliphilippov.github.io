Etymology

The symbol Star of David may come from the old name 'Magen David', which means 'shield of David'. One popular story says it was shown on King David's shield. The shape is made from two overlapping triangles, one pointing up and one pointing down, forming a six-pointed star, and that is why the Star of David is now a sign of 'Jewish identity'.
