Etymology

PFC stands for perfluorocarbon, where the prefix per- means 'completely' and fluorine is the element that covers the molecule. So a PFC is like a molecule 'completely covered' with fluorine, which makes it very stable and long-lasting in the air and therefore a powerful greenhouse gas from some industries.
