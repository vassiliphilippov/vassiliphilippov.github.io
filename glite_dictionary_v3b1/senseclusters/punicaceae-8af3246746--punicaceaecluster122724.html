Etymology

Punicaceae comes from the Latin word Punica, the name for the pomegranate, and Punica meant 'from Phoenicia' (a region by the Mediterranean). So Punicaceae literally names the plant family of the pomegranate, which is why the word refers to that fruit's family in botany.
