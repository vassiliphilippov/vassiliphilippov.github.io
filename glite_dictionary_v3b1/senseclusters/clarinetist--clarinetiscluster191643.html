Etymology

clarinetist comes from clarinet (a small wind instrument) plus -ist, a part that means 'a person who does something'. The name clarinet comes from the root clar- meaning 'clear' (like clear) and meant 'a small, bright trumpet', so a clarinetist is 'a person who plays the clarinet'.
