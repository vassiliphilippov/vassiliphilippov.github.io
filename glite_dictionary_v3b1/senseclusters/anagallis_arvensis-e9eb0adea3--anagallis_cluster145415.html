Etymology

Anagallis arvensis is a small wild flower whose bright blossoms close up just before rain; gardeners noticed this and called it the 'poor man's weather glass' because the plant seemed to show when bad weather was coming, and its red blooms led to the common name 'scarlet pimpernel'.
