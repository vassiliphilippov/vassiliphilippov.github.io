Etymology

The word aubergine came into English from French and originally from Arabic al-badinjan, a name that passed through Persian and Indian languages for the plant. A helpful way to remember it is that British aubergine is the same vegetable Americans call 'eggplant', because some old varieties were white and looked like an 'egg'.
