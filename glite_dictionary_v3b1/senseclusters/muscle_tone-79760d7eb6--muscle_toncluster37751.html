Etymology

Muscle tone comes from the simple idea of muscles holding a small, constant tension that doctors and therapists can feel by touch. The word muscle points to the body part and tone here means that gentle 'tension' or 'firmness', so 'muscle tone' describes how 'ready' muscles are for movement.
