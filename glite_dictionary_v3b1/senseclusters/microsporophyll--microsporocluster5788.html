Etymology

microsporophyll breaks into micro- 'small', sporo- 'spore' meaning a tiny reproductive part, and -phyll 'leaf', as in chlorophyll. So a microsporophyll is a 'small spore leaf', which explains why it is a leaf that bears microspores.
