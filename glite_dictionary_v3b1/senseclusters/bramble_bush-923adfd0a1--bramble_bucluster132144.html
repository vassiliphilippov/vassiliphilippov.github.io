Etymology

bramble bush paints a simple picture: a bramble is a low, tangled plant with sharp thorns and a bush is a small plant. People saw these thorny, berry-filled tangles that could scratch you when you reached for fruit, so bramble bush means 'a prickly shrub' that often has berries.
