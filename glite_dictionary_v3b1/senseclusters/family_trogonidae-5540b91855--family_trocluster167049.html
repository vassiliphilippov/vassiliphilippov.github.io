Etymology

family Trogonidae comes from the word trogon, which may come from an old Greek word meaning 'to gnaw' because these birds often peck and chew soft wood to make nest holes. So, family Trogonidae simply means the 'group' of trogon birds, the bright tropical birds you find in warm forests.
