Etymology

conformal projection comes from map-making: conformal means 'keeping small shapes the same' and projection is a way to show the globe on flat paper, like shining a light from a globe onto a screen. So a conformal projection 'keeps local shapes and angles' correct even if it stretches or shrinks area.
