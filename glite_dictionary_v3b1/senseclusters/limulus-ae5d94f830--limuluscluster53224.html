Etymology

Limulus is the Latin genus name given by early naturalists to the animal we call a 'horseshoe crab'. Scientists use this formal name for the living species Limulus polyphemus, so when you hear Limulus think of the horseshoe crab and its famous blue blood used in medical tests.
