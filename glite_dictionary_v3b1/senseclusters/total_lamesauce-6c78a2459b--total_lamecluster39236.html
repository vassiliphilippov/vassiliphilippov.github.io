Etymology

total lamesauce comes from putting together lame (meaning weak or silly) and sauce (something used to make food or a story seem better). Think of a thin, tasteless sauce that cannot hide a bad meal — so a poor, obvious excuse is called total lamesauce, meaning 'a very weak or unconvincing excuse'.
