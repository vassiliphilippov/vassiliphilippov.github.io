Etymology

guerrilla comes from Spanish: guerra meaning 'war' and the small ending -illa meaning 'small', so it originally meant 'small war'. That's why a guerrilla is a fighter in a small, irregular group who uses hit-and-run attacks instead of fighting as a full army.
