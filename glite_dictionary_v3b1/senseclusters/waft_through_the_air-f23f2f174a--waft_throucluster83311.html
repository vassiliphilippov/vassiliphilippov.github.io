Etymology

waft through the air gives a clear picture: imagine the smell of fresh bread or flowers lightly floating on a gentle breeze, with waft meaning a soft motion and through the air showing where it moves. That's why it means 'to move lightly and smoothly through the air', often used for scents, soft sounds, or light things.
