Etymology

bullpup likely comes from comparing the gun's short, strong look to a young bull dog or pup, often imagined as a 'bulldog puppy' that is short and stocky. So a bullpup gun has the parts that load and fire placed behind the trigger, making the whole weapon short and easy to handle while still keeping a long barrel.
