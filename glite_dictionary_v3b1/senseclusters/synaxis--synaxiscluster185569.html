Etymology

synaxis comes from the Greek part syn- meaning 'together' and an old word for 'gathering', so it literally means a 'coming together'. A helpful way to remember it is to think of synagogue or synchronize, which also use syn- for 'together', and a synaxis is therefore a religious coming together or service.
