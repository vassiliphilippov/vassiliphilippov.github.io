Etymology

The word commonplaceness comes from commonplace plus the ending -ness; common means 'shared' or 'ordinary' and place means 'place', so commonplace once named something everyone knew or kept in a shared book, and -ness makes it the 'state of being' that way. That's why commonplaceness means the 'quality of being ordinary' or unremarkable.
