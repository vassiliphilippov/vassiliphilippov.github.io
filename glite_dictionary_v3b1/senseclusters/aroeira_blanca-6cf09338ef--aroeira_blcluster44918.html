Etymology

aroeira blanca comes from two words: aroeira, a local name for a small tree that makes a sticky, fragrant resin, and blanca, which means 'white'. People called it this because the tree often has pale bark or white flowers and a scented sap used in traditional remedies, so the name helps you remember it is a small aromatic tree used in folk medicine.
