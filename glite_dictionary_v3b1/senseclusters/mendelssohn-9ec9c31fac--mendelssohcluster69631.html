Etymology

Mendelssohn is a family name that originally meant 'son of' Mendel. Like English names such as Johnson, the -sohn part means 'son', and Mendel is a Jewish form of a name that means 'comfort', so the name marked someone as 'Mendel's son', which explains why it is the surname of the composer Felix Mendelssohn.
