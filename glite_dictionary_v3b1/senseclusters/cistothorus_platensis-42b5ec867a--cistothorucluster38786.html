Etymology

The name Cistothorus platensis may come from old words that give a clear picture: Cistothorus suggests a tiny singer hiding in a low 'box' of grass, and platensis points to the wide, flat meadows where it lives. So the name helps you remember the small 'sedge wren' that lives and sings in wet meadows.
