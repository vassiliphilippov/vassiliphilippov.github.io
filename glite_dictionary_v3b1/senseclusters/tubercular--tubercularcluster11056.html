Etymology

tubercular comes from the Latin tuberculum, meaning 'small lump', and from the disease name tuberculosis, which causes 'tubercles' in the lungs. A helpful way to remember the noun tubercular is that it named a person suffering from that disease — a person affected by tuberculosis.
