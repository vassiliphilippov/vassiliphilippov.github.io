Etymology

Orlop deck was the name sailors gave to the very lowest deck on old sailing ships, the space below the others where they kept heavy stores and the ship's ropes and cables. Because it sat 'under' the other decks and was used for 'storage', the name now means the lowest deck used for keeping supplies.
