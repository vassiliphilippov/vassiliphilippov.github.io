Etymology

nerve plexus comes from the Latin word plexus, which means 'braid' or 'a woven shape', together with nerve for the body's nerves. Imagine many nerves crossing and twisting like a braid, and that's why nerve plexus means 'a network of joined nerves' that controls a part of the body.
