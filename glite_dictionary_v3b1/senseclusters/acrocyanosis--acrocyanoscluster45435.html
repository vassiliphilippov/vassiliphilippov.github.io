Etymology

Acrocyanosis breaks into three parts: acro- meaning 'tip' or 'end', cyan- meaning 'blue', as in the color cyan, and -osis meaning 'condition'. So acrocyanosis is a 'blue condition of the tips', which explains the bluish fingers or toes you get when blood flow is poor, especially in the cold.
