Etymology

galactic spaceship is a compound word that creates a simple picture: galactic means about the galaxy and spaceship is a ship that flies in space, so you picture a very large ship moving among stars. That's why the term means 'a spacecraft for travel between galaxies', often seen in 'science fiction'.
