Etymology

unalarmed is made of un- ('not') and alarm, which began as the battle cry all'arme meaning 'to the arms' and later came to mean a warning or a feeling of fear (think 'alarm bell'). So, in the sense of a person, unalarmed means 'not feeling worried or frightened'.
