Etymology

functionally illiterate comes from putting functionally ('in everyday use') together with illiterate ('cannot read or write'). Imagine a person who can read a few words but cannot 'function' at work, fill out forms, or follow written instructions, so the phrase means 'unable to use reading and writing for everyday tasks'.
