Etymology

I'll be a monkey's uncle may come from people who reacted with shock to the idea that humans are related to monkeys. Saying you'd be a monkey'suncle creates a funny, impossible picture and shows you do not believe something, so today the phrase means 'very surprised' or 'don't believe it'.
