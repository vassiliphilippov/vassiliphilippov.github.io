Etymology

The word paradichlorobenzene breaks into parts: para- ('opposite'), di- ('two'), chloro- ('chlorine') and benzene ('ring' in chemistry). So the name says 'two chlorine atoms opposite each other on a benzene ring', which fits the chemical often used in mothballs to keep insects away.
