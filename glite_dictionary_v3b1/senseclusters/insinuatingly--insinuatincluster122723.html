Etymology

Insinuatingly comes from Latin parts in- 'into' and sinus 'curve' and makes us think of something that slips in slowly. The same root is in sinuous ('curvy'). So, to speak insinuatingly is to slip a hint into someone's mind in a sly or indirect way.
