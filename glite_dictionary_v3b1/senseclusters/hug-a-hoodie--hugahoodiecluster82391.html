Etymology

hug-a-hoodie mixes the simple word hug ('a warm squeeze') with hoodie, a soft sweatshirt with a hood ('a head cover'). So, a hug-a-hoodie is an oversized, very cozy hoodie that feels like a wearable hug and is perfect for lounging and staying warm.
