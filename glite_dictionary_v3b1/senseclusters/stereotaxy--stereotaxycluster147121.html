Etymology

stereotaxy comes from Greek parts stereos ('solid') and taxis ('arrangement' or 'order'), so it literally means 'placing or arranging in space'. That's why stereotaxy is the precise, three-dimensional method doctors use to find or reach a point inside the brain.
