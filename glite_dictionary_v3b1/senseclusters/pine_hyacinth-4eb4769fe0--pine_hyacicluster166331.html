Etymology

pine hyacinth: The plant name pine hyacinth comes from the two words pine and hyacinth: pine because the plant often grows on or climbs pine trees, and hyacinth because its purple, bell-like flowers look like 'garden hyacinths'. So the name helps you remember it is a climbing plant with hyacinth-like blooms.
