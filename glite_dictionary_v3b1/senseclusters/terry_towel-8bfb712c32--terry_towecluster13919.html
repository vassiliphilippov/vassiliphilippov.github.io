Etymology

terry towel comes from terry, the name of a cloth that may come from an old French word for 'to pull', because cloth makers pulled up many small loops of thread on the surface. Those loops make the towel soft and very good at 'absorbing' water, so terry towel means a towel with a looped surface that dries you quickly.
