Etymology

wild cherry is a noun that comes from the simple picture of a wildcherry tree that grows naturally in the woods, not planted in a garden. Its small, tart fruits are often eaten by birds, so the name means 'a cherry that grows naturally' or 'a small, tart fruit'.
