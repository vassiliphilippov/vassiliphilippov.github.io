Etymology

non-insulin-dependent diabetes mellitus is a medical name that tells the meaning. non-insulin-dependent means it 'does not need insulin injections', and diabetes mellitus is an older name for the 'sugar' disease. So, the full term describes a milder adult form of diabetes often controlled by diet or pills rather than by insulin.
