Etymology

The word doxepin is a constructed drug name based on its chemical family, but a helpful way to remember it is to hear dox- like the English word 'doze' and -epin as the name ending. Because doxepin is used for depression, anxiety and to help with 'sleep', thinking 'doze' links the name to what it does.
