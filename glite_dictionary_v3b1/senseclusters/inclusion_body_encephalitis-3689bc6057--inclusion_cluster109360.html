Etymology

inclusion body encephalitis is a medical name that describes what doctors see: inclusion bodies are tiny 'clumps' inside brain cells and encephalitis means 'inflammation of the brain'. That's why the name points to a severe brain illness where the measles virus makes those clumps and causes damage.
