Etymology

The word knickerbockers comes from a playful Dutch name, Knickerbocker, that the American writer Washington Irving used for a comic old New Yorker. People began to call the short, baggy trousers he described 'knickerbockers', later shortened to knickers, so today the word means loose trousers gathered at the knee.
