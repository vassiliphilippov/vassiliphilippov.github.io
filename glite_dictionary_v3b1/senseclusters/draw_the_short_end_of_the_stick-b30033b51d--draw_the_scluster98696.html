Etymology

The idiom draw the short end of the stick comes from a simple game: people put sticks of different lengths in a pile and each person draws one, and getting the short end meant you lost and had to do the worst job. So, the phrase now means 'to be chosen for something unpleasant or to be at a disadvantage'.
