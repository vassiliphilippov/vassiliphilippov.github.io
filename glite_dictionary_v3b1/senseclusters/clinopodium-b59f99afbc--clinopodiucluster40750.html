Etymology

Clinopodium breaks into clino- and -podium, where clino- is like incline meaning 'to lean' and -podium is like pod meaning 'foot'. A helpful way to remember the name is to picture a 'little leaning foot', which fits many small, low-growing herbs in the Clinopodium group, such as wild basil, that sit close to the ground.
