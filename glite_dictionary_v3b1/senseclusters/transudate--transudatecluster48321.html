Etymology

transudate comes from Latin parts trans- 'across' and sudare 'to sweat', so it originally meant a liquid that 'sweats across' a membrane. That's a helpful image: a transudate is a clear, low-protein fluid that passes through tissues because of pressure, not because of inflammation.
