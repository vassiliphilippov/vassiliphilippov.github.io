Etymology

Sibley tent is named after an army officer, Henry Sibley, who designed a simple cone-shaped 'shelter' that stood on a single central tripod and had a top ventilator to let smoke and air out. That's why the name now means a large, light, cone-shaped military tent used for quick 'shelter'.
