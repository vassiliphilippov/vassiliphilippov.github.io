Etymology

Poppy Day comes from a strong image after World War I: bright red poppy flowers grew on battlefields and near soldiers' graves, and a famous poem linked the flower to the dead. Because of that image, people began wearing a red poppy on Poppy Day 'to remember' and 'honor' those who died.
