Etymology

The word honky-tonk comes from two parts, honky and tonk, and one popular story says tonk copies the 'tonk' sound of a cheap piano while honky named the white customers who went there, so it became a name for rough, cheap bars with music and dancing. That's why a honky-tonk means a lively, sometimes rough bar or dance hall, often with country music.
