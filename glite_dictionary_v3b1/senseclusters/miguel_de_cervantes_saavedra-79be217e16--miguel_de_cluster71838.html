Etymology

Miguel de Cervantes Saavedra was a Spanish writer best known for the book 'Don Quixote', which tells of a man who attacks windmills because he thinks they are giants. That strong picture of a man fighting windmills is an easy memory hook and helps explain why Cervantes is seen as one of the first writers of the modern novel.
