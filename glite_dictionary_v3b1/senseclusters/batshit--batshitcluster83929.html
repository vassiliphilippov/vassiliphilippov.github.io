Etymology

Batshit comes from two parts: bat, the animal used in English to suggest 'crazy', like in 'batty' or 'bats in the belfry', and shit, a strong swear word that makes the idea more extreme. So, to call something batshit is to call it 'extremely crazy' or wildly unreasonable.
