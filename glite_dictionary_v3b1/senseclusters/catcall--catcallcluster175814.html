Etymology

catcall comes from the parts cat and call, from the idea of a 'sharp' sound like a cat's cry or a small theatre whistle used to show 'disapproval'. A helpful way to remember it is that to catcall someone is to make that rude, high 'call' at them — a shout or whistle aimed at a person.
