Etymology

ridgeway comes from ridge ('a long raised line or the top of a hill') and way ('a road or path'), so it literally means a path along the ridge. That's why a ridgeway is a road that runs along the top of a hill, like walking on the land's 'backbone' to enjoy wide views.
