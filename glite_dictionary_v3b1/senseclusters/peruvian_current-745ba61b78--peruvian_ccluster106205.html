Etymology

Peruvian current comes from the cold stream of water that runs along the coast of Peru and was named for Peruvian (Peru) and the moving water, the current. Fishermen and sailors noticed this 'cold ocean current' brings many fish and life to the coast, so the name now means that cold flow of water near Peru.
