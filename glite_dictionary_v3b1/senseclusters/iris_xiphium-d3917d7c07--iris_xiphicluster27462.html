Etymology

The plant name Iris xiphium comes from old Greek words: Iris means 'rainbow' and xiphium comes from a word for 'sword'. People named it this because the flower has bright, rainbow-like colors and long, sword-shaped leaves, so the name describes how the plant looks today.
