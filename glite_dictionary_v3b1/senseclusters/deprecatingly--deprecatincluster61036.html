Etymology

deprecatingly comes from the verb deprecate, with parts de- 'away' and precari 'to pray', the same root as prayer. A helpful way to remember the disapproving sense is to think of it as 'asking to push something away', so to speak about something deprecatingly is to move its value away with words and show dislike or criticism.
