Etymology

benevolent dictator puts benevolent, meaning 'kind', next to dictator, meaning 'a person who decides alone'. Imagine a ruler who has all the power but uses it to help people instead of to hurt them, so the phrase means 'a ruler who holds absolute power but uses that power for the good of the people'.
