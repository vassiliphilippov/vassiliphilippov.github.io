Etymology

amberbell combines amber and bell; amber means a warm 'yellow-brown' colour and bell means 'bell-shaped'. It names an eastern North American plant with single yellow flowers often marked with brown or purple and spotted inside, so the name helps you picture a small amber-coloured bell of a flower.
