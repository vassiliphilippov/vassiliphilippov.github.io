Etymology

Hypoxis comes from Greek parts hypo- 'under' and oxys 'sharp', the same hypo- you see in hypodermic and the oxys part you can think of in oxygen. A helpful way to remember Hypoxis is to picture a small, grass-like plant with thin leaves and a sharp, star-shaped yellow flower above the ground.
