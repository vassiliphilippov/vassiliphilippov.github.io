Etymology

Deinstitutionalization breaks into de- meaning 'remove', institution meaning a 'place or system set up to care for people', and -ization meaning 'the process of'. So, deinstitutionalization is the process of moving people out of large institutions, like psychiatric hospitals, and into care in their own communities, which explains its modern meaning.
