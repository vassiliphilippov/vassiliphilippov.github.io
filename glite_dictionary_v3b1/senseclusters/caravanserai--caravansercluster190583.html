Etymology

The word caravanserai comes from Persian and Turkish parts: caravan means 'a group of travelers and their animals' and serai means 'palace' or 'inn', so it paints the image of a 'palace for caravans'. That's why a caravanserai is a roadside inn with a large courtyard where travelers and caravans could rest.
