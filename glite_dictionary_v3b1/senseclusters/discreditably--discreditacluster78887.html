Etymology

discreditably comes from dis- meaning 'not' and credit meaning 'trust' or 'praise', as in 'credit card' or when you 'give someone credit'. So to act discreditably is to behave in a way that makes people stop trusting or praising you, causing shame or a loss of respect.
