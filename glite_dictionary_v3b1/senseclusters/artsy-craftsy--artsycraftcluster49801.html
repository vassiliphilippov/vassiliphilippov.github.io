Etymology

artsy-craftsy mixes art and craft with a playful -sy ending to make a rhyme, so it sounds cute and informal. That's why the word is used for things that try to be 'creative' or 'homemade' but feel a little amateur or overly decorative, meaning art-like but slightly fake or too busy.
