Etymology

cycloidal comes from the Greek root cycl- meaning 'circle' and the ending -oid meaning 'like' or 'shape'. You can see the same cycl- in cycle and bicycle, so cycloidal simply means 'circle-like' and is used for arches, paths, or shapes that look like part of a circle.
