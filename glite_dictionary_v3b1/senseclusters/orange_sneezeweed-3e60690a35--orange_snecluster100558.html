Etymology

orange sneezeweed may come from a folk use: people dried the plant and used its dust as a powder 'to make someone sneeze', so they called it sneezeweed. The word orange refers to its bright flower color, and that's why the name today points to the orange-flowered plant that can also 'make animals ill'.
