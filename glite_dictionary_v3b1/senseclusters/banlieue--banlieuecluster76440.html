Etymology

The word banlieue comes from two old French parts: ban meaning 'authority' or 'public order' and lieue meaning 'a league', an old measure of distance. It originally named the land just outside a city's official area — the place under the city's ban and a short distance away — so today banlieue means the suburbs outside a French city, sometimes linked with social or economic problems.
