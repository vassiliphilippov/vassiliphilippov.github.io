Etymology

The idiom wipe the floor with comes from the simple image of cleaning: when you wipe a dirty floor you drag a cloth or mop so the dirt goes away. So, to wipe the floor with someone means to beat them very easily, like removing dirt, and that is why it means 'to defeat someone completely'.
