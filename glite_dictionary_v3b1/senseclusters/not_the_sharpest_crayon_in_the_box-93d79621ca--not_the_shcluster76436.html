Etymology

not the sharpest crayon in the box creates a picture of a box of crayons where some crayons are very sharp and one is blunt and cannot draw fine lines. So, saying someone is not the sharpest crayon in the box means 'not very smart', using the crayon image to make the idea easy to remember.
