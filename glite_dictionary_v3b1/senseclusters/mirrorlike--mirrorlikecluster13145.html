Etymology

The word mirrorlike comes from two parts: mirror, from an old word meaning 'to look' or 'to wonder', and -like, which means 'similar to'. So something mirrorlike is 'similar to a mirror' and has a smooth, shiny surface that reflects light and gives a clear image.
