Etymology

Vioxx is a modern brand name likely made by joining Vio (a short, catchy start) with COX, the name of a body enzyme linked to pain and swelling. So, a helpful way to remember Vioxx is to think of a medicine that targets COX to reduce 'pain' and 'inflammation'.
