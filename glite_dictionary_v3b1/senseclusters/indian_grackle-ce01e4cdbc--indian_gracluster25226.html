Etymology

The compound noun Indian grackle combines place and bird: Indian shows where the bird comes from and grackle is an old name for noisy black birds that often copy sounds. One popular idea is that people noticed this glossy Asian starling can 'mimic' human voices, so the name helps us remember it is a black, talkative bird.
