Etymology

Sanson-Flamsteed projection gets its name from two mapmakers, Sanson and Flamsteed, whose methods were joined into one way of drawing maps. This 'projection' is a way of drawing the world on a flat map that keeps areas correct, so it's often used for maps showing regions near the equator.
