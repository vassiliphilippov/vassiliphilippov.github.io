Etymology

human T-cell leukemia virus-1 is a clear name: human means it infects people, T-cell is a kind of white blood cell it attacks, leukemia means 'blood cancer', and virus-1 shows it was the first such virus found. So the name tells you it is a virus that infects T-cells and can lead to leukemia.
