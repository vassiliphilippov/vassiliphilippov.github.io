Etymology

The word centigrade is made of centi- meaning 'hundred', as in century or cent, and -grade meaning 'step' or 'degree', like the English grade. It was named because the scale has 100 steps between water's freezing point '0' and its boiling point '100', so centigrade refers to that 0 to 100 temperature scale.
