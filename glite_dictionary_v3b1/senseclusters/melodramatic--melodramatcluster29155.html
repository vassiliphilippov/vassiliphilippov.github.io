Etymology

melodramatic comes from melody ('song') and drama ('play'), and from the older word melodrama for a musical play full of strong feelings. Because those shows used loud music and big, over-the-top acting, something 'melodramatic' today means showing emotions in an exaggerated, theatrical way.
