Etymology

2 Esdras takes its name from Esdras, the Latin form of the Hebrew figure 'Ezra'. The '2' shows it was treated as a second book linked to him by old church writers, so the title means 'the second book connected to Ezra' and that is why this religious book is called 2 Esdras today.
