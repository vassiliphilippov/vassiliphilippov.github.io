Etymology

401(k) plan gets its name from a part of U.S. tax law called 401(k), a rule from the 1970s that let workers save money 'before tax' for 'retirement'. So today 401(k) plan means a personal 'retirement' savings plan where you put money from your pay 'before tax' and often your employer adds some money too.
