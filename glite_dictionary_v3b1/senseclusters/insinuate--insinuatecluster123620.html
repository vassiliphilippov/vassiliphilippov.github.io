Etymology

insinuate comes from Latin parts in- 'into' and sinus 'fold' or 'curve', so imagine sliding a quiet idea into the fold of a conversation. A helpful way to remember it is to think of slipping a hint in slowly instead of saying it directly. That's why to insinuate means to suggest something in an indirect, often unpleasant, way.
