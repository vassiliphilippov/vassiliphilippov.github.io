Etymology

mortifyingly comes from the Latin root mort meaning 'death', the same part you see in mortal and mortuary. The older verb mortify meant 'to make dead', and the image of 'killing' your pride helps explain why something mortifyingly embarrassing makes you feel as if your pride has died.
