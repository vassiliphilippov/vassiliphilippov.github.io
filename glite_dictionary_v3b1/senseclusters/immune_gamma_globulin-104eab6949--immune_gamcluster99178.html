Etymology

immune gamma globulin is the name for blood proteins; the words gamma and globulin just name this kind of protein, and these proteins act like tiny helpers that stick to germs and help the body fight them. Doctors inject immune gamma globulin to give quick, temporary 'protection' against disease, so the name helps you remember it means proteins used to protect the body.
