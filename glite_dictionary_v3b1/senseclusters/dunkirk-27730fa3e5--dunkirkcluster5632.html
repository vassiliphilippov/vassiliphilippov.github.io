Etymology

Dunkirk used as a noun means a desperate last effort because of the famous 1940 evacuation at the town of Dunkirk, when many soldiers were rescued in a dramatic, last-minute way. The town's name itself comes from old Dutch duin 'dune' and kerk 'church', so today 'Dunkirk' also stands for a brave final rescue.
