Etymology

bacteriolysis comes from bacterio- meaning 'bacteria' and -lysis meaning 'breaking apart' or 'dissolving'. So, bacteriolysis is the breaking open or destruction of bacterial cells, often by antibodies, enzymes, or antibiotics.
