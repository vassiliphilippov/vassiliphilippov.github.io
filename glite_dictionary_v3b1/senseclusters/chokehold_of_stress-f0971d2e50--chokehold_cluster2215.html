Etymology

chokehold of stress comes from a strong image: a chokehold is when an arm squeezes someone's throat so they can hardly breathe, and stress is the thing doing the squeezing. So the phrase means 'feeling so overwhelmed and trapped by stress that you can't think or relax'.
