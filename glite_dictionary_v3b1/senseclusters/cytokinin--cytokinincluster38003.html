Etymology

cytokinin comes from the parts cyto- meaning 'cell' and -kinin related to 'movement', like in kinetic. A helpful way to remember it is to think of a chemical that sets cells moving to divide and stay young. That's why cytokinin is a plant hormone that makes cells divide and helps keep leaves from aging.
