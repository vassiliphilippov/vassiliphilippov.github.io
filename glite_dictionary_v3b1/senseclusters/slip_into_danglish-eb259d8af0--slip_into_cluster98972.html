Etymology

slip into Danglish uses the image of someone quietly slippinginto another set of clothes, a small move done without thinking. Like that easy shift, a speaker moves into a Danglish 'mix' of Danish and English 'without meaning to', so the phrase means 'to begin to unintentionally speak in a mixture of Danish and English'.
