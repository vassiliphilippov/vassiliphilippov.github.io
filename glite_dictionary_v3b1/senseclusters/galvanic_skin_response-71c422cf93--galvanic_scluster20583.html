Etymology

galvanic skin response is named after the scientist Luigi Galvani, who found electric reactions in frog legs, so galvanic means 'electric'. When you feel strong emotions, the tiny electric signals in your skin change as a response, so the phrase means 'a change in the skin's electricity when you are stressed or excited'.
