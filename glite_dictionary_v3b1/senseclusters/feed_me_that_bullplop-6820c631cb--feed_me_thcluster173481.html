Etymology

feed me that bullplop gives a clear picture: someone is trying to feed you bullplop, a rude mix of 'bull' and the soft 'plop' that makes you think of animal poop, so you picture refusing it. That's why it means 'to tell someone nonsense or lies' or 'to try to trick someone'.
