Etymology

Papaver argemone comes from old Latin and Greek words. Papaver is the Latin word for 'poppy', and argemone may come from a Greek name for plants used for 'eye problems' or for their bright, eye-like flowers, so the name helps us remember it is a poppy with bright orange-red flowers.
