Etymology

Arab-Israeli War refers to a war between Arab countries and Israel. One clear memory hook is the 1967 conflict in June that lasted only six days, so people often call that event the 'Six-Day War', and the name Arab-Israeli War simply points to the two sides in that short conflict.
