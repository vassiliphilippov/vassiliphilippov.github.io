Etymology

Marantaceae comes from the name of the Italian botanist Maranta plus the plant-family ending -aceae, which means 'family' and is used for groups of plants such as Rosaceae, the rose family. So Marantaceae is the 'Maranta family' and refers to the group of plants that includes arrowroot and the prayer plant.
