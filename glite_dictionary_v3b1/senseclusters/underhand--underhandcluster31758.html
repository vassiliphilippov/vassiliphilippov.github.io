Etymology

underhand comes from the parts under 'below' and hand 'hand'. A helpful way to remember the meaning 'secret or dishonest' is to imagine doing something 'under someone's hand' or 'under the table' so it feels hidden and sly, which is why underhand means secret or unfair.
