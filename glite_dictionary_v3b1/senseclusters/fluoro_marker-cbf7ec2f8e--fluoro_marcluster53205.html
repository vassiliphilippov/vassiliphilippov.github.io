Etymology

fluoro marker: The name fluoro marker comes from the part fluoro, short for the word 'fluorescent' which means it gives off bright light under a blacklight, and the part marker, a pen used to mark text. So, fluoro marker today means a highlighter pen that makes bright, glowing lines.
