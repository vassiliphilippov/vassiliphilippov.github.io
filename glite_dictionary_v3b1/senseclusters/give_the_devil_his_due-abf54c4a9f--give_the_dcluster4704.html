Etymology

The saying give the devil his due comes from the image of giving someone what they deserve, even the devil, because due means what is owed. One popular story says people used that picture to remind others to admit a person's good points even if they do not like them, so today it means 'to acknowledge someone's good qualities even if you do not approve'.
