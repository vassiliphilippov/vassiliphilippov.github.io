Etymology

pardonnez mon français is a French phrase that English speakers borrowed as a funny apology, and the words pardonnez and français mean 'forgive' and 'French', so it literally says 'forgive my language'. One popular story says people used to say 'pardon my French' when they used a foreign word or a swear, and today pardonnez mon français is used the same way to warn or excuse rough or rude language.
