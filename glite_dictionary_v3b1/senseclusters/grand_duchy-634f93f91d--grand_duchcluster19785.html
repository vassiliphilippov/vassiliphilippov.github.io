Etymology

Grand duchy comes from rulers called grand dukes or grand duchesses who led a small country. Imagine a leader called a 'grand' duke, higher than a duke but below a king, and the land he ruled was called a duchy, so a grand duchy means 'the land ruled by a grand duke'.
