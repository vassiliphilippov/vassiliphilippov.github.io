Etymology

automobile battery combines automobile (a self-moving vehicle) with battery, a word for a pack of power cells. Imagine a large box of cells that stores a lot of electricity to 'drive' the car for many miles, so the phrase now means the big rechargeable battery that powers electric vehicles.
