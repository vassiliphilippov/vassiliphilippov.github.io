Etymology

hypozeuxis comes from Greek parts hypo- meaning 'under' and zeuxis meaning 'yoke' or 'join', like the English word yoke. Think of it as each short clause getting its own 'yoke' with a subject and verb, which is why hypozeuxis is the name for a list like 'I came, I saw, I conquered'.
