Etymology

stiltedness comes from the simple word stilt and the ending -ness. A stilt is a long pole people use to walk high above the ground, and walking on stilts looks stiff and awkward, so stiltedness means 'the quality of being stiff, unnatural, or overly formal' in speech or manner.
