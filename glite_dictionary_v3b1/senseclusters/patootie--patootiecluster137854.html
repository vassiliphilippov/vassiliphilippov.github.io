Etymology

patootie is a playful American nickname, probably made by mixing potato (think 'little potato') with fun nicknames like tootsie or cutie. A helpful way to remember it is to picture a small, cute 'potato', which is why patootie is used for someone's 'butt' or for a darling, adorable person.
