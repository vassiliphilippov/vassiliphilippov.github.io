Etymology

conversance comes from the Latin root convers- meaning 'to live with' or 'be with', plus the ending -ance that turns it into a noun. It is related to conversation, which long ago meant 'living together' and later 'talk', so to have conversance with a subject is to be familiar with it or to have real experience of it.
