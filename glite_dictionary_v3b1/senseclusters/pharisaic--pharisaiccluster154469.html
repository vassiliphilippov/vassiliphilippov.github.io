Etymology

pharisaic comes from the religious group Pharisees, whose Hebrew name perush meant 'separate' or 'set apart'. In the Bible they are shown as very strict about rules and sometimes more interested in looking holy than in true kindness, so pharisaic now describes showy or hypocritical piety.
