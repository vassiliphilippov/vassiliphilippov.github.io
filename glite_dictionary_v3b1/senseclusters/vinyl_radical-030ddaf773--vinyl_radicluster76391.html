Etymology

vinyl radical comes from chemistry and names a tiny, very active part of a molecule. Think of vinyl as the small two-carbon piece and radical as an 'open' hand or bond that is ready to 'grab' or 'join' another atom, so the name explains why this group easily reacts to make new molecules.
