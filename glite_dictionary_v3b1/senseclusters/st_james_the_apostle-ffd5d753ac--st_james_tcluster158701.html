Etymology

St. James the Apostle is the name of James, one of Jesus's twelve followers and often called an apostle ('one sent to teach'). One popular story says he travelled far and was killed, and people later believed his tomb was in the Spanish town of Santiago, so pilgrims still walk long routes there to honour him.
