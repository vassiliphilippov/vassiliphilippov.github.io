Etymology

Cyclohexanol breaks into three parts: cyclo- which means 'ring' and is like the word cycle, hex- which means 'six' and is like hexagon, and -ol which marks an 'alcohol'. So cyclohexanol is literally an alcohol built on a ring of six parts, which is why chemists use that name.
