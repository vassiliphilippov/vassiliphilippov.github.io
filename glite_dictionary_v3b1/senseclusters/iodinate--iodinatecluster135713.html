Etymology

The word iodinate comes from iodine, the chemical whose name came from a Greek word meaning 'violet' because its gas is violet, and the ending -ate, which is used in words like carbonate and nitrate to mean 'to make or add'. So, to iodinate is to 'add iodine to' a compound.
