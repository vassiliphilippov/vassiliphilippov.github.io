Etymology

mysophobia comes from the Greek parts myso- meaning 'dirt' or 'filth' and -phobia meaning 'fear', the same -phobia you see in arachnophobia. So, mysophobia is the strong fear of dirt or germs, which is why people with it may avoid touching things or clean all the time.
