Etymology

locus classicus comes from old Latin words: locus meaning 'place' and classicus meaning 'classic'. Scholars used the phrase when they pointed to one well-known 'place' in a book as the best example of an idea, so today locus classicus means the 'best or most typical passage' to show a point.
