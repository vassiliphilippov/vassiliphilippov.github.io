Etymology

waterfowler breaks into water and fowler, and fowler comes from an old word for 'bird', the same root as the common English fowl. So a waterfowler is literally someone who hunts 'water birds' like ducks, which is why today it means a person who hunts ducks or other waterfowl, often as a sport.
