Etymology

sodium bichromate: The chemical name sodium bichromate tells a simple story: sodium is the common metal part, and bichromate hints at 'two' chromium atoms and the salt's bright red‑orange 'color'. Because the salt is used in dyeing and other industries and is toxic, the name helps you remember both its colour and that it contains chromium.
