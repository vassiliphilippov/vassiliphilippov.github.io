Etymology

bicycle rack comes from a simple, literal picture: a bicycle is a bike and a rack is a frame that holds things. People used a frame where they could 'park' and 'lock' their bikes, so the frame became known as a bicycle rack, meaning a place to leave and secure a bicycle.
