Etymology

coronary sinus comes from old words: coronary means 'crown' because the heart's blood vessels run around the heart like a crown, and sinus means 'bay', a small curve where blood can gather. That is why the name fits the short vein that sits around the heart and collects blood to return it to the right atrium.
