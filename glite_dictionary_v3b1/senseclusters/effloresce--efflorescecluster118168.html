Etymology

effloresce is also used for minerals that make a white, powdery 'bloom' on surfaces because the Latin parts ex- 'out' and flor- 'flower' give the image of crystals 'flowering' out of the stone. That's why a salt or mineral crust on a wall or brick is described as efflorescence.
