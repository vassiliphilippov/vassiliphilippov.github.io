Etymology

go tutti-frutti comes from Italian tutti-frutti, meaning 'all fruit', a name used for colourful sweets and ice creams made from many fruits. So to go tutti-frutti means to mix lots of bright, playful colours or styles together, like a dessert that has every fruit.
