Etymology

preaching comes from the Latin verb praedicare, which meant 'to announce' and has parts like pre- 'before' and dic- 'to say', the same root as English words like dictate. So, 'preaching' can mean telling people strong moral views or advice in a public or bossy way, which is why it often feels unwanted.
