Etymology

tetrasomy comes from the parts tetra- meaning 'four' and -somy from Greek soma meaning 'body', the same root you see in chromosome. So, tetrasomy is easy to remember: it literally says 'four bodies', and it refers to having four copies of one chromosome instead of the usual two.
