Etymology

The word exorbitantly comes from Latin parts ex- meaning 'out of' and orbita, a word for 'track' or 'path' that gives us orbit, as in the path of a planet. So to charge exorbitantly is to go 'out of the usual path' in price, meaning to be much too high.
