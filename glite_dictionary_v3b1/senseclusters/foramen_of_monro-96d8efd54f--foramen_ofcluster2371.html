Etymology

Foramen of Monro may come from the Latin word foramen, meaning 'hole', and from a Scottish doctor named Monro who described it. So the name is like 'Monro's little hole', a small opening that connects the brain's side spaces called the lateral ventricles to the middle space called the third ventricle.
