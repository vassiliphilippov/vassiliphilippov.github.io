Etymology

mutilation comes from the old Latin verb mutilare, which means 'to maim' or 'to make imperfect', and from mutilus meaning 'maimed'. So when we speak of the mutilation of a building, statue, or landscape, we mean it has been badly damaged or disfigured, as if important parts were cut away.
