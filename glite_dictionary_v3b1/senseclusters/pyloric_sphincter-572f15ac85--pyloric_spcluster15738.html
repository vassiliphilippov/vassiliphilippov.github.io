Etymology

pyloric sphincter comes from words that mean a small 'gate' and a ring that 'squeezes': pyloric points to the gate at the stomach exit and sphincter is the ring muscle that tightens. Think of it as a little gatekeeper muscle that opens to let food into the small intestine and closes to keep food in, so it means the ring of muscle that controls food leaving the stomach.
