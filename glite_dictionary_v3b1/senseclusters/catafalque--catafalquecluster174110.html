Etymology

catafalque comes from an old Italian word catafalco, made from Greek parts like kata- meaning 'down' and a part meaning 'to stand' (like the English 'stand'). So a catafalque is a raised or 'standing' platform for a coffin, which is why it is the decorated stand used at funerals.
