Etymology

streptococcus tonsillitis gets its name from what it is: streptococcus refers to tiny round germs that often form a 'chain of little beads', and tonsillitis means the 'tonsils' are swollen and inflamed, so the phrase describes 'a bacterial infection of the tonsils'.
