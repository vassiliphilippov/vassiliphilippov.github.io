Etymology

soleplate is made of two simple parts: sole meaning 'bottom', as in the sole of a shoe, and plate meaning 'a flat piece'. So a soleplate is the flat bottom part of an iron or the base plate at the bottom of a wall, which explains its modern use as the flat base in tools and building.
