Etymology

metofluthrin is a modern chemical name made of parts; a helpful way to remember it is that the ending -thrin shows it is like other insect-killing chemicals, so you can think of -thrin as 'bug killer', and the first part meto- marks it as a made, synthetic version. That's why metofluthrin is the name of a man-made mosquito repellent.
