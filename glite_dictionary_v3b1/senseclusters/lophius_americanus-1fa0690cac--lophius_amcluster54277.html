Etymology

Lophius americanus is the scientific name for an anglerfish often called 'monkfish' because it has a long, wormlike 'lure' on its head that works like a tiny 'fishing rod' to attract prey. That's why the name and the idea of an 'angler', a fisher, match how this fish catches food.
