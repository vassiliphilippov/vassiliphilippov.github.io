Etymology

Fatima is an Arabic name from the root f-t-m, which means 'to wean' or 'to stop nursing'. So the name brings to mind a caring, motherly woman, and that image helps explain why many Muslims, especially Shiites, remember Fatima as a model of devotion and strength.
