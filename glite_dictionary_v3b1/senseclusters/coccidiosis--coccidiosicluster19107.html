coccidiosis

0.00/m9.9

Etymology

coccidiosis comes from coccidia, the name for tiny round parasites, and that name comes from the Greek kokkos meaning 'berry' because they look like little dots, plus the ending -osis meaning 'disease'. So, coccidiosis is the 'disease of the little berry-like parasites' and means an infection that often affects animals' intestines.
