Etymology

The compound stock-index futures comes from a simple picture: a stock is a share, an index is a list that shows many stock prices, and futures are a promise or ticket to trade at a set price later. So, stock-index futures are like a ticket that locks a price for the whole list of stocks, which is why investors use them to 'bet on' or 'protect against' the market's future moves.
