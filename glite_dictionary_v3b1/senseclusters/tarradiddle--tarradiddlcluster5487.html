Etymology

The word tarradiddle probably began as a playful mix of a nonsense sound tarra- and the word diddle, which means 'to fool' or 'cheat'. A helpful way to remember it is to picture someone 'diddling' with words, saying small, silly things that are not important, which is why tarradiddle means foolish or empty talk.
