Etymology

round-trip light time shows a simple picture: a light signal leaves Earth, reaches a spacecraft, and then comes back, so the light makes a round-trip and the time is how long that journey takes. That's why the phrase measures the 'delay' before Earth gets an answer from a distant spacecraft.
