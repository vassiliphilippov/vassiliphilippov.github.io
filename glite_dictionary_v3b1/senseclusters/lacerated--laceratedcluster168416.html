Etymology

Lacerated comes from the old Latin root lacer, which means 'torn' or 'mangled', and you can see the same root in laceration, a medical word for a deep tear. So lacerated means 'torn or cut in a rough, jagged way', like skin or cloth ripped by broken glass.
