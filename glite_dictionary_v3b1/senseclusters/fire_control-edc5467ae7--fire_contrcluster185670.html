Etymology

fire control comes from the simple idea of controlling fire, meaning the shooting of guns, and using control to aim and time the shots. In old battles gunners measured distance and set their guns so the shots would hit the right place, so today fire control means the 'process' or 'system' that prepares and directs weapons onto a 'target'.
