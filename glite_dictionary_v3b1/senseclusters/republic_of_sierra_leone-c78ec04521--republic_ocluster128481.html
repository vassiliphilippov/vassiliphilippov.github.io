Etymology

Republic of Sierra Leone comes from old Portuguese words: Sierra meaning 'mountain' and Leone meaning 'lion', so early sailors called the coast the 'lioness mountains'. The word Republic means 'a country with its own government', so the full name simply identifies the country of the 'lioness mountains'.
