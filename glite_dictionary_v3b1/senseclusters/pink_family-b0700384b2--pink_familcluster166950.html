Etymology

The name pink family comes from a small flower called a pink, not from the color. One popular story says the word 'pink' came from an old word meaning 'to make small cuts' because the flower's petals look as if someone trimmed them with little scissors, so the pink family means the group of plants with those cut-looking petals.
