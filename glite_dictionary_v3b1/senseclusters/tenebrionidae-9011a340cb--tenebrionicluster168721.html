Etymology

Tenebrionidae comes from the Latin tenebrae, meaning 'darkness', plus the family ending -idae meaning 'family'. So Tenebrionidae are the 'dark' beetles (the darkling beetles, like mealworms), and the name fits because many of them live in dark, dry places.
