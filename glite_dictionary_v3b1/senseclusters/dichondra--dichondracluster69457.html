Etymology

dichondra comes from Greek parts di- meaning 'two' and chondr- meaning 'lump' or 'grain'. A helpful way to remember it is to picture its small, round leaves and soft, low mat as little paired discs covering the ground, which is why dichondra is used as a grass substitute.
