Etymology

Cirsium helenioides comes from old Greek and Latin words. Cirsium means 'thistle' and helenioides means 'like Helenium', a daisy-like flower, so the name creates a simple picture of a prickly plant with a showy purple, daisy-like head, which matches the plant today.
