Etymology

The phrase on the scrapheap comes from the image of a pile of old, broken things and pieces of metal called a scrapheap, where useless items are thrown away. That's why being on the scrapheap means 'no longer wanted' or 'thrown away', like jobs or ideas people see as useless.
