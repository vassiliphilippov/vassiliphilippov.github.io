Etymology

Mother Seton is the name given to Elizabeth Ann Seton, an American woman who became a nun and started the Sisters of Charity and many schools to help the poor. She was the first person born in the United States to be named a 'saint', so people call her Mother Seton to remember her life of 'charity' and teaching.
