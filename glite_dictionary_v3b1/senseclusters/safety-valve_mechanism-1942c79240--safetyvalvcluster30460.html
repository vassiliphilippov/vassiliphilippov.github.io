Etymology

Safety-valve mechanism comes from a simple machine image: a safety-valve is a small part that opens to let out extra steam or gas and a mechanism is the moving parts that make it work. When the valve opens it 'releases pressure' so the device does not explode, so the phrase now means 'a device that prevents dangerous buildup and keeps things safe'.
