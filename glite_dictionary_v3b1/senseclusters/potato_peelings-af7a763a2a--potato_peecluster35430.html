Etymology

potato peelings comes from the kitchen act of peeling a potato, when the thin pieces of skin, the peelings, are left behind and often fried until crisp and eaten. So, potato peelings now means 'thin strips of potato skin', especially when fried and served as a 'snack' or 'garnish'.
