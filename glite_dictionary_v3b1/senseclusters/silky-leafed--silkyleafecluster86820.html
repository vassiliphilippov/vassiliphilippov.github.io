Etymology

The word silky-leafed comes from silk (the smooth, shiny cloth) and leaf, with -ed meaning 'having'. So, think of leaves that look and feel like silk, shiny and very smooth, and you will remember a silky-leafed plant has leaves that are 'like silk'.
