Etymology

The phrasal verb wear down comes from the image of wear and down: repeated rubbing or pressure slowly makes things thinner or smaller, like shoe soles wearing thin or rocks becoming smooth. That's why it now means 'to make someone or something weaker or less able to resist' by constant pressure over time.
