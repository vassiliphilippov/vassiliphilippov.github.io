Etymology

Strophanthus comes from the Greek roots strophos meaning 'twisted' and anthos meaning 'flower', which creates a clear image because these tropical vines and shrubs have long, twisted flowers. That's why the name fits this group of plants, some of whose seeds are strong poisons once used as arrow poison.
