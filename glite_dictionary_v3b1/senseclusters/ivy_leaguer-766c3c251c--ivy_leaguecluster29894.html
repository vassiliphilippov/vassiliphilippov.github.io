Etymology

Ivy Leaguer comes from the picture of thick Ivy climbing the old brick walls of a group of famous colleges that played sports together, and the word Leaguer comes from the word 'league' for that group. So an Ivy Leaguer is 'a student or graduate of an Ivy League university'.
