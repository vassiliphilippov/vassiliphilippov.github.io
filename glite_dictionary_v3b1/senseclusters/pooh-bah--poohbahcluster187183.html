Etymology

pooh-bah comes from a funny character in the comic opera The Mikado who held many official jobs, and his name joins the scorn sounds pooh and bah, like saying 'I don't like this' or 'I don't respect this'. So a pooh-bah today is someone who thinks they are very important and acts pompous.
