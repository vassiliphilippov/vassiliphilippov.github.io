Etymology

alkanol comes from alkane, a simple chain of carbon and hydrogen, plus the suffix -ol which means 'alcohol' and appears in words like ethanol and methanol. So an alkanol is an alkane that has the 'alcohol' part 'OH', which is why it is an alcohol.
