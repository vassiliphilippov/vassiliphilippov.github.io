Etymology

The noun archaeological planning frame names a simple square wooden tool archaeologists place over a small area of a dig to make a fixed 'window' for drawing and measuring. That's why the frame gives a clear, measured space for planning and 'recording', so the name means a tool used to map and record objects on a site.
