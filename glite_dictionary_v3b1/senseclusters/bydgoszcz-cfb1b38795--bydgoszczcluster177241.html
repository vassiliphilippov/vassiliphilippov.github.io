Etymology

The word Bydgoszcz may come from old Polish parts byd- and -goszcz, which mean 'cattle' and 'guest' or 'host', so a helpful image is a place that 'hosts' animals. That's why it is easy to remember Bydgoszcz as a riverside market and port where animals and goods were brought and traded.
