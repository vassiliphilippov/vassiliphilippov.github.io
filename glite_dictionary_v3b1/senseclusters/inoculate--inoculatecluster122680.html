Etymology

inoculate comes from Latin parts in- 'in' and oculus 'eye', from the old idea of putting a small 'eye' into something so it will grow there. A helpful way to remember the modern use is this: to inoculate a person with an idea is to put a small seed of thought into their mind so they are prepared or made resistant to other ideas.
