Etymology

Dacrydium cupressinum may come from old Greek and Latin words: Dacrydium from a Greek word meaning 'tear', a picture for its small resin drops or cone shape, and cupressinum meaning 'like a cypress'. So the name paints a simple picture of the tall evergreen we call rimu, which helps explain why this scientific name fits the tree.
