Etymology

crepitation comes from the Latin root crepit-, which means 'to crackle' or 'rattle' and is like the everyday word 'creak'. So, crepitation names a sharp, crackling or snapping sound, which is why doctors use it for lung crackles and people use it for twigs or a fire.
