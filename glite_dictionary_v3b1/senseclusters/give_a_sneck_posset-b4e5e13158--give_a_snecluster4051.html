Etymology

give a sneck posset comes from a simple old image: a sneck was a tiny latch and a posset was a small warm drink, so together they named something very small and of little value. One popular story says that saying you would not even 'give a sneck posset' meant you would not give even that tiny thing, so the phrase came to mean 'to care at all', often used with 'not' to say someone does not care.
