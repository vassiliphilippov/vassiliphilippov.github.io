Etymology

Arianrod comes from Welsh parts arian meaning 'silver' and rhod meaning 'wheel', so her name paints a picture of a 'silver wheel'. That image of a bright, turning wheel helps explain why she is a goddess of the moon and stars and a symbol of feminine mystery.
