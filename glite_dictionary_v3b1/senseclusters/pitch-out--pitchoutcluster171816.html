Etymology

pitch-out is formed from pitch ('to throw') and out ('away' or 'outside'), and as a verb it means to throw or toss the ball outward on purpose. So to pitch-out is simply to make that outward throw, whether to stop a steal in baseball or to pass to a running back in football.
