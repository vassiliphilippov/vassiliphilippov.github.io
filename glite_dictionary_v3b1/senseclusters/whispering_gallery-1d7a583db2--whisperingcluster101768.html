Etymology

The name whispering gallery comes from old buildings with round domes where a soft voice at one spot travels along the curved wall and can be heard clearly far away. Because the sound seems to 'whisper' around the dome and across the gallery, the name now means a place under a dome where quiet speech reaches listeners at distant points.
