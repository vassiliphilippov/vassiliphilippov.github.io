Etymology

knee-deep in the Big Muddy paints a picture of someone standing knee-deep in a wide river of sticky mud called the Big Muddy, so each step makes them more stuck and it is hard to get out. That's why it means being 'deeply involved in a difficult situation that is getting worse'.
