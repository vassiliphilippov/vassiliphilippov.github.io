Etymology

Intravenous pyelogram is a medical name that tells the test: intravenous means into a vein and pyelogram means a 'picture' of the kidney area. In this exam a doctor puts a dye into a 'vein' so X-rays can take 'pictures' of the kidneys and tubes, so the name now means this X-ray test.
