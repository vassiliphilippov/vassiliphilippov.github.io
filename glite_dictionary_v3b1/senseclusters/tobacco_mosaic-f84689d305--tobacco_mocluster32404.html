Etymology

Tobacco mosaic gets its name from how infected tobacco leaves look: the word mosaic describes a patchwork of light and dark areas on the leaf, like small tiles. A tiny virus makes that patchwork, so today 'tobacco mosaic' means 'a disease that makes leaves look patchy'.
