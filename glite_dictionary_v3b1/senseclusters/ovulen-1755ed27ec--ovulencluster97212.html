Etymology

Ovulen was formed from ovule (from Latin ovum, 'egg') plus the common ending -en. A helpful way to remember it is to think 'egg' because Ovulen is a trade name for a birth control pill that helps prevent pregnancy, so the name points to its action on the egg and reproduction.
