Etymology

kick some tail is slang that comes from a rough image: to kick someone's tail, meaning their rear, and make them fall back or run away. One popular story says that image of forcing someone to retreat turned into the idea 'to defeat or outperform someone, often decisively', so people use it to mean winning by a lot.
