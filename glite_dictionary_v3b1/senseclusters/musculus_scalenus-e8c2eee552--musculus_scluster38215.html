Etymology

musculus scalenus comes from older words: musculus means 'muscle' and scalenus may come from a Greek word meaning 'uneven' or 'slanted'. The name creates a simple picture — these muscles run at different angles from the neck to the first two ribs, so they help turn the neck and lift the ribs for deep breathing.
