Etymology

hemocoel comes from Greek parts haima ('blood') and koilos ('hollow'), so it means a 'blood hollow'. A helpful way to remember the medical sense is to think of an unwanted 'blood-filled space' that can form after an injury, which is why doctors call such cavities a hemocoel.
