Etymology

Stevens' power law comes from psychologist S.S. Stevens, who ran experiments asking people to judge things like brightness or pain and found a simple math rule. The power part names the math rule and the law part means a general rule, so the name points to the idea that 'how strong something feels' grows by that power rule as the real stimulus grows.
