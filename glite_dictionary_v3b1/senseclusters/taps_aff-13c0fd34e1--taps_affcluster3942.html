Etymology

taps aff may come from a Scottish way of saying 'tops off': taps (like 'tops', meaning shirts) and aff (meaning 'off'), so picture people taking their shirts off in warm weather — that explains the 'taking off one's shirt' meaning. The same words also make sense for a notice about water, because if the water taps are aff it means the 'water is shut off', so the phrase can be used for that warning too.
