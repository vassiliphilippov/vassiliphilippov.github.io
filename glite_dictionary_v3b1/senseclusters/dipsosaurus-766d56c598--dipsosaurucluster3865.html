Etymology

Dipsosaurus comes from Greek parts dipsa 'thirst' and sauros 'lizard', and you can recognise the sauros part from dinosaur. So Dipsosaurus is like a 'thirst lizard', which fits the desert iguana that lives in hot, dry places.
