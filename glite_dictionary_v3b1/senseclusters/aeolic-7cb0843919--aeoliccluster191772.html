Etymology

Aeolic comes from the place name Aeolis, the home of the ancient Aeolian people, and that name comes from Aeolus, a figure linked to the 'wind' whose name meant 'quick' or 'changeable'. So, Aeolic simply means the Greek dialect spoken in Aeolis.
