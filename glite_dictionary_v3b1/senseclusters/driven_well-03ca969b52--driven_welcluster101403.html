Etymology

driven well comes from the simple act of 'driving' a metal pipe into the ground until it reaches 'water'. Workers would push and hammer the pipe down so the pipe itself becomes the well, and the word driven shows how the pipe was forced into the ground. That's why driven well means a well made by pushing or hammering a pipe into the ground to get 'water'.
