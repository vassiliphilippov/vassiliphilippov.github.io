Etymology

The word sanatorium was also used in the past for hospitals for people with mental illness, and this comes from the same root as the English word sane, meaning 'healthy in mind'. A helpful way to remember this older use is that it meant a 'place for health', but today calling a mental hospital a sanatorium can sound old or hurtful.
