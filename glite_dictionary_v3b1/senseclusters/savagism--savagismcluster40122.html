Etymology

savagism comes from savage and the ending -ism. Long ago savage came from the Latin word silva, meaning 'wood', so it first meant 'from the woods' and 'wild', and later was used for violent or cruel behaviour, which is why savagism can mean extreme cruelty or violent behavior.
