Etymology

The word duncish comes from dunce (a word for a slow learner) plus the ending -ish, which means 'like' or 'somewhat'. A helpful way to remember it is that dunce was once the name of a scholar that later became an insult, so duncish means 'a bit like a dunce', stupid or slow to understand.
