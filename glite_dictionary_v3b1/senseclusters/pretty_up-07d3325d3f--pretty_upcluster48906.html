Etymology

pretty up comes from the simple idea of pretty (meaning 'to make attractive') plus up (a word that often means 'more' or 'complete'). Imagine adding makeup, fresh flowers or new cushions to lift a look — so pretty up means 'to make oneself or something more attractive'.
