Etymology

The expression fond regard comes from the picture of looking at someone with warm feelings: the word regard means to 'look at' or think about, and fond means to feel 'warm' or 'affectionate'. So having fond regard is like looking on someone with caring feelings, which is why it means 'affectionate or warm feelings toward'.
