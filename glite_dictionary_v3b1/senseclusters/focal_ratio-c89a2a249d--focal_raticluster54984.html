Etymology

The term focal ratio comes from comparing the lens focal length (the distance light travels to make an image) to its diameter (the size of the front opening). Imagine a long, thin tube and a short, wide one: the short, wide tube gives a brighter image, so the ratio tells you how 'fast' or 'bright' the lens will be.
