Etymology

leishmaniasis americana is named after the doctor William Leishman, so leishmaniasis is the illness linked to his name and americana shows it comes from the Americas. This American form usually makes sores on the skin and in the mouth, so the name helps you remember both the region and the part of the body it affects.
