Ariocarpus fissuratus may come from Latin parts that describe how the plant looks: Ariocarpus is the group name and fissuratus means 'split' or 'cracked', like the cactus's warty, cracked surface. So the name helps you picture this small, often spineless cactus with a rough, fissured appearance.
