Etymology

stone-root is made of two simple words, stone ('hard rock') and root ('part of a plant under the ground'), and a helpful way to remember it is that the plant has strong roots and was once used in old remedies for 'stones'. So, the name explains why stone-root refers to a plant known for its notable root and its traditional medicinal use.
