Etymology

vinyl ether comes from its two parts: vinyl, a small chemical piece, and ether, a kind of liquid once breathed in to make people sleep for surgery. So the name points to a liquid 'anesthetic' with a vinyl part, which is why vinyl ether was used as an inhaled anesthetic.
