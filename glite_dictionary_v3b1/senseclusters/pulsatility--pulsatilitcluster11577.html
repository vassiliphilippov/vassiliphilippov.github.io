Etymology

pulsatility comes from pulse, meaning 'a regular beat' like your heartbeat, and the ending -ity, meaning 'state'. So, pulsatility is the 'state of having beats' or regular rises and falls, which is why doctors use it to talk about blood flow or hormone release.
