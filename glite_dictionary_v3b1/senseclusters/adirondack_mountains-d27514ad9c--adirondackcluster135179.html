Etymology

Adirondack Mountains may come from a Mohawk word 'ha-de-ron-dah' that meant 'bark-eaters', a name given to people who sometimes ate tree bark in hard times. Early settlers used that word for the wild, tree-covered mountains, so today it is the name of the mountain range.
