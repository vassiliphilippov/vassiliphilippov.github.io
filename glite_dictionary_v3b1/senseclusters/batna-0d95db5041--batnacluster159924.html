Etymology

BATNA is an acronym for Best Alternative to a Negotiated Agreement, which says your best option if a deal fails. A helpful way to remember BATNA is to think of it as your 'Plan B' or backup plan when talks break down, and that is why negotiators use it to know when to walk away.
