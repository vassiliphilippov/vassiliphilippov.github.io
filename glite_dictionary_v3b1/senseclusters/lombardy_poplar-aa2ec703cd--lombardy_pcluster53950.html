Etymology

Lombardy poplar gets its name from Lombardy in Italy, where people planted rows of tall, narrow poplars that look like thin columns along roads and fields. So the name links the place and the tree, and today Lombardy poplar means 'a tall, narrow poplar' often used to mark roads or stop the wind.
