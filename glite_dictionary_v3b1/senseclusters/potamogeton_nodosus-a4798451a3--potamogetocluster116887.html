Etymology

Potamogeton nodosus may come from old Greek and Latin words: Potamogeton comes from a word for 'river', so it names a water plant, and nodosus is Latin for 'knotty' or 'with nodes'. Those 'nodes' are the places on the stem where its floating leaves grow, so the name fits this pondweed found in calm lakes.
