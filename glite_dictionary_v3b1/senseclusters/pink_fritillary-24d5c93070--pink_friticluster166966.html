Etymology

pink fritillary gets its name from pink, the flower's color, and fritillary, an old word for a checked or spotted pattern on the petals. One popular story says 'fritillary' once meant a small box for dice because the squares looked similar, so the name helps you picture a small pink bell flower with a patterned surface.
