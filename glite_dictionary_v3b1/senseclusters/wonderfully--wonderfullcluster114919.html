Etymology

wonderfully comes from the simple word wonder, meaning 'surprise' or 'admiration', plus -ful meaning 'full of' and -ly which turns the word into one that describes how something is done. So, to do something wonderfully is to do it in a way that is 'full of wonder' or very impressive.
