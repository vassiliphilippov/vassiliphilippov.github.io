Etymology

runcinate leaf comes from an old Latin word for a cutting tool, which gives a clear picture: the leaf has deep teeth or lobes that bend back toward the stem like the teeth of a saw pointing to its handle. So, a runcinate leaf is one whose edges 'curve toward the base'.
