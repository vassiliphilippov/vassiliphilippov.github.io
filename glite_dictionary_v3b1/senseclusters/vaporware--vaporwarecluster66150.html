Etymology

vaporware comes from vapor ('steam' or 'not solid') + ware ('goods' or 'products'), as in software and hardware. So when something is called vaporware, it is only 'steam'—described or announced but not likely to be made—which is why we use it to say a project is unlikely to become real.
