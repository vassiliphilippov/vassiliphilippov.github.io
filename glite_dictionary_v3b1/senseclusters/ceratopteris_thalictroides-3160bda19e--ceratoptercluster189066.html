Etymology

Ceratopteris thalictroides may come from old Greek: cerato- means 'horn' and -pteris means 'fern', while thalictroides means 'like Thalictrum', a plant with divided leaves. So the name paints the image of a 'horned fern' with split, feathery leaves in the water, which helps you remember this aquatic fern.
