Etymology

negligibility comes from the same root as neglect and negligible, from Latin parts neg- 'not' and legere 'to pick up', so it started with the idea of 'not taken up' or 'ignored'. So, negligibility is the quality of being so small or unimportant that you can 'ignore' it.
