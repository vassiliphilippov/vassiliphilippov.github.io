Etymology

peculiar institution was a polite name used by Southern leaders in the 1800s for 'slavery'. They used the softer words peculiar, meaning 'special to them', and institution, meaning 'an accepted part of society', to make slavery sound normal and important, so the phrase came to mean 'slavery'.
