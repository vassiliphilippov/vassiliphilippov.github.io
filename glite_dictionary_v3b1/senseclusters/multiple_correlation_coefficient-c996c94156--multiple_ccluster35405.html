Etymology

The term multiple correlation coefficient comes from the parts multiple, correlation, and coefficient, meaning 'many', 'a connection', and 'a number'. Think of it as one number that tells how well several factors together are linked to one result, so it shows the combined effect and helps 'predict' the outcome.
