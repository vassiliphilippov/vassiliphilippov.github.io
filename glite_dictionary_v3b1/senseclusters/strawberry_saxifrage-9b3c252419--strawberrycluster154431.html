Etymology

strawberry saxifrage gets its name from two images: its small red-and-white flowers and its habit of spreading by runners like a strawberry, while saxifrage was an old name for plants that grow in rocky cracks and were called 'rock-breakers'. So the name helps you picture a low, ground-cover plant with strawberry-like flowers that spreads to cover the soil.
