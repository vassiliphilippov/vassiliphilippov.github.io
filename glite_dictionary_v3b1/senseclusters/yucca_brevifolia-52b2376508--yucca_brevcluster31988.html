Etymology

Yucca brevifolia joins the plant name Yucca and the Latin word brevifolia, which means 'short leaves', so imagine a yucca with short, stubby leaves. Early settlers also called it the 'Joshua tree' because its twisted branches looked like a man raising his arms in prayer, and that image explains why Yucca brevifolia refers to the big, branched desert tree we know today.
