Etymology

Russian tumbleweed is named for a plant from Russia and nearby lands that dries and then breaks off at the base so the whole bush can roll away. The round, prickly bush tumbles across fields in the wind, scattering seeds and causing big weed problems, so the name now means a prickly bush that 'tumbles' and spreads itself across the land.
