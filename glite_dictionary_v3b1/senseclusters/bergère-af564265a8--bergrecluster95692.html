Etymology

bergère for the chair also comes from French berger meaning 'shepherd', because 18th-century design loved cozy countryside scenes and this soft, enclosed armchair suggested the comfort of country life. So a bergère today is an upholstered armchair with closed sides and an exposed wooden frame.
