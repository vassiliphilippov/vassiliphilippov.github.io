Etymology

lord of gingerbread is a playful title that mixes the idea of a lord, a person in charge, with gingerbread, the spiced cookie. Picture someone wearing a crown of cookies and always making the best gingerbread, so the phrase means 'the best maker' or 'an expert' in gingerbread.
