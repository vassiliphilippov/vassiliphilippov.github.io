Etymology

publishing empire paints a picture of an 'empire' ruled by one person, but instead of countries the ruler controls magazines, newspapers and websites. The word publishing names the businesses and empire shows they are all under one owner, so the phrase means 'a large group of publishing companies owned or controlled by one person or company'.
