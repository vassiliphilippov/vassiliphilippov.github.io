Etymology

expunging comes from the Latin parts ex- 'out' and pungere 'to prick', and it originally meant 'to prick out' or cut words from a page, a idea you can see in puncture ('to prick'). So, to expunge something is to erase or remove it completely, for example taking it out of a record.
