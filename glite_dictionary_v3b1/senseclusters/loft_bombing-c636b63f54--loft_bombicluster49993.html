Etymology

loft bombing comes from the picture of a plane flying low then pulling up to loft its bombs during a bombing run so they fly in a high arc. By sending the bombs forward this way, the aircraft can drop them and get away from the explosion, so the phrase means this way of bombing to 'escape the blast area'.
