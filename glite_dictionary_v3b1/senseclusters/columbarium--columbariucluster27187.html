Etymology

The word columbarium comes from the Latin columba meaning 'dove' and the ending -arium meaning 'a place for'. It first meant a 'dovecote', a wall of small nests for pigeons, and today a columbarium is a wall of small niches that hold funeral urns, like nests holding ashes.
