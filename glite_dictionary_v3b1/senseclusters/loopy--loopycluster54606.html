Etymology

Loopy comes from loop meaning 'a round or curved shape' and the ending -y meaning 'full of', and a helpful way to remember it is to imagine ideas going in circles in someone's head. So, if a person is loopy they act a little strange or silly, as if their thoughts are not straight.
