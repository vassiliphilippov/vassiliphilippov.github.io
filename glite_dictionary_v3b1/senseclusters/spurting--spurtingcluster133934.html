Etymology

spurting probably comes from a word that copies the sound of a quick burst of liquid, the same idea behind the short word spurt. A helpful way to remember it is to imagine a broken tap 'spouting' water in a narrow, powerful jet, so that's why spurting means liquid forced out suddenly and strongly.
