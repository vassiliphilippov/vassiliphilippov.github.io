Etymology

MRI stands for magneticresonanceimaging. Think of magnetic as 'like a magnet', resonance as a kind of 'echo' or 'vibration', and imaging as 'making pictures'. So an MRI is a machine that uses magnets and radio waves to make detailed pictures of the inside of your body.
