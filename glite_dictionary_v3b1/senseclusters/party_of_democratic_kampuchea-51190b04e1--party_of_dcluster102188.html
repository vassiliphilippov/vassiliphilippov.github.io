Etymology

Party of Democratic Kampuchea is the official-sounding name the group used. Kampuchea is the local word for Cambodia, so the name made them look like a normal party. That's why the name refers to the communist group known as the 'Khmer Rouge' that ruled Cambodia and caused many deaths.
