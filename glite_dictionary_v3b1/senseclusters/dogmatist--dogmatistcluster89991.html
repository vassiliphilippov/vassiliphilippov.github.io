Etymology

Dogmatist comes from the Greek word dogma, meaning 'opinion' or 'teaching', and the ending -ist, meaning 'a person'. Picture someone who holds one 'teaching' tightly and does not listen to other ideas, which is why a dogmatist is a person who stubbornly sticks to their own beliefs.
