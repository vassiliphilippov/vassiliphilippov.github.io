Etymology

counterbombardment breaks into counter- meaning 'against' and bombardment, from bombard (like the word bomb) meaning a 'heavy attack' with guns or explosives. So counterbombardment is an attack made 'against' the enemy's guns or weapons to destroy them or stop them from working.
