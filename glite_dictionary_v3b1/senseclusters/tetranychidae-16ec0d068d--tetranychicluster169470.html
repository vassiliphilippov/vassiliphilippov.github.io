Etymology

Tetranychidae comes from the scientific name Tetranychus, built from the Greek part tetra- meaning 'four' and a second part that is thought to mean 'claw'. That picture of a small 'four-clawed' creature is a helpful way to remember that Tetranychidae is the family of tiny, spider-like plant mites called spider mites that cling to leaves and can damage crops.
