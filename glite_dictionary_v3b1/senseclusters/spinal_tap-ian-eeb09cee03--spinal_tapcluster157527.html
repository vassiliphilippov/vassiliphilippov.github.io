Etymology

Spinal Tap-ian comes from the movie This Is Spinal Tap, a funny film that pretends to be a real documentary about a rock band full of silly, over-the-top mistakes. So calling something Spinal Tap-ian means it is 'funny, exaggerated, and a little absurd'.
