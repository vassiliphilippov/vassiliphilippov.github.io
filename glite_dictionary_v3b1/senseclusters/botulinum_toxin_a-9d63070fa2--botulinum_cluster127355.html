Etymology

Botulinum toxin A gets its name from an old Latin word 'botulus' meaning 'sausage', because early cases of the poisoning were linked to rotten sausages and people named the germ botulinum after that. Today, Botulinum toxin A is used in very small amounts as a medicine, often called 'Botox', to relax muscles and reduce wrinkles.
