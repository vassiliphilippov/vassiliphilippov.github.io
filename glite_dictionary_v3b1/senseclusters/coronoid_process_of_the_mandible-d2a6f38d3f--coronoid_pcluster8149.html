Etymology

coronoid process of the mandible may come from an old Greek word for 'crown' because the thin, triangle-shaped piece of bone on the jaw looks like a small crown or a bird's beak. A process is a part that sticks out and the mandible is the 'lower jaw', so the coronoid is the bony bit that sticks up and gives the chewing muscle a place to attach, which is why the name fits today.
