Etymology

The noun electronic stylus comes from joining electronic, meaning 'working with electricity', and stylus, a small pointed 'pen' used to write or draw. Imagine a little pen that uses electricity to touch a screen, so it means 'a pen-like tool for writing, drawing, or controlling a touchscreen'.
