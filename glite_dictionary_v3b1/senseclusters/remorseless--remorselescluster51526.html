Etymology

remorseless comes from remorse, which itself comes from the Latin parts re- ('again') and mordere ('to bite'), so remorse is like being 'bitten again' by guilt. So, to be remorseless is to have no 'biting' guilt or pity, which is why the word describes someone cruel and unrelenting.
