Etymology

Phalaropidae comes from the bird name Phalaropus and the ending -idae, which means 'family'. Phalaropus is made from old Greek parts meaning 'coot' and 'foot', a simple picture that points to their special swimming feet and explains why Phalaropidae is the family of these small water birds.
