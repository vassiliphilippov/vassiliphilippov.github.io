Etymology

The term remittance man comes from families who sent a 'remittance', a regular payment, to keep a man living abroad. The image of a lonely man living far away on that money, often because his family wanted him away, explains why the term came to mean 'a man supported by payments from home, often seen as an embarrassing or unwanted relative'.
