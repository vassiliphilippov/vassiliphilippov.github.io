Etymology

Ascophyllum nodosum gets its name from the parts Ascophyllum meaning 'leaf bag' and nodosum meaning 'knotty'. Its long stems have round bladders that look like little knots, so people call it 'knotted wrack' and often use it as a large brown seaweed for fertilizer.
