Etymology

Country borage gets its name from everyday village use: borage was an old name for plants with soft, often slightly hairy, scented leaves, and country shows it was the common, home-grown herb of the fields and kitchens. So the name helps you remember that 'country borage' is a simple aromatic herb used for cooking and healing.
