Etymology

SLS stands for sodium lauryl sulfate, the initials of a common cleaning chemical; sodium is like 'salt', and the whole ingredient makes lots of 'foam', which is why SLS is used in shampoos and toothpastes but can 'irritate' sensitive skin.
