Etymology

Urdu comes from an old Turkish and Persian word ordu meaning 'camp' or 'army', because it began as the 'language of the camp' used by soldiers and people from many regions. That's why Urdu mixed words from different languages and later became a common literary language in Pakistan and parts of India.
