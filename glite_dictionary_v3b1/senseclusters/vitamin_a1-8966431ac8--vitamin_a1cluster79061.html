Etymology

vitamin A1: The name vitamin A1 comes from early scientists who called vitamins 'vital amines' and used the letter A for the first group they found. Because this substance was found in fish liver oil and carrots and helps the eyes, vitamin A1 now means the eye-helping chemical retinol.
