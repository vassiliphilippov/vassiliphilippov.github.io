Etymology

downy cheat comes from downy (soft, hairy leaves) and cheat, an old farming word for a weed that 'cheats' crops by growing among them. So it paints the picture of a soft, hairy grass that sneaks into fields and takes water and light from other plants, which explains why it is a weed that competes with native grasses.
