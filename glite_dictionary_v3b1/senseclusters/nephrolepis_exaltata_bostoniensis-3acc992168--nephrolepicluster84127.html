Etymology

Nephrolepis exaltata bostoniensis is the long scientific name for the common 'Boston fern', a houseplant with long, curved leaves that hang like a green waterfall. The name's parts, Nephrolepis, exaltata and bostoniensis, may come from the fern's arching shape and the fact it became popular in Boston, so the name helps you remember this arching indoor fern.
