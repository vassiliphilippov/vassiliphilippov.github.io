Etymology

The word sclerophyllous comes from Greek parts sclero- meaning 'hard' and -phyllous from phyllon meaning 'leaf', as in chlorophyll. So a sclerophyllous plant has 'hard' or thick leaves that help it save water and live in dry or poor soil.
