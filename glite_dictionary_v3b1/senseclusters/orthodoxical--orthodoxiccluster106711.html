Etymology

The word orthodoxical comes from Greek parts ortho- meaning 'straight' or 'correct' and doxa meaning 'belief' or 'opinion'. It is closely related to orthodox, and the everyday word orthodontist also uses ortho- for 'straight'. So, orthodoxical describes beliefs or practices that are seen as the 'correct' or traditional way.
