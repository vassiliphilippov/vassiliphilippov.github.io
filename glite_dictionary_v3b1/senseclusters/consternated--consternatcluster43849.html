Etymology

consternated comes from the old Latin word consternare, made of con- 'together' and sternare 'to throw down' or 'to lay flat'. Imagine being 'thrown flat' by a sudden shock, and that picture helps explain why consternated means feeling sudden worry, confusion, or dismay.
