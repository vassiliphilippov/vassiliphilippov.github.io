Etymology

superload combines the prefix super-, meaning 'above' or 'extra' and found in familiar words like supermarket and superhero, with the common word load, meaning 'weight' or what something carries. So, a superload is an unusually heavy or extra-large weight placed on a bridge or vehicle, which is why engineers watch for it and special permits are often needed.
