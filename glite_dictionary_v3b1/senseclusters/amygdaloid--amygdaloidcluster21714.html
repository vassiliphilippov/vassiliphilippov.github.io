Etymology

The adjective amygdaloid comes from the old Greek amygdalē meaning 'almond' and the ending -oid meaning 'like', so it simply means 'shaped like an almond'. A helpful way to remember it is to think of an almond or the brain part amygdala, which is also named for the same almond shape.
