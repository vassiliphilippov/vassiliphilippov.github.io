Etymology

Ninkharsag comes from Sumerian parts Nin meaning 'lady' and kharsag meaning 'mountain' or 'sacred hill'. So she is the 'lady of the mountain', a mother figure tied to the earth and to birth and new life, which explains her role as a creator and mother goddess.
