Etymology

common plantain The name common plantain comes from an old Latin word 'plantago' meaning the 'sole of the foot' because plantain's broad, flat leaves look like a foot and people used them to help heal cuts and sores. Common simply means it grows everywhere, so the name helps you remember this low, wide-leaved weed found in many lawns.
