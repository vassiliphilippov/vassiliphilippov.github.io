Etymology

Aspidistra elatior comes from old Greek and Latin words. Aspidistra links to a Greek word for 'shield' because its wide, shiny leaves look like shields, and elatior means 'taller', so the name gives you a picture of a strong, taller houseplant that is also called the 'cast-iron plant' because it is 'hard to kill'.
