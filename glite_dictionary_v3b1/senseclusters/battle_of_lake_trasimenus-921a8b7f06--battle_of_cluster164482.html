Etymology

The Battle of Lake Trasimenus was a 'surprise attack' by the general Hannibal in 217 BC when he hid his soldiers in the hills and attacked the Roman army as it marched along the lake, trapping many men between the water and the hills. That's why the Battle of Lake Trasimenus is remembered as one of Hannibal's great victories and a shocking defeat for Rome.
