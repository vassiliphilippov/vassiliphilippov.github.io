Etymology

acute glaucoma is a medical name that gives a simple image: acute means sudden and glaucoma is when fluid in the eye cannot drain, so the eye acts like a tight water balloon when the drain is blocked. So, acute glaucoma means a sudden, painful rise in eye pressure that can cause 'vision loss' and needs quick treatment.
