Etymology

UT1 stands for Universal Time, and the '1' shows it is a precise version. It is 'based on the Earth's rotation' and measured at Greenwich, the place used as the 'zero' line of longitude, so UT1 is the time used in astronomy and navigation when very exact time is needed.
