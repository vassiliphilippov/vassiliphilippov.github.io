Etymology

embroidery stitch comes from the simple picture of using a needle and thread to make one small loop or pass in cloth. Each time the needle goes in and out it makes one stitch, and in embroidery those stitches together form the pattern, so embroidery stitch means 'a single decorative stitch'.
