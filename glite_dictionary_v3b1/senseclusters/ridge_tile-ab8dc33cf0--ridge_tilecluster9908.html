Etymology

Ridge tile is a simple name from old roofs: the word ridge is the top line where two sides of a roof meet and the word tile is a flat piece that covers a building. A ridge tile sits along the roof peak to keep out rain and sometimes looks decorative, so the name came to mean 'a specially shaped tile used to cover and protect the roof ridge'.
