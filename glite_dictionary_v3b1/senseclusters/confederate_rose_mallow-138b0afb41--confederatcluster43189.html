Etymology

Confederate rose mallow may come from gardeners in the 'southern United States' who grew this plant so often that the place name Confederate stuck, and from its large, rose-like blooms on a mallow shrub. So the name helps you picture a shrub with big, color-changing flowers grown in the American South.
