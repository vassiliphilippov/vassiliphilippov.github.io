Etymology

Iris persica comes from two old words: Iris, the Greek name for the 'rainbow' (and a goddess) because many iris flowers are colorful, and persica, a Latin word meaning 'from Persia' (now Iran). So Iris persica literally means a 'Persian iris', which fits this bulbous plant that came from western Asia and has pale lilac flowers.
