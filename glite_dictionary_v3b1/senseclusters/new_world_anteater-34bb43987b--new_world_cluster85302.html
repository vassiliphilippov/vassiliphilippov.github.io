Etymology

New World anteater is a name that joins New World, meaning the 'Americas', and anteater, meaning an animal that 'eats ants'. The picture is simple: a long-tongued, often toothless mammal from the 'Americas' that eats ants and termites, so the name is used for those American mammals today.
