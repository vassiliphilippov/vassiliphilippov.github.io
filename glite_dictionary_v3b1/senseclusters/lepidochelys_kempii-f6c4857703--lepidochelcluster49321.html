Etymology

Lepidochelys kempii comes from old Greek parts: lepido- meaning 'scale' and -chelys meaning 'turtle', and the second name kempii is named after a man called Kemp who helped find the species. So the scientific name points to the sea turtle known as 'Kemp's ridley'.
