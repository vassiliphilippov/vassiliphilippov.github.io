Etymology

frazzled probably comes from the verb frazzle, which is like frizz and fray and once had the sense 'to wear out' or 'to tangle'. A helpful way to remember it is to picture your nerves like hair that is all frizzed and messy, so being frazzled means very tired or stressed.
