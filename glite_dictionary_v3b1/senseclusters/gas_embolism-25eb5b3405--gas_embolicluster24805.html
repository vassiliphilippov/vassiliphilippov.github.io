Etymology

gas embolism paints a simple picture: tiny gas bubbles travel in the blood and get stuck, like stones blocking a pipe. That's why the word embolism means 'a thing that blocks' and the whole phrase means 'air or gas in the bloodstream that blocks blood vessels', which can be very dangerous.
