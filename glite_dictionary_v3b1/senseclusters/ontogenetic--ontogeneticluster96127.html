Etymology

ontogenetic comes from Greek roots ont- meaning 'being' and -genetic from genesis meaning 'birth' or 'origin', the same root as generate and gene. So ontogenetic is a useful word for the 'birth and life story' of a single living thing, its development from before birth to adulthood.
