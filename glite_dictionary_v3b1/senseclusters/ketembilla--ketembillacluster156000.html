Etymology

ketembilla is the local name in Sri Lanka and India for a small, spiny shrub that produces a maroon-purple, gooseberry-like fruit. So, remembering a little shrub that grows tart-sweet 'gooseberries' helps you recall that ketembilla refers to the plant itself.
