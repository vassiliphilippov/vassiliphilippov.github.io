Etymology

prairie golden aster is named for how and where it grows: prairie points to the wide dry grasslands, golden describes its bright yellow flowers, and aster is a word meaning 'star' because the flower looks like a small star. So the name tells you it is a yellow, daisy-like plant that grows on the prairie.
