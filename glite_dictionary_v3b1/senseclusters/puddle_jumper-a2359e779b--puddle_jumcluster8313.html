Etymology

puddle jumper may come from a simple picture: something small and light that can cross shallow water. The name joins puddle and jumper — imagine a small boat that skims over puddles or a child who literally jumps across puddles, and that's why it can mean both 'a small boat for shallow water' and 'a playful person who jumps puddles'.
