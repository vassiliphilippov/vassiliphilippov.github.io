Etymology

slough of despond comes from John Bunyan's old story where a traveler sinks into a muddy bog called the slough because of despond, a word for 'deep sadness'. That image of being stuck in a swamp helps explain why the phrase means 'a state of deep sadness that is hard to escape'.
