Etymology

The word anorthopia comes from the parts an- 'not', ortho 'straight', and -opia 'sight', and ortho is the same root in orthodontist, which relates to straight teeth. So anorthopia literally means 'not-straight sight', which is why it describes vision where straight lines look curved.
