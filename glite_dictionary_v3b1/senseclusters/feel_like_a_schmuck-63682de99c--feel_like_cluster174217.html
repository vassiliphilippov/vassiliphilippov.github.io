Etymology

feel like a schmuck uses schmuck, a Yiddish word that originally meant 'jewel' or 'ornament' but later became a rude name for someone acting foolish. So when you feel like a schmuck, you feel 'foolish' or 'embarrassed' because you see yourself as the fool.
