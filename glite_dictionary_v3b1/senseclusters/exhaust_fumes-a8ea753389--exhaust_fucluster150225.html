Etymology

The noun exhaust fumes comes from a simple picture: a car engine pushes out waste gases, so exhaust means the engine's used gases and fumes are the smoky gases that come out of the tailpipe. That's why the phrase means 'gases or smoke produced by a vehicle engine'.
