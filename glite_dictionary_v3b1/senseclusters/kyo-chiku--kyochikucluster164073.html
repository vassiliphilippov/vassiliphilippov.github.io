Etymology

kyo-chiku comes from Japanese parts kyo, often meaning 'capital' or linked to 'Kyoto', and chiku, which means 'bamboo'. So the name fits this plant: kyo-chiku is a very large, strong bamboo with hollow stems that can be used like a tree for building and furniture.
