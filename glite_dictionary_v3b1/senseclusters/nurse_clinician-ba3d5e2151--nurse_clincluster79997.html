Etymology

nurse clinician joins the words nurse, a trained caregiver, and clinician, someone who sees patients in a clinic. Imagine a nurse in a clinic who, after extra training, can make medical decisions and give medicines; that's why the term means a nurse who can 'diagnose and prescribe' and care for patients more independently.
