Etymology

Photinia arbutifolia may come from Greek and Latin roots: Photinia from a Greek word meaning 'shiny' and arbutifolia meaning 'leaves like Arbutus' or the 'strawberry tree'. So the name points to the plant's glossy leaves and red, berry-like fruits and helps you remember this evergreen shrub.
