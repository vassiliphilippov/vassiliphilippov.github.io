Etymology

Stereoscopic picture comes from an old viewing trick: two photos of the same scene taken from slightly different angles are seen together so your eyes join them into a '3D' image. The word stereo means 'two' and picture means 'image', so a stereoscopic picture is two photos made to look like a single 'three-dimensional' scene.
