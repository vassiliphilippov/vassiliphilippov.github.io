Etymology

quackgrass may be a folk name; a helpful way to remember it is to think of the part quack like a 'quack' doctor — something 'fake' or unwanted — because this grass looks like normal grass but is actually a stubborn weed. It is also called couch grass, and because it spreads quickly by underground roots and keeps coming back, that explains why it is such a nuisance.
