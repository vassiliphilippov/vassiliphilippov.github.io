Etymology

Titus Oates was an Englishman who in the 1600s made up a story about a secret 'Popish Plot' to kill the king and convinced many people it was true. His false story caused fear and unfair trials, so today his name is remembered as a symbol of a 'false accuser' or dangerous liar.
