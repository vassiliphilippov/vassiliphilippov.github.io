Etymology

whininess comes from the verb whine + -ness; whine is an old word that copies a long, high, unhappy sound, like a child or a dog making a sad noise ('to make a long, high, complaining sound'). So whininess is simply the quality of complaining in an annoying, high‑pitched way.
