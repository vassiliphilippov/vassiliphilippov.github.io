Etymology

maladjustive comes from the prefix mal- meaning 'bad' or 'wrong' and the word adjust meaning 'to change to fit', so it literally means 'bad at adjusting'. So maladjustive describes a person or behaviour that does not change or fit well to a situation and therefore fails to adapt.
