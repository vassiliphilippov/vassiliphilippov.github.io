Etymology

Eusebius Hieronymus is the full name of the scholar better known as Saint Jerome, who lived in the late 300s and early 400s and translated the Bible into Latin, creating the Vulgate. Because his work made the Bible readable for many people, he is remembered as a 'translator' and a 'saint'.
