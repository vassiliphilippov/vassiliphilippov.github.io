Etymology

cyanamide begins with cyan- meaning 'blue', think of the color cyan, and ends with -amide, a part that comes from ammonia, the common nitrogen chemical. So the name shows it is a nitrogen compound, which is why cyanamide is used in fertilizers and to make other chemicals.
