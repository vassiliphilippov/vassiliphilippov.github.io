Etymology

non-insulin-dependent diabetes: The medical name non-insulin-dependent diabetes literally says the illness is not dependent on injected insulin. Because many people with this form still make some insulin and can often control it with diet and exercise, the phrase came to mean 'type 2 diabetes' today.
