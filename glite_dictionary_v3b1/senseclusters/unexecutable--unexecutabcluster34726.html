Etymology

The word unexecutable starts with un- meaning 'not', then execute meaning 'to carry out' or 'to run', and the ending -able meaning 'can be done'. So, unexecutable simply means something that 'cannot be carried out' or 'cannot be run', often used for a computer file or a plan that will not work.
