Etymology

wiseling is made from wise + the small-person ending -ling, like duckling or underling, where -ling can mean 'small' or 'person'. A helpful way to remember the modern, negative sense is to think of a 'small-wise' person who only looks wise, so a wiseling is someone who pretends to be wise but is not.
