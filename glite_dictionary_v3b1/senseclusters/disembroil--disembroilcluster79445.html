Etymology

disembroil is made of dis- and embroil; dis- is the same part you see in disconnect or disagree, meaning 'remove' or 'not', and embroil comes from a word that meant 'to tangle' or 'be in trouble'. So, disembroil means to 'undo a tangle' and free someone or something from a difficult or confused situation.
