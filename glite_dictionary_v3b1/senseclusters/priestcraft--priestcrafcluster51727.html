Etymology

The word priestcraft joins priest and craft, where craft once meant both 'skill' and 'trick', like the English word crafty. So priestcraft is easy to remember as priests using skill or tricks to control people for personal or group gain, which is why it means the use of religious power to control or benefit themselves.
