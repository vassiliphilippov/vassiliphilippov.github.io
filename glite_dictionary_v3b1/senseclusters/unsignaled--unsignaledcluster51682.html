Etymology

unsignaled starts with the prefix un- meaning 'not' and the word signal meaning 'a sign or warning', so it describes something done with 'no warning' or 'not shown'. Think of a driver who turns without showing a signal — that is an unsignaled turn, which helps explain the modern meaning.
