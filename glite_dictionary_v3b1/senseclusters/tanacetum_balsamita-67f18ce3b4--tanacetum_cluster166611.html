Etymology

The plant name Tanacetum balsamita combines the old name for the tansy family, Tanacetum, with balsamita, which points to a 'balsam' or sweet, resin smell. Because people used the leaves in tea, salads and potpourri, the name helps us remember this herb by its scent and use.
