Etymology

hypercholesteremia breaks into hyper- ('too much'), cholesterol ('a fat in the blood'), and -emia ('blood'), like hyper- in hyperactive and -emia in anemia. So hypercholesteremia means having too much cholesterol in the blood, which is why it raises the risk of heart disease.
