Etymology

yearner comes from the verb yearn, which means 'to long' or 'to want something very much'. It comes from an old English word meaning 'eager', so a yearner is someone who feels a strong, lasting desire, and a helpful way to remember it is to imagine wanting something for a whole 'year'.
