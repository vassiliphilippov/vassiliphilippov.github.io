Etymology

The term epiphytic plant gives a simple picture: a small plant sitting on a tree branch, using the tree just for support, with epiphytic showing 'on' and plant the living plant. It takes water from the air and rain, not from the host, so epiphytic plant means 'a plant that grows on another plant for support without harming it'.
