Etymology

ambonoclast comes from the Greek parts ambon meaning 'pulpit' and -clast meaning 'breaker', as in the word iconoclast. So an ambonoclast is someone who 'breaks' or attacks public teachings and long traditions, which is why it means a person who challenges established beliefs.
