Etymology

servomechanical combines servo- (from a word like service meaning 'to serve' or 'help') and mechanical meaning 'machine' or 'using machines'. So a servomechanical device is like a 'serving machine' that watches its own movement and corrects itself, which is why it is controlled automatically and stays accurate.
