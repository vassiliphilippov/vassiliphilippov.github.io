Etymology

The name wireless fidelity may come from the old music term high fidelity, called 'Hi-Fi', which meant very clear sound. Makers picked a similar-sounding name so wireless fidelity ('Wi-Fi') would sound like a reliable, high-quality wireless connection, and that's why it now means the system for wireless internet.
