Etymology

Vacuum bomb gets its name from how it works. The bomb spreads fuel into the air and, when it burns, the explosion briefly creates a vacuum or 'empty space' that pulls air back in and causes a stronger 'blast', so the name tells you it makes a very powerful, damaging explosion.
