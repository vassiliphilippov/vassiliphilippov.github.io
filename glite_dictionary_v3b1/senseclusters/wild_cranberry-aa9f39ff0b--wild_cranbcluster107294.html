Etymology

wild cranberry combines wild and cranberry; one popular story says 'cranberry' may come from people who thought the flower looked like a crane's beak, so they called it a 'crane berry'. The word wild shows the plant grows naturally, so the name points to the low shrub with small red berries you find in the wild.
