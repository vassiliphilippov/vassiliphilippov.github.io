Etymology

Antiarrhythmic medication comes from the parts anti- (meaning 'against') and arrhythmic (meaning 'without a steady rhythm'), giving the image of a medicine that fights an 'irregular heartbeat'. That's why it means a drug used to prevent or fix an 'abnormal heart rhythm'.
