Etymology

sixty-one is simply the parts sixty and one, so it means 'sixty plus one'. The sixty part comes from six plus the ending -ty, which means 'ten' or a group of ten like in twenty, so sixty-one is literally 'six tens plus one' and is one more than sixty.
