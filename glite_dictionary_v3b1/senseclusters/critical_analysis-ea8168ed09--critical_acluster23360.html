Etymology

Critical analysis comes from two simple words: critical, meaning someone who 'judges', and analysis, meaning looking at something by breaking it into parts. Imagine a reader with a red pen dividing a text to find good and bad points, so the phrase means a careful study that looks closely and 'judges' strengths and faults.
