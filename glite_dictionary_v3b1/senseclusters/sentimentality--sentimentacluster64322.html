Etymology

sentimentality comes from the Latin sentire meaning 'to feel' and from sentiment, a 'feeling', and it shares the same root as sense and sensitive. So sentimentality is like 'too much feeling' — showing emotions in a way that is overly emotional or not real, which is why we use it for writing or films that feel exaggerated.
