Etymology

smoothbore comes from the simple parts smooth and bore, where smooth means 'without roughness' and bore means 'a hole or tube'. So a smoothbore gun is one with a barrel that is 'smooth' inside, with no grooves to make the shot spin, which helps explain why it is less accurate at long distances.
