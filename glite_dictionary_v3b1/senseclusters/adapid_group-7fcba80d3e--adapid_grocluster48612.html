Etymology

adapid group comes from the fossil name Adapis, after the first small primate bones found near Paris in the 1800s. Because those bones were from small, mostly daytime animals that look like the ancestors of lemurs, adapid group now means a group of 'early primates' thought to be 'related to lemurs'.
