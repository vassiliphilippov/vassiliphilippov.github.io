Etymology

Paul the Apostle was first called Saul, and one famous story says he saw a bright light on the road to Damascus and then changed his life to travel and teach about Jesus. Because he traveled to many places and wrote letters to new churches, people call him an 'apostle' and remember him for those journeys and writings.
