Etymology

smorgasbord comes from Swedish smörgås ('buttered bread' or 'sandwich') and bord ('table'), so imagine a table piled with many different foods. That image makes it easy to remember that smorgasbord can also mean a wide 'variety' or 'assortment' of things.
