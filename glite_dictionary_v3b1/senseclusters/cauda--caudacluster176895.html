Etymology

cauda comes from the Latin cauda, which means 'tail', so think of the simple English word 'tail'. In anatomy it names tail-like parts; for example the cauda equina is a bundle of nerves at the base of the spine that looks like a horse's tail, which is why cauda refers to structures that act or look like a tail.
