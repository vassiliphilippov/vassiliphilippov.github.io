Etymology

stress incontinence comes from the words stress and incontinence. Imagine putting extra 'pressure' on your lower belly when you cough, laugh, or lift something and the urine 'leaks' because the muscles that hold it are weak. So, stress incontinence means 'urine leaks during physical activity'.
