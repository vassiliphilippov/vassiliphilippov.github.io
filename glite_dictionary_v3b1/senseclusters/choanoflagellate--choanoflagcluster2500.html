Etymology

choanoflagellate comes from Greek parts choano- meaning 'funnel' or 'collar' and flagellum meaning 'whip'. A choanoflagellate is a single-celled creature with a collar and a whip-like tail for catching food, so the name describes its shape and how it feeds.
