Etymology

Trichopterous insect comes from Greek words: trich- meaning 'hair' and -pterous meaning 'wing', so the name gives the picture of a 'hairy-winged' insect. That fits the 'caddis fly', which has fuzzy wings and young that live in water and build small protective cases, so the name helps you remember its look and life.
