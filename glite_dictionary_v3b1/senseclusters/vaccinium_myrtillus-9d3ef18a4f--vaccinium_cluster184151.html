Etymology

Vaccinium myrtillus comes from Latin words for small berry plants: Vaccinium named berry shrubs and myrtillus means 'little myrtle', because the plant's leaves and dark berries reminded people of the myrtle. That's why the Latin name refers to the bilberry we know today.
