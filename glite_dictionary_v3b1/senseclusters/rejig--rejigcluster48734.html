Etymology

rejig comes from re- meaning 'again' and jig meaning a 'quick move' or a small tool used to hold parts. Think of giving a machine a little 'jig' or dance to move its pieces into new places, so to rejig something is to rearrange or adjust equipment or processes to make them work better.
