Etymology

clack comes from a simple sound word that copies a quick, sharp noise, like the sound of people clicking their tongues when they chatter; a helpful way to remember it is to think of fast, noisy talk. So, to clack about something is to talk quickly and lightly, like making repeated little noises with your mouth.
