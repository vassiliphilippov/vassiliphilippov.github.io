Etymology

nomograph comes from Greek parts nomo- meaning 'rule' or 'law' and -graph meaning 'to draw' or 'write', and the -graph part is the same as in photograph. So a nomograph is a drawn chart that shows a math rule and lets you read answers by aligning lines instead of doing long calculations.
