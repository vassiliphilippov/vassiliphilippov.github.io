Etymology

The Feast of Tabernacles, also called Sukkot, takes its name from the word tabernacle, which means a temporary shelter. Long ago the Israelites lived in simple huts during 40 years in the desert, and families still build small shelters called 'sukkahs' to remember that time. So the festival is about remembering those shelters and giving thanks.
