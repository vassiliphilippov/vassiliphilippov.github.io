Etymology

Puerto Rican comes from the island name Puerto Rico, which in Spanish means 'rich port'. One popular story says early Spanish sailors called the island that because it had a good port and some gold, and people from there became known as Puerto Ricans, so the term means 'someone from Puerto Rico'.
