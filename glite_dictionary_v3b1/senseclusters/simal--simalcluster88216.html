Etymology

The word simal comes from Indian languages such as Hindi simal and Bengali shimul, where it names the silk-cotton tree. A helpful way to remember it is to think of the tree's soft seed fibers like 'silk' used to stuff mattresses and pillows, so simal refers both to the tree and to the stuffing made from it.
