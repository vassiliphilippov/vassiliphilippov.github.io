Etymology

Penstemon centranthifolius is named for what the flower and leaves look like: Penstemon suggests a flower with a small hairy 'beardtongue' inside the tube, and centranthifolius means it has leaves like the plant Centranthus. So the name helps you picture a red, tube-shaped flower with a fuzzy tongue inside and familiar leaves, which fits this hummingbird-attracting wildflower.
