Etymology

carrick bitt comes from old ship work: it named a pair of strong posts on the deck where sailors wound the anchor chain. Wrapping the chain around the bitt kept the anchor fast and helped the 'windlass', a machine that lifts the anchor, so the term now means the posts that support a windlass or hold the anchor.
