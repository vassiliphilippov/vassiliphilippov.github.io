Etymology

The phrase good head on one's shoulders comes from the simple image of a head sitting firmly on a person's shoulders, able to guide and steady the body. So, calling someone a good head on their shoulders means they think clearly and make sensible choices, that is, 'to have sound judgment or practical common sense'.
