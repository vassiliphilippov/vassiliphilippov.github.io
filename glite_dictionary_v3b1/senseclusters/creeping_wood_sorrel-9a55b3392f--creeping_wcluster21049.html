Etymology

creeping wood sorrel comes from what the plant does and how it tastes: creeping because it spreads across the ground, wood because it often grows in woods, and sorrel because its leaves are slightly 'sour'. So the name helps you remember this small, spreading weed with clover-like leaves and yellow flowers.
