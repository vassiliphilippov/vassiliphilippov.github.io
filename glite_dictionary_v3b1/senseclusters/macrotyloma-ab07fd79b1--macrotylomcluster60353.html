Etymology

Macrotyloma comes from Greek roots: macro- meaning 'large' and -tyloma related to a word for a 'knot' or 'swelling', which gives the image of a thick pod. So, think 'large pod' to remember Macrotyloma, a group of climbing plants that are grown for food.
