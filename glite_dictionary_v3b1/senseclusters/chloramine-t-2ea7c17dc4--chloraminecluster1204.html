Etymology

chloramine-T breaks into parts that tell you what it is: chloro- means 'chlorine' and -amine means 'a nitrogen part', so it is a chlorine-containing nitrogen chemical. The final T marks a special chemical group in the molecule, and because it has chlorine it is used to kill germs and act as a disinfectant.
