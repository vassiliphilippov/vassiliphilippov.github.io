Etymology

common maidenhair may get its name from the fern's very thin, soft leaves that look like a young woman's hair; people used maiden and hair to paint the picture of its delicate fronds and dark, shiny stalks. So the words maiden ('young woman') and hair point to the plant's fragile, hair-like leaves and explain the name for this fern.
