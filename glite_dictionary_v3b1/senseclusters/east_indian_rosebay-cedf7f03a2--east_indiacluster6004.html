Etymology

East Indian rosebay may come from its two parts: East Indian meaning 'from eastern India' and rosebay meaning a shrub with rose-like flowers. The image of a showy, rose-like shrub from eastern India helps explain why the name refers to a tropical 'shrub' with glossy leaves and sweet-smelling night flowers.
