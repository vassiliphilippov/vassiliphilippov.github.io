Etymology

Babylonian weeping willow may come from a famous Bible picture: people taken to 'Babylon' sat by the rivers and hung their harps on willow branches as they cried. The tree's long, drooping branches look like tears, so weeping and willow explain why the name now means a willow whose branches seem to 'weep'.
