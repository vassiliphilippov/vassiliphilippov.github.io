Etymology

Polyporus comes from Greek parts poly- ('many') and poros ('pore' or 'small hole'), so it means 'many pores'. These shelf-like fungi often have lots of tiny holes on the underside where they release spores, so thinking 'many holes' makes the name easy to remember.
