Etymology

Magellanic Cloud comes from the explorer Ferdinand Magellan, who in the 1500s wrote about a faint, cloud-like patch he saw in the southern sky. Sailors called such bright patches 'clouds', and because Magellan noted them the name now refers to the two 'small galaxies' visible from the Southern Hemisphere.
