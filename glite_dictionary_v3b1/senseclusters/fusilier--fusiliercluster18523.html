Etymology

fusilier comes from the French fusil, which means a 'small gun' or 'flintlock', and that word came from an old word for 'spindle' because the gun looked long and spindle-like. So a fusilier was originally a soldier who carried a fusil, which is why the name means that kind of soldier.
