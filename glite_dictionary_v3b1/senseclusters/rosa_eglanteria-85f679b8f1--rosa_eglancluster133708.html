Etymology

Rosa eglanteria is the name of a wild rose also called 'sweet briar'; Rosa means 'rose' and eglanteria comes from an old word for 'sweet briar' because the leaves smell sweet when you rub them and the plant makes bright red 'hips' that attract birds. So the name points to the plant's sweet scent and red fruits.
