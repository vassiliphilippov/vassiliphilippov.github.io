Etymology

Relafen is a brand name for the drug nabumetone, a pain medicine that reduces swelling. A helpful way to remember it is to see rela- like the word 'relief', and the ending may remind you of ibuprofen and other pain medicines, so Relafen is a medicine for relief from pain and inflammation.
