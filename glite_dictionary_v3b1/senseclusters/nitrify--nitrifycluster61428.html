Etymology

nitrify comes from the root nitri- from nitre, a word for 'salt' or a 'salt-like chemical', and the ending -fy which means 'to make'. This nitri- root is the same one in nitrogen and nitrate, so to nitrify is to 'make' or treat something with nitrogen chemicals, as farmers do when they add nitrates to soil to help plants grow.
