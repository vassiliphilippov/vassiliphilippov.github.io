Etymology

The word hyperbolize comes from Greek parts hyper meaning 'over' and bole meaning 'throw', giving the image of 'throwing beyond' the truth. Think of hyper like in hyperactive which means 'too much', so to hyperbolize is to make something seem 'too big' or more extreme than it really is.
