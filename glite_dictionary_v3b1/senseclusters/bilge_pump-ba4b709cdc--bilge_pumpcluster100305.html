Etymology

bilge pump comes from the words bilge (the ship's lowest part or 'belly' where water collects) and pump (a machine that moves water). Picture the ship's belly filling with water and the pump pulling it out, and you see why bilge pump means 'a pump that removes water from the bottom of a ship or boat'.
