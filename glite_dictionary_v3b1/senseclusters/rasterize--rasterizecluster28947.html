Etymology

rasterize comes from raster, a word for a 'grid' or pattern of lines that goes back to an old word for 'rake', and the ending -ize means 'to make'. So, to rasterize is to change an image into a grid of pixels, which is exactly what happens when you convert a picture for the screen.
