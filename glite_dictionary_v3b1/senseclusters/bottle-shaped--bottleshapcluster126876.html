Etymology

Bottle-shaped comes from the common word bottle, a container with a narrow neck, and the part -shaped, which means 'in the form of' or 'having the shape of'. So, bottle-shaped describes something that is 'in the shape of a bottle', like a vase or an animal body that is narrow at one end.
