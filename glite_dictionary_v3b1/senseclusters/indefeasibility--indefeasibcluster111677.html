Etymology

indefeasibility starts with in- meaning 'not' and shares a root with the familiar word defeat, which means to 'overturn' or 'cancel' something. So indefeasibility is the quality of not being overturned or cancelled, which is why we use it in law for rights or titles that cannot be undone.
