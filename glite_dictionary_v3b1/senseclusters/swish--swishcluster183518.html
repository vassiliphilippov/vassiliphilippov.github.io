Etymology

A helpful way to remember swish meaning 'elegant' is to think of the smooth sound of fine clothes moving, the soft 'swish' of silk or a smart dress. Because that pleasing, smooth noise is linked with fine outfits, swish came to mean stylish and luxurious.
