Etymology

Cross-Florida Waterway gets its name from the simple picture of a cross (to go across) of Florida by a waterway (a route for boats). It uses rivers, canals and Lake Okeechobee so small boats can travel between the Atlantic and the Gulf, so the name is a literal 'shortcut' across the state.
