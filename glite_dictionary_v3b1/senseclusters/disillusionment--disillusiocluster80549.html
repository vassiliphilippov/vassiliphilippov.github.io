Etymology

disillusionment breaks into dis-, as in disagree, meaning 'not' or 'remove', and illusion, meaning 'a false idea'. It is like taking off a blindfold or mask and seeing the truth, so disillusionment is the disappointment you feel when something is not as good as you believed.
