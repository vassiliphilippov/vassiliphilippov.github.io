Etymology

pulmonary trunk uses a simple picture: pulmonary means 'lung' and trunk is like the main stem of a tree that splits into branches. So the name helps you remember that this large blood vessel is the main stem leaving the heart that splits into right and left branches to take blood to the 'lungs'.
