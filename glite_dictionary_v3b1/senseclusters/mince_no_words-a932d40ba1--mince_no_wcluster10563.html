Etymology

The idiom mince no words comes from the verb mince, which means 'to cut into tiny pieces', and the idea of chopping words small to make them sound softer. When someone minces no words, they refuse to cut their words and speak 'plainly and directly', so the phrase means 'to speak plainly and directly'.
