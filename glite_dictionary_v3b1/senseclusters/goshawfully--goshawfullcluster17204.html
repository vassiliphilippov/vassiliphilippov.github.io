Etymology

The word goshawfully comes from joining gosh, a mild form of 'God', and awfully, which once meant 'full of wonder' and later came to mean 'very'. So goshawfully is a friendly, old-fashioned way to say 'extremely', which is why it makes words like 'hot' or 'tired' much stronger.
