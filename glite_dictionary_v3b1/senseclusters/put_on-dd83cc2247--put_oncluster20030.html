Etymology

The phrasal verb put on here covers playing music and getting ready: it comes from placing a record or song onto a player so the music starts, and from placing clothing onto your body to go out. You put a record on to play music or put on a dress to leave, so it means 'to play music', 'to dress', or 'to get ready to enjoy yourself'.
