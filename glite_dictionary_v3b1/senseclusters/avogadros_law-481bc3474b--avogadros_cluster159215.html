Etymology

Avogadro's law is named after the scientist Amedeo Avogadro, who pictured equal-size balloons of different gases at the same temperature and pressure and thought each would hold the same number of tiny beads, 'molecules'. So, Avogadro's law means that equal volumes of gas under the same conditions contain the 'same number of molecules'.
