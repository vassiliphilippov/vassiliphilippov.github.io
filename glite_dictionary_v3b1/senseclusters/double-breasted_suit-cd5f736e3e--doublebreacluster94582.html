Etymology

The noun double-breasted suit comes from the jacket's front: double means two and breasted points to the 'breast' or front, where two wide flaps overlap and fasten with two rows of buttons. Because the front overlaps and has extra buttons, the jacket looks stronger and more 'formal', so the name now refers to that smart, classic style.
