Etymology

press sheet comes from the old printing process: a large sheet of paper put through the press that held many pages before it was cut and folded. Because it came straight from the 'press' and was still one big 'sheet', printers called it a press sheet, so today it means a printed sheet 'not yet folded or cut'.
