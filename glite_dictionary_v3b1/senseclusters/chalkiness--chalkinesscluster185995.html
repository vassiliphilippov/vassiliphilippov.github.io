Etymology

The word chalkiness comes from chalk, the soft white rock used for school chalk that leaves white dust, and the ending -ness that means 'a state or quality'. So chalkiness means 'being like chalk', for example something powdery, dry, or that leaves a white dust in taste, texture, or look.
