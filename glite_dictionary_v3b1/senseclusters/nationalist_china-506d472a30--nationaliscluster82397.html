Etymology

Nationalist China gets its name from the 'Nationalist party' that led China before 1949. When that party and its leader Chiang Kai-shek lost the civil war and moved their government to the island of Taiwan, people used 'Nationalist China' to mean the 'Republic of China' there, so today the phrase refers to that government.
