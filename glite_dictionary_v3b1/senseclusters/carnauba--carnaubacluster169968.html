Etymology

carnauba is the Portuguese name carnaúba, taken from a native Tupi word for a Brazilian palm tree. People call this palm the 'tree of life' because it gives food, strong leaf fiber and a brittle yellowish 'wax', so the plant and its wax share the same name.
