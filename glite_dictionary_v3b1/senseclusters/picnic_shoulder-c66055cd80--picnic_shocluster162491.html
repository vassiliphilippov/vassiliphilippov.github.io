Etymology

Picnic shoulder comes from a simple idea: the lower part of a pig's shoulder was often cured or smoked and taken to a picnic because it was easy to carry and share. So picnic shoulder now means the lower part of the pig's shoulder, a 'cut of pork' often smoked or slow-cooked.
