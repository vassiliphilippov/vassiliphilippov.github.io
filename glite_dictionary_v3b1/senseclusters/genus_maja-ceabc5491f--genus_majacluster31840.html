Etymology

genus Maja comes from scientific naming: genus means a 'group' or 'type', and Maja is the name given to this group of spider crabs. Think of a family called 'Maja' with many spiny, long-legged crabs, and you have the image behind why genus Maja means that group of spider crab species today.
