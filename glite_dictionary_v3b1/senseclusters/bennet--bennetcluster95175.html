Etymology

The word bennet may come from an old root ben- meaning 'good' or 'blessed', the same root you see in benefit, so a helpful way to remember it is to think of its small white flowers as a 'blessing' in the garden. That's why bennet names a small, pleasant flowering plant gardeners often like.
