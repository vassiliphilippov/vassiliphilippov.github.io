Etymology

Oregon myrtle comes from a simple name story: people long ago in Oregon called this tree Oregon because it grows there and added myrtle because its leaves smell like the common myrtle or bay leaves. That's why 'Oregon myrtle' now means an aromatic coastal tree with hard, useful wood.
