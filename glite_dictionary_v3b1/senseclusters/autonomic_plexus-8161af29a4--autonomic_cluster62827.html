Etymology

autonomic plexus comes from words that make a clear picture: autonomic means 'automatic' or 'working by itself' and plexus means a braided net of nerves. Imagine that net quietly controlling breathing, digestion and other body jobs you do 'without thinking', and that's why the term means the network of nerves that controls those automatic functions.
