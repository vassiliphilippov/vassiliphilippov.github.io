Etymology

brown Betty may come from a simple image: the dish turns a deep brown when the apples and crumbs bake, and 'Betty' was a friendly name people used for home-cooked dishes. So, the name makes you think of a warm, home-made 'dessert' of baked apples, which is why it refers to that sweet pudding today.
