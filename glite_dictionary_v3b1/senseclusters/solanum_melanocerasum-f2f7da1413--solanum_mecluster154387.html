Etymology

Solanum melanocerasum may come from words that describe the plant's look: Solanum is the name for the nightshade family, while melano- means 'black' and -cerasum is like the old word for 'cherry', so the name paints the picture of a small black, cherry-like berry. That's why the name fits a garden nightshade grown for its small orange or black berries.
