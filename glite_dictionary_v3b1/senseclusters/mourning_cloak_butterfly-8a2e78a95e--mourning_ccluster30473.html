Etymology

The name mourning cloak butterfly may come from the insect's dark, soft wings that look like a heavy coat. The phrase mourning cloak refers to the dark coat people wear in 'mourning', and the butterfly's dark wings with a pale yellow edge look like that coat, so the name helps you remember this butterfly.
