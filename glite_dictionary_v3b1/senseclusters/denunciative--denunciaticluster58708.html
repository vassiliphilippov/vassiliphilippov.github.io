Etymology

denunciative comes from the verb denounce, which is made of de- and nounce, the same nounce you see in announce and pronounce, and originally meant 'to make known' often as a warning. So a denunciative remark is one that publicly warns or strongly condemns someone, which is why it sounds harsh.
