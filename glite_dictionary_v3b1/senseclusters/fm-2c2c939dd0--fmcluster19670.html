Etymology

Fm stands for the element fermium, which is named after the physicist Enrico Fermi, and the ending -ium is a usual ending for metals. Because Fermi worked on nuclear science, Fm is a man-made, radioactive metal with atomic number 100 that is found and studied only in scientific research.
