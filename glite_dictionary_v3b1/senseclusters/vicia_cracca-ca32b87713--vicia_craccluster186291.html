Etymology

Vicia cracca gets its name from the Latin Vicia, meaning 'vetch', and from cracca, a word that may point to its dense clusters of purple flowers. So the name paints a simple picture: a 'vetch' with a purple 'tuft', which helps you remember this climbing plant.
