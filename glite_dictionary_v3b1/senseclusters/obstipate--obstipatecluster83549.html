Etymology

obstipate comes from Latin parts ob- meaning 'against' or 'completely' and stipare meaning 'to pack'. Like the similar word constipate, it paints a picture of the bowels being 'packed' so they stop moving, which is why to obstipate is to cause severe constipation.
