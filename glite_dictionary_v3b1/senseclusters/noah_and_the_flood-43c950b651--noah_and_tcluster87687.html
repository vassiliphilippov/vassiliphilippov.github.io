Etymology

Noah and the Flood is the Bible story of Noah, who built a large boat called an ark to save his family and animals when God sent a huge flood that covered the earth. That clear image of an ark with rising water explains why the phrase means the 'great flood' from the Bible and is used to talk about complete destruction or a huge, world-changing event.
