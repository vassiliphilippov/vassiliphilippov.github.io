Etymology

The phrase dubious pelf puts together dubious (meaning doubtful) and the old word pelf, which meant loot or money gained in a dishonest way. Picture a hidden bag of coins that looks stolen, and you have the memory for why the phrase means 'wealth of questionable origin'.
