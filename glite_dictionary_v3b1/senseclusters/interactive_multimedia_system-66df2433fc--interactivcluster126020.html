Etymology

interactive multimedia system combines two simple ideas: interactive, which means you can 'choose' or 'control' what happens, and multimedia, which means 'many media' like text, sound, and video. Imagine a museum screen you touch to play a video or hear audio and you will see why the phrase means a system that mixes different media you can interact with.
