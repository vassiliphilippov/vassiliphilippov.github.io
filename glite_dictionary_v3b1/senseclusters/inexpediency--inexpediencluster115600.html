Etymology

inexpediency comes from in- meaning 'not' and expedient, from a Latin verb meaning 'to make ready' and the same root as expedite and expedition. So, if something is 'inexpedient' it is 'not practical' or 'not useful for the job', and inexpediency is the state of being unsuitable or unwise for a purpose.
