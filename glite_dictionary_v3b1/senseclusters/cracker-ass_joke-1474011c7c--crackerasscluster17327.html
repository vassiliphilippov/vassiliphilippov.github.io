Etymology

cracker-ass joke comes from a slang way of making an insult: cracker used to mean 'cheap' or 'lame' and ass makes the word harsher. So a cracker-ass joke gives the image of a really weak, low-quality joke, and that's why people use it to call a joke 'very bad' or 'not funny'.
