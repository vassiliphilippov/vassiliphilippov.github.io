Etymology

The noun high commissioner comes from the time of the British Empire and the later group of countries called the Commonwealth. Think of a senior official given a special 'commission' to represent one friendly country to another: high shows senior rank and commissioner shows an official role, so a high commissioner is a top diplomat who serves like an 'ambassador' between Commonwealth countries.
