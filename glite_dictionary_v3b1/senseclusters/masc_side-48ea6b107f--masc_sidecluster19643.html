Etymology

masc side probably comes from talking about a person's different 'sides', with masc as a short form of 'masculine' and side meaning a part of them. Imagine someone showing the masc side by dressing or acting in a more male way. That's why masc side means the part of a person's character that shows 'masculine' traits.
