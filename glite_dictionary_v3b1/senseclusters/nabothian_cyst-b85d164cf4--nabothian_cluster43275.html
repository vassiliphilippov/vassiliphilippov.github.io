Etymology

nabothian cyst is a medical name that comes from a doctor named Naboth who first described the small, mucus-filled bumps on the cervix. The word cyst means a little sac, so the name paints the image of a tiny sac of mucus from a blocked gland, which is why it refers today to a usually 'harmless' mucus-filled lump.
