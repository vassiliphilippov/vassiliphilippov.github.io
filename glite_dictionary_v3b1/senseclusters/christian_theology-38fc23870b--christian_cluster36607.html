Etymology

Christian theology joins Christian (about Jesus and his followers) and theology, a word from old Greek that means 'study of God' or 'talk about God'. So Christian theology simply means the 'study of God' within the Christian faith, which is why it refers to the academic study and teachings of Christianity.
