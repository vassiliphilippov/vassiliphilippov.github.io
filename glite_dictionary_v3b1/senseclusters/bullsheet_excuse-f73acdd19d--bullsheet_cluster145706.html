Etymology

The idiom bullsheet excuse comes from the rude word 'bullshit', where bull stands for nonsense and sheet is a polite swap for the rude part. Imagine a loud bull blowing a lot of hot air—this picture helps you remember it means an excuse that is clearly false or 'nonsense'.
