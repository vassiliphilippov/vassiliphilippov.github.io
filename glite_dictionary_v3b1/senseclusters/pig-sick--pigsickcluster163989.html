Etymology

pig-sick comes from the image of being as sick as a pig after eating too much or from strong disgust. A helpful way to remember it is to think of pig as in pig out ('eat too much') and sick ('ill' or 'fed up'), so pig-sick means feeling very ill or completely fed up.
