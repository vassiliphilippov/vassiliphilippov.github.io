Etymology

confiture comes from the French confit, from the Latin conficere, which means 'to prepare' or 'to make'. That idea of fruit 'prepared' and kept in sugar led to the modern meaning, and the same root appears in confection ('a sweet'), so confiture today means a jam or preserve.
