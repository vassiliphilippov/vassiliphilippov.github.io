Etymology

cavalierly comes from cavalier, which originally meant a 'horseman' or rider and shares its root with cavalry. Because mounted soldiers were seen as proud and free, cavalier came to mean 'proud' or 'not caring about others', so to act cavalierly is to behave in a proud, careless way that ignores proper concern.
