Etymology

tomato concentrate comes from the simple kitchen idea of making tomatoes thicker by removing most of their water: ripe tomatoes are cooked or pressed until only a rich, thick concentrate remains. That's why a small spoonful adds a 'stronger' tomato taste to sauces and soups.
