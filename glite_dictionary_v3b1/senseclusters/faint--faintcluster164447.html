Etymology

faint comes from an old French word feint and the Latin fingere, which mean 'to pretend' or 'to make'; a helpful way to remember the first meaning is to think of someone whose strength is nearly 'made away' so they feel weak. That's why faint can mean feeling weak and close to losing consciousness.
