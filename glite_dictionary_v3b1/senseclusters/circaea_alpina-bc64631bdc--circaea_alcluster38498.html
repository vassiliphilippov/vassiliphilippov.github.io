Etymology

Circaea alpina is named from myth and place. The first part, Circaea, comes from Circe, a famous witch in old stories, so people called it an 'enchanter's' nightshade, and the second part, alpina, means 'from the Alps' or mountains, which is why the name fits a plant that lives in high, shaded mountain areas.
