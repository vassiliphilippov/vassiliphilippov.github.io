Etymology

To couch an idea comes from the Old French verb coucher 'to lie down', and a helpful way to remember it is to think of 'laying down' your words carefully; to couch something is to put it into a particular form. That's why when you couch a sentence you are choosing how to 'lay out' your meaning.
