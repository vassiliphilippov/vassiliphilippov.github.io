Etymology

downy ground cherry: The name downy ground cherry comes from how the plant looks: small round berries like cherries sit near the ground inside a papery husk, and the leaves and stems are soft and downy, or 'fuzzy'. So the words downy and ground cherry simply describe its fuzzy parts and cherry-like fruit.
