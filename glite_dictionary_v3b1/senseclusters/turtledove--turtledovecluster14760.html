Etymology

The word turtledove comes from an old word turtur that copies the bird's soft 'turr-turr' coo and the word dove meaning the bird. So the name itself sounds gentle, which helps explain why turtledoves are a long-standing symbol of devoted love, and the 'turtle' part is about the sound, not the reptile.
