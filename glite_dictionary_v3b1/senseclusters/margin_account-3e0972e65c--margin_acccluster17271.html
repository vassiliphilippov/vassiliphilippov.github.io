Etymology

margin account comes from the words margin and account, where margin meant the 'edge' or small 'extra' written in money records. Brokers used that extra as a 'safety' when they let people 'borrow' to buy shares, so a margin account is an account where you borrow money to buy investments and the broker keeps your shares as protection.
