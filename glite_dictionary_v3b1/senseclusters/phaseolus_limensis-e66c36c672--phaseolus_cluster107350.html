Etymology

Phaseolus limensis is the Latin name for the Lima bean plant. The name joins Phaseolus, an old Latin word for 'bean', and limensis, which means 'from Lima' in Peru, so the name points to the bean and its place of origin and helps you remember it is the 'Lima bean' plant.
