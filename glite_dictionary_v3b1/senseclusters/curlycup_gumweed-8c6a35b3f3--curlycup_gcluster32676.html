Etymology

curlycup gumweed: The name curlycup gumweed comes from two clear images: the flower has a cup of curled leaves, the curlycup, and the plant has a sticky, 'gum-like' sap, the gumweed. So the name describes the sticky, yellow-flowering plant you find on prairies.
