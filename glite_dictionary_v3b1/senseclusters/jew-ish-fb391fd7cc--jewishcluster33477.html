Etymology

Jew-ish is made of Jew and the little ending -ish, which means 'somewhat' or 'like' and appears in words like greenish or in speech as five-ish ('around five'). So Jew-ish means 'somewhat Jewish' — someone who follows some Jewish traditions or shares Jewish culture but is not fully part of the religion or community.
