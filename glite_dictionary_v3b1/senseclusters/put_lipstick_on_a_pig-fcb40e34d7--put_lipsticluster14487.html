Etymology

The idiom put lipstick on a pig comes from the vivid picture of someone trying to make a pig look nicer by putting lipstick on it. So, even after you put lipstick on a pig, it is still a pig, and the phrase means 'to make only small changes that make something look better without fixing the real problem'.
