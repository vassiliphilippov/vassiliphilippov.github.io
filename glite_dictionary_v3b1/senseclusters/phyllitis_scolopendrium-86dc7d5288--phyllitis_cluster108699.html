Etymology

Phyllitis scolopendrium may come from old words: Phyllitis meaning 'leaf' and scolopendrium meaning 'hart's-tongue'. One popular story says people called the fern this because its long, simple fronds look like a deer's tongue, so the name points to the plant's shape today.
