Etymology

Aleksandr Nikolayevich Scriabin was a Russian composer and pianist who mixed unusual chords with ideas about colour and light. Imagine him at the piano trying to 'paint' with sound, and that image explains why his music felt new and helped move music toward 'modern' styles.
