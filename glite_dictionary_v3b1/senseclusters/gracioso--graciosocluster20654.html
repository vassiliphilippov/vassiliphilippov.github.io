Etymology

gracioso comes from Spanish and ultimately from the Latin word gratia which means 'grace' or 'favor', the same root as English grace and gracious. So in Spanish Golden Age theatre the gracioso was a charming, witty servant who used his charm to make the audience laugh and comment on the play.
