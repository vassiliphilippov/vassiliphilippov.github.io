Etymology

18-karat gold comes from an old system where a karat is one of 24 equal parts of pure gold; 18 of those parts are real gold and the rest are mixed metals. So 18-karat gold means '75 percent pure gold', which gives jewelry a warm color and more strength than pure gold.
