Etymology

The word peculiarity comes from peculiar, which originally meant something that 'belongs' to a person's own property and comes from pecu meaning 'cattle' or 'wealth'. So a peculiarity is a feature that 'belongs' to someone — a special or unusual quality that makes them different.
