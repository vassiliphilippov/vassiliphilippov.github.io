Etymology

unrecognized comes from the prefix un- meaning 'not' and the verb recognize, which originally meant 'to know again' because re- means 'again' and the rest means 'to know'. So, when a person or their work is unrecognized, they are 'not noticed' or not given the credit they deserve.
