Etymology

The name bone-ash cup comes from the material and the shape: it is a small cup made from bone-ash, the white powder left when animal bones are burned. These heat-proof cups were used to hold metal samples in a furnace to 'test metals' and help 'separate precious metals', so the name tells you exactly what the object does.
