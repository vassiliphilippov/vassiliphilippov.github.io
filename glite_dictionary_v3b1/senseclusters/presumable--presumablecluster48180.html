Etymology

presumable comes from the verb presume, where the prefix pre- means 'before' and the rest is like the familiar word assume, which means 'to take' or 'accept'. So to presume is to 'take' something 'before' you have full proof, and presumable means 'likely' or 'can be assumed'.
