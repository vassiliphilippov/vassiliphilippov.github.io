Etymology

The noun rolled biscuit comes from how the dough is made: cooks use a rolling pin to flatten the dough and then cut out round pieces before baking. That's why rolled refers to the dough being 'rolled' flat and the name means a biscuit made from rolled and cut dough rather than a 'drop' biscuit.
