Etymology

acute hemorrhagic encephalitis is made of three parts: acute meaning 'sudden', hemorrhagic meaning 'with bleeding', and encephalitis meaning 'swelling of the brain'. Put together, the phrase creates the image of a sudden, severe swelling in the brain with bleeding, so it names that dangerous illness.
