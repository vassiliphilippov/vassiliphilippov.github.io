Etymology

pudding comes from the Old French word boudin, which meant 'sausage' and from Latin botellus meaning 'little sausage'. Long ago puddings were mixtures put into skins and boiled or steamed, so in Britain the name grew to mean the soft sweet course at the end of a meal.
