Etymology

phosphorous acid gets its name from the element phosphorus and from the adjective phosphorous: the ending '-ous' showed an acid with less oxygen than the related 'phosphoric acid'. So, phosphorous acid is the phosphorus acid that has less oxygen than 'phosphoric acid'.
