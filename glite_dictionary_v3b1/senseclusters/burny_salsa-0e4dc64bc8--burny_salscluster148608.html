Etymology

burny salsa joins burn (the hot, burning feeling in your mouth) and salsa (a spicy sauce). One popular idea is that the name comes from the picture of a sauce so hot it 'burns' you, so it means 'a very spicy salsa that creates a burning sensation in the mouth'.
