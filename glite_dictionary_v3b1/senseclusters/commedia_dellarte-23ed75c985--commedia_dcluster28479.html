Etymology

commedia dell'arte comes from Italian, where commedia means 'comedy' and dell'arte means 'of the art' or 'of the artists'. It named groups of actors who wore masks, used the same funny characters, and often made up their lines in short scenes, so the phrase now means that lively style of theatre where actors created the words as they performed.
