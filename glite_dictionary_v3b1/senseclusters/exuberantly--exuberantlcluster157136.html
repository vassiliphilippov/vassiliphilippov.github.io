Etymology

exuberantly comes from the Latin parts ex- ('out') and uber ('full' or 'rich'), so it originally meant 'overflowing'. Imagine someone 'overflowing' with energy or joy — that's why exuberantly means 'in a lively, enthusiastic way'.
