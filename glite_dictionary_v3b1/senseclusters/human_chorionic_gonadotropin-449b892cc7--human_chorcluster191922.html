Etymology

human chorionic gonadotropin is a name that tells the story: human means it is found in people, chorionic points to the placenta, the organ that links mother and baby, and gonadotropin says it is a hormone that acts on the reproductive organs. Because the placenta makes this hormone early in pregnancy, tests look for it, so the name is used to mean a hormone that shows 'pregnancy'.
