Etymology

Potentilla anserina may come from old Latin words. The part Potentilla is the plant group name and the part anserina comes from 'goose' because geese ate it or its leaves look like a goose's foot, and its silvery leaf undersides gave it the common name 'silverweed', so the name fits a low, creeping plant with shiny, silvery leaves.
