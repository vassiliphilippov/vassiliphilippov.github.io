Etymology

Sticky-outy bangs is a fun, modern name that gives a clear picture: sticky-outy means hair that seems to 'stick out' from the forehead, and bangs are the front hair. So, the phrase means front hair that is styled to 'stick out' instead of 'lie flat'.
