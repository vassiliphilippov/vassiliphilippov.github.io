Etymology

GTD stands for gestational trophoblastic disease, where gestational means 'relating to pregnancy' and trophoblastic comes from roots meaning 'tissue that feeds or grows'. So GTD names the group of conditions in which unusual tissue grows in the womb during or after pregnancy.
