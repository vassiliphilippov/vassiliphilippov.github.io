Etymology

celecoxib breaks into cele + cox + -ib: the cox part refers to the enzyme 'COX' that helps cause pain and swelling, and the -ib ending shows it 'blocks' that enzyme. So, celecoxib is a medicine that blocks COX to reduce pain and inflammation, often used for arthritis.
