Etymology

Spotsylvania County gets its name from an early Virginia leader, Alexander Spotswood, plus the old ending 'sylvania', which means 'woods'. So the name originally meant 'Spotswood's woods', and today it is the name of the county in Virginia known for its Civil War history.
