Etymology

The name cockspur hawthorn may come from the long, straight thorns that look like a rooster's 'spurs'. The words cockspur and hawthorn together give a clear image of a very thorny hawthorn, so the name helps you remember that this tree has strong thorns and is often used as a 'barrier' plant.
