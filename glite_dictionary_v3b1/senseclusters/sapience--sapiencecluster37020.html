Etymology

sapience comes from the Latin root sapere meaning 'to taste' or 'to have sense', the same idea behind a sage. Today people also use sapience for AI when a machine seems to make wise, careful choices rather than just follow data, so the word points to 'wisdom-like' judgment even in technology.
