Etymology

lamper eel likely comes from the older fish name lamprey plus the word eel, because people used the familiar word for a long, eel-like fish to describe a lamprey-like animal with a round, sucking mouth. One popular idea is that lamprey comes from a word meaning 'to lick', so picture a long fish that 'licks' or holds on to other fish—that's why the name refers to a jawless, 'sucking' fish today.
