Etymology

The word Ononis comes from an old Greek plant name and is often linked to onos 'donkey' because donkeys were said to eat it. Its English name 'restharrow' remembers that the plant's strong roots could 'stop' a harrow, so Ononis is the scientific name for those small, tough restharrow plants.
