Etymology

Nemophila aurita may come from Greek parts nemo meaning 'wood' and phila meaning 'lover', plus the Latin aurita meaning 'eared'. So the name suggests a 'woodland-lover with little ear-like parts', a simple picture that fits this wildflower which grows in California meadows and has small ear-shaped parts on its violet flowers.
