Etymology

Sonora lac comes from the place name Sonora and the word lac, meaning a sticky resin made by small insects on shrub twigs. Local people scraped this resin from the twigs and used it as a local 'medicine', so the name tells you it is the resin from Sonora used 'as medicine'.
