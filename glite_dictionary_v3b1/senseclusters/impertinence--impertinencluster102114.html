Etymology

impertinence comes from the parts im- ('not') and pertinent (from a root meaning 'to relate' or 'be relevant'). So, someone showing impertinence speaks or acts as if their words do not 'belong' or are out of place, which is why the word now means rude or disrespectful behavior.
