Etymology

mephenytoin combines me- (from 'methyl', a small chemical part) and phenytoin, the name of a better-known anti-seizure drug. So mephenytoin is a chemical cousin of phenytoin used to treat seizures, which explains why it is an anti-seizure medicine but can be more toxic.
