Etymology

genus Lycopersicon may come from Greek parts: lyco meaning 'wolf' and persicon meaning 'peach', so early writers pictured the tomato as a 'wolf peach'. That's why the name was used for the group of tomatoes, even though today these plants are usually called part of the genus Solanum.
