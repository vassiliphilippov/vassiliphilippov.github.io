Etymology

Henry David Thoreau lived simply in a small cabin by Walden Pond and wrote the book 'Walden' about his life in nature. Because he chose 'simple living' and refused to obey laws he thought were unfair ('civil disobedience'), people often use his name to talk about nature, simple life, or peaceful protest.
