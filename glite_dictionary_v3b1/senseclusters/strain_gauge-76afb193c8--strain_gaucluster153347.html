Etymology

The name strain gauge comes from the words strain, meaning the force that makes something 'stretch' or change shape, and gauge, a small 'tool' that shows size or amount. Imagine a tiny device stuck to a metal beam that bends a little and the gauge shows that movement, so a strain gauge is a device that 'measures' very small stretches.
