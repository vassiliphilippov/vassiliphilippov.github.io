Etymology

degradability comes from the parts de- meaning 'down' and grade meaning 'step' or 'level', the same root you see in the word grade. So degradability is about how easily something can be taken 'down' a step or 'break down' over time, which is why we use it to talk about materials that naturally fall apart.
