Etymology

chancellery comes from the Latin word cancellarius, from cancelli which means 'lattice' or 'bars' that once stood in front of a clerk's desk. A cancellarius was the official who worked behind that screen, so a chancellery became the office or building of a chancellor, where important papers and work are kept.
