Etymology

empyreal comes from Greek parts en- 'in' and pyros 'fire', because people long ago pictured the highest heaven as being 'in the fire' or bright light. So empyreal now means 'relating to the sky or the heavens' and suggests something very beautiful and awe-inspiring.
