Etymology

plumpish comes from the familiar word plump, which means 'softly rounded' or full, plus the small ending -ish, which means 'somewhat' or 'a little like'. So plumpish means 'a bit fat or rounded', an easy image to remember like a baby with soft, full cheeks.
