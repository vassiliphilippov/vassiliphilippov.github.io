Etymology

Tragopogon porrifolius may get its name from old words meaning goat and beard because its fluffy seed head looks like a goat's beard. It is also called 'oyster plant' or 'salsify' because the long root tastes a little like oysters, so the name helps you remember it is a plant with a fluffy top and an edible, oyster-flavoured root.
