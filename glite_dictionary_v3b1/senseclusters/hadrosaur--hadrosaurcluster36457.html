Etymology

hadrosaur comes from Greek parts hadro- meaning 'thick' or 'large' and -saur meaning 'lizard', the same -saur you see in dinosaur. So a hadrosaur is literally a 'large lizard', which fits these big, mostly plant-eating dinosaurs with a duck-like bill.
