Etymology

The word lumpiness comes from the simple word lump ('a small mass or swelling') plus the ending -ness ('state or quality'). So, lumpiness is the 'state of having lumps' or not being smooth, which is why we use it for bumpy mashed potatoes or a sweater with bumps.
