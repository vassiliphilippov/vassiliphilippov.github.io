Etymology

angel maker may come from the simple image of someone who 'makes' angels by doing kind acts, with maker showing the person who creates that feeling. One popular story says people who comfort the sick or help children seemed to 'make' angels around them, so today angel maker means 'a person who brings kindness and joy'.
