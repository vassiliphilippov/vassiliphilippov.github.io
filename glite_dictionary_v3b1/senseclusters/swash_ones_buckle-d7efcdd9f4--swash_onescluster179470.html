Etymology

swash one's buckle may come from old showy fighters called 'swashbucklers' who would strike the air or their belt so a metal buckle flashed and made noise. That loud, showy move of swash and the shiny buckle made people notice, so today the phrase means 'to boast' or 'to show off'.
