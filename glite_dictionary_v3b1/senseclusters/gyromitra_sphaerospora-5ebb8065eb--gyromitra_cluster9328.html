Etymology

Gyromitra sphaerospora comes from old Greek and Latin words: Gyromitra suggests a 'round, folded cap' like a small twisted hat and sphaerospora means 'round spores'. So the name describes this false morel mushroom's rounded, wrinkled head and its round spores.
