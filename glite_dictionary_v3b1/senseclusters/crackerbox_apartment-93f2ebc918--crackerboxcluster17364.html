Etymology

crackerbox apartment may come from the picture of a small, thin wooden cracker box used to hold crackers. Because a cracker box is tiny and cheap, calling a home a crackerbox apartment suggests it is very small and poorly made, so it means 'a small, cramped, and often poorly built apartment'.
