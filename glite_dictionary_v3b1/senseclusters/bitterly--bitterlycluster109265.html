Etymology

The word bitterly comes from bitter, which means a 'sharp' and 'unpleasant' taste, and a helpful way to remember the emotional use is to think of a bad memory that leaves a bad taste. So if someone is bitterly angry or disappointed, their feeling is strong and unpleasant, like that taste that stays with you.
