Etymology

The word sourly comes from the simple word sour plus the ending -ly, where sour originally meant 'acid' or 'tart' in taste. A helpful image is a person making a sour face after bad food — so to speak or act sourly is to be 'unpleasant' or 'bitter', which is why the word now means showing resentment or displeasure.
