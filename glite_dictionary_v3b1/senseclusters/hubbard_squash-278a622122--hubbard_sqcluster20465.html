Etymology

Hubbard squash may come from the name of a grower or seller called Hubbard who first brought this very large, bumpy squash to markets, and people began calling the vegetable by that name. That's why Hubbard squash today means 'a large winter squash with a warty skin and sweet orange flesh'.
