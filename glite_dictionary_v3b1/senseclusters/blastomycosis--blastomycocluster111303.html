Etymology

blastomycosis comes from the name of the fungus Blastomyces: blasto- means 'bud' or 'sprout' (a helpful image is the little buds the fungus makes), myco- means 'fungus' and -osis means 'disease'. So, blastomycosis points to a 'budding fungus disease', which matches its modern meaning as a fungal infection caused by Blastomyces.
