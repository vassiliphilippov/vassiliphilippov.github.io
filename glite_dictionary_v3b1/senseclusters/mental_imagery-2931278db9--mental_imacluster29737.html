Etymology

mental imagery comes from the simple idea of making pictures inside your mind: mental means 'in the mind' and imagery means 'pictures'. When you close your eyes and see a scene like a photo in your head, that is mental imagery, so the phrase means 'the ability to form images in the mind'.
