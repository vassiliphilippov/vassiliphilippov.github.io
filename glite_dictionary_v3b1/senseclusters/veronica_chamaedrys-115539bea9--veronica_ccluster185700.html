Etymology

Veronica chamaedrys may be named after Saint Veronica, and long ago people used her name for this small, pretty blue flower. The second part, chamaedrys, comes from old words meaning 'low' or 'on the ground', which fits because the plant grows close to the earth and makes a low ground cover.
