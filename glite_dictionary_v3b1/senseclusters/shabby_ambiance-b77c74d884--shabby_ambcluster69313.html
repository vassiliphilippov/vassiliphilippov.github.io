Etymology

Shabby ambiance uses shabby, meaning 'worn' or 'old', and ambiance, meaning the feeling of a place. Picture a small café with faded curtains and scratched tables. The whole room gives a 'run-down' feeling, so the phrase means the atmosphere looks 'worn' or 'in poor condition'.
