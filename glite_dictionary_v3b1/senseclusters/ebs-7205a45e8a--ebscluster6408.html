Etymology

EbS comes from the phrase east by south, where east means 'toward the right on a map', by means 'near' or 'toward', and south means 'down on a map'. Sailors used this short form for the compass point one point 11°15' south of due east, so EbS simply tells you to head a little bit south of east.
