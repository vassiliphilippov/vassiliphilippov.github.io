Etymology

Saponaria vaccaria comes from old Latin words: Saponaria meaning 'soap' and vaccaria meaning 'cow'. The plant's roots and leaves make a soapy foam when rubbed and it often grew near farms, so the name paints the picture of a 'soap' plant tied to 'cows' and explains why we call it cow soapwort.
