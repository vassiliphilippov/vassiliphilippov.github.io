Etymology

The word intersubjectivity combines inter- meaning 'between' and subject meaning 'person' or 'topic', and a helpful way to remember it is to think of it as what happens 'between people' who share ideas; so intersubjectivity is the shared understanding that makes communication and social interaction possible.
