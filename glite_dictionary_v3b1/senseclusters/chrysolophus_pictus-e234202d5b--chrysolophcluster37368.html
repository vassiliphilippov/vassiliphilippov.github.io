Etymology

Chrysolophus pictus is the scientific name for the golden pheasant. The name comes from Greek chryso- ('gold') and lophus ('crest') plus Latin pictus ('painted'), so it creates the picture of a bird with a golden crest and bright, 'painted' feathers.
