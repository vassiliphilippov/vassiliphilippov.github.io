Etymology

ENL is a medical short name for erythema nodosum leprosum, where erythema (from Greek erythros) means 'redness', nodosum (from Latin nodus) means 'knot' or 'node', and leprosum links it to leprosy. So ENL describes the red, painful skin 'nodes' or bumps that happen in people with leprosy.
