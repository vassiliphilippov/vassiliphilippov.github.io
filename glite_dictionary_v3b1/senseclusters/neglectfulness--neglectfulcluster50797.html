Etymology

neglectfulness comes from neglect, which comes from Latin parts ne- meaning 'not' and legere meaning 'to pick or gather', the same root you see in collect. So to neglect is like 'not picking up' or 'not taking care', and neglectfulness is the habit of not paying attention to duties or things you should look after.
