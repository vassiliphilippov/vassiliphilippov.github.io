Etymology

fine-toothed comes from a comb or saw with very small teeth, because fine means 'small' and tooth means the little 'teeth' on a comb. A fine-toothed comb catches very small bits, so to do something with a fine-toothed approach means to check it very carefully and find even the smallest mistakes.
