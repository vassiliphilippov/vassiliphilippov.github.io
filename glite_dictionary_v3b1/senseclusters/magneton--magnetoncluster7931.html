Etymology

magneton comes from magnet, originally a 'stone that attracts iron', and the ending -on, the same ending in electron and proton used for tiny particles. So a magneton is a small particle-related unit used to measure an atom's or particle's 'magnetic moment', that is, how strong its magnetism is.
