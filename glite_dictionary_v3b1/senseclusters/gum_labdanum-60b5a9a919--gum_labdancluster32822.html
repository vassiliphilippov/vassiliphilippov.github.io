Etymology

gum labdanum is a sticky brown resin taken from rockrose shrubs; the word gum shows it is sticky and labdanum is the old name for this plant resin. One popular story says shepherds found the resin on goats' beards or on leather combs after the animals brushed the bushes, and perfumers use it as a 'fixative' to make a perfume's warm scent last.
