Etymology

burninate the weeds comes from a funny image of hot fire made from burn plus a made-up ending -inate. Imagine a comic dragon 'burninating' the land or someone burning all the weeds; that's why the phrase means 'to destroy weeds by burning them' and is often used as a joking, exaggerated way.
