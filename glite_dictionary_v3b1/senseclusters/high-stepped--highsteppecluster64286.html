Etymology

high-stepped comes from the two simple words high ('up' or 'tall') and step ('a movement of the foot'), so it paints a clear picture of someone or an animal lifting their feet very high when they walk, like a parade horse or a proud person. That's why high-stepped means moving with a high, showy stride.
