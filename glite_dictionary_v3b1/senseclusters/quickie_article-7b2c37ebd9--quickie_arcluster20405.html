Etymology

quickie article comes from the word quick meaning 'fast' plus the friendly ending '-ie' that makes something small or informal, together with article meaning a short news or magazine piece. So the phrase gives the image of a small piece written very quickly, and that's why a quickie article means 'a short, informal article made quickly'.
