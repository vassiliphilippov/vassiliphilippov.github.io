Etymology

Booker T. Washington was a man born into slavery who later set up the Tuskegee Institute, a school in Alabama where he taught practical skills and urged Black people to gain 'education' and 'self-reliance'. That's why his name is remembered today as a symbol of leadership in teaching and practical progress.
