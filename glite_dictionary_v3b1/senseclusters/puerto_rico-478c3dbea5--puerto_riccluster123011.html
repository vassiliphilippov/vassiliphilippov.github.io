Etymology

Puerto Rico comes from the Spanish words Puerto and Rico, which together mean the phrase 'rich port'. Early Spanish sailors used that name for the island's busy port, and the phrase stuck, so today Puerto Rico is the Caribbean island that is a territory of the United States.
