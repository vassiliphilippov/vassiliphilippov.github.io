Etymology

's Gravenhage may come from old Dutch words Graven meaning 'count' and hage meaning 'hedge' or 'fenced area' where a count kept a hunting ground. The count's fenced place became the town center, so 's Gravenhage is the Dutch name for 'The Hague'.
