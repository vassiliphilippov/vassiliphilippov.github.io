Etymology

Pyotr Ilych Tchaikovsky is a Russian name: Pyotr is 'Peter', Ilych means 'son of Ilya', and Tchaikovsky is the family name. He became one of the most famous composers, and today his name brings to mind the big, emotional music of ballets like 'Swan Lake'.
