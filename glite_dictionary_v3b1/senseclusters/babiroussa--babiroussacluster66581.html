Etymology

babiroussa comes from Malay/Indonesian parts babi and rusa, which mean 'pig' and 'deer'. It is called this because the wild pig's long tusks curve upwards toward its face like deer horns, so the name helps you remember it is a pig with deer‑like tusks.
