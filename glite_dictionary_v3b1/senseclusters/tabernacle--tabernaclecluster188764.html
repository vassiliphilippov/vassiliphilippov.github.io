Etymology

tabernacle comes from the Latin tabernāculum, which meant a 'small tent' or 'hut' and is related to taberna 'shop' (the same root as tavern). In the Bible the tabernacle was a portable tent that held the Ark, so the word still brings to mind a holy tent or safe place for worship.
