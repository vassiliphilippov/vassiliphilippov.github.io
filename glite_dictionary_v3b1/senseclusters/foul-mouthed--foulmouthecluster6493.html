Etymology

foul-mouthed comes from the picture of a 'dirty' mouth: foul means 'dirty' or 'bad' and mouth is the same word you know. So a foul-mouthed person often uses 'dirty' or rude words, which is why the word describes someone who speaks in a rude way.
