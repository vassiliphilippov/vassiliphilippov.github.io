Etymology

Onopordum comes from Greek parts: onos meaning 'donkey' and pordos which may mean a 'fart' or bad 'smell', so a helpful way to remember it is 'donkey thistle'. This fits the plant because Onopordum species are large, woolly, prickly thistles often found in fields and open places.
