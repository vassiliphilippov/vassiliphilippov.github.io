Etymology

ethmoid sinus comes from two old words: ethmoid from Greek meaning 'sieve' and sinus meaning 'small space' or 'cavity', so the name paints a picture of a sieve-like bone with many tiny air pockets between the nose and the eyes. That's why ethmoid sinus means 'one of the small air-filled cavities behind the bridge of the nose'.
