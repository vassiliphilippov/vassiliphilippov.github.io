Etymology

Gaddafi comes from an Arabic family name from Libya, written al-Qaddafi, which means 'from the Qaddafa tribe'. Because Arabic sounds can be written in different ways in English, you will see many spellings like Gaddafi, Qaddafi, or Kadhafi, but they all refer to the same name.
