Etymology

Paspalum dilatatum may come from old words that describe how the plant looks: Paspalum named bunch grasses and dilatatum means 'spread out', so imagine a tall grass that grows in wide clumps across fields. That's why the name fits a tall grass farmers use in pastures as food for animals.
