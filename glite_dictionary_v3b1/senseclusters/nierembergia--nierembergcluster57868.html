Etymology

nierembergia is a plant name made from the last name Nieremberg plus the common Latin ending -ia, which usually means 'named for'. To remember it, imagine a person called Nieremberg who loved small, bell-shaped flowers, which helps explain why nierembergia is the name for these low, bell-like garden plants.
