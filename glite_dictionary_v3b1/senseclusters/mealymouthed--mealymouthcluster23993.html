Etymology

The word mealymouthed comes from mealy and meal, which mean 'ground grain' or 'flour', and a helpful image is someone speaking with flour in their mouth so their words sound soft and unclear. That's why mealymouthed describes someone who avoids saying things plainly, often to avoid offense.
