Etymology

sacramentalism comes from sacrament, which comes from Latin sacer meaning 'holy' or 'sacred', together with -ism meaning 'belief'. Because a sacrament is a 'holy ritual', sacramentalism can also mean putting too much importance on rituals instead of personal faith.
