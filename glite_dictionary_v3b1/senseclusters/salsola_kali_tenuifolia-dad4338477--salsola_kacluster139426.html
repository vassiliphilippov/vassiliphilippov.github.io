Etymology

Salsola kali tenuifolia is the Latin name for the prickly bush people call the 'Russian thistle' or 'tumbleweed'. When the plant dries it often breaks off at the base and rolls like a ball across fields, spreading seeds, so people see it as a harmful 'weed'.
