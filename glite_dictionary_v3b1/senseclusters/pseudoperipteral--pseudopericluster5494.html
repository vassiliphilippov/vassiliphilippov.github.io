Etymology

The word pseudoperipteral comes from Greek parts pseudo- meaning 'false' and peri- meaning 'around', the same 'peri-' you see in perimeter ('edge around'). So a pseudoperipteral building looks like it has a ring of columns all around, but some of those columns are 'false' because they are built into the walls instead of standing free.
