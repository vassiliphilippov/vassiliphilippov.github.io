Etymology

The idiom last chicken in the shop may come from a simple market scene: when only one bird is left, that last chicken stands out in the shop, seen by some as very wanted and by others as odd. So calling someone the last chicken in the shop means they act like they are 'special' or must be noticed, and they can also feel a little 'left out'.
