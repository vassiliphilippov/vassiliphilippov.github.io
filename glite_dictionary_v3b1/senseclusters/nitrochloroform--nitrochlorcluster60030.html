Etymology

nitrochloroform comes from the parts nitro- (meaning 'with nitrogen', as in nitrogen) and chloro- (meaning 'chlorine', as in chlorine or chloroform). So the name says it is a chemical that contains chlorine and nitrogen, which helps explain why it is a heavy, toxic liquid that causes eye irritation and was used as a pesticide and in tear gas.
