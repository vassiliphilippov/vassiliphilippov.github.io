Etymology

The noun ice yacht comes from joining ice and yacht: it is a yacht-like boat with runners like skates and a sail so it can move over frozen water. Picture a sleek boat on skates racing across a frozen lake, and you see why the name means 'a sailing craft made to travel on ice'.
