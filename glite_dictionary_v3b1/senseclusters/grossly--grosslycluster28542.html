Etymology

The word grossly comes from the adjective gross, which itself comes from an old French word gros meaning 'large' or 'thick'. Because gross was used for things that are 'very large' or 'clearly wrong', and you see it in phrases like 'gross amount' or when something is called 'gross', grossly means 'to a very great or serious degree'.
