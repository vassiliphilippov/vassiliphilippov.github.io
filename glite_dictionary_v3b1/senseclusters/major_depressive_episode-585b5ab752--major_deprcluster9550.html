Etymology

major depressive episode is like a simple picture: major means very large or serious, depressive comes from a word that means 'to press down' so you can imagine being pushed down, and episode means a time or scene. So the phrase refers to a strong, lasting period when someone feels 'very low', loses interest, and cannot do their normal activities.
