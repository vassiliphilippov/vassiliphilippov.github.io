Etymology

buccinator muscle may come from the Latin words buccina (a small 'trumpet') or bucca ('cheek'). Picture a trumpet player blowing and squeezing the cheek. The muscle flattens the cheek to help 'blowing', keep food while 'chewing', and make 'speaking' work, so the name fits its job.
