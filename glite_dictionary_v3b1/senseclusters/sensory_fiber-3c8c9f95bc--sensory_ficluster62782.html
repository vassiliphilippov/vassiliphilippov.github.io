Etymology

The term sensory fiber shows a simple picture: sensory is about the senses and fiber is a tiny thread. Imagine tiny thread-like nerves 'carrying' touch, pain, or temperature to the brain, so the term means 'a nerve that carries sensory messages to the brain'.
