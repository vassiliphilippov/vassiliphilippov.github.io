Etymology

Grand Teton National Park is named after the big mountain called the Grand Teton. One popular story says French trappers called the sharp peaks 'les trois tétons', meaning 'three breasts', because the mountains looked like breasts, and the tallest peak became the Grand Teton. That's why the park's name points to the tall, rugged mountains and the wild landscape people visit today.
