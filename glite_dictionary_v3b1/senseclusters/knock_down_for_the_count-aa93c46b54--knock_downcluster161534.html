Etymology

knock down for the count comes from boxing: when a boxer is hit and knocked down to the floor the referee makes a count to ten, and if the boxer cannot stand the fight is over. So, knock down for the count means 'to be knocked down and unable to continue' or 'to be completely defeated'.
