Etymology

Strait of Ormuz is the name for a very narrow sea channel, a strait near the island of Ormuz that links the Persian Gulf to the Gulf of Oman. Because so many oil tankers must pass through this small gap, it is like a single road for ships, so blocking it would quickly stop oil traffic and make the strait 'very important' for world oil supply.
