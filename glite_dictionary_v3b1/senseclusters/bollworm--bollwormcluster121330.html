Etymology

bollworm is a simple compound of boll and worm, where boll means a plant's round seed pod (think of a 'ball') and worm means a 'young caterpillar'. So a bollworm is literally a caterpillar that eats the round seed pods of cotton and similar plants, which is why it destroys cotton bolls.
