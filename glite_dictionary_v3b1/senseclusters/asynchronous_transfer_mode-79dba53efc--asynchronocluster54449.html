Etymology

asynchronous transfer mode describes a way data is sent: asynchronous means 'not at the same time', transfer means sending, and mode means a method, so many small, equal pieces of data are sent one by one without waiting and different kinds (voice, video, files) can move together quickly. That's why the name is used for 'a fast way to send different kinds of data'.
