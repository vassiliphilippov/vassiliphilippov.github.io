Etymology

lashings probably comes from the verb lash, which meant 'to strike' or 'to hit' and gives the picture of something being given in a strong, heavy way. A helpful way to remember it is to imagine being given 'lashings' of food or praise, lots and lots, so lashings means 'a very large amount'.
