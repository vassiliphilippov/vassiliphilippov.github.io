Etymology

just ceebs may come from saying the letters 'C' and 'B' for 'can't be bothered', which quickly sounds like ceebs. So just ceebs is a quick shrug meaning 'I can't be bothered' or 'I don't want to', and that is why people use it when they feel too lazy to do something.
