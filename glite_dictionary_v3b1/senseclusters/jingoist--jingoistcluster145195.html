Etymology

jingoist comes from a song from the 1800s in Britain that used the phrase 'by jingo' as a loud, warlike shout meaning 'by Jesus'. People who used this shout showed strong, aggressive patriotism, so a jingoist is someone who loudly supports fighting or war to protect their country.
