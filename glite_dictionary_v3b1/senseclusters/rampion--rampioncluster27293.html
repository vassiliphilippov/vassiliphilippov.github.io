Etymology

rampion comes from the Latin word rapunculus, which means 'little turnip' and points to the plant's edible root, and the same root also gave the fairy‑tale name Rapunzel. That's why rampion is remembered as a wild bellflower with blue blooms and a small root people sometimes eat in salads.
