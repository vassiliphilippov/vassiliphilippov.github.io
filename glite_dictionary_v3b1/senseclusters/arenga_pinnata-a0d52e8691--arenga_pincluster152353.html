Etymology

Arenga pinnata comes from a local Malay name and a Latin word: the first part, Arenga, comes from the Malay word 'aren' for this sugar palm, and the second part, pinnata, means 'feathered' because the long leaves spread out like a feather. So the name points to a palm with feather-like leaves whose sap and fibers people use for wine, rope and sago.
