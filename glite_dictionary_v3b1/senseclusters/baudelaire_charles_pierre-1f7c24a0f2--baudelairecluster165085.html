Etymology

Baudelaire, Charles Pierre is the French poet who wrote 'Les Fleurs du mal', a collection that mixes beauty and dark, shocking images — picture a bouquet of strange, wilting flowers. That's why his name often stands for modern poetry full of strong, sad, or surprising pictures.
