Etymology

Psychoticism comes from the Greek root psyche meaning 'mind' and the word psychotic, which is linked to psychosis meaning 'a state when the mind is out of touch with reality'. So the word can also be used to talk about that kind of mental state, for example when someone sees or believes things that are not real.
