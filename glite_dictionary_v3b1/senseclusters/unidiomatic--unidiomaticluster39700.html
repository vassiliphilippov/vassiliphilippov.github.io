Etymology

The word unidiomatic starts with un- meaning 'not' and the rest comes from idiom, a word for a phrase that is natural to native speakers. An idiom comes from an old Greek root meaning 'own' or 'personal', so an idiomatic phrase is the way a language naturally says something. So, unidiomatic means 'not natural' or 'not what native speakers usually say'.
