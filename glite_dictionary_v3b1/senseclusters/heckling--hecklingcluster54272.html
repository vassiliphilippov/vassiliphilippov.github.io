Etymology

Heckling comes from an old word heckle, which once meant 'to comb' or 'to pull out' fibres when people made cloth. A helpful way to remember it is to think of 'pulling at' someone, which is why heckling means loudly interrupting or shouting at a speaker to bother or challenge them.
