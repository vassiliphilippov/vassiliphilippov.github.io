Etymology

theological virtue comes from Christian writers who used theological to mean 'about God' and virtue to mean a good habit, so they spoke of gifts like 'faith', 'hope' and 'charity' as being 'given by God'. So today theological virtue means a moral quality believed to come from God and needed for being 'saved'.
