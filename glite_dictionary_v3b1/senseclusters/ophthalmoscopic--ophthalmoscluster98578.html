Etymology

The word ophthalmoscopic comes from the Greek parts ophthalm- 'eye' and -scopic 'to look'. Think of telescope and microscope, which use -scope to mean 'look'. So an ophthalmoscopic exam is 'looking into the eye', which is why it means an eye examination.
