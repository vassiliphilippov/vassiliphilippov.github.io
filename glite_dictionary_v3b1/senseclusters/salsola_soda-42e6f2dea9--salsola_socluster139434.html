Etymology

Salsola soda gets its name from Latin words: salsola meaning 'salt' and soda meaning 'soda ash', because the plant grows in salty places and people once burned it to make soda ash for glass and soap. So the name points to a salt-loving plant used to produce 'soda ash'.
