Etymology

Iris xiphioides may come from Greek words: Iris was the 'rainbow' goddess, so the name shows the plant's colorful flowers, and xiphioides means 'sword-like', about its long, narrow leaves. That's why the name helps you picture a bright iris with sword-shaped leaves.
