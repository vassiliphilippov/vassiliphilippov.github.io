Etymology

Styloid comes from an old Greek root styl- meaning 'pillar' or 'stake' and the ending -oid meaning 'like'. Think of a small pillar or the pointed tip of a stylus, a thin writing tool, so styloid means 'like' a thin, pointed shape and is used for slender, pencil-like parts in the body.
