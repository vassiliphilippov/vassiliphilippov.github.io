Etymology

braggadocio comes from a funny old name, Braggadocchio, used for a loud boaster in old stories and is built on the simple English verb brag ('to boast'). So a helpful way to remember braggadocio is to picture someone making big, showy claims with little real substance, which is why it means showy, empty boasting.
