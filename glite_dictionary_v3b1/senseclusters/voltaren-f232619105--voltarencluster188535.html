Etymology

Voltaren is a brand name for a pain medicine used to reduce pain and swelling. A helpful way to remember it is to think of the Volt- part like 'volt', as in an electric shock, so you can imagine the medicine 'switching off' sharp pain quickly and lowering inflammation.
