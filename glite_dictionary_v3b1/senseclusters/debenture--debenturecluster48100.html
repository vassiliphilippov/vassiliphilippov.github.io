Etymology

The word debenture comes from Latin debere, meaning 'to owe', and it began as a paper that said someone owed money, like a written record of a debt; you can see the same root in debt and debit. So a debenture today is a written promise to repay a loan, and holding one makes you a lender, not an owner.
