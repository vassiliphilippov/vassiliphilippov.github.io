Etymology

tactical warning comes from soldiers giving a short alert: tactical means about quick action in a fight and warning is a fast alarm. Imagine a siren or shout right 'after an enemy attack' telling troops about 'immediate threats', so the phrase means a quick warning given when danger is already happening.
