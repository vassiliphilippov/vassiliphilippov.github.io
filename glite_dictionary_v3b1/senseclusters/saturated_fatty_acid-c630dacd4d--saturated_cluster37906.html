Etymology

saturated fatty acid uses saturated in the everyday sense of 'full' and fatty acid for a fat molecule. Imagine a chain of carbon atoms like a row of empty seats; when every seat is filled with hydrogen atoms, the chain is 'saturated' and cannot take more. That's why saturated fatty acid means a fat molecule that is 'full' of hydrogen and is often found in animal fats.
