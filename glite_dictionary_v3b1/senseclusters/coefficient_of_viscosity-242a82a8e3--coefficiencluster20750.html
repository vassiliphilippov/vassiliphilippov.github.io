Etymology

coefficient of viscosity comes from two parts: coefficient, a number that tells how big or small something is, and viscosity, the idea of how 'sticky' or 'thick' a liquid feels. Imagine honey moving slowly and water moving fast; the coefficient of viscosity is the number that tells how much a liquid holds back flow, so it helps compare 'thick' and 'thin' liquids.
