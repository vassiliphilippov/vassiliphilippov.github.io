Etymology

Cake mold comes from the simple image of a mold as a hollow shape and a cake filling it. Bakers pour batter into a cake mold so the cake 'takes on' the mold's shape and keeps that shape after baking. That's why cake mold means a pan used to bake cakes in a particular shape.
