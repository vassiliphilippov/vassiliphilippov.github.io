Etymology

minimal brain dysfunction was a medical label doctors used to say there was a very small problem in the brain that made a child have trouble paying attention or controlling activity. The words minimal, brain, and dysfunction give the image of a tiny brain fault causing learning or behaviour problems, and that is why the phrase came to mean what we now call 'ADHD'.
