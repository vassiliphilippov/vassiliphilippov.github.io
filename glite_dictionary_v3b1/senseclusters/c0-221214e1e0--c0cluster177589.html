Etymology

C0 is the largest size in the international paper C series, which was made so a sheet from the A series (for example A0 or A4) will 'fit' into a C envelope. A helpful way to remember it is to think of C as a 'container' or 'cover', so C0 simply means a very large envelope size that holds an A0 sheet.
