Etymology

The noun Sunyavadi approach comes from the word Sunyavadi, which means 'emptiness'. Imagine an empty cup that holds nothing. Seeing that emptiness helps people let go of worries and find 'inner peace', so the phrase means a way of thinking that teaches calm and letting go.
