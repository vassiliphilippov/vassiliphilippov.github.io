Etymology

The word decipherability comes from decipher, which is de- (meaning 'undo') plus cipher (meaning 'code' or 'secret writing'). The part cipher comes from Arabic sifr meaning 'zero', and because a cipher came to mean a secret number or code, decipherability is about how easy it is to 'undo the code' and read or understand something.
