Etymology

Asian horseshoe crab gets its name from its look: the hard shell is shaped like a horseshoe and it has a long tail like a crab, while 'Asian' tells where it lives. People called it a 'horseshoe crab' because of this strange, old shape even though it is 'not a true crab', so today the name means the species of these hard-shelled animals found along eastern Asia.
