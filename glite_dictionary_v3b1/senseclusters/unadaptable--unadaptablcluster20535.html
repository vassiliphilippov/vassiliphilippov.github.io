Etymology

unadaptable breaks into un- + adapt + -able, where un- means 'not', adapt means 'to change to fit', and -able means 'can be'. So, unadaptable means 'not able to change to fit', which is why we use it for people, animals, or ways of working that cannot adjust to new conditions.
