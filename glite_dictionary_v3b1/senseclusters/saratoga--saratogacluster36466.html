Etymology

saratoga is the common Australian name for a large freshwater fish known for a red or pink spot on each scale. A helpful way to remember it is to think of Saratoga like a place-name — imagine a bright, jewel-like fish from a river town, which fits the fish's showy, sparkling scales.
