Etymology

common wolffia is a plant name made of two parts: wolffia was taken from the surname of an early botanist, and common shows this is the widespread kind. These plants are tiny green dots that float on the water like grains, so the name helps us remember it is a very small, widespread water plant — the 'smallest flowering plant'.
