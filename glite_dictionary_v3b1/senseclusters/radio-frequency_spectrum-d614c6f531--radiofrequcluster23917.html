Etymology

The term radio-frequency spectrum comes from the old idea of spectrum, which first meant the range of colours in a rainbow. People started to use spectrum for any 'range', and added radio and frequency to show what kind of range. That's why radio-frequency spectrum means 'the range of radio signals used for wireless communication'.
