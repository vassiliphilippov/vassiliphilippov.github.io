Etymology

New Look comes from Christian Dior's 1947 clothes that had a tight bodice, a narrow waist and a very full skirt. One popular story says an excited magazine editor said 'it's such a new look' when she saw the new shape, and that's why New Look now means that dramatic style of women's clothes after the war.
