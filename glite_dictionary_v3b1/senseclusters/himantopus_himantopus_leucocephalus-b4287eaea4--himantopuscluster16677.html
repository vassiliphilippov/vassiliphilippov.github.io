Etymology

Himantopus himantopus leucocephalus comes from old Greek words: Himantopus means 'strap-foot' and leucocephalus means 'white-headed', a neat picture of a long-legged stilt with a white head and dark wings. That's why the name describes this bird's long legs and mostly white body today.
