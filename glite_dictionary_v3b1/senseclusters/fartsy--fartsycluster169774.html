Etymology

The word fartsy comes from the playful phrase artsy-fartsy, where artsy comes from art meaning 'creative work' and the extra 'fartsy' was added as a silly rhyme to make fun of people who try too hard to seem artistic. So fartsy describes something that tries too hard to be artistic and looks showy.
