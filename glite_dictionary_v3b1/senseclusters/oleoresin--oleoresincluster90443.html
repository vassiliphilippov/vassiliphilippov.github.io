Etymology

Oleoresin comes from two parts: oleo- meaning 'oil' and resin meaning 'a sticky plant substance'. So, oleoresin is a plant product that is both oily and sticky — a simple mix of 'oil' and 'resin' used for flavor, scent, or things like turpentine.
