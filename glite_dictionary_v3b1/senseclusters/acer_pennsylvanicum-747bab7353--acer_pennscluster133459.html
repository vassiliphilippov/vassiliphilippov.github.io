Etymology

Acer pennsylvanicum gets its name from Latin plant naming: the genus Acer means 'maple' and the species pennsylvanicum means 'from Pennsylvania'. So the name literally says 'maple from Pennsylvania', which helps you remember this tree often called the striped maple for its striped bark and yellow leaves.
