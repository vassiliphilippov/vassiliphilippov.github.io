Etymology

orthopedic insole literally names a special insole, the soft or firm piece inside a shoe, made for orthopedic care to help bones and movement. Think of a shaped inner sole that supports the arch and holds the foot straight, so it means a shoe insert 'to support' the foot and 'correct foot alignment'.
