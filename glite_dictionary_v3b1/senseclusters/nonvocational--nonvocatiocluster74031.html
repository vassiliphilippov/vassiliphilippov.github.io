Etymology

The word nonvocational breaks into non- meaning 'not' and vocation meaning 'a calling' or 'job', the same idea you see in the common word vocation. So nonvocational describes courses or programs that are 'not for a job' and focus on general learning or personal interest rather than training for a specific job.
