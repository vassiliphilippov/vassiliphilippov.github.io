Etymology

Myxophyceae comes from Greek parts myxo- meaning 'slime' and phyceae meaning 'seaweed' or 'algae', so it literally means 'slimy algae'. That's why the name was used for blue-green algae with a slimy look, though today scientists usually call them cyanobacteria.
