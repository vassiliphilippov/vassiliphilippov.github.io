Etymology

Taurotragus comes from two Greek parts, tauros meaning 'bull' and tragos meaning 'goat'. It makes you picture a 'bull-goat' because elands are very large like bulls and have goat-like beards and horns, so the name helps you remember they are big African antelopes.
