Etymology

Ursus arctos horribilis comes from old Latin and Greek words: Ursus means 'bear', arctos also means 'bear', and horribilis means 'terrible'. Early scientists used this name because the grizzly looked large and fierce, so the name literally means a 'terrible bear' and points to the animal's size and power.
