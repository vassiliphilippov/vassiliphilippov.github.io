Etymology

Vernonia is the scientific name for a group of flowering plants and it was named for a plant collector called Vernon, with the -ia ending used in many plant names to mean 'of' or 'belonging to'. So Vernonia can be remembered as 'Vernon's plants', which explains why it is the name for this group of purple-flowered plants.
