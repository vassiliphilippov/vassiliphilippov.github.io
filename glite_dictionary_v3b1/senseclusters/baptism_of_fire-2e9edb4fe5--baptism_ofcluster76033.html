Etymology

baptism of fire comes from the image of a new person facing danger for the first time: baptism is a ceremony that marks a beginning and fire stands for battle or danger, so being 'baptized by fire' meant a tough first fight. That's why today baptism of fire means 'a first hard test' that shows what you can do.
