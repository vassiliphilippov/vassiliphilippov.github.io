Etymology

lords-and-ladies comes from a simple picture people have used for the plant: it has a standing central stalk like a little 'lord' and a hooded leaf around it like a 'lady', and it often has both 'male' and 'female' flowers together. So the name helps you remember the plant's shape and that it contains both 'male' and 'female' parts.
