Etymology

industrial arts comes from two simple ideas: industrial, meaning work with machines and factory tools, and arts, meaning skilled hand work or craft. In early trade and technical schools, students learned to use tools and build real things with their hands, so the name came to mean a 'subject' where you learn practical, useful skills.
