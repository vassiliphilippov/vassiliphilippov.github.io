Etymology

imperial gallon comes from when Britain set one official size for a 'gallon' so the whole empire used the same amount; gallon was an old word for a liquid measure and imperial shows it was the empire's official size. So, today imperial gallon means the British gallon of about 4.545 liters used for liquids.
