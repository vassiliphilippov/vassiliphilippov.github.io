Etymology

Stercorariidae comes from the Latin word stercorarius, from stercus which means 'dung'. A helpful way to remember this is that early observers saw these birds chase others until they 'dropped' or 'spat out' food, so the name stuck to these aggressive seabirds that often steal food from other birds.
