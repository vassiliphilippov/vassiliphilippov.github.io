Etymology

Phoca vitulina comes from old words: Phoca means 'seal' and vitulina may mean 'little calf'. One popular story says people used 'little calf' because the small, spotted, soft look of young seals reminded them of calves, and that image explains why the Latin name refers to the 'harbor seal' today.
