Etymology

Nemophila menziesii may come from Greek words: nemo meaning 'wood' and phila meaning 'loving', so Nemophila means 'wood-loving'. The second part, menziesii, comes from a plant collector named Menzies, so the full name helps you remember this delicate wildflower often found in fields and near woods.
