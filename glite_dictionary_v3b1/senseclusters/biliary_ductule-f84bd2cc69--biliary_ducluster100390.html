Etymology

biliary ductule comes from the parts biliary, which means 'bile' or the liver fluid, and ductule, which means a very small 'duct' or tube. Imagine a tiny tube inside the liver carrying yellow-green 'bile' into larger tubes, so it means 'a small duct that carries bile into the larger ducts'.
