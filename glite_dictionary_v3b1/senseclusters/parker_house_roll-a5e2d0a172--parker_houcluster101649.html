Etymology

Parker House roll may come from the Parker House hotel in Boston, where one popular story says a baker folded a round of dough in half before baking to make each roll. That folding gave the roll a warm, 'soft, buttery' inside, so today Parker House roll means a small dinner roll with a 'folded' shape.
