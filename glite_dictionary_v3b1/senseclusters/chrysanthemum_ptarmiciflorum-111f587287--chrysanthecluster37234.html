Etymology

Chrysanthemum ptarmiciflorum mixes Chrysanthemum, a flower name, and ptarmiciflorum, a word linked to 'sneezing'. The plant's hairy, dusty stems and white leaves can make you sneeze, so the name is a handy memory for this dusty white shrub.
