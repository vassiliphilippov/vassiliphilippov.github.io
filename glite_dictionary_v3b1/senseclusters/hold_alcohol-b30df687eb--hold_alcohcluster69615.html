Etymology

hold alcohol may come from the simple image of a container: to hold means 'to keep' something inside, like a cup that holds wine. So if a person's body can hold alcohol, it keeps the drink without showing its effects, which is why the phrase means 'to be able to drink without becoming drunk'.
