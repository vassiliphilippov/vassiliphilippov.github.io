Etymology

tuck for a cloth fold comes from the same idea of folding fabric to make it neat, so a small sewn fold is simply a piece of cloth that has been 'tucked' in. So a tuck in a dress or blouse is a narrow fold that shapes the garment and makes it look tidy.
