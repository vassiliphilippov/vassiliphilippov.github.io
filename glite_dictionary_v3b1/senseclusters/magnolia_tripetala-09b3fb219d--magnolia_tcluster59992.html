Etymology

Magnolia tripetala comes from Latin and gives a simple picture: Magnolia is the tree name and tripetala may mean 'three petals', so imagine a magnolia with noticeable flower parts. That's why the name fits a small tree with large, umbrella-shaped leaves and creamy white flowers.
