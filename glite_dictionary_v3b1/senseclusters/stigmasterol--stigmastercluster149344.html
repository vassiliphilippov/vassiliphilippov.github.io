Etymology

stigmasterol breaks into stigm- and -sterol, and the -sterol part is the same as in cholesterol and steroid, so it tells you this is a cholesterol-like molecule. A helpful way to remember stigm- is to think of 'stigma', a plant part or 'mark', which points to the fact that stigmasterol comes from plants and is a plant sterol found in cell membranes and used in supplements and research.
