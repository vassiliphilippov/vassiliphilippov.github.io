Etymology

weaver's hitch may come from craft workers called weavers who needed a quick knot, a hitch, to fasten a rope to another rope while they made nets or cloth. Because it was easy to make and undo, the name now means a knot used to 'temporarily' tie a rope to the middle of another rope or to an eye.
