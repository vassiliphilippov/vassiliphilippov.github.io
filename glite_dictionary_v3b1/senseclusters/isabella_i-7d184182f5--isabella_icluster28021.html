Etymology

Isabella I was a Spanish queen and the I stands for 'the First', meaning she was the first queen named Isabella. She married Ferdinand and helped unite Spain, sent Columbus to look for new lands, and set up the Spanish Inquisition to try to make people follow one religion, so her name is linked to 'uniting Spain' and 'sponsoring exploration'.
