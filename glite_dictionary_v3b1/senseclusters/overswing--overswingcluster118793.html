Etymology

overswing comes from over- meaning 'too much' and swing meaning 'a movement' or a 'change'. Picture a pendulum that goes past the middle and keeps swinging to the other side — that extra motion is an overswing. So, an overswing in markets or opinion is a move that goes beyond what is reasonable.
