Etymology

Foot-and-mouth disease is named for the places the illness attacks: the foot and the mouth of farm animals like cows and sheep. One clear image is animals with painful 'sores' on their feet and in their mouths, so the name means a 'very contagious' disease that damages animals and spreads quickly.
