Etymology

crystallogen mixes crystal and the ending -gen, which means 'to make' or 'produce', as in oxygen and hydrogen. A helpful way to remember it is that a crystallogen names an element that forms crystal-like structures or behaves like others in the same family, such as carbon and silicon.
