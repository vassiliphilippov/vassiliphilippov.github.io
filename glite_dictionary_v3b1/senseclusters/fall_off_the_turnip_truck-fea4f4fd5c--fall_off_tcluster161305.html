Etymology

The idiom fall off the turnip truck comes from the image of someone literally falling off a farmer's turnip truck, arriving in town as if they are new and do not know how things work. So when people say someone didn't fall off the turnip truck, they mean that person is not 'naive' or 'easy to fool'.
