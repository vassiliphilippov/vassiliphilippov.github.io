Etymology

Entomostraca comes from Greek parts entomo- meaning 'insect' and ostraca meaning 'shells'. So the name suggested 'insect-like shells' and was used for many small, shell-covered water animals called crustaceans, which explains why older books grouped these tiny creatures together even though the name is no longer used.
