Etymology

poppet valve is a kind of valve with a small, mushroom-shaped poppet (a disc) that moves straight up and down to 'open' or 'close' an engine passage. So, the image of the little disc lifting to let gas through and dropping to stop it explains why the name refers to a valve that controls flow.
