Etymology

Lepiota americana is the scientific name for a mushroom and comes from two parts: Lepiota comes from an old Greek word meaning 'scale', because the cap has small scales, and americana means 'from America'. That's why the name points to a pale, scaly mushroom with a stem that is swollen at the base.
