Etymology

Orcinus orca is a scientific name made from older words: Orcinus may come from the Roman god of the underworld, giving a dark, powerful image, and orca was an old word for a large sea animal. Because this whale is black-and-white and a strong hunter, the name fits its fierce 'killer whale' reputation.
