Etymology

The word vicar comes from the Latin vicarius, meaning 'substitute' or 'one who stands in for another'. A helpful connection is vice- as in vice-president, which also means 'in place of', so a vicar is a priest who stands in for or represents a higher church leader and cares for the local church.
