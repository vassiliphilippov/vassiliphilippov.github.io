Etymology

Brioschi is an Italian family name that became the brand name for a fizzy antacid. A helpful way to remember it is the part brio, which in Italian means 'liveliness' or 'energy', like the drink's fizz. So, Brioschi feels like a lively, fizzy remedy that quickly calms an upset stomach.
