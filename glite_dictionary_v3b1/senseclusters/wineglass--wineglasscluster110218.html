Etymology

wineglass is made of two simple words, wine and glass, so it literally means a 'glass for wine'. The part wine comes from an old word for wine that many languages share, and glass is the clear container you drink from. A wineglass often has a bowl on a stem so you can hold it without warming the drink, which is why it is the special glass used to serve wine.
