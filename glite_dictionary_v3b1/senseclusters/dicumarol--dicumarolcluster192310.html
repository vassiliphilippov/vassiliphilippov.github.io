Etymology

dicumarol comes from the parts di- ('two') and coumarin, a plant chemical found in sweet clover. A helpful way to remember it is to think of a 'double' form of coumarin that was found when spoiled clover made animals bleed, so dicumarol became the name for an early blood-thinning drug.
