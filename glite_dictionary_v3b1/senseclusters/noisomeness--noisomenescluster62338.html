Etymology

noisomeness comes from older words that meant 'harm' or 'hurt', not from noise. A helpful way to remember it is to think of noxious, which means 'harmful', because noisomeness is the quality of being unpleasant or harmful to the senses, like a very bad smell or polluted water.
